Lucerne fans in Lugano: road closures, traffic delays

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 8 October 2026

## TL;DR - Saturday: FC Lugano-FC Lucerna at 18 in Cornaredo - Away supporters' procession between 16 and around 17.30 - Station forecourt closed from 15.50 - Expected

Context

TL;DR

  • Saturday: FC Lugano-FC Lucerna at 18 in Cornaredo
  • Away supporters' procession between 16 and approximately 17.30
  • Station forecourt closed from 15.50
  • Estimated waits: 15-30 minutes

Key facts

  • Match → FC Lugano-FC Lucerna, Saturday at 18
  • Location → Cornaredo stadium
  • Initial closure → station forecourt from 15.50
  • Visiting fans' procession → between 16 and approximately 17.30
  • Estimated wait → between 15 and 30 minutes

Next Saturday, at 18, FC Lugano and FC Lucerna will face each other at Cornaredo stadium. The Lugano City Police has announced that the arrival of the visiting fans will affect traffic in the Cornaredo and Molino Nuovo areas, as well as the connection between the FFS station and via San Gottardo.

The procession before the match

The group of Lucerna fans will reach the stadium on foot between 16 and approximately 17.30. The first measure will take effect at 15.50, however, when the forecourt of Lugano station will be closed to public transport. For anyone who needs to pass through that point, the key time to bear in mind is therefore the closure time, not just kick-off.

From the FFS station, the fans will proceed north along via San Gottardo, on the stretch between the Besso tunnel and via Tesserete; from there, the procession will take via Tesserete, via Torricelli and via Trevano, to reach the south car park of Cornaredo stadium. The passage of the supporters will entail the complete closure of the roads concerned, with waiting times expected to be between 15 and 30 minutes.

The return and questions about costs

The restrictions will be reinstated at the end of the match. At around 20, the visiting fans will leave Cornaredo for the FFS station along the same route. The city police also reports possible slowdowns on via Ciani. Depending on the influx of local fans, the road could be partially closed.

The notice thus highlights two distinct moments: the transfer to the stadium in the afternoon and the return journey after the match. Residents are advised to plan alternative journeys during the hours affected by the closures. For public transport, the point to bear in mind is the measure concerning the station forecourt. On the political front, Tamara Merlo, a municipal councillor from Più Donne, has submitted an interpellation to the Municipal Council. She asks about the extent of the traffic restrictions and, above all, how much the security operations, traffic management, cleaning and restoration of public space cost each time. She also asks whether part of the expense is invoiced to FC Lugano. The Municipal Council’s responses are expected.

Operational details

For a cross-border commuter who needs to get to Lugano in the afternoon, the connection is real but limited: the journey may coincide with one of the time windows indicated by the Police. The news identifies a specific area, the connection between the station and the stadium, and a second moment of possible criticality after the match.

How to read the time windows

The sequence has three useful stages for organizing the day. At 15.50, the measure concerns the station forecourt and public transport. Between 16 and approximately 17.30, the away fans' procession on foot comes into play. On the roads concerned, the blockage is total and the estimated wait is between 15 and 30 minutes. Around 20, the return phase begins, following the same route and with the additional possibility of slowdowns or partial closure on via Ciani, linked to the influx of home fans.

For those who work across the border, the distinction helps avoid confusing three situations. A passage through the station may already be affected before kickoff. A motorist on the procession route must take into account a wait indicated in the notice, rather than treating it as a random delay. Anyone traveling after the match must also consider the variable of via Ciani, which the Police presents as possible, not automatic.

The practical criterion is to check whether one's journey coincides with the hours of the closures. If it does, the public is invited to plan alternative journeys. Therefore, a cross-border commuter should not turn the procession route into a traffic recommendation: it is a list of roads subject to blockages. Frequently Asked Questions
At what time does the station forecourt close, and what does this mean for public transportation?
The forecourt of Lugano station will be closed to public transport starting at 15:50, as announced by the city police. This closure affects buses and other services that pass through the area, so anyone who needs to use public transport must take the closing time into account and not just the kickoff time at 18:00.
How long a wait should be expected during the procession of Lucerna fans?
During the walking procession of visiting fans, scheduled between approximately 16:00 and 17:30, a total traffic block will be imposed on the roads concerned (via San Gottardo, the section between the Besso tunnel and via Tesserete, then via Tesserete, via Torricelli and via Trevano up to the stadium's south parking lot). The estimated wait is between 15 and 30 minutes.
What are the possible limitations on the return after the match, and where can they occur?
After the match, at around 20:00, Lucerna fans will leave Cornaredo Stadium for the FFS station, following the same route as on the way there. The Police warns of possible delays on via Ciani and, depending on the turnout of local fans, a partial closure of that road. It is recommended to check the situation in real time before setting off.
