What is the objective of Renzetti and Rusconi's request regarding teleworking in the cantonal administration?
Renzetti and Rusconi (Plr) asked the Ticino government about teleworking in the cantonal administration with the aim of promoting flexibility and efficiency. They hope that the adoption of these working methods will translate into concrete benefits for taxpayers, optimizing resources and administrative performance in the Canton of Ticino.
In which Swiss canton is the discussion on teleworking taking place in the Public Administration mentioned in the article?
The discussion on teleworking in the Public Administration, as described in the article, takes place specifically in the Canton of Ticino. The request for information was submitted by Renzetti and Rusconi (PLR) to the Ticino government, focusing on the implications and benefits of this working mode for the region and its taxpayers.
What are the positive implications of teleworking in the Cantonal Administration for taxpayers, according to the article?
According to the article, teleworking in the Cantonal Administration could have significant and positive implications for taxpayers. In particular, it is expected to increase the flexibility of operations and improve the efficiency of services. The intention is that these improvements translate into a direct benefit for citizens, both in terms of costs and the quality of public services.
