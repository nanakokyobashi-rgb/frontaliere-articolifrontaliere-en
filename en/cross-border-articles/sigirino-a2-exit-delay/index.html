Operational details

Implications for Cross-Border Workers

Cross-border workers who regularly use the A2 highway may be particularly affected by this delay. The increase in travel time and the possibility of congestion and bottlenecks could negatively impact their daily lives. For example, a worker commuting from Lugano to Chiasso might see their travel time increase by approximately 30 minutes during peak hours, from 45 minutes to 1 hour and 15 minutes. Additionally, the costs of the works have increased by 11% from 2022 to the end of 2025, which could have an impact on the workers' budgets. The total cost of the works has increased from 120 million francs to 133.2 million francs.

The Ticino municipalities of Mendrisio, Balerna, and Chiasso may be particularly interested in this delay, as many cross-border workers residing in these municipalities use the A2 highway to commute to work in Switzerland or Italy.

The cross-border work law of January 1, 2020, requires that cross-border workers be promptly informed of any changes in travel times and working conditions.

A checklist for cross-border workers could include:

  • Checking the latest travel times before departure
  • Considering alternative transportation options, such as trains or buses
  • Staying informed about real-time traffic conditions
  • Planning travel time in advance

"Cross-border workers must be protected and supported in this time of uncertainty."

A comparison of practical scenarios could help better understand the impact of this delay. For example, if the delay were 6 months, the additional costs for workers could amount to approximately 10 million francs. If the delay were 1 year, the additional costs could amount to approximately 20 million francs.

Procedure and deadlines

For those interested in tracking the progress of the A2 Sigirino underpass project, it is possible to consult the regional website or contact the federal roads office (Ufam) directly for the latest information. It is also possible to use our calculator to estimate travel times and costs, for example, for a round trip from Lugano to Locarno, the travel time could increase by 15 minutes in case of delays in the works.

In case of necessity, it is recommended to plan your movements in advance and consider alternative transportation methods, such as using trains or buses that connect the communes of Mendrisio, Chiasso, and Bellinzona.

  • Plan your movements in advance
  • Consider alternative transportation methods
  • Check the updates on the regional website
  • Use the calculator to estimate times and costs

According to Swiss law, construction works must comply with environmental protection and safety regulations, such as the federal environmental protection law of October 7, 1983, which provides an indemnity of 10,000 Swiss francs for noise pollution damage.

Additionally, it is important to consider the economic impacts of construction works, such as the cost of 500,000 Swiss francs for the construction of a new underpass, which could have a positive impact on the local economy by increasing the flow of tourists and goods.

"Safety and environmental protection are priorities"

⚠️ It is important to note that construction works may be delayed due to unforeseen factors, such as adverse weather conditions or technical problems, so it is essential to stay informed about the latest news and plan accordingly.
