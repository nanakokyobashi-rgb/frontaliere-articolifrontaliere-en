  • Amount: Not specified

Sergio Savoia, former coordinator of the Greens, has announced his return to politics by joining Avanti con Ticino&Lavoro. Interviewed by Gianni Righinetti on La domenica del Corriere, Savoia explained that he has been looking at the new political movement with great curiosity. «It’s a new political movement that I’ve been looking at with great curiosity from the start, made by friends and people I know very well and trust. A movement from the bottom up that had characteristics that interested me: not ideological, rather centrist, very focused on problems and not on ideologies. So I thought, if I were to get back into politics, I would go there».

Sergio Savoia's political choice

Sergio Savoia has chosen Avanti con Ticino&Lavoro for the 2027 cantonal elections. The movement was officially presented on Saturday, October 3. «With Sergio, we share a fundamental belief: politics is a service to citizens», emphasized Amalia Mirante. «We are building a strong team with people of different experiences, skills, and sensitivities, but united by the will to work and take responsibility. Every day it becomes more evident that Ticino needs real political change».
