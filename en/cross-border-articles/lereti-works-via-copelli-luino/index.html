Luino: Lereti works on via Copelli until November 27

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 October 2026

Renewal of the water-supply network on via Copelli begins: 400 metres of new pipes and alternating one-way traffic with traffic lights are planned.

Context

TL;DR

  • Lereti works began on Monday, October 5, on via Copelli
  • Replacement of 400 meters of piping planned
  • Alternating one-way traffic controlled by a traffic-light system
  • Indicative completion: November 27, barring unforeseen circumstances

Key facts

  • Work: renewal of the water supply network
  • Section: from the roundabout with via Gorizia to km 17+117 of SP23
  • Pipelines: 200 meters of supply and 200 meters of distribution
  • Connection: one user connection to the new pipes
  • Restoration: road surfacing at the end of the work

The project's third lot

Lereti's construction site for the renewal of the water supply network began on the morning of Monday, October 5, on via Copelli, in Luino. The project is part of the company's investment plan in Varesotto, designed to increase the efficiency of the water service, reduce leakage, and improve infrastructure monitoring through digitalization.

The work is not an isolated intervention. It is the third lot of an overall project launched last year with the pipelines on via Gorizia. At that construction site, one kilometer of new network was laid and, in September, the final resurfacing of the asphalt was completed. The sequence now continues on via Copelli with work connected to the same investment program.

The section concerned lies between the roundabout with via Gorizia and kilometer 17+117 of provincial road 23. The schedule gives November 27 as the completion date, but only indicatively: the source specifies that the timetable remains subject to any operational contingencies or weather-related problems.

The project involves approximately 200 meters of supply pipeline and a further 200 meters of distribution network. It also includes connecting one user to the new pipes. Overall, the replacement covers 400 meters of pipes, in addition to excavation, connection, and the subsequent restoration of the road.

For anyone traveling along via Copelli, including a cross-border commuter who happens to use this section on their journey, the practical reality is the presence of the construction site and traffic-light control. The source does not indicate alternative routes, activation times, or waiting times.
