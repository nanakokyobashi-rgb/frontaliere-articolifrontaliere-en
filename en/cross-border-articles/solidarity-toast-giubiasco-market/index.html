On Thursday 8 October the Giubiasco Covered Market will host «Aperò In Sem», the evening with which the Kiwanis Club Bellinzona e Valli celebrates 40 years of activity. The appointment is scheduled from 6pm to 11pm and is organized in collaboration with the Ticinesi Winegrowers and Winemakers Association, indicated in the source with the acronym AVVT.

What the evening entails

The program focuses on a tasting of a selection of the best Ticino wines, accompanied by local cured meats and cheeses. The gastronomic offer is planned for the whole evening, so as to combine the club's celebratory moment with an event dedicated to the flavors of the territory.

The collaboration with AVVT also links the club's anniversary to the presence of Ticino wines, while local cured meats and cheeses accompany the proposal for the entire evening.

The proceeds will be donated entirely to Ares and L'Ora, two associations committed to supporting the community. The initiative thus combines the Kiwanis anniversary party with a collection in favor of two associations.

Mid-evening will feature risotto prepared by Kiwanian chef Graziano Gemetti. To close the evening will be the traditional bread cake, prepared by the Master Bakers, Pastry Chefs and Confectioners of Ticino, Mesolcina and Calanca. The scan of the program therefore adds two distinct gastronomic moments to the tasting of local wines and products.

A Ticino group will provide musical entertainment. The photographic exhibition "Portraits of denied work" will also be open to visitors in the market spaces, with Nathalie Vigini as curator and the L'ORA Association as promoter.

The choice of the Giubiasco Covered Market brings together tasting, cuisine, music and exhibition in a single space, according to the program indicated for the event. Entrance is set at 40 francs per person. Pre-sale is available on Bigglietteria.ch. The source thus presents an evening with a defined time, a gastronomic and musical program, a photographic exhibition and a precise destination for the proceeds: Ares and L'Ora.

Frequently Asked Questions
When and where does Aperò In Sem take place?
The evening is scheduled for Thursday, October 8 at the Covered Market of Giubiasco. The indicated time is from 6 pm to 11 pm. The event is linked to the 40 years of activity of the Kiwanis Club Bellinzona e Valli and is organised in collaboration with the Ticino Winegrowers Association.
Who organises the evening and to whom does the proceeds go?
The initiative is organized by the Kiwanis Club Bellinzona e Valli to celebrate 40 years of activity, in collaboration with the Ticino Winegrowers Association. The proceeds of the event will be entirely donated to the associations Ares and L'Ora, committed to supporting the community.
What does the Aperò In Sem program include?
Tastings of Ticino wines with cured meats and local cheeses are scheduled throughout the evening. In the middle of the evening, participants will be able to taste the risotto prepared by Graziano Gemetti. The end will be entrusted to the bread cake of the Master Bakers Confetti Chefs of Ticino, Mesolcina and Calanca. There will also be music and a photographic exhibition.
How much does admission cost and where do you buy it?
Admission is set at 40 francs per person. Pre-sale is available at Ticket Office.ch. The source also indicates that the evening takes place from 6 pm to 11 pm at the Giubiasco Covered Market, with tastings, risotto, bread cake, music and a photographic exhibition.
