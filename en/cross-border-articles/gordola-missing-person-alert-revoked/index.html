Gordola: missing-person notice revoked

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 October 2026

Ticino cantonal police have withdrawn the alert for a 41-year-old woman from Gordola, who had been reported missing since October 2. The announcement had been issued on Saturday.

The statement

Ticino Cantonal Police have revoked the missing person announcement concerning a 41-year-old woman from Gordola. The woman had been reported missing since October 2, while the notice had been issued on Saturday. These are the essential details of the update reported by the source.

The report therefore concerns a specific person, a specific place and a specific date. Gordola is the location indicated in the statement; October 2 is the date from which the 41-year-old woman had been reported missing. The reference to Saturday, on the other hand, concerns the dissemination of the announcement. Ticino Cantonal Police report that that announcement has been revoked.

The text adds no other details to the account. The update concerns the announcement already issued and reports its revocation. For local news, the verifiable fact is therefore limited: Ticino Cantonal Police have revoked the notice concerning the 41-year-old woman from Gordola.

The news keeps the same report at the center from beginning to end. The location and age complete the identification provided by the source: Gordola and 41 years old. The text concerning this notice adds no other elements about the case.
