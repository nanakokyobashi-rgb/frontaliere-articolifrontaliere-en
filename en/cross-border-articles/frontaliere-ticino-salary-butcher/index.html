The new rules for border workers in Ticino

The New Frontier Agreement was signed on 1 February 2023 and entered into force on 1 January 2024. This agreement introduced new rules for frontier workers in Ticino, who are subject to Swiss taxes. Border workers can no longer benefit from the tax credit in Italy to avoid double taxation.
