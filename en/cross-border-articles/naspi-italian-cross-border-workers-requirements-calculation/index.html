NASpI for ex-frontiers: requirements, calculation, duration and demand (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 17 July 2026 · Updated on 25 September 2026

Practical guide to the Italian unemployment benefit for those who have lost their job in Switzerland. Contribution requirements, monthly amount, maximum duration and application procedures.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Requirements: 13 weeks of contributions in the last 4 years, Swiss periods included; the 30 days of actual work are no longer required since 2022
  • Calculation: 75% of the average monthly salary up to €1,456.72, plus 25% of the excess (maximum €1,584.70 in 2026), reduced by 3% a month from the sixth month (the eighth for those aged 55 or over)
  • Duration: Up to 24 months (proportional to the contributions paid)
  • Question: Online on INPS within 68 days of the termination of the relationship

Key facts

  • What: NASpI Allowance (New Social Insurance for Employment)
  • Who: Managed by INPS for workers in Italy and for cross-border workers resident in Italy, even without Italian contributions (Art. 65 of Regulation (EC) 883/2004)
  • Minimum requirement: 13 weeks of contributions in the previous 4 years
  • Maximum amount 2026: €1,584.70 per month
  • Maximum duration: 24 months (reduced for those with fewer contributions)
  • Application deadline: 68 days from job loss

The NASpI allowance is the main form of income support for employees who lose their jobs through no fault of their own. For former Italy-Switzerland border workers resident in Italy, access does not depend only on Italian contributions: under Article 65 of Regulation (EC) 883/2004 the benefit is paid by INPS as the State of residence, which also counts the Swiss insurance periods certified by the portable document U1 and calculates the amount on the Swiss salary. Cross-border pension planning remains crucial.

Essential Requirements

To access the NASpI you need: 1. Status of involuntary unemployment: Dismissal, expiry of contract, resignation for just cause 2. Sufficient contributions: At least 13 weeks of contributions in the 4 years prior to unemployment, including periods worked in Switzerland by cross-border workers resident in Italy

The requirement of 30 days of actual work in the previous 12 months no longer applies to terminations from 1 January 2022.

Practical scenarios for former frontiersmen

Case 1: Cross-border worker resident in Italy with 30 months of work in Ticino (without parallel INPS contributions) → Entitled to NASpI: INPS counts the Swiss periods (about 130 weeks) and pays it for about 65 weeks, i.e. half. The Swiss allowance is not paid, because for a wholly unemployed cross-border worker the State of residence pays (Art. 65 of Regulation (EC) 883/2004).

Case 2: Worker with 6 months in Italy (about 26 weeks of contributions) + 2 years in Switzerland as a cross-border worker resident in Italy → If all the periods fall within the previous 4 years, they add up to about 130 weeks and give entitlement to NASpI for about 65 weeks, i.e. half the contribution weeks.

Case 3: Unemployed person who worked 12 months in Lombardy before becoming a border worker → Can apply for NASpI if, adding Italian and Swiss periods together, they have at least 13 weeks of contributions in the 4 years before unemployment.

⚠️ Note: periods worked in Switzerland are added to Italian ones for the contribution requirement, but NASpI and the Swiss allowance cannot be combined: for a wholly unemployed cross-border worker only the State of residence pays. Article 7 of Law 83/2023, which ratified the new Italy-Switzerland tax agreement, provides for an amount equal to the Swiss benefit during the first three months where that is higher, but as of September 2026 the measure is not yet applied, pending the implementing circular.

Key points

How to apply

Follow these steps to apply for NASpI: 1. Employment Center Registration : Mandatory within 68 days of job loss (online or at Italian CPI) 2. Documentation: Prepare employment contract, dismissal letter, service certificate 3. Online question: Access the INPS portal with SPID/CNS, fill in the NASpI form 4. Availability statement: Immediate confirmation that you are ready for new job opportunities

Useful 📊 tool: Use the calcolatore di stipendio netto to estimate the tax impact if you accept a new job in Ticino.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Delay CPI registration (loses days of compensation)
  • Do not update the unemployment status on INPS (suspension of payments risk)
  • Claim NASpI and Swiss allowance at the same time (they cannot be combined: for a wholly unemployed cross-border worker only the State of residence pays)

What happens after the question?

INPS reviews the requirements and communicates the outcome within 90 days. In case of acceptance:

  • The allowance is due from the eighth day after the end of the employment relationship or, if the application is filed later, from the day after the application
  • Monthly payments postponed via bank transfer
  • Obligation to participate in training/relocation initiatives

💡 Tip: Keep all INPS and CPI communications. Report income changes or new employment now.

Frequently Asked Questions
Can I apply for the NASpI if I only worked 6 months in Italy before becoming a border worker?
Yes. You need at least 13 weeks of contributions in the 4 years before unemployment, and periods worked in Switzerland also count if you lived in Italy during that job (Art. 65 of Regulation (EC) 883/2004). Since 2022 the 30 days of actual work are no longer required.
Is the NASpI allowance taxed in Switzerland?
No, the NASpI is provided by the Italian INPS and taxed in Italy. In Switzerland it is not considered income from work, so it is not subject to tax at source.
Can I accumulate NASpI and Swiss allowance?
No, it is not possible to receive both at the same time, and a wholly unemployed cross-border worker cannot choose either: under Article 65 of Regulation (EC) 883/2004 the benefit is paid by the State of residence, i.e. INPS if you lived in Italy during your last job.
