Working as a tile maker in Ticino: salary, requirements and title recognition

If you are a tile maker interested in working in Ticino, it is important to know the rules and requirements necessary to work as a border worker. In this article, we will provide you with useful information on how to work as a tile maker in Ticino, including your salary possibilities and how to recognize your professional title.

Requirements to work as a tile maker in Ticino

To work as a tile maker in Ticino, you have to meet some requirements. First, you must be a national of an EU Member State or a country with which Switzerland has a bilateral agreement. In addition, you must have an employment contract with an employer in Switzerland and you must be able to demonstrate that you have the necessary skills to perform the job of tile maker.

Permissions and authorizations

As a border tile maker, you do not need a G permit to work in Ticino. However, if you want to work as a tile maker in a sector other than frontier work, for example as a tile maker in a construction company, you may need to apply for a G permit.

Working as a tile maker in Ticino: salary, requirements and title recognition

INTRODUCTION

The New Frontier Agreement, which entered into force on 23 December 2020, has opened up new job opportunities for border workers in Ticino. Among these, the possibility of working as a tile maker, an ever-expanding sector in the Canton of Ticino.

SALARY AND REQUIREMENTS

The minimum wage for a tile maker in Ticino is CHF 4,500,- per month, as established by the Collective Agreement of 15 February 2018, valid until 31 December 2022. However, it is important to note that salaries may vary depending on the municipality and the company.

To work as a tile maker in Ticino, you need to have a recognized professional qualification, such as the tile maker certificate issued by Switzerland or a country that is a member of the Frontier Agreement. In addition, it is necessary to have a good knowledge of the Italian language, since it is the official language of the Canton of Ticino.

TITLE RECOGNITION

The New Frontier Agreement provides for the automatic recognition of professional qualifications issued in the member countries of the agreement. This means that a certified tile maker in Italy, for example, can work in Ticino without having to take further examinations or qualification tests.

Key points

The New Frontier Agreement

The New Frontier Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020 and is in force from 1 January 2024. This agreement provides that border workers can work in Ticino without a G permit, but with certain specific conditions and requirements.

Requirements for border tile workers in Ticino

To work as a tile maker in Ticino as a border worker, you must have the qualification of tile maker and the G permit. In addition, the presence of an employment contract stipulated with an employer registered in the register of companies of the Canton of Ticino is required.

The employment contract must be concluded in a language understandable by both parties and must contain information relating to remuneration, working times and holidays. In addition, the employer must be able to demonstrate that they have the necessary insurance for frontier workers.

Possible recognition of the qualification

The qualification of tile maker is recognized in Ticino, but it is necessary to verify the specifications required for the work in question. According to the regulations in force, the qualification of tile maker must be issued by a training institution recognized by the Canton of Ticino.

Concrete examples

For example, a border tile worker working in a company in Locarno may have an employment contract with an employer registered in the business register of the Canton of Ticino.

Frequently Asked Questions
When did the New Frontier Agreement between Switzerland and Italy come into force?
The New Frontier Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020 and entered into force on 1 January 2024. This agreement establishes regulations for cross-border workers, simplifying procedures and defining requirements for those who wish to work in Ticino, such as tile workers.
Does a border tile maker need a G permit to work in Ticino?
No, a tile worker working as a border worker in Ticino does not need a G permit. This permit is only required if you intend to work in sectors other than the specific one of 'border work', for example as a tile worker in a construction company that does not fall within the definition.
What is the average salary of a tile maker working in Ticino?
The average salary of a tile maker in Ticino is around CHF 4,500 per month. The minimum wage established by the Collective Agreement is CHF 4,500,- per month. With more experience (e.g. 5 years), you can reach about CHF 5,500/month, or even CHF 6,000 in municipalities such as Lugano or Bellinzona.
How does professional title recognition work for an Italian tile maker in Switzerland?
The New Frontier Agreement provides for the automatic recognition of professional qualifications issued in the member countries of the agreement. This means that a qualified tile maker in Italy can work in Ticino without having to take further examinations or qualification tests, as long as they demonstrate the necessary skills for the profession.
