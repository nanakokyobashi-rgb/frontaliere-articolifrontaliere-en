Accident on A9 near Turate: two injured at dawn (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 May 2026

Two people injured in an accident on the A9 motorway before the Turate exit. Firefighters, 118, and highway police intervened.

Key Facts

  • Where: A9 Highway, Turate exit
  • Who: Firefighters, 118, traffic police
  • Amount: Two injured, four people involved

In the early hours of May 3, 2026, around 6:00 AM, a road accident occurred on the A9 highway before the Turate exit. Two vehicles were involved in the incident, with a total of four people on board, all male. Two of the four people were injured. Firefighters, 118, and traffic police intervened at the scene to manage the emergency and assist the injured.
