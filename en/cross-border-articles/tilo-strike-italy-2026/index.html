National strike in Italy from 7 to 8 September 2026: repercussions on TILO CONNECTIONS

Context

In a nutshell

  • National strike in Italy from 7 to 8 September 2026
  • Impact on TILO CONNECTIONS
  • TILO services stopped in Italy, not in Switzerland

Key facts

  • What: National strike in Italy
  • When: From 7 to 8 September 2026
  • Where: Italy
  • Who: Frontier workers using TILO SERVICES
  • Amount: Not specified

A national strike planned in Italy between Monday 7 and Tuesday 8 September 2026 will have repercussions on TILO connections. Agitation is scheduled from 9.18 pm on Monday to 9.00 pm on Tuesday. During this period the TILO RE80, S10, S30, S40 and S50 services will not circulate in Italy, while the TILO connections in Swiss territory will not be involved.

If there are no trains on line S50, the TILO connections to and from Malpensa Airport will be replaced by buses between Stabio and Malpensa Airport, without intermediate stops. The guarantee time slots remain in force, from 06.00 to 09.00 and from 18.00 to 21.00. In these intervals, the links included in the list of 'Minimum Guaranteed Services', which can be consulted on the Trenord website, will circulate.

Possible repercussions are foreseen until the end of the strike. Travellers are therefore advised to pay attention to the announcements and directions circulated at stations and on board trains.

Operational details

Impact on Frontiersmen

The strike will have a significant impact on border workers who use TILO services to move between Italy and Switzerland. Border workers must plan their journeys carefully, taking into account the guarantee time slots and possible replacements with buses. It is important to regularly consult the announcements and indications issued at the stations and on board the trains to be updated on any changes to the services.

Minimum Guaranteed Services

During the guarantee time slots, from 06.00 to 09.00 and from 18.00 to 21.00, the connections included in the list of 'Minimum Guaranteed Services' will circulate. This list is available on the Trenord website and provides detailed information on the trains that will continue to run despite the strike. Border crossers are invited to check the list and plan their movements accordingly.

Substitute Means

If there are no trains on line S50, the TILO connections to and from Malpensa Airport will be replaced by buses between Stabio and Malpensa Airport, without intermediate stops. This replacement service has been arranged to ensure continuity of transport to the airport, albeit with some limitations.

Key points

What to Do During the Strike

During the strike, border workers must pay particular attention to the announcements and indications circulated in the stations and on board the trains. It is advisable to regularly consult the Trenord website to be updated on any changes to the services and to know the list of 'Minimum Guaranteed Services'. If necessary, you can use alternative means such as replacement buses or other public transport services.

Planning Movements

For border workers who have to move during the strike, it is essential to follow the instructions provided by the authorities and TILO service managers. It is advisable to plan any trips in advance and check the guarantee time slots to make sure you can reach your destination without problems.

Conclusion

The national strike in Italy will have a significant impact on TILO services, but with adequate planning and attention to the indications provided, border workers will be able to face any inconvenience with serenity. It is important to remain informed and flexible in order to deal with any changes to transport services.
