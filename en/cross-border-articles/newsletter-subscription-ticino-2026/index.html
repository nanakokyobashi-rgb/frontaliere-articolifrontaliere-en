In brief

  • Discover the dynamics between Switzerland and Italy

Key facts

  • What: Free newsletter subscription
  • When: Available immediately
  • Where: Online on TVS
  • Who: TVS
  • Cost: Free

TVS's newsletter is a simple and free way to stay updated on the latest news regarding work, the economy, and life in Ticino. With various frequency options, you can choose to receive daily, weekly, or monthly updates directly in your email inbox. This service is particularly useful for cross-border workers who want to stay informed about the dynamics between Switzerland and Italy, including economic, political, and cultural relations.

How to subscribe

To subscribe to the newsletter, visit TVS's website and enter your email address. Select your preferred frequency from the available options: daily, weekly, or monthly. After completing the subscription process, you will receive a confirmation email. Once you confirm your subscription, you will start receiving the latest news and insights regularly.

Frequency options

The newsletter offers different frequency options to suit your needs:

  • Daily: Receive a summary of the latest news every working day.
  • Weekly: Discover the most popular services of the week.
  • Monthly: A selection of articles to travel and discover curiosities about Switzerland.

Exclusive content

In addition to general news, the newsletter includes specific sections such as 'Italian-Swiss relations' and 'Work and economy', which explore in detail the cross-border dynamics and job opportunities in Ticino. This content is particularly useful for cross-border workers who want to delve into the practical implications of the relations between Italy and Switzerland.

Concrete actions for cross-border workers

To make the most of TVS's newsletter, it's important to follow some concrete steps. First, make sure to subscribe to the newsletter by choosing the frequency that best suits your needs. If you want to receive daily updates, select the 'Daily' option. If you prefer a weekly summary, opt for the 'Weekly' option. Finally, confirm your subscription by clicking on the confirmation link you will receive via email.

Deadlines and procedures

Once subscribed, you will regularly receive the latest news and in-depth analysis directly in your email inbox. Make sure to check your email regularly to avoid missing important information. Additionally, if you need assistance, you can contact TVS support to get help.

Site tools
