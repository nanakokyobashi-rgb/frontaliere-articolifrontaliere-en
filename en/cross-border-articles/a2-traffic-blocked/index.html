Motorists have been stuck on the A2 for three to four hours and are piling up on the roadway. Some drivers wrote on social media that they had been blocked for hours and had no water or food. Frustration is palpable.

According to data from the Department of Transportation and Communications, traffic on the A2 increased by 30% compared to the same period last year. This is partly due to the increase in the number of motorists using the road to reach Switzerland, but also to the increase in the number of trucks transporting goods.

The A2 has been closed over 100 times in the last two years due to accidents or technical problems. This results in an average wait of more than two hours for motorists, highlighting a recurring criticality for border crossers and other travellers using this route.
Are there plans to improve roads on the A2 or build new infrastructure?
Yes, Switzerland has passed legislation for the construction of a new highway that will connect Switzerland to Italy. This project involves an investment of 1.5 billion Swiss francs and is expected to be completed by 2025, with the aim of alleviating the current congestion.
What practical tips can I follow to avoid getting stuck on the A2 or handling the situation?
