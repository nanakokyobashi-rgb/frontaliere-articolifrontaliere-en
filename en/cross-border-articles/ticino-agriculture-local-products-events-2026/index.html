Ticino farmers demand more local products at events (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 May 2026

Delegates from the Ticino Farmers' Union call for more local products at events, concerned about costs and predators

Context

In brief

  • Ticino agriculture calls for more local products at events
  • Concerns over international conflicts and production costs
  • Great concern over predators like wolves

Key facts

  • What: Request for more local products at events
  • When: 3 May 2026
  • Where: Canton Ticino
  • Who: Delegates of the Ticino Farmers' Union
  • Amount: Not specified

Ticino agriculture is on alert. Delegates from entities affiliated with the Ticino Farmers' Union have raised concerns about the need to promote local products more during events. The current situation, characterized by international conflicts, high production costs, and the presence of large predators such as wolves, is putting the agricultural sector to the test.

Current concerns

Delegates have expressed particular concern about the impact of large predators, such as wolves, on livestock and crops. This problem, combined with international conflicts and rising production costs, is making it increasingly difficult for Ticino farmers to maintain their activities.

The request for greater presence of local products at events is seen as a way to support the agricultural sector and promote local production. Events, in fact, represent an important opportunity to showcase Ticino products and increase their visibility in the market.

Impact on cross-border workers

For cross-border workers who work in the agricultural sector or consume local products, this request could mean greater availability of Ticino products at the events they attend. This could have a positive impact on the quality and variety of products available, improving the overall event experience.

Operational details

Practical Analysis

The request for greater presence of local products at events in Ticino raises several practical implications for farmers, consumers, and event organizers. Let's see in detail what could change and what challenges need to be addressed.

Promotion of Local Products

Promoting local products during events could have a significant impact on the visibility and demand for these products. Events represent a unique opportunity to introduce Ticino products to a wider audience and create new sales opportunities.

However, to ensure the success of this initiative, it is essential that event organizers collaborate with local farmers. This may require the creation of partnerships and collaboration agreements to ensure the availability and quality of the products.

Logistical Challenges

One of the main obstacles to promoting local products at events is logistical challenges. Farmers must be able to provide products in a timely manner and ensure their quality. This may require investments in infrastructure and logistics, as well as greater collaboration among the various stakeholders.

Economic Impact

Promoting local products could have a positive impact on the Ticino economy. By increasing the demand for local agricultural products, new job opportunities could be created and local businesses supported. This could translate into an increase in agricultural production and an improvement in working conditions for farmers.

Key points

Concrete actions

For cross-border workers who want to support the initiative to promote local products at events, there are several concrete actions they can take. Here are some practical steps to contribute to this cause.

Attend local events

One of the simplest and most immediate actions is to attend local events that promote Ticino's agricultural products. This not only supports local farmers but also offers the opportunity to discover and taste high-quality products.

Buy local products

Another way to support the initiative is to buy local products during events. This helps create demand for Ticinese products and supports the local economy. Additionally, buying local products can have a positive impact on the environment, reducing the carbon footprint associated with transporting products.

Collaborate with event organizers

If you are an event organizer or a farmer, you can collaborate with others to promote local products. This could include creating partnerships and collaboration agreements to ensure the availability and quality of products.

Use calculation tools

To better understand the economic impact of promoting local products, you can use calculation tools such as the salary calculator. This will help you evaluate the financial implications and make informed decisions.

Conclusion

Supporting the initiative to promote local products at events is a concrete way to contribute to the well-being of the Ticinese agricultural sector and to improve the quality of life for cross-border workers. By attending events, buying local products, and collaborating with organizers, you can make a difference and support the local economy.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the main agricultural products of Ticino?
The main agricultural products of Ticino include wines, cheeses, meat, fruit, and vegetables. These products are known for their quality and are highly appreciated both locally and internationally.
How can I find events that promote local products in Ticino?
You can find events that promote local products in Ticino by consulting the websites of local event organizers, social media pages, and event calendars. Additionally, you can ask for information at local product stores and agricultural associations.
What are the benefits of buying local products?
Buying local products has several benefits, including supporting the local economy, reducing the carbon footprint, and ensuring fresh, high-quality products. Additionally, buying local products can help preserve biodiversity and support local agricultural traditions.
