On the occasion of World Asthma Day 2026, the Pediatric Pulmonology and Allergology Clinic at F. Del Ponte Hospital in Varese, in collaboration with the Municipality of Varese and the Italian Society of Pediatric Respiratory Diseases (SIMRI), is promoting an initiative dedicated to the prevention and early diagnosis of asthma in young children. On Tuesday, May 5, 2026, from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM, free pediatric spirometry and oximetry will be available at Palazzo Estense. During the event, informational leaflets with useful tips for recognizing asthma symptoms in children, created by SIMRI, will also be distributed.

Access is open to all and free of charge. This initiative aligns with World Asthma Day, celebrated annually on the first Tuesday of May, which in 2026 falls on May 5. The theme for this year’s observance, “Access to anti-inflammatory inhalers for everyone with asthma – still an urgent need,” emphasizes the importance of ensuring proper and timely treatment for all patients.

The Importance of Early Diagnosis

Bronchial asthma is one of the most common chronic conditions in childhood, affecting about 1 in 10 children in Western countries. In Italy, the prevalence is around 10% among the pediatric population. The condition significantly impacts children’s quality of life and that of their families, and it accounts for substantial healthcare costs, school absences, hospitalizations, and emergency room visits. Accurate and early diagnosis is essential for effective management of the disease. Symptoms such as breathing difficulties, wheezing, chest tightness, and dry cough should prompt a spirometry test, a simple, non-invasive exam that can be performed even in children aged 5-6 and older.

What to Do If Your Child Shows Symptoms of Asthma

If your child exhibits symptoms such as difficulty breathing, wheezing, a feeling of chest tightness, or persistent dry cough, it is crucial to promptly consult your trusted pediatrician. Early diagnosis allows for better management of the condition and helps prevent more serious crises. Spirometry is a key tool: a simple, non-invasive test that provides valuable data to confirm asthma and monitor treatment effectiveness. In case of acute symptoms or severe breathing difficulty, it is essential to go immediately to the nearest Emergency Room, preferably at facilities like the Regional Hospital of Lugano or the Varese Polyclinic, which collaborate with pulmonology specialists.

Remember that childhood asthma can be effectively managed with an appropriate treatment plan, reducing the risk of complications.
