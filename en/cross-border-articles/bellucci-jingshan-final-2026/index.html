Mattia Bellucci falls one title short in Jingshan

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 5 October 2026

Bellucci loses the Jingshan Open final to Harris in the decisive tie-break after failing to convert two match points. The Varese native rises to No. 86 in the ATP rankings.

Mattia Bellucci lost the final of the Jingshan Open in China, a Challenger tournament played on hard court. The player from Varese was defeated by South African Lloyd Harris after 2 hours and 20 minutes, at the end of a match decided only in the third-set tiebreak.

The turning point came when the score was 6-5 to Bellucci. The Castellanza player, who served first in the deciding set, earned two consecutive match points on Harris’s serve. The experienced South African saved both and took the final to a tiebreak, which he closed out 7-4. The difference was therefore three points, in a set without a break.

The match set by set

The start had put Bellucci on course for the fifth Challenger title of his career. The left-hander won the first set 6-1, with tennis described as aggressive and allowing the opponent no chances. Harris, however, responded in the second set, winning 6-3 and restoring balance to the final.

The Cape Town native brought his experience onto the court: in his career he has also beaten Nadal and was ranked number 31 in the world. In the third set, neither player managed to break serve; Bellucci had the bigger opportunities, but did not close out the match when he had the chance. In the tiebreak, Harris took the lead and held it until 7-4.

For Bellucci, this is the second Challenger final he has lost in 2026, after the one in Cap Cana against Navone in the spring. The result does not erase his run in China: the player from Varese joined the tournament at the last moment and had to come through qualifying. His victories in Jingshan earned him 50 ATP points. From Monday, according to the source, he should be number 86 in the rankings. The match report thus leaves a bitter scoreline, but also a concrete rise in the rankings and a complete run that began in qualifying.
