Working as a border worker in Ticino: what does it mean?

If you are a cross-border worker living in La Thuile and working in Valais, you have probably heard of the New Frontier Agreement. This agreement, signed on 23 December 2020, entered into force on 1 January 2024 and changes the rules for cross-border workers.

Tax at source: how does it work?

Tax at source is a tax that is withheld from workers' wages. In Switzerland, this tax is only withheld for cross-border workers. The New Frontier Agreement provides that this tax is only withheld in Switzerland, instead of being paid in both Italy and Switzerland.

Concrete example: how does the deductible work?

Let's say you are a cross-border worker earning 4,000 euros per month. The deductible for new frontier workers is 10,000 euros per year. This means that if your annual income is less than €10,000, you will not pay the Tax at source. In our example, your annual income would be 48,000 euros (4,000 euros per month x 12 months), so you would pay the Tax at source only on sums over 10,000 euros.

Live in La Thuile and work in Valais as a border worker: the advantages

Living in La Thuile and working in Valais as a border worker can be a great choice for those looking to save money and enjoy a quieter life. La Thuile, a municipality in the province of Aosta, is an ideal place for those who love nature and tranquility. Valais, on the other hand, offers a variety of job opportunities, thanks to its solid and dynamic economy.

How the deductible works

The deductible is a tax benefit that allows frontier workers to avoid paying taxes on their income. To take advantage of this allowance, frontier workers must reside in Switzerland for at least 183 days per year. The deductible is 10,000 euros for new frontier workers and 7,500 euros for old frontier workers.

Concrete examples

Suppose a frontier worker resides in La Thuile and works in Valais. If he earns €50,000 per year, he could take advantage of the €10,000 deductible and only pay taxes on the remaining income (€40,000). This would mean a saving of about 10,000 euros per year.
