Lugano beats Bienne 3-2, third positive result

On 10 October 2026 Lugano conquered the Tissot Arena in Bienne, prevailing 3-2 in a tight match decided in the third period. For the bianconeri it is the third consecutive useful result: after the win in Lausanne and the point earned the day before at home against Zurich, they gain another three points.

Robert Mayer guarded Lugano's goal, while Olle Lycksell took the place of Rasmus Kupari. The first period ended goalless, despite chances created by both sides. The game opened at 23'47'', when Skinner capitalised on an assist from Andersson to score 1-0 and his sixth goal of the season.

Five minutes later, Innala equalised with the help of Fazzini, taking advantage of a mistake by the Bernese defence. At 30'15'' Tanner put Lugano ahead for the first time, on a pass from Olsson. Bienne replied at 38'12'' with Sever, assisted by Haas, making it 2-2 before the second break.

The decisive goal

In the final period the turning point came at 50'42''. Innala scored his brace after an assist from Thürkauf, taking him to seven seasonal goals. Lugano then protected the lead until the final buzzer, managing patiently a frantic finish.

The sequence of goals is therefore 1-0, 1-1, 1-2, 2-2 and 2-3. The result brings Lugano's tally to seven points in the last three games and confirms the progress of recent outings, after a subdued start to the season. The match was played in front of 5'713 spectators. The referees were Piechaczek and Hungerbühler, with Stalder and Humair overseeing. The report notes 2x2' of penalties for Bienne and 3x2' for Lugano. The decisive goal closed a back-and-forth that opened in the second third: Lugano had first equalised, then taken the lead, subsequently conceded 2-2 and finally responded again.
