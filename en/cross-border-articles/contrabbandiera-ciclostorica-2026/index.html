La Contrabbandiera 2026: vintage cycling across Italy and Switzerland (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 May 2026

On Sunday, May 24, 2026, in Uggiate with Ronago, the vintage cycling event La Contrabbandiera will take place, combining a passion for classic cycling and border landscapes.

Context

In brief

  • La Contrabbandiera 2026 will take place on Sunday, May 24, in Uggiate con Ronago.
  • A 45 km route with 500 meters of positive elevation gain.
  • Event coinciding with the 12th gathering of vintage vehicles.

Key facts

  • What: Ciclostorica La Contrabbandiera 2026.
  • When: Sunday, May 24, 2026, at 9:00 AM.
  • Where: Departure from Piazzale Europa, Uggiate con Ronago.
  • Who: Organized by BMX Ciclistica Olgiatese ASD.
  • Cost: Not specified.

Get ready with wool jerseys and shoulder holsters: the vintage cycling event that touches the border area between Como, Varese, and Ticino is back. The 2026 edition will start on Sunday, May 24, at 9:00 AM from Piazzale Europa in Uggiate con Ronago, coinciding with the 12th gathering of vintage vehicles organized by the association 'I röd vècc de fruntiera'. The route will cover 45 km with 500 meters of positive elevation gain, passing through places of great historical and landscape charm: from Venegono Superiore to the ancient village of Castiglione Olona, from the suggestive Molera quarries to the Trotto Mill, and then returning to Uggiate con Ronago along the old Valmorea railway track. As per tradition, several refreshment points will be set up along the route. At the end of the ride, participants can join the friends of the automotive gathering for a convivial lunch.

La Contrabbandiera 2026 represents a unique opportunity for cross-border workers who live and work between Italy and Switzerland. The event not only celebrates the passion for vintage cycling but also offers a chance to explore the border landscapes, often overlooked in daily life. For cross-border workers who love cycling, this event is a way to connect with the local community and discover new cycling routes.

Comparison with Similar Events

Compared to other cycling events, La Contrabbandiera stands out for its focus on historical and scenic aspects. While other races may concentrate on competition, this vintage cycling event emphasizes the experience and fun, making it accessible to cyclists of all levels. Additionally, the combination with the vintage vehicle gathering adds an extra layer of interest for enthusiasts of vintage mobility.

Registration Procedure

To participate in the ciclostorica La Contrabbandiera 2026, you need to follow a simple registration procedure. Here are the steps to follow: 1. Contact the Organizer: Send a registration request to BMX Ciclistica Olgiatese ASD in Olgiate Comasco. 2. Fill out the Form: Complete the registration form provided by the organizer. 3. Pay the Fee: Make the payment of the participation fee, if applicable. 4. Confirmation of Participation: Receive the confirmation of participation and the final instructions.

Preparing the Bicycle

Ensure that the vintage bicycle is in good condition and ready for the route. Here are some tips:

  • General Check: Verify that all components of the bicycle are in good condition.
  • Tires and Brakes: Check the tire pressure and the efficiency of the brakes.
  • Chain and Gears: Make sure the chain and gears work correctly.
  • Lighting: Verify that the lights work correctly, especially if you plan to ride in the dark.

Vintage Attire

To respect the theme of the event, it is important to wear vintage-style clothing. Here are some suggestions:

  • Wool Jerseys: Wear wool jerseys for an authentic look.
  • Crossbody Bag: Use a crossbody bag to carry personal items.
  • Helmet: Wear a vintage helmet for safety.
  • Shoes: Use vintage shoes compatible with the bicycle pedals.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the route of the vintage bike ride La Contrabbandiera 2026?
The route of the vintage bike ride La Contrabbandiera 2026 covers 45 km with 500 meters of positive elevation gain, starting from Piazzale Europa in Uggiate with Ronago and passing through places of great historical and scenic charm such as Venegono Superiore, the ancient village of Castiglione Olona, the Molera Quarries, and the Molino del Trotto, before returning to Uggiate with Ronago along the old Valmorea railway track.
What is required to participate in the vintage bike ride?
To participate in the vintage bike ride La Contrabbandiera 2026, the use of vintage bicycles and vintage-style clothing is required. You can contact the BMX Ciclistica Olgiatese ASD in Olgiate Comasco for information and registrations.
Will there be refreshment points along the route?
Yes, several refreshment points will be set up along the route where participants can rest and refuel. At the end of the bike ride, a convivial lunch is planned in collaboration with the friends of the automotive rally.
