App against depression reimbursed by health insurance: how it works (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 13 May 2026

From July 1st, 2026, the Deprexis app will be reimbursed by Swiss health insurers. Here's what changes for Ticino cross-border workers

Context

In brief

  • App Deprexis reimbursed by health insurers from July 1, 2026
  • Cost of 300 euros for 3 months, approximately 1,200 euros per year
  • In Ticino, depression cases increased by 12.5% in 2025

Key facts

  • What: App Deprexis reimbursed by health insurers
  • When: From July 1, 2026
  • Where: Switzerland, including cross-border workers in Ticino
  • Who: Federal Office of Public Health (FOPH), GAIA
  • Amount: 300 euros for 3 months, approximately 1,200 euros per year

From July 1, 2026, the German app Deprexis can be prescribed like a drug and reimbursed by Swiss health insurers. This tool, approved by the Federal Office of Public Health (FOPH), represents a significant innovation in the field of psychological therapies. Deprexis, developed by the German company GAIA, is already in use in several countries and will also be available to cross-border workers in Ticino.

In Ticino, depression cases increased by 12.5% in 2025, with quarterly peaks around +17%. This data underscores the importance of additional tools to address depression, especially in a region with a large cross-border population.

How Deprexis works

Deprexis is a mobile or laptop application that offers personalized therapeutic support. It does not replace traditional therapy but can be a valuable additional tool. The app was developed based on clinical studies that demonstrated its effectiveness. However, it is important to emphasize that Deprexis should not replace traditional therapy but integrate with it.

Operational details

Practical Analysis

The introduction of Deprexis reimbursed by Swiss health insurers raises several practical questions for Ticino cross-border workers. Here are some aspects to consider:

Prescription Procedure

At the moment, it is not yet clear whether specific prescriptions for Deprexis will be necessary or if general prescriptions for psychotherapy will cover this type of hybrid therapy. Gabriele Barone, psychologist and psychotherapist at the OSC Psychology Service, stated that the situation is not yet very clear and that there is no official communication from the health insurers.

Access and Use

Deprexis offers immediate and location-independent access to personalized therapy. This can be particularly useful for cross-border workers who live in Italy and work in Switzerland, as they can access therapy without having to travel. However, it is important to consider that the app does not replace traditional therapy and that the cost remains significant.

Comparison with Traditional Therapy

Deprexis can be a valid additional tool for addressing depression, but it does not replace traditional therapy. It is important that cross-border workers consult their doctor or psychologist to assess whether Deprexis is a suitable tool for their needs. Furthermore, it is crucial to continue with traditional therapy to achieve the best results.

Practical Scenarios

Suppose a Ticino cross-border worker is already undergoing psychological therapy. In this case, it might be useful to integrate traditional therapy with Deprexis to obtain additional support. However, it is important to discuss this option with their therapist to assess if it is the right choice.

Key points

Concrete action

If you are a cross-border worker from Ticino and are considering using Deprexis, here are some concrete steps to follow:

Step 1: Consultation with your doctor

The first step is to consult your doctor or psychologist to evaluate whether Deprexis is a suitable tool for your needs. Discuss the pros and cons of the app and decide with your therapist whether it is the right choice for you.

Step 2: Prescription

Once you have decided to use Deprexis, your doctor or psychologist will need to prescribe the app. At this stage, it is not yet clear whether specific prescriptions will be required for Deprexis or whether general prescriptions for psychotherapy will also cover this type of hybrid therapy. Make sure to clarify this point with your doctor.

Step 3: Access to the app

After obtaining the prescription, you will be able to access Deprexis. The app offers immediate and location-independent access to personalized therapy. Make sure to follow the instructions provided by the app to achieve the best results.

Step 4: Integration with traditional therapy

Deprexis does not replace traditional therapy. It is important to continue with traditional therapy to achieve the best results. Discuss with your therapist how to integrate Deprexis into your current therapy.

Step 5: Monitoring and evaluation

Monitor your progress with Deprexis and evaluate whether the app is having a positive impact on your mental health. Discuss your progress with your therapist and evaluate whether changes need to be made to your therapy.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is Deprexis?
Deprexis is a mobile or laptop app that offers personalized therapeutic support to help manage depression. It was developed by the German company GAIA and approved by the Swiss Federal Office of Public Health (FOPH).
How does Deprexis work?
Deprexis provides immediate and location-independent access to personalized therapy. The app is based on clinical studies that have demonstrated its effectiveness. However, it does not replace traditional therapy and should be integrated with it.
How much does Deprexis cost?
The cost of Deprexis is 300 euros for a three-month subscription, totaling approximately 1,200 euros per year. The rates in Swiss francs have not yet been determined, but they are expected to be similar.
