Como-Switzerland trains: 4 weekends of closures in 2026

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 October 2026

## TL;DR - Four closure weekends between October and November 2026 - Stop from Friday at 20:20 to Monday at 04:20 - RE80 skips Como San Giovanni and Como

Context

TL;DR

  • Four closure weekends between October and November 2026
  • Service suspended from Friday at 20:20 to Monday at 04:20
  • RE80 skips Como San Giovanni and Como Camerlata
  • There is a connection with S11 trains at Cucciago

Key facts

  • Works → scheduled maintenance by RFI
  • Tunnel → Monte Olimpino 1
  • Diversion → Monte Olimpino 2
  • S10 and S40 → to and from Chiasso
  • Check → online, FFS Mobile or Contact Center FFS

Cross-border rail traffic between Como and Switzerland will be subject to changes on four weekends between October and November 2026. The Monte Olimpino 1 tunnel will be affected by maintenance work planned by Rete Ferroviaria Italiana (RFI) and will therefore remain closed during the scheduled periods. The closure will begin at 20:20 on Friday and end at 04:20 on Monday morning.

For cross-border commuters, the time window is the first thing to check: the closure covers the specified weekend, from Friday evening until the early hours of Monday. The change affects the TILO RE80, TILO S10, TILO S40 and EuroCity services, but not in the same way. The overview therefore distinguishes between the tunnel used, stops and route limits.

RE80, S10, S40 and EuroCity

TILO RE80 trains bound for or coming from Milano Centrale will use the Monte Olimpino 2 tunnel. Consequently, during the closure, the trains will not stop at Como San Giovanni or Como Camerlata.

An exceptional stop will be introduced at Cucciago, allowing passengers to reach or leave the Como stations by connecting to Trenord S11 trains. For the RE80 service, this is the solution planned during the closure.

TILO S10 and TILO S40, on the other hand, will have their routes shortened. They will run only to and from Chiasso. EuroCity trains to and from Milano Centrale will also be diverted via Monte Olimpino 2 and will not stop at Como San Giovanni.

These instructions apply both to services heading towards Milano Centrale and to those in the opposite direction. To navigate skipped stops, diversions and limitations, passengers are advised to check the timetable for the individual train before setting out. The indicated tools are online consultation, the FFS Mobile app and the Contact Center FFS. For rail commuting, you can find out more about mezzi pubblici Ticino.

Operational details

For a cross-border commuter, the notice should be read as a set of instructions linked to the service chosen, not as the same change for everyone. The route between Como and Switzerland can change in three ways: diversion with stops skipped, service limited to Chiasso, or diversion with the Como San Giovanni stop excluded. This distinction helps interpret your journey without assigning the same change to RE80, S10, S40 and EuroCity.

The first comparison to make is between the scheduled service and the station concerned. If the journey involves RE80 and boarding or alighting was scheduled at Como San Giovanni or Como Camerlata, those stops are not available during the window. Cucciago is the exceptional stop indicated for making the connection with Trenord's S11 towards the stations in Como or in the opposite direction.

For S10 and S40, the restriction applies in both directions: the train runs to Chiasso or departs from Chiasso. For EuroCity, the change concerns the tunnel and the Como San Giovanni stop: the connection to and from Milano Centrale is diverted via Monte Olimpino 2.

The boundary of the time window also matters. A departure after 20:20 on Friday falls within the closure, as does a journey scheduled before 04:20 on Monday morning. The same time period is indicated for the four weekends. For this reason, the calendar alone is not enough: it must be matched with the selected service.

Key points

When one of the four weekends approaches, the commuter's procedure starts with the individual service and continues with checking whether assistance is available. The source indicates precise tools and deadlines.

Procedure before departure

1. Check the timetable of the individual train online before setting off. The check must concern the service actually selected, not just the general connection.

2. Use the FFS Mobile app as a second consultation channel. This allows you to check the timetable of the service indicated for the journey before departure.

3. If you prefer telephone contact, call the Contact Center FFS. From a Swiss landline, the number is 0848 44 66 88, at a cost of 0,08 CHF/min.

4. If you need assistance boarding or alighting from trains in Swiss territory, book it at least one hour before departure. From Switzerland, the toll-free number 0800 007 102 is available; from abroad, you can call +41 800 007 102. The service is active from 5:00 to 24:00.

5. If the support request concerns Italian territory, submit it at least 48 hours in advance to 800 210 955. The number is active every day from 8:15 to 19:45.

The two assistance requests therefore have different deadlines: one hour in advance in Swiss territory and 48 hours in advance in Italian territory. The numbers also vary depending on where support is needed. Otherwise, the timetable check remains available online, on the FFS Mobile app or via the Contact Center FFS.

Frequently Asked Questions
When will the Monte Olimpino 1 tunnel be closed?
The tunnel will remain closed on four specific weekends between October and November 2026. For each window, the closure will begin at 20:20 on Friday and end at 04:20 on Monday morning. The measure is related to maintenance work scheduled by Rete Ferroviaria Italiana (RFI).
How will TILO RE80 change?
Services to and from Milano Centrale will run through the Monte Olimpino 2 tunnel and will not stop at Como San Giovanni and Como Camerlata. An exceptional stop is planned at Cucciago, where passengers will be able to connect with Trenord's S11 trains to reach or leave the stations in Como. The change applies during the closure windows.
What will happen to TILO S10, S40 and EuroCity?
TILO S10 and S40 will operate only to and from Chiasso. The EuroCity trains to and from Milano Centrale will instead be diverted via the Monte Olimpino 2 tunnel and will not stop at Como San Giovanni. The changes apply during the closure.
How do you check the schedule or book assistance?
Before departure, the individual train must be checked online, on the FFS Mobile app or via the FFS Contact Center. From a Swiss landline, the number is 0848 44 66 88, at a cost of 0,08 CHF/min. For assistance in Switzerland, use 0800 007 102 or +41 800 007 102, at least one hour in advance; in Italy 800 210 955, with at least 48 hours' notice.
