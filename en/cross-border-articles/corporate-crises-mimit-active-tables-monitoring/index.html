Business crises, at Mimit 37 active tables: Beko Europe under observation (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 14 August 2026

The company crisis tables at the Ministry of Business and Made in Italy fall to 37, while the monitoring tables rise to 46.

Context

In a nutshell

  • 37 tables currently active at the Ministry of Business and Made in Italy
  • Workers at risk fell to less than 30,000 compared to the initial 70,000
  • Monitoring tables on concluded disputes increased from 33 to 46
  • Beko Europe is waiting at the Mimit for the next 1 October for the verifications

Key facts

  • What: Management and monitoring of corporate crises of national importance
  • When: Data updated during the legislature and the current year
  • Where: Rome, Ministry of Business and Made in Italy
  • Who: Mimit, companies, trade unions and territorial institutions
  • Amount: 16 new agreements reached since the beginning of the year

There are fewer than 30,000 workers at risk. On the other hand, the monitoring tables on disputes already concluded rose from 33 to 46. There are 37 tables currently active at the Ministry of Business and Made in Italy for the management of corporate crises of national importance. At the beginning of the legislature there were 55. In the same period, the number of workers at risk also decreased, from around 70,000 to just under 30,000. The data provide a picture of the activity of the business crisis structure of the Mimit, engaged in the analysis and coordination of disputes through discussions with companies, trade unions and local institutions. The reduction of tables still open is accompanied by an increase in activity dedicated to crises for which it has already been achieved

Operational details

The evolution of ministerial activity and monitoring tables

The institutional framework drawn up by the Ministry of Business and Made in Italy highlights a strategic change in the management of industrial disputes during the legislature. The structure dedicated to business crises has changed its operational centre of gravity, going from the mere management of the acute emergency to the systematic implementation of a subsequent control phase. The increase in the monitoring tables, from 33 to 46, testifies to the desire to oversee the effective maintenance of the agreements signed with the social partners and local administrations over time.

This methodological transformation is directly reflected in the management of complex disputes. In the face of a contraction in open tables from 55 to 37 and a reduction in workers potentially exposed to occupational risks — down from about 70,000 to less than 30,000 — the dicastery maintains a high focus on large companies. An emblematic example of this operational line is represented by Beko Europe, whose call to the Mimit is scheduled for next October 1 precisely to allow a timely verification of the status of implementation of the commitments made.

Key points

The control procedure and the institutional comparison method

The operating model adopted by the Ministry of Business and Made in Italy is based on a constant and coordinated analysis that directly involves company representatives, trade unions and the competent territorial institutions. The procedure does not end with the signing of the initial framework agreement, but continues through periodic meetings scheduled within the dedicated monitoring tables, as confirmed in the specific case of Beko Europe.

The primary objective of this subsequent verification phase is to ensure timely compliance with the agreements signed, preventing any deviations from the agreed industrial plans and guaranteeing the production continuity of the safeguarded plants. With 46 monitoring tables currently operational and a total balance of 44 agreements concluded in the last three years, the ministerial structure ensures continuous monitoring of disputes of national importance.
