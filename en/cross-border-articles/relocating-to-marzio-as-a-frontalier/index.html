We know what it means to be a frontier worker in Switzerland and Italy. The New Frontier Agreement introduced new rules for frontier workers, but what are the advantages and disadvantages of moving to Marzio?

Context

In a nutshell

  • The New Frontier Agreement entered into force on 1 January 2024. - It is signed on 23 December 2020 and ratified by Law 83 of 13 June 2023. - The old agreement provided for a concession of 7,500 euros, while the new agreement provides for a deductible of 10,000 euros.

What has changed with the New Frontier Agreement

The New Frontier Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020 and ratified by Law 83 of 13 June 2023. This new agreement entered into force on 1 January 2024 and provides for some important changes compared to the old agreement. One of the main novelties is the €10,000 deductible, which is higher than the previous benefit of €7,500.

Concrete examples

Let's imagine that we are a border worker working in Lugano and living in Marzio. Our annual income is 60,000 euros. In the old agreement, we would have been entitled to a concession of 7,500 euros, while with the new agreement we can benefit from a deductible of 10,000 euros. This means that we could save 2,500 euros per year.

Operational Checklists

To benefit from the €10,000 deductible with the New Frontier Agreement, we must meet the following conditions:

  • We are citizens of an EU Member State. - We reside in Switzerland for at least 90 days a year. - We work in Switzerland for at least 183 days a year. - Our annual income is less than 120,000 euros. - We have no other sources of income in Switzerland.
