Varese-Ticino agreement: S40/S50 every 30 min and RE50 to Malpensa

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 29 September 2026

The Regional Council has ratified the agreement: S40 and S50 every 30 min between Varese and Mendrisio. The RE50 for Malpensa and Lugano is planned.

Context

In brief

  • Green light for ratification between Lombardy and Canton Ticino
  • S40 and S50: Varese-Mendrisio every thirty minutes
  • RE50 planned between Malpensa, Varese and Lugano
  • S30: Cadenazzo-Luino-Gallarate to be strengthened

The Regional Council gave the green light to ratifying the new agreement between Lombardy and Canton Ticino for the development of cross-border mobility. The measure directly concerns the Varese area, affected every day by travel to Switzerland and connections with the Malpensa area.

Its significance is underscored by Giuseppe Licata, regional councillor and vice-president of the Special Committee «Enhancement and protection of mountain and border areas; Relations between Lombardy and the Swiss Confederation». For Licata, the agreement must make the mobility system more integrated and offer cross-border commuters and other citizens concrete alternatives to the private car. The province of Varese, located between Lombardy, Canton Ticino and Malpensa, is identified as one of the network's central points.

The planned routes

Rail consolidation centers on the S40 and S50 lines. The source indicates that they will continue to connect Varese with Mendrisio, Como, Lugano and Bellinzona, ensuring an integrated service every thirty minutes between Varese and Mendrisio. The planning also includes the prospect of the RE50 Malpensa-Varese-Mendrisio-Lugano, designed to improve the connection between the Varese area, Ticino and the airport.

For the Upper Varese area, the agreement provides for work on the S30 Cadenazzo-Luino-Gallarate line, with the aim of progressively increasing frequencies and connections. Licata asks that Varese remain a leading player in the subsequent phases and that the objectives become concrete and reliable services. He also requested that a meeting with the councillor be scheduled to discuss developments and implementation: the session is already scheduled for October 1.

The section is not limited to trains. New bus connections are planned in the border areas between the provinces of Varese and Como, along with coordination between road and rail services and completion of the range of cross-border tickets and season passes. On the Como-Varese corridor, the agreement also provides for mutual recognition, at least on certain routes or services, of some Swiss tickets and the Lombardy regional passes Io Viaggio and Io Viaggio in Famiglia.

Rounding out the picture is the study of the former Castellanza-Malnate Olona-Mendrisio railway. The scenarios outlined include rail reactivation, tram solutions and cycling infrastructure. Operational details

The practical perspective for cross-border commuters

For those who work across the border, the agreement should be read as a network of connected corridors. The source does not concentrate the measures on a single route: it brings together S40, S50, S30, the prospect of RE50, buses, travel tickets and the study on the former railway. The concrete impact will therefore depend on the corridor used by the commuter and the type of service involved.

From the news to daily monitoring

For a cross-border commuter or a commuter, following implementation means distinguishing the connection of interest from the measure concerning it. The text of the agreement makes it possible to set up a four-step check, without confusing a rail service, a planning outlook and a study of the right-of-way.

Four operational steps

1. Start from the reference corridor. Consider whether the journey concerns Varese-Mendrisio and the onward routes toward Como, Lugano or Bellinzona, the connection with Malpensa, the Upper Varese area or the Como-Varese corridor.

2. Classify the measure. For S40 and S50, the source refers to connections that will continue and to an integrated service every thirty minutes between Varese and Mendrisio. For RE50 and S30, by contrast, identify a planning perspective and a goal of gradual increase, respectively.

3. When assessing buses and travel tickets, focus the check on the border areas between the provinces of Varese and Como and on the routes or services covered by the recognition. The agreement mentions some Swiss tickets, Io Viaggio and Io Viaggio in Famiglia along the Como-Varese corridor.

4. Note the first institutional step indicated in the text: Licata has requested a meeting with the councillor to examine developments and implementation in greater depth, already scheduled for the session on primo ottobre. This sequence makes it possible to follow the news without conflating different levels. The rail connection, integration with buses, recognition of tickets and the study of the former railway right-of-way in fact have different functions within the same agreement. For those who work in Canton Ticino, the point to keep separate is the everyday corridor from the scenario concerning future planning.

The Castellanza-Malnate Olona-Mendrisio line requires a separate check: the study will have to identify possible scenarios involving railway reactivation, tramway solutions and cycling infrastructure. On the services front, however, the criterion indicated by Licata is to turn objectives into concrete and reliable connections, with greater coordination between rail and bus services.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the new frequencies planned for the Varese-Mendrisio connection?
The agreement ratified by the Regional Council provides that the S40 and S50 lines guarantee an integrated service every thirty minutes between Varese and Mendrisio. These connections will also continue to serve the directions to Como, Lugano and Bellinzona, offering a structured alternative to the private car for commuters.
What does the agreement provide for the connection to Malpensa Airport?
The planning includes the prospect of the RE50 line, which will connect Malpensa, Varese, Mendrisio and Lugano. This axis is designed to improve accessibility between the Varese territory, the Canton of Ticino and the airport, integrating the existing railway network with a new planned connection.
How will the recognition of cross-border travel documents be handled?
On the Como-Varese corridor, the agreement provides for the mutual recognition of some Swiss titles and the Lombard regional titles' Io Viaggio 'and' Io Viaggio in Famiglia '. This recognition will apply at least on certain routes or services, making the offer of tickets and subscriptions for those who cross the border more coordinated.
