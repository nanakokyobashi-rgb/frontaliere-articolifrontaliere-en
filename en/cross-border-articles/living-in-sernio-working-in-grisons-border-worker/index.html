The New Frontier Agreement represents an important change for border workers working in Switzerland and residing in Italy. The agreement provides for the creation of a transitional regime for border workers already present before 17 July 2023, which will be applied from 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2033. The transitional regime provides for a reduction of CHF 7,500 for existing frontier workers, while new frontier workers will have a deductible of CHF10,000.

For example, if a frontier worker resides in Sernio and works in Graubünden, he will be eligible for the CHF 7,500 deductible if he is already present in Switzerland before 17 July 2023. In this case, you will not have to pay income taxes up to a limit of CHF 7,500.
