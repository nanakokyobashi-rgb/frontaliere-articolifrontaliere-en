Varese Prefecture: mobility call for 6 places (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 September 2026

The Ministry of the Interior opens voluntary mobility for 6 positions in Varese. Application deadline set at 30 September 2026 through the inPA portal.

Context

In brief

  • The Ministry of the Interior opens positions for voluntary mobility.
  • Six positions available at the Prefecture of Varese.
  • Online applications on inPA by September 30, 2026.

Key facts

  • What: Call for voluntary mobility for public employees.
  • When: Application deadline September 30, 2026.
  • Where: Offices of the Prefecture of Varese.
  • Who: Ministry of the Interior.
  • Positions: 6 total positions between the area of Officials and Assistants.

The selection procedure launched by the Ministry of the Interior concerns the recruitment of personnel already in the Public Administration. The call, published on September 1, 2026, is part of a broader national plan that provides for the coverage of a total of 282 positions throughout the Italian territory, involving both central and peripheral structures. Specifically, for the Varese site, the local prefectural offices open the doors to six new professional resources who wish to transfer to this site.

Detail of the required profiles for the Varese site

The distribution of positions for the Varese province has been defined to meet specific organizational needs of the civil offices. Specifically, the call provides for the inclusion of two Administrative Officials, two figures destined for the qualification of Economic-Financial Official, one profile of Computer Official, and one position dedicated to the role of Administrative Assistant. This is a public selection based on qualifications and interview, designed exclusively for those already in service with other public administrations with a full-time and indefinite contract. The selection aims to integrate the skills already acquired by the candidates within the structure of the Prefecture, ensuring operational continuity in the administrative and technical areas provided for in the call.

Operational details

The voluntary mobility process described by the Ministry of the Interior is configured as an opportunity for transfer for state employees. Unlike public competitions for new hires, this procedure is reserved exclusively for personnel who already hold a corresponding role within the Italian public sector. The correspondence between the candidate's current professional profile and that required in the notice is the essential requirement to submit their candidacy. This mechanism ensures that the transferred resources are able to operate immediately in the roles of official or assistant required by the Prefecture of Varese.

Key points

The management of applications is exclusively carried out electronically, a mandatory step for anyone wishing to participate in the selection process. Interested parties must access the Single Portal for Recruitment of the Public Administration, known as inPA, no later than September 30, 2026. It is within this platform that candidates must complete the insertion of all personal, professional, and educational data required by the notice. Attention to the correct completion of the profile on the portal is essential, as the selection procedure is entirely based on the documentation uploaded digitally during the registration phase.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the open positions at the Prefecture of Varese?
The call published by the Ministry of the Interior provides for the inclusion of six new professional resources. Specifically, the distribution of posts includes two Administrative Officers, two Economic and Financial Officers, an IT Officer and a position dedicated to the role of Administrative Assistant.
Who can participate in the mobility procedure?
The selection is reserved exclusively for staff already in service at a Public Administration with a full-time and indefinite contract. Workers outside the public sector are not allowed and there are no exceptions to the nature of the contract requested.
How and by when is it possible to send the application?
Applications must be submitted exclusively electronically through the single inPA recruitment portal no later than 30 September 2026. You must log in, upload the qualifications and professional certifications required by the full call available on the platform.
