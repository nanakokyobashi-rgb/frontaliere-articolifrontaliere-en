An operating deficit of just under two million francs is the central figure in Castel San Pietro’s 2027 Budget, in the Canton of Ticino. The Municipal Executive approved the message during last Monday’s meeting, forwarding it to the Municipal Council.

The budget provides for total expenditure of 16'186’399 million francs, an increase of 7,49% compared with the 2026 Budget. Revenue, including municipal tax revenue, is estimated at 13’408’950 million francs, an increase of 4,98% compared with 2026.

In the financial document, the Executive proposes separate tax multipliers for individuals and legal entities, with an increase of 5 percentage points compared with 2026: 75% for the former and 80% for the latter.

The Municipal Executive presents this choice as a way to partially offset the tax relief introduced at cantonal level. The two percentages are also described as being in line with the 2025-2028 financial plan, discussed during the ordinary meeting devoted to the 2025 Budget.

Submission to the Municipal Council

In its general commentary, the Municipal Executive quantifies the negative result at 1’968’649 million francs and attributes it to the increase in operating expenses, while tax revenue and operating income grow modestly. The Executive presents the budget as being in line with its medium-term financial strategy, set out in the financial plan and policy guidelines, and indicates equity of more than 20 million francs.

The message thus brings together the expenditure forecast, the revenue estimate and the proposal concerning the multiplier in a single financial framework. The figures and percentages refer to the 2027 Budget and, for the comparisons indicated, to the 2026 Budget; the text attributes the proposal to the Municipal Executive and its transmission to the Municipal Council.

Operational details

The comparison between the figures is possible because the two changes have the same basis: the 2027 Budget is measured against the 2026 Budget. Expenditure increases by 7,49%, while revenue increases by 4,98%. The arithmetic difference between the two rates is 2,51 percentage points: the calculation results from 7,49 minus 4,98 and does not constitute a new percentage announced by the Municipality.

Key points

The framework described by the Municipal Executive also includes its own resources and the choices set out for the medium term. Equity is stated to exceed 20 million francs. Moreover, according to the Executive, the extraordinary surpluses recorded in 2018 and subsequent years made it possible to implement measures to redistribute these resources to individuals and legal entities in the short term.

The financial strategy referred to in the general commentary calls for containing expenditure under municipal responsibility and a significant reduction in the works plan, based on revised priorities. It also includes balancing the accounts of the drinking-water, waste and wastewater services, as well as an increase in tax revenue.
