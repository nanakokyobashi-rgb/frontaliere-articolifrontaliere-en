The broad committee in favor of neutralizing property valuations in Ticino has presented the arguments in support of the initiative, highlighting the impact on purchasing power, social consequences, and economic stability. The initiative, which will be put to a popular vote in June 2026, aims to defend a lifetime's sacrifices, particularly for property owners. The Grand Council has already neutralized the valuations, but the final decision rests with the people.

The initiative was promoted by a committee that collected over 17,000 signatures, demonstrating widespread popular support. Among the main supporters are Andrea Gehri, Cristina Maderni, and Daniele Caverzasio, who have emphasized the importance of protecting the savings and property investments of Ticino residents.

Comparison with the Previous Situation

Before the neutralization of valuations, property owners in Ticino were subject to tax assessments that could vary significantly over time. This could lead to uncertainties and instability in the real estate market. With the neutralization of valuations, greater stability and predictability are sought, thus protecting the lifelong sacrifices of owners.

Concrete Scenarios

If the initiative is approved, cross-border workers could benefit from greater stability in the real estate market, with more predictable prices and greater availability of housing. However, it is important to consider possible side effects, such as an increase in demand and property prices, which could make purchasing more difficult for some cross-border workers.

Tax Implications

The neutralization of property valuations could also have tax implications for cross-border workers. In particular, the stability of property values could influence withholding taxes and AVS contributions.
