The fire in the agricultural area

On the afternoon of Wednesday 23 settembre, a large fire broke out in the municipality of Turate, on via Como, inside a farm. The fire developed in an agricultural storage building located in the countryside between via Como and the A36 Pedemontana motorway.

The tall, dense column of smoke produced by the fire was visible several kilometres away. The update issued describes an intervention that is still ongoing, with several Fire Brigade crews engaged in extinguishing the fire and securing the affected area. Rescuers are operating with vehicles intended for activities at the site.

There may also be animals in the shed. At present, their condition is unknown. The presence of the animals is indicated in the same update describing the Fire Brigade's work to extinguish the fire and secure the area.

The Fire Brigade command stated that several crews are engaged at the scene and that rescue operations are underway. The situation therefore concerns an operational intervention that is still ongoing in the municipal territory of Turate, with activities focused on extinguishing the fire and securing the affected area.

The update issued indicates the location of the emergency, the possible presence of animals in the shed, and the deployment of crews and vehicles to respond. The fire remains at the farm on via Como, while activities continue in the area indicated by the Fire Brigade.

Operational details

Visible smoke and slower traffic

The plume from the fire was clearly visible from numerous points in the lower Varesotto area and along State Road 336. The tall, dense column of smoke was also visible from several kilometers away. The reduced visibility and the large amount of smoke alarmed the entire area, carrying the incident well beyond the point where the fire broke out.

Regarding traffic, the source reports major disruption. Traffic was heavily slowed in the area between Turate and Cislago. The situation caused by the smoke was compounded by the continuous arrival of emergency vehicles, engaged in operations at the agricultural depot.

To put out the flames and secure the area, several crews of firefighters from neighboring provinces are working together at the scene. The update describes a deployment of vehicles with different functions: a fire engine, an aerial ladder truck for operations from above, a water tanker for water supply, and additional vehicles and personnel sent from Como by the Provincial Command.

Key points

Investigations still ongoing

The rescue operations are currently being carried out. The statement from the Fire Brigade indicates that the various teams working at the scene are engaged in extinguishing the fire and securing the affected area. Operational work therefore remains at the center of the update issued on the fire in Turate.

The warehouse is also linked to developments in the operation due to the possible presence of animals. The source reports that they may be inside, but specifies that at present it is not known what condition they are in. This detail accompanies the description of the rescue activities still underway at the farm.

The second aspect concerns the causes of the fire. At present, the causes that triggered the blaze still need to be ascertained. Law enforcement officers and technicians are beginning the first investigations to clarify how the incident occurred.

The text released thus distinguishes the rescuers' actions from the investigations into the incident: on the one hand, extinguishing the fire and securing the area are continuing; on the other, the first investigations entrusted to law enforcement officers and technicians are getting underway.
