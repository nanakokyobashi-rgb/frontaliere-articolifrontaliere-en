Ammonia leak in Chiasso: cause identified

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 28 April 2026

The ammonia leak at Chiasso's ice rink was caused by a defective seal. Three employees slightly intoxicated.

Context

In brief

  • Ammonia leak at Chiasso ice rink
  • Cause: Faulty gasket in a compressor fitting
  • Three employees slightly intoxicated

Key facts

  • What: Ammonia leak
  • When: Tuesday morning, discovered around 8:30 AM
  • Where: Chiasso ice rink
  • Who: Three municipal workers
  • Amount: Three employees intoxicated

The ammonia leak that occurred on Tuesday morning at the Chiasso ice rink was caused by a technical fault. According to Corrado Tettamanti, commander of the Mendrisiotto Fire Department, the problem was due to a gasket inside a compressor fitting. The leak was discovered around 8:30 AM by three municipal workers, who immediately closed the door and called for help, thus preventing the spread of ammonia in the surrounding area. The three employees, including one woman, were slightly intoxicated.

Safety implications

The prompt action of the municipal workers prevented the situation from worsening, demonstrating the importance of a rapid response in similar emergencies. This incident underscores the need to keep refrigeration systems in perfect condition and to adequately train staff in emergency management.

Safety procedure

In similar cases, it is essential to follow a clear and well-defined procedure. Staff must be trained to recognize the signs of an ammonia leak and know how to act immediately to contain the damage. Additionally, it is essential to have an emergency plan that includes the evacuation of personnel and the calling of emergency services.

Comparisons with previous situations

This incident reminds us of other cases of ammonia leaks in sports facilities, where timely intervention has prevented more serious consequences. For example, in a similar case that occurred in another city, the immediate response allowed the damage to be limited and the health of those present to be protected.
