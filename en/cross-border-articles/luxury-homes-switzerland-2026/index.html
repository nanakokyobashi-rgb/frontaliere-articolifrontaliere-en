Luxury homes in Switzerland: prices still high but trend slowing (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 May 2026

Prices of luxury real estate in Switzerland rose by 3% in 2025, but the market shows signs of weakening. Here's what changes for cross-border workers.

Context

In brief

  • Luxury property prices +3% in 2025
  • Market slowdown expected for 2026
  • St. Moritz the most expensive location

Key facts

  • What: Increase in luxury property prices
  • When: 2025
  • Where: Switzerland
  • Who: UBS
  • Amount: +3% national average

In 2025, luxury properties in Switzerland became more expensive, but the growth trend is progressively slowing down. According to a study published by UBS, the prices of high-standard properties increased by an average of 3% in a year. This increase is lower than the dynamics recorded in the overall Swiss real estate market. The Alpine regions drove demand, with average price increases of 6%, driven by wealthy foreign buyers. St. Moritz remains the most expensive location in the country, with an average price of 52,000 francs per square meter, followed by Gstaad and Verbier with around 45,000 francs. Outside the Alps, Cologny on Lake Geneva and Küsnacht on Lake Zurich are the most expensive locations.

Implications for cross-border workers

For a 150 square meter apartment in a privileged position, in the Swiss luxury municipalities, you need to spend between 4 and 5 million francs. Single-family homes with a plot of over 1,000 square meters often exceed 10 million. Despite an average annual increase of 5% in the last five years, UBS observes signs of market weakening, especially on Lake Geneva and Lake Zurich. For 2026, the bank predicts weaker development, also penalized by the weak economic situation.

The slowdown in Switzerland's luxury real estate market could have several implications for cross-border workers looking to invest in high-end properties. With prices showing signs of stagnation, particularly in Ticino, it's crucial to carefully consider the right time to buy or sell.

Investment Opportunities

For cross-border workers employed in Switzerland and residing in Italy, purchasing a luxury property could be an interesting option. However, it's essential to carefully evaluate the financial situation and market prospects. With prices potentially stabilizing or even decreasing, it might be the right time to look for advantageous deals.

Tax Considerations

Purchasing a luxury property in Switzerland also involves tax implications. It's important to consider the withholding tax, AHV, and BVG, as well as any potential tax benefits for cross-border workers. Consulting a tax expert can help navigate the complexities of the Swiss and Italian tax systems.

Comparison with Other Regions

While alpine regions continue to see an increase in prices, other areas like Ticino show stagnation. This could offer opportunities for cross-border workers looking to buy in less expensive but still prestigious areas. It's important to compare prices and market conditions across different regions to find the best investment opportunity.

Impact on Demand

The influx of wealthy buyers from Italy and the strong performance of Lugano's financial center are supporting the market. However, the overall slowdown in the luxury real estate market could influence long-term demand. Cross-border workers should closely monitor market trends to make informed decisions.
