Landslide in Sementina: traffic congested

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 9 October 2026

Landslide on the cantonal road in Sementina at 16.00 The carriageway is covered with debris and rocks Road closed and traffic diverted in both directions

Around 16.00 on Friday, material and rocks spilled onto the cantonal road in Sementina, near the football field. The entire carriageway was covered by debris, with an immediate impact on traffic in the Bellinzona area.

The initial information was gathered on site by Rescue Media. The police intervened and ordered the closure of the section, accompanied by a diversion of traffic in both directions. Anyone passing through the affected section was therefore unable to continue along the closed stretch.

According to initial information, no injuries have been reported. However, one vehicle was reportedly damaged. The situation remains subject to checks by specialists, who have been called upon to determine whether there is a danger of further landslides. The source does not anticipate a decision on reopening, because the safety assessment still needs to be carried out.

The traffic situation

The incident had consequences beyond the point of the landslide. ViaSuisse confirmed congested traffic also between Quartino and Cadenazzo. For those travelling on the Magadino Plain, the evening was shaping up to be difficult, with slowdowns and prolonged waits between Cadenazzo and Gudo, in both directions.

The reports also include the stretch between Monte Carasso and Giubiasco, the one between Camorino and Contone, and the descent from Monte Ceneri towards Cadenazzo. These are the connections indicated by the source as being affected by slowdowns; no alternative routes or travel times are described, however.

The data to follow for the journey therefore remains twofold. On the one hand, there is a closure ordered by the police at the point overrun by debris; on the other, there are the congestion levels recorded on several routes.
