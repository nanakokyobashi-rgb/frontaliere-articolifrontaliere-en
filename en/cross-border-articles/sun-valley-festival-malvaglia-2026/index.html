Sun Valley Festival in Malvaglia: three days of music and fun (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 May 2026

From May 22 to 24, 2026, Malvaglia hosts the Sun Valley Festival with international artists and activities for all ages.

Context

In brief

  • Sun Valley Festival in Malvaglia from May 22 to 24, 2026
  • International artists and activities for all ages
  • Camping and food area available

Key facts

  • What: Sun Valley Festival
  • When: May 22-24, 2026
  • Where: Malvaglia, Canton Ticino
  • Who: Organized by Asilo Beach of Malvaglia
  • Tickets: Available for presale on ticketino.com

The Sun Valley Festival returns to Malvaglia from May 22 to 24, 2026, promising three days of music and fun in the picturesque Boschetto area. Organized by the Asilo Beach association of Malvaglia, the event offers a unique opportunity to enjoy live performances and activities for all ages. The opening night will feature performances by Giovani Wannabe, Andrea Cerrato, Rumatera, Sgaffy, and DJ Feed, while on Saturday, May 23, the festival will kick into high gear with an explosive lineup including Dan and his brothers, Klischée, The Rockstar Show, DJ Gollum, Molella, and Edmmaro. On Sunday, the grand finale with Uncle Bard & The Dirty Bastards, The Rumpled, Simon & Mac, Ludwig, Babalos, and Gianni Tunga & Ciucio Jr.

In addition to music, the festival will offer activities such as motorinata, children's party, and helicopter sightseeing flights. For those who want to fully experience the event, a camping area and a food area with numerous food trucks will be available. Free parking and nighttime shuttles to Biasca, Bellinzona, Faido, and Olivone will be provided for participants. Tickets are available for presale on ticketino.com and at the box office in the evening at a higher price, subject to prior sell-out.

Operational details

Practical Analysis: What to Expect

The Sun Valley Festival offers a unique opportunity for cross-border workers and residents of the Canton of Ticino to enjoy a high-level musical event without having to travel far. The proximity of Malvaglia to major centers like Bellinzona and Lugano makes it easily accessible, offering a quality entertainment alternative in the heart of Ticino.

Implications for Cross-Border Workers

For cross-border workers who work in Switzerland and live in Italy, the festival is an opportunity to spend a fun and relaxing weekend without having to face long journeys. The availability of free parking and nighttime shuttles makes the event even more accessible, allowing you to enjoy the music and activities without worrying about getting home.

Comparison with Other Festivals

Compared to other music festivals in Switzerland, the Sun Valley Festival stands out for its intimate atmosphere and the variety of musical genres offered. The combination of rock, pop, electronic, and folk music makes it suitable for a diverse audience, offering something for everyone. Additionally, the attention to logistical aspects, such as free parking and shuttles, makes it a convenient option for those seeking a quality musical experience.

Practical Scenarios

For those who decide to attend the festival, it is advisable to book tickets in advance to avoid sell-outs. Additionally, consider the camping option to make the most of the experience, especially for those coming from afar. The activities planned for Saturday, such as the motorinata and panoramic flights, offer added value, making the festival a complete experience.

Key points

What to do to participate

To participate in the Sun Valley Festival, you need to follow a few simple but important steps. Here's a practical guide to ensure you don't miss anything at the event.

Step 1: Buy the tickets

Tickets are available for presale on ticketino.com. It is advisable to buy tickets in advance to avoid sell-outs and take advantage of favorable prices. Alternatively, tickets will be available at the evening box office, but at a higher price.

Step 2: Plan your trip

If you're coming from far away, consider the camping option to make the most of the experience. The festival offers free parking and nighttime shuttles to Biasca, Bellinzona, Faido, and Olivone, making the return journey easier. If you prefer comfort, you can also consider using the taxi or Nez Rouge service.

Step 3: Participate in the activities

Don't miss the activities scheduled for Saturday, May 23rd, such as the motorinata, the children's festival, and panoramic helicopter flights. In case of bad weather, these activities will be postponed to Sunday, May 24th, with updates on social media. Every evening, from 4 PM to 8 PM, there will be a warm-up at the camping site with local DJs, offering a festive atmosphere from the afternoon.

Step 4: Enjoy the food area

The food area will be freely accessible, with numerous food trucks and the Asilo Beach Food station. Take advantage of this opportunity to taste flavors for every palate and complete your festival experience.

Step 5: Return home safely

Use the free parking and nighttime shuttles for the return trip to Biasca, Bellinzona, Faido, and Olivone. If you prefer a more personalized service, you can use the taxi or Nez Rouge service, available every evening.

Frequently Asked Questions
Where can I buy tickets for the Sun Valley Festival?
Tickets are available for advance purchase on ticketino.com and at the box office in the evening at a higher price, unless sold out in advance.
Are there free parking spaces near the event?
Yes, the festival offers free parking near the event and free night buses back to Biasca, Bellinzona, Faido, and Olivone.
What happens in case of bad weather?
In case of bad weather, the activities scheduled for Saturday, May 23, will be postponed to Sunday, May 24, with updates on social media channels.
