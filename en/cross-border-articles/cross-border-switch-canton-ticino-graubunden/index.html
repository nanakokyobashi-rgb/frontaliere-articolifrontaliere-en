Canton change from Ticino to Graubünden for border workers

What changes for G permit, tax at source, withholdings and commuting when you move the workplace from Ticino to Graubünden.

Context

  • The change of employment between cantons requires the updating of the G permit.
  • The withholding tax varies according to the cantonal barèmes set locally.
  • The New Agreement signed on 23/12/2020 regulates concurrent taxation.

Key facts

  • What: Change canton of employment from Ticino to Graubünden
  • When: In force with the New Agreement from January 1, 2024
  • Where: Canton of Ticino, Graubünden and Italian border strip
  • Who: Border crossers with permission G and cantonal migration authorities
  • For the purposes of Article 9 of the Agreement, “old border crossers” are those who complied with the tax requirements and who worked in Graubünden, Ticino or Valais on 17 July 2023 or in the period from 31 December 2018 to 17 July 2023. For income from work covered by Article 9, taxation remains exclusively at source in Switzerland; for other workers the ordinary regime applies, with taxation in Italy, credit for Swiss tax and Swiss withholding tax at 80% of the ordinary rates. The professional transition from an employer active in the Canton of Ticino to a company based in the Canton of Graubünden involves a series of precise administrative and tax steps. Switzerland, which is not an EU or EEA member, manages access to the labour market through federal regulations coordinated by the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) and enforced by individual cantonal offices. When an employed person changes his place of employment by crossing cantonal borders, the residence permit for cross-border workers must incorporate the contractual variation.

Updating of the residence permit and documentation

The G permit is linked to the contractual relationship with the Swiss employer. When you stop working in Ticino and start a new contract in Graubünden, the Graubünden migration authority must issue or update the permit. Notification of the new employment relationship is usually up to the employer before the effective commencement of benefits. The procedure requires the presentation of the employment contract, a copy of the valid identity document and proof of daily or weekly return to your Italian residence in the border strip. The territorial change also affects the registration with the cantonal employment registers, since each canton maintains an independent management of employment registers.

Operational details

Moving to work in the Graubünden directly affects the tax levied on the pay slip. The withholding tax rates are established by cantonal laws and administered by the cantonal tax administrations under the supervision of the Federal Tax Administration (AFC/ESTV). Graubünden apply withholding tax tables with their own rates, different from those in force in Ticino. Withholding tax on employee income is withheld in Switzerland and never at the same time in both countries.

Regulatory framework and social security withholdings

From a bilateral perspective, the Italy-Switzerland Convention against double taxation of 9 March 1976 applies, integrated by the New Agreement on cross-border workers signed on 23 December 2020 and applied from 1 January 2024, ratified in Italy with Law 83 of 13 June 2023. Old cross-border workers remain taxed only in Switzerland (art. 9 of the 2020 Agreement). The threshold of 10,000 euros is not a general exemption: art. 4 of Law 83/2023 limits it to the income referred to in the art. 1, paragraph 175, of Law 147/2013.

In terms of social contributions, the federal rates remain unchanged: the AVS/AI/IPG deduction is equal to 5.3% to be paid by the employee, the AD/AC unemployment insurance absorbs 1.1% up to the salary cap of CHF 148,200, while the UVG non-professional accident coverage fluctuates between 0.7% and 1.5%. The LPP occupational pension applies rates between 7% and 18% based on the age group starting from 25 years. As regards health insurance, G permit holders retain the right to choose between the Swiss LAMal coverage (with deductibles for adults from CHF 300 to CHF 2500) and the Italian national health service.

Key points

The operational transition between the Canton of Ticino and the Graubünden requires careful planning of commuting routes and bureaucratic obligations. The customs transit points change depending on the geographical direction: from the Brogeda motorway transit or from the Mendrisiotto passes you frequently move on to Alpine itineraries such as the San Bernardino Pass, Val Chiavenna or Val Poschiavo, with different travel times and seasonal conditions.

Step by step procedure for the cantonal change

To manage the change of canton smoothly, it is advisable to follow an orderly sequence:

  • Verify the correct stipulation of the new employment contract with the employer in Graubünden.
  • Send the documentation to the migration office of the Canton of Graubünden for the issue of the updated G permit.
  • Request the company administration to apply the correct withholding tax table.
  • Communicate any change to the LPP pension fund for the transfer of vested benefits.
  • Keep the Swiss salary certificates for completing the tax return in Italy for tax credit purposes.

Frequently Asked Questions
What happens to the G permit if I change canton from Ticino to Graubünden?
The G permit must be updated at the migration office of the Canton of Graubünden via the new employer. Residence permits remain subject to the active employment relationship and compliance with the conditions of re-entry into the border strip.
Is the tax at source in Graubünden identical to that in Ticino?
No, tax rates at source are set by cantonal laws and administered by individual tax administrations. Graubünden apply specific tariff barriers, resulting in a different tax levy than Ticino.
How is double taxation avoided in Italy?
Income tax is withheld at source in Switzerland. For new frontier workers subject to declaration in Italy, double taxation is neutralized through the credit for taxes paid abroad in the EC framework of model 730.
