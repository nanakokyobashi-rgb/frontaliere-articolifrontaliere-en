Ladies Run 2026: road closures in Lugano (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 5 May 2026

Traffic changes for the running event on May 9, 2026. Here's what changes for drivers and cross-border workers

Context

In brief

  • Lungolago Lugano closed 15:00-21:00
  • Bus lane Riva Caccia closed to traffic
  • Public transport on normal lane

Key facts

  • What: Road closures for Ladies Run
  • When: 9 May 2026
  • Where: Lugano
  • Who: Lugano City Police
  • Amount: Not specified

On the occasion of the Ladies Run running event, scheduled for Saturday, 9 May 2026, the Lugano City Police has announced changes to traffic. Lungolago will be closed to vehicular traffic from 15:00 to 21:00, starting from the LAC roundabout to Piazza Castello. The bus lane on Riva Caccia will be affected by the race and consequently will be closed to traffic. Public transport will run on the normal lane.

The Police invites citizens and drivers to consult the official indications and to plan their movements. For cross-border commuters who use these roads to get to work, it is essential to plan alternative routes. The city's parking areas may have access changes, so it is advisable to check the updated information.

Impact on cross-border commuters

Cross-border commuters working in Lugano and residing in Italy should pay particular attention to the changes in traffic. The closure of Lungolago and Riva Caccia could significantly affect travel times. It is advisable to use updated navigation applications or consult the Lugano City Police website to avoid delays.

Practical tips
