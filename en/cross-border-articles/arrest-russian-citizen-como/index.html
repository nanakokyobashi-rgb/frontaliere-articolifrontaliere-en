Yesterday afternoon, Wednesday, the Como State Police arrested a 26-year-old Russian citizen with no fixed address in Italy and irregular status in the country. The man had unlawfully entered the headquarters of the Fire Brigade Command on via Valleggio, in Como. The report also mentions some damage, including the glass window of a garage.

The report and initial response

Around 15.00, officers from the police headquarters patrol unit arrived at the Fire Brigade Command headquarters after being informed that someone was inside the facility. Before the police arrived, the personnel on site had restrained the 26-year-old following the damage. The patrol then took custody of the man, who was found to be agitated.

During a personal search, the Russian citizen immediately showed little cooperation. He then had an outburst of anger in the service vehicle and attempted to escape. The officers promptly restrained him. The sequence described therefore begins with the unauthorized presence at the headquarters, continues with the reported damage, and culminates in the attempt to evade control after being detained.

The initial response took place entirely in Como and involved the headquarters on via Valleggio, Fire Brigade personnel and officers from the police headquarters patrol unit. At this stage, the source places the unauthorized entry, the damaged glass window and the attempted escape before the further checks carried out by the State Police.
