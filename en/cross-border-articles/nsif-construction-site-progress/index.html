Operational details

The Nsif site in Lugano is in progress, with 60% of the concrete and half of the expected steel used. This means that the project is on schedule.

This means that the work team was able to maintain a constant pace and make the most of the available resources.

This means that the site is able to use the necessary resources to complete the project without having to make further requests for approval.
