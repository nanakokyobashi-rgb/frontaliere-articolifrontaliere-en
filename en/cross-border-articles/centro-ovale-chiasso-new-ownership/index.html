Centro Ovale Chiasso: new ownership and end of Ellipticum

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 29 September 2026

After the failure of the Ellipticum project on 9 settembre, the Centro Ovale di Chiasso passes to a fashion multinational, destination still uncertain.

In Chiasso the Oval Center is starting over, but without a defined project. After eight years of vacancy and a succession of failed initiatives, Ellipticum also reached the end of the line. The company that promoted it went bankrupt on September 9, after having announced for two years an opening that never materialized.

The project, presented in 2024 though never officially, was supposed to bring gaming‑related activities, e‑sports and the multimedia world into the building. The bankruptcy left the Basso Mendrisiotto Water Service and the Municipality of Chiasso exposed for unpaid taxes.

The dossier still open

The Mendrisio Prosecutor’s Office declared the bankruptcy. The same SA appealed and obtained a stay of execution. Now the dossier is in the hands of the Chamber of Executions and Bankruptcies of the Court of Appeal; according to the source, a final decision should arrive in the coming days.

In the meantime a new change of ownership has occurred. According to RSI information, a large fashion multinational is set to take over. It is not known, however, whether the Oval Center will become a production site, a retail outlet or something else. Chiasso mayor Bruno Arrigoni explained that the Municipality has not yet had direct contact: he asked the new owner's lawyer and is awaiting a response for an initial introductory meeting.

"We hope this change will be the right one, as having this empty building at the entrance of Chiasso, just after the customs, is certainly a negative signal."

The previous chapter dates back to 2011, when the centre, then called Polaris, was inaugurated with great fanfare. Migros’s departure after just two years dealt a hard blow. Then the other stores closed one after another, leading to the total emptying in 2018. For now, the only new information is the change of ownership; the building’s future use and the final outcome of the procedure remain open.

Key points

To follow the matter without confusing announcements and decisions, a cross-border commuter can stick to four checks, all anchored in the available data. The method helps determine when a formal step becomes operational information, without filling the gaps with hypotheses.

1. Separate the bankruptcy from the ownership. Ellipticum’s bankruptcy was decreed by the Mendrisio Magistrate’s Court, but the SA’s appeal has suspensive effect. Until the decision of the Enforcement and Bankruptcy Chamber of the Court of Appeal, the legal case remains open.

2. Await the official use. The fashion multinational is identified as the new owner according to information from RSI, but it has not been clarified whether the building will house production, a retail outlet or something else. This is the first concrete information to await before speaking of services or jobs.

3. Read the institutional contact correctly. Mayor Bruno Arrigoni said that he had requested contact with the new owner’s lawyer and was waiting for a reply for an initial introductory meeting. A meeting does not amount to an opening date: that date is not in the source.

4. Keep the real-estate case separate from the route. The only practical reference is the building at the entrance to Chiasso, after customs. For crossing times, the tempi attesa valichi tool can be consulted, without attributing to Centro Ovale information that has not been communicated.

This sequence avoids two confusions: considering Ellipticum still operational and treating the multinational as if it had already announced its establishment. The next verifiable development will be the chamber’s decision; the other awaited step is the first contact between the Municipality and the new owner. Only afterward can it be determined whether there will be effects for Chiasso and for those entering the city from customs.

Frequently Asked Questions
What happened to the Ellipticum project?
The Ellipticum project, which involved gaming, e-sports and multimedia activities, came to an end. The promoter company declared bankruptcy on September 9, leaving uncovered debts with the Municipality of Chiasso and the Lower Mendrisiotto Water Service for unpaid taxes.
Who is the new owner of the Oval Center?
According to CSR information, ownership of the building has passed to a large multinational fashion company. However, it is not yet known whether the complex will become a production centre, a point of sale or have another destination, as the Municipality of Chiasso has not yet had direct contact with the new property.
What is the recent history of the Oval Center?
Opened in 2011 as Polaris, the centre suffered a blow with the departure of Migros after two years. Subsequently, other stores closed, leading to the total emptying of the building in 2018, which remained empty for eight years.
