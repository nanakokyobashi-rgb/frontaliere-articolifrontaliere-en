How the new Frontier Agreement works

The new Frontier Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020 between Switzerland and Italy. The agreement entered into force on 1 January 2024 and provides for the creation of a common tax regime for border workers working in Switzerland.

Switzerland applies a withholding tax for frontier workers, which varies according to income and nationality. Taxation may be higher for frontier workers than for Swiss workers.

Italy, on the other hand, offers a tax credit to border workers to avoid double taxation. This means that frontier workers can request a refund of the tax paid in Switzerland.

To avoid double taxation, Switzerland applies a withholding tax on the frontier worker's income. Subsequently, Italy offers a tax credit, allowing the worker to recover some or all of the taxes already paid in Switzerland.
