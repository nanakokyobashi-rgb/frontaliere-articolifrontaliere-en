Swiss Market Index up 2.3% in Zurich (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 May 2026

The Swiss Market Index soars to around 13,352 points, with a 2.30% increase. The podium is occupied by Richemont, Amrize, and Holcim.

Context

In brief

  • Swiss Market Index at 13'352 points (+2.30%)
  • Richemont +6.10%, Amrize +5.13%, Holcim +4.19%
  • Logitech -1.90%, Alcon -5.19% (dividend)

Key facts

  • What: Swiss Market Index
  • When: 6 May 2026
  • Where: Zurich Stock Exchange
  • Who: Investors and listed companies
  • Amount: +2.30%

The Swiss Market Index recorded a significant increase, reaching approximately 13'352 points, with a progress of 2.30%. This rise was supported by better-than-expected quarterly and annual results. In particular, the companies that recorded the best performances were Compagnie financière Richemont Sa (+6.10%), Amrize Ag (+5.13%) and Holcim Limited (+4.19%). On the other hand, Logitech International Sa (-1.90%) and Alcon Incorporated (-5.19%) were lagging behind, the latter with a decline to be relativized due to the detachment of a substantial dividend.

Performance of other exchanges

Other European exchanges also recorded a significant increase. The Dax-40 in Frankfurt rose by 2.32%, the Ftse-Mib in Milan by 1.95%, the Ftse-100 in London by 2.13%, the Cac-40 in Paris by 2.34% and the Ibex-35 in Madrid by 2.16%. These data indicate a positive trend that involves the whole of Europe.

Exchange rates and currencies

Exchange rates recorded the following variations: 91.62 cents of francs for one euro and 77.89 cents of francs for one US dollar. Bitcoin reached a theoretical value of approximately 63'728 francs per unit. These data are important for cross-border workers operating between Italy and Switzerland, as they directly influence purchasing power and financial planning.
