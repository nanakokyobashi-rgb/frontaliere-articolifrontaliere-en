The new AVS reform comes into effect from 1 January 2024, introducing significant changes for cross-border workers who work in Switzerland and reside in Italy. The new provisions mainly concern the contribution rates and the calculation methods of AVS contributions. For cross-border workers, it is crucial to understand how these changes may affect their income and pension contributions.

Contribution rates

The new AVS contribution rates have been revised to ensure greater fairness and sustainability of the pension system. Cross-border workers will need to adapt to the new rates, which may result in an increase or decrease in contributions depending on the income bracket. It is important to check your specific situation to avoid surprises.

For example, a cross-border worker residing in Varese with an annual income of 60,000 CHF will see an increase in the AVS contribution from 10.2% to 10.5% of the salary. This means an annual increase of 300 CHF, which will be withheld directly from the salary. For those earning 80,000 CHF, the increase will be 400 CHF annually.

Impact on cross-border workers

The AVS reform will have a significant impact on cross-border workers, particularly those working in Canton Ticino. The new rules may affect the calculation of pension contributions and, consequently, the net income received. It is advisable to consult a tax expert to better understand the practical implications of the reform.

Practical Analysis

The new AVS reform, which came into force on January 1, 2024, introduces several novelties that Ticino cross-border workers need to know to adapt to the new provisions. It is essential to understand how the new contribution rates affect your income and pension contributions.

Comparison with the previous situation

Before the reform, AVS contribution rates were calculated differently. For example, a cross-border worker living in Mendrisio with a monthly income of 5,000 CHF paid 10.2% of their income for AVS, with a monthly contribution of 510 CHF. With the new reform, the rates have been revised to ensure greater equity and sustainability of the pension system. This could result in an increase or decrease in contributions depending on the income bracket.

Concrete Scenarios

Suppose a cross-border worker earns 6,000 CHF per month. With the new contribution rates, the AVS contribution could increase or decrease depending on the income bracket. For example, if the cross-border worker lives in Lugano and falls into the income bracket between 5,000 and 8,000 CHF, the AVS contribution could increase from 10.2% to 11.4%, bringing the monthly contribution from 612 CHF to 684 CHF. Conversely, if the cross-border worker lives in Chiasso and falls into the income bracket between 3,000 and 5,000 CHF, the AVS contribution could decrease from 10.2% to 9.8%, bringing the monthly contribution from 510 CHF to 490 CHF.
