Mps deputies seek clarification on the early withdrawal of 700,000 francs and the pension calculation for the resigning minister

The interpellation reopens an issue already discussed

Claudio Zali's departure from the Council of State of the Canton of Ticino prompted Giuseppe Sergi and Matteo Pronzini, Mps deputies, to submit an interpellation to the government. The two are asking for clarifications about the pension entitlement of the Lega minister who resigned and argue that his pension case is still far from clear. The issue had already emerged in 2018, after Pronzini's reports.

The reports led, in March of the following year, to an order dismissing the case signed by Attorney General Andrea Pagani with regard to Zali. The measure concerned the advance withdrawal intended to finance his primary residence, requested and obtained by the State Councillor in March 2016. The date indicated for the order is 6 marzo 2019.

The move from the Court of Appeal to the government

Sergi and Pronzini start from that order. According to the interpellation, when Zali moved from the Court of Appeal to the Council of State, the occupational pension contributions were taken in by the Canton of Ticino, as provided for by the cited law. In return, the years spent in the service of the State as a teacher and magistrate would have been counted as years of service on the Council of State to determine the pension entitlement upon leaving the government.

The two parliamentarians' reading is that Zali had been entitled to the maximum pension entitlement from day one without paying a franc in pension contributions. They link this situation to the former pension rules for State Councillors, which they describe as still valid for the majority of the members of the current Executive.

The administrative chronology includes further steps. On 28 settembre 2015, an internal opinion from Ipct, sent by email to the entire Council of State by the then deputy director, indicated that the advance withdrawal would have an impact on the future pension entitlement. On 27 novembre 2015, Zali forwarded to Ipct the application for an advance withdrawal, a pledge arrangement and authorization for a registration notice in the land registry, for approximately 700mila franchi. On 2 dicembre 2015, the Council of State granted the withdrawal through a government resolution and indicated that it would entail a reduction in the pension entitlement. The amount had been reported by Corriere del Ticino on 8 maggio 2018.

During 2020, parliament amended the remuneration and pension provisions of members of the Executive. The resolution had reserved the right to review the calculation of the insurance period in the event of future rules. The questions now concern the reference annual salary, who calculated the pension entitlement Zali will receive starting on 1° ottobre, the percentage reduction linked to the 700mila franchi, and the effects of the amendment on the insurance period.

Operational details

What a cross-border worker can infer

The source's practical limitation

For someone who is a cross-border worker and works in Ticino, the news has a precise scope. The text concerns Claudio Zali's pension and the operation of the pension scheme for members of the cantonal Executive. It does not present a change concerning the G permit, AVS, LPP, LAMal, remittances or withholding tax. These topics are not linked to the interpellation.

This distinction is necessary to avoid automatically transposing the case into the everyday life of a commuter. The approximately 700 thousand francs mentioned in the interpellation are the amount of the withdrawal requested by Zali and granted by the Council of State in the case described. The source does not present them as a parameter for a payslip or for the contributions of a cross-border worker. Nor does it provide a final value for the pension, an already calculated percentage or a reference annual salary: these are precisely some of the points on which Sergi and Pronzini are asking for an answer.

The text nevertheless makes it possible to distinguish three aspects. The first is the pension position accrued in the transition from the Appeals Court to the Council of State. The second is the effect that Ipct's internal opinion and the resolution of 2 December 2015 attributed to the early withdrawal. The third is the possible consequence of the 2020 parliamentary amendment on the calculation of the insurance period. The interpellation does not say that these three aspects have already been brought together in a definitive calculation.

For the cross-border reader, the question to keep in mind is therefore which information is documented and which remains to be clarified. The source documents the existence of the political request and reconstructs the cited acts; it does not document a new rule for those who cross the border. To navigate the separate topics of Permesso G/B and AVS/LPP e rendita, it is better not to use this case as a substitute for a specific guide. The topic of withholding tax is also not addressed by the article.

The most prudent reading is this: the case concerns an individual position held by a State Councillor, the rules applicable to that role and the questions addressed to the government. As long as the available material does not include the calculation, the response to the questions and the exact effects of the 2020 reform, any further conclusion about the pension treatment of a cross-border worker would remain outside the source.

Key points

How to follow the dossier

Four steps based on the cited documents

1. Start with the subject of the interpellation. The questions are addressed to the Council of State and concern the reference annual salary, the party that calculated the pension starting from 1° ottobre, the percentage reduction linked to the withdrawal of 700mila franchi, and the effect of the change in the calculation of the insurance period.

2. Keep the 2015 documents distinct. The internal Ipct opinion appears on 28 settembre 2015; Zali's question is dated 27 novembre 2015; the government resolution arrives on 2 dicembre 2015. This sequence makes it possible not to confuse the opinion with the decision.

3. Separate the judicial step from the administrative part. The order not to proceed of 6 marzo 2019 concerns the matter of the early withdrawal and bears the signature of public prosecutor Andrea Pagani. The source cites it as the starting point of the interpellation, not as a response to the four current questions.

4. Verify the point left open since 2020. Parliament changed the remuneration and pension provision of Executive members; the 2015 resolution had reserved the right to review and amend the calculation of the insurance period in the event of new rules. The interpellation asks what effect that change had on Zali's pension. The text provided does not report the government's response.

For a cross-border reader, this is a reading procedure, not a form to fill out. The source does not indicate documents to submit, offices to contact, or a deadline for sending personal questions. It does, however, indicate the names of the acts and institutions that make up the matter: Ipct, Council of State, parliament, Court of Appeal and public prosecutor. These are the coordinates not to mix up when trying to understand whether a figure concerns a withdrawal, a reduction in the pension or the calculation of the years.

It is not possible to derive from this reconstruction a calculation for one's own payslip, nor an answer about pension provision or withholding tax. For this reason, the case should be kept separate from personal tools dedicated to cross-border work. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the central points of Sergi and Pronzini's interpellation?
MEPs Giuseppe Sergi and Matteo Pronzini (MPs) asked for clarification on the life annuity of Claudio Zali, which starts on 1 October. The questions specifically concern the reference annual salary, the person who made the calculation, the percentage of reduction applied due to an advance withdrawal of about 700 thousand francs and the effect of the 2020 parliamentary amendment on the calculation of the insurance period.
What concerns the decree of no place to proceed of March 6, 2019?
The decree, signed by Attorney General Andrea Pagani, concerns the early withdrawal of about 700,000 francs obtained by Claudio Zali in March 2016 for the financing of his primary home. This non-proceeding measure was cited by MEPs Sergi and Pronzini as a starting point for the current request for clarification.
What impact did the early withdrawal have on Zali's life annuity?
According to an internal opinion of the IPCT of 28 September 2015 and a government resolution of 2 December 2015, the early withdrawal of about 700,000 francs should have led to a reduction in future life annuities. The current interpellation asks for confirmation of what was the exact percentage of reduction applied in the final calculation of the pension.
How does the 2020 pension reform affect the Zali case?
During 2020, the parliament changed the remuneration and pensions of the members of the Executive. Since the 2015 government resolution had reserved a review of the calculation of the insurance period in the event of new regulations, MPs now ask to know the effect that this regulatory change had on the calculation of Claudio Zali's life annuity.
