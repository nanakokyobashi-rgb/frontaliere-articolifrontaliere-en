A renewed lakeside promenade by 2026

In Porto Ceresio, an Italian town just steps away from the Swiss border, work has started on renovating the pavement of the lakeside promenade near Piazzale Luraschi. The project also includes replacing the wooden walkway, ensuring a coordinated improvement of the entire area. According to the municipal administration, the work commenced thanks to favorable weather conditions, ideal for proper execution. The goal is to make the lakeside safer, more functional, and aesthetically pleasing, ready to welcome residents and visitors ahead of the summer season.

📊 Porto Ceresio, besides being a local tourism hub, is a strategic passage for many cross-border workers traveling to Ticino. The improved lakeside could enhance the quality of life for those living in the area and regularly crossing borders such as Ponte Tresa and Gaggiolo.

Implications for cross-border workers and tourism

For cross-border workers living near the border, the renovation of Porto Ceresio's lakeside promenade could positively impact local livability. A well-maintained and functional area not only improves accessibility but also enhances the value of the border region. Moreover, with the new lakeside promenade, the town could attract increased tourist flows, generating economic benefits for local businesses, many of which maintain close ties with Canton Ticino.

⚠️ The construction site is located near a strategic area for parking and access points, so cross-border workers are advised to check for potential changes to local traffic patterns during the construction period.

📊 According to a recent study by the Chamber of Commerce, increased tourism in the border area could lead to a 12% rise in commercial activity revenues by 2027. This trend could also represent an opportunity for cross-border workers seeking new job prospects.
