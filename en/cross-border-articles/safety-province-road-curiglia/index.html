Curiglia with Monteviasco: Safety work on provincial road 6 (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 15 June 2026

Safety measures for provincial road 6 in Curiglia continue, with temporary interventions and the emergence of new weaknesses in the landslide-affected slope.

Context

In brief

  • The Province of Varese is completing works on provincial road 6 in Curiglia.
  • Rockfall net installation intervention: approximately 35,000 euros.
  • Could be concluded by June 16.
  • New vulnerabilities have emerged on the affected slope.

Key facts

  • What: Securing of provincial road 6 in Curiglia.
  • When: Intervention started on June 9, scheduled for June 16.
  • Where: Curiglia con Monteviasco.
  • Who: Province of Varese.
  • Amount: approximately 35,000 euros.

The closure of provincial road 6, which occurred on Saturday, June 6, due to a landslide, isolated the village of Curiglia. To restore traffic, provincial authorities launched an emergency intervention on the slope affected by the collapse, involving the installation of metal rockfall nets. The estimated cost of approximately 35,000 euros will allow reinforcement of the landslide area, with the aim of completing the works by Tuesday, June 16, barring unforeseen events.

However, the works have revealed a more complex reality than anticipated: new vulnerabilities have been identified in areas immediately adjacent to the one affected by the landslide. This suggests the need for further security interventions to ensure the overall stability of the slope.

Fabio Passera, provincial councilor delegated to Infrastructure and Transport, commented: "We are satisfied, as the Province of Varese, to have been able to provide a prompt response to the securing of this stretch of SP6."

Passera also reminded that much remains to be done, emphasizing the importance of a broader intervention on the Piero-Lozzo agro-silvo-pastoral road, which connects Val Veddasca with Curiglia and Val Dumentina. This road represents a strategic alternative in case of further interruptions on SP6, the only main access artery to the village.

Operational details

Analysis of Practical Implications For cross-border workers who daily cross the Curiglia pass, the closure of provincial road 6 has represented a significant obstacle. The safety intervention, still in progress, has temporarily limited access, with possible repercussions on commuter traffic. The estimated completion date by June 16th allows us to foresee a return to normality, but the new vulnerabilities encountered on the Fraglia side indicate that there may be further delays or reinforcement interventions. Some alternative solutions, such as the Piero-Lozzo road, are already at the center of studies and monitoring. Its future reopening could offer a safer route and less subject to interruptions. This road, which connects the Val Veddasca with Curiglia, represents a strategic element for the connection between the territories and could become a turning point for those who work in Ticino and reside in the Canton Ticino. In the context of this emergency, cross-border workers must maintain high attention to official communications from the Lombardy Region and the Canton Ticino, for updates on reopening times and alternative routes. Collaboration between local and provincial authorities is fundamental to guarantee a timely and reliable flow of information. Furthermore, the restoration of traffic could involve adjustments to modes of transport, with the use of public transport or alternative vehicles. It is advisable to check travel times and any variations in connections, also consulting regional portals and traffic apps. In conclusion, the management of the vulnerabilities emerged during the works highlights the importance of preventive maintenance and structured emergency plans. The safety of the routes represents a key factor not only for residents but also for cross-border workers who depend on these communication routes.
