The course and its objectives

In 2026, approximately 80 operators took part in four editions of the course for the command staff of first-response organizations, referred to by the acronym SMEPI. Organized between Biasca and Giornico, the sessions were overseen by the Technical Commission for Training in Civil Protection.

The course was designed to improve the management of emergencies requiring the coordinated intervention of multiple services. It did not involve responses to real events: the activities used simulated scenarios to develop the ability to assess situations, make operational decisions and activate responses.

The three scenarios

The exercises tested participants in three situations. The first concerned a fire at the Heavy Vehicle Control Centre in Giornico. The second reproduced a public-order incident at the Industrial Services Centre of the Ente Ospedaliero Cantonale (EOC), in Biasca. The third was a weather-related emergency at Casa anziani leventinese in Giornico.

The work was led by instructors and carried out with the support of the managers of the infrastructures involved. In this way, participants were able to operate in their respective roles and apply the common procedures established through coordination between organizations.

Participants included heads of police, fire brigades and ambulance services, as well as Civil Protection officers and specialists from the Cantonal Technical Services. SMEPI serve as coordination structures where the organizations involved jointly organize the response to an emergency.

The program does not end with the four 2026 editions. An additional phase is planned for next year in another region of the canton, with different scenarios and new participants. The aim is to strengthen preparedness and cooperation among the organizations called upon to respond in crisis situations.
