G apprenticeship leave: extended duration for the entire internship (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 23 July 2026

The Council of States approves the motion for a G permit valid for the entire duration of the apprenticeship, exceeding the obligation of annual renewal.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Approved motion for multi-year G permits for apprentices.
  • The permit will cover the entire duration of the training contract.
  • Passed the annual renewal requirement for young border workers.

Key facts

  • What: Permitted release G for the entire duration of the apprenticeship.
  • When: Recent approval of the Council of States.
  • Where: Canton Ticino and other border cantons.
  • Who: Council of States (proposal by Martin Schmid).
  • Amount: Approximately 700 young cross-border apprentices involved.

The Council of States approved with 34 votes in favour and 3 abstentions the motion asking to grant cross-border apprentices a G permit valid for the entire duration of their internship in Switzerland, surpassing the current practice that requires an annual renewal. The proposal, filed by the councilor to the States of Grison Martin Schmid, aims to modify the existing legal bases. This parliamentary decision comes despite opposition from the Federal Council, which had recommended rejecting the motion on the basis of the Agreement on the Free Movement of Persons (FTA) and a recent Federal Court ruling of 19 May 2025 (2C_699/2023). According to the Government, an apprentice must be equated to a student, thus limiting the residence permit to one year, even if the course of study has a longer duration. Operational details

The current annual renewal procedure for the G permit for apprentices has been identified by the promoters of the motion as an unnecessary administrative burden. For many Ticino companies, especially those in the Mendrisiotto region, hiring a frontaliero apprentice is a necessary solution to fill available internship positions. The obligation to renew the permit every twelve months not only involves additional administrative costs and time, but also acts as a disincentive for small businesses that fear bureaucratic complications. Beyond the purely logistical aspect, there is a disparity in treatment compared to other frontaliero workers. Currently, those who sign a dependent employment contract lasting more than a year can obtain a G permit valid for five years. The motion of Martin Schmid proposes to apply the same logic to apprentices, as the duration of their training contract is known from the moment of signing, varying typically between two and four years.

Key points

For businesses and young apprentices, the new procedure, once implemented, will represent a step forward towards the simplification of bureaucratic procedures. While the motion is now under review by the National Council, it is essential that companies stay informed about the evolving regulations. Proper management of the necessary documentation for the issuance of the G permit remains a key element for those working in the Canton Ticino. Apprentices who intend to pursue a training path in Switzerland must ensure they have a regular apprenticeship contract, a fundamental element that justifies the request for the permit at the competent cantonal authorities. In the meantime, until the new legislative changes come into effect, companies must continue to follow the current annual renewal procedures. Frequently Asked Questions
What exactly changes with the passage of the motion?
The motion aims to amend the legal bases to allow the issuance of a G permit for border workers valid for the entire duration of the apprenticeship contract (from two to four years), eliminating the current annual renewal requirement required in cantons such as Ticino.
Why was the Federal Council against the proposal?
The Federal Council had opposed recalling the Agreement on the Free Movement of Persons (FTA), which limits the residence permit for trainees to one year, and citing a ruling of the Federal Court of 19 May 2025, which classifies the apprentice as a student and not as an employee.
How many border apprentices are there in Ticino?
Although there are no accurate and up-to-date federal statistics, cantonal estimates indicate the presence of about 700 border apprentices in Ticino, equal to about 8% of the total. In specific institutes such as SPAI in Mendrisio, the share also reaches 33%.
