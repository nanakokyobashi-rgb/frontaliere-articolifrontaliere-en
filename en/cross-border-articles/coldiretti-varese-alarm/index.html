"Rising fuel prices are hitting agricultural and agri-food businesses hard, which are already facing a difficult economic season." According to data provided by Coldiretti, in the canton of Varese alone there are over 3,000 farms that have experienced an increase in fuel costs of more than 25%. Key points

In a nutshell

The fuel race above 2 euros per liter is having a significant impact on the entire agri-food chain in Ticino. Tractors, mowers, animal and fodder carriers, refrigerated vans, deliveries and market participation all depend on fuels. Companies cannot automatically pass on price increases to producers, creating new tensions along the supply chain.

The alarm for businesses

Ticino farms are facing a difficult situation due to the fuel race. According to a survey conducted by the Association of Farmers of Ticino, 70% of agricultural companies have already recorded an increase in production costs due to the energy crisis. The numbers are alarming: a tractor that consumes 100 litres of diesel per day could see its cost increase by more than 800 Swiss francs per month. This means that an agricultural enterprise with 10 tractors could register an increase in costs of 8,000 Swiss francs per month.
