Operational details

Italian border workers living in Tronzano Lago Maggiore can work in Ticino, thanks to the New Tax Agreement between Italy and Switzerland. Income tax at source is only withheld in Switzerland for frontier workers.

The New Tax Agreement, signed on 21 June 2013, introduced important changes for Italian border workers working in Switzerland. Under the terms of the agreement, income tax at source is only withheld in Switzerland, rather than being withheld in both countries. This means that Italian frontier workers can enjoy more favourable taxation than in the past.

For example, if an Italian frontier worker works in Switzerland and earns 50,000 Swiss francs a year, the income tax at source will be withheld only in Switzerland, instead of also being withheld in Italy. This means that the Italian border crossing will be able to enjoy greater financial liquidity, thanks to the reduction in taxation.

In addition, the New Tax Agreement also introduced the possibility of deducting transport and living expenses in Switzerland from employment income. This means that Italian frontier workers can deduct expenses related to transport in Switzerland, such as expenses for the purchase of a train ticket, and expenses related to stay in Switzerland, such as accommodation expenses.

Under this agreement, Italian frontier workers residing in Switzerland and working in Ticino can benefit from a reduction in the rate of tax on employee income. The applicable tax rate is 5%, while in Italy the tax rate is 20% for income below 15,000 euros.

Frequently Asked Questions
How much is the daily transport for a cross-border commuter from Tronzano Lago Maggiore to Lugano?
The cost of daily transportation from Tronzano Lago Maggiore to Lugano is around 30-40 Swiss francs per day, according to 2022 data. This amount should be factored into your monthly budget, along with other expenses such as housing and health insurance.
What are the main economic opportunities for those who work in Ticino as cross-border workers from Italy?
The main opportunity is the wage difference. In 2022, the average salary in Ticino was around 6,500 Swiss francs per month, significantly higher than the approximately 3,500 euros per month average in Italy. This allows for a potential increase in purchasing power.
How does taxation work for Italian cross-border commuters working in Switzerland according to the New Tax Agreement?
Thanks to the New Tax Agreement of 2013, withholding tax on earned income is withheld exclusively in Switzerland. This avoids double taxation and increases the worker's net liquidity, also simplifying tax compliance.
Can I deduct transport and accommodation costs in Switzerland if I work as a cross-border commuter?
Yes, the New Tax Agreement allows you to deduct the expenses incurred for transport and stay in Switzerland from your taxable employment income. This can further reduce the overall tax burden for cross-border commuters.
What are the operational aspects to consider for a cross-border commuter who lives in Italy and works in Ticino?
It is crucial to consider health insurance in Ticino, daily transportation costs, expenses for any accommodation in Switzerland, and income taxes withheld at source in Switzerland. Careful planning is crucial to managing life as a cross-border commuter.
