Public Administration Job Day in Como: dates and details

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 28 September 2026

On October 1 at Villa Gallia and Villa Saporiti: 28 public entities present sought-after profiles, competitions and professional opportunities.

Context

In brief

  • The Job Day takes place giovedì 1° ottobre, from 9.30 to 14.00
  • Event at Villa Gallia and Villa Saporiti
  • 28 public and local organizations are on the program
  • Free participation with registration on Eventbrite

Key facts

  • What → Public Administration Job Day
  • When → giovedì 1° ottobre, from 9.30 to 14.00
  • Where → Villa Gallia and Villa Saporiti
  • Who → 28 public and local organizations
  • What is presented → activities, sought-after profiles, open competitions and selection processes
  • Access → free participation and registration on Eventbrite

Giovedì 1° ottobre, from 9.30 to 14.00, Villa Gallia and Villa Saporiti will host the Public Administration Job Day. The initiative is intended for those who want to learn more about public-sector work, the professional opportunities available and how to access the various institutions.

During the day, it will be possible to meet 28 public and local organizations directly. Representatives will present the activities of the institutions, the professional profiles they are seeking, open competitions and how to take part in the selection processes. Direct interaction will make it possible to gather information and navigate the various career opportunities offered by the Public Administration.

Among the participants are Agenzia delle Entrate, ALER Varese-Como-Monza Brianza- Busto Arsizio, ASCI – Azienda Sociale Comuni Insieme, ASF Autolinee, Azalea Cooperativa Sociale, Azienda Sociale Lariana, Azienda Sociale Comasca Lariana, Camera di Commercio Como-Lecco, Casa Circondariale di Como, CFP Como, Como Acqua and several municipalities in the area. Also present will be CSV Insubria, Guardia di Finanza, INAIL, INPS, Consorzio Mosaico, OPI Como, Provincia di Como, Questura di Como, Regione Lombardia, ATS Insubria and Università degli Studi dell’Insubria.

The program also includes seminars and in-depth sessions. One of the scheduled events is a meeting on InPA, from application to preparation for the competitive examination, with practical guidance on consulting opportunities, correctly submitting the application and preparing for the tests. The Servizio Civile Universale, the Leva Civica and internship opportunities with Public Administrations will also be covered.

«With this Job Day, we want to offer citizens, and young people in particular, the opportunity to learn more about a world that often seems complex or not very accessible,» emphasizes Fiorenzo Bongiasca, President of Provincia di Como.

To follow other career opportunities as well, it is possible to consult annunci di lavoro, keeping the general catalog separate from the list of organizations participating in the initiative.

Operational details

Useful guidance, with a precise scope

For a cross-border worker who currently works in Canton Ticino, the concrete connection that can be drawn from the news remains limited to the possibility of learning about professional opportunities in Public Administration in Como. The source describes a day of meetings, information and guidance; it does not present it as an event dedicated to cross-border employment or as a change to tax or social security rules.

What the day actually offers

The text contains no information on G permits, AVS, LPP, LAMal, tax rebates, withholding tax or double taxation. Nor does it discuss the Brogeda border crossing. These topics should therefore neither be attributed to the participating organizations nor used to interpret the Job Day as an update on the relationship between working in Switzerland and living in the Como area. The useful point for those considering a different career path is another: speaking directly with the participating organizations and understanding which profiles they are seeking, which competitions are open and how the selection processes work.

Key points

How to participate

The procedure outlined for taking part in the Job Day is simple and involves registering on the Eventbrite platform. Participation is free and registrations are open.

The steps indicated by the program

1. Register via Eventbrite, where it is possible to sign up for the initiative.

2. Attend on Thursday 1° ottobre, during the time slot from 9.30 to 14.00, at Villa Gallia and Villa Saporiti.

3. Identify the institutions of greatest interest and meet their representatives directly. The program allows participants to ask for information about the activities, the professional profiles sought, open competitions and how to take part in the selection processes.

4. Attend the meeting dedicated to InPA, following the instructions on how to consult the available opportunities, correctly submit the application and prepare for the competition exams.

5. Pay attention to the sessions dedicated to the Universal Civil Service, Civic Service and internships at Public Administrations.

The source does not indicate any other mandatory steps, documents to attach to the registration or specific requirements for accessing the venue. The documented action is therefore registration on Eventbrite, followed by participation in the scheduled meetings and seminars. Speaking with the representatives of the institutions helps gather information and navigate the professional opportunities available.

To continue looking for opportunities after the event, consult annunci di lavoro.

Frequently Asked Questions
When and where will the Public Administration Job Day be held in Como?
The event takes place on Thursday 1 October, from 9.30 am to 2.00 pm. The host locations are Villa Gallia and Villa Saporiti. Participation is free, but prior registration is required through the Eventbrite platform to access the initiative.
What public bodies and entities will participate in the event?
There will be 28 public and local entities, including the Revenue Agency, INPS, INAIL, Guardia di Finanza, the Lombardy Region, the Province of Como, the Como Police Headquarters, ATS Insubria, the University of Insubria and several local municipalities, as well as the Como-Lecco Chamber of Commerce, the Como District House, CFP Como, Como Acqua and various social and cooperative companies.
What information and career opportunities are available?
The representatives of the bodies will present the activities, the professional profiles sought, the open competitions and the selection procedures. The program also includes seminars on InPA for the application and preparation of competitions, insights on the Universal Civil Service, Civic Leverage and internship opportunities at the Public Administrations.
