Valbrona extends into the province of Como a few kilometres from the Ticino border, representing a growing choice for those who wish to work in Ticino while maintaining residence in Italy. The attraction lies in the cost-of-living differential — rents, services and real estate cost less than in the Canton of Ticino — combined with Swiss wages higher than in Italy. However, moving to Valbrona as a border worker requires a careful assessment of logistical, fiscal and administrative factors that go far beyond the simple comparison of house prices.

Operational details

Real life cost: benefits and offsets

The housing differential between Valbrona and Lugano is concrete. A two-room apartment for rent in Valbrona costs approximately 20-40% less than in Lugano or common high-end Ticino. If the objective is to buy property, the price per Italian square metre is significantly lower: real estate investment and accumulation of assets are more accessible in Valbrona. Groceries have similar prices in the two countries thanks to large chains (Coop, Migros) on both sides, although local Italian products cost slightly less in Valbrona.

However, daily commuting erodes much of the economic advantage. Those who use private cars face fuel, maintenance, insurance with CH-IT coverage, and often paid parking at the Ticino employer. Those who choose public transport buy monthly subscriptions that must be evaluated in the overall calculation. It is not uncommon for net housing savings (after deduction of transport costs) to fall to 10-15% per annum.

Step 2: Inform your Swiss employer of your intention to transfer to Italy as a border worker. The employer will initiate the procedure for the G Permit with the cantonal authorities in Ticino. Required documents: employment contract, copy of ID, Italian residence certificate, employment declaration. Step 3: Register with the Como Province Tax Agency (or competent) and notify the transfer of residence. Update your address with your previous Italian municipality of residence. ### Phase 2: Housing Research and Logistics Optimization (2-3 months) Step 4: Identify strategic areas in Valbrona for commuting. The Zelbio fraction is closer to Brogeda (~5 km); other areas are in Chiasso (~10 km). Use Google Maps to test real-time travel times during peak hours (7-9 AM) from various possible residences to your workplace. Step 5: Calculate the total monthly transportation cost: fuel + car maintenance + insurance + parking, or public transportation subscription. Compare the savings from living in Valbrona vs Lugano with this additional cost. It should remain positive by at least 15-20% annually, otherwise the trade-off is not beneficial. Step 6: Verify with your employer if they offer free or discounted parking for border workers. Many large Ticinese companies provide parking spots to compensate for the cost of private transportation for commuters. ### Phase 3: Insurance Obligations (3-4 weeks) Step 7: Decide on the health insurance regime. Contact specialized brokers (e.g. border area intermediaries) to receive quotes for Swiss LAMal vs Italian CMI. Evaluate coverage, deductibles, and premiums. LAMal is usually recommended for full-time workers in Ticino. Step 8: Insure your vehicle with a company offering CH-IT coverage (international circulation). Obtain the Swiss vignette (valid for 1 year, ~CHF 40). Verify the validity of your Italian car vignette and RC insurance for the Italian side. Frequently Asked Questions
How much time do I spend travelling from Valbrona to Lugano/Bellinzona during working hours?
From Valbrona to Lugano via Brogeda it takes on average 30-40 minutes in normal traffic conditions (8-10 AM). During peak hours (7-9 AM, 17-19 PM), times increase to 50-60 minutes. In Bellinzona the time is 15-20 minutes longer. Those who choose public transport (motorway + possible train) must add 20-30 minutes for the connection from the Italian municipality to the station. Always check with Google Maps during the actual commute time before signing a rental agreement.
Should you move to Valbrona rather than live directly in Ticino?
It depends on your personal priorities. The housing savings (20-40% on rents) are real, but the cost of commuting (car/public transport) reduces the net benefit to 10-15% per annum. Those who want to buy real estate find Valbrona very competitive for asset investment. Those who prefer living spaces, local Italian community, and quality of home life benefit from the Italian choice. How does the G Permit work and what is the processing time?
Permit G is a cross-border work licence requested by the Swiss employer from the cantonal authorities of Ticino. The permit allows the Italian resident border crosser to cross the border on a daily basis for work reasons. Processing time: 2-4 weeks from submission of the application. The permit is linked to the specific employer and is valid for many years (3-5 years, renewable). Changing jobs involves a change procedure or a new request.
How is border taxation calculated in Valbrona?
The border worker pays tax at source directly in Switzerland (withheld on the gross paycheck). In Italy, from 1 January 2024 (New Frontier Agreement), the border worker benefits from a deductible of €10,000 per year (if new border worker) or €7,500 (if already border worker before 17 July 2023, transitional regime until 2033), and a tax credit in declaration 730 to avoid double taxation. Which health insurance should I choose as a border worker in Valbrona?
You have two options: LAMal (Swiss health insurance) or CMI (Italian insurance with CH reciprocity). LAMal offers full coverage in Ticino, adult allowance CHF 300-2,500 per year, and is usually recommended for border workers working full-time in Ticino. CMI often has lower premiums and mainly covers Italy with reciprocity for emergencies in Switzerland, but is less comprehensive for Ticino specialist care.
