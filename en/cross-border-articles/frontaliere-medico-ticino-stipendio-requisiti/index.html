Requirements to work as a border doctor in Ticino

To work as a border doctor in Ticino, you must have a degree in medicine and surgery from a recognised Swiss or international university. In addition, it is necessary to be registered in the register of doctors of the Canton of Ticino. You can consult the website of the State Council of the Canton of Ticino for detailed requirements.

Recognition of qualification

Recognition of the qualification is necessary to work as a border doctor in Ticino. It is possible to request recognition of the qualification from the Council of State of the Canton of Ticino. The cost for the recognition of the qualification is CHF 500.00. You can pay the fee by bank transfer or cash.
