Seasonal work in Ticino represents an important opportunity for many Italian cross-border workers. For Italian cross-border workers, seasonal work in Ticino is regulated by specific regulations that provide for the G permit, withholding tax, and seasonal contracts. These aspects make seasonal work in Ticino an attractive option for those seeking flexibility and earning opportunities.

G permit for seasonal work

The G permit is the document required to work seasonally in Ticino. This permit is valid for a maximum of 9 months a year and allows Italian cross-border workers to work in specific sectors such as agriculture, tourism, and catering. The G permit is issued by the cantonal authorities and must be renewed annually. To obtain the G permit, cross-border workers must submit an application to the competent authorities, providing documents such as the employment contract and passport.

Concrete actions for seasonal cross-border workers

For Italian cross-border workers who wish to work seasonally in Ticino, it is important to follow a series of concrete steps. First, it is necessary to find an employer offering a seasonal contract. Subsequently, it is important to submit the application for a G permit to the competent cantonal authorities. Once the permit is obtained, cross-border workers can start working in Ticino for a maximum of 9 months per year.

Step-by-step procedure

1. Find an employer: Look for seasonal job offers in Ticino through employment agencies, specialized websites, and local advertisements. 2. Submit the application for a G permit: Once an employer is found, submit the application for a G permit to the competent cantonal authorities. The application must be accompanied by documents such as the employment contract and passport. 3. Obtaining the G permit: The cantonal authorities examine the application and, if everything is in order, issue the G permit. The G permit is valid for a maximum of 9 months per year and must be renewed annually. 4. Start of seasonal work: Once the G permit is obtained, cross-border workers can start working in Ticino for a maximum of 9 months per year. 5. Managing taxation: During the period of seasonal work, cross-border workers are subject to withholding tax. It is important to submit an annual tax return in Italy, including the income earned in Switzerland. 6. Renewal of the G permit: At the end of the seasonal contract, cross-border workers can submit a new application for a G permit for the following year, if they wish.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the maximum duration of a seasonal contract in Ticino?
The maximum duration of a seasonal contract in Ticino is 9 months per year. This allows Italian cross-border workers to work seasonally for an extended period without needing an indefinite work permit.
What are the most common sectors for seasonal work in Ticino?
The most common sectors for seasonal work in Ticino are agriculture, tourism, and catering. These sectors offer numerous seasonal job opportunities, especially during peak seasons such as summer and the Christmas holidays.
How can I obtain the G permit to work seasonally in Ticino?
To obtain the G permit, you need to submit an application to the competent cantonal authorities, providing documents such as the employment contract and passport. The cantonal authorities review the application and, if everything is in order, issue the G permit.
