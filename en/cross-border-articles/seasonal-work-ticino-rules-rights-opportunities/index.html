Seasonal work in Ticino represents an important opportunity for many Italian cross-border workers. For Italian cross-border workers, seasonal work in Ticino is regulated by specific regulations that provide for the G permit, withholding tax, and seasonal contracts. These aspects make seasonal work in Ticino an attractive option for those seeking flexibility and earning opportunities.

G permit for seasonal work

The G permit is the document required to work seasonally in Ticino. This permit is valid for a maximum of 9 months a year and allows Italian cross-border workers to work in specific sectors such as agriculture, tourism, and catering. The G permit is issued by the cantonal authorities and must be renewed annually. To obtain the G permit, cross-border workers must submit an application to the competent authorities, providing documents such as the employment contract and passport.

Seasonal contracts

Seasonal contracts in Ticino are regulated by specific regulations that provide for a maximum duration of 9 months a year. These contracts are offered in sectors such as agriculture, tourism, and catering. Italian cross-border workers who work seasonally in Ticino must comply with the contractual conditions established by Swiss law. Seasonal contracts usually provide for hourly or monthly remuneration, depending on the sector and the specific conditions of the contract.

Taxation for seasonal cross-border workers

Italian cross-border workers who work seasonally in Ticino are subject to withholding tax. This tax system provides that part of the salary is withheld by the employer and paid directly to the Swiss tax authorities. Withholding tax is calculated based on income and the duration of the employment contract. Seasonal cross-border workers must file an annual tax return in Italy, including income earned in Switzerland. This process is simplified thanks to the tax agreements between Italy and Switzerland, which avoid double taxation.

Opportunities for seasonal work

Canton Ticino offers numerous opportunities for seasonal work in sectors such as agriculture, tourism, and catering. Italian cross-border workers can find seasonal job offers through employment agencies, specialized websites, and local advertisements. It is important for cross-border workers to inform themselves about seasonal job opportunities and submit job applications promptly to increase the chances of being hired.

Procedure to Obtain the G Permit

For Italian cross-border workers to obtain the G permit, they must follow a specific procedure. First, they need to find an employer in Ticino offering a seasonal work contract. Subsequently, the employer must submit a G permit application to the competent cantonal authorities. The application must be accompanied by documents such as the work contract, passport, and any professional qualifications. The cantonal authorities review the application and, if everything is in order, issue the G permit. The G permit is valid for a maximum of 9 months per year and must be renewed annually.
