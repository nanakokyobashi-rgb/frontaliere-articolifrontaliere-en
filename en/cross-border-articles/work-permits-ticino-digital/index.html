Work permits in Ticino: from 3 August all online (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 18 July 2026

From 3 August 2026, the procedure for work permits in Ticino becomes entirely digital. Goodbye to paper for border workers, residents and domiciliaries.

Context

Brief Overview

  • Digitalization of all permit requests from August 3, 2026.
  • Definitive elimination of paper submission and handwritten signature.
  • Valid procedure for G, B, C permits and practices managed by companies.
  • Greater traceability and reduced waiting times for practices.

Key Facts

  • What: Digital permit application obligation for residence and work permits.
  • When: Monday, August 3, 2026.
  • Where: Canton Ticino, Migration Office.
  • Who: Frontiersmen, residents (B), domiciliaries (C) and employers.
  • Volume: Approximately 700 files managed daily.

The Population Section of the Canton Ticino has announced a radical transformation in the bureaucratic management of residence and work permits. Starting from Monday, August 3, 2026, every request, renewal, or modification of a permit must be submitted exclusively in digital form. This decision marks the definitive end of the hybrid procedure that characterized the last few years, eliminating the obligation to print the online completed form, sign it manually, and send the paper package by mail to Bellinzona.

…

Operational details

The switch to the fully digital procedure brings concrete advantages for those who interface with the Migration Office. Until now, the hybrid system introduced in 2017 required a double step: online guided compilation followed by paper materialization. This method entailed the need for cantonal offices to scan and re-record documents received by post, significantly lengthening processing times and increasing the risk of human error. With the new system, the document cycle remains digital from start to finish, guaranteeing greater speed in taking charge of the practice.

Operational benefits for companies and workers

The operational innovations introduced offer more flexible management tools. Among the main benefits, it is possible to save the file at any time during compilation, allowing it to be resumed later without having to start the entire procedure from scratch. In addition, the traceability of the wrapping will be total, since each phase of the request will remain digitally recorded in the system, allowing users to know the progress of their practice. In case of technical difficulties, the Canton has foreseen the direct support of the Cantonal Contact Center, which will replace the interactions based on sending paper documents, which from 3 August 2026 are likely to no longer be accepted.

Key points

For those who must face a renewal practice or a new request in the proximity of the fateful date of August 3, 2026, it is necessary to plan carefully the transition. The first recommendation is to verify the deadline of your own permit in a timely manner. If the term falls around the start of the new procedure, it is advisable to consult your employer to define which channel to use, avoiding sending paper envelopes that may not be processed by the system anymore. The preparation must take place upstream: it is necessary to have all the documents in digital format, including the identity document, the employment contract, and any other required attestation.

Frequently Asked Questions
What if I submit a paper application after August 3, 2026?
From 3 August 2026, the procedure becomes exclusively digital. Sending paper documentation is likely to no longer be accepted by the Migration Office, as the new system is designed to manage a digital cycle from start to finish. It is essential to contact the cantonal Contact Center in case of doubt about the procedure, avoiding the sending of postal envelopes.
Do the requirements to obtain the permit change with digitization?
No, the legal requirements, the conditions for obtaining permission, as well as the documents to be attached, remain the same as the current ones. Digitalization only concerns the demand transmission channel, making the forwarding process more efficient and traceable, but it does not change the current rules on migration.
Do companies need to update their internal procedures?
Yes, in particular companies with high turnover must prepare by updating the internal procedures, verifying who in the company has the access credentials to the cantonal digital services and organizing the accompanying documentation in electronic format, since the old method of sending by mail will no longer be operational.
