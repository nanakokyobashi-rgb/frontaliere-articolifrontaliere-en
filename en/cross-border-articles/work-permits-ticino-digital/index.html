Work permits in Ticino: from 3 August all online

From 3 August 2026, the procedure for work permits in Ticino becomes entirely digital. Goodbye to paper for border workers, residents and domiciliaries.

Context

TL;DR

  • Full digitization of permit applications from August 3, 2026.
  • Permanent elimination of paper submissions and handwritten signatures.
  • Procedure valid for permits G, B, C and practices managed by companies.
  • Greater traceability and reduction of waiting times for practices.

Key facts

  • What: Mandatory digital application for residence and work permits.
  • When: From Monday 3 August 2026.
  • Where: Canton Ticino, Migration Office.
  • Who: Cross-border workers, residents (B), domiciled (C) and employers.
  • Volume: Approximately 700 packages handled daily.

The Population Section of the Canton of Ticino has announced a radical transformation in the bureaucratic management of residence and work permits. Starting from Monday 3 August 2026, each permit request, renewal or modification must be submitted exclusively digitally. This decision marks the definitive end of the hybrid procedure that has characterized recent years, eliminating the obligation to print the form completed online, affix the handwritten signature and send the paper package by post to Bellinzona.

A considerable volume of files

The impact of this change is notable when considering the day-to-day operations of the Office of Migration. On average, the organization handles approximately 700 files per day, a number that encompasses a wide range of administrative situations. Among these, the largest audience is made up of cross-border workers with G permit, residents with a B permit and domiciled residents with a C permit. No less important is the role of Ticino companies, which by law submit the applications on behalf of their employees. The transition to digital is not an arbitrary choice, but an operational necessity also dictated by the delays and technical times linked to the renewal of the federal SIMIC application, which pushed the Canton to proceed autonomously to optimize the document management cycle. This digitalisation does not change the legal requirements, but transforms the channel for transmitting requests, making the process more linear and less prone to transcription errors or loss of paper documents.

Operational details

The transition to the entirely digital procedure entails concrete advantages for those who interface with the Migration Office. Until now, the hybrid system introduced in 2017 required a double step: guided online compilation followed by paper materialization. This method meant that the cantonal offices had to scan and re-register the documents received by post, significantly extending processing times and increasing the risk of human errors. With the new system, the document cycle remains digital from start to finish, ensuring greater speed in handling the case.

Operational advantages for companies and workers

The operational innovations introduced offer more flexible management tools. Among the main benefits, we highlight the possibility of saving the application at any time during the compilation, allowing it to be resumed at a later time without having to start the entire procedure from scratch. Furthermore, the traceability of the package will be total, since each phase of the request will remain digitally recorded in the system, allowing users to know the progress of their case. In the event of technical difficulties, the Canton has provided direct support from the Cantonal Contact Center, which will replace interactions based on sending paper documents, which from 3 August 2026 risk no longer being accepted.

It is essential to clarify that digitalisation only concerns the transmission channel. The legal requirements, the required checks, the documents to be attached and the conditions for obtaining the permit remain identical to the current ones. Anyone who did not meet the necessary requirements before this reform will not obtain the right simply because the application is sent via the web. For Ticino companies, in particular those operating in high-turnover sectors such as construction, private healthcare, industry and retail trade in the Mendrisiotto and Lugano areas, this change represents an opportunity to update internal human resources management procedures, verifying the credentials for access to cantonal digital services and organizing the accompanying documentation exclusively in electronic format.

Key points

For those who have to face a renewal procedure or a new request close to the fateful date of August 3, 2026, it is necessary to plan the transition carefully. The first recommendation is to promptly check the expiry of your permit. If the deadline falls close to the start of the new procedure, it is advisable to consult your employer to define which channel to use, avoiding sending paper packages that may no longer be processed by the system. The preparation must take place upstream: it is necessary to have all the documents in digital format, including the identity document, the employment contract and any other required certification.

Practical advice for the transition

Despite the online saving function, it is important to remember that the legal deadlines for submitting applications remain unchanged. There is no need to leave the procedures pending by trusting the system's memory, since the responsibility for timeliness remains with the applicant or the employer. For those approaching the Swiss job market for the first time, the advice is to carefully study the basics: type of permit, requirements, withholding tax and insurance obligations such as the KVG. An updated overview is always available in the reference guides, while the official information and updated forms must be taken only from the website of the Population Section of the Canton of Ticino, which remains the only reliable source for verifying any transitory indications or technical updates linked to the activation of the new application.

Frequently Asked Questions
What if I submit a paper application after August 3, 2026?
From 3 August 2026, the procedure becomes exclusively digital. Sending paper documentation is likely to no longer be accepted by the Migration Office, as the new system is designed to manage a digital cycle from start to finish. It is essential to contact the cantonal Contact Center in case of doubt about the procedure, avoiding the sending of postal envelopes.
Do the requirements to obtain the permit change with digitization?
No, the legal requirements, the conditions for obtaining permission, as well as the documents to be attached, remain the same as the current ones. Digitalization only concerns the demand transmission channel, making the forwarding process more efficient and traceable, but it does not change the current rules on migration.
Do companies need to update their internal procedures?
Yes, in particular companies with high turnover must prepare by updating the internal procedures, verifying who in the company has the access credentials to the cantonal digital services and organizing the accompanying documentation in electronic format, since the old method of sending by mail will no longer be operational.
