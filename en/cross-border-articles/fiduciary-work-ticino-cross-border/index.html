Requirements to work as a trustee in Ticino

To work as a trustee in Ticino, you must meet the requirements established by federal law. According to the federal law of 20 December 2019 (LSA 2019 103), the border worker must be in possession of a G permit in order to exercise the profession of trustee in Ticino.

Permission G

The G permit is a document that certifies the possibility of working as a trustee in Ticino. To obtain the G permit, the border worker must submit an application to the Swiss Immigration Office and pay an amount of CHF 250.

Permit Requirements G

To obtain the G permit, the border crossing must meet the following requirements:

  • Be a citizen of a Member State of the European Union
  • Have a monthly income of at least CHF 3,800
  • Having health insurance and disability insurance
  • Have life insurance of at least 5,000 CHF
  • Have at least 5 years of work experience as a trustee
