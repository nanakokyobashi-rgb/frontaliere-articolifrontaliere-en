The verdict in Varese

On venerdì 25 settembre, at the end of the hearing that opened shortly after 9:30 a.m. with the sound of the bell, the Varese Court of Assizes delivered its verdict in the trial against Marco Manfrinati.

The defendant, accused of the murder of Fabio Limido and the attempted murder of Lavinia Limido, was sentenced to life imprisonment. The operative part of the judgment also provides for two months of daytime isolation. The proceedings concern the events that took place on 6 maggio 2024 on via Ciro Menotti, in the Casbeno district.

The courtroom confrontation

Manfrinati was not present in the courtroom. His defense attorney, Elio Giannangeli, returned to the general mitigating circumstances and premeditation, which the defense had already asked to have excluded.

At the opening, attorney Fabio Ambrosetti, representing the family acting as civil party, announced that on 14 ottobre the Cassazione will rule on the final level of judgment in the stalking trial.

The rebuttals then followed. Prosecutor Claudia Maria Contini focused on the aggravating circumstances of stalking and premeditation, as well as on the mitigating circumstance requested by the defense for the unjust act of another, namely provocation. Ambrosetti joined the prosecution’s conclusions.

The prosecutor argued that:

«Fabio Limido had been in the crosshairs for years».

After the reading of the operative part of the judgment, the defense announced an appeal. Varese Public Prosecutor Antonio Gustapane expressed appreciation for the verdict of the Court of Assizes, describing it as proportionate to the charges brought against the defendant.

Operational details

Reading the aggravating circumstances

Two counts, different assessments

The operative part distinguishes between the two offenses charged against Marco Manfrinati. Premeditation was recognized only for the attempted murder of Lavinia Limido. For the murder of Fabio Limido, however, the same aggravating circumstance was not recognized.

Stalking was recognized for both counts. The remaining aggravating circumstances related to cruelty and diminished ability to defend oneself were instead dropped. The decision therefore contains different assessments depending on the fact examined: premeditation concerns only one of the two counts, while stalking concerns both.

The civil parties were awarded compensation of over two million. The figure is indicated in the source as an overall amount, without any further breakdown among the recipients or individual items.
