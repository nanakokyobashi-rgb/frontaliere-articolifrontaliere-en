Tuesday, October 20, at 21, the first event of Eventi in Jazz 2026 is scheduled at the Cinema Teatro Dante di Castellanza. The event is organized by the Municipalities of Castellanza and Busto Arsizio; the artistic direction is entrusted to Mario Caccia. The announced schedule includes two evenings at the same theater and maintains the same start time for both: the first on October 20, the second on October 30.

The Davide Pannozzo Trio features Davide Pannozzo on guitar, Etienne Stadwijk on keyboards, and Steve Ferrone on drums. The proposal spans soul, funk, blues, and jazz, leaving ample room for improvisation and groove. The second event

Friday, October 30, again at 21, Simply Bop Big Band will be the featured act. The band consists of twenty musicians and dedicates the evening to trumpeter Emilio Soana, described as a leading figure in Italian jazz. Several guests are also expected on stage: Paolo Tomelleri, Rudy Migliardi, and four trumpeters from the national scene. The second date therefore stands out for its big-band format, its tribute, and the presence of guest musicians, while the first is entrusted to Pannozzo’s trio.
