Works at Gallarate station: changes to the S5 line and replacement buses (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 September 2026

From 14 September to 5 October 2026, maintenance work at Gallarate station. Changes to the S5 Treviglio-Gallarate-Varese line and replacement buses.

Context

In brief

  • Maintenance works at Gallarate station
  • Changes to the S5 line Treviglio-Gallarate-Varese
  • Substitute buses between Busto Arsizio, Gallarate, and Varese

Key facts

  • What: Maintenance works at Gallarate station
  • When: From September 14 to October 5, 2026
  • Where: Gallarate station, Lombardy
  • Who: Trenord
  • Amount: Not specified

The scheduled maintenance works at Gallarate station will lead to changes in the circulation of trains on the S5 line Treviglio-Gallarate-Varese in the months of September and October. The variations will particularly affect the nights from September 7 to 8 until September 11 to 12 and from October 5 to 6 until October 7 to 8, 2026. During these periods, some evening and night trains will be canceled and replaced by buses.

Details of Changes

The train S5 24582, departing from Treviglio at 22:40 and expected to arrive in Gallarate at 00:23, will be canceled from Busto Arsizio to Gallarate. To continue the journey, bus 7582A is scheduled, departing from Busto Arsizio at 00:21 with stops in Gallarate at 00:37, Cavaria con Premezzo at 00:49, Albizzate at 00:56, Castronno at 1:04, and Gazzada Schianno at 1:13, until arrival in Varese at 1:28.

The train S5 24584, departing from Treviglio at 23:10 and expected to arrive in Gallarate at 00:53, will also be canceled on the Busto Arsizio-Gallarate section. The service will be replaced by bus 7584A, departing from Busto Arsizio at 00:51 and arriving in Gallarate at 1:06.

On the same days, the bus 582A Gallarate-Varese, normally departing at 00:30 and arriving at 1:28, will be canceled and replaced by bus 7582A coming from Busto Arsizio.

Operational details

Implications for Commuters

The changes to the S5 line will have a significant impact on commuters who use this line to travel between Treviglio, Gallarate, and Varese. During the work periods, travelers will need to adapt to the modified schedules and use replacement buses to complete their journey.

Replacement Bus Schedules and Stops

The stops for the replacement buses will generally be in the squares in front of the stations. However, there will be some exceptions:

  • In Busto Arsizio, the stop will be in piazzale Volontari della Libertà.
  • In Cavaria con Premezzo, at the intersection of via Ronchetti and via Stazione.
  • In Albizzate, at the intersection of via Mazzini and via Matteotti.
  • In Castronno, at via Roma 28.
  • In Gazzada Schianno, near the restaurant "Da Serafino," at piazza San Bernardino 1.

Trenord reminds that the bus schedules may vary depending on road traffic conditions and that bicycles are not allowed on the replacement buses.

Advice for Commuters

To avoid inconvenience, commuters are advised to check the updated schedules and plan their trips in advance. It is also recommended to keep an eye on any additional communications from Trenord regarding further changes or updates.

Concrete Examples and Regulations

For example, commuters living in Mendrisio and working in Varese will need to adapt to an alternative route that could add up to 30 minutes to their daily journey. The current regulations stipulate that Trenord will compensate travelers with a 50% refund of the ticket cost for days when the work causes delays of more than 30 minutes.
