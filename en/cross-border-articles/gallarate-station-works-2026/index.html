The scheduled maintenance works at Gallarate station will lead to changes in the circulation of trains on the S5 line Treviglio-Gallarate-Varese in the months of September and October. The variations will particularly affect the nights from September 7 to 8 until September 11 to 12 and from October 5 to 6 until October 7 to 8, 2026. During these periods, some evening and night trains will be canceled and replaced by buses.

Details of Changes

The train S5 24582, departing from Treviglio at 22:40 and expected to arrive in Gallarate at 00:23, will be canceled from Busto Arsizio to Gallarate. To continue the journey, bus 7582A is scheduled, departing from Busto Arsizio at 00:21 with stops in Gallarate at 00:37, Cavaria con Premezzo at 00:49, Albizzate at 00:56, Castronno at 1:04, and Gazzada Schianno at 1:13, until arrival in Varese at 1:28.

The train S5 24584, departing from Treviglio at 23:10 and expected to arrive in Gallarate at 00:53, will also be canceled on the Busto Arsizio-Gallarate section. The service will be replaced by bus 7584A, departing from Busto Arsizio at 00:51 and arriving in Gallarate at 1:06.

On the same days, the bus 582A Gallarate-Varese, normally departing at 00:30 and arriving at 1:28, will be canceled and replaced by bus 7582A coming from Busto Arsizio.
