Access to the profession and permits

To work as a security agent, a candidate must meet the requirements of the employer and the cantonal authorities. The employer manages the G permit application and forwards it to the Migration Office of the Canton of Ticino. Switzerland is not a member of the EU or EEA, so the employment relationship is governed by specific agreements. The New Frontier Agreement, signed on 23 December 2020 and in force since 1 January 2024, introduced important changes to taxation and eliminates double taxation through a tax credit in box CE of the Italian Form 730. Tax is withheld at source only in Switzerland, in accordance with the rules administered by the Federal Tax Administration (AFC).

Operational details

Working as a security agent in Ticino involves joining the Swiss social security system, which differs significantly from the Italian system. Every worker pays AVS, AI and IPG contributions, equal to 5.3% paid by the employee, as well as unemployment insurance contributions (AD/AC) of 1.1%, calculated on an annual salary ceiling of CHF 148,200. Social protection also includes accident insurance (LAINF), with rates ranging from 0.7% to 1.5%, and the second pillar (LPP), mandatory from age 25, with rates ranging from 7% to 18% according to age group.

Frequently Asked Questions
Which social contributions are withheld from the salary of a cross-border security agent?
A cross-border worker in Switzerland pays AVS, AI and IPG contributions (5.3% paid by the employee), unemployment insurance contributions (1.1% up to a ceiling of CHF 148,200), accident insurance (LAINF, between 0.7% and 1.5%) and second-pillar contributions (LPP, between 7% and 18% from age 25).
How is taxation managed for cross-border workers under the new agreement?
The new agreement, in force since 1 January 2024, provides for employment income tax to be withheld at source only in Switzerland. To avoid double taxation, the worker must declare income in Italy using box CE of Form 730 and claim a tax credit for taxes already paid in Switzerland.
What is the right to choose health insurance?
G-permit cross-border workers have the right to choose health insurance and can select the Swiss system (LAMal) or the Italian system. The choice affects monthly premiums and applicable deductibles, which for adults usually range from CHF 300 to CHF 2,500.
