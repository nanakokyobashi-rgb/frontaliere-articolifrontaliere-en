Comerio, testing for the pedestrian bridge in Via Giardini

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 29 September 2026

Comerio: Monday, September 28, the pedestrian bridge in Via Giardini will be tested from 7 pm to 7 am on the 29th. From 17:00 no parking in Via Sacconaghi.

Context

In brief

  • On Monday 28 September, static testing begins.
  • Testing on the structure starts at 19.
  • The affected section of the state road will be closed to traffic.
  • Measurements end at 7 on Tuesday 29.

Key facts

  • Location → via Giardini, Comerio
  • Operation → static testing of the new pedestrian bridge
  • Start → 19 on Monday 28 September
  • Expected end → 7 on Tuesday 29 September
  • Traffic → temporary closure of the affected section
  • Traffic flow → two-way traffic during the works
  • Parking → no parking with forced removal on via Sacconaghi from 17

Testing on the new structure

In Comerio, on via Giardini, work to build the new pedestrian bridge is entering its final phase. On Monday 28 September, starting at 19, the structure will undergo static testing, the technical step considered essential for verifying the strength and safety of the work ahead of its opening to pedestrian traffic.

To allow the checks to be carried out in complete safety, the section of the state road affected by the tests will be temporarily closed to vehicle traffic. The planned schedule sets the completion of the operations and technical measurements at 7 on Tuesday 29 September.

Throughout the duration of the works, traffic will be two-way until the completion of the works and technical measurements. Two-way traffic is therefore indicated for the period linked to the tests on the new structure, until the end of the technical verification.

From 17 on Monday 28 September, a no-parking restriction with forced removal will also be established on via Sacconaghi. The measure is planned at the same time as the verification operations and precedes by two hours the start of the static testing, scheduled for 19.

The construction of the bridge is therefore in the final phase of the works. The static testing accompanies the process toward opening to pedestrian traffic and takes place while the construction schedule reaches the final interventions identified to make the structure accessible and usable by citizens.

Operational details

The final phase of the construction site

The new pedestrian bridge on via Giardini is reaching the testing stage while the completion of the work is in its final phase. The static verification is therefore part of the final stage of the works and directly concerns the stability and safety of the structure, elements indicated as necessary before opening to pedestrian traffic.

Alongside the technical control, the parts concerning access to the bridge remain to be completed. The access ramps and related complementary works are in fact included in the program of upcoming interventions. Their function is explained in the works project: to make the structure fully accessible and usable by citizens.

Ramps and complementary works

The framework of the works therefore distinguishes testing from access works, while placing them in the same final phase. The static test serves to verify the stability and safety of the work. The ramps and complementary interventions instead serve to complete the necessary conditions for the accessibility and usability of the bridge.

The opening to pedestrian traffic is the step towards which both the verification of the structure and the completion works are oriented. The program described for via Giardini thus combines the safety of the work and the possibility of use by citizens, without separating the static control from the final planned works.

Key points

The next steps in Comerio

The continuation of the work for the new pedestrian bridge is marked by a precise deadline and interventions that will continue in the following days. The phase of checks and technical surveys related to testing is scheduled until 7:00 AM on Tuesday, September 29. That reference closes the period indicated for operations on the structure.

Parallel to the testing, the program includes the completion of the access ramps and complementary works in the coming days. The completion of these parts accompanies the new structure towards full accessibility and usability by citizens.

From verification to pedestrian opening

The sequence of works keeps the various steps distinct. On one hand, the technical surveys must be concluded within the scheduled time to verify the stability and safety of the structure. On the other hand, the ramps and complementary works must be completed to make the bridge fully accessible. These are the two activities indicated in the final implementation phase.

The calendar does not present the testing as the material conclusion of every intervention. The static load test is the technical step planned ahead of the opening to pedestrian transit, while the work on the ramps and connected interventions continues in the final part of the works.

For Via Giardini, the result expected from the program is therefore a structure ready to be used by pedestrians, with safety checks completed and accesses finalized. The realization of the bridge goes through these close stages: first the technical control of the new structure, then the completion of the works necessary for its accessibility and usability.

The intervention thus remains linked to a dual schedule. The testing operations have a deadline set for 7:00 AM on Tuesday, September 29; the access ramps and complementary works will be completed in the following days, accompanying the bridge towards its opening to pedestrian transit.
