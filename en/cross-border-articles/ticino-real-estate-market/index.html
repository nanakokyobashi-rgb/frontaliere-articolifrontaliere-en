The real estate market in Ticino is extremely variable and largely depends on the area. The Locarno area, for example, has higher average prices than Lugano and Mendrisio. According to a recent survey, apartments in Locarno cost on average 7,000 francs per square metre, while in Lugano and Mendrisio the price is 6,000 and 5,000 francs per square metre respectively.

A recent trend is the increase in the number of private individuals who choose to invest in real estate in Ticino, especially those with a tourist destination. According to research conducted by a real estate agency in Ticino, 30% of real estate owners in Ticino plan to increase their share of short-term rental properties during 2022. This phenomenon is favoured by the growing tourist demand and the availability of vacant accommodation in many areas of Ticino.

For buyers, it's important to consider applicable tax regulations. For example, the Value Added Tax (VAT) is 0% for new home purchases, while for existing home purchases it is 7%. Average prices for apartments in Ticino vary by area. For example, the city of Lugano has the highest prices, with an average price of 3,500 francs per square meter for newly built apartments, while the cheapest area is that of Mendrisio, with an average price of 2,500 francs per square meter.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the average prices per area in Ticino?
Average prices per area in Ticino are variable and depend on the area. The Locarno area has higher average prices than Lugano and Mendrisio.
What's the difference between renting and buying?
Renting is cheaper than buying. However, it is important to consider market trends and average prices by zone before making a decision.
What are the main tax regulations for those who buy a property in Ticino?
For purchases of new homes in Ticino, the value added tax (VAT) is 0%. For existing homes, VAT is 7%. Property taxes are affordable, with an annual average of 30 francs per square meter, making Ticino one of the most advantageous regions in Europe in this regard.
Are there specific investment trends in the Ticino real estate market, such as tourist rentals?
Yes, Ticino is seeing an increase in private investment in tourism properties. Research found that 30% of Ticino landlords planned to increase their share of short-term rental properties in 2022, driven by growing tourism demand and the availability of vacant accommodation.
What factors are contributing to the increase in demand for housing in Ticino?
Demand is driven by several factors, including growing demand for private sector housing and limited demand for affordable housing. The increase in Ticino workers living in Italian Switzerland and looking for more spacious homes, and families with university children also contribute.
