The chronology of the incident

From 18 on Wednesday, October 7, road traffic between Varese and Gallarate remained paralyzed because of a crash that occurred on the A8 motorway, in the section between Castronno and Solbiate Arno. The report therefore places the origin of the disruption on the motorway network, but describes consequences that also extended to ordinary roads.

The crash caused a long tailback on the A8. Instead of remaining concentrated on the motorway, the traffic spilled onto the state road, shifting the pressure onto the road connection between the centers mentioned in the report. The sequence described is clear: first the tailback on the motorway, then the transfer of traffic onto the state road and, finally, a further slowdown at the point regulated by the traffic light.

The most critical point was in fact the bridge on the main ordinary road. Here, the traffic light regulating alternating one-way traffic created a bottleneck, contributing to the lengthening of the tailback. The line already began at Castronno cemetery and reached approximately one kilometer, according to the description of the situation.

This figure conveys the local scale of the incident: not merely an accident confined to the section of the A8 affected by the crash, but a disruption that also involved the road used by traffic spilling over from the motorway. The report thus follows the path of the congestion, from the site of the accident to the bridge and the tailback near the cemetery.

The final update indicates a return to normal around 20. The reported time window therefore runs from the start of the disruption at 18 until normal traffic conditions were restored in the evening. The report closes with this point, having described the effect of the crash on the A8 and on ordinary roads.

From the A8 to the ordinary road network

The pressure point on the bridge

The sequence described in the news report can be read as a succession of road stages, all concentrated along the same connecting corridor. The initial event occurred on the A8, between Castronno and Solbiate Arno. The immediate consequence was the queue on the motorway; the next stage brought traffic onto the State Road. Once it reached the main road in the ordinary road network, the flow encountered the traffic light regulating alternating traffic on the bridge.

The source identifies that very point as the bottleneck that worsened the situation. The alternating-traffic light is not described as an isolated element, but as the factor that did the rest after traffic spilled onto the State Road. The resulting queue already began at Castronno cemetery, with its reported length being approximately one kilometer.

The return to normality

The 20 update

The latest development in the report concerns how the situation evolved. After the disruption that began at 18, the situation returned to normal around 20. This statement closes the evening's timeline for Wednesday, October 7, and establishes the time of the final update on traffic between Varese and Gallarate.

This information should be read together with the description of the preceding phase. Before conditions returned to normal, the queue had affected the A8 and traffic had spilled onto the Statale; on the main ordinary-traffic road, the bridge's alternating-direction traffic light had done the rest. The return to normality was therefore the indicated outcome after a phase in which the queue had already reached the Castronno cemetery, for approximately one kilometer.

The final time updates the traffic conditions already described. The transition from paralyzed traffic to normal conditions is placed around 20, while the reference to 18 marks the beginning of the disruption. These are the two time boundaries reported in the account.
