How to get Permit G

To obtain the G Permit, polymechanical border guards must comply with the following requirements:

  • Have at least 2 years of experience as a polymech
  • Possess a certificate of professional qualification issued by a recognized training institution
  • Have a good knowledge of the Italian language
  • Submit to a professional suitability examination

In addition, multi-mechanical border guards must submit a job application to the Border Police Authority of the Canton of Ticino and pay the application fee of CHF 100.

Operational details

Requirements to work as a polymechanic in Ticino as a border worker

The requirements to work as a polymechanist in Ticino as a border worker are:

  • Have a recognised qualification in Switzerland
  • Have a G Permit
  • Have a good knowledge of German or French

Recognition of the qualification

The Italian qualification is recognised in Switzerland. Polymechanical frontier workers in Ticino can work with their Italian qualification. According to the federal law of 24 March 2000, Swiss students and graduates can access Italian study courses without further formalities. 📚

For example, a polymechanical frontier worker with a high school diploma in Italy can access the training course for Swiss polymechanics without having to repeat the entrance exams. This is because the Italian qualification is recognised as equivalent to the Swiss one.

Permission G

The G Permit is a work permit for border workers. Polymechanical frontier workers in Ticino can work with the G Permit. According to the Ticino law of 15 June 2004, border workers must obtain the G Permit in order to work in Ticino. This permit is valid for a maximum of two years and can be renewed.

For example, a multi-mechanical border crossing with a G Permit can work in a company in Lugano for up to two years. If the company so requests, the border worker may request the renewal of the G Permit.

Key points

What to do to get Permit G

To obtain the G Permit, polymechanical border guards in Ticino must:

  • Submit the application for Permit G to the AFC/ESTV by the deadline of June 30 of each year
  • Pay the G Leave fee, which is currently CHF 200 (source: AFC/ESTV, 2023)
  • Submit the required documentation, including the certificate of professional qualification, the certificate of registration in the register of polymaths and the certificate of residence in Switzerland
  • In addition, you must have at least 3 years of work experience as a polymech in Switzerland and have passed the professional qualification exam

Deadlines

The deadline to submit the G Leave application is June 30 of each year. It is important to note that if the application is not submitted by the deadline, it will not be possible to obtain the G Leave for the current year.

Concrete examples

A concrete example of how to obtain the G Permit is that of a polymech who works in Lugano. This worker has passed the professional qualification examination and has at least 3 years of work experience as a polymech in Switzerland. He submits the G Leave application to the AFC/ESTV by the deadline of 30 June and pays the fee of CHF 200. Once he has obtained the G Permit, he can continue to work as a polymech in Switzerland without any problems.
