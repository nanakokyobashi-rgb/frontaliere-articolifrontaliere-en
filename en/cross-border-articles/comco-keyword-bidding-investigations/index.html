COMCO investigates illegal keyword bidding deals (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 May 2026

The Competition Commission is probing into illicit agreements between travel companies and online casinos for keyword bidding

Context

In brief

  • COMCO opens two investigations into keyword bidding
  • Travel and online casino sectors involved
  • Possible illegal agreements between competitors

Key facts

  • What: Investigations into keyword bidding
  • When: April 30, 2026
  • Where: Switzerland
  • Who: COMCO
  • Amount: Not specified

The Competition Commission (COMCO) has opened two investigations into advertising through search engines. The first concerns the travel sector and the second the online casino sector, as stated in a notice issued on Thursday. To stand out from their competitors, companies can bid on search engines for specific keywords, known as 'keyword-bidding'. This way, they enhance their visibility, influencing the results that users will see first during their internet searches, explains COMCO.

According to information received through self-disclosures, several companies in the travel sector and several online casinos would have agreed not to compete on major search engines, refraining from bidding on keywords related to competing brands. This approach could constitute an illegal agreement and have harmful effects for consumers, indicates COMCO. Indeed, search engine results may have been influenced to the point of making the comparison between competitors more difficult.

Impact on consumers

The first investigation, it is added in the notice, concerns three companies offering travel packages in Switzerland. The second involves almost all online casinos in the Confederation. COMCO is investigating to verify whether these agreements have actually influenced competition and consumers.

The COMCO investigations into keyword bidding have significant implications for cross-border workers employed in Switzerland, particularly in sectors such as online travel and casinos. Before and After Comparison

Before the investigations, the companies involved could agree not to compete on search engines, thus influencing user search results. This made it difficult for consumers to compare offers and find the best options. After the investigations were opened, the COMCO is looking into these practices and may take measures to ensure fair competition.

If you are a cross-border worker in the online travel or casino sector, it is important to be aware of the implications of these investigations. Comparison Between Companies

The COMCO investigations could lead to increased transparency and competition in the online travel and casino sectors. This could benefit consumers by offering them more choices and better deals. However, the companies involved may face sanctions and changes in their marketing practices.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is keyword bidding?
Keyword bidding is an online marketing practice where companies bid on search engines for specific keywords. This affects the visibility of search results and can make it difficult for consumers to compare offers.
