The vote on the Fortress

On 27 settembre, the citizens of Bellinzona will be called upon to express their views on Municipal Message 1019, dedicated to enhancing the UNESCO heritage of the Three Castles, the Murata and the city walls. The Municipal Council approved the message in marzo 2026. PLR Bellinzona invites voters to vote Yes.

The proposal concerns a complex that has been a World Heritage site for over twenty-five years. The PLR's position starts from this recognition and identifies the project as an opportunity to turn it into a driver of the City's development. The idea is to create a modern cultural and tourism offering capable of attracting visitors, extending their stay and generating benefits for businesses, restaurants, hotels and jobs throughout the region.

The project also concerns the museum offering. 35 years have passed since the last works, and the credit would make it possible to renovate and expand the exhibition spaces. Access to the Castles would be improved with the aim of making it easier and safer, bringing the visitor experience up to the standard of a site of global significance.

A unique defensive system

The Fortress presents the Castles as a single defensive system, as recognized by UNESCO. The proposal also includes new spaces for events. According to the PLR, these spaces would benefit the population, which could continue to access the Castles.

The political message looks beyond an individual visit: according to the PLR, Bellinzona would have the opportunity to move from being a simple stopover to a true destination. The decision on 27 settembre therefore brings together heritage enhancement, museum renewal, accessibility, events and tourism promotion. The source also links the project to the local and regional economy, but the party's position remains above all an invitation to vote in favor of Message 1019.

Operational details

How much does the investment weigh on the City

The second profile to assess is economic. The PLR estimates an economic impact of more than 14 million francs and links it to support for small and medium-sized enterprises, described as the backbone of the City. In the framework described by the source, the project therefore does not remain confined to the spaces of the Castles: the effects indicated concern commerce, restaurants, hotel facilities and jobs throughout the region.

For cross-border commuters, the source does not indicate a specific effect. The concrete connection remains territorial and runs through the regional economic activities and jobs mentioned. The text speaks of an overall impact, without turning this indication into an individual figure or a number of hires. The sectors involved, however, remain clear: small and medium-sized enterprises, commerce, restaurants and hospitality.

The source distinguishes between the value of the investment and the portion directly supported by the City. A large part of the operation would be financed by the Canton, the Confederation and private parties. The share charged to Bellinzona is indicated as approximately 4.5 million francs. The PLR presents this participation as limited compared with the overall funding structure.

Key points

How to read the ballot before 27 September

The source makes it possible to construct a simple reading procedure for those who want to approach the vote with the essential information. It is not an additional administrative procedure: it is a sequence for distinguishing the subject, effects and political position.

1. Identify the question. The reference is Municipal Message 1019, concerning the enhancement of the Three Castles, the Murata and the city walls. The date indicated is 27 September. 2. Verify the cultural objective. La Fortezza presents the Castles as a single defensive system recognized by UNESCO. The project aims to renew and expand the exhibition spaces 35 years after the last interventions. 3. Consider public use. The elements indicated include easier and safer access and new spaces for events. According to the text, the population will be able to continue accessing the Castles. 4. Separate costs and impacts. The proposal indicates a share of approximately 4.5 million to be borne by the City, while PLR points to support from the Canton, Confederation and private individuals, as well as an estimated economic impact of more than 14 million francs.

After this reading, the political information to record is the recommendation of PLR Bellinzona: vote Yes. The decision concerns the citizens called to the municipal vote. For the cross-border reader, this distinction avoids interpreting the project as an individual measure: the text refers to the City, visitors, businesses, hotel facilities and jobs throughout the region.

The ballot can also be used to distinguish what is already recognized from what is being put to a vote. The Castles' world heritage status is described as having been established for over twenty-five years; the renewal of the spaces, the new offering for events and the unified enhancement are instead the elements of the project supported by PLR.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is voted in Bellinzona on September 27?
The municipal vote concerns Municipal Message 1019 on the enhancement of the UNESCO heritage of the Three Castles, the Murata and the city walls. The City Council approved the message in March 2026. PLR Bellinzona calls on citizens to vote Yes.
What investment is borne by the City?
According to the PLR's position, the City's share is limited to about 4.5 million francs. Much of the investment would be supported by Canton, Confederation and private individuals. The same source indicates an estimated inducement of more than 14 million francs in support of small and medium-sized enterprises.
What would change for the Castelli offer?
The credit would make it possible to renew and expand the exhibition spaces and improve their use. The project also includes new spaces for events. The proposal aims to make access easier and safer and to present the Castles as a single defensive system, as recognized by UNESCO.
How long have the Castles been a World Heritage Site?
For over twenty-five years, according to the source. The project also marks 35 years since the last interventions on the museum offer. The PLR argues that UNESCO recognition should become an engine of development and therefore calls for a Yes vote on Message 1019 on 27 September.
