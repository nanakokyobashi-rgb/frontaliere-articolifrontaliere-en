Key facts

  • What: Appeal against license revocation for driving under the influence
  • When: 3 months of waiting, appeal filed in 2026
  • Where: Varese Court
  • Who: Cross-border worker
  • Amount: 1500 euros spent on the appeal

A professional from Varese finds herself in a critical situation: her job is at stake due to the lengthy times of the Italian justice system. It all started with an administrative penalty of 3 months for license revocation, following a drive under the influence with an alcohol level slightly above the permitted limit of 0.5. The decision to appeal, costing 1500 euros, has left her professional life in suspense. The three months of suspension have almost passed, but the Justice of the Peace has not yet provided any feedback.

Impact on work

The choice to file an appeal has prevented the professional from requesting a work hours driving permit. This means that, without the possibility of driving, her job is at risk. Fortunately, thanks to the flexibility of her work, she has managed to organize herself, but for those with rigid schedules and fixed work organizations, the situation could be dramatic. The question arises: what to do if the job depends on driving and justice does not respond in a timely manner?

Operational details

Practical analysis: what to do while waiting for the appeal

The wait for a judicial decision can be a period of great uncertainty, especially when your job depends on the ability to drive. Here are some practical tips for those in a similar situation:

Evaluate work options

If your job depends on driving, it's important to evaluate all available options. In some cases, it may be possible to work from home or find temporary solutions that do not require the use of a car. It is crucial to talk to your employer to find an agreement that allows you to continue working while waiting for the court's decision.

Seek legal support

Having good legal support is fundamental. A lawyer experienced in administrative law can help present the appeal effectively and follow all necessary steps. Additionally, they can provide information on the average response times of the court and any important deadlines.

Document every step

It is important to document every step of the process, from communications with the court to expenses incurred. This can be useful in case of any complaints or claims for compensation. Additionally, keeping a detailed record can help avoid errors and keep the situation under control.

Consider the possibility of a provisional permit

In some cases, it may be possible to request a provisional driving permit. This permit allows you to drive while waiting for the court's final decision. It is important to inform yourself about the conditions and requirements to obtain this permit and to submit the application as soon as possible.
