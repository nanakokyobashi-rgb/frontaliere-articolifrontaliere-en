Illicit use of ATMs: trial in Varese and implications (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 29 June 2026

A woman accused of improper use of a neighbor's ATM: trial started on June 29 with purchases of less than 300 euros.

Context

In short - Process for improper use of the ATM initiated in Varese - Disputed purchases of less than 300 euros in the province - Investigations based on points card and booking at the hairdresser - Postponed to 14 December for listening to the offended party ## Key facts - What : Improper use and falsification of payment instruments - When: 29 June (process initiated); purchases in 2023 - Where: Province of Varese, Castiglione Olona, Venegono Inferiore - Who: Accused woman, Judge Davide Alvigini, PM Nicola Ronzoni, lawyer Fabrizio Bini - Amount: Total disputed purchases < 300 euros; hairdresser payment 28 euros On 29 June a trial entered into force at the court of Varese, which sees a woman accused of having improperly used an ATM belonging to a person close to her sister's house. The contested purchases were made in 2023 in several shops in the province of Varese, including supermarkets in Varese and Venegono Inferiore. The investigations, conducted by the Carabinieri after a complaint by the offended party, have made it possible to reconstruct the movements thanks to the association between the payments made and the supermarket's points card. A relevant episode concerns a payment of 28 euros made on 13 May at a hairdresser in Castiglione Olona, where it was possible to trace the telephone number used to book the appointment. Operational details

The practical analysis of this case shows how the management of electronic payments can be central in the investigation of misuse offences. The reconstruction of the disputed movements was possible thanks to the association between the supermarket points card and the payments made, as well as the tracking of the telephone reservation at the hairdresser. If we consider the cross-border context between Italy and Switzerland, the case highlights how attention to the security of payment methods must also be maximized for border crossers operating between the two nations. Although not directly mentioned in this story, the issue may be relevant for those who work in Canton Ticino and manage accounts and cards both in Italy and in Switzerland. The crime of improper use and falsification of payment instruments, in accordance with the provisions of article 493-ter of the Criminal Code, may result in legal consequences even for modest amounts, as in the disputed case (<300 euros). The application of Article 131-bis, invoked by the defence, allows the exclusion of punishment in cases of particular tenuity of the fact, but the final decision is up to the judge. ### Implications for border residents For border residents and border crossers, the story underscores the importance of constantly monitoring their payment instruments and checking for suspicious movements. Key points

From an operational point of view, those who manage payment instruments such as ATMs and credit cards must adopt periodic control procedures on movements and transactions. In case of suspected misuse, it is essential to file a complaint with the competent authorities as soon as possible, as in this case. The investigation procedure involves the involvement of the police who, through the collection of elements (for example, supermarket card points and reservations in public establishments), can reconstruct the disputed movements. Merchant testimony and transaction verification are key steps to ascertaining. Pending the next hearing scheduled for 14 December, those living on the Ticino-Italy border can draw practical ideas for the management of their payment instruments: constantly monitor expenses, keep receipts and documentation, activate automatic notification systems for each operation, and update their information at banks and businesses. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the accusations made in the Varese trial?
The woman is accused of having improperly and falsely used payment instruments other than cash, as provided for in Article 493-ter of the Criminal Code. The disputed purchases were made with the ATM of a neighbor of the sister's house in several commercial establishments in the province of Varese.
What role did the points card and telephone booking play in the investigation?
The investigations allowed to reconstruct the disputed movements thanks to the association between the payments made and the points card of the supermarket, in addition to the telephone number used to book an appointment at a hairdresser. These elements were decisive to identify the person responsible for the payments.
When will the next trial hearing take place?
The proceedings were postponed to 14 December, the date on which the offended party who had filed the complaint will be heard. At that time, the defence request relating to the application of Article 131-bis of the Criminal Code will also be evaluated.
