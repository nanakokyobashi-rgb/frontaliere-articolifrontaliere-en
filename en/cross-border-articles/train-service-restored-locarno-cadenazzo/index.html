Rail traffic restored between Locarno and Cadenazzo (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 30 May 2026

A faulty switch between Tenero and Gordola causing morning delays has been fixed.

Key facts

  • What: Fault at a railway switch
  • When: From 2 a.m. to 11:45 a.m.
  • Where: Between Tenero and Gordola
  • Who: Passengers on the Locarno-Cadenazzo line
  • Impact: Disruption of rail traffic

At 2 a.m., a fault occurred at a railway switch between Tenero and Gordola, causing the interruption of traffic on the Locarno-Cadenazzo line. This issue created significant inconvenience for passengers, despite the introduction of replacement buses from the early hours of the morning. The fault was resolved at 11:45 a.m., allowing train services to resume from noon.

The situation

The fault affected the core of a switch between Tenero and Gordola, blocking train traffic for the entire morning. Passengers faced significant inconvenience, despite the replacement buses provided. The situation returned to normal only after 12 p.m., when trains resumed regular service.

Cross-border workers using the Locarno-Cadenazzo line to reach their workplaces in Ticino experienced delays and inconvenience.
