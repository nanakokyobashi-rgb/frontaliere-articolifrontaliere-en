In the space of two months, an accommodation facility in Villa Guardia was hit three times.

According to the investigators' reconstruction, the young man struck on the night of 12 August, then on the night of 30 September and finally on the night of 8 October. The source indicates the latter as the most recent episode. The three dates outline the series contested against the twenty-year-old and connect the facts to the same structure of Villa Guardia.

The dispute therefore concerns three distinct episodes, but traced by the investigators to a single alleged perpetrator. The text presents the young man as a 20-year-old Italian citizen and reports that the complaint took place in a state of freedom. The charge indicated by the Carabinieri is that of continued aggravated theft.

The operational reference in the news is the Fino Mornasco station, whose military reported the twenty-year-old. The temporal sequence starts from August 12th, passes through September 30th and ends, in the order of the events reported, with the night of October 8th. The connection between the episodes is made clear in the investigators' reconstruction, which speaks of a series of thefts attributed to the young man.

Operational details

The modus operandi described by investigators

The common trait among the three episodes, according to the text, is the way the young man would have entered the premises. The entrance doors were forced or access was gained using specific Passkeys. The source describes these two methods without attributing different routes inside the premises to the episodes.

Once inside, the twenty-year-old headed straight for the cash register. This is the step linking entry to the economic objective indicated in the investigation: the cash kept in the register. In the three episodes, the total amount collected amounts to 940 euros.

The central point, in the reconstruction, is therefore the repetition of the same operational sequence on the three indicated nights: the young man would have overcome the entrance and then reached the cash register, with a total economic result of 940 euros.

Key points

The search and the items collected

The rapid investigations coordinated by the Como Public Prosecutor's Office led to the issuing of a local and home search decree. The measure concerned the suspect's home and car.

Several items of clothing were found in the 20-year-old's home and car. The source mentions sneakers, t-shirts, a polo shirt and a sweatshirt. The clothes coincided perfectly with those filmed by the video surveillance systems during the thefts.

Comparison with images

It is this comparison, according to the reconstruction reported, that connects the suspect to the three episodes: on the one hand the clothes found in his home and in his car, on the other those filmed by the video surveillance systems during the thefts. The source indicates the investigations and the subsequent discovery of the leaders as what framed the boy.

The text underlines the perfect identity between the items found and those immortalized by video surveillance systems during the criminal events. The outcome communicated by the Carabinieri is the denunciation of the 20-year-old Italian citizen in a state of freedom. The Como Public Prosecutor's Office coordinated the investigations and issued the search decree; the soldiers of the Fino Mornasco station found the clothes described. The picture reported remains that of an alleged perpetrator accused of continued aggravated theft.
