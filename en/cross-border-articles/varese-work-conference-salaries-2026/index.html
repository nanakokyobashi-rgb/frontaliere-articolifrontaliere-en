The day of discussion

On Saturday, October 10, Sala Montanari in Varese will host a day of debate and discussion promoted by Sinistra Italiana Lombardia and Alleanza Verdi e Sinistra. The regional conference «Fondata sul lavoro» will focus on wages, workplace safety, combating job insecurity and protecting purchasing power. The scheduled time for the event is from 9 to 18.

The event concludes the listening initiative «Primavera del Lavoro», developed across Lombardy to gather the concerns of workers and young people. The collection was carried out through a questionnaire prepared with the Unione dei giovani di sinistra, whose data will open the proceedings. The presentation will be delivered by provincial secretary Niccolò Minonzio, Giovanna Amodio and Luca Cappello.

The program starts at 9:30 with the questionnaire results. At 10:15, the roundtable on wages and the cost of living will bring together Bocconi historian and economist Massimo Amato, Nino Cartosio of Fiom Cgil Varese and regional councilor Onorio Rosati. At 11:45, the schedule will move on to company labor disputes, with the Rsu of Electrolux, Beko, ST, Polo della cosmesi and Gruppo Siae.

The afternoon

At 14, the discussion will address safety and combating job insecurity, with Paolo Borghi, Enzo Mingione of Democratizing Work Italia, Stefano Ruberto of Cgil Milano and AVS senator Tino Magni. At 15:30, contracts and representation will be discussed with representatives of Cgil Lombardia, Uil Lombardia, Cisl Lombardia, Cub and Federico Martelloni of the national secretariat of Sinistra Italiana. The closing remarks, scheduled for 17, will be delivered by Tino Magni.

Operational details

From the questionnaire to labor disputes

The structure of the conference sequences three levels of discussion: the collection of demands, the discussion of general labor issues, and direct listening to companies in crisis. It is an approach that starts with data from the regional questionnaire, moves through wages and the cost of living, and arrives at the disputes brought forward by the Rsu. The program does not anticipate the questionnaire's numerical results: it only indicates that they will be presented at the opening.

A precise scope

The political part is entrusted to the assessments of Tino Magni and Onorio Rosati. Magni describes the current situation through frozen contracts, involuntary part-time work, and cut-rate contracts. Rosati, on the other hand, stresses the need to outline an alternative proposal to the regional government's policies for the next ten years. The source thus connects the discussion both to working conditions and to the outlook for regional planning.

The section on the five companies in crisis brings the discussion from the general level to the experiences of the Rsu. The program provides no details on the individual disputes, nor does it indicate outcomes, figures, or decisions for Electrolux, Beko, ST, Polo della cosmesi and Gruppo Siae. The available information is their direct participation in the 11:45 session.

Frequently Asked Questions
Where and when is the 'Founded on Work' conference held in Varese?
The event will be held on Saturday 10 October in the Sala Montanari, located in via dei Bersaglieri 11 in Varese. The overall schedule is from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., with the start of work scheduled for 9:30 a.m. with the presentation of the results of the regional questionnaire.
What are the central themes of the debate promoted by Sinistra Italiana Lombardia?
The conference will focus on four pillars: wage levels, safety in the workplace, combating precariousness and protecting purchasing power. The meeting represents the conclusion of the 'Primavera del Lavoro' path, which gathered the demands of workers and young people in the Lombardy territories.
Which companies will participate in the corporate litigation session?
At 11:45 there will be a session dedicated to corporate disputes with the direct participation of the RSUs of five companies: Electrolux, Beko, ST, Polo della Cosmei and SIAE Group. This part of the program will allow you to deepen the specific critical issues found in these companies.
