A Sunday of sport in the heart of Varese

Almost 4 thousand participants flocked to the Giardini Estensi for StraWoman Varese 2026, the non-competitive run-walk dedicated to women and now in its sixteenth edition. The sunny morning and mild temperatures of an autumn Sunday accompanied what was described as record attendance, but the event’s main appeal was its message: sport, health and prevention.

The format remained far removed from the logic of a race. No timer and no ranking accompanied the five-kilometer route through the city, tackled by running or walking. The route thus brought together movement and collective participation, according to a program that also made room for the Village’s informational activities.

An event built around health

The event was organized by Sport Experience together with Eventi WoW. For the fourth consecutive year, Humanitas Medical Care participated as scientific partner, placing women’s health prevention among the day’s central themes.

The Breast Park Humanitas Medical Care was set up at the Giardini Estensi, a space dedicated to breast cancer prevention. Its inclusion in the Village brought the health topic into the same event dedicated to movement.

The charitable component was added to the sporting and health components. The 2026 edition supports Fondazione Humanitas per la Ricerca, through the Pink Union program, and WeWorld, with the Spazi Donna aimed at women in situations of vulnerability or violence.

The route was tackled by running or walking, without time measurement. The absence of a ranking and timer left the program serving to bring together movement, health and awareness.

The Varese stage is part of the 21 stages of the national StraWoman 2026 calendar, which began in March and is set to continue until November. Participation at the Giardini Estensi gave the day a particularly large scale, transforming the city center into a collective event dedicated to movement and prevention.

Operational details

The Village as a space for prevention

The Breast Park Humanitas Medical Care, set up at the Giardini Estensi, brought breast cancer prevention into the event’s program. The space was designed to provide information in a simple and engaging way, keeping healthcare at the center of the day.

At the Village, specialists from Humanitas Medical Care Varese and professionals from the Breast Unit of Humanitas Mater Domini met with the public. The discussion covered the main signs of the disease, the importance of checkups and some myths associated with breast cancer prevention.

Three activities for discussing prevention

The initiatives used different approaches to address the same topics. “Knock Down the Symptoms” was inspired by the traditional tin-can toss game. “Spin Prevention” featured a wheel of fortune dedicated to tests and checkups. “Toss the Ring,” on the other hand, provided an opportunity to discuss diagnostic precision and the use of artificial intelligence software to support specialists during mammography.

The presence of the “Sorrisi in Rosa” ambassadors also brought personal stories to the Village. The Humanitas project collects and shares the stories of women who have faced the disease, with the aim of raising awareness of the importance of prevention.

Solidarity alongside health

The event combined prevention with a fundraising campaign supporting two organizations working in the areas of health and women’s inclusion. The first is Fondazione Humanitas per la Ricerca, through Pink Union, a program dedicated to research into diseases affecting women. The second is WeWorld, with the Spazi Donna centers for women experiencing situations of vulnerability or violence.

The result is a program that, according to the event’s format, combines health information, personal stories and solidarity. Movement remains the starting point of StraWoman, but the Village and its initiatives broadened the day to include checkups, knowledge of the signs of the disease and support for research.

Key points

The event schedule

The StraWoman Varese 2026 program was structured in successive stages, from attendance at the Village through the end of the morning. This sequence made it possible to combine the run-walk with activities dedicated to prevention and the closing events.

The Village opened at 8, providing the space where the initiatives of the Breast Park Humanitas Medical Care also took place. The warm-up began at 9.30, the preparatory stage before the start. The run-walk began at 10.30.

From the route to the awards

After the start, participants tackled a 5-kilometer route through the city. The event was not timed and had no ranking, and could be completed by running or walking. Its noncompetitive nature therefore remained an essential part of the format, along with collective participation.

At the end of the five kilometers, the program continued with the cool-down and the awards. The conclusion retained the structure planned for the morning, following the transition from the Village's informational phase to movement and the city route.

A stage in the national calendar

The Varese stage is one of the 21 stages in the national StraWoman 2026 calendar. The calendar began in March and will continue until November. This places the Varese event within an event spread across multiple stages, but the day at the Giardini Estensi had one distinctive feature: particularly high attendance.

That turnout transformed the city center into a major collective event dedicated to physical activity and prevention. The morning's sequence, from the opening of the Village to the awards, thus brought together the five-kilometer route, activities focused on breast cancer and support for Fondazione Humanitas per la Ricerca and WeWorld.

Frequently Asked Questions
How many participants took part in StraWoman Varese 2026?
Nearly 4,000 people attended the event, according to organizers Sport Experience and WoW Events, marking a record attendance for the sixteenth edition of the non-competitive run-walk.
What was the route and formula of the event?
The route included five kilometres without a time trial or classification, to be tackled by running or walking through the city of Varese, maintaining the collective and non-competitive dimension typical of StraWoman.
What prevention and solidarity initiatives were present at the Village?
At the Village, the Breast Park Humanitas Medical Care was set up with information activities on breast cancer; the event supported the Humanitas Foundation for Research through the Pink Union program and WeWorld with the Women's Spaces for women in situations of fragility or violence.
