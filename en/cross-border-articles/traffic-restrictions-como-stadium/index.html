From 19 October at 6.30am, the area of Via Vittorio Veneto in Como will be affected by temporary changes to traffic in view of two Como 1907 matches at the Giuseppe Sinigaglia stadium. The provisions will remain in force until midnight on Saturday 24 October, with the possibility of continuing beyond that deadline if the operations related to the meetings make it necessary.

The calendar includes the UEFA Champions League match against Manchester United, set for Wednesday 21 October at 6.45pm, and the Serie A match against Sassuolo, scheduled for Saturday 24 October at 3pm. After the defeat in the Champions League, Como 1907 must therefore face a tight sequence of events, while the city prepares the stands in the stadium area.

The section affected by the prohibitions

The parking ban will come into effect on both sides of Via Vittorio Veneto, from Largo Borgonovo to the pedestrian crossing near electricity pole number 6921. The measure provides for forced removal and also applies to vehicles with a permit for people with disabilities. The space will be used for the technical means of television filming and to install mobile barriers intended to protect the equipment.

A second ban will concern five parking spaces between electricity poles number 6925 and 6926. At that point the area must remain free to allow vehicle access to the service road.

In the same time interval, transit will be prohibited in the stretch between Largo Borgonovo and the pedestrian crossing near pole 6921. Traffic will be directed on the service road of Via Vittorio Veneto towards Via Puecher. The local police indicate the manhole and priority to pedestrians, inviting them to follow the temporary signs. Further measures are also planned for other areas and streets of the stadium, which will be published later.

Operational details

For cross-border commuters who need to reach the stadium area for a work trip or for an appointment, the notice should be read as a combination of different prohibitions. The provision is not limited to removing some parking spaces: it closes a section of Via Vittorio Veneto to traffic and imposes specific regulations on the service road as well. The difference is practical: leaving the car and crossing the area are two operations subject to separate rules.
