THUN - On the second day of the party congress in Thun (BE), PS delegates said yes to three initiatives. The vote concerns the popular initiative on bees, the one on the internet and the one on the mobility voucher. The source presents the three texts as proposals related to environmental protection, sustainable mobility and digital security.

The mobility voucher

In April, the Umverkehr association launched the signature collection for the mobility voucher, a project that aims to support public transport in Switzerland and rail links with neighboring countries.

The text also provides for a tax on airline tickets and private airplanes, in addition to supporting public transport and rail links.

The congress expressed these positions on the second day of the party congress in Thun (BE). For the mobility voucher, the central point remains the signature collection launched in April by Umverkehr, together with the objective of supporting public transport and rail links to neighboring countries. The request for the tax completes the content of the text supported by the delegates.

Operational details

Bees, pollination and resources

One of the day's three approvals concerns the Initiative for Bees, the project dedicated to pollination. The proposal seeks to oblige the Confederation and the Cantons to ensure that cultivated and wild plants are pollinated by these insects. To achieve this goal, the text calls for the necessary resources to be invested.

The initiative was launched in May. Its support was decided by the PS delegates gathered on the second day of the congress in Thun. The proposal therefore concerns ensuring the pollination of cultivated and wild plants and investing the necessary resources.

The text is supported by the beekeepers' associations from the three language regions. The list also includes Naturfreunde Schweiz, also referred to as Amici della natura Svizzera, and the Future 3 foundation. The source presents these organizations as supporters of the Initiative for Bees.

Key points

Digital security and artificial intelligence

Finally, the PS delegates supported the Internet Initiative, the text devoted to digital security. The proposal concerns platforms, including search engines, and artificial intelligence developers, referred to in the source by the abbreviation IA.

The text puts forward two related requests. It asks the parties involved to examine the risks associated with their services and adopt measures to limit them. The two requested actions are therefore analyzing the risks of digital services and taking action to reduce them. The stated objective is digital security through limiting risks.

The launch took place in March. The initiative was promoted by an interparty committee, while the text was drafted by the Aargau entrepreneur Guido Fluri. The source therefore identifies the interparty committee as the promoter and Guido Fluri as the drafter of the text.

In the report on the Thun congress, the Internet Initiative is the last text presented among the three supported by the delegates. Its content remains linked to platforms, search engines and AI developers: these parties are called upon to analyze the risks of the services and take measures to limit them. The proposal completes the triptych devoted to the environment, mobility and digital security.
