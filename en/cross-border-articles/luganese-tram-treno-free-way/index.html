The Federal Court rejected the only appeal filed against the realization of the priority stage of the Lugano Train Network. The confirmation, the news reports, came from the Department of the Territory, which attributes to the ruling the final approval of the implementation of the project.

The sequence of the procedure

On May 11, three private individuals had submitted the censure to the High Court. The censorship also included the request to refer the proceedings to the Federal Administrative Court for further investigation of the appellants' properties. However, the text specifies that the main infrastructure works planned had not been contested.

The distinction is decisive to read the outcome without enlarging the content of the appeal. The judicial initiative concerned the applicants' properties and the requested referral to the Taf; it is not described as an opposition to the main infrastructure works of the priority stage. The data therefore delimits the perimeter of the pronunciation reported by the Department of the territory.

A previous step had concerned the suspensive effect. In June, the Federal Court had not granted the application, thus allowing the preliminary work to proceed. The official start was marked in Manno on 29 October, with a ceremony and the laying of the first walls.

The chronology distinguishes two moments: on the one hand, the preliminary progress already allowed after the decision on the suspension, on the other hand, the rejection of the only appeal, which has now arrived. For the Dt, the result is "an important step forward" in the process of a priority and crucial work for Ticino. According to the same source, the project will halve travel times and alleviate mobility difficulties in Lugano, throughout the Malcantone and in the lower Vedeggio Valley. The news finally links the work to an important funding from the Confederation, without indicating the amount.
