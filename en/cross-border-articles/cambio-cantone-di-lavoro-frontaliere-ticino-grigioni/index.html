Changing the working canton for border crossing Ticino Grigioni: what changes

From 1 January 2024, the new Frontier Agreement comes into force, which modifies the rules for frontier workers who move from the canton of Ticino to Graubünden or vice versa. This agreement is valid until 2033 and introduces some important novelties for the workers concerned.

The main novelty is the deductible for new frontier workers, which has been set at €10,000. This means that workers who move from one canton to another can enjoy a more favourable tax treatment for the first 10,000 euros of income.

A concrete example can help you better understand the novelty. Imagine a worker who lives in Lugano and works in Sondrio. Before 1 January 2024, the worker had to pay income taxes in both cantons. Now, thanks to the new Frontier Agreement, the worker can enjoy the deductible of €10,000 and pay taxes only on income above this figure.

With the entry into force of the Frontier Agreement, border workers who intend to change their working canton to Ticino or Graubünden will have to deal with new rules and procedures. These changes were introduced to simplify and standardise the rules relating to frontier workers, who often find themselves navigating a sea of different rules and procedures between the two cantons.

What's changing

The transitional regime, which has been established until 2033, provides that border workers who intend to change their working canton can do so without having to comply with the exemption. This means that they will not have to pay the excess of €10,000 to be able to work in the new canton. However, it is important to note that this franchise is not abolished, but has simply been deferred until 2033.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the changes introduced by the New Frontier Agreement?
The New Frontier Agreement introduces a transitional regime until 2033 and a deductible for new frontier workers of €10,000.
How can I change working canton?
To change working canton, you need to submit an application to the Federal Tax Administration (AFC/ESTV).
Who benefits from the transitional regime until 2033 for the cantonal exchange rate?
The transitional regime, valid until 2033, allows border workers already in business who change canton of work between Ticino and Grisons not to pay the excess of €10,000. This allowance is deferred for them, unlike 'new' frontier workers who start working after 1 January 2024 and who are subject to this allowance.
Which Italian and Swiss municipalities are affected by the new Ticino-Grison Frontier Agreement?
The new Frontier Agreement concerns workers moving between the Canton of Ticino and the Canton of Graubünden. The municipalities concerned include Lugano, Bellinzona, Locarno (CH) and Sondrio, Chiavenna, Tirano, Morbegno (IT), as well as many others listed, which fall within the relevant border areas.
How does the €10,000 deductible work and to whom does it apply?
The deductible of €10,000 applies to 'new' border crossers who, starting from 1 January 2024, move between Ticino and Graubünden. It allows them to pay taxes only on income that exceeds this figure, guaranteeing a more favourable tax treatment on the first 10,000 euros of income.
