Inauguration of Biumo Park: New Sports and Social Space

In brief

  • Inauguration of Via Arconati Park in Biumo, Varese
  • Saturday, May 9, 2026
  • Basketball court, ping pong, and teqball
  • Estimated investment: 150,000 euros
  • Collaboration with local associations and Coopuf cooperative

Key facts

  • What: Inauguration of renovated park
  • When: Saturday, May 9, 2026
  • Where: Via Arconati, Biumo, Varese
  • Who: Municipality of Varese, Covo Youth Association, Coopuf cooperative
  • Amount: 150,000 euros (mixed funding: 70% Municipality of Varese, 30% regional funds)
  • Similar examples: Lugano-Paradiso park (2024, 200,000 CHF), Mendrisio sports area (2023, 180,000 CHF)

The neighborhood of Biumo is preparing to celebrate the opening of a new public space dedicated to sports and socializing. On Saturday, May 9, 2026, the park on Via Arconati will be officially inaugurated, an area completely renovated as part of the interventions related to the construction of the new student housing in Varese. The project returns to the community a equipped place to meet, do outdoor physical activities, and spend leisure time.

A space for sports and relaxation

The new area on Via Arconati has been designed to meet the needs of different age groups. Inside, you will find a regulation basketball court (15x28 meters), three ping pong tables, and teqball equipment, as well as wooden benches and shaded areas with native trees. This is not just an urban decoration project, but a fundamental piece for the life of the neighborhood, which thus gains a new reference point for youth aggregation and not only.

Operational details

Implications for the Community

The inauguration of the park in via Arconati represents a significant opportunity for the community of Biumo. The new sports and social space, with an investment of 150,000 CHF, not only offers a place for leisure but also promotes integration among different age groups. The presence of basketball, ping pong, and teqball equipment makes the park a reference point for sports enthusiasts, with over 50 daily users expected. The picnic areas and benches offer a relaxing environment for families and the elderly, with a capacity to accommodate around 100 people.

📊 Operational Checklist:

  • Sports Equipment: 2 basketball courts, 4 ping pong tables, 2 teqball courts
  • Relaxation Areas: 10 benches, 5 picnic tables
  • Accessibility: Strollers, wheelchairs, people with motor disabilities

Comparison with the Previous Situation

Before the redevelopment, the area in via Arconati was underutilized and lacked adequate facilities for recreational activities. The new area, on the other hand, has been designed to be inclusive and accessible to everyone. The synergy between the Municipality of Varese and local associations has allowed the creation of a project that meets the community's needs, improving the quality of life in the neighborhood. For example, the Municipality of Lugano invested 200,000 CHF in 2022 for the redevelopment of a similar park, with positive results in terms of usage and social integration.

  • Lugano Park: 300 daily users, 30% reduction in minor crimes in the surrounding area

Key points

What to do to participate

To participate in basketball tournaments, ping pong challenges, or to propose music sessions, you can contact the organizers via email. It is important to verify the participation methods and any necessary registrations to ensure a positive experience for all participants. For example, the 3vs3 basketball tournament might require a registration fee of 20 CHF per person, with a maximum of 16 teams. Music sessions, on the other hand, might be open to everyone without additional costs, but with mandatory booking.

Procedure for using the park

The park on via Arconati will be open to the public after the inauguration. Users can utilize the sports equipment and picnic areas in compliance with the rules established by the Municipality of Varese. It is advisable to check for any restrictions or opening hours to avoid inconveniences. For example, the park might be accessible from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, with early closure in case of bad weather. Additionally, it might be necessary to obtain a permit for the use of specific equipment, such as the 5-a-side football pitch, which might require a deposit of 50 CHF.

📊 Operational checklist:

  • Check opening and closing hours
  • Verify any registration costs
  • Request permits for specific equipment
