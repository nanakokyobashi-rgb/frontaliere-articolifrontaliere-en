The neighborhood of Biumo is preparing to celebrate the opening of a new public space dedicated to sports and socializing. On Saturday, May 9, 2026, the park on Via Arconati will be officially inaugurated, an area completely renovated as part of the interventions related to the construction of the new student housing in Varese. The project returns to the community a equipped place to meet, do outdoor physical activities, and spend leisure time.

A space for sports and relaxation

The new area on Via Arconati has been designed to meet the needs of different age groups. Inside, you will find a regulation basketball court (15x28 meters), three ping pong tables, and teqball equipment, as well as wooden benches and shaded areas with native trees. This is not just an urban decoration project, but a fundamental piece for the life of the neighborhood, which thus gains a new reference point for youth aggregation and not only.

Day program

The inauguration will follow a program focused on active participation. The gates will open at 1:00 PM to allow citizens to organize a picnic on the lawn. The institutional moment is scheduled for 3:00 PM with the ribbon cutting in the presence of the city authorities. Immediately after, the space will come to life with informal basketball tournaments open to everyone, ping pong challenges, and musical moments ranging from jam sessions to DJ sets.

Operational checklist

  • 1:00 PM: Gates open and community picnic
  • 3:00 PM: Ribbon cutting with authorities
  • 3:30 PM: Basketball tournaments (maximum 4 teams of 5 players)
  • 4:30 PM: Ping pong challenges (sign-ups on site)
  • 5:30 PM: Musical sessions (jam sessions and DJ sets)

Synergy between institutions and associations

The event is born from close collaboration between the Municipal Administration and some very active realities in the territory, such as the Covo Youth Association and the Coopuf cooperative. This synergy aims to make the inauguration a true neighborhood party, where sports and music become tools for integration. To participate in the tournaments or propose yourself for the musical sessions, you can contact the organizers by email at info@covoassociazione.it.

Comparisons with practical scenarios

The park on Via Arconati fits into a context of urban renewal similar to that of some Ticino municipalities. For example, the park in Lugano-Paradiso, inaugurated in 2024 with an investment of 200,000 CHF, saw a 30% increase in youth attendance in the first year of activity. Similarly, the sports area in Mendrisio, opened in 2023 with a cost of 180,000 CHF, recorded a 25% increase in community events.

Regulations and funding

The project was funded through a mix of municipal and regional funds, in line with Italian regulations for urban renewal. The Municipality of Varese allocated 70% of the budget (105,000 euros), while the remaining 30% (45,000 euros) comes from regional funds destined for the promotion of sports and social well-being. The reference legislation is the Ministerial Decree of September 14, 2014, which regulates urban renewal interventions and the destination of public funds.

Conclusion

The inauguration of the park on Via Arconati represents a significant step for the neighborhood of Biumo, offering a new space for aggregation and well-being. With an investment of 150,000 euros and close collaboration between institutions and associations, the project aims to become a reference point for the community, in line with the best urban renewal practices observed in Ticino and other Italian regions.
