The concert

On Sunday 25 October 2026, at 6 pm, Uto Ughi will be the protagonist at the Teatro Condominio Vittorio Gassman in Gallarate, for an event dedicated to great classical music. The famous violinist will return to the city's stage one year after his previous concert.

He will be accompanied by the Virtuosi del Lemene, together with soloists Glauco Bertagnin on violin and Walter Zampiron on flute. The presence of the two musicians will complement that of the ensemble in a concert conceived as a dialogue between soloists and orchestra.

The programme will be dedicated to Antonio Vivaldi and Johann Sebastian Bach. The evening will bring together some of the most famous and evocative compositions of the Baroque repertoire, in a selection that will highlight elegance and virtuosity.

Ughi's return brings one of Italy's best-known international performers back to Gallarate. The programme and the participation of the Virtuosi del Lemene will help to highlight the legacy of classical music, creating a high-profile event.

The event therefore stems from the meeting between the famous violinist, the ensemble that will accompany him and the two soloists chosen for the evening. The stage of the Teatro Condominio will thus host a musical journey focused on Vivaldi and Bach, with the violin and flute included in the programme dedicated to the Baroque repertoire.

Operational details

The return after a year

The promise kept

For Gallarate, the concert marks the return of an artist already known to the city. During last year’s event, Uto Ughi had met the city’s audience at Teatro Condominio, leaving open the possibility of performing again.

The promise is now fulfilled with the concert on October 25. Councillor for Culture and Educational Activities Claudia Mazzetti linked the new event to the positive relationship the violinist had built with the venue, the audience and the city.

“His return, after last year’s concert, gives me great pleasure: he had greatly appreciated the theatre, the audience and the welcome from the city of Gallarate. I am pleased that he kept his promise.”

In Mazzetti’s comments, the evening is presented as an event of the highest calibre. The councillor also identifies two aims associated with the event: to showcase Uto Ughi, considered one of the world’s greatest violinists, and to bring classical music, described as a heritage, back to the forefront.

With this event, Teatro Condominio continues its cultural journey, after hosting initiatives in different genres in recent years. The venue has alternated between drama and music, with the aim of broadening its offering and also making room again for the great classical repertoire.

The new evening with Ughi thus fits within this path already outlined by the source. The previous meeting with the audience and the welcome received by the violinist are the elements that accompanied his return to the stage in Gallarate, alongside the desire to devote space to classical music.
