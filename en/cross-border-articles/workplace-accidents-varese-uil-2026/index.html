The data for the first half of 2026 on the territory of Varese, made known through the reworking of the Inail data by the Uil Lombardia, highlight a complex situation for the safety of workers. As the coordinator Uil Antonio Massafra comments, in Varese there is an increase in accidents of 6.0%, with a total of 5,355 complaints registered in the first six months of the year. In the face of this general increase, the data on fatal complaints are of particular concern, which rise to 7 cases compared to 2 in the same period of the previous year. At the same time, there was a slight drop in occupational diseases of 2.3%, corresponding to 170 complaints in the province. These numbers call for a profound reflection on health protection and prevention in the workplace.

Operational details

Sectoral analysis and risk categories

The detailed examination of the data disseminated by the UIL makes it possible to identify the productive sectors and the categories of workers that require targeted and urgent interventions. According to the indications of the union, it is necessary to intervene decisively in the sectors considered historically most exposed to risk, namely transport, logistics, manufacturing, construction and health. Within these work areas, psychosocial burdens and work organisation play a decisive role in the genesis of occupational accidents and diseases. Varese is part of some dynamics in contrast with regard to occupational diseases, with 170 complaints and a decrease of 2.3%, but the jump in complaints with a fatal outcome from two to seven cases in a single year resets any margin of tolerance and strongly returns prevention to the center of the territorial debate.

A further element of attention emerged from the processing concerns workers born outside the European Union, among which there is an increase in accidents of 10.6%. This data highlights the need to strengthen communication, understanding of safety procedures and accessible training also for foreign workers included in the local and regional productive fabric. Key points

Trade union requests and institutional intervention plans

Faced with this scenario, trade unions are making precise demands to reverse course and ensure higher standards of protection. Secretary-General Antonio Albrizio called for the adoption of an inter-institutional regional plan aimed at coordinating controls and prevention actions. Among the priority requests are the maintenance of stable staff in the Psal services of the ATS and in the Labour Inspectorate, the establishment of a database shared between the responsible bodies and greater transparency on the results of the supervision carried out in the territory. The stated objective is twofold: to intensify targeted controls and to make safety training not a mere formal fulfilment, but an effective tool for safeguarding human life.
