December 23, 2020 is the date the New Cross-Border Workers Agreement was signed; its entry into force is set for January 1, 2024. Italian ratification is Law 83 of June 13, 2023. For anyone seeking vocational training or retraining in 2026 related to work in Canton Ticino, the first fact to focus on is therefore the agreement’s already verified timeline, not a new effective date in 2026.

The tax framework not to be confused with the course

The expression «new 2026 tax agreement» does not shift these dates. The text distinguishes between old cross-border workers, who already had that status before July 17, 2023, and new cross-border workers. The former are subject to the €7'500 exemption and the transitional regime from 2024 to 2033; for the latter, the stated allowance is €10'000. This distinction is the first documented comparison to keep separate when a family with children assesses whether the retraining is sustainable.

On the cross-border worker’s employment income, withholding tax is withheld only in Switzerland. Italy avoids double taxation through the tax credit indicated in box CE of form 730: it is not correct to describe the mechanism as ordinary withholding in both countries. The Italy-Switzerland Convention for the Avoidance of Double Taxation was signed on March 9, 1976.

Switzerland is not a member of the European Union or the European Economic Area. This is the international framework to bear in mind when discussing cross-border workers and recognition of training, without automatically attributing to a course tax or professional effects that the source does not document. For the holder of a G permit, the insurance aspect remains distinct from that of withholding tax: LAMal is health insurance and not a health tax.

The same verification sequence applies both to those who maintain their current employment relationship and to those considering a change: the thresholds and dates are verifiable data, while the outcome of the course remains to be demonstrated with information not reported here.

Key points

The figures that can be checked

The Swiss part of the comparison is based on contribution items and the payslip, not on generic estimates of a course's returns. In the available framework, AVS/AI/IPG is indicated at 5,3% for the employee. AD/AC is 1,1% with a cap of CHF 148'200. LAINF is indicated between 0,7% and 1,5%, while LPP varies from 7% to 18% by age group starting at age 25.

Contributions and institutional responsibilities

These values describe the contribution framework that can be used to interpret an employment situation in Ticino, but they do not constitute a budget for a course. It is not correct to automatically add up AVS, LPP, LAMal and withholding tax to obtain the return on retraining: they are different items, and the source does not attribute a specific effect on training to them.

Tax rates, including withholding tax and federal and cantonal rates, are established by federal and cantonal laws. At the federal level, they are administered by AFC/ESTV and, at the cantonal level, by the cantonal tax administrations. UFAS does not set these rates: it is concerned with social security and AVS/AI. BFS collects statistical data and does not set rates.
