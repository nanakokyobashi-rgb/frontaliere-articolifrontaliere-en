The New Frontier Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020 and represents an important step forward for frontier workers living in Italy and working in Switzerland. This agreement aims to simplify the lives of border workers, reducing journey times and improving working conditions.

One of the key points of the New Frontier Agreement is the extension of the G permit to border workers. The G permit allows border workers to work in Switzerland without having to obtain a separate work permit. This means that border workers can work in Switzerland without having to undergo lengthy and complex bureaucratic procedures.

For those who want to move to Oliveto Lario and work in Ticino, it is necessary to obtain the G permit and register with the Revenue Agency. In addition, it is advisable to look for a job in Ticino and consider the area and the cost of living.

The requirements for the G permit

To obtain the G permit, you must reside in Oliveto Lario and have a fixed income in Switzerland. The G permit is valid for a maximum of 6 months and can be renewed once. The cost of the G permit is CHF 100 for the application and CHF 50 for the renewal.

Registration with the Revenue Agency

Registration with the Revenue Agency is necessary to declare tax presence in Switzerland. It is advisable to register by 31 March of the year following the transfer. The registration fee is CHF 100.
