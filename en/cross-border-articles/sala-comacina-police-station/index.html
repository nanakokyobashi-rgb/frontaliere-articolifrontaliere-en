On Friday, October 16, at 11, the inauguration ceremony of the Traffic Police detachment on Lake Como will take place in Sala Comacina. The ribbon-cutting ceremony will be the centerpiece of the event, attended by national and local institutional representatives.

Among the announced participants is Matteo Salvini, Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Infrastructure and Transport. He will be joined by Matteo Piantedosi, Minister of the Interior, Nicola Molteni, Undersecretary at the Interior Ministry, and Vittorio Pisani, Chief of Police.

Roberto Greppi, mayor of Sala Comacina, will represent the municipality. The ceremony will also be attended by numerous other mayors and local authorities. The attendees are defined by their roles: the ribbon-cutting will be attended by the Minister of Infrastructure and Transport, who also serves as Deputy Prime Minister, the Minister of the Interior, the Undersecretary at the Interior Ministry and the Chief of Police, together with the mayor, the other mayors and local authorities. The announcement thus sets the place and time of the inauguration: Sala Comacina, on Lake Como, Friday, October 16 at 11.

The significance attributed to the inauguration comes from Nicola Molteni, Undersecretary at the Ministry of the Interior, who links the event to security in the Lake Como area.
