A free 100% plant-based meal to discover in Lugano (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 5 May 2026

Greenpeace offers a free plant-based meal in Lugano to promote sustainable eating. Discover the details of the initiative.

Context

In brief

  • Greenpeace offers a free vegetarian meal in Lugano on May 6.
  • The event takes place on the square between the Cassarate river and the EST USI-SUPSI Campus.
  • The menu includes bowls with Swiss ingredients such as peas, broad beans, and soy.

Key facts

  • What: A free 100% vegetarian meal
  • When: Wednesday, May 6, 2026, from 11 am to 2 pm
  • Where: Square between the Cassarate river and the EST USI-SUPSI Campus, Lugano
  • Who: Greenpeace Switzerland in collaboration with local schools
  • Cost: Free, with online registration required

Greenpeace Switzerland, in collaboration with several schools in the Lugano-Viganello district, invites the public to discover the benefits of a more plant-based diet. The event is scheduled for Wednesday, May 6, from 11 am to 2 pm, on the square between the Cassarate river and the EST USI-SUPSI Campus. On this occasion, a fully plant-based meal made with Swiss products will be offered for free, subject to online registration until all available spots are filled.

The initiative aims to highlight the advantages of a diet richer in plant-based proteins, which contributes to the protection of the climate, the environment, and health. The lunch is organized together with the Liceo cantonale di Lugano 3, the Scuola media di Viganello, and the Centro professionale tecnico del settore tessile di Lugano-Viganello, which includes the Scuola d'arti e mestieri della sartoria and the Scuola specializzata superiore di abbigliamento e design della moda.

Practical Analysis: Implications for Cross-Border Workers

The Greenpeace initiative offers cross-border workers in Ticino a unique opportunity to explore more sustainable food choices. Participating in an event like this can have several practical benefits. First, it can help reduce the environmental impact of daily food choices. Additionally, it can offer a culinary experience that might positively influence long-term eating habits.

Comparison with the Previous Situation

Before initiatives like this, cross-border workers might have had fewer opportunities to discover the benefits of a plant-based diet. Events organized by Greenpeace offer an educational and practical context that can facilitate the transition to a more sustainable diet. This is particularly important in a context like Ticino, where sustainability is a growing priority.

Future Scenarios

If Greenpeace's initiative is successful, it could be replicated in other locations in Ticino or Switzerland. This could lead to greater awareness and adoption of plant-based diets among cross-border workers and the local population. Additionally, it could stimulate further similar initiatives, contributing to the creation of a more sustainable food culture.

Educational Activities

During the event, participants will have the opportunity to participate in various educational activities. These activities can include workshops, presentations, and practical demonstrations on how to prepare delicious and nutritious plant-based dishes. This can be particularly useful for cross-border workers who want to adopt a plant-based diet but don't know where to start.

For cross-border workers who wish to participate in Greenpeace's initiative, there are some concrete actions to follow. Make sure to complete the registration in time to secure a spot at the event.
