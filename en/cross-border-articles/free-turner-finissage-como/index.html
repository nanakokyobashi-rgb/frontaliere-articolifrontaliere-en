Frequently Asked Questions
When does the exhibition of the Masterpieces from the Tate end?
The exhibition ends on Sunday 27 September 2026 in Como. The finissage is the evening event indicated for the completion of the Masterpieces by the Tate. Access to the public is free from 18:00 to 21:00, with last admission at 20:30. The source presents the evening as a last tribute to international art and does not indicate other dates.
What are the times of free admission?
The public can freely access the exhibitions from 18:00 to 21:00 on Sunday 27 September 2026. The last entry is scheduled at 20:30. The source then distinguishes the cut-off time to enter from the final time of the evening band. No different times are indicated for individual exhibitions or additional access conditions.
Where are the exhibitions dedicated to artists?
The exhibitions dedicated to William Turner are shown in the Broletto and in the Pinacoteca civica. In San Pietro in Atrio, on the other hand, there are exhibitions dedicated to the artists Jim Lambie and David Batchelor. The announcement brings the three venues together in the same evening of finissage, with free admission in the range between 18:00 and 21:00.
