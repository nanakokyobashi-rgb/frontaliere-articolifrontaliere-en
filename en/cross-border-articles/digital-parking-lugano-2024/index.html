Digital parking in Lugano: how the new system works (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 May 2026

Parking in Lugano is becoming increasingly digital. Discover how the new payment and surveillance system works.

Context

In brief

  • New digital system for parking in Lugano
  • Payment via FFS Park&Rail app
  • Video surveillance to prevent unwanted parking

Key facts

  • What: Introduction of digital systems for payment and parking surveillance
  • When: Recently implemented
  • Where: Lugano station and selected Coop parking lots
  • Who: FFS and Coop
  • Cost: 16 CHF for a daily card

In Lugano, parking is becoming increasingly digital and technological. A recent example is the introduction of the FFS Park&Rail system at Lugano station. This new system allows you to pay for parking via app, eliminating the need for tickets or cash. Technology is also revolutionizing parking surveillance, with Coop introducing a video monitoring system to prevent unwanted parking.

How the Park&Rail system works

The FFS Park&Rail system has been introduced to facilitate parking payment for drivers who want to leave their car near the stations to continue their journey by train. Users can register on the app, enter their vehicle license plate, and pay digitally. Once the ticket is purchased, there's no need to display any ticket in the car thanks to the license plate registration. Additionally, the ticket validity can be extended at any time.

Problems and criticisms

Despite the advantages, the new system has sparked some criticism. Some users have reported difficulties in paying with cash, as the only option is to purchase a daily card at the ticket machine for 16 CHF. Additionally, those who are not very familiar with technology might have trouble using the app.

Coop's video surveillance

Coop has introduced a new video surveillance system to prevent parking lots from being occupied by external users. Vehicles are automatically registered through license plate detection upon entry and exit. Coop ensures that all data is protected according to the law and used exclusively to monitor parking duration.

Implications for cross-border workers

Cross-border workers who commute to Ticino and use parking lots in Lugano will need to adapt to the new digital system. It's important to familiarize themselves with the Park&Rail app and ensure they have a digital payment method to avoid issues. Additionally, Coop's video surveillance might affect parking availability, making it necessary to plan ahead.

What to do if you're not used to technology

For those who are not very familiar with technology, it might be helpful to ask for assistance from a more tech-savvy family member or friend. Additionally, practicing the use of the app in a less crowded parking lot can help familiarize yourself with the system before using it in a busier area.

Conclusion

The new digital parking system in Lugano represents a step forward in the modernization of urban infrastructure. Despite some criticism, the advantages in terms of efficiency and practicality are evident. Cross-border workers will need to adapt to this change, but with a bit of practice, using the new system will become simpler.

Practical Analysis

The new digital system for parking in Lugano has several practical implications for cross-border workers commuting to Ticino. First, it's important to understand how the Park&Rail system works and what its advantages and disadvantages are.

Advantages of the Park&Rail System

One of the main advantages of the Park&Rail system is convenience. Users can pay for parking directly from their smartphone, without having to look for a parking meter or carry cash. Additionally, license plate registration eliminates the need to display a ticket in the car, reducing the risk of fines for non-payment.

Disadvantages of the Park&Rail System

However, there are also some disadvantages. For those not used to using apps or who don't have a smartphone, the system can be complicated. Moreover, the only way to pay in cash is to purchase a daily card for 16 CHF, which might not be convenient for those using the parking for short periods.

Comparison with the Previous System

Comparing the new system with the previous one, it's clear that digitalization has brought some improvements. For example, the ability to extend the validity of the ticket at any time is a significant advantage for those who have unexpected delays. However, the lack of cash payment options might be a problem for some users.

Practical Scenarios

Imagine a scenario where a cross-border worker arrives in Lugano for work and needs to park their car. With the new system, they will need to download the Park&Rail app, register, and enter their vehicle's license plate. Once done, they can pay for parking and won't have to worry about displaying a ticket. If work runs longer than expected, they can extend the ticket's validity directly from the app.

Tips for Cross-Border Workers

For cross-border workers who are not very familiar with technology, it's advisable to practice using the app in a less crowded parking lot before using it in a busier area. Additionally, it's useful to have a digital payment method like a credit card or a PayPal account linked to the app to avoid issues.

Conclusions

In conclusion, the new digital parking system in Lugano offers many advantages in terms of convenience and practicality. Key points

Action

Now that you know the new digital system for parking in Lugano, it's time to take action. Here's what you need to do to use the Park&Rail system and ensure you don't have any problems.

Step 1: Download the Park&Rail app

The first step is to download the Park&Rail app by FFS to your smartphone. The app is available for both iOS and Android and can be easily found in the respective stores.

Step 2: Register and enter your vehicle's license plate

Once you've downloaded the app, you'll need to register by entering your personal information and your vehicle's license plate. Make sure to enter the data correctly to avoid problems during payment.

Step 3: Pay for parking

After entering the license plate, you can pay for parking directly from the app. Make sure you have a digital payment method such as a credit card or a PayPal account linked to the app.

Step 4: Extend the validity of the ticket if necessary

If your stay in Lugano lasts longer than expected, you can extend the validity of the ticket directly from the app. This will help you avoid fines for non-payment.

Step 5: Use Coop's video control system

If you park in an area managed by Coop, make sure to follow the parking rules to avoid being fined. The video control system automatically records the vehicle's license plate upon entry and exit, so it's important to ensure you do not exceed the allowed parking time.
