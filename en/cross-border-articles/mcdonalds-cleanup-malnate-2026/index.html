On Monday 5 October the Malnate McDonald's team left their work spaces for a day dedicated to cleaning some areas of the city. The activity was part of "Together with you for the environment", the McDonald's project which invites its staff and local communities to collect abandoned waste and redevelop the area.

16 people took part, including employees, customers and supporters of the restaurant. With pliers and bags, the group worked in the middle school area, near the municipal gym and in the commercial area of ​​via Gasparotto. In the balance of the day, the waste collected reached 20 kilos, including materials of various kinds.

The initiative was carried out under the patronage of the Municipality of Malnate. The project involves employees and local communities, while the declared objective was to contribute to the cleaning of spaces and draw attention to the importance of not leaving waste.

From the restaurant to the city spaces

For the restaurant staff, the collection also represented the opportunity to leave the usual work environment and experience a shared moment, different from the daily routine. Chiara Muzio, new licensee of the Malnate McDonald's from 1 December 2025, explained the meaning attributed to the appointment.

«It is a concrete way to take care of the place where we work, part of our daily life.»

In his speech, Muzio described the restaurant as part of the community, as well as a place to eat or work. He considered particularly significant not only the weight of the waste collected, but also the fact that many people dedicated part of the day to an activity useful to everyone.
