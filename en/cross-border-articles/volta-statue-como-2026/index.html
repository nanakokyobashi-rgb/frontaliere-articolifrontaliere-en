Volta statue in Como overrun with weeds (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 May 2026

The monument dedicated to Alessandro Volta in Como is once again in a state of neglect, just a few months after the bicentennial of his birth.

In brief

Key facts

  • What: Statue of Alessandro Volta overrun by weeds
  • When: Reported in 2023 and again in 2026
  • Where: Como, Italy
  • Who: Lorenzo Cantaluppi, municipal councilor of FdI
  • Amount: Not specified

In July 2023, the issue of Alessandro Volta's statue being overrun by weeds had already been brought up in the municipal council. Lorenzo Cantaluppi, a municipal councilor of FdI, had denounced the neglect of the monument. Three years later, just a few months before 2027, the year of the bicentennial of Alessandro Volta's birth, it is once again Lorenzo Cantaluppi who reports the same problem. Here's what he writes: 'Three years after my denunciation in the Municipal Council, we are back to square one: the statue of Alessandro Volta is once again overrun by weeds. While this Administration wastes time in useless polemics with Rome and the National Committee, the monument dedicated to our most illustrious citizen is left to neglect and decay.' Cantaluppi criticizes the administration for the lack of concrete interventions and asks for an immediate cleaning of the monument in view of next year's celebrations.

The current situation

The statue of Alessandro Volta, located in Como, is an important monument dedicated to the famous scientist and inventor. However, in recent years, the monument has been neglected, with weeds growing around and on the statue itself. This state of abandonment has been reported several times, but it seems that no adequate measures have been taken. Lorenzo Cantaluppi, municipal councilor of FdI, has expressed his concern about the lack of maintenance and has asked for urgent intervention to restore the dignity of the monument.
