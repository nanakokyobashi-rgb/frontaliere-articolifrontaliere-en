Pedestrian cycle Piambello: towards completion (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 14 August 2026

Last works in progress between Porto Ceresio and Bisuschio for the pedestrian cycle of Piambello. Chairman Sartorio plans to close the construction site by next spring.

Key facts

  • What: Completion of the pedestrian cycle of the Piambello.
  • When: Expected by next spring.
  • Where: Section between Porto Ceresio and Bisuschio, with underpass SS 344.
  • Who: Mountain Community of Piambello, president Paolo Sartorio.
  • Amount: Construction sites open over about 3.5 kilometres.

The Piambello pedestrian cycle is about to reach its goal, entering the final phase of the project. The work, promoted by the Piambello Mountain Community, aims to create a route dedicated to slow mobility, connecting Lake Ceresio, starting from Porto Ceresio, to the valley that leads to Arcisate and Induno Olona. The ambition is to integrate this new route into a wider system of routes, extending towards Valganna, Lavena Ponte Tresa, Valcuvia and Laveno Mombello. Paolo Sartorio, president of the Mountain Community of Piambello, took stock of the progress of the works, highlighting how the current phase sees construction sites active on a stretch of about 3.5 kilometers that connects Porto Ceresio to Bisuschio. Among the most significant works in progress is the construction of a new underpass of State Road 344 in the territory of Bisuschio. Operational details

The infrastructure of the Piambello pedestrian cycle path is proposed as a key element to improve the quality of life of residents, offering new spaces dedicated to sports and leisure. The philosophy of the project, focused on "lived nature", aims to create a deeper bond between people and the surrounding environment. Walking, running or cycling on this route means being able to immerse yourself in the landscape, away from the chaos of traffic, in a context of absolute safety. In addition to the benefits for the local community, the bike path has also been conceived with an eye towards sustainable tourism. The direct connection with Ceresio and the potential integration with other routes already existing or under development on the territory could transform the track into a real tourist attraction. This is part of a growing trend of demand for outdoor experiences and forms of slow mobility, which enhance the discovery of the territory in an ecological and immersive way. To reach this important goal, it will still take a little patience on the part of the citizens, who in these months are living with the inevitable inconveniences related to the construction sites in progress. The Piambello Mountain Community has already set an important appointment: next spring, at the end of the interventions, a large inauguration is planned open to all citizens to celebrate the completion of the work.

Key points

What to do now and what to expect

The current phase of work on the Piambello pedestrian cycle path requires patience on the part of the citizens, who must deal with the temporary hardships caused by the construction sites. However, the finish line is fast approaching. The Piambello Mountain Community has indicated next spring as the final completion period of the work.

Once the interventions are finished, the pedestrian cycle path will be a protected and accessible route for pedestrians, cyclists and outdoor physical activity practitioners. The "nature lived" philosophy will guide the user experience, offering a total immersion between meadows and woods, away from traffic.

Next steps:

  • Monitoring of the works: Continue to follow the developments of the construction sites to have an updated picture of the completion times.
  • Inauguration planning: Next spring will see the organisation of an official inauguration event, open to all citizens, to celebrate the new infrastructure.
  • Integration with existing routes: The pedestrian cycle is expected to fit into a wider system of routes, expanding the possibilities for slow mobility in the region.

Frequently Asked Questions
When will the Piambello pedestrian cycle be completed?
The final completion of the work is scheduled for next spring. At the end of the work, a grand opening open to the public will be organised.
What are the traits affected by the work in progress?
Currently, the construction sites are open on a stretch of about 3.5 kilometers between Porto Ceresio and Bisuschio. The most significant intervention is the construction of a new underpass for State Road 344 in Bisuschio.
What is the philosophy behind the pedestrian cycle project?
The philosophy is that of "nature lived", a path immersed in meadows and woods designed to allow citizens to walk, run or cycle away from traffic and in safety, rediscovering contact with the environment.
What benefits will pedestrian cycling bring in addition to local traffic?
In addition to improving the quality of life of residents by offering spaces for sports and leisure, the pedestrian cycle aims to promote sustainable tourism. The connection with Ceres and other routes could turn it into an important tourist infrastructure.
