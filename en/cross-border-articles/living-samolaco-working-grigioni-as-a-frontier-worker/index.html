Permits required to work in Switzerland

To work in Switzerland as a border worker, you must have a residence permit B, which allows you to work in Switzerland. To obtain this permit, you must meet certain conditions, such as having an employment contract with a Swiss employer and having demonstrated that you have sufficient knowledge of the German or Italian language.
