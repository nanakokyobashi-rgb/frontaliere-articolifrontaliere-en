What are the Inclusion Allowance processing?

The Inclusion Allowance processing is the process through which the National Institute for Social Security (INPS) verifies the list of beneficiaries and the corresponding amount of the Inclusion Allowance. During this process, INPS examines the applications submitted by applicants and verifies if they meet the requirements to receive the benefit. Once the verification is complete, INPS pays the Inclusion Allowance to eligible beneficiaries.

Operational details

The Income Support (IS) is a financial aid measure introduced in 2018 to combat poverty and promote social integration of the most vulnerable citizens. The payment of IS is made through a debit card, the Inclusion Card, which can be used to withdraw cash and make purchases at affiliated commercial establishments.

The processing of the ordinary recharge of the Income Support is expected to start on Monday, May 25, 2026, although as always, INPS timelines may vary from case to case. In recent months, the Institute has managed the processing progressively, distributing the cases over several days. This means that some beneficiaries may see the processing on the first available day, while others may only see it later.

The official INPS date for the end-of-month ordinary recharge remains Wednesday, May 27, 2026. However, as has happened in the past, credits may start as early as the previous day, Tuesday, May 26, for at least some beneficiaries. For this reason, many users await the start of processing on May 25, which usually anticipates the payment on the Inclusion Card by about 24 hours.

For beneficiaries residing in Ticino, the recharge of the Income Support can be made at the INPS offices in Bellinzona, Lugano and Mendrisio. It is important to remember that to withdraw the money on the Inclusion Card, a valid identity document and the card itself are required. Moreover, cash can only be withdrawn at automated teller machines affiliated with the PostePay circuit, such as those present in the branches of the Cantonal Bank of Ticino or Raiffeisen Switzerland.

As for the amounts, the Income Support is 400 euros per month for single-person households, 500 euros for two-person households, and 600 euros for households with three or more members. The amount is paid automatically every month, without the need to submit any application.

However, it is important to note that the Income Support is not an acquired right, but a temporary measure subject to verification of poverty conditions. Therefore, it is essential that beneficiaries renew their application every six months, otherwise the payment will be suspended.

Furthermore, it is important to remember that the Income Support can be cumulated with other pension and assistance benefits, such as disability pension or NASpI, but not with the citizenship income. In any case, it is always advisable to contact INPS or a CAF to verify one's specific situation and receive detailed information on the regulations in force.
