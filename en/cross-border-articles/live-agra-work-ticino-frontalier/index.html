The New Frontier Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020 and will enter into force on 1 January 2024. This agreement provides new rules and benefits for Ticino border workers.

Who are the Ticino border workers?

Ticino border workers are those who reside in Italy and work in Switzerland, mainly in the Canton of Ticino. According to 2022 data, there are about 40,000 Ticino border workers working in Switzerland.

What are the new rules?

The New Frontier Agreement provides for some significant changes for Ticino border workers. The main novelty is the introduction of a maximum income exempt from income tax in Switzerland, equal to CHF 20,000 per year. This means that border crossers with an income below this amount will not have to pay income tax in Switzerland.

The new agreement provides for exemptions for old frontier workers and exemptions for new frontier workers. This means that border crossers will benefit from new rules and benefits.

Who are the frontiersmen?

Border workers are workers who reside in a country bordering Switzerland, such as Italy, France or Austria, and work in Switzerland. In particular, in the Canton of Ticino, border workers are largely native to Italy.

What does the agreement provide for?

The agreement provides for two types of exemptions for old border workers:

  • Exemption from the obligation to register with the Immigration Office
  • Exemption from the obligation to submit the entry and residence declaration

In addition, the agreement provides deductibles for new frontier workers:

  • Deductible of €10,000 for new frontier workers who intend to work in Switzerland
  • Deductible of €5,000 for new frontier workers who intend to work in Switzerland for a period of less than 90 days

Concrete examples

For example, if an Italian border worker residing in Agra (SO) intends to work in Switzerland, he will benefit from the €10,000 deductible. If you work for less than 90 days, you will be eligible for the €5,000 deductible.

What exemptions or deductibles are available?

The exemptions or deductibles available for frontier workers living in Agra and working in Ticino vary depending on their individual situation. However, some of the most common exemptions or deductibles include:

  • Exemption from income tax (IR) for work income received in Switzerland
  • Value added tax (VAT) exemption for expenses incurred in Switzerland
