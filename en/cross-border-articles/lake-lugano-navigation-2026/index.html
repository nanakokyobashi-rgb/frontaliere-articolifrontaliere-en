Lugano Lake Navigation: 2026 Season Kicks Off

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 22 March 2026

The Lugano Lake Navigation season begins with special events and electrification projects for a sustainable future.

Context

TL;DR

  • Lugano Lake Navigation starts season March 29, 2026.
  • 5M CHF investment for electric boats by 2028.
  • New entities for Lakes Maggiore and Ceresio.
  • 200th anniversary of navigation on Lake Verbano in July.

Key facts

  • Start season: March 29, 2026
  • Free access day: March 28, 2026
  • Investment: 5 million Swiss francs for electric boats
  • New entities: One for Lake Maggiore, one for Lake Ceresio
  • Passenger increase: 15% increase, over 200,000 annual visitors by 2027
  • Electric boat: Lugano Elettrico, end of 2023
  • New connections: Locarno-Cannobio, early 2024
  • Sustainability investment: 2 million Swiss francs

March 29, 2026, marks the official start of the tourist season for Lugano Lake Navigation, an eagerly awaited event for both residents and tourists. After a challenging period characterized by tensions between navigation companies, unions, and cantonal authorities, there is a forward-looking optimism. To celebrate the opening, a free access day to the boats on Lake Ceresio will take place on March 28. This event will not only allow visitors to explore the lake but will also illustrate future electrification and sustainability projects, which involve an investment of around 5 million Swiss francs for the purchase of new electric boats by 2028.

Simone Bianchi, director of Lugano Lake Navigation, explained that progress is more visible on Lake Verbano, with ongoing discussions about the governance model to be adopted for the new navigation companies. 'We are waiting for reactions to our proposals to proceed with this project, essential for the development of navigation in the region,' stated Bianchi. The creation of two new entities, one for Lake Maggiore and one for Lake Ceresio, is considered a priority for the future. These new entities could generate a 15% increase in the number of passengers, with estimates predicting over 200,000 annual visitors by 2027.

The mayor of Lugano, Michele Foletti, expressed his willingness to delve into the matter as soon as a concrete project for these two entities is presented. Current regulations, which provide for financial support from the Canton, allow for coverage of up to 50% of operating costs, further incentivizing the development of navigation.

In the context of celebrations, July will see the 200th anniversary of navigation on Lake Verbano with special events in Locarno, where the recently restored steamboat Piemonte will dock, symbolizing local tradition. This event will not only highlight the history of navigation but will also provide networking opportunities among lakeside municipalities, such as Melide and Morcote, and the navigation company. The promoted initiatives are not only meant to celebrate but also to strengthen the bond between the lakeside municipalities and the navigation company, essential for integrating the lake into the local public transport network.

With careful planning and collaborative commitment, Lugano Lake Navigation is preparing to write a new chapter in its history, promoting sustainable tourism integrated into the local community.

Operational details

From a technical perspective, Lugano Lake Navigation is investing in electrification projects to make lake transport more sustainable. This includes the anticipated arrival of a new electric boat, the 'Lugano Elettrico,' expected by the end of the year, which will connect Porlezza and Lugano in just 20 minutes. This will not only improve transport efficiency, reducing waiting times by 30%, but also enhance the region's attractiveness, which is crucial for commuters and tourists.

The establishment of new navigation companies, such as 'Navigazione del Lago di Lugano SA,' represents a significant innovation in the region's transport landscape. Details regarding governance and the participation of lakeside municipalities, including Lugano, Melide, and Gandria, will be crucial for ensuring the future sustainability of these projects. Another innovative element is the introduction of direct weekly connections between Locarno and Cannobio, planned for 2024, which will not only facilitate travel between Italy and Switzerland but also contribute to greater economic and tourist integration between the two shores of the lake.

Local authorities and the navigation company are actively addressing issues that have arisen in recent months, such as the need to coordinate schedules with public transport services, and the openness to closer collaboration with municipalities is seen as a fundamental step to gain the necessary consensus for these developments. According to an analysis by the Ticino State Council, the total investment in the electric fleet is about 2 million Swiss francs, with a projected 15% increase in annual passenger numbers.

Despite the roadmap still being defined, optimism prevails among industry stakeholders, who see these innovations as an opportunity to revitalize tourism and the local economy. With the implementation of these projects, a significant increase in tourist flow is expected, projecting a 20% growth over the next three years, resulting in a positive impact on restaurants, shops, and accommodation facilities along the lake.

Key points

For commuters and residents in the region, the 2026 navigation season offers new job and leisure opportunities. Having an efficient and integrated transport system is essential not only for tourism but also for the daily lives of those who live and work in nearby areas like Lugano and Ponte Tresa. For example, the new connection between Lugano and Campione d'Italia, active from May 2026, will reduce travel time by about 15 minutes, facilitating commutes for cross-border workers.

The innovations related to the electrification of boats and the introduction of new schedules, which will include additional services during weekends, represent a step forward towards a more sustainable and convenient future. The community is therefore invited to participate in the opening events, such as the navigation festival scheduled for June 10, 2026, and to take advantage of the new transport opportunities.

It is essential to stay informed about the progress of Lugano Lake Navigation and its initiatives, which could impact quality of life and costs.
