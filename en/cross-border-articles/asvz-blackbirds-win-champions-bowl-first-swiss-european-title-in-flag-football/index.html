ASVZ Blackbirds win Champions Bowl: first Swiss European title in flag football

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 14 October 2026

The ASVZ Blackbirds triumph at the 2026 Champions Bowl, winning the first historic European flag football title for a Swiss team.

Context

The ASVZ Blackbirds of Zurich won the Champions Bowl 2026, the leading European tournament for flag football clubs. It is the first success of a Swiss team in the competition. The final, played on October 11 in Poreč, Croatia, ended 46-14 against Molosses Flag Asnières, a team from the Paris region.

The tournament ran from October 9-11 and the Blackbirds were unbeaten. After finishing in first place in both group stages, the Zurich team beat the Kranjski Jazbeci, from Slovenia, and the Italian Parma Panthers in the quarterfinals.

In the end, the Blackbirds' defense intercepted six passes: two apiece for Claudio Ferrari and for the brothers Justin and Oliver Verhoek. Attacking quarterback Dennis Sieger threw six touchdowns and scored another on the run; Frédéric Eppisser received four of the touchdown passes. The Blackbirds also conceded fewer points than all other teams in the men's competition.

"This title is the reward for years of hard work. When we founded the club, almost ten years ago, we would not have dared to dream of such a result, "said player-coach Fabiano Piccinno. "In the final we played an almost perfect game. I'm incredibly proud of this team. ”

Operational details

For the ASVZ Blackbirds it is the eighth participation in the Champions Bowl. In 2024 they finished in second place and in 2025 in third. The club, founded in 2017, also won six national titles and also won the shortened Covid season, played without an official championship.

The result comes on the eve of flag football's Olympic debut in Los Angeles 2028. The non-contact discipline will be part of the Olympic programme for the first time.
