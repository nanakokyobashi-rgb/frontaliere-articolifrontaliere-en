How does the tax credit work?

The tax credit is a tax benefit that allows cross-border workers to deduct the tax paid in Italy from their income declaration in Switzerland. For example, if a cross-border worker resident in Lugano (CH) paid CHF 10,000 in taxes in Italy for 2023, he can deduct this amount from his Swiss income declaration for 2024. This means that his taxable income in Switzerland will be reduced by CHF 10,000, resulting in a large reduction in the taxes he has to pay.

Let's assume that a cross-border worker resident in Bellinzona (CH) has an income of CHF 80,000 and has paid CHF 20,000 in taxes in Italy for 2023. If he applies the tax credit, his taxable income in Switzerland will be reduced to CHF 60,000, resulting in a reduction in taxes payable of CHF 4,000.
