Frequently Asked Questions
How many border crossers have increased in the Canton of Ticino in the last quarter?
The number of border workers in the Canton of Ticino increased by 505 units (+0.64%) compared to the previous quarter, according to data just published by the Federal Statistical Office (BFS). This indicates growth in the most recent period analysed, signalling an expansion of the cross-border workforce.
How has the number of border workers in Ticino changed compared to last year?
Compared to the same quarter of the previous year, the number of border workers in the Canton of Ticino recorded a decrease of 457 units, equal to a decrease of 0.57%. Despite the quarterly increase, there is therefore a slight decrease on an annual basis, indicating a decline in the long term.
What is the gender breakdown of frontier workers in the Canton of Ticino?
In the Canton of Ticino, the gender breakdown of border workers sees a clear majority of men, who represent 61.6% of the total (48,700 units). Women make up the remaining 38.4% (30,420 units) of the cross-border workforce, highlighting a gender disparity.
What are the most represented age groups among border workers in Ticino?
The age groups most represented among border workers in the Canton of Ticino are those between 50-54 years old (11,323 units), followed by 35-39 years old (10,887 units) and 40-44 years old (10,849 units). The 30-34 age group has 9,661 border guards, showing a concentration in the central and mature ages.
