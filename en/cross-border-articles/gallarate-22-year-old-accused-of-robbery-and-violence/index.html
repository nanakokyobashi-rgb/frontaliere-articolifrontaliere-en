Gallarate: 22-year-old accused of robbery and violence

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 October 2026

A 22-year-old man was reported while at liberty for aggravated robbery and sexual assault following an attack near the station in Gallarate; the victim, a 36-year-old woman, was discharged with a prognosis of seven days.

The intervention in Gallarate

In the early hours of Thursday 21 September, in the area of Gallarate railway station, the Carabinieri intervened after an emergency call came in to 112. The incident led to a 22-year-old being reported while at liberty, accused of aggravated robbery and sexual assault.

The first intervention was carried out by officers from the Operational and Radiomobile Unit at around one in the morning, in via Curioni. On site, they found a woman alone, described as being in an evident state of shock, and provided her with first aid. The call therefore drew the officers' attention to the area near the railway station, where the woman had asked for help.

The woman told the officers that she had been approached shortly beforehand by a young man and that her mobile phone had been forcibly taken. The account concerns an evening spent with some friends and a subsequent assault in the station area. According to the officers' reconstruction, the phone theft was accompanied by sexual violence and physical consequences.

After the request for help, the officers began investigations at the scene. The text thus links the call to 112, the assistance provided in via Curioni and the investigation launched by the Gallarate Station. The events were reconstructed based on the woman's account and the officers' immediate checks.

The charges communicated concern two allegations: aggravated robbery and sexual assault. The young man, identified in the source as 22 years old, was reported while at liberty. The report places the Carabinieri intervention in the station area and links the reconstruction to the call to 112, the assistance provided in via Curioni and the checks carried out by the Gallarate Station.

The account therefore includes Piazza Giovanni XXIII, where the woman was spending the evening, the area near the railway station and via Curioni, where the officers provided first aid. These are the places indicated in the source to describe the beginning of the incident and the intervention by the Carabinieri.

Operational details

The reconstruction of the night

The sequence recounted to the officers begins at Piazza Giovanni XXIII, where the woman was spending the evening with some friends. After her cellphone was taken, she followed the young man in an attempt to get the smartphone back. The young man was riding an electric scooter, and the route led to the vicinity of the railway station, on Via Curioni.

According to the officers' reconstruction, the young man took advantage of the absence of passersby to attack the woman. He restrained her, tore her T-shirt, and forced her to submit to groping and sexual acts. The same reconstruction reports that the assault caused her physical injuries and abrasions.

During the frantic moments of the assault, the woman noticed a medium-sized kitchen knife. The man had it hidden in the waistband of his trousers. The detail of the knife appears in the officers' reconstruction together with the description of the absence of passersby.

The passing of a pedestrian interrupted the violence. The young man fled, and the woman was able to raise the alarm and request help. The officers found her in a state of shock when they arrived on Via Curioni.

The hospital visit

After receiving initial assistance, the woman was transported under a green code to the emergency department of the Sant'Antonio Abate Hospital in Gallarate. The transfer was for the necessary clinical examinations and dressings. Medical staff found various superficial wounds and then discharged her with a seven-day prognosis for recovery.

The visit to the emergency department represents the subsequent medical stage after the initial assistance provided on Via Curioni. The source indicates the green code, the clinical examinations, the dressings, the superficial wounds, and the discharge with a seven-day prognosis. The discharge came after the examinations and dressings, with the seven-day period indicated for recovery.

Key points

Developments in the investigation

The inquiries by the Gallarate Carabinieri Station began immediately, after the woman filed her complaint on 21 September. The source indicates that the officers worked from the earliest stages of the case to determine the identity of the alleged perpetrator. According to the text, the result came within a few hours.

The young man was traced to his home and taken to the station. The source describes him as a 22-year-old of Peruvian origin. At the station, the victim recognized him without a shadow of a doubt. This step is reported before the young man was referred to the Judicial Authority.

From the complaint to the referral

The term used in the report for the outcome of the inquiries is referral to the Judicial Authority. The young man was therefore reported without being detained on charges of aggravated robbery and sexual assault. The source also reports that the investigation is continuing in close coordination with the authority to establish every detail of the case.

The investigative chronology indicated is brief but precise: complaint on 21 September, immediate inquiries, identification within a few hours, being traced at his home, being taken to the station, and recognition by the woman. After these steps, the 22-year-old was referred to the Judicial Authority.

The source also points out a discrepancy in the indication of the woman's age: the opening says 35, while the section on the reconstruction and the headline identify her as 36. The information is reported this way in the source; the investigative developments, however, remain those attributed to the Carabinieri of the Gallarate Station: identification of the young man, recognition at the station, and continuation of the investigation to clarify the case.
