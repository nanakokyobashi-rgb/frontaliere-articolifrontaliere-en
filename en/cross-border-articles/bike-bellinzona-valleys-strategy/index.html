Bellinzona and Valleys Tourism has presented to the press its strategy dedicated to the world of two wheels, a path that has been underway for years and is now more structured. The goal is clear: to position the destination as a reference point for enthusiasts and the general public, thanks to a diversified and continuously evolving offer. Central to this is the work with local partners, including the Regional Development Authority for Bellinzona and the Valleys, Ticino Tourism, other Regional Tourist Organizations, and local associations such as Blenio Bike and the newly formed Leventina Bike.

Growing offer between mountain bike and road

The destination boasts 13 official mountain bike trails and a flow trail at Nara, appreciated by experts and beginners alike. These are complemented by the Bike Parks of Airolo and Nara, expanding realities that are investing in the development of the summer offer with packages and dedicated activities, also contributing to destagionalization. Among the ongoing projects is a new mountain bike trail between Campra, Gorda, and Nara, which will be integrated into the Quattro and Cinque Passi Bike itineraries around the Gottardo massif, strengthening the interregional network with destinations such as Andermatt, Disentis-Sedrun, and Goms.

Operational details

Targeted Promotion and International Vision

The strategy is also based on structured promotion, with collaborations with influencers and testimonials that have contributed to strengthening the visibility of the destination and the growth of digital communities. These are complemented by partnerships with specialized media such as ALVENTO for the Italian and international market and Ride.ch for the national public. The activities are part of a coordinated strategy with Ticino Turismo and Switzerland Tourism, with a focus on foreign markets, particularly Italy. Among the initiatives, participation in events such as the Cycling Tourism Fair and the Cycle Week in Zurich.

Bike Events, Drivers of Visibility

Events represent a pillar of the strategy and in recent years have registered significant growth. Among the main appointments, the passage of the Giro d'Italia on May 26 stands out, with a stage that will cross Bellinzona, Biasca, the Riviera, the Leventina, and the Valle di Blenio, offering an international showcase. From the Giro will also be born a new event, the Biasca-Faido-Carì Bike Day on May 23, destined to be repeated in the coming years.

The strategy of Bellinzona and Valli Turismo not only promotes cycling tourism but also contributes to the destagionalization and diversification of the region's tourist offer. This integrated approach, which combines infrastructure, events, and promotion, aims to create a complete cycling ecosystem, attractive to all types of cyclists, from professionals to occasional enthusiasts.

Key points

What to do for cross-border workers

For cross-border workers interested in taking advantage of the new opportunities offered by Bellinzona and Valli's cycling strategy, it's important to know the available routes and upcoming events. It is recommended to visit the official Bellinzona and Valli Turismo website for detailed information on routes, events, and bike-friendly facilities. Additionally, you can participate in organized events, such as the Biasca-Faido-Carì Bike Day, to experience a unique adventure and meet other cycling enthusiasts.

Procedure to participate in events

1. Inform: Visit the official Bellinzona and Valli Turismo website to learn about event details and routes. 2. Book: Some events may require advance booking. Check the registration methods and ensure you have all the necessary documents. 3. Prepare: Make sure you have the right equipment, such as bicycles, helmets, and sportswear. For mountain bike routes, it might be useful to have a specific bike and safety gear. 4. Participate: Enjoy the experience and take advantage of the opportunities offered by the events and cycling routes.

Useful tools

For cross-border workers who want to plan their free time and discover new activities, the cost calculator can be useful for managing the budget and organizing outings. Additionally, the event calendar provides a comprehensive overview of cycling events and other interesting happenings in the region.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the main mountain bike trails available in Bellinzona and the surrounding valleys?
The region offers 13 official mountain bike trails, including a flow trail at Nara and the Bike Parks of Airolo and Nara. Additionally, a new trail is being developed between Campra, Gorda, and Nara, which will be integrated into the Quattro and Cinque Passi Bike itineraries around the Gottardo massif.
What cycling events are planned in the region?
Among the main events are the passage of the Giro d'Italia on May 26 and the Biasca-Faido-Carì Bike Day on May 23, which will be repeated in the coming years. Additionally, the cantonal project Ticino Traverso is scheduled for October 2-4.
How can I participate in the cycling events organized by Bellinzona and Valli Turismo?
To participate in the events, you can visit the official website of Bellinzona and Valli Turismo for detailed information and registration procedures. Some events may require advance booking, so it's important to check the details in advance.
