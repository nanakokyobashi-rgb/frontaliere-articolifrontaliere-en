Gambarogno Trail: New Interreg Project in Varese (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 31 May 2026

The study of feasibility for the Interreg project to create a cross-border trail connecting Gambarogno to Lombardy has been presented in Varese.

Context

In brief

The feasibility study for the Maggiore Lake Trail project was presented in Varese. The project, part of the Interreg program, aims to create a structured connection that unites the shores and interior of Lake Maggiore, involving Gambarogno in a path that transcends geographical and administrative barriers between Switzerland and Italy. The mayor of Gambarogno, who participated in the event, emphasized the historical unity of the territory, regardless of national borders. The presentation in Varese underscores the commitment of local authorities to promote transnational collaboration that values the landscape and tourist attractions of the area.

Focus on transnational collaboration

The integration between the Canton Ticino and the Italian border regions is the driving force behind this proposal. It is not just about infrastructure, but about a strategic vision that aims to strengthen the bond between local communities through shared infrastructure. Participation in an Interreg project highlights the importance of European and Swiss funds for cooperation projects that facilitate joint development. For cross-border workers and residents, the development of walking paths and slow mobility routes represents an opportunity to experience the territory in a new way, reinforcing that common identity that the mayor of Della Santa emphasized during the event. The focus is now on the implementation phase, where cooperation between Gambarogno institutions and Italian partners will be crucial for the success of the trail.

Key points

For those who wish to understand how these developments affect their daily life, it is crucial to monitor the official updates of the project. The procedure for the implementation of the 'Maggiore Lake Trail' involves several bureaucratic steps that will involve local authorities, technical offices of the Canton Ticino, and Italian counterparts. It is advisable for residents and workers in the area to stay informed about the public consultations that may be held in the coming months. Frequently Asked Questions
What does the feasibility study for the Maggiore Lake Trail entail?
The study, presented in Varese, outlines the guidelines for creating a transnational trail that connects Gambarogno to Lombardy. The goal is to promote territorial integration and mobility without borders, valorizing the common landscape resources between Switzerland and Italy through the Interreg program.
What role does Gambarogno play in the project?
Gambarogno is a key player in the project. The mayor of Gambarogno, Della Santa, emphasized the importance of this territorial connection, highlighting that the territory unites communities beyond any administrative border, making the Ticinese municipality a fundamental pillar for the success of the transnational trail.
How can I monitor the project's progress?
It is recommended to follow the official channels of the Gambarogno authorities and the Interreg partners. The operational phases will require coordination between the technical offices of Switzerland and Italy; participating in public consultations is the main way to stay informed and have your voice heard.
