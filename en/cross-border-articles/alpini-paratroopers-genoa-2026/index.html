In the Ligurian capital, on May 9, 2026, the 97th National Meeting of the Alpini took place. Among the most spectacular events was the landing of paratroopers on Genoa's beach. Thousands of Black Feathers arrived from all over Italy to participate in this gathering, which represents a moment of great unity and tradition for the National Alpini Association.

The event saw the participation of numerous paratrooper Alpini, who performed an impressive landing on the beach, attracting the attention of numerous spectators. This type of event not only celebrates the history and values of the Alpini but also their ability to operate in complex contexts and demonstrate their preparation and professionalism.

The landing of the paratroopers

The landing of the paratroopers on Genoa's beach was one of the highlights of the Meeting. This type of exercise requires great preparation and coordination and represents a concrete example of the operational capabilities of the paratrooper Alpini. Spectators were able to admire not only the landing technique but also the precision and discipline of the participants.

The event also had a strong media impact, with numerous videos and photographs circulating on social networks, contributing to spreading knowledge and appreciation for the work of the Alpini. This type of initiative is fundamental to keeping the tradition alive and involving new generations.
