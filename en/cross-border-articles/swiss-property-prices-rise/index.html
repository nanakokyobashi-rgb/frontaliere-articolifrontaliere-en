The ImmoScout24 index also showed a rise for the month of July. The price requested by bidders for an apartment in flat property increased by 0.3% compared to June, while for single-family homes the progression is 0.1%. Compared to the same period last year, there were significant progresses: +4.7% (apartments) and +3.2% (houses) respectively.

These figures are particularly interesting for those looking for a house or apartment in municipalities such as Lugano, Bellinzona or Locarno, where demand is high and prices are rising. According to data from ImmoScout24, the average price of an apartment in flat property in Lugano is around CHF 620,000, while in Bellinzona it is around CHF 450,000.
