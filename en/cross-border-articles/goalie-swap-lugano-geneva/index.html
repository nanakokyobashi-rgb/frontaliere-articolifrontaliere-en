On October 8, 2026, Lugano announced the change between the pipes with Geneva Servette. Robert Mayer arrives in black and white, while Joren Van Pottelberghe takes the opposite direction. The two clubs finalized a cross-loan agreement valid until the end of the 2026/27 season, as indicated in the communication from Hockey Club Lugano.

Mayer's destination is the Cornèr Arena. The goaltender arrives from Geneva Servette with a profile built in Switzerland's top league: 397 games played, as well as a national title and a Champions Hockey League title in his honours. Born on October 9, 1989, in Havirov, Czechia, Mayer holds dual Swiss and Czech citizenship.

Van Pottelberghe's reverse path

Van Pottelberghe is instead restarting in Geneva after just over two years in Lugano. The 29-year-old was born in Zug and arrived in Ticino in the summer of 2024. A few months later, he suffered a serious injury, an episode that affected his experience wearing the black-and-white jersey. In total, he defended Lugano's goal on 23 occasions.

Lugano's general manager Janick Steinmann thanked the goaltender for his time in Lugano, recalling the work done to return to the ice after the injury and wishing him the best for his new challenge in Geneva. Regarding the new arrival, Steinmann explained that the club chose to sign Mayer until the end of the season, highlighting his determination, enthusiasm, experience in the League and ability to play at a high level in Switzerland.

«For the rest of the season we have chosen to rely on Robert Mayer. He is a highly motivated player and happy to wear the HCL jersey.»

The announced arrangement therefore remains a loan. The official communication links the operation to the 2026/27 season and clearly identifies the two paths: Mayer to Lugano, Van Pottelberghe to Geneva.
