Connections and Travel Times from Verrayes

The choice of Verrayes as a residence for a border worker working in Valais implies an accurate assessment of connections and journey times. Although Verrayes offers a serene living environment, its location, despite being in the Valais Canton, can lead to longer journey times compared to municipalities located closer to borders or major communication routes. It is important to consider the local road network and its efficiency, especially during periods of heavy traffic or adverse weather conditions, which in high mountains can be more frequent. For example, reaching workplaces such as Sion or Martigny da Verrayes could take a journey time that must be compared to that of other more strategically positioned locations. The use of public transport, if available and efficient, can be an alternative, but even in this case it is necessary to check its capillarity and schedules with respect to work needs. The proximity to the border crossings, although Verrayes is not a border municipality, is another factor to be considered indirectly, as the efficiency of internal transport in the Valais affects the overall fluidity of commuting. Key points

What to Consider Before Moving to Verrayes Before finalizing your move to Verrayes and starting work as a cross-border worker in Valais, it's crucial to conduct a series of checks and planning. Firstly, you must inform yourself about the requirements for obtaining the Permit G, a document essential for cross-border workers residing in Italy and employed in Switzerland. This permit governs access to the labor market and residence conditions. The choice of residence municipality, in this case Verrayes, should be weighed in relation to commuting times to your workplace. It's advisable to simulate the route on weekdays to get a realistic estimate of travel times and associated costs (fuel, vehicle wear, highway tolls if applicable, or public transport costs). Evaluating the cost of living is another fundamental step: comparing prices for rentals, utilities, food, insurance, and services in Verrayes with those in your current Italian residence will give you a precise idea of the economic impact of the move. No less important is understanding the Swiss and Italian tax systems, particularly the novelties introduced by the New Cross-Border Agreement from January 1, 2024. The tax exemptions (CHF 10,000 for new cross-border workers and CHF 7,500 for old cross-border workers in a transitional regime until 2033) modify the calculation of withholding taxes, retained exclusively in Switzerland. Italy applies a tax credit to avoid double taxation. It's also useful to inform yourself about Swiss social contributions, such as AVS/AI/IPG (5.3% employee), AD/AC (1.1%), LAINF (0.7-1.5%) and LPP (from 7% to 18% by age bracket from the 25th year), which impact your paycheck. Choosing health insurance (LAMal) is another aspect to address carefully, considering the options available for cross-border workers with Permit G and applicable deductibles. Finally, it's advisable to consult specific guides or experts in cross-border taxation to ensure you have all the necessary information and to best plan every aspect of your move and working life in Switzerland. Preparation is the key to a serene and advantageous cross-border experience. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the main tax changes for border workers with the New Agreement?
The New Frontier Agreement, in force since 1 January 2024, introduced new tax exemptions. For new frontier workers, the deductible is CHF 10,000. For old frontier workers, an exemption of CHF7,500 is provided, with a transitional regime valid until 2033. The tax at source is withheld exclusively in Switzerland, while Italy avoids double taxation through the EC 730 tax credit.
What are the average travel times from Verrayes to the main workplaces in the Valais?
Travel times from Verrayes to the main work centres in the Valais, such as Sion or Martigny, can vary significantly depending on traffic, weather conditions and the means of transport used. It is advisable to simulate the route during peak hours to obtain a realistic estimate. Verrayes' location could result in longer commuting times compared to municipalities closer to major communication axes or border crossings.
What are the employee's Swiss social contributions?
The Swiss social contributions borne by the employee include the AVS/AI/IPG at 5.3%, the AD/AC at 1.1%, the LAINF with a rate between 0.7% and 1.5%, and the LPP (Pension Fund) with percentages ranging from 7% to 18% by age group from 25 years. These contributions are withheld directly from the paycheck.
