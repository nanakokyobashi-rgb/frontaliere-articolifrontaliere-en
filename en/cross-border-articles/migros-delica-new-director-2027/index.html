The news of Migros Delica's change at the top marks an important transition for one of the Migros group companies. From 1 January 2027, in fact, Moritz Werner will officially assume the leadership of the company that produces well-known own brands. The announcement was made by Migros, specifying that the new director will take the place of Thomas Gubler. Thelatter will leave the position to retire, after completing a company career of 40 years in total within the structure, of which three years spent in the role of CEO. The outgoing management of Gubler has been characterized by a particular focus of the activity abroad on a few long-term customers, obtaining excellent commercial results especially in the segment of coffee capsules compatible with Nespresso and in the chocolate confectionery sector.

Operational details

The industrial landscape that belongs to Migros Delica is rooted in the structured merger that took place in 2021. In thatyear, in fact, the company was formally born from the union of several historical realities of the Swiss food sector: Chocolat Frey, Delica, Midor, Riseria and Total Capsule Solutions. This impressive industrial aggregation operates today under the umbrella of Migros Industrie, an economic sector of national importance that employs a total of about 1900 employees divided between five operating offices located in the territory of Switzerland and several operating subsidiaries located abroad. The company's daily production activity focuses on the production of a wide range of consumer food products, including chocolate-based bars and products, assorted snacks, kitchen items and coffee capsules. These goods are produced both to supply the network of Migros stores and supermarkets, and to meet the demands of large third-party commercial customers both on the Swiss domestic market and on international markets.

Key points

The transition to the top of Migros Delica represents a key step in the long-term planning of the Swiss group's corporate governance. The date set for the handover is January 1, 2027, at which time Thomas Gubler will conclude his long professional career lasting fortyyears to access the retirement period. For employees, business partners and observers of the Swiss economic market, the coming months will be characterised by the completion of Gubler's mandate and the preparation of the operational takeover by Moritz Werner. The administrative continuity guaranteed by the choice of an internal manager ensures the management stability of the production activities carried out in the company's five Swiss offices and foreign branches.
