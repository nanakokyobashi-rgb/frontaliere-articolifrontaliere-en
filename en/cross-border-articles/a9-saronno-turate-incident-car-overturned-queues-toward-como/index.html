The affected section

At around 11:30 on Friday, October 9, a vehicle overturned along the A9 Lainate-Como-Chiasso motorway, in the section between Saronno and Turate. Two people were involved in the impact. The accident affected traffic heading toward Como and the Swiss border.

The rollover occurred for reasons still under investigation. The known information includes the location, time and number of people involved, while the circumstances remain to be determined.

The indicated point is a section of the A9 motorway between Saronno and Turate. Operational details

The most immediate information on the repercussions concerns the queue recorded shortly after the crash. In the section between Origgio and Turate, up to 2 kilometers of traffic were reported, starting at kilometer 15 and heading north. The report describes the area in which traffic experienced slowing, distinct from the section where the vehicle overturned, indicated between Saronno and Turate.

Guidance for travelers

Autostrade per l'Italia reported, for those traveling toward Como and Switzerland, the recommended entrance at Turate. The indication was provided to avoid the queue reported on the A9.

For users arriving from Lainate and heading north, the exit at the Origgio interchange was instead recommended. The communication therefore distinguishes two situations: access to Turate for those traveling toward Como and the Swiss border, and the exit at Origgio for those coming from Lainate and continuing north.

The kilometer reference, the stretch of the queue, and the two interchanges make up the traffic picture circulated after the accident. Origgio and Turate are the points cited for the queue and for the instructions to users, while kilometer 15 marks the beginning of the queue in the northbound direction.

The maximum reported length is 2 kilometers. This figure refers to the period after the accident and accompanies Autostrade per l'Italia's recommendations on choosing the entrance or exit. The route affected remains the one toward Como and Switzerland, with traffic also indicated in the northbound direction.

The instructions both refer to northbound traffic, but change depending on the access point: Turate is the recommended entrance for those traveling toward Como and Switzerland, while Origgio is the indicated exit for users coming from Lainate. The traffic message thus associates each point with the direction indicated by Autostrade per l'Italia.

Key points

The follow-up to the response on the A9 section concerns rescue operations, investigations and restoration of the roadway. Emergency medical services responded with an ambulance to assist the people involved. Fire Brigade crews from the Varese provincial command also operated alongside the medical personnel.

Tasks on site

The Traffic Police were alerted to manage traffic and conduct the necessary investigations. Their intervention complements that of the Fire Brigade and the ambulance, as part of an operation involving assistance, traffic control and investigations into the accident.

In the affected section, the relevant local authorities were also placed on standby for rescue and roadway restoration activities, together with the emergency services and the Traffic Police.
