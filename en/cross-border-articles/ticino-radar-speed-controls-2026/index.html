Radar always active: where from 4 to 10 May

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 1 May 2026

The cantonal police announce speed checks in various locations in Ticino from 4 to 10 May 2026.

Context

In brief

  • Mobile and semi-stationary radars active in Ticino from May 4 to 10, 2026
  • Checks in 30 locations between Bellinzona, Locarno, Lugano, and Mendrisio
  • Cantonal and municipal police involved

Key facts

  • What: Speed checks with mobile and semi-stationary radars
  • When: From May 4 to 10, 2026
  • Where: In various locations in Ticino
  • Who: Cantonal and municipal police
  • Fine: CHF 250 for speeding up to 20 km/h, up to CHF 1,200 for excesses over 50 km/h

The Cantonal Police of Ticino has announced that, despite the May 1 bank holiday, speed checks will not stop. From May 4 to 10, 2026, mobile and semi-stationary radars will be active in various locations in the canton. The initiative aims to ensure compliance with speed limits and road safety.

Check locations

Speed checks will be carried out in various areas of Ticino, involving both cantonal and municipal police. Here are the locations concerned:

  • Bellinzona District: Bellinzona (via San Francesco, via Orico), Giubiasco (via Cantonale), Cadenazzo (via San Gottardo), Sant’Antonino (via Cantonale), Sementina (via Cantonale), Gnosca (via Cantonale), Monte Carasso (via Cantonale)
  • Locarno District: Orselina (via Cardada), Locarno (via San Gottardo, via della Pace), Muralto (via Cantonale), Ascona (via Cantonale), Golino (via Cantonale), Losone (via Cantonale)
  • Vallemaggia District: Avegno (via Cantonale)
  • Lugano District: Lugano (via Nassa, via San Salvatore), Massagno (via Cantonale), Origlio (via Cantonale), Viganello (via Cantonale), Davesco-Soragno (via Cantonale), Sonvico (via Cantonale), Montagnola (via Cantonale), Grancia (via Cantonale), Paradiso (via Cantonale), Maroggia (via Cantonale), Cassarate (via Cantonale), Breganzona (via Cantonale), Manno (via Cantonale), Gravesano (via Cantonale), Agno (via Cantonale)
  • Mendrisio District: Balerna (via Cantonale), Stabio (via Cantonale), San Pietro (via Cantonale), Besazio (via Cantonale)

Semi-stationary radars

In addition to mobile checks, semi-stationary radars will also be active in two specific locations:

  • Sant’Antonino (via Cantonale, limit 50 km/h)
  • Magadino (via Cantonale, limit 80 km/h)

The Cantonal Police reminds all drivers to respect speed limits to ensure road safety and avoid penalties. Checks will be carried out at varying times and locations to maximize the effectiveness of operations.

  • Respect safety distances

Practical comparisons

  • Urban scenarios: In urban areas such as Lugano and Bellinzona, where the limit is often 50 km/h, exceeding by 20 km/h results in a fine of CHF 250.
  • Extra-urban scenarios: On roads such as via Cantonale in Magadino, where the limit is 80 km/h, exceeding by 30 km/h results in a fine of CHF 500.
