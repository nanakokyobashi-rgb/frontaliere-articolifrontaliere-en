The renewal of the Migros Group's National Collective Labour Agreement for the five-year period 2027-2031 was officially announced. The central new feature is the harmonization of employment conditions across the company: under the new L-GAV, the sectoral agreements previously applied in logistics, meat processing and the leisure sector will be superseded and abolished.

For a large group of employees, the stated effect is a reduction in weekly hours from 43 to 41, with no changes to the salary received. The text therefore links the reduction in hours to equal pay, without providing an individual quantification of the increase.

The new framework will cover the entire workforce, regardless of employment percentage. Even employees working less than 20% will benefit from the same guarantees and contractual conditions. In case of illness or injury, they will receive 100% of net salary for the first 90 days; thereafter, coverage will amount to 80% of gross salary.

Purchasing power and wage measures

From January 1, 2027, the program will award employees ten Cumulus points for every franc spent across the entire range of Migros-branded supermarkets and in the group's self-service restaurants. The benefit will translate into an effective 10% discount on purchases, provided through shopping vouchers.

For 2027, Migros has planned an overall investment of 1.6% of the total payroll, including specific increases for individual companies. For Food Retail, the nationwide allocation reaches up to 0.3%; for other sectors, the ceiling may reach 0.7%. Individual subsidiaries will independently communicate how the measures will be implemented.
