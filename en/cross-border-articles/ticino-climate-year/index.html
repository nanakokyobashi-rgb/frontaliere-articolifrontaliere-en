Spring

Spring in Ticino is generally hot and sunny, with temperatures hovering around 18°C in April and 22°C in May. Rainfall is rather scarce during this period, with an average of 60 mm of precipitation per month. The season is ideal for visiting the tourist resorts of Ticino, such as Bellinzona, Locarno and Lugano.

Summer in Ticino is hot and humid, with temperatures that can reach 30°C in July and August. Rainfall is more frequent during this period, with an average of 80 mm of precipitation per month.
