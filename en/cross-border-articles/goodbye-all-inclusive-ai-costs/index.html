Goodbye to fixed AI costs: new usage-based rates (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 8 May 2026

Companies using artificial intelligence will now pay based on actual usage, significantly impacting operating costs.

Context

In brief

  • AI shifts from fixed costs to consumption-based models
  • Companies will pay based on actual usage
  • Significant impact on operational costs

Key facts

  • What: Shift from fixed costs to consumption-based pricing for AI
  • When: May 8, 2026
  • Where: Globally, impacting companies in Ticino
  • Who: Companies using artificial intelligence
  • Amount: Not yet specified

The honeymoon is over for artificial intelligence. After years of fixed costs, companies using AI will now face consumption-based rates, significantly impacting their budgets. This change, announced by OpenAI, marks a turning point in how companies manage AI-related costs.

Impact on cross-border workers

For cross-border workers in Ticino using AI in their activities, this change could mean greater attention to cost management. Companies may need to review their budgets and allocate resources more efficiently. This could also influence corporate policies and investment decisions.

What changes for companies

Companies using AI will now pay based on actual usage rather than a fixed fee. This means costs could vary monthly, depending on usage. Companies will need to carefully monitor AI usage to avoid cost surprises.

Possible scenarios

1. Increased costs: If AI usage increases, costs could rise significantly. 2. Reduced costs: If AI usage decreases, costs could be reduced. 3. Budget restructuring: Companies may need to reallocate resources to better manage variable costs.

Comparison with the previous situation

Before this change, companies paid a fixed fee for AI usage, regardless of actual usage. This model was more predictable but less flexible. With the new consumption-based model, companies will have greater flexibility but will need to manage greater cost variability.
