The remuneration of border veterinarians in Ticino is influenced by the tax and social security regulations of the Canton. According to Ticino law, the salaries of border veterinarians are subject to a 30% tax at source on taxable income. For example, if a border veterinarian earns 80,000francs a year, the source tax would be 24,000 francs.

In addition, border veterinarians in Ticino are obliged to pay the contribution to the AVS, which is currently set at 2.4% of taxable income. If the frontier veterinarian earns CHF 80,000 per year, the contribution to the AVS would be CHF 1,920.

According to the law, border veterinarians are entitled to a basic pension of CHF 3,600 per month, plus a contribution of CHF2,400 per month for each year of contribution.
