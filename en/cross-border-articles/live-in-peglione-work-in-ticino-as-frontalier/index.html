Border workers residing in Peglio and working in Ticino must consider the following implications:

  • The possibility of obtaining a G/B permit to work in Ticino, as provided for by the federal law of 24 March 2006, which introduced the G/B residence permit for frontier workers.   • The deductible of €7,500- €10,000 for old border workers, as provided for by the Ticino law of 10 June 2015, which introduced the deductible for border workers who have worked in Switzerland for at least 5 years. This allowance allows frontier workers to benefit from a reduction in social security contributions.
