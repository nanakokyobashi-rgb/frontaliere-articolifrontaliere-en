Key facts

  • Cosa: Chiusura della Val Bedretto

  • Dove: Da Airolo in poi
  • Chi: Comune di Bedretto
  • Motivo: Alto rischio di frane
  • Impatto: Mobilità locale compromessa

The Val Bedretto has been closed until further notice, starting from Airolo. The population remaining in the valley is advised to stay indoors as there is a high risk of landslides. This notice was issued by the Bedretto municipality, which specified that the measure is in effect from 18:00 of this evening. The Val Bedretto is an important route connecting Ticino to Switzerland, and its closure has a significant impact on local mobility.

Operational details

The Val Bedretto, which crosses the Ticino region, is an important road connecting Ticino to Switzerland. Its closure, announced by the Bedretto municipality, has an immediate impact on the daily lives of residents and visitors. The road, which has been closed from Airolo, is a fundamental route for the transport of goods and people between the two regions. According to information provided by the Bedretto municipality, the closure was necessary due to a high risk of landslides, which pose a significant threat to road safety.
