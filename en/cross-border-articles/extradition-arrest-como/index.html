Extradited from Switzerland, arrested in Ponte Chiasso

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 1 October 2026

Ponte Chiasso: On 18 September, a 28-year-old Nigerian man was handed over from Switzerland and arrested. He must serve four years, nine months and 18 days in prison.

The arrest in Ponte Chiasso

On 18 September, in Ponte Chiasso, the staff of the binational office of the Border Police of Como Ponte Chiasso arrested a 28-year-old Nigerian citizen. The man had been handed over by the Swiss authorities to the Italian authorities, because he was the recipient of a European arrest warrant.

The mandate concerns "mafia-type associations, including foreign ones". According to reports from the State Police, the act concerns facts committed in Turin and other cities starting from 2017. The news therefore places in the same provision both the reference to the associations indicated and the time frame of the facts recalled.

The 28-year-old, homeless in Italy, had been in detention in the Confederation since June 24, 2026 solely for the purpose of extradition. The handover to the Italian authorities and the arrest in Ponte Chiasso represent the next step described in the news. The place of the intervention is precisely indicated: the binational office of the Border Police of Como Ponte Chiasso.

The State Police provides the framework relating to the warrant, while the arrest is attributed to the staff of the border office. The three initial elements of the affair are thus defined: detention in Switzerland, surrender to the Italian authorities and the arrest of the Nigerian citizen in Ponte Chiasso. The source links all these steps to the European arrest warrant.

The reference to Como appears in the name of the binational office that carried out the arrest. Ponte Chiasso, on the other hand, is the place indicated for the intervention, while Turin and the other cities are linked to the facts referred to in the warrant. The reconstruction thus remains anchored to the places and acts cited by the State Police.

Operational details

The imprisonment order

Date, office and signature

In addition to the European arrest warrant, the 28-year-old is the recipient of an execution order for imprisonment. The State Police indicates April 9, 2024 as the date of issue and attributes the act to the Public Prosecutor's Office at the Court of Appeal of Turin - Criminal Executions Office. The measure is signed by the deputy prosecutor Marco Grandolfo.

The source thus places two references side by side: the European warrant, linked to mafia-type associations, including foreign ones, and to the acts committed in Turin and other cities starting from 2017; the execution order, linked instead to imprisonment. The office, date and signature are specified.

Key points

The follow-up indicated by the source

The transfer after the fulfilment

The final part of the news concerns the transfer following the arrest. Once the formalities were completed, the 28-year-old was transferred to a prison. This is the operational outcome indicated by the source after the handover from the Swiss authorities to the Italian ones and the intervention of the binational office of the Border Police of Como Ponte Chiasso.

The sentence to be served is quantified in four years, nine months and 18 days of imprisonment. The title summarizes it as a sentence of almost five years, while the text provides the precise division between years, months and days. The data allows the transfer to be read as the step related to the execution of the imprisonment indicated in the order issued by the Public Prosecutor's Office at the Court of Appeal of Turin - Criminal Execution Office.

The sequel described therefore remains that of prison: the last step reported is the transfer of the man. The story remains linked to Ponte Chiasso for the place of arrest, to Switzerland for the previous detention and to Turin for the acts and facts recalled by the State Police.

The final picture keeps together the different places mentioned: the Confederation, where the 28-year-old had been detained since June 24, 2026 for the purpose of extradition; Ponte Chiasso, where he was arrested on September 18; Turin and other cities, where according to the warrant the facts were committed starting in 2017. The source thus indicates the prison transfer as a documented follow-up to the extradition.
