Recognition of the diploma:

To work as a physiotherapist in Ticino, it is essential to have a recognized diploma. The Diploma of Medical Experience (dem) or the Diploma in Medicine and Surgery (DMC) are fundamental requirements. For these qualifications, recognition is guaranteed in Switzerland, but not in all countries. Therefore, before emigrating, it is essential to verify the recognition in Switzerland.

📊 THE G PERMIT:

The G permit is required to work in Ticino and must be issued by the Regional Health and Social Services Directorate of Italian Switzerland. To obtain it, you must submit an application for recognition of the diploma and a medical certificate certifying your knowledge of the Italian language.

💡 THE ENABLING INTERFACE:

Once you have obtained the G permit, you must pass a qualification process to work in Ticino. The qualification process includes several steps, including the presentation of a curriculum vitae, the declaration of having no criminal record or compulsory military service, proof of knowledge of the Italian language and verification of professional competence.
