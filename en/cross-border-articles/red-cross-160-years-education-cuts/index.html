Celebration at Lugano's Ciani grove, but concerns remain over cuts to educational staff

In brief

  • Swiss Red Cross celebrates 160 years at Ciani Park in Lugano
  • Reduction from 35 to 13 layoffs planned

  • 30% cuts for youth centers in Lugano and Mendrisio

  • What: Celebration of the 160th anniversary of the Swiss Red Cross
  • When: May 8, 2024
  • Where: Ciani Park, Lugano

Operational details

Implications for cross-border workers

The reduction of educational staff at the Swiss Red Cross could have a significant impact on the services offered to cross-border workers residing in Ticino. In particular, youth centers, which accommodate adults, may experience a decrease in service quality due to staff reductions. This could affect the Swiss Red Cross's ability to support young cross-border workers in their integration and training journey. For example, at the Lugano center, the number of educators has decreased from 12 to 8, with a 33% increase in workload. In Mendrisio, the situation is similar, with a reduction from 7 to 5 educators, increasing the educator-user ratio from 1:15 to 1:20.

Comparison with the previous situation

Before the cuts, youth centers had a higher number of educators, allowing for more personalized and continuous support. With the reduction in staff, the remaining educators will have to manage a larger number of users, which could lead to a decrease in the quality of service offered. This is a crucial point for cross-border workers, who often face particular challenges related to double taxation and the management of health insurance. For example, the double taxation agreement between Italy and Switzerland, in effect since 2012, provides that cross-border workers pay taxes in Switzerland, but with a tax credit in Italy. However, managing these regulations can be complex without adequate support.

Key points

Procedure to Obtain Support

1. Contact the Sottoceneri Section: For detailed information on available services, you can directly contact the Sottoceneri section of the Swiss Red Cross. For example, the Lugano center offers personalized consultations for cross-border workers, focusing on tax and pension issues. In 2023, the center assisted over 1,200 cross-border workers, providing support for an average of 45 minutes per person. 2. Attend Events: Participating in events organized by the Red Cross can help you stay informed and receive direct support. For example, in Mendrisio, workshops on topics such as family budget management and social integration are regularly held. In 2023, these events saw the participation of approximately 800 cross-border workers. 3. Use Online Services: The Red Cross also offers online services that can be used to obtain information and support. The portal dedicated to cross-border workers, for example, provides practical guides and forms to fill out to request assistance. In 2023, the site recorded over 5,000 monthly accesses.

Useful Tools

For cross-border workers who need additional support, tools such as the salary calculator can help manage finances better, and the insurance comparator can help find the most suitable solution for their needs. For example, a cross-border worker residing in Chiasso and working in Italy can use the calculator to estimate the taxes due in Switzerland, considering an annual income of 60,000 CHF. The comparator, on the other hand, can help compare Swiss health insurance policies, with an average saving of 15% compared to standard rates.

Frequently Asked Questions
What services does the Swiss Red Cross offer to cross-border workers?
The Swiss Red Cross offers a wide range of services to cross-border workers, including educational support, social assistance, and integration activities. These services are essential to help cross-border workers face challenges related to double taxation and the management of health insurance.
How can I contact the Sottoceneri section of the Swiss Red Cross?
To contact the Sottoceneri section of the Swiss Red Cross, you can visit their official website or go to their headquarters in Lugano. You can also attend the events they organize to get direct information and support.
What are the implications of cuts to educational staff for cross-border workers?
Cuts to educational staff could lead to a decrease in the quality of services offered to cross-border workers, particularly in youth centers. This could affect the Swiss Red Cross's ability to support young cross-border workers in their integration and education journey.
