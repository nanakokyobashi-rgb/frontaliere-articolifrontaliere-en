Around 6 pm, on a hot summer evening, Misha and his team find themselves at the alpine maintenance center in Faido. Vehicles are prepared for the operation that allows the highway to be closed and work to start when the roadway is clear.

Closing before maintenance

The closure is the most delicate step: the cars continue to pass without slowing down, while the men have to go down to the street to place the cones and install the electronic signs that indicate the reduction in speed. Misha observes that drivers have less and less patience and that, during these operations, it is not uncommon to be insulted.

Once the carriageway has been diverted, both north and south, the security agents are in position to monitor the construction site. The highway becomes empty, desolate, and Misha and his team can finally work safely.

The work proceeds in small groups: some workers wash the walls of the tunnels with trucks equipped with large brushes, others intervene on the asphalt with specific vehicles, while a third team uses the water lance to clean, one at a time, fans and billboards.

The closure also allows you to replace the lights, fix the guardrails and check the manholes. It's a dangerous business, but Misha is very proud of the galleries he runs and can even see from home. He and his companions all come from Upper Ticino, and taking care of that stretch of highway is a source of pride for them. At dawn the cones are removed, the first cars resume their path and no one knows what happened during the night.

Five steps to recognize

Second step: close and signal. The men place the cones and install the electronic signs. In the scene described, their task consists of signaling the speed reduction while cars continue to pass. This is the most delicate point in the entire procedure.
