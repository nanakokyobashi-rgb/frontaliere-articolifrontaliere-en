Who are the border computer scientists in Ticino?

Border computer scientists in Ticino are workers who reside in Switzerland, but work in another country, generally Italy. This type of work is very common in Ticino, where the proximity to Italy and the presence of many technology companies make cross-border work easy.

How much does a border computer scientist earn in Ticino?

The total salary of a border computer scientist in Ticino may vary depending on his experience, the level of qualification and the type of work he carries out. However, according to 2022 data, the total salary of a border computer scientist in Ticino can be around CHF 80,000 per year.

The gross salary

The gross salary of a border computer technician in Ticino is about CHF 60,000 per year. This means that the gross salary is about 75% of the total salary.

Tax at source

Tax at source is only applied in Switzerland and not in Italy. This means that the border computer scientist in Ticino will not have to pay the source tax in Italy, but will have to pay the source tax in Switzerland.

Problem Summary

The border computer scientist in Ticino is a professional who works in Switzerland, but resides abroad. This creates tax complexity, as gross pay is subject to different tax rates. It is important to understand how tax at source works and how overall pay is calculated.

How source tax works

The tax at source is a tax that is applied directly to the gross salary of the border computer scientist. In Switzerland, source tax is only levied on gross pay, while in Italy, for example, source tax is also levied on net pay. In this scenario, the tax at source in Switzerland is 5%, while in Italy it is 10%.

Concrete example

Suppose that the border computer scientist in Ticino earns a gross salary of CHF 60'000 per year. In Switzerland, the tax at source is 5%, so the net pay will be CHF 57,000. If the border computer scientist in Ticino resided in Italy, the tax at source would be 10% on salary
