Gambarogno: works on the cantonal road, delays of up to 30 minutes

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 October 2026

From October 12 to 16, paving works on the cantonal road in Gambarogno, between the FFS underpass and the road uphill to Caviano - Scaiano. Delays of up to 30 minutes.

The announcement about the cantonal road

From Monday, October 12 to Friday, October 16, work is scheduled on the cantonal road in Gambarogno. The work involves paving the section between the FFS underpass and the road leading uphill to Caviano - Scaiano.

The location is indicated using two precise reference points. The FFS underpass identifies one end of the affected section; the road leading uphill to Caviano - Scaiano identifies the other. According to the announcement, the work planned for next week will take place between these two points.

The Municipality and the Department of the Territory are announcing the work. The two entities mention it in a notice accompanying the announcement of the work and the expected delays for road traffic. The consequence indicated for those traveling through the area is the possibility of delays of up to 30 minutes.

The traffic information accompanies the announcement of the work. It is therefore not only an indication of the location and dates: the notice also specifies that delays should be expected for road traffic during the stated period. The communicated duration is up to 30 minutes.

The announcement is defined by its essential elements: Gambarogno as the locality, the cantonal road as the infrastructure affected, the section between the FFS underpass and the road leading uphill to Caviano - Scaiano as the location of the work, the period from October 12 to 16 as the time window, and the expectation of delays of up to 30 minutes as the effect on traffic.
