The BancaStati Group closed the first half of 2026 with a net profit of CHF 47.6 million and net revenues of CHF 152.7 million.

Key facts

  • What: Semi-annual financial results 2026
  • When: Thursday
  • Where: Bellinzona
  • Who: BancaStat Group
  • Amount: Profit of 47.6 million

The half-yearly financial results of the BancaStat Group were released today, outlining an economic framework that the President of the General Management Fabrizio Cieslakiewicz defined as characterized by real satisfaction. The Group closed the first half of 2026 recording a profit that was substantially stable and amounted to CHF 47.6 million. This figure was reached within a macroeconomic context labelled as challenging, mainly due to the current level of benchmark rates which exerted tangible pressure on the bank's net revenues.

These net revenues stood at 152.7 million, showing a percentage decrease of 6.1% when compared to the same period of the previous year, i.e. 2025. Despite this percentage decrease, the top management of the institute wanted to clarify that these values remain at levels considered excellent if observed in a long-term historical perspective. The overall balance sheet shows the banking group's ability to react to changes

Operational details

A more in-depth analysis of the statements issued by senior management shows that the current situation of commercial rates and margins represents a constant challenge for operators in the banking and financial sector. According to Fabrizio Cieslakiewicz, President of the General Management, the results of the first six months of the year are certainly positive and demonstrate without a doubt the goodness of the business model adopted, even when faced with commercial margins reduced to minimum terms. The 152.7 million net revenues and 47.6 million net profit represent milestones for real satisfaction, confirming a historical trend in constant positive evolution, regardless of the exceptional and very recent years.

With regard to future months, the estimates outlined by the banking institution maintain a line of continuity with what has been observed so far. Except for unforeseen events, management believes that the performance of the year between now and the end of the year will not deviate significantly from the data that emerged in the first half of the year. This forecast is also based on the analysis of the general macroeconomic outlook, taking into account in particular the fact that no imminent changes in monetary policy are foreseen by the competent authorities.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the BancaStat Group's 2026 half-year profit?
The half-yearly profit recorded by the BancaStat Group during the first six months of 2026 amounted to CHF 47.6 million. This figure has been defined as substantially stable by management, despite a macroeconomic context labelled as challenging and characterised by the current level of benchmark rates that has put pressure on the institution's net revenues.
What is the trend in mortgage loans and assets under management?
In the half-year under review, the mortgage loans granted by the Group showed an increase of 1.9%, bringing the total volume to CHF 13.08 billion. Even more marked was the progression of assets under management, which increased by 4.1% to 25.07 billion, driven by net inflows of 554.4 million.
