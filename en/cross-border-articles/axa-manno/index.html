Axa Manno: insurance consultant for border workers In the context of cross-border work in Ticino, insurance management can become a complex undertaking. That's why Axa Manno offers specific advice and assistance for border workers, people who work and live in Switzerland but are originally from another country, such as Italy or France. Switzerland, and in particular the Canton of Ticino, is one of the regions most populated by frontier workers. According to data from the State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) in 2022, Ticino is home to more than 40,000 border workers, who represent about 15% of the Canton's population. This number is constantly growing, due to its strategic location and rapidly expanding economy. Axa Manno specialises in services for border workers, offering advice and assistance for insurance management. Border workers may need different insurance than those who live and work only in Switzerland. For example, they may need insurance against unemployment in Switzerland, or against death or disability in the event of a work-related accident. In addition, they may need insurance for their residence in Switzerland, such as home accident insurance. The legislation on insurance for border workers is regulated by the Federal Act on Social Insurance (LASS) of 1982, which has been amended several times over the years.
