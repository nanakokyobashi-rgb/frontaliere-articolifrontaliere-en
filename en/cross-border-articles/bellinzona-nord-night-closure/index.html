Bellinzona Nord, nighttime closure of the interchange

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 8 October 2026

Chiusura tra venerdì 9 e sabato 10 ottobre

Context

TL;DR

  • Closure between Friday, October 9 and Saturday, October 10
  • Stop from 20.00 to 5.00
  • Direction: Chiasso/San Gottardo
  • Alternatives: Bellinzona Centro 46 or Biasca 44

Key facts

  • What → Bellinzona Nord motorway entrance
  • Direction → Chiasso/San Gottardo
  • Time → from 20.00 to 5.00
  • Reason → resurfacing of the wearing course
  • Alternatives → Bellinzona Centro (no. 46) and Biasca (no. 44)
  • Authority → Federal Roads Office (USTRA)
  • Variable → weather conditions
  • Signposting → on site

On the night between Friday, October 9 and Saturday, October 10, the motorway entrance at the Bellinzona Nord interchange will remain closed to traffic in the direction of Chiasso/San Gottardo. The time window communicated by USTRA runs from 20.00 to 5.00.

The notice from the Federal Roads Office therefore concerns a specific access point and a specific direction. For a cross-border commuter who uses that entrance as part of their journey, the first information to note is the indicated overnight window, without extending it to other times not communicated.

Why it is being closed

The traffic suspension will serve to rehabilitate the wearing course of the interchange pavement. USTRA therefore attributes the measure to work on the road surface in the access area.

During the works, motorists will have the Bellinzona Centro (number 46) and Biasca (number 44) motorway interchanges as alternatives. The notice lists both possibilities, without assigning them an order of preference or specifying how long it takes to reach them.

The closure will be adequately signposted on site. USTRA urges road users to comply with the displayed signs and apologizes for any inconvenience.

Weather conditions could lead to changes to the work schedule. Anyone traveling through the area on the night in question will therefore have to consider the alternatives indicated and follow the signage present on site. The communication specifies the time window, direction, technical reason and the two alternative interchanges.

Operational details

The impact on the journey

The practical issue for anyone who must pass through the area during the indicated night is managing the closed access. The source offers two alternatives, but does not lay out a complete route. For this reason, the notice does not provide a preference, a distance, a travel time, or an estimate of the queues.

Choosing between the alternatives

A cross-border commuter whose route includes Bellinzona Nord can keep the two interchanges named by USTRA in reserve: Bellinzona Centro, number 46, and Biasca, number 44. The direction indicated in the notice is Chiasso/San Gottardo. However, based on the notice, it is not possible to establish which alternative is faster or better suited to each driver. Assigning a priority would mean adding information that was not provided.

The timing must also be read precisely. The closure is scheduled from 20.00 to 5.00 on the night between Friday, October 9 and Saturday, October 10. The notice does not describe any other time windows or quantify any potential disruption. For this reason, a commuter can plan around the stated time window without turning the announcement into a traffic estimate.

The weather could affect how the work is organized. According to USTRA, the work schedule may therefore change, without clarifying whether this would entail a postponement, bringing it forward, or a reformulation. Operationally, the instruction remains to follow the signage displayed on site.

For a cross-border commuter, separating confirmed facts from assumptions avoids presenting as official a route that was not indicated. The closure of the entrance, the time window, the direction, the reason for the work, and the two alternative interchanges are official. Practical preparation

Preparation for the journey can remain anchored to the information provided by USTRA. There is no need to add a route that the source does not indicate: it is enough to distinguish when access is closed, which direction is affected and which alternatives are named.

The operational sequence

  • Mark the night concerned: between Friday, October 9 and Saturday, October 10, from 20.00 to 5.00.
  • Check whether your access is Bellinzona Nord and whether the planned direction is Chiasso/San Gottardo.
  • Keep the Bellinzona Centro, number 46, and Biasca, number 44, interchanges available.

  • Consider the possibility of changes related to weather conditions.
