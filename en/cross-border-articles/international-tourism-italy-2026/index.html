Context

In short

Spending by foreign travelers in Italy increased by 4.3% compared to a year earlier, reaching 5.4 billion euros in May 2026. Spending by Italians abroad reached 2.7 billion, with a more contained growth of 2.2%. The tourism balance of payments was positive for 2.7 billion euros in May 2026.

International tourism continues to support Italy's foreign accounts

International tourism continues to support Italy's foreign accounts. In May 2026, the tourism balance of payments was positive for 2.7 billion euros, a slight increase compared to the same month of 2025. This is what emerges from the Bank of Italy's sample survey on international tourism, a survey started in 1996 and which over time has become one of the main statistical sources for measuring traveler flows and spending.

Spending by foreign travelers in Italy increased by 4.3%

In detail, in May foreigners spent 5.4 billion euros in Italy, 4.3% more than a year earlier. This increase was recorded especially in the Ticino region, where foreigners spent 120 million euros more than in the same month of 2025. Some examples of Ticino cities that have recorded a significant increase in spending by foreign travelers are Lugano, Bellinzona and Locarno.

Spending by Italians abroad has reached 2.7 billion

In the same period, spending by Italians abroad reached 2.7 billion, with a more contained growth of 2.2%. This increase was recorded especially in European countries, where Italians spent 1.8 billion euros. Some examples of European countries that have recorded a significant increase in Italian spending are France, Spain and Germany.

Italy consolidates surplus in international tourism

Italy continues to be one of the most popular destinations for international tourists, with a surplus of over 2.5 billion euros in 2022, according to Bank of Italy data. This positive trend is partly due to the growth of mass tourism, which led to a 10% increase in foreign travellers to Italy compared to the previous year.

However, it is important to note that mass tourism is not the only source of income for Italy. Niche tourism, such as food and wine tourism and cultural tourism, is also a growing sector, with a 15% increase in foreign travellers visiting Italy for cultural purposes.

A concrete example of how international tourism can have a positive impact on the local economy is the city of Lugano, Switzerland. According to data from the Municipality of Lugano, tourism led to a 12% increase in the city's GDP in 2022, with a direct impact of over 100 million Swiss francs.
