Cocquio Trevisago: unauthorized gathering on via Appennini

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 5 October 2026

Sunday, October 4, around twenty motorcycles and three cars took over via Appennini for speed races and wheelies. Carabinieri officers from Besozzo intervened.

The trials on Via Appennini

Around twenty motorcycles and three cars gathered on Sunday, October 4, on Via Appennini, in Cocquio Trevisago, for an unauthorized gathering. The gathering included various trials, with specific activities for motorcyclists and car drivers.

The motorcycles took part in timed wheelie contests. The cars, on the other hand, competed in speed and braking trials. For these maneuvers, the drivers used the handbrake to make quick U-turns and immediately resume the race. The reconstruction therefore distinguishes between two modes: on the one hand, timed wheelies, and on the other, driving trials based on speed, braking and quick U-turns.

The source describes the gathering as unauthorized. The presence of the vehicles on Via Appennini was connected to the contests described. There were three cars involved, while there were around twenty motorcycles, according to the reconstruction of the incident.

Some residents and condominium residents in the area raised the alarm. The citizens called 112 and reported the situation to the carabinieri from Besozzo Station. The report came while the trials were underway and led to the immediate intervention of law enforcement. The first documented step was therefore the residents' call, followed by the arrival of the carabinieri in the Via Appennini area.

The text places the gathering, the motorcycle and car maneuvers, the residents' alarm and the intervention of Besozzo Station in this sequence. The initial phase of the reconstruction focuses on the incident and the immediate response to the report.

Operational details

The local context

For residents and condominium owners, what happened on via Appennini adds to incidents already observed. The source reports that the previous Sunday some residents had witnessed similar scenes. When the gathering took place again, the call to 112 therefore came from people who had already seen the same kind of behavior in the area.

The issue highlighted by residents concerns not only motorcycle and car maneuvers. In the same area, more abandoned waste and cars left there were found, described in the source as car wrecks. The deterioration is associated with a road that has to coexist with these elements and with the repeated reports.

Reports about the waste

The waste that was found was probably dumped during the weekend of September 26 and 27. Here too, the source uses cautious wording: it indicates a probable period, without attributing the dumping to a specific person or group. A similar situation had been reported in mid-September.

The sequence of time references is the one reported in the account: a similar report in mid-September, then the weekend of September 26 and 27 indicated as the possible time when the waste was dumped, followed by the new report linked to the gathering on Sunday, October 4. It is the residents of the area who, through their calls and their mobilization, link the condition of the road to the incidents observed.

A private road for public use

Via Appennini is described as a private road for public use. The phrase sums up the local issue described by the source: the space is private, but used by the public, and has to coexist with waste and car wrecks.

The area's 54 «condominium owners» mobilized, asking the institutions for solutions and action. The source does not indicate what measures were decided, but documents the collective request and the repeated reports about the area's deterioration.

Key points

The aftermath of the intervention

The Carabinieri's check had different outcomes among the participants. One young man managed to escape. The first hypothesis reported is that he may have avoided the check because he had no documents, no insurance, or a modified motorcycle. These possibilities are indicated as initial hypotheses and not as definitive findings.

For the others present, however, the check ended with them being stopped and identified through their documents. The reconstruction therefore distinguishes the position of the young man who managed to escape from that of the other participants, who were stopped at the scene. The outcome described concerns the document check for the people who were stopped and one young man's escape from the check.

The request of the 54 condominium residents

Alongside the checks on the participants, the report records the mobilization of the area's 54 “condominium residents.” The residents are asking the institutions for solutions and action regarding the situation reported on Via Appennini. The request concerns a private road for public use, where the source reports the presence of car wrecks, abandoned waste, and new gatherings.

The mobilization of the condominium residents gives the aftermath a collective dimension. It is not merely a matter of the check carried out after the call to 112: the report also refers to a request addressed to the institutions by the 54 condominium residents. The documented aftermath therefore consists of an intervention by the Carabinieri with different outcomes for the participants and a request for solutions for the area. The source places both elements in connection with the situation described on Via Appennini.

Frequently Asked Questions
What happened in Via Apennini in Cocquio Trevisago?
On Sunday, October 4, an unauthorized rally was held that saw the participation of about twenty motorcycles and three cars. The participants gave rise to dangerous manoeuvres, including timed soaring races for motorcycles and speed and braking tests with the use of handbrakes for cars. The situation was reported by residents and condominiums in the area, who requested the immediate intervention of the police of Besozzo Station.
What were the results of the intervention of the carabinieri?
Upon the arrival of the military, most of the participants were stopped and subjected to identification through document control. A young man, however, managed to escape control. The authorities hypothesize that the escape may have been dictated by lack of documents, absence of insurance or the presence of irregular changes to the vehicle, although these are at the time of assumptions not yet confirmed by definitive investigations.
What is the current situation in Via Apennini reported by the condominiums?
The 54 condominiums in the area, which have mobilised the institutions, have long denounced the deterioration of the area, described as a private road for public use. In addition to unauthorized gatherings, citizens report the presence of abandoned waste, probably dating back to the weekend of September 26 and 27, and car carcasses. The local community urgently requires concrete interventions to restore legality and decorum along the way, underlining the frequent repetition of such episodes.
