Varese: conference on renewable energy communities (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 September 2026

The Varese Chamber of Commerce is organising a conference on Renewable Energy Communities for businesses, local authorities and private citizens.

  • Initiative with Malpensa Insubria CER Association

Key facts

  • What: conference on Renewable Energy Communities for businesses, local authorities, and private individuals
  • When: event date not specified in the source
  • Where: organized by the Varese Chamber of Commerce
  • Who: Varese Chamber of Commerce and Malpensa Insubria CER Association
  • Theme: production, consumption, and sharing of clean energy
  • Objective: transforming sustainability into a concrete resource for the territory
  • Initiative: presentation of a new Chamber of Commerce tender to support photovoltaic plants intended for entry into CER

The Varese Chamber of Commerce is promoting a meeting dedicated to Renewable Energy Communities, the so-called CER, designed to illustrate how businesses, local authorities, and private citizens can produce, consume, and share clean energy in the territory. The event arises from the collaboration between the Varese Chamber of Commerce and the Malpensa Insubria CER Association, with the declared objective of transforming environmental sustainability into a concrete resource for the economic and social system of the territory.

An initiative to reduce the impact of energy costs

The initiative aims to provide practical answers to those who intend to reduce the impact of energy costs through the use of renewable sources. According to the Chamber of Commerce, being part of a CER allows access to more efficient resource management, increasing the competitiveness of small and medium-sized enterprises and improving the sustainability of public services offered by local authorities.

Operational details

The conference organized by the Chamber of Commerce of Varese addresses a topic that, while originating in the economic context of Varese, has some points of contact with the daily lives of workers who cross the border between Italy and the Canton Ticino. The reflection on energy self-sufficiency and protection from price fluctuations echoes a concern shared by many cross-border families, who are always attentive to the trends in their utility bills and fixed costs. Why the energy theme is also of interest to cross-border workers The source highlights how energy self-sufficiency is a fundamental factor in protecting businesses and families from price fluctuations generated by the international geopolitical context. This statement has practical implications for those who work in the Canton Ticino and live in the province of Varese or in the Italian border area: the possibility of participating in a CER on the Italian side could offer an additional tool for managing domestic energy consumption, even while operating in a system — the Swiss one — characterized by different tariff dynamics. For workers who cross the border daily — from Ponte Tresa to Chiasso, from Brogeda to Gaggiolo — household energy expenses represent a significant portion of the monthly budget, especially during periods of tariff increases. The prospect of collective energy management, shared on the model of CERs, fits into this framework as a potential tool for cost containment. The new Chamber of Commerce tender: what is known and what is missing The new tender promoted by the Chamber of Commerce of Varese aims to financially support the realization of photovoltaic systems intended for entry into CERs, offering direct financial aid to those who choose to invest in solar energy. However, the source does not specify the amount of the contributions, the access requirements, or the deadlines for submitting applications. For cross-border workers residing in the Varesotto who wish to evaluate this opportunity, it will therefore be necessary to wait for the official publication of the tender. A significant aspect concerns the potential audience: the Chamber of Commerce of Varese groups together businesses and economic operators in the provincial territory, and among these are also workers who operate in the Canton Ticino with a G permit. The fact that the tender aims to support those who invest in solar energy to enter a CER suggests an openness to the involvement of different categories of subjects, including small and medium-sized enterprises — a segment in which a significant portion of cross-border workers operate. Network logic and diffuse value on the territory The source emphasizes that joining a CER means moving beyond the traditional consumption model to embrace a network logic. This vision — sharing the energy produced on the territory to generate a diffuse value — recalls the models of cross-border cooperation that have characterized for years the economic relationship between the Canton Ticino and the Italian border provinces. Although they are distinct areas (energy vs. taxation/labor), the principle of local-scale collaboration remains a common denominator.

Key points

For readers of Frontaliere Ticino who wish to delve into the topic of Renewable Energy Communities starting from the news of the Varese conference, it is important to distinguish between what the source announces and what will need to be verified with the competent authorities. The source does not provide operational details on the Chamber's tender — amounts, deadlines, requirements — nor on the procedure for joining the CER for citizens and businesses in Varese. ### Hypothetical scenario: what a border worker residing in Varese might consider A worker who returns every evening from the Canton Ticino and resides in the province of Varese could, hypothetically, consider participating in a CER as a tool to reduce the energy costs of their home. The source indicates that CERs are open to local authorities, businesses, and private citizens, and that the Chamber's tender supports those who install photovoltaic systems to join these communities. To verify the actual applicability of this opportunity to their case — including the cadastral requirements of the property, the ownership of the utility, and the availability of a suitable system — it will be necessary to wait for the publication of the tender and contact the Chamber of Commerce of Varese directly. ### Useful tools on the site In terms of personal economic planning, for a border worker who wants to understand the impact of energy expenses on the overall family budget, it may be useful to review their payslip and the net available. The payslip simulation tool allows you to calculate what is actually available each month, item by item, including Swiss pension deductions such as AVS and LPP. For those who want to evaluate the overall cost of living between the Canton Ticino and Varese, the cost of living comparator provides an overview of the differences in daily expenses, subscriptions, and services. Two complementary tools that help to include the energy theme in a broader vision of family economic sustainability. ### Suggested next steps While waiting for the official details of the Varese Chamber of Commerce tender, the concrete steps that an interested border worker can take are essentially three: monitor the publication of the tender on the official channels of the Chamber, evaluate the technical feasibility of a photovoltaic system on their home by consulting a qualified installer, and — in the meantime — keep track of their Swiss pension and insurance situation, including LAMal coverage and AVS contribution payments. For an overall view of their net salary and deductions, the border worker salary calculator remains the reference tool for navigating the items on the Swiss payslip.
