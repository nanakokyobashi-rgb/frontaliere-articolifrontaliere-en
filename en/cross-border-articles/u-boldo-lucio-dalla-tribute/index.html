Uboldo: Summer 2026 Departs with "Hi Lucio" (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 26 June 2026

On June 27, Falcone e Borsellino Park in Uboldo will host a tribute to Lucio Dalla with the free concert of the group "Volpi senza Tana", opening the "Summer Air" exhibition.

Context

In short - On June 27 in Uboldo "Summer Air" opens with a tribute to Lucio Dalla - The free concert is in the Falcone and Borsellino Park - The group "Volpi senza Tana" will perform the artist's most beloved songs ## Key facts - What : Opening of the summer festival with a concert dedicated to Lucio Dalla - When: Saturday, June 27 at 9 pm - Where: Falcone and Borsellino Park in Uboldo - Who: Municipality of Uboldo, group "Volpi senza Tana" - Amount: Free admission The Municipality of Uboldo has announced that Saturday, June 27 at 9 pm in Falcone and Borsellino Park in via A. M. Ceriani 21 will hold the inaugural event of the summer festival "Aria d 'Este". The evening will be dedicated to Lucio Dalla, one of the greatest Italian songwriters, with a concert entitled "Ciao Lucio". The concert, with free admission, will see the performance of the musical group "Volpi senza Tana", which will offer some of Dalla's most beloved songs. The choice of a tribute to the Bolognese singer-songwriter aims to involve the different generations, recalling the atmospheres and emotions of his songs. The event is part of a summer billboard that the Municipality of Uboldo organizes every year, offering music, entertainment and moments of aggregation in the municipal park. Operational details

Practical analysis and implications for border workers For border workers who live in Italy and work in Switzerland, as in the Canton of Ticino, events such as that of Uboldo represent an opportunity for aggregation and leisure near the border. Participation in free concerts allows you to experience the summer in a more complete way, without having to go too far from your place of residence. From a logistical point of view, the proximity between Uboldo and the passes of Brogeda and Gaggiolo makes it easy to reach the park for those who reside in neighbouring areas, even by public transport or private cars. For those who work in the Canton of Ticino, participation in this type of event can also represent an opportunity to socialise with Italian colleagues or friends, strengthening a sense of community and local belonging. In addition, cultural events like this can help improve the quality of life of border crossers, offering moments of relaxation and culture without having to face long journeys. From a practical point of view, it is important to remember that participation in summer public events may be subject to restrictions in case of bad weather, and that free access implies the possibility of arriving early to find better places. For frontier workers, the end of work times and the possibility of using public transport in compliance with the service time slots are aspects to be considered. How to participate and what to expect To participate in the event on 27 June, just go to the Falcone and Borsellino Park in Uboldo by 9 pm. Admission is free and no tickets or advance reservations are required. It is advisable to arrive a little early, especially in bad weather, to ensure a good place. For those who wish to learn more about the activities of "Aria d 'Este", you can consult the official website of the Municipality of Uboldo or follow the social pages of the institution for updates. The evening will be enlivened by the music of "Volpi senza Tana", a group that has already enjoyed success on other occasions, and which will perform a repertoire dedicated to Lucio Dalla. The line-up will include some of the artist's most iconic hits, such as "Caruso", "Piazza Grande" and "Attenti al lupo". As the first event of "Aria d 'Este", the event is part of a framework of initiatives that aims to strengthen the sense of community through culture and music.
