Practical Implications for Frontiersmen

Tax Exemption

One of the main innovations concerns the tax exemption, which has been increased to €10,000 for new frontier workers. Old frontier workers, i.e. those who were already such before 17 July 2023, benefit from a transitional regime until 2033 with a deductible of €7,500. This change allows frontier workers to keep a larger part of their net salary.

Double Taxation Convention

The Italy-Switzerland Double Taxation Convention, signed on 9 December 1976, ensures that tax at source is withheld only in Switzerland. This avoids double taxation thanks to the Italian tax credit, allowing border workers not to pay taxes twice on the same income.

Practical Actions for Cross-Border Commuters

For cross-border commuters who wish to work as warehouse workers in logistics in Ticino, it is important to follow some practical procedures to ensure a smooth transition.

Procedure for Permit G

Cross-border commuters must obtain a G permit to work in Switzerland. This permit allows you to work in Switzerland and reside in Italy, giving you the flexibility to reconcile work and private life.

Tax Deadlines

It is important to meet tax deadlines in both Switzerland and Italy. In Switzerland, withholding tax is withheld directly from the paycheck, while in Italy it is necessary to submit the tax return within the established deadlines.

Health insurance

Cross-border commuters with a G permit must choose the health insurance (KVG) that best suits their needs. You can opt for deductibles ranging between CHF 300 and CHF 2500 for adults.
