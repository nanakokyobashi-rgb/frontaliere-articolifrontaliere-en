Salary ticket: the proposal to buy in Ticino (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 25 September 2026

Ticket-salary in Ticino: the Young People of the Centre propose to allocate up to 5% of the salary to local purchases, with a tax voucher of 25%.

Operational details

What would change in practice

For the cross-border worker, the proposal links two distinct elements: a voluntary portion of the salary and a possible tax benefit connected to local spending. The ticket could amount to up to 5% of the salary, while the tax voucher would correspond to 25% of the salary ticket. However, the text indicates neither the amount of a reference salary nor the actual value of the voucher in francs. It is therefore not possible to calculate the individual benefit based solely on the percentages.

The situation described by the source starts from a choice based on one’s wallet. Many consumers shop in Italy or online because health insurance premiums and rents weigh on purchasing power. The proposed system would introduce an incentive linked to companies that are tax-resident in the area. For those working in Ticino, the practical convenience would therefore depend on the available offering and on how the ticket could be used.

The issue for those living across the border

The source does not clarify whether the system would be open to every cross-border worker or how it would connect to the G permit, withholding tax or double taxation. Nor does it specify any possible effects on tax revenue sharing, AVS, LPP, LAMal or INPS. These elements cannot be derived from the proposal presented and remain open questions for those living in Italy and working in Canton Ticino.

The relationship between tax relief and public revenue also remains to be verified. The promoters foresee lower tax revenues for the state, but hypothesize higher revenue from small and medium-sized enterprises if local spending were to increase. Parliament will therefore have to assess the idea as a whole, together with economic freedom and the ability of supply to support demand.

Key points

Before considering participation

The source does not yet indicate a form, a deadline, a registration procedure, or a list of affiliated shops. The reason is the status of the proposal: it is a generic parliamentary initiative, and it is now up to Parliament to determine if it is valid and compliant with laws and the principle of economic freedom.

Three checks to keep separate

1. Proposal status. Before considering the ticket as a concrete possibility, it is necessary to distinguish the announcement by the 'Giovani del Centro' from an approved measure. No effective date is indicated in the text.

2. Circuit rules. The proposal must clarify which companies fiscally resident in the territory may participate and in which environment they operate. The fact that the ticket is intended for local activities does not equate to an already available list.

3. Tax treatment. The source mentions a tax voucher on 25% of the salary-ticket, but does not explain how the advantage would be applied. For a cross-border worker, it remains to be verified, once defined, the relationship with the G permit, withholding tax, and double taxation.

The same caution applies to purchases. The text indicates that the ticket would be usable at companies fiscally resident in the territory; it does not say it could be spent in Italy or online. Neither are the stickers described, except as a possible advertising signal outside the shops, nor the methods by which a customer could recognize participating activities.

Frequently Asked Questions
What do the Youth of the Centre propose?
They propose a voluntary cantonal system that allows you to allocate up to 5% of your salary to a ticket to be used by companies tax resident in the territory. The objective indicated in the source is to support local consumption, in a situation where purchasing power is eroded and many purchases are made in Italy or online.
What tax advantage does the ticket-wage provide?
President Giovanna Pedroni explains that those who join would receive a tax voucher on 25% of the ticket-salary. According to the proposal, this should allow you to pay slightly less tax. However, the source does not indicate the actual amount of the discount or describe how it would be applied in the paycheck.
Who can use the ticket?
The proposal does not yet fully define either the audience of members or the list of companies. Vice-President Luca Taglialatela points out that the type of companies and the environment in which they operate will have to be detailed in the future, when the initiative will be further analysed. The ticket would be usable at companies tax resident in the territory.
Is the measure already in place?
No. The source presents a generic parliamentary initiative elaborated by the Youth of the Centre. It is now up to Parliament to assess whether the idea is valid, whether it respects the laws and whether it is compatible with the principle of economic freedom. The text does not indicate a date of entry into force, an accession procedure or operational deadlines.
