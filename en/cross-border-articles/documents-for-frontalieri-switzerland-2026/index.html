To start working in Switzerland as a cross-border worker, it is essential to have all the documents in order. The first step is to obtain the G permit, which allows you to work in Canton Ticino. This permit is essential to sign a work contract with a Swiss company. In addition to the G permit, you need a signed work contract that specifies the employment conditions and salary. Another crucial document is health insurance, mandatory for all workers in Switzerland. It is important to choose an insurance that covers medical expenses and is recognized by the Swiss authorities. Furthermore, cross-border workers must register with the AVS, the Swiss pension system, and open a bank account in Switzerland to receive their salary. These steps are fundamental to ensure a smooth and legal transition into the Swiss job market.

Main documents

Among the main documents required are the G permit, the work contract, health insurance, and AVS registration. The G permit is issued by the Swiss authorities and allows you to work in Canton Ticino. The work contract must be signed by both parties and must include all employment conditions, such as working hours, salary, and benefits. Health insurance is mandatory and must be taken out before starting work. AVS registration is necessary to ensure pension coverage and must be done within one month of starting work.

Procedures and deadlines

The procedures to obtain the necessary documents may vary depending on the individual situation. It is important to follow the deadlines indicated by the Swiss authorities to avoid legal problems. For example, the G permit must be requested before starting work, while health insurance must be taken out within one month of starting work. AVS registration must be done within one month of starting work. Opening a bank account in Switzerland can be done at any time, but it is advisable to do so before starting work to facilitate salary payment.
