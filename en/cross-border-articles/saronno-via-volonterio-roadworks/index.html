Saronno, roadworks on Via Volonterio: traffic changes (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 26 September 2026

Starting Monday, September 28, sidewalk work on Via Volonterio: no parking, one-way traffic toward Via Varese, and detours until October 16.

Context

In brief

  • Work begins on Monday 28 settembre
  • Roadworks until 16 ottobre
  • Parking prohibited on both sides
  • One-way traffic towards via Varese

Key facts

  • Where: via Volonterio in Saronno
  • Works: sidewalk reconstruction
  • Hours: from 8.30 to 18
  • Expected duration: about two weeks
  • Expected completion: 16 ottobre
  • Prohibition: parking with forced removal
  • Direction of travel: towards via Varese

From Monday 28 settembre, via Volonterio in Saronno will be affected by roadworks to reconstruct the sidewalks. The notice comes from the Comune di Saronno, which specifies working hours from 8.30 to 18 and an expected duration of about two weeks, with completion of the works scheduled for 16 ottobre.

The construction site will cover the section between the roundabout on via Pagani and via Vittime del Lavoro. In this section, parking will be prohibited on both sides of the road, with forced removal in effect. The carriageway will also be narrowed to allow the works to be carried out.

The affected section and the initial changes

For those travelling along via Volonterio from the city centre, one-way traffic towards via Varese will be introduced in the section affected by the works. The change will coincide with the presence of the construction area and the reduction of the space available on the carriageway.

The Comune also reports a second restriction, concerning those arriving from the overpass. Once they reach the roundabout at the intersection with via Pagani Grossi and viale Prealpi, they will be prohibited from continuing along via Volonterio towards the Hospital. Traffic will therefore be diverted onto the aforementioned streets.

The measure therefore concerns two distinct situations: traffic through the section of via Volonterio between the roundabout on via Pagani and via Vittime del Lavoro, and access towards the Hospital for those coming from the overpass. The restrictions are connected to the sidewalk reconstruction works and are scheduled during the period indicated in the municipal notice. For those who also need to check other parts of their route, mappa dei valichi is available, while the source concerns exclusively the changes in Saronno.

Operational details

What changes for those passing through Saronno

The most immediate change concerns the possibility of stopping along via Volonterio. In the construction-site section, stopping will be prohibited on both sides, and vehicles left on the road may be removed. The restriction affects the entire section indicated by the Comune, from the roundabout at via Pagani to via Vittime del Lavoro.

For those arriving from the city centre, traffic will not be maintained in both directions: one-way traffic will direct the route towards via Varese. Those coming from the overpass, however, will have to consider the prohibition on travelling towards the Hospital once they reach the roundabout between via Pagani Grossi and viale Prealpi. At that point, diversions are planned along the streets indicated in the notice.

How to organize the passage

The practical procedure begins by identifying the destination. Those traveling along via Volonterio from the city center must consider the one-way system toward via Varese and the narrowed carriageway in the section between the roundabout at via Pagani and via Vittime del Lavoro. Those arriving from the overpass must instead pay attention to the roundabout with via Pagani Grossi and viale Prealpi, because from there it will not be permitted to continue along via Volonterio toward the Hospital.

Frequently Asked Questions
When do the works start in via Volonterio in Saronno?
Work on the refurbishment of the sidewalks begins on Monday, September 28. The Municipality of Saronno indicates a working time from 8.30 am to 6 pm and an expected duration of about two weeks. The presumed end date of the works is 16 October.
Where will parking be prohibited?
The parking ban will concern both sides of Via Volonterio in the section between the roundabout of Via Pagani and Via Vittime del Lavoro. The prohibition will be applied with a forced removal regime, while the roadway will be restricted due to the presence of the construction site area.
What changes for those arriving from the overpass?
Those who travel via Volonterio coming from the overpass, once they reach the roundabout at the intersection with via Pagani Grossi and viale Prealpi, will not be able to continue on via Volonterio towards the Hospital. The municipal notice provides for detours in the aforementioned routes.
In which direction will the one-way be established?
For those who travel via Volonterio coming from the city centre, in the section affected by the works, the single direction of travel in the direction of via Varese will be established. The measure is connected to the narrowing of the roadway necessary for the remaking of the sidewalks.
