Cornaredo: a billion promises and a million cubic meters of concrete (cross-border guide)

Discussions on the revival of the Cornaredo area in Lugano, with criticism of extreme densification and unkept promises

Key facts

  • What: Discussions on the revival of the Cornaredo area
  • When: Over 20 years after the plan's inception
  • Where: Lugano, Canton Ticino
  • Who: Municipal councilors from various parties
  • Amount: 1 billion francs invested

The Cornaredo area in Lugano is at the center of heated discussions. In an interpellation to the Municipality, municipal councilors Sara Beretta Piccoli (PVL), Carlo Zoppi (PS), Tamara Merlo (Più Donne), Luisella Petrini Fumasoli (Avanti con Ticino e Lavoro), and Angelo Petralli (Il Centro) expressed concerns about the development of the new neighborhood. Over 20 years after the plan's inception, the current scenario is radically different from the idyllic visions of the original Masterplan. The interlocutors deplore extreme densification, with private building complexes perceived as exaggerated and out of context. A glaring example is the 15-story tower, approximately 45 meters high, under construction near the Vedeggio-Cassarate gallery, a symbol of forced verticalization that seems to respond more to speculative profit logic than to real quality housing needs.

The rejection of the Cassarate roundabout by the Federal Court has worsened the situation. This crucial junction was fundamental for the sustainable mobility of the project, but without it, the promises of protected bike lanes and safe pedestrian connections have remained on paper. Vehicle traffic continues to congest the area without effective relief valves.

Operational details

Analysis of Unfulfilled Promises

The promises made at the time of the construction application for the 'Parco Cornaredo' project have not been kept. Fundamental sports facilities, such as the underground private ice rink and the underground shooting range for the Civici Carabinieri Lugano, have not been realized. This has raised suspicions that these promises were used only to obtain facilitated building permits and to soften the impact of massive construction.

Impact on Sustainable Mobility

The rejection of the large roundabout on the Cassarate by the Federal Court has had a significant impact on the sustainable mobility of the Cornaredo area. Without this crucial junction, the promises of protected bike lanes and safe pedestrian connections have remained on paper. Vehicle traffic continues to congest the area without effective relief valves, making it more difficult for cross-border commuters to move around the zone.

Urban Planning Logic and Extreme Densification

The urban planning logic that justifies the authorization of towers up to 15 floors in an area lacking a definitive road layout and public services proportional to such density has been called into question. The questioners ask the Municipality to clarify this logic and explain how it intends to recover the vision of sustainable mobility now that the large roundabout has been rejected.

Key points

What cross-border workers should do

Cross-border workers who work in Lugano and reside in Italy should be aware of the implications of the redevelopment of the Cornaredo area. It is important to monitor the Municipality's decisions regarding sustainable mobility and the promised infrastructures. If the infrastructures are not implemented, cross-border workers may face greater difficulties in reaching their workplaces and in their overall quality of life.

Procedure to obtain information

To obtain updated information on the status of the Cornaredo area and the Municipality's decisions, cross-border workers can directly contact the competent municipal office. It is also possible to participate in public meetings and consultations to express their concerns and requests.

Conclusion

The discussions on the redevelopment of the Cornaredo area have a significant impact on cross-border workers in Lugano. It is important to monitor the Municipality's decisions and actively participate in public consultations to express their concerns and requests. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the main criticisms of the revival of the Cornaredo sector?
The main criticisms concern extreme densification, the lack of adequate infrastructure, and unfulfilled promises, such as the realization of sports facilities and sustainable mobility.

The rejection of the large roundabout has had a significant impact on the sustainable mobility of the Cornaredo sector, making it more difficult to implement protected bike lanes and safe pedestrian connections.
What do the interpellants ask the Municipality?
The interpellants ask the Municipality to clarify the investments planned in the Lugano Transport Plan, the authorization of towers up to 15 floors, and the quality of life in the neighborhoods adjacent to the Vedeggio-Cassarate exit.
