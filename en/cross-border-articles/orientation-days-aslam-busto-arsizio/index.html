Aslam Orientation Days for young migrants start in Busto Arsizio (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 26 June 2026

At the Malpensa Fair in Busto Arsizio, the first event of the BRIDGE project to train 300 young migrants in 4 years, responding to the demand for skilled labour.

Context

In short - The Orientation Days of Aslam in Busto Arsizio have started. - Four days to present free courses. - Objective: to train 300 students in 4 years. ## Key facts - What: Aslam's Orientation Days have opened. - When: Thursday, June 25. - Where: MalpensaFiere, Busto Arsizio. - Who: Promoted by ASLAM. - Amount: Not yet specified. The BRIDGE project – Building Resources for Integration, Development, Guidance and Empowerment – was launched to respond to the growing demand for skilled labour in the local manufacturing and industrial sectors. The estimated shortage of employees exceeds 50,000 units. The initiative aims to train 300 young migrants between the ages of 18 and 35 in four years, with free courses that include 750 hours of training between classes and internships. The goal is to create a direct link between training and job placement. Thursday's event illustrated the training offer, the teaching methodologies and evaluated the attitudes and skills of the participants. Courses were presented in areas such as mechanics, welding, plant engineering, wood, leather goods and self-repair, with a part dedicated to soft skills, safety and civic education. The project involves collaborations between ASLAM, Varese Chamber of Commerce, Employment Centres, CPIA and other third sector and reception bodies. Operational details

Analysis of the practical implications For young Italian migrants residing in the territory, this initiative represents a concrete opportunity for integration into the local labour market, particularly in technical and industrial sectors where the demand for qualified personnel exceeds the supply. The free training and internship mode in the company allow you to acquire skills that can be spent immediately. Compared to the previous situation, this initiative can facilitate the recognition of EQF level 3 professional qualifications, which could be positively evaluated by companies in Switzerland and Italy. The possibility of participating in 750-hour courses with theoretical and practical training, and the presence of modules on soft skills and security, improve the prospects for the insertion of young migrants in the industrial sector. For border workers already working in the Canton of Ticino, this initiative can represent a path of retraining or specialization, even if the source does not specify an official recognition in Switzerland. However, the link between labour demand and training is clear and can help reduce the estimated shortage of over 50,000 workers in the sector. An important element is the collaboration between public and private bodies, which guarantees a training offer updated to the needs of the market. Key points

What to do and how to participate For young migrants interested in the courses, the first action is to wait for the next dates of the information meetings, such as those of 2 July in Lentate sul Seveso and 14-15 July in Milan Bovisa. It is important to check the official channels of ASLAM and partner associations for how to sign up and requirements. The participation path includes: 1. Submit an application for registration, verify compliance with the age requirements and basic knowledge of the Italian language. 2. Participate in skills and competency assessment sessions. 3. If selected, enroll in 750-hour courses, which include classroom classes and internships. 4. Follow the modules on soft skills, workplace safety and civic education. For those who already work or are in Italy, it is advisable to contact local employment centres or support associations to check the compatibility of the courses with your needs and for any awards. To go deeper and plan your journey, you can use the site's calculator to estimate the economic benefits of vocational training. We remind you that the opportunity is free and aimed at young people between 18 and 35 years old, with basic knowledge of the Italian language. In addition, we recommend that you follow the updates on upcoming meetings and consult the ASLAM website for detailed information on registrations, requirements and how to participate.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the main objective of Aslam Orientation Days?
