Cariplo Foundation: 5 million for 48 Varese projects in 2025 (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 May 2026

The Cariplo Foundation has approved the 2025 budget, allocating nearly 5 million euros to 48 projects in the province of Varese.

Context

In brief

  • Fondazione Cariplo approves 2025 budget with 187.2 million euros
  • 48 projects funded in Varese for over 4.9 million euros
  • Contributions in culture, social, research, and environment

Key facts

  • What: Approval of the 2025 budget
  • When: 2025
  • Where: Province of Varese
  • Who: Fondazione Cariplo
  • Amount: 4.9 million euros
  • Projects: 48

In 2025, Fondazione Cariplo distributed over 187.2 million euros to support 1,265 projects, an increase from the 164.5 million euros in 2024. On the financial side, the year ended with a surplus of 353.9 million euros and a market value of 13.049 billion euros as of December 31, 2025. The province of Varese was awarded 48 grants totaling over 4.9 million euros, distributed among art and culture (20 grants, 1.1 million), personal services (14 grants, 1.4 million), scientific research (2 grants, 0.2 million), and environment (3 grants, 0.1 million). These are added to the so-called cross-cutting challenges with 9 grants for approximately 2.1 million euros.

Operational details

Analysis of Funded Sectors

The contributions from Fondazione Cariplo in Varese have been distributed across various sectors, each with a specific impact on the territory and the community. In the cultural sector, the 20 grants totaling 1.1 million euros aim to enhance the artistic and cultural heritage of the province, offering new opportunities for growth and development. The 14 grants totaling 1.4 million euros in the personal services sector are intended to improve the quality of life and support local communities. The 2 grants totaling 0.2 million euros in scientific research support innovative projects that can have a significant impact at both the local and national levels. Finally, the 3 grants totaling 0.1 million euros in the environmental sector aim to promote sustainability and the protection of the territory.

Cross-cutting Challenges

Cross-cutting challenges, with 9 grants totaling approximately 2.1 million euros, include projects that span multiple sectors and have a broader impact on the community. These projects include initiatives to strengthen local communities, contributions to Community Foundations, and emblematic projects that can have a lasting positive effect on the territory. Cross-cutting challenges are a key element of Fondazione Cariplo's strategy, which aims to create a positive and sustainable impact across all sectors.

Key points

What to do to participate in the projects

For cross-border workers interested in participating in projects funded by the Fondazione Cariplo, it is important to stay updated on the available opportunities. You can consult the Fondazione Cariplo website for information on calls for proposals and ongoing projects. Additionally, it is useful to participate in local initiatives and activities promoted by the Community Foundations, which can offer new opportunities for growth and development.

Procedures to access the contributions

To access the contributions from Fondazione Cariplo, it is necessary to submit an application to participate in the specific calls for proposals. Applications must be submitted by the indicated deadlines and must meet the required criteria. It is important to carefully read the calls for proposals and follow the instructions provided to increase the chances of success. Additionally, it is useful to consult the websites of the local Community Foundations for information on further funding opportunities.
