Traffic on the A2: slowdowns and queues towards Ticino (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 6 July 2026

July brings queues on the A2 motorway. Let's analyse the waiting times at the Gotthard, Airolo and Chiasso Brogeda border for frontier workers.

Context

In short - Queues up to 8 km to the Gotthard heading south. - 40-minute waits for those entering Ticino. - Slowdowns at the Chiasso Brogeda customs office. - Road works between Lugano Nord and Collina d 'Oro. ## Key facts - What: Highway slowdowns on the A2 and customs lines. - When: Month of July, morning and noon surveys. - Where: A2 San Gottardo, Airolo, Chiasso Brogeda, Lugano Nord. - Who: Road users and border commuters. - Amount: 10 minutes of average wait at the Chiasso border. The month of July is confirmed to be a critical period for road traffic along the main artery of the Canton of Ticino. At 10 am, traffic monitors were already reporting 8 kilometres of queue at the Gotthard tunnel, heading south. As the hours passed, the situation changed: at noon, the column had shrunk to 4 kilometers, resulting in waiting times estimated at about 40 minutes for those heading south. At the same time, northbound traffic recorded queues of 3 kilometers in the section between Quinto and Airolo, with delays of about 30 minutes. The authorities have kept the CUPRA lane open, while the Airolo entrance in the direction of the tunnel is currently closed. The cross-border sector is also affected by these critical issues. Operational details

The analysis of vehicle flows during the month of July shows that cross-border mobility is closely interconnected with the management of motorway infrastructures. For a worker who resides in Italy and serves in Ticino, the management of travel time becomes a fundamental element of the daily routine. When blockages such as those reported between Lugano Nord and the Collina d 'Oro tunnel occur, the risk of accumulating significant delays increases, affecting not only personal well-being but also the correct management of work commitments. It is important to consider that the traffic dynamics recorded, such as the 10-minute wait in Chiasso Brogeda, represent an average that can vary rapidly depending on the time of passage. Compared to periods of low season, the month of July imposes a time surcharge that must be integrated into calcolatore's planning of travel times. The closure of the Airolo entrance in the direction of the tunnel, combined with the work in progress, demonstrates how road maintenance, although necessary, can generate critical bottlenecks. For border crossers, this means having to evaluate alternative routes or anticipate departures, aware that the road network of the Canton of Ticino, in particular the A2, often operates close to its maximum capacity. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the most critical areas for traffic in Ticino in July?
The main critical issues are concentrated along the A2 motorway, in particular near the Gotthard tunnel, where significant queues are recorded. Other areas affected by slowdowns include the stretch between Quinto and Airolo, the Chiasso Brogeda border and the segment between Lugano Nord and the Collina d 'Oro tunnel due to road works.
How much waiting time can be expected at the Chiasso border?
According to the surveys, the slowdowns at the Chiasso Brogeda border involve waits of about 10 minutes in the direction of Italy. These times may vary depending on the volume of daily traffic and the time of passage, so it is advisable to monitor the situation constantly.
What to do if the Airolo entrance closes?
Currently the entrance of Airolo in the direction of the gallery is closed. Commuters should plan their route taking into account this limitation and consider using alternative highway entrances or secondary routes to reach their destination, paying attention to on-site road signs.
