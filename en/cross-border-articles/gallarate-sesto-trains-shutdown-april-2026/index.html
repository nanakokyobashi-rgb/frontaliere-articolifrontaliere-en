From 10:30 PM on Friday, April 18, 2026, to 5:10 AM on Monday, April 20, 2026, a complete train stoppage is scheduled between the Gallarate and Sesto Calende stations on the Milan–Domodossola line. The interruption, decided by Rete Ferroviaria Italiana (FS Group), is necessary to carry out infrastructure upgrades and the inauguration of a new pedestrian and cycling bridge in Vergiate. The stoppage affects all regional and long-distance services, with direct consequences for cross-border workers who commute daily on this route.

Why the works require a total suspension

According to the official communication, the suspension is necessary to ensure the safety of the construction sites and allow the works to proceed according to schedule. In particular, Eurocity trains on the Venice/Milan Central–Geneva/Basel route will be cancelled between Milan Central and Domodossola, and vice versa, on Saturday, April 19, 2026. For those needing to reach Ticino from this direction, direct connections to Brig or Lausanne—key access points for workers heading to Lugano, Bellinzona, or Locarno—will no longer be available.

The suspension is a significant setback for commuters who rely on this route daily.

Alternatives for cross-border workers: buses and route changes

Passengers will have access to replacement bus services between Milan Central and Domodossola, with mandatory seat reservations. The services are organized by Trenitalia (FS Group) and offer frequent departures, though schedules and routes may vary depending on road traffic. ⚠️ Please note: bicycles and large pets (with the exception of guide dogs) are not permitted on the buses. These restrictions could pose an inconvenience for those who regularly travel with their bikes or accompany pets.

📊 Key details:

  • Buses will depart from Milan Central and terminate at Domodossola.
  • Reservations are required and can be made through Trenitalia’s official channels.
  • Travel times are expected to be longer than on regular train services.

Consequences for regional trains

Regional trains operated by Trenitalia (FS Group) on the Milan–Domodossola route will be limited to Sesto Calende on the northern side and Gallarate on the southern side. Passengers who usually use these services to travel toward Varese or Como will need to arrange alternative transport or accept long wait times. Detailed schedules for the replacement buses have not yet been published, but longer travel times are anticipated compared to regular train services.

A weekend to avoid for those passing through Gallarate

For cross-border workers who daily cross the Italian-Swiss border via Chiasso or Mendrisio, this stoppage represents a major disruption. The Milan–Domodossola line is one of the primary connections for those traveling from Lombardy to Ticino, whether for work or study. The cancellation of Eurocity services and the limitation of regional trains will force many to reconsider their travel plans, potentially leading to delays or additional costs for private transport or taxis.

Disruption timeline

  • Saturday, April 19, 2026: Total interruption between Gallarate and Sesto Calende.
  • Sunday, April 20, 2026: Same scenario, with services resuming at 5:10 AM on Monday.

Possible scenarios for those needing to reach Ticino

Commuters who normally use trains for work face three main options:

1. Book replacement buses: the most economical alternative, but requires advance booking and is not available at all times. Services run between Milan Centrale and Domodossola, but from there, commuters will need to arrange onward travel to Switzerland, perhaps by regional train to Brig or Airolo. Note: buses do not depart from all stations on the route, so it may be necessary to reach Gallarate or Sesto Calende by other means.

2. Use private vehicles or taxis: for those with the financial means, renting a car or taking a taxi offers a quick solution, but at a high cost (€50–100 per trip). This may be the only option for those with strict schedules or who need to transport bulky luggage.

3. Postpone travel: those able to work remotely or adjust their return to Ticino may avoid traveling during the weekend of the stoppage. However, this is not always feasible for those with fixed appointments or work deadlines.

What if I have already booked a train?

Trenitalia has announced that passengers with tickets for canceled trains can request a full refund or free rebooking on a later service. It is advisable to contact customer service or use the official app to manage bookings. Those with monthly or annual passes should check whether the suspension is covered by insurance policies or contractual guarantees.

📊 Alternative options to consider

This stoppage highlights the vulnerability of rail connections for cross-border commuters, especially during strikes or planned works. For those traveling daily from Varese or Como, it may be worth exploring alternative solutions such as:

  • Trains from Gallarate to Luino, then buses to Cadenazzo (Ticino).
  • Regional trains from Milan to Como, followed by a ferry on Lake Como to Porlezza or Menaggio.
  • Private shuttles organized by companies or cross-border commuter cooperatives.

While less convenient, these options could reduce the risk of future disruptions.

Step by step: what to do to organize your journey during the stoppage

Here’s a practical guide to help you navigate the situation day by day.

  • Verify the confirmation of your replacement bus booking (if applicable) via the Trenitalia app or the official website.
  • Look for alternatives to reach Ticino, especially if you usually travel with a bike or transport pets.
  • Download the provisional timetables for replacement buses, which will be updated regularly on trenitalia.com.

2. Book a replacement bus (if necessary) — deadline to be confirmed

  • Go to the "Buy" section on the Trenitalia website and select the Milan Centrale – Domodossola route.
  • Choose your preferred time and pay for the mandatory booking (additional cost not specified in the source).
  • Keep the digital ticket on your phone or print it to show if requested.

⚠️ Not all times are covered by replacement buses. Check availability before booking.

3. Organize your onward journey to Ticino

  • If your Eurocity trains are cancelled, from Domodossola you can reach Brig by regional train (hourly frequency, 20-minute journey, €8-10).
  • From Brig, Ticino is reachable by direct regional trains to Lugano (20 minutes, €12-15) or Bellinzona (15 minutes, €10).
  • Quick alternatives: From Domodossola, take a shared taxi to Locarno or Ascona (€30-50 per person, depending on the number of passengers).

4. Manage luggage, bikes, and pets

  • Bikes: Not allowed on replacement buses. If you need to bring a bike, consider:
  • Renting in Domodossola (€10-15/day) or transporting it on a regional train (subject to availability).
  • Shipping via express courier (similar cost to renting but safer).
  • Pets: Only guide dogs are allowed. Large pets cannot board replacement buses. Consider:
  • Pet boarding near Domodossola station (€15-25/day).
  • Private transport services (taxi or shuttle).

5. Book a taxi or rental car (if necessary)

  • Shared taxis: Services like Taxi Locarno or Taxi Bellinzona offer shared rides at reduced prices (€25-40 per person).
  • Car rental: Companies like Hertz or Europcar in Domodossola (€50-80/day). Watch out for penalties for returning the car in Ticino.
  • Carsharing: Services like Share’ngo operate in the Domodossola area but require advance booking.

6. Request a refund (if you had a purchased ticket)

  • Cancelled Eurocity trains: Full refund possible via Trenitalia or authorized sales points.
  • Limited regional trains: Check if partial routes qualify for partial refunds (procedure not yet specified).
  • Subscriptions: Contact Trenitalia customer service or your subscription office to explore recovery options.

7. Stay updated

Frequently Asked Questions
How can I book replacement buses for cancelled Eurocity trains?
You need to book through Trenitalia’s website or app, selecting the Milano Centrale – Domodossola route. Booking is mandatory, and seats are limited. Detailed schedules are not yet published but will be progressively updated on the official website.
Will tickets for cancelled trains be automatically refunded?
No. Passengers with already purchased tickets must request a full refund or a change to a later train via Trenitalia’s customer service or the official app. Automatic refunds are not provided.
Can I bring my bike on the replacement buses?
No. Replacement buses do not allow the transport of bicycles. Consider alternatives such as bike rental in Domodossola or using regional trains to transport your bike.
What are the most affordable alternatives to reach Ticino from Milano during the stoppage?
The most budget-friendly options include booked replacement buses (ticket plus booking fee), regional trains from Domodossola to Brig and then to Lugano/Bellinzona, or carpooling via services like BlaBlaCar. Shared taxis are more expensive but offer greater flexibility.
Can guide dogs board replacement buses?
Yes. Guide dogs are the only exception allowed for transporting large animals on replacement buses. Other animals are not permitted.
