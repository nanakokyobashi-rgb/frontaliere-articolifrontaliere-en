Key points

G-permit and self-employment in Switzerland for Ticino border workers: an essential guide

Ticino borders can work in Switzerland through the use of the G permit, an authorization issued by the Federal Office of Social Insurance (UVAS). To apply for the G permit, border workers must submit an application to the registration office of the place of residence, with documents such as a passport and identity card. The registration office will evaluate the request and may request additional information, such as certificates of knowledge of the Swiss language or proof of ownership of a property in Switzerland.

As for the type of work, Ticino border workers can choose between two options: working independently or as employees.

To work independently, border workers must register with the Federal Tax Office (UFI) and pay income taxes. They can work in sectors such as catering, hospitality, art and fashion, as well as in family or agricultural activities. For example, according to Swiss statistics, in 2020 the hospitality sector employed about 2% of the Ticino workforce, while art and fashion incorporated 1%.
