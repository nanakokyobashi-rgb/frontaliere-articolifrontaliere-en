A 32-year-old resident of Switzerland and a 25-year-old from the province of Monza arrested for the 90mila euro robbery at Stroili in Tavernola.

The latest operation

On 25 settembre, the Como State Police arrested two Italian nationals as part of the investigation into the robbery at the Stroili jewelry store inside the Bennet in Tavernola. The operation was coordinated by the Public Prosecutor’s Office and carried out pursuant to a pretrial detention order issued by the GIP of Como.

They are a 32-year-old resident of Switzerland and a 25-year-old resident of the province of Monza. According to the evidence gathered during the investigation, the two are believed to have been involved in the heist committed on 24 aprile, when four people with their faces covered reached the shopping center in a rental car.

The robbery took place within a few minutes. While one of the group waited in the car, the others entered the jewelry store armed with a pistol and with their faces concealed by balaclavas. They threatened the employees and smashed the glass display cases with hammers, taking numerous gold jewelry items.

The total value of the stolen jewelry was estimated at approximately 90mila euro. Due to its manner and speed, the action was described as resembling a scene from a Hollywood action film.

Operational details

The investigation into the gang

The previous arrests

The arrests on September 25 represent a new stage in the investigative activity launched after the assault on the Bennet jewelry store in Tavernola. The Como Mobile Squad reconstructed the participants’ roles and gathered evidence considered significant against all those involved in the robbery.

The investigation also concerns the mastermind behind the heist. The source reports that the most recent operation forms part of a broader context, in which the other three members of the gang had already been arrested in April and May 2026. The robbery had been committed by four individuals, with tasks divided between those waiting outside and those entering the store.

During the investigations following the arrests, searches were carried out. Those activities also uncovered part of the stolen goods, before they could be fenced. The recovered jewelry was returned to the Stroili jewelry store, which had been targeted in the assault.

The reconstruction includes several details described by investigators: the rental car used to reach Bennet, the covered faces, the gun shown to the employees, and the hammers used to smash the display cases’ glass. The haul consisted of numerous gold jewelry items, taken within a matter of minutes.

The Mobile Squad’s work focused both on the actual execution of the robbery and on identifying the participants and the alleged mastermind. The previous arrests and searches added details to the reconstruction of the heist and enabled some of the jewelry to be recovered.

Key points

What happens now

The procedural phase

After notification of the pretrial detention order, the two men arrested on 25 September were taken to the Como Prison. Their position remains linked to the investigation records and the evidence gathered by the Como State Police, coordinated by the Public Prosecutor’s Office.

The next step indicated by the source is the confirmation in court of the evidence that emerged during the investigations. The arrests were carried out on the basis of the order issued by the GIP of Como, while the strength of the evidence gathered will have to be verified in the proceedings.

From an investigative standpoint, the most recent operation fits into a sequence that had already led to the arrest of the other three members of the gang in the months of April and May 2026. The searches had also made it possible to recover some of the jewelry stolen from Stroili and return it to the store.

The matter remains focused on the proceedings relating to the robbery on 24 April at Bennet in Tavernola. The elements cited include the haul of approximately 90mila euro, the involvement of four individuals with different roles, the use of a rental car and the action carried out with a pistol, balaclava and hammers.

For the two arrested men, the next stage will therefore be the procedural one, identified in the records as the venue for confirmation of the investigative evidence. Until that stage, the reconstruction remains the one that emerged from the work of the State Police, the Como Mobile Squad and the Public Prosecutor’s Office.
