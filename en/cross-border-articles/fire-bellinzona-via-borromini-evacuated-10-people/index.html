Fire in Bellinzona building: 10 residents evacuated

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 May 2026

A fire broke out this morning in a building in Bellinzona, causing the evacuation of 10 people and a mild intoxication.

In brief

  • Fire in a building in Bellinzona this morning

Key facts

  • What: Fire in a building
  • When: This morning, shortly after 8 AM
  • Where: Via Francesco Borromini, Bellinzona
  • Who: Cantonal police, Bellinzona City Police, Croce Verde Bellinzona, Bellinzona firefighters
  • Amount: 10 people evacuated

This morning, shortly after 8 AM, a fire broke out in a building on Via Francesco Borromini in Bellinzona. According to the Cantonal Police, the blaze started in the kitchen of a ground-floor apartment. As a precaution, it was necessary to temporarily evacuate about ten people present in the building. On the scene, officers from the Cantonal Police and Bellinzona City Police, rescuers from Croce Verde Bellinzona, and Bellinzona firefighters who extinguished the flames, which remained confined to the premises. One tenant reported symptoms of mild intoxication.

Impact on the community

The fire had a significant impact on the local community, especially for those residing in the affected building. The temporary evacuation caused inconveniences, but fortunately, the flames were quickly contained, limiting the damage. The rapid response of law enforcement and rescuers prevented more serious consequences.
