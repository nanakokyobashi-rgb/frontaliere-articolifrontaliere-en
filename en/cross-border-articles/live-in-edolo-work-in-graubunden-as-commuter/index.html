Connections and Travel Times

The connections between Edolo and Graubünden are well-developed, offering various transportation options. By car, the journey can take about 1 hour and 30 minutes, depending on traffic. For those who prefer the train, there are direct connections that take about 1 hour and 45 minutes. Additionally, there are bus services that can be a more economical solution, with travel times similar to those of the train.

Cost of Living

For example, the average cost of renting a two-bedroom apartment in Edolo is about 500 euros per month, while the same type of apartment in Chiasso, Switzerland, can cost up to 1,500 Swiss francs (about 1,400 euros). Connections and Travel Times

The connections between Edolo and the Grisons can vary depending on the means of transport chosen. By car, the travel time can range from 1 to 2 hours, depending on traffic. For example, the journey from Edolo to Chiavenna, one of the main crossing points, takes about 1 hour and 30 minutes under normal traffic conditions. Public transport offers a more economical solution, but travel times can be longer. The train from Edolo to Chiavenna, with a change in Brescia, can take up to 3 hours. It is also important to consider road conditions and possible delays during commuting. During winter, mountain roads may be subject to closures due to snow, further extending travel times.

Cost of Living

The cost of living in Edolo is generally lower than in Switzerland. This can represent a significant advantage for cross-border commuters, who can save on housing costs and other daily expenses. For example, a two-bedroom apartment in Edolo can cost around 500-700 euros per month, while the same type of apartment in Chiavenna can cost between 1,000 and 1,500 euros. However, it is important to consider the additional costs related to commuting, such as fuel and public transport tickets. A full tank of fuel for an average car can cost around 60-70 euros, while a monthly public transport pass between Edolo and Chiavenna can cost around 100 euros.

Advantages and Disadvantages of Moving

Living in Edolo and working in Graubünden presents a series of advantages and disadvantages. Among the advantages are the lower cost of living and the possibility of saving on housing costs. For example, the average cost of an apartment in Edolo is about 1,200 CHF per month, while in Chur, Graubünden, it can exceed 2,000 CHF. However, the disadvantages include longer travel times and additional costs related to commuting. The travel time can vary between 1 and 2 hours depending on the means of transport used, with ticket costs that can reach up to 100 CHF per month.

What to Consider Before Moving

Before moving to Edolo and working in Graubünden, it is important to consider several factors.
