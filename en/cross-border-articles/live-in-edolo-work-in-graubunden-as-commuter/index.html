The connections between Edolo and Graubünden are well-developed, offering various transportation options. By car, the journey can take about 1 hour and 30 minutes, depending on traffic. Additionally, there are bus services that can be a more economical solution, with travel times similar to those of the train.

Cost of Living

The cost of living in Edolo is generally lower than in Switzerland. For example, the average cost of renting a two-bedroom apartment in Edolo is about 500 euros per month, while the same type of apartment in Chiasso, Switzerland, can cost up to 1,500 Swiss francs (about 1,400 euros). The costs of food and utilities are also significantly lower in Italy.

Operational details

Connections and Travel Times

The connections between Edolo and the Grisons can vary depending on the means of transport chosen. By car, the travel time can range from 1 to 2 hours, depending on traffic. For example, the journey from Edolo to Chiavenna, one of the main crossing points, takes about 1 hour and 30 minutes under normal traffic conditions. Public transport offers a more economical solution, but travel times can be longer. The train from Edolo to Chiavenna, with a change in Brescia, can take up to 3 hours. It is also important to consider road conditions and possible delays during commuting. During winter, mountain roads may be subject to closures due to snow, further extending travel times.

Cost of Living

The cost of living in Edolo is generally lower than in Switzerland. This can represent a significant advantage for cross-border commuters, who can save on housing costs and other daily expenses. For example, a two-bedroom apartment in Edolo can cost around 500-700 euros per month, while the same type of apartment in Chiavenna can cost between 1,000 and 1,500 euros. However, it is important to consider the additional costs related to commuting, such as fuel and public transport tickets. A full tank of fuel for an average car can cost around 60-70 euros, while a monthly public transport pass between Edolo and Chiavenna can cost around 100 euros.

Recommended Areas

Edolo offers several residential areas that can be suitable for cross-border commuters. It is important to choose an area that is well-connected to the Grisons and offers essential services such as schools, shops, and public transport. Some recommended areas include the center of Edolo and the nearby suburbs. The center of Edolo is well-served by public transport and offers a variety of shops and services. The suburbs, such as the Ponte di Legno area, offer a quieter environment and are a short distance from the center.

Regulations and Benefits

Cross-border commuters working in Switzerland must comply with specific regulations. For example, as of January 1, 2021, cross-border commuters must obtain a specific work permit to work in Switzerland. This permit is valid for a maximum of 24 months and can be renewed. Additionally, cross-border commuters must pay taxes in Switzerland for the work done in the country, but they can benefit from tax breaks in Italy. For example, cross-border commuters can benefit from a 50% tax deduction on income earned in Switzerland, up to a maximum of 45,000 euros.
