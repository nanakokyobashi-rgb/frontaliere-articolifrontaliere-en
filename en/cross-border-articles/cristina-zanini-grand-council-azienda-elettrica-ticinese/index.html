The socialist deputy and former mayor of Lugano, Cristina Zanini, has decided to leave the Grand Council to join the board of directors of Aet, the Ticino electricity company.

Cristina Zanini, a socialist deputy and former municipal councilor of Lugano, has decided to leave the Grand Council to join the board of Aet, the Ticino Electricity Company. Elected in 2023, Zanini held the position of member of the environment, energy, and territory commission (Cate) and the Aet sub-commission. In her place, Nicola Corti returns to parliament, who had already been a member of the cantonal legislature from 2019 to 2023. Corti, a lawyer by profession, had been a member of the Justice and Rights Commission and the Constitution and Laws Commission, of which he had also been president.
