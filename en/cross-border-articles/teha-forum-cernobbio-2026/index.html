Cernobbio, capital of the world: the Teha Forum in Villa d 'Este (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 5 September 2026

From 4 to 6 September 2026, Villa d 'Este will host the 52nd TEHA Forum with international political leaders and economists.

Context

In brief

  • TEHA Forum at Villa d’Este from September 4 to 6, 2026
  • Participation of international political leaders and economists
  • Discussions on geopolitics, economy, technology, and society

Key facts

  • What: 52nd edition of the TEHA Forum
  • When: From September 4 to 6, 2026
  • Where: Villa d’Este, Cernobbio
  • Who: Political leaders, ministers, economists, scientists
  • Amount: Not specified

Cernobbio will once again be the center of international debate with the 52nd edition of the TEHA Forum, which will be held from September 4 to 6, 2026 at the historic Villa d’Este. The event will bring together political and institutional leaders, ministers, economists, scientists, and key figures in the international economy to discuss the major changes reshaping global balances.

Forum Program

The Forum will begin on Friday, September 4, at 8:45 AM with the institutional greetings of the President of the Republic Sergio Mattarella, followed by the introductory speech of Valerio De Molli, Managing Partner and CEO of The European House – Ambrosetti and TEHA Group. The first day will be dedicated to major international scenarios and future global challenges, with in-depth discussions on Italy’s competitiveness, the world economy, new scientific frontiers, and future cities.

Among the expected protagonists at Villa d’Este are the Minister of Enterprises and Made in Italy Adolfo Urso, the Minister of Foreign Affairs Antonio Tajani, the economist Nouriel Roubini, and the physicist and former CERN Director-General Fabiola Gianotti. Laurence D. Fink, CEO of BlackRock, will also participate via video link.
