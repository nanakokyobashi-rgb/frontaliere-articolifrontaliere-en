Moving to Aprica from border crossings: pros and cons (cross-border guide)

€10,000 tax-free allowance and a requirement to reside in Aprica for at least 183 days a year for cross-border workers relocating there

Context

The new law on the transfer of frontier workers to Aprica provides for a deductible of €10,000 and a need to reside in Aprica for at least 183 days per year. However, you may have to pay taxes in Switzerland if you earn more than €10,000 per year.

What does the €10,000 deductible mean

The €10,000 deductible means that if a border worker earns less than €10,000 per year, they will not be required to pay taxes in Switzerland. This is an advantage for border crossers who have a low or middle income. However, if your income is more than €10,000, you will need to pay taxes in Switzerland.

How the law works

The legislation provides that border workers must reside in Aprica for at least 183 days a year. This means that if you reside in Aprica for less than 183 days, you will not be able to enjoy the €10,000 deductible. Also, if you earn more than €10,000 per year, you will have to pay taxes in Switzerland.

Concrete example

Suppose a frontier worker earns €15,000 per year and resides in Aprica for 6 months per year. In this case, you will not be able to enjoy the deductible of €10,000 and you will be required to pay taxes in Switzerland for income in excess of €10,000.

Transfer requirements

To move to Aprica by border crossing, you must reside in Aprica for at least 183 days a year. This means that if you work in Italy, you will have to return to Aprica for at least 6 months a year. This requirement is provided for by Article 13 of the Treaty of Friendship, Commerce and Navigation between Switzerland and Italy, signed on 29 June 1960.

Taxes in Switzerland

If you earn more than €10,000 per year, you may have to pay taxes in Switzerland. This means that if you work in Italy and earn €15,000 per year, you may have to pay taxes in Switzerland on €5,000. This amount is subject to change depending on the economic conditions and regulations in force.

Key points

If you are a border worker working in Lugano or other Ticino cities and are considering moving to Aprica, it is important that you know the pros and cons of this decision. Aprica is a municipality in the province of Sondrio, in Lombardy, which borders Switzerland and offers a strategic position for frontier workers.

Pros

  • Cost of living: Aprica offers a lower cost of living than Ticino cities, so you could save money on your home and daily life.
  • Taxes: Lombardy has a lower income tax (IRPEF) than Switzerland, so you could save money on your income.
  • Access to Ticino cities: Aprica is located near Ticino cities, so you can easily go to work or shop.

Against

  • Language: The official language of Lombardy is Italian, but you may find it difficult to communicate with locals who do not speak Italian.
  • Public services: Aprica may not have the same quality public services as the cities of Ticino.
  • Mobility: You may face long journeys to reach Ticino cities for work or shopping.

How many days do I have to stay in Aprica to be considered a border worker?
To be considered a border worker and enjoy the exemption, it is mandatory to reside in the municipality of Aprica for at least 183 days a year. This requirement, fundamental for the application of the legislation, is enshrined in Article 13 of the Treaty of Friendship, Commerce and Navigation between Switzerland and Italy of 1960. Failure to comply prevents access to the tax benefits provided.
Since when is the €10,000 deductible for border workers in force and which municipalities in Ticino does it involve?
The €10,000 deductible for frontier workers came into force on 1 January 2023. This provision is not limited only to Aprica, but extends to all the municipalities of the Canton of Ticino, including cities such as Biasca, Bellinzona and Lugano. It is crucial for border crossers to know this date and the breadth of the geographical application for proper tax planning.
What are the advantages of moving to Aprica for a border worker working in Ticino?
Moving to Aprica offers several advantages to border workers working in Ticino. It benefits from a generally lower cost of living than Swiss cities, allowing for significant savings on housing and daily expenses. In addition, Lombardy, where Aprica is located, has a lower income tax (IRPEF) than Switzerland, contributing to greater tax savings. Its strategic location also guarantees easy access to Ticino's cities.
