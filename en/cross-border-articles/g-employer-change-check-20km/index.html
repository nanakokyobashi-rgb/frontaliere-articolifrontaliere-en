The New Cross-border Workers Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020, which came into force on 1 January 2024. For the cross-border worker who changes employer to the Canton of Ticino in 2026, the first check is therefore chronological: 2026 is the year of the procedure to be addressed, not the start date of the agreement. The Italian ratification dates back to Law 83 of 13 June 2023.

The dossier must keep together the new employment relationship, the Permesso G/B and cross-border taxation. Switzerland is not a member of the EU/EEA, which should not be confused with the specific regulations of the Canton of Ticino and with the Italy-Switzerland agreements.

The calendar of the agreement

The Italy-Switzerland Convention against double taxation was signed on 9 March 1976. The New Cross-Border Agreement instead has 23 December 2020 and 1 January 2024 as references. Separating the two acts avoids attributing the entry into force of the more recent agreement to the 1976 Convention. For this reason, the agreement should not be dated 2026: the date of entry into force remains 1 January 2024.

Taxes and deductibles

For earned income, withholding tax is applied exclusively in Switzerland. In Italy, double taxation is neutralized through the tax credit indicated in the EC framework of 730. In the new pay slip, therefore, the Italian credit should not be confused with a second withholding.

Federal and cantonal laws establish withholding tax rates; At the federal level, management is the responsibility of the AFC/ESTV, while the respective tax administrations intervene in the cantons. UFAS and BFS have different tasks: the first follows social security and the second collects statistical data; therefore neither determines these rates.

The deductible must be linked to the category provided for in the agreement. Old cross-border commuters, already such before 17 July 2023, have an exemption of €7,500 in the transitional regime 2024–2033; new cross-border commuters have a deductible of €10,000. When changing employers, category verification precedes any automatic reading of the 2026 calendar.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the tax treatment of cross-border commuters who change employers?
On earned income, withholding tax is withheld only in Switzerland, not in both countries. Italy avoids double taxation with the tax credit indicated in the EC framework of 730. The change of employer therefore requires you to read the new payslip without adding a second Italian withholding tax to the Swiss withholding.
Does the new tax agreement come into force in 2026?
No. The New Cross-Border Commuters Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020 and has been in force since 1 January 2024. The Italian ratification is Law 83 of 13 June 2023. 2026 is the year of the procedure considered, not the year of entry into force of the agreement.
What exemption applies to old and new cross-border commuters?
Old cross-border commuters, who were already cross-border commuters before 17 July 2023, have an exemption of €7,500 in the 2024–2033 transitional regime. New cross-border commuters have an exemption of €10,000. The distinction should not be replaced by a classification based solely on the year 2026.
Which items should I check between contributions and health insurance?
When comparing the two paychecks, the source indicates AHV/IV/EO at 5.3% for the employee, ALV/HC at 1.1% with a cap of CHF 148,200, UVG at 0.7–1.5% and BVG at 7–18% for the age group from the age of 25. The KVG is the health insurance: cross-border commuters G are entitled to option and the adult deductibles are CHF 300–2,500.
