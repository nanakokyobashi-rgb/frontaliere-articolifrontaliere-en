The career of speech therapist in Ticino: salary and requirements

The career of speech therapist is a highly demanded and lucrative profession in Ticino. According to 2022 data, the average salary for a speech therapist in Ticino is about CHF 70,000 per year. However, it is important to note that salary may vary depending on experience, training, and job position.

To work as a speech therapist in Ticino, you need to have a degree in speech therapy or an equivalent qualification. The bachelor's degree in speech therapy is a three- or five-year course of study that provides the knowledge and skills needed to work as a speech therapist. Degree courses in speech therapy are offered by several universities in Switzerland and abroad.

Requirements to work as a speech therapist in Ticino

To work as a speech therapist in Ticino, you must meet the following requirements:

  • Degree in speech therapy or equivalent qualification
  • Work experience of at least 2 years as a speech therapist
  • Knowledge of Italian and other official languages of Switzerland
  • Certificate of professional qualification issued by a recognised organisation
