Fire in via Borromini: ten people evacuated (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 May 2026

A fire in a ground-floor apartment caused the evacuation of ten people in Bellinzona. One person suffered slight intoxication.

Context

In brief

  • Fire in via Borromini, Bellinzona
  • Ten people evacuated
  • One person slightly intoxicated

Key facts

  • What: Fire in a ground-floor apartment
  • When: May 3, 2026, shortly after 8:00 AM
  • Where: Via Francesco Borromini, Bellinzona
  • Who: Cantonal police, Bellinzona City Police, Croce Verde Bellinzona, Bellinzona firefighters
  • Amount: Not specified

This morning, Bellinzona saw a flurry of activity with firefighters, police, and ambulances drawing attention. According to the Cantonal Police, shortly after 8 AM, a fire broke out in a building on Via Francesco Borromini. Preliminary investigations suggest that the fire originated in the kitchen of a ground-floor apartment, though the exact cause is still under investigation. As a precaution, around ten people present in the building were temporarily evacuated. The scene was attended by officers from the Cantonal Police and the Bellinzona City Police, as well as rescuers from Croce Verde Bellinzona and firefighters from Bellinzona, who extinguished the flames, which were confined to the kitchen. One resident reported symptoms of mild intoxication.

Interventions and Rescue

The authorities' response was prompt and well-coordinated. The Cantonal Police managed the situation with the assistance of the Bellinzona City Police, while rescuers from Croce Verde Bellinzona provided medical aid. The Bellinzona firefighters quickly extinguished the flames, limiting the damage to the affected area. The evacuation of the ten people present in the building was carried out as a precautionary measure to ensure everyone's safety.
