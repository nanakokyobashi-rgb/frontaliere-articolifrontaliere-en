Hantavirus on Hondius: human-to-human transmission confirmed (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 5 May 2026

The WHO confirms human-to-human transmission of hantavirus on the cruise ship Hondius. Three deaths and five suspected cases.

Context

In brief

  • Human-to-human transmission of hantavirus on the Hondius ship
  • Three deaths and five suspected cases
  • Ship blocked in Cape Verde

Key facts

  • What: Human-to-human transmission of hantavirus
  • When: May 5, 2026
  • Where: Cruise ship Hondius
  • Who: World Health Organization (WHO)
  • Amount: Three confirmed deaths

The situation on the cruise ship Hondius has worsened with the confirmation by the World Health Organization (WHO) that hantavirus can spread from person to person. This has increased alarm among passengers and crew, with the ship still blocked at the port of Praia, in Cape Verde. The WHO's director for epidemic and pandemic prevention, Maria Van Kerkhove, revealed that the outbreak may have been triggered by transmission among people in close contact.

Origin of the outbreak

The WHO also provided updates on the evolution of the outbreak, revealing that it did not originate on the ship, as initially hypothesized, but in South America. The two Dutch spouses who died would have been infected in South America and then spread the virus to other passengers. This has led the WHO to initiate procedures to trace passengers on the Sant'Elena-Johannesburg flight, on which the Dutch woman had boarded on April 25, to prevent further spread of the infection.

Current situation

In addition to the confirmed cases, including a British man hospitalized in South Africa and improving, there are five suspected infections, not yet confirmed. Among these, two crew members, one English and the other Dutch, who will be evacuated to the port of Praia before being transferred to the airport for air evacuation. The Dutch cruise company Oceanwide Expeditions has communicated that two sick passengers will be evacuated to the Netherlands on a date not yet specified.
