The news of 14 October 2026 brings the closure of Easy Work, a temporary employment agency with headquarters in Corso San Gottardo 106, to Chiasso. By 31 December the company will definitively lower the shutter and stop all activities, following a decision taken by its shareholders.

The choice concerns the entire workforce. There are seven administrative employees and 257 temporary workers: 264 people who, according to what was communicated, are destined to lose their jobs. All contracts will be cancelled. Communications relating to cancellations will be delivered by the end of October.

For the border reader, Chiasso is the geographical point of the story; the source, however, does not indicate how many of the workers involved reside in Italy or cross the border daily. The data available concerns Easy Work's workforce and includes both categories indicated.

The dismissal procedure

The company has informed the staff of the start of a collective dismissal procedure. Before the layoffs become effective, the consultation phase has begun: workers can propose different measures to avoid terminations, reduce their number or limit their consequences.

The trade union discussion has already started with the OCST. Regional deputy secretary Nenad Jovanovic confirmed a meeting with Easy Work managers to review the perspectives of those involved. Above all, on the table are opportunities for professional reintegration and the possibility of introducing a social plan for those who, at the end of the relationship, have not yet found another job.

Eduardo D'Angelo, sole director, explains the origin of the decision. The termination, he clarified, was decided by the shareholders and does not derive from economic difficulties: "It is not a failure." D'Angelo said he was confident in the possibilities of relocating the 264 workers and assured the company's commitment to managing the closure in their interest.
