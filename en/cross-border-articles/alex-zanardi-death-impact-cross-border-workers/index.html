Alex Zanardi, born in Bologna on October 23, 1966, has died at the age of 59. A former Formula 1 driver and symbol of Italian sport, Zanardi competed in 44 Grand Prix between 1991 and 1999, securing one pole position and one podium finish. Before making his mark in the United States, where he won two Cart championship titles in 1996 and 1997, he also raced in Formula 3000 and the DTM.

His life and career changed dramatically after the accident on September 15, 2001, at the Lausitzring in Germany during an American championship race. Zanardi lost both legs, but this did not stop him. He became one of Italy's greatest Paralympic athletes, winning numerous medals, including four golds at the London 2012 and Rio 2016 Games.
