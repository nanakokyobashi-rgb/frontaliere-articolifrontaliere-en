Closure of the sixth calende iron bridge: road guide (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 16 July 2026

From 20 to 26 July the Iron Bridge is closed for maintenance. Activated a free boat and changes to the V125 bus line between Varese and Arona.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Total closure of the Iron Bridge from 20 to 26 July.
  • Free boat service to cross Ticino.
  • Changes to the schedules of the extra-urban bus line V125.
  • Negotiations in progress for the free use of the A26 motorway section.

Key facts

  • What: Extraordinary maintenance and structural consolidation.
  • When: From 20 to 26 July.
  • Where: Ponte di Ferro between Sesto Calende and Castelletto Sopra Ticino.
  • Who: RFI, Municipalities of Sesto Calende and Castelletto Sopra Ticino, Autolinee Varesine.
  • Status: Free river connection service active for pedestrians.

The construction site for the extraordinary maintenance and structural consolidation of the Ponte di Ferro, located between Sesto Calende and Castelletto Sopra Ticino, will impose a total closure to vehicular transit in the period between 20 and 26 July. This intervention, managed under the supervision of RFI, will require a significant reorganization of mobility for all commuters and citizens who cross the Ticino River daily for work or personal reasons. The municipalities of Sesto Calende and Castelletto Sopra Ticino have coordinated mitigation activities to limit discomfort, promoting a direct dialogue with the bodies involved.

Operational details

The closure of the bridge extends beyond private mobility, directly impacting local public transportation. Autolinee Varesine has announced that the extra-urban line V125, which connects Varese to Arona via the bridge, will undergo significant changes to its service schedule for the entire week from July 20 to July 26. The timings for the Varese-Sesto Calende and vice versa routes will remain unchanged; however, the route connecting Sesto Calende, Castelletto Sopra Ticino, and Arona will adopt a specific modified schedule to address the increased travel times caused by the road closure. It is crucial that workers and regular users of the line carefully check the new timetables published on the official Autolinee Varesine website. The bridge closure, although temporary, has a direct impact on traffic flows, leading to congestion in nearby areas. Currently, negotiations are underway between municipalities and Autostrade per l'Italia to potentially grant free access to the A26 section between Sesto Calende and Castelletto Sopra Ticino exits. A decision is expected soon, and authorities are committed to promptly informing the public. This uncertain situation requires careful planning for those who must commute during peak hours, as the potential opening of the autostrada to free traffic could serve as a relief valve for private vehicle traffic, reducing waiting times. For those who need to monitor costs and travel times, it remains useful to consult the dedicated tools for transport in Ticino and border areas to stay updated on any further logistical changes that may arise during the week of construction.

Key points

To effectively navigate this week of inconveniences, it is crucial to follow a preparation procedure that allows you to manage your movements without unexpected delays. Firstly, it is recommended to regularly consult the official website of Autolinee Varesine to check for specific updates on the V125 route, as last-minute changes may be necessary depending on the actual impact of the construction work on local traffic. For those who usually use their own private vehicle, it is necessary to monitor the official channels of the municipalities of Sesto Calende and Castelletto Sopra Ticino to receive official confirmation of the possible exemption of the toll on the A26, which would represent a direct saving and a reduction in travel time.

Checklist for the commute

  • Verify the specific V125 schedule on the official website of Autolinee Varesine.
  • Plan in advance the alternative route if not using the ferry.
  • Monitor the official channels of the municipalities for the exemption of the A26 toll.
  • Consider the possibility of advancing or delaying departures to avoid peak hours.

Frequently Asked Questions
What days is the Iron Bridge closed?
The Iron Bridge will be closed to vehicular transit from 20 to 26 July to allow extraordinary maintenance and structural consolidation work scheduled by RFI.
Is there a substitute service for pedestrians?
Yes, thanks to the dialogue between the Municipalities of Sesto Calende and Castelletto Sopra Ticino, a free river connection service has been activated through a dedicated boat, operating throughout the week of closure with specific hours.
How do the V125 bus schedules change?
Autolinee Varesine has prepared a specific timetable for the V125 route between Sesto Calende, Castelletto and Arona. Trips on the Varese-Sesto Calende axis remain unchanged, while the route concerned undergoes changes to manage longer travel times.
Will the A26 motorway be free during the closure?
The Municipal Administrations are in contact with Autostrade per l 'Italia to obtain free access to the section between Sesto Calende and Castelletto Sopra Ticino. A response is expected very soon and will be communicated to citizens by the local authorities.
