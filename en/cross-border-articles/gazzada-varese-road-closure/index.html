Gazzada-Varese expressway closed on September 24 (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 25 September 2026

The SS 707 Raccordo Gazzada-Varese will be closed toward Varese from 9 to 17 between km 3+100 and km 4+500 for Anas works.

On Thursday 24 September, the SS 707 Raccordo Gazzada-Varese will be closed to traffic in the direction of the provincial capital. Anas's order sets the start of the restriction at 9 and the end at 17, concentrating the stoppage within a specific daytime period.

The stretch covered by the order

The closure concerns the carriageway heading toward Varese between km 3+100 and km 4+500. The stretch measures approximately 1,4 kilometers and, according to the source, is the section identified by the order. The opposite direction is not included in the description of the closure.

Anas has ordered the restriction to allow works to be carried out along the junction. The measure affects all users heading toward Varese. For a cross-border commuter, the detail to verify on their route is therefore whether the journey takes them onto that carriageway and within that time window: the order does not distinguish between commuters, residents or other users.

A 50-kilometer-per-hour speed limit is also being introduced in the work area. Anas also specifies that, during the activities, further requirements may be adopted if necessary to ensure traffic safety and protect the road. The source does not specify what measures could be added or when.

The notice thus establishes four operational coordinates: road, direction, kilometers and time. The closure does not concern the entire road network toward Varese, but the section of the SS 707 between the two points indicated. For anyone organizing a business trip, this defines the certain information without providing a forecast of the overall travel time.

The source does not indicate alternative routes, quantify any queues or communicate a duration for the works beyond the period set for Thursday 24 September. It is therefore not possible to derive either a replacement route or an expected delay from this notice. Anyone seeking traffic information can contact the Anas Operations Room at 02 8266853.

The number is the indicated reference for traffic information and may be used by anyone who needs to assess travel through the affected period. The notice remains limited to the closure of the carriageway toward Varese, the 50-kilometer-per-hour speed limit in the work area and the possibility of further safety-related requirements.

A four-step check

To use the notice without adding assumptions, it is useful to turn the information in the order into a brief check before departure.

1. Set the direction. Check whether the journey is heading toward Varese. The source limits the measure to the carriageway in that direction; it does not describe an extension to the opposite carriageway.

2. Verify the date and time window. Compare the day, Thursday 24 September, with the expected time. The closure is indicated from 9 to 17. If the journey falls within that window, consider the notice as information directly referring to that departure.

3. Compare the stretch. Check whether the route passes between km 3+100 and km 4+500, that is, the approximately 1,4-kilometre section indicated in the order. Do not replace these references with a generic estimate of the area: the source delimits the stretch with the two kilometre markers.

4. Keep the contact at hand. For traffic information, the published reference is the Anas Operations Room, reachable at the number 02 8266853. Anas notes that during the works, additional requirements may be introduced for traffic safety and the protection of the road.

These steps do not produce an alternative itinerary, because the source does not indicate one. They serve to establish whether the notice matches one's journey. If one of the elements is missing, it is advisable not to fill the gap with unpublished data: the order cited does not provide substitute roads, delay estimates, additional costs, or information on public transport.

A cross-border commuter can supplement the check of their route with storico traffico/code ai valichi if their journey also includes a border crossing; however, this tool does not replace the order and does not add data to the news item. The source does not, in any case, provide information on queues or waits at border crossings.

The separation between what is known and what is not indicated avoids practical errors. The limit of 50 kilometres per hour must be read together with the closure: the source places it in the works area, while the carriageway toward Varese remains affected by the restriction during the indicated window. It is therefore not confirmation of free passage through the closed stretch.

To complete the preparation, note down the Anas number and the expected travel time, then check whether the direction and kilometres coincide with those in the notice. To estimate the economic cost of the journey by car, use the costo auto pendolare.

Frequently Asked Questions
On what day and at what times is the SS 707 scheduled to close?
Closing is scheduled for Thursday, September 24, from 9 am to 5 pm. The order of Anas concerns the SS 707 Raccordo Gazzada-Varese in the direction of Varese. The limitation therefore refers to the time slot and the carriageway indicated in the provision, not to the road in each direction. The source does not communicate other times or an extension to different roadways.
Which stretch of highway will be affected?
The section concerned goes from km 3+100 to km 4+500 and measures about 1.4 kilometres. The closure concerns exclusively the Varese direction. In the work area there is also a speed limit of 50 kilometres per hour. The measure affects all users heading for the capital. The source does not indicate deviations or other traits involved.
Where can I ask for information on roads?
For information on road traffic, you can contact the Anas Operations Room on 02 8266853. Anas specifies that during the work further requirements may be adopted, if necessary for the safety of traffic and the protection of the road. The notice does not specify what they will be or when they may become effective.
