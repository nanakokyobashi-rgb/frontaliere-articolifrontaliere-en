MAGGIA - Today was a day of celebration at the eco-hotel Cristallina in Coglio to mark Teresina Cerini's 100th birthday. Born Sartori on May 1, 1926, she was surrounded by her children, grandchildren, and great-grandchildren. The festivities were also attended by Maggia mayor Andrea Sartori, highlighting the strong bond with the territory and the celebrant's family.

Born and raised in Lodano with her two sisters and three brothers, Teresina Cerini lived a simple and hardworking life. For years, she worked as a farmer, tending to the cattle in the Alzasca and Fiumina alpine pastures and producing cheese, an activity that deeply marked her life. In 1949, she married Olivo Cerini, with whom she built a family, having three children: Alma, Ezio, and Sergio. Today, she is a grandmother and great-grandmother, surrounded by the love of the generations she has helped to create.

What to Do to Celebrate a Centenary

Celebrating a centenary is a special event that requires planning and attention to detail. Here are some steps to follow to organize a memorable celebration:

1. Planning: Start planning well in advance. Decide on the date, location, and theme of the celebration. Make sure to involve family and friends in the planning process. 2. Invitations: Send out invitations well in advance to give everyone time to organize. You can use paper or digital invitations, depending on preferences. 3. Location: Choose a location that is significant to the person being celebrated. In this case, the eco-hotel Cristallina in Coglio was a perfect choice for Teresina Cerini. 4. Food and Drinks: Plan a menu that reflects the traditions and tastes of the person being celebrated. Make sure to include local dishes and specialties that they love. 5. Entertainment: Organize entertainment that is suitable for the age and interests of the person being celebrated. This can include music, dancing, poetry readings, or storytelling. 6. Memories: Create special memories for the person being celebrated and the guests. You can prepare photo albums, videos, or other souvenirs. 7. Photography: Make sure to have a professional photographer to capture the special moments of the celebration. 8. Thanks: Don't forget to thank everyone who contributed to the celebration, including family, friends, and the community.

How to Keep Local Traditions Alive

Keeping local traditions alive is essential to preserving the cultural identity of a community. Here are some suggestions for doing so:

1. Education: Teach the younger generations the importance of local traditions. Organize workshops, courses, and activities that involve the youth. 2. Community Events: Organize community events that celebrate local traditions. These events can include festivals, fairs, markets, and other cultural activities. 3. Documentation: Document local traditions through photographs, videos, and stories. This will help preserve the historical and cultural memory of the community. 4. Collaboration: Collaborate with other communities and organizations to promote and preserve local traditions. Collaboration can lead to larger and more meaningful projects. 5. Financial Support: Seek financial support for projects that promote local traditions. This can include public funds, private donations, and sponsorships.
