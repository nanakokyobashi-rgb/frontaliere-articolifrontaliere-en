Tax Implications

Cross-border commuters living in Saint-Christophe and working in Valais must consider the tax implications of this choice. Withholding tax on employment income is only deducted in Switzerland, while Italy avoids double taxation with a tax credit. It is important to know the tax rates and Swiss contributions, which include AVS/AI/IPG, AD/AC, LAINF, and LPP.

Health Insurance

Cross-border commuters have the option to choose health insurance (LAMal) in Switzerland. Deductibles for adults range from CHF 300 to CHF 2500. It is important to know the available options and choose the one that best suits your needs.
