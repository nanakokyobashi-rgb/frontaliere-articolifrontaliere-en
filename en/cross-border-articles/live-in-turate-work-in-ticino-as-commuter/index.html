Connections and Travel Times

Cross-border commuters living in Turate and working in Ticino can choose from various means of transport to reach their workplace. Travel times vary depending on the chosen means. For example, by car, the travel time can range from 30 to 45 minutes, depending on traffic. Public transport offers an alternative solution, with similar travel times but with the addition of possible intermediate stops.

Concrete Examples

For those living in Turate and working in Lugano, the travel time by car is approximately 35 minutes without traffic. Using public transport, the journey can take about 45 minutes, with intermediate stops in Como and Chiasso. For those working in Mendrisio, the travel time by car is approximately 25 minutes, while by public transport it can take up to 35 minutes.

Regulations and Costs

From January 1, 2024, cross-border commuters must comply with new regulations for cross-border work. Income tax rates for cross-border commuters are set at 24% for 2024, with a maximum amount of 120,000 CHF. Above this threshold, taxation increases to 35%.

Procedure to Obtain the Work Permit

To work in Ticino, cross-border workers must obtain the G work permit. The procedure involves submitting a series of documents, including the employment contract, residence certificate, and passport. It is important to carefully follow the instructions provided by the Swiss authorities to avoid delays in the procedure. For example, an Italian citizen residing in Como must submit the signed employment contract, the residence certificate issued by the municipality of Como, and a copy of the valid passport. The application must be sent to the Cantonal Immigration Office of Ticino, located in Bellinzona.

Deadlines and Required Documents

The deadlines for submitting documents vary depending on the specific case. It is important to inform yourself with the competent authorities to know the deadlines and required documents. For example, for a French citizen residing in Saint-Genis-Pouilly, the application must be submitted within 14 days from the start of work. The required documents include the employment contract, residence certificate, and passport. It is advisable to check the specific deadlines on the website of the Cantonal Immigration Office of Ticino.
