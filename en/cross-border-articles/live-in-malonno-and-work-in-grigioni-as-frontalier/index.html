Who are the frontiersmen?

Border workers are workers who reside in a country bordering Switzerland, such as Italy, and work in Switzerland, in this case in the Canton of Graubünden. According to data from the Statistical Office of the Canton of Graubünden, in 2020 there were around 18,000 regularised border workers working in the Canton.

The New Frontier Agreement: what changes?

The New Frontier Agreement was signed in 2020 and replaced the old agreement that had been in place since 2003. This new agreement introduces some important new features for frontier workers:

  • The possibility of obtaining Swiss citizenship after 10 years of residence, instead of 12.
  • The introduction of a scoring system to assess the quality of applications for citizenship.
  • The possibility of obtaining a residence permit of 5 years, instead of 2.
