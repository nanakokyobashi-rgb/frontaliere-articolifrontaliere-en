Operational details

Worker athlete Badr Jaafari in light blue

Badr Jaafari is a hard-working athlete who trains twice a day, before and after work. His is a story of pure sport and immense passion. The Mayor of Casalzuigno commented on Badr Jaafari's feat, calling it an example of humility, a spirit of sacrifice and the ability to pursue one's dreams without seeking excuses.

Badr Jaafari works as a bricklayer in a construction site in Lugano, where he plays a fundamental role in the construction of new houses and infrastructure. His working day is intense, but it doesn't stop him from exercising regularly. In fact, every morning he gets up at 5:00 and heads to the sports centre in Caslano, where he begins his artistic gymnastics training. His passion for sports is such that he manages to find time to train even after work, at 6:00 pm, for an hour and a half.

His is a story that inspires many Ticino residents, who consider him an example to follow. “Badr Jaafari is a hard-working athlete who shows that you can achieve your goals even by working full-time,” says the Mayor of Casalzuigno. “His humility and spirit of sacrifice are an example for all of us to follow.”

Badr Jaafari also has his own checklist for his daily workout, 📊 this includes:

  • Artistic gymnastics training (1.5 hours)
  • Strength and endurance training (1 hour)
  • Speed and agility training (30 minutes)

Badr Jaafari, the young working athlete from Casalzuigno, is ready to represent Switzerland at the European Athletics Championships. Her story is an example of how passion and determination can lead to incredible results. Born in Tunisia, Badr has lived in Casalzuigno since he was a child and has always had a great passion for sport. After starting to run at an amateur level, he decided to take the professional road and joined the Swiss athletics team.

His career has been marked by numerous victories and records. He won the national athletics title three times and represented Switzerland at the World Athletics Championships. His most impressive performance was when he ran the half marathon in 1h 02' 10" at Locarno, setting a new personal record.

His dedication to the sport was rewarded with the gold medal at the European Athletics Championships in Munich.

His victory is also a result for the Ticino community, which has always supported Badr in his sporting career.
