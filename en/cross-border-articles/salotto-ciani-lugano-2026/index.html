Il Salotto lights up Asilo Ciani in Lugano (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 5 May 2026

On Saturday, May 9, 2026, Asilo Ciani in Lugano hosts 'Il Salotto', an event combining art and music with local young artists.

Key facts

  • What: Cultural event 'Il Salotto'
  • When: Saturday, May 9, 2026, from 17.00 to 23.00
  • Where: Asilo Ciani, Lugano
  • Who: Organized by Art Lab Events
  • Cost: Not specified

The event 'Il Salotto' will light up Asilo Ciani in Lugano on Saturday, May 9, 2026, from 17.00 to 23.00. This meeting of different languages, visions, and sensitivities is organized by the non-profit association Art Lab Events and sponsored by the City of Lugano. The event celebrates the union between art and music, transforming the dialogue between disciplines into an immersive experience. The protagonists are young local artists, called to share their creative path in a dynamic and contaminated context.

The main guest of this edition is Mattia Labadessa, who presents himself to the public in his dual role as a cartoonist and musician. In both expressions, his stylistic signature emerges, a cynical irony embodied by the bird-man with red plumage, a symbol capable of transforming anxiety into creative resistance. Next to him, Fenice's musical proposal stands out for a never monotonous balance between rebellious texts and more intimate moments, through a fusion of pop, urban, and electronic music.
