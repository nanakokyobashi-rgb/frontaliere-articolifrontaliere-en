Frequently Asked Questions
When did the new tax agreement for Italy-Switzerland border workers come into force?
The New Frontier Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020 and replaces the previous Italian-Swiss Double Taxation Convention of 1976. This agreement has important consequences for border workers who reside in Italy, such as in Charvensod, and work in Switzerland, for example in Valais.
How does the taxation for border workers working in Valais change with the new agreement?
With the new agreement, border workers working in Valais and living in Charvensod can benefit from more favourable taxation. They will be exempt from Swiss taxes on foreign labour income, allowing them to keep a more significant share of their income. For example, out of 50,000CHF, they could keep about 30,000 CHF instead of 20,000CHF.
What are the new deadlines for the tax returns of Italy-Switzerland border workers?
Border workers must submit an annual tax return by 31 March of each year, in electronic form. In addition, a monthly tax return is required by the 15th of the month following the month in which the income was earned, for example by the 15th of February for January income.
