The new cross-border agreement, signed on December 23, 2020, and effective from January 1, 2024, has brought some significant changes for cross-border workers. The Italian ratification occurred with Law 83 of June 13, 2023. This agreement has introduced new tax and contribution rules that directly affect cross-border commuters.

Connections and Travel Times

Cross-border commuters living in Induno Olona and working in Ticino have several options to reach their workplace. The most common border crossings are Brogeda, Gaggiolo, and Ponte Tresa. Travel times vary depending on traffic and road conditions, but generally, it takes about 30-45 minutes to reach the Swiss border.

Cost of Living

The cost of living in Induno Olona is generally lower compared to Ticino. This can represent an economic advantage for cross-border commuters, who can save on rent, food, and other daily expenses. However, it is important to consider the costs of commuting, such as fuel and tolls.

Recommended Areas

Induno Olona offers several residential areas suitable for cross-border commuters. The most popular areas are those near major road connections, which allow for quick access to the Swiss border. It is advisable to choose an area with good services, such as schools, shops, and public transportation.

Practical Analysis

The new border agreement introduced some significant changes for cross-border workers. One of the main innovations is the increase in the deductible for new frontier workers, which goes from €7,500 to €10,000. This means that new frontier workers can benefit from a higher deductible than frontier workers already in business before 17 July 2023.

Impact on Frontiersmen

The increase in the deductible can represent an economic advantage for new frontier workers, who can save on taxes. However, it is important to consider that the higher deductible also leads to an increase in medical expenses in case of illness. Key points

Action

For commuters who live in Induno Olona and work in Ticino, it is important to follow certain procedures to obtain the G permit and benefit from the new tax and contribution rules introduced by the new commuter agreement.

Procedure for the G Permit

Commuters who live in Induno Olona and work in Ticino must obtain the G permit. This permit allows you to work in Switzerland without having to reside in the country. The procedure for obtaining the G permit involves submitting a series of documents, including the employment contract and the residence certificate.

Taxes and Contributions

Commuters are subject to withholding tax on employment income, deducted in Switzerland. Italy avoids double taxation with a tax credit. Swiss tax rates include AVS/AI/IPG at 5.3%, AD/AC at 1.1% (with a cap of CHF 148,200), LAINF from 0.7% to 1.5%, and LPP from 7% to 18% depending on the age bracket. In Italy, IRPEF is 23% up to €28,000, 35% from €28,001 to €50,000, and 43% over €50,000.

Health Insurance

Commuters have the option to choose health insurance (LAMal). Deductibles for adults range from CHF 300 to CHF 2,500. It is important to choose a deductible that suits your needs and budget.

Refunds

Commuters can benefit from tax refunds. These refunds allow you to recover part of the taxes paid in Switzerland. It is important to inform yourself about the procedures for requesting refunds and the necessary documents.
