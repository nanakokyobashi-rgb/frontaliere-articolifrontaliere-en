Context

Disruptions are also possible before and after the strike. LUGANO - Disruptions are expected for train travelers in Italy. Several unions have called a 24-hour nationwide strike for Ferrovie dello Stato Italiane (FS) staff, scheduled to run from approximately 9:15 PM on Monday until 9:00 PM on Tuesday.&#10;&#10; According to the railways, delays and cancellations are also possible in Ticino. TILO reports that the RE80, S10, S30, S40, and S50 services will be affected and will not run in Italy. If the S50 trains are not running, TILO connections to and from Malpensa Airport will be replaced by buses between Stabio and Malpensa Airport, without intermediate stops.&#10;&#10; Timetable disruptions could occur before and after the strike begins, and could also affect long-distance connections.&#10;&#10; Some trains should still run. The guaranteed time slots, from 6:00 to 9:00 and from 18:00 to 21:00, remain in effect. Travelers are advised
