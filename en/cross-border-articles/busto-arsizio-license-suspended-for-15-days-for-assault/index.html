Busto Arsizio: license suspended for 15 days for assault

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 October 2026

The Police Commissioner of Varese suspended the license of a bar in Busto Arsizio for 15 days after the September 20 assault involving stones being thrown near a couple with a stroller; the injured person was taken to the hospital in Gallarate.

Context

In brief

  • License suspended for 15 days by the Questore of Varese
  • Assault on the evening of 20 settembre in Busto Arsizio
  • Stones thrown near a couple with a stroller
  • Injured man taken to the hospital in Gallarate

Key facts

  • Measure → suspension of the license for 15 days
  • Authority → Questore of Varese
  • Location → bar in Busto Arsizio
  • Intervention → State Police and Carabinieri
  • Incident → brawl, stone-throwing and assault
  • Injured man → taken to the hospital in Gallarate
  • Legal basis → Article 100 of the Tulps

The decision after the assault

The State Police notified the owner of a bar in Busto Arsizio of the measure by which the Questore of Varese ordered the suspension of the establishment’s license for 15 days. The decision was prompted by serious incidents that caused disturbances to public order and safety.

The incident that led to the measure took place on the evening of 20 settembre. A State Police patrol car and a Carabinieri patrol intervened at the premises following a report of a brawl. When they arrived, the fight had already ended. The officers collected the testimonies of those present and reviewed the surveillance footage.

The investigation established that shortly beforehand, a violent assault had taken place against a man. Some people had thrown stones at him, among other things putting at risk the safety of a passing couple with a stroller. Subsequently, one of the group members physically attacked the man, throwing him to the ground.

Medical personnel had to intervene for the injured man. After providing initial aid, they took him to the hospital in Gallarate. The notification therefore concerns the license of an establishment in Busto Arsizio and follows the reconstruction of a sequence involving stone-throwing and physical assault, as well as the presence of passersby exposed to danger.

The measure was communicated to the owner by the State Police. The duration set by the Questore of Varese is 15 days, with the stated reason being the protection of public order and safety.

Operational details

The Police Headquarters’ investigation

The administrative inquiry began with a request from the Busto Arsizio Public Security Police Station. The report concerned in particular the serious incident of violence that took place at the venue and the intervention of law enforcement. The inquiry was subsequently conducted by the Administrative Police Division of the Varese Police Headquarters.

The initial evidence was gathered during the intervention by the State Police patrol unit and the Carabinieri patrol. Although the fight had already ended, the officers heard statements from those present and viewed the surveillance footage. The checks brought to light what had happened shortly before the report, including the assault on the man and the danger created by stones being thrown near the couple with a stroller.

The proceedings also refer to previous incidents concerning the same public establishment. According to the findings, the bar had previously been subject to similar licence suspension measures under Article 100 of the Consolidated Law on Public Security, the Tulps.

Description of the clientele

Again according to the findings, the venue reportedly represented a regular gathering place for people with police records and socially dangerous individuals. The source links this situation to a consequent exposure to risks to public order and safety.

This element adds to the report by the Police Station and the reconstruction carried out by the Police Headquarters’ offices. The roles of the various offices are indicated separately: the Police Station reported the incident, the Division conducted the inquiry, and the Chief of Police ordered the suspension. The measure is based on the evidence gathered through the activities of the Administrative Police Division and on the seriousness of the incident.

Key points

The follow-up to the measure

The administrative follow-up described by the source is the notification to the bar owner of the decision adopted by the Police Commissioner of Varese. The State Police notified the measure ordering the suspension of the establishment's license for 15 days. The measure therefore concerns the public establishment in Busto Arsizio and has a defined duration.

The basis for the decision

The regulatory reference indicated is Article 100 of the Consolidated Text of Public Security Laws, the Tulps. The same provision is cited for the previous suspension measures already adopted against the public establishment. The source links the application of the article to the need to intervene after incidents that caused disturbances to public order and safety.

The Police Commissioner of Varese ordered the suspension after considering the seriousness of the facts and the evidence collected. The stated purpose of the measure is to protect public order and citizens' safety. The order establishes a temporary suspension of the license, set at fifteen days, and links it to the protection of the community.

The sequence of acts concludes with the notification carried out by the State Police to the owner. Before that came the request from the Busto Arsizio Public Security Police Station and the work of the Administrative Police Division of the Varese Police Headquarters; after assessing the evidence, the Police Commissioner adopted the measure provided for by Article 100 of the Tulps. The result reported by the source is a temporary suspension of the license, justified by the established violence and the risks identified for public order and safety.

Frequently Asked Questions
What measure was taken by the Questore of Varese?
The Quaestor of Varese has ordered the suspension of the license of the Busto Arsizio bar for a period of fifteen days. The measure was notified by the staff of the State Police to the holder of the public service, after the investigations into the violent attack that took place on the evening of 20 September.
When and where did the assault that led to the suspension take place?
The attack took place on the evening of 20 September in Busto Arsizio, near the bar subject to the measure. A group of people threw stones at a man, also endangering a couple of passers-by with a stroller; later one of the attackers physically hit the victim, throwing her to the ground.
What is the legal basis for the measure suspending the license?
The measure is based on Article 100 of the Consolidated Law on Public Security (Tulps). This rule allows the Quaestor to suspend the license of a public exercise when incidents occur that disturb public order and safety, such as in the case of brawling and stone throwing ascertained by the Quaestor's investigations.
