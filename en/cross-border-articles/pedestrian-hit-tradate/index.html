The collision and first aid

On Wednesday, October 7, shortly before 20:00, a 58-year-old man was struck by a vehicle on Via Albisetti, in Tradate. The incident occurred in the evening, near the market square and in front of Unieuro. These are the reference points indicated by the source to precisely locate the incident.

The rescuers, who arrived with an ambulance from Croce Rossa di Busto and a medical emergency vehicle, assisted the 58-year-old and then transported him to hospital.

The man's condition initially caused concern. However, the update reported by the source clarifies that the 58-year-old is injured but not in life-threatening danger. This is the medical information that completes the picture of what happened after he was taken to hospital.

The Carabinieri of the Compagnia di Saronno were also alerted. Their intervention was requested for the scene surveys and to reconstruct what happened. The report therefore indicates, alongside the rescuers' intervention, work dedicated to reconstructing what happened on Via Albisetti.

The reported sequence remains clear: the man was struck shortly before 20:00; the rescuers reached the area around the market square; the injured man was taken to hospital; the Carabinieri were alerted for the scene surveys and reconstruction. The reported health outcome for the 58-year-old is that he is injured but not in life-threatening danger.
