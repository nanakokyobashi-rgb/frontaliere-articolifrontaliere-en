A8 Milano-Varese, Busto Arsizio exit closed for one night (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 September 2026

Closure from 10 pm on 9 to 5 am on 10 September for work on safety barriers. Castellanza and Gallarate alternative exits.

Context

In brief

  • Night closure of Busto Arsizio exit from 10:00 PM on 9 to 5:00 AM on 10 September
  • Inspection and maintenance work on safety barriers on A8
  • Alternative exits: Castellanza or Gallarate depending on the direction

Key facts

  • What: Closure of Busto Arsizio exit on A8 Milano-Varese
  • When: 10:00 PM Wednesday 9 September — 05:00 AM Thursday 10 September
  • Where: Busto Arsizio exit, province of Varese
  • Why: Inspection and maintenance work on safety barriers
  • Reopening: 05:00 AM Thursday 10 September
  • Who: Autostrade (manager of the section)

The scheduled closure

The Busto Arsizio exit will be blocked from 10:00 PM on Wednesday 9 to 5:00 AM on Thursday 10 September, both for those coming from Milan and for those arriving from the Varese direction. The closure was scheduled by Autostrade to allow inspection and maintenance activities of the safety barriers.

Unlike what it might seem, this is not a total interruption of the highway: only the Busto Arsizio exit will be inaccessible during the communicated time window. This means that vehicles in transit will necessarily have to change their route using the indicated alternative exits.

Alternative routes for those coming from Milan

Autostrade recommends two options for drivers arriving from Milan. The first, more direct, is to anticipate the exit at the Castellanza junction, which precedes Busto Arsizio in the direction of Varese. Alternatively, you can continue on the A8 towards Varese and exit at Gallarate, thus postponing the change of road by a few kilometers.

Operational details

Impacts on the mobility of border commuters

Ticino border crossers who daily travel on this route to Varesotto — one of the main poles of occupational attraction for those residing in the Canton of Ticino — will face a minor but not negligible disruption. A night closure represents a limited criticality in time, yet its operational management can influence the rhythms of evening return and morning departure for those who work across borders.

The province of Varese, with its industrial and commercial centres, absorbs a significant flow of commuters from Lugano, Mendrisio and the Ticino Riviera di Levante. For many of them, the A8 represents the main route of connection with the areas of use. A closure of a few hours, even if at night, requires a reorganization of the routes that can lengthen transit times.

Travel strategy: choose in advance

The information advantage for those who move daily is predictability. As the closure is planned and communicated in advance, border crossers have the opportunity to plan their journey with awareness. Those who normally go out to Busto Arsizio will have to evaluate whether to opt for Castellanza (choice of anticipation) or for Gallarate (choice of continuation), based on their final location.

Key points

Operational checklist for the night of September 9th

If you are a cross-border commuter who regularly travels on the A8 towards Varesotto, the night between September 9th and 10th requires some preparation:

Before leaving:

  • Check which exit you normally use (if it is Busto Arsizio, plan an alternative already in the car)
  • Consult an offline map of routes to Castellanza or Gallarate based on your destination
  • If the trip is in the evening (between 9 PM and 11 PM), consider leaving slightly earlier to avoid backtracking caused by sudden closures
  • Notify your employer if your return time may be affected

During the trip:

  • Stay focused: the need to change routes can distract from safe driving
  • If you use a navigator, enter the final destination and not just the usual exit, so the app will automatically suggest an alternative route
  • Castellanza exit: it is shorter, perfect if you know the local traffic that follows
  • Gallarate exit: further away, but you stay on the main road longer

Timing and impact on schedules

The reopening of the exit is scheduled for 5 AM on Thursday, September 10th. This means that those leaving home in the morning (between 5:30 AM and 6:30 AM) will find the interchange already in operation, without the need to resort to alternative routes. Conversely, the criticality focuses on the previous evening's return and very early departures (before 5 AM).

For a cross-border commuter who normally returns from work between 6 PM and 10 PM, the critical window is real: the closure starts at 10 PM, so those leaving Varesotto at 9:30 PM-9:45 PM may find traffic converging towards alternative exits while they themselves approach the closure point.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the exact closing times of the Busto Arsizio exit?
The exit of the Busto Arsizio junction will be closed from 22:00 on Wednesday 9 September to 5:00 on Thursday 10 September. It is scheduled to reopen at exactly 5am. Those who leave early in the morning (after 5:30 am) will have no problem.
I come from Milan and I have to reach Busto Arsizio: what alternative exit do you recommend?
You have two options. The first is to advance the exit to Castellanza (a few kilometers before Busto Arsizio), or continue towards Varese and exit at Gallarate, from where you can reach Busto Arsizio along a road downstream. Choose according to your familiarity with the territory.
Does the closure also affect those who come from Varese?
Yes, the closure concerns motorists from both directions. From Varese, it is advisable to advance the exit to Gallarate, or continue towards Milan and use the Castellanza junction. Both alternatives allow you to circumvent the closed point.
How much time could you add to my trip by using an alternative exit?
It depends on the distance between the Busto Arsizio exit and your final destination, as well as the local road you will follow. The source does not specify the exact additional journey times, so we recommend that you test the alternative route in advance using your satnav.
Is this closure temporary or will it become permanent?
It is a temporary and scheduled closure, aimed at the inspection and maintenance of safety barriers. The exit will reopen on September 10 at 5 am, restoring normal circulation.
