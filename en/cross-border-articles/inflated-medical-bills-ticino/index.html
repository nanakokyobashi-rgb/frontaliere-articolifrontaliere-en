For some time now, patients in the Canton of Ticino have been facing an increasingly concerning issue: inflated medical bills. An investigation by 'Patti chiari' (RSI, 20 March 2026) revealed that every year in Switzerland healthcare providers bill at least 4 billion francs in unjustified amounts, according to health insurers' checks. The issue has become so significant that since 2022, it is mandatory for every patient to receive a detailed copy of the invoices for visits and healthcare services. However, many do not check these documents, and thus find themselves paying amounts that do not correspond to the services received. Numerous patients have reported brief consultations charged as if they lasted much longer. For example, a 10-minute visit was billed as if it lasted 25 minutes, resulting in a considerable overcharge. It is clear that this practice is not isolated; a survey by ACSI showed that 60% of respondents have received at least one incorrect bill. Reported issues also include incorrect items, duplicates, and charges for treatments not performed. One patient reported correcting their bill after disputing an unjustified amount. The topic is sensitive: confronting the doctor to discuss a mistake could jeopardize the trust relationship, but patients have the right to transparent and fair services. For this reason, it is crucial to raise awareness of one's rights and the importance of checking bills.

The landscape of inflated bills is not just an economic issue. It has direct repercussions on health insurance premiums, which continue to rise, putting a strain on the finances of Ticino residents, who are already under pressure due to the increasing cost of living. In 2026 premiums in Ticino rose by 7.1%, against a Swiss average of 4.4%: the highest increase among the cantons. Poor transparency and incorrect billing practices must be addressed decisively. The first step is to ask the doctor or facility for a correction; for serious cases, patients can turn to the Ethical Commission of the Medical Association of Canton Ticino. Only through greater vigilance and awareness can citizens protect themselves and their rights, contributing to a fairer and more transparent healthcare system.

Operational details

Examining the issue of inflated medical bills more closely, it is essential to consider the regulatory requirements in place. Since 2022, as mentioned, it is mandatory for doctors to provide patients with a detailed copy of the services rendered. This measure was introduced to ensure greater transparency in the healthcare sector and to combat irregularities. However, despite this regulation, many patients do not feel comfortable disputing bills, fearing it might compromise their trust relationship with the doctor. The situation is unsustainable: with 60% of people experiencing errors in their bills, it is clear that there is an urgent need for greater vigilance. The most common errors include incorrect recording of visit times, where, for example, a group physiotherapy session was billed as individual therapy, resulting in significantly higher costs. The primary responsibility falls on patients, who must take a proactive approach to verify bills and contest any discrepancies. At the same time, greater training and awareness among healthcare providers are necessary to prevent similar situations from occurring.

Healthcare facilities and doctors must recognize their role in ensuring the accuracy of billing and should be willing to correct any errors without creating friction. ACSI has launched a campaign to educate patients on how to check their bills and know how to act in case of disputes. By prohibiting unfair practices, the entire healthcare system can be improved, making it fairer and more sustainable for everyone. In Ticino, where the healthcare system is already under economic pressure, it is essential that citizens trust the services they receive and can rely on a system that works for them. Expert advice and access to clear information are vital to improving the current situation.

In case of a dispute, patients can follow these steps: 1. Carefully check the received bill. 2. Compare costs with the services actually received. 3. Gather all necessary evidence. 4. First ask the doctor or facility for a correction. 5. For serious cases, contact the Ethical Commission of the Medical Association of Canton Ticino. By following this procedure, patients can avoid being hit by costly errors and ensure that their right to fair care is respected.

Frequently Asked Questions
What happens if you receive an unjustified medical bill?
If you receive an unjustified medical bill, check it carefully and compare the charges with the services you actually received. Gather all the necessary evidence and raise the issue with the doctor or healthcare provider. If the matter is not resolved, for serious cases you can contact the Ethical Commission of the Medical Association of Canton Ticino (Commissione deontologica dell'Ordine dei medici del Canton Ticino).
How can I contest an incorrect medical bill in the canton of Ticino without compromising the relationship with my doctor?
First ask the doctor or facility, respectfully, to correct the bill, providing evidence of the discrepancies. For serious cases, if the matter is not resolved, contact the Ethical Commission of the Medical Association of Canton Ticino.
What concrete steps can a patient take if a healthcare facility does not respond to a complaint about an incorrect bill?
Check the bill, compare the charges with the services received, gather the evidence and ask the doctor or facility for a correction. For serious cases, if they do not reply or do not correct the bill, contact the Ethical Commission of the Medical Association of Canton Ticino; you can also ask a consumer protection organisation for assistance.
How do inflated medical bills affect health insurance premiums in Canton Ticino?
Unjustified bills weigh on the costs that health insurers must cover, and therefore on premiums. In 2026 Ticino recorded the highest percentage increase among the cantons: +7.1% against a Swiss average of +4.4%.
What are the responsibilities of healthcare facilities in ensuring the correctness of billing?
Healthcare facilities are responsible for ensuring that billing is correct by giving patients detailed copies of the services provided, as required by the rules in force since 2022. They must correct any errors without creating friction and adopt transparent practices to avoid irregularities. Healthcare professionals must be properly trained to prevent billing errors and to comply with ethical and professional standards.
