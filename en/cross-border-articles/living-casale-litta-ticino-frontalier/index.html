Operational details

Border taxation: the new 2024 agreement

From 1 January 2024, the new Italy-Switzerland tax agreement (signed 23 December 2020, ratified IT with Law 83/2023) introduces significant changes. Old frontier workers (settled before 17 July 2023) enjoy an exemption of €7,500 until 2033 with a transitional regime. New frontier workers benefit from a €10,000 deductible. Tax at source is withheld ONLY in Switzerland, not in both countries. The Italian-Swiss Convention of 9 December 1976 prevents double taxation by means of a tax credit (EC framework, model 730).

Swiss rates: progressive Ticino federal and cantonal tax, plus AVS/AI/IPG 5.3% (employee), LAINF 0.7–1.5%, LPP 7–18% by age group (from 25 years). In Italy, progressive income tax: 23% up to €28,000, 35% up to €50,000, 43% over €50,000. The border worker fills out the Italian form 730 declaring the Swiss income and requesting the tax credit to avoid double taxation.

Key points

Become a Frontalier: the step-by-step procedure

Step 1: Preliminary documentation

Gather a valid ID document (passport or Italian ID card), tax code, residence certificate from Casale Litta Municipality, and extended civil status certificate. These documents are necessary for the G Permit application.

Step 2: Job search and formal offer

Identify an employer in Ticino (Lugano, Mendrisio, Bellinzona, Locarno) willing to hire a frontaliere. The job offer must specify the role, gross salary in CHF, and start date.

Step 3: G Permit application at SEM

Once the offer is received, the employer or employee submits the G Permit application to the SEM (State Secretariat for Migration) or the competent cantonal office in Ticino (Department of Education and Culture). The procedure requires completed forms, signed employment contract, and identity documents. The authorization takes around 2-4 weeks.

Step 4: Opening a bank account and affiliation with the pension fund

After the G Permit is authorized, the frontaliere registers with the AVS at the competent federal office and with the LPP fund at the bank or the employer's insurance. Simultaneously, they open a Swiss bank account with a Ticino bank (branches in Chiasso, Mendrisio) to manage their salary and payments.

Step 5: INPS registration and Italian tax declaration

Notify the INPS of the frontaliere's condition to avoid double contributions. Complete the Italian tax return (model 730 or Unico) indicating Swiss work income, request for tax credit (CE framework), and 2024 exemption (€7,500 or €10,000 depending on seniority).

What work permit do you need to live in Casale Litta and work in Ticino?
The G Permit (border worker) allows you to work in Switzerland while maintaining your residence in Italy. It is the standard permit for frontier workers. The worker would automatically join the AVS (old-age pension), AI (disability), IPG (unemployment) and the LPP fund (second pillar) from the first day of work. The alternative is Permit B, which requires the transfer of the actual residence in Switzerland with significantly higher housing costs.
How is the tax calculated on a Swiss border worker's salary?
Tax at source is withheld ONLY in Switzerland, not in both countries. The border crossing is subject to federal and cantonal progressive Ticino tax, plus AVS/AI/IPG contributions (5.3% employee), LAINF (0.7–1.5%), LPP (7–18% by age group from 25 years). In Italy, Swiss income is subject to progressive IRPEF (23% up to €28,000, 35% up to €50,000, 43% over), but the border worker is entitled to the tax credit (CE framework, model 730) to avoid double taxation. What are the journey times from Casale Litta to the main Ticino work centres?
They depend on the destination and the conditions at the passes (Chiasso, Brogeda, Gaggiolo). For Lugano or Mendrisio, daily commuting is possible in 60–90 minutes under normal conditions. For Bellinzona or Locarno, the time increases to 120–150 minutes. Morning (7–9) and evening (17–19) rush hours can cause queues at crossings, significantly lengthening times. It is recommended to check the waiting times at the crossings before finalising the choice of work area.
What does it take to become a border worker at Casale Litta? What are the main steps?
