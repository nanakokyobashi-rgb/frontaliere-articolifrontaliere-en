Giro d'Italia in Ticino: roads closed to traffic (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 7 May 2026

On May 26, the Giro d'Italia passes through Ticino. Here are the roads closed to traffic and the closure times.

Context

In brief

  • Giro d'Italia in Ticino on May 26
  • Roads closed from 11:45 to 17:30
  • Disruptions expected on highways and main roads

Key facts

  • What: Road closures for the Giro d'Italia
  • When: May 26, 2024, from 11:45 to 17:30
  • Where: Bellinzona, Claro, Arbedo-Castione, Riviera, Biasca, Serravalle, Acquarossa, Blenio, Pollegio, Giornico, Faido, Carì
  • Who: Road Gendarmerie of the Cantonal Police and Organizing Committee of the Ticino Stage
  • Cost: Not specified

The Giro d'Italia will pass through Ticino with the 16th stage, Bellinzona-Carì, scheduled for Tuesday, May 26. The race will turn the territory pink, but it will cause some traffic disruptions. The Road Gendarmerie of the Cantonal Police and the Organizing Committee of the Ticino Stage have announced the roads that will be closed to traffic on the day of the race, between 11:45 and 17:30.

Highways

The exit ramps of the Bellinzona Nord (45) highway junction will be completely closed to traffic from 11:45 to 14:15. The highway exits of Bellinzona Centro, Biasca, Giornico, Chiggiogna, and Varenzo may also experience disruptions.

Bellinzona

In Bellinzona, the affected roads include Via Pellandini, Viale stazione, Piazza Collegiata, Via Nosetto, Piazza Nosetto, Via Camminata, Via Dogana, Piazza Governo, Via Orico, Piazza Orico, Viale Portone, Via Mirasole, Via L. Lavizzari, Via V. Vela, Via H. Guisan, Via S. Gottardo, with closures from 11:45 to 14:15.
