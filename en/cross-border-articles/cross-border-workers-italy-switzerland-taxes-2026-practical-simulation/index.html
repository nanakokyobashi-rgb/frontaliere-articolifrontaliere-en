The dates that separate the regimes

The threshold indicated for old cross-border workers is 17 July 2023. Those who were already such before that date are old cross-border workers. An exemption of €7,500 is foreseen for them, together with a transitional regime valid from 2024 to 2033 and exclusive Swiss taxation.

For new cross-border commuters, from 2024, taxation will be concurrent. Switzerland applies the withholding tax provided for in the agreement; Italy taxes income after the €10,000 allowance and recognizes the Swiss tax credit. In the 730 form, the credit for income produced abroad is managed in part G.

The fiscal and contribution perimeter

The Italy-Switzerland Double Taxation Agreement was signed on 9 March 1976. Switzerland is not a member of the EU or EEA.

For the Italian IRPEF from 2026, the source indicates 23% up to €28,000, 33% from €28,001 to €50,000 and 43% above €50,000. The rule is reported in Law 199/2025, article 1, paragraph 3. For incomes 2024–2025 the reference indicated is 23%, 35% and 43%.

Tax rates, including withholding tax, are established by federal and cantonal laws. At federal level they are administered by AFC/ESTV and the cantonal tax administrations; UFAS does not determine these rates and BFS collects statistical data, but does not set them. In the Swiss pay slip, AVS/AI/IPG is worth 5.3% for the employee, AD/AC 1.1% with a cap of CHF 148'200, LAINF 0.7–1.5% and LPP 7–18% for the age group from 25 years.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between old and new frontiersmen in 2026?
Old frontier workers, active before 17 July 2023, benefit from an exemption of €7,500 and a transitional regime until 2033 with exclusive Swiss taxation. The new frontier workers, on the other hand, have a deductible of €10,000 and are subject to concurrent taxation between Italy and Switzerland.
How does the Italian IRPEF for border workers work in 2026?
From 2026, the Italian IRPEF for frontier workers is 23% up to €28,000, 33% from €28,001 to €50,000 and 43% over €50,000. These rates apply when income is subject to taxation in Italy, such as for new frontier workers after the €10,000 deductible.
What contributions are planned for border workers in Switzerland?
Border workers in Switzerland pay contributions for AVS/AI/IPG (5.3%), AD/AC (1.1% cap CHF 148'200), LAINF (0.7–1.5%) and LPP (7–18% by age group from 25 years). These contributions are separate from taxes and are distinct in nature.
