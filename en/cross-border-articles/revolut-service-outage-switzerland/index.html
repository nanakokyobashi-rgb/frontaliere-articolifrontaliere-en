Up to 1,3 million users in Switzerland may have been affected by the Revolut outage. The British digital bank reported difficulties accessing some services on the morning of Tuesday, October 20, 2026. The operations affected included payments, deposits and currency conversions.

The disruptions began shortly after 8:00 in Switzerland. The source also reports repercussions for the services of banks, companies and online platforms in Europe. For this reason, the incident is described as an outage extending beyond the relationship between Swiss users and Revolut alone, although no list of the entities affected is provided.

The stated cause and the return of services

The stated cause is an outage at Google Cloud, whose European data center in London was affected by serious network disruptions. Google stated that the problem had been resolved and that services were gradually returning to operation.

Recovery is not yet complete: customers may still be unable to create new workloads, while restoration for existing ones is estimated, approximately, to be complete by the afternoon. The source does not provide a more precise time.

In a message issued on October 20, Revolut reassured customers: all users' money and data are safe. The company added that it had been working on a solution since the problem was identified that morning and apologized to the people affected for the disruption.
