Growing sectors: wanted refugees, prospects for job seekers in Ticino

The Ticino labour market is experiencing significant growth, offering excellent opportunities for job seekers. The key sectors that are establishing themselves are the technology sector and the healthcare sector, both characterised by high demand for labour and strong professional growth.

Wanted refugees: technology sector

The technology sector is one of the fastest growing sectors in Ticino. The growing demand for professionals with skills in software development, engineering and IT is attracting large numbers of refugees to Switzerland. According to Swiss government data, Switzerland took in around 200,000 refugees in 2020, with most of them concentrated in Ticino and the other northern regions.

According to research conducted by Confindustria ticinese, the number of jobs in Ticino in the IT and technology sector could increase by 20% by 2025.

Wanted Refugees: Healthcare

The healthcare sector is also one of the fastest growing sectors in Ticino. The shortage of medical personnel in particular is a crucial problem for the Ticino healthcare offer. According to the Swiss Ministry of Health, Switzerland welcomed around 20,000 refugees in 2020, with most of them concentrated in Ticino and the other northern regions.
