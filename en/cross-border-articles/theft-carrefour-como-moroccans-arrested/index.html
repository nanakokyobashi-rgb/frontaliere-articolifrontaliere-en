Two Moroccans arrested for Carrefour theft in Como (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 5 May 2026

Two homeless Moroccan citizens were arrested for attempted robbery and aggravated theft after an incident at the Carrefour on via Recchi.

Context

In brief

  • Two Moroccans arrested for theft and assault on Carrefour security guard in Como
  • A 31-year-old for attempted robbery, a 23-year-old for attempted aggravated theft
  • Stolen bottle of wine returned

Key facts

  • What: Attempted robbery and aggravated theft
  • When: 05/05/2026, midnight
  • Where: Carrefour in via Recchi, Como
  • Who: State Police of Como
  • Amount: Stolen bottle of wine

The State Police of Como arrested two Moroccan citizens without fixed abode in Italy for an incident that occurred last night at the Carrefour in via Recchi. A 31-year-old was arrested for attempted robbery, while a 23-year-old was arrested for attempted aggravated theft. The incident began when the 23-year-old tried to steal a bottle of wine by hiding it in his pants. The security officer noticed and asked him to leave the merchandise, but the young man threatened and punched him in the chest. The accomplice, the 31-year-old, tried to flee but was stopped by the officers. The stolen merchandise was returned to the security officer. After consulting the Judicial Authority and evaluating the specific police prejudices, the two were arrested.

Security implications

This episode once again raises the issue of security in large shopping centers and supermarkets. The presence of security personnel is fundamental to prevent theft and assaults, but also to ensure a safe environment for customers and workers. The police of Como acted promptly, demonstrating the importance of effective collaboration between law enforcement and private security personnel.

Operational details

Analysis of Crime Dynamics

The episode at the Carrefour in Como reflects a growing trend of thefts and assaults in public places. The presence of homeless individuals and a lack of economic resources can contribute to such behaviors. However, it is important to consider that most people in vulnerable situations do not resort to crime. Authorities must address the problem with a balanced approach, ensuring public safety without criminalizing entire communities.

Impact on the Community

Episodes like this can influence the perception of security in the local community. It is crucial that authorities communicate transparently with citizens, providing accurate information and reassurances about the management of the situation. Collaboration between police, supermarkets, and the community is essential to prevent future incidents and maintain a safe environment for everyone.

Preventive Measures

To prevent thefts and assaults, supermarkets can adopt various measures. Installing advanced surveillance systems, increasing security personnel, and training staff on how to handle conflict situations are just a few examples. Additionally, collaboration with local law enforcement can improve emergency response and prevent incidents.

Comparison with Other Cities

Como is not the only city facing security problems in supermarkets. Similar episodes are recorded in many other Italian cities. However, the response of the authorities can vary significantly. In some cities, the police act quickly and effectively, while in others the response may be slower. Collaboration between different agencies and the sharing of best practices can improve security in all cities.

Key points

What to do in case of theft or assault

If you witness a theft or an assault in a supermarket, it's important to stay calm and follow certain procedures. First of all, try not to intervene directly, but immediately notify the security personnel or the police. Provide all the necessary information to identify the perpetrators and help the authorities to intervene quickly.

Procedure for security staff

Security personnel must be trained to handle conflict situations. In case of theft or assault, they must follow specific protocols to ensure their own safety and that of others. This includes the use of communication tools to alert the police and the application of non-lethal disarming techniques if necessary.

Collaboration with law enforcement

Collaboration between security personnel and law enforcement is crucial. Security staff must be able to provide accurate and timely information to the authorities, facilitating the identification and arrest of the perpetrators. Moreover, sharing information between different agencies can improve the prevention and management of similar incidents in the future.
