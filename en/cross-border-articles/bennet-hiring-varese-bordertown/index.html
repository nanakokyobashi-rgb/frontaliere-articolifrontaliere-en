Bennet is looking for staff at Lavena Ponte Tresa (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 2 September 2026

On Friday, September 4, the Bennet hypermarket in Lavena Ponte Tresa hosts a day dedicated to work with open positions for sale and fresh departments.

Key facts

  • What: Recruitment for Bennet hypermarket
  • When: Friday, September 4th, 2026, 1:00 PM–6:00 PM
  • Where: Lavena Ponte Tresa (Varese province, Italy)
  • Who: Bennet (leading distribution company from Como)
  • Positions: Sales assistants, fresh department workers, customer service roles
  • Application: Email personale@bennet.com or come in person

Bennet is launching a new recruitment drive for its hypermarket in Lavena Ponte Tresa, strengthening its presence in the upper Varesotto area and offering concrete job opportunities, including for young people. The initiative comes at a time when the retail sector continues to seek resources to manage operational peaks and consolidate customer service.

On Friday, September 4th from 1:00 PM to 6:00 PM, at the Bennet hypermarket in Lavena Ponte Tresa, there will be a day dedicated exclusively to recruitment. Candidates can submit their paper CVs and meet directly with Bennet's Human Resources team for an initial operational contact and discussion on the open positions.

Which positions are open

Bennet's searches cover various operational areas. They are looking for sales assistants involved in managing product ranges in commercial spaces and arranging merchandise. Simultaneously, the company is seeking collaborators interested in an internal training path in the fresh departments, where activities of production, processing, and food transformation take place.

Operational details

Bennet is a well-established company in the retail sector in Como, with a historic presence in the province of Varese. The hypermarket in Lavena Ponte Tresa represents a strategic location in a geographically crucial area for cross-border logistics: the municipality overlooks Lake Ceresio (known as Lake Lugano on the Ticino side) directly bordering the Canton Ticino. For Ticino residents, this opening represents a concrete opportunity in the nearby Italian job market, without moving away from the border area. Lavena Ponte Tresa is just a few kilometers from the main cross-border crossings and the Ticino border. Formation and career prospects The emphasis that Bennet places on internal training is a central element of the proposal. Selected candidates will benefit from structured field training programs, with direct tutoring from experienced colleagues and constant updates on operational procedures. The company emphasizes that professional growth is favored over time, even for candidates entering the sector without prior experience. This reduces barriers to entry into the retail market. The path in the fresh departments is particularly relevant in terms of acquiring technical skills in food production, processing, and transformation. Such qualifications increase professional employability in the European job market and offer future career mobility. Implications for Ticino cross-border workers A Ticino resident who wishes to work in Lavena Ponte Tresa as a Bennet employee will need to apply for the Permit G: the work permit for cross-border workers residing in the Canton Ticino and working in Italy with daily return. Taxation on an Italian salary is regulated by the Italo-Swiss Convention of December 9, 1976, and the New Cross-Border Agreement (in force from January 1, 2024). The income from dependent work is subject to withholding tax in Italy, while Switzerland recognizes a specific tax credit to avoid double taxation. To learn more about the procedures, timelines, and documents required to apply for Permit G, we recommend consulting the official guide to cross-border work permits or contacting your municipality of residence in Ticino and the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM).

Key points

The application procedure for Bennet is simple and straightforward. Those wishing to participate in the selection have two options: come to the hypermarket in Lavena Ponte Tresa on September 4th from 13.00 to 18.00 with a paper CV, or send your resume in advance via email to personale@bennet.com to be included in the list of candidates before the dedicated day.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who can apply to the Bennet hypermarket in Lavena Ponte Tresa?
Anyone interested in working in the large-scale retail sector. Bennet also welcomes candidates with no previous experience, as long as they are motivated. The dedicated day of 4 September is open to all. If you are a Ticino border worker, you will need to have a valid G Permit before starting work.
What are the hours and working conditions offered?
Bennet offers mainly day shifts and requires availability to work on holidays, organized with rotations to ensure operational continuity. The exact times will be communicated during the interview with the Human Resources team on the dedicated day of 4 September.
How do I send my resume to Bennet?
You can show up on September 4 from 1:00 pm to 6:00 pm at the Lavena Ponte Tresa hypermarket with a paper CV and identity document, or send your CV by email to personale@bennet.com in advance to appear on the candidate list before the day.
What is the training path in fresh departments?
It is an internal training program on food production, processing and transformation. Bennet offers support in the field from experienced colleagues and constant updates, with the aim of promoting professional growth over time, even for candidates with no previous experience.
If I am a Ticino border worker residing in Ticino, am I entitled to the G Permit?
Yes, if resident in Ticino and selected by Bennet, you will be entitled to apply for the G Permit (work permit for border workers from Canton Ticino) before starting work. You will need to contact your municipality of residence or the Secretary of State for Migration (SEM) for the required procedure and documents. The 1976 Italian-Swiss Convention and the New Frontier Agreement (in force from 1 January 2024) regulate the taxation of your income.
