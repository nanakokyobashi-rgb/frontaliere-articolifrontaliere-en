Equivalence of Italian qualifications in Switzerland: what to do

This page concerns the recognition in Switzerland of an Italian qualification and the steps to verify. Here are some pointers to help you understand whether your education is worth the same access to jobs and career opportunities as a Swiss education.

First of all, remember that the equivalence of the Italian qualification is not automatically recognized in Switzerland. You must submit an official request to SECO, the Swiss integration and citizenship authority.

According to SECO, Italian degrees can be equivalent to Swiss degrees if:

  • The duration is similar or identical;
  • You have the same academic and disciplinary training;
  • You have the same training objectives and the same learning requirements;
  • We have the same evaluation standards.

For example, an Italian degree in Economics and Finance equivalent to a Swiss degree in Economics and Business (which have the same duration and requirements) could be worth the same access to jobs and career opportunities.

To submit your request, you must follow these steps.

1. Contact SECO: To find out the procedure and specific requirements, contact the administration of your canton or consult the SECO website.

2. Submit your request: Once you have received the information, submit your equivalence request to SECO. You must attach:

  • A copy of your Italian qualification;
  • A declaration of your educational and professional value;
  • A declaration of your level of knowledge of the official Swiss language;
  • A letter of recommendation from your Italian employer.

3. Wait for the decision: Your request will be analyzed and evaluated by SECO. If your Italian degree is equivalent, SECO will communicate the decision to you. If it is not, it will propose that you submit additional evidence to justify your Italian qualification.

Finally, remember that the equivalence of your Italian qualification in Switzerland can impact your access to jobs and career opportunities. Therefore, it is essential to carefully evaluate your training and present your request for equivalence in a detailed and detailed manner.
