From the report to the discovery

Three men were arrested in the Mendrisiotto last Wednesday: two Slovaks aged 39 and 28 and a 30-year-old Czech, all residing in their respective countries of origin. The three are suspected of having participated in scams targeting elderly people through the so-called “shock calls.”

The victim’s relatives triggered the investigation into a scam that took place on September 21. The report led to work by the cantonal police and the Lugano police, ultimately resulting in the identification of a vehicle with Czech license plates.

On Wednesday, in Chiasso, staff from the Federal Office for Customs and Border Security located the trio’s vehicle, finding cash and valuables inside.

The three are suspected of having committed a scam that morning in Melano. Another one, already planned for the afternoon, was thwarted. Their possible involvement in other similar incidents remains to be verified.
