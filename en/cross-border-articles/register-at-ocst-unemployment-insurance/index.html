No appointments needed. You can advertise online from home in minutes. Register with the Regional Employment Office responsible for your domicile — within the first day of unemployment.

To register, you need some documents. These include: identity card, employment contract and dismissal certificate. You can upload these documents directly from home through our secure online listing.

Here's how to do it:

1. Go to the OCST website and select your location. 2. Click the "Register" button and follow the instructions. 3. Upload your required documents. 4. Fill out the registration form and submit it.

⚠️ Attention: if you already have an application in progress at another unemployment fund, you will have to communicate your unemployment within 48 hours.
