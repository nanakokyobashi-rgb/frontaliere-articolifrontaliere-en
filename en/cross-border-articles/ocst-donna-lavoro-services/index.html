OCST woman-work: protection and support (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 14 August 2026

Support services, advice and legal protection for workers in difficulty, with courses and professional guidance.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Support for labour disputes
  • Career Change Orientation

Key facts

  • What: Support and consulting service
  • When: Released in March 2025
  • Where: OCST Offices
  • Who: OCST woman-worker
  • Amount: Not specified

In March 2025, the OCST women's organisation reaffirmed its commitment to providing concrete support and advice to women workers who need to assert their rights in the professional context. The activities are aimed at anyone facing problems in the workplace, offering targeted assistance to resolve disputes with employers or to manage sensitive situations. The structure operates through its local offices, ensuring a point of reference for those in difficulty and in need of guidance or personalised help. The legal protection and defence of wage rights are fundamental pillars of the activity carried out in favour of employees. Professional Dispute Assistance

Workers who face dismissals deemed unfair, problems related to not receiving the salary due or situations of bankruptcy of their company can find targeted support. The organisation also intervenes in cases of problems with insurance companies

Operational details

In addition to the resolution of immediate disputes, the institution offers paths dedicated to those who wish to change their professional trajectory or start a path of growth of their skills. Specific courses and targeted consultations are organized to deal with career changes, overcome moments of emotional blockage related to a difficult work environment and improve the overall well-being of the person. This integrated approach aims to promote a world of work characterised by greater equity and equal opportunities for all workers active in the territory.

Fundamental laws and rights

Knowing the regulations in force

A central aspect of the information activity concerns the dissemination of regulations that protect female labour. Detailed information is made available regarding leave rights, compliance with equal pay and the correct management of working hours established by the provisions in force. The in-depth knowledge of these regulatory instruments allows workers to recognise any violations and to act promptly to defend their professional position, relying on expert and personalised support.

Key points

To access the services offered and receive personalised advice, interested parties can go directly to the organisation's regional offices. Active listening and detailed analysis of the individual position make it possible to define the most suitable strategy to address each specific problem, whether it is related to salary disputes, relational difficulties in the company or the need for professional retraining. Collaboration with external support networks enriches the offer of guidance, ensuring constant support at every stage of working life.

Frequently Asked Questions
What services does OCST woman-work offer?
The organisation offers support for problems with the employer, unfair dismissals, non-payment of wages, difficulties with insurance companies, harassment, orientation for job changes and training courses.
How can I access counselling?
You can receive personalised help and advice by going directly to the organisation's offices in the area.
Is information on contractual rights provided?
Yes, all the necessary information is provided regarding the rights regarding leave, equal pay and compliance with the working hours provided for by the regulations.
