Operational details

Prison guards play a fundamental role in the management of penitentiary institutions. They work closely with prisoners, providing them with assistance and support. Training for this role is crucial, as it requires in-depth knowledge of the rules and procedures for managing prisons. The Canton of Ticino offers complete training and a stable career for those interested in working in this sector. Candidates must have a good knowledge of German and French, in addition to Italian. The Canton of Ticino has recently announced the availability of 15 job positions as prison guards. These positions are available at the prisons in Lugano, Bellinzona, and Locarno. The choice of working in one of these institutions offers the opportunity to live and work in municipalities like Lugano, with a population of around 63,000 inhabitants, or Bellinzona, with a population of around 43,000 inhabitants. To be admitted to the training course, candidates must be at least 21 years old and no older than 40 years old. Additionally, they must have a high school diploma and a good knowledge of the official languages of the Canton of Ticino. The training lasts around 12 months and includes both theoretical and practical lessons. The courses are held at the police school of the Canton of Ticino, in Giubiasco. The law on personnel in the Canton of Ticino, in force since January 1, 2019, establishes that public employees must have in-depth knowledge of the rules and procedures for managing prisons. Furthermore, the law provides that public employees must follow a course of continuous training to update their knowledge and skills. The salary for prison guards in the Canton of Ticino is around 55,000 Swiss francs per year, plus an allowance of 5,000 Swiss francs for night and holiday work. Additionally, public employees are entitled to sickness and accident insurance, as well as a pension. To work as a prison guard in the Canton of Ticino, it is necessary to follow a series of steps: sending the application for admission to the training course, passing a selection test, attending the training course, passing a final exam, and being hired as a prison guard. > 'Our goal is to offer high-quality training to prepare our employees to perform their role effectively and safely,' said the director of the police school of the Canton of Ticino. Compared to other Swiss cantons, the Canton of Ticino offers more complete training and a more stable career for prison guards. For example, the Canton of Bern offers 9 months of training, while the Canton of Zurich offers 15 months of training. Additionally, the Canton of Ticino offers a higher salary than other cantons, with a 10% increase compared to 2020. In summary, working as a prison guard in the Canton of Ticino offers the opportunity to have a stable career and complete training. Candidates must have a good knowledge of the official languages of the canton and follow a training course to be admitted to this role. The salary is competitive, and public employees are entitled to sickness and accident insurance, as well as a pension.

Frequently Asked Questions
When does the call for applications for prison guards in the Canton of Ticino expire?
The call for applications expires on 31 March 2026.
What is the duration and cost of the training course for prison guards in the Canton of Ticino?
The training course lasts 12 months (6 months of theory + 6 months of practice) and costs 12,000 Swiss francs.
What are the language requirements to apply as a prison guard in the Canton of Ticino?
The candidate must be fluent in Italian, the main language of the canton, and have a good knowledge of both German and French. Mastery of at least two of the three languages is verified during the selection test, with written and oral tests assessing communication skills and understanding of prison rules in all official languages.
How much does a prison guard earn in Ticino and what additional allowances do they receive?
The annual basic salary is around 55,000 Swiss francs. In addition, there is an allowance of 5,000 francs for night and holiday work, bringing the total compensation to around 60,000 francs. In addition, the employee benefits from health and accident insurance, compulsory pension contributions and any premiums for continuing education.
How does the selection test for the prison guards competition of the Canton of Ticino take place?
The process involves a written test on prison law and conflict management, followed by a physical test (running, weightlifting and endurance). After the physical assessment, candidates undergo a motivational interview and a psychological assessment. Only those who pass all stages are admitted to the 12-month training course.
