An explosion last night at the Las Quintas coal mine in Cucunubá, Cundinamarca, caused the death of four miners. This was reported by the fire captain of Cundinamarca, Álvaro Farfán Vargas, who stated that authorities are now focusing their efforts on investigating the causes. The disaster reignites the debate on the issue of safety in mining activities in Colombia, just days after a similar incident that caused nine deaths.

The incident has raised concerns about safety in Colombia's coal mines, a sector already under scrutiny after a previous incident that caused nine victims. Authorities are now working to understand the causes of the explosion and to prevent future incidents.
