Context

At 4.30 pm, the All Time Shareholders broadcast will host Marco Contini, head of Banca Etica's territorial initiative group. Contini will talk about the initiatives carried out and future projects of the group of volunteers active in Ticino. Banca Etica is known for its commitment to promoting ethical and sustainable finance, a particularly relevant issue for border workers working in Ticino and residing in Italy.
