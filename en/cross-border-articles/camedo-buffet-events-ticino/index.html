Camedo, the buffet ready to become the perfect event location

The small village of Camedo, nestled in the heart of Ticino and just steps away from the Italian border, is gearing up for a significant transformation that could breathe new life into the local economy. The old FART (Ferrovie Autolinee Regionali Ticinesi) building, known for its buffet, will soon be repurposed into a hub dedicated to regional promotion. Work began in January 2023 and is expected to continue until late spring, with a total investment of 450,000 Swiss francs, partially funded by the Municipality of Centovalli and partially by cantonal grants.

A project for economic revival

The goal of this initiative is to create a multifunctional center capable of hosting conferences, exhibitions, and other events, attracting visitors and professionals from both Switzerland and Italy. Once operational, the venue is expected to accommodate up to 150 attendees per event, with an annual schedule of up to 50 events. Comparisons with similar projects, such as the Dannemann Center in Brissago or the Dimitri Theater in Verscio, suggest that such initiatives can generate annual revenues exceeding 500,000 francs through hospitality, dining, and related services.

An opportunity for the region

This project aligns with broader tourism promotion strategies for Ticino, as outlined in the Regional Development Plan approved by the Canton in 2021. The facility will feature modular spaces, an exhibition area, a conference room equipped with audiovisual technology, and a small catering kitchen. Demand for the venue is already high, with early interest from wedding planners, corporate meeting organizers, and art exhibition curators. Events have already been booked for the summer season.

Operational details

The transformation of the old FART building in Camedo into a hub for regional promotion represents a strategic opportunity to revitalize the local economy and capitalize on a key location in the Locarno region. This project, with an estimated investment of 1.2 million Swiss francs, was approved by the Municipality of Centovalli in 2022 under the Cantonal Economic Promotion Law (LPrEco), which allows up to 25% contributions for regional development initiatives.

The former buffet, which served railway travelers for years, will be replaced by a modern, multifunctional facility. The new building will include modular meeting rooms capable of hosting up to 150 people, a fully equipped kitchen for tailored catering services, and a large parking area with 50 spaces. This setup will enable the organization of corporate conferences, training seminars, and small international congresses. For instance, a company based in Bellinzona could use the venue for a two-day seminar, taking advantage of the natural surroundings for team-building activities.

Camedo's strategic location, just 20 minutes by car from Locarno and close to the Italian border, is another key advantage. Its proximity to the Centovallina railway, which connects Domodossola to Locarno, ensures sustainable mobility for both participants and organizers. In line with sustainability goals, the project includes the installation of solar panels on the roof, supporting Ticino's objective to reduce CO2 emissions by 50% by 2030.

📊 Practical comparison: Currently, many SMEs in the region travel to Lugano or even beyond the Gotthard for events, incurring logistical costs that can exceed 5,000 CHF for venue rentals and services. The new Camedo facility aims to offer a local alternative with more competitive rates, stimulating internal demand.

Key points

Camedo, the buffet ready to become the perfect event location

For event organizers, choosing Camedo as a venue could prove to be both a captivating and strategic decision. Located in the heart of Centovalli, at the border between Switzerland and Italy, this small village offers a unique combination of breathtaking natural landscapes and modern facilities, perfectly suited for medium-sized events.

A mix of nature and functionality

Camedo’s main facility, formerly known for its railway buffet, has recently been expanded to include a 120-square-meter multifunctional hall, ideal for corporate meetings, workshops, or private celebrations. The hall is equipped with high-speed Wi-Fi, state-of-the-art audiovisual equipment, and a catering area with a professional kitchen, making it perfect for events requiring customized dining services. For those seeking a more intimate setting, a smaller 40-square-meter room is also available, designed for small meetings or training sessions.

An economical and original option

One notable aspect is the competitive cost compared to other venues in Canton Ticino. For example, renting the multifunctional hall for a day costs approximately 500 CHF, with an additional 25 CHF per person for buffet services. By comparison, a similar-sized venue in Locarno could cost up to 1,000 CHF per day, excluding catering. The availability of an outdoor area with stunning mountain views makes Camedo an especially attractive option for spring and summer events.

Regulatory and practical aspects

Organizing an event in Ticino requires compliance with specific regulations. For instance, events with more than 300 attendees must submit an authorization request to the Municipality of Centovalli at least 30 days in advance, in accordance with the Cantonal Public Events Regulation (effective since January 1, 2022). Additionally, live music events must contribute to SUISA, with fees varying based on the number of attendees (e.g., 132 CHF for an event with 100 guests).

Checklist for organizing an event in Camedo

  • Booking: Check room availability at least 2 months in advance.
  • Permits: Obtain necessary authorization from the Municipality of Centovalli.
  • Budgeting: Calculate costs, including rental, catering, equipment, and any applicable fees.
  • Transportation: Arrange shuttles or inform participants about train connections (the Camedo buffet is located next to the FART station).
  • Promotion: Create promotional materials highlighting the location’s unique natural setting.
