A8: nighttime closures for Castronno-Solbiate overpass

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 9 October 2026

TL;DR

  • The A8 closes between Castronno and Solbiate Arno Albizzate
  • First night: October 12, toward Milan
  • Second night: October 13, toward Varese

Key facts

  • Work → maintenance work on an overpass
  • First window → 21:00-5:00, from October 12 to 13
  • Second window → 21:00-5:00, from October 13 to 14
  • First exit → Castronno for vehicles toward Milan
  • Second exit → Solbiate Arno Albizzate for vehicles toward Varese

  • Entry toward Milan → Cavaria interchange

Monday, October 12, from 21:00 to 5:00 on Tuesday, October 13, the A8 Milan-Varese will be closed to traffic between the Castronno and Solbiate Arno Albizzate toll stations for maintenance work on an overpass. The closure will affect vehicles heading toward Milan. For a cross-border commuter who uses this connection for their commute to work, the first thing to note is the combination of night and direction: the work will affect both directions over two nights, with different arrangements.

The first night: direction toward Milan

Motorists heading south will have to exit at Castronno. Signs will direct them onto the ordinary road network: the indicated route runs via the SS341 state road and Via Monte Rosa, Via Lario, Via San Carlo, Via Cavour, Via Pasubio, Via Santa Croce, Via Castiglioni and Via Maggiolino. The diversion continues via Via Caronno Varesino, Via delle Industrie, Viale Europa, Via Molinello and Via Grandi, until rejoining the A8 at the Solbiate Arno Albizzate toll station.

The second night: direction toward Varese

From 21:00 on Tuesday, October 13, to 5:00 on Wednesday, October 14, the closure will affect the Varese-bound carriageway between Solbiate Arno Albizzate and Castronno. Those traveling northbound will have to leave the motorway at Solbiate Arno Albizzate and follow the ordinary road network. The closure will remain in effect from 21:00 to 5:00 the following morning.

Operational details

What changes for the commuter

The practical reading of the notice requires separating three elements: the night concerned, the direction of travel and the point at which to exit or re-enter. The first closure concerns vehicles heading toward Milan; the second concerns those heading toward Varese. For a cross-border commuter traveling along the A8, confusing the two time windows means following the wrong detour: the source assigns a different exit to each direction and indicates a reverse route on the following night.

The distinction between closure and entry

During the works, the Solbiate Arno Albizzate interchange will be completely closed for entry in both directions. It is therefore not enough to choose the right direction: that access point must also be ruled out. The source indicates two precise alternatives. Anyone who needs to enter the A8 toward Varese is advised to use the Castronno station. Those traveling toward Milan can enter via the Cavaria interchange. Castronno and Cavaria are not presented as indistinct alternatives, but are associated with two different directions.

Key points

How to organize the journey

Four-step procedure

1. Mark the correct time window. For the Milan direction, consider from 21:00 on Monday, October 12th, to 5:00 on Tuesday, October 13th. For the Varese direction, consider from 21:00 on Tuesday, October 13th, to 5:00 on Wednesday, October 14th. The first thing to check is therefore the direction, not just the date on the calendar.

2. If you are heading toward Milan, exit at Castronno. Follow the signs on the SS341 and then the sequence of via Monte Rosa, via Lario, via San Carlo, via Cavour, via Pasubio, via Santa Croce, via Castiglioni and via Maggiolino. Continue on via Caronno Varesino, via delle Industrie, viale Europa, via Molinello and via Grandi. The planned re-entry is at the Solbiate Arno Albizzate toll station.

3. To reach Varese, motorists must exit at Solbiate Arno Albizzate and follow the regular road network in reverse.

4. If you need to enter the motorway during the works, do not use the Solbiate Arno Albizzate interchange: the source indicates that it is completely closed to entry in both directions. For Varese, the Castronno station is recommended; for Milan, the Cavaria interchange. This access procedure is separate from the exit routes.

During the diversion, the signage placed along the regular road network remains the reference. To check traffic before departure, consult storico traffico e code ai valichi.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the dates and times of the closures on the A8?
The first closure runs from 21:00 on Monday, October 12, to 5:00 on Tuesday, October 13, in the direction of Milan. The second runs from 21:00 on Tuesday, October 13, to 5:00 on Wednesday, October 14, in the direction of Varese.
Where do I need to exit if I am traveling toward Milan on the night of October 12?
In the direction of Milan, you must exit at Castronno and follow the detour on SS341 and municipal roads until re-entering the A8 at the Solbiate Arno Albizzate toll booth.
Can the Solbiate Arno Albizzate interchange be used to enter the motorway during the works?
No, the Solbiate Arno Albizzate interchange is completely closed to incoming traffic in both directions. To head toward Varese, use the Castronno station; for Milan, the Cavaria interchange.
