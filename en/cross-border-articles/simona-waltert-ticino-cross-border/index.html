Simona Waltert advances to Rome's second round (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 8 May 2026

The Grisons tennis player qualifies for the second round of the WTA 1'000 in Rome, joining three other Swiss players

Context

In brief

  • Simona Waltert qualifies for the second round in Rome
  • Victory in three sets against Yuliia Starodubtseva
  • Will face Hailey Baptiste in the next round

Key facts

  • What: Qualification for the second round of the WTA 1'000 in Rome
  • When: 7 May 2026
  • Where: Foro Italico, Rome
  • Who: Simona Waltert
  • Amount: Not yet specified

The Grisons tennis player Simona Waltert has reached the second round of the WTA 1'000 in Rome, joining Belinda Bencic, Rebeka Masarova, and Viktorija Golubic in the Swiss poker. The match, played over two days due to rain, saw Waltert prevail over Ukrainian Yuliia Starodubtseva in three sets (7-5, 4-6, 6-1) after just under three hours of play. Started on Wednesday afternoon, the match was interrupted and then postponed to the next day due to the precipitation.

In the second round, Simona Waltert will face American Hailey Baptiste, who recently overcame Belinda Bencic in the eighth finals of the Madrid tournament. The Grisons player, ranked WTA 91, qualified for the main draw of the Foro Italico by passing through the qualifiers.
