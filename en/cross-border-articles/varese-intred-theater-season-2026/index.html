Varese: Teatro Intred opens 2026/27 season

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 9 October 2026

On October 11, the 2026/27 season of Teatro INTRED begins at Varese.

Context

TL;DR

  • The 2026/27 season of Teatro INTRED kicks off in Varese on October 11
  • Fabio De Luigi opens the lineup with BIOL
  • Gianluca Gotto arrives on October 17 with Questione di Karma
  • In November, music, theater, public outreach, and a tribute to Michael Jackson

Key facts

  • Season → 2026/27
  • Place → Teatro INTRED of Varese

  • First appointment → Fabio De Luigi, BIOL | In the flesh tour
  • Second appointment → Saturday 17 October, Gianluca Gotto, Question of Karma

October's debut

On Sunday, October 11, Fabio De Luigi will officially open the 2026/27 season of the Teatro INTRED in Varese. De Luigi will be the protagonist of BIOL | In flesh and bones tour, the event chosen to raise the curtain on the new calendar of the Varese room.

The evening focuses on the comedy that made De Luigi recognisable. The show focuses on characters, situations and aspects of everyday life, which De Luigi interprets with irony, rhythm and great interpretative ability. The return to the stage is awaited by the audience: the opening is therefore entrusted to a proposal in the name of fun and comedy.

A week later, on Saturday 17 October, Teatro INTRED will change registers with Gianluca Gotto and Question of Karma. Gotto is described as a writer, traveler and disseminator. The meeting will combine reflections and stories, leaving room for questions about the meaning of the experiences that run through our lives. It is an event that extends the season also to the size of the meeting and reflection.

The two events in October anticipate the identity of the season. The 2026/27 calendar brings together on the city stage established protagonists of the Italian scene and proposals of different genres, from music to comedy to theatre, aimed at spectators with different interests. The programming is rich and still evolving, with a path destined to accompany the Varese public in the coming months.

The INTRED Theatre is thus confirmed as a central space for the cultural and entertainment life of Varese. The structure welcomes different artists and proposals, transforming each appointment into a moment of encounter with the public. The opening with De Luigi and the transition to Gotto make visible, already in the first two dates, the variety announced for the bill: first the comedy of a return to the stage, then the reflection entrusted to a meeting with a writer, traveler and disseminator.

Operational details

November, the lineup expands

Three offerings for the first part of the month

As November arrives, Teatro INTRED expands the lineup announced for Varese. The month's calendar will bring leading figures from music, theater, entertainment, and public outreach to the stage, confirming the alternation between different genres and forms of expression.

The month will begin on Sunday, November 8, with Forza Venite Gente, the famous musical dedicated to the figure of San Francesco and presented in a special anniversary edition. According to the presentation, it is a show appreciated by several generations, weaving together music, theater, and emotion in a story still capable of engaging the audience.

On November 13, the stage will welcome Roberta Bruzzone with L'Epoca della Rabbia. The event will focus on the behaviors and dynamics, social and personal, that characterize the time in which we live. This offering also shifts register from the musical and brings observation and in-depth analysis to the program.

This first part of November will end with Human Nature Live Show, the tribute show dedicated to Michael Jackson. The show aims to evoke on stage the energy, musical dimension, and theatricality that distinguished the King of Pop. The lineup thus includes a musical and theatrical event that differs both from the musical about San Francesco and from the event with Bruzzone.

The November sequence shows the variety of the lineup: a musical story, an event dedicated to analyzing behaviors, and a tribute built around music and entertainment. Teatro INTRED presents this structure as part of a program designed for different audiences, without limiting itself to a single form of entertainment or theater.

Radio Bruno is listed as Teatro INTRED's radio media partner. The partnership accompanies a season that, after the events announced for November, will continue to offer music, theater, entertainment, and public outreach in the city's calendar.

Key points

The remainder of the season and ticketing

A schedule gradually taking shape

The October dates represent only the starting point: Teatro INTRED’s 2026/27 program will continue in November and beyond, with other highly anticipated events. In the following months, the offering will gradually be enriched with important names, quality performances, and different ways of experiencing theater in Varese.

The source therefore presents the program as a work still in progress. After the first part already announced, the schedule will continue to add new offerings, maintaining its approach for different audiences and its alternation between entertainment, music, theater, and educational content.

Ticket office contact details for Teatro di Varese are provided for spectators. Inquiries can be sent to biglietteria@teatrodivarese.com. The number 0332.482665 is also available during ticket office opening hours.

Advance ticket sales are available online at Ticketone.it. The Ticketone network’s points of sale are also listed, with the option to search for the nearest one. Before purchasing tickets, the notice invites readers to consult the page dedicated to information and regulations.

Frequently Asked Questions
When will the 2026/27 season of Teatro INTRED in Varese begin?
The 2026/27 season of the Teatro INTRED in Varese will officially begin on Sunday 11 October 2026. The opening is entrusted to the show 'BIOL | In flesh and bones tour' with Fabio De Luigi. The second appointment is scheduled for the following Saturday, October 17, with Gianluca Gotto and 'Question of Karma'.
What are the main appointments scheduled for November 2026?
The November line-up includes three main proposals: Sunday 8 November 'Forza Venite Gente', a musical dedicated to Saint Francis in an anniversary edition; on 13 November the appointment with Roberta Bruzzone and 'L 'Epoca della Rabbia'; and in the first part of the month the 'Human Nature Live Show', a tribute to Michael Jackson.
How do I buy tickets for shows?
Pre-sales are available online at Ticketone.it and at Ticketone circuit points of sale. The physical ticket office can be reached at 0332.482665 or by email at biglietteria@teatrodivarese.com. The opening hours are from 7 September 2026 to 31 May 2027, with specific bands on weekdays and afternoons on weekends.
