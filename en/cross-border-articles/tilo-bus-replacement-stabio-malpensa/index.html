TILO S40 and S50 Replaced by Buses for a Month

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 29 May 2026

From June 7 to July 5, 2024, TILO S40 and S50 services will be replaced by buses between Stabio, Varese, Gallarate, and Malpensa Airport.

Context

In brief

  • TILO S40 and S50 train connections replaced by buses for one month.
  • Bus replacement service between Stabio and Malpensa Airport.
  • Longer travel times expected.
  • Dedicated assistance for passengers with disabilities.

Key facts

  • What: Temporary replacement of TILO trains with buses.
  • When: From June 7 to July 5, 2024.
  • Where: Between Stabio, Varese, Gallarate, and Malpensa Airport.
  • Who: Rete Ferroviaria Italiana (RFI) and Trenord.
  • Amount: Not specified.

From June 7 to July 5, 2024, the TILO S40 and S50 train connections will be replaced by buses due to infrastructure enhancement works carried out by Rete Ferroviaria Italiana (RFI). This intervention affects the routes between Stabio, Varese, Gallarate, and Malpensa Airport, significantly impacting commuters who use these lines to travel between Italy and Switzerland.

Replacement bus service

The replacement bus service will be managed by Trenord and includes two types of connections:

  • A bus every 30 minutes between Stabio and Gallarate, stopping at all intermediate stations of the S40 and S50 lines.
  • A direct bus every hour between Stabio and Malpensa Airport, with no intermediate stops.

Passengers traveling between Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 of Malpensa Airport can use the internal airport services.

Limitations and travel times

Bicycles cannot be transported on the replacement buses. Additionally, seating will be limited, with longer travel times compared to trains. This may cause inconvenience to commuters, especially during peak hours.

Assistance for passengers with disabilities

TILO informs that passengers with disabilities can request assistance for boarding and alighting buses by calling the toll-free number 0800 007 102 from Switzerland or +41 800 007 102 from abroad, available from 5:00 AM to midnight. To organize assistance in Italian territory, passengers must communicate their needs to the dedicated number 800 210 955 at least 48 hours before departure.

This temporary replacement has been planned to allow for infrastructure enhancement works but may impact the travel times of cross-border workers who use these lines to reach their workplace.

Operational details

The replacement of TILO trains with buses represents a temporary but significant change for cross-border commuters who travel daily between the Canton of Ticino and Lombardy. This intervention, necessary to improve the railway infrastructure, will have a direct impact on travel times and travel comfort.

Impact on cross-border commuters Cross-border commuters who use the S40 and S50 lines to get to work will have to adapt to longer journey times and less seating availability. This could cause inconvenience, especially for those who have to comply with strict working hours or need to move quickly between home and office.

Comparison with the previous situation Before this replacement, TILO trains guaranteed fast and frequent connections between Stabio, Varese, Gallarate and Malpensa Airport. The replacement with buses, although temporary, introduces variables such as road traffic and the limited capacity of the vehicles, which could affect the punctuality and efficiency of the service.

Practical scenarios

  • Cross-border workers with flexible hours: Those who have the possibility to adapt their working hours could choose to travel in less congested time slots, reducing the risk of delays.
  • Cross-border commuters with the need for quick travel: For those who need to reach the workplace quickly, it may be useful to consider alternatives such as carpooling or the use of private vehicles.

During this time, it is advisable to plan your journeys further in advance, taking into account longer journey times and possible congestion of replacement buses. In addition, those with specific needs, such as transporting bicycles, will have to find alternative solutions, as buses do not allow this type of transport.
