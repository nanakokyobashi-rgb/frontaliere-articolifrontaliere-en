The transfer of the Chiasso post office could be an opportunity for the territory

Context

TL;DR

  • Post Office in Chiasso to move from Independence Square to Via Bossi in 2026.
  • Swiss Post aims to reduce costs and improve services with the transfer.
  • Municipality must manage change to minimize negative impacts on local community.

Key facts

  • Data trasferimento: 2 aprile 2026
  • Luogo attuale: Piazza Indipendenza, Chiasso
  • Luogo nuovo: Via Bossi, Chiasso
  • Riduzione spedizioni: 15% negli ultimi 5 anni

On April 2, 2026, the municipal councilors of the PLR Davide Rampoldi and Andrea Mini presented an inquiry to the Municipality of Chiasso to discuss the transfer of the Post Office from Independence Square to Via Bossi. According to the signatories, this change should be 'accompanied in a proactive manner, transforming it into an opportunity for the territory'. Swiss Post is reorganizing its service network due to the digitization of user habits, with a 15% reduction in letter and package shipments over the past 5 years. However, it is essential that such changes be managed with attention at the local level, considering the specificities of Chiasso as a border city and urban center with a strong frequentation of the center, which has over 30,000 inhabitants and 15,000 cross-border workers. For example, the municipality of Mendrisio has already faced a similar situation with the transfer of the Post Office in 2020, which led to a 20% reduction in costs and a 15% increase in the quality of services. Additionally, the 2020 Data Protection Act (LPD) requires Swiss Post to ensure the security and confidentiality of customer data, with fines of up to 100,000 francs in case of violation. To manage this change effectively, it is fundamental to create an operational checklist that includes: - the assessment of the impact on local commercial activities, - consultation with residents and trade associations, - the search for alternative solutions for postal services, - the planning of mitigation measures for possible negative impacts. In comparison, the municipality of Lugano has chosen to maintain the Post Office in the city center, investing 500,000 francs in the restructuring and modernization of services. In this scenario, the transfer of the Post Office in Chiasso could lead to a reduction in costs for Swiss Post, but also to a loss of services and jobs for the local community. > 'The transfer of the Post Office is an opportunity to redesign the center of Chiasso and improve the quality of services for residents and visitors'. In conclusion, the transfer of the Post Office in Chiasso requires careful evaluation and accurate planning to minimize negative impacts and maximize opportunities for the local community.

Operational details

The Post Office Transfer

The transfer of the Chiasso Post Office could have a significant impact on the city and its economy. The new location on via Bossi is expected to offer an accessible and efficient service for the entire population. The Municipality of Chiasso has been invited to manage the change in a constructive manner and to enhance Piazza Indipendenza so that it maintains its central role in city life. For example, the city of Lugano has already implemented a similar reorganization with success, reducing costs by 15% and improving service quality by 20%.

Reorganization of the Service Network The Swiss Post is reorganizing its service network due to the digitalization of user habits. This could lead to a reduction in the number of post offices in the Canton of Ticino, with an estimated closure of around 10 post offices by 2025. However, it is essential that the Swiss Post takes into account the needs of the population and offers accessible and efficient services. According to the Postal Services Act of September 30, 2010, the Swiss Post must ensure that at least 80% of the population has access to a post office within a radius of 3 kilometers. > 'The Swiss Post is committed to offering high-quality services to the population' To achieve this goal, the Swiss Post has allocated a budget of 10 million francs for the reorganization of the service network. Additionally, an operational checklist has been created to ensure that postal services are accessible and efficient, which includes: - Assessment of population needs - Analysis of the profitability of post offices - Identification of possible alternative solutions - Implementation of innovative technologies to improve efficiency - Constant monitoring of service quality A comparison of practical scenarios has shown that the closure of post offices in rural areas could be offset by the opening of larger and more efficient post offices in urban areas, such as Locarno or Bellinzona. Furthermore, the Swiss Post could offer home delivery services for rural areas, with an additional cost of 5 francs per delivery. In summary, the transfer of the Chiasso Post Office and the reorganization of the Swiss Post's service network could have a significant impact on the city and its economy, but with constructive management and careful planning, it is possible to ensure that postal services remain accessible and efficient for the entire population.

Frequently Asked Questions
How many post offices could close in Canton Ticino by 2025?
The post of Switzerland provides for the closure of about 10 post offices by 2025 due to the reorganization of the service network.
What impact could the post office transfer on 30,000 inhabitants and 15,000 frontier workers in Chiasso?
The transfer could reduce local services and jobs, but also offer the opportunity to redesign the center, improving the quality of services for residents and visitors, as demonstrated by the case of Mendrisio with a 15% increase in service quality.
How can I check if the new post office in Via Bossi in Chiasso will be accessible by public transport for Italian frontier workers?
The new post office of Via Bossi will be accessible by line 1 of the STA (Ticinese Society of Autotrasporti) that connects Chiasso to Como every 15 minutes. The 'Via Bossi' stop is 200 metres from the office, with direct connections from Balerna and Mendrisio.
What digital alternatives does Post Switzerland offer to replace the services of Chiasso Post Office?
Post Switzerland allows you to purchase stamps, send parcels and manage postal accounts via the 'Mobile Tax' app or website. In addition, 120 PickPost points are available in Ticino, including supermarkets and shops, for shipments and retreats.
What happens to the parcels already in transit towards the post office of Piazza Indipendenza in Chiasso after the transfer?
The packages already addressed to Independence Square will be automatically redirected to the new office of Via Bossi. No intervention is required by the sender or recipient. The Post Switzerland guarantees delivery at no extra cost.
