Tiziano Pasta, UDC councilor in Mendrisio, reported the passage of illegal immigrants through a hole in the fence in Vacallo. During his night patrols, Pasta observed groups of seven or eight people entering Switzerland illegally. He alerted the border guards, who intervened and took away the illegal immigrants. Pasta expressed concern for the safety of people living nearby and called for the closure of the gap.

The municipal pedestrian path leading to the Swiss border has become an illegal crossing point to Italy. Pasta explained that the path, which starts from via Dosso Lugano, ends about 50 meters from the torn fence near the Italian Guardia di Finanza lookout. According to Pasta, the path is used by illegal immigrants, refugees, and criminals.

Most of these episodes are concentrated in the most porous border areas, such as Vacallo and Chiasso.

Regulations and dates

The Federal Act on the Surveillance of Foreigners (LStr) of December 16, 2005, regulates the entry and stay of foreigners in Switzerland. According to Article 118 of the LStr, those who enter Switzerland illegally can be expelled and face a re-entry ban. Penalties can amount to up to 10,000 Swiss francs.

Procedure to Report Illegal Crossings

If cross-border workers or residents in the Vacallo area observe illegal crossings, it is important that they immediately report it to the competent authorities. The Swiss Border Guards can be contacted at the emergency number 117, while the Italian Guardia di Finanza is reachable at 117 or 156. It is useful to provide precise details such as the time, exact location, and a description of the people involved. For example, in the municipality of Novazzano, timely reports led to a 20% increase in interceptions in the first half of 2023.
