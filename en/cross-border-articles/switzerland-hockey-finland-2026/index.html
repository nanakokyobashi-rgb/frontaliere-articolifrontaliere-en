Switzerland beats Finland: encouraging growth ahead of World Cup

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 May 2026

Switzerland wins against Finland in a pre-World Cup friendly, with a convincing performance.

Context

In brief

  • Switzerland beats Finland 5-4 on penalties
  • Rochette scores a brace and the decisive penalty
  • 2026 Hockey World Cup in Switzerland starting from May 14

Key facts

  • What: Friendly hockey match between Switzerland and Finland
  • When: May 7, 2026
  • Where: Catena Arena, Switzerland
  • Who: Swiss national hockey team
  • Score: 5-4 on penalties

Switzerland showed encouraging growth ahead of the Hockey World Cup, which will be held in Switzerland starting May 14, 2026. The match against Finland, played on May 7, 2026, saw the Swiss team prevail with a final score of 5-4 on penalties. A result that bodes well for their upcoming home World Cup.

The match featured the debut of key players like Niederreiter, Meier, Hischier, and Josi, who joined forces with the rest of Jan Cadieux's team. The addition of these reinforcements allowed the team to make a significant leap in quality. From the first minutes, the Swiss pressure was felt, with Niederreiter and Frick putting the Finnish defense in difficulty. The first goal came courtesy of Egli, who capitalized on a play by Thürkauf to put away a puck deflected by an opposing defender.

Finland, also reinforced with North American players, reacted with determination, but Switzerland maintained control of the game, finding the second goal with Suter and Rochette. However, Finland equalized with Puljujärvi and Saarijärvi, taking the match to extra time. The final decision came on penalties, where Rochette and Moy scored the decisive goals for Switzerland.

Match analysis

The match showed a more organized and determined Switzerland compared to previous outings. The arrival of reinforcements from across the Atlantic made the difference, allowing the team to play with greater confidence and precision. The Swiss defense proved to be well-disposed on the ice, despite moments of difficulty against the Finnish attacks.

Rochette's performance was particularly notable, with a brace and the decisive penalty. Egli and Thürkauf also contributed significantly to the final result. The match highlighted Switzerland's ability to react to difficulties and find offensive solutions even in complex situations.

Implications for the World Cup

The victory against Finland represents a positive signal for Switzerland ahead of the World Cup. The team has shown that it can compete with the best national teams and has the resources to face future challenges. The entry of key players and the cohesion of the group will be decisive factors for the success of the Swiss team.

The match also showed the importance of a solid defense and a well-coordinated attack. Switzerland will need to continue working on these aspects to maintain the level of performance achieved in this friendly.
