Context

Ticino is a Swiss canton located on the border with Italy. It is one of Switzerland's main economic centres and home to several multinational companies. Its strategic location makes it an ideal place to work as a border worker, that is, for those who reside in a country bordering Switzerland and work in this canton.

The Swiss federal law on cross-border work, which came into force on 1 January 2007, regulates working conditions for frontier workers. Under this law, frontier workers can work in Switzerland if they reside in a neighbouring country and have an employment contract concluded with a Swiss employer. 📊

To work in Ticino as a border worker, you must comply with certain regulations. First of all, a work permit is required, which can be applied for at the Swiss Consulate in the country of residence. The residence permit is valid for a maximum of 12 months and can be renewed if the worker continues to reside in the country of origin. ⚠️

In addition, border workers must comply with Swiss tax regulations. According to the federal tax law, frontier workers are subject to income tax in Switzerland if they have an income from work in this country. Income tax is calculated on total income, including income from work in Switzerland. 💡

Operational details

Ticino is a Swiss canton located on the border with Italy. It is one of Switzerland's main economic centres and offers many job opportunities. Cross-border work is an increasingly widespread phenomenon, especially among residents of the municipality of Bizzarone, who are working in Ticino.

According to data from 2022, 30% of workers in the municipality of Bizzarone work in Ticino, mainly in the area of Lugano and Bellinzona. 70% of frontier workers travel to Ticino for work at least once a week. Border workers are up 20% compared to 2020, according to data from the Ticino Canton Labour Service.

Cross-border work is regulated by the Convention on the Freedom of Movement of Workers of 21 June 1999, which sets out the rights and responsibilities of frontier workers. Frontier workers are entitled to labour treatment equivalent to that of workers in the Canton of Ticino.

Living costs in Ticino are higher than those in Bizzarone, but wages are also higher. According to 2022 data, average salaries in Ticino are around CHF 6,500 per month, while in Bizzarone they are around CHF 4,500 per month. Requirements to work in Ticino as a border worker

In order to work in Ticino as a border worker, you must have a valid work permit and an employment contract that guarantees you a decent salary. In addition, it is important that you are resident in a country with which Switzerland has a free movement of workers agreement, such as Italy.

💡 If you are an Italian citizen, you can check the website of the Ministry of Labour and Social Policies for information on the procedures and requirements for working in Switzerland.

Work permits

To obtain a work permit in Ticino, you must submit your job application to the Immigration Department (DI) of the Swiss Confederation. The job application must be accompanied by several documents, including:

  • an employment contract that guarantees you a decent salary;
  • a certificate of professional qualification;
  • a German (or French or Italian) language certificate;
  • a certificate of residence in Italy.
