Carabinieri maxi blitz in Varese: 12 workers in black and serious safety deficiencies lead to the closure of 10 activities and sanctions for 775 thousand euros.

Context

In a nutshell

  • 14 companies inspected by THE NIL in Varese
  • 12 black workers identified, including one irregular person
  • 10 activities suspended for serious safety violations and undeclared work
  • 19 people reported at large

Key facts

  • What: Control operation on black work and safety
  • When: July 2026
  • Where: Province of Varese
  • Who: Carabinieri of the Labour Inspectorate (NIL)
  • Amount of fines: 560,505.24 euros
  • Administrative penalties: 214,350 euros
  • Contributions recovered: 23,281.95 euros

The operation conducted by the Carabinieri of the Labour Inspectorate (NIL), in collaboration with the military of the Provincial Command of Varese, involved 14 business activities distributed throughout the province. The inspection activity has brought to light a critical framework characterised by serious violations of current regulations on safety in the workplace and numerous cases of the use of irregular labour. During the checks, which involved the identification of 33 total workers, it emerged that 12 of them were serving completely in the dark. Among these, the authorities have identified a foreign citizen without a regular residence permit on the national territory. Operational details

The inspection activity has produced a significant economic impact, with a total balance that reaches almost 775,000 euros between fines and administrative sanctions. This operation takes place in a context of constant surveillance, aimed at countering the phenomenon of irregular work, a plague that often intersects with the world of border workers operating in the productive sector between Italy and the Canton of Ticino. For workers who provide services in companies subject to similar checks, the protection of their position is fundamental. Contractual regularity is not only a legal obligation, but it represents the only guarantee instrument for access to pension and welfare contributions.

Key points

For workers operating in entrepreneurial realities subject to controls, the verification procedure of their own position is a fundamental step. It is necessary to ensure that the employer has carried out regular hiring, that pay slips are compliant and that tax withholdings are correctly paid. In the event of doubts about their position, the worker can refer to patronages or trade unions for an in-depth verification of the contractual documentation. Regularity, in addition to being an obligation, guarantees access to all rights, including those related to social security and protection.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the main violations alleged in the operation?
Violations include failure to assess business risks, lack of fire-fighting devices, use of non-compliant equipment, improper installation of video surveillance, hygiene and health deficiencies, failure to carry out health surveillance, and facilitation of illegal immigration.
How many activities have been suspended in Varese?
The NIL Carabinieri ordered the immediate suspension of 10 business activities following the inspection of 14 companies. Suspensions are due to serious safety violations, employment of irregular workers, or a combination of both.
What is the total amount of penalties?
The economic balance of the checks includes fines of 560,505.24 euros and administrative sanctions of 214,350 euros, in addition to the recovery of 23,281.95 euros of social security and welfare contributions paid.
