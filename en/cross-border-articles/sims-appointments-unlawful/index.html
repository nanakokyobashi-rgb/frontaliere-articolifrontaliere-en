Frequently Asked Questions
Why did the Council of State decide not to appeal to the Federal Supreme Court after the annulment of the SIMS appointments by the Cantonal Administrative Court?
The decision was taken after legal investigations and assessments of opportunity, in consideration of the impact that an appeal would have had on the Ticino municipalities and on the financial resources of the Canton. The legal services highlighted procedural obstacles and case law that made the chances of upholding the appeal low.
What is the estimated cost for a new SIMS competition by the Council of State of the Canton of Ticino?
The Council of State estimated that the costs of a new competition would be around 50,000 francs.
What is the expected timeframe for the publication and selection of new SIMS members?
The call for applications will be published within 2 weeks, with applications closing within 4 weeks. The evaluation and selection will last approximately 6 weeks, and the final appointment within 8 weeks.
