Operational details

The setting at source in Switzerland for frontier workers is one of the main novelties of the new frontier agreement. This means that workers in Ticino will no longer have to pay tax on their pay in Italy. Instead, they will be able to claim a tax credit to cover taxes paid in Switzerland. This advantage is particularly useful for workers in Ticino, as they will no longer have to face the long waits at the Chiasso crossing.

What does setting at source mean?

The source setting is a mechanism that allows frontier workers to pay taxes on their pay in Switzerland, rather than in Italy. This means that workers will no longer have to pay tax in Italy and will be able to claim a tax credit to cover taxes paid in Switzerland.

Concrete example

Let's imagine we have a frontier worker earning 4,000 Swiss francs per month and having to pay the tax in Switzerland of 7.7% (as of 1 January 2023, the average tax rate in Switzerland is 7.7% on all incomes). The worker could claim a tax credit of about 308 Swiss francs per month (4,000 x 0.077).

Key points

To move to Uggiate-Trevano as a border worker, you must follow the procedures established by the new border agreement. This includes submitting a transfer request to the Labour Service (SL) and submitting a copy of the employment contract. In addition, you must register with the Federal Statistical Office (FSO) and apply for a residence permit.

According to the federal law of 21 March 2019, foreigners who intend to move to Uggiate-Trevano must submit a transfer request within 30 days of arrival in the municipality.

For example, if an Italian citizen intends to move to Uggiate-Trevano with his family, he will have to submit a transfer request for himself and his family members within 30 days of arrival in the municipality.

In addition, a copy of the employment contract and identity card must be presented.

The Labor Service (SL) will evaluate the transfer request and may request additional information or documents.

According to the Ticino law of 25 May 2020, foreigners who intend to move to Uggiate-Trevano must register with the Federal Statistical Office (UFS) within 15 days of arrival in the municipality.

To register with the UFS, you must present a copy of your identity card and residence card.

The cost of registration with the UFS is CHF 30 per person.
