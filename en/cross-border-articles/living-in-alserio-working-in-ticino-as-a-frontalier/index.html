Living in Alserio and working in Ticino as a border worker

The new border agreement introduced by the European Union for border workers living in Alserio and working in Ticino has brought new tax rules. Border crossers who already have a residence permit can benefit from a transitional regime until 2033, while new border crossers will have to pay a deductible of €10,000.

Transitional regime until 2033

Border workers who already reside in Alserio and work in Ticino can benefit from a transitional regime until 2033. This means that they will not be subject to the new tax rules and will be able to continue working in the cross-border labour sector without any problems. For example, a border worker who works as a nurse in a clinic in Lugano and resides in Alserio will be able to continue working without having to pay the deductible of €10,000.

Deductible of €10,000 for new frontier workers

New frontier workers wishing to work in Ticino will have to pay a deductible of €10,000. This means that they will have to pay a sum of money to be able to work in the cross-border work sector. For example, a frontier worker who works as a teacher in a school in Bellinzona and resides in Alserio will have to pay the deductible of €10,000 in order to continue working.

Starting to work in Ticino as a border worker

  • What to do: Apply for a residence permit at the immigration office in Lugano
  • Where to go: Lugano Immigration Office
  • Deadlines: 1 January 2024

If you are a border worker wishing to work in Ticino, you must apply for a residence permit at the immigration office in Lugano by 1 January 2024. This residence permit allows you to work in Ticino and enjoy other rights such as the right to work, health and education.

# Residence Permit Application Requirements

To apply for a residence permit, you must meet the following requirements:

  • Have an employment contract with an employer in Ticino
  • Have sufficient financial security to support you during your stay in Ticino
  • Having a guarantee of food and accommodation in Ticino
  • Have insurance coverage for medical expenses
  • Have a study plan or work plan

Documents required to apply for a residence permit

To apply for a residence permit, you must provide the following documents:

  • An employment contract with your employer in Ticino
  • A statement of financial security
  • A guarantee of food and accommodation in Ticino
