Frequently Asked Questions
What happened to the Ritom hydroelectric power plant?
On the morning of Wednesday, September 9, 2026, at around 9:30 am, a sudden explosion occurred inside the Ritom hydroelectric plant, located in Piotta, in Alta Leventina, Canton of Ticino. The explosion caused the partial collapse of the historic structure dating back to 1917.
What were the consequences of the incident?
The explosion and the consequent structural collapse overwhelmed two workers who were inside the plant at the time of the event. The two workers sustained serious injuries and were promptly rescued by the Fire Brigade, the health workers of Tre Valli Soccorso and urgently transported to the hospital, also with the help of a Rega helicopter.
What is the importance of the Ritom plant and the Gotthard line?
The Ritom hydroelectric power plant, with its historic 1917 structure, played a crucial role in powering the Gotthard railway line. This line is a key artery for Swiss national connections and also plays a significant role for the Ticino economy and cross-border commuter flows.
What are the causes of the explosion and what is expected for the future?
The exact causes of the explosion have not yet been determined. The cantonal police have delimited the area to allow rescue and technical surveys. The investigating authorities have opened a thorough investigation to clarify the dynamics of the incident, identify any causes and prevent future similar events. The reactivation of the railway line will depend on the outcome of the investigations and technical evaluations.
