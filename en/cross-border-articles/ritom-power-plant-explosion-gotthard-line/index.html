Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 10 September 2026

Wednesday morning, 9 September 2026: explosion and partial collapse in the historic Ritom hydroelectric plant (1917). Two workers seriously injured, ongoing investigation.

Context

In brief

  • Explosion at the Ritom hydroelectric plant.
  • Partial collapse of the historic 1917 structure.
  • Two workers seriously injured.
  • Investigations underway into the causes.

Key facts

  • What: Explosion and partial collapse of hydroelectric plant.
  • When: Wednesday morning, September 9, 2026, around 9:30.
  • Where: Ritom hydroelectric plant, Piotta, Alta Leventina (Canton Ticino).
  • Who: Injured workers, firefighters, medical personnel, cantonal police.
  • Details: Historic structure dating from 1917, powered the Gotthard railway line.

A sudden explosion shook the morning of Wednesday, September 9, 2026, around 9:30, inside the historic Ritom hydroelectric plant. The plant, located in Piotta, in the Alta Leventina, Canton Ticino, was the scene of a serious incident that caused the partial collapse of its original structure, dating back to 1917. This historic plant played a crucial role in supplying electricity to the Gotthard railway line, a key artery for national connections and potentially for the transit of goods and people, including cross-border workers who use this route. The structural collapse had immediate and dramatic consequences, sweeping away two people who were inside the plant at the moment of the explosion. The two workers sustained serious injuries, requiring a prompt and effective response from rescuers. The rescue operation was immediately activated on site, with the intervention of the Biasca and Alta Leventina fire brigades, assisted by the medical personnel of Tre Valli Soccorso. To ensure the urgent transport of the injured to hospital in the shortest possible time, a Rega helicopter was also deployed, underscoring the gravity of the situation and the need for immediate specialist medical care. The cantonal police promptly cordoned off and secured the entire perimeter of the plant, a necessary measure to allow both the delicate rescue operations and the subsequent technical surveys indispensable for understanding the dynamics of the event. The exact causes of the explosion remain unknown at present, a detail that has led the investigating authorities to open a thorough investigation. The aim of the investigation is to clarify the exact dynamics of the incident, identify any pre-existing structural weaknesses or anomalies in the infrastructure that may have contributed to the explosion, in order to prevent similar future incidents. The safety of critical infrastructures, such as those that power strategic railway lines, is a matter of primary importance to ensure service continuity and the safety of workers and citizens. The Gotthard line, in particular, also plays a significant role for the Ticino economy and commuter flows, making every disruption or incident an event to be monitored closely for its potential repercussions. Authorities are working to gather all necessary information to reconstruct what happened and to establish any possible responsibilities. The partial collapse of the historic structure adds an additional layer of complexity to recovery operations and investigations, given the historical and structural importance of the building. The priority remains the health of the injured and the securing of the area. Investigations will continue in the coming days to shed full light on the incident and its deep causes, providing updates as new verifiable details emerge. Managing a facility of this scale requires high safety standards and rigorous periodic checks, an aspect that will certainly be at the heart of the ongoing verifications by the competent authorities. The reconstruction of the plant, if necessary, and the full reactivation of the Gotthard railway line will depend on the outcome of the investigations and subsequent technical assessments. Public attention is now focused on the condition of the injured and the progress of the investigations aimed at establishing the truth about the explosion. Transparency in communications regarding the incident will be fundamental to maintain the trust of citizens and sector operators. Further information will be disseminated as soon as available and verified by the competent authorities, in accordance with the ongoing investigative procedures. The stability and reliability of Swiss energy infrastructures are crucial for the country.

Operational details

The accident at the Ritom hydroelectric power plant, although an event limited to a specific infrastructure, raises questions about the safety of energy networks that support essential services such as rail transport. The Gotthard line, in fact, is not only a vital artery for Switzerland, but also a fundamental connection for commuters who cross the border between Italy and Canton Ticino every day for work. Although the source does not specify a direct impact on border crossers' flows or travel times due to this accident, it is permissible to consider the potential indirect implications arising from an interruption or slowdown on the railway line. A malfunction or damage to such a strategic plant could, in theory, result in temporary changes to rail services, forcing border crossers to look for alternative routes or suffer unforeseen delays. The dependence on the power grid provided by the Ritom plant for the operation of the Gotthard line makes this incident a reminder of the importance of maintaining very high safety standards for all critical infrastructure. Ongoing investigations will need to ascertain whether there was negligence or whether the event was caused by unforeseen factors, but the outcome will certainly have a bearing on future plant maintenance and updating policies.
