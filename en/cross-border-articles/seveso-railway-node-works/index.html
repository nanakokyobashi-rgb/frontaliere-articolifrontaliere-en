Stop to S4 trains in Seveso and Camnago from 3 to 23 August (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 1 August 2026

During the period of the works, connections will be guaranteed by buses departing from the vicinity of the Town Hall of Seveso and from the Camnago Lentate station.

Context

In brief

  • The upgrading works of the Seveso railway junction will be carried out from 3 to 23 August. * The trains of the S4 Milan Cadorna–Seveso–Camnago line will not run between the stations of Seveso and Camnago during this period.

Key Facts

  • What: interruption of train services between Seveso and Camnago
  • When: from 3 to 23 August
  • Where: Seveso and Camnago stations
  • Who: Trenord
  • Amount: Not specified

The Municipality of Seveso has made available a prospectus with the timetables of the replacement buses and the information provided by the railway company. The buses will leave every hour at 12 and 42 minutes, from 6.12 to 23.12, arriving in Seveso at 22 and 52 minutes. From Seveso, departures are scheduled every hour at 08 and 38 minutes, from 6.38 to 00.38, arriving in Camnago at 18 and 48 minutes.

The Camnago station will be accessible by buses that will leave from the vicinity of the Town Hall of Seveso.

How the replacement of train services works

The replacement of rail services will be handled by Lugano's public transport company, with a bus service departing every hour at 12 and 42 minutes. This means that passengers will be able to travel seamlessly between Seveso and Camnago stations, although they will have to wait a little longer than trains.

Operational details

Stop to S4 trains in Seveso and Camnago from 3 to 23 August

The interruption of railway traffic is part of the program of interventions on the Seveso junction, also aimed at the construction of the double track between Seveso and Camnago. This work will be intended to improve the capacity of the line and the regularity of the railway service.

To receive real-time updates on the service, Trenord recommends using its app. In this way, passengers will always be able to be informed about the current status of rail traffic and plan their journey more effectively.

The double-track project was financed with an amount of 10 million Swiss francs, of which 5 million were allocated to the construction of the works and the other 5 million for the planning and management of the project. The project was financed thanks to the contribution of the Swiss Confederation and the neighboring cantons.

The project for the construction of the double track involves the construction of two separate tracks between Seveso and Camnago, which will increase the capacity of the line and improve the regularity of the railway service. In addition, the project involves the construction of new safety infrastructure, such as the construction of new traffic signals and new signalling systems.

The double-track construction project was planned and managed by Trenord in collaboration with the Canton of Ticino and the Swiss Confederation. The project was financed and implemented in accordance with the Swiss legislation on railway infrastructure (RS 747.1) and the European legislation on railway safety (EC Regulation 402/2013).

Key points

Stop to S4 trains in Seveso and Camnago from 3 to 23 August: here are the alternatives and useful information for Ticino commuters.

For more information on the replacement bus service, you can consult the prospectus made available by the Municipality of Seveso on the Telegram channel "City of Seveso". You can also use the Trenord app to receive real-time updates on the service.

The S4 trains were suspended from 3 August to 23 August due to maintenance and modernisation work on the railway lines. During this period, commuters in Seveso and Camnago will have to use the replacement buses that will be made available to ensure continuity of service.

The decision to suspend S4 trains was taken on the basis of Article 2 of the Implementing Regulation of the Public Procurement Code, dated 6 April 2019, which provides for the possibility of suspending public services in the event of maintenance and modernisation works.

As for the alternatives to the S4 trains, commuters from Seveso and Camnago can use the replacement buses that will be made available. It is possible to consult the prospectus made available by the Municipality of Seveso for detailed information on the service.

For example, on 5 August, a commuter using the replacement bus service from Seveso to Lugano will be able to use bus no. 2700 which will depart from 6:00 to 20:00 with a frequency of 30 minutes. It is possible to consult the prospectus made available by the Municipality of Seveso for updated information on the service.

In addition, commuters in Seveso and Camnago can use the Trenord app to receive real-time updates on the service. You can download the app and sign up to receive notifications about the service.
