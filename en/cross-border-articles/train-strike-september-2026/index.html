The national protest proclaimed by Cub Trasporti and Sgb will involve the personnel of the Fs Group, Trenitalia and Trenord. Guaranteed time slots and replacement buses are planned for the connection with Malpensa.

A bitter start of the week for travelers and the many commuters who use the railway network daily. A new national protest risks paralyzing traffic on the tracks starting from the evening of Monday 7 September, continuing throughout Tuesday 8 September.

The mobilization, called by the union labels Cub Trasporti and Sgb, will officially start at 21.18 on Monday and end at 21.00 on Tuesday. Twenty-four hours of work stoppage will see the involvement of workers of the Fs Italiane Group, Trenitalia, Trenitalia Tper and Trenord, with the concrete risk of cancellations, delays and variations in the timetable of many trains.

Trains running on the evening of Monday 7 September will complete their journey provided that the departure time is before 21.18 and that the arrival at the final destination occurs by 22.18. Regarding the entire day of Tuesday 8 September, the companies guarantee the coverage of only the runs included in the lists of essential minimum services. As usual on weekdays, the time slots with the highest worker attendance will be protected, namely those in the morning between 6.00 and 9.00 and those in the evening between 18.00 and 21.00.

Operational details

Impact on Commuters

Travelers heading to Malpensa Airport may also be affected by the changes. Should airport service trips be cancelled, Trenord has already set up a replacement coach service with no intermediate stops. The buses will operate the connection between Milan Cadorna station (with departures established from via Paleocapa 1) and Malpensa Aeroporto for the RE54 line, as well as covering the cross-border route between Stabio and the Varese airport for the S50 line.

Discomfort, transport companies recall, could occur even before the official start of the strike and continue in the hours immediately following its conclusion. For those who choose to give up the planned trip, it is possible to request a full refund of the ticket or to reschedule the trip under equivalent conditions. For Intercity and Arrows trains, the request for reimbursement must be submitted within the departure time indicated on the ticket, while for regional trains the request must be forwarded by midnight of the day before the protest.

Frequently Asked Questions
When will the train strike start and end?
The strike will start at 9.18 pm on Monday 7 September and end at 9.00 pm on Tuesday 8 September.
What time slots will be guaranteed during the strike?
The guaranteed time slots will be those in the morning between 6.00 am and 9.00 am and those in the evening between 6.00 pm and 9.00 pm.
Will there be replacement buses for commuters heading to Malpensa?
Yes, Trenord has arranged a substitute coach service with no intermediate stops for connections to Malpensa Airport.
