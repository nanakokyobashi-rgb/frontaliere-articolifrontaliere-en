Lombardy-Ticino Transport: Tilo Breaks Record with 25 Million Passengers

Di Laura Bianchi · Published on 5 March 2026

Tilo trains have transported 25 million passengers between Ticino and Lombardy in 2025, a 3.7% increase from the previous year. The steady growth highlights the trust of commuters and occasional travellers in the railway service.

The annual data released by the Tilo railway company has revealed a significant increase in rail traffic between Lombardy and Canton Ticino. In 2025, Tilo trains carried a remarkable 25 million passengers, confirming a positive trend that has been ongoing for several years. This result represents a 3.7% increase compared to the 24.1 million passengers recorded in 2024, and a 50% increase from the 2019 figures, when travellers numbered 16.7 million.

The growth in regional and cross-border rail traffic is a clear sign of the trust that commuters and occasional travellers place in the service offered by Tilo. The railway company, which operates along the network between Varese and Canton Ticino, has implemented new integrations between trains and buses, facilitating interchange and daily commutes for commuters and cross-border workers.

"Tilo's record numbers in 2025 confirm the importance of this service for the region," stated Tilo's General Manager, Giovanni Rossi. "We are proud to have contributed to improving mobility between Lombardy and Ticino, offering an efficient and sustainable alternative to traditional means of transport." Rossi added.

Operational details

Tilo's railway service offers a range of advantages for commuters and occasional travellers. Firstly, Tilo trains are equipped with modern comforts such as power outlets, Wi-Fi connection, and dedicated spaces for bicycles. Additionally, the company offers convenient fares, with monthly and annual subscriptions that allow passengers to save on transportation costs.

In terms of frequency, Tilo trains run on regular and scheduled timetables, ensuring a reliable and punctual service. The company has also implemented a real-time information system, allowing passengers to stay updated on any delays or schedule changes.

Regarding accessibility, Tilo trains are designed to be inclusive and accessible to all. Railway stations are equipped with dedicated paths for people with disabilities, and the trains are fitted with reserved spaces for wheelchairs and strollers.

"Tilo's service is an example of how public transport can be efficient and accessible to all," stated the Director of the Swiss Federal Office of Transport, Martin Meyer. "We are happy to see that more and more people are choosing the train as a means of transport, and we will continue to work to further improve the service." Meyer added.

Frequently Asked Questions
How many passengers have used Tilo trains between Lombardy and Canton Ticino in 2025?
In 2025, Tilo trains transported 25 million passengers, an increase of 3.7% compared to 2024 and 50% compared to 2019.
What is the economic impact of Tilo railway traffic on Canton Ticino?
Tilo railway traffic has a positive economic impact on the Canton Ticino, as stressed by DFE, improving mobility and contributing to the economic development of the region.
How does the Tilo annual subscription work for borderers between Lombardy and Ticino?
The annual Tilo per Frontier subscription costs approximately 1,200 CHF (approx. 1,250 EUR) and offers unlimited travel between the network stations. It also includes access to discounted rates on buses and regional trains in Ticino. It can be purchased online or at station ticket offices.
What are the busiest Tilo railway stations between Lombardy and Ticino?
The most popular stations are Lugano, Varese, Como Lago, Mendrisio and Chiasso. Lugano recorded more than 5 million passengers a year, followed by Varese with approximately 4 million. These stations are key hubs for commuters and cross-border travellers.
Tilo offers discounts for students or young people between Lombardy and Ticino?
Yes, Tilo offers reduced rates for under 26 students, with annual subscriptions at approximately CHF 600 (630 EUR). Students must submit a valid ID at the time of purchase. Group and family discounts are also available.
