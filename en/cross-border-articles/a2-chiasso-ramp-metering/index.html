The Federal Roads Office (FEDRO) is examining more than 120 sites for installing metering systems at motorway ramps. This was reported by SonntagsZeitung. However, this is not a definitive decision: it will only proceed if checks confirm its feasibility and a positive effect on traffic.

The system is based on traffic lights that regulate access to the highway, allowing vehicles to pass at intervals. In this way, close entries are limited and the flow on the main roadway is more regular. In Switzerland, 15 plants are already operational.

For FEDRO, ramp metering is a measure to be activated when traffic is intense.

From assessment to A2

For those traveling on the A2 towards Chiasso, the framework already includes two DoRa systems, also referred to as ramp metering, at the Lugano Sud and Melide/Bissone ramps. The FEDRO list also reports four other hypotheses: one concerns Lugano North towards Chiasso; the others, towards Basel, Lugano Sud, Melide/Bissone and Mendrisio. The distinction is concrete: the first two systems are active, while the four items are presented as hypotheses.

In the Basel region, 19 plants are being examined. An internal FEDRO note from January 2025 considers it possible that the dosage generates traffic jams on the roads downstream. The cantons of Basel-Stadt and Schaffhausen reportedly declared that they were not actually involved, while the Union of Swiss Cities also fears consequences for those who travel on foot or by bicycle.

Frequently Asked Questions
How many points is the USTRA evaluating for dosing?
The Federal Office of Roads is evaluating over 120 possible points to install dosing systems at highway ramps. The objective is to proceed only if the verifications will confirm the feasibility and a positive effect on traffic. The figure therefore indicates an examination phase and is not equivalent to the announcement of installations already approved in all points.
Where are the systems on the A2 towards Chiasso already active?
On the A2 towards Chiasso, two DoRa systems, or ramp metering, are already active at the Lugano Sud and Melide/Bissone ramps. The USTRA list also indicates four other hypotheses: Lugano Nord towards Chiasso and, towards Basel, Lugano Sud, Melide/Bissone and Mendrisio. The four items are presented as hypotheses, not as activations already carried out.
Does dosing eliminate queues on highways?
No. The system is designed to allow vehicles to enter at intervals, avoiding too close inputs and making the flow on the main road more regular. An internal USTRA memo from January 2025, however, notes that dosing can result in columns on downstream roads. The positive effect must in any case be verified.
When will Cantons and Municipalities be involved?
The USTRA states that Cantons and Municipalities will be involved when moving on to concrete projects. The issue arose because the Cantons of Basel-City and Schaffhausen reported that they had not been concretely involved; the Union of Swiss Cities fears repercussions also for pedestrians and cyclists.
