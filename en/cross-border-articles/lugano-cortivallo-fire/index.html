Shortly after 3.00 today, Sunday, a fire broke out in an apartment building on Via Cortivallo in Lugano. The cantonal police reported this, having responded to the scene together with officers from the Lugano Municipal Police, firefighters and rescuers from Croce Verde.

The flames started in an apartment located on the second floor. The blaze produced a thick cloud of smoke that filled the building. After extinguishing the fire, the firefighters ventilated the building.

The body of a deceased person was found in the apartment concerned. The cantonal police specify that formal identification is still underway.

The response also involved the occupants of the building. Twenty-two tenants were temporarily evacuated as a precaution. Medical personnel assisted six residents at the scene, who were experiencing mild symptoms after inhaling smoke. No one had to be taken to hospital.

The statement therefore separates the discovery in the apartment from the measures taken for the rest of the building: on the one hand, the lifeless body and formal identification, and on the other, the temporary evacuation and assistance to residents.

The investigation coordinated by Public Prosecutor Chiara Buzzi will have to clarify the causes of the fire and reconstruct the exact sequence of events.
