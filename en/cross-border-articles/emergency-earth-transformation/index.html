Earth Emergency tells the story of the great transformation (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 September 2026

Series of meetings on ecological and social transformation from 16 September to 9 December at the SUPSI Campus in Mendrisio.

Context

In brief

  • "Emergenza Terra" event cycle until December
  • First event on September 16 with a film screening
  • Free conferences and shows open to all

Key facts

  • What: "Emergenza Terra" event cycle
  • When: From September 16 to December 9
  • Where: SUPSI Campus in Mendrisio
  • Who: SUPSI Department of Environment, Construction and Design
  • Cost: Free entry

"Emergenza Terra" tells the story of the great transformation the world is undergoing due to climate change. The event cycle, promoted by the SUPSI Department of Environment, Construction and Design, kicks off on September 16 with an evening dedicated to the theme "The Great Transformation. Ecology and Society in the Anthropocene." The program includes conferences and shows on various aspects of ecological and social transformation, with notable speakers such as Annalisa Manera, Stefano Liberti, Paola Giacomoni, Enrico Giovannini, Paolo Vineis, and Luca Savarino. The events take place at the SUPSI Campus in Mendrisio and are open to all, free, and by registration.

Operational details

Implications for cross-border commuters

The "Emergenza Terra" meeting cycle offers a unique opportunity for cross-border commuters to delve into crucial themes related to ecological and social transformation. The free and open events allow active participation in discussions on how to address climate change and environmental challenges. Participation in these meetings can enrich cross-border commuters' understanding of the practical and theoretical implications of sustainability, contributing to greater awareness and shared responsibility.

Comparison with the previous situation

Before the start of this meeting cycle, cross-border commuters had few opportunities to participate in events dedicated to ecological and social transformation in such a structured and free manner. SUPSI, with this cycle, offers a platform to discuss and reflect on themes that will have a significant impact on the daily lives and future of cross-border commuters. Active participation in these events can help cross-border commuters better understand the challenges and opportunities related to sustainability, promoting greater awareness and shared responsibility.

Key points

Concrete actions for border workers

Border workers interested in participating in the "Earth Emergency" cycle of meetings can register free of charge on the website www.supsi.ch/go/emergenza-terra. Registration is required to ensure access to events and to receive updates on any schedule changes. It is important to actively participate in these meetings to deepen knowledge on sustainability issues and to contribute to the discussion on how to address environmental challenges.

Registration Procedure

1. Go to www.supsi.ch/go/emergenza-terra. 2. Select the event of interest. 3. Fill in the registration form with your details. 4. Confirm registration.

Deadlines and additional information

The events are held at the SUPSI Campus in Mendrisio and are open to all. The film evening is scheduled on the square in front of the Campus entrance and, in case of bad weather, it will move to the Multisala Teatro cinema, in via Vincenzo Vela 21 in Mendrisio. It is advisable to check the site for any updates on the program and how to participate.

Useful tools

To learn more about the issues discussed during the meetings, border workers can consult the SUPSI website and actively participate in the discussions.
