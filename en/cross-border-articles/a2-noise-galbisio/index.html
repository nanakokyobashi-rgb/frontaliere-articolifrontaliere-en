A2 and noise: City plans the Galbisio terrace

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 5 October 2026

The City reiterates the protection of Galbisio from the noise and visual impact of the A2. For Lepori, it is the first formal step after the endorsement of Pab5.

The City reiterated its intention to protect the hamlet of Galbisio from the noise and landscape impacts of the A2. The indicated prospect is the planning of the Galbisio terrace, the step that gives shape to the announced protection for the area.

Municipal councillor Lepori links this moment to the Pab5 endorsement and describes it, within the framework reported by the source, as the first formal step. The sequence is therefore at the heart of the news: the Pab5 endorsement precedes the City's willingness to plan the terrace. It does not describe a work already completed, but the start of a planning process.

Two impacts to address

The issue is presented through two aspects. The first is acoustic, meaning it concerns the noise associated with the presence of the motorway. The second is landscape-related and concerns the effect of the A2 on the hamlet's territory. The City's reiterated intention encompasses both: Galbisio is considered both a place to protect from noise and an area where the impact on the landscape must be addressed.

For those who live at the border and work in Canton Ticino, the point of interest is the reference to an infrastructure that may become part of the daily geography of cross-border commuters. The source, however, focuses the announcement on planning the terrace and protecting the hamlet. It does not attribute to Lepori or the City any information about schedules, traffic, closures or route changes.

This boundary is also useful for reading what follows. The first formal step does not amount to a description of a timetable, a construction site or a final result. The available information is more limited: after the Pab5 endorsement, the City reiterates its intention to proceed with planning. This is the news reported for Galbisio in the Bellinzonese area.

The headline therefore combines the issue of noise with the landscape dimension. In the text, the terrace is not described in terms of its features, but is indicated as the direction of the planning. For now, the news establishes the institutional step and the objective of protecting Galbisio from the motorway's impact.

2. Separate the two reasons. The news does not speak only of noise: it links the noise impact to the landscape impact. For the cross-border commuter, these are the two stated reasons for the planning.
