A brief pursuit, which took place on the evening of October 7 in San Fermo della Battaglia, in the Como area, ended with the detention of a 29-year-old Romanian man. The man was driving a black Audi A3 that turned out to have been stolen; checks also confirmed that he was driving despite having had his license revoked.

The stop prompted by the report

The operation began following a report from the Como Police Headquarters to the Operations Center.

The 29-year-old failed to stop and accelerated to evade the check, thus initiating a brief pursuit. According to the Carabinieri, during the flight he allegedly carried out several dangerous maneuvers, including a high-speed overtaking maneuver near an intersection, which could have endangered other road users.

The flight ended on via delle Rimembranze. There, the officers managed to stop the car after it was spotted in the Cavallasca area. The transition from the report to the stop is described in the source as a brief pursuit.

What emerged from the search

The search of the car led to the discovery of an angle grinder with an abrasive disc attached and 23 grams of hashish. Checks then confirmed that the driver, already known to law enforcement, was driving with a revoked license.
