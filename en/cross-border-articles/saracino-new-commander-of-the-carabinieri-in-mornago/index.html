Saracino, new commander of the Carabinieri in Mornago

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 October 2026

Lieutenant with Special Charge Anacleto Antonio Saracino leads the Carabinieri Station of Mornago, under the Gallarate Company, responsible for public safety in the municipalities of Mornago, Sumirago, Casale Litta and Inarzo.

Special Assignment Lieutenant Anacleto Antonio Saracino is the new Commander of the Mornago Carabinieri Station. This morning, the officer was received by Colonel Alessandro De Vico, Provincial Commander of the Carabinieri in Varese. The provincial commander extended his greetings and wished him success in his work for the new institutional mission leading the local station.

The station and the new responsibility

The Mornago Carabinieri Station falls under the Gallarate Company and represents a local station of fundamental strategic importance. The Carabinieri command’s primary task is to oversee public security and territorial control.

The station’s activities concern the residents of four communities: Mornago, Sumirago, Casale Litta and Inarzo. The command’s area of responsibility is therefore not limited to the municipality in which the station is located, but includes the four communities indicated in the source.

Saracino’s reception by the Provincial Commander marks the beginning of his new institutional mission. De Vico’s greetings and good wishes are connected to his taking charge of the station, while its placement under the Gallarate Company defines the structure in which the Mornago Station operates. The report thus presents a local assignment with a precise scope: one station, a chain of command and four communities entrusted to the station for public security and territorial control functions. This is the framework of the new command indicated by the source.

Operational details

From Puglia to Lombardy

The new commander takes up the position after a career in the Carabinieri that began in the province of Lecce. Saracino is originally from that area and began his professional experience in 1991, when he was just over twenty years old.

The first stages in the Carabinieri

The first training listed in his service history is the 161st Auxiliary Carabinieri Course, attended at the Carabinieri Cadet School in Chieti. After completing the course, Saracino was assigned to the 11th Carabinieri Battalion in Bari. These were the initial stages of the career that took him from Puglia to his subsequent experience in Lombardy.

At the end of the 47th Non-Commissioned Officers Cadet Course, the NCO in fact arrived in Lombardy. His first two years of service in the region took place in Milan, at the 3rd Lombardy Carabinieri Battalion. The source therefore places the move to a new area of service during this phase, after the experience gained in Bari.

In 1999, he was transferred to the Carnago Carabinieri Station. Saracino served in that unit for more than twenty years. This represents the longest part of the career described in the source and links his name to a prolonged presence at a Carabinieri station in the province of Varese.

The professional sequence thus includes the start in 1991, training in Chieti, assignment to Bari, the two years in Milan and the long period spent in Carnago. Each step is identified as part of the career preceding his current responsibility in Mornago. The reconstruction lists the locations and courses mentioned in the source without adding any other assignments or stages beyond those documented.

Key points

The new role in Mornago

The most recent phase of Saracino's career took place at the same Station he is now called upon to lead. In 2023, he was assigned to the Mornago Carabinieri Station, where to date he has held the role of subordinate non-commissioned officer.

The transition to leading the station thus comes after a period of service already completed in Mornago. The source describes this development as a path of continuity in the area: the 2023 assignment and the work as a subordinate non-commissioned officer directly precede the assumption of command.

The central point of what follows is the transformation of the role held at the station. Saracino is not presented merely as the service member assigned to a new station, but as the non-commissioned officer who, after serving in Mornago, now assumes its official leadership. This is the succession indicated: first the assignment to the location, then the work as a subordinate non-commissioned officer, and finally the new appointment as Commander.

The new responsibility thus closes the part of the career described in the source relating to his time in Mornago and opens the institutional mission of leading the station. His previous service at the station and the command just assumed are linked by the continuity in the area referred to in the article. For local reporting, the documented transition is therefore from a subordinate role to official responsibility for the Mornago Carabinieri Station.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who is the new commander of the Mornago Carabinieri Station?
The new commander is Lieutenant Carica Speciale Anacleto Antonio Saracino, an officer from Puglia who began his career in the Armed Forces in 1991 and, after a long period in Carnago, was assigned to Mornago in 2023 where he served as a junior non-commissioned officer before assuming command.
Which municipalities fall within the jurisdiction of the Mornago Carabinieri Station?
The Mornago garrison is responsible for public safety and territorial control in the four municipalities of Mornago, Sumirago, Casale Litta and Inarzo, as specified by the source indicating the command perimeter of the station.
What is the hierarchical dependence of the Mornago Carabinieri Station?
The Mornago Carabinieri Station depends on the Gallarate Company and operates under the provincial direction of Colonel Alessandro De Vico, Provincial Commander of the Varese Carabinieri, who welcomed Saracino with a greeting and good luck.
