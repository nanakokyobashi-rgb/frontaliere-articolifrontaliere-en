Key facts

  • What: Sale of the former orphanage on via Tommaso Grossi
  • When: April 16, 2026
  • Where: Como, Italy
  • Who: Como municipal administration
  • Amount: 7,505,555 euros

Twenty days after the historic news of the sale of the former orphanage on via Tommaso Grossi in Como, the identity of the auction winner remains unknown. On April 16, confirmation came of the transfer of the property by the municipal administration for an amount of 7,505,555 euros, with a starting bid set at 7,276,965 euros and only one offer submitted. The notary Mario Mele confirmed the procedure. The palace, known as Palazzo Cernezzi, has hosted various activities over the years, including housing, a pizzeria, a school, and the headquarters of the former district. Now, after 20 days, the legitimate question is: who has acquired the former orphanage?

The mayor Alessandro Rapinese has been contacted several times to ask for information about the auction winner, but without success. Apart from some vague rumors about a possible international fund or a company just across the border, no official information has been provided. The mayor had stated that he had not yet received the minutes at the time of the announcement, and after 20 days the situation remains unchanged. There may be legal terms that prevent communication, but the request remains legitimate to know such possible requirements.
