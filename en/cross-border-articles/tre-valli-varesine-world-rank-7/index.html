Operational details

The internal metric of the portal

The ranking drawn up by ProCyclingStats does not coincide with the official ranking of the International Cycling Union (UCI). To build the ranking, the portal applies an autonomous criterion that measures the overall strength of the runners at the start. The result of a competition therefore depends on the sum of the individual odds of the cyclists lined up at the start.

The calculation starts from the ranking of the individual athletes. The presence of a cyclist in the top ten in the world ranking is worth 50 points for the race. For subsequent positions, the score decreases progressively up to a point for athletes placed within the first thousand places. The sum of these odds produces the score assigned to the race and allows PCs to sort competitions based on the level of the group of starters.

It is this approach that gives technical meaning to the 913 points of the Tre Valli, without transforming them into a figure in the UCI ranking. The statistic measures the value of cyclists present at the start according to the scale of the portal; it is not presented by the source as the official ranking of international cycling.

At the top of the list, ahead of the Lombard competition, is the Tour de France, which leads with 1567 points. The top positions also include the World Road Championships, Lombardy, Milan-Sanremo, the Giro d 'Italia and the Tirreno-Adriatico. The list indicates the small group of tests that precedes the Tre Valli in the ProCyclingStats ranking and completes the numerical table of the ranking.

The score is then linked to the sum of the individual odds and the ranking band occupied by each athlete at the start. The internal metric of the portal translates the value of the group of starters into numbers according to the scale described by the source.

Frequently Asked Questions
What position does Tre Valli Varesine occupy in the ProCyclingStats ranking?
Tre Valli Varesine was ranked seventh in the ProCyclingStats ranking, scoring 913 points. This placement puts it in front of important competitions such as Liège-Bastogne-Liège (eighth), La Vuelta a España (ninth) and Paris-Roubaix (eleventh), confirming its world level in professional cycling.
How does ProCyclingStats calculate the race score?
The portal uses an internal metric based on the ranking of individual athletes. A cyclist in the top ten in the world is worth 50 points, while the values go down in a scalar way up to a point for those in the top thousand. The total score of the race is the sum of the individual shares of the starters, reflecting the value of the group at the start.
Which race precedes the Tre Valli Varesine in the standings?
At the top of the list is the Tour de France, which leads the ranking with 1567 points. Other races preceding the Tre Valli include the Road World Championships, Il Lombardia, Milano-Sanremo, Giro d 'Italia and Tirreno-Adriatico.
Why do the top teams participate in the Tre Valli Varesine?
