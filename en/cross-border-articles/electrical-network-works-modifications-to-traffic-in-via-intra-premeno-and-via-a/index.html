Changes to the roads in Via Intra Premeno

The municipality of Verbania has placed restrictions on the circulation in Via Intra Premeno and via alla Campagna to allow important works of installation of electrical infrastructures commissioned by Elecnor on behalf of e-distribuzione. These limitations will be in effect from July 20 to August 7, 2026.

The e-distribution operators, a company controlled by Enel, commissioned Elecnor to install the electrical infrastructure in the area of via Intra Premeno and via alla Campagna. This intervention is necessary to ensure the safety and stability of the electrical system in the area.

The municipality of Verbania has determined that the restrictions on movement will be in force from 8:00 am on 20 July until 6:00 pm on 7 August 2026. During this period, the Via Intra Premeno and the Via alla Campagna will be closed to vehicular traffic, except for residents and authorised vehicles.

The municipality of Verbania has also established that the traffic restrictions will be managed in collaboration with the State Police and with the public transport company of Verbania. The e-distribution operators have ensured that the works will be carried out in such a way as not to interfere with the movement of residents and tourists.

Key points

Road changes in via Intra Premeno: useful information for residents and motorists

The Ticino Region has announced a series of road changes in Via Intra Premeno, one of the main connecting roads between the municipalities of Verbania and Premeno. The works, scheduled to be completed by the end of the year, aim to improve the safety and fluidity of traffic in this strategic area.

For more information, residents and motorists are invited to contact the municipality of Verbania, which has provided the following clarifications:

"Our priority is to ensure the safety of citizens and drivers. The redevelopment of the Intra Premeno route is an important step towards achieving this goal." (Sandro Tognini, Mayor of Verbania)

The redevelopment of the Via Intra Premeno was planned in two phases. The first phase, which has already been completed, saw the rehabilitation of about 1 kilometre of road, with the replacement of about 200 metres of stone curb. The second phase, which will take place between July and September, involves the rehabilitation of an additional 500 meters of road, with the replacement of about 300 meters of stone curb.
