Pedotti Primary School in Luvinate devoted a morning to the Welcome Celebration. There was no traditional lesson for the children: the programme offered experiential activities and numerous workshops coordinated by the Teaching Staff, with safety and prevention as the common thread.

On Saturday, experts, professionals, volunteers and parents gathered at the school to describe and explain their activities to the pupils.

Activities for the pupils

The most significant presence was that of the State Scientific Police. The officers illustrated evidence-gathering activities, using fingerprints, microscopes and special equipment. The demonstration brought tools and procedures related to the work of the Scientific Police into the school.

Alongside the Scientific Police, numerous Red Cross volunteers also took part. With the pupils, they covered the basic rules of First Aid and how to ask for help effectively. The workshop thus added the topics of intervention and requesting assistance to the programme.

Other activities included recognizing and defending against cyberbullying, useful English words for asking for help in a large city, and actions for a safe school. This last activity was linked to educating aware and active citizens. The morning thus brought together different experiences, all included in the programme of the Welcome Celebration.

Operational details

A network of experiences within the school

Themes and contributions

The Welcome Festival did not focus on a single workshop. The organizers created a structured morning in which children and adults were able to approach different skills united by the themes of safety and prevention.

The contribution of the Red Cross concerned the basic rules of first aid and how to ask for help effectively. The State Scientific Police, on the other hand, demonstrated the work of searching for evidence through fingerprints, microscopes and special equipment. Alongside these activities, the program included cyberbullying, English for asking for help in a big city and actions for a safe school.

The cyberbullying workshop was dedicated to recognizing and defending oneself against this phenomenon. The English activity had a practical purpose indicated in the program: learning useful words for asking for help in a big city. The safe-school program, on the other hand, aimed to make students aware and active citizens.

Other activities took place to complete the week. The Carabinieri, the Intermunicipal Civil Protection Service and the Local Police had already come to the school for other learning experiences. The source presents these events as part of the same commitment to safety and prevention.

The work involved the Teaching Staff together with experts, professionals, volunteers and parents. The school therefore hosted different contributions, keeping the workshops within a single program dedicated to the students. Searching for evidence, first aid, cyberbullying, the English language and behaviors for a safe school were the topics proposed during the week and throughout the morning.

Key points

Families and school together

The role of the Parents' Association

During the morning and then at lunchtime, the Luvinate Parents' Association, as usual, organized moments of meeting and conviviality with families and parents. Its involvement accompanied the day dedicated to workshops with a space aimed at family participation.

Lunch and the meetings completed a Welcoming Celebration built around the school. The experiential component, coordinated by the Teaching Staff, was joined by the moments of sharing organized by the association. The presence of parents and families was already planned among the contributions of the morning, together with those of experts and professionals.

The Pedotti Primary School chose this path to launch the school year. The program brought the community, families and school together through activities on safety and prevention. The source describes this choice as the beginning of a new great alliance among these parties.

During the week, meetings with the Carabinieri, the Intermunicipal Civil Protection Service and the Local Police had prepared other opportunities for learning. The Welcoming Celebration then brought the State Scientific Police, the Red Cross, experts, parents and the Teaching Staff together at the school.

The program did not include a morning of traditional lessons, but experiential activities aimed at primary school children. The closing with the moments of meeting and lunch kept families and parents at the center, while the workshops addressed first aid, asking for help, cyberbullying, English and actions for a safe school.

The final thanks went to the Luvinate Parents' Association, which organized the welcome and conviviality. For Pedotti, the start of the school year was thus linked to collaboration among school, families and community.
