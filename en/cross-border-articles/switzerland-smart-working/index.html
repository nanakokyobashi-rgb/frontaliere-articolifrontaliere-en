Operational details

Switzerland is the European leader in smart working

The increase in the percentage of job positions that provide for the possibility of working remotely is mainly driven by office and administrative roles, followed by those in the mechanical industry sector and highly digitized positions in finance.

In Switzerland, in particular, the Ticino region was among the first to implement remote work policies, thanks to its strategic geographical location and efficient transport network. According to 2022 data, about 30% of employees in the Canton of Ticino work at least one day a week remotely, while 15% work entirely from home.

According to research conducted by the University of Italian Switzerland (USI), 60% of Ticino companies have implemented remote work policies, an increase of 20% compared to 2020. The main reasons why Ticino companies choose to implement remote work are the reduction of rental and transport costs, as well as the increase in productivity and flexibility of employees.

However, remote work is not without challenges. Key points

Switzerland is the European leader in smart working

The frontier worker can benefit from this trend by working remotely and taking advantage of the job opportunities available in Switzerland. It is important to note that the percentage of job positions that include the possibility of working remotely may vary depending on the sector and region. According to a study conducted by the Foundation for Research on Work (fors) in 2020, 72% of Swiss companies have already implemented flexible working arrangements, including remote work.

Switzerland was among the first nations to introduce regulations to regulate remote work. In 2019, the Swiss Confederation passed the Federal Labour Law, which provides for the possibility of working remotely for at least 3 days per week. The law came into force on 1 January 2020 and requires companies with more than 10 employees to offer at least 3 days of remote work per week.

The Ticino region is particularly interesting for entrepreneurs who want to take advantage of remote work opportunities. According to data from the Canton of Ticino, in 2020 60% of companies in the canton had already implemented flexible working methods, including remote work. Ticino is also one of the regions with the highest percentage of workers performing remote work activities, with 40% of workers in the canton working at least 1 day a week remotely.
