Frontier workers: Giorgetti signs the decree on contributions (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 18 September 2026

Economy Minister Giancarlo Giorgetti signed the decree on state contributions for border municipalities based on the Rome-Berna 2020 agreement.

Context

In brief

  • Minister Giorgetti signs decree on contributions
  • Recipients are border municipalities
  • Resources for the 20-kilometer strip

Key facts

  • Minister: Giancarlo Giorgetti
  • Role: Minister of Economy and Finance
  • Reference agreement: 2020 Rome-Bern Agreement
  • Cantons involved: Ticino, Grisons, and Valais
  • Fund involved: Fund for the economic development of border territories

Italian Minister of Economy and Finance Giancarlo Giorgetti signed the decree this afternoon concerning the determination of state contributions to border municipalities. The official communication was released directly by the Italian Ministry of Economy and Finance. The measure officially originates within the framework of the agreement between Rome and Bern signed in 2020, marking a step of great institutional significance for the management of state economic resources allocated to territorial areas affected by commuting and geographical proximity to the Swiss Confederation.

The territories receiving the resources

The decree signed by Minister Giorgetti specifically grants municipalities located in the 20-kilometer strip along the border with Italy the resources deriving from the Fund for the economic development of border territories. This geographical perimeter directly involves Italian and foreign territories connected to the Swiss cantons of Ticino, Grisons, and Valais. The signing of the measure represents the concrete implementation of the understandings defined within the bilateral framework between Italy and Switzerland, laying the foundations for the effective disbursement of funds established by current regulations and international agreements signed in past years by the respective central governments.

Operational details

The signing of the decree by the Italian Minister of Economy and Finance Giancarlo Giorgetti defines the operating rules for determining the state contribution to border municipalities. This measure, originated as part of the agreement between Rome and Bern in 2020, directly affects the territories located in the 20-kilometre strip along the border with Italy, including the territorial areas bordering the cantons of Ticino, Grisons and Valais. Access to the resources of the Fund for the economic development of border territories is a central element for the management of local economic dynamics linked to the presence of workers and cross-border flows.

Analysis of the provision and territories

The impact of the ministerial decree is reflected on the financial endowment of the Italian and foreign border municipalities affected by the legislation. The division of the resources managed through the Fund for the economic development of the border territories responds to the directives established by the Italian Ministry of Economy and Finance on the basis of the agreement signed between Rome and Bern in 2020. The municipalities included in the 20-kilometre band of the cantons of Ticino, Grisons and Valais are the direct beneficiaries of this state allocation, designed to support local economies and territories directly involved in the management of the cross-border phenomenon between the two countries.

Key points

The administrative path linked to the decree signed by the Minister of Economy and Finance Giancarlo Giorgetti now provides for the application of the ministerial provisions in accordance with the provisions of the agreement between Rome and Bern of 2020. Local administrations and institutional stakeholders must refer to the official channels of the Italian Ministry of Economy and Finance to verify the operational methods of disbursement and distribution of the resources of the Fund for the economic development of the border territories provided for the municipalities of the 20-kilometer band in the cantons of Ticino, Graubünden and Valais.

Institutional procedure and audits
