Basic regulatory and tax aspects

The taxation system for those who live in Clivio and work in Ticino provides for withholding tax to be withheld in Switzerland. To avoid double taxation, Italian law recognizes a specific tax credit, which must be reported in section CE of Form 730. The transitional regime under Article 9 of the Agreement applies only to those who meet the treaty definition and worked in the Graubünden, Ticino or Valais between December 31, 2018 and July 17, 2023; the covered employment income is taxed exclusively in Switzerland. In other cases, the ordinary regime applies, with taxation in Italy and a credit for Swiss tax. The 10.000 euro amount under Article 4 of Law 83/2023 is merely the limit for the income referred to in Article 1, paragraph 175, of Law 147/2013, not a general tax-free allowance. The Convention for the avoidance of double taxation between Italy and Switzerland, originally signed on March 9, 1976, constitutes the legal reference framework for bilateral relations in this area.

From a social security and pension perspective, cross-border workers pay contributions in Switzerland at the prescribed rates, which include the AVS/AI/IPG schemes, with an employee share of 5.3%, AD/AC unemployment insurance at 1.1% calculated up to a ceiling of 148.200 francs, LAINF coverage ranging from 0.7% to 1.5%, and LPP contributions for occupational pensions varying between 7% and 18% depending on age bracket from age 25 onward. In Italy, the applicable IRPEF rates follow the current brackets, set at 23% up to 28.000 euros, 33% for the bracket between 28.001 and 50.000 euros, and 43% for income exceeding the 50.000 euro threshold. Tax rates and withholding taxes are established by federal and cantonal laws and administered by the Federal Tax Administration AFC/ESTV at the federal level and by the competent cantonal administrations.

Frequently Asked Questions
Where is the income of the border worker who lives in Clivio and works in Ticino taxed?
Income from employment is subject to withholding tax withheld in Switzerland. Italy avoids double taxation by recognizing a special tax credit through the CE framework of model 730.
Who qualifies for the transitional regime in Article 9?
Article 9’s transitional regime applies only to workers who meet the Agreement’s definition and worked in Graubünden, Ticino or Valais between 31 December 2018 and 17 July 2023; covered employment income is taxable exclusively in Switzerland. The ordinary regime applies in other cases, with Italian taxation and credit for Swiss tax where applicable. The €10,000 in Article 4 of Law 83/2023 is only the limit for income referred to in Article 1(175) of Law 147/2013, not a general allowance. How does the right of option for LAMal health insurance work?
Border workers with a G permit who hold an activity in Switzerland have the right of option between the Swiss health system (LAMal) and the Italian one. The Swiss health insurance fund provides deductibles for adults ranging from 300 to 2,500 Swiss francs.
