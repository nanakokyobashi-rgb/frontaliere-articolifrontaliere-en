Canottieri Varese signs friendship pact with Nagano Rowing

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 28 September 2026

On Friday, September 25, in a videoconference between Schiranna and Shimosuwa, the Canottieri Varese Company and the Nagano Rowing Association signed a twinning agreement for cultural exchanges, shared training and technical development, with the first showcase in 2027.

On Friday, September 25, in the morning, Società Canottieri Varese and Nagano Rowing Association signed a twinning agreement and international partnership via video conference. The signing connected the historic headquarters in Schiranna with the Japanese authorities gathered in Shimosuwa, giving shape to an agreement built on cultural exchanges, shared training, technical development and local synergies between Italy and Japan.

Connected from Schiranna were Canottieri Varese president Paolo Consonni and Varese deputy mayor Ivana Perusin. From Japan, Yoshiki Kinoshita, federal president of the Nagano Rowing Association, and Toru Miyasaka, mayor of Shimosuwa, took part. The signing came after an initial virtual contact between the youngsters of the two clubs, which took place last year.

The agreement and its Olympic roots

The roots of the agreement date back to the welcome extended to the Italian delegation during the 2020 Tokyo Olympic Games. Kinoshita recalled that Shimosuwa had welcomed thirty-six members of the Italian national team. On that occasion, technical director Francesco Cattaneo had introduced the group by saying that he had the strongest team in the world; the president of the Nagano Rowing Association linked that assessment to the medals won.

«Varese is one of the nerve centers of Italian rowing,» said Yoshiki Kinoshita.

In his remarks, the Japanese president described the friendship with a club rich in history and tradition as a source of great joy. He identified contact with Varese's culture and passion as a particular stimulus for younger athletes, considered the future of the sport. The stated aim is to safeguard and nurture the bond, making the collaboration an opportunity to make rowing even more fascinating.

Consonni presented the twinning as an important step for the Varese club. He recalled that the shared passion for oars narrows an enormous geographical distance and identified 2027 as the first major showcase for an in-person meeting. On that occasion, Canottieri Varese intends to welcome the Japanese crews at the Schiranna course. The simultaneous signing of the official documents and the final photographs closed the ceremony, bringing the tradition of the club and the city toward an international dimension.

Frequently Asked Questions
When was the agreement signed between Canottieri Varese and Nagano Rowing Association?
The agreement was signed on Friday 25 September morning, by videoconference that connected the historic headquarters of Schiranna in Varese with the Japanese authorities gathered in Shimosuwa. The signing was attended by President Paolo Consonni and Deputy Mayor Ivana Perusin for Varese, and Yoshiki Kinoshita, President of the Nagano Rowing Association, along with Mayor Toru Miyasaka of Shimosuwa.
What are the main objectives of the twinning agreement?
The agreement provides for cultural exchanges, shared training and technical development between the two partnerships, as well as territorial synergies that include tourism, trade and industry. The parties also intend to enhance the historical heritage of Canottieri Varese and promote long-term institutional and sporting meetings.
What is the first major showcase planned for the live meeting between the two companies?
The first opportunity to meet in person is indicated in the World Rowing Masters Regatta of 2027, when the Canottieri Varese plans to welcome the Japanese crews to the Schiranna basin. This event represents the first step in transforming the friendship signed in videoconference into a shared sports experience.
