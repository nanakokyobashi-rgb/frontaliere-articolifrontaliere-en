Openjobmetis Varese faces a crucial challenge in Bologna. The match on May 10, 2026, scheduled for 17:00 at PalaDozza, could decide the fate of the white and red team. A victory would guarantee access to the playoffs, while a defeat could leave the team dependent on the results of other teams.

The 2018 precedent

The last time Pallacanestro Varese reached the playoffs was in 2018. On that occasion, Caja's team managed to win a crucial match against Virtus Bologna. The game ended with a score of 69-71, thanks to a great performance by Larson and the decisive free throws by Avramovic. This success allowed the team to qualify for the playoffs.

The current situation

Today, Virtus Bologna is a very different team from 2018. Now known as Olidata, Virtus has already won the regular season and secured the first seed for the playoffs. Despite this, the team does not intend to relax against Varese. Bolognabasket's director, Bruno Trebbi, stated that Virtus will play with determination, both as a matter of principle and for internal competition.

The challenge on the court

The match promises to be intense. Virtus, with eight foreigners on the team but only six foreign players allowed on the court, will have to manage internal competition. Against Varese, Virtus will not be able to count on the injured Matt Morgan but could recover Vildoza and leave out Yago Dos Santos or Smailagic. Kastritis, coach of Openjobmetis, emphasized the importance of focusing on what the team can control: energy on the rebound and attention to fall back when the ball ends up in Bologna's hands.

The implications for the playoffs

A victory in Bologna would be fundamental for Openjobmetis. Not only would it guarantee direct access to the playoffs, but it would also avoid the team having to cheer for other teams. If Varese loses and Trento wins, it will be the Aquila to qualify for the post-season. There is also a remote possibility that Varese finishes seventh in case of success and simultaneous defeat of Trieste, but this eventuality is considered unlikely.

The issue of away games

Another interesting aspect concerns away games. After the clashes between the ultras of the two factions in 2018, away games are still banned in both directions. This ban has been maintained for safety reasons, but it might be time to review it. Most fans do not cause problems, and the nominal ticket should reduce risks. Managing problematic situations rather than banning away games could be a better solution.
