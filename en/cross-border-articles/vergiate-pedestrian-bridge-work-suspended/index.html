Vergiate, suspended the work on the catwalk until September (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 14 July 2026

Work on the new Vergiate catwalk is temporarily at a standstill. Recovery scheduled for the end of September pending new RFI support.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Work on the Vergiate catwalk temporarily suspended.
  • Necessary stop while waiting for the new supports prescribed by RFI.
  • Recovery of the construction site scheduled for the end of September.

Key facts

  • What: Pedestrian walkway construction site suspension.
  • When: July 2026.
  • Where: Vergiate, between via Don Locatelli and statale del Sempione.
  • Who: Mayor Daniele Parrino and Rfi.
  • Status: New media required during production.

The construction works of the new pedestrian walkway located in Vergiate, in the immediate vicinity of the railway bridge that connects via Don Locatelli with the Sempione state road, have suffered a temporary halt. As officially communicated by the local administration, the shutdown of the construction site was foreseen by the timetable of the works, which were started in recent weeks with the night installation of the structure.

The mayor of Vergiate, Daniele Parrino, clarified through social media channels that this interruption is necessary to wait for the completion of the production of the new technical supports prescribed by Rfi (Italian Railway Network). Currently, these essential components for the safety and stability of the work are under construction. The official resumption of construction activities is scheduled for the end of September. Frequently Asked Questions
Why have the catwalk works been suspended?
The suspension was decided to wait for the implementation of the new technical supports prescribed by Rfi (Italian Railway Network), currently in production. This measure is necessary to ensure the compliance and safety of the facility.
When is work scheduled to resume?
According to the mayor of Vergiate, Daniele Parrino, the official resumption of construction activities is scheduled for the end of September 2026.
Does the suspension affect border crossers' traffic?
The construction site is limited to the pedestrian walkway between Via Don Locatelli and the Sempione road. Currently there are no direct repercussions on the main vehicular traffic, although it is advisable to monitor any updates of the municipality in view of the resumption of work in September.
