UISP Karate: the new season starts from Arcisate

The UISP karate sporting year starts again from Arcisate, where the long-awaited meeting took place which brought together regional technical representatives, masters, instructors, aspiring instructors and practitioners with high-grade black belts. The meeting brought together those who bring experience and those who are entering the technical path, with the aim of sharing skills, experience and enthusiasm in building the new season.

For UISP Lombardia, the appointment represented a moment of joint work. The meeting was not only dedicated to the transmission of movements, but to the construction of a path capable of combining technique, knowledge and participation. The presence of experts and new talent has allowed different experiences to be concentrated in the same space, with a shared direction for the season that has just begun.

The study of kata

In the limited time available, each master instructor summarized and illustrated with great accuracy the steps of one of the 26 Shotokan kata. The presentation allowed participants to understand and delve deeper into the technique, meaning and application of each form in reality.

The discussion was friendly and open. Those in attendance were able to interact and present any technical variations of the illustrated kata, adding historical curiosities, applications and sporting aspects related to competitions to the preparation. The training described by Filippo Sanfilippo, Uisp regional technical manager, was therefore developed through demonstration, in-depth analysis and dialogue.

The restart of the season thus arises from the collaboration between the different components of the movement. The indicated objective is to create a cohesive group, capable of participating in a common path in the internships scheduled for the black belt courses.

Operational details

A training that brings together technique and principles

The value of the meeting described by Sanfilippo lies in the way in which technical preparation is linked to understanding of the discipline. The sequence of a kata is not presented as a set of movements to be repeated, but as a work that includes technique, meaning and application in reality. Added to these elements are the variations discussed by the participants, historical curiosities and aspects related to the competitions.

This setting offers a broader reading of the training. The in-depth study does not only concern the precision of execution, but also the ability to understand the essence and spirit of each individual kata. Transmission to the new generations is indicated as a task to be carried out without distorting the technique, maintaining the link with a discipline which, according to the intervention, comes from very far away and has been handed down for many centuries.

The educational dimension

Alongside the technical heritage, the text highlights the principles of karate: self-control, universal respect and self-knowledge. Technique, principles and constant practice are described as elements capable of forming more balanced and correct people, ready to help and give to others without making noise.

The growth indicated for the new season is therefore threefold: technical, educational, competitive and sporting. Experience and innovation must meet in stages, on and off the tatami, to encourage shared progress. Sanfilippo also expresses the belief that introducing discipline into schools could lead to better future generations.

Key points

From the seminar to the common path

The intervention indicates a precise direction for those participating in the training, but does not provide a calendar, times, contacts or registration methods. It is therefore not possible to add administrative procedures or indicate appointments other than those described. The actions that can be taken from the text concern exclusively technical work, comparison and continuity of the path.

The steps indicated by the intervention

  • Take the sequence of one of the 26 Shotokan kata as the object of study.
  • Deepen together technique, meaning and application in reality.
  • Use friendly discussion to discuss any technical variations.
  • Connect the practice to the principles of self-control, universal respect and self-knowledge.
  • Continue with the internships scheduled for the black belt courses.

The transition from a moment of advanced training to a common path requires, in the perspective described by Sanfilippo, the active participation of everyone. The skills of the most expert technicians must meet the new generations, while practice must keep technique, principles and consistency together. The expected result is not only growth in preparation, but also educational, competitive and sporting growth.

The same logic applies to transmission to new generations. The text invites us to safeguard the technique without distorting it and to preserve its essence, also making the principles that accompany it understood. The idea is to build a cohesive group, capable of growing through collaboration and discussion, with a commitment that concerns the tatami and what happens outside the tatami.
