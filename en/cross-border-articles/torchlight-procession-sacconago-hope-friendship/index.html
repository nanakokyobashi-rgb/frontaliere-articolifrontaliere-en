A Community Event with Deep Roots

The Torch of Sacconago is an event that gathers around 200 people every year, not only from Sacconago but also from neighboring towns such as Agno, Magliaso, and Caslano. The tradition, which began in 1953, has become a reference point for the community, bringing families and friends together in a moment of sharing and spirituality. The torch, which symbolically represents light and hope, is lit every year on August 15th and travels a route of about 2 kilometers, starting from the center of the village and arriving at the church of Madonna in Campagna.

The Crystal Torch: A Touch of Elegance

The crystal torch, crafted by Franco Mello, a local artisan, has become a distinctive symbol of the event. Every year, Mello dedicates about 50 hours of work to create a unique torch, using high-quality crystals and traditional techniques. The cost of the torch is around 500 Swiss francs, an investment that the community is happy to make to keep this tradition alive.

The Group Chat: Real-Time Sharing

The group chat, created for the occasion, allowed those who could not be present to live every moment of the event. During the evening, over 100 images and 50 videos were shared, allowing family and friends far away to follow the torch's journey. The chat also facilitated fundraising for the event, with donations reaching 2,000 Swiss francs.

The Presence of Local Authorities

The presence of local authorities, including the mayor of Agno and the municipal councilor of Magliaso, underscored the importance of the event for the community. The authorities actively participated in the ceremony, contributing to a sense of unity and belonging among the participants.

Don Paolo's Prayer

The prayer of Don Paolo, the parish priest of the Madonna in Campagna church, united all participants in a moment of reflection and spirituality. Don Paolo reminded everyone that each person can make a difference, encouraging the attendees to uphold the values of hope and friendship in their daily lives.
