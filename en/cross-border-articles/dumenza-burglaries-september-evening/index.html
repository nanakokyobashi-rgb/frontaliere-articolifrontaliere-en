Dumenza, three home burglaries and a failed heist

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 27 September 2026

Three homes were targeted on via Santuario, via Fiume and via Dante. On via XX Settembre, the thieves fled after hearing the owners.

Context

In brief

  • Three burglaries committed in Dumenza in a single evening
  • Via Santuario, via Fiume and via Dante targeted
  • The break-in on via XX Settembre failed
  • The Carabinieri of the Dumenza Station are investigating

Key facts

  • What: three burglaries and one attempted burglary
  • When: Friday 25 September
  • Where: via Santuario, via Fiume, via Dante and via XX Settembre
  • Loot on via Santuario: 600 euros in cash
  • Loot on via Fiume: gold
  • Loot on via Dante: gold and money
  • Outcome on via XX Settembre: escape after the owners were present
  • Investigation: Carabinieri of the Dumenza Station

Three burglaries were carried out and a fourth attempted over the course of a single evening. This is what happened in Dumenza on Friday 25 September, with several homes targeted by unknown individuals and the Carabinieri of the Dumenza Station engaged in the investigation.

One of the burglaries took place on via Santuario. The thieves managed to enter the home and took away 600 euros in cash. In this case, the loot consisted of money.

Another incident was reported on via Fiume. Here too, the criminals managed to get inside a house; after searching for valuables, they took away some gold.

The third burglary was carried out on via Dante. Gold and money were stolen from this home before the perpetrators managed to leave and disappear without a trace.

The fourth case concerns via XX Settembre and had a different outcome. The burglary was not completed: the unknown individuals heard the owners inside the home and decided to flee. They too managed to disappear without a trace.

The homes involved were therefore four, located on four different streets. In three cases, the thieves’ entry ended with property being stolen: cash on via Santuario, gold on via Fiume, and gold and money on via Dante. In the fourth incident, however, the residents’ presence interrupted the action.

The people involved reported the incidents to the Carabinieri of the Dumenza Station, who are working to reconstruct the sequence of events.

Operational details

The fact that emerges from the evening is the concentration of four incidents in homes in Dumenza. The streets indicated are via Santuario, via Fiume, via Dante and via XX Settembre, with three completed thefts and one interrupted attempt.

The loot also reflects different situations. In via Santuario, 600 euros in cash were taken; in via Fiume, the perpetrators searched for valuables and stole gold; in via Dante, they took gold and money.

The Interrupted Break-In

The events on via XX Settembre differ from those at the other addresses. The unknown individuals had begun their action, but the presence of the owners inside the home prompted them to give up. The theft thus remained at the attempted stage, and the thieves fled.

In the other three cases, however, entry into the home was followed by the theft of property. In via Santuario, the loot consisted of cash; in via Fiume, of gold; in via Dante, of gold and money. After leaving, the perpetrators vanished without a trace.

The perpetrators are described as unknown individuals or criminals. The people concerned reported what had happened to the Carabinieri of the Dumenza Station, who are carrying out investigations to reconstruct the various stages of the evening.

The reports concern the same evening, Friday 25 September, but four homes with different outcomes. The picture includes cash, gold, gold and money, as well as an attempt without a completed theft. The presence of the owners therefore changed the outcome of the incident on via XX Settembre, while at the other addresses the thieves managed to get away after taking the property.

Key points

The follow-up to the incident is entrusted to the investigations by the Carabinieri of the Dumenza Station. The officers are trying to reconstruct what happened in the four homes and identify those responsible for the burglaries, as reported after the notifications from the people involved.

At the time of the report, the perpetrators remain unknown. After carrying out the three burglaries, they managed to get away without leaving a trace; on via XX Settembre, they fled after hearing the owners.

The appeal to residents

The message addressed to the public concerns any suspicious people or cars. Residents are urged to report to the Carabinieri any noticed on Friday evening in the areas concerned or, more generally, in the Dumenza area. The request is part of the investigations already begun by the local Station.

The appeal is not limited to the streets where the incidents were reported. The source also refers to the Dumenza area as a whole, focusing on observations gathered on Friday evening. According to the appeal, any information could prove useful to the investigation.

The information requested is therefore connected to the four incidents: the three burglaries on via Santuario, via Fiume and via Dante, and the attempted burglary on via XX Settembre. The aim of the investigations remains to reconstruct what happened and try to identify those responsible.

For now, the indicated course of action is the Carabinieri's investigations and the appeal to residents. Residents are being asked to provide any useful information, while the Carabinieri continue their investigations.
