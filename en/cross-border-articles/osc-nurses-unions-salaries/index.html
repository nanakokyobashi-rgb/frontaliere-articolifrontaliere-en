In 2019, the Joint Committee responsible formally established an adjustment for the nursing staff of the Cantonal Socio-Psychiatric Organization. According to the source, the planned change concerns moving from grade 4 to 5 for nurses and from grade 5 to 6 for nurses with specialization.

The reclassification would serve to recognize the training, skills and responsibilities required of those working in the delicate context of the Osc. The source also indicates a possible strengthening of the profession's appeal. Annual increases, depending on the role held, would range from 5'159 to 8'941 Swiss francs.

A request still unresolved

However, the adjustment has remained frozen. Vpod and Ocst therefore continued their mobilization with the Staff Committee: after submitting a petition in June 2022, they publicly stated their position several times and sent the Council of State several formal requests, the last in January 2026.

After the latest meeting on the issue, the Government reiterated its refusal and, for financial reasons, denied recognition that the unions describe as already established by the social partners. In the face of the block on the reclassification and yet another refusal, the unions and the Staff Committee invited Osc staff to Mendrisio, at 14.30 on Tuesday, November 10, for an assembly.

The meeting will serve to take stock of the dispute and discuss actions in support of the claim. The source also notes that the meeting will address recovery of the cost of living for public, parapublic and social and healthcare staff, an issue connected to the petition and the union picket scheduled for November 24 at 18 in front of the Government Palace.

Reclassification and cost of living

The demand for reclassification and cost-of-living compensation appear in the same mobilization, but the text presents them as two issues to discuss. The first issue concerns recognition of Osc salary classes; the second concerns public-sector, parapublic and social-healthcare staff and will also be linked to the demonstration on November 24.

The source reports that the Government justified the refusal on financial grounds and that the budget also blocks this salary adjustment. Neither the amount of the budget nor a specific figure for cost-of-living compensation is reported. The documented sequence runs from the decision of the Joint Commission in 2019 to the petition of June 2022, then to the formal request of January 2026 and the Government's latest negative response.

Key points

The second event indicated in the source is the union rally on November 24 at 18 in front of the Government Palace. It is being organized by Ocst, Vpod and Sit. The rally concerns recovery of the cost-of-living adjustment for public, parapublic and social and healthcare personnel, while the November 10 meeting is convened for Osc staff and to discuss actions concerning reclassification.
