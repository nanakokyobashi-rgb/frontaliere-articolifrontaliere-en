Frequently Asked Questions
When does the new Border Arrangement come into force?
The new Borders Agreement entered into force on 1 January 2024. This agreement, signed on 23 December 2020, was ratified by Italy with Law 83 of 13 June 2023.
What is the new tax exemption for frontier workers?
For new frontier workers, i.e. those who were not active in Ticino before 17 July 2023, the tax exemption was raised to CHF 10,000. The old frontier workers maintain the exemption of CHF7,500, with a transitional regime until 2033.
Where do I pay taxes if I work in Ticino and live in Brenta?
Income tax at source is only withheld in Switzerland. Italy applies a tax credit to avoid double taxation, which can be managed through the CE framework of the model 730.
What are the Swiss social contributions to consider?
The main Swiss social contributions include AVS/AI/IPG (5.3%), AD/AC (1.1%), LAINF (0.7-1.5%) and LPP (Pension Fund, progressive rates from 7% upwards for the age group 25-34 years).
