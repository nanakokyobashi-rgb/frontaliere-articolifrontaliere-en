Key points

Action

The next institutional tables were set to discuss the Beko crisis and find solutions for the workers and the territory. An extraordinary Provincial Council dedicated to the Beko and Electrolux crises is scheduled for 29 July. Between 20 and 25 September there will be a new specific provincial table on Beko, also extended to companies in the induced sector, with the aim of quantifying the economic repercussions on the territory.

Finally, on October 1, a ministerial table on the industrial level will be held at the Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy, in the presence of the national secretariats of FIM, Fiom and Uilm. The unions also inform that they are waiting for the convening of a further technical table with the Lombardy Region and announce that constant updates will follow after the next meetings, without excluding any mobilisation initiatives in support of the dispute.

The Beko crisis affected about 1,200 workers in the province of Varese alone, including many residents in the municipalities of Cazzago, Bodio and Montegrino. According to estimates, the closure of the factory would have an economic impact of about 150 million euros on the territory, with a loss of gross income of about 100 million euros.
