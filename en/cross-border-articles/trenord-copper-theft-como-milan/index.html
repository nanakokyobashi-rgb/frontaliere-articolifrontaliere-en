Theft and blocking of traffic

The morning opened with heavy inconvenience for commuters on the Como-Milano line of Trenord. A theft of copper cables along the route caused a breakdown in the management and safety systems of rail traffic, making it impossible to carry out normal services. Trenord has suspended all races between Fino Mornasco and Milan Cadorna, compromising circulation from the first hours.

The theft of conductive material is not a trivial problem: copper cables are crucial elements for the operation of railway systems. Their subtraction has generated a chain effect of delays, route changes and cancellations on the entire affected node. The network technicians immediately intervened, committed to restoring the correct functioning of the systems and gradually resuming the service.

What it means for those who move across borders

Border commuters who use this route face a double challenge: not only do they have to manage a sudden blockage of circulation, but they also have to reorganize travel times in a way that is compatible with working hours in Lombardy. A person who normally takes 45 minutes to reach Milan from Como may have to calculate twice as much time using shuttle buses. For those who work in sectors that have strict schedules (healthcare, manufacturing, finance), this uncertainty generates stress and the risk of delays at work.

The blockade also impacts those who depend on the Como-Milan connection to reach other destinations in Lombardy (Lecco, Monza, Brianza). The province of Como, strategically close to Ticino, is in fact an important pole of attraction for cross-border work. Checking the opzioni di mobilità alternatives becomes essential to plan commutes compatible with the work commitment.

Alternative with replacement buses

Trenord has set up a replacement bus service to cover the stops involved in the blockade. This is an important solution to limit inconvenience to travellers, although buses do not guarantee the same speed and capacity as trains. Travel times are significantly extended: the shuttles must follow ordinary roads and do not benefit from the infrastructural advantages of the railway.

Key points

How to Organize Yourself During the Traffic Blockade

For commuters who need to reach their workplace despite the disruption, the key word is constant monitoring. The first step is to check the Trenord app or the official website to verify the real-time situation: do not wait until the last minute to discover a blockade. Replacement bus shuttles follow specific schedules and depart from designated collection points, different from the usual stops. Identifying the exact departure point is crucial.

Those who have the possibility to use private cars might consider this option for the days of blockade, aware that the restoration periods could extend beyond the morning. For those who use this route daily, it is advisable to stay connected to the station announcements, which provide updates on the progress of the work.

A second element of planning concerns time flexibility: negotiating with the employer for staggered hours or remote work for the days of blockade can significantly reduce stress. If your company allows remote working, inform your team of the disruption in advance.
