Thunderstorms and hail in Lugano: level 3 alert

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 12 May 2026

MeteoSwiss has issued a level 3 bad weather alert for Lugano and Bellinzona. Hail in Capriasca, risk of landslides and flooding

Context

In brief

  • Level 3 weather alert for Luganese and Bellinzonese
  • Hail in Capriasca, roads whitened
  • Risk of landslides, flooding, and uprooted trees

Key facts

  • What: Level 3 weather alert
  • When: 11 May 2026
  • Where: Luganese and Bellinzonese
  • Who: MeteoSwiss
  • Amount: Not specified

MeteoSwiss has issued a level 3 alert for the Luganese and Bellinzonese regions. The federal agency warns of the risk of landslides on steep slopes, sudden flash floods in torrents, flooding, hail damage, broken branches, uprooted trees, and lightning. Precipitation intensified within minutes, turning into hailstones that whitened roads and surfaces in Capriasca.

The bad weather is affecting the entire canton, with particular criticalities in the Luganese and Bellinzonese areas. Hail has caused damage to crops and local infrastructure, while heavy rains have increased the risk of landslides and flooding.

Implications for cross-border workers

Cross-border workers commuting to Luganese and Bellinzonese should pay particular attention to adverse weather conditions. Roads may be closed or made dangerous due to hail and landslides. It is advisable to constantly monitor MeteoSwiss updates and follow local authorities' instructions.

Additionally, cross-border workers using border crossings such as Brogeda and Gaggiolo should be prepared for possible delays or temporary closures due to weather conditions. It is important to plan alternative routes and check road conditions before setting off.

What to do in case of weather alert

In case of a level 3 weather alert, it is crucial to follow the instructions of local authorities. Avoid going out if not necessary, and if already traveling, seek shelter in a safe place. It is important to constantly monitor MeteoSwiss updates and follow law enforcement instructions.

For cross-border workers commuting to Luganese and Bellinzonese, it is advisable to contact their employer to verify any changes in working hours or access methods to workplaces. In some cases, it may be necessary to work from home or use alternative means of transport.

Monitoring weather conditions

To monitor real-time weather conditions, cross-border workers can use the official MeteoSwiss apps or consult the agency's website. It is important to stay updated on forecasts and any alerts issued by the authorities.

Additionally, it is advisable to subscribe to local authorities' alert services to receive real-time notifications of any emergencies or road closures. This can help better plan movements and avoid dangerous situations.
