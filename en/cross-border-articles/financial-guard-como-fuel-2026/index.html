Finance Police: fuel checks in Como (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 May 2026

Four distributors fined for gasoline price violations. Here's what changes for cross-border workers.

Context

In brief

  • Guardia di Finanza steps up fuel checks in Como
  • Four distributors fined for price violations
  • Pump prices monitored to protect consumers

Key facts

  • What: Checks on fuel distributors
  • When: 4 May 2026
  • Where: Como
  • Who: Guardia di Finanza
  • Amount: Not specified

The Provincial Command of the Guardia di Finanza in Como has intensified checks on fuel distributors in the Como area. This action follows the increases in fuel prices recorded nationally, caused by international geopolitical tensions. The aim is to ensure that the prices at the pump are not influenced by speculative practices or covert maneuvers, thus protecting the purchasing power of families and the production costs of companies.

In particular, the Guardia di Finanza has identified violations at four fuel distributors. Among the irregularities found are the discrepancy between the displayed prices and those actually applied, the failure to visibly display the price differential between 'served' and 'self', and two omissions in communicating price changes to the Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy.

Implications for cross-border workers

Cross-border workers who work in Switzerland and reside in Italy often use Italian fuel distributors to refuel their vehicles. The violations found can have a direct impact on transportation costs and the purchasing power of these workers. It is essential that prices are transparent and compliant with current regulations to avoid unjustified surcharges.

Current regulations require fuel distribution plants to communicate the prices applied at the pump through the 'Osservaprezzi carburanti' portal of the Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy, at least on a weekly basis. This monitoring is essential to ensure adequate price control and to prevent speculative practices.

Control procedures

The Guardia di Finanza has intensified checks in the Como area to intercept and counter any form of speculative phenomenon. The activities of the Corps are aimed at ensuring that the dynamics of prices are not affected by distorting practices or covert maneuvers. The checks include verification of the conformity of the displayed prices with those actually applied, as well as the correct communication of price changes to the Ministry.

Concrete actions

For cross-border workers who use fuel distributors in Como, it is important to always verify the correspondence between the displayed prices and those actually applied. In case of discrepancies, irregularities can be reported to the competent authorities, such as the Guardia di Finanza, to ensure the transparency and correctness of prices.

Furthermore, it is advisable to regularly monitor fuel prices through the 'Osservaprezzi carburanti' portal to always be informed about changes and available offers. This tool can help cross-border workers better plan their transportation expenses and avoid unjustified surcharges.
