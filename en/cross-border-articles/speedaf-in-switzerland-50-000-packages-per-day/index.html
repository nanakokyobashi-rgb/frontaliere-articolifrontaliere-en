Speedaf in Switzerland: 50,000 packages per day (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 13 September 2026

Speedaf, a Swiss subsidiary founded in April, aims to handle up to 50,000 shipments per day with centres in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne and headquarters in Freiburg.

Context

In brief

  • Speedaf lands in Switzerland to compete with Posta, Planzer, DPD and DHL.
  • The Chinese group aims to handle up to 50,000 shipments per day.
  • Swiss subsidiary founded in April and already operational, headquartered in Fribourg.
  • 60% is Posta's market share in the parcel sector in 2025.

Key facts

  • What: Entry of Chinese logistics giant Speedaf into the Swiss parcel market.
  • When: Subsidiary founded last April, operational within one or two years.
  • Where: Legal headquarters in Fribourg, distribution centres planned in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne.
  • Who: Speedaf, with the support of founding shareholders including ZTO Express.
  • Amount: Internal model estimated at 1.80 francs per parcel, with a profit threshold of 1.20 francs.

…

Operational details

How much Speedaf is aggressively calculating for entry into the Swiss market is shown by an internal planning template posted on LinkedIn by a company manager. For a selected delivery area Speedaf is assumed to receive CHF 1.80 per parcel. The profit threshold is just under 1.20 francs. There would therefore be just over 60 cents of profit left per submission. These are numbers that raise the ears of the sector: the calculated profit threshold would be very low and rather unrealistic, says a representative of the branch in statements made to AWP. In his opinion, however, the new competitor should not be underestimated. Chinese competition needs to be taken seriously, especially if you look at what is currently happening in the European automotive industry. It is also remarkable that a foreign supplier with direct access to large quantities of shipments from China builds its own Swiss network. In this way, it is not excluded that Speedaf will grow rapidly: within one or two years the company could rise among the large suppliers of the Swiss parcel market. Decisive will be to see how much financial wind the company has. Success will also depend on the quality of delivery. The Post Office, as the dominant operator, carefully observes the new competitor, but is sure of itself. The company has successfully faced intense competition in the parcel market for many years, a spokesperson said.

Key points

The evolution of the logistics market in Switzerland fits into an economic and regulatory context in constant ferment, where the entry of new non-European players forces traditional companies to review their operational and service strategies. Sector authorities, including PostCom, monitor market share trends and the impact of new flows on national competitive dynamics. The structure of operating costs and the ability to maintain high delivery standards will be the discriminating factors for assessing the viability of Speedaf's business model in the medium and long term. Future scenarios for Swiss logistics

The next steps in Speedaf's development will depend on how quickly the company can complete the infrastructure on the ground, establishing agreements with local partners and independent fleets to cover the last mile. Attention remains high also on the union and regulatory front, where the request for a sector-wide collective contract is invoked by historic market leaders to avoid competitive distortions based solely on lowering management and labor costs. Swiss users and companies thus find themselves facing a logistics landscape in rapid evolution, characterized by increased pressure on prices and a diversification of shipping channels tightly linked to the flows of global e-commerce originating from Asian markets.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is Speedaf's daily target for parcels in Switzerland?
Speedaf aims to handle up to 50,000 shipments per day, according to the internal model that estimates a revenue of 1.80 francs per package and a profit threshold of 1.20 francs per shipment, leaving about 60 cents of net profit for each package.
Where are Speedaf's regional distribution centers located in Switzerland?
The source indicates that Speedaf plans to open regional distribution centers in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne, each of about 500 square meters, while the central sorting center is expected to extend over about 4000 square meters, with a possible location in the Härkingen region in the canton of Solothurn.
What is Swiss Post's market share in the parcel sector according to 2025 data?
According to PostCom, Swiss Post held in 2025 a 60% share of the entire parcel market and even 74% in the domestic sector, a position that Speedaf intends to challenge with its aggressive entry into the Swiss market.
