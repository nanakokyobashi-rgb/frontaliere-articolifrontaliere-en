Operational details

On 1 January 2024, Italy and Switzerland ratified a new border agreement, which provides for substantial changes for border workers working in Ticino. Among the most significant innovations, a deductible of 10,000 Swiss francs, which exempts frontier workers from tax at source in Switzerland.

This means that frontier workers working in Ticino will no longer be subject to tax at source in Switzerland, provided that certain conditions are met. For example, frontier workers must have an annual income of less than CHF 100,000 and must have a fixed-term or permanent employment contract with a Swiss employer.

The transitional regime for old border workers has been extended from 2024 to 2033, so border workers who already worked in Ticino before 2024 will be able to continue to enjoy these benefits until 2033. 📊
