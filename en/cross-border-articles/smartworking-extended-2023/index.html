Context

In brief

  • The smartworking measure has been extended until 30 June 2023 for Ticino cross-border workers. - This means that cross-border commuters will be able to continue working from home until that date.

Key Facts

  • What: extended smartworking
  • When: until 30 June 2023
  • Where: Ticino
  • Who: Ticino cross-border commuters
  • Amount: Not applicable

The smartworking measure has been extended to give cross-border workers time to adapt to the new rules and to avoid health problems. According to the Federal Act of 20 June 2020, cross-border commuters are subject to specific regulations regarding remote working.

Smartworking for cross-border workers extended until 30 June 2023

To take advantage of the extended smartworking measure, cross-border commuters must continue to work from home and comply with public health regulations. Cross-border commuters can consult the website of the Federal Tax Administration for up-to-date information on the smart working measure.

According to the Federal Act of 13 April 2022 on the Protection of Cross-Border Workers, cross-border commuters can continue to work from home until 30 June 2023. This measure has been extended to help cross-border commuters manage the crisis situation caused by the COVID-19 pandemic.

To be eligible for the smartworking measure, cross-border commuters must comply with the following conditions:

  • Have an employment contract with a Swiss employer
  • Have a monthly income of at least 3,200 Swiss francs
  • Have a work-from-home workplace that meets public health regulations
  • Have a stable and fast internet connection
  • Have a computer or mobile device with compatible operating system

Cross-border commuters can consult the website of the Federal Tax Administration for up-to-date information on the smartworking measure and to download the application form. The application form must be completed and submitted by May 15, 2023.
