Key facts

  • What: Theatrical show Il ratto delle Sabine
  • When: Saturday, October 10
  • Where: San Stanislao Oratory in Fagnano Olona
  • Organizer: La Marmotta Theatre Company
  • Authors of the work: Gustav Moser and Franz Schonthan
  • Direction: Francesco Giuffrida

A large audience gathered yesterday evening, specifically on Saturday, October 10, at the San Stanislao Oratory located in Fagnano Olona to take part in a cultural and charitable event of great significance for the local community. The occasion was the celebration of the fortieth anniversary of the foundation of the La Marmotta Theatre Company, which wanted to celebrate this important historical milestone by bringing to the stage a brilliant performance that was greatly appreciated by those present. About two hundred spectators, including residents of Fagnano and people coming from other neighboring towns, packed the oratory hall to watch the comedy titled Il ratto das Sabine, a three-act play originally written by Gustav Moser and Franz Schonthan, presented to the public with artistic direction and staging entrusted to Francesco Giuffrida. The performance offered moments of genuine entertainment and amusement, confirming the artistic vitality of an amateur group that for four decades has constituted a fundamental cultural reference point for the surrounding area, capable of engaging enthusiasts of all ages through lighthearted yet carefully crafted and non-superficial texts, able to combine the pleasure of acting with social cohesion and concrete solidarity towards those in need.

Operational details

The celebratory evening organized by the La Marmotta Theatre Company was not limited solely to the festivities for the prestigious association anniversary, but transformed into a moment of profound emotional sharing and collective memory for the entire Fagnano community. The cultural event indeed represented a precious official occasion to remember the figure of Gabriele Giorgetti, a young man who tragically and prematurely passed away during the month of February at the extremely young age of twenty-four. Drawing an extremely positive balance of the kermesse was Roberta Giuffrida, who holds the position of president within the theatrical association. During her public speech, the president emphasized how the citizens' response went far beyond the most optimistic expectations on the eve, recording a massive public turnout that made the hall completely packed in every seat. The economic and supportive aspect of the evening recorded figures of absolute significance through the generosity of the attending spectators, who made it possible to realize an extraordinarily successful charitable fundraiser. The entire amount collected during the initiative amounts to over thirteen hundred euros, or rather, to be extremely precise and strictly adhere to the official data communicated by the organizers, it exceeded 1,600 euros. These financial resources were entirely destined to feed the fund named Gabriele's WYD (La GMG di Gabriele), a solidarity project born specifically to concretely support the economic expenses that numerous young people will have to face in order to take part in the upcoming edition of World Youth Day, scheduled for next summer in the capital of South Korea, namely Seoul. President Roberta Giuffrida also highlighted how it would not have been possible to imagine a better way to publicly celebrate such a significant milestone as the fortieth anniversary of activity, warmly thanking everyone who contributed to the achievement of this noble collective goal.

Key points

The artistic journey and public activities of the La Marmotta Theatre Company certainly do not stop at the Fagnano Olona event, but continue with a busy schedule of events and theatrical performances designed to bring the joy of their art to other municipalities in the Lombardy region as well. The next official stage appointment for the company has already been precisely scheduled in the autumn calendar and is set for Saturday, November 7. On that date, the actors and directors of the association will move to the stage of the Cineteatro located in the town of Binago, where a new unmissable performance of the famous comedy The Rape of the Sabines will be staged, thus offering a new audience the opportunity to appreciate the show that garnered so much acclaim and thunderous applause on the opening night in Fagnano. This new stop confirms the dynamism and constant commitment profuse by the members of the company in promoting theater culture and the values of socialization through artistic volunteering. The local editorial staff continues to closely follow the cultural events of the community, documenting initiatives that unite the territory and offer spaces for reflection and aggregation for all citizens, enhancing local associative excellences and their important charitable purposes in favor of the new generations and young people engaged in paths of growth and international sharing.
