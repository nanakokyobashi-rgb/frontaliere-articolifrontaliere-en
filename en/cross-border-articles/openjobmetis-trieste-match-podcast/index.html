The ending that decides the race

85-84. It is the result with which Openjobmetis beats Trieste at the Itelyum Arena, at the end of a challenge resolved only seven tenths from the end. The decisive free throw was scored by Amedeo Della Valle, making his debut in the red and white jersey.

The success gives Varese its second consecutive victory in the championship, after the one obtained in Scafati. The source's report, however, accompanies the result with a less positive evaluation in terms of performance: the game and management of the match are described as unconvincing.

The match is at the center of a new episode of «Dal Parquet – VareseNews' post-match», the podcast dedicated to the challenges of Openjobmetis. In the episode Damiano Franzetti assigns votes to the players; space also for coach Kastritis' statements, the reconstruction of the decisive action, the reactions of some fans and the technical analysis of coach Roberto Conti.

The source also indicates the production credits: the photography is by Mattia Martegani, while the technical support is by Thomas Massara. The next reported event is «Luci a Masnago», scheduled for Thursday 15 October live at 2.45pm on Radio Materia and subsequently also available in podcast form.

The program is present on VareseNews channels and on the main audio platforms, including Spreaker, Spotify, Amazon Music and Apple Podcasts, as well as on YouTube. For those who want to follow the evening beyond just the scoreboard, the audio story adds voices, evaluations and technical readings to the finale decided by Della Valle's free throw.
