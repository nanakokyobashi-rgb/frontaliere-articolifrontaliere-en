The timeline of the interventions

Between 6.59 and 7.36 on Friday, rescuers were involved in four interventions in as many municipalities in the province of Varese. The sequence passed through Varese, Rancio Valcuvia, Tradate and Gallarate during the first part of the morning.

The first incident was reported at 6.59 in Varese, on via Peschiera. After a vehicle struck an obstacle, one person received assistance; local police officers also arrived at the scene.

At 7.09, the intervention concerned Rancio Valcuvia, along state road 394. Here, a car and a motorcycle collided. The source reports the involvement of a 26-year-old woman, who was assisted under a green code.

At 7.19, twenty minutes later, in Tradate, on via per Appiano, the fall of a 44-year-old motorcyclist triggered the alarm. The man was assisted under a yellow code by an ambulance and a medical car. The Carabinieri of the Saronno company also intervened at the scene.

The fourth and final intervention in the report was at 7.36, in Gallarate, on viale Milano. The collision involved a 37-year-old cyclist. The cyclist received assistance under a green code; local police also intervened at the scene.

In the initial tally, the source refers to three people assessed under a green or yellow code. The incidents remain distinct and took place in four municipalities in the province during the same part of the morning.
