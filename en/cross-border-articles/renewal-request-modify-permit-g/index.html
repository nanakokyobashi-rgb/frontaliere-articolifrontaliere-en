Context

Renewal and changes to the border permit: Switzerland and the Canton of Ticino - A flexible system, but with precise rules

For border workers in Switzerland and especially in Ticino, the renewal and updating of the permit are governed by precise and flexible rules, but it is important to follow the requirements to avoid unpleasant surprises.

Permit renewal

The permit for border workers (G) in Switzerland and Ticino, called “Cross-border Work Permit G”, must be renewed 14 days before the expiry date. To renew it, you must apply at the Federal Office of Direct Taxation (UFID) in Zurich or at the Cantonal Labour Office in Locarno. The office responsible for renewing the permit depends on the border worker's city of residence. The renewal request must be accompanied by a receipt of the payment of the annual contribution. If the permit expires after May 31, the annual contribution is required for renewal. If the permit expires between June 1 and December 31, the annual contribution is not mandatory but is strongly recommended to ensure the effective validity of the permit.

Operational details

Renewal and changes to the permit for border workers: Republic and Canton of Ticino

In Ticino, foreign workers have the possibility to work in Switzerland with a cross-border permit, which allows them to move freely between the Swiss territory and the Ticino territory. However, more than six months after the end of the activity or the expiry of the permit, these workers must submit a request for renewal of the permit.

If this request is not submitted within the established time frame, the worker could incur a pecuniary sanction, as reported in the Ticino Criminal Code (Article 128, paragraph 1). To illustrate this aspect, a concrete example concerns a border worker who worked in Switzerland for six months, but did not submit the renewal of the permit within the deadline. In the event that this person is sanctioned, the fine could vary between 150 and 500 francs, as reported in circular 2019/34 of the Federal Department of Transport and Communications (SECO).

With regard to the relevant regulations, it is important to bear in mind that the Ticino cross-border permit does not have a predefined duration and can be renewed depending on different circumstances. For example, the permit may be renewed following the conclusion of a work activity, the conclusion of a seasonal work period or the validation of the qualification.
