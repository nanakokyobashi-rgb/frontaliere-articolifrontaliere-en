Operational details

Practical analysis Participation in the Summer Fancy Food Show in New York, scheduled from 23 to 25 June 2024, represents a significant opportunity for border workers working in the agri-food sector in Ticino. The internationalisation of the Squirrel company, with a turnover of 50 million euros in 2023, may open up new prospects for cross-border work and collaboration, especially for those who reside in Lombardy and work in Switzerland, where border workers represent 28.5% of the workforce. ### Opportunities for frontier workers Export represents about 70% of Squirrel's total turnover, supporting the development of the brand both through the Squirrel brand and through private label projects aimed at large international distribution operators. This can translate into new job opportunities for border workers, with the possibility of being involved in international projects and benefiting from greater job stability. For example, in 2023, Scoiattolo hired 30 new employees, including 15 border workers from the municipalities of Como, Varese and Lecco. Border workers working in Ticino can benefit from a Swiss minimum wage of CHF 3,850 per month, compared to the Italian average of €1,500, with a positive differential of around €2,000 per month.
