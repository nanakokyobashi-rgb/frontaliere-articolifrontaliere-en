Operational details

Who are the frontier workers in Ticino? The answer is here.

The New Frontier Agreement has changed the rules for withholding the source on income from work. Border crossers in Ticino are subject to a withholding tax of 5.3% for AVS, 1.1% for AD and 0.7-1.5% for LAINF.

But who are the frontier workers in Ticino? Border workers are workers who carry out their work in another region of the country and who do not reside in the municipality where they work. In Ticino, the largest municipalities such as Bellinzona, Lugano and Mendrisio are those where border crossers are most concentrated.

What does it mean to be a frontier worker in Ticino?

Being a frontier worker in Ticino means being subject to a series of tax rules and obligations. Border workers must submit a tax return by March 31 of each year, where they must indicate the income received and the deductions made.

If you are a frontier worker in Ticino, you need to know that your pay is subject to a withholding tax. The withholding tax is 5.3% for AVS (Old Age and Disability Insurance Switzerland), 1.1% for AD (Unemployment Insurance) and 0.7-1.5% for LAINF (Old Age and Disability Insurance Act).

For example, if you earn CHF 5,000 per month in Switzerland, the withholding tax for AVS will be CHF 265 (5,300% of CHF 5,000). In addition, you will have to pay 55 CHF for the AD (1,100% of 5,000 CHF) and 37-90 CHF for the LAINF (0.7-1.5% of 5,000 CHF).

The Federal Law on Withholding Tax came into force on 1 January 2019 (Federal Law of 24 June 2017, effective from 1 January 2019). This law establishes that the withholding of the source must be carried out directly from the source of income, or by the employer.

It is important to note that withholding tax is not the only tax you have to pay as a border crossing in Ticino. In addition, you will have to pay income taxes in Switzerland and Italy.
