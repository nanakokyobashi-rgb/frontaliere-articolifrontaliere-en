Ticino: Are Cross-Border Teachers Working Without a Valid Work Permit? The Government Responds

Recently, the State Council of the Canton of Ticino provided clarifications regarding a parliamentary inquiry raised by Deputy Lorenzo Quadri, who questioned the employment of cross-border teachers without a valid work permit. The Executive's response clarified that the current practices in place do not present substantial irregularities. Over the past five years, only one case of violation was identified, which was promptly sanctioned by the competent authorities.

According to the statement, cross-border teachers from European Union (EU) or European Free Trade Association (EFTA) countries can begin their employment immediately after submitting their work permit application, without needing to wait for formal issuance. This procedure complies with Article 7 of Annex I of the Agreement on the Free Movement of Persons, in effect since June 2002. This regulation ensures equal treatment for EU/EFTA citizens compared to local workers, facilitating easier access to the Swiss labor market.

📊 Data and On-Site Checks During inspections conducted in Ticino schools, 53 municipalities confirmed compliance with hiring procedures for cross-border teachers. For instance, in the municipality of Mendrisio, which employs a significant number of cross-border teachers, authorities stated that all teachers submitted their work permit applications before starting their duties. A similar situation was observed in Chiasso and Lugano, where checks confirmed no irregularities.

Based on the data provided, cross-border teachers account for approximately 25% of the total teaching staff in Ticino schools. In 2022, for example, out of about 3,200 total teachers, slightly more than 800 were from Italy. Most of them reside in the provinces of Como and Varese and commute daily across the border to work.

💡 Operational Checklist for Cross-Border Teachers 1. Submit the application for a G permit before starting employment. 2. Provide a copy of a valid and official employment contract. 3. Ensure compliance with Swiss health insurance requirements. 4. Adhere to the residence limit within 90 km of the border.

⚠️ Priority for Residents The State Council reiterated that the current legislation is already strict enough in giving priority to Swiss residents in the labor market, as stipulated by Article 21 of the Federal Act on Foreign Nationals and Integration (FNAI). However, the presence of cross-border workers is deemed necessary to fill the demand for qualified personnel, especially in fields like education, where the demand often exceeds the supply of local candidates.

In summary, the Ticino Government reassured that the control system is functioning correctly and that any irregular cases are promptly addressed. The goal remains to ensure a balance between access to work for cross-border workers and the protection of the local labor market.

Operational details

The procedure for obtaining a work permit for cross-border workers in the education sector in Ticino is well-defined and subject to strict regulations. According to the Federal Act on Foreign Nationals and Integration (FNAI), permit applications must be submitted no later than three months before the start of employment. Failure to meet this deadline triggers a contravention procedure, which may result in financial penalties. In the specific case of the Canton of Ticino, the Migration Office is the authority responsible for monitoring and enforcing these rules.

A concrete example of irregularity was recorded during the 2025/26 school year when a cross-border teacher hired at a middle school in the Mendrisiotto submitted their permit application more than a month late. Following this infraction, the teacher was fined 500 Swiss francs. According to the Migration Office, this was the only case of non-compliance with the procedure during that school year. In Lugano, Locarno, and Bellinzona, no similar irregularities were reported during the same period.

School administrators, both at the cantonal and municipal levels, are responsible for ensuring that cross-border teachers have a valid work permit before signing their contracts. To facilitate compliance with procedures, the Ticino Government has issued an operational checklist, which includes the following key points:

  • Submit the permit application within 90 days of starting work.
  • Attach a copy of the signed employment contract.
  • Provide proof of stable residence in a cross-border municipality (e.g., Como or Varese).

The current regulations, updated in January 2023, stipulate that without a valid permit, the contract cannot take effect. However, the Government has stated that the current system functions correctly and does not require modifications. Data confirms a high level of compliance, with a conformity rate exceeding 99% for the year under review.

Key points

For cross-border workers aspiring to work as teachers in Ticino, it is essential to submit the work permit application promptly, adhering to all stipulated deadlines. The Canton of Ticino, which hosts over 70,000 cross-border workers, requires careful management of bureaucratic procedures to ensure compliance with current regulations. For example, teachers wishing to work in municipalities such as Lugano, Mendrisio, or Bellinzona must submit their work permit application (G permit for cross-border workers) no later than 8 days after starting their activity. This deadline is regulated by Article 11 of the Agreement on the Free Movement of Persons (AFMP) between Switzerland and the European Union.

⚠️ Possibility of Working Before Permit Issuance: In Ticino, cross-border workers can begin working before the formal issuance of the permit, provided the application has been duly submitted and the activity falls within regulated sectors. However, failure to submit the application within the stipulated timeframe may result in administrative penalties, such as fines of up to 5,000 CHF.

💡 Operational Checklist for Cross-Border Teachers: 1. Obtain a signed employment contract from a Ticino-based employer. 2. Submit the G permit application to the Migration Office of the Canton of Ticino (online or in paper format). 3. Verify that the employer has submitted any required notifications to the municipality of professional residence (e.g., Lugano or Chiasso). 4. Understand taxation: cross-border workers are subject to source taxation with progressive rates ranging from 0% to over 15%, depending on income.

📊 Practical Scenario Comparison: A teacher residing in Como with a gross monthly income of 4,500 CHF will be taxed in Switzerland at an approximate rate of 5%, whereas a teacher residing in Varese may fall into a higher tax bracket due to potential differences in applicable personal deductions.

Frequently Asked Questions
How does the procedure for hiring frontier teachers in Ticino work?
Frontier teachers can start working immediately after submitting the application for work permit, without having to wait for formal release. This procedure is in accordance with Article 7 of Annex I to the Agreement on the free movement of persons, in force since June 2002.
What is the rate of compliance with the rules for frontier teachers in Ticino?
The compliance rate is higher than 99% as indicated by the data of the year analysed.
What are the health insurance requirements for frontier teachers in Ticino?
Frontier teachers in Ticino must ensure health insurance in accordance with Swiss requirements. This means having a cover that respects Swiss regulations, thus ensuring proper medical care during your working stay in Ticino. Insurance is a fundamental requirement for obtaining a work permit.
How many Ticino schools have confirmed compliance with recruitment procedures for frontier teachers?
In Canton Ticino, 53 municipalities confirmed compliance with recruitment procedures for frontier teachers. This includes schools in municipalities such as Mendrisio, Chiasso and Lugano, where controls have evidenced the absence of irregularities in recruitment practices.
What is the role of school leaders in the process of recruiting frontier teachers in Ticino?
School managers, both of the cantonal and communal schools, have the task of checking in advance that frontier teachers have a valid work permit before signing the employment contract. This control is essential to ensure that all regulations are respected, and to prevent any irregularities in the recruitment process.
