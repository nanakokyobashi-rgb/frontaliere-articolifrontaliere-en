Border crossers in Ticino: flows, mobility and areas of origin

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 15 July 2026

One out of two border crossers arrives from Como. Analysis of the flows, destinations and use of the car based on the survey of the Department of the territory.

Context

In a nutshell

  • 65% of those who enter Ticino are border workers and students.
  • The province of Como covers 46% of the entrances, that of Varese 41%.
  • 50% of commuters say they have no alternative to the car.
  • Mendrisiotto and Lugano are the main destinations.

Key facts

  • What: Investigation of the flows of border crossers and students at the 21 Ticino crossings.
  • Who: Department of the Territory (DT).
  • Como origin: 46% of the total.
  • Varese origin: 41% of the total.
  • Destination Mendrisiotto: 47% of the total.
  • Destination Lugano: 41% of the total.
  • Car use: 50% with no alternatives.

The analysis, conducted by the Department of the Territory (DT) at the 21 most important crossings of the Canton of Ticino, photographs a reality in which 65% of the people who cross the border are border crossers and students. The numbers, made known through widespread monitoring, confirm the central role of the Province of Como and that of Varese in fuelling the cross-border labour market. Specifically, the Como origin accounts for 46%, while the Varese origin accounts for 41%. Once past customs, the distribution of workers is predominantly focused on two specific geographical areas. Operational details

Data analysis raises practical issues relevant to those who experience commuting on a daily basis. The availability of free parking at the destination, which affects 67% of commuters, undoubtedly acts as a disincentive to the use of public transport, making the car the most rational choice for the individual worker. However, this scenario clashes with the reality of traffic at peak times, where the concentration of flows towards Mendrisiotto and Lugano saturates the main arteries. Modal differences between the routes.

The study also highlights divergent behaviours based on the railway section used. While on the Como-Chiasso axis there is a decrease in the number of regular users, partially offset by an increase in occasional users, the situation changes radically on the Varese-Stabio line. Thelatter shows a high regularity: over 80% of travellers choose the train with a multi-day frequency. This discrepancy indicates how the effectiveness of rail connections, when they guarantee certain times and frequency, can actually compete with the car. Frequently Asked Questions
What percentage of commuters enter Ticino from Como?
According to the survey of the Department of the territory, the Province of Como represents 46% of the origin of border workers and students entering Canton Ticino.
What are the main destinations for frontier workers?
The main destinations for those crossing the border are Mendrisiotto, which welcomes 47% of commuters, and Lugano, which absorbs 41%.
Why do most workers use the car?
50% of workers declare that they have no valid alternatives to private transport, while 23% prefer the car for reasons of comfort. In addition, 67% of commuters have free parking at their work destination.
