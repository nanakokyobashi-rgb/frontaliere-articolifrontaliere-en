Mendrisiotto companies compared on work-life balance

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 8 October 2026

## TL;DR - Meeting in Chiasso on October 1 last year - Around thirty businesses involved - Cantonal survey open until November 30 ## Facts

Context

TL;DR

  • Meeting in Chiasso last October 1
  • Around thirty companies involved
  • Cantonal survey open until November 30

Key facts

  • Organizer: Pro Familia Svizzera italiana
  • Location: Chiasso
  • Participants: around thirty companies and entrepreneurial ventures
  • Tools: Best Practices platform and Family Score
  • Survey deadline: November 30

Last October 1, in Chiasso, companies from Mendrisiotto discussed work-life balance in an initiative promoted by Pro Familia Svizzera italiana. The City of Mendrisio, the Municipality of Chiasso and the Regional Development Agency for Mendrisiotto and Basso Ceresio also collaborated in organizing it.

At the Networking Café People, business, future, around thirty companies and entrepreneurial ventures, differing in sector and size, met. The discussion initiated a shared reflection on needs and tools, in order to seek solutions tailored to each company’s specific characteristics.

“Promoting work-life balance does not necessarily require costly measures: solutions suited to one’s own context need to be found.”

During the discussion, it was clarified that action may concern work organization, planning, processes or internal communication. According to the message that emerged, seeking balance requires organizational skills, flexibility and collaboration from both sides.

The tools presented to businesses

During the meeting, concrete and easily accessible tools were presented, including the Best Practices platform and Family Score. The source places them within activities supporting and assisting employers. These activities are supported through a cantonal fund. The discussion thus paired the general reflection with references already incorporated into support for businesses.

During the Networking Café, the cantonal survey on work-life balance officially got underway. It was presented by Rachele Santoro, equal opportunities delegate for Canton Ticino. The fact-finding survey, jointly promoted by Pro Familia Svizzera italiana and Canton Ticino, is intended for companies in any sector and of any size, through their employers.

Those who participate are asked to share practices, experiences and needs. According to the information provided, the voice of businesses will help build a picture of the cantonal reality and will serve as a concrete reference for future initiatives and support measures for businesses. Registrations remain open until November 30.

Operational details

The most concrete part of the discussion concerns how to view work-life balance: not as a standard measure, but as a search for solutions compatible with the company's reality. The message that emerged indicates that fostering it does not necessarily require major investments. One lever may lie in work organization, planning, processes or internal communication.

Taken together, these four items lead to an operational conclusion: the source does not propose a package that is the same for all companies. Rather, it asks them to start from their own circumstances and check where there is room for a better balance between professional and personal needs. This step concerns employers, but affects people's daily lives, because the two dimensions coexist.

The message also emphasizes collaboration from both sides. It does not specify working hours, organizational arrangements or benefits that apply equally to all companies. Best Practices and Family Score are also presented as support and assistance tools, not as automatic measures that can be applied without adaptation.

For a cross-border worker working in the Mendrisiotto, the connection remains limited. The text speaks of companies, employers and reconciling work and personal life, but does not distinguish between resident workers and cross-border workers, does not indicate how many are involved and does not describe measures intended for those who cross the border. The relevant element is therefore the general topic of company organization; no specific procedure related to cross-border work emerges.

On the website, the reading can be supplemented by the pages dedicated to congedo parentale and asilo nido, without attributing these measures to the meeting in Chiasso.

Key points

The survey offers a concrete point of access for businesses that want to bring their experience into the cantonal framework. To turn the available information into a practical path, the steps indicated by the news item concern above all employers.

Four steps to participate

1. Check your profile. The survey is intended for employers in any sector and of any size. It is therefore not limited to the businesses present at the Networking Café. 2. Focus the contribution. The survey asks participants to share practices, experiences and needs related to reconciling work and private life. These are the elements to contribute to the survey. 3. Respect the deadline. Participation remains open until November 30. The date is the operational reference indicated for taking part in the cantonal survey. 4. Use the available tools. The Best Practices platform and the Family Score are part of the support and guidance provided to employers. They can therefore be considered alongside the reflection initiated by the survey, without presenting them as obligations.

After the responses have been collected, the stated aim is to build a snapshot of the cantonal reality. The voice of businesses should also become a concrete reference for directing future initiatives and support measures. For this reason, the contribution is not described merely as an individual opinion, but as part of a fact-finding survey promoted by Pro Familia Svizzera italiana with the Canton of Ticino.

For the cross-border commuter reader, the source does not indicate a separate question or an individual path. The documented focus remains the comparison between professional and personal needs and cooperation among the parties. Any practical translation therefore comes through the company’s reality and its choices, without adding measures that the text does not describe.

When and where did the meeting about companies in the Mendrisiotto take place?
The meeting was held in Chiasso on 1 October last. It was promoted by Pro Familia Svizzera italiana in collaboration with the Ente regionale per lo sviluppo del Mendrisiotto e Basso Ceresio, the City of Mendrisio and the Municipality of Chiasso.
Which tools were presented to businesses during the Networking Café?
The Best Practices platform and the Family Score were presented, described as tools to support and assist employers, included among the activities supported by a cantonal fund.
Until when is it possible to participate in the cantonal survey on work-life balance?
Participation in the survey is open until November 30. The survey, promoted by Pro Familia Svizzera italiana together with the Canton of Ticino, asks employers to share practices, experiences and needs related to work-life balance.
