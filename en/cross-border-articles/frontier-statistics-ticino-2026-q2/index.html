The 2026-Q2 quarter was characterised by an increase in the number of border crossers in Ticino. According to data published by the Federal Statistical Office (BFS), the number of border workers increased by 505 units compared to the previous quarter (2026-Q1), for a total of 79,121 units.

This increase represents a growth rate of 0.64% compared to the previous quarter. Although the growth rate may seem modest, it is important to note that it is a net increase of 505 units, representing 0.64% of the total number of border crossers in Ticino.

In terms of context, it is interesting to note that the increase in the number of border workers in Ticino can be linked to the economic and social policies of the Canton. The growth of the frontier population can be an indicator of economic growth and labour demand in the sector.
