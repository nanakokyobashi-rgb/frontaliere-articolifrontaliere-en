Albanian arrested with 54g of cocaine in Capolago (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 May 2026

A 24-year-old Albanian was arrested with 54 grams of cocaine in a car on the A2 in Capolago. Here are the details of the operation.

Context

In brief

  • Albanian man, 24, arrested with 54 grams of cocaine
  • Operation coordinated by Cantonal Police
  • Stopped on A2 in Capolago territory

Key facts

  • What: Arrested for cocaine possession
  • When: April 29, 2024
  • Where: Capolago (Segoma area), on the A2
  • Who: Cantonal Police, Lugano City Police, Mendrisio City Police, Chiasso Municipal Police
  • Amount: 54 grams of cocaine

A 24-year-old Albanian man, residing in Albania, was arrested as part of an anti-drug investigation. This was announced by the Public Prosecutor's Office and the Cantonal Police. The young man was stopped on April 29 in the territory of Capolago (Segoma area) while he was in a car heading north on the A2. The search of the vehicle by collaborators of the Federal Customs Administration and Border Security (UDSC) allowed the discovery of approximately 54 grams of cocaine.

The operation, coordinated by the Cantonal Police, was supported by the anti-drug services of the Lugano City Police, the Mendrisio City Police, and the Chiasso Municipal Police. The alleged offenses are aggravated violation of the Federal Narcotics Act and violation of the Federal Aliens Act.
