Key points

To obtain the G permit, border workers must submit an application to the Immigration Service of the Canton of Ticino. The application must be submitted within 30 days of arrival in Ticino. The G permit is valid for five years with a contract of more than one year; with a shorter contract it lasts as long as the contract.

Permit Requirements G

To obtain the G permit, cross-border workers must meet the following requirements:

  • Be a citizen of a non-EU country
  • Have an employment contract with a company in the Canton of Ticino
  • Have a minimum income of CHF 2,500 per month (Article 5 (1) of the Regulation on Foreigners of the Canton of Ticino of 1 January 2020)
  • Have medical and hospital insurance coverage
  • Have no criminal or administrative record
