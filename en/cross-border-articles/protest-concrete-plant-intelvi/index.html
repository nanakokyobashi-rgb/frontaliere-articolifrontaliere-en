Protest in Castiglione d'Intelvi over concrete batching plant (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 26 September 2026

Over 750 signatures collected in six days against the concrete batching plant and crusher planned in Castiglione d'Intelvi for the Tremezzina Bypass.

More than 750 signatures in six days: the mobilization against the proposal for a concrete batching plant in Castiglione d’Intelvi has reached a significant initial threshold. The project also includes a crusher intended to process stones from the excavations. The area identified is populated and not far from schools, nursing homes and places of cultural interest, including the Oratory of the Madonna del Restello.

The Intelvi Valley has thus returned to the center of news coverage because of operations linked to the construction site of the Tremezzina Bypass. After the Cava Citrini affair, which ended positively for residents, attention has shifted to a second plant proposal. A longtime source for the newspaper, whose identifying details are known but who is not identified, describes a protest that has now erupted.

«An uproar is breaking out in the Intelvi Valley, people have finally realized what a concrete batching plant is! Especially because of the six-year duration specified in the project, noise and environmental pollution, and traffic that will bring the valley to its knees».

The same source recalls that residents had managed to get the first proposed location at Cava Citrini abandoned. After years without any action, it adds, an area at the entrance to Castiglione would now be in preparation. This reference links the new mobilization to the previous protest, shifting attention to the second proposal.

From concrete production to the crusher

The concrete batching plant is described as an industrial system for storing, measuring out and precisely mixing the ingredients of fresh concrete. Alongside it, the crusher would process the excavated stones. For the proponents, the duration specified in the project, six years, makes the choice of location decisive.

The protest and its scope

The document circulated by residents specifies that the mobilization is not against the Tremezzina Bypass. The opposition concerns the plant’s location and the anticipated consequences for health, the environment, road traffic, safety and the valley’s tourism and residential character. The concerns include dust and noise, water consumption, possible effects on soil and groundwater, as well as the passage of heavy vehicles carrying stones, concrete and water from the lake.

Key points

How to follow the mobilization

The operational guidelines contained in the document focus on information, participation and institutional dialogue. The channel indicated by citizens is the intelviradar.it portal, activated to ensure transparency, regular updates and access to information about the project and the initiatives.

From the portal to the assembly

1. Consult intelviradar.it for updates on the project and the mobilization. 2. Learn more on the portal about the reasons for the protest and the issues contested by citizens. 3. Look for information on how to sign the petition promoted by the community. 4. Follow the request for a public assembly with Mario Pozzi, identified as the Mayor of the municipality most affected, and with all representatives of the institutions involved.

The only timeline reported in the source concerns the collection of signatures: more than 750 signatures were reached in six days. This figure measures the speed of the mobilization, but is not presented as a deadline for signing. The document does not indicate a date for the assembly, a deadline for the petition or a work schedule.

To avoid misunderstandings, the organizers’ request should be kept distinct from the main project. The text specifies that the protest does not concern the Variante della Tremezzina, but rather the location of the facility and its possible consequences. Those consulting the portal can therefore focus on the concrete batching plant, the related crusher, the road network and the requests addressed to the administrations.

Citizens are asking institutions and the competent administrations, starting with the Municipalities of the Valley, for greater transparency toward the population and a review of the project. The request is justified by the protection of the environment, public health and the future of Val d’Intelvi. The source records this request for dialogue, without reporting the outcome of an assembly or a review.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the reasons for the protest in Castiglione d 'Intelvi?
The mobilization concerns the location of the concrete mixing plant and the crusher, not the Tremezzina Variation itself. Citizens fear for public health, the environment, the viability and the tourist vocation of the valley. In particular, risks related to dust, noise, water consumption, possible effects on soil and groundwater, as well as the passage of heavy vehicles on fragile and winding mountain roads are indicated.
What is the concrete mixing plant and how long will it be active?
The cementing plant is described as an industrial system for storing, dosing and mixing the ingredients of fresh concrete. Along with it, there is a crusher for processing the stones from the excavations. According to the project, the expected duration of the plant's activity is six years.
How can citizens follow the protest initiative?
Citizens can consult the intelviradar.it portal for constant updates, learn more about the reasons for the protest and sign the petition. A public meeting was also requested with Mario Pozzi, Mayor of the municipality most impacted, and with all the representatives of the institutions involved to ensure greater transparency and a review of the project.
