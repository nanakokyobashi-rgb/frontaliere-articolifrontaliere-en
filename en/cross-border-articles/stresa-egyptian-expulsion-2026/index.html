Key facts

An Egyptian citizen was expelled from Stresa, in the province of Verbano-Cusio-Ossola, after Italian authorities rejected his request for political asylum. The man, identified as an illegal immigrant, attempted to flee but was stopped and subsequently expelled. This case raises important issues for cross-border workers who commute to Switzerland and reside in Italy, particularly regarding asylum rights and expulsion procedures.

The incident is part of a broader context of intensified checks along the Italian-Swiss border, involving both cross-border workers and residents. Italian authorities have increased document checks and expulsion operations for those not in compliance with residence permits. This can have significant repercussions for transborder workers, who must ensure they have all their documents in order to avoid similar problems.

Implications for cross-border workers

For cross-border workers who commute to Switzerland and reside in Italy, it is crucial to always be in compliance with residence and work documents. Italian authorities are increasing checks, and those without the necessary documents risk expulsion. It is important to periodically verify the validity of your residence permit and ensure you have all the required documents to avoid unpleasant situations.
