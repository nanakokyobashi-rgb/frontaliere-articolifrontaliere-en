Key points

Concrete actions for cross-border workers

In case of an assault or accident on a train, cross-border workers should follow these procedures:

1. Move away from the dangerous area: If possible, move to a safe area and away from the aggressor. For example, if the incident occurs on a train traveling between Chiasso and Lugano, passengers can move to nearby carriages or towards the front or rear of the train, where there are usually fewer people and more space to move.

2. Call the authorities: Use the phone to contact the police or local law enforcement. In Switzerland, the emergency number is 117 for the police and 112 for general emergency services. It is important to provide the train number and exact location, for example, 'Train IC 567, carriage 4, direction Lugano'.

3. Provide precise information: Describe the aggressor and the situation with clear and concise details. For example, indicate height, clothing color, any tattoos or distinctive marks. If possible, also provide information about the victim and the context of the assault.

4. Stay calm: Keep calm and follow the instructions of the authorities. Calm is fundamental to managing the situation and to avoid making it worse. It is also important to encourage other passengers to stay calm and not to intervene directly.

5. Document the incident: If possible, take photos or videos of the aggressor and the scene, without putting your own safety at risk. These documents can be useful for police investigations. However, it is important not to put your own safety at risk to obtain this evidence.
