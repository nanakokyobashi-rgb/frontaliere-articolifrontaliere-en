Three concerts for the 30th anniversary of the musical village

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 9 October 2026

Three concerts scheduled for 11, 17 and 18 October The appointments are at Galliate Lombardo, Porto Ceresio and Clivio The Foundation will double every euro donated

The opening in Galliate Lombardo

On Sunday 11 October at 6pm the Council Hall of Galliate Lombardo will host the first appointment of the solidarity initiative linked to the «30 years of musical village» project. The concert opens the calendar of events announced for October.

The month is also referred to as Philanthropy Month. The support comes from the Varesotto Community Foundation, which will double every euro donated to the project. The project was proposed by the Borgo musica and selected by the Varesotto Community Foundation.

On stage are the maestros Tommaso Angelini, on the violin, and Andrea Ferrario, on the classical guitar. With them there will be their respective students, called to be the protagonists of a chamber concert. The formula therefore focuses on the musical work of the two Maestros and the students participating in the Galliate Lombardo event.

The fundraising accompanies the October calendar and the preparation of the celebrations for the 30th anniversary of the foundation of the musical village. The Foundation will double every euro donated to the project, according to the support mechanism indicated in the announcement.

Operational details

The second appointment is scheduled for October 17th at 5.30pm at the «Salvatore Ferrara» Palace of Culture in Porto Ceresio. The venue will host a concert by the Cello Ensemble, with the extraordinary participation of Francesca Bongiorni.

The date is included in the exhibition «The world of dolls», curated by Imeria and Stefano Ferrara. The presentation therefore links the musical performance to the exhibition and precisely indicates the place where the concert will take place: the Palazzo della Cultura named after Salvatore Ferrara.

The training chosen for Porto Ceresio is different from that indicated for the other events of the project. In fact, the program entrusts this stage to a cello ensemble and announces, alongside the musicians, the extraordinary participation of Francesca Bongiorni. These are the two artistic elements highlighted in the event sheet.

The placement in «The world of dolls» is also an integral part of the announcement. The exhibition is curated by Imeria and Stefano Ferrara, while the concert is the musical event scheduled in the same venue. Porto Ceresio thus becomes the second location included in the October calendar of the Musical Village, with an afternoon date and a program dedicated to cellos.

Key points

"Classiche Corde" is scheduled for October 18th at 11am, the concert for Momenti Musicali scheduled in Clivio, at the Auditorium in via Trentini 31. The announced lineup brings together Francesca Cavallo on the harp and Andrea Ferrario on the guitar.

The musical program includes music by Bach, with Johann Sebastian and Carl Philip Emmanuel indicated in the presentation, as well as Castelnuovo-Tedesco, Sor, Hindemith and Caramiello. The list of composers accompanies the Clivio date and defines the repertoire announced for the appointment.

Furthermore, the funds raised will be used to organize a concert in an important hall in Varese designed to celebrate the thirtieth anniversary of the foundation of the musical village.

In that room the Canova Chamber Orchestra will play directed by Maestro Enrico Pagano, together with some of the Maestros of the musical village as soloists. The concert planned in Varese represents the result that the collection hopes to make possible, while «Classiche Corde» is the event in which the venue, time, performers and music are already indicated.

The text therefore connects Clivio's date to the prospect of the celebrations for the anniversary of the musical village. The selection of songs and the presence of Cavallo and Ferrario constitute the program of the third appointment.

Frequently Asked Questions
When is the first concert of the project held?
The first appointment is Sunday, October 11 at 6 p.m., in the Council Chamber of Galliate Lombardo. The concert involves Maestros Tommaso Angelini, on the violin, and Andrea Ferrario, on classical guitar, together with their students. La fonte presents it as a chamber concert and as the first appointment of the solidarity initiative linked to the "30 years of Borgo musicale" project.
Where does the concert on October 17 take place?
On 17 October at 5.30 pm the program moves to the Palazzo della Cultura "Salvatore Ferrara" in Porto Ceresio. A concert by the Cello Ensemble is scheduled with the extraordinary participation of Francesca Bongiorni. The appointment is held within the exhibition "The world of dolls", curated by Imeria and Stefano Ferrara.
What is fundraising for?
The Community Foundation of Varese has selected the project "30 years of Musical Village" and will double every euro donated. The collection is linked to the hope of realizing, in an important hall in Varese, a concert by the Canova Chamber Orchestra conducted by Maestro Enrico Pagano, with some Maestros of the musical village as soloists, for the celebrations of the 30th anniversary of its foundation.
