The Lohnausweis is a fundamental document for cross-border workers employed in Switzerland and residing in Italy. This certificate, issued by Swiss authorities, contains detailed information about the salary, tax deductions, and pension contributions. For Ticino cross-border workers, the Lohnausweis is mandatory and must be presented annually for the Italian tax return. The document includes data such as gross salary, tax withholdings, AVS and LPP contributions, and other relevant information for the tax declaration.

What the Lohnausweis contains

The Lohnausweis is a comprehensive document that provides a detailed overview of a cross-border worker's income and tax deductions. The information contained in the certificate includes:

  • Annual gross salary
  • Withholding taxes at source
  • AVS (Old-Age and Survivors' Insurance) contributions
  • LPP (Professional Pension) contributions
  • Deductions for health insurance (LAMal)
  • Other mandatory deductions and contributions

This information is essential for correctly completing the Italian tax return and for avoiding errors that could lead to penalties or paying more taxes than due.

How to obtain the Lohnausweis

To obtain the Lohnausweis, Ticino cross-border workers must follow a specific procedure. Generally, the Swiss employer is responsible for issuing the certificate, which must be delivered to the worker by the end of the fiscal year. However, it is important to verify with your employer the deadlines and methods of delivering the document.

Operational details

Practical Implications of the Lohnausweis

The Lohnausweis has several practical implications for cross-border workers in Ticino. Firstly, it provides official proof of income earned in Switzerland, which can be used to apply for social benefits, mortgages, or other advantages. Additionally, the certificate is essential for tax declarations in Italy, as it contains all the necessary information to correctly fill out the tax form.

Comparisons with the Previous Situation

Before the introduction of the Lohnausweis, cross-border workers in Ticino had to rely on less detailed and often incomplete documents for their tax declarations. This could lead to errors and an increased risk of tax penalties. With the introduction of the Lohnausweis, the process has become more transparent and secure, reducing the risk of errors and simplifying the tax declaration process.

Practical Scenarios

Let's imagine that a cross-border worker in Ticino receives the Lohnausweis and notices that the withholding taxes are lower than expected. In this case, it is important to contact the employer to verify the correctness of the document and, if necessary, request a correction. Additionally, it is advisable to consult an accountant to understand if it is possible to recover the unpaid taxes or if there are other tax implications to consider.

Another common scenario is when a cross-border worker receives the Lohnausweis and realizes that some relevant information is missing, such as LPP contributions. In this case, it is important to contact the employer to obtain a corrected version of the document. Additionally, it is advisable to keep a copy of the Lohnausweis along with other tax documents for future checks.

Key points

Concrete actions for cross-border workers

For Ticino cross-border workers, it is important to follow some concrete actions to correctly manage the Lohnausweis. First of all, it is essential to keep the certificate in a safe and easily accessible place. Additionally, it is advisable to make a copy of the document and keep it separately, in case the original is lost or damaged.

Verification of information

Once the Lohnausweis is received, it is important to carefully verify all the information contained in the document. In particular, it is necessary to check that the gross salary, tax withholdings, and pension contributions are correct. If discrepancies are noticed, it is important to immediately contact the employer to request a correction.

Presentation to the accountant

The Lohnausweis must be presented to your accountant or CAF for the preparation of the tax return. It is important to verify with your accountant the deadlines and methods of presenting the document, in order to avoid delays or errors. Furthermore, it is advisable to provide the accountant with all the necessary information for a correct preparation of the declaration, such as any deductible expenses or additional contributions.

Keeping documents

It is important to keep the Lohnausweis together with other tax documents for any future checks. In particular, it is advisable to keep the certificate for at least five years, which is the prescription period for income declarations in Italy. Furthermore, it is useful to keep a copy of the Lohnausweis in digital format, so that you always have access to the document even in case of loss of the original.
