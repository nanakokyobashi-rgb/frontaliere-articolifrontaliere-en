Living in Lasnigo and working in Ticino as a border worker

Cross-border work is a reality that is increasingly present in Ticino, with many Italian citizens working in Switzerland and maintaining their residence in Italy. In Lasnigo, a municipality in the province of Como, there are many examples of people who have chosen to live and work in Ticino.

Tax at source in Switzerland for frontier workers

In Switzerland, the company paying a border employee is only required to withhold the tax at source in Switzerland. This means that the Italian tax is not withheld in Switzerland, but will be paid in Italy, at the time of filing the tax return.

New Frontier Agreement

On 23 December 2020, the New Frontier Agreement was signed, which will enter into force on 1 January 2024. This agreement aims to simplify the lives of border crossers and reduce tax complexities. In particular, the agreement provides for the creation of a single tax return for border workers, who will only have to file a tax return in Italy.

Who is a frontier worker in Ticino? 📊 A border worker is a citizen of an EU country who lives in Switzerland and works in Switzerland. In this case, a border worker who lives in Lasnigo (municipality of Italian Switzerland) and works in Ticino (Swiss canton) can enjoy some tax breaks.

Taxes and fees in Switzerland and Italy Tax at source in Switzerland for frontier workers is only withheld in Switzerland. This means that the frontier worker will not have to pay taxes in Italy on their work income. However, if the frontier earns income also in Italy, it may have to pay taxes also in Italy, depending on the applicable tax convention.

The Italian-Swiss Double Taxation Convention The Italian-Swiss Double Taxation Convention was signed on 9 DECEMBER 1976. This convention provides that income earned in Switzerland by an Italian citizen is exempt from tax in Italy, provided that the income has already been taxed in Switzerland.
