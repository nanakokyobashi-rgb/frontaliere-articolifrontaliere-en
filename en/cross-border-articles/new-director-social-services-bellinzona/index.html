The city of Bellinzona has officially appointed Federica Giudici as the new Director of Social Services, a decision made following a rigorous selection process. Operational details

Federica Giudici, a graduate in social work and holder of an executive master in business administration, has been officially appointed as the director of the Social Services of Bellinzona in July 2024. Her appointment represents a crucial moment for the city, as it comes at a time when pressure on social services is continuously growing, due to an increase in the number of needy individuals and challenges related to inclusion. ## Responsibilities and strategic objectives As director, Federica Giudici will be responsible for managing the personnel of the Social Services, which currently consists of around 35 staff members, including social workers, educators, and administrators. Her primary mission will be to ensure effective coordination of initiatives and projects in favor of citizen sociality, ensuring that no vulnerable group is overlooked. A concrete example of the work carried out is the 'Active Integration Bellinzona' project, which in 2023 supported over 120 people in conditions of unemployment, providing professional training and facilitating their reintegration into the labor market. 📊 Key numbers In 2023, the Social Services of Bellinzona managed around 1,250 requests for economic assistance, with a dedicated budget of 3.8 million Swiss francs. This figure reflects the importance of stable and competent leadership in managing public resources and ensuring fair and transparent use of available funds. ## Projects of inclusion and integration Among Giudici's primary objectives is the development of targeted social inclusion projects. In detail, there are plans to expand collaborations between neighboring municipalities, such as Giubiasco and Arbedo-Castione, to promote synergies in supporting migrant families. In 2022, Bellinzona launched a pilot program for linguistic and cultural integration, which involved 85 participants mainly from non-EU countries. 💡 Regulatory focus The activity of the Social Services is based on the directives of the Cantonal Law on Social Assistance (LAS), which was reformed in 2020 with the introduction of new evaluation criteria for the right to economic support. The law provides for a maximum monthly amount of 986 CHF for single individuals and up to 2,450 CHF for families of four. ## Operational challenges and future prospects The management of Social Services in a city like Bellinzona requires a multidisciplinary approach. Among the main challenges are the increasing demand for psychological support, exacerbated by the impact of the pandemic, and the need to develop preventive measures to avoid economic hardship situations. In this sense, Giudici has expressed her intention to implement a monitoring system based on social KPIs to evaluate the effectiveness of the projects undertaken. > 'Our priority is to make social services more accessible, efficient, and able to respond to the real needs of citizens.' ⚠️ Operational checklist - Mapping of urban areas with the highest concentration of social fragility - Strengthening partnerships with NGOs and third sector entities - Continuous training for staff, with a focus on emergency interventions - Annual review of the social balance to maximize the impact of available resources With Federica Giudici's guidance, Bellinzona is preparing to strengthen its role as a reference point for sociality and integration in the Canton of Ticino.
