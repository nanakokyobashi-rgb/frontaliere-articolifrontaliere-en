How permit G works

To work in Ticino, border workers must have a G permit. This permit is issued by the State of origin, in this case Italy. The G permit must be requested within 90 days of departure for Switzerland. The cost of the G permit is €30.00 for border crossers under 25 years of age, while for those over 25 years of age it is €60.00. It is important to note that the G permit is not an entry visa, but a document required to work in Switzerland.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the requirements for refunds?
The requirements to obtain the refreshments are described in the New Frontier Agreement, signed on 23 December 2020. The border worker must comply with the requirements and provide the information required to obtain refunds.
How can I take advantage of refreshments?
To take advantage of the refreshments, the border worker must comply with the requirements and provide the required information. If the border crossing does not meet the requirements, refills may be reduced or cancelled.
When did the New Switzerland-Italy Border Agreement come into force and what are its main effects?
The New Frontier Agreement, signed on 23 December 2020, is in force from 1 January 2024. Its main effects include the avoidance of double taxation through the tax credit for Italy and the introduction of a deductible of €10,000 for new frontier workers, reducing taxes in Switzerland.
What is the cost and deadline to apply for the G permit to work in Ticino?
The G permit is essential for working in Ticino and must be requested within 90 days of departure for Switzerland. The cost is €30.00 for border crossers under 25 years of age and €60.00 for those over 25 years of age. It is a necessary document to work, not an entry visa.
Who can benefit from the €10,000 deductible and how does it work fiscally?
The excess of €10,000 applies exclusively to *new* frontier workers. It means that the first €10,000 of income will not be taxed in Switzerland. For income above this threshold, taxes will have to be paid in Italy. It is not a tax incentive, but a specific benefit for this category.
