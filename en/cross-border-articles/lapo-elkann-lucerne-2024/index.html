Lapo Elkann, a well-known Italian entrepreneur and designer, has decided to move to Lucerne, Switzerland. As he explained in an interview, his choice is mainly motivated by the country's security and neutrality. 'Switzerland is neutral and safe. I didn't do it for taxes, but because of the current global situation,' Elkann declared. This decision comes at a time of growing geopolitical instability, and Lucerne, with its tranquility and quality of life, represents an ideal choice for many expats.
