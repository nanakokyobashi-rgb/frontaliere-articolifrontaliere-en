On 1 January 2024, the New Cross-border Agreement came into force, signed on 23 December 2020 and ratified in Italy with Law 83 of 13 June 2023. For a cross-border family facing the birth of a child in 2026, the date is the first point to set: the new 2026 tax agreement does not coincide with the year of entry into force.

The editorial checklist dedicated to birth revolves around three practices: registry registration, family allowances and consular procedures. The practical simulation should therefore be read as a comparison between three strands, not as a single calculation. The birth date opens the administrative verification; work in the Canton of Ticino must instead be read within its tax and social security framework.

The cross-border worker's tax framework

The rule indicated for earned income is clear: for cross-border workers, withholding tax is only withheld in Switzerland. Italy avoids double taxation through the tax credit in the EC 730 framework. The checklist must therefore not transform the income into a double tax, nor present Switzerland as part of the EU or EEA: Switzerland is not an EU/EEA member.

The Italy-Switzerland Double Taxation Convention was signed on 9 March 1976. It is the date to be associated with the bilateral framework, while the New Cross-Border Agreement has a distinct sequence: signature on 23 December 2020, Italian ratification with Law 83 of 13 June 2023, which entered into force on 1 January 2024.

For the worker with G permit, the KVG chapter remains that of health insurance and includes the right of option for cross-border commuters G. The deductibles for adults indicated by the source range from CHF 300 to CHF 2,500. The reference is separate from the registry registration, but belongs to the administrative map that a border family may wish to keep tidy in 2026.

The practical news, therefore, is not a new rate linked to birth. It is the need not to confuse registry, checks and consulate with withholding tax, double taxation and KVG.

Rates and contributions to be kept separate

The source indicates three brackets for Italian IRPEF from 2026: 23% up to €28'000, 33% from €28'001 to €50'000 and 43% over €50'000. For 2024–2025, it instead reports 23%, 35% and 43%. These data should be read as the Italian IRPEF framework, distinct from the Swiss withholding described above.

On the Swiss side, AVS/AI/IPG is 5.3% for the employee; AD/AC is 1.1% with a cap at CHF 148'200; LAINF is indicated as between 0.7% and 1.5%; LPP ranges from 7% to 18% by age bracket, starting at age 25. These are contribution and pension items, not family allowances.

The respective federal and cantonal laws define the tax rates; administration is handled by AFC/ESTV at federal level and by the cantonal tax administrations at cantonal level. UFAS deals with social security, while BFS collects statistical data: neither of the two sets the rates. Distinguishing these responsibilities helps avoid errors in the practical simulation.

Key points

The operational checklist can be completed in five steps, using only the entries and references from the simulation.

Five steps for 2026

1. Open three separate sections: civil registry registration, family allowances, consular procedures. These are the three chapters indicated for the birth of a child in a cross-border worker's family. Do not combine them into a single generic item, because the comparison must show which procedure is being examined.

2. Establish the worker's status: G permit and tax category. If the cross-border worker already had that status before July 17, 2023, note the scenario with an exemption of €7'500 and transitional regime 2024–2033. If they fall among the new cross-border workers, note the tax-free allowance of €10'000. The distinction precedes the calculation.

3. For employment income, record only one withholding: withholding tax in Switzerland. In the Italian step, enter the tax credit from section CE of the 730, so that the simulation represents the double-taxation rule without turning it into a levy in both countries.

4. Keep the healthcare and social security portion in a separate box. LAMal means health insurance; G cross-border workers have the right of choice, and the adult deductibles indicated are CHF 300–2'500. In the same check, you can report AVS/AI/IPG at 5.3%, AD/AC at 1.1% up to the cap of CHF 148'200, LAINF between 0.7% and 1.5%, and LPP between 7% and 18% from age 25, by age bracket.

5. Verify the correct tax reference: federal and cantonal rates depend on federal and cantonal laws; at the federal level, the administration is AFC/ESTV, while the cantonal tax administrations also operate. Do not assign this task to UFAS or BFS. For the Italian part, section CE of the 730 is the point cited by the source.

The result is a readable simulation: three birth-related procedures, one selected tax scenario, a separate chapter for healthcare and social security. To turn income alone into a numerical calculation, use the calcolatore fiscale.

Frequently Asked Questions
What practices does the checklist for the birth of a border child include?
It includes personal registration, family allowances and consular procedures. The simulation treats them as three distinct steps and joins them to the tax chapter of the border, without transforming the birth into a new rate. Income from work remains regulated by withholding tax only in Switzerland and by the Italian tax credit in the EC framework of 730.
When did the New Frontier Agreement come into force?
The New Frontier Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020, ratified in Italy with Law 83 of 13 June 2023 and is in force from 1 January 2024. 2026 is the year of birth simulation, not the year of entry into force of the agreement. The Italy-Switzerland Double Taxation Convention dates back to 9 March 1976.
How do old and new frontiersmen stand out in the simulation?
An old frontier worker is someone who was already a frontier worker before 17 July 2023: for this category, the source indicates an exemption of €7,500 and a transitional regime from 2024 to 2033. The new frontier workers have a deductible of €10,000. These data are used for income tax comparison and should be kept separate from the three birth-related practices.
Is the frontier worker's income taxed in both countries?
No. For the frontier worker's income, the withholding takes place only in Switzerland. Italy avoids double taxation through the tax credit indicated in the EC framework of 730. The simulation should therefore not add a Swiss source tax and a second Italian withholding tax on the same income. The reference is the tax rule indicated by the source.
What does the LAMal mean for a border crossing with a G permit?
LAMal is health insurance. Border workers with a G permit have the right of option; for adults, the source indicates deductibles between CHF 300 and CHF 2,500. This chapter should be kept separate from personal registration, family allowances and consular practices, because it concerns health coverage and not birth registration.
