Implications for Workers Any accident involving a worker in Canton Ticino raises fundamental questions about workplace safety and compensation procedures. When such a serious event occurs, like the one recorded in Vallemaggia, the worker is covered by accident insurance (LAINF). For cross-border workers and residents in Switzerland, this coverage is mandatory and usually borne by the employer. In this specific case, accident management procedures involve opening a case file with the competent insurance institution.

It is important to remember that, according to current regulations, accident insurance not only covers medical, hospital, and transportation expenses — as in the case of the Rega intervention — but also provides daily allowances if the worker is unable to perform their duties. Procedures after an injury

If a worker is the victim of an accident on Swiss territory, the first procedure to follow is to immediately report the accident to his employer. The latter has the obligation to notify the accident insurance of the event. In the event of a serious accident, such as the one that occurred in Piano di Peccia, it is the authorities who intervened on site - in this case the cantonal police - who manage the first bureaucratic steps, but it is up to the employer to ensure that the insurance procedure is started correctly.

Documentation required

It is essential for workers to keep every medical certificate certifying the duration of the prognosis and the inability to work. These documents are the basis for calculating the benefits paid by the insurance. In emergency situations, such as the Rega intervention, the costs are usually covered by accident insurance, avoiding direct costs to the patient. It is advisable, once the acute phase has passed, to request a copy of the police report for any future insurance or legal needs.

Frequently Asked Questions
What does accident insurance cover in Switzerland?
Accident insurance (LAINF) covers medical, hospital, transportation and post-injury treatment. In addition, it provides daily allowances to compensate for the loss of earnings during the period of incapacity for work. In the event of an accident in Vallemaggia, the insurance covers the intervention of Rega and the necessary care for the 29-year-old.
What should a worker do after an injury?
The worker must immediately report the accident to the employer, who in turn has the obligation to notify the insurance institution. It is essential to keep all medical certificates attesting to the prognosis and inability to work in order to proceed with the claim for compensation.
Are accidents on site always covered?
Yes, accidents that occurred during working hours are classified as occupational accidents. In Switzerland, the employer is required by law to take out accident insurance for its staff, thus ensuring the financial and health protection of the employee involved in workplace accidents.
