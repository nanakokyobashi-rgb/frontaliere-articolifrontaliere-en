Operational details

The decisive step for the territory will be the entry into operation of the Riva San Vitale pumping and water treatment plant, a project that has been discussed for about 50 years. According to Marco Romano, the station is expected to be delivered by the end of the year and become operational during 2027. From that moment on, the Mendrisiotto region will also be able to rely on lake water, guaranteeing municipalities greater complementarity of sources. Whenever a municipal resource experiences difficulties, lake water can step in to support the network; other municipalities that can no longer use their own sources will instead be able to fully benefit from lake supply. This infrastructural evolution is part of a geographical and climatic context that directly involves the entire cross-border area of Canton Ticino, where the management of natural resources and primary services requires a regional vision and targeted investments.

The management of water resources

Greater availability, however, does not mean letting our guard down regarding consumption. Mayor Simone Castelletti recalled that Stabio can count on the precious resource of the Santa Margherita well, but the very last few years have shown how necessary it is to use it carefully. Therefore, even with new reservoirs and new sources, the appeal to the population to avoid waste remains valid. Because water, as the mayor summarized, remains the blue gold of the territory. The planning and constant maintenance of distribution networks represent fundamental pillars to guarantee the continuity of essential services throughout the district, connecting traditional underground reserves with the future opportunities offered by lake abstraction.

Frequently Asked Questions
Where is the new water tank located and how much did it cost?
The new water tank of Bella Cima is located in Stabio, in the Canton of Ticino. The work cost about 4 million francs and is part of the regional strategy for water security in Mendrisiotto, combining the efforts of the Municipality of Stabio and the Regional Aqueduct Consortium of Mendrisiotto.
What is the overall capacity of the new Bella Cima tanks?
The new Bella Cima plant has a total capacity of over 3,000 cubic metres divided into two new tanks. About half of this capacity represents the reserve necessary for the normal operation of the network, while the other half is intended to cope with water emergencies.
How does the water supply connected to the Santa Margherita well work?
The water comes from the underground collection well of Santa Margherita, located in the plain of Stabio. The water is pumped simultaneously to the municipal network and to the Bella Cima tank; when the pumps are not in operation, gravity allows the accumulated water to reach the population.
