Miazzina, unions on 9 October: 'We ask for respect'

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 October 2026

Trade-union mobilization on 9 October at 14 at Eremo di Miazzina, organized by Cgil Novara Vco, Cisl Piemonte Orientale and Uil Novara Vco on work, care and support for families.

The call

The meeting promoted by Cgil Novara Vco, Cisl Piemonte Orientale and Uil Novara Vco, together with their respective Funzione Pubblica categories, is scheduled at Eremo di Miazzina on Friday 9 October at 14. The organizations have announced the mobilization, identifying three related themes: work, assistance and support for families.

The message chosen to present the initiative places respect for those who work, those receiving care and families at the center.

“We ask for respect: for those who work, for those receiving care, for families.”

The unions explain that respect for rights must not remain a statement of principle. In their view, it must translate into concrete choices, responsibilities and commitments on everyone’s part. The mobilization is therefore described as a call for attention directed at different people and stakeholders.

The demands

For those who work, the demand calls for dignity, safety and protections. For people who need care and assistance, it calls for answers. For families who care every day for those who are most vulnerable, it asks for support.

The appeal also involves institutions, politics and employers. Among other commitments, the organizations ask the latter to guarantee wages currently unpaid, decent working conditions, safety and stability. Greater attention to reconciling private and professional life is also called for.

To politicians, however, the statement asks that commitments be transformed into decisions, resources and services capable of affecting people’s lives, preventing them from remaining merely announcements. The stated objective is not to create divisions: the unions want to involve everyone who has a responsibility in building a fairer society.

The statement is signed by general secretaries Michele Piffero for Cgil Novara Vco, Elena Ugazio for Cisl Piemonte Orientale and Stefano De Grandis for Uil Novara Vco, together with representatives of their respective Funzione Pubblica categories. The choice of 9 October is presented as the way to bring work, care and families back to the same table, considered parts of the same community and its future.

Operational details

Three levels of the mobilization

The practical reading of the statement starts with the allocation of responsibilities. There is not a single undifferentiated request: the text separates the needs of those who work, those who need care and assistance, and families who look after the most vulnerable people. At the same time, it distinguishes what is being asked of employers from what is being asked of policymakers.

This distinction provides a first criterion for interpreting the appeal. For workers, the issue of unpaid wages is accompanied by requests concerning the quality of working conditions, safety and stability. The statement does not indicate amounts, periods or individual positions: it therefore does not make it possible to reconstruct individual cases.

In terms of care, the request concerns responses for people who need assistance. No specific services or operating procedures are indicated. For families too, the text expresses a specific need, support, but does not describe a particular procedure or measure.

The distinction also concerns the parties addressed. Employers are asked to take action on wages and conditions; policymakers are asked to turn commitments into decisions, resources and services. Responsibilities are therefore not conflated, but assigned to different actors.

Key points

Four steps for reading the appointment

1. Pin down the confirmed information. The appointment is scheduled for Friday 9 October at 14, at the Eremo di Miazzina. These are the organizational details reported by the source; no other logistical information is added.

2. Separate the three themes. The mobilization concerns work, assistance and support for families. Distinguishing these areas makes it possible to read the requests without turning them into a single indistinct demand.

3. Associate each request with the relevant interlocutor. For employers, the statement indicates wages unpaid to date, decent conditions, safety, stability and work-life balance. For institutions and politics, on the other hand, it refers to decisions, resources and services.

4. Consider the stated objective. The initiative is not presented as a tool for creating divisions. The trade unions state that they want to involve all the parties that have a responsibility in building a fairer society.

These steps help avoid attributing to the mobilization content that the statement does not contain. No documents to submit, reservations, participation procedures, individual contacts or procedures for obtaining unpaid wages are indicated. Therefore, it is not possible to add operational instructions beyond the appointment details and the requests addressed to the various parties.

The follow-up must also be kept distinct from the source's content. Anyone seeking further information on the issue of job stability can consult the annunci lavoro, while the mobilization remains focused on the discussion between work, care and families. Frequently Asked Questions
When and where will the trade union mobilisation take place?
The mobilisation is scheduled for Friday 9 October at 2 pm and will take place at the Hermitage of Miazzina. The initiative is promoted by Cgil Novara Vco, Cisl Piemonte Orientale and Uil Novara Vco, together with the respective categories of the Public Function.
What are the central themes of the mobilisation and the key message?
The three themes connected to the centre of the mobilisation are work, assistance and support for families. The message chosen is: "We ask for respect: for those who work, for those in care, for families", stressing that respect for rights must be translated into concrete choices and commitments by all.
What specific requests are made to employers and policy?
Employers are asked to ensure unpaid wages, decent working conditions, security, stability and greater attention to reconciling private and professional life. Politics, on the other hand, is required to transform commitments into decisions, resources and services that positively affect people's lives, avoiding simple announcements.
What is the stated objective of the mobilisation?
The stated objective is not to create conflicts, but to involve all those who have a responsibility in building a more just society. The choice of 9 October aims to bring work, care and families back to the same table, considering them fundamental parts of the same community and its future.
