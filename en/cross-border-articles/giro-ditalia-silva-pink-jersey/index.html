Key facts

  • What: Victory of Guillermo Silva in the second stage of the Giro d'Italia
  • When: 9 May 2026
  • Where: Veliko Tarnovo, Bulgaria
  • Who: Guillermo Silva (Astana), Florian Stork, Giulio Ciccone
  • Distance: 221 km the length of the stage

Guillermo Silva has written a page in cycling history by becoming the first Uruguayan to win a stage of a Grand Tour. In the second stage of the Giro d'Italia, the Burgas-Veliko Tarnovo of 221 km, Silva conquered the pink jersey, outsprinting the German Florian Stork and the Italian Giulio Ciccone. The 24-year-old, member of the Astana team, showed great determination, catching up with a group of high-level cyclists who had tried to break away during the ascent to the Lyaskovets monastery, 11 km from the finish line.

Implications for cycling

Silva's victory represents a historic moment not only for Uruguay but also for international cycling. His performance has shown that young talents can compete at the highest levels. For fans and cycling enthusiasts, this victory is a sign of hope and renewal in the sport.

The stage was also marked by a mass crash 23 km from the finish line, which involved several cyclists, including Adam Yates. The crash forced the organizers to temporarily neutralize the race, allowing the cyclists to resume the race five kilometers from the finish line. Yates was able to rejoin the race, while his teammate Jay Vine had to abandon the race.
