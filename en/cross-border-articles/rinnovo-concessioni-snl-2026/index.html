The Società Navigazione del Lago di Lugano (SNL) is preparing to present its plans for the renewal of concessions and expansion of services for 2026. According to President Agostino Ferrazzini, the renewal of concessions is a crucial step that will allow for the planning of investments and the strengthening of the role of navigation as a modern and sustainable infrastructure for the entire transfrontier region.

The SNL has already started working on the new concessions, which will be evaluated and approved by December 31, 2026. The new connections, themed experiences, and large events on Lake Maggiore and Lake Ceresio will be at the center of attention.

The renewal of concessions is a crucial step for the SNL, which is preparing to present its plans for 2026. The details are still unclear, but it is known that it will be an important year for the company.

The SNL is a company that operates in the navigation sector on Lake Maggiore and Lake Ceresio. The company has been operating in this sector for years and has already implemented many important projects.
