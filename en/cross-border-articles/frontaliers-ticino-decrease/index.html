Context

According to data from the Cantonal Statistical Office (USTAT), the number of frontier workers in Ticino is steadily decreasing. In the third quarter of 2023, the number of frontier workers was over 80,000, but has since declined by about 1,000 units.

The change is particularly evident in municipalities such as Lugano and Bellinzona, which have been among the most affected by the decrease in the number of border crossers. In 2023, for example, the municipality of Lugano recorded a 10% decrease in the number of border crossings compared to the same period in 2022. On the contrary, the municipality of Bellinzona saw a decrease of 5% in the same period.

The context

Border work is a tax regime that allows citizens of an EU Member State to work in Switzerland without being subject to Swiss taxes. This scheme was introduced to incentivise the mobility of workers between EU Member States and Switzerland.

However, the tax agreement between Switzerland and Italy changed the rules of the game. According to the agreement, border workers working in Switzerland will have to pay Italian taxes on their income, even if they do not reside in Italy. This could make the border crossing less advantageous for border people, who may decide to abandon their status and move to Ticino.
