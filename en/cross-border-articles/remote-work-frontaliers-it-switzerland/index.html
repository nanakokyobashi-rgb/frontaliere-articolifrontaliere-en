Law no. 217 of 2025 enters into force on 20 January 2026, introducing the structural recognition of cross-border teleworking.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Law no. 217 of 2025 enters into force on 20 January 2026.
  • Cross-border teleworking is structurally recognised.
  • Border workers can carry out up to 25% of their annual work in teleworking mode from their home in Italy.

The benefits of cross-border teleworking

Cross-border teleworking represents a great opportunity for border workers in the Canton of Ticino. With Law no. 217 of 2025, in fact, teleworking is structurally recognised, allowing workers to carry out up to 25% of their annual work in teleworking mode from their home in Italy. This means border workers can work from home and save time and money on transport and daily life.

Concrete examples

For example, if a Bellinzona frontier worker works 40 hours per week, they can carry out up to 10 hours of teleworking per week from their home in Italy. This means you can save about 2 hours a day of time and money on transportation and daily life.

Operational Checklists

To take advantage of cross-border teleworking, frontier workers must:

  • Check if your work activity is compatible with cross-border teleworking.
  • Inform your employer of the possibility of teleworking.
  • Check if your employment contract provides for the possibility of teleworking.
  • Comply with safety regulations

The reform of cross-border work in Ticino represents a step towards a more modern balance between competitiveness, quality of life and tax certainty. The measure applies to all frontier workers, including those who fall under the transitional regime, offering uniform treatment and regulatory certainty for all.

The reform introduces a regime that is more flexible and adaptable to the needs of businesses and workers. Border workers will enjoy more favourable working conditions, with more reasonable working hours and greater control over their working time. This will improve the quality of life of workers and increase the productivity of companies.

The reform also provides for the creation of a transitional regime for border workers who are already present on the Ticino labour market. This regime will make it possible to avoid economic disasters for workers and companies that depend on their work.
