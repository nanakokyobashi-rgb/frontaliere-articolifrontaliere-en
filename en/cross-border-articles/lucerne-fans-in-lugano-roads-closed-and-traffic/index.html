Lucerne fans in Lugano: roads closed and traffic

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 8 October 2026

## TL;DR - Match at 6 p.m. at Cornaredo stadium - Parade between approximately 4 and 5:30 p.m. - Delays of 15 to 30 minutes on the affected roads - Via Ciani

Context

TL;DR

  • Match at 18 at Cornaredo stadium
  • Procession between 16 and around 17.30
  • Waiting times of between 15 and 30 minutes on the affected roads
  • Via Ciani: possible slowdowns after the match

Key facts

  • Match → FC Lugano-FC Lucerna
  • Location → Cornaredo stadium
  • First closure → 15.50, station forecourt for public transport
  • Route → FFS station, via San Gottardo, Cornaredo
  • Delays → between 15 and 30 minutes
  • Return → around 20
  • Parliamentary question → Tamara Merlo, Più Donne

Next Saturday, at 18, FC Lugano and FC Lucerna will face each other at Cornaredo stadium. The Lugano City Police reported significant repercussions for road traffic linked to the visiting supporters’ procession. The temporary closures will affect the Cornaredo and Molino Nuovo areas, as well as the connection between the FFS station and via San Gottardo.

Before kickoff

Between approximately 16 and 17.30, Lucerna supporters will walk to the stadium from the railway station. From 15.50, however, Lugano station forecourt will not be accessible to public transport. For those using mezzi pubblici Ticino, this is the first restriction indicated by the Police.

The route will cross via San Gottardo from south to north, along the section between the Besso tunnel and via Tesserete. From there, the procession will continue along via Tesserete, via Torricelli and via Trevano, reaching the southern car park of Cornaredo stadium.

As the procession passes, all affected roads will be completely closed to traffic. According to the Police, motorists should expect delays of between 15 and 30 minutes. The restriction will therefore extend both to the stadium area and to the connection with the FFS station.

Departure after the match

Restrictions are also expected at the end of the match. Around 20, the visiting supporters will leave the stadium heading towards the FFS station, following the same route used on the way there. Temporary closures and delays for motorists have also been announced during this phase.

The Police notice also reports possible slowdowns on via Ciani after the final whistle. Depending on the influx of local supporters, the road could be partially closed. For the affected hours, residents are invited to plan alternative journeys.

Politically, the issue also concerns the costs of the measures. Recently, Tamara Merlo, a municipal councillor from Più Donne, submitted a parliamentary question to the Municipal Council. The request concerns the extent of the traffic restrictions during the supporters’ procession and, above all, the amount of expenses incurred by the Municipal Council each time.

The questions include security measures, traffic management, cleaning and restoration of public space, as well as the possibility that part of the cost could be invoiced to FC Lugano. The Municipal Council’s responses are awaited.

Operational details

The most useful piece of information, for anyone planning a trip toward Lugano, is the breakdown into two distinct periods. This is not a single restriction tied to the match time: the first concerns the approach of the visiting fans, the second their return toward the FFS station. For a cross-border commuter passing through Lugano, this distinction makes it possible to read the notice according to the time of travel.

Two windows to distinguish

| Window | Indicated effect | What to consider | | From 15.50 | Station forecourt closed to public transport | The first restriction precedes the procession on foot | | From 16 to around 17.30 | Complete closure of affected roads | The estimated waiting times are between 15 and 30 minutes | | Around 20 | Visiting fans' departure again toward the FFS station | The passage along the same route is repeated | | After the final whistle | Possible slowdowns on via Ciani | The partial closure depends on local crowd flow |

The distinction between the station and the roadway is relevant. At 15.50, the notice indicates that the forecourt is closed to public transport; during the procession, by contrast, it refers to a complete traffic block on the affected roads. These are two different effects, occurring in the same period leading up to the match.

The return journey also requires separate consideration. Around 20, the movement takes place in the opposite direction, but along the same route: FFS station, via San Gottardo, via Tesserete, via Torricelli and via Trevano. For anyone who needs to reach the station after the match, the practical reference is therefore the dispersal window, not just the match time.

Via Ciani adds a conditional element. The Police mention possible slowdowns and a possible partial closure linked to the influx of local fans. It is therefore not presented as an automatic measure, whereas the closures along the visiting fans' procession are indicated as planned.

The same information makes it possible to distinguish the disruption for travelers from that for the Municipality. The first details concern waiting times and closures; Tamara Merlo's inquiry, on the other hand, concerns the costs of security, traffic, cleaning and restoration of public space. Key points

Checklist before setting off

A cross-border commuter who needs to reach Lugano or return toward the FFS station can turn the notice into a simple check based on the times and roads indicated by the police.

1. Identify the time window. If the journey falls between approximately 15.50 and 17.30, consider both the closure of the station forecourt to public transport and the visiting supporters' pedestrian procession. If it takes place around 20, consider instead the departure from the stadium.

2. Check the starting or ending point. The notice identifies the FFS station as the procession's origin and destination. Those using public transport can consult mezzi pubblici Ticino, while still using the closure of the forecourt from 15.50 as the reference.

3. Check the roads involved. The route includes the section of via San Gottardo between the Besso tunnel and via Tesserete, followed by via Tesserete, via Torricelli and via Trevano, up to the south parking lot of Cornaredo. If the journey involves one of these roads while the supporters are passing through, the notice provides for a total closure and waits of between 15 and 30 minutes.

4. Make a second check for the return journey. After the final whistle, around 20, the visiting supporters' procession will follow the same route toward the station. Add via Ciani to the check, where delays are possible and, depending on the influx of local supporters, a partial closure may occur.

The police invite the public to plan alternative journeys during the affected hours. This applies to both phases: before the match, when the movement starts from the station, and afterward, when the flow moves from Cornaredo toward the FFS station. The most prudent approach is therefore to separate the outward and return journeys, rather than consider the match as a single traffic event.

For a cross-border commuter, this checklist avoids confusing the closure of the forecourt with the road blockade: the former concerns public transport at the station, while the latter concerns all roads affected by the procession's passage. The notice consistently specifies the match at 18, the afternoon window, the departure around 20 and the estimated wait of between 15 and 30 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions
When will the station forecourt be closed to public transportation?
According to a communication from Lugano City Police, the station forecourt will be closed to public transport as early as 15.50, before the start of the procession of visiting fans. This is the first restriction indicated for those who use public transport.
What are the expected waiting times on the roads affected by the passage of the fans?
The Police estimates waiting times of between 15 and 30 minutes on the roads affected by the procession of visiting fans, who will travel along via San Gottardo, via Tesserete, via Torricelli and via Trevano to the south car park of Cornaredo Stadium. During this stretch, a complete traffic closure will be imposed.
What time will the fans head back to the station, and what should cross-border commuters take into account?
Around 20:00, after the final whistle, the visiting fans will leave the stadium heading toward the FFS station, following the same route used on the way there. Temporary roadblocks and delays for motorists are also expected during this phase, so anyone who needs to reach the station after the match must plan accordingly.
