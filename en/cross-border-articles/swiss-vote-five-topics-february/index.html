Five issues on the table

The popular vote on 28 February 2027 will concern five issues. Among them is the abolition of the ban on building new nuclear power plants, a proposal that the Federal Council has designed as an indirect counter-proposal to the popular initiative «Stop the blackout».

The popular initiative on foie gras, which proposes banning its import, is also among the questions. The democracy initiative is also planned: it aims to standardize naturalization procedures and allow naturalization after five years of legal residence in Switzerland, regardless of the type of permit.

The agreement on free trade between EFTA (the European Free Trade Association) and Malaysia is also among the issues. The list is rounded off by the Federal Council's proposal to give precedence to collective labour agreements (CLAs) over cantonal minimum wages.
