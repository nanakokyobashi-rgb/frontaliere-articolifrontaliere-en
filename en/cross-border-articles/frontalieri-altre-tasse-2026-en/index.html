Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 28 July 2026

The Canton of Ticino will impose a one hundred percent tax at source on 'old' border workers who opt for the Omnibus Decree.

Context

New rules for the 'old' border guards of the Canton of Ticino: increasing taxes

The Canton of Ticino has decided to impose a tax at source of one hundred percent on 'old' border workers who will opt for the Omnibus Decree. The new rule concerns workers who worked in Ticino between 31 December 2018 and 17 July 2023 with daily return, with tax residence in a municipality within 20 km of the border.

According to the rules in force, the border crossers concerned will have to submit a tax return by 31 March 2024. The new rule applies to those who have worked in municipalities such as Bellinzona, Lugano, Mendrisio, Locarno, Ascona, Brissago, Gambarogno, Gordola, Ascona, Brissago, Locarno and surroundings.

The decision of the Canton of Ticino was taken to ensure consistency with federal regulations on income tax. In particular, the Omnibus Decree was passed to simplify and streamline tax rules in Switzerland.

The new rule will result in an increase in taxes for 'old' border workers who will opt for the Omnibus Decree. For example, a worker who earned 60,000 Swiss francs in 2022 and opted for the Omnibus Decree, will have to pay an income tax of 4.5% (0.45 x 60,000 = 27,000 Swiss francs) + a fixed rate of 3.2% (0.032 x 60,000 = 1,920 Swiss francs), for a total of 28,920 Swiss francs.

On January 1, 2024, the Omnibus Decree will enter into force, a regulation that concerns border workers in the Canton of Ticino. According to calculations made by the Federal Tax Office (UFI), about 2,000 workers could be affected by this new rule. 📊

The legislation provides that frontier workers who opt for the Omnibus Decree will have to pay the tax at source of one hundred percent. This means that the portion of tax previously deducted from the salary will be paid directly by the employer, rather than being withheld by the worker.

A concrete example can be useful to better understand the situation. Suppose a frontier worker in the municipality of Bellinzona has an income of 60,000 Swiss francs per year. The portion of tax previously deducted from the salary was 10%, or 6,000 Swiss francs. If he opted for the Omnibus Decree, the tax share would be increased to 100%, or 60,000 Swiss francs. This means that the worker would have to pay 54,000 Swiss francs more each year.
