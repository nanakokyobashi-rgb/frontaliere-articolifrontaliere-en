Operational details

Italy and Switzerland signed the Double Taxation Convention on 9 December 1976. This convention provides that taxes paid in one country are deductible by the other country. This means that if an Italian citizen moves to Switzerland, the taxes paid in Italy will be deductible from Switzerland and vice versa.

The cost of living in Bosisio Parini is lower than that of Lugano, the nearest city in Switzerland. The average price of an apartment in Bosisio Parini is around 1,500 euros per month. In comparison, the average price of an apartment in Lugano is around 3,000 euros per month. 📊

The recommended area for border crossers is the Bosisio Parini area, which offers a quiet life and a good quality of life. The area is served by a good public transport network and is located a few kilometres from Lugano.

With regard to regulations, the transfer of an Italian citizen to Switzerland is subject to the rules of the Swiss Federal Council. Art. 34 of the Swiss Constitution states that "every person has the right to choose his residence and to move freely within the territory of the country". In addition, art. 23 of the Law on Direct Taxes states that "direct taxes are payable by those entitled under the law".
