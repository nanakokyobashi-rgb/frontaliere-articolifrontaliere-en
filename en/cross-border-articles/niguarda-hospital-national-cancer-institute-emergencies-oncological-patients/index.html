Milan: Niguarda and Istituto Tumori join forces for oncological emergencies

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 September 2026

The Niguarda Hospital and the IRCCS Fondazione Istituto Nazionale dei Tumori in Milan have signed an operational protocol to ensure continuity of care for cancer patients in case of emergencies.

Context

In brief

  • Operational protocol between Niguarda Hospital and the National Cancer Institute of Milan
  • Objective: to avoid interruptions in the care pathway of cancer patients
  • Coordination by ATS Milan and the University of Milan

Key facts

  • What: Operational protocol between Niguarda Hospital and the National Cancer Institute
  • Where: Milan
  • Who: Niguarda Hospital, National Cancer Institute, ATS Milan, University of Milan
  • Amount: Not specified

Niguarda Hospital and the Fondazione IRCCS National Cancer Institute (INT) of Milan have signed an operational protocol to ensure continuity of care for cancer patients in case of emergencies. The protocol, coordinated by ATS of the Metropolitan City of Milan and the School of Medical Oncology of the University of Milan, aims to avoid interruptions in the care pathway of patients. The National Cancer Institute, a reference center for cancer care and research, does not have its own Emergency Department, so patients who face an emergency may need to access another hospital facility.

Protocol Objective

The protocol aims to ensure that the transition from one institution to another occurs without interrupting the continuity of care, by putting the emergency department of Niguarda in direct communication with the specialists of the INT. When a patient being treated at the National Cancer Institute arrives at the Niguarda Emergency Department, emergency professionals can promptly coordinate with the oncologists of the INT, sharing clinical information, ongoing therapies, and care priorities. The goal is to allow Niguarda doctors to intervene while considering the oncological pathway already undertaken by the patient.

The primary objective remains to stabilize the person and address the emergency, but the connection between the teams also serves to avoid inappropriate interventions and, when conditions allow, to facilitate the patient's return to their reference oncology center. According to the protocol, in most cases, the resumption of care by the National Cancer Institute occurs within 24-48 hours of the Emergency Department notification.

Even when immediate transfer is not possible, the exchange between specialists allows for continuity in clinical decisions and therapy management. The project fits into the model of clinical-assistance networks promoted by the Lombardy Region, with the aim of strengthening collaboration between facilities and professionals and reducing the fragmentation of care pathways.

In this case, the Lombardy Oncological Network and the emergency-urgency system are connected, creating a structured link between a reference oncology institute and a large Emergency Department.

Operational details

Implications for Cross-Border Workers

The operational protocol between Niguarda Hospital and the National Cancer Institute of Milan has important implications for cross-border workers who work in Lombardy and reside in Ticino. This agreement ensures that cross-border cancer patients can receive continuous and coordinated care in case of emergencies, avoiding interruptions in their treatment path.

Procedure for Cross-Border Patients

When a cross-border patient undergoing treatment at the National Cancer Institute arrives at the Niguarda Emergency Department, emergency professionals can promptly coordinate with the oncologists at the INT, sharing clinical information, ongoing therapies, and care priorities. This allows Niguarda doctors to intervene while considering the oncological path already undertaken by the patient.

Possible Scenarios

A cross-border worker in a similar situation could benefit from this protocol, ensuring that their therapies are not interrupted and that they continue to receive appropriate care. The protocol provides that in most cases, the resumption of care by the National Cancer Institute will occur within 24-48 hours of the Emergency Department notification.

Actions for Cross-Border Workers

Cross-border workers facing a medical emergency can rely on this protocol to ensure the continuity of their care. It is important that patients and their families are informed about this procedure and that they promptly turn to the Niguarda Emergency Department in case of emergency.

Deadlines and Procedure

The operational protocol is already in effect, and patients can immediately benefit from this collaboration between the two facilities. There are no specific deadlines to be met, but it is crucial that patients follow the doctors' instructions and present themselves at the Niguarda Emergency Department if needed.
