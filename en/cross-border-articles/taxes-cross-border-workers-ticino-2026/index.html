Cross-border workers in Ticino can breathe a sigh of relief. According to TVS, there will be no changes in taxes for 'old' cross-border workers. This means that the current tax regime, which includes withholding tax and refunds, will remain unchanged for 2026 as well. Important news for those who live in Italy and work in Switzerland, especially in a context of economic uncertainty.

What changes for cross-border workers

For 'old' cross-border workers, i.e., those who started working in Switzerland before 2021, there will be no significant changes. This includes the maintenance of withholding tax, a system that greatly simplifies income tax declarations for those working in Ticino. Furthermore, refunds, i.e., the tax refunds that cross-border workers can obtain, will remain unchanged. This is a crucial point, especially for those who have already planned their finances based on the current regime.

The importance of refunds

Refunds are a fundamental part of the tax system for cross-border workers. These refunds allow recovering part of the taxes paid, thus reducing the overall tax burden. For many cross-border workers, refunds represent a significant amount that can make a difference in the family budget. The confirmation that this system will remain unchanged is therefore good news.

Frequently Asked Questions
What changes for 'old' cross-border workers in Ticino?
For 'old' cross-border workers in Ticino, there will be no changes in the tax regime. This means that withholding tax and refunds will remain unchanged. It's important to monitor any changes in Italian and Swiss regulations that could affect refunds and withholding tax.
How does withholding tax work for cross-border workers?
Withholding tax is a system where taxes are paid directly by the employer. This means that the cross-border worker does not need to worry about filing a tax return in Switzerland, greatly simplifying tax management. However, it's important to remember that cross-border workers must still declare their income in Italy, where they may benefit from certain deductions and benefits.
What are refunds and how can I obtain them?
Refunds are tax refunds that cross-border workers can obtain. These refunds allow you to recover part of the taxes paid, thus reducing the overall tax burden. To obtain refunds, you need to file a tax return in Italy and follow the procedures provided for by Italian law.
