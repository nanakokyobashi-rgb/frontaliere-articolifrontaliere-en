Context

Briefly

Operational details

The evolution of the Swiss labour market poses new practical challenges for those working in the sector and for candidates. The adoption of artificial intelligence represents a significant structural transformation: 79% of Swissstaffing member operators say they already use AI-based systems. The main objective is twofold: to increase internal efficiency and improve the quality of recruitment processes at a time when the number of applications for each vacancy has significantly increased. In some cases, companies receive more than 100 applications per single position, requiring specialized support in the evaluation of dossiers. For professionals looking for employment, this means that the selection has become more rigorous and based on advanced technological criteria, where the ability to present a profile consistent with digital requests is fundamental.

Opportunities and challenges for staff

Despite increasing automation, the figure of the staff provider remains central. If in the past the challenge was the shortage of labour, today the priority is the careful selection among a high volume of profiles. This change requires constant investments in technologies and in the reorganization of business processes. Key points

To orient yourself in this scenario, it is essential to understand how recruitment dynamics affect your job position. With a market that favors flexibility, those looking for a job in Switzerland must be ready to face increasingly digitized selection processes. Preparing your application dossier is the first operational step. The use of analysis tools to assess your contribution and tax situation, especially in view of temporary employment, is highly recommended to avoid surprises in the paycheck. It is important to remember that deductions, such as those for AVS or accident insurance, are calculated strictly based on the hours worked and the type of contract.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the trend of temporary work in Switzerland according to Swissstaffing?
In the first half of the year, temporary work in Switzerland recorded a 1.5% growth in hours worked compared to the same period of the previous year, interrupting a three-year downturn.
What is happening to open-ended contracts?
The segment of permanent placements has suffered a significant contraction, with a decrease of 19.4%, due to the growing economic and geopolitical uncertainty that pushes companies to be more cautious in fixed hiring.
What is the role of artificial intelligence in the industry?
79% of Swissstaffing operators already use artificial intelligence. This technology is mainly used to increase efficiency and improve recruitment processes, managing the increase in the volume of applications for vacancies.
What are the prospects for the next six months?
The CEOs of the companies affiliated with Swissstaffing remain cautiously optimistic: 33% of them expect an increase in activity in the next six months, although the actual development depends on the evolution of geopolitical events and the economic situation.
