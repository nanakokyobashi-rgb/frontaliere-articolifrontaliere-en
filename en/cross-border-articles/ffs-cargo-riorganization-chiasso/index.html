Lega seeks guarantees for Chiasso's railway presence and FFS Cargo jobs (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 21 May 2026

The Lega party is seeking guarantees for the continued presence of the railway in Chiasso and the preservation of jobs following the reorganization of FFS Cargo.

Context

In brief

  • The Lega/UDC/Independents has submitted an interpellation to the Chiasso Municipality expressing strong concern about the future of the city's railway presence and the safeguarding of jobs.
  • The text, signed by Claudio Schneeberger, highlights how Chiasso historically represents a strategic railway node for freight traffic, logistics, and international connections between Switzerland and Italy.
  • The Lega fears that the reorganization could nonetheless result in changes of location, function, or job for various employees active on the territory.

Key facts

  • What: Reorganization of FFS Cargo
  • When: Not specified
  • Where: Chiasso, Ticino
  • Who: FFS Cargo, Chiasso Municipality, Lega/UDC/Independents
  • Amount: Not specified

Operational details

The reorganization of FFS Cargo is causing concern among citizens and authorities in Chiasso. The text presented by Lega/UDC/Independents highlights the historical importance of the Chiasso railway hub for freight traffic and logistics, as well as for international connections between Switzerland and Italy. The main concern is that the reorganization could involve changes of location, function, or position for various employees active on the territory, with possible negative repercussions on employment and the strategic role of the city. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the concerns of Lega/UDC/Independents regarding the reorganization of FFS Cargo?
Lega/UDC/Independents express strong concern about the future of the railway presence in Chiasso and the safeguarding of jobs following the reorganization of FFS Cargo. They fear that the reorganization could still result in changes of location, function, or role for various employees active in the territory, with possible negative repercussions on employment and the strategic role of the City.
What actions does the Municipality of Chiasso intend to take to protect the jobs affected by the reorganization of FFS Cargo?
The Municipality intends to collaborate with the Council of State, with the municipalities of Mendrisio and with the social partners to protect the jobs affected by the reorganization of FFS Cargo. The concrete actions it intends to take in collaboration with these actors are not specified in the text of the source.
How can I get information and concrete guarantees about the future of the railway hub in Chiasso?
To get information and concrete guarantees about the future of the railway hub in Chiasso, the Municipality intends to request an urgent meeting with the management of FFS Cargo and FFS. If you are an interested citizen, you can contact the Municipality of Chiasso for information on the discussions held or planned with FFS Cargo, FFS, and higher authorities.
