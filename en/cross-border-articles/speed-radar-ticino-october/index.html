From Monday, October 19 to Sunday, October 25, 2026, the Cantonal Police, together with the municipal police forces, will conduct new speed checks on the roads of Canton Ticino. The published list concerns checks using mobile and semi-stationary radar units in numerous locations throughout the region.

The notice also concerns the routes used daily by Italian cross-border commuters traveling to work across the border, with checks planned in various areas. The locations listed include Stabio, Morbio Inferiore, Balerna, Lugano, Agno, Bioggio and Locarno. The notice also indicates checkpoints in the districts of Lugano, Mendrisio and Locarno.

The municipal police forces list Bellinzona, Cadenazzo, Carasso, Giubiasco, Lumino, Preonzo and Sementina. The list also includes Arcegno, Gerra Gambarogno, Locarno, Losone, Minusio, Orselina, Riazzino, Tegna, Verscio and Vogorno. Another group comprises Agno, Bioggio, Canobbio, Gravesano, Loreto, Lugano, Manno, Melide, Molino Nuovo and Sorengo. For the Cantonal Police, Capolago and Riva San Vitale are listed.

The purpose of the checks

The checks aim to prevent road accidents and raise motorists’ awareness of compliance with speed limits. The Cantonal Police reminds motorists that excessive speed is one of the main causes of accidents, which can have very serious or fatal consequences.

In addition to mobile radar units, semi-stationary checkpoints are planned in four locations during the week. The published list does not include checks conducted using unmarked cars and laser guns, which are used to combat serious violations of the Federal Road Traffic Act.
