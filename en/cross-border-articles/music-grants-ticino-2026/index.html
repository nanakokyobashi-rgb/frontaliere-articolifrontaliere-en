A call dedicated to music

In Bellinzona, the DECS is offering three scholarships of 20,000 francs each, intended to support artistic creation in the music sector. Through USC, he launched a call for original and unreleased musical projects; applications remain open until 12pm on 9 November 2026.

After extensive consultation with the various cultural sectors, the DECS adopted the cantonal cultural policy programmatic lines 2024-2027, in which direct support for artistic creation was among the objectives that emerged from that process. The announcement transforms that direction into a concrete intervention.

The financing of these projects was made possible by the entry into force, in January 2026, of the amendment to the Law on Support for Culture (LCult). The new regulation of the same law has also been operational since September 2026: together, the two stages define the framework for directly supporting artistic creation through the call.

The competition offers three contributions of 20,000 francs each. The candidate projects must combine original and unpublished content, coherence with the artistic path of the applicant and relevance with respect to the musical context of reference.

The decision on the assignment rests with the DECS. Before the decision is made, the process undergoes advance notice from a commission of industry experts coordinated by the USC. The text thus distinguishes the evaluation role of the commission from the decision-making role of the Department.

By 12.00 on 9 November 2026, the application must be sent via email to decs-sc@ti.ch; the wording "music announcement" must be inserted in the object. The full text of the announcement can be found online and was published in the Official Sheet no. 192 of 8 October 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who is promoting the call for music scholarships in Ticino and what is it for?
The call is promoted by the DECS, through the USC, and aims to support original and unpublished musical projects. This initiative is part of the Cantonal Cultural Policy Guidelines 2024-2027, adopted by the DECS to directly encourage artistic creation. The possibility of financing these projects has been made operational thanks to the amendment of the Law on the Support of Culture (LCult), which came into force in January 2026, and the new operating regulations from September 2026.
What is the amount of the scholarships and what are the requirements for the candidate projects?
The competition includes three contributions of 20,000 francs each. The projects must be original and unpublished, consistent with the applicant's artistic path and current with respect to the musical context of reference. These criteria ensure that the proposals are innovative and well integrated into the contemporary music scene, while enhancing the artistic experience of the proposer.
How and when can applications for music scholarships be submitted?
Applications must be sent by e-mail to decs-sc@ti.ch by 12:00 noon on 9 November 2026. It is essential to include the wording "music call" in the subject of the message. The complete call, with all the details and conditions, was published in the Official Gazette no. 192 of 8 October 2026 and can be consulted online for all interested parties.
Who evaluates the applications and makes the final decision on the awarding of the scholarships?
The final decision on the awarding of scholarships is up to the DECS. Before this decision, the file is examined by a commission of experts in the field. This commission, coordinated by the USC, provides advance notice of the proposals submitted, thus distinguishing the evaluation role of the commission from the decision-making role of the Department.
Does this call also offer opportunities for cross-border workers?
The call is a local cultural opportunity intended to support musical creation, it is not linked to tax, social security or cross-border work measures. The 20,000 francs are a contribution to the project, not a salary or social security item. For cross-border commuters, the opportunity consists of participating in a cultural initiative of the Canton of Ticino, evaluating the artistic criteria without attributing effects on the competition on their job or tax position.
