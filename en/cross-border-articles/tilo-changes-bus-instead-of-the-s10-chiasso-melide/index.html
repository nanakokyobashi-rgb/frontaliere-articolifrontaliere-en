TILO changes: bus instead of the S10 Chiasso-Melide (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 11 September 2026

From 13 September 2026, the 4:11 TILO ride from Mendrisio to Chiasso will be replaced by a bus, with early departure, until 19 October 2026.

Context

From Sunday September 13 2026, the Ticino railway network undergoes a partial but relevant modification for those traveling in the very early morning hours. According to the official communication from TILO, a specific connection on the S10 line will be replaced by buses between the stations of Chiasso and Melide. The affected train departs from Mendrisio at 4:11, a crucial service for shift workers and commuters who need to reach their workplaces in Italy or other parts of Ticino before dawn. The modification does not affect the entire S10 service, which links Biasca, Bellinzona, Lugano, Chiasso and Como, but a selected night‑and‑morning connection. The changes will affect some nights up to October 19, a limited period but long enough to impact the routine of those who must keep precise schedules for entering the workplace. TILO has specified that the substitute bus service features an earlier departure time compared to the normal rail connection. This means travelers must pay attention not only to the lengthening of travel times, typical of road transport on rail routes, but also to the different arrival time. The agency has urged users to check the updated timetable before each departure, via the online site or the FFS Mobile app, to avoid unpleasant surprises. The S10 line holds strategic importance for cross‑border mobility: it is one of the main arteries enabling the connection between Canton Ticino and Italy, facilitating the daily movements of numerous cross‑border workers who cross the border for work. The modification, although confined to very early morning slots, can have significant consequences for those working shifts or who must adhere to strict entry times at their Italian workplaces.

Operational details

The practical impact of this change on cross-border workers results in a series of necessary adaptations to their daily routine. Those who habitually use the 4:11 train from Mendrisio to reach workplaces in Italy, particularly those based in Chiasso or its immediate vicinity, will now have to deal with a road-based alternative. The replacement bus, although connecting the same points, generally requires longer travel times compared to the train, a factor that can mean arriving at the destination several minutes later than the usual schedule.

For shift workers, the effect is amplified: a delay of even 10-15 minutes in arriving in Chiasso can mean a delayed shift start or, in more critical cases, the need to request late entry permission from the employer. The source emphasizes how this time slot specifically affects that group of commuters who start their workday in the very early hours, when available schedule alternatives are more limited and flexibility of choice is minimal.

A particularly relevant aspect concerns the planning of the reverse evening or night journey. Those who must return from Chiasso to Mendrisio late in the evening may find themselves having to manage a delayed arrival time, with possible repercussions on rest hours or subsequent connections to their residences. The change, although temporary, therefore requires a reconsideration of the entire home-work-home route for those who use this line daily.

Key points

To better manage this temporary change, TILO recommends always checking the departure and arrival times before going to the station, especially on nights affected by the change of service. The simplest procedure is to open the SBB Mobile app or consult the TILO online time portal, where trips replaced by buses and changed times are clearly highlighted. It is advisable to carry out this check the night before or early in the morning, before leaving your residence.

For border crossers who regularly use the S10, it may be useful to consider timetable alternatives: departures slightly later but with a regular train, or the use of other public transport that connects Mendrisio to Chiasso without passing through Melide, depending on your final destination. In some cases, the advance of the bus could prove to be advantageous for those who have to arrive in advance of the standard working hours.

Please note that the changes are temporary and will end on October 19, 2026. After that date, the service will return to normal railway programming on the S10 line between Chiasso and Melide. For those who need more detailed information or assistance in planning their trip, the official TILO website and the information points at the stations remain available for clarification and support in choosing the transport solution that best suits their needs.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the start and end date of the replacement of the S10 train with buses between Chiasso and Melide?
The replacement starts on Sunday 13 September 2026 and ends on 19 October 2026. During this limited period, the specific morning run of the S10 line is carried out by bus instead of train, mainly affecting the very first hours of the day.
What specific trip is affected by the change and at what time does it start from Mendrisio?
The connection concerned is the train that leaves from Mendrisio at 4:11 in the morning, essential for shift workers and commuters who must reach jobs in Italy or in other areas of Ticino before dawn. This service is replaced by a bus with an earlier departure time than the normal rail connection.
How can cross-border commuters check updated schedules to avoid surprises?
TILO invites users to consult the updated timetable before each departure, via the online website or the SBB Mobile app. It is advisable to carry out this check the night before or early in the morning, before leaving your residence, to avoid unpleasant surprises related to changes of service or delays.
