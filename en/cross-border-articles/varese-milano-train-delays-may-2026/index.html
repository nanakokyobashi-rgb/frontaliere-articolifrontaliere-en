Delays on Varese-Milan trains from 5 to 11 May (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 May 2026

Trenord announces schedule changes and delays up to 3 minutes for commuters from Varesotto and the Milan area

Context

In brief

  • Delays on Varese-Milan trains from May 5 to 11
  • Delays up to 3 minutes on various lines
  • Technical work on the railway network

Key facts

  • What: Delays and disruptions on trains
  • When: From May 5 to 11, 2026
  • Where: Railway lines in the Varesotto area and the Milanese area
  • Who: Trenord
  • Amount: Delays up to 3 minutes

From May 5 to 11, 2026, commuters on the railway lines in the Varesotto area and the Milanese area will face delays and disruptions. Trenord has announced changes to the schedule due to work on the network, which will cause delays in arrival times. The changes will affect various lines used daily by commuters, including the S5 Treviglio-Varese and some regional connections.

In particular, trains on the "RE4" and "R23" lines (Domodossola-Milan), "R5" (Porto Ceresio-Varese-Gallarate-Milan), "R21" (Luino-Gallarate-Milan), and "S5" (Treviglio passing through Gallarate-Varese) are expected to have delays of up to 3 minutes compared to the scheduled times. The delays are due to technical interventions on the railway line, necessary to ensure the maintenance and improvement of the infrastructure.

Impact on Commuters

The changes in schedules, although minor, could affect the organization of daily commutes, especially during peak hours. Commuters who use these lines to reach their workplace or school should pay attention to the updated schedules and plan for possible delays. Trenord invites travelers to consult the official website and app, where schedules are updated in real-time, to verify any changes before departure.
