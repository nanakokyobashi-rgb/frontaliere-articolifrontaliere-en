Audience Analysis

The film, which was a cornerstone of the so-called “Disney Renaissance,” was praised for its articulate plot, but received criticism for its 35-millimeter version. Audiences found the original version blurry, grainy and confusing, especially when projected into an urban context with artificial lights that interfered with the image. This has led to discussions about how modern technology can enhance the traditional cinematic experience.

📊 Technical data : The resolution of 35 mm movies is about 2,000 lines per inch (lpi), while modern digital projections can reach up to 4,000 lpi. This means that images projected in 35 mm can appear blurry compared to modern digital representations.

💡 Practical example: During the pre-festival in Locarno, the projection of the film used a 4K digital projector, which significantly improved the image quality compared to the original 35 mm version. This has shown how modern technology can enhance the traditional cinematic experience.

In that case, the audience reported problems with image and sound quality, with some parts of the film being grainy and the volume oscillating unevenly.
