Salary tables in Switzerland are set by the Federal Government and may vary depending on the sector and workplace. For example, according to the 2022 salary table, a border dental assistant in Switzerland can earn between CHF 60'000 and CHF 90'000 per year, depending on experience and qualification.

Double taxation agreements, on the other hand, are agreements between the Swiss State and foreign States to avoid double taxation of income. For example, the Double Taxation Convention between Switzerland and Italy (ratified on 22 February 2007) states that the income of Italians working in Switzerland will be taxed only in Switzerland, unless they are resident in Italy.
