While Switzerland recorded a 5.2% decline in tourist overnight stays in March 2026, Ticino stood out with a 3.8% increase. This data, made public by the Federal Statistical Office (FSO), represents a significant countertrend compared to the rest of the country. In detail, Ticino recorded 133,800 overnight stays, a figure that contrasts with the national average of 3.2 million overnight stays, down 5.2% compared to the previous year.

Analysis of the national decline

The national decline was influenced by several factors, including the war in the Middle East and a downturn anticipated by initial estimates. Overseas guests recorded a -4.8%, while domestic demand decreased by 5.7%. The most affected regions were the Grisons (-13.7%), Bern (-8.2%), Lucerne (-5.1%), Valais (-4.6%), and Zurich (-2.7%).

Focus on Ticino

Ticino, on the other hand, recorded a 3.8% increase in overnight stays, a figure worth noting. This increase could be linked to various factors, including the political stability and security of the canton, which continue to attract tourists despite the complex international context.

What to do for cross-border workers

For cross-border workers interested in working in the hotel or catering sector in Ticino, it is important to follow some specific procedures. First of all, it is necessary to obtain a G permit, which allows you to work in Switzerland for a determined period. This permit can be requested at the competent cantonal office or through the Ticino Canton online portal.

Step-by-step to obtain the G permit

1. Verify the specific requirements for the hotel or catering sector. 2. Contact the competent cantonal office to obtain the G permit application form. 3. Fill out the form and attach the required documents, such as the employment contract and the certificate of employment. 4. Submit the application to the cantonal office and wait for the response. 5. Once you have obtained the permit, you can start working in Switzerland.

Deadlines and required documents

It is important to respect the deadlines for the G permit application and to ensure that you have all the necessary documents. Among these, the employment contract, the certificate of employment, and a valid passport. Furthermore, it is necessary to verify the specific requirements for the sector, such as knowledge of the local languages and the required professional skills.
