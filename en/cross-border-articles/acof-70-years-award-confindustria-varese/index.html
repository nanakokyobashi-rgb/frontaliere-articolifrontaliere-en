In 2026 ACOF Olga Fiorini celebrates her 70 years and receives recognition from Confindustria Varese. The parchment celebrates the anniversary and thanks those who carry on the educational adventure born with Olga Fiorini.

Paolo Praderio, operational director of the Servizi Confindustria Varese company, went to the ACOF headquarters in via Varzi, in Busto Arsizio, to pay homage to ACOF, which he defined as "a precious excellence for the territory". The initiative took place on behalf of the association of industrialists of the province, with the president Luigi Galdabini, the past president Roberto Grassi and the general director Silvia Pagani.

Confindustria Varese also recognized the contribution of Mauro and Cinzia Ghisellini, directors of the school structure, and of Sergio Scaltritti, manager of the training area and member of the Board of Directors. The recognition combines the history of ACOF with the commitment of those who accompany its path today.

From the school for seamstresses to a complex reality

The experience desired by Olga Fiorini began as a simple school for seamstresses. Over time ACOF has grown in many directions, to the point of today representing a cornerstone of the Italian school system. The structure works across 20 locations, with 3,500 students and almost 800 employees and collaborators.

Director Mauro Ghisellini links the award to the commitment to growing and educating young people and to the service offered to many Varese companies. Training, in his explanation, prepares young people who are then sent to companies with the necessary skills.

For director Mauro Ghisellini, the recognition represents a source of enormous pride: it demonstrates both the commitment to helping young people grow and educate and the service offered to many Varese companies.

The school's message is therefore linked both to education and to the relationship with local businesses. For ACOF, the recognition confirms the spirit with which the project was started by Olga Fiorini.
