In brief

Key Facts

  • What: New Cross-Border Agreement
  • When: January 1, 2024
  • Where: Ticino, Italy
  • Who: Cross-Border Commuters
  • Amount: Allowance €10'000

Moving from Pianello del Lario to Ticino is an increasingly common choice among Italian workers. The geographical proximity and tax advantages make this option attractive. However, there are several aspects to consider before making a decision.

Connections and Travel Times

Pianello del Lario is well-connected to Ticino via various roads and highways. Travel times vary depending on the chosen mode of transport and traffic conditions. By car, the journey can take about 30-45 minutes, while public transport can take up to an hour.

Cost of Living

The cost of living in Pianello del Lario is generally lower than in many Swiss locations. However, it is important to consider additional costs related to commuting, such as fuel, tolls, and public transport tickets.

Recommended Areas

Pianello del Lario offers several residential areas suitable for cross-border commuters. The most popular areas are those close to major road connections and public transport. It is advisable to choose an area that also offers essential services such as schools, shops, and healthcare facilities.

Advantages and Disadvantages of Moving

Among the advantages of moving are lower living costs and the opportunity to live in a quieter environment compared to Swiss cities. However, there are also disadvantages, such as travel times and additional commuting costs.

Practical Analysis

The practical analysis of the implications for cross-border commuters reveals several aspects to consider. Travel times and additional costs related to commuting can significantly affect the quality of life. However, the lower cost of living in Pianello del Lario can offset these disadvantages.

Before/After Comparison

Before the new cross-border agreement, commuters benefited from a tax exemption of €7,500. With the new agreement in effect, new commuters will have a tax exemption of €10,000, while existing commuters will retain the previous exemption with a transitional regime until 2033.

Concrete Scenarios

A commuter in a similar situation could benefit from the new cross-border agreement, thereby reducing the tax burden. However, it is important to consider the additional costs related to commuting and choose the exemption that best suits your needs.

Implications for Commuters

The implications of the new cross-border agreement for commuters are significant. The increased exemption can reduce the tax burden, but it is important to consider the additional costs related to commuting and choose the exemption that best suits your needs.
