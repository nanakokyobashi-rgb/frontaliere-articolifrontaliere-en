December 23, 2020 is the date of signature of the New Cross-Border Agreement. The agreement entered into force on 1 January 2024 and was ratified in Italy with Law 83 of 13 June 2023. For the cross-border worker who changes employer in 2026, the procedure to be monitored combines four steps: respecting the notice, managing the renewal of the G permit, maintaining continuity of contributions and updating the documents. They are the four operating points of the corner of this guide; it is therefore not enough to look at the new salary.

The tax framework that accompanies the change

Tax on earned income is withheld in Switzerland for cross-border workers. Italy avoids double taxation with the tax credit indicated in the EC framework of 730. The Italy-Switzerland Double Taxation Convention was signed on 9 March 1976; Switzerland is not a member of the EU or EEA.

The rule reported does not present a withholding in both countries: it indicates only the Swiss withholding and the Italian tax credit. In the lexicon of the Ticino-Italy relationship the term rebates also appears; here the verified tax rule is that of the Swiss withholding tax and the Italian credit.

The tax distinction to be kept in the documentation is that between old and new cross-border workers. New cross-border commuters have an excess of €10,000. The time reference indicated is 17 July 2023, while the change of employer is the procedure to be managed in 2026.

The operational photography is therefore double: working document, with G permit and notice; tax and social security framework, with withholding tax, AVS and LPP. The cross-border worker who works in the Canton of Ticino must update the first data without losing sight of the second, even if the daily journey crosses a pass like Brogeda. The change of employer falls within this framework: 2026 is the year of the procedure, not that of a new entry into force of the agreement.

Who administers the withdrawal

Tax rates, including federal and cantonal ones, are established by federal and cantonal laws. At the federal level they are administered by AFC/ESTV, while for the cantonal level the cantonal tax administrations intervene. UFAS, which concerns social security and AVS/AI, does not set tax rates; the same goes for BFS, which collects statistical data.

For the Italian IRPEF from 2026, the indicated framework is 23% up to €28,000, 33% from €28,001 to €50,000 and 43% over €50,000, according to Law 199/2025, art. 1 c. 3. For 2024–2025 income, the rates reported are 23%, 35%, and 43%. The comparison between years must be kept separate from the Swiss withholding tax.

KVG is a separate chapter

KVG is health insurance, not a health tax. Cross-border workers with a G permit have the right of option; for adults, the indicated deductibles range from CHF 300 to CHF 2,500. When changing employer, this item must be kept separate from tax withholdings and AVS/LPP social security: they are different chapters of the cross-border worker's documentation.

Key points

The change of employer is best handled as a documentary sequence, not as a single update at the end. The practical track must link the notice, the G permit, the contributions and the tax and health documentation.

Operation Sequence

1. Line up the dates of the ending report and the new report. Enter the notice period provided by the job and keep the documents linked to both phases. The sequence must make the passage between output and input legible.

2. Update the permit file G. Treats the renewal of the document together with the change of employer and verifies that the data of the new report is consistent in the documentation to be updated. Check the name of the employer and the dates on the documents.

3. Protect contributory continuity. Compare the final payroll of the previous relationship with the first of the new employment, looking for the items AVS/AI/IPG, AD/AC, LAINF and LPP. For social security reading, you can add the calcolatore AVS/LPP to the verification of documents.

4. Please double-check your tax perimeter. New cross-border workers have a €10,000 IRPEF allowance, while old cross-border workers remain taxed only in Switzerland (Art. 9 of the 2020 Agreement). for new ones, the excess of €10,000 is indicated. It also verifies that the source tax remains read together with the 730 CE framework tax credit, without a withholding tax in both countries.

For taxation, the administrative reference indicated is AFC/ESTV at the federal level and the cantonal tax administrations; not UFAS or BFS.

Frequently Asked Questions
Is the frontier worker's income taxed in both countries?
No. The rule reported provides for withholding tax to be withheld at source in Switzerland. Italy avoids double taxation with the 730 CE framework tax credit. The Italy-Switzerland Double Taxation Convention was signed on 9 March 1976. The change of employer requires you to double-check tax documents, but the source does not introduce a second Italian withholding tax on the same income.
Who is an old frontier worker and what deductible applies?
Old frontier workers are those who were already such before July 17, 2023. Old cross-border workers remain taxed only in Switzerland (Art. 9 of the 2020 Agreement). The new frontier workers, on the other hand, have a deductible of €10,000. The distinction must therefore be verified with respect to the date indicated, not replaced with the date of the change of employer in 2026.
What rates to check on the new paycheck?
The source indicates AVS/AI/IPG at 5.3% for the employee and AD/AC at 1.1%, with a cap of CHF 148'200. It also reports LAINF at 0.7–1.5% and LPP at 7–18% by age group from the 25th year. This data is used to compare the items of the previous report and the new employment, without automatically adding them together as if they were a single deduction.
Is LAMal a tax linked to the change of employer?
LAMal is health insurance, not a health tax. Border workers with a G permit have the right of option. For adults, the deductibles shown are between CHF 300 and CHF 2,500. In the control of the change of employer, the LAMal item must be kept separate from the tax at source and the AVS/LPP contributions, because the source presents them as distinct areas.
