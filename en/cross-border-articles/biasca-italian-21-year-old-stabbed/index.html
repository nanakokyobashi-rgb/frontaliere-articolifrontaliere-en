Ticino, 21-year-old Italian stabbed: his life is at risk

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 5 October 2026

In Biasca, a 21-year-old Italian citizen residing in the Leventina district was seriously stabbed: his life is in danger.

Context

TL;DR

  • Request for assistance shortly before 15:00
  • Man stabbed on Via Bellinzona in Biasca
  • Victim: 21-year-old Italian man residing in Leventina
  • The young man's life is in danger

Shortly before 15:00, the Joint Alarm Center, known by the acronym Cecal, received a request for assistance. The report concerned a man who had been seriously stabbed on Via Bellinzona in Biasca. The Cantonal Police reported this.

Officers from the Cantonal Police and the Tre Valli Police responded at the scene. Tre Valli Soccorso rescuers also worked with them, providing first aid to the young man. The injured person is a 21-year-old Italian citizen residing in the Leventina district.

According to the doctors, the young man is in serious condition and his life is in danger. The Cantonal Police statement did not accompany this assessment with any other clinical data.

The Cantonal Police said that investigations are underway to clarify the causes, circumstances and responsibility relating to what happened. The statement also specifies that, at present, no further information will be provided.

Operational details

The statement makes it possible to follow the progression from the request for assistance to the presence at the scene, without providing a reconstruction of the stabbing. The request reached Cecal; officers and rescuers then arrived at the indicated location.

The presence of the Cantonal Police and the Tre Valli Police is indicated by the official note. For assistance to the injured person, however, Tre Valli Soccorso is cited: its rescuers provided first aid to the 21-year-old. The source does not attribute any other duties to the various responders and does not describe any further stages of the assistance.

The geographical references also remain limited. The scene of the intervention is Via Bellinzona, in Biasca; the victim is stated to reside in the Leventina district. The statement also identifies the boy as an Italian citizen. These are the available details about the person and the area involved.

The clinical assessment reported comes from the doctors: the condition is serious and the young man's life is in danger. The communication adds no other medical data beyond this assessment and the first aid provided by the rescuers.

Key points

The next steps indicated by the Cantonal Police consist of the inquiries already under way. The stated objective is to clarify three aspects: the causes of what happened, how it unfolded, and the responsibilities relating to the incident. The note does not anticipate conclusions on any of these aspects.

At the same time, an operation has been launched to search for the perpetrator or perpetrators. The wording used by the Cantonal Police leaves both possibilities open, without providing an identity or indicating a number of people being sought. No further details are communicated on this point either.

For now, therefore, the official indication remains limited to the inquiries and searches under way. The Cantonal Police specified that no further information will be provided at the moment. The note communicates neither the outcome of the search nor a reconstruction of the incident; it merely confirms which checks have been initiated and which responsibilities still need to be clarified.
