Fribourg thrives on hockey like Ticino: 2026 World Cup at BCF Arena

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 May 2026

Fribourg will host the 2026 hockey World Cup with 30 Group B matches. John Gobbi: '80,000 people for the title celebration'

Context

In brief

  • Fribourg to host the 2026 hockey World Championship at BCF Arena
  • 80,000 people for Gottéron's title celebration
  • 200,000-250,000 expected attendees during the tournament

Key facts

  • What: 2026 hockey World Championship
  • When: 2026
  • Where: BCF Arena, Fribourg
  • Who: Gottéron, John Gobbi (CEO)
  • Amount: 200,000-250,000 expected attendees

Fribourg is preparing for an unforgettable year. After the historic title of Gottéron, the city will host the 2026 hockey World Championship at the BCF Arena. The privately-owned venue will host Group B of the world championship, with teams like Canada, Sweden, Czechia, and Slovakia. John Gobbi, CEO of Gottéron, described an unprecedented enthusiasm: '80,000 people gathered in the city for the title celebration, with over 30,000 in front of the rink.'

Economic and sporting impact

The new arena played a crucial role in the club's success. 'None of this would have been possible without the new rink, neither in terms of atmosphere nor economically,' said Gobbi. The structure allowed reinvestment in the company, improving the first team, youth sector, and women's movement. Ticket sales are expected to bring between 200,000 and 250,000 attendees to the canton during the tournament, a significant economic boost for the region.

The Swiss National Team

Gobbi also commented on the situation of the Swiss National Team, which approached the World Championship in a problematic way. 'I don't think it will leave any traces,' he said, emphasizing the solidity of the group and the very high level of the Swiss championship. The team, with nine survivors of the final and four new NHLers, is ready to compete even without Fischer.

Implications for cross-border workers

For cross-border workers who commute to Ticino and live in Italy, the event represents a unique opportunity to experience the atmosphere of a major international tournament. The geographical proximity and ease of access to Switzerland make Fribourg an accessible destination for a weekend of sports and entertainment. Additionally, the event could have a positive impact on the local economy, with an increase in demand for services and accommodations.

Procedure for purchasing tickets

Tickets for the 2026 hockey World Championship will be available online on the official website of the BCF Arena. It is advisable to book in advance to secure the best seats. For cross-border workers, it is important to verify any travel restrictions or required documents for entry into Switzerland during the event.

Comparison with Ticino

Ticino is known for its passion for hockey, with a strong sporting tradition and a community of passionate fans. The main difference lies in the international dimension of the tournament, which attracts teams and fans from all over the world.
