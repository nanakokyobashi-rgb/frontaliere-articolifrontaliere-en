La costruzione della Rete Tram-Treno sta per iniziare. Lunedì mattina davanti alla stampa è stato annunciato che nella seconda metà di ottobre si comincerà con i muri di controriva sulla tratta Bioggio-Manno. Nei primi mesi dell’anno prossimo toccherà al pozzo di collegamento alla stazione ferroviaria a Besso e al portale lato ovest della galleria di Breganzona alla Crespera.

In pratica è la prima parte del progetto: una galleria di oltre due chilometri che collega con il centro città di Lugano. I cantieri non sono invisibili e quindi la popolazione sarà informata, ha assicurato Alberto Del Col, direttore della società Rete Tram Treno.

«Con cantieri di queste dimensioni – ha dichiarato al Radiogiornale della RSI - è fondamentale continuare a tenere curato quello che è il rapporto con tutti i coinvolti sia contrattualmente sia le autorità, ma poi anche e soprattutto la popolazione, perché la popolazione, se è vero che un domani avrà i vantaggi che porta un progetto di questo tipo, è anche vero che nel corso dei cantieri, chi più chi meno, si accorgerà di quello che succede e quindi è giusto che sia informata e giusto che sappia quali sono le misure che mettiamo in atto per restare entro i parametri di legge».

Operational details

Impact on cross-border commuters

The works for the Tram-Train Network will have a significant impact on the daily life of cross-border commuters who use this route to travel between Italy and Switzerland. The Tram-Train Network company has assured that measures will be taken to limit inconveniences and that the population will be informed about the procedures and timelines of the works.

Measures to limit inconveniences

The Tram-Train Network company has stated that it will be crucial to continue maintaining a good relationship with all stakeholders, both contractually and with the authorities, but especially with the population. While the population will benefit from this project in the future, it is also true that during the construction phase, everyone will notice the changes. Therefore, it is right that they are informed and know the measures we are taking to stay within legal parameters.

Work procedure

The works will begin with the counter-river walls on the Bioggio-Manno section in the second half of October. In the first months of next year, the connection shaft to the Besso railway station and the west portal of the Breganzona-Crespera tunnel will be proceeded with. These are the first steps of a project aimed at connecting the city center of Lugano with a tunnel of over two kilometers.

Cost and timelines

The total cost of the project is 760 million francs, and the goal is to deliver the work by 2035. So far, ten mandates have been awarded for a total amount of nearly 45 million francs. The project approval procedures were completed in June with the Federal Court's ruling, which denied the suspensive effect to the only pending appeal.

Key points

Concrete actions for cross-border commuters

Cross-border commuters using the Bioggio-Manno route must be aware of the ongoing work and the measures taken to limit inconveniences. It is important to follow the instructions of the Rete Tram Treno company and to regularly inform themselves about the procedures and timelines of the work.

Work procedure

The work will begin with the counter-river walls on the Bioggio-Manno route in the second half of October. In the first months of next year, the connection shaft to the Besso railway station and the western portal of the Breganzona to Crespera tunnel will be proceeded with. These are the first steps of a project aimed at connecting the city center of Lugano with a tunnel of over two kilometers.

Cost and timelines

The total cost of the project is 760 million francs, and the goal is to deliver the work by 2035. So far, ten mandates have been awarded for a total amount of nearly 45 million francs. The project approval procedures were completed in June with the Federal Court ruling that denied the suspensive effect to the only pending appeal.

Political figures involved

A political figure was missing from the press conference. The responsibilities of the Department of Territory have been removed from State Councillor Claudio Zali and are now in the hands of his Government colleague Raffaele De Rosa. However, Diego Rodoni, director of the Construction Division at the Department of Territory, assured that the procedures have been followed correctly and that the technicians have continued to work independently of the political situations.
