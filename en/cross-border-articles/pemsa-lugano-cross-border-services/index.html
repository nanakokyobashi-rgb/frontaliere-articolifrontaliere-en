Frequently Asked Questions
What is the role of an agency like Pemsa in Lugano?
Pemsa works in the placement and management of personnel, facilitating the meeting between candidates and companies in Canton Ticino, ensuring compliance with local regulations and collective agreements.
How is taxation for border workers managed?
Border crossers are subject to tax at source in Switzerland. To avoid double taxation, it is necessary to declare income in Italy using the tax credit in the EC framework of the model 730.
Is the G permit necessary to work through an agency?
Yes, the G permit is mandatory for workers residing in Italy and working in the Canton of Ticino. The request is forwarded by the employer to the cantonal authorities.
