Living in Re and working in Ticino as a border worker

The New Frontier Agreement introduced new rules for frontier workers working in Switzerland. Among the most important new features are the tax exemptions, which allow frontier workers to avoid paying taxes on income from work in Switzerland. This means that frontier workers can maintain a higher income without having to pay taxes in Switzerland.

For example, if a frontier worker works in Lugano and has an income of CHF 80,000 per year, he can benefit from the tax exemption and not pay taxes in Switzerland. Instead, you may have to pay taxes in Italy, but only on income above CHF 15,000 per year.

The tax exemption is applied to frontier workers who work in Switzerland and have a working income of at least CHF 15,000 per year. Border workers can benefit from the tax exemption even if they work in different cantons of Switzerland, but they must reside in Italy.

How the transitional regime works

Border workers can benefit from a transitional regime until 2033, during which they can continue to work in Switzerland without having to pay income taxes. This scheme was introduced to help border workers maintain their residence in Switzerland and continue to work in the sector.

Concrete examples

Let's imagine we have a border worker who works in Lugano and earns CHF 80,000 per year. Thanks to the New Frontier Agreement, this frontier worker will not have to pay income taxes in Switzerland, thus saving CHF 18'000 per year (CHF 80'000 - CHF 10'000 deductible).

Operational Checklists

To benefit from the transitional regime, frontier workers must:

  • Have a residence in Switzerland
  • Working in the sector
  • Earn less than CHF 100,000 per year
  • Have no other sources of income in Switzerland

Key points

If you are a border worker working in Ticino, you should know that the New Border Agreement has introduced new rules for border workers working in Switzerland.

To benefit from this scheme, you must be in possession of a G or B permit and have worked in Switzerland for at least six months. This means that if you are a frontier worker working in a company in Lugano and reside in Bellinzona, you can benefit from this allowance if you have the G permit and have worked for six months.

Tax exemptions are a very advantageous scheme for frontier workers, as they allow them to avoid paying income taxes in Switzerland. For example, if a frontier worker earns CHF 60,000 a year, they may not pay labour income taxes in Switzerland thanks to this scheme.

To benefit from this scheme, you must be in possession of a G or B permit and have worked in Switzerland for at least six months. In addition, you must be in possession of an employment contract that has been registered with the labour office of the Canton of Ticino. The employment contract must be registered within 15 days from the date of commencement of work.
