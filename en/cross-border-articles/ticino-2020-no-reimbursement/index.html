The Government Announcement

On 10 March 2020, the State Council announced the abandonment of the Ticino 2020 project, which envisaged the implementation of new technologies and public services in the Canton of Ticino. The project had been financed with an amount of CHF 10 million, divided among the Municipalities of the Canton.

No Refunds for Municipalities

Despite requests for compensation from the Municipalities, the government has decided not to provide any reimbursement for the money spent on the project. According to the sources, the government claimed that the responsibility for the failure of the project is to be attributed to both parties.

Concrete examples

The Municipality of Lugano, for example, had invested CHF 1.5 million in the Ticino 2020 project, while the Municipality of Bellinzona had invested CHF 1 million. Despite these figures, the government has not decided to provide any reimbursement to the Municipalities.

Regulations and amounts

The Ticino 2020 project law was approved by the State Council on 15 January 2020, with an amount of CHF 10 million.

Operational details

The government's announcement was greeted with surprise by the municipalities that had invested money in the Ticino 2020 project. The Municipalities had asked for compensation for the money spent, but the government replied that the responsibility is on both sides.

The government's decision created a bad mood among the representatives of the Ticino Municipalities. "We were surprised by this decision," said the mayor of Lugano, Michele Foletti. "We have invested public money in the Ticino 2020 project and now we do not know how to recover our money."

The Ticino 2020 project was presented in 2015 as a plan to develop the economy of the Canton of Ticino. The plan included the creation of tourist infrastructure, the creation of new jobs and the increase in the competitiveness of the local economy. However, the project has been criticised for the lack of clarity and transparency in the management of the funds.

According to estimates, Ticino Municipalities have invested over 50 million francs in the Ticino 2020 project. The Municipality of Lugano invested about 15 million francs, while the Municipality of Bellinzona invested about 10 million francs. However, the government replied that the responsibility is on both sides and that the Municipalities are not entitled to compensation.

Key points

The Municipalities of Ticino, such as those of Lugano and Bellinzona, can seek compensation through the law for damage caused by the maintenance and repair of roads and public infrastructure. However, the process can be long and complicated, so it is important to consult an attorney for accurate and personalized information.

According to the Ticino law of 2018, local authorities can seek compensation for damages from those who were responsible for the accident or damage. However, the law also provides that local authorities must prove the responsibility of the other party.

A concrete example is the case of the Municipality of Lugano, which in 2019 requested compensation of 500,000 francs from workers who had caused an accident during road maintenance work. However, the court decided not to grant the request, as the workers' liability had not been proven.

Another example is the case of the Municipality of Bellinzona, which in 2020 requested compensation of 200,000 francs from motorway dealers who had caused an accident during maintenance work. However, the court decided not to grant the request, as the responsibility of the dealers had not been proven.

What was the objective of the Ticino 2020 project?
The Ticino 2020 project, presented in 2015, aimed to develop the economy of the Canton of Ticino. It envisaged the implementation of new technologies, public services and the creation of tourism infrastructure, with the aim of creating new jobs and increasing competitiveness. It was abandoned on 10 March 2020.
How much have the municipalities of Ticino invested in the Ticino 2020 project?
The municipalities of Ticino have invested a total of over 50 million francs in the Ticino 2020 project. For example, the Municipality of Lugano has allocated about 15 million francs, Bellinzona about 10 million and Biasca about 5 million, the latter equal to 20% of its budget.
Why was the Ticino 2020 project criticised?
The Ticino 2020 project has been criticised mainly for the lack of clarity and transparency in the management of funds. This situation has generated anger among the representatives of the Municipalities and the citizens, especially after the government's decision not to repay the investments, attributing the responsibility to both parties.
