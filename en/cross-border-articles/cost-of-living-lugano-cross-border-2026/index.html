Living in Lugano as a cross-border worker involves a series of costs that are fundamental to know before making a decision. Data updated to 2026 shows that the average rent for an apartment in the city is around 1,800 CHF per month. This amount can vary depending on the area and the size of the apartment, but it is a good starting point for those considering moving.

Public transport in Lugano is efficient and convenient, with a monthly subscription costing around 70 CHF. This includes unlimited access to buses, trams, and local trains, making it easy to move around without having to depend on a car.

Mandatory health insurance according to LAMal is another significant cost. For a cross-border worker, basic insurance can cost around 300 CHF per month, depending on the provider and the chosen plan. It is essential to choose insurance that covers your specific needs, especially if you have a family.

The monthly food expenses for one person can vary, but on average, they amount to around 600 CHF. This includes both basic products and some luxury items. Shopping in Switzerland can be more expensive compared to Italy, but the quality of the products is generally very high.

Rent and accommodation

Rent is one of the most significant costs for those who decide to live in Lugano. A two-room apartment in the city center can cost between 1,500 and 2,000 CHF per month, while a larger apartment or one in a more peripheral area can be slightly cheaper. It is important to also consider additional costs such as utilities, which can add another 150-200 CHF per month.

Public transport

Public transport in Lugano is very efficient and covers the entire city and its surroundings. A monthly subscription for public transport costs around 70 CHF, but there are also weekly or daily options if the service is used less frequently. For those who work in Switzerland, it might be convenient to consider a combined subscription that includes public transport in Italy as well.

Health insurance

Health insurance is mandatory in Switzerland and must be subscribed within three months of arrival in the country. The average cost of basic insurance according to LAMal is around 300 CHF per month, but it can vary depending on the provider and the chosen plan. It is possible to choose between different levels of coverage, depending on your needs and available budget.

Food expenses

Food expenses in Lugano can be more costly compared to Italy, but the quality of the products is generally very high. A monthly budget of around 600 CHF should be sufficient for one person, but it can vary depending on eating habits and personal preferences. It is possible to save money by shopping at local supermarkets or taking advantage of weekly offers.

Other expenses

In addition to the main costs, there are other expenses to consider when living in Lugano. For example, car insurance can cost between 100 and 200 CHF per month, depending on the vehicle and the provider. Expenses for leisure time, such as cinema, restaurants, and sports activities, can also impact the monthly budget. It is important to plan these expenses in advance to avoid surprises.

Conclusion

Living in Lugano as a cross-border worker can be a rewarding experience, but it is fundamental to know the associated costs. With good planning and careful budget management, it is possible to enjoy all the advantages of living in this beautiful city without financial stress.

Key points

What to do to move to Lugano

Moving to Lugano as a cross-border worker involves several bureaucratic and practical steps. Here's a step-by-step guide to help you organize your move effectively.

1. Obtain the residence permit

The first step to moving to Lugano is obtaining the residence permit. This document is necessary for living and working in Switzerland. You can request it at the cantonal migration office of Canton Ticino. Make sure you have all the necessary documents, including your passport, work contract, and proof of residence.

2. Find accommodation

Once you have obtained the residence permit, it's important to find accommodation in Lugano. You can search for rental apartments online or through local real estate agencies. Make sure to visit the apartment before signing the contract and verify that all conditions are clear and transparent.

3. Subscribe to health insurance

Health insurance is mandatory in Switzerland and must be subscribed to within three months of arriving in the country. You can choose from different providers and insurance plans. It's important to compare the available options and choose the one that best suits your needs and budget.

4. Open a bank account

Opening a bank account in Switzerland is essential for managing your finances. You can do this at a local bank or an online bank. Make sure you have all the necessary documents, including your passport, residence permit, and proof of address.

5. Register with the municipality

Once you have moved to Lugano, it's important to register with the municipality of residence. This step is necessary to obtain the proof of residence and to access municipal services. You can do this at the municipal registry office.

6. Subscribe to public services

Finally, it's important to subscribe to public services such as electricity, gas, and water. You can do this with local providers or online. Make sure to compare the rates and conditions offered by different providers to find the most convenient solution.
