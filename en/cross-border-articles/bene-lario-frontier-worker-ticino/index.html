Bene Lario is one of the favourite destinations for those who decide to work in Ticino while maintaining their residence in Lombardy. The choice to move from frontier involves a series of economic and bureaucratic considerations that revolve around the tax regime in force. The main innovation of recent years concerns the application of the New Frontier Agreement , signed on 23 December 2020 and operational from 1 January 2024 with Italian ratification through Law 83 of 13 June 2023.

The regulatory change represents a turning point for those considering relocation to this area of the province of Como. The agreement redefines how taxation is calculated on the paycheck of workers who reside in Italy but serve in Switzerland. For new work frontiersmen

Operational details

Moving to Bene Lario: advantages and disadvantages

The choice to reside in Bene Lario while working in Ticino involves a series of aspects that go beyond pure tax convenience. The border community already settled in this area highlights some practical advantages related to geography and quality of life, along with challenges that must be considered realistically.

From a housing point of view, Bene Lario offers a lower cost of living than that of the Canton of Ticino in several sectors: rent, purchase of real estate, catering services and commerce. Those who choose to live in Italy and work in Switzerland capitalise on this difference, reinvesting the savings margin in the family budget or in long-term project capital (AVS/LPP social security savings, third pillar 3a).

Cross-border connections to Ticino are a decisive element. Bene Lario is located in a position that allows you to reach various crossings and work destinations: the territory can be reached from the main road axes towards the Italian border (A9, Lario state road). Travel times to the main Ticino work centres vary depending on the specific destination, the vehicle used and the traffic conditions at the crossings (Chiasso, Brogeda, Gaggiolo, Ponte Tresa). In the summer season and during peak periods, it is advisable to estimate an increase in transit times.

Key points

Procedure: How to Start Cross-Border Commuting from Bene Lario to Ticino Anyone deciding to move to Italy as a resident and accept a job offer in Ticino must follow a sequence of administrative formalities. The critical points concern the work permit, Swiss tax registration, enrollment in pension funds, and health insurance. To work in Switzerland, an Italian citizen needs a Permit G (cross-border) or, alternatively, Permit B (residence with work rights). Permit G is issued by the Canton where the work is performed (e.g., Lugano, Mendrisio, Bellinzona depending on the company). The procedure requires a signed job offer from the company, submission of the application through the employer to the cantonal migration office (SEM), and issuance of the permit within a few weeks. Permit G authorizes cross-border work while maintaining residence in Italy. ### Tax Registration and Contributions Once hired and in possession of the permit, the cross-border worker is automatically enrolled in the Swiss pension system. AVS (old-age and survivors' insurance) and AI (disability insurance) are mandatory: the deduction is 5.3% of the gross salary for the employee. AD (unemployment insurance) covers the risk of job loss with an additional 1.1% deduction up to the annual ceiling (CHF 148,200). LAINF (occupational accident insurance) is the employer's responsibility and covers work-related incidents. Finally, LPP (professional pension insurance / second pillar) varies between 7% and 18% of the salary depending on the age class (obligatory from the 25th birthday) and the company's pension plan. On the Italian side, income from Switzerland is declared through the model 730 or RdC (category income), with the application of the €10,000 exemption for new cross-border workers and the deduction of the tax credit for withholding taxes already paid to the Canton. INPS and the Revenue Agency manage, respectively, the verification of Italian social security contributions (minimum integration) and the correct application of the 2024 Border Workers Agreement. ### Choosing Health Insurance Health insurance is a crucial step. In Switzerland, enrollment in a LAMal (Federal Law on Health Insurance) is mandatory: it is coverage with a deductible (from CHF 300 to CHF 2,500 for adults) and variable monthly premiums according to the company and risk. Cross-border workers have the right to choose: they can opt to remain enrolled in the Italian healthcare system (SSN + private supplementary insurance) or switch to Swiss LAMal. The choice affects access to services, delivery times, direct costs, and the network of usable facilities. To proceed effectively, it is advisable to: - Request from the Swiss employer all necessary documentation for registration with federal authorities (employment declaration, company data, name of the SUVA intermediary if present). - Open a bank account with a bank operating in both countries (CHF and EUR) to simplify payroll and recurring payment management. - Evaluate the convenience between Swiss LAMal and Italian supplementary insurance with the support of an intermediary familiar with both systems. - Verify with the Italian municipality of residence the registration as a foreign resident and communication with the Guardia di Finanza for compliance with declarative obligations. - Consult the salary calculator to simulate the net paycheck and verify the impact of the €10,000 exemption on the annual declaration. Moving as a cross-border worker from Bene Lario to Ticino is an economically advantageous choice for many, but it requires careful planning regarding taxes, permits, social security, and healthcare. Having clear documentation from the start and following the scheduled administrative timelines significantly reduces stress and costs during the transition.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between Permit G and Permit B for those who work in Ticino?
The G Permit (border) allows work in Switzerland while maintaining residence in Italy, without the obligation to move. Permit B requires actual residence in Switzerland. Those who choose Bene Lario as a residence almost always opt for the G Permit, which has a faster procedure (a few weeks) and less housing bureaucracy.
With the €10,000 deductible, how much do I pay in taxes in Italy?
Income up to €10,000 is exempt from Italian taxation if you are a new frontier worker. Above this limit, the Italian IRPEF applies on the expected ranges (23% up to €28,000, 35% from €28,001 to €50,000, 43% over €50,000), but the tax credit for taxes already paid in Switzerland (withheld at source from the Canton) further reduces the burden. Consult model 730 with an accountant for the exact calculation.
Should you choose the Swiss LAMal or stay in the Italian SSN?
It depends on health habits and budget estimates. The Swiss LAMal offers fast performance and high quality but has fixed monthly costs and deductibles. The Italian NHS is free but requires private supplementary insurance for non-covered benefits. Many border workers choose LAMal for direct access to the Ticino EoC, especially if they work close to the border.
What are the fixed monthly costs of a border crossing in Bene Lario?
They vary according to the means of transport (private car vs. public transit), the LAMal chosen, and the living expenses in Italy. The article generically describes the lower cost of living in Italy, but does not provide specific figures because they depend on personal choices and market fluctuations. Use the [calcolatore stipendio](nav:calculator) to simulate the net paycheck.
What happens if you change jobs or move back to Switzerland?
If you stop work in Ticino, Permit G automatically ceases; you have 14 days to leave the Canton or convert it to Permit B if you find new work. If you decide to move to Switzerland, apply for Permit B at your new canton of residence. The accumulated AVS/LPP contributions remain credited and flow into your social security pillar.
