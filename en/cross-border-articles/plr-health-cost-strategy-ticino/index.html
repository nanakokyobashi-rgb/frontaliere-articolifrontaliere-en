On September 21, 2026, Alessandra Gianella, on behalf of the FDP group, presented a motion to the Grand Council aimed at implementing a cantonal strategy to contain healthcare spending. The initiative stems from the need to intervene structurally in the costs fueling the growth of health insurance premiums, moving beyond the simple provision of subsidies. The context highlighted by the text is that of a Ticino which recorded an average premium increase of 7.1% in 2026, a figure higher than the 4.4% recorded at the national level. Analyzing the progression by age group, the motion underlines how for adults the increase was 6.9%, for young adults 7.3%, and for minors 7.7%. The projections by bonus.ch mentioned in the document also indicate the possibility of a further national price hike of between 4.5% and 5% for 2027.

Operational details

The analysis proposed by the FDP focuses on the need for more rigorous and transparent healthcare governance within the Canton of Ticino. The motion calls for the introduction of a quarterly, concise, and accessible monitoring of compulsory insurance costs, based on data already available at the federal level. The goal is to break down expenses by major service groups, comparing them with national averages and those of other comparable cantons. In addition to this control tool, an in-depth annual analysis is proposed to identify the causes of expenditure deviations and the sectors where costs are growing most rapidly. Such data should serve as the basis for a cantonal containment strategy, to be updated at least once per legislative period, which defines priorities, measurable objectives, and concrete measures under cantonal jurisdiction.

Key points

For citizens and workers residing or active in Ticino, the issue of rising LAMal premiums represents a primary expense. The FDP motion directly impacts household budgets, which face constant increases and growing indirect fiscal pressure. It is crucial to understand that while the political debate focuses on cost-containment strategies, individual policyholders must continue to actively manage their insurance status. The transparency requested by the motion, if translated into public reports and accessible monitoring, will allow for a clearer view of the factors influencing healthcare spending, facilitating more informed choices. For those working in Ticino, it is essential to constantly monitor price dynamics and available coverage.

What impact has public spending on RIPAM had in recent years?
Public expenditure for the Reduction of Health Insurance Premiums (RIPAM) and for the taking charge of AVS/AI supplementary benefit recipients' premiums recorded significant growth. It went from 307.7 million francs in 2020 to 426 million in 2025, with a forecast of 453.5 million for 2026. This represents a 47% increase in just six years, raising doubts about the financial sustainability of the current subsidy model.
By how much did health insurance premiums increase in Ticino in 2026?
In 2026, Ticino recorded an average increase in health insurance premiums of 7.1%, a figure higher than the national average of 4.4%. The increase was diversified by age group: +6.9% for adults, +7.3% for young adults and +7.7% for minors. The projections of bonus.ch also suggest a further possible increase at the national level for 2027 between 4.5% and 5%.
