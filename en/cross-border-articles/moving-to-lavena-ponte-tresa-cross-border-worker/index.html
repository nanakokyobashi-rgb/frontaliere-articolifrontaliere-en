The municipality of Lavena Ponte Tresa offers one of the most attractive living solutions for those working daily in Ticino and seeking a strategic location along the border. The geographical position allows for quick access to the main Ticino work centers through the local pass, reducing travel time compared to other areas in the region. From a fiscal perspective, border workers residing in this area are subject to the provisions of the New Agreement on Border Workers, which came into force on 1 January 2024 following the signing of the agreement on 23 December 2020 and the ratification by Italy with Law 83 of 13 June 2023. Regarding the taxation of income from work, the income tax is withheld exclusively in Switzerland, while Italy eliminates double taxation through the tax credit inserted in the CE box of the model 730. For the old border workers, i.e., those who were already border workers before 17 July 2023, an exemption of €7,500 and a transitional regime covering the period 2024-2033 are provided, while for the new border workers a franchise of €10,000 applies.

Operational details

Living in Lavena Ponte Tresa involves a series of logistical and organizational assessments related to transborder mobility. Traffic near the customs checkpoint during peak hours is a key factor for those traveling by car to the Canton Ticino. Many workers choose to combine the use of private vehicles with local public transportation or the Lugano-Ponte Tresa railway, known as TILO, to optimize travel times and avoid congestion at border crossings. Choosing this location also means confronting the cost of living and the purchasing power generated by Swiss francs compared to expenses incurred in Italy. Proximity to the Ticino territory facilitates the management of daily work tasks, reducing commuting stress compared to those living in Italian provinces further away from the border.

Key points

The planning of the transfer to Lavena Ponte Tresa requires a series of administrative and procedural steps to be followed carefully. Before proceeding with the change of residence or the rental of a property, it is advisable to verify the necessary documentation for the issuance or update of the work permit in Switzerland, coordinating with the competent authorities such as the SECO and the SEM, as well as the Ticinese cantonal offices. Once settled in the border commune, it is necessary to regularize one's fiscal position in Italy by submitting the tax return using the model 730 and correctly filling out the CE form to benefit from the tax credit and avoid double taxation on dependent income earned in Switzerland. It is also essential to monitor the deadlines related to the LAMal choice and communication with the Revenue Agency and INPS for their competence.

Practical procedures and residence change management

To successfully handle the move and the start of a new life as a frontaliere in Lavena Ponte Tresa, it is advisable to follow an operational checklist:

  • Verify the actual travel times during peak hours towards the Canton Ticino
  • Check the personal documentation and requirements for the lease or purchase of real estate
  • Manage the LAMal option in accordance with the current regulations for frontaliers
  • Prepare the documentation for the tax return in Italy and the management of the tax credit
  • Verify the opening of a suitable bank account for managing the dual currency euro-swiss franc

Frequently Asked Questions
How is the income of the border worker in Lavena Ponte Tresa taxed?
Income tax at source is only withheld in Switzerland for frontier workers. Italy eliminates double taxation through the tax credit inserted in the EC framework of model 730, based on the provisions of the New Agreement and the Convention against Double Taxation.
What are the rules for old and new frontiersmen?
Old frontier workers, i.e. those who were already such before 17 July 2023, benefit from an exemption of 7,500 euros and a transitional regime valid from 2024 to 2033. For new frontier workers, on the other hand, a deductible of 10,000 euros applies.
How does LAMal health insurance work for residents of Lavena Ponte Tresa?
Border workers with a G permit who hold an activity in Ticino have the right of option for LAMal health insurance, being able to choose Swiss coverage with deductibles for adults between 300 and 2,500 Swiss francs or opt for the Italian system.
