Living in Bregnano and working in Ticino: a practical guide for border workers (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 22 September 2026

Travel times, costs, taxes and procedures: everything border workers from Bregnano need to know before accepting a job in Ticino.

Context

In brief

  • Average commuting time Bregnano-Chiasso: 45-60 minutes (traffic permitting)
  • Cost of living in Ticino 20-30% higher than in Bregnano (e.g. Lugano rent: +28% vs Como)
  • New cross-border worker tax regime active since 1 January 2024 (Italy-Switzerland Agreement 2020)
  • Tax exemption: €10,000 for new cross-border workers (employment started after 17/07/2023)

Key facts

Distance: 25 km Bregnano-Chiasso Main border crossings: Brogeda (A9), Ponte Chiasso (SS340), Fornasette (secondary) Travel time: 45-60 min (car), up to 90 min during rush hour Cost of living: +20-30% Ticino vs Lombardy (e.g. monthly shopping: CHF 450 vs €300 per household) Withholding tax: 5.3% AVS/AI + 1.1% AD (income up to CHF 148,200) Tax exemption: €10,000 (new cross-border workers)

The route from Bregnano to Chiasso, the main gateway to Canton Ticino, covers approximately 25 km. By car via the A9 motorway and the Brogeda crossing, it takes 45-60 minutes with smooth traffic. However, queues at crossings (especially Brogeda and Ponte Chiasso) and congestion during commuting hours (6:30-9:00 and 17:00-19:00) can extend travel times to 90 minutes. A commuter leaving Bregnano at 6:30 a.m. averages 50 minutes, while at 7:30 a.m. the time increases to 75 minutes. The rail alternative (S11 Como-Chiasso line) involves a change at Como San Giovanni and similar duration (65-80 minutes), with a monthly integrated TILO-Trenord pass at CHF 180 for the Italian-Swiss section. For those working in Lugano (30 km from Bregnano), travel time increases to 70-90 minutes by car, with tolls of CHF 40/month for the A9.

Cost of Living: Practical Comparison

The cost of living in Ticino exceeds the Bregnano area by 25-35%, with peaks in rents: in Lugano, a studio apartment starts at CHF 1,500 (vs. €650 in Como city). A two-room apartment in Mendrisio costs CHF 1,800, compared to €800 in Olgiate Comasco. Food expenses show surcharges of 18-22%: a liter of milk costs CHF 1.80 (Coop Ticino) vs. €1.20 (Conad Lombardia), while a kilogram of Barilla pasta reaches CHF 2.50 vs. €1.50. Fuel (gasoline 95) stands at CHF 1.82/liter (2024 average) vs. €1.75 in Italy, but Swiss diesel (CHF 1.75) is more convenient. According to the Federal Statistical Office (2023), household utilities cost 30% more: electricity at CHF 0.28/kWh vs. €0.22 in Lombardy.

Commuting: Options and Costs

Private car commuting entails monthly costs of CHF 350-450: fuel (CHF 200 for 1,200 km), highway vignette (CHF 40/year), parking (CHF 150-250/month in Lugano). Italian tolls (A9 Como-Chiasso: €5 per leg) add €200/month for 20 working days. Public transport offers the Arcobaleno pass (zones 10-20-Mi1): CHF 285/month for Chiasso-Lugano-Como, with TILO trains every 30 minutes (6:00-22:00). The new X30 bus line (Chiasso-Lugano, 45 minutes) costs CHF 7.60 per ride. Average travel times: 50 minutes by car vs. 70 minutes by train from Como to Lugano.

What to Consider Before Relocating

Tax Aspects: Double Taxation

Swiss withholding tax (currently 10.25% in Ticino for 2023) applies exclusively to employment income earned in the canton. Italian-sourced income such as rents (e.g., €12,000 annual from a Milan apartment) or dividends must be declared in Italy via the quadro CE of the 730 form, leveraging tax credits to prevent double taxation under the 2015 Italy-Switzerland agreement. New cross-border workers have 30 days from employment start to register with the Italian Revenue Agency (AA9/12 form).

Health Insurance (LAMal)

Within 3 months of hiring, cross-border workers must obtain basic LAMal-compliant insurance in Switzerland. In 2023, average Ticino premiums range from CHF 350 (Helsana, CHF 2,500 deductible) to CHF 480 (CSS, CHF 300 deductible) monthly per adult. Insurer choice (e.g., Groupe Mutuel, Concordia) impacts costs: a 40-year-old in Lugano typically pays CHF 420/month with standard deductible. ⚠️ Failure to enroll on time incurs fines up to CHF 5,000.

Social Security: AVS and LPP

Mandatory contributions deducted from Swiss paychecks include:

  • AVS/AI: 5.3% of salary (max CHF 49,290 annually in 2023)
  • AD/AC: 1.1% (up to CHF 147,840 only)
  • LPP: Mandatory from age 25. A 35-year-old earning CHF 80,000 contributes 7% (CHF 5,600 annually), with employers adding at least 50%. Vested funds are transferable only after 5 years.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long does it take to reach Ticino from Bregnano?
By car, with fluid traffic, 45-60 minutes via the A9 and the Brogeda crossing. By public transport, considering coincidences, 70-90 minutes.
What is the tax exemption for new frontier workers?
From 1 January 2024, new frontier workers (hired after 17 July 2023) will benefit from a €10,000 exemption on Swiss source tax.
Do I have to pay for health insurance in Switzerland?
Yes, frontier workers must take out basic health insurance (LAMal) in Switzerland within 3 months of recruitment, with average monthly premiums of CHF 350-450.
