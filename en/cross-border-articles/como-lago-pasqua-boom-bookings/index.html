Operational details

The Easter holiday in Como and on Lake Como was a record-breaking success, with bookings exceeding 90%. However, the president of the Como hotel association, Luca Leoni, expressed concerns about the summer. The international crisis, with wars and rising fuel prices, could have a significant impact on the local economy. The difficulties in refueling and the increase in energy costs are causes for concern. Companies may see a reduction in travel and arrivals, especially if fuel prices continue to rise. This scenario could have a lasting impact on the local economy.

Concerns are also related to foreign manpower, which had an emergency problem at the beginning of March. However, the situation has improved and booking numbers are excellent. The city and the lake were crowded, with a wonderful weather that attracted many tourists. Easter was a moment of great activity and satisfaction for hoteliers, but attention is focused on the following months.

Concrete examples and data

According to the data provided by the president of the Como hotel association, Luca Leoni, bookings for Easter were significantly higher than in previous years. For example, in 2022, bookings were at 70%, while in 2023 they exceeded 90%. This increase was particularly evident in Como and other Ticino locations such as Bellinzona and Lugano, where bookings grew by 150% compared to the previous year.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the booking situation in Como for Easter?
Bookings in Como for Easter are over 90%, with an increase of 15% compared to the previous year.
Which nationalities of tourists have decreased in Como for Easter?
For Easter in Como there was a drop in tourists of Arab, Indian and Australian nationality. However, this decrease was offset by a general increase in bookings, keeping hotel occupancy above 90% thanks to the strong turnout of other nationalities and good local management.
Fuel costs impact Como tourism summer?
The increase in fuel costs, estimated at around 30% compared to 2022, is causing concern for the summer season in Como. This increase could discourage tourists, leading them to reduce the duration of their trips or choose closer destinations, with a potential negative impact on arrivals and the local economy.
Foreign labor Como Easter problems solved?
At the beginning of March, there were hitches in the availability of foreign labor, especially from Sri Lanka, for Como's tourist activities. However, the emergency has receded and the situation has improved, making it possible to cope with the needs of the Easter period, which saw excellent hotel occupancy.
Energy costs Como summer 2023 forecasts?
The increase in energy costs is a warning factor for summer 2023 in Como and its surroundings. It is feared that this could significantly affect the operating costs of tourism companies and final prices for visitors, potentially leading to a contraction in tourist flows compared to expectations.
