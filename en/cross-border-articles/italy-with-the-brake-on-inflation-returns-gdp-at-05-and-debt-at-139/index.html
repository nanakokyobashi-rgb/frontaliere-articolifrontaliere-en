Frequently Asked Questions
How is the Italian economy growing in 2026 according to forecasts?
The Bank of Italy expects GDP growth of 0.5% for the whole of 2026. In the second quarter of 2026, GDP increased by 0.2% compared to the previous quarter, after +0.3% in the first. A slowdown to 0.4% is estimated in 2027, with a recovery to 0.9% in 2028.
What is the forecast for inflation in Italy for 2026 and the coming years?
The Bank of Italy expects an average price increase of 3.1% for the whole of 2026. After averaging 1.6% in 2025, harmonized inflation reached 2.9% in July 2026. A return to 2% in 2027 and 1.9% in 2028 is expected.
Are there positive signs on employment in Italy in 2026?
Yes, employment in Italy shows favourable signs, reaching the highest levels of the historical series considered. An increase of about 7% in employment is estimated from 2010 to 2026, going from about 22.5 million to over 24 million, indicating a positive trend in the labour market.
How is Italian public debt evolving in 2026 and recent years?
Italian public debt is expected to rise, rising from 134.7% of GDP in 2024 to 137.1% in 2025 and estimated at 138.6% in 2026. Despite an improvement in the deficit, which is expected to fall to 2.9% in 2026, debt continues to grow.
What is the situation of commercial relations and the Italian banking system in 2026?
Foreign relations are positive, with a surplus of 6 billion euros in the first five months of 2026 and a balance of assets of 21.7 billion. The banking system is sound, maintaining high levels of capital and liquidity, with non-performing loans well below the peaks of the previous decade.
