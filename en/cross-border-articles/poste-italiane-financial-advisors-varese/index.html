Poste Italiane is looking for new professionals in the province of Varese and in some municipalities in Ticino such as Lugano, Mendrisio, Chiasso, Bellinzona and Locarno for the promotion and sale of financial, insurance and financing services. The search is active until 26 August and the selected candidates will be placed inside the post offices, working closely with customers. Financial advisors represent a network of professionals with technical and commercial skills able to assist the customer in their choices and offer the product that best suits their needs.

Operational details

Requirements and opportunities

Candidates must have an interest in the world of financial, insurance and investment markets, good interpersonal, communication and goal orientation skills. Preferential requirements are the possession of a qualification in economic and legal disciplines, knowledge of financial markets and instruments, financial, insurance and investment products/services, and knowledge and competence of the regulations provided for the provision of investment advice (MIFID II Directive, 23/06/2018) and/or distribution of insurance products (IDD Directive, 20/07/2017).

Working in the Poste Italiane Group represents a great opportunity for growth and strengthening of its skills in addition to the possibility of making the most of its experience as part of the largest service infrastructure in Italy, with over 20,000 employees and a turnover of 4.5 billion euros in 2022. The presence throughout the Italian territory and the centrality in the socio-economic fabric of the country offer the possibility of working in a dynamic and innovative context, with access to advanced technologies and an extensive network of contacts.

Concrete examples and local references

For those who work in Ticino, the proximity to municipalities such as Lugano, Mendrisio and Bellinzona offers the opportunity to easily integrate the network of Poste Italiane, with offices in Locarno and Lugano.

Key points

How to Apply

Poste Italiane is seeking financial advisors to work in the province of Varese, offering an excellent opportunity for those who wish to work in the financial and insurance sectors. Candidates can submit their applications by August 26 via Poste Italiane's website. For more information and to submit your application, visit Poste Italiane's official website.

This position is ideal for those seeking stable and well-remunerated employment. Poste Italiane is one of Italy's largest service infrastructures, with over 20,000 employees and an annual turnover exceeding 20 billion euros. The province of Varese is an important economic hub, with a population of approximately 800,000 and a GDP exceeding 10 billion euros.

Checklist for Application

  • Verify admission requirements: the candidate must have a degree in economics, finance, or a related field, with at least 2 years of work experience in the financial sector.
  • Prepare a detailed curriculum vitae: include all work experiences, personal projects, and relevant certifications.
  • Write a cover letter: highlight your skills and motivation to work for Poste Italiane.
  • Prepare a portfolio: if you have experience in the financial sector, include examples of projects or analyses you have conducted.
