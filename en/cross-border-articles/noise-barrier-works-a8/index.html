The first week of November includes changes to nighttime traffic along the A8 Milan-Varese and the D08 Gallarate-Gattico Branch. Autostrade per l’Italia has announced a plan of temporary closures to allow new modernization and enhancement works on the noise barriers.

The company has scheduled the work for the late evening and early morning hours. The stated reason is operational: nighttime work sites should allow the activities to be carried out safely, reducing the impact on daytime traffic.

The first window: A8 and D08

The first set of restrictions is scheduled from 23:00 to 5:00 on the nights of November 2–3 and 4–5, respectively between Monday and Tuesday and between Wednesday and Thursday. On the A8, toward Milan, the section from Gallarate to the connection with the A36 Pedemontana Lombarda will be closed to traffic.

For those traveling toward Milan, the exit will be at Gallarate; from there, the detour will follow via Vittorio Veneto, via Sanzio, via Vespucci, via Lombardia, via Pier Capponi Mornera and via Buonarroti, then continue along State Road 33 del Sempione and State Road 336 della Malpensa, rejoining the A8 at the Busto Arsizio interchange.

During the same hours, traffic on the D08 will be interrupted between Besnate and the connection with the A8. Entry to the Besnate station will remain prohibited in all directions. The plan therefore affects two distinct connections: the A8 for those traveling toward Milan and the branch that leads from Besnate to the main motorway.

The measures extend beyond the Gallarate-Busto Arsizio section: the Solbiate Arno Albizzate access is in fact closed in both directions to allow maintenance of an overpass, as stated in the notice.

Operational details

Two windows, different routes

The plan clearly distinguishes the first nighttime window from the subsequent phase. Both the times and the section of the A8 affected by the closure change. The D08, however, remains closed from Besnate toward the interchange with the A8, with entry to the Besnate toll station blocked.

For travel toward Milan, the alternative route indicated for the D08 uses Provincial Road 26, State Road 33 and State Road 336, until re-entering at Busto Arsizio. For those traveling toward Varese, the suggested detour passes through the towns and leads to the Castronno entrance. Those heading instead to the A26 can use the Sesto Calende Vergiate entrance.

The second phase therefore involves a different exit point on the A8. Those heading to Milan must leave the motorway at Castronno and follow the signs for the ordinary road network until re-entering at Busto Arsizio. For entrances closed toward Milan, the instruction is to head toward Busto Arsizio; for the Varese direction, Castronno remains the reference toll station.

This difference makes the plan complex: in the first window, the main reference point for the exit is Gallarate, while in the second it is Castronno. The alternatives nevertheless remain anchored to the same local, provincial and state-road connections already indicated for the previous nights.

Key points

The second phase and the locations that remain closed

The program will conclude with a phase scheduled from 21:00 on Thursday, November 5, to 5:00 on Friday, November 6. During those hours, traffic on the A8 will be interrupted between Castronno and the junction with the Pedemontana, toward Milan. The Solbiate Arno Albizzate and Cavaria interchanges will also be closed to entry.

The continuing closure at Solbiate Arno Albizzate

The plan contains two provisions concerning Solbiate Arno Albizzate. Access remains prohibited in both directions due to maintenance work on an overpass. In the second phase, moreover, the interchange is listed among the entrances closed toward Milan. The text therefore links the continuing closure to maintenance of the overpass and places Solbiate among the locations to consider during the longest night as well.

On the D08, by contrast, the closure continues on the stretch between Besnate and the A8 toward Varese and Milan. In this phase too, access to the Besnate station will remain prohibited. For the D08, the alternative routes on the state and provincial road network already indicated for the previous nights remain confirmed.
