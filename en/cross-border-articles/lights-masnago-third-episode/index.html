The team's moment

On Thursday 15 October, from 2.45 pm, Radio Materia will broadcast the third seasonal episode of "Luci a Masnago", a programme dedicated to Varese basketball. The event will focus on the very difficult moment of Varese Basketball, told in the source through a sequence of results and a new physical problem.

After the success in the championship against Trieste, the team lost again in the cup against the Greeks of Peristeri. On thatoccasion, Amedeo Della Valle, the most anticipated player, suffered a new muscle injury. The transmission will consider both passages: the championship victory and the subsequent cup setback, accompanied by the player's physical problem.

In the line-up conducted by Damiano Franzetti, Francesco Brezzi and Luca Antonelli there will be space to analyse the position of Kastritis coach. Brezzi will be connected remotely. The program will also compare the tactical variations between the two matches and will address the use of Kamagate, along with the other themes announced in the episode.

The comparison between Trieste and Peristeri is therefore the pivot of the new appointment of "Luci a Masnago": on the one hand a victory arrived by a breath, on the other a new fall in the cup. In the middle, the situation of Della Valle and the issues related to the position of Kastritis, tactical choices and the use of Kamagate. These are the elements that the broadcast will bring to the attention of Radio Materia listeners on Thursday afternoon.

Operational details

Marco Passera, voice of Varese basketball

Marco Passera, a former red-and-white player, a true Varese native, and much loved by the fans, will be in the studio. The episode will revisit his playing career and discuss his current activities in basketball, maintaining a direct link with the Varese scene.

Passera is a point guard born in 1982 and won the Italian youth championship with Robur. Between 2007 and 2010, he wore the Cimberio jersey. He also played for a long time on courts throughout Italy. These are the career details that the source provides to introduce the guest to the audience of «Luci a Masnago».

Today, he is the coach of Sette Laghi Gazzada, a team in Serie B Interregionale. Alongside his work on the bench, Passera is co-founder of Varese Basket School together with Martino Rovera. During the episode, he will talk about both of his current activities: his work with Sette Laghi Gazzada and the one connected to the basketball school he shares with Rovera.

The guest's account will therefore cover his sporting career and the activities mentioned in the program's presentation. Passera will be called on to talk about what he does today after his years with Robur, his time at Cimberio, and his long career on Italian courts. His presence brings to the episode a former red-and-white player who now works as a coach and co-founder of a basketball project.
