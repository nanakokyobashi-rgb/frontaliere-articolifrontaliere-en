What is the current exchange rate between euro and franc according to the source?
According to the data reported, the euro stands at 0.9423 Swiss francs. The dollar is trading at 0.8268 francs. These values reflect the specific timing of the trades described, in which the franc lost ground after the SNB's decisions.
What has the Swiss National Bank decided on rates?
The SNB confirmed the benchmark rate at zero percent, as widely predicted by analysts. However, the institute reiterated that it monitors exchange rate fluctuations in both directions, maintaining its willingness to intervene in the market if circumstances so require.
How does the weak franc affect the frontier worker's income?
If the income is expressed in francs, the nominal value does not change. However, if you convert to euros, a weaker franc tends to produce a lower euro equivalent at parity of amount. The source points out that this is a conditional scenario based on market quotation.
