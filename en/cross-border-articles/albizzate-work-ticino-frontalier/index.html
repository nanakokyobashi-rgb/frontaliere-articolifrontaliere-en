Albizzate, a municipality in the province of Varese, is a well-established choice among those who work in Ticino while maintaining their Italian residence. The proximity to the border and the earnings in Swiss francs make this location attractive for border crossers, but the transfer requires precise knowledge of taxation, permits and repercussions on the paycheck.

Those who decide to live in Albizada and work in Ticino accede to the border regime governed by the Italy-Switzerland Convention of 9 December 1976 and by the new border agreement that entered into force on 1 January 2024. Operational details

The tax comparison: before and after the new 2024 agreement

The new agreement has significantly changed the rules for frontier workers. Existing frontier workers benefit from an exemption of €7,500 of exempt income, with a transitional regime until 2033. The new frontier workers have a deductible of €10,000. This difference has repercussions on the Italian declaration and on the monthly net.

For a frontier worker earning CHF 5000 gross monthly, the Ticino cantonal and federal source tax is calculated according to the local tables and withheld directly. Those who regularly return to Italy with a G permit present the EC framework in the Italian declaration, where the deductible (€7,500 or €10,000) reduces the taxable amount.

About 5.3% is paid on the Swiss paycheck for AVS, 1.1% for AD/AC, and the LPP varies. Italian IRPEF is progressive: 23% up to €28,000, 35% between €28,001 and €50,000, 43% over €50,000. Thanks to the deductible, many frontier workers remain in the first Italian band, obtaining a reduced overall tax burden.

LAMal: mandatory and right of option

In Switzerland, health insurance (LAMal) is mandatory. Border workers with a G permit have the right of option: they can maintain their registration with the Italian National Health System or register with a Swiss bank. Key points

Step-by-Step Procedure for G Permit Request

If you decide to transfer your residence to Albizzate and work in Ticino, the first step is to obtain the G permit at the cantonal migration office (SEM).

1. Pre-booking and preliminary consultation: Contact the SEM for a consultation. Required documents include passport or ID card, employment contract, and Italian residence certificate.

2. Submission of the application: Send the G permit application to the SEM. The Ticino employer can support with statements of employment and contractual stability.

3. INPS registration: In parallel, register with the Italian INPS as a dependent foreign worker, declaring Swiss income and frontier worker status.

4. LAMal choice: Within 3 months of starting work, decide whether to maintain Italian insurance or register with a Swiss health insurance fund. This choice determines coverage and costs.

5. Income declaration: Complete the Italian tax return with the CE form for foreign-dependent income, indicating Swiss source tax and applicable exemption (€7,500 or €10,000).

Timelines and Deadlines

The G permit application is usually processed within 4-8 weeks from the complete submission. It is recommended to start the procedure before the start date of work. INPS registration is instantaneous online, while LAMal choice has legal deadlines to avoid automatic penalties.

Frequently Asked Questions
Should I choose permit G or permit B?
The G permit is for those who regularly return to Italy and is the most common among those who live in Albizada. The B permit is for those who move to Switzerland for at least 5 years. Choose G if you maintain a family and home in Albizada; choose B only if you intend to transfer everything to Ticino. The G permit is simplified from an administrative and fiscal point of view.
How much does Switzerland tax me as a border worker?
The tax at source varies according to the federal and cantonal Ticino tables. Federal, cantonal and municipal tax is withheld from the Swiss gross. The effective rate depends on income, age, and family carrying capacity. The [calcolatore stipendio](nav:calculator) gives you a precise estimate. Typically, the effective rate ranges from 10% to 20% of the gross, depending on your personal situation.
What is the income allowance in the new 2024 agreement?
If you were already a border worker before 17 July 2023, you have an exemption of €7,500 of exempt income from 2024 to 2033. If you are a new frontier worker, you have a deductible of €10,000. This exemption reduces the Italian taxable amount declared in the EC framework of your income tax return, significantly lowering the personal income tax due.
How do I avoid double taxation?
Switzerland withholds tax at source on your salary. In Italy, fill in the EC framework in the declaration and apply the foreign tax credit, deducting the Swiss tax paid. The Italy-Switzerland Convention of 9 December 1976 regulates this mechanism. If the Swiss tax is in excess of the Italian taxes due, you get refunds from the Revenue Agency.
Is health insurance mandatory in Switzerland?
Yes, LAMal is mandatory in Switzerland. As a border worker with a G permit, you have the right of option: you can stay in the Italian National Health System or register with a Swiss bank. Swiss deductibles range from CHF 300 to CHF 2,500 depending on the policy. Consider both options to choose the best one for you and your family.
