Frequently Asked Questions
What are the total registration data in Ticino for the week 2026-W38?
According to the Federal Office of Roads ASTRA/OFROU, in the week 2026-W38 the Canton of Ticino registered a total of 13,414 new registrations. This figure, part of the weekly report, includes different types of power supply, including electric vehicles, plug-in hybrids, petrol and diesel, offering a complete picture of the movement of the Ticino vehicle fleet in the period considered.
How many electric cars have been registered in Ticino this week?
The ASTRA/OFROU weekly report for the week 2026-W38 indicates that 1555 new electric cars have been registered in Ticino. This number represents a significant part of the total new registrations registered in the canton during the period monitored by the federal office.
Can I use this data to make annual car sales forecasts?
No, it is necessary to adopt a cautious reading. The ASTRA/OFROU data are provisional and refer exclusively to the 2026-W38 survey week. It is not correct or appropriate to transform the result of a single week into a forecast for the entire year, as registration flows can vary considerably over time.
