The Department of Education, Culture and Sport of Canton Ticino has published the official school holiday calendar for 2026. The dates include Christmas, Carnival, Easter, and summer breaks, essential elements for family and professional planning, especially for cross-border workers. These workers, often commuting between Italy and Switzerland, must coordinate holidays and work permits, also taking into account cantonal holidays and school closures.

The end of schools in 2026 marks an important moment for families with school-age children, as it coincides with holiday planning and cross-border work management. Christmas holidays extend over several weeks, followed by Carnival holidays, which have particular cultural significance in Ticino.

Main school holiday dates 2026

The calendar includes winter Christmas holidays spanning December and January, with the end of lessons set just before Christmas. Carnival holidays take place in early February and represent a significant break in the winter semester. Easter holidays fall in spring, allowing cross-border parents to organize their work and family commitments. Finally, the summer break traditionally starts mid-June, marking the end of the academic year.

Operational details

The school holidays in Ticino are a crucial reference point for cross-border workers who work in Switzerland but live in Italy. The precise scheduling for 2026 directly impacts the management of the G permit and the days in which it is possible to take time off work without losing health insurance coverage and social security benefits such as AVS and LPP.

Key points

Knowing and correctly using the school holiday calendar of the Canton Ticino for 2026 is essential for every cross-border worker who wants to best organise his or her work and family.

Practical procedure for cross-border workers

1. Consult the official calendar published by the Department of Education of the Canton Ticino, available online on the institutional portals. 2. Plan work absences in line with school holidays, requesting specific permits from the employer in Switzerland. 3. Check the LAMal insurance coverage for the absence periods and keep the documents related to the G or B permit up to date. 4. Take into account possible delays or slowdowns at the Brogeda or Gaggiolo border crossing during holiday periods, organising travel in advance. 5. Coordinate the management of withholding tax and refunds with your tax adviser, considering the impact of absences and vacations.

Moreover, it is advisable to stay updated on any regulatory changes or official communications from the Canton Ticino and the relevant authorities, to avoid surprises and optimise your work and tax situation.

The coordination between family, work and regulations is the key to an effective management of school holidays, avoiding difficulties and ensuring continuity of rights and social‑security coverage for those living in Italy and working in Switzerland.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the main dates for school holidays in Ticino for 2026?
The 2026 school holidays in Canton Ticino include breaks for Christmas, Carnival, Easter, and the summer break which starts around mid-June. These dates are official and published by the cantonal Education Department.
How do school holidays affect the lives of cross-border workers in Canton Ticino?
School holidays influence the work and family planning of cross-border workers, often requiring leave permits and careful management of the G permit, LAMal insurance, and source deductions.
What procedures should cross-border workers follow to manage absences during school holidays?
Cross-border workers should consult the official calendar, request leave from their employer, verify LAMal coverage, consider crossing times at border posts, and coordinate tax management with consultants.
