The debut in Masnago

On Sunday, September 27, at 7 PM, Openjobmetis Varese hosts Virtus Bologna at the Itelyum Arena in Masnago for Round 1 of the LBA. It is the start of the new journey in Serie A, with the match scheduled as the final event of the distributed round.

The great classic of Italian basketball arrives at a particular moment for both teams. Varese has to deal with an already shortened roster, while Bologna arrives with a renewed lineup and hierarchies yet to be defined. The question posed on the eve of the match is therefore also internal: which of the two will manage to discover itself first during the game?

Virtus has changed a lot and has also changed sponsors, with Costa, the cruise multinational. The club has embarked on a path oriented toward savings compared to previous years, but it remains designated as the number 2 powerhouse of the tournament. Under the guidance of Spaniard Alex Mumbrù, it has lost some of the talent available in the past and replaced it with speed, athleticism, and an aggressive attitude.

The seasonal balance of the Bologna team started with a defeat in the Supercup against Milan and a loss in the Euroleague debut against Fener. However, the Istanbul match showed a dangerous and unpredictable team. Some gaps remain in long-range shooting, a sector in which Kastritis could attempt some solutions, as in the friendly match lost against Maxima.

On the Varese front, William McDowell-White is certainly absent. Amedeo Della Valle, the guard from Piedmont, trained separately on Friday due to knee discomfort: the problem is not worrying in the medium term, but his participation against Virtus remains in doubt. The preseason has already accustomed Openjobmetis to playing without one or the other, even if both improve the team.

Operational details

The game will be decided by the rotations

The value of the test lies not only in the strength of the opponents, but in the need to build a team during the game. Varese and Bologna still have to define their pecking order, and the absences make the coaches' choices more visible. For Openjobmetis, the problem is above all distributing the playmaking duties and maintaining offensive incisiveness without two players who, when available, improve the team's overall performance.

Varese, playmaking duties to be shared

McDowell-White's certain absence leaves game management to Librizzi, Hale and Duke. Their task will be shared, with attention also focused on Librizzi's fouls. Hunter will be called upon more than expected to be the team's primary offensive option, a role he likes anyway. David, on the other hand, will also have to make an impact in plays when the ball is in the hands of the others.

This distribution does not establish a definitive pecking order, but reflects the emergency situation described on the eve of the game. If Della Valle does not play, the solution will be the one already tested during the preseason; if he is listed, his presence could change the rotations. The final choice also concerns Villa, whom Kastritis could use as the twelfth man or as the eleventh if Della Valle is listed only to make up the numbers.

Frequently Asked Questions
When and where is the Varese-Bologna game played?
The meeting between Openjobmetis Varese and Virtus Bologna is scheduled for Sunday 27 September at 7 pm. The match will take place at the Itelyum Arena in Masnago, as part of the first round of the LBA.
What are the conditions of the Varese players?
The Varese team will have to deal with the certain absence of William McDowell-White. Amedeo Della Valle, on the other hand, remains in doubt due to a knee discomfort that led him to train apart from Friday, although the problem is not worrying in the medium term.
Are there any restrictions for Virtus Bologna fans?
Yes, a travel ban was ordered for Bianconeri fans, a decision taken on Friday night due to events that took place eight years ago.
