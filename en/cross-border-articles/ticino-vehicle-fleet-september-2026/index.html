Key facts

The updated data for September 2026, released by the Federal Roads Office (FEDRO/ASTRA), provides a detailed snapshot of the composition of the vehicle fleet circulating in the Canton of Ticino. The survey, based on IVZ open data, indicates a total fleet size of 336,430 vehicles. This figure represents the foundation upon which the regional automotive market activity is built during the period under consideration. In September 2026, new registrations reached a total of 13,293 units, confirming a constant dynamism in the renewal of the circulating fleet. At the same time, the phenomenon of importing used vehicles recorded 1,532 new entries, reflecting the choices of users operating in the territory. The analysis of the distribution by fuel type reveals a clear predominance of traditional combustion engines, with 191,237 petrol-powered vehicles and 75,354 diesel vehicles. In parallel, new technologies are gaining ground, with 12,967 pure electric vehicles and 40,632 plug-in hybrids recorded in the total stock. Smaller categories, such as gas vehicles, account for 155 units, while the 'others' category includes 16,085 units. For cross-border commuters who cross the Brogeda or Gaggiolo border crossings daily, understanding the density of the vehicle fleet helps to interpret the volume of traffic that flows onto the road arteries every day, particularly along the A2 motorway corridor. The Federal Roads Office (FEDRO/ASTRA), through open data, allows for constant monitoring, which is essential for assessing the evolution of mobility in a region like Ticino, characterized by a high number of incoming commuters. The precision of these counts provides a necessary knowledge base for anyone who needs to plan their travel or is considering changing their vehicle. The distinction between stock, new registrations, and used imports also allows for the isolation of the various components that fuel road traffic, an aspect that closely affects those who commute between Italy and Switzerland, ensuring a clear view of the volumes involved on a monthly basis.

Analysis of fuel types and fleet dynamics

The Ticino vehicle fleet, analyzed in its monthly historical series for September 2026, shows a heterogeneous composition. Looking at new registrations made in the reference month alone, the total of 13,293 vehicles is divided into 5,119 gasoline, 1,233 diesel, 5,032 plug-in hybrids, and 1,570 electric vehicles. It is interesting to note how the share of plug-in hybrids in new registrations, equal to 5,032, indicates a growing preference for this technology compared to the 1,570 purely electric vehicles. The gas vehicle category recorded 0 new registrations in this specific month, while 339 vehicles fall into the 'others' category. Regarding used vehicle imports, which amount to 1,532 units, the distribution shows 697 gasoline vehicles, 286 diesel, 467 plug-in hybrids, and 60 electric. A single gas vehicle and 21 units classified as 'others' complete the picture of used imports. This mix of new entries into the Ticino fleet is fundamental to understanding not only spending power but also the technological trends that inevitably reflect on the types of vehicles that cross-border commuters encounter during their work commutes. The comparison between new registrations and used imports highlights how the Ticino market is fueled by both flows, with greater weight given to direct replacement through the purchase of new vehicles.
