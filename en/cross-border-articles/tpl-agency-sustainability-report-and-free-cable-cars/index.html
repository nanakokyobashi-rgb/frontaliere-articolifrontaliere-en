TPL Agency: Sustainability Report and free cable cars (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 16 September 2026

The TPL Agency presents the Sustainability Report and offers a day of free access to seven cableway installations from 16 to 22 September.

Context

In brief

  • Participation in the European Mobility Week
  • Presentation of the second Sustainability Report
  • Free rides on the seven cableways
  • Event scheduled for Friday, September 18 in Como

Key facts

  • Entity: Agency for Public Local Transport of the Como, Lecco and Varese Basin
  • Mobility period: September 16 to 22
  • Report date: Friday, September 18, 10:00 AM
  • Event venue: Chamber of Commerce of Como-Lecco, via Parini 16, Como
  • Report technical support: Marsh Advisory according to GRI standards
  • Free cableways date: Sunday, September 20

The Agency for Public Local Transport of the Como, Lecco and Varese basin has confirmed its participation in the European Sustainable Mobility Week, announcing a series of events dedicated to citizens and commuters who daily travel along the Lombardy border territories. The initiative, scheduled from September 16 to 22, develops through two main directives aimed at promoting the use of eco-friendly vehicles, management transparency, and the efficiency of local public transport. The first key moment is set for Friday, September 18 at 10:00 AM at the Chamber of Commerce of Como-Lecco, located at via Parini 16 in Como, where the entity's second Sustainability Report will be publicly presented.

ESG report details and institutional dialogue

The public meeting organized by the TPL Agency will see the participation of the territory's institutional leaders, including the Presidents of the Provinces of Como, Lecco and Varese, as well as the President of FederMobilità, with moderation entrusted to industry experts. The programmatic and reporting document was drafted with the support of Marsh Advisory and following international GRI standards. The report details the financial resources invested for the renewal of the bus fleet, the upgrading of transport infrastructure, salary adjustments for sector company personnel, and the launch of tenders for the assignment of services, whose procedures were finalized in August 2025. Giovanni Stefano Galli, President of the TPL Agency, emphasized how the document reflects a shared commitment aimed at supporting mobility development in full compliance with territorial transparency and efficiency, offering a clear picture of the public entity's environmental, social, and governance performance.

Operational details

The second major initiative promoted by the Public Transport Agency of the Como, Lecco, and Varese basin moves directly into the field, offering a concrete incentive for soft and collective mobility for residents, visitors, and commuters. On Sunday, September 20, access to all seven cableway systems in the agency's jurisdiction area, including funiculars and cable cars, will be completely free for the entire day. This extraordinary measure has been designed to stimulate the use of collective public transport, enhancing ropeway transport systems not only as a tourist attraction, but as a true ecological and integrated alternative for discovering the territory and reducing the environmental impact related to private mobility.

Integrated mobility and connections in the cross-border basin

For those who regularly travel in the Insubric area and border territories, the efficiency of local public transport networks is a decisive factor for the quality of daily travel, often intersecting with intermodality needs. The attention paid by the TPL Agency to upgrading infrastructure, renewing buses, and ensuring regular service is directly reflected in the fluidity of connections linking the main Lombard urban centers with routes toward Switzerland. Planning your journeys using rail and bus connections, combined with the opportunities offered by promotional days such as the free opening of cableways, allows you to experience alternative mobility forms capable of easing road traffic at the region's crucial hubs. You can find more details on the status of connections and infrastructure by visiting the dedicated page on Ticino public transport to better plan your work or leisure trips.

Participation in the events of the European Sustainable Mobility Week represents a useful opportunity to become aware of public transport development plans in the provinces of Como, Lecco, and Varese.
