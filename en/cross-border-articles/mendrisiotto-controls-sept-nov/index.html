The operation aimed to monitor transit flows, detect any suspicious situations and counter potentially unlawful conduct. The activities were directed in particular at preventing and combating crimes against property and cross-border criminal activity. According to the source, coordinating the presence in the area also made it possible to tighten checks on people and vehicles in transit.

The resources of the two forces were concentrated at strategic points in the area. The collaboration made it possible to bring together different areas of expertise and to search for people or vehicles that were being sought, as well as to ascertain any violations of the regulations in force. The overall tally includes 43 checkpoints, 461 vehicles checked and 699 people checked.

During the checks, a counterfeit identity document was found. During a vehicle inspection, a hidden compartment was discovered: 1,5 grams of cocaine were found and seized inside. Three wanted people were also identified.
