Border health: 120 euros per month for medical salaries (cross-border guide)

Di Laura Bianchi · Published on 31 July 2026

Lombardy applies the contribution from 3% to 6%: funds to border doctors to curb the flight to Switzerland.

Context

Briefly

  • A 20% increase in salaries for doctors and nurses within 20 km of the border.
  • Measure financed by contribution of old frontiersmen from 3% to 6% net.
  • A frontiersman earning 4,000 Swiss francs net will pay around 120 euros per month for the SSN.
  • What: Health contribution borne by old frontiersmen and 20% incentive for border healthcare workers
  • When: Second half of April 2026
  • Where: Border area within twenty kilometers from Switzerland and Lombardy region
  • Who: Regional Councillor Massimo Sertori, Lombardy Region, healthcare employers
  • Amount: From 30 to 200 euros per month at the expense of frontiersmen; 20% bonus for doctors (around 10,000 euros per year) and nurses (5,400 euros per year)

The Lombardy plan to value healthcare personnel

In April 2026, the operational details are defined to counter the emigration of Italian healthcare personnel attracted by Swiss salaries. The Lombardy Region aims to increase salaries of doctors and nurses working in public structures within a radius of twenty kilometers from the Swiss border by 20%. The measure aims to make Italian border hospitals attractive, where professionals are increasingly difficult to engage due to salaries across the border that are three or four times higher than those in Italy.

Operational details

The historical roots of the old frontier regime

To understand the reasons for the measure, it is necessary to retrace the history of the tax and health treatment reserved for those who reside in Italy and work abroad. Those who have held the G permit since before July 2023 fall under the transitional regime and benefit from the historical mechanism of exclusive cross-border taxation. For 27 years the old frontier workers have benefited from the benefits of the Italian National Health Service together with their family members, while paying the tax at source exclusively to the Swiss authorities and without having to pay income taxes to the Italian Revenue Agency.

Until 1999, commuters paid sick pay in Switzerland for their own personal coverage and paid a bill of about 600 thousand lire to guarantee assistance to their family members in Italy. With the transition of health management to the Regions and the introduction of the agreement between Switzerland and the European Union on the right of option, the framework has changed. Each border worker had the right to choose within three months of hiring whether to subscribe to the Swiss LAMal coverage (with premiums starting from about 350 euros per month for the individual worker) or rely on the Italian SSN. However, due to a prolonged regulatory vacuum, the old border workers did not pay additional fees for Italian health care while maintaining its full coverage.

Key points

Modalities of operation, controls, and self-certifications

The regional offices are preparing the dedicated informatic applications for the collection of the health contribution. One of the open issues concerns the retrieval of the complete list of old border workers: the Swiss fiscal authorities keep the lists confidential, protecting the data of foreign residents in a similar way to bank secrecy. In the face of Switzerland's refusal to transmit the complete lists of cross-border workers, the Lombardy Region will proceed through mandatory self-certifications for taxpayers, accompanied by rigorous sampling controls based on cross-referenced data available from Italian databases.

For workers who need to plan their monthly exits and verify their fiscal position vis-à-vis the healthcare system and taxes, it is recommended to follow these procedural steps:

1. Retributive status verification: ascertain whether the employment contract is prior to 17 July 2023 to establish membership in the category of old border workers. 2. Calculation of the net taxable base: quantify the net salary received in Swiss francs and convert it to the reference exchange rate to identify the 3% quota. 3. Contribution threshold verification: consider that the law provides for a monthly contribution band ranging from a minimum of 30 euros to a maximum of 200 euros. 4. Choice of medical coverage: compare the Italian contribution with the costs of Swiss health insurance through the LAMal health coverage guide. 5. Monitoring of refunds and border crossings: consult the evolution of agreements between Italy and Switzerland and examine the map of transit points such as Brogeda to plan daily commuting.

Frequently Asked Questions
How much is the health contribution for old border workers?
The contribution established by the Financial 2024 ranges from a minimum of 30 euros to a maximum of 200 euros per month. The Lombardy Region has chosen to apply the minimum rate of 3% of net income (with the right to increase up to 6%). For example, on a net salary of CHF 4,000, the withholding is approximately EUR 120 per month.
How are the funds collected by the Lombardy Region used?
The resources collected do not go to the central government of Rome but remain in the Lombard territory. They are used to increase the salaries of doctors (about 10,000 euros per year more) and nurses (about 5,400 euros per year) serving in public hospitals within twenty kilometers of the Swiss border by 20%.
What happens if Switzerland does not hand over the list of border guards?
The regional administration also proceeds without the lists provided by the Canton or the federal authorities, using self-certification forms prepared in the computer applications and activating random checks and controls on the population residing in the border areas.
