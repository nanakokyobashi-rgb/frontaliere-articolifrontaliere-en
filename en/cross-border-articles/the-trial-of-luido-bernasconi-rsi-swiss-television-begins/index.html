Implications for cross-border workers

The trial of Luido Bernasconi does not have direct implications for cross-border workers. However, it is essential to note that the trial may impact the reputation of RSI Radiotelevisione svizzera and the trust of its users, particularly those working in the canton of Ticino.

Cross-border workers employed in Ticino may be interested in understanding how this trial could influence their daily work life, especially when considering the regulations related to cross-border work. For example, according to the cross-border work law of June 21, 2019, cross-border workers may be subject to specific working conditions and tax regulations.
