Key points

To take advantage of this opportunity, you must submit your G or B permit application to the AFC/ESTV by December 31, 2023. The procedure is simple and can be done online.

Once the application is submitted, you will be able to work in Switzerland without having to pay the tax at source. It is important to note that the deductible of €10,000 is only valid for new frontiersmen. For example, if you earn a monthly salary of €5,000, you might avoid paying tax at source for the first 10 months of work.

If you are already a border worker, it is important to check the conditions for the €7,500 deductible. According to the rules of 15 December 2022, frontier workers working in Switzerland can benefit from a deductible of €7,500 if their salary is less than €15,000 per month.

For more information, you can consult the AFC/ESTV website or contact customer service. The salary calculator is a useful tool to understand how the deductible works and how it applies to the salary amount.

For example, if you earn a salary of €4,000 per month and work in Switzerland for 6 months, the deductible of €7,500 could cover the entire amount of the salary, excluding tax at source.
