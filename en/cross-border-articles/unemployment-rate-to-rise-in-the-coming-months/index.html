The third-quarter picture

In the third quarter, the cantonal rate reached 2,87%, 0,3 percentage points higher year on year. Among Swiss residents, the rate was 2,2%, while among foreigners the figure was 4,17. The increase compared with the same period of the previous year was 0,2 percentage points for the former and 0,5 for the latter.

The share of women among all unemployed people rose to 44,5% compared with the previous period, although it was down compared with that of last year. The youth unemployment figure, on the other hand, increased compared with the previous year and stood at 5,7%.
