Railway traffic on the S30 line between Cadenazzo and Luino is completely interrupted. A technical failure of the railway system has made it impossible for trains to circulate on the cross-border route. The interruption will continue at least until Wednesday, September 9, 2026 at 4:00 AM, directly affecting cross-border workers who depend on this connection to reach their workplace in Lombardy.

To ensure a minimum connection between Ticino and Italy, replacement bus services have been arranged. However, the situation remains complicated: passengers have been explicitly advised to account for significant waiting times and possible delays. A particularly important warning: connections cannot be guaranteed. This means that those relying on coordinated transfers between different means of transport could find themselves stranded for long periods at stations.

Operational details

How the service disruption affects cross-border commuters

The S30 line disruption directly impacts those who have built a mobility routine on this route. For many cross-border commuters, deciding how to travel over the next two days is not straightforward. The replacement bus is available, but requires rigid planning: waiting times at stops are predictable, but bus schedules do not perfectly align with the train schedules they are meant to replace. Those accustomed to reading the newspaper during their train commute will instead need to find a seat on a potentially crowded bus.

The psychological aspect is also at stake: the bus is slower, exposes commuters to road traffic, introduces variability. A 24-30 hour railway interruption generates stress among cross-border commuters, especially if they have no plan B. Those who have access to a private car might consider driving through border crossings, but this introduces additional fuel costs. Those entirely dependent on public transport have no choice but to adapt to the replacement buses and accept the inevitable delays.

What changes in daily travel management

The official recommendation to 'inform yourself shortly before departure' is not a mere suggestion. It means contacting railway operators, consulting mobility apps (SBB Mobil, Trenord), checking for updates on delays or cancellations. A positive development in repairs could accelerate restoration; a negative development could prolong the interruption. Between 30-60 minutes before departure, it is essential to have the most current information, because the situation can change rapidly.

Key points

How to Organize During the Interruption

If you regularly travel on the Cadenazzo–Luino route for work, consider these concrete steps in the coming two days:

Contact your employer: Report the railway service interruption before the morning. Many employers, especially in Lombardy, understand cross-border service disruptions and can allow a delayed departure or authorize exceptional remote work for that day.

Check official bus schedules: SBB Mobil, Trenord and local bus operators will provide updated schedules. Don't rely on third-hand information or social media. Official apps are the source of truth.

Add buffer time: If your normal journey takes 45 minutes by train, expect 90 minutes by bus. Leave 30-45 minutes earlier than you normally would. This safety margin protects you from surprises.

Consider alternative transport: If you have access to a private car and fuel costs are manageable, consider a private ride or carpooling with other cross-border workers in the same situation. Ask in Ticino cross-border worker WhatsApp groups if anyone is organizing a ride to the Luino area.

Timeline and Deadlines

The interruption continues until Wednesday, September 9, 2026 at 4:00 AM. This means that Tuesday, September 8 (today) the service is blocked, and will remain so for the entire day and the following night. Starting at 4:00 AM on Wednesday morning, normal railway traffic on the route is expected to resume. If you work night shifts or are returning very late Tuesday evening, check if there are replacement buses in the evening hours as well, since some routes may be reduced or cancelled during the night.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long is the S30 line between Cadenazzo and Luino interrupted?
The interruption continues until Wednesday 9 September 2026 at 4:00 am. Starting from thattime, normal rail traffic on the Cadenazzo-Luino route is expected to be restored. During the outage period, replacement buses are available, albeit with possible delays and no guaranteed connections.
Can I count on the connections of the replacement buses?
No, unfortunately coincidences cannot be guaranteed during the outage period. It is advisable to add 30-45 minutes to the usual travel time and check with the operators (SBB Mobil, Trenord) just before departure for the most up-to-date bus schedules.
How do I report the disruption to my employer in Italy?
Contact the employer or department manager in advance, preferably by email or phone before the morning. It reports the technical failure on the S30 railway line and the consequences on travel times. Many employers in Lombardy understand cross-border disruptions and can grant a delayed departure or authorise exceptional smart working.
What is the cause of the interruption of the S30 line?
The interruption is due to a technical failure in the railway system of line S30. According to the available communications, no further details have been provided on the specific nature of the fault or the exact repair times beyond the deadline of 9 September at 4:00 am.
Where can I find the updated times of the replacement buses?
Check SBB Mobil (official SBB app), the Trenord site, or the official communication channels of local rail operators. It is advisable to check the information 30-60 minutes before departure, as schedules can change quickly depending on the status of the repair work.
