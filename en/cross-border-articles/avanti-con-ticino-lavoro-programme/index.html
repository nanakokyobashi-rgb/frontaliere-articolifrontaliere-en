The Avanti con Ticino&Lavoro party has decided to submit its 2027-2031 legislative program to the realities of the territory before its final approval. This innovative approach aims to ensure that the needs and concerns of the Ticino community are at the heart of future policies. The document was sent to 174 associations, professional organisations, bodies, companies, trade unions and Ticino civil society organisations. The consultation will also be open to the population, who will be able to consult the program and send their contributions through the movement's website and social channels.

Operational details

The Avanti con Ticino&Lavoro program is divided into six chapters dedicated to the main areas of cantonal policy: economy and labor, social protection, health, territory and mobility, security and social cohesion, quality of institutions and public finances. The party decided to start the discussion when the document is still editable, in order to refine the program to the realities of the territory.

Economy and Labour

One of the main objectives of the programme is to improve the local economy. The party intends to promote innovation and digitalization of Ticino businesses. For example, the "Ticino Digitale" project aims to make the canton one of the most digitized in Europe, with an investment of 50 million francs for the year 2023. This project includes the creation of a technology hub in Bellinzona, which will host startups and technology companies, thus creating 1,000 new jobs.

Social Protection

Social protection is another pillar of the programme. The party proposes to improve the pension system, with a 5% increase in the retirement rate for workers over 65. This change, which will take effect in 2024, aims to ensure greater financial security for older citizens.
