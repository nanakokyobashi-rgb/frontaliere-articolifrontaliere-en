Permits and Requirements

To work as an engineer in Ticino, you must obtain a G permit, which allows you to work as a border worker. This permit is issued by the Customs and Duties Office of the Canton of Ticino. The process of applying for the G permit can be complex and requires specific documents, such as the engineering diploma and identity card.

Requirements to work as an engineer in Ticino

The requirements to work as an engineer in Ticino are:

  • Engineering diploma awarded by an accredited institution
  • G leave to work as a border worker
  • Identity card
  • Work documents (employment contract, etc.)

Recognition of the qualification

The qualification can be recognised in Switzerland if it has been issued by an accredited institution. This means that the engineering diploma issued by a foreign university can be recognised in Switzerland if the university has been accredited by a recognised body.

Practical analysis

  • The new tax agreement between Switzerland and Italy provides for the exemption of CHF 7,500 for old frontier workers.
  • The new frontier workers have a deductible of CHF 10,000.
  • Double taxation is avoided thanks to the tax credit.
  • The border workers' tax return is managed by the Revenue Agency.

Requirements to work as an engineer in Ticino

To work as an engineer in Ticino, you must reside in the region for at least 183 days a year. Border workers can choose to stay in one of the 8 municipalities of the Bellinzona district, such as Bellinzona, Lugano or Mendrisio.

Title recognition

The title of engineer is recognised in Switzerland if it has been issued in an EU or EFTA country, such as Germany, France or Norway. Otherwise, you must take a qualifying exam at the Ecole Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL) or the University of Italian Switzerland (USI).

Taxes and contributions

Border crossers are subject to taxes and contributions in Switzerland, such as federal income tax (IRF) and municipal tax. However, thanks to the tax agreement with Italy, frontier workers can benefit from an exemption of CHF 10,000 for the first year of work in Switzerland.

Key points

Action

  • To obtain the G permit, you must submit the application to the AFC/ESTV within the established deadline, usually 30 days before the start of the work.
  • It is advisable to consult a lawyer to check the specific requirements and to make sure you have all the necessary documents.
  • In addition, it is important to note that the G permit is valid for a maximum of 2 years and can be renewed.
  • It is possible to apply for a work visa for foreign engineers who wish to work in Ticino.

Requirements

  • To work as an engineer in Ticino, you need to have a recognised educational qualification in Switzerland, i.e. a doctorate or an engineering diploma.
  • You must also have at least 2 years of work experience as an engineer and demonstrate that you have performed tasks similar to those required by work in Ticino.
  • You are also required to have a basic knowledge of the German or Italian language, depending on the region in which you will be working.

Title recognition

  • The qualification recognised in Switzerland is issued by the Swiss Federal Council.
  • It is possible to request recognition of the qualification from the competent authority.
  • The cost of recognition of the qualification is CHF 300.
