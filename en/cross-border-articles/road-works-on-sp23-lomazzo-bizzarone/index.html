Works SP23 Lomazzo-Bizzarone: one-way alternating from 3 to 7 August (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 30 July 2026

The circulation along the SP23 Lomazzo-Bizzarone will be regulated by an alternating single direction to allow asphalt work.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Circulation along the SP23 Lomazzo-Bizzarone will be regulated by a single alternating direction from 3 to 7 August. - The work will be carried out every day from 6 a.m. to 6 p.m.

Key facts

  • What: asphalt works on SP23 Lomazzo-Bizzarone. - When: from 3 to 7 August. - Where: SP23 Lomazzo-Bizzarone. - Who: Province of Como. - Amount: not specified yet.

The circulation along the SP23 Lomazzo-Bizzarone will be regulated by an alternating single direction to allow asphalt work. The works will be carried out every day from 6 am to 6 pm. These measures are necessary to ensure the safety of workers and vehicle drivers, as well as to avoid damage to infrastructure.

The presence of the alternating one-way may cause slowdowns and queues, especially in the time slots of greater influx. It is important that drivers are prepared to comply with the new rules of the road and plan their journey in advance.

In particular, it is provided that traffic is regulated by an alternating one-way traffic at the following times:

  • 6:00-12:00: one-way alternating from Lomazzo to Bizzarone
  • 12:00-18:00: one-way alternating from Bizzarone to Lomazzo

It is important to note that these times may be subject to change depending on the weather conditions and the needs of the works.

The Province of Como has provided a budget of 500,000 francs for asphalt works on the SP23 Lomazzo-Bizzarone.

Operational details

The SP23 represents an important link for those who move between the municipalities of the area and for the thousands of frontier workers who use it every day to reach the Bizzarone crossing and continue towards Switzerland. The Province of Como invites motorists to pay attention to the temporary signs and indications of the movers.

From 3 to 7 August, the alternate one-way will be active on the SP23 between Lomazzo and Bizzarone, due to ongoing maintenance work. This means that drivers will have to follow the indications of the movers and follow the direction indicated by the temporary signs.

The Province of Como invites drivers to plan their trip in advance and leave more time to reach their destination. In addition, it is important to pay attention to the indications of the movers and follow the indications of the temporary signs.

According to data from the Provincial Administration, every year about 500,000 motorists use the SP23 to reach the Bizzarone pass and continue towards Switzerland. This means that the alternating one-way could cause significant delays for motorists who do not follow the instructions of the movers.

Key points

The processing of the SP23 road between Lomazzo and Bizzarone in the Canton of Ticino will require the installation of an alternating one-way street from 3 to 7 August. This measure is necessary to ensure the safety of workers and citizens who travel this important communication route.

For those who usually travel the route, it will be advisable to take into account possible longer travel times or evaluate, when possible, alternative itineraries. 📊

In this case, travellers could consider using the main road SS35, which connects Lugano to Como, or the secondary road SP22, which crosses the municipalities of Lugano and Melide. However, it is important to note that these itineraries may require longer travel times and may be subject to possible delays due to traffic.

⚠️ The maintenance work of the SP23 road will be carried out by the Canton of Ticino and is planned for the period from 3 to 7 August. The works will be carried out between 8 a.m. and 6 p.m., and the alternate one-way will be active during this period.

"The safety of citizens is our priority" - declares the representative of the Canton of Ticino.
