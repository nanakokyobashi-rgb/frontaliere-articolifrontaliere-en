Key points

Working in Switzerland: What You Need to Know

To work in Switzerland, you need to obtain a work permit. This permit is issued by the Canton of Ticino. The procedure for requesting a work permit is relatively simple and quick. The application must be submitted at the Canton of Ticino office in Lugano, and it requires the presentation of various documents and the payment of a release fee.

Required Documents

To obtain a work permit, you need to present a series of specific documents. These include:

  • ID card or passport: It should be valid for at least 6 months. - Residence permit: If you are already in Switzerland, you need to present your current residence permit. - Work contract: A valid work contract must be presented. - Certificate of registration with the labor registry: This certificate is necessary to demonstrate that the worker is registered as such. - Health insurance certificate: You need to have a valid health insurance coverage.

Procedure and Fees

The procedure for requesting a work permit is simple and quick. The release fee for the work permit varies depending on the type of permit requested. For example, for a work permit at indefinite term, the fee is approximately 200 CHF. The procedure can be completed in about 2-3 weeks, but it is advisable to check the deadlines and the regulations in force.

Practical Examples

A practical example is the case of an Italian worker who wants to work in Lugano. The worker must submit the application at the Canton of Ticino office in Lugano, pay the release fee, and wait for the issuance of the work permit. If everything is in order, the permit will be issued within 2-3 weeks.
