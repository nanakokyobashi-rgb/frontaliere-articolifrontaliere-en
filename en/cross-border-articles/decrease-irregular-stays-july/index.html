Irregular stays declining at southern border in July (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 16 August 2026

In July, cases registered by the Federal Office of Customs and Border Security fell to 249 in Ticino.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Irregular stays in July at a height of 249 in Ticino
  • Down from 336 cases in June
  • Total illegal crossings in Switzerland amounting to 630

Key facts

  • What: Detection of irregular stays at the border
  • When: July
  • Where: Ticino Canton and southern border
  • Who: Federal Office of Customs and Border Security
  • Amount: 249 cases registered in Ticino in July

Irregular stay data recorded in the monthly statistics of the Federal Office of Customs and Border Security fell again in July. A further decrease was also noted at the southern border, in Ticino, where the largest number of cases recorded by the federal customs authorities is concentrated, in particular in the sectors of Chiasso Stradale, Ponte Tresa and Stabio, hotspots of cross-border traffic. During the month of July, a total of 249 cases were detected in the cantonal territory, showing a decrease compared to the 336 cases that had been recorded during the previous month of June.

National data analysis

Looking at the entire Swiss territory, 630 border crossings were considered illegal. This figure shows a clear inferiority compared to the 739 passages recorded in the month of June and represents less than half when compared to the month of July last year, a period in which 1,564 cases had been recorded. Operational details

Nationality and citizenship of migrants

Among the migrants who entered Switzerland non-canonically during the month of July, Turkish citizens were the most represented, according to the Federal Office of Customs and Border Security. The analysis of flows recorded along national borders, in particular at the Chiasso Brogeda, Stabio and Ponte Tresa crossings, allows to monitor the origin and distribution of people who cross borders irregularly, providing an updated framework to the competent authorities for customs management and border security. This dynamic directly affects the cantonal operating costs and the management of the registration centres managed by the Confederation according to the Asylum Act of 26 June 1998.

Suspected Passers and Readmissions

From the point of view of the suspected passers-by intercepted by the customs authorities, there are no particular deviations from the current trend and the comparison with the previous year. In July, 8 suspicious passersby were intercepted, compared to 7 recorded in June and 10 detected in the same period of the previous year. During the first seven months of the year, the total number of passers-by identified amounted to 68, with financial penalties that, according to article 116 of the Federal Law on Foreigners and their Integration, can lead to fines of up to 500,000francs in cases of crime

Key points

Flow management and customs controls

The monitoring activity of the Federal Office of Customs and Border Security continues regularly along all routes and crossings affected by migratory and cross-border flows, such as those of Chiasso-Strada and Ponte Tresa. The monthly collection of statistical data makes it possible to trace the evolution of phenomena related to irregular stays and activities to combat the facilitation of illegal immigration, applying the Federal Law on Foreigners and their Integration of 16 December 2005. The figures for July confirm the decline already observed in other recent months, offering an official numerical baseline to assess the trend of security along the southern border and in the rest of the country.

For workers and businesses straddling the border, this stability is also reflected in day-to-day administrative management.
