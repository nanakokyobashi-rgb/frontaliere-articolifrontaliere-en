Varese, onboard ticket costs twice as much: the testimony (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 26 September 2026

A student at Università dell'Insubria paid 3,20 euro for an onboard bus ticket, twice the normal price according to her testimony, after card payment did not work.

The on-board ticket purchased on a bus heading to the Bizzozero university campus cost 3,20 euro according to the receipt attached to the report. The amount is double the normal fare indicated by the student.

The student at Università dell'Insubria wrote to VareseNews. She and some classmates had not bought the ticket in advance and were not subscribers: they thought they could purchase it on board.

On board, however, the card payment device was not working, according to her account. “It was therefore only possible to buy the ticket with coins,” the student recounts. The driver issued the tickets, applying the surcharge provided for those who had not purchased them beforehand and did not have a pass.

The fare applied

The surcharge for purchasing on board is provided for by the service rules. Therefore, formally speaking, the driver's behavior is not an anomaly. The letter instead raises a question about occasional access: how easy is it to use the bus in Varese without being regular users?

In the reported case, it was possible to buy the ticket on board, but card payment was not available on the service in question, leaving coins as the only option. The receipt made the effect of the surcharge visible: 3,20 euro.

Information for Bizzozero

The report comes as the Bizzozero Campus changes its access, traffic flow and parking arrangements. From 23 settembre, new arrangements linked to work for new parking areas and, later, a multifunctional building came into force.

Università dell'Insubria invited students and staff, whenever possible, to favor public transport. It points to urban lines C and E and the additional services of the university reinforcement service as alternatives to the car.

The university is also continuing the Bus Insubria service between central Varese and Bizzozero, carpooling, and agreements for shared bicycles and scooters. The initiatives dedicated to university mobility focus on the Varese Campus and the need to reduce the number of cars heading to the campus every day.

Operational details

The point for those who use the service occasionally

The report separates two aspects that often end up in the same incident. On the one hand, there is a fare rule: a ticket bought on board entails a surcharge. On the other, there is the experience of a user who decides to take the bus without a pass and encounters electronic payment unavailable on the service described. The rule may be applied correctly, but the service may be less straightforward for those who use it only occasionally.

Preparing or deciding at the last minute

The useful comparison is not between those who respected or violated a rule. In the case described, no one disputes that the surcharge is provided for. The comparison is between two ways of accessing the service: organizing in advance, or boarding and relying on the possibility of purchasing the ticket on board. The second option exists, but the report shows that it depends on the conditions present on the vehicle and that, in that case, it required coins.

It says that the receipt in question showed that amount and that the student considered it twice the regular price.

A cross-border commuter who found themselves in a similar situation and had to use a Varese urban bus occasionally would have a limited practical fact. The source provides no information about cross-border connections, timetables or different fares: it therefore does not make it possible to construct a route or calculate costs for someone arriving from outside. It does, however, make it possible to recognize the general problem of accessing the service without advance preparation.

The University’s invitation to leave the car, phrased as when possible, places ease of use within the choice. Anyone considering the bus must take the ticket, available payment method and surcharge into account; the source does not quantify the comparison. costo auto pendolare therefore remains a separate tool.
