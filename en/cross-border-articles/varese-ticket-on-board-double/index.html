The ticket purchased on board a bus headed to the Bizzozero university center cost 3.20 euros according to the receipt attached to the report. The amount is double the normal travel ticket indicated by the student.

The student from the University of Insubria wrote to VareseNews. She and some companions had not bought the ticket in advance and were not season ticket holders: they thought they would buy it on the bus.

On board, however, the card payment device was not working, according to the story. «It was therefore possible to buy the ticket only with the coin» says the student. The driver issued the tickets, applying the expected surcharge to those who had not purchased them before and did not have a season ticket.

The rate applied

The surcharge for purchasing on board is foreseen by the rules of the service. Therefore, on a formal level, the driver's behavior is not an anomaly. The letter instead raises a question about occasional access: how easy is it to use the bus in Varese without being a regular user?

In the reported case, there was the possibility of buying on board, but the card was not available on the reported journey and the currency remained. The receipt made the effect of the increase visible: 3.20 euros.

Directions to Bizzozero

The report comes as the Bizzozero Campus changes access, roads and parking. From 23 September, new methods related to the works for new parking areas and, later, for a multifunctional building came into force.

The University of Insubria has invited students and staff, when possible, to prefer public transport. He indicates the urban lines C and E and the additional trips of the university reinforcement service as alternatives to the car.

The University also carries out the Insubria Bus between the center of Varese and Bizzozero, car pooling and agreements for sharing bicycles and scooters. The initiatives dedicated to university mobility pay attention to the Varese Campus and the need to reduce the number of cars heading to the campus every day.
