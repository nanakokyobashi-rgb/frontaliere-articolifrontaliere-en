Tradate, accident involving an electric vehicle: one person injured

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 27 September 2026

Incident at 7.01 on Via Bruno Passerini in Tradate: a 25-year-old young man involved. Ambulance, advanced rescue services and carabinieri on site.

At 7.01 on Friday, September 25, emergency services were alerted to a road accident that occurred in Tradate, on via Bruno Passerini, at number 1. A 25-year-old young man who was riding an electric micromobility vehicle was involved in the accident.

The report triggered the medical response that morning. An ambulance from Croce Rossa and a first-level advanced rescue vehicle arrived at the scene. The two units were coordinated by Soreu dei Laghi, the service indicated in the reconstruction of the response.

The ambulance was dispatched under a yellow code. The advanced rescue vehicle, meanwhile, was reportedly operating under a green code. The carabinieri of the Saronno Company were also alerted.

The news therefore concerns an incident that occurred on a specific street in Tradate, in the early hours of the day, involving a young man and an electric vehicle intended for micromobility. The source does not describe any other vehicles involved and does not indicate any other people affected by the accident.

At the time of the initial reconstruction, the response was still ongoing. No further information was available about the young man's condition or the dynamics of the accident. These are the details communicated about the incident: the alert time, the location, the age of the person involved, the rescue vehicles mobilized and the presence of the Saronno carabinieri.
