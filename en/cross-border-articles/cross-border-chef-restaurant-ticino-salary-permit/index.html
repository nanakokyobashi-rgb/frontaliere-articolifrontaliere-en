Introduction

Working as a cook in Ticino border catering offers a good opportunity for border workers with a regular G permit. This article will provide you with detailed information on salary, hours and industry CCLs for this position.

Salary

According to Swiss and Italian regulations, Ticino border cooks can be remunerated with a salary ranging from 2,200 to 3,000 francs per month, plus any bonuses. A concrete example is that of a cook who works for a border restaurant in Ticino who pays a basic salary of 2,500 francs per month and loyalty bonuses that can bring the total to about 3,000 francs per month.

Working hours

Ticino border cooks generally work 6 to 7 days a week, with alternating evening and day shifts. Longer shifts can be granted to frontier workers with proven experience and good referencing.
