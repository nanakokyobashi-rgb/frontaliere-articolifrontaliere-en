Family Bonus 2026: Benefits for Cross-Border Workers (cross-border guide)

Di Laura Bianchi · Published on 26 February 2026 · Updated on 25 September 2026

Discover all the bonuses and benefits for cross-border worker families in 2026: family allowances, tax deductions, and new measures.

Context

TL;DR

  • Cross-border families living in Italy combine Swiss and Italian benefits
  • Ticino family allowances 2026: CHF 215/month per child, CHF 268 for children in education
  • INPS daycare bonus 2026: up to €3,600 a year, for authorised nurseries in Italy
  • The €950 Italian tax deduction applies only to dependent children aged 21 to 29; younger children get the INPS single allowance

Key facts

  • Family Allowances: CHF 215/month per child up to 16 (CHF 2,580 a year), CHF 268/month for children in education up to 25 (CHF 3,216 a year), Ticino 2026
  • Daycare Bonus: INPS contribution up to €3,600 a year (children born from 2024, neutralised ISEE up to €40,000); €3,000 to €1,500 for children born earlier
  • Tax Deductions: €950 per child only for dependent children aged 21 to 29 (or disabled from 21); under 21 the INPS single allowance (assegno unico) applies
  • Application Deadline: no fixed deadline for Swiss family allowances; arrears can be claimed for up to 5 years
  • Tax Return Submission: 730 form by 30 September 2026; Redditi PF online by 2 November 2026
  • Daycare Requirements: residence in Italy and a public or private nursery authorised under Italian regional law
  • Regulatory Application: Swiss allowances also for children living in Italy (EU/EFTA export), for cross-border workers with a G permit

In 2026, the families of cross-border workers employed in Ticino and living in Italy can rely on benefits from two countries. Switzerland pays family allowances through the employer; Italy, based on residence, pays the INPS single universal allowance (assegno unico) and the daycare bonus, plus the IRPEF deduction for older children.

Family Allowances

In Ticino the child allowance is CHF 215 a month (CHF 2,580 a year) from birth up to age 16; the education allowance is CHF 268 a month (CHF 3,216 a year) for children in education, up to age 25 at the latest. Cross-border workers with a G permit also receive them for children living in Italy, because Switzerland exports them to EU/EFTA states, provided they earn at least CHF 630 a month. The claim is submitted to the employer, who pays the allowance with the salary on behalf of the family allowance fund the company belongs to.

If one parent works in Switzerland and the other in Italy, the priority rules of Regulation (EC) 883/2004 apply, including to Switzerland: if the children live in Italy, where the other parent works, Italy pays the single allowance first and the Swiss fund only pays any difference. If only the cross-border worker is employed, Switzerland has priority and INPS may pay a differential supplement of the single allowance if that is higher.

Daycare Bonus

The daycare contribution is not a Ticino measure: cantonal support for nurseries and childminders is reserved for families resident in Ticino. Families living in Italy can apply to INPS for the daycare bonus (bonus asilo nido): in 2026 it is worth up to €3,600 a year for children born from 1 January 2024 with a neutralised ISEE up to €40,000, and €1,500 above that threshold or without ISEE. For children born before 2024 the amounts are €3,000, €2,500 or €1,500 depending on the ISEE. It only covers the fees of public or private nurseries authorised under regional law.

📊 Key Data:

  • Ticino family allowance amounts 2026: CHF 2,580-3,216 a year per child
  • INPS daycare bonus 2026: up to €3,600 a year
  • Regulatory application: cross-border workers with a G permit, including children resident in Italy

Operational details

The fiscal implications mainly concern new cross-border workers, who declare their Swiss salary in Italy, also considering the additional IRPEF tax in border municipalities. Under the new tax agreement between Italy and Switzerland they recover the taxes paid in Switzerland through a tax credit; for children, the ordinary IRPEF rules apply.

Tax Deductions for Dependent Children

Since March 2022, the deduction for children under 21 has been replaced by the INPS single universal allowance (assegno unico), which is paid to people resident and domiciled in Italy and is calculated on the ISEE. The €950 per child deduction has not increased: since 2025 it applies only to dependent children aged 21 to 29, or from 21 with a certified disability. In the tax return you use the data from the salary certificate (Lohnausweis) issued by the Swiss employer.

⚠️ Important Deadlines:

  • Swiss family allowances: no fixed deadline, arrears can be claimed for up to 5 years
  • Italian tax return: 730 form by 30 September 2026, Redditi PF online by 2 November 2026
  • Daycare bonus: INPS application by 31 December 2026, valid until August of the year the child turns 3

Daycare Bonus Requirements

  • Residence in Italy and Italian or EU citizenship (or a valid residence permit)
  • Child under 3 enrolled in a public or private nursery authorised under regional law
  • Invoices or receipts for the fees paid

💡 Practical Tip: Keep your salary certificate, nursery receipts and an up-to-date ISEE together. For the tax return, see our guide.

Key points

These bonuses represent a tangible opportunity for cross-border workers to ease the financial burden of family management. However, it is crucial to plan ahead and leverage the available tools to maximize fiscal advantages.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the family benefits for frontier workers in Ticino in 2026?
Ticino family allowances are CHF 215 a month per child up to 16 and CHF 268 a month for children in education up to 25, i.e. CHF 2,580 to CHF 3,216 a year, also for children living in Italy. In Italy, based on residence, there are the INPS single allowance and the daycare bonus of up to €3,600 a year; the €950 IRPEF deduction only remains for dependent children aged 21 to 29.
How do cross-border workers apply for the daycare bonus in 2026?
The daycare bonus is requested online from INPS by 31 December 2026, and the application remains valid until August of the year the child turns 3. You need residence in Italy, a public or private nursery authorised under regional law and receipts for the fees paid. Ticino's cantonal support for nurseries is reserved for families resident in Ticino.
How do cross-border workers obtain tax deductions for children in Italy in 2026?
The €950 deduction only applies to dependent children aged 21 to 29 (or disabled from 21); younger children get the INPS single allowance. New cross-border workers claim it in the 730 form, due by 30 September 2026, or in the Redditi PF return online by 2 November 2026, using the data from the salary certificate issued by the Swiss employer.
