One of the main attractions of Bisuschio is its geographical location. The municipality is located a few kilometers from the border with Italy, making it easy for border workers to reach their places of work. In addition, the presence of an international airport in Malpensa, about a 30-minute drive from Bisuschio, offers an additional fast and convenient transport option.

The Ticino Immigration Law (Law on Exploitation of Foreigners' Labour of 1 September 1983) stipulates that foreigners may work in Ticino only if they are in possession of a residence permit. This permit can only be obtained if you have an employment contract with an employer registered with the Chamber of Commerce and Industry of the Canton of Ticino. The application for a residence permit must be submitted to the Swiss Consulate in Italy within 8 working days of the date of arrival in Switzerland.
