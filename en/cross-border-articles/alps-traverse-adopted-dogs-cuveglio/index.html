Third week of walking for Cristian and Mara along the Great Alpine Crossing together with their adopted dogs in a kennel.

Key facts

  • What: Great Alpine Crossing on foot
  • When: From Monday 10 August to the beginning of September
  • Where: From Ventimiglia to Cuveglio
  • Who: Cristian Blasi and Mara Migliorini with dogs
  • Amount: Approximately 800 kilometres and significant difference in altitude

The third week of walking for Cristian Blasi and Mara Migliorini, originally from Cuvio, engaged in a route of about 800 kilometers along the Great Crossing of the Alps, officially began on Monday, August 10. By the end of the second week, the couple's odometer already scored about 267 kilometers. The trip takes advantage of the company in turn of the four dogs owned by the couple, all adopted in a kennel: after the first stages that saw Nanà and Sammy as protagonists, the relay now continues with the arrival of Akira, who joined the group during the day on Sunday. This running initiative aims to promote the adoption of animals from shelters, demonstrating how an adopted dog can integrate perfectly into daily life and travel with his family in challenging contexts. Operational details

The choice to undertake a path of this magnitude together with their adopted pets responds to the precise desire to raise public awareness on the issue of adoptions in shelters. We often tend to consider an animal from a kennel context as a bearer of insurmountable limits or difficulties, but the experience lived by Cristian and Mara intends to overturn this common perception. Respecting the specific needs, times and characteristics of each dog, it is possible to share excursions, trips and long crossings without incurring particular criticalities, demonstrating a high level of bonding between human and animal. The daily logistics organization requires careful planning of the stages, taking into account both the physical condition of the hikers and the well-being of the dogs that alternate along the way. Managing the weight in your backpack, choosing suitable shelters and using camping equipment when necessary are central elements of this hiking adventure. The itinerary runs entirely along the Piedmontese alpine chain, offering outstanding naturalistic scenery but at the same time requiring constant physical preparation to cope with the differences accumulated day after day. The conclusion of the long march is expected approximately in a time window between 2 and 6 September, with the arrival in the territory of residence located between Cuvio and Cuveglio. To celebrate the conclusion of the venture and share the last kilometres with anyone who wants to take part in the event, Cristian and Mara have decided to officially open the last part of the route to the public. The appointment for this final stage is set in the stretch that connects Laveno to Cuveglio, offering supporters, mountain enthusiasts and supporters of the project the opportunity to walk together with the couple and the dogs in this final stretch.
