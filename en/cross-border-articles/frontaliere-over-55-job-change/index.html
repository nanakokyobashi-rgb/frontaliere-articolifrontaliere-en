Context

In a nutshell

The New Frontier Agreement, which entered into force on 23 December 2020, provides for a transitional regime for border workers who were already border workers before 17 July 2023. New frontier workers, on the other hand, are not subject to this transitional regime. This regime has been established for 10 years.

Change border job after age 55: protections and impact on second pillar and pension

With the expiry of the transitional regime for frontier workers on 17 July 2033, many Ticino workers could change jobs. As for the protections, there is no significant news. Retirement, on the other hand, will have a significant impact, especially with regard to the second pillar.

Pension impacts

For frontier workers who have never joined the transitional regime, the pension will be calculated on the basis of the unipolar system, without a second installment. For those who have joined, the pension will be calculated according to the two-faced system, with a second installment. The first installment is based on total income, and the calculation of the second installment depends on several factors, including age on January 1 of the first year of retirement, contributions paid, contributions paid, and an age coefficient.

Practical analysis

Frontier workers over 55 can change jobs without losing the benefits of the second pillar thanks to the New Frontier Agreement. This means they can keep their rights to the second pillar even if they change jobs.

The benefits of the new border agreement

The New Frontier Agreement introduced new protections for job-changing border workers over 55. For example, if before 1 July 2022 a frontier worker over 55 changes jobs, he/she will be able to maintain his/her rights to the second pillar and to the old-age pension, provided that the job has been taken out by a company that is in good standing with social contributions and that has its residence in Ticino.

The requirements to maintain the benefits of the second pillar

To maintain the benefits of the second pillar, the frontier worker over 55 must comply with the following requirements:

  • must be over 55 years old at the time of termination of previous employment
  • must have worked at least 24 months at a company that is in compliance with social contributions (mandatory for the first year of work at that company)
  • must be resident in Ticino
