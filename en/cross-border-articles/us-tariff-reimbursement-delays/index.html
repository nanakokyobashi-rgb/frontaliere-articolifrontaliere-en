US Tariffs: Delayed Refunds, What Happens to Cross-Border Workers?

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 7 March 2026

The US Customs Agency is working on a refund system for illegal tariffs, but the process could take up to a month. What does this mean for cross-border workers?

Context

TL;DR

  • US Customs can't refund illegal tariffs immediately
  • Cross-border workers may wait up to a month for refunds
  • Keep all receipts and documents for refund requests

Key facts

  • Tariffs declared: Illegal by the Supreme Court
  • Refund delay: Up to a month for cross-border workers
  • Required documents: Receipts and import-related documents
  • Refund process: Complex and time-consuming
  • Impacted workers: Cross-border workers who paid illegal tariffs
  • Supreme Court ruling: Strikes down most import tariffs
  • Refund eligibility: Depends on amount paid and goods type
  • Processing times: May vary based on request volume

The announcement by the US Customs Agency has caused concern among cross-border workers who work in the United States. Official Brandon Lord stated that the agency is unable to immediately refund the tariffs imposed by the Trump administration, which have been declared illegal by the Supreme Court. This means that cross-border workers who have paid these tariffs may have to wait up to a month to receive their refund.

The refund process is complex and time-consuming. The agency is working to develop a system that will efficiently refund the tariffs, but this process requires time and resources. Cross-border workers who have paid the illegal tariffs may have to wait up to a month to see the refund on their account.

The situation is particularly delicate for cross-border workers who have paid tariffs on high-value goods imports. These tariffs, now illegal, may have resulted in a significant financial outlay for cross-border workers, and the delay in refunds could cause financial difficulties. It is important for cross-border workers to be aware of their rights and the procedures to follow to obtain a refund.

For cross-border workers who have paid the illegal tariffs, it is crucial to keep all receipts and documents related to the imports. These documents will be necessary to prove the amount paid and to request the refund. It is advisable to organize the documents so that they are easily accessible when it is time to submit the refund request.

It is also important to be aware of the deadlines and timelines for submitting refund requests. The US Customs Agency may set specific deadlines for submitting requests, and cross-border workers will need to ensure they meet these terms to avoid losing their right to a refund.

In this context, it is essential for cross-border workers to stay informed and up-to-date on the latest news regarding tariff refunds. The US Customs Agency's website should provide updated information on the refund process and any deadlines to be met.

It is important to emphasize that the refund process for illegal tariffs is complex, and the US Customs Agency is working to simplify and speed up the process. However, cross-border workers will need to be patient and organized during this waiting period.

Operational details

The Supreme Court's decision to strike down most import tariffs has had a significant impact on the American customs system. Trade lawyers have stated that the ruling could apply to nearly all imports for which the now-illegal tariffs were paid. This means that a significant number of importers may be eligible to receive refunds.

The refund process is governed by specific rules and procedures. The US Customs Agency is working to develop a system that will efficiently refund the tariffs while complying with the regulations. This process requires time and resources, but it is essential to ensure that refunds are issued correctly and transparently.

For cross-border workers who have paid the illegal tariffs, it is important to understand the procedures to follow to obtain a refund. Firstly, it is necessary to check if they are eligible for a refund. This depends on the amount paid and the type of goods imported. It is advisable to consult an expert in customs matters to assess their situation and determine if they are entitled to a refund.

Once eligibility is confirmed, it is necessary to follow the procedures established by the US Customs Agency to submit the refund request. This process may require completing specific forms and providing supporting documentation for the request. It is crucial to carefully follow the agency's instructions to avoid delays or errors in the procedure.

Additionally, it is important to be aware of the processing times for refund requests. The US Customs Agency may set specific timelines for evaluating and processing requests. Cross-border workers will need to be patient and wait for the process to be completed, bearing in mind that the times may vary depending on the volume of requests to be processed.
