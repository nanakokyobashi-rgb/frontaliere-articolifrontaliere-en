Reduce recovery times after the A2 Mezzovico incident (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 25 September 2026

Lorenzo Quadri (Lega) presents a motion to reduce restoration times after the accident on the A2 in Mezzovico: closure lasting over 5 hours, queues up to 13 km.

Context

In brief

  • Lorenzo Quadri calls for faster response times after accidents
  • In Mezzovico, the A2 remained closed for more than five hours
  • The queues reached 13 kilometres
  • The motion proposes coordination and the use of drones

Lorenzo Quadri, National Councillor of Lega dei Ticinesi, presented a motion to the Federal Council to significantly reduce the time required to restore motorway traffic after accidents. The request stems from an incident on the A2 in Mezzovico: northbound traffic remained blocked, and full reopening came more than five hours after the accident.

The source reports two dates: 5 August in the standfirst and 8 August in the passage reproducing the motion. The text nevertheless highlights the same operational elements: blocked traffic, full reopening more than five hours later, and queues of up to 13 kilometres. In the motion submitted in Bern, Quadri describes some timeframes as “really difficult to justify.”

The problem identified in the motion

The source does not directly mention cross-border workers. For people in this situation, the practical connection exists only when their own route coincides with the road network concerned. The source does not quantify how many workers were involved, indicate an alternative schedule, or propose a replacement route. It does, however, describe the consequences that, according to the motion, situations of this kind can cause: inconvenience to users, lost work hours, delays in transporting people and goods, higher consumption, and repercussions on the secondary road network.

The source presents a request to the Federal Council, not the announcement of a procedure already in force. It calls for the timeframes to be reviewed and, where necessary, for the way operations are organized to be reconsidered. The management of national roads, the text specifies, falls within the remit of the Confederation and Ustra, the Federal Roads Office.

The requested review does not concern a single entity. Quadri points to coordination between Ustra, cantonal authorities, the police, emergency services and companies tasked with vehicle recovery. The political objective is to reduce the time required to restore normal traffic conditions, without separating this need from rescue, safety and fact-finding activities. These elements define the scope of the motion; the source does not attribute the duration of the closure to a specific body and does not announce a decision by the Federal Council.

Operational details

The practical point for those who commute

From full reopening to partial reopening

The proposal does not indicate a new route to avoid the A2. It asks for the phase following an accident to be measured differently: the Federal Council should verify target times for at least a partial reopening of the carriageway. The condition is precise: the necessary rescue operations, safety measures and investigations must be guaranteed.

For the cross-border commuter facing a closure, the distinction puts the moment when part of the carriageway could become available again in the foreground, even without a full reopening. However, this is the implication of the request, not an announced service. The motion does not set out minutes, indicate a lane or guarantee passage. Based on the source, it does not change the situation of anyone who has to cross the section.

The value of the proposal lies in connecting two requirements that the text holds together: significantly reducing restoration times and maintaining the necessary investigations. A cross-border commuter can use this distinction to read any future communications: a partial reopening is not equivalent to a full one, and neither is taken for granted by the motion.

The chain of responsibilities

The second aspect is organizational. Quadri asks for procedures and coordination between Ustra, cantonal authorities, the police, rescue services and companies tasked with vehicle recovery to be reviewed and, if necessary, revised. The source does not say which group determined the duration of the closure at Mezzovico: the case cannot become an accusation against a single party.

The request for an intercantonal comparison opens another perspective. The motion proposes comparing motorway closure and reopening times after similar accidents, looking for differences in the procedures and intervention methods of the various Cantons. No ranking, results or better procedure are provided. For the cross-border commuter, the comparison would make sense only as a basis for understanding any differences, once the requested elements are available.

Technology is the final piece. The motion calls for examining a more extensive use of technological means to collect and secure evidence, citing surveys conducted via drones. The source does not announce drones already being used on the A2 and does not link their use to a precise time. It presents them as a possibility to be examined, within a management approach that must keep surveys and restoration together. Anyone following the effect of closures on their own route can consult the storico traffico/code ai valichi, keeping mobility data separate from the parliamentary request.

Key points

How to read the proposal without confusing it with a decision

Commuter checklist

The source presents a parliamentary act and the measures requested, not an individual procedure for the user. For a cross-border commuter, the concrete action compatible with the available data is therefore an orderly review: distinguish the episode cited, the requests addressed to the Federal Council, and what might eventually be decided afterward. The following sequence only restates the elements present in the motion.

1. Identify the stretch. If one's journey concerns the A2 at Mezzovico northbound, the reference is the blockage described in the source. One can note the full reopening more than five hours after the accident and queues extending up to 13 kilometres. No times or routes not indicated by the text should be added. 2. Separate the phases. The request for target times concerns at least a partial reopening after rescue operations, safety and the necessary investigations have been ensured. These requirements are the conditions explicitly set out in the act. 3. Identify the parties. The scope indicated includes the Confederation and Ustra, cantonal authorities, police, emergency services and companies tasked with recovering vehicles. The source does not assign the cross-border commuter an application to submit to any of these bodies. 4. Check the comparison. The act calls for an intercantonal comparison of closure and reopening times after similar accidents. The text provided contains no results, rankings or differences already measured. The information to look for, if it is produced, is therefore the requested comparison, not an advance conclusion. 5. Distinguish the technology. Drones are cited as a notable example for collecting and securing evidence. The source mentions their use as a possibility to examine, but does not report their use in the Mezzovico case. To monitor their own route, the cross-border commuter can supplement reading the news report with consulting storico traffico/code ai valichi, without considering the tool an official communication about the A2.

This procedure also helps read the economic consequences without inflating them. The motion lists lost working hours, delays in the transport of people and goods, higher consumption and repercussions on the secondary road network. However, it does not assign an amount to an individual user and does not quantify the cost of a traffic queue. For a cross-border commuter, those four elements are categories of impact described by the source, not rights or figures already recognized.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the incident that motivated Lorenzo Quadri's motion?
The accident occurred on the A2 motorway in Mezzovico, heading north. The circulation remained blocked and the complete reopening took place more than five hours after the event, while the columns of vehicles reached a maximum length of about 13 kilometers. This incident prompted National Councillor Lorenzo Quadri to present the motion to the Federal Council.
What exactly does the motion ask the Federal Council?
The motion asks to verify the recovery timelines and, where necessary, review the organization of operations. It proposes to define target times for an at least partial reopening of the roadway after rescue, safety and investigations have been guaranteed. It also calls for coordination between Ustra, cantonal authorities, police, rescue services and companies in charge of vehicle recovery, as well as the more extensive use of drones for surveys.
What are the consequences mentioned in the motion for users, including border crossers?
The motion lists several inconveniences: difficulties for users, lost working hours, delays in the transport of people and goods, increased fuel consumption and repercussions on the subordinate road network. For those who live as a border worker, the discomfort occurs when their route coincides with the motorway section affected by the closure.
