OCST services are provided according to specific regulations. The page dedicated to the association presents a range of services that, for cross-border workers, begins with the employment relationship and extends to cross-border tax and social issues.

For cross-border workers, the list brings together workplace support, social information and benefits reserved for female and male OCST members.

The scope of assistance

The first level is legal assistance and/or advice. It concerns issues relating to the employment relationship and payroll, but also includes the social insurance schemes AVS, AI, LADI and LPP. The same area covers illness and accidents, family allowances, taxation and tenancy. For a cross-border worker, the list therefore brings together several areas of advice.

Support is provided for strike actions, as are contributions for training. Access to these services is governed by specific regulations, which members must consult to verify the conditions.

The Cross-Border Workers' Office

Assistance for cross-border workers includes advice and information on cross-border tax law, social security, occupational pensions, social insurance, work permits, family allowances and unemployment benefits. The services also include coverage, at the member’s expense, of the costs of emergency hospitalisation in the event of illness and free membership in CISL.

This support is complemented by the presence of patronato offices: INAS operates at the OCST offices in Chiasso, Mendrisio, Bioggio and Locarno, while ACLI is present at the Lugano office. Both are affiliated with the trade union.

The picture is completed by regular information meetings, the newspaper «il Lavoro», the OCST website, the current-affairs newsletter and the Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn channels. For special offers, the procedure differs: in some cases, a form must be requested via the form; in others, the possibility must be reported to one’s insurer. This distinction, already indicated on the page, determines the first step for using the benefit.

Key points

The operating procedure starts with the subject matter and ends with the channel indicated for the benefit. To avoid unforeseen steps, the member can follow a sequence based on the methods described on the page.

1. Identify the service

The first choice is to distinguish legal advice from the commercial agreement. For issues concerning employment relationships, payslips, AVS, AI, LADI, LPP, illness, accidents, family allowances, taxation or tenancy, the page places the matter under legal and juridical assistance and/or advice. If the issue concerns cross-border tax law, social security, occupational pension provision, work permits or unemployment benefits, the indicated reference is the Cross-Border Workers' Office. Subsidies for strike action and training contributions are among the listed services and follow specific regulations.

2. Submit the correct request

For special offers, use the form to request the application form. The page indicates this in several places, so the form is the step to follow when the agreement requires it. For insurance coverage, the indicated procedure is different: notify your insurer of the possibility. The channel specified by the offer should not be replaced with a generic one.

3. Apply the card or code

For Brico SA, Ristorante Elvezia 31, Old Wild West di Mendrisio, Pizzarella-Burgerella and Pescheria Lawrence, the source indicates presenting the OCST card. For Masa Coffee, the discount code is «OCST». For the bank account, the OCST promotional code is available from the OCST contact person; opening takes place 100% online through the CA and OCST - Corporate partner offer. These are different operating methods and must be kept separate.

4. Check timing and access points

For the admission ticket, the indicated condition is a 15% discount until December 23 and 20% on Tuesdays, for OCST members and three accompanying persons. For an assistance case, the local contacts are Patronato INAS at the OCST offices in Chiasso, Mendrisio, Bioggio and Locarno, and Patronato ACLI at the Lugano office. To stay up to date, the page lists periodic meetings, «il Lavoro», the website, newsletter, Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn.

The same criterion applies to the other benefits: the cross-border worker can consult the specific offer before choosing whether to request the form or use the code. Frequently Asked Questions
What areas does OCST assistance cover for cross-border workers?
OCST offers legal assistance and advice on employment relationships and payslips, as well as covering areas such as social insurance (AVS, AI, LADI, LPP), sickness, accidents, family allowances, taxation and tenancy. The Cross-Border Workers Office is specifically dedicated to cross-border tax law, social security, work permits and unemployment benefits. Subsidies for strikes and contributions for training are also provided, according to specific regulations.
How can commercial and insurance benefits be obtained?
The procedures vary depending on the offer: for some, it is necessary to request a form via the appropriate form, while for others you need to inform your insurer of the possibility. For establishments such as Brico SA, Ristorante Elvezia 31, Old Wild West di Mendrisio, Pizzarella-Burgerella and Pescheria Lawrence, it is sufficient to present your OCST card. For Masa Coffee, the code 'OCST' is used, while for the bank account, the OCST promotional code is required from the contact person.
Where are the affiliated offices and patronages located?
OCST has offices in Chiasso, Mendrisio, Bioggio, Locarno and Lugano. The INAS patronato operates from the offices in Chiasso, Mendrisio, Bioggio and Locarno, while the ACLI patronato is located at the Lugano office. These organizations provide qualified support on social security and pension matters, complementing the main trade union activity and ensuring an extensive local presence for cross-border workers.
