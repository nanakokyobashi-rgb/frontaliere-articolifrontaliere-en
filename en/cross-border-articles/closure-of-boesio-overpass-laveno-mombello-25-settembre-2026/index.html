Closure of Boesio overpass Laveno Mombello 25 settembre 2026 (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 26 September 2026

Friday 25 September 2026, from 9:00 to 17:00, total closure of the Boesio Overpass (S.P. 32 and S.P. 69) for the installation of three restriction gantries; after the works, limits of 3,5 t, 3,5 m and 30 km/h.

Operational details

The day of 25 settembre should be read as a sequence of two different rules. The first is a complete and temporary suspension; the second is a conditional reopening. For anyone planning to pass through Laveno Mombello, distinguishing the two phases is the operational point: during the works the measure concerns all vehicles, after the works traffic becomes possible again with defined thresholds and a maximum speed of 30 km/h.

Two moments, two conditions

PhaseCondition
During the operationsTraffic suspended for all vehicles from 9:00 to 17:00, until completion of the works
After the operationsReopening with a ban on vehicles over 3,5 tonnes, vehicles over 3,5 meters and a maximum limit of 30 km/h

The first moment leaves no room for an assessment based on the vehicle's dimensions: the suspension is general. Mass and height instead come into play in the subsequent phase, when the overpass becomes usable again but only in compliance with the set limits.

This difference also clarifies the purpose of the intervention. The installation does not merely reintroduce the previous ban on vehicles over 3,5 tonnes. Data from the monitoring system showed that this restriction was not always observed. The new portals are therefore intended to physically prevent the passage of vehicles with a height exceeding 3,5 meters, while the mass limit remains expressed as a traffic ban.

A cross-border commuter who needs to include that stretch in a business trip therefore has a local detail to put on the agenda: the full closure during the indicated time period, followed by a reopening with restrictions. The source describes the road conditions, detours and safety measures of the overpass; it does not provide any other elements regarding cross-border work.

Key points

To turn the arrangement into a concrete check, it is advisable to follow a brief sequence, keeping the workday separate from the subsequent phase.

Procedure for those who need to cross

1. Check the date. The suspension is scheduled for Friday, September 25, 2026. If the crossing is planned between 9:00 and 17:00, consider that S.P. 32 and S.P. 69 will be closed at the Boesio Overpass.

2. Consider the actual duration. The indicated time is valid until 17:00, but the order specifies that the closure will continue regardless until the operations are completed.

3. Choose the detour based on the direction. From Cerro to Luino, the municipal route passes via Via Dalmazia, Via Don Bosco, Via Filzi, Via Martiri della Libertà, and Via Gaggetto. From Luino to Cerro, the sequence is Via Gaggetto, Via Martiri della Libertà, Via Filzi, and Via Dalmazia.

4. Check the vehicle in the subsequent phase. After installation, the overpass will be accessible with a ban on vehicles with a mass exceeding 3.5 tonnes, a ban on vehicles with a height exceeding 3.5 meters, and a maximum speed limit of 30 km/h. Portals will be placed at the three access points to the structure.

The 17:00 deadline should therefore not be read as an automatic reopening if the operations are not concluded. In checking the vehicle, the mass threshold and the height threshold must be considered separately: the order prohibits passage for those exceeding the weight limit and physically prevents vehicles over the height limit from passing.

Frequently Asked Questions
When is the Boesio Overpass expected to be completely closed?
Total closure is scheduled for Friday, September 25, 2026, from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm. The provision specifies that the closure will continue in any case until the installation operations of the three gauge limiting portals are completed.
What are the deviations to follow during closing?
During the closure, traffic will be diverted to the municipal roadway. From Cerro towards Luino you must travel via Dalmazia, via Don Bosco, via Filzi, via Martiri della Libertà and via Gaggetto. From Luino to Cerro, the route is via Gaggetto, via Martiri della Libertà, via Filzi and via Dalmazia.
What limits will remain in place after the overpass reopens?
After the installation of the portals, the overpass will be accessible with a prohibition of transit for vehicles with a mass greater than 3.5 tons, a prohibition for vehicles with a height greater than 3.5 meters and a maximum speed limit of 30 km/h. Limits were introduced to physically enforce previous often-violated restrictions.
