On Friday 25 September 2026, road traffic will be temporarily suspended at the Boesio overpass, in the Municipality of Laveno Mombello. The stop concerns the S.P. 32 «of the Due Pievi» and the S.P. 69 «Santa Caterina» and serves to allow the installation of three profile limiting portals at the accesses to the building.

The closure will affect all vehicles from 9:00 to 17:00, and in any case until the operations are completed. In the indicated area, passage on the overpass will therefore be suspended for vehicular traffic, without distinction linked to the mass or height of the vehicles.

The measure affects the two roads near the overpass. During the closure, traffic will be diverted onto municipal roads. Those traveling from Cerro towards Luino will have to follow via Dalmazia, via Don Bosco, via Filzi, via Martiri della Libertà and via Gaggetto. In the opposite direction, from Luino towards Cerro, the route includes via Gaggetto, via Martiri della Libertà, via Filzi and via Dalmazia.

Reopening after surgery

Once the operations have been completed, the overpass will be accessible again, but with precise limits. Vehicles with a mass exceeding 3.5 tonnes and vehicles with a height exceeding 3.5 meters will remain prohibited. The maximum speed limit of 30 km/h will also remain in force.

The choice of portals is linked to a problem already noted: the previous ban on vehicles over 3.5 tonnes was not always respected, as highlighted by the data from the monitoring system installed on the overpass. The new devices, positioned on the three accesses to the building, will serve to physically prevent the passage of vehicles higher than 3.5 metres.

The limitations were adopted after inspections and investigations carried out on the Boesio overpass as part of the monitoring and safety assessment activities of existing bridges. The reopening will therefore mark the end of the total suspension foreseen for the works, but will maintain the restrictions indicated.

For those planning a passage from Laveno Mombello, distinguishing the two phases is the operational point: during the works the measure concerns all vehicles, after the works circulation is possible again with defined thresholds and with a maximum speed of 30 km/h.
