Health Insurance for Cross-Border Workers and Their Families

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 25 February 2026 · Updated on 7 October 2026

Swiss LAMal or the Italian National Health Service for the whole family: 2026 premiums, rules for spouses and children, the EHIC and supplementary cover.

Context

TL;DR

  • Cross-border workers living in Italy choose between Swiss LAMal and the Italian National Health Service within 3 months: the choice is final.
  • In 2026 an adult resident in Italy pays CHF 279.00–487.20 per month with LAMal; a minor pays CHF 64.20–203.70.
  • If only one parent works, the whole family follows that choice.
  • The EHIC is not an alternative insurance system; supplementary insurance may be added but does not replace LAMal or the Italian National Health Service.

Key facts

  • Choice: Within three months of starting work, final
  • LAMal premiums 2026: Adults CHF 279.00–487.20/month; minors CHF 64.20–203.70/month; young adults aged 19–25 CHF 239.60–475.30/month
  • Cost sharing: CHF 300 deductible and 10% co-payment up to CHF 700 per year; no deductible for minors
  • One parent works: The whole family follows that choice; with LAMal everyone uses the same health insurer and pays an individual premium
  • Other parent works in Italy: The children remain insured in Italy
  • Both parents work in Switzerland: Each chooses for themselves and the children follow one system

In Canton Ticino, cross-border workers resident in Italy have two options: mandatory Swiss LAMal or, through the right of option, the Italian National Health Service. Private supplementary insurance can be added, but it does not replace either system.

LAMal: Swiss insurance for cross-border workers

For residents in Italy, the 2026 LAMal premium for an adult ranges from CHF 279.00 to CHF 487.20 per month without accident cover, depending on the insurer; it does not depend on the canton and cannot be reduced by choosing a higher deductible.

If only one parent works, the whole family follows that choice: with LAMal everyone insures with the same Swiss health insurer and pays an individual premium.

For someone working in Lugano with family in Varese or Como, the rule is clear: if the other parent works or receives a pension in Italy, the children remain insured in Italy and LAMal concerns only the cross-border worker; if both parents work in Switzerland, each chooses for themselves and the children follow one system.

Operational details

Italian National Health Service, EHIC and supplementary insurance: what to know

Anyone choosing the Italian National Health Service remains insured in Italy; in Switzerland, the European Health Insurance Card (EHIC) gives access to medically necessary care during the stay, with a flat-rate contribution of CHF 92 every 30 days of care (CHF 33 for minors). Planned care in Switzerland requires prior authorisation from the ASL.

  • European Health Insurance Card (EHIC): Medically necessary care during a stay in Switzerland, not planned treatment
  • Supplementary insurance: Can cover dental expenses, physiotherapy, or private treatments not included in public systems

⚠️ The cost of supplementary insurance depends on age, coverage and limits: request a quotation and compare it with what the Italian National Health Service or LAMal already covers.

Practical tips for cross-border workers
