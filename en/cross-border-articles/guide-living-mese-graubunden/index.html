Living in Mese (Sondrio, Italy) and Working in Graubünden: A Tax Guide

By Marco Ferrari (editorial byline of the newsroom, AI-generated text) · Published on 2 September 2026 · Updated on 31 October 2026

Mese is an Italian municipality in the Province of Sondrio: a guide for residents who work in Swiss Graubünden, covering Permit G, withholding tax, social insurance and LAMal.

Context

At a glance

  • Residence: Municipality of Mese, Province of Sondrio (SO), Lombardy, Italy.
  • Work: Canton of Graubünden, Switzerland.
  • Key point: Mese is an Italian municipality; it is not a municipality in the Canton of Graubünden.
  • Check before acting: Permit G, withholding tax, social insurance and health-insurance rules depend on the individual case and the rules in force.

The geographical distinction

The name can be confusing because Mese and Graubünden appear in the same sentence. The correct distinction is simple: Mese is the Italian municipality where the person lives, while Graubünden is the Swiss canton where the job is located. The official website of the Municipality of Mese identifies Mese (SO). For the municipality name, province and code, use the official Italian municipality nomenclature published by ISTAT.

Mese should therefore not be searched for in a list of Graubünden municipalities. For the Swiss side, use the Swiss official municipality directory maintained by the Federal Statistical Office and, when the canton-level detail is needed, the official list of municipalities of Graubünden. In this guide, “Graubünden” means the place of work, not the place of residence.

Permit G and residence

A person living in Mese and working in Graubünden may fall under the cross-border-worker regime, but the classification must be checked with the employer and the competent authority. Permit G documents employment in Switzerland by a person resident abroad; it does not turn Mese into a Swiss municipality and does not itself transfer the person’s residence to Graubünden.

Keep current proof of Italian residence and ask which documents are needed for the application. The contract, employer, return pattern and any remote work can affect the applicable obligations. A general guide is not a substitute for checking the individual case.

Operational details

Tax and social insurance

The Swiss employer applies the deductions required for work in Graubünden, including any cantonal withholding tax. The income must also be handled in the Italian tax return under the double-taxation agreement and the Italian tax authority’s instructions. The practical treatment depends on the start date of the employment, the worker’s status, the workplace and any other income. Check the rules for the relevant tax year before filing.

A Swiss payslip may include AVS/AI/IPG, unemployment insurance, accident insurance and occupational pension contributions under the LPP. These are different items, not one single tax, and they do not all receive the same treatment in an Italian return. Net pay cannot be estimated by subtracting one percentage from gross pay: use the salary certificate, the actual deductions and the worker’s family and insurance situation.

Health insurance

Many cross-border workers have an option between the Swiss LAMal system and Italian health coverage, subject to deadlines and conditions. The option cannot be inferred simply from the fact that the job is in Graubünden. Check the authority’s notice, the employment start date and the rules for the worker’s category before taking out a policy or requesting an exemption.

Assessing the financial benefit

Compare net salary with the real cost of living in Mese: transport to the workplace, fuel or passes, parking, vehicle maintenance, insurance, occasional overnight stays and commuting time. Comparing costs with Graubünden is also useful when residence remains in Italy, because some daily expenses may arise across the border. A careful calculation should include holidays, sickness, the thirteenth salary, pension contributions and the CHF-EUR exchange rate.

Rates, allowances and procedures change. Figures copied from an older guide should not automatically be applied to a new contract: obtain an updated calculation from a professional or the competent office.

Key points

Practical checklist

1. Confirm the geography: record the residence as Municipality of Mese (SO), Italy, separately from the workplace in Graubünden. 2. Request the right permit: the employer checks the Permit G application and submits it to the competent Swiss authority. 3. Collect the documents: contract, identity document, proof of residence and any certificate requested by the authority. 4. Check the tax regime: clarify withholding tax, any allowance, the Italian tax return and the foreign-tax credit for the current year. 5. Handle health coverage: check whether an option exists, the deadline and the consequences of the choice. 6. Keep evidence: payslips, certificates, official notices and receipts are useful for later checks.

Official municipality sources

For every evergreen article connecting an Italian residence with a Swiss canton, the municipality name should be checked separately in the two official nomenclatures:

  • ISTAT — Codes of municipalities, provinces and regions, for the Italian municipality of residence.
  • Federal Statistical Office — Swiss official municipality directory, for Swiss municipalities.
  • Canton of Graubünden — Our municipalities, for the cantonal check.

These sources prevent an Italian municipality from being confused with a Swiss municipality. For tax or insurance information, use the current pages of the competent authorities and always state the reference year.

Frequently Asked Questions
Is Mese a municipality in the Canton of Graubünden?
No. Mese is an Italian municipality in the Province of Sondrio, Lombardy. Graubünden is the Swiss canton where the workplace may be located.
Which source confirms that Mese is Italian?
The primary source is the Municipality of Mese website. For the official name, province and code, also consult ISTAT’s official Italian municipality nomenclature.
Which permit is needed to work in Graubünden while living in Mese?
In many cases this is Permit G, but the application and requirements depend on the employment relationship and the individual situation. Check with the employer and the competent cantonal authority.
Where can I check Swiss municipalities?
Use the Federal Statistical Office’s Swiss official municipality directory; for Graubünden, you can also consult the canton’s official list.
Does this guide replace tax advice?
No. Tax, pension and health-insurance treatment depends on the year, worker status and contract. A concrete decision requires current sources and, when needed, professional advice.
