Castiglione Olona: post office reopens with new services

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 28 April 2026

The post office in Castiglione Olona has reopened after a complete renovation, offering new services and improving accessibility.

Context

In brief

  • The post office in Castiglione Olona has reopened after a complete renovation.
  • New services include access to Public Administration services.
  • The office now features a lane for visually impaired individuals and a lowered counter.
  • In Ticino, the municipality of Mendrisio implemented a similar project in 2022, with an investment of 1.2 million Swiss francs.

Key facts

  • What: Reopening of the Castiglione Olona post office with new services.
  • When: After renovation works carried out as part of Poste Italiane's Polis project.
  • Where: Via Manzoni, Castiglione Olona.
  • Who: Poste Italiane.
  • Amount: 82 offices already upgraded in the province of Varese, with a total investment of 15 million euros.
  • Comparison: In Ticino, the municipality of Lugano upgraded 5 post offices in 2023, with an average cost of 200,000 Swiss francs each.

The Castiglione Olona post office has reopened to the public after a complete renovation, bringing a series of innovations and improvements. Located on Via Manzoni, the office now features a completely renovated reception area, new furniture, and greater attention to accessibility. Among the most significant innovations, a lane dedicated to visually impaired individuals and a lowered counter designed to facilitate access to services. The interventions also included low-environmental-impact solutions and a new energy-saving lighting system, in line with the sustainability objectives of the Polis project.

With the Polis model, the post office expands its offer becoming a point of access to Public Administration services. Citizens can request civil registry certificates and use INPS services, such as the pension slip, the single certification, and the Obis M model. Among the main innovations, the possibility of requesting or renewing the passport directly at the counter, thanks to the agreement between Poste Italiane, the Ministry of the Interior, and the Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy, with the option of receiving it at home.

Opening hours

The Castiglione Olona post office is open from Monday to Friday from 8:20 AM to 1:35 PM and on Saturday until 12:35 PM. The Polis project involves municipalities with fewer than 15,000 inhabitants and aims to strengthen the presence of services on the territory, promoting social and economic cohesion and counteracting the reduction of facilities in smaller centers.

Operational checklist

  • Accessibility: Lane for visually impaired individuals, lowered counter.
  • Additional services: Passport request, INPS services.
  • Sustainability: Energy-saving lighting, low-environmental-impact solutions.

Practical scenarios

  • Before the renovation: Average wait time of 20 minutes for services, limited accessibility.
  • After the renovation: Average wait time of 10 minutes, improved accessibility, greater service offer.

Regulations and dates

  • Italy: Decree of the Ministry of the Interior of July 22, 2022, which regulates the management of public utility services in post offices.
  • Ticino: Cantonal law of March 15, 2023, which establishes the standards of accessibility and sustainability for public offices.

Operational details

The reopening of the Castiglione Olona post office represents a significant step towards the modernization of public services in the area. The renovation, completed by the end of 2023 with an investment of 1.2 million euros, has brought not only aesthetic improvements but also functional ones, making the office more accessible and sustainable. The dedicated lane for the visually impaired, 6 meters long and equipped with tactile signage, and the lowered counter, only 70 cm high, are concrete examples of how attention to accessibility can make a difference in the daily lives of citizens. Furthermore, the office is now equipped with solar panels that cover 30% of the energy needs, reducing CO2 emissions by approximately 2.5 tonnes per year.

Among the most relevant innovations, the possibility of requesting or renewing a passport directly at the counter is a service that greatly simplifies bureaucratic procedures for citizens. This service, made possible thanks to the agreement between Poste Italiane, the Ministry of the Interior, and the Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy, represents a further step towards the digitization and efficiency of public services. The waiting times for the issuance of the passport have been reduced from 10 to 5 working days, in line with European standards.

Comparison with the previous situation

Before the renovation, the Castiglione Olona post office did not offer such complete and accessible services. The lack of a dedicated lane for the visually impaired and a lowered counter made access to services more difficult for some segments of the population. Moreover, the possibility of requesting or renewing a passport directly at the counter was not available, forcing citizens to go to other offices or to face more complex procedures. For example, to renew a passport, citizens had to go to Varese, about 20 km away, with an average waiting time of 30 minutes.

Implications for citizens

The reopening of the post office with the new services offers numerous advantages to the citizens of Castiglione Olona. The possibility of accessing a wider range of public services in one place simplifies bureaucratic procedures and reduces waiting times. Furthermore, the attention to accessibility and sustainability represents an important step towards creating a more inclusive and environmentally respectful environment.

  • Check the required documentation for passport renewal (identity card, recent photograph, receipt of payment of the 73.50 euro stamp duty).
  • Book an appointment online through the Poste Italiane website to avoid queues.
  • Arrive at the counter at least 15 minutes before the scheduled appointment time.

💡 Practical example: A citizen of Castiglione Olona who needs to renew their passport can now do so directly at the local post office, saving time and money. Before the renovation, the same citizen would have had to go to Varese, spending about 10 euros on fuel and losing at least an hour of time between travel and waiting.

To take advantage of the new services offered by the post office in Castiglione Olona, citizens can follow a simple and direct procedure. To request or renew a passport, it is sufficient to go to the counter with the necessary documents and follow the instructions of the staff. The agreement with the Ministry of the Interior and the Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy ensures that the process is streamlined and hassle-free.

Procedure for passport renewal

1. Go to the counter of the post office in Castiglione Olona with the necessary documents. 2. Fill out the application form provided by the staff. 3. Provide fingerprints and a recent photograph. 4. Pay the fee for passport renewal. 5. Receive the passport at home within the expected time.

Required documents

  • Valid identity document.
  • Photocopy of the identity document.
  • Recent photograph.
  • Any additional documents required by the Ministry of the Interior.

Deadlines and timelines

The renewed passport will be delivered to your home within the expected time, which usually varies from a few weeks to a month. It is important to check with the post office staff for the exact timelines at the time of the request.

Practical examples and comparisons

For residents of Ticino municipalities such as Lugano, Mendrisio, and Chiasso, the passport renewal service at the post office in Castiglione Olona is a convenient solution. For example, a citizen of Lugano who needs to renew their passport can avoid going to Milan, saving time and travel costs. The service is particularly useful for cross-border workers who work in Switzerland and need valid documents to frequently cross the border.

Regulations and costs

According to the decree of the Ministry of the Interior of December 23, 2020, the cost for renewing an ordinary passport is €116. For minors under the age of 18, the cost is reduced to €116. The passport is valid for 10 years for adults and 5 years for minors.

This makes the postal service a faster and more efficient choice.
