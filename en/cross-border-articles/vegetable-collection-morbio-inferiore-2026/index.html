Morbio Inferiore: less frequent and pricier green waste collection (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 May 2026

The PLR of Morbio Inferiore reports a less frequent and more expensive green waste collection service compared to neighboring municipalities

Context

In brief

  • Less frequent collection of plant waste in Morbio Inferiore
  • Similar or higher costs compared to neighboring municipalities
  • Residents report practical inconveniences

Key facts

  • What: Interpellation by the PLR of Morbio Inferiore on the plant waste collection service
  • When: May 7, 2026
  • Where: Morbio Inferiore, Canton Ticino
  • Who: PLR Group of Morbio Inferiore
  • Amount: Costs of the tags similar or less advantageous compared to neighboring municipalities

The PLR group of Morbio Inferiore has submitted an interpellation to the Municipality to request clarifications on the plant waste collection service, deemed inadequate compared to that offered in neighboring municipalities. The document highlights how the current frequency of collection is lower than similar realities such as Vacallo and Balerna, despite the costs of the tags being substantially similar for the smaller containers and even less advantageous for those of 240 liters. A situation that, according to the signatories, penalizes the citizens of Morbio. This is added to the reports of some residents, who complain about a deterioration of the service and the need to keep the green waste for longer periods, with consequent practical inconveniences.

Operational details

Analysis of Critical Issues

The PLR's interpellation from Morbio Inferiore highlights several critical issues with the green waste collection service. In particular, the reduced frequency of collection compared to neighboring municipalities and the higher costs for larger containers represent a significant problem for residents. These practical inconveniences, such as the need to store green waste for longer periods, have a negative impact on the quality of life of citizens.

Comparison with Neighboring Municipalities

A comparison with neighboring municipalities such as Vacallo and Balerna shows that the green waste collection service in Morbio Inferiore is less efficient and more expensive. In neighboring municipalities, a system based on a single-colored band has been introduced, making the service simpler and more functional. This system could also be adopted in Morbio Inferiore to improve service efficiency and reduce costs for residents.

Proposals for Improvement

The PLR's interpellation proposes several solutions to improve the green waste collection service in Morbio Inferiore. In particular, it suggests introducing a simpler and more functional collection system based on a single-colored band. Additionally, it proposes reviewing the service schedule immediately or starting next year, restoring a more adequate frequency. These solutions could improve service efficiency and reduce inconveniences for residents.

Key points

Concrete Actions for Residents

Residents of Morbio Inferiore can take several concrete actions to improve the green waste collection service. First, it is important to participate in Municipal Council meetings and public assemblies to express concerns and suggest solutions. Additionally, citizens can organize working groups to monitor the quality of the service and propose improvements.

Procedure to Report Discomfort

To report discomfort or problems with the green waste collection service, residents can follow the following procedure: 1. Contact the Municipality of Morbio Inferiore via email or phone. 2. Provide specific details about the discomfort encountered, such as the frequency of collection and the costs of the ties. 3. Ask the Municipality to intervene to improve the service. 4. Participate in Municipal Council meetings to discuss the proposed solutions.

Useful Tools

To learn more about the green waste collection service and the procedures for reporting discomfort, residents can consult the website of the Municipality of Morbio Inferiore. Additionally, they can use the cost calculator to compare the costs of the service with those of neighboring municipalities.

Conclusion

The interpellation of the PLR of Morbio Inferiore highlights the need to improve the green waste collection service in the municipality. Improvement proposals, such as the introduction of a simpler and more functional collection system and the review of the service scheduling, could help resolve the reported criticalities and improve the quality of life of residents. Citizens can actively participate in the improvement process by reporting discomfort and proposing solutions.
