Key facts

On Sunday, October 18, at 11 a.m., Porto Ceresio will inaugurate the former Regia Dogana on Via Mazzini, the historic building located directly opposite the railway station. The ribbon-cutting ceremony will accompany the reopening to the public of a fully restored site destined to become the town’s new Tourist Office.

The restoration has returned to the local community a building of architectural and cultural significance. The project was made possible by financial support from Regione Lombardia and the Autorità di Bacino Lacuale. The funding made it possible to protect and repurpose one of the most distinctive views on the Italian shore of the lake, preserving both the building’s historical dimension and its new public use.

Redevelopment with a specific service

The site is located in a strategic position for arrivals. Opposite the station, the Tourist Office will provide a first point of welcome, guidance and information for those reaching Porto Ceresio by train and for those visiting the lakeside village. Those using mezzi pubblici will therefore find the station to be the designated reference point for locating the new site.

The function described by the source is twofold: enhancing a restored building and giving the town a recognizable place for visitors and tourists. The project is not limited to preserving the historic appearance, but involves repurposing the building through a service aimed at the public.

The project is part of the urban development plan called «Nuovo Baricentro Ceresino». The strategy concerns this specific area of Porto Ceresio and aims to redesign and enhance it, with new public spaces, modern services and gathering places intended for residents and tourists. The new office thus forms part of a broader plan.

The Municipal Administration described the restoration as a significant investment in enhancing Porto Ceresio and its heritage, emphasizing that the restoration preserves the building’s memory, gives it a future and reintegrates it into the life of the town.

The ribbon-cutting ceremony is open to all residents.
