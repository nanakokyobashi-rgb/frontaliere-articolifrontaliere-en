From 2027 to 2031, the new national collective labor agreement for Migros will be in force. The company indicates better conditions for employees, with measures concerning shopping, working hours, and payment in the event of illness or accident.

The most immediate benefit concerns purchases: employees will be able to shop with a 10% discount through shopping vouchers. The source does not specify an amount for the vouchers, nor does it describe the procedure by which they will be distributed or used. The verifiable information is therefore the discount percentage and its connection to the shopping vouchers.

The CCL will apply regardless of the employment percentage. Those working at a percentage below 20% will also benefit from the same conditions. For a cross-border worker employed by Migros, this is the part to read most carefully in their employment relationship: the criterion indicated is the employment percentage, not a different threshold described in the text.

Working hours and income protection

Regarding working hours, the company emphasizes that many employees will move to 41 hours per week instead of 43, while maintaining the same salary. The wording concerns many employees, does not establish that the reduction applies to everyone, and does not indicate which positions are included.

Benefits in the event of illness or accident will also be uniform. Full net salary will be paid for a maximum of 90 days; subsequently, 80% of gross salary will be paid. The distinction between net and gross is an essential part of the measure: the text does not permit converting 80% gross into a net figure or extending the first period beyond 90 days.

When reading the payslip, the source therefore provides two different and consecutive references: net pay for the first phase and gross pay for the subsequent one.
