Slow march in Lugano against oil risks

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 May 2026

Five 'act now!' activists organized a slow march in Lugano to denounce the dangers of oil.

Context

In brief

  • Five activists from 'act now!' organized a slow march in Lugano.

  • The action was organized at an Eni service station in Savosa.

Key facts

  • What: Slow march and artistic action against the risks of oil.
  • When: Sunday, May 3, 2026, starting at 15:45.
  • Where: Lugano, at an Eni service station in Savosa.
  • Who: Activists from the 'act now!' movement.
  • Amount: Not yet specified.

On the afternoon of Sunday, May 3, 2026, five activists from the 'act now!' movement organized a slow march through the streets of Lugano. The initiative was preceded by an artistic action at a service station in the economic capital of Ticino. This is the third non-violent action organized as part of the 'Oil Kills' campaign.

Activists from act now! headed to the Eni service station located in Savosa around 15:45. They painted a huge canvas (10x5 meters) denouncing the deadly dangers of oil, before carrying out a slow march and then hanging it on a busy road bridge.
