Roman Josi recently joined the Swiss national team during preparations for the 2026 Hockey World Cup. The historic captain explained his letter addressed to the federation in the Fischer affair, showing great enthusiasm for the upcoming tournament. The World Cup at home is now just around the corner: on May 15th, we will make our debut against the United States. The team led by Jan Cadieux is still in the midst of preparations for the final friendlies. Meanwhile, some NHL players have joined the group, including Roman Josi.

Josi's letter

In a few days, the great hockey celebration will begin. Roman Josi has recounted to SRF the turbulent days he has recently experienced and has expressed great enthusiasm for the much-awaited tournament. It is a matter of the heart to stand by Patrick Fischer. The dismissal of Patrick Fischer deeply affected Josi. That is why he wrote a letter to the federation, asking them to reconsider the decision. 'The disappointment was very great. We lived so many beautiful moments with 'Fischi'. I wanted to support him and stand by his side,' Josi declared.

The letter was not, however, a gesture against the new head coach Jan Cadieux. Josi explained that he is in contact with him and supports him 100 percent. His support for Fischer was rather a 'matter of the heart,' linked to the shared memories.
