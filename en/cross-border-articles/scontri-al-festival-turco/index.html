Investigation

The investigation into the incident was conducted by the Basel police in collaboration with the Basel prosecutor's office. The prosecutor's office sentenced 11 Kurds to between 9 and 36 months in prison for their roles in the violence.

Analysis of the situation revealed that the clash was caused by a dispute between two groups of Kurds. The dispute was fuelled by differences of opinion and interest. The violence was triggered by a group of individuals using firearms and blunt objects.
