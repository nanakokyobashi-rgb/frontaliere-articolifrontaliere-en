The train was whistling: railway festival in Gorla Minore

The eighteenth edition of «Fischiava il treno – Ul trenen daa Vall d’Uôna» will take place on Sunday, October 11, 2026, from 12:00 to 18:00, on the valley floor near the old Gorla Minore station.

The Circolo Acli di Gorla Minore is organizing the event together with the Circolo Acli di Bergoro, under the patronage of the municipality. The event aims to rediscover the roots of the Olona Valley and its connection with railway history.

The day's program

The day will begin with greetings from the authorities and the parish priest's blessing at 12:30; this will be followed by a shared lunch featuring local food.

The afternoon program is designed for all ages. There will be displays of model trains, reenactors in period clothing and photographic exhibitions dedicated to traditional trades. These offerings will be accompanied by poems, books and old-fashioned games.

Also scheduled are burraco tournaments with the «Donne del Mercoledì», fishing on the bridge and the planting of flowers in the children's garden. The program thus brings together railway heritage, documentation on the trades of the past and initiatives dedicated to the youngest children.

During the afternoon, the program includes the distribution of roasted chestnuts and the «trenino dolce della solidarietà». The festival is part of the ongoing project «Adotta la Ferrovia e la Valle per riscoprirla e riviverla».

According to the organizers, the event will take place only in good weather. This condition applies to the entire event, scheduled on the valley floor next to the old Gorla Minore station.
