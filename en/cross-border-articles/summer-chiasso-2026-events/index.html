Key points

To participate in the summer events in Chiasso, it is advisable to consult the official calendar of the Municipality and book in advance for some events. Many of the events are free, while others may require an entrance ticket. You can find all the necessary information on the official website of the Municipality of Chiasso or at the local tourist office. Don't forget to check the dates and times of the events to plan your summer in Chiasso to the best.

How to participate

To participate in the events, you can buy tickets online or at authorized points of sale. Some events may require advance booking, so it is advisable to check the participation conditions. For free events, it is sufficient to show up, but it is always a good idea to arrive a little early to secure a good spot.

Where to stay

Chiasso offers a wide range of accommodation options, from luxury hotels to budget apartments. You can find accommodations suitable for all needs and budgets. For those who prefer a more budget-friendly vacation, there are also campsites and hostels that offer an authentic and convenient experience.

What to bring

To enjoy the summer events in Chiasso to the fullest, it is advisable to bring comfortable clothing suitable for the summer climate. Don't forget to bring a bottle of water and sunscreen to protect yourself from the sun. If you plan to participate in sports events, bring the necessary equipment and sportswear.
