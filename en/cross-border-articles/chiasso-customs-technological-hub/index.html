Operational details

The Chiasso Customs Office is preparing to become a modern technological hub. The Chiasso customs office is a strategic point for the traffic of goods and people between Switzerland and Italy. Every year, thousands of people and tonnes of goods cross the border. With the introduction of advanced technologies, such as automatic detection systems and digital management of documents, the Chiasso customs office aims to improve the efficiency and security of controls. Athos Capoferri, director of the Chiasso customs office, stated: 'Our priority is to leverage the most advanced technologies to improve the management of goods and people flows, while ensuring security and regulatory compliance.' In 2022, over 1.2 million vehicles and 3.5 million people were checked as they crossed the border between Switzerland and Italy. The Chiasso customs office is committed to enhancing its capacity to manage goods and people flows thanks to the introduction of new technologies. The customs director, Athos Capoferri, stated: 'Our priority is to leverage the most advanced technologies to improve the management of goods and people flows, while ensuring security and regulatory compliance.' One of the main technologies introduced is the automatic vehicle detection system, which enables rapid identification of vehicles crossing the border. This system was installed in 2020 and has already proven its effectiveness, reducing vehicle waiting times by over 30%. Furthermore, the Chiasso customs office has introduced a digital document management platform, enabling rapid examination and validation of transport and goods documents. The Chiasso customs office constantly works to improve its management of goods and people flows. In 2022, CHF 2.5 million was invested in modernizing infrastructures and technologies. Additionally, the Chiasso customs office collaborates with Italian and Swiss border authorities to share information and enhance control security. The Chiasso customs office is committed to ensuring security and regulatory compliance. In 2020, new legislation was enacted, requiring all goods transporters to use automatic detection systems and to clearly and easily display transport and goods documents. The legislation also provides for the creation of a register of goods transporters not complying with regulations. Frequently Asked Questions
When does the Chiasso customs digitization project begin?
The project began in 2023 and should be completed by 2026.
How many vehicles and people have transited through Chiasso customs in 2022?
In 2022, they passed through the customs of Chiasso more than 1.5 million vehicles and more than 2 million people.
What are the main technologies introduced to Chiasso customs to improve efficiency?
Chiasso customs introduced the automatic vehicle detection system and a digital document management platform. The automatic detection system helps to quickly identify vehicles, reducing waiting times by more than 30%. The digital management platform allows you to quickly examine and validate transport and freight documents, improving the overall efficiency of customs controls.
How much was invested in modernising the infrastructure and technologies of Chiasso customs in 2022?
In 2022, 2.5 million Swiss francs were invested in the modernization of the infrastructure and technologies of Chiasso customs. This investment is part of the plan to improve the ability to manage goods flows and people through the introduction of advanced technologies.
How does Chiasso Customs ensure compliance with the regulations for freight carriers?
Chiasso customs guarantees compliance with regulations by requiring freight carriers to use automatic detection systems and to expose transport and goods documents clearly and easily accessible. A new legislation has also been introduced which provides for the creation of a register of freight carriers that do not comply with regulations, to improve safety and compliance.
