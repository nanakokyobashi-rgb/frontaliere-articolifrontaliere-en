Frequently Asked Questions
Am I exempt from income tax at source?
Yes, if you are a frontier worker living in Biandronno and working in Ticino.
How can I use the deductible?
I can use the deductible to reduce my taxation in Switzerland.
Since when is the new Switzerland-Italy border tax agreement in force?
The new agreement between Switzerland and Italy entered into force on 1 January 2024. It was signed on 23 December 2020 and ratified in Italy with Law 83 of 13 June 2023. From this date, frontier workers benefit from the exemption from tax at source on income from work.
What steps do I need to take to qualify for the tax exemption at source?
To benefit from the exemption from the tax at source, you must first verify your tax status as a border worker. Next, let your employer in Switzerland know about your condition. It is essential to receive a tax exemption certificate at source directly from your employer.
How much is saved by the new tax at source agreement for frontier workers?
With the new agreement, frontier workers are exempt from income tax at source. For example, a border worker who earns €40,000 per year will save around €6,000 annually, as they will no longer have to pay this tax that was due before the new regime came into force.
