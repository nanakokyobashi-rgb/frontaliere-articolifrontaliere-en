Operational details

Practical analysis: how to deal with work sensitivity ### Creating a suitable work environment For those who are highly sensitive to work, it is essential to create a quiet work environment free of excessive stimuli. This may include using noise-cancelling headphones, finding isolated spaces, and limiting unnecessary social interactions. ### Managing stress and discomfort Work can cause stress, stomach pain, and panic attacks. It is important to find strategies to manage these symptoms, such as breathing techniques, relaxation breaks, and light physical activity. ### Assessing one's abilities and limitations It is essential to assess one's abilities and limitations and communicate any difficulties with the employer. This may include requiring less stressful tasks or finding a more suitable work environment. ### Developing strengths Sensitivity can lead to strengths such as creativity and conscientiousness. It is important to recognize and develop these qualities to improve your efficiency and job satisfaction. ### Research and Support There are resources and support available for those who are highly sensitive to work. Psychotherapist Lilly Maggiori can offer advice and support to address work challenges and improve quality of life.

Frequently Asked Questions
What does it mean to be highly sensitive to work?
Being highly sensitive to work means perceiving sensory and emotional stimuli in a particularly intense way and processing information deeper than average. This can lead to difficulties at work such as stomachaches, migraines and panic attacks.
How can I create a work environment that is suitable for those who are sensitive?
To create a suitable work environment, it is essential to identify quiet places, use noise-cancelling headphones, limit social interactions, and schedule isolation breaks.
What strategies can I use to manage stress and discomfort at work?
You can use breathing techniques, relaxation breaks, and light physical activities to manage stress and discomfort at work.
How can I develop the strengths of my work sensitivity?
You can recognize your qualities, develop creativity and improve conscientiousness to take advantage of the strengths of your work sensitivity.
Where can I find more information and support?
You can consult the psychotherapist Lilly Maggiori or visit the website of the Swiss Association for High Sensitivity for more information and support.
