Road rage in Leipzig: attack was announced (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 8 May 2026

A 33-year-old man ran over several people in Leipzig, killing two and injuring six. The act was announced in a message to his wife.

Context

In brief

  • A 33-year-old man has run over several people in Leipzig.
  • Two victims have died, six injured.
  • The act was foreshadowed in a message to his wife.

Key facts

  • What: A hit-and-run driver has run over several people in Leipzig.
  • When: Monday afternoon, April 29, 2026.
  • Where: Leipzig city center, Germany.
  • Who: A 33-year-old man, resident of Leipzig.
  • Amount: Two dead and six injured, two of them seriously.

The Leipzig hit-and-run driver had already foreshadowed his act in April. According to the Zeit, the 33-year-old German had written in a chat message to his wife that he had 'not driven a car among the crowd for a long time'. At the newspaper's request, a spokesperson for the Leipzig police confirmed that the message was in the possession of the authorities, but 'only on the evening of the day of the incident'. It would have emerged during the subsequent investigations into the wild ride.

Before the incident, the man and his wife were already living separately. According to the Zeit's research, she had apparently already turned to social services in March. In April, she also filed several complaints against her husband, for threats, defamation, and insult. At least once, the man would have threatened to kill other family members. As several people close to the investigation told the Zeit, the man would be suffering from psychosis and would have himself spoken of hearing delusional voices. After sending the chat message, on April 17, the 33-year-old voluntarily went to a psychiatric hospital. On April 29, he left the clinic again.

The man, a resident of Leipzig, on Monday afternoon in the city center sped through a busy pedestrian area with a car. He ran over several people: a 63-year-old woman and a 77-year-old man died. Another six people aged between 21 and 87 were injured, according to investigators, two of them seriously: a 75-year-old man and an 84-year-old woman. All are now out of danger. The motive is still unclear. According to investigators, there are no indications of a political or religious context. The suspect has meanwhile been transferred to a psychiatric hospital.
