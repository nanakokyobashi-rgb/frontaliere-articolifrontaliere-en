Pentagon releases new UFO documents and images (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 8 May 2026

The Pentagon has released 162 previously unseen documents on unidentified anomalous phenomena as part of a program initiated by Donald Trump.

Context

In brief

  • The Pentagon has published 162 previously unreleased documents on UFOs
  • The files include photos from the Apollo missions and exchanges between the State Department and NASA
  • One image depicts three dots in the lunar sky

Key facts

  • What: Previously unreleased documents and images of unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP)
  • When: May 8, 2026
  • Where: Washington, USA
  • Who: Pentagon, State Department, NASA
  • Amount: 162 previously unreleased documents

The Pentagon has published a series of 'never-before-seen' files and videos on so-called 'unidentified anomalous phenomena' (UAP), as part of an initiative to make the government's knowledge about extraterrestrial phenomena more transparent. The approximately 162 previously unreleased documents, disclosed as part of the program initiated by Donald Trump 'Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters', include black-and-white photos from the Apollo 1 space mission in 1969 and Apollo 17 in 1972, as well as exchanges between the State Department and NASA. One of the images, taken from the lunar surface, appears to depict a group of three tiny dots in the sky. Another document contains the FBI's interrogation of a person identified as a drone pilot, who, in September 2023, reported seeing in the sky a 'linear object' with such an intense light that it was possible to 'distinguish bands within the light itself'.
