Requirements for insurance coverage

In order to benefit from LAINF's insurance coverage in the event of an accident while on the move, border workers must meet certain specific requirements. First, they must be subject to the Swiss law on accident insurance, as provided for by the Federal Act of 20 March 1981 on Accident Insurance (LAI). In addition, they must be in possession of an insurance contract with the LAINF or be enrolled in equivalent insurance.

It is important to note that the complaint must be filed within 24 hours, otherwise insurance coverage may not be guaranteed. In addition, it is necessary to obtain a certificate of illness, which may be requested by the attending physician or the general practitioner.

Injury en route to the border: LAINF coverage and reporting procedure

Introduction

Border workers who work in Switzerland and reside in Italy are subject to different rules and regulations that may vary depending on the region and the type of work. In the event of an accident while travelling, it is essential to know the procedures to be followed and the insurance coverage available.

LAINF Cover

The LAINF (Italian Law on Compulsory Insurance for Accidents in the Workplace) guarantees insurance coverage for accidents in the workplace, including those on the move. Coverage is mandatory for all employees and independent workers who carry out work activities in Italy. The amount of coverage is set at € 10,000.00, but may vary depending on the type of job and the region of residence.

Complaint Procedure

In the event of a commuting accident, it is essential to report the accident within 24 hours to INAIL (National Institute for Workplace Accident Insurance) at the Lugano office. You can also report the incident through the INAIL website or by calling the toll-free number 0771 513 513.

Practical analysis

Injury en route to the border is covered by the Law on Social and Supplementary Assistance (LAINF) for border workers. You must file a complaint within 24 hours and obtain a sick certificate. The procedure is as follows:

1. File a complaint within 24 hours. 2. Obtain a sickness certificate. 3. Send the sickness certificate to SECO.

The LAINF came into force on 1 January 2019 and covers accidents that occur in the first 30 days of work in Switzerland. According to the rules of the LAINF, the border worker who is injured en route to the border must file a complaint within 24 hours to the competent LAINF office. The competent LAINF office is that of the municipality in which the worker is registered.

For example, if a Bellinzona worker is injured in Lugano, they must file a complaint within 24 hours with the LAINF office in Bellinzona.

Concrete example

A worker from Lugano, registered at the LAINF office in Lugano, injures himself in Como, Italy, while on his way to his workplace in Bellinzona. You must file a complaint within 24 hours with the LAINF office in Lugano and obtain a sickness certificate. Subsequently, you will have to send the sickness certificate to SECO.

Frequently Asked Questions
How do I get a sick certificate?
By submitting a request form to SECO.
What is the complaint procedure?
1. File a complaint within 24 hours. 2. Obtain a sickness certificate. 3. Send the sickness certificate to SECO.
What are the requirements to be covered by the LAINF for traveling injuries?
For LAINF coverage in the event of a commuting accident, you must be subject to the Swiss accident insurance law. It is also necessary to have an insurance contract with the LAINF or a recognized equivalent insurance. These requirements are essential in order to benefit from the insurance protection provided.
What is the maximum amount covered by the LAINF for a commuting injury?
The insurance coverage provided by LAINF for accidents on the road has a ceiling. The maximum guaranteed amount is CHF 150'000. This sum covers, within the established limits, the expenses related to the accident suffered during the commute or vice versa.
What happens if I do not file a complaint within 24 hours in the event of an accident while on the road?
If you do not file an accident report within 24 hours, LAINF insurance coverage may not be guaranteed. It is therefore crucial to respect this timeframe to ensure that the insurance can intervene and cover the costs related to the accident.
