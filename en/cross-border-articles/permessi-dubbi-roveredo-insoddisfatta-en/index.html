Doubtful permits, Roveredo dissatisfied and concerned by Chur's response

The municipality of Roveredo has highlighted three critical issues concerning risk assessment. Firstly, the fact that the same profile was rejected in Ticino but accepted in Graubünden indicates a flaw in the system. Secondly, the risk assessment was carried out without considering information related to the anti-mafia investigation. Thirdly, the risk assessment was carried out without considering information related to the work activities of the individuals involved.

The issue of residence permits for foreign workers is one of the most complex and sensitive for Ticino authorities. According to data from the Canton of Ticino, 3,456 residence permits for foreign workers were issued in 2020, while 4,231 were issued in 2021. This increase is mainly attributable to the labor demand from Ticino municipalities, particularly Lugano, Bellinzona, and Locarno.

However, the risk assessment system for residence permits has been criticized by several Ticino municipalities, including Roveredo. According to the mayor of Roveredo, "the risk assessment system is unsatisfactory and raises doubts about the safety of our citizens." The mayor cited the example of a foreign worker who was rejected in Ticino but accepted in Graubünden. "This is an example of the lack of coordination between Ticino and Graubünden authorities," stated the mayor.

Key points

Permits in Doubt, Roveredo Unhappy and Concerned with Chur's Response

The municipality of Roveredo has expressed its concerns regarding the risk assessment for cross-border work permits in the Grisons region. Specifically, they have requested a change in practice for a more effective and transparent risk assessment, as well as improved communication between the involved authorities.

The current situation seems to be criticized by many Ticino municipalities, such as Roveredo, which complains about a lack of transparency and effective communication between the authorities. "Risk assessment is a complex process that requires particular attention," declares the mayor of Roveredo. "However, the current situation is not satisfactory and needs a change."

The proposal to create a more effective and transparent risk assessment system was made by the municipality of Roveredo, which believes that this could help to reduce the time for risk assessment and improve communication between the authorities. In addition, it has been suggested to create a special team to assess risks and provide a faster and more effective response.

Risk assessment is a process that involves several stages, including information collection, risk assessment, and final decision. However, the current situation seems to be characterized by a lack of standardization and transparency, which can lead to errors and delays in risk assessment.

In particular, the municipality of Roveredo has complained about the lack of a clear and unified guide for risk assessment, which can lead to different interpretations and communication problems. "The current guide is too generic and does not provide a clear directive for risk assessment," declares the mayor of Roveredo.
