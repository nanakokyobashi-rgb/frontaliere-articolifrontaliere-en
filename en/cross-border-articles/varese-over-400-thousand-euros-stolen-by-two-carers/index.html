Varese: over 400 thousand euros stolen by two carers

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 9 October 2026

Over 400 thousand euros disputed to two caregivers The complaint started from the daughter of the elderly The VIP of Varese ordered a preventive seizure

Context

TL;DR

  • Over 400 thousand euros challenged to two caregivers
  • The complaint was filed by the elderly person's daughter
  • The Gip of Varese has ordered a preventive seizure
  • The procedure is still under preliminary investigation

Key facts

  • Accused → two women, one Italian and one Polish
  • Territory → province of Varese
  • Disputed sums → over 400 thousand euros
  • Funding in an account → approximately 1.2 million euros
  • Gold and other values → over 60 thousand euros
  • Asset seized → an insurance policy

Over 400 thousand euros were allegedly stolen from an elderly person entrusted to the care of two carers, one Italian and one Polish, both domiciled in the province of Varese. The two women were reported to the Public Prosecutor's Office of Varese for embezzlement, contested in competition.

At the center of the dispute are the movements on the elderly man's accounts and the financial operations carried out in the period close to his death and immediately following. The case emerged from the complaint filed by the daughter, who, after her father's death, had detected anomalies in his bank accounts.

The reconstruction of the Financial Police

The soldiers of the Financial Police Company of Saronno carried out the investigations under the coordination of the Varese Prosecutor's Office. According to the reconstruction of the investigators, the two women would have made improper use of the delegation that allowed them to operate on the elderly man's accounts. In the period leading up to his death and immediately afterwards, financial resources amounting to over 400 thousand euros were apparently disinvested.

After having been transferred to other accounts via bank drafts, the sums would have been reused in the subscription of further funds and policies. Furthermore, the sums flowing into one of the accounts would have brought the total funding to around 1.2 million euros.

During the checks, transfers of money between accounts and purchases of gold and other valuables, for more than 60 thousand euros, were also reconstructed. Once the activity was concluded, the investigating judge of the Court of Varese ordered the preventive seizure of an insurance policy, the value of which exceeds 400 thousand euros, linked to the sums examined in the investigation.

The process

The two women were reported for embezzlement. The case is still the subject of preliminary investigations; the assessment of responsibilities is left to the subsequent phases of the procedure. The operation is part of the Financial Police's initiatives dedicated to the protection of the most vulnerable people, especially in cases where the particularly vulnerable conditions of the victim could facilitate the undue subtraction of assets and financial resources.

The procedure is still under preliminary investigation. For this reason, the reconstruction of the investigators and the denunciation of the two women do not amount to the definitive ascertainment of responsibility. Key points

How to follow the documented steps

The sequence told by the source can be read by distinguishing the origin of the report, the financial assessments, the measure ordered by the GIP and the procedural phase. There are, in the text, no deadlines or further requirements to be added to this reconstruction.

From reporting to seizure

The first step is the discovery of anomalies. After the death of her father, the daughter noticed abnormal movements in her current accounts and filed a complaint against the two caregivers.

The second step concerns the investigative activity. The Company of the Finance Guard of Saronno carried out the investigations with the coordination of the Public Prosecutor's Office of Varese; the military examined the operations carried out on the accounts of the elderly man and the delegation that allowed the two women to operate on them.

The third step is the reconstruction of the disputed movements: disinvestments for over 400 thousand euros, transfers made by cashier's checks, sums then reused in the subscription of further funds and policies, as well as transfers between accounts and purchases of gold and other values for more than 60 thousand euros. The source also reports that on one of the accounts the total provision reached about 1.2 million euros.

The fourth step concerns the provision of the Gip of the Court of Varese: the judge ordered the preventive seizure of an insurance policy, worth more than 400 thousand euros, linked to the sums examined in the investigation.

The fifth step concerns the status of the procedure. The two women have been sued for alleged misappropriation in the competition, but the proceedings are still in the preliminary investigation phase. The responsibilities must therefore be ascertained in the following phases.

The source does not indicate other measures, required documents or deadlines to be met. The only reported financial outcome is the preventive seizure of the policy; any liability remains to be verified according to the development of the procedure. According to the complaint of the elder's daughter, they allegedly abused the power of attorney that allowed them to operate on the father's bank accounts, subtracting sums through financial transactions after his death.
