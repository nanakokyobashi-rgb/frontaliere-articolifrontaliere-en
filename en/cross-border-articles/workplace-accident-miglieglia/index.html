Injury at Miglieglia: the condition of the fallen worker is serious (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 16 August 2026

A 59-year-old road maintenance worker was seriously injured during brushcutting work along the cantonal road in Miglieglia.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Accident at work in Miglieglia along the cantonal road
  • A 59-year-old employee fell into a two-meter ditch
  • Conditions worsened during the night, life in danger
  • Police intervention, Green Cross and rescue services

Key facts

  • What: Accident at work with falling into a cliff
  • When: Yesterday around 11 am
  • Where: Cantonal road, via Cantonale a Miglieglia
  • Who: 59-year-old Swiss citizen domiciled in Lugano
  • Amount: No economic data specified
  • Outcome: Head injury and worsening life-threatening conditions
  • Rescues: Cantonal Police, Malcantone West Police and Green Cross of Lugano

The incident occurred yesterday just before 11 am along the cantonal road, specifically in Via Cantonale a Miglieglia. The accident at work involved a 59-year-old road maintenance worker, a Swiss citizen domiciled in Lugano. The man was carrying out brushcutting work when, for reasons that will have to be clarified in detail by the official investigation opened by the competent authorities, he lost his balance. The loss of balance caused him to fall into a ditch from an estimated height of about two meters, with an impact that caused a serious head injury.

Operational details

Clinical dynamics and updates

The initial information released immediately after the intervention of the rescuers spoke of wounds classified as of medium severity, suggesting a clinical course under control. However, the road maintenance worker's health picture took a dramatic turn for the worse over the next night. The worsening of the 59-year-old's clinical condition was confirmed by medical bulletins and official communications issued by the Cantonal Police, which monitors the evolution of the judicial and health situation linked to the accident.

Currently, the clinical situation of the worker is critical to the point that his life is considered endangered by the health workers who care for him in the hospital. This dramatic development shifts the focus to ascertaining responsibilities and verifying the safety conditions in which green maintenance personnel operated along cantonal roads. The cantonal police investigators are collecting useful elements to reconstruct the exact succession of events, assessing whether the individual protections and preventive measures adopted during the brushcutting were appropriate to the type of steep terrain that characterizes the Miglieglia area and the Malcantone area.

Key points

Intervention of the authorities and safety checks

The investigation opened by the Canton Police continues with the aim of fully shedding light on the technical and environmental causes that led to the 59-year-old losing his balance. The investigations focus on the conformation of the road edge along the Via Cantonale and on the operational procedures followed during the brush-clearing work, an activity that exposes operators to risks related to the slope of the land and the proximity to the steep cliffs typical of the Ticino region. The cooperation between the Canton Police and the Malcantone Ovest Police was crucial in the early hours to secure the area and allow access for the rescue vehicles of the Croce Verde of Lugano.

The severity of the accident draws general attention to safety on road construction sites and in land maintenance activities managed along the cantonal road network. For those operating in the maintenance and services sector in Ticino, the management of risks related to work in impervious areas represents a central element of verification by the competent bodies in matters of occupational accident prevention. In the next few hours, investigators will evaluate the position of the managers of the company or entity in charge of the brush-clearing work in Miglieglia, to verify compliance with all current regulations regarding worker protection and the prevention of occupational risks.
