Regulations and incentives

Cross-border workers must also consider tax regulations. In Switzerland, capital gains on securities are not taxed, but interest and dividends are subject to a 35% withholding tax. Additionally, as of January 1, 2024, cross-border workers residing in Ticino can benefit from a 20% tax incentive on investments in Swiss mutual funds, with a maximum annual cap of 10,000 CHF.

3. Monitor the markets: Keep an eye on market performance and the quarterly results of the companies in which you have invested. For example, the Swiss Market Index (SMI) has recorded a 12% growth in the last year, but with some companies showing performance below expectations, such as Swisscom, which saw a 5% decline in the last quarter.

4. Adapt your strategy: Be ready to modify your investment strategy based on market changes. For example, if the Swiss real estate market shows signs of slowing down, it might be opportunistic to reduce exposure to this sector and move funds to more dynamic sectors such as technology or healthcare.
