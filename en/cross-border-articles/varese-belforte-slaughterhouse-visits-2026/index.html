Varese: former Belforte Slaughterhouse opens for cleanup and guided tour

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 16 October 2026

On October 17 at Varese, the former Belforte slaughterhouse opens to the public from 14.00 to present the cleanup and remediation work funded by PNRR, followed by an exhibition.

On Saturday 17 October, from 2.00 pm, the former Civic Slaughterhouse of Belforte opens to the public. The event will be held in via Tonale in Varese and is open to the entire neighborhood and citizens. At the center of the meeting are the environmental reclamation and restoration works of the green area, supported by the European funds of the PNRR - Next Generation.

The project is part of the regeneration process of the Belforte neighborhood area. The interventions are presented as the step that returns the green area to collective use, making it available again for the community.

A program in two parts

The program follows a precise sequence. First, there will be a public meeting dedicated to the presentation of the environmental remediation and remediation works. Following this, the same space will host an exhibition accompanied by a mini guided tour.

The second part is organized as part of the Fundamentals Festival, described in the source as a widespread literary and cultural festival. The former Civic Slaughterhouse thus also becomes the place of a cultural proposal linked to the regeneration process of the area.

Access to the meeting is open to the entire neighborhood and citizens. To participate in the guided tour, however, online booking is required via the dedicated link. This distinction allows us to read the appointment as a structured day: a public moment to learn about the interventions and, subsequently, a visit linked to the exhibition and the festival.

For a cross-border commuter, the connection with this news is geographical and cultural: the event takes place in Varese and concerns the transformation of a space in the Belforte district. The source does not describe an employment measure, but a public event linked to urban regeneration and a cultural series.

The practical value of the communication lies in the distinction between the two moments. Anyone who wants to learn about the works supported by the European funds of the PNRR – Next Generation should refer to the meeting at 14.00. Anyone also interested in the cultural aspect should consider the exhibition and guided mini-tour scheduled afterward.

The second step concerns the exhibition accompanied by the mini tour. In this case, online booking is necessary. The procedure indicated by the source is therefore essential for anyone who wants to take part in the guided tour, while the public meeting is presented as open to the entire neighborhood and the public.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the date and time of the opening of the former Civic Slaughterhouse of Belforte?
The former Civic Slaughterhouse of Belforte will open to the public on Saturday, October 17, starting at 2:00 p.m. The event will be held in Via Tonale in Varese and is aimed at the entire neighborhood and citizens. This event is an opportunity to learn about the progress of the urban regeneration project.
What interventions are presented during the meeting at the former Belforte Slaughterhouse?
During the meeting, the remediation and environmental rehabilitation of the green area of the former Civic Slaughterhouse will be presented. These interventions are funded through the European funds of the PNRR – Next Generation and aim to return the area to the collective use of the community, making it available again.
Do I need a reservation to attend the event and the guided tour?
No reservation is required for the public meeting to present the works and it is open to the whole neighbourhood and to the public. However, to participate in the exhibition and the mini guided tour, which will be followed as part of the Fundamental Festivals, you need to book online through the dedicated link.
What is the main objective of the Belforte area regeneration project?
The main objective of the project is the regeneration of the Belforte district area, with the intention of returning the green area to collective use. The remediation and environmental rehabilitation interventions, supported by the European funds of the PNRR – Next Generation, aim to make space available again for the community.
What is the Fundamentals Festival mentioned in the article?
The Fundamental Festival is a widespread literary and cultural festival, in the context of which the exhibition and mini-guided tour at the former Civic Slaughterhouse are organised. This festival helps to integrate the cultural aspect into the regeneration process of the area, offering an additional opportunity to use the redeveloped space.
