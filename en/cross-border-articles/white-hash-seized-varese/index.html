Varese, the State Police seize the new White Hash

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 October 2026

On October 5 at Varese, the State Police seized White Hash, a substance with a high THC content. A young man who had been of legal age for only a few days was reported.

The check in central Varese

On the afternoon of last October 5, the Varese State Police seized a new type of narcotic substance during a foot patrol through the streets in the city center. The operation involved a young foreign national who had been of legal age for only a few days.

The officers were carrying out their usual duty when they noticed the man's suspicious behavior. When he saw the uniforms, the young man tried to get rid of a small bag he was carrying by throwing it on the ground. The officers immediately recovered the package.

The first check concerned precisely the discarded bag. A substance was found inside it, which was then subjected to the subsequent tests described in the report. The discovery therefore took place during the foot patrol conducted by the State Police in central Varese.

The episode unfolded from the officers' presence in the streets of the center to the young man's gesture in front of the uniformed officers. The small bag, initially in his possession, was left on the ground and immediately recovered by the officers. The account provided by the State Police thus links the operation to the behavior observed during the patrol.

The location of the patrol, the reaction upon seeing the uniforms, and the recovery of the package are the elements that mark out the first part of the operation described by the State Police. The young man had been of legal age for only a few days and was a foreign national. The bag recovered by the officers was therefore subjected to the tests indicated in the communication from the State Police.

Operational details

The new development reported by the Polizia di Stato concerns the type of substance identified. The material found in the sachet was grainy and white and was referred to in slang as White Hash. The source describes it as a new type of narcotic, characterized by a high amount of Thc.

In the report, Thc is indicated as the active ingredient in hashish and marijuana. Subsequent narcotics tests confirmed the substance’s positive result. The verification therefore accompanied the identification of the material found, after officers recovered the wrapping.

The progression from the discovery to the narcotics test distinguishes the stages of the verification. First, the sachet containing the grainy, white material was recovered; subsequently, the test confirmed the positive result. Key points

The follow-up reported by the State Police concerns two separate outcomes. The substance found was seized, while the young man was reported to the administrative authority for possession of narcotic substances for personal use.

The communication links the seizure to the material recovered by the officers and the report to the young man involved during the patrol.
