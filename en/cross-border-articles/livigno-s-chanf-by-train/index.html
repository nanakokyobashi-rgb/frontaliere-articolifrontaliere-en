According to the data provided by the pre-feasibility study, the potential demand for this connection could be between 68,000 and 193,000 passengers per year.

This project could be an important step for the development of the Ticino territory, in particular for border areas such as Livigno and S-chanf. According to the Federal Act on Rail Traffic of 14 December 2008, the federal government is obliged to ensure the creation of efficient and safe rail connections for all Swiss cantons.
