Operational details

In recent days, the works to replace the sound-absorbing barriers on the A8 motorway between Milan and Varese have been announced. This intervention is part of the broader extraordinary maintenance plan of the A8 motorway, which involves an investment of about 3.5 million euros.

The works will be carried out in two phases. The first phase, which began on 1 August, involves the replacement of sound-absorbing barriers along a stretch of about 5 km. The second phase, scheduled for September, will concern the maintenance of the support structures and dividing barriers.

According to information provided by the A8 motorway, the work will be carried out with a team of 10 workers, who will work in shifts of 8 hours a day, 6 days a week. The expected duration of the works is about 4 weeks, depending on weather conditions.

The replacement of sound-absorbing barriers is a necessary measure to ensure the safety of travellers. In fact, as reported by European legislation (Directive 2008/96/EC), sound-absorbing barriers must be replaced every 5 years, to ensure their effectiveness and prevent possible accidents.

The replacement of the sound-absorbing barriers on the A8 motorway will have a significant impact on the mobility of Ticino citizens. According to estimates of the A8 motorway, the works will have an impact of about -15% on the traffic capacity of the motorway, during peak hours.

To avoid possible slowdowns, the A8 motorway recommends using alternative routes, such as the A9 motorway or the state road 36 del Naviglio. In addition, you can use public transport services, such as buses and trains, to reach your destinations.
