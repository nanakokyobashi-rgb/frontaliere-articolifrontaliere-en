Teenager fatally hit by train in Bern

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 September 2026

Saturday night in Bern: a teenager was hit by a train at the Europaplatz station.

Context

In short

  • Teenager hit by a train in Bern
  • Fatal accident at Europaplatz station
  • Formal identification of the victim not yet carried out

Key facts

  • What: Fatal accident
  • When: Saturday evening, shortly after 10:40 PM
  • Where: Europaplatz station, Bern
  • Who: Teenager
  • Amount: Not specified

Saturday evening of blood in Bern. A teenager was hit by a train and suffered fatal injuries at Europaplatz station. The accident occurred shortly after 10:40 PM, according to the Bernese cantonal police. For reasons still to be clarified, the young person was on the tracks, between the platform and a departing train. Hit by the train, he died at the scene due to the injuries sustained. The main hypothesis remains that of an accident. The authorities specify that there are concrete clues about the identity of the victim, but formal identification has not yet been carried out. The railway line remained closed for nearly two and a half hours, with the activation of replacement buses.

Intervention of the authorities

In addition to several services of the Bernese cantonal police, the intervention involved the firefighters of the Bern Protection and Rescue Service, BLS personnel, the transport police, an emergency doctor, two ambulances, the Institute of Forensic Medicine of the University of Bern, and the Care Team. The Bernese cantonal police have opened an investigation to clarify the circumstances of the accident.
