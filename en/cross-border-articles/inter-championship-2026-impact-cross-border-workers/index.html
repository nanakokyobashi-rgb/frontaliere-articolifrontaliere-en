Inter beat Parma 2-0 and become Italian champions for the twenty-first time in their history. After 39 years, the Nerazzurri celebrate the tricolor at home. The goals that allowed Chivu's boys to reach the finish line with three games to spare bear the signatures of Thuram and Mkhitaryan. At San Siro, a good Parma could do nothing against the enthusiasm and hunger of Inter, eager to capitalize on the first match point.

It took some time for the home team to break through the organized low block of Parma. Once again, the Nerazzurri unlocked the result at the end of the first half, thanks to Thuram's goal, who has flourished with the arrival of spring after the difficulties of winter. At 46', in fact, the Frenchman receives a superb through ball from the edge of the box by Zielinski and scores the 1-0 against Suzuki. Before the goal, the best chance for Chivu's boys came at 25' on Barella's left foot from inside the area, which hit the crossbar: Suzuki then reacted to anticipate Thuram's arrival, ready for the easiest of tap-ins.
