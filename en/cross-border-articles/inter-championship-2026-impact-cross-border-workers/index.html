Inter wins Serie A: what changes for cross-border workers

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 May 2026

Inter wins its 21st scudetto. Impacts for cross-border workers employed in Ticino and living in Italy.

Context

In brief

  • Inter Italian champions after 39 years
  • 21st Scudetto for the Nerazzurri
  • Decisive goals by Thuram and Mkhitaryan

Key facts

  • What: Inter win the Scudetto
  • When: May 3, 2026
  • Where: San Siro Stadium, Milan
  • Who: Inter vs. Parma
  • Score: Final result 2-0

Inter beat Parma 2-0 and become Italian champions for the twenty-first time in their history. After 39 years, the Nerazzurri celebrate the tricolor at home. The goals that allowed Chivu's boys to reach the finish line with three games to spare bear the signatures of Thuram and Mkhitaryan. At San Siro, a good Parma could do nothing against the enthusiasm and hunger of Inter, eager to capitalize on the first match point.

It took some time for the home team to break through the organized low block of Parma. Once again, the Nerazzurri unlocked the result at the end of the first half, thanks to Thuram's goal, who has flourished with the arrival of spring after the difficulties of winter. At 46', in fact, the Frenchman receives a superb through ball from the edge of the box by Zielinski and scores the 1-0 against Suzuki. Before the goal, the best chance for Chivu's boys came at 25' on Barella's left foot from inside the area, which hit the crossbar: Suzuki then reacted to anticipate Thuram's arrival, ready for the easiest of tap-ins.

Impact on cross-border workers

The victory of Inter does not have a direct impact on the working conditions of cross-border workers, but it represents a moment of celebration that could influence the mood and motivation of workers. Many cross-border workers who work in Ticino and live in Italy could feel inspired by this sports success, which reflects passion and determination.

Parma, despite the defeat, showed they did not come to Milan to be sacrificial victims and built some interesting offensive plays. The best chance came at 20', when Pellegrino in the center of the area did not find the net with his left foot volley. At halftime, Esposito, physically troubled, came off, and Bonny came on, while at 66' Lautaro also returned to the field.

In the second half, Inter seek the double, having fun and containing the guests' counterattacks without problems. At 70', Nicolussi Caviglia is providential in anticipating Mkhitaryan a few steps from the goal, two minutes later Dumfries shoots high the invitation from the left of Dimarco. The 2-0 goal, however, is just a matter of minutes and comes at 80': Lautaro sprints on the edge of offside and puts the ball in the center for Mkhitaryan who only has to tap it in. In the final, Elphege's consolation goal is disallowed for offside of the assistman Almqvist. Suzuki saves on the line, instead, the possible hat-trick of Frattesi. At the final whistle of Bonacina, the Nerazzurri party starts with three games to spare.
