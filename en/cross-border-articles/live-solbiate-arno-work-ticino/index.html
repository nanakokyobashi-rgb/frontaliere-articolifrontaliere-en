On 1 January 2024, the new border agreement between Italy and Switzerland came into force, opening up new opportunities for cross-border workers. But what exactly does it mean for a border worker who lives in Solbiate Arno and works in Ticino?

Working in Ticino without tax at source

Thanks to the new agreement, border workers can work in Ticino without having to pay the tax at source in Italy. This means that they will not have to pay income tax in Italy, but will be able to benefit from the tax credit in Switzerland.

For example, if a frontier worker works in Ticino and earns 50,000 Swiss francs a year, he will not have to pay income tax in Italy. However, you will need to file your tax return in Switzerland and benefit from the tax credit.
