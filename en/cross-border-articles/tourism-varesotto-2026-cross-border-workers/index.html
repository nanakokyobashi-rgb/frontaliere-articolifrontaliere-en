Tourism in Varesotto: 2026 figures and opportunities for cross-border workers (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 7 May 2026

The 2026 data confirms the growth of tourism in Varesotto, with 3.2 million overnight stays. Opportunities for cross-border workers between lake and city.

Context

In brief

  • 3.2 million overnight stays in Varesotto in 2026
  • Somma Lombardo tops the chart with 483,251 arrivals
  • Varese and Saronno among the main tourist hubs

Key facts

  • What: 2026 tourism data in Varesotto
  • When: January-December 2026
  • Where: Province of Varese
  • Who: Lombardy Region
  • Amount: 3,272,972 total tourist arrivals

Tourism in the province of Varese continues to show signs of growth and consolidation, with numbers that tell of an attractive territory both for international tourism, thanks to its proximity to Malpensa and Milan, and for local tourism. Presented during the meeting organized by Councillor Caruso in Gallarate, the data from the regional DAI Destinations platform, referring to the period January-December 2026 and focused on overnight stays, depict a province capable of combining different forms of tourism, with established hubs but also emerging destinations.

Somma Lombardo tops the chart of municipalities with the highest number of arrivals, recording 483,251 overnight stays, equal to 15% of the provincial total. A figure closely linked to its proximity to Malpensa and the presence of accommodation facilities dedicated to airport flows, but which also testifies to the strategic role of the area as a gateway to the territory. Immediately after comes Varese with 360,585 arrivals, equal to 11%, confirming the provincial capital as a reference point for cultural, congress, and naturalistic tourism, as well as sports tourism, both in terms of practice on vacation and in terms of hosting competitive events.

Operational details

Analysis of Opportunities

The Varesotto, with its dual tourist vocation, offers numerous opportunities for cross-border workers who wish to explore the region during their free time. The proximity to Malpensa and Milan makes the area easily accessible, while the presence of accommodation facilities dedicated to airport flows guarantees a wide choice of accommodation.

Cultural and Naturalistic Tourism

Varese, with its 360,585 visitors, is a significant cultural and naturalistic hub. Cross-border workers can take advantage of this destination to participate in cultural events, visit museums, and enjoy the surrounding nature. The city also offers numerous sports activities, making it an ideal destination for those who love physical activity.

Lake Tourism

Lakeside resorts such as Maccagno with Pino and Veddasca and Angera are perfect for those seeking a bit of tranquility and relaxation. These destinations offer a wide range of outdoor activities, such as hiking, lakeside walks, and water sports. Cross-border workers can easily reach these locations for a day trip or a long weekend.

Proximity Tourism

Saronno, with its 286,674 visitors, is an example of proximity tourism. Thanks to its proximity to Milan and the presence of frequent rail connections, Saronno almost serves as a peripheral district of the big city. Cross-border workers who work in Milan can easily reach Saronno for a relaxing break or to participate in local events.

Key points

What to do for cross-border workers

For cross-border workers who want to explore Varesotto, there are several options to consider. Here are some practical tips for planning your next trip.

Plan your visit

Before you leave, it's important to plan your visit. Decide what type of tourist experience you want to have: cultural, naturalistic, or relaxation. Once decided, you can choose the destination that best suits your needs.

Choose accommodation

Varesotto offers a wide range of accommodations, from facilities dedicated to airport flows to more traditional ones. Choose the accommodation that best suits your needs and budget. Remember that some facilities may offer discounted rates for cross-border workers.

Organize activities

Once you arrive at your destination, organize the activities you want to do. Whether it's a visit to a museum, a walk along the lake, or a day of sports, make sure you have everything you need to enjoy your experience to the fullest.

Use the cost calculator for your budget

To manage your budget effectively, use the cost calculator to estimate the expenses of your visit. This will help you plan your finances better and avoid unexpected expenses.

Conclusion

Varesotto is an ideal tourist destination for cross-border workers who want to explore the region during their free time. With its dual tourist vocation and proximity to Malpensa and Milan, Varesotto offers numerous opportunities for leisure and relaxation. Plan your visit, choose the most suitable accommodation, and organize the activities you want to do to enjoy your experience to the fullest.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the main tourist destinations in the Varesotto area?
The main tourist destinations in the Varesotto area include Somma Lombardo, Varese, Saronno, Maccagno with Pino and Veddasca, Angera, Vergiate, and Gallarate. Each location offers different opportunities, from cultural and natural tourism to lake tourism and proximity tourism.
How can I easily reach the Varesotto area?
The Varesotto area is easily accessible due to its proximity to Malpensa and Milan. You can reach the area by car, train, or bus. Many locations are well connected by public transport, making access easy even without a car.
What activities can I do in the Varesotto area?
In the Varesotto area, you can engage in a wide range of activities, including cultural visits, natural excursions, water sports, lakeside walks, and participation in local events. The choice depends on your preferences and the type of experience you wish to have.
