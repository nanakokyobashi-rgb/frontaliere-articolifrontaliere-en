Operational details

For cross-border workers, the importance of the Single Certification 2026 fits into the broader context of income tax returns in Italy. Although employment income is taxed at source in Switzerland, a cross-border worker residing in Italy is required to declare this income on Form 730. The filing process is essential in order to benefit from the tax credit mechanism, which offsets taxes already paid in Switzerland and prevents the same income from being taxed twice. Section CE of Form 730 is the specific section dedicated to income earned abroad and taxes paid, where the applicable credit is calculated. This credit is essential for reducing or eliminating Italian tax on Swiss income, in line with the provisions of the 1976 Convention and the 2020 New Cross-Border Workers Agreement.
