More. From Livigno to the Engadine in ten minutes. (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 25 July 2026

A project for a railway connection between Livigno and S-chanf has been presented.

Context

The project for a rail link between Livigno and S-chanf was presented last week during an information event in Livigno. The project involves the construction of a 14.64 km long railway tunnel, which will reduce the travel time between Livigno and S-chanf to 7-10 minutes. This new rail connection could revolutionize the way people move between the province of Sondrio and the Engadine.

According to the project's estimates, the railway tunnel could be completed by 2030, provided that requests for funding from the Confederation are approved. The estimated cost for the implementation of the project is about CHF 1.2 billion, of which 70% is covered by federal funds. The rest will be covered by regional and private funds.

The rail link between Livigno and S-chanf will be an important step forward for sustainable mobility in Switzerland. Currently, the journey time between Livigno and S-chanf is about 1 hour and 20 minutes, with local and regional public transport. With the new rail connection, people will be able to reach S-chanf in just 10 minutes, greatly reducing travel time and improving mobility throughout the region.

Operational details

The railway connection project between southern Graubünden and central Graubünden, through the canton of Graubünden, is still at an early stage. According to a press release, a pre-feasibility study has been developed so far. This study focuses on assessing the feasibility conditions of the project, taking into account factors such as the topography of the territory, transport needs and logistics.

According to reports, the railway connection would have a length of about 27 kilometres and could be built using innovative technologies to minimise the environmental impact. The project involves the construction of a railway line connecting Livigno to the Engadine, passing through the municipality of Poschiavo. This connection would have a significant impact on the infrastructure of Graubünden, greatly improving mobility and connection between the different areas of the canton.

The main objective of the project is to reduce travel times between Livigno and the Engadine, which are currently about 45 minutes. With the construction of this rail connection, travel time could be reduced to about 10 minutes. This would have a positive impact on mobility and connection between the different areas of Graubünden, improving the quality of life for residents and tourists.

Key points

The financial contribution of Italy will be decisive for the possibilities of carrying out the project, as explained by the director of the Ticino regional development agency: > "The support of Italy is fundamental to overcome the limits of budget and human resources that we are encountering".

The project in question involves the creation of a railway tunnel that will connect the Livigno valley to the Engadine valley, reducing travel time between the two places from more than an hour to about ten minutes. This tunnel will be a real strategic infrastructure for the region, allowing the transport of goods and passengers quickly and efficiently.

To finance this project, Italy has allocated a contribution of 200 million euros, which will be used for the construction of the tunnel and for the construction of the necessary infrastructure. In addition, the Italian government has also decided to increase the share of funding for the project, from a 30% contribution to a 40% contribution.

The implementation of this project is of fundamental importance for the Ticino region, which needs modern and efficient infrastructure to support economic and social development. The railway tunnel will be an important connecting element between the region and the rest of the country, enabling the transport of goods and passengers quickly and efficiently.
