Roberto Gatti built a career between Italy and Switzerland, first as a player and then as a coach, with Varese becoming his sporting home and Chiasso at the center of his first promotion from the bench. The new episode of «Figli di un gol minore» retraces this journey, from his origins beneath the Mole to the eleven promotions won on pitches in the two countries.

From the Mole to Varese

Raised in the shadow of Grande Torino, with his father passing on his passion to him, Gatti took his first steps at Barcanova. In the early '80s, he arrived at Varese and found his place beneath Sacro Monte. His debut, at a very young age, came in Serie B, under the guidance of Catuzzi, described in the source as an innovator. Beppe Marotta also worked at that Varese, at the time a promising executive.

As a player, Gatti developed a precise tactical identity. He was a defender of another era, committed to pure man-marking, but he was also among the first to experiment with the early theories of zonal play. His personal ties to the Varese area and the club's subsequent bankruptcy took him across the border. In Switzerland, he wore the shirts of Lugano and Chiasso, collected promotions and experienced high-level football across the Alps, alongside numerous Swiss internationals.

His move to the bench came almost by chance, precisely at Chiasso. At just thirty, he took charge of the first team and secured his first historic promotion to Serie B. It was from that moment that the reputation of «Mister Promozione» took shape: a pragmatic and charismatic coach, capable of achieving eleven promotions between Switzerland and Italy. The clubs he led include Mozzate, Inveruno, Sant'Angelo and Solbiate, along with Solbiatese's recent triumph in the Eccellenza playoffs.

Operational details

The journey that earns a nickname

The reputation of «Mr. Promotion» takes shape after his move from Chiasso and his numerous experiences in Switzerland and Italy. The source describes him as a pragmatic and charismatic coach, capable of achieving as many as eleven promotions. The towns of Mozzate, Inveruno, Sant'Angelo and Solbiate are part of the same trajectory.

His approach is based on managing large squads and on the quality of individual players. These are the elements through which Roberto Gatti is presented as a provincial leader, capable of leaving an indelible mark in every dressing room. The profile is not limited to results: it also encompasses the way he leads large squads and brings out the quality of individual players.

Two snapshots of the journey

Among the most iconic moments is the dramatic survival secured in the Serie D relegation playoffs with Sant'Angelo. Survival was decided in the 91st minute by a memorable bicycle kick, an episode cited among the most significant of Gatti's journey.

The other highlighted passage is more recent and concerns Solbiatese, the protagonist of a triumph in the Eccellenza playoffs. The result is part of the journey of the eleven triumphs built by the coach between the two countries, following experiences gained in towns such as Mozzate, Inveruno, Sant'Angelo and Solbiate.

The account thus juxtaposes survival secured in stoppage time and a triumph achieved in the playoffs. These are two episodes evoked to describe the journey of a coach who has built his reputation in provincial football, through the management of large squads, the quality of individual players and the ability to leave a mark in dressing rooms.

Key points

From address book to podcast

For a decade, «Sons of a minor goal» was a column in VareseNews dedicated to the most beautiful and fascinating stories of amateur football. Its evolution was born with Radio Materia, the VareseNews website: today the project is also a podcast with the voices of the protagonists.

The audio format includes interviews with the interpreters of "bread and salami" football. The storytelling field includes the great former players loaned to the lower categories, the great-small businesses born on the provincial fields and the adventures lived around the world, including in Vanuatu.

The episode dedicated to Roberto Gatti is part of this series of stories. Its history, between Varese, Chiasso, Lugano and the other squares led on the bench, represents for the same source a perfect example of what it means to be "Children of a minor goal". The podcast thus brings the voice of the protagonists to the fore, according to the formula announced with the birth of Radio Materia.

The new episode can be heard every day at 12 noon on Radio Materia and on all audio streaming platforms. The daily appointment accompanies the passage of the column from the written story to the series of interviews, keeping the stories of amateur football and the people who have lived them at the centre. The story of Gatti enters this path as one of the stories related to the eleven promotions between Switzerland and Italy.

Frequently Asked Questions
How many promotions has Roberto Gatti won as a coach?

Where did Roberto Gatti play in Switzerland?
He wore the jerseys of Lugano and Chiasso, collecting promotions in a transalpine football of high technical level with numerous Red Cross national teams, after his beginnings at Barcanova and his arrival at Varese in the early 1980s.
