Varese, steals from MediaWorld and shoves security guard: arrested

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 29 September 2026

Young man arrested for robbery at MediaWorld in Varese after stealing a phone and pushing a security guard.

Context

In brief

  • Arrest in flagrante delicto at MediaWorld in Varese
  • A phone and electronic equipment worth a few hundred euros
  • A shove against the security guard led to the charge of improper robbery
  • Arrest validated, ban on staying in the Varese area

Key facts

Location → MediaWorld in Varese Charge → improper robbery, article 628, second paragraph, of the criminal code Intervention → Carabinieri of Varese Hearing → direct trial before the single judge of Varese Outcome → arrest validated and ban on residing in the Varese area Penalty → from 5 to 10 years and a fine from 927 to 2,500 euros

On Monday morning, the Carabinieri of Varese brought before the single judge a case that began at the exit of the city's MediaWorld store. A young foreign citizen, in his early twenties, was arrested in flagrante delicto after trying to leave the shop with a phone and other electronic and computer material, for a value indicated at a few hundred euros.

According to the reconstruction contained in the source, the attempt to make it to the exit was not limited to the theft of the goods. The moment the security guard tried to intervene, the man allegedly pushed him. It is this violent behavior, occurring immediately after the theft, that supports the charge of improper robbery, rather than just the hypothesis of theft.

The intervention of the Carabinieri took place when the man had already reached the exit of the large store specializing in the sale of computer equipment. The arrest was carried out in flagrante delicto and the stolen goods, according to reports, were later returned to their rightful owners.

On Monday morning, the case arrived in the courtroom with a direct trial procedure. The public prosecutor indicated in the source is Lucilla Gagliardi; the single judge of Varese validated the arrest. The man was released, with a ban on residing in the Varese area, a measure linked to the fact that he resides outside the province of Varese.

The charge concerns article 628, second paragraph, of the criminal code. For simple improper robbery, the source indicates imprisonment from five to ten years and a fine from 927 to 2,500 euros. The defendant remains innocent until proven otherwise: the validated arrest describes a procedural step, not a conviction.

Operational details

What the accusation means

The source also explains the legal meaning of the expression improper robbery. This occurs when a person has taken possession of someone else's property and, immediately afterward, uses violence or threats to retain possession of the item or to secure impunity for themselves or others. In the case described, the element that shifted the charge from theft to robbery was therefore the pushing of the security guard while attempting to leave the store.

The distinction indicated in the text concerns the timing of the violence. In robbery proper, violence or threats are used to take possession of the property. In improper robbery, however, they are used immediately after the taking, to retain possession of the goods or to avoid being identified or arrested.

The sentencing framework reported for the basic form ranges from five to ten years' imprisonment, with a fine of between 927 and 2.500 euros. The source also specifies that, in the presence of aggravating circumstances such as the use of weapons or the involvement of several people acting together, the penalties may increase.

In the account of the case, the source mentions the stolen electronic and computer equipment and the pushing of a security guard. The value of the stolen goods, described as a few hundred euros, therefore does not exhaust the description of the accusation. The shift from theft to improper robbery is attributed to the conduct following the taking, which occurred while the man was trying to make his way out of MediaWorld di Varese.

The explanation contained in the source serves to distinguish two different moments: the moment when the property is taken and the immediately following moment, in which violence or threats may be used to retain it or to secure impunity. It is this second phase that the reported charge focuses on.
