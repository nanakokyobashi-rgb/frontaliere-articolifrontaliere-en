Pentecost 2026: traffic returning to Ticino (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 25 May 2026

After the Pentecost long weekend, the return to Ticino promises fewer traffic jams than in previous years. Here's what to expect.

Context

In brief

  • Pentecost 2026 return: fewer queues expected
  • Monday, May 25th peak traffic day
  • Maximum 2 km of queue at Airolo

Key facts

  • What: Pentecost return
  • When: May 25th, 2026
  • Where: A2 motorway, San Gottardo south portal
  • Who: Federal Office for Roads (FEDRO)
  • Amount: 21 km of queue in 2025

After the long Pentecost weekend, the return to Ticino is expected to be less problematic compared to previous years. According to FEDRO forecasts, Monday, May 25th will be the last day with high traffic. The south portal of the San Gottardo, particularly at Airolo, will be the critical point, but no critical situations like those recorded between Friday and Saturday are expected, with a peak of 21 kilometers of queue and waiting times that exceeded four hours.

Pentecost 2025: the comparison

Last year, during the Pentecost holiday, traffic at Airolo reached a maximum wait time of one hour and 20 minutes. Traffic remained intense from 10 AM to 8 PM, then gradually decreased during the night. In previous years, both in 2023 and 2024, delays never exceeded 40 minutes. This Sunday, some returns were also recorded, with a queue that fluctuated between one and two kilometers throughout the day. Traffic heading north was sustained but within the norm, with delays that never exceeded 30 minutes.

Operational details

Traffic conditions analysis

The USTRA forecasts indicate that the return traffic to Ticino will be less intense compared to previous years. However, it's important to consider various factors that can influence the journey. For example, weather conditions can play a crucial role. In case of bad weather, queues could lengthen, despite optimistic forecasts. Additionally, unforeseen events such as accidents or roadworks can cause delays.

Comparison with previous years

To get a clearer picture of the situation, it's useful to compare data from previous years. In 2025, traffic peaked at 21 kilometers of queue, with waiting times exceeding four hours. In 2024, however, delays never exceeded 40 minutes. This comparison shows how traffic conditions can vary significantly from one year to the next.

Possible scenarios

There are several possible scenarios for the Pentecost 2026 return. If the forecasts prove accurate, traffic should be manageable, with waiting times lower than in previous years. However, if adverse weather conditions or other unforeseen events occur, queues could lengthen. In this case, it's important to have a plan B, such as alternative routes or flexible departure times.

Practical advice

For cross-border workers who need to cross the Alps heading south, there are some practical tips to follow. First, it's advisable to leave early in the morning or late at night to avoid peak hours. Additionally, it's useful to monitor real-time traffic conditions using navigation apps. Finally, it's important to have water, snacks, and a portable phone charger on hand.

Key points

What to do in case of traffic jams

If you find yourself in a situation of heavy traffic, there are some precautions you can take to make the journey more manageable. Make sure you have water, snacks, and a portable phone charger at your disposal. If possible, try to avoid peak hours, which usually occur between 10 AM and 8 PM. Use navigation apps that provide real-time traffic updates, such as Google Maps or Waze, to find alternative routes.

Trip Planning

To plan your trip, it is useful to consider several factors. First of all, choose a departure time that allows you to avoid peak hours. Additionally, monitor real-time traffic conditions using navigation apps. Finally, make sure you have everything you need to make the trip more comfortable, such as water, snacks, and a portable phone charger.

Use of alternative routes

In case of excessive traffic jams, it is useful to consider alternative routes. Navigation apps can suggest alternative routes that avoid areas of heavy traffic. However, it is important to verify that these routes are safe and suitable for your vehicle. Additionally, consider that alternative routes may be longer or less familiar, so plan your trip accordingly.

Monitoring traffic conditions

Monitoring real-time traffic conditions is essential to avoid excessive traffic jams. Use navigation apps that provide real-time traffic updates, such as Google Maps or Waze. These apps can alert you in case of accidents or roadworks, allowing you to plan your trip more efficiently.
