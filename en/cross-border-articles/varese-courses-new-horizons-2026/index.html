Varese Corsi: 6,327 Enrollments, 2026 Courses (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 7 May 2026

Varese Corsi Net reports 6,327 enrollments and introduces flight simulations and Vocal Fitness for English. New synergies among teachers, institutions, and businesses.

Context

In Brief - Varese Corsi Net Records 6,327 Enrollments in 2026 - 634 Courses Activated with 281 Teachers Across 52 Venues - New Courses: Flight Simulations and Vocal Fitness ## Key Facts - What: Presentation of New Varese Corsi Net Courses - When: May 5, 2026 - Where: Spazio Libero di Varese News - Who: Faculty, Institutions, Sponsors, and Businesses - Enrollments: 6,327 Varese Corsi Net has unveiled the new horizons of its project combining training, culture, and social aspects. The May 5, 2026, meeting brought together faculty, institutions, and businesses at Spazio Libero di Varese News, which has hosted part of the courses since 2025. The project, active since 1984, recorded 6,327 enrollments in the current season, with 281 teachers distributed across 52 venues in the province. Marcello Vitella of Yop Comunicazione outlined the year's progress, highlighting growth in the under-30 age group thanks to collaboration with the University of Insubria. Among the new courses presented for the next term are flight simulations and the 'Vocal Fitness' method proposed by Steve Depper for learning English phonetics. ### New Courses and Synergies The training plan aims to promote the culture of psychophysical well-being through proposals ranging from risotto-making to pilates, up to first aid courses. The meeting gave voice to various territorial realities, such as the Centro Gulliver, which will open its communities for therapeutic sports paths. Another example of integration between profit and non-profit comes from the Orti di Bregazzana, where cooking courses for parents and children are held to strengthen relationships through manual work. The transversality of the network also involves technological partners like Buy tec, focused on the sustainability of refurbished goods, and historical institutions like the Istituto De Filippi, which makes its kitchens available for corporate team-building activities. At the meeting's opening, Marco Giovannelli, director of VareseNews, drew attention to the silent strength of this complex ecosystem. In his speech, he recalled how the courage of the Varese Municipality in entrusting the new management allowed the activation of unprecedented energies. Also present was Mayor Davide Galimberti, who confirmed the public entity's willingness to invest in this service, deeming it fundamental for roots in the productive and commercial fabric. ### Future Objectives The goal for the next eight years of management is to consolidate a qualitatively important network that generates value beyond mere educational offerings. Varese Corsi Net continues to grow, offering new training opportunities and creating synergies among various territorial actors.
