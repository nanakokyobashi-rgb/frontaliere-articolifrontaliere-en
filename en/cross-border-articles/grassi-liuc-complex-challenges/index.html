Grassi: 'Complex challenges, but Liuc is ready'

Di Laura Bianchi · Published on 1 May 2026

Roberto Grassi, new president of Liuc-Università Cattaneo, tackles the challenges of the manufacturing world and innovation.

Context

In brief

  • Roberto Grassi is the new president of Liuc-Università Cattaneo in Castellanza
  • Liuc is a university born from businesses and for businesses
  • Main challenges include demographic winter and artificial intelligence

Key facts

  • What: Appointment of Roberto Grassi as president of Liuc
  • When: Not yet specified
  • Where: Castellanza, Italy
  • Who: Roberto Grassi, entrepreneur and former president of Confindustria Varese
  • Amount: Not yet specified

Roberto Grassi is the new president of Liuc-Università Cattaneo in Castellanza. An entrepreneur and former head of Confindustria Varese, he takes over during a transitional phase that he defines as 'in continuity and harmony.' The appointment comes after Riccardo Comerio's move to the presidency of ITS Incom, a decision made following recent internal changes in the system.

'It's a path of continuity and harmony. Varese has a unique climate, made up of personal as well as institutional relationships. My availability also stems from a phase of reorganization; for Riccardo Comerio, managing both roles would have been complex. I was asked to step in and I accepted. For us, Liuc is the most important investment made over the years as a confindustrial system. It's a great honor but also a responsibility, especially in a not-so-simple phase, which is also stimulating.'

Operational details

In recent times, there has been much talk about networking and creating a system. Is this really a concrete priority today? And what are the next steps in that direction? 'Today, creating a system is no longer a slogan but a necessity. Collaborations, shared projects, and innovation initiatives demonstrate this. The next step will be to update the strategic plan, in continuity with what has been done in recent years, to define shared growth and development objectives, both internationally and in terms of education.'

How important is the Varese context in this journey? 'It's very important. It's a territory with a strong industrial culture and quality relationships. This makes the difference and allows us to tackle even the most complex challenges with a shared approach.'

Continuous training and innovation

Continuous training is another crucial topic for Liuc. 'Skills today age quickly, and companies struggle to find qualified people. We need to invest in the growth of our employees. Liuc is at the forefront in this field, offering customized paths to meet the needs of businesses.'

Technological innovation, particularly artificial intelligence, represents a significant challenge. 'AI is changing the way we work and do business. Liuc is committed to training professionals capable of managing these new technologies, offering cutting-edge courses and laboratories.'

Collaborations and shared projects

Collaborations with other universities and research centers are fundamental. 'We have over 150 international connections in 40 countries. This allows us to exchange knowledge and best practices, enriching our educational and research offerings.'

Key points

To tackle the challenges of the future, Liuc is updating its strategic plan. 'The next step will be to define shared growth and development goals, both internationally and in terms of education. This will allow us to be even more competitive and to offer quality education.'

What to do for cross-border workers

Cross-border workers who work in Ticino and reside in Italy can benefit from the opportunities offered by Liuc. 'Our university is open to everyone and offers flexible educational paths, suitable for the needs of cross-border workers.'

Enrollment procedure

To enroll in Liuc courses, you need to follow a few simple steps: 1. Visit the official Liuc website and consult the educational offer. 2. Choose the course of interest and verify the admission requirements. 3. Fill out the online enrollment application. 4. Send the required documentation. 5. Wait for the admission confirmation. 6. Make the tuition payment. 7. Start the educational path.

Useful tools

For further information and to calculate the cost of training, you can use the Liuc calculator. This tool allows you to get an accurate estimate of the costs and opportunities available.

Conclusion

Liuc-Università Cattaneo di Castellanza is ready to face the challenges of the future with an innovative and collaborative approach. Thanks to its strong connection with the business world and its quality educational offer, Liuc represents a reference point for those seeking cutting-edge education. For cross-border workers, Liuc offers unique opportunities for professional and personal growth.
