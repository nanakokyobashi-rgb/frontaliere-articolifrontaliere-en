Need extra help working on commissions

The Ortelli motion called for the introduction of an assistant secretary/parliamentary researcher to assist the work in the committees. The request was presented with the aim of simplifying the work of parliamentarians and increasing the efficiency of committees.

The question that arises is: how much time and resources are needed to manage the work of the committees? According to data from 2022, the State Council of the Canton of Ticino has dedicated about 30,000 hours of work to the work of the commissions. If we consider that each committee has a minimum of 10 members, this means that each member has dedicated about 3 hours per week to committee work.

This is a significant number, considering that the Ticino parliamentarians also have other commitments, such as the management of their professional and family activities. It is therefore understandable that the request of a parliamentary deputy secretary/researcher was submitted.

The expected annual cost for the Canton of Ticino for the deputy secretary/parliamentary researcher would be about CHF 80,000 for salary. To this would be added a contribution of about 10,000 francs for mission treatment, bringing the total cost to about 90,000 francs per year.
