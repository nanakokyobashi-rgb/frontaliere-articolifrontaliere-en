Varese: gas and water works on three streets until April 2027 (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 26 September 2026

Starting September 28, 2026, Lereti (Gruppo Acinque) will begin work on Via Masolino da Panicale, Pagliano, and Giovannini. No traffic or parking.

Operational details

The practical meaning of the plan changes depending on the reader's position relative to the three roads. The source provides for a prohibition on transit and parking, with an exception for residents and pedestrians. For a cross-border commuter who has access to one of the roads involved, the decisive factor is therefore the relationship between their destination and the active construction phase: it is not enough to know that the works are in Varese; one must understand which section is affected at that moment.

A Restriction That Follows Progress

The progressive nature of the intervention changes daily management. The three roads do not have a single common deadline: the plan is divided into three phases, with periods that overlap and with the application of restrictions linked to the progress of the works. This schedule makes it possible to interpret the measure by section, without automatically turning the entire set of roads into a single construction area.

For those traveling for work, the difference between the phases matters more than the overall duration alone. A planned passage near Masolino da Panicale requires a different reading from one heading toward Pagliano or Giovannini, because each section is associated with its own time window. mappa dei valichi can be used as an additional tool for navigating the border route, while the phase dates remain the reference for the Varese construction site.

The Expected Benefit After the Construction Work

The restriction is temporary, but the project has a broader infrastructure objective. The complete replacement of the pipes and the transfer of approximately 30 connections accompany the restoration of the road. The new infrastructure will be integrated with more advanced and digitized monitoring and management systems: according to the source, they will help identify any anomalies more quickly, better schedule maintenance and limit new interventions on the roadway over time. For the reader, the comparison is therefore between a phase of regulated access and an expected outcome of greater reliability, safety and reduced water and gas leakage.

Key points

To turn the schedule into a practical decision, it is advisable to follow a short sequence. The data to relate are the street, the date of the phase, and the exception provided for residents and pedestrians.

Four-step procedure

1. Identify the street of your route and check whether it coincides with Masolino da Panicale, Pagliano or Giovannini. For via Giovannini, the section up to the intersection with via Masolino da Panicale must be considered; for Masolino, the section up to the intersection with via Pagliano applies.

2. Match the date to the correct phase. Phase 1 starts on 28 September 2026 and ends on 30 November 2026; phase 2 concerns via Pagliano from 15 October 2026 to 25 March 2027; phase 3 concerns via Giovannini from 28 October 2026 to 2 April 2027.

3. Distinguish transit and parking from the personal category. The rule reported is a prohibition for both modes, with an exception for residents and pedestrians. A cross-border worker who does not fall into these categories must therefore take into account the limitation indicated for the section concerned.

4. Consider the actual progress of the construction site. The works are carried out progressively, and the schedule is subject to unforeseen construction-site issues. The check must therefore be linked to the relevant phase, without using a single date as an undifferentiated reference for all the streets.

After the completion of the works, the planned operational step is the restoration of the road surfaces by resurfacing the affected areas. The replacement of the pipes and the transfer of the service connections are also linked to infrastructure equipped with digitized monitoring and management systems, designed to identify anomalies more quickly and schedule maintenance.

When the planning also includes a border crossing, the local check can be supplemented by checking waiting times. To complete the planning of the crossing, consult tempi di attesa ai valichi.

Frequently Asked Questions
What roads in Varese are affected by the construction sites and on what dates?
The works are carried out in three phases: the first concerns via Masolino da Panicale (up to the intersection with via Pagliano) from 28 September to 30 November 2026. The second concerns Via Pagliano from 15 October 2026 to 25 March 2027. The third concerns Via Giovannini (up to the intersection with Via Masolino da Panicale) from 28 October 2026 to 2 April 2027.
What are the planned traffic restrictions?
During the execution of the works, transit and parking are prohibited. These restrictions will be applied progressively according to the progress of the construction site and, unless unforeseen, will be valid for all vehicles, except for residents and pedestrians.
What are the interventions and what are the expected benefits?
The intervention, carried out by Lereti (Acinque Group), involves the complete replacement of the pipes and the transfer of about 30 connections. The goal is to increase safety, improve reliability, and reduce gas and water leakage. The new networks will be equipped with digitized monitoring systems to detect anomalies more quickly and better schedule maintenance.
