Context

Preparatory work for the former Schools square in Lugano is underway

Starting on Monday, August 17, the five temporary interchange points that will replace the Botta canopy will begin to be prepared. The preparatory work will be completed by autumn 2027 and will not involve changes to roads or public transport, which will continue to operate according to the current structure.

The project involves the construction of five new temporary interchange points, which will be located at the following points:

  • Near Lugano station
  • At the car park in Via Nassa
  • Near the middle school in Via F. A. Borromini
  • At the car park in via A. Vespucci
  • Near Lugano-Paradiso station

The construction works of the temporary interchange points will be carried out in two phases. The first phase involves the construction of the five temporary interchange points, while the second phase involves the construction of connections between the temporary interchange points and the public transport network.

According to the plans, the construction works of the temporary interchange points will start on August 17 and will be completed by autumn 2027. The costs of the works are estimated at about 3.5 million francs, which will be financed by the Municipality of Lugano.

The construction of temporary interchange points is planned to ensure the mobility of the citizens of Lugano during the renovation of the former Scuole square.

Job Details

  • The works will be carried out by the company "Lugano Costruzioni" in collaboration with the City of Lugano. - The total cost of the works is 2.5 million francs, of which 1.5 million francs are financed by the Lugano Territory Development Fund (FST). - The construction of the former Schools square is scheduled for the end of 2028, in line with the development plan of the city of Lugano 2025-2030. - The area will be equipped with a new infrastructure for mobility services, including the public transport station and a parking lot for 200 cars.

Impact on mobility

  • During the works, the five temporary interchange points will be open at 6:00 in the morning and will close at 22:00. - The City of Lugano recommends using public transport to reduce traffic and emissions. - Walking and cycling mobility will be boosted with the creation of new cycle paths and pedestrian paths.
