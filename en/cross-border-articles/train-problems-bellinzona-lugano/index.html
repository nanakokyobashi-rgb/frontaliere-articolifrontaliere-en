Operational details

To better understand the problems that have affected the railway line between Bellinzona and Lugano, it is necessary to analyze the technical and infrastructure causes that have led to these disruptions. Delays and cancellations of trains can be caused by a range of factors, including technical problems with the trains themselves, malfunctions of the railway infrastructure, or adverse weather conditions. For example, in 2022, regional trains between Bellinzona and Lugano experienced an average delay of 4.2 minutes, with a peak of 12.5 minutes during the summer months due to maintenance work on the railway line. > Data provided by Swiss Federal Railways (SBB) indicate that in 2022, there were 157 maintenance interventions on the line, of which 37 were unplanned. It is essential that the competent authorities take measures to resolve these problems and guarantee the regularity of the railway service. To this end, SBB has allocated 120 million francs for the modernization of railway infrastructure between 2020 and 2025, of which 20 million are specifically earmarked for the Bellinzona-Lugano line. This investment includes the upgrade of signalling systems and the adaptation of Bellinzona, Lugano, and Giubiasco stations. - Lugano station, for example, underwent renovation work in 2020, with an investment of 8 million francs to improve accessibility and safety. ⚠️ Commuters who use the line daily are particularly affected by the disruptions. According to a survey conducted in 2022 among line users, 75% of respondents reported having experienced delays or cancellations in the previous 6 months. 📊 A possible solution to mitigate the disruptions could be the introduction of a real-time train monitoring system, which would allow for more rapid intervention in case of problems. SBB has already implemented a similar system on the Zurich-Bern line, reducing delays by 20%. 💡 It is also essential that cantonal and municipal authorities collaborate with SBB to improve the planning of maintenance work and minimize the impact on commuters. For example, the Canton of Ticino has already allocated 5 million francs in 2020 to improve public mobility in the Lugano area, part of which was earmarked for railway traffic optimization projects. The regularity of the railway service is essential for commuters who travel daily between Bellinzona and Lugano for work or study. It is therefore crucial that the competent authorities take concrete measures to resolve the technical and infrastructure problems affecting the line and guarantee a more reliable and efficient service.
