How much does a border CNC operator earn in Ticino?

The border CNC operator in Ticino is a worker who carries out work in Switzerland, but resides abroad. This type of worker is subject to Swiss tax rules and can benefit from specific tax exemptions and benefits.

Taxable income

Taxable income is determined by the difference between gross salary and documented expenses. For example, if a border CNC operator in Ticino earns a gross salary of CHF 80'000 per year and has documented expenses of CHF 10'000, the taxable income will be CHF 70'000.

Tax at source

The tax at source is 7.84% for income below CHF 22,400 and 8.84% for income above CHF22,400. For example, if the border CNC operator in Ticino has a taxable income of CHF 70'000, the tax at source will be CHF 5'562 (7.84% of CHF 70'000).

How much does a border CNC operator earn in Ticino?

The salaries of border CNC operators in Ticino can vary considerably depending on their experience and skills. In general, salaries in Switzerland are higher than those in Italy, but there are also differences within the canton of Ticino.

According to 2022 data, provided by the Swiss Confederation, the average salaries in Ticino are as follows:

  • CNC border operator with less than 2 years' experience: CHF 60,000 - 70,000 per year.
  • CNC border operator with experience between 2-5 years: CHF 80,000 - 90,000 per year.
  • CNC border operator with experience of more than 5 years: CHF 100,000 - 120,000 per year.

It is important to note that these are only average data and that actual salaries can vary greatly depending on the specific company, sector and working conditions.
