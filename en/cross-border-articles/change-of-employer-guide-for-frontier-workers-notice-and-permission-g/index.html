Frequently Asked Questions
When should I request the renewal of the G permit?
Within 30 days of the first day of employment with the new employer.
How can I maintain contributory continuity?
By continuing to pay taxes and contributions in Switzerland.
How much notice is required of a Swiss-Italian border worker when changing employers?
A frontier worker must give 30 days' notice to his/her current employer before changing employment. This period is crucial to ensure the maintenance of contributory continuity in Switzerland, as established by the Federal Migration Act (FMA).
What is the Swiss law governing the change of employer for frontier workers?
The relevant legislation is the Federal Migration Act (FMA). It establishes the obligation of 30 days' notice to the current employer and the need to request the renewal of the G permit within 30 days of the start of the new employment.
What is the amount of the annual fee that border crossers have to pay in Switzerland?
The annual tax established for border workers working in Switzerland amounts to CHF 250.00. This amount is provided for by the Federal Migration Act (FMA) and must be paid for the period you worked in the country.
