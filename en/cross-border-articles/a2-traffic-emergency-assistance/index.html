Traffic in tilt: you only help if the queue lasts hours (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 9 August 2026

The cantonal police clarify the criteria for extraordinary assistance to drivers stranded on the motorway after the chaos of the weekend.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Extraordinary assistance only for extended stays over 2-5 hours.
  • Saturday evening on the A2: heavy but slow-moving traffic.
  • No intervention by the Civil Protection for lack of critical conditions.

Key facts

  • What: Criteria for activating extraordinary assistance measures.
  • When: Saturday evening, 8 August 2026.
  • Where: A2 motorway, Mezzovico area.
  • Who: Ticino cantonal police, Captain Cristiano Nenzi.
  • Situation: Incident with 7 injured, road block and queues of 21 km.
  • Criterion: Stability of vehicles exceeding 2-5 hours.

The road chaos that occurred Saturday night along the A2 motorway, at the height of Mezzovico, has reignited the debate on the management of road emergencies in Canton Ticino. As a result of a violent collision between a car and a motorhome, which caused seven injuries and the temporary closure of the road, thousands of motorists, including several returning border workers, remained bottled up for hours. The cantonal police, through Captain Cristiano Nenzi, officer in charge of the fifth department of the road gendarmerie, clarified that the necessary conditions did not exist to activate the extraordinary assistance measures, such as the distribution of water or the intervention of the civil protection soldiers.

Operational details

The Mezzovico accident highlighted the vulnerability of the Ticino road system, especially during events that require the partial or total closure of the A2. For frontier workers, constantly in transit along the routes that connect the border with the production centres of the Canton, the management of flows represents a daily challenge. The increasing use of navigation applications by users, aimed at avoiding columns, often ends up dumping heavy traffic on local and secondary roads. Captain Nenzi explicitly confirmed that these alternative routes, suggested by the algorithms, are not always effective from the road point of view, causing a further obstacle to local circulation and complicating traffic management operations by law enforcement.

The impact on local roads

The phenomenon of transit traffic leaving the motorway to clog the towns of Lugano not only aggravates congestion, but significantly limits the freedom of movement of residents and hinders police activities in the area. This dynamic creates a sort of 'trap' in which the travel time, instead of decreasing, is likely to increase due to the saturation of the minor road network. Key points

In road emergency situations, caution and correct information remain the main tools for the border worker. Although it is not possible to predict accidents like the one that occurred in Mezzovico, the management of your travel time can be optimized by consulting official sources. Before travelling, it is advisable to check the traffic status on dedicated portals such as Viasuisse, which provides real-time updates on waiting times and column lengths. In the event of a prolonged blockage of the A2, the best strategy is often to wait for the situation to evolve on the motorway, rather than trying alternative routes that are punctually congested, increasing the risk of fines or getting stuck in areas less equipped for emergency management.

Frequently Asked Questions
When does the Cantonal Police intervene with assistance measures?
Extraordinary assistance measures, such as the distribution of water or emergency aid, are only activated when a significant downtime is expected, usually exceeding 2, 3, 4 or 5 hours. The main criterion is the static state of the blocked vehicles.
Why were no aid distributed to motorists on Saturday night?
According to the cantonal police, despite the accumulated delays, the column was in slow motion and the necessary conditions of prolonged staticness did not occur. In addition, the night weather conditions were considered less critical than in the afternoon, making no extraordinary intervention necessary.
What are the risks related to the use of road navigators during an accident?
The use of navigation apps to avoid columns pushes many drivers towards alternative routes that are not always suitable. This behaviour ends up congesting the local road network, burdening residents and hindering police activities, often making the alternative route less efficient than the motorway one.
