The division of the second pillar AVS/LPP represents a significant change for Ticino cross-border workers. Even after a long separation, it is possible to proceed with the division of pension benefits. This article explores the practical implications and procedures for cross-border workers residing in Italy and working in Switzerland.

Practical implications

The new legislation allows cross-border workers to divide the second pillar even after a prolonged period of separation. This means that, regardless of the duration of the separation, cross-border workers can request the division of their pension benefits. It is important to note that this possibility has been introduced to ensure greater equity and financial security for cross-border workers.

Procedures and deadlines

To proceed with the division of the second pillar, cross-border workers must follow a series of specific steps. First, they need to submit an application to the competent office of Canton Ticino. The application must be accompanied by documents attesting to the separation and the duration of the employment relationship in Switzerland. Once the application is submitted, the office will proceed with the evaluation and, if everything is in order, issue a division decree.

Division decree: If everything is in order, the office will issue a decree for the division of the second pillar. 5. Division of benefits: The pension benefits accumulated during the period of work in Switzerland will be divided equally between the two spouses.
