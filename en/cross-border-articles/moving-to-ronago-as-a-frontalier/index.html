Under the Lugano Agreement of 21 June 1992, frontier workers moving to Switzerland can benefit from tax exemptions. However, the New Frontier Agreement introduces new rules that may affect the tax situation of frontier workers. From 1 January 2024, frontier workers moving to Ronago will be able to benefit from a CHF 15,000 deductible on working income, instead of CHF 10,000 as planned until now.

For example, if a frontier earns CHF 80,000 a year and moves to Ronago, he will be eligible for the CHF 15,000 allowance. In this case, the income tax at source will only be withheld on the remaining CHF 65,000. If, on the other hand, the border worker earns CHF 100,000 per year and moves to Ronago, the excess of CHF 15,000 will not be enough to cover the income tax at source, which will be withheld on the remaining CHF 85,000.
