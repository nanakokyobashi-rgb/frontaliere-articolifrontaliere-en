Living in Montano Lucino and working in Ticino is a convenient choice for many border workers. Travel times vary depending on the chosen crossing point, but in general, border workers can reach their place of work in Ticino in about 30-45 minutes. The cost of living in Italy is generally lower than in Ticino, which is an additional advantage for those who decide to move. In addition, the new border agreement, signed on 23 December 2020 and in force from 1 January 2024, offers new tax opportunities for border workers.

Journey times

Travel times depend on the chosen crossing. For example, those who live in Montano Lucino and work in Lugano can use the Chiasso crossing, which offers a travel time of about 30 minutes in normal traffic conditions. Those who work in Bellinzona can opt for the Gaggiolo pass, with a journey time of about 45 minutes.

Connections and Journey Times

Living in Montano Lucino and working in Ticino offers different road and rail connections. Travel times vary depending on the chosen crossing. Cost of Living

The cost of living in Montano Lucino is generally lower than in Ticino. This is a further advantage for border crossers who decide to relocate. However, it is also important to consider transportation costs and any additional expenses related to commuting.

Recommended Zones
