The dedication at the entrance

A plaque dedicated to Kevin Palmieri was placed at the entrance of the sports centre of the San Vito Oratory in Gornate Olona. The name of the child, who died in January 2024 due to a sudden illness, is thus associated with the place where he loved to play football.

Kevin was eight. The structure of the oratory was for him a space linked to his passion for the ball: the source describes him as he ran happily behind a ball, expressing what the family calls his innate passion for football. The plaque was placed at the entrance of the sports centre, in a visible point outside the San Vito Oratory.

The text chosen to remember him recalls Kevin's smile and ties him to the dreams of every boy and girl. The images shown on the plaque are those of a rolling ball, a hug after a goal and the joy of being together. The message then carries the child's memory into the gestures and emotions that belong to the game.

The dedication is not just about the name engraved on the billboard. For the community of Gornate Olona, the entrance to the oratory is the place indicated by the family as the place where the heart of the community beats and where Kevin loved to play. The laying of the plaque makes that bond public and leaves his name in the sports centre linked to football.

The Palmieri family reacted with a message of thanks after installation. The text, disseminated on social media, expresses emotion at the fact that Kevin's memory remains right at the entrance of the San Vito Oratory.
