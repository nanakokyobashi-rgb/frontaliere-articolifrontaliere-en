Como renews lighting on Viale Varese, 80-thousand-euro project

The Municipality of Como officially launched the project to renew public lighting along two sections of viale Varese. The city council, led by Mayor Alessandro Rapinese, approved the guidance document for designing the project, declaring the measure immediately enforceable.

The objective indicated by the source is to overcome the frequent breakdowns and inconveniences that often leave motorists and pedestrians in the dark. The construction work will concern the central reservation, that is, the strip separating the traffic lanes from the parking area.

The scope of the project

The first section concerned lies between piazza Cacciatori delle Alpi and via Borsieri. The second is located between via benzi and viale Cattaneo. The project is therefore limited to two specific segments of the avenue and does not coincide with the replacement of all the lighting in the area.

The system serving the pedestrian green area adjacent to the historic walls is excluded. According to the source, that space has an independent network and decorative fixtures, separate from the system included in the project.

The existing infrastructure has old buried cables without adequate protection or conduits. This configuration prevents maintenance work from being carried out without breaking up the asphalt. The support poles also show corrosion at the base.

The new project provides for the re-excavation of trenches, the installation of conduits and inspection chambers, the construction of foundation footings, and the installation of new electrical lines. It also includes poles, arms and latest-generation LED light fixtures, described as more efficient and higher-performing. The work will be organized in phases to ensure service continuity and road safety during construction.

Operational details

The cost estimate separates expenditure on the works from the technical and financial items. The total investment amounts to 80mila euro. Of this amount, 60mila euro are allocated directly to the works; this item includes 12mila euro for labor and 1.800 euro for safety costs.

The remaining 20mila euro in the cost estimate are allocated to ancillary items: in addition to VAT at 22%, they include reserves for contingencies and price revisions, technical incentives, and internal insurance coverage. This amount is separate from that for the works and does not correspond to a second construction site.

The stated objective is to complete the project by spring 2027.
