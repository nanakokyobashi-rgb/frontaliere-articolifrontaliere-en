Agrinatura 2026 is in full swing and confirms, already at the end of the first two days, a clear result: the event has increasingly taken on a defined profile as a high-level agro-food destination, capable of bringing together dissemination, agricultural identity, and contemporary cuisine. A clear quality leap in the central arena managed by Coldiretti Como Lecco, which has registered constant and qualified participation, with cooking shows and workshops often sold out and an attentive, involved, and participating audience.

The opening was marked by a strong symbolic moment: the ribbon-cutting of the twenty-fifth edition, accompanied by the large celebratory cake made by Coldiretti Como Lecco. Brought to the center of the scene and shared with the authorities, the cake represented the sense of a manifestation that in a quarter of a century has consolidated its role, today relaunched with a clear centrality of the gastronomic theme.

…

Operational details

Agrinatura is no longer just an agricultural fair, but a place where food – thanks to its direct producers – becomes a tool for storytelling, synthesis, and perspective for contemporary agriculture. "AgriNatura demonstrates how agriculture can be told and experienced through food, which is the first tool for relating to citizens," emphasizes Fortunato Trezzi. "In these first two days, we have seen extraordinary participation and increasing quality of the proposals: the kitchen thus becomes the most direct language to enhance the work of our companies and to transmit the value of the short chain and the territory."

Workshops and Tastings

The educational workshops and tastings have been a highlight of the event. From the dishes of chef Roberto Conti, with his risotto with wild garlic and Gorgonzola from Comasca, to the preparations of the farmer chefs, every moment has been an opportunity to deepen the knowledge of the short chain and local agricultural production. The informative meetings on cheeses and biodiversity have offered an effective balance between education and show, involving the public in a unique experience.

The Role of Coldiretti Como Lecco

Coldiretti Como Lecco has curated the central arena, ensuring constant and qualified participation. The cooking shows and workshops, often sold out, have demonstrated the public's interest and attention towards agricultural and gastronomic themes. The large celebratory cake, made for the occasion, has symbolized the success and importance of this event, which has consolidated its role over a quarter of a century.

Key points

For cross-border workers who work in Ticino and reside in Italy, Agrinatura represents a unique opportunity to discover the agricultural and gastronomic excellence of the territory. The event offers the chance to participate in workshops and tastings, deepening the knowledge of short supply chains and local production techniques. Furthermore, informative meetings on cheeses and biodiversity can be a valuable tool to better understand the agricultural dynamics and typical products of Ticino.

What to do to participate

1. Check the program: Consult the official Agrinatura website to know the event calendar and available workshops. 2. Book the workshops: Some workshops may require advance booking. Make sure to book on time to not miss the opportunity to participate. 3. Attend the show cooking: Show cooking are open to the public and do not require booking. Arrive early to secure a front-row spot. 4. Tastings and in-depth analysis: Take advantage of tastings and informative meetings to discover local products and production techniques. 5. Interact with producers: Take the opportunity to speak directly with producers and chefs, asking questions and deepening your knowledge.
