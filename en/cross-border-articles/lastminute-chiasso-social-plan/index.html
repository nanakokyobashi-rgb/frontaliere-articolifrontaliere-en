Operational details

Although the OCST union expressed satisfaction for reaching an agreement in a short time, it is essential to emphasize that these measures are designed exclusively to mitigate the negative effects of a dismissal, without being able to cancel the employment consequences. For a border worker operating in the Canton of Ticino, the loss of employment involves specific challenges, such as the management of the disoccupazione and the correct calculation of the allowances due according to current regulations. ### Implications for the frontier worker The structure of the agreement defines a perimeter of protection that goes beyond simple liquidation.
