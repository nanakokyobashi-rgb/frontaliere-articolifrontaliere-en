Giubiasco, viale Olgiati closes at night for paving works (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 8 June 2026

Scheduled nighttime closure of viale Olgiati, Giubiasco due to paving works: here are the hours and affected stretch.

Context

In brief

  • Road paving works in Giubiasco
  • Complete closure of viale C. Olgiati from 20:00 to 06:00
  • Private vehicle access prohibited during works
  • Possible changes in case of bad weather

Operational details

For cross-border commuters who regularly use the road network of Canton Ticino, especially those travelling in the areas bordering Bellinzona and Giubiasco to reach their workplace, the management of night-time construction sites represents a factor to consider when planning their journeys. Even though the work on Viale Olgiati takes place during night hours, the implications can affect those who work shifts or who are travelling late in the evening or in the early morning hours towards the border crossings.

Impact on Local Mobility

The night-time closure implies that usual routes may experience temporary diversions. Although the construction site is located in a specific section, it's advisable to check for alternative routes near the intersection of Via Zorzi and Via Caratti. Road management in Ticino is often affected by maintenance work which, although necessary to ensure road safety, can create minor delays. For workers, monitoring traffic bulletins and official communications from the Bellinzona Urban Services Department is an excellent practice to avoid getting stuck or being forced into sudden diversions.

Key points

To best address road closures and variations in road conditions in Ticino, a proactive approach is necessary. Workers who move daily must be ready to manage traffic changes and always keep track of official Canton Ticino communications. There are no navigation tools that can predict with millimeter precision every weighbridge, so the habit of checking official pages before setting off remains the most effective method to avoid unpleasant surprises.

Managing Pendular Flows

If you need to cross the Giubiasco area next week, keep these basic steps in mind: 1. Regularly check the information channels of the Bellinzona Municipality or Canton for any updates on the extension or duration of works. 2. If your route passes via Olgiati Avenue, plan in advance an alternative route that does not use the stretch between Zorzi Street and Caratti Street. 3. Add an extra 10-15 minutes to your travel time, especially if you're heading to border gates during work hours or when the weighbridge is in operation or being set up. 4. Strictly adhere to temporary signage that will be installed; road signs take precedence over GPS navigators, which may not be updated in real time.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the closing hours of Olgiati Avenue?
The stretch of C. Olgiati Avenue affected by the work will be closed every night from 8 PM to 6 AM the following morning, for the entire duration of the planned works for the next week.
Which road section is affected by the work?
The closure concerns the stretch that begins shortly after the intersection with Zorzi Street and extends almost to Caratti Street, in Giubiasco.
Will access to private properties be blocked?
Yes, during the closing hours (from 8 PM to 6 AM), vehicular access to private properties adjacent to the affected stretch will not be usable.
Can the work schedule change?
Yes, in case of unfavorable weather conditions, the paving work schedule may change from what was initially planned.
