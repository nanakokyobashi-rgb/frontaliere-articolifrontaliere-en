On Monday, the Bellinzona City Council will vote on an initial credit of 14 million. The financing is intended to acquire the former LATI site in Sant'Antonino and design the new fire station. The overall operation is estimated at 34 million.

The solution is supported by the majority report of the Management Commission. President Pietro Ghisletta considers the former LATI site an interesting area, located in an industrial zone with considerable development potential remaining. The project would also make it possible to bring the firefighters, Civil Protection and the entire operational services sector of the City of Bellinzona under one roof. According to Ghisletta, travel times have also been analyzed and are considered suitable.

The NoCasa committee's letter

The vote comes after intense months and just days after a new letter sent to the municipal councillors by a group of firefighters gathered in the NoCasa committee. The signatories remain anonymous for fear of repercussions and declare themselves opposed to moving the station to the former LATI site.

«We hope that none of you will be influenced by party interests or directives imposed from above».

The opposing side is also represented by a minority report, which calls for rejecting the option. Among the signatories is Brenno Martignoni Polti, according to whom a station tasked with covering a territory must guarantee rapid response and cannot accept situations that are still unclear. The Noce representative points to the significant traffic linked to the nearby shopping center and argues that there is currently no certainty that the existing rescue capabilities can be maintained.

Some opponents would have preferred the former Birreria in Carasso, an option already mooted decades ago. However, the Municipal Council considers it more expensive, with an estimate of approximately 44 million. Ghisletta explained that the Management Commission met with the firefighters' representatives, including Tuto Rossi and Rodolfo Quatraccioni, and also heard from Civil Protection several times. The new project was presented to the entire City Council and taken into consideration in the report.

Operational details

A practical reading of the dossier

The choice between ex LATI and ex Birreria di Carasso hinges on three elements reported in the source: location, emergency response capacity and the cost of the project. For a cross-border reader, the impact described is therefore local. The fire station would be located in an industrial area or in the area of ex Birreria, while opponents point to the traffic generated by the shopping center near ex LATI.
