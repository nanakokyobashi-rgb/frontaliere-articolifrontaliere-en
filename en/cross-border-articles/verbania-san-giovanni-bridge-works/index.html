Verbania, works on the San Giovanni bridge: modified traffic (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 13 September 2026

Night-time interventions by Acqua Novara.VCO on the San Giovanni bridge at Verbania from 14 to 18 September with one-way alternation.

Context

In brief

  • Water network intervention in Verbania
  • Construction site active from September 14 to 18
  • Nighttime schedule from 22:00 to 06:00
  • Alternating one-way traffic with traffic light system

Key facts

  • What: Replacement of a section of the water network
  • When: From September 14 to 18
  • Where: Bridge over the San Giovanni stream, Canottieri Intra area
  • Who: Acqua Novara.VCO

Extraordinary maintenance work on the water network in the municipal area of Verbania will begin in the coming hours, with direct impacts on local traffic and road connections frequently used by those moving in the area for work or personal reasons. The intervention has been planned by Acqua Novara.VCO and will specifically affect the area located near Canottieri Intra, a crucial point for urban circulation and transit flows affecting the lower Verbano area and connections towards the border and main routes.

Details of the water intervention

The schedule provides for the targeted replacement of a section of the local water network considered strategic for the proper functioning of underground infrastructure. To minimize disruption to vehicle and resident traffic, the operator has decided to concentrate the work exclusively during nighttime hours. The construction site will remain operational from September 14 to 18, covering a timeframe of five consecutive days during which the technical team will be engaged in excavations and laying new pipes. The choice of nighttime hours responds to the need to safeguard daytime traffic, reducing the congestion that usually characterizes peak hours in this area of Intra.

Operational details

Analyzing the operational management of the construction site, it is clear how the planning has been designed to ensure the continuity of the vehicular passage even in the presence of significant technical limitations. The changes to the roads on the bridge over the San Giovanni stream will come into force every day from 22:00, extending until the first light of dawn, precisely until 06:00 the next morning. This time interval of six hours for each night will allow the operators to proceed with the excavations and the replacement of the pipelines in total safety.

Traffic and traffic impacts

During the entire duration of the night works, the transit of the vehicles will never be completely interrupted, thus guaranteeing the passage through the San Giovanni bridge. However, in order to allow construction operations to take place in the Canottieri Intra area, traffic will be regulated through the establishment of an alternating one-way route managed directly by a temporary traffic light system. This solution will require special attention from drivers in transit at night, who will have to take into account short waits near the construction site traffic light. Check the connections and roads in the area to better plan your movements and monitor any slowdowns caused by road construction sites.

Key points

To better deal with the temporary changes to road traffic planned in Verbania, it is advisable to take some logistical precautions, especially for those who travel in the evening and at night along the bridge over the San Giovanni stream. Those who usually travel between 22:00 and 06:00 from 14 to 18 September in the Canottieri Intra area should consider the presence of the traffic light system and the consequent alternating one-way traffic, calculating slightly higher time margins for their travels on the road.

How to move around during work

Before traveling, it is advisable to check the traffic status and consider alternative routes if you have to comply with particularly strict road maps. Cooperation with the indications of the construction site and compliance with the temporary signs installed by Acqua Novara.VCO are essential for the safety of workers working on the water network and all road users.
