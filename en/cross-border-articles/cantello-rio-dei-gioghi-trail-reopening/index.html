Frequently Asked Questions
When will the Rio dei Gioghi trail officially reopen?
The official inauguration of the Rio dei Gioghi trail is scheduled for autumn, although the source does not specify an exact date. Before this, a technical reopening scheduled for the end of August is necessary, a time necessary for rainfall and natural settlement to allow the bottom to compact adequately after the recovery and safety works.
Who financed the restoration work on the Rio dei Gioghi trail?
The recovery and redevelopment of the Rio dei Gioghi trail was financed under the Interreg VI-A Italy-Switzerland programme through the SINTAB (Integrated Development for Transboundary Nature and Biodiversity) project, aimed at enhancing the Local Park of Supercommunal Interest (PLIs) of the Bevera Valley, in which the trail is included.
Is the Rio dei Gioghi trail part of any wider hiking network?
Yes, the route of the Rio dei Gioghi trail constitutes a section of the Italian Trail traced by the Italian Alpine Club (CAI), inserted in the context of the PLIs of the Bevera Valley, a protected natural area that extends over Italian territories and has supra-communal relevance, potentially connectable to routes to Swiss territory.
