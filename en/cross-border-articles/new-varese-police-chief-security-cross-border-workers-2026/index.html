New Varese police chief targets fraud, theft, and robbery

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 May 2026

Paolo Iodice introduces himself as the new Varese police chief, focusing on security and combating cross-border crime

Context

In brief

  • Paolo Iodice is the new police chief of Varese
  • Focus on fraud, theft, and robberies
  • Continuity of work done by predecessors

Key facts

  • What: Appointment of the new police chief of Varese
  • When: May 4, 2026
  • Where: Varese, Italy
  • Who: Paolo Iodice
  • Amount: Not specified

The new police chief of Varese, Paolo Iodice, officially introduced himself this morning. During the meeting with the press, Iodice emphasized the importance of continuing the work done by his predecessors, with a particular focus on the safety of citizens. 'We will uphold the continuity of the service provided so far to the community of Varese,' declared Iodice, thanking those who entrusted him with this role. The new police chief expressed great enthusiasm and dedication for his new position, highlighting his knowledge of the territory and its challenges.

Iodice has worked in the past in the Varese police headquarters as a deputy, developing an in-depth knowledge of the territory and the local reality. This experience will be valuable in addressing future challenges. 'I am fortunate to know this territory with all its challenges and characteristics,' Iodice stated, praising the respect for rules, hard work, and generosity of the people of Varese.

Safety and crime

The safety of citizens is one of the main priorities of the new police chief. Iodice mentioned several challenges of the territory, including predatory crimes such as fraud, pickpocketing, theft, and robberies. In particular, he highlighted fraud targeting the elderly and drug trafficking managed by transnational organizations, especially in the woods. 'My priority is to organize the best possible deployment of territorial control services,' declared Iodice, emphasizing the importance of prevention and repressive activities.

Iodice also spoke about his professional experience, which has led him to work in various Italian cities, from Parma to Avellino, from Caserta to Crotone. He has participated in operations on the territory, such as nighttime raids against the Camorra, and has carried out investigative activities. 'I have spent my professional life on the streets,' he affirmed with pride, emphasizing his commitment to serving civil society.

Implications for cross-border workers

For cross-border workers who work in Switzerland and reside in Italy, the appointment of a new police chief in Varese could have significant implications. The fight against cross-border crime is a priority, and this could lead to an increase in controls and police operations in border areas. Cross-border workers should be aware of the new security measures and cooperate with the authorities to ensure a safe environment for everyone.

Comparison with the previous situation

The previous police chief, Carlo Mazza, left his position due to age limits. Iodice expressed esteem and friendship for his predecessors, particularly Dr. Morelli and Dr. Mazza. Continuity of the work done so far is a key element for Iodice, who intends to maintain the high standards of safety and service to the community of Varese.
