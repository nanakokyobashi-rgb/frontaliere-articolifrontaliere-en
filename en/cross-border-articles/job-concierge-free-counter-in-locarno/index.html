Job Concierge: free counter in Locarno

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 28 September 2026

ECAP Ticino Unia’s mobile service offers free consultations without an appointment. Next meeting in Locarno on 1° ottobre.

Context

In brief

  • Next appointment: Thursday 1° October
  • In Locarno, from 14 to 16
  • Free consultations with no appointment required
  • Service reserved for those not registered with the URC

Key facts

  • Organizer: ECAP Ticino Unia
  • Location: Spazio ELLE, Piazza G. Pedrazzini 12
  • Service: mobile help desk for job searches
  • Venue: different from the one initially communicated

Thursday 1° October Portinerie Lavoro will stop in Locarno with a free help desk dedicated to people looking for a job. The appointment is scheduled from 14 to 16 at Spazio ELLE, at Piazza G. Pedrazzini 12.

The project is promoted by ECAP Ticino Unia, a nonprofit organization for adult education. Its format is that of a mobile help desk, designed to offer guidance to those facing a job search and needing support in preparing their application.

Activities listed

Access to the consultations does not require an appointment. Those who come can receive guidance on preparing their application dossier, developing a personal strategy for looking for work, and being directed toward the resources considered most suitable. These are the areas indicated for the service.

ECAP Ticino Unia also clarified that the meeting will not take place at the venue indicated in the initial communication. The updated venue is Spazio ELLE in Locarno, at Piazza G. Pedrazzini 12. The project was launched only a few weeks ago and, according to the promoting organization, several people have already used the help desk.

Operational details

A service for job searching

Portinerie Lavoro brings together in a single desk three areas related to job searching: preparing the application dossier, defining a personal strategy, and identifying the most suitable resources. The source presents these activities as the core of the free consultations organized by ECAP Ticino Unia.

Access requirement

The distinction also concerns the public the project is aimed at. The desk is reserved for people who are not registered with the Regional Placement Offices, indicated by the acronym URC. The requirement accompanies the walk‑in formula announced by the promoting body.

The initiative is mobile and continues in the territory after a few weeks of launch. In this initial period, ECAP Ticino Unia reports that several people have already used the service. The figure only confirms that the desk has been used and is not accompanied by further information on the results obtained.

The activities remain focused on the tools of application and orientation: the dossier, the individual job‑search strategy, and the link with the resources deemed most suitable. The source does not indicate specific job positions associated with the Locarno appointment.

Promotion is handled by ECAP Ticino Unia, a non‑profit organization for adult education. For the Portinerie Lavoro project, the body offers free consultations to support people engaged in job searching.

During the meeting, the areas of support indicated are the preparation of the application dossier, the construction of a personal strategy for job search and the orientation towards the most suitable resources.

After the start of a few weeks, the project therefore continues with a new presence in the Locarno area. The source communicates for this stage the day, the time slot, the updated place and the free nature of the counter.

The data reported by ECAP Ticino Unia, according to which several people have already used the service, accompanies the continuation of the initiative. In Locarno, the consultancy will be available directly at the ELLE Space, with access without reservation and as part of the itinerant project promoted by the non-profit organization for adult education.
