The program between city, lake and Val d'Intelvi

On Saturday 10 and Sunday 11 October, the FAI Autumn Days return to the province of Como, the autumn event with which the FAI dedicates a major street initiative to the cultural and landscape heritage of the country. The FAI Youth Group of Como and the FAI Delegation of Como invite everyone to use the weekend to discover new places, from the city to the more remote Val d'Intelvi.

The openings are unprecedented and very varied: from Palazzo della Rienza, whose historical and resilient presence tells the story of the identity of the Como area, to the manufacturing realities of Cantiere Navigazione Laghi and Colombo Industrie Tessili with Visgomma. The title of the edition, «MAKE the difference!», arises precisely from this breadth of proposals.

The program also includes the opening circuit in Val d'Intelvi, built with the support of local associations. The valley is presented as a lesser-known but rich reality, already in the past a bridge between Italy and Europe. For the FAI, the route enhances places that preserve the memory of the territory and tell stories and characters through inconspicuous assets.

Aurora Cairoli, FAI Youth Como Group Leader, links the initiative to the valorisation of the entire Como area, from the city to the Val d'Intelvi. Ilaria Grisoni, Head of the FAI Delegation of Como, describes the next FAI Autumn Days 2026 as an opportunity to discover new places in the province, with a historical, landscape, cultural and industrial proposal. Grisoni thanks the owners, school guides and volunteers for the team work that makes the event possible.

From the lake to the factories

At the Cantiere Navigazione Laghi, in Tavernola, the visit enters the places where the lake's boats and hydrofoils are cared for. The shipyards, operational for almost two centuries and normally accessible to professionals, show workers, towing and dry docks and a moored unit. In Fino Mornasco, a single visit unites Colombo Industrie Tessili, active for over sixty years, and Visgomma, founded in 1946 and now part of the Colombo Group: the former follows the fabric from creation to finishing, the latter produces technical rubber articles especially for rail transport and special construction.

Two visit formulas, not just one appointment

The program should be read as a choice between different experiences, not as a single path. The Cantiere Navigazione Laghi is linked to the daily relationship between the Como community and the lake; Colombo Industrie Tessili and Visgomma instead propose entry into a production site. Val d'Intelvi adds a circuit of openings supported by local associations. This difference allows you to orient your day based on the type of place you want to get to know.

The difference between general visit and special shift

In Fino Mornasco the source distinguishes the ordinary guided tour from the "Machines in motion" initiative. The special shifts are reserved for FAI members, require reservations and allow you to see some machinery operating at full speed, safely. They are scheduled for Saturday at 2.30pm and Sunday at 11am and 2.30pm. For these shifts the suggested contribution starts from €5 and each group can have a maximum of 15 people. Those who wish can also take part in a quiz.

How to organize the visit

To transform the program into a concrete choice, it is best to follow five steps based on the published information:

  • 1) Choose the place starting from your interest: memory of the territory at Palazzo della Rienza, work on the lake at the Cantiere or industrial production at Fino Mornasco.
  • 2) Mark the correct address: Via per Cernobbio 15 for the construction site, Via Guanzasca 6 for Colombo Industrie Tessili and Visgomma.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the dates and times of the FAI Autumn Days in Como?
Saturday 10 and Sunday 11 October visits are available from 10:00 to 16:00 at the Cantiere Navigazione Laghi and until 16:30 at the Industrie Tessili Colombo and Visgomma. Departures are approximately every 30 minutes and each visit lasts approximately 50 minutes.
What is the "Machines in Motion" initiative and how to participate?
It is a special round reserved for FAI members, which requires a reservation. Groups are limited to 15 people and the suggested contribution starts from €5. The shifts are Saturday at 2:30 pm and Sunday at 11:00 am and 2:30 pm, to see the machines operate at full speed in safety.
What are the exact addresses of the two visiting sites?
Cantiere Navigazione Laghi: Via per Cernobbio 15, 22100 Tavernola (hamlet of Como). Industrie Tessili Colombo e Visgomma: Via Guanzasca 6, 22070 Fino Mornasco (CO).
