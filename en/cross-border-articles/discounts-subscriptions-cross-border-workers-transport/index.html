Key facts

Cross-border workers employed in Ticino and residing in Lombardy can benefit from discounted public transport subscriptions. These discounts are available for both trains and buses connecting the two regions. The benefits are designed to facilitate the daily commuting of cross-border workers, offering convenient solutions for public transport.

Types of subscriptions

There are several types of subscriptions that cross-border workers can use. Among these, monthly and annual subscriptions offer the highest discounts. Additionally, there are specific subscriptions for those who use both trains and buses, allowing for further savings.

Cross-border workers can request discounted subscriptions at the ticket offices of railway stations or through the websites of transport companies. It is important to verify the specific requirements for each type of subscription, such as residence in Lombardy and place of work in Ticino.

How to request the discounts

To request the discounts, cross-border workers must present the necessary documentation, which includes the work permit and an identity document. Some subscriptions also require proof of residence in Lombardy. Once the documentation is presented, the discounted subscription can be activated and used for daily trips.
