Accident on the SS707 in Varese, link road closed for hours

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 October 2026

Collision between two cars on SS707 at the outskirts of Varese: the link road between Largo Flaiano and the Laghi interchange closed for hours. Two injured, yellow code.

The accident on the SS707

At 7.02 the alert was raised on the outskirts of Varese for an accident along the SS707, the state road connecting to the A8 motorway. The accident involved two cars and made it necessary to close the affected section to allow rescue operations, investigations, and traffic management.

The SS707 remained closed for several hours between Largo Flaiano and the Laghi interchange, with traffic halted in both directions. Law enforcement closed the section at Largo Flaiano for vehicles heading toward Milan, while the operation at the scene was still underway.

The first information came from the AREU operations center, Soreu Laghi. The accident involved two people who were assisted: a 55-year-old man and a 67-year-old woman. This was the initial account reported for the accident between the two cars.

The Traffic Police also diverted the flow of cars toward the city center, near the Laghi interchange. The measure was adopted together with the closure, so as to allow the planned activities on the state-road section. The effect on traffic was immediate: slowdowns and queues in the area providing access to the provincial capital.

The initial medical assessment indicated a yellow code for the people involved. The source reports that they sustained serious injuries, but not injuries considered severe enough to put their lives at risk. The two road references contained in the report are therefore Largo Flaiano, the closure point for vehicles heading toward Milan, and the Laghi interchange, from which traffic was diverted toward the city center.

Operational details

Traffic management

The closed section and the diverted flow

The morning's traffic management concerned the road connection with the A8 motorway. The source identifies SS707 as the state road affected by the accident and places the closure between Largo Flaiano and the Laghi interchange. The measure, which lasted several hours, affected both directions of travel; near Largo Flaiano, the section was closed to vehicles heading toward Milan.

At the interchange for the Laghi, however, the flow of cars was directed toward the city center. The State Police managed this diversion while rescue operations, accident investigations and traffic management continued. The route taken by cars was thus shifted to the access area to the provincial capital, where the source reports slowdowns and queues.

The description of the traffic situation identifies two distinct points of the operation: Largo Flaiano, where the closure for vehicles heading toward Milan was concentrated, and the Laghi interchange, used to divert traffic toward the center. The SS707 section remained at the center of the activities required after the collision.

Operations at the scene

Alongside the State Police, the Varese Fire Brigade and the Varese Local Police intervened at the scene. The Fire Brigade worked to secure the area, while the Local Police took part in managing traffic. Rescue vehicles from the Varese Red Cross also arrived.

The activities indicated in the source therefore addressed three immediate needs: securing the area, assisting the people involved and regulating traffic. The closure of the state road made it possible to carry out these operations together with the accident investigations, while the diversion directed cars toward the city center. The result described for access to Varese was a morning marked by queues and slowdowns, while the responding forces managed traffic.

Key points

Traffic reopened

The medical toll

After hours of closure and on-site operations, the source update reports that traffic has reopened. The SS707 was therefore reopened after the difficult morning described on the outskirts of Varese, at the end of the phase during which the section between Largo Flaiano and the Laghi interchange had remained closed for hours.

For the people involved, the rescue process continued after the initial treatment provided on site. The source reports that they were transported to hospital and specifies that one of the injured people was taken into care and evacuated to the Ospedale di Circolo di Varese.

The yellow code declared for the people involved describes serious injuries, but without danger to life. In the report, this is the medical toll associated with the collision between the two cars: two people assisted, initial treatment on site and subsequent transfer to hospital.

The latest update

The documented follow-up concerns traffic: traffic was reopened after the closure that lasted for hours and after the flow was diverted toward the city center. The previous phase had involved the section between Largo Flaiano and the Laghi interchange and had caused slowdowns and queues in the access area to the provincial capital.

The operational sequence indicated by the source includes rescue operations, investigations and traffic management. First, the people received treatment on site; then transport to hospital was arranged. On the road, the closure affected the indicated segment, while the diversion had been organized at the Laghi interchange.

The final update to the news therefore combines reopened traffic and assistance to the two people involved, with one of the injured people directed to the Ospedale di Circolo di Varese. For the morning of the accident on the outskirts of Varese, these are the developments reported by the source.
