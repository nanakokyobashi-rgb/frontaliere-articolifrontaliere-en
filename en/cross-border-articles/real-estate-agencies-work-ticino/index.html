Frequently Asked Questions
Why don't successful agencies monitor the competition?
According to the source, time spent observing the activities of others is considered a sign of empty agendas. Professionals focus exclusively on operational activities, such as property visits and checking tenants' solvency, seeing distraction from competitors as a waste of time.
What are the main activities carried out by a real estate agency?
The activities include the preparation of the properties for the visits, the detailed explanation of the technical characteristics (such as sun exposure), the strict control of solvency through executions and payroll extracts, the realization of professional photographic services and the closing of leases.
What does a tenant have to do to be considered reliable?
To be considered tenants of value, it is essential to present yourself with complete and accurate documentation, including extracts of executions, references and pay slips. This preparation allows you to quickly pass the pre-qualification phase managed by the real estate agency.
