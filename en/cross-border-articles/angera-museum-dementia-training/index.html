Angera: free course on museums and dementia on 12 September (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 September 2026

On September 12, the Archaeological Museum of Angera hosts a free course for professionals on museum accessibility for people with dementia.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Free course on dementia and museums in Angera on 12 September
  • Aimed at museum, social and social-health professionals
  • Compulsory registration by Sunday, September 6
  • Organised by the Municipality of Angera with the contribution of the Lombardy Region

Key facts

  • What: Course 'Tact issue. Practices and tools for working in the museum with people with dementia and those who take care of them
  • When: Saturday, September 12, from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.
  • Where: Civic Archaeological Museum of Angera, via Marconi 2
  • Who: Cristina Bucci and Luca Carli Ballòla (Tuscan Museum System for Alzheimer's)
  • Seats: 25 available
  • Deadline: Sunday, September 6
  • Cost: Free

The Civic Archaeological Museum of Via Marconi in Angera opens its doors on Saturday 12 September to host 'Question of touch', an entirely free theoretical and practical training course. The initiative, which will be held from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., aims to answer a specific question: how to make museums welcoming places for people with dementia and their caregivers. The project is part of the program 'The Prescribed Museum. New paths for accessibility, well-being and active ageing ', promoted by the municipal administration of Angera with the support of the Lombardy Region, as part of the Single Cultural Notice 2026. The main objective is to experiment with new ways of using cultural heritage and the landscape, focusing on well-being and active ageing.

Operational details

In-depth look at methodologies and participation

The course is aimed at a selected group of 25 participants, who will engage with the challenges related to museum accessibility in contexts of dementia. The methodology adopted by the Sistema Musei Toscani per l’Alzheimer, which teachers Cristina Bucci and Luca Carli Ballòla will bring to Angera, is based on the integration between museum skills and socio-health skills. This multidisciplinary approach is essential to overcome the barriers that often limit the enjoyment of cultural heritage by caregivers and their assisted individuals. During the day, participants will have the opportunity to analyze how the presence of sensory stimuli can transform the museum experience, making it a moment of well-being and cognitive stimulation. The management of the relationship during the visit is one of the pillars of the teaching: techniques to facilitate interaction with works of art will be explored, transforming the museum object into a relational mediator. A peculiar aspect of the initiative is the willingness to put professionals from different fields in dialogue, creating a common language that can be applied daily in Lombardy museums. The choice to limit the places to 25 units responds to the need to ensure high-quality training, allowing moments of direct confrontation and laboratory activities that would not be possible in large groups. This educational structure reflects the experimental approach of the 'Il Museo prescritto' project, which aims to define replicable models of active aging. For anyone working in the socio-health sector, participating in this day means acquiring concrete tools to integrate the cultural offer into care and assistance plans, enhancing the territory and its museum resources not only as places of conservation but as inclusive and dynamic spaces. The training is therefore configured as an operational bridge between the care of the person and the enhancement of the heritage, combining geriatric sensitivity with the historical and artistic competence of the trainers. Participation requires a predisposition to network work and the sharing of good professional practices, essential elements for the success of a project based on universal accessibility and collective well-being.

Key points

Registration procedures and operational methods

To participate in the training day on September 12 in Angera, it is necessary to follow a specific registration procedure, given the limited number of 25 available places. Participation requests must be sent no later than the deadline set for Sunday, September 6, to the email address prenotazioniangera@gmail.com. In the request email, it is essential to provide a series of necessary information for selection and logistical management: the participant's full name, the museum or institution they belong to, their role, a valid email address, and a phone number. It is important to note that, in case of a number of requests exceeding the available places, the organizers will proceed according to the chronological order of receipt of the emails. The declared objective of the organizers is to ensure the participation of the largest possible number of museum realities. For this reason, if necessary, the organization reserves the right to initially confirm only one participant per institution, placing other potential candidates from the same institution on a waiting list. Strict adherence to the provided instructions is essential to ensure the correct management of registrations. Interested parties can find further details and updates by consulting the official Facebook page of the Civici Musei di Angera. For those who wish to delve into the themes of accessibility and the management of time and resources in the work context, or for anyone needing assistance in planning professional paths in Ticino, we invite you to use our calculator for managing payroll and monthly entitlements. Proper management of one's resources and professional updating are fundamental steps for those working in cross-border contexts or in areas that require constant adaptation to local regulations. We remind you that participation in the course is free, but the limited number of places makes the timeliness of sending the application a necessary condition to secure a place within this important training initiative dedicated to inclusion and active aging.

Who can participate in the training?
The course is aimed at museum professionals, educators, social and socio-health workers. Given the nature of the initiative, which requires moments of direct discussion and laboratory activities, the available places are limited to 25. Participation is free and at the end of the day, scheduled from 9 am to 5 pm on Saturday 12 September at the Civic Archaeological Museum in Via Marconi 2, a certificate of participation will be issued to all those present.

Registrations are mandatory and must be sent by Sunday 6 September to the email address prenotazioniangera@gmail.com.
