Double ATM assault Ossona night 25-26 September

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 27 September 2026

In the night between Friday 25 and Saturday 26 September, just before 4 am, two explosions hit the Banco Bpm in Piazza Litta and the Postamat in Via Baracca in Ossona. They investigate the Carabinieri of the Compagnia di Abbiategrasso.

Operational details

The significance of the sequence lies not only in the two ATMs targeted in Ossona, but in the repetition of the same pattern across the area indicated by the source. The explosions occur at night, cause extensive damage and are accompanied by rapid getaways; the haul, however, is not always the outcome. The news reports line up incidents with different outcomes.

Damage, haul and different outcomes

In Bodio Lomnago, the thieves had managed to steal the money. In Busto Garolfo, during the same period, the safe had held firm and the gang had been forced to flee empty-handed. The comparison shows two opposite endings within the same series: in one case the money is stolen, in the other the ATM's resistance makes the assault pointless. The comparable datum therefore remains the outcome of the action.

The calendar also reinforces the perception of continuity. After the incidents of September 12 and 5, the text recalls two explosions in Saronno in July, the June attacks in Bodio Lomnago and Busto Garolfo, as well as those in Tradate and Gallarate. The sequence then goes back to March, with the Bpm ATM in Malnate, and to February, with the Bper ATM in Fagnano Olona. It is not the individual municipality that defines the phenomenon, but the succession of places cited between Altomilanese and Varesotto.

Key points

To follow the story without mixing verified data and initial reconstructions, the reader can use a simple sequence. The method starts from the latest episode and proceeds backwards, keeping places, times, objectives and outcomes separate.

Five steps to read the chronicle

1. Fix the double location. Ossona is not a single target: the source distinguishes the Banco Bpm in Piazza Litta from the Postamat on Via Baracca. This distinction prevents turning two close episodes into a single assault.

2. Fix the moment. The night is that between Friday 25 and Saturday 26 September, and the bangs arrived just before 4 a.m. The temporal data must be kept distinct from the times of the other actions, such as 2:30 a.m. in Travedona Monate.

3. Separate hypothesis and investigation. The hypothesis of the initial checks speaks of at least two groups. The investigation, however, is attributed to the carabinieri of the Abbiategrasso Company. The two pieces of information do not carry the same weight and should not be presented as a definitive conclusion.

4. Record the outcome when indicated. Bodio Lomnago and Busto Garolfo provide the clearest comparison: money stolen in the first case, a resistant safe and escape empty‑handed in the second. The result must therefore be reported without extending it to the other episodes.

5. Reconstruct the series by date. After Ossona, the source mentions Travedona Monate and Inarzo, then the episodes placed between July, June, March and February. The chronology helps to gauge the frequency without automatically assigning a single perpetrator to all the assaults.

This procedure allows you to stay within the available facts: two counters, an initial reconstruction, a carabinieri investigation, and a series of precedents spread across the Altomilanese and Varesotto areas. Frequently Asked Questions
What were the objectives of the double assault on Ossona?
The targets were the Banco Bpm located in Piazza Litta and the Postamat in Via Baracca, both hit by explosions just before 4 o'clock at night between Friday 25 and Saturday 26 September. According to the first reconstructions, at least two groups acted almost simultaneously, as reported by the investigations of the Abbiategrasso police.
What is the connection between the Ossona assault and previous attacks on ATMs in the area?
The assault on Ossona is part of a series of nocturnal explosions at ATMs that have affected the Altomilanese and Varese in recent months, with precedents in Travedona Monate (12 September) and Inarzo (5 September), as well as episodes in Saronno, Bodio Lomnago, Busto Garolfo, Tradate, Gallarate, Malnate and Fagnano Olona between February and July 2026.
How does the article suggest reading and reconstructing the chronicle of the assaults without confusing established data and hypotheses?
The article proposes five steps: fix the double place (Banco Bpm and Postamat), fix the moment (night 25-26 September, just before 4 o'clock), separate hypotheses (at least two groups) from the investigation (Carabinieri of Abbiategrasso), record the outcome when indicated (e.g. money stolen from Bodio Lomnago, safe resistant to Busto Garolfo) and reconstruct the series by dates, starting from the last episode and proceeding backwards.
