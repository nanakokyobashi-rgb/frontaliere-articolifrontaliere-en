When did the New Frontier Agreement between Switzerland and Italy come into force?
The New Frontier Agreement, signed on 23 December 2020, officially entered into force on 1 January 2024. From this date, the new tax provisions governing the taxation of the incomes of Italian-Swiss frontier workers are fully operational, aiming to simplify procedures and reduce bureaucracy.
Are new frontier workers entitled to a tax exemption?
Yes, the New Frontier Agreement introduced a €10,000 deductible for new frontiersmen. This means that the first 10,000 euros of income will not be subject to tax at source in Switzerland. It is a significant advantage for those who start working as border workers, reducing the initial tax burden.
How do you avoid double taxation for frontier workers with the new agreement?
With the New Frontier Agreement, Italy avoids double taxation through the tax credit mechanism. This means that, after the tax at source has been withheld in Switzerland, Italy recognises a credit equal to 100% of the taxable income, ensuring that the frontier worker does not pay taxes twice on the same income.
