Provincial works and network nearing completion

The picture communicated to Como

The meeting held in Como marked the completion of the laying of optical fiber in the entire province. The interventions are part of the public projects «Bando Bul – Ultra Broadband» and «Piano Italia 1 Giga». Franco Lucente, Councilor for Transport and Sustainable Mobility of the Lombardy Region, spoke via video link.

The passage across the 145 municipalities requires precision: the source indicates that Open Fiber is completing the ultra-broadband network envisaged by the Bul Plan. It is therefore not described as already completed in all the municipalities involved. The connected real estate units are just over 144,000 and for these it is possible to request the activation of the service.

The status indicated for the Italy 1 Giga Plan is different. Open Fiber has completed interventions in 50 municipalities in Como; There are over 12,700 civic addresses where it is possible to request activation. The program concerns gray areas, while the Bul Plan affects white areas, i.e. rural and peripheral areas where there is no private interest in investing.

The meeting served to share the results, illustrate the opportunities for the municipalities involved and collect observations and critical issues from local administrators. Lucente indicated that ultra-broadband supports the economic and social growth of the province, especially in areas not covered by private investments. In the Como area, the level of participation in ultra-broadband services is 23%, compared to a national average of 17%.

The interventions in the white areas involved 1,429 Lombardy municipalities, with 261 million in public funds, of which 70 million regional. In the province of Como, public resources exceed 27.9 million euros, with a contribution from the Lombardy Region amounting to 7.4 million. Massimiliano Vari, Marco Luttati, Daniele Mariuzzo and Sergio Galli also participated in the meeting.

Operational details

The difference between white areas and gray areas

Two programs not to be confused

The Bul Plan and the Italia 1 Giga Plan address two different infrastructure situations. White areas are rural and peripheral zones in which there is no private interest in investing. Gray areas, on the other hand, are those where the private operator has no interest in using its own funds to enhance connectivity up to 1 Gbps. In both cases, the intervention is public, but the scope does not coincide.
