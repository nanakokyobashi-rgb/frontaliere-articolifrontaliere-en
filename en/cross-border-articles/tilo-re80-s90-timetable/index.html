From 22:00 on Friday, October 30 to 22:00 on Sunday, November 8, train schedules between Lugano and Chiasso will change. FFS attributes the change to infrastructure works between Vezia and Capolago and announces a combination of earlier departures and cancellations.

What changes for Tilo connections

From Lugano, some Tilo RE80 services will depart up to seven minutes earlier. The explicitly stated consequence is the loss of the connection with InterCity services. The notice therefore distinguishes between earlier departures and cancelled services.

Some Tilo RE80 services terminating at Chiasso are also cancelled on the section between Lugano and Chiasso. The same communication indicates the cancellation of some Tilo S90 services between Lugano and Mendrisio. The notice therefore covers both the Lugano-Chiasso route and the segment toward Mendrisio served by S90 services.

FFS specifies that, during peak hours, measures will be taken to keep rail traffic operating. For those who use the train in Canton Ticino, the key distinction is the type of intervention: the earlier departure of some RE80 services may result in a missed connection with the InterCity, while other RE80 and S90 services are cancelled.

Operational details

The changes announced by FFS do not all have the same nature. The relevant comparison is between earlier departures, the cancellation of RE80 connections, and the cancellation of S90 connections on a different route. This distinction avoids treating all services as if they had undergone the same change.

Frequently Asked Questions
When do the Tilo timetables between Lugano and Chiasso change?
The changes indicated by FFS apply from 22:00 on Friday, October 30, until 22:00 on Sunday, November 8. The change concerns Tilo services between Lugano and Chiasso and is related to infrastructure work between Vezia and Capolago. The same notice also includes S90 services between Lugano and Mendrisio.
Which Tilo services are modified or discontinued?
From Lugano, some Tilo RE80 services depart up to seven minutes earlier and may miss their connection with InterCity services. Some RE80 services terminating at Chiasso between Lugano and Chiasso are also cancelled. The notice also reports the cancellation of some S90 services between Lugano and Mendrisio.
Do the changes also affect connections with the InterCity trains?
Yes. FFS report that some Tilo RE80 services departing from Lugano up to seven minutes earlier result in missed connections with InterCity services. The notice also advises passengers to pay attention to any possible changes to those same InterCity services.
