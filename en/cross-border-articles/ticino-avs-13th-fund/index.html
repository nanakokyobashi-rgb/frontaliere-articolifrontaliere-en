AVS Fund in Ticino: the 13th payment for young people

Di Laura Bianchi · Published on 4 October 2026

A Fund was presented in Ticino: those who will be able and willing to waive the 13th AVS payment will support projects dedicated to the younger generations for the benefit of the community.

The presentation

Presented today in Ticino, the Fund is being established with a formula that shifts a possible individual waiver toward a collective objective. The financing, according to the announcement, will be provided by those who will be able and willing to waive the 13th AVS payment in order to donate it to projects aimed at new generations in Ticino.

The central point is therefore the choice of the individual pensioner. The source does not describe the waiver as an obligation and does not present the Fund as a generalized levy: it speaks of people who will be able and willing to allocate their 13th AVS payment to the new initiative. Financial resources and personal willingness are the only conditions mentioned in the text.

The allocation is defined broadly but precisely: projects dedicated to young people and, through them, a benefit for the community. However, the projects, areas of intervention, amount of the 13th AVS payment to be donated, or the time when the Fund will begin collecting the waivers are not listed.

The announcement also does not indicate who will select the initiatives, what procedure those wishing to participate will have to follow, or whether specific communications for donors will be planned. For this reason, the news records the establishment and funding principle of the Fund, but does not yet offer operational guidance.

The generational pact formula links two groups named in the title, pensioners and young people, but does not alter this scope: the source does not attribute statements to individuals or institutions and does not report figures, deadlines, or regulatory references. Thus, only two elements remain central: waiving the 13th AVS payment and donating it to initiatives for new generations.

For those following the AVS issue, the point to retain is the voluntary transformation of the 13th payment into a donation intended for new generations. The territorial reference is Ticino; the source does not extend the scope to occupational categories or cross-border workers. Operational details

The scope for the cross-border reader

What can be said, and what cannot

For a cross-border worker, the practical reading must start with a limitation: the announcement concerns a possible destination for the 13th AVS payment, but does not present a measure specifically dedicated to cross-border work. The only explicit connection with social security is the reference to AVS. The text does not say whether the Fund is aimed at workers, pensioners residing in a specific place, or people belonging to an administrative category.

Consequently, no information can be derived regarding the G permit, LPP, LAMal, refunds, withholding tax, or INPS. Nor do Brogeda, the new 2026 tax agreement, or double taxation appear. These references are not part of the announcement and cannot be used to supplement it.

The reference to the Canton of Ticino must also be read within its scope: it indicates where the projects for the younger generations are directed. It does not identify the donors’ place of residence, their employer, or their tax status. The text does not connect the Fund to permits, payslips, transfers, border crossings, or relations with Italian authorities.

Key points

How to read the announcement before taking action

A three-step check

The source does not provide a procedure for joining the Fund. Therefore, the most appropriate action, given the information available, is to separate the confirmed facts from the questions that remain open, without turning an announcement into an operational circular.

1. Start with the certain fact. The 13th AVS payment may be allocated to the Fund by those who will be able and willing to waive it. The stated purpose is projects dedicated to the younger generations in Ticino.

2. List the gaps. The amount, timing of the waiver, method of joining, any documents, criteria for selecting projects, and management of donations still need to be clarified. The source provides none of these elements.

3. Keep your personal situation separate. For cross-border workers, the announcement does not make it possible to infer any effects on permits, work, taxation, or commuting. No connection is indicated with administrative procedures other than the possible donation of the 13th AVS payment.

This check avoids confusing the Fund's principle with an instruction to apply immediately. The text does not mention a form, an office, a deadline, or a confirmation channel for anyone intending to waive the 13th AVS payment. Without this data, it is not possible to devise a more detailed procedure while remaining faithful to the source.

The wording chosen for the initiative nevertheless remains clear about its voluntary nature: it does not describe an automatic waiver, but rather the decision of those who will be able and willing to allocate their 13th payment to projects for young people. Any personal assessment must therefore remain distinct from the information not contained in the announcement.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the Fund presented in Ticino?
It is a Fund presented today in Ticino and described as a generational pact in favour of the community. It will be financed by those who can and want to give up the 13th AVS to donate it to projects dedicated to the new generations. The source does not yet indicate amounts, specific projects or operating methods to join the Fund.
Is waiving the 13th AVS pension mandatory?
No. The text talks about people who will be able and willing to give up the 13th AVS. The formula indicates a voluntary choice, but the source does not describe how it will be exercised, what documents will be needed or if there will be deadlines. It is therefore not possible to derive a practical procedure in addition to the principle of renunciation intended for the Fund.
Which projects will the funds be allocated to?
The source only indicates the general destination: projects dedicated to the new generations in Ticino. No initiatives, sectors, selection criteria or persons in charge of management are listed. The reference to the community describes the purpose of the initiative, but does not replace a list of eligible projects.
Does the news introduce changes for cross-border commuters?
The text does not mention frontier, G permit, LPP, LAMal, taxation, refunds, INPS or double taxation. The only explicit social security information is the 13th AVS and its possible donation. For this reason, the source does not allow to establish effects on cross-border work, tax at source or other practices.
