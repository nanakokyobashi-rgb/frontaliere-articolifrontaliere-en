Massive Atlantic raid: 30 tons of cocaine seized (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 8 May 2026

The Spanish Guardia Civil intercepted 30.2 tons of cocaine aboard the Arconian merchant ship, the largest seizure in Spain in a single operation.

Context

In brief

  • Major raid in the Atlantic: 30 tons of cocaine seized
  • International operation against drug trafficking
  • 23 people arrested

Key facts

  • What: Seizure of 30.2 tons of cocaine
  • When: May 1, 2026
  • Where: Off the coast of Western Sahara
  • Who: Spanish Civil Guard
  • Amount: 812 million euros

The Spanish Civil Guard carried out the largest drug seizure in a single operation, intercepting 30.2 tons of cocaine aboard the merchant vessel Arconian, flying the flag of the Comoros. The operation, coordinated by Judge Ismael Moreno of the Audiencia Nacional, led to the arrest of 23 people. The cargo, consisting of 1,279 packages of cocaine, was valued at over 812 million euros. The cargo ship was intercepted on May 1 off the coast of Western Sahara while sailing from Sierra Leone to Libya. During the boarding, agents arrested 17 crew members and six other people hidden on the ship, armed with three rifles, a pistol, and ammunition. The drugs were hidden in a long metal cavity inside the vessel. The operation was conducted with the support of the U.S. DEA and the intelligence unit of the Dutch police.

Operational details

Analysis of implications for cross-border workers

The drug seizure operation in the Atlantic has highlighted the importance of international cooperation in the fight against drug trafficking. For cross-border workers who work in Switzerland and reside in Italy, this operation may have significant implications. Enhanced security measures at borders can affect the crossing times at border crossings, such as Brogeda and Gaggiolo. It is important that cross-border workers are aware of these possible implications and prepare accordingly.

Comparison with the previous situation

Before this operation, border controls were less stringent and waiting times were generally shorter. However, with the increase in surveillance and control activities, cross-border workers may face longer waiting times. It is advisable to inform yourself in advance about the least congested border crossings and plan your trips accordingly. Additionally, it is important to keep all necessary documents, such as the G permit, passport, and work documents, at hand to facilitate checks.

Concrete scenarios

In case of enhanced controls, cross-border workers may face longer waiting times at border crossings. It is advisable to plan trips with an additional time margin to avoid delays. Additionally, it is important to be aware of new regulations and security procedures that may be introduced. Cross-border workers should monitor official communications and local news to stay updated on the latest developments.
