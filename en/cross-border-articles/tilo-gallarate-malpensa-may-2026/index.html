TILO: route changes for Malpensa on May 30-31 (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 28 May 2026

TILO S50 trains will have Gallarate as their terminus next weekend due to work on the Italian network.

Context

In brief

  • TILO S50 trains will not reach Malpensa on May 30-31
  • Temporary terminus at Gallarate due to infrastructure works
  • Passengers must continue with Trenord's RE51 line

Key facts

  • What: Change in route for TILO S50 trains
  • When: Saturday 30 and Sunday 31 May 2026
  • Where: TILO S50 line, terminus Gallarate
  • Who: Passengers heading to Malpensa
  • Amount: Not specified

During the upcoming weekend, TILO S50 connections will have Gallarate as their terminus due to infrastructure enhancement works on the Italian network. This change, effective from 00:01 on Saturday, May 30 until the end of service on Sunday, May 31, will require passengers heading towards Malpensa terminals to continue their journey on Trenord's RE51 line.

Impact on passengers

Passengers should expect longer travel times than usual. The online timetable is currently only partially updated, so it is advisable to check the schedules before departure. This change may cause inconvenience for cross-border commuters using the TILO S50 line to reach Malpensa, especially for those with flights departing or arriving during the weekend.

Assistance for passengers with disabilities

In Ticino, passengers with disabilities who require assistance can contact the toll-free number 0800 007 102 (from Switzerland) or the number +41 800 007 102 (from abroad), available from 5:00 to 24:00. In Italy, passengers with disabilities are invited to communicate their needs at least 48 hours before departure to the dedicated number 800.210.955 (from Italy, active every day from 8:15 to 19:45).

Cross-border workers who use the TILO S50 line to reach Malpensa will need to plan their journey more carefully. The route change could lead to delays and longer waiting times, especially for those catching a flight. It is advisable to depart earlier to avoid unpleasant surprises.

Comparison with the previous situation

Normally, TILO S50 trains arrive directly at Malpensa, providing a quick and convenient connection for travelers. However, during the weekend of May 30-31, passengers will need to disembark at Gallarate and continue with Trenord's RE51 line. This change may cause inconvenience, particularly for those unfamiliar with alternative connections.
