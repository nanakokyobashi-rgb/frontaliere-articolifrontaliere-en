The match is decided in two halves

Castellanzese needed just five minutes to break the deadlock at the «Chiesa» stadium. Boccadamo drove forward decisively down the right flank and whipped in a cross for Chessa, who beat Leoni from close range with a precise header. It was the first incident in a match that was lively and hard-fought from the start.

The black-and-green side continued to press. Three minutes after taking the lead, Schirò found the net after making the most of an assist from Chessa himself, but the referee disallowed the goal for offside against Castellanzese's centre-back. Sant'Angelo reacted first with a sliding effort from Nechita off a Garatti free kick, then levelled after fifteen minutes. Maltoni rose in the area and beat Bongini with a header. The visitors protested the play for an alleged foul on Matella.

Castellanzese did not lose its shape. It continued to build from the back, moving the ball along the ground, and after the half-hour mark it threatened again. Giuliani ran at the home defence and picked out Chessa in the centre of the area; the forward's shot went just wide.

The opening of the second half followed the same script as the first, but this time Sant'Angelo's error proved decisive. Bernardini made a naive mistake while clearing, Valmori stayed alert and took advantage of Leoni's rash exit, heading the ball into the net to make it 2-1.

Late in the match, Sant'Angelo were reduced to ten men after Garatti was sent off for a second yellow card. Bolzoni's side controlled the game without facing any particular danger. Matella came close to a third goal after a combination between Chessa and Valmori, but his shot went wide. The late efforts of Ferrari and Colombo, together with the entire squad's attentive defensive work, kept the result safe.

After the wins against Solbiatese and Crema, the victory over Sant'Angelo is the third in a row. The win moves Castellanzese into the very top positions of Group D of Serie D and confirms the black-and-green side's consistent form.
