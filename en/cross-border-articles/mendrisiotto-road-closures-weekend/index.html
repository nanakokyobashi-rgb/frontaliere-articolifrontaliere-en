Mendrisiotto: three events and roads closed at the weekend (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 16 September 2026

From 18 to 20 September Rally del Ticino, Penziamo and Chiasso-Pedrinate cause road closures, limitations for pedestrians and diverted buses in Mendrisiotto.

Context

In brief

  • Three sporting events in the Mendrisiotto region
  • Road closures from September 18 to 20
  • Restrictions for pedestrians as well
  • Buses with alternative routes
  • Area: Mendrisiotto
  • Period: From September 18 to 20
  • Events: Rally del Ticino, Penziamo, Chiasso-Pedrinate
  • Information channel: Municipal Police of Chiasso

The weekend in the Mendrisiotto region promises to be particularly busy for commuters, residents, and cross-border workers due to the simultaneous occurrence of three major events that will affect the area with inevitable repercussions on local mobility. The Municipal Police of Chiasso - Region I Mendrisiotto Sud has officially communicated the various traffic restrictions and road closures scheduled to host the 28th Rally del Ticino, the 15th edition of Penziamo, and the 12th Chiasso-Pedrinate Historical Re-enactment.

Friday's closure schedule

Motorists and drivers traveling through the border area and neighboring municipalities will need to pay close attention to the time slots and sections affected by traffic blocks. The first restrictions will start on Friday, September 18, on the occasion of the Rally del Ticino. The roads designated for the car racing competition will be completely closed to vehicular traffic between 8:00 PM and 11:30 PM. Going into the geographical detail provided by the competent authorities, in the municipality of Balerna the road sections involved in the measure will be via Passeggiata, via Motta, and via Sottobisio. At the same time, in the municipal territory of Novazzano, the same closure will affect via Passeggiata and via Resiga, effectively blocking ordinary traffic and requiring advance planning of travel for anyone who needs to move during that specific evening time slot.

Operational details

Safety devices and pedestrian mobility

The management of motor and running events of this scale requires extraordinary measures to protect public safety and ensure the smooth running of competitions without unexpected events. For security reasons strictly established by the police authorities, during the time slots indicated by official press releases, pedestrian transit will also be strictly prohibited along all roads directly affected by the races. This provision applies without exception during the time windows of the various events, thus requiring residents, workers and people present on site to anticipate or postpone their travel on foot. It should be noted that residents of the areas directly involved have already been informed in advance by the responsible bodies, thus reducing the inconvenience associated with sudden interruptions of local traffic.

Key points

Impact on public transport and race Sunday

Local public transport systems will undergo inevitable changes to adapt to the road closures imposed by events in the Mendrisiotto region. During critical Saturday time slots, operating buses to and from Pedrinate must follow an alternative route, diverting their regular path through via Cattaneo, via Manzoni and via Motta to bypass traffic-restricted sections. The situation will evolve further on Sunday, September 20, entirely dedicated to the Chiasso-Pedrinate Historical Reenactment. On this specific holiday, from 7 AM until 7:30 PM, traffic will be completely blocked in the via Favre tunnel, via Cattaneo and via Favre itself in Chiasso, as well as in via Camponovo in Pedrinate. Along the sections directly affected by the passage of historic vehicles, public transport service will be temporarily suspended for the entire duration of the event. On this Sunday occasion as well, rigorous safety regulations will enforce an absolute ban on pedestrian transit on the involved roads during the dynamic phases of the event, eliminating pedestrian mobility in the barricaded sectors.

Frequently Asked Questions
What events cause roads to be closed in Mendrisiotto?
During the weekend of 18 to 20 September, the Mendrisiotto region is affected by the concurrence of three important events: the twenty-eighth Ticino Rally, the fifteenth edition of Penziamo and the twelfth Chiasso-Pedrinate Historical Reenactment. These competitions inevitably have repercussions on local mobility, with road closures made official by the Chiasso Municipal Police.
What are the times and routes affected by Friday closures?
The first restrictions take effect on Friday 18 September on the occasion of the Ticino Rally, with roads totally closed to vehicular traffic in the time slot between 20:00 and 23:30. In Balerna the sections involved are via Passeggiata, via Motta and via Sottobisio, while in Novazzano the closure concerns via Passeggiata and via Resiga.
How does public transport change over the weekend?
The local public transport system undergoes changes to adapt to the closures. On Saturday the buses to and from Pedrinate follow an alternative route diverting through via Cattaneo, via Manzoni and via Motta. Sunday 20 September, during the Chiasso-Pedrinate Historical Reenactment from 7 to 19.30, traffic is blocked in the tunnel of via Favre, in via Cattaneo and in via Camponovo in Pedrinate, and the public transport service is temporarily suspended for the entire duration of the event.
