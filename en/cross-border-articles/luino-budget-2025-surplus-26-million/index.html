Luino ends 2025 with a surplus of 2.6 million (cross-border guide)

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 4 May 2026

The 2025 final budget of the municipality of Luino shows a surplus of 2.6 million euros, with revenues exceeding 31.5 million.

Context

In brief

  • Luino closes 2025 with a surplus of 2.6 million
  • Accrued revenues exceeding 31.5 million euros
  • Committed expenses around 28.8 million

Key facts

  • What: 2025 Final Budget of the Municipality of Luino
  • When: 2025
  • Where: Luino, Italy
  • Who: Municipality of Luino, led by Enrico Bianchi
  • Amount: 2.6 million euros surplus

The 2025 final budget of the Municipality of Luino, recently approved, highlights a surplus of 2.6 million euros. This result is due to accrued revenues exceeding 31.5 million euros and committed expenses of around 28.8 million. The overall administration result exceeds 12 million, growing compared to the previous year. These figures indicate an overall balanced management, although more than half of the surplus is set aside and an additional portion is earmarked by law or for specific purposes.

Main expenses

The most significant item on the expenditure side remains social spending, with around 5 million euros allocated to interventions for families, minors, and vulnerabilities. This data confirms the impact of the difficulties that emerged in the years marked by the pandemic and the economic crisis. Alongside social spending, a significant portion of resources has been directed towards the protection of the territory and the environment, including maintenance, ordinary management, and works against hydrogeological instability. Investments have also been made in the education sector, including educational services and interventions on school buildings.

Operational details

Practical Analysis

The 2025 final budget of the Municipality of Luino offers a detailed snapshot of the town's financial situation. For cross-border workers, it's important to understand how these resources will be used and what the practical implications might be for their daily lives.

Social Investments

Approximately 5 million euros have been allocated to social interventions, a significant figure representing nearly 28% of current expenditure. This investment is crucial for cross-border worker families, who often face challenges related to dual residency and differences in healthcare and education systems between Italy and Switzerland. Social services can provide support in areas such as childcare, support for single-parent families, and assistance for those in vulnerable situations.

Territory Protection

A significant portion of resources has been directed towards protecting the territory and the environment. This includes maintenance, routine management, and works to prevent hydrogeological instability. For cross-border workers, better territory management can mean a higher quality of life, with safer infrastructure and a cleaner environment. Furthermore, investments in this sector can help prevent natural disasters, protecting properties and families.

Education

Investments have also been made in the education sector, with interventions in school buildings and educational services. For cross-border workers with children, access to well-equipped schools and quality educational services is fundamental. These investments can facilitate the integration of cross-border children into the local school system and offer better educational opportunities.

Key points

Concrete actions

For cross-border workers who wish to delve into the 2025 final budget of the Municipality of Luino and better understand how these resources will be used, there are several concrete actions they can take.

Consulting the full budget

The full final budget is available on the Municipality of Luino's website. Cross-border workers can access this document to obtain a detailed view of the municipality's revenues and expenses. This can help better understand how resources are allocated and what the municipality's priorities are.

Participating in municipal assemblies

Cross-border workers can participate in municipal assemblies to express their opinions and suggestions regarding the use of municipal resources. This is an effective way to influence the municipality's future decisions and ensure that the needs of cross-border workers are taken into account.

Using municipal services

Cross-border workers can benefit from the municipal services offered, such as social, educational, and environmental services. Using these services can improve the quality of life and provide support in areas of greatest need.

Monitoring future decisions

It is important to monitor the municipality's future decisions regarding social and educational investments. Cross-border workers can follow local news and participate in public consultations to ensure their needs are met.

Using the site's tools

For further information and to calculate the impact of municipal decisions on their financial situation, cross-border workers can use the salary calculator available on the site. This tool can help better understand how municipal decisions may influence their income and expenses.
