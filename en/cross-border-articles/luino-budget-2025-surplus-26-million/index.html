Luino ends 2025 with a surplus of 2.6 million

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 4 May 2026

The 2025 final budget of the municipality of Luino shows a surplus of 2.6 million euros, with revenues exceeding 31.5 million.

Context

In brief

  • Luino closes 2025 with a surplus of 2.6 million
  • Accrued revenues exceeding 31.5 million euros
  • Committed expenses around 28.8 million

Key facts

  • What: 2025 Final Budget of the Municipality of Luino
  • When: 2025
  • Where: Luino, Italy
  • Who: Municipality of Luino, led by Enrico Bianchi
  • Amount: 2.6 million euros surplus

The 2025 final budget of the Municipality of Luino, recently approved, highlights a surplus of 2.6 million euros. This result is due to accrued revenues exceeding 31.5 million euros and committed expenses of around 28.8 million. The overall administration result exceeds 12 million, growing compared to the previous year. These figures indicate an overall balanced management, although more than half of the surplus is set aside and an additional portion is earmarked by law or for specific purposes.

Main expenses

The most significant item on the expenditure side remains social spending, with around 5 million euros allocated to interventions for families, minors, and vulnerabilities. This data confirms the impact of the difficulties that emerged in the years marked by the pandemic and the economic crisis. Alongside social spending, a significant portion of resources has been directed towards the protection of the territory and the environment, including maintenance, ordinary management, and works against hydrogeological instability. Investments have also been made in the education sector, including educational services and interventions on school buildings.

Financial situation

From a financial point of view, the Municipality has not resorted to new borrowing and closes with a cash availability exceeding 12 million. An element that strengthens the solidity of the entity, but which will have to face future needs and the ability to turn available resources into concrete interventions.

Implications for cross-border workers

For cross-border workers residing in Italy and working in Switzerland, this budget could have indirect implications. A solid municipal budget can mean greater stability and investments in public services, which can improve the quality of life for all residents, including cross-border workers. Furthermore, the balanced management of resources can translate into a better offer of educational and social services, fundamental for cross-border worker families.

Comparisons and scenarios

Comparing the 2025 budget with those of previous years, a constant growth in revenues and a prudent management of expenses can be noted. This approach could be a model for other municipalities, especially in an still uncertain economic context. For cross-border workers, it is important to monitor how these resources will be used in the coming years, particularly in the social and education sectors, which are areas of great interest for families.
