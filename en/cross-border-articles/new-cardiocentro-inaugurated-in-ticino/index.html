New Cardiocentro Ticino unveiled (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 May 2026

The new Cardiocentro Ticino offers integrated services and improved specialist care for the region. Inaugurated in Lugano on 4 May 2026.

Context

In brief

Operational details

Practical Analysis

The inauguration of the new Cardiocentro Ticino has numerous practical implications for cross-border workers who live and work in Ticino. The integration of medical services under one roof significantly simplifies access to care and improves the quality of healthcare. In addition, the expansion of facilities and investment in highly specialized medicine contribute to containing healthcare costs and improving the efficiency of the healthcare system.

Comparison with the Previous Situation

Before the inauguration of the new Cardiocentro, cardiology, thoracic surgery, vascular surgery, and angiology services were distributed across various EOC facilities. This resulted in greater complexity in accessing services and less integration between different specialties. With the new Cardiocentro, all these services will be brought together under one roof, facilitating access and improving the quality of healthcare.

Procedures and Access to Services

To access the services of the new Cardiocentro Ticino, cross-border workers must follow the standard procedures of the Cantonale Hospital Authority (EOC). It is advisable to check the specific requirements and necessary documents for access to medical services. Additionally, it is essential to stay up-to-date on any legislative changes and new regulations related to healthcare.

Future Scenarios

In the future, the new Cardiocentro Ticino could become a reference point for cantonal and regional healthcare. The integration of services and expansion of facilities could attract a larger number of patients and specialists, further improving the quality of care offered. In addition, investment in highly specialized medicine could contribute to containing healthcare costs and improving the efficiency of the healthcare system.

Step-by-Step Guides

1. Verification of Requirements: Check the specific requirements for access to the services of the new Cardiocentro Ticino. Make sure you have all the necessary documents. 2. Booking: Book a visit or appointment with your desired medical service. You can do this online or by contacting the Cardiocentro directly. 3. Documentation: Bring all the required documents with you during your visit. Make sure you have a copy of your residence permit and ID. 4. Access to Services: Follow the procedures indicated by the Cardiocentro staff to access medical services. Make sure to follow all the instructions provided. 5. Updates: Stay up to date on any regulatory changes and new provisions related to healthcare. Check the Cardiocentro website regularly for up-to-date information.
