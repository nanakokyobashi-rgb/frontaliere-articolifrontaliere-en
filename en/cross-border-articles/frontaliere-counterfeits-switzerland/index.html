Operational details

The border agent had to clear the rest of the cargo and pay a fine for the failure to declare customs on entry into Switzerland. The Office of Customs and Excise (UDSC) explained that the introduction into Swiss customs territory or the removal from the latter of counterfeits related to trademarks and designs is in principle prohibited. The same applies to goods imported, exported or in transit in tourist traffic for private purposes.

According to article 15 of the Swiss Customs Regulation (RDS) of 2003, which governs duty-bound and free goods, "whoever introduces or removes from this customs territory duty-bound or free goods without declaration or with incomplete or false declaration, shall be punished with a fine of at least CHF 1'000.00 and up to CHF 100'000.00". 📊

The Agno case is just the latest in a long line of breaches of Swiss customs rules. In 2020, the UDSC found that an Italian citizen attempted to introduce into Swiss territory without declaration 15 designer bags of the "Louis Vuitton" brand with a total value of CHF 10,000.00. The citizen was punished with a fine of CHF 5,000.00.

Key points

If you are a frontier worker and you intend to transport goods to the Swiss territory, make sure that you declare them correctly and do not introduce counterfeits. Swiss customs are very strict with those who introduce counterfeits into the national territory. If you have been intercepted with a load of counterfeits, you may be fined and called back to your duties.

Article 5 of the Customs Act of 10 October 1920 states that "anyone who introduces or transfers counterfeit or altered goods risks a fine of Fr. 1,000 to Fr. 100,000. ”This means that if you have been intercepted with a load of counterfeits, you could be fined up to 100,000 francs.

Can I bring fake branded bags or clothes for personal use to Switzerland?
No, the introduction of counterfeits related to trademarks and designs is prohibited in the Swiss customs territory, even if intended for tourist traffic or private purposes.
