Fogliaro celebrates St. Joseph the Worker with mass, food and market

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 30 April 2026

The Fogliaro district in Varese celebrates St. Joseph the Worker with a day of events including solemn mass, stalls and local gastronomy

Context

In brief

  • Fogliaro celebrates St. Joseph the Worker on May 1, 2026
  • Solemn Mass at 11:00 and Holy Rosary at 18:00
  • Craft market and gastronomic stall for the entire day

Key facts

  • What: Feast of St. Joseph the Worker
  • When: May 1, 2026, from 11:00 to 18:00
  • Where: Streets adjacent to the parish church of San Giuseppe, Via Santa Caterina, 15, Varese
  • Who: Parish of San Giuseppe in Fogliaro, with the patronage of the Municipality of Varese
  • Cost: Not specified

The Fogliaro district in Varese celebrates St. Joseph the Worker with a day rich in events that combines religious devotion and community involvement. The feast, which will take place on May 1, 2026, begins at 11:00 with the solemn Mass at the parish church of San Giuseppe. In the afternoon, at 18:00, the recitation of the Holy Rosary is scheduled. Throughout the day, in the streets adjacent to the church, a craft market will be set up, appreciated for its variety of handicraft products. Additionally, at noon, the gastronomic stall will open, offering participants the opportunity to taste local specialties. The event is organized by the Pastoral Community Santi Gottardo and Giovanni Paolo II, which groups the parishes of Bregazzana, Fogliaro, Velate, Rasa, and Sant'Ambrogio, with the patronage of the Municipality of Varese.

The geographical proximity between Varese and the Canton of Ticino facilitates the participation of cross-border workers, who can easily reach Fogliaro to enjoy the day of celebration.

Comparison with similar events in Ticino

In Ticino, similar events like the Feast of St. Joseph in Lugano or the Workers' Day in Bellinzona offer analogous opportunities for celebration and community. However, the Fogliaro festival stands out for its craft market and local gastronomy, which reflect the traditions of the Varese area. Cross-border workers can appreciate the cultural diversity and participate in events that celebrate both their culture of origin and that of their place of residence.

Travel Planning

1. Check transport schedules: Verify the train and bus schedules to reach Varese from Lugano or other locations in the Canton Ticino. Plan your route by car: If you decide to travel by car, check traffic conditions and available parking nearby. Use a GPS navigator to find the most efficient route.

1. Solemn Mass: Arrive at the church at least 15 minutes early to find a seat. The solemn mass begins at 11:00 and lasts about an hour. 2. Gastronomic stall: Taste the local specialties at the gastronomic stall. Make sure to try the traditional dishes and support local producers. 4. Holy Rosary: Participate in the recitation of the Holy Rosary at 18:00 to conclude the day in a spiritual atmosphere.

Frequently Asked Questions
Where is the parish church of San Giuseppe in Fogliaro located?
The parish church of San Giuseppe is located at Via Santa Caterina, 15, Varese. It is easily accessible by public transport or by car, with parking available nearby.
What are the event schedules during the festival?
The festival begins with a solemn mass at 11:00 AM, followed by the opening of the gastronomic stall at noon. The artisan market is open all day, and the recitation of the Holy Rosary is scheduled for 6:00 PM.
Is it necessary to book to participate in the festival?
It is not necessary to book to participate in the festival. However, for information and bookings, you can contact the number 333 208 7165.
