Right to work

Under the New Frontier Agreement, frontier workers can work in Italy without needing a work visa, but must register with the Bellinzona Chamber of Commerce and Industry within 30 days of their arrival. In addition, they must have an employment contract written and signed by both parties.

Introduction

The New Frontier Agreement, signed on 23 December 2020, entered into force on 1 January 2024. This agreement aims to simplify border transfers between Switzerland and Italy, ensuring greater flexibility and security for the citizens involved.

Benefits of the New Frontier Agreement

  • Transparency and ease: The New Frontier Agreement guarantees greater transparency and ease in border transfers, reducing waiting times and costs associated with bureaucratic procedures.
  • Greater security: the agreement guarantees greater security for the citizens involved, thanks to the creation of a more efficient monitoring and control system.
  • Job opportunities: The New Border Agreement offers job opportunities to the citizens involved, thanks to the creation of jobs in border areas.

Key points

Tax Settings

Border workers working in Ticino and residing in Italy should consider tax settings before moving to Claino con Osteno. The New Frontier Agreement provides for a deductible of €10,000 for new frontier workers. Old frontier workers can benefit from a transitional regime from 2024 to 2033.

Transitional regime

Border workers who have already worked in Ticino before 2024 can enjoy a transitional regime until 2033. This means they won't be subject to the most stringent tax settings. However, it is important to note that the transitional regime is not automatic and border workers must request authorisation from the Council of State.

Deductible for new frontier workers

The €10,000 deductible for new frontier workers is great news for those considering moving to Claino con Osteno. This exemption means that new frontier workers will not be subject to the most stringent tax settings for the first 5 years of work. However, it is important to note that the deductible is not valid if the border worker has already worked in Ticino before 2024.
