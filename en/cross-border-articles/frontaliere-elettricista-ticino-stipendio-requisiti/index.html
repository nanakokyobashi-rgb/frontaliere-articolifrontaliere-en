Frontier electrician in Ticino: average salary, requirements and G permit (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 24 July 2026

Find out the requirements and the G permit needed to work as an electrician in Ticino as a border worker.

Context

In a nutshell

  • The G permit is no longer necessary to work as an electrician in Ticino as a border worker as of 1 January 2024.
  • The average salary for an electrician in Ticino is about CHF 60,000 per year.

Key facts

  • Requirements: recognised qualification and G permit (no longer necessary from 1 January 2024).
  • Permits: G (no longer required as of January 1, 2024).
  • Average salary: CHF 60,000 per year.

The new Frontier Agreement, signed on 23 December 2020 and in force from 1 January 2024, provides that border workers can work as electricians in Ticino without the need for a special permit. However, you must have a recognised qualification and obtain a G permit.

Requirements and permits

To work as an electrician in Ticino, border workers must have a recognised qualification. This means that they must have completed a training course in electricity recognised by an accredited body. In addition, it is no longer necessary to obtain the G permit as of January 1, 2024.

Average salary

The average salary for an electrician in Ticino is about CHF 60,000 per year. However, it is important to note that salary may vary depending on location and type of employment. For example, an electrician working in Lugano could earn about CHF 65,000 per year, while an electrician working in Bellinzona could earn about CHF 55,000 per year.

To work as an electrician in Ticino, you must have a professional license issued by Switzerland or be in possession of an equivalent professional license issued by another Free Trade Agreement (FTA) member country. In addition, it is necessary to have a good knowledge of the Italian language, since it is the official language of Ticino.

The application for a work permit is necessary to work as an electrician in Ticino. The work permit is issued by the Immigration Office of the Canton of Ticino and is valid for 1 year. An application for a work permit must be submitted within 8 weeks from the date of commencement of work.
