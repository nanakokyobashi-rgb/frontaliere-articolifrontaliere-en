For example, according to data from the Federal Statistical Office (UFS), inflation in the Canton of Ticino was 2.3% in 2022, compared to 2.1% nationally. Additionally, the unemployment rate in the Canton of Ticino was 4.5% in 2022, compared to 3.5% nationally.

The Ticino Region has recognized the severity of the situation and announced its intention to invoke countermeasures to combat the decline in purchasing power and support LAMal. Among the countermeasures planned are:

  • Financial support for businesses and workers

  • Promotion of social inclusion policies

The LAMal initiative

The LAMal initiative aims to create a unique data collection for the Canton of Ticino. This unique data collection could help improve the economic situation and combat the decline in purchasing power. The LAMal initiative was announced by the Ticino Region and aims to involve various entities and institutions in the Canton of Ticino to create a unique data collection.
