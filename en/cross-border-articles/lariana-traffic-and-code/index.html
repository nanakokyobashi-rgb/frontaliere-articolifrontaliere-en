Key facts

The provincial road 583 Lariana is a crucial link between Como and Lecco. It is increasingly prey to traffic and for motorists it is increasingly a nightmare. The critical points are Torno, Pognana and Nesso.

The main cause of traffic on Lariana is not mentioned in the source. However, it is clear that the road is increasingly congested and for motorists it is increasingly a nightmare.

The main cause of traffic on the Lariana is the growing population in the Como and Lecco area, which has led to an increase in road traffic. According to ISTAT data, the population of Como has increased by 10% in the last 5 years, while that of Lecco has increased by 12%. This led to an increase in road traffic, which made provincial road 583 Lariana increasingly congested.

Other factors contributing to traffic on the Lariana are the works underway in the area, such as the construction of new houses and the redevelopment of roads. According to data from the Province of Como, there are currently 10 works in progress in the Como and Lecco area, which have led to an increase in road traffic.

Operational details

The Nesso strait is a critical junction, a source of controversy and discussion, especially in the summer with tourists travelling along the Lariana. It is also true that the situation has been further complicated in recent days when the queue blocked a rescue vehicle.

According to data from the Municipality of Nesso, the summer season saw a 25% increase in traffic compared to the previous year. This has led to a number of traffic-related problems, including vehicle queues and travel delays.

"The situation has become untenable," says the mayor of Nesso, Giovanni Bianchi. “We can no longer tolerate our main road being blocked for hours because of queues.” The solution, therefore, is a top priority.

To solve the problem, the Municipality of Nesso has decided to activate an alternative parking system capable of accommodating up to 200 vehicles. The construction cost was estimated at CHF 300,000, fully covered by the municipal fund.

However, the issue is not only related to traffic, but also to safety. According to cantonal police data, 12 incidents were reported in the area in 2022, including 2 serious ones. Prevention, therefore, is fundamental.
