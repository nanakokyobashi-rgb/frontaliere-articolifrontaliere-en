Pedemontana: no toll payments via SMS or email

## TL;DR - Pedemontana reports fake toll charges via e-mail, SMS and WhatsApp - The messages threaten a block or administrative seizure - Any tolls.

In Varese, Autostrada Pedemontana Lombarda reported a new phase of the fake toll scam. The company that manages the Lombardy motorway section says that the fraudulent messages, already circulated in recent days, have become more aggressive: they arrive via e-mail, SMS and WhatsApp and now also evoke the risk of vehicle block or administrative immobilization.

Pedemontana is monitoring the phenomenon and informing customers. The central clarification is unequivocal: the company never requests toll payments through SMS or e-mails containing links to make the payment.

The scam's new tactic

According to the company's recent findings, compared with the initial attempts, the scammers have made the texts more credible and increased the pressure on recipients. The message creates a false sense of urgency: if the amount is not paid immediately, the vehicle may supposedly be blocked or administratively immobilized. The source makes clear that this claim is false.

The scammers constantly change the addresses included in the messages: the links lead to fake domains unrelated to the company, in an attempt to evade security filters and IT blocks. The communication may also display official logos and trademarks, which do not change its fraudulent nature.

The threat, therefore, concerns more than simply paying a toll: the messages aim to steal personal data, banking information or PIN codes for credit cards. The pressure urges users to act quickly, but the company reminds them that any outstanding tolls must be verified exclusively on the official portal pedemontana.com.

For any clarification, the notice invites users to contact the company's call center, available at the toll-free number 800 936 360. Pedemontana finally recommends not opening links in suspicious SMS, e-mails or WhatsApp messages and verifying payment requests through official channels.

Operational details

The most delicate point for anyone receiving a request is distinguishing the visual appearance from the actual procedure. Pedemontana's findings show that scammers focus precisely on the former: more credible texts, official logos and changing addresses. The element to assess is therefore not the logo, but the channel used to request payment. The company states that SMS messages and e-mails with links are not the method it uses to request toll payments.

A changing threat, not an official procedure

The comparison with the first attempts described by the company concerns how the recipient is persuaded. Recent messages add a consequence presented as imminent: the blocking or administrative seizure of the vehicle if payment is not made immediately. Pedemontana describes this scenario as false. The threat therefore serves to create pressure and does not confirm that a toll exists.

There are three levels that must not be confused. The logo belongs to the visual appearance and may appear in the message. The address is the technical link and may lead to a fake domain, not traceable to the company. The sentence about the seizure is the content that seeks to speed up the decision. Verification must remain separate from all three elements and go through the official portal indicated in the notice.

The constant variation of addresses adds another difficulty. According to Pedemontana, the links change in an attempt to bypass security filters and IT blocks. For this reason, a convincingly written text does not become trustworthy merely because it resembles a company communication. The same caution applies to official logos and trademarks, which may be used in fraudulent communications.

Key points

When a message asks for toll payment, the procedure indicated by Pedemontana is straightforward. There is no need to follow the path laid out by the sender: stop before proceeding to the link, verify the request through the official channel, and use the company’s contact details if doubts remain.

The sequence to follow

1. Stop before proceeding to the link. If the request arrives via SMS, email or WhatsApp and contains a link for payment, do not click it. The company’s instruction applies even when the text seems credible or uses an official logo.

2. Isolate the threat. If the notice mentions a block or administrative hold in the event of immediate non-payment, bear in mind that Pedemontana describes this consequence as false. Do not use the urgency indicated in the message as a basis for deciding.

3. Go only to the indicated channel. Any outstanding tolls must be checked exclusively on the official portal pedemontana.com. This is the step to use to check the request without following the address you received.

4. If doubts remain, use the company contact. The call center of Autostrada Pedemontana Lombarda can be reached at the toll-free number 800 936 360. The notice indicates it as a reference for situations of doubt.

5. Protect your data. Fraudulent messages aim to obtain personal data, banking information and PIN codes for credit cards. Do not enter these details on a page reached through the suspicious link.

This procedure prevents turning a received communication into an automatic payment. There is no need to determine whether the domain is merely unusual or already known: the rule indicated by Pedemontana is to check for any outstanding amounts on the official portal and use the call center when doubts remain. The check must be carried out before making any payment requested by the message.

Frequently Asked Questions
How to recognize a fake toll message according to Pedemontana?
Pedemontana states that it never requests payment via SMS, email, or WhatsApp with a link; any request containing a link is fraudulent, even if it includes official logos or credible-sounding text.
What to do if you receive an SMS asking for toll payment and threatening to block the vehicle?
Do not click the link, ignore the threat of blocking or administrative seizure (defined as false by Pedemontana), check your status on the official pedemontana.com portal and, if still in doubt, call the toll-free number 800 936 360.
Where can you actually check whether a toll is due on the Autostrada Pedemontana Lombarda?
The only official channel indicated by Pedemontana for checking any outstanding amounts is the pedemontana.com website; do not use links received via messaging, but access the portal directly.
