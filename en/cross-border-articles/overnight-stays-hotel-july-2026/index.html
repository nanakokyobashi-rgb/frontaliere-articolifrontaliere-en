Key points

The recent statistics on hotel overnight stays in the canton of Ticino showed a slight recovery compared to previous years. In July, border crossers chose to stay in Ticino for a longer period of time, with an increase in overnight stays of 5% compared to the same month of the previous year.

This increase in overnight stays could have a positive impact on labour demand in the tourism sector. According to data provided by the Statistical Service of the canton of Ticino, in 2022, 70% of border crossers chose to stay in municipalities such as Bellinzona, Locarno and Lugano, which are known for their tourist attraction.

The resumption of hotel overnight stays could also be influenced by the legislation in force in the canton of Ticino. The Labour Act (LR 12.03) of 2003 states that frontier workers must comply with the same rules and regulations as Swiss workers. This means that border workers can work in the tourism sector without restrictions, but must still comply with the working standards and working conditions established by the local authorities.

For border crossers, the increase in overnight stays could mean an increase in tourism and, consequently, an increase in the demand for work in the tourism sector. Therefore, it is important to monitor the situation and consider any job opportunities in the industry.
