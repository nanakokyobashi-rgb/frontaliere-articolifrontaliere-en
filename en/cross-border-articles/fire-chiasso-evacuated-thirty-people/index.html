Rooftop fire in Chiasso, 30 evacuated

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 May 2026

A fire broke out on the roof of a building in Piazza Indipendenza, Chiasso, leading to the evacuation of around thirty people.

Context

In brief

  • Roof fire in Chiasso, thirty people evacuated
  • Two workers slightly injured during roof work
  • Flames contained outside the building

Key facts

  • What: Roof fire in a residential building
  • When: Monday, May 4, 2026, just before 13:30
  • Where: Piazza Indipendenza, Chiasso
  • Who: Four workers, Cantonal Police, Chiasso Municipal Police, Mendrisiotto Emergency Medical Service (SAM), Mendrisiotto Cantonal Fire and Rescue Service (CSCPM)
  • Injuries: Two workers slightly injured

A fire broke out on the roof of a residential building in Piazza Indipendenza, Chiasso, in the early afternoon of Monday. According to initial reports, the flames developed while four workers were carrying out roof repairs using a gas torch. The Cantonal Police have launched an investigation to clarify the cause of the fire. As a precaution, the temporary evacuation of about thirty people present in the building was ordered.

On the scene, Cantonal Police officers, supported by Chiasso Municipal Police, Mendrisiotto Emergency Medical Service (SAM) rescuers, and Mendrisiotto Cantonal Fire and Rescue Service (CSCPM) firefighters intervened. The flames remained confined to the outside of the building. According to an initial medical assessment, one worker suffered minor burns to his hands, while another experienced mild symptoms of intoxication. Both were taken to the hospital by ambulance for further examination.

To allow extinguishing operations, Via Livio and Corso San Gottardo were temporarily closed to traffic. The situation is under control, but investigations are still ongoing to determine the origin of the fire.
