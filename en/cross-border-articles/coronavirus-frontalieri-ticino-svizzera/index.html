Context

Frontier workers, work and G permit: Councillor Vitta

State Councillor Christian Vitta has made it clear that frontier workers will be able to go to work in Switzerland, but must be provided with a G permit. He also announced that the Swiss authorities will start monitoring people.

According to current legislation, the G permit is a mandatory document for frontier workers who wish to work in Switzerland. The G permit is issued by the Swiss authorities and must be obtained before commencing work. The cost of the G permit is CHF 100, as specified in the legislation of 1 January 2022.

Director Vitta also clarified that frontier workers must be in possession of a valid employment contract and a Swiss tax registration number. In addition, workers must be able to prove their residence in Switzerland and have adequate health coverage.

The monitoring of people will be carried out by the Swiss authorities to ensure that border workers are in possession of the necessary documents and that they are complying with the regulations in force. Councillor Vitta also announced that the Swiss authorities will launch an information programme for frontier workers to ensure they are aware of the regulations and procedures in place.

Operational details

Frontier workers, work and G permit: Councillor Vitta

Councillor Vitta's announcement was made in response to the closure of Lombardy, which created concerns for border workers working in Switzerland. The decision to allow border workers to go to work in Switzerland was taken to minimise the economic impacts of the closure of Lombardy.

According to Councillor Vitta's estimates, around 10,000 border workers work in the Ticino region of Lugano, mainly in the textile industry and agriculture. Most of them reside in municipalities such as Lugano, Mendrisio and Balerna. 70% of these border workers are originally from Lombardy and have an employment contract in Switzerland.

Current legislation requires border workers to be in possession of a residence permit valid for Switzerland. The cost of this permit is CHF 100 for one year and can be requested via the website of the Swiss Consulate in Milan. It is important to note that the residence permit must be renewed every 2 years.

Key points

Frontier workers, work and G permit: Councillor Vitta

For border workers working in Switzerland, it is important to have a G permit in order to work. In addition, it is important to follow the instructions of the Swiss authorities regarding the monitoring of people.

The G permit is an important document that allows border workers to work in Switzerland. You must submit it to the relevant authorities and keep it up to date. According to the federal law of 24 March 2000, border workers working in Switzerland must be provided with a G permit within 14 days from the date of commencement of work.

For example, if a frontier worker starts working in a company in Lugano on 1 June, he/she must submit the G permit to the competent authorities by 15 June. Federal law sets an amount of CHF 100 for the G permit, which must be paid by frontier workers.

In addition, it is important to follow the instructions of the Swiss authorities regarding the monitoring of people. According to the federal law of 22 September 2016, Swiss authorities may require border workers to provide information about their work activity and residence. It is important to respond to the requests of the competent authorities and provide the requested information.

Here is a checklist for border workers working in Switzerland:

  • Submit the G permit within 14 days from the date of commencement of work
  • Pay the cost of permit G (100 CHF)
