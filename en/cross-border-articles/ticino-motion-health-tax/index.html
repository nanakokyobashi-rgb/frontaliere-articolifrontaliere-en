In recent days, the so-called 'health tax' has returned to the center of public debate also in the Canton of Ticino. The source reports that an inter-party motion dedicated to the Italian measure has been presented in the Grand Council.

The point raised by the motion concerns the relationship with the Agreement on the taxation of cross-border workers. According to the complaint reported by the source, the Italian measure would be in violation of article 9 of the same agreement. The contestation is thus traced back to a specific norm and the provision on the right to tax employment income.

The scope of the contestation

According to the available text, article 9 would exclusively attribute to Switzerland the right to tax the employment income of 'old cross-border workers'. The clarification on the audience is a central part of the news: the source does not present the phrase as referred indiscriminately to every cross-border worker and does not describe other categories.

The title accompanies the motion with a threat of blocking reimbursements by Ticino. The text does not indicate a date, a procedure, or conditions for a possible block. Therefore, the news documents the presentation of the motion and the position it expresses; it does not document a block that has already occurred.

The temporal reference and the categories

According to the verified domain reference, the New Cross-Border Workers Agreement was signed on December 23, 2020 and has been in force since January 1, 2024. However, the source text provides no dates and merely refers to the Agreement on the taxation of cross-border workers. This temporal reference alone does not demonstrate the alleged violation.
