Lumino-Castione cycle path: second phase of the works

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 October 2026

From Monday, October 12 to the end of April 2027, works between via Quatorta and the Castione FFS station, with two new bus stops

Context

TL;DR

  • Second phase from Monday, October 12 to the end of April 2027
  • Route between via Quatorta and Castione FFS station
  • Two new bus stops along via Bellinzona
  • Weekday works from 7 to 17.30

Key facts

  • Works → second phase of the new cycle path
  • Route → former Ferrovia Retica line between via Quatorta and Castione FFS station
  • Municipalities → Lumino and Arbedo-Castione
  • Stops → two new public transport stops
  • Hours → Monday-Friday, 7-17.30

Starting Monday, October 12, the new cycle path between Lumino and Castione is entering the second phase of works. The programme announced by the Department of the Territory is scheduled to run until the end of April 2027.

The construction site concerns the creation of the route along the former Ferrovia Retica line, in the section between via Quatorta, in the Municipality of Lumino, and Castione FFS station. These are the two endpoints indicated for the section concerned.

The scope of the works

The reference to via Quatorta identifies the point of the route located in Lumino. The other endpoint is Castione FFS station. The section of the former Ferrovia Retica line on which the new cycle path is planned runs between these two reference points. The communication therefore places the second phase on a precise route, with an explicitly indicated starting point and endpoint.

The project also includes two new public transport stops along via Bellinzona, on the border between the Municipalities of Lumino and Arbedo-Castione. The stops are part of the same works and add a component dedicated to buses alongside the creation of the cycle path.

Schedules and traffic management

The activities will take place Monday to Friday, between 7 and 17.30. The Department of the Territory therefore indicates both the overall period of the works, from Monday, October 12 to the end of April 2027, and the daily schedule communicated for the works.

During the activities, traffic will be regulated on site. Road users are invited to follow the construction-site signage and the instructions of the staff present, exercising the utmost caution when crossing the affected area. The communication thus sets out the schedule, location and passage arrangements for those in the work area.

Operational details

For a cross-border commuter crossing the area between Lumino and Castione on the way to work, the practical implication concerns how to get through during the construction work. The notice establishes a work window and entrusts traffic management to on-site staff: the user must therefore read the signs and follow the instructions in place.

The schedule from the commuter's perspective

The first point to bear in mind is the overall period: from the announced start date until the end of April 2027. The second is the daily window, from Monday to Friday between 7 and 17.30. Together, these two elements identify the period and times when the works are scheduled.

The source indicates that traffic will be regulated on site, but does not quantify any potential waiting time. For a commuter, therefore, the practical reference is not a preset estimate: what matters are the signs visible in the area and the directions given by the staff on site.

Key points

The notice makes it possible to prepare for the transition with a simple sequence in line with the available information. A cross-border commuter who needs to cross the area can use this operational checklist.

Five steps to follow

1. Mark the start of the works: the second phase is scheduled to begin on Monday, October 12. 2. Keep in mind the stated duration, until the end of April 2027. 3. If you cross the area from Monday to Friday between 7 and 17.30, take into account the communicated working hours. 4. As you approach the affected area, follow the construction signage and the instructions of the personnel present. 5. During the crossing, proceed with the utmost caution; along via Bellinzona, also take into account the construction of the two new public transport stops on the border between Lumino and Arbedo-Castione.

The fourth and fifth steps are the ones directly connected to the instructions addressed to users. The source does not ask you to follow a specific alternative route: it asks you to follow the signage and the personnel who will regulate traffic on site. This is the concrete procedure to apply when entering the area affected by the works.

The same sequence can be used to organize a journey toward the Castione FFS station or along the section near via Quatorta, keeping the overall duration of the works and the indicated daily time slot distinct.
