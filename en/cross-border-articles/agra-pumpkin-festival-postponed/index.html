The 43rd Pumpkin Festival in Agra will not take place on the originally scheduled date. The organizers have chosen to postpone the autumn event to Sunday, October 25, one week after the event scheduled for October 18.

The decision came after discussions among the Pro Loco, the Municipality and the other organizations involved in the festival. The assessment focused on the bad weather expected on the original date. The change therefore concerns the date of the event, moving the gathering in the village of Agra to the following Sunday.

The postponement was announced by the organizers as a shared decision among the parties involved in the initiative. The new event is scheduled for the following Sunday and moves the festival to October 25. The discussions involved the parties participating in organizing the festival and led to the designation of a new Sunday for holding the autumn event.

Operational details

The event program

The autumn event in Agra had been announced with a program centered around food, stalls, music and activities for families. The description of the festival therefore included culinary experiences and offerings aimed at families.

Among the elements listed by the organizers were pumpkin-based dishes and roasted chestnuts. These offerings were accompanied by craft stalls, market stalls and initiatives designed for children. Music completed the program announced for the autumn event.
