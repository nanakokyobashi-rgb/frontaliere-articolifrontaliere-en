Operational details

Let's analyse the practical implications for frontier workers: the New Frontier Agreement entails the exemption of €7,500 for old frontier workers and €10,000 for new frontier workers. This means that frontier workers will enjoy a more favourable tax treatment than in the past.

Tax exemption for old frontier workers

Old frontier workers, i.e. those who have already had access to the Swiss labour market before the entry into force of the New Frontier Agreement, will be able to benefit from an additional tax exemption of €7,500. For example, a border worker of Italian origin who has already worked in Switzerland for 5 years will be able to enjoy this exemption, thus reducing his tax burden.

Tax exemption for new frontier workers

New border crossers, on the other hand, will be able to benefit from an additional tax exemption of €10,000. For example, a border worker of Italian origin who is about to start working in Switzerland will be able to enjoy this exemption, thus reducing his tax burden.

Comparison with other Ticino municipalities

The New Frontier Agreement could have a different impact on border crossers moving to other municipalities in Ticino. For example, border workers who move to Bellinzona will benefit from a more favourable tax treatment than those who move to Lugano.

When did the New Italy-Switzerland Cross-Border Commuter Agreement come into force?
The New Cross-Border Commuter Agreement, signed on 23 December 2020, entered into force on 1 January 2024. From this date, the new tax provisions are fully operational, including the exemption of €7,500 for old cross-border commuters and €10,000 for new ones, modifying the previous regime for those working between the two countries.
What is the difference between 'old' and 'new' cross-border commuters for tax exemption purposes?
The 'old cross-border commuters' are those who have already had access to the Swiss labour market before 1 January 2024 and benefit from a tax exemption of €7,500. The 'new cross-border commuters', on the other hand, are those who start working in Switzerland from 1 January 2024 onwards, being able to enjoy a tax exemption of €10,000.
