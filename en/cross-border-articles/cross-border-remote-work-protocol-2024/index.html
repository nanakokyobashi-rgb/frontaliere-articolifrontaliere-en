On November 28, 2023, from Rome, the Ministry of Economy and Finance communicated that Italy and Switzerland had signed at technical level a Protocol aimed at amending the Frontier Workers Agreement concluded between the two countries on December 23, 2020. The stated objective was to regulate the treatment of teleworking.

The amendment to the Frontier Workers Agreement

The Protocol formalizes solely the additions and amendments provided for in the Declaration of Intent of November 10, signed by Minister Giancarlo Giorgetti and Federal Councillor Karin Keller-Sutter. Teleworking may be performed up to a maximum of 25% of working hours.

The signing of the Agreement was expected by May 31, 2024, while the rules would have started to apply as early as January 1, 2024 thanks to a transitional mutual agreement.

The news should be read as a communication from November 2023: the source describes the technical understanding, the planned effective date and the deadline for signing, without documenting subsequent developments.
