Stefania Filetti elected to the national secretariat of CGIL (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 17 September 2026

The General Assembly of the CGIL meeting in Rome elected Stefania Filetti by secret ballot in the new confederal secretariat composed of ten people.

What: Election to the national secretariat of Cgil Who: Stefania Filetti Where: Rome Organization: Cgil Previous role: General Secretary of Cgil Varese

The general assembly of Cgil, meeting in Rome, elected Stefania Filetti by secret ballot to the organization's new confederal secretariat. The nomination proposal came directly from General Secretary Maurizio Landini, placing Filetti within a renewed team of ten people, evenly divided between five women and five men. This election represents the highest point of a long trade union career that has its roots in Italian industrial departments and has developed through a rigorous path, step by step, without sudden leaps or external accelerations compared to daily groundwork in workplaces.

From the blue collar to the national secretariat

Stefania Filetti's trade union history began in the late 1980s at the Alfa Romeo plant in Arese, a large factory she entered with a work-training contract. This was one of the last generations of workers hired after the factory's transfer to Fiat. Assigned to the mechanics department, Filetti began experiencing the reality of industrial production and the factory every day. It was precisely within that department, in the early 1990s, that she took the first step in her representation activity, becoming a Fiom delegate. This was not an arrival from the outside or from bureaucratic structures, but an experience born directly from the production line and daily contact with the working conditions of her colleagues. Her commitment was consolidated in 1992 with her first official steps as a trade union delegate, laying the groundwork for a gradual transition to roles of greater responsibility within the structure of Fiom Milan, in the historic Sempione area, which took place in 1998 when she became a trade union official.

Operational details

Territorial roots in the Varese area

Stefania Filetti's journey continues and takes deep roots in the provincial territory starting from 2005, the year she joined the Fiom of Varese. This stage marks a further deepening of the dynamics related to metalworking and local industrial disputes. Two years after her arrival in the Varese structure, she was elected to the metalworkers' secretariat, and then in 2011 she took on the role of general secretary of the category. Here too, her growth took place entirely within the blue-collar category from which her professional and trade union experience had started. Subsequently, her scope of action expanded from the category dimension to the general confederal dimension of the Cgil of Varese. In 2013 she joined the provincial secretariat and in 2018 she assumed the position of organizational secretary, a managerial and political role that allowed her to get to know in detail the complex territorial organization of the organization, including the Chambers of Labour, the trade union offices and the Spi locations.

June 2020 marks another fundamental step with her election as general secretary of the Cgil of Varese, succeeding Umberto Colombo. During the years spent at the head of the Varese Chamber of Labour, Filetti managed a particularly complex period characterized by industrial crises, open trade union disputes, the constant defense of employment and wages, as well as issues related to precariousness and safety in the workplace. In recent years, she has directly addressed the mobilizations promoted by Cgil on referendums concerning work and citizenship, the defense of public healthcare and the protection of workers in contracting out. The approach given to her work finds synthesis in a reflection expressed precisely in 2020: when an idea and a thought become a collective condition shared by the majority, the growth of the common line transforms into authentic trade union militancy. An approach that ideally unites her origins in the factory with the top confederal responsibilities assumed today at the national level.

Key points

A path without shortcuts

Stefania Filetti's entry into the national secretariat of Cgil, together with the other two new members Michele De Palma, general secretary of Fiom, and Serena Sorrentino, president of the Fundamental Program Commission, ideally concludes a geographical and professional journey that touched Arese, Milan, Varese and finally Rome. The uniqueness of this career lies in the absence of shortcuts or level jumps: every role was preceded by hands-on experience in the field, moving from factory worker to union delegate, from official to secretariat member, until holding the position of general secretary of the provincial Fiom first and of the Varese Cgil later. This trajectory demonstrates how the construction of top-tier trade union leadership can still be based on the direct and daily knowledge of workplaces, bringing a wealth of concrete experiences gained since the days of the Alfa Romeo mechanical department to the highest decision-making levels of the organization.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who was elected to the national secretariat of CGIL?
Stefania Filetti was elected to the new confederal secretariat of CGIL by the general assembly meeting in Rome, following the proposal of General Secretary Maurizio Landini. The previous position held by Stefania Filetti was that of Secretary General of the CGIL of Varese.
How is the new CGIL confederal secretariat composed?
The new renewed team of the CGIL confederal secretariat is composed of ten members, equally divided between five women and five men. Together with Stefania Filetti, the new entrants include Michele De Palma, Secretary General of Fiom, and Serena Sorrentino, President of the Fundamental Programme Commission.
What were the main milestones in Stefania Filetti's trade union career?
Stefania Filetti's trade union career began at the end of the 1980s at the Alfa Romeo plant in Arese, where she entered with a job training contract in the mechanical department. At the beginning of the nineties she became a delegate of Fiom, and then became a trade union official in 1998 in Milan. In 2005 she joined the Fiom of Varese, becoming general secretary of the category in 2011 and subsequently general secretary of the CGIL of Varese in June 2020.
