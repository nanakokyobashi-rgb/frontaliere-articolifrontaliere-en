The municipal administration of Casciago has started a phase of structural interventions at La Pinetina Park, a green area frequented by different age groups. These days, activities are focusing on the completion of the new perimeter fence, as well as a series of general cleaning operations and securing the entire space. These works, promoted directly by the municipality, aim to transform the park into a more livable and peaceful place. Mayor Mirko Reto underlined, through institutional social channels, the desire to ensure an adequate environment for both children and adults, fostering social cohesion within the country. The management of the construction site follows a planning that requires, as expressed by the first citizen, the collaboration of citizens for temporary hardships.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the main objectives of the works at La Pinetina Park?
The main objective of the Casciago administration is to ensure a safer and more welcoming space for all age groups, from children to adults. The intervention focuses on the new fence and securing the perimeter, also improving roadability to prevent balls from ending up on the street during games in the 3-on-3 basketball court.
Who is managing the redevelopment of the park?
The redevelopment of the La Pinetina Park is taken care of directly by the municipal administration of Casciago, under the guidance of the mayor Mirko Reto, who coordinates the cleaning and safety interventions.
Why is the new fence considered important?
The fence is essential to improve the safety of the surrounding roads. By preventing the balls from going out on the road during sports activities, the risks to passers-by and vehicles are reduced, making the park a more livable and peaceful place for the entire community.
