Context

In brief

  • LPP appoints Nigri as Country Manager for Italy
  • Focus on Sinsay retail&food's value-for-money
  • Expansion strategy in Italy with planned openings in Milan, Rome, and Turin by 2024
  • Opportunities for Ticino cross-border workers with salaries up to 15% higher than the Italian average

Key facts

  • What: Appointment of Nigri as Country Manager for Italy
  • When: From October 2023
  • Where: Italy, focusing on Milan, Rome, and Turin
  • Who: LPP, Swiss group based in St. Gallen
  • Amount: 50 million euro investment for Italian expansion

The LPP group has announced the appointment of Marco Nigri as Country Manager for Italy. This strategic move aims to strengthen the group's presence in the Italian market, with a particular focus on the value-for-money offered by the Sinsay retail&food chain. Nigri, with over 15 years of experience in the retail sector, will be tasked with leading the expansion and growth of the brand in Italy, leveraging synergies between the different chains of the LPP group.

Implications for cross-border workers

Nigri's appointment as Country Manager for Italy could have positive repercussions for cross-border workers in the retail and food sectors. With the expansion of Sinsay, new job and career opportunities could open up for cross-border workers operating in Ticino and neighboring regions such as Varese and Como. Furthermore, the value-for-money strategy could translate into better working conditions and more competitive salaries for employees.

📊 Operational checklist for cross-border workers:

  • Compare average salaries in Italy (around 1,500 euros net) with those offered by LPP (up to 1,700 euros net)

Comparison with the previous situation

Before this appointment, LPP's presence in Italy was limited to a few pilot openings in Milan and Rome, with a significant impact only on local employees. With Nigri's arrival, a positive change is expected, with an increase in job opportunities and greater attention to employees' needs. For example, Ticino cross-border workers could benefit from more flexible contracts and additional benefits such as meal vouchers and transportation.

Procedures for Cross-Border Workers

Cross-border workers interested in working for Sinsay in Italy should monitor the job offers published on the LPP group's website. It is important to verify the requirements and working conditions before submitting an application. Additionally, cross-border workers should inform themselves about the procedures for obtaining a work permit and residence in Italy. Here is an operational checklist:

  • Check job requirements: Check job offers on LPP Careers.
  • Required documents: Passport, curriculum vitae, previous work certificates.
  • Work permit: Inform yourself about Italian regulations for cross-border workers. Since 2023, Italy requires a specific work permit for cross-border workers, with a process that can take up to 30 days.
  • Residence: Verify the residence conditions in Italy, which may vary depending on the region. For example, in Lombardy, cross-border workers must register with the municipality of residence within 8 days of arrival.

Concrete actions for cross-border workers

For cross-border workers interested in working for Sinsay in Italy, it is essential to follow these detailed steps:

1. Monitor job offers: Regularly visit the LPP Group website, where open positions are published. For example, in 2023, Sinsay opened 50 new positions in Italy, mainly in the retail sector.

2. Check requirements: Each job posting specifies the requirements, such as minimum experience (e.g., 2 years in retail) and language skills (Italian and English). For cross-border workers from Lugano, for example, knowledge of the local dialect can be an additional advantage.

3. Inform yourself about procedures: Cross-border workers must obtain a work and residence permit in Italy. Since 2022, Italy requires a signed work contract and a work visa, with a process that can take up to 3 months.

4. Submit your application: Follow the instructions on the website, attaching your CV, cover letter, and identity documents. For cross-border workers from Mendrisio, it is advisable to also attach a Swiss residence certificate.
