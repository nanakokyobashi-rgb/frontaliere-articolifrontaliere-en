Frequently Asked Questions
What are the areas of Gallarate reported by FediCons?
FediCons reports concern several areas of the historic centre, including Via Cavour, Via Milano, Piazza San Lorenzo, Piazza Risorgimento, Piazza Libertà, the pedestrian area, Via San Giovanni Bosco, Via Don Minzoni and Via Carlo Noè, as well as the 24-hour supermarket area.
What is FediCons' proposal to improve safety?
FediCons proposes an increase in the staff of the local police with the aim of achieving the ratio of one officer per thousand inhabitants, as well as greater supervision of the territory and an improvement in public lighting to make the areas of the centre more welcoming and safe.
What is FediCons' position towards young people and foreigners?
The association specifies that it does not want to criminalize foreigners or young people as such, but to counteract specific behaviors, such as those attributed to groups called 'maranza', which make some public places unlivable and create tensions or situations of degradation.
