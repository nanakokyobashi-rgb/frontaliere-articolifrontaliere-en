Concerts in Switzerland: prices fly in the summer of 2026 (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 14 July 2026

Outdoor festivals are becoming more expensive: prices rise by more than 50% in ten years. Cost analysis and impact on fan pockets.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Festival prices have increased by an average of 41% over the last ten years.
  • Some passes have undergone increases of more than 50% compared to 2016.
  • The increases far exceed the official inflation rate of 7.7%.
  • Costs now include services such as camping, transportation, and climate contributions.

Key facts

  • What: Ticket price for Swiss outdoor festivals
  • When: Analysis based on the last decade (2015-2026)
  • Where: Festivals throughout Switzerland, including St. Gallen, Gampel and Nyon
  • Who: AWP Press Agency and Swiss Music Promoters Association (SMPA)
  • Amount: Average increase of 41% on the categories of tickets analysed

The summer concert season in Switzerland opens under the sign of a significant upward price revision. An in-depth analysis conducted by the AWP news agency has highlighted how access to live music events has become considerably more expensive for the public than it was ten years ago. The phenomenon is not confined to a single region, but reflects a national trend that has seen increases far exceed the overall inflation rate, recorded at 7.7% since March 2015.

Operational details

The analysis of operating costs reveals the motivations behind this tariff escalation. Organizers point the finger at the increase in artist fees, which had to compensate for the collapse of revenue from the sale of traditional records, favoring a strategy based almost exclusively on live performances. In addition to this, there are expenses for technical production, logistical infrastructure, energy, and increasingly stringent safety regulations. The management of a modern festival requires a greater expenditure for personnel and external services.

Key points

To tackle the high prices of festivals, it is essential to have a well-planned approach. The tariff transparency offered by organizers allows us to understand today what is included in the price, such as public transportation, camping, or climate contributions, elements that in the past could be managed separately. For those who work as frontiersmen, managing the budget requires special attention to net income, often influenced by fiscal and contributory deductions, such as AVS or LPP, which affect disposable income. Knowing exactly how much is left in the paycheck is the first step to deciding which musical events to include in your summer calendar.

Frequently Asked Questions
Why have festival prices increased so much?
The increase is due to a set of factors: growth in artists' cachets, higher production costs (technical, logistics, energy) and higher security and personnel expenses. In addition, the public today requires a much higher level of comfort and services (hygiene, catering, sustainability) than in the past, transforming the event into a complete experience.
What amenities are usually included in the ticket price?
Many festivals, such as the Openair in St. Gallen, include in the cost of the ticket additional services such as access to the campsite, the use of public transport to reach the event site and a specific contribution to environmental sustainability (contribution to climate).
Is the rise in ticket prices in line with inflation?
No. The AWP analysis finds an average increase of 41% on all the categories of tickets examined, a figure that well exceeds the general Swiss inflation, which since March 2015 stands at 7.7%.
