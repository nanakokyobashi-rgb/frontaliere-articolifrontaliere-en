How much does a border security agent earn in Ticino?

The income of a border security agent in Ticino can vary greatly based on job position and experience. According to the estimates of the Federal Office of Fiscal Affairs (UQF), a border security agent with 2-3 years' experience can earn around CHF 50,000 per year. However, with experience and promotion, income can increase up to CHF 80'000 per year.

Concrete example: a border security agent with 5 years of experience at Chiasso border control can earn around CHF 65'000 per year, based on 2022 data.

Taxes and conventions

Border workers are subject to a tax at source in Switzerland, but not in Italy, thanks to the double taxation convention. This convention, signed in 1974, provides that the incomes of border workers are taxed only in the country of residence. In this case, Switzerland applies a tax rate of 20% on the income of frontier workers, while Italy does not apply any tax.

# Factors Affecting Income

The job position and experience are the main factors that influence the income of a border security agent in Ticino. For example, a border security agent with work experience of at least 5 years and a working position as section chief can earn between CHF 6,500 and CHF8,000 per month, while a border security agent with work experience of at least 3 years and a working position as a border security agent can earn between CHF 5,000 and CHF6,500 per month.

Expenses to consider

Border workers must pay AVS (social insurance) and LPP (occupational insurance), but not LAMal (health insurance), as they do not reside in Switzerland. The AVS is a social insurance that covers the expenses of health care, social assistance and pension, while the LPP is a social security insurance that covers the expenses of pension and health care. The amount of expenses to be paid for the AVS and the LPP may vary depending on the job position and the experience gained.
