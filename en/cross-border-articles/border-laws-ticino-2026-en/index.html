Introduction to new laws

As of 1 January 2026, a series of new legislative regulations specifically intended to protect frontier workers in the Canton of Ticino came into force. These laws, which involve both the Canton of Ticino and the whole of Switzerland, aim to improve the safety and well-being of workers who cross the border on a daily basis for work.

The main actions assumed by governments are as follows:

  • Clear definition of requirements for pension and health insurance.
  • Regulations on recruitment rights and working conditions of female border workers.

Operational details

New border laws in Ticino: everything you need to know

With the entry into force of the new regulations, frontier workers in Ticino will see a number of significant changes in their working conditions and associated rights. This analysis focuses on the practical aspects of the new laws.

Right to health care

Frontier workers will now have access to a broader medical care service that includes basic coverage and additional options. 📊

For example, the new law provides that border workers subscribed to the Ticino health system can benefit from basic coverage that includes routine medical examinations, laboratory tests and specialist consultations. 💡

The basic coverage is valid for a period of 12 months, with the possibility of extension. 📅

Frontier pension

New requirements for retirement will be introduced, ensuring balanced conditions between contributions and repayment at the time of retirement. 📊

Concrete examples include the possibility of accumulating pension contributions even during periods of work in Switzerland, with a minimum of 5 years of contributions needed to obtain a pension. 💡

For frontier workers working in Ticino municipalities such as Lugano, Bellinzona or Locarno, this new legislation represents a significant improvement. 📅

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the new requirements for retirement?
Contributory obligations have increased and the conditions for access to the pension benefit have strengthened. It is advisable to consult the official CAFT website for up-to-date details.
How do I access health care?
Border workers have access to a basic medical care package and can opt for additional options based on their needs. Detailed documentation is available on the CAF website.
Where can I obtain more information?
For all up-to-date information, please visit the official cantonal laws website [website] or contact the Border Assistance Center (CAF) directly.
