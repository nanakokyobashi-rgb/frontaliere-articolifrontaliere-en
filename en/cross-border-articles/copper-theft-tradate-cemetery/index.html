The inspection in Tradate

The new incident affected Tradate cemetery, overlooking the Varesina. The theft left part of the roof exposed, resulting in damage and water infiltration. The affected section is the one facing the market square, where the inspection established that several copper sheets had been removed.

Some citizens reported the situation after noticing water in the first-floor corridor, which had become slippery and potentially dangerous. The subsequent inspection established that the sheets had been removed and identified the affected part of the roof.

The reports thus led to the inspection: first, water was noticed in the passageway on the first floor, then several missing copper sheets were identified. The situation described therefore includes both the theft of the metal and the consequences for the roof and the indoor areas reached by the infiltrations.
