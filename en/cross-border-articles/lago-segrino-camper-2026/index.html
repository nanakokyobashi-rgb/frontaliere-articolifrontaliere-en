Lake Segrino: No to 105 camper pitches, asphalt area

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 2 May 2026

The 'Ilaria Alpi' Environment Circle opposes the project of 105 camper pitches in Canzo, near Lake Segrino, a community site of importance.

Context

In brief

  • Project for 105 camper pitches in Canzo near Lake Segrino
  • Circolo Ambiente 'Ilaria Alpi' criticizes the choice to asphalt the area
  • Water and sewer management issues highlighted

Key facts

  • What: Project for 105 camper pitches
  • When: 02/05/2026
  • Where: Canzo, Lake Segrino, Como province
  • Who: Circolo Ambiente 'Ilaria Alpi'
  • Amount: 105 camper pitches

Lake Segrino, a natural gem in Como province, is at the center of a new controversy. The project to create a camper area with 105 pitches in Canzo has sparked protests from Circolo Ambiente 'Ilaria Alpi'. According to the group, the choice to asphalt a large area near the lake is unacceptable, especially considering that the zone is a Site of Community Importance (SCI) and should be preserved. The group's alternative proposal is to restore the area to green space, creating an ecological connection with Parco Lago del Segrino.

Water management issues

Circolo Ambiente 'Ilaria Alpi' has also highlighted the problems of water and sewer management. According to the environmental opinion of Parco Lago Segrino, Canzo's sewers are still 66% of the mixed type, which could cause problems with the increase in clear water due to the impermeabilization of the area. Furthermore, the Parco's opinion highlights the need to upgrade the water and sewer networks, avoiding the contribution of meteorological waters in the mixed and black sewer network.

Criticisms of the project

Roberto Fumagalli, president of Circolo Ambiente 'Ilaria Alpi', stated: 'We are against the spread of asphalt in an area that should be preserved from a naturalistic point of view. Our proposal is to create a green and wooded area, to maintain a more natural environment, given the proximity to the beautiful lake.'

Operational details

  • ### Implications for the Environment and the Community

The project to create a camper area with 105 pitches in Canzo could have serious implications for the local environment and community. The decision to asphalt a large area near Lake Segrino, a site of community importance, has been criticized by the 'Ilaria Alpi' Environment Circle. According to the circle, the asphalt could damage the local ecosystem and compromise the water quality of the lake.

Water Management Issues

One of the main problems highlighted by the 'Ilaria Alpi' Environment Circle concerns the management of water and sewage. Canzo's sewage system is still 66% of the mixed type, which could cause problems with the increase in clear water due to the impermeabilization of the area. Furthermore, the environmental opinion of the Parco Lago Segrino highlights the need to upgrade the water and sewer networks, avoiding the contribution of meteorological waters in the mixed and black sewer network.

Alternative Proposed by the Circle

The 'Ilaria Alpi' Environment Circle has proposed to restore the area to green, creating an ecological connection with the Parco del Lago del Segrino. This solution would allow the preservation of the natural environment and improve the quality of life for the local community. Furthermore, the creation of a green and wooded area could attract more tourists, contributing to the economic development of the area.
