Key facts

  • What: Quarterly results and bank integration
  • When: July 2026 (reference to 3 years after March 2023)
  • Where: Zurich
  • Who: Sergio Ermotti, CEO of UBS
  • Status: Integration in advanced stage

The integration of Credit Suisse required constant commitment and patience on the part of the shareholders, but what has been achieved so far has resulted in tangible and lasting results for the institution. A little more than three years after the announcement of the takeover of the competing bank, which took place in March 2023, UBS CEO Sergio Ermotti commented on the progress of the process in a statement attached to the quarterly results published early in the morning in Zurich.

The executive, who led this complex transition phase, stressed that the acquisition was not a gift received, but a milestone achieved through rigorous commitment and difficult decisions. The path, as expected, was not linear. The determination of personnel and the management of operational challenges have been fundamental to transform the current banking structure. Frequently Asked Questions
What does the integration between UBS and Credit Suisse mean for clients?
Integration required considerable effort but, according to CEO Sergio Ermotti, is leading to tangible results. For clients, this means being able to rely on an institution with a more resilient balance sheet, solid capital generation and a strategy focused on customer proximity and rigorous risk management, ensuring greater long-term stability.
What are UBS's current objectives after the acquisition?
UBS focuses today on completing integration, implementing growth plans and exceeding performance targets. The group aims to remain a reliable partner for the communities in which it operates, employing financial resources in profitable opportunities and maintaining a commitment to returning capital to shareholders.
How is the integration path described by the CEO?
CEO Sergio Ermotti defined the path as non-linear, stressing that it required very hard work on the part of employees and difficult decisions. He also reiterated that the acquisition of Credit Suisse was not a gift, but a prize won with commitment and patience from shareholders.
