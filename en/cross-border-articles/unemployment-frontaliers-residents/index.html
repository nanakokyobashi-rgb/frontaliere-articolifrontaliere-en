Unemployment in Ticino now follows the same rules for everyone, both residents and cross-border workers. This change represents a significant step towards equality of rights and obligations between workers residing in Switzerland and those crossing the border daily.

What changes for cross-border workers

Cross-border workers employed in Ticino and residing in Italy now have the same rights and obligations regarding unemployment as residents. This means they can access the same benefits and must meet the same conditions to receive unemployment benefits. The new regulations aim to simplify procedures and ensure greater equity among workers.

Procedure for unemployment benefits claim

For cross-border workers, the procedure to claim unemployment benefits is now identical to that of residents. They must submit their application to the competent office of the Canton Ticino, providing all required documentation. Necessary documents include the unemployment certificate issued by the employer, the residence certificate, and the work permit.

Rights and obligations

With the new rules, cross-border workers have the same rights as residents in terms of benefit duration and amount. However, they must also comply with the same obligations, such as being available to actively seek new employment and participating in any training or professional retraining programs.

Key points

Concrete actions for cross-border workers

With the new unemployment regulations in Ticino, cross-border workers must follow specific procedures to request and receive unemployment benefits. Here is a step-by-step guide to navigate the new system.

Step 1: Verify the requirements

Before submitting the application, it is important to verify that all legal requirements are met. The main requirements include:

  • Having worked for a minimum period of 12 months in the last 24 months
  • Being unemployed and available to seek new employment
  • Being registered in the unemployment insurance system

Step 2: Gather the documentation

To submit the application, it is necessary to gather all the required documentation. The necessary documents include:

  • Unemployment certificate issued by the employer
  • Residence certificate
  • Work permit
  • Identity document

Step 3: Submit the application

The unemployment application must be submitted to the competent office of the Canton Ticino. It is possible to submit the application in person or by mail. Once the application is submitted, you need to wait for the response from the competent authorities.

Step 4: Receive the benefits

Once the application is approved, the unemployment benefits will be paid according to the modalities provided by law. It is important to respect all the obligations provided, such as the availability to seek new employment and participation in any training or professional requalification programs.

Step 5: Monitor deadlines

It is important to monitor the deadlines provided by law to avoid losing acquired rights. The main deadlines include:

  • Submission of the application within 30 days of the date of unemployment
  • Monthly communication of job search
  • Participation in any training or professional requalification programs
