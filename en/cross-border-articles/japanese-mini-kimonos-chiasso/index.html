Threads of the East: mini kimonos on display in Chiasso

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 October 2026

In Chiasso, from October 8 to 22, the exhibition Threads of the East by SAMS presents ten mini kimonos made from vintage silks, at the intersection of Japanese culture and local tailoring.

In Chiasso, from October 8 to 22, «Trame d'Oriente» will be on display, the collection created through the project of the School of Arts and Crafts of Tailoring (SAMS) of Biasca. The exhibition will feature ten mini kimonos made from vintage silk sourced from authentic Japanese kimonos.

The exhibition follows presentations at Castelgrande, in Bellinzona, and at the Venice Biennale. The project was proposed under the patronage of the Municipality of Chiasso and brings sartorial tradition into dialogue with Japanese culture and the reuse of period fabrics.

Ten unique pieces and research into textiles

The collection consists of ten one-of-a-kind pieces, whose patterns, colors and symbolism are inspired by Japanese tradition. Vintage silks were selected to create them, then reinterpreted through new shapes, folds and tailoring details. The vintage material thus becomes the starting point for a new formal construction, without losing its connection to Japanese culture.

The work on the mini kimonos also gave the female students of SAMS of Biasca the opportunity to continue their research with other creations inspired by Japanese aesthetics. The experimentation focused on techniques, shapes, volumes and different tailoring solutions. The source thus presents the exhibition as the result of a clearly defined collection and, at the same time, of a broader research process.

Opening and public access

The vernissage is scheduled for Thursday, October 8 at 18 in Sala Diego Chiesa in Chiasso. Registration is required to attend the opening. The exhibition will remain open to the public until October 22. The source specifies the date and time of the vernissage and the closing date of the exhibition, but does not provide any other opening hours or registration procedures.
