Operational details

Environmental Impact and Innovation

Colacem achieved significant environmental progress in 2025, with a total investment of 46 million euros in the three-year period 2023-2025. This commitment translates into a reduction in the clinker/cement ratio, which fell to 0.71, and an increase in the use of recycled materials, which reached 8.2%. In addition, energy recovery through alternative fuels reached 23%, contributing to the company's environmental sustainability.

Social and Cultural Projects

In addition to environmental investments, Colacem has also dedicated significant resources to social and cultural projects. Initiatives such as "Material & Imaginary" and "Library in the Factory" demonstrate the company's commitment to promoting training, safety and dialogue with local communities. These projects involve students, schools, associations and institutions, contributing to shared cultural and technical growth.

Future Challenges and Decarbonization Goals

Looking ahead, Colacem is committed to continuing to invest in emission reduction and process innovation, in line with European decarbonisation targets. The company also requires truly sustainable regulatory and economic instruments to accompany this transition, combining environmental ambition, industrial competitiveness and protection of the European production system.
