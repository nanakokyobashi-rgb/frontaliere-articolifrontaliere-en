In brief

  • Thun, Swiss champion for the first time in 128 years
  • Ticinese coach Mauro Lustrinelli among the architects of success
  • Mathematical victory achieved with Sion's win over St. Gallen

Key facts

  • What: Victory in the Swiss football championship
  • When: 3 May 2026
  • Where: Stockhorn Arena, Thun
  • Who: Thun team, coach Mauro Lustrinelli
  • Amount: First victory in 128 years of history

Last season, Thun was still in Serie B. Today, they are the Swiss football champions. This is an extraordinary rise for Thun, which after 128 years of existence has won its first championship in history, a competition it has almost consistently dominated. The victory was in the air for weeks, but when mathematical certainty was achieved today, the joy of the fans exploded. A victory that also contains a bit of Ticino. Indeed, the coach Mauro Lustrinelli is Ticinese, one of the architects of this incredible success.

A historic success

The victory of Thun represents a historic milestone not only for the team but also for the entire Bernese Oberland region. The team, which only last season played in Serie B, has made a meteoric rise that has surprised experts and delighted fans. Joy exploded when Sion beat St. Gallen with a clear 3-0, mathematically delivering the title to Thun.

The role of Ticino

The presence of Ticinese coach Mauro Lustrinelli is a significant element of this victory. Lustrinelli, with his experience and competence, played a crucial role in guiding the team to this historic milestone. His leadership and ability to motivate players were fundamental to Thun's success.
