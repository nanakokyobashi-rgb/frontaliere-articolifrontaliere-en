The Department of the Territory (DT) and the Municipality of Biasca have announced the start of the last phase of work for the arrangement of the Biasca interchange node, located in the SBB station area. The road maintenance and redevelopment operations will enter the last phase starting on Monday, August 31, 2026, with a conclusion scheduled for September 12, 2026.

Operational planning details

The work schedule is divided into three main moments. From Monday 17 August 2026 to Friday 4 September 2026, the activities will take place during the day, specifically from 07.00 to 17.30. During the first two weeks of this period, the workers will proceed to lay the wear layer on the yards in front of the SBB station. Although the cantonal road section remains regularly open to traffic during the day, the road closures on Via Bellinzona between 17 August and 4 September 2026 can generate queues and slowdowns.

The risk of congestion is particularly high during the morning and late afternoon rush hours, when the flow of vehicles to Bellinzona or border crossings is at its peak. Frequently Asked Questions
When do the works at the Biasca node end?
The works for the arrangement of the Biasca interchange node will officially end on 12 September 2026, after the last phase of laying the sound-absorbing wear layer and the interventions on the roundabouts of Via Gen. Guisan and Via A. Giovannini.
Will traffic be blocked during the day?
No, during the day the section of cantonal road in question will remain regularly open to traffic. However, between 17 August and 4 September 2026, there may be occasional narrowing of the carriageway on Via Bellinzona.
Is access to public transport and relief guaranteed on average?
Yes, for the entire duration of the works, the authorities have confirmed that access to the public transport stops by road and to the area in front of the SBB station will always be guaranteed, as well as the transit of rescue vehicles.
