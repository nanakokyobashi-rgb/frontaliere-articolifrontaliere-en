The fifth Pumpkin and Chestnut Festival takes place from Friday, October 23rd to Sunday, October 25th at the Baita del Fondista in Cunardo and is organized by the Zamaroni-Martinoli Preschool in Bedero Valcuvia. For the school, the initiative serves a dual purpose: it is a fundamental tool for self-financing and a moment of conviviality.

School at the Center

Today, 22 children from Bedero Valcuvia and neighboring municipalities are welcomed by the school; the festival contributes to its self-financing.

The cuisine combines quality, local flavors, and traditions with an openness to innovation. It is run by volunteers who have gained experience over the years at the various festivals hosted at the Baita.

This year introduces a Friday evening dinner alongside the usual weekend events: dinner on Saturday and lunch and dinner on Sunday. Starting at 9:00 PM on Saturday, musical entertainment will be provided by a band led by the preschool's music teacher. The flyer includes the complete program and menu; the festival will be held regardless of weather conditions, in a covered and heated space.
