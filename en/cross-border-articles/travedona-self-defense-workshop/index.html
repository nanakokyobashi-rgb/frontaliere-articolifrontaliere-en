The program

On Saturday 10 October, Travedona Monate will host a self-defense workshop organized by Accademia dello Sport Varesina. The event will take place in the gym of the municipal middle schools, from 9:00 to 13:30. Participation is completely free and the initiative is open to participants aged 11 and over.

The session will be led by instructor Stefano Gallucci. The program includes the practical aspects of self-defense, but does not stop at techniques: the morning will also address prevention, awareness and the management of risky situations. These are the elements identified to complement the practical component.

The initiative therefore combines practice with attention to safety. The proposal considers both how to deal with a risky situation and the ability to prevent and manage it. Self-defense is presented alongside content on awareness, without limiting the workshop to the practical session alone.

The indicated age range, from 11 years old, defines the initiative’s access to the public. The gym of the municipal middle schools is where the workshop and the activities planned during the morning will take place. The program therefore unfolds between 9:00 and 13:30.

The event’s initial outline thus consists of a specific venue, a morning time slot, Gallucci’s guidance and a free format for anyone aged at least 11. Prevention, awareness and risk management are added to these details, completing the presentation of the workshop.

Operational details

Bullying and protection of people

The workshop's specific focus will be bullying. The in-depth discussion is aimed at both young people and adults and seeks to provide useful tools for recognizing problematic situations, preventing them, and addressing them correctly and consciously.

The topic is therefore addressed through three connected steps: recognizing the problem, preventing it, and managing it. The source does not limit the focus to a single age group: the reference includes young people and adults, with tools designed to interpret problematic situations and address them in the way indicated by the program. The reference to young people and adults goes hand in hand with the goal of addressing the topic correctly and consciously.

During the morning, the involvement of Sos dei Laghi di Travedona is planned, together with a contribution from the Squadra mobile di Varese. The two presences will help enrich the workshop with in-depth sessions dedicated to safety, prevention, and the protection of people.

The program therefore includes two in-depth sessions connected to the presence of Sos dei Laghi and the Squadra mobile di Varese. Their contribution complements the practical part of self-defense and broadens the morning's content with the themes of safety, prevention, and protection.

Bullying is thus incorporated into a program that includes practice, awareness, and prevention. The activity does not present self-defense as its sole content: the program supplements risk management with tools for correctly recognizing and addressing problematic situations.
