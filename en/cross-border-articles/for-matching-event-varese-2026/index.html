On Thursday, October 22, 2026, at the Ville Ponti Congress Centre in Varese, the Province of Varese and the Varese Chamber of Commerce will present the new Report on the labor market in the province of Varese. The meeting will examine how data can help interpret the labor market and address corporate crises.

The announced schedule runs from 9.00 to 12.30. Subtracting the start time from the end time, the planned overall duration is three hours and thirty minutes. The morning is therefore organized around two stages: the presentation of the document and the subsequent discussion among representatives from different fields.

The document at the center of the morning

The report, entitled «For Matching: interpreting the data to manage crises and foster employment recovery», was created through collaboration between the two promoting bodies and falls within territorial policies devoted to action on industrial disputes and economic recovery.

The report aims to provide practical support for monitoring changes in the production system, identifying difficulties in advance, and guiding public and private strategies. The meeting aims to turn data analysis into concrete measures in support of employment and the competitiveness of the Varese area.

Once the data have been presented, the program provides for a roundtable with representatives from the institutional, economic, academic and trade union sectors. The meeting is intended for institutions, businesses, social partners and industry experts, who will discuss how to interpret the labor market and possible courses of action for the local production sector.
