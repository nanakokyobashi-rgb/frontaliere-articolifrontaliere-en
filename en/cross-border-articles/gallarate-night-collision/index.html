The accident on via Stelvio

Shortly after 4, on via Stelvio in Gallarate, a car collision required emergency responders to assist three young people. Ambulances rushed to youths aged 18, 20 and 24, while the response had been classified as a red code.

Subsequently, the youths appeared to be in less serious condition and the code was downgraded to yellow. Two of the three were taken to the hospitals in Legnano and Gallarate. The source names the two facilities, but does not associate each young person with a specific hospital.

The incident took place at night and the location given is via Stelvio. As for how it happened, the report mentions a car collision and adds no other details. It does, however, specify the approximate time, shortly after 4, the ages of the three people involved, the sequence of the emergency code, and the transfer of two people.

The report therefore describes a response that began as a red code and was then downgraded to yellow. The update on their condition indicates a situation that appeared less serious, but does not change the essential facts: three youths involved and two hospital transports. The article provides no further individual details about the youths.
