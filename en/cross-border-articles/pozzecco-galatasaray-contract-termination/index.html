Gianmarco Pozzecco and Galatasaray are close to an early termination of the contract. The turning point comes after the recent home defeat to Bursaspor, which came at the end of a league start marked by three consecutive losses.

Rumors coming from Turkey, confirmed by sources close to the Istanbul club, indicate that the negative streak has prompted the management to move toward ending its relationship with the coach. The defeat to Bursaspor is thus the latest episode in a start judged to be negative.

The agreement signed by Pozzecco was valid until the end of the 2026/27 season. The coach arrived in Turkey at the end of 2025 and took operational control of the team the following January. The separation therefore looks set to come before the expiry date stipulated in the agreement.

The journey that began in Varese

The news is of particular significance to the Varese public. Born in Gorizia in 1972, Pozzecco tied his name to Pallacanestro Varese, where he was a historic floor general and symbol from 1994 to 2002.

In 1999, he was a leading figure in the Star's run, which culminated in the Roosters Varese championship title led by Charlie Recalcati and the subsequent Italian Super Cup. Flair, talent and charisma made him a central figure in the city's basketball scene.

After ending his playing career, Pozzecco began his coaching career: he returned to the Varese bench in the 2014/2015 season, then coached Dinamo Sassari, served as an assistant in Milan and held the position of head coach of the Italian national team from 2022 to 2025.
