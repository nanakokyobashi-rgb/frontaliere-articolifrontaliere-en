Frequently Asked Questions
After how long can a person with status S receive a B permit?
After at least five years of continuous residence in Switzerland, the person is entitled to a B permit linked to status S. The cantonal migration authority checks the requirements and issues the document; it is granted for one year at a time and remains linked to temporary protection.
How long will protection status S be maintained for people from Ukraine?
On 19 August 2026 the Federal Council decided to extend status S and Programme S until 4 March 2028, unless there is a fundamental change in the situation. This date does not make the linked B permit permanent.
Is there one deadline or fixed fee for a B-permit application in Ticino?
The federal and cantonal sources consulted do not state one universal deadline or fixed fee for every case. The documents and procedure must be checked with the competent cantonal migration authority.
