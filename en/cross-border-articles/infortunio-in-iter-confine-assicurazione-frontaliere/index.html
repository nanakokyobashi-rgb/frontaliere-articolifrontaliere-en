The law and regulations

The law governing accident coverage for border workers is the Federal Act on Accident Insurance (LAINF), passed on 20 June 1911. This Act was subsequently amended and supplemented by the Accident Insurance Act (LAINF) of 19 June 1947 and the Accident Insurance Act (LAINF) of 25 June 1982.

According to the LAINF, all Swiss workers are obliged to have accident coverage. This means that border workers travelling to work in Switzerland must have LAINF coverage.

Travelling accident at the border for the border worker: LAINF coverage and procedures

Border workers who go to work in Switzerland crossing the border with Italy must know how to proceed in the event of an accident. LAINF coverage is mandatory for Swiss workers. Border crossers must know how to proceed in the event of an accident and where to go to receive the necessary coverage.

The Accident Insurance Act (LAINF) came into force on 1 January 2018 and replaces the Occupational Accident and Illness Insurance Act (LPP) 1964. LAINF has introduced new rules for the coverage of accidents and occupational diseases, with the aim of ensuring greater protection for workers.

According to Article 1 of the LAINF, coverage is compulsory for all workers working in Switzerland. This also includes border workers who go to work in Switzerland crossing the border with Italy. Coverage is provided by compulsory insurance, which covers occupational accidents and illnesses.

In the event of an accident, the border worker must go to the nearest medical centre and present their LAINF insurance. The medical center will assess the situation and provide the necessary treatment. LAINF coverage also covers medical expenses, treatments, and benefits needed for healing.
