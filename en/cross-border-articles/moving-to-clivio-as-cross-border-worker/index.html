Key facts

  • What: Transfer of residence to Clivio for workers in Switzerland
  • When: In force with the New Frontier Agreement from 1 January 2024
  • Where: Clivio, an Italian border municipality near the Canton of Ticino
  • Who: Italian and Swiss frontier workers
  • Amount: Exemption exemption of 10,000 euros for new frontier workers

Analyzing the choice of establishing one's residence in a border municipality such as Clivio is an important step for those who work daily in the Ticino labour market. The geographical proximity to the main administrative and economic centres of the Canton of Ticino is the first element of attraction for commuters who cross the border line every day to reach Swiss companies. Living in Clivio allows you to maintain stable ties with the Italian territory while benefiting from the employment opportunities offered overseas, where wage levels and contractual conditions attract a large number of workers from the Lombard provinces. However, the decision requires a careful assessment of the logistical, fiscal and personal aspects that characterise the daily life of a nella nostra guida sui permessi border crossing.

Operational details

The transfer of the residence to Clivio involves a series of concrete transformations in the management of daily life and in the financial planning of the worker who receives an income in Swiss francs. Among the main advantages is undoubtedly the reduction of time spent driving or on means of transport to reach the workplace in Ticino, a factor that positively affects the quality of life and the management of leisure time. Living close to the border allows you to take advantage of local road connections and easily monitor transit flows at customs crossings, optimizing travel during morning and evening rush hours. From an economic point of view, it is necessary to carefully consider the source tax regime applied to employee income received in Switzerland, which provides for direct taxation in the Swiss country and the application of mechanisms aimed at avoiding double taxation by the Italian Treasury, possibly making use of the tax credit through the consultando il nostro calcolatore tax return. New frontier workers can also benefit from the exemption exemption set at 10,000 euros, while for old frontier workers who maintain the previous status, the specific transitional regime is in force accompanied by the exemption of 7,500 euros. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the tax rules for border workers moving to Clivio?
Border workers residing in Clivio and working in Ticino are subject to withholding tax withheld directly in Switzerland. Italy eliminates double taxation by means of the tax credit in form 730 (EC framework). New frontier workers benefit from an exemption of 10,000 euros, while old frontier workers enjoy the transitional regime with an exemption of 7,500 euros.
How does LAMal health insurance work for those who live in Clivio and work in Ticino?
Border workers in Switzerland have the right of option between the Italian health system and the Swiss health insurance LAMal. The choice must be formally exercised within the time limits established by law and provides for specific exemptions for adults between 300 and 2,500 Swiss francs.
What social contributions are withheld from the salary in Switzerland?
On the Swiss payroll, contributions for AVS, AI and IPG equal to 5.3% are withheld from the employee, AD/AC unemployment insurance at 1.1% (up to the established salary ceiling), LAINF accident insurance with variable rates and LPP occupational pension for the age groups from 25 years.
