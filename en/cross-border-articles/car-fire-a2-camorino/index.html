Car fire on A2 near Camorino: traffic chaos

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 May 2026

A burning car caused delays on the A2 motorway northbound between Lugano Nord and the Monte Ceneri tunnel.

Context

In brief

  • Car on fire on the A2 near Camorino
  • Delays heading north
  • Police and firefighters intervened

Key facts

  • What: Car on fire
  • When: Afternoon
  • Where: A2 near Camorino
  • Who: Police and firefighters
  • Amount: Not specified

Afternoon marked by traffic on the A2 highway. Delays were reported heading north between the Lugano Nord junction and the Monte Ceneri tunnel. The cause? A vehicle that caught fire just before the Bellinzona Sud exit. Police and firefighters arrived at the scene and extinguished the flames. Since 15:00, traffic has returned to normal.

Common causes of road fires

Road fires can be caused by various reasons, including:

  • Mechanical failures
  • Electrical short circuits
  • Fuel leaks
  • Collisions
  • Arson

Preventive measures

To reduce the risk of road fires, it is essential to adopt some preventive measures:

  • Keep the vehicle in good condition
  • Perform periodic checks of the electrical system
  • Avoid overloading the engine
  • Report any malfunctions
  • Use safety devices such as fire extinguishers

What to do in case of an accident

In case of a road accident, it is important to follow certain procedures to ensure your safety and that of others. Here is a step-by-step guide on what to do:

1. Stop in a safe place

If you are involved in or witness an accident, it is crucial to stop in a safe place, away from traffic. Make sure the vehicle is turned off and the hazard lights are on.

2. Report the accident

Contact the relevant authorities, such as the police or fire department, providing all necessary information. It is important to stay calm and provide precise details about the location and severity of the accident.

3. Provide assistance

If possible, assist the people involved in the accident without putting your own safety at risk. In case of injuries, immediately call for medical help and follow the instructions given.

4. Gather information

Gather useful information for any insurance procedures, such as photos, videos, and testimonies. It is important to document the accident scene as detailed as possible.
