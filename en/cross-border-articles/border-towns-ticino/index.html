In brief

Operational details

Economic and social importance

Border municipalities of the Canton of Ticino are a pillar for the local economy and social life. They serve as a meeting point between different cultures, promoting a continuous exchange that enriches both nations. Moreover, they represent an essential hub for trade and employment, with many cross-border workers commuting to Switzerland while residing in Italy.

Impacts on cross-border workers

For cross-border workers, the border municipalities are strategic both for daily logistics and for access to services. Proximity to the border crossings facilitates commuting and reduces travel times, improving quality of life and work efficiency. In addition, their geographical position often allows greater interaction and integration with Swiss culture, fostering both economic opportunities and interpersonal relationships.

How to find the ideal border town

To choose the border town best suited to your needs, it is important to consider several factors:
