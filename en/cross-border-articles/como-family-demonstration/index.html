Como, hundreds at the #OraBasta demonstration

This morning, a stream of parents, grandparents and ordinary citizens crossed the lakeside promenade and city center for the #OraBasta demonstration. The initiative, promoted by the Comitato Como genitori a misura di famiglia, brought together hundreds of participants.

The protest stems from opposition to the Rapinese administration's policy of shutting down dialogue. The demand voiced along the route concerns greater attention to families and their needs. In the report, the mobilization is described as an occasion during which participants publicly reiterated their concerns, calling for consideration and listening from the administration as citizens.

The presence of parents, grandparents and ordinary citizens accompanied a demonstration focused on families' needs. The account also states that the initiative took place peacefully. The figure recorded so far is a turnout of hundreds of people, gathered to ask the administration for listening and attention.

Operational details

The starting point was piazzale Somaini, chosen, according to the report, not by chance. It is in the immediate vicinity of the Corridoni primary school, the subject of a long-running dispute between, on one side, parents and teachers and, on the other, the municipal administration.

The discussion involves parents and teachers on one side and the municipal administration on the other. The choice of starting point places the demonstration alongside the school matter mentioned by the source. The Comitato Como genitori a misura di famiglia began the mobilization near the primary school, before parents, grandparents and ordinary citizens crossed the lakeside promenade and the city center.

The connection between piazzale Somaini and the Corridoni is the local element that the report highlights alongside the protest against the Rapinese administration's policy of closing itself off to dialogue. The report thus places the reference to the school alongside the call for greater attention to families and their needs. Participation, described as peaceful, numbered in the hundreds.

Key points

The report on the demonstration is marked as being updated. In the material available so far, the report provides a snapshot of a morning with hundreds of participants and a peaceful passage along the lakeside promenade and through the city center.

The message accompanying this presence is a request to be taken into consideration and listened to as citizens. The same mobilization calls for more attention to families and their needs and challenges the Rapinese administration's policy of closing itself off to dialogue. These are the elements that the Comitato Como genitori a misura di famiglia brought to the #OraBasta demonstration.

There is also the reference to the Corridoni primary school: piazzale Somaini is a stone's throw from the school, for which the text recalls a long-standing tug-of-war between parents and teachers on one side and the municipal administration on the other. The source does not indicate any other subsequent stages, but leaves the report on this morning's mobilization as ongoing.
