The accident on the provincial road

On Wednesday 23 September, a road accident disrupted traffic between Lozza and Gazzada Schianno. The collision occurred on the uphill stretch of the provincial road rising from the Olona valley floor.

The affected stretch is used by those arriving from the Vedano bridge and crossing the territory of Lozza before heading towards Gazzada Schianno. The road indicated by the source therefore follows a specific local connection, with the bridge on one side and the Gazzada area on the other.

According to the initial information gathered, there were reportedly no injuries. The circumstances of the accident are still being reconstructed: the first available update therefore concerns the absence of injuries reported in the initial checks and the location of the collision along the provincial road.

The heaviest consequences were felt in traffic. Traffic ground to a halt in both directions, affecting the uphill stretch that passes through Lozza. The incident therefore affected not only the point where the collision occurred, but also the road connection between the Vedano bridge and Gazzada Schianno.

The location makes it possible to distinguish the references mentioned in the report: the Olona valley floor is the point from which the provincial road rises; the Vedano bridge is the reference point for those arriving from that side; Lozza is the territory crossed; Gazzada Schianno is the direction towards which the road leads.

The picture remains that of an accident whose circumstances are still being reconstructed and with no injuries according to the initial information, but with an immediate and documented effect on traffic. Wednesday 23 September was marked by gridlocked traffic in both directions along the uphill stretch.

The route is described according to the direction of travel: for those arriving from the Vedano bridge, the road crosses Lozza and leads towards Gazzada Schianno. This description is also the one used to locate the accident within the local road network.

Traffic between the two roundabouts

The extent of the queues

The most detailed information concerns the extent of the queues. On the Gazzada Schianno side, the queue started at the Keynes roundabout. From there, the slowdown also extended in the opposite direction, with repercussions reaching as far as the Vedano bridge roundabout.

The source therefore identifies two endpoints of the traffic situation. The Keynes roundabout represents the starting point of the queues on the Gazzada Schianno side; the Vedano bridge roundabout is the point up to which the repercussions reached in the other direction. Between the two reference points lies the uphill stretch of the provincial road that passes through Lozza.
