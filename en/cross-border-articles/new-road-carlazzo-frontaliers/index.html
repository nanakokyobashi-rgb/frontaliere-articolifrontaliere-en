New road to Carlazzo: 1.2 million to connect SP10 and Via San Pietro

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 28 September 2026

The Program Agreement between the Province of Como and the Municipality of Carlazzo finances a road of 1,242,383.84 euros, with 1,042,383.84 euros from the Municipality and 200,000 euros from the Province, to be built by 2026.

Province of Como and the Municipality of Carlazzo have signed a Program Agreement for the construction of the new connecting road between Via per San Pietro and Via Provinciale. The route is planned near the former plant nursery area, in the municipal territory of Carlazzo, along SP 10 della Val Cavargna.

The project has a total cost of 1.242.383,84 euros. The Municipality of Carlazzo will finance the project with 1.042.383,84 euros, while the Province of Como will contribute 200.000 euros. The funds come from their own budgets and will be disbursed during 2026.

The connection and cross-border workers’ traffic

The new route will connect SP 10 della Val Cavargna with Via per San Pietro without passing through the historic center of Carlazzo. Today, that part of the town is affected by significant traffic, particularly due to the daily passage of cross-border workers. The road network is described as narrow, winding and unsuitable for the transit of medium-sized vehicles.

The President of the Province of Como, Fiorenzo Bongiasca, explained: «With this Program Agreement, the Province of Como confirms its commitment to the safety and efficiency of the provincial road network».

For those crossing the border for work, the concrete element of the agreement is the planned connection that will bypass the historic center. The text links the new road to easing traffic in the town, improving residents’ quality of life and facilitating the journeys of those who cross the border every day. No opening date, travel times or numerical estimate of the reduction in traffic are provided.

The road will also serve as access to the future development of sports facilities that the Municipality is developing precisely in the former plant nursery area. The Municipality will handle all technical and administrative activities, from design to final inspection, taking on the role of contracting authority. The Province will supervise the implementation of the project. The Mayor of Carlazzo, Piera Antonella Mazza, described the project as one that has been awaited for many years and is destined to benefit the historic center.

Key points

Follow the progress of the project

For the cross-border commuter who crosses the border every day, the procedure set out in the agreement makes it possible to follow the project's development through precise administrative steps. The Municipality of Carlazzo is the contracting authority and will handle all technical and administrative activities, from design to final acceptance. The Province of Como, on the other hand, will be responsible for overseeing implementation and paying its contribution in two installments.

1. Distinguish the agreement from the road that is already open. The text authorizes the construction of the link, but does not indicate a date for opening it to traffic. 2. Wait for the executive project approved by the Municipal Council. Upon receipt of this project, the Province will disburse 80% of its contribution. 3. Follow the construction phase. The Municipality remains responsible for the project's activities, while the Province oversees the implementation of the project. 4. Verify the completion of the works and approval of the final acceptance certificate. Only at the end of this step will the remaining 20% of the provincial contribution be disbursed. 5. Consider the route's final function. Once built, the link will connect the SP 10 of Val Cavargna with Via per San Pietro without passing through the historic center, and will also serve the area of the future sports facilities.

This sequence helps to correctly interpret future updates. Approval of the executive project is the first financial milestone indicated by the source; completion of the works and final acceptance represent the final one. Until those steps have been completed, it is not possible to associate a usage date or a quantitative effect on cross-border commuter traffic with the project.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the total cost of the new road and how is it funded?
The total cost of the work is 1,242,383.84 euros. The Municipality of Carlazzo contributes 1,042,383.84 euros, while the Province of Como makes 200,000 euros available. The resources are own budget and will be disbursed during 2026.
What is the main purpose of the new road link?
The connection will connect the SP 10 of Val Cavargna with the Via per San Pietro without crossing the historic centre of Carlazzo. It serves to ease the traffic of frontier workers, improve the quality of life of residents and facilitate access to future sports facilities in the former nursery area.
When will the provincial funds be disbursed and under what conditions?
The provincial contribution of 200,000 euros will be paid in two tranches: 80% (160,000 euros) upon receipt of the executive project approved by the Municipal Council and the remaining 20% (40,000 euros) at the end of the works, after testing. Both payments will take place during 2026.
