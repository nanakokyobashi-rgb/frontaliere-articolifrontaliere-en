Living in Vilminore di Scalve and working in Graubünden is a convenient choice for many cross-border commuters. Switzerland, not being a member of the EU/EFTA, has specific agreements with Italy to avoid double taxation.

This makes life easier for cross-border commuters, allowing them to reside in Italy and work in Switzerland without major inconveniences.

The cost of living in Vilminore di Scalve is generally lower than in many Swiss cities. This can be a significant advantage for cross-border commuters, who can save on housing costs and other daily expenses. Operational details

Practical implications for cross-border commuters

The new cross-border commuter agreement has important practical implications for those living in Vilminore di Scalve and working in Graubünden. The €10,000 exemption for new cross-border commuters represents a significant advantage, allowing them to save on tax costs. Additionally, the transitional regime for old cross-border commuters, with an exemption of €7,500, offers a more gradual transition to the new system.

Tax rates and contributions

Tax rates and social security contributions are established by Swiss federal and cantonal laws. For cross-border commuters, it is important to know the rates for AVS/AI/IPG (5.3% on the employee), AD/AC (1.1% up to CHF 148,200), LAINF (0.7–1.5%), and LPP (7–18% for age groups from 25 years). In Italy, the IRPEF varies from 23% up to €28,000, to 35% from €28,001 to €50,000, and to 43% over €50,000.

Health insurance

Cross-border commuters have the option to choose health insurance (LAMal) in Switzerland, with deductibles ranging from CHF 300 to CHF 2,500. It is important to choose the deductible that best suits your needs, also considering the costs of healthcare services.

Concrete procedures

For cross-border commuters who wish to move to Vilminore di Scalve and work in Graubünden, it is essential to follow some concrete procedures. It is necessary to obtain a G or B permit, depending on the duration of the stay in Switzerland. Additionally, it is important to register with the competent authorities and comply with the current tax and social security regulations.
