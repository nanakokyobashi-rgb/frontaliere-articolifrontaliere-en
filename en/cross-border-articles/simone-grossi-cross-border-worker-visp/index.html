This morning, Swiss authorities confirmed the tragic news of the death of Simone Grossi, an Italian cross-border worker residing in Ticino. The incident occurred in Visp, a locality in the canton of Valais. The exact circumstances of the tragedy have not yet been clarified, but authorities are conducting thorough investigations to determine the cause of death.

Simone Grossi, a cross-border worker, was well-known in the community of cross-border workers who daily cross the border between Italy and Switzerland to go to work. The news has deeply shaken the community, particularly those who work and live between the two countries.

The incident involving Simone Grossi raises important questions about the safety of cross-border workers. Many cross-border workers travel long distances every day to reach their workplaces, often facing difficult road and weather conditions. This tragic event could lead to increased attention on safety measures for workers crossing borders.

Swiss and Italian authorities are collaborating to provide support to the family of Simone Grossi and to ensure that all legal and administrative procedures are followed correctly.
