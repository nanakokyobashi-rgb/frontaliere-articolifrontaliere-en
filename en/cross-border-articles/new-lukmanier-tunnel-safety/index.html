New Lukmanier tunnel for better road safety (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 May 2026

A new tunnel will be built at Lukmanier to improve road safety.

Context

In brief

  • The new Lukmanier tunnel will improve road safety in the Canton of Ticino.
  • The tunnel construction is planned in the Canton of Ticino, between the municipalities of Acquarossa and Faido.
  • The goal is to reduce road accidents and improve traffic flow.
  • The project will be realized with cantonal and federal funds, for an estimated amount of 120 million Swiss francs.

Key facts

  • What: Construction of a new tunnel at Lukmanier.
  • When: construction start planned for 2025, completion by 2030.
  • Where: Canton of Ticino, Switzerland, between the municipalities of Acquarossa and Faido.
  • Who: Canton of Ticino and Swiss Confederation.
  • Amount: 120 million Swiss francs.

The Canton of Ticino and the Swiss Confederation are working together to improve road safety in the Lukmanier region. The construction of a new tunnel is planned to reduce the number of road accidents and improve traffic flow. The project will be financed with cantonal and federal funds, as part of the 2020-2030 road investment plan.

Road safety is a priority for the Ticino and Swiss authorities. The new Lukmanier tunnel will be equipped with advanced safety technologies, such as traffic monitoring systems and LED lighting. The construction of the tunnel will be carried out to minimize the impact on the environment and traffic.

Project details

The new Lukmanier tunnel will be an important intervention to improve road safety in the region. The project involves the construction of a tunnel approximately 2 kilometers long and a service road for local traffic. The construction will be carried out in several phases to ensure the safety of workers and vehicles in transit.
