The analysis of motor liability insurance in Switzerland is a central theme for every frontier worker who moves daily between the province of Como or Varese and the economic centres of the Canton of Ticino such as Lugano, Mendrisio or Bellinzona. The management of insurance coverage, the specificities of the Swiss bonus-malus system and the correct registration of the vehicle require a careful assessment of the regulatory and tariff differences with respect to the Italian market. The border worker often finds himself comparing two different systems, where insurance companies operate according to parameters established by the Federal Law on Road Traffic of 1958 and subsequent amendments, without forgetting the importance of correctly calculating the total expenditure linked to cross-border mobility.

Operational details

Registration and bureaucratic steps

A crucial junction for the border guard concerns the registration of the vehicle and the residence. Customs and tax regulations impose strict checks on the use of cars with foreign or Swiss license plates by residents in Italy. When a worker establishes his domicile in Mendrisio or his residence in Como, or maintains his residence in Italy using a vehicle registered in Switzerland, specific provisions come into play that regulate the temporary circulation or the need to proceed with the nationalisation of the vehicle. The competent authorities regularly verify the conformity of the insurance and tax position, making correct planning of circulation documents essential. With article 93-bis of the Italian Highway Code, introduced in 2023, those who have resided in Italy for more than three months cannot drive vehicles with a foreign license plate, under penalty of fines ranging from 400 to 1,600 euros and the withdrawal of the registration card, with specific exceptions for border workers employed by companies based in Lugano that use company cars registered in the Confederation and explicitly authorized by customs model 15.34.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the main differences between the Swiss car CR and the Italian one for a border crossing?
The differences mainly concern the criteria for calculating the premium, the mandatory coverage ceilings, the management of the bonus-malus system and the deductibles applied by the insurance companies in the respective reference markets.
Is it possible to transfer the Italian merit class to Switzerland?
Many Swiss insurance companies evaluate the risk certificate from Italy to determine the initial merit class, although the application criteria may vary from one insurer to another.
What documents are needed to take out a car policy in Switzerland?
Generally, the identity document, work permit, vehicle registration and risk certificate issued by the previous insurance company are required.
