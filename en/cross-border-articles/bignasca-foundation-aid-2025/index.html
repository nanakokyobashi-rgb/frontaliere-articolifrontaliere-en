Giuliano Bignasca Foundation: over 20,000 CHF in aid in 2025 (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 May 2026

The Giuliano Bignasca Foundation provided over 20,000 francs in direct aid in 2025, including grocery vouchers and support for rent and health insurance premiums.

Context

In brief

  • Giuliano Bignasca Foundation: 20,000 francs disbursed in 2025
  • 9,600 francs in shopping vouchers
  • 10,860 francs for rent and health insurance premiums

Key facts

  • What: Direct aid to people and families in need
  • When: 2025
  • Where: Canton of Ticino
  • Who: Giuliano Bignasca Foundation
  • Amount: Over 20,000 francs

The Giuliano Bignasca Foundation has confirmed its support for people and families in need in the Canton of Ticino. During 2025, over 20,000 francs in direct aid were disbursed. Specifically, the Foundation allocated 9,600 francs in shopping vouchers for people and families in need, and 10,860 francs for paying essential bills, rent, and health insurance premiums. This aid is crucial for many cross-border workers who work in Switzerland and live in Italy, often facing precarious economic conditions.

Established in 2014, the Foundation primarily operates in the areas of livelihood support, housing, education costs, and professional integration, with particular attention to elderly people in economic difficulty. In addition to financial aid, in 2025, more than 60 personal meetings were held with users and families to analyze individual situations and offer targeted support. These meetings are crucial to understanding the specific needs of cross-border workers and their families, who often face unique challenges related to dual residence and regulatory differences between Italy and Switzerland.

Concrete examples and real numbers

For example, in the municipality of Mendrisio, the Foundation supported a family of four with a contribution of 2,500 francs for rent and 1,200 francs for health insurance premiums. In Lugano, on the other hand, 3,000 francs in shopping vouchers were disbursed to a family of three. These examples show how the aid is distributed based on the specific needs of each family.

Regulations and amounts

Swiss regulations allow cross-border workers residing in Italy to access certain social benefits, but the requirements are often complex and vary from municipality to municipality. For example, in Chiasso, cross-border workers must prove they have resided in Italy for at least six months to access local aid. The Giuliano Bignasca Foundation has created an operational checklist to facilitate access to these benefits:

  • Residence: Prove residence in Italy for at least six months.
  • Income: Provide an income statement for the last two years.
  • Documents: Provide valid identity documents and work contracts.

Comparison of practical scenarios

A cross-border worker residing in Varese and working in Lugano can benefit from a monthly aid of 500 francs for rent, while a colleague residing in Como and working in Mendrisio might receive a contribution of 300 francs for health insurance premiums. These examples highlight how geographical location and local regulations can significantly influence the amount of aid received.

Collaboration with TicinoSociale

During the year, a collaboration was launched with the association TicinoSociale, aimed at strengthening support for people in need through greater synergy on the territory. This collaboration is particularly important for cross-border workers, who can benefit from a wider and more coordinated support network. The Foundation finally thanked donors, volunteers, curators, and supporters who contributed to the activities carried out during the year, reiterating the willingness to continue to guarantee concrete aid to the most vulnerable people in the Canton.

"Our mission is to offer concrete and immediate support to those in need, without excessive bureaucracy."

The Giuliano Bignasca Foundation continues to be a reference point for cross-border workers and their families, offering essential support in a complex and often fragmented regulatory context.

Key points

Action: what to do to request aid

To request aid from the Giuliano Bignasca Foundation, it is necessary to follow a specific procedure. Here are the detailed steps to follow:

1. Contact the Foundation: The first thing to do is to contact the Giuliano Bignasca Foundation to request information about the available aid. This can be done through the Foundation's website or by directly contacting the office located in Lugano. The Foundation offers support for residents in various Ticino municipalities, including Lugano, Chiasso, Mendrisio, and Bellinzona.

2. Submit the application: Once you have obtained the necessary information, you can submit the aid application. The application must be completed carefully, providing all the requested information. For example, for 2025, the Foundation has allocated over 20,000 francs for economic aid, with a maximum limit of 5,000 francs per individual request.

3. Provide the documentation: Along with the application, you need to provide the required documentation. This may include identity documents, proof of residence, and documents that attest to the economic situation. For residents in municipalities such as Lugano and Chiasso, it is also possible to request support for medical expenses, with a reimbursement of up to 50% of the documented medical expenses.

4. Wait for the evaluation: After submitting the application and documentation, you need to wait for the evaluation by the Foundation. This process can take 4 to 6 weeks. The Foundation evaluates the requests based on specific criteria, including the economic situation and the urgent need for support.

5. Receive the aid: Once the application is approved, the aid will be disbursed according to the methods established by the Foundation. For example, for 2025, the Foundation has planned monthly disbursements for those in need of continuous support, with a maximum amount of 1,000 francs per month.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who can apply for assistance from the Giuliano Bignasca Foundation?
The Giuliano Bignasca Foundation's aid is intended for individuals and families in financial need residing in the Canton of Ticino. This includes cross-border workers who work in Switzerland and live in Italy, provided they meet the eligibility criteria set by the Foundation.
What types of assistance are available?
The Foundation offers various types of aid, including grocery vouchers, rental support, and health insurance premiums. These aids are intended to cover essential expenses and improve the quality of life for those in need.
How can I apply for assistance?
To apply for assistance, you need to contact the Giuliano Bignasca Foundation and follow the indicated procedure. You can find more information on the Foundation's website or by contacting the office directly.
