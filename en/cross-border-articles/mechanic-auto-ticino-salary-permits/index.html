Key facts

To work as a car mechanic in Switzerland, particularly in Ticino, you must have the G Permit and the Certificate of Knowledge of the Swiss Italian Language (sector CCL). Both tools are critical to ensuring regulatory compliance and getting work done effectively.

The G Permit, which replaces the A Permit, is necessary to carry out any work activity in Switzerland, including as a car mechanic. This permit, issued by the Swiss Confederation, is valid for three years and can be renewed. The cost of the G Permit depends on the profession and the amount of declared income. In this specific case, the cost is approximately 800 Swiss francs.

The sector CCL is a mandatory requirement for car mechanics in Switzerland. This certificate, issued by the Ticino Region, certifies knowledge of the Italian language and Ticino culture. The Ticino Region offers free sector CCL courses at its offices. The validity of the certificate varies from two to four years, depending on age and previous knowledge of the Italian language.

An overview of the cross-border labor market

The new Cross-Border Agreement, which came into force in 2022, has brought important changes to the cross-border work sector in Ticino. The exemption for new cross-border commuters has been raised to €10,000, while for old cross-border commuters there is a transitional regime that allows the exemption of €7,500 within a period of 24 months. This change has a significant impact on the cross-border employment sector, particularly for qualified professionals such as auto mechanics. ### Salary and CCL by sector According to data collected by the main Ticino banking institutions, the average salary for a car mechanic in Ticino is around €2,500 per month. However, salary may vary depending on specialization, experience and job position. To obtain the sector CCL, the candidate must pass a written and oral exam organized by the Bank of Italy. The test, available in Italian and English, analyzes theoretical and practical knowledge of the regulations, techniques and operating procedures of the sector. ### G Permission To work in Switzerland as a cross-border worker, you must have the G Permit. This document is issued by the Bank of Italy based on current regulations. To request the permit, the candidate will have to present a series of documents such as identity card, passport, registration certificate, curriculum vitae and cover letter. The duration of the permit may vary depending on the type of work and the host company.

Key points

Working as a car mechanic in Ticino: salary, sector CCL and G permit

A car mechanic residing in Ticino can choose to work in Switzerland with the G permit, which allows him to carry out professional activities in Swiss territory. To obtain this permit, you must submit an application to the AFC/ESTV and provide the required documents. Here is some useful information for those who want to work as a car mechanic in Ticino.

Salary

According to the 2021 report from the Federal Office for Social Services (UVSS), the average gross salary of a car mechanic living in Ticino is around 4,000 Swiss francs per month. However, this amount may vary depending on the size of the company and your position at the company. For example, an auto mechanic at a large auto chain might earn a higher salary than an auto mechanic at a small repair shop.

Sector CCL

To work in Switzerland with the G permit, you need to have a sector CCL (Free Movement Communication) or a work authorisation. The sector CCL can be obtained after a period of professional experience of at least 1 year and regular attendance at a professional training institute.

For example, the Ticino Auto Sector Technical School (STATS) offers training courses for the issue of the sector CCL in car mechanics and repair. For detailed information, please see the STATS website: <https://www.stats.ch/en/content/mechanic_and_repair_of_automobiles>
