Working as a car mechanic in Ticino: salary, sector CCL and G permit

A car mechanic residing in Ticino can choose to work in Switzerland with the G permit, which allows him to carry out professional activities in Swiss territory. To obtain this permit, you must submit an application to the AFC/ESTV and provide the required documents. Here is some useful information for those who want to work as a car mechanic in Ticino.

Salary

The average gross salary of a car mechanic living in Ticino is around 4,000 Swiss francs per month. However, this amount may vary depending on the size of the company and your position at the company. For example, an auto mechanic at a large auto chain might earn a higher salary than an auto mechanic at a small repair shop.

Sector CCL

To work in Switzerland with the G permit, you need to have a sector CCL (Free Movement Communication) or a work authorisation. The sector CCL can be obtained after a period of professional experience of at least 1 year and regular attendance at a professional training institute.

For example, the Ticino Auto Sector Technical School (STATS) offers training courses for the issue of the sector CCL in car mechanics and repair. For detailed information, please see the STATS website: <https://www.stats.ch/en/content/mechanic_and_repair_of_automobiles>

G Permission

To work in Switzerland with the G permit, you must submit an application to the AFC/ESTV and provide the following documents:

  • Identity and residence authorization (G permit or residence card)
  • Sector CCL or work authorization
  • Professional profile and technical support
  • Certificate of good conduct
  • Legal requirement (if necessary)

Special rules and permits

To work in Switzerland with the G permit, you can apply for a particular permit if you work in a city with a population of less than 150,000 or if you have significant work experience in a certain industry or sector.

For example, the municipality of Sierre offers a special permit for personnel involved in the production of cars and car components: <https://www.sierre.ch/en/services/permette-particuli/sierre-production/>.

Operational checklist

To work in Switzerland with the G permit, you can follow the following operational checklist:

  • Request the sector CCL from the professional training institute
  • Submit an application to the AFC/ESTV for the G permit
  • Receive free movement notice or work authorization
  • Request special permission if necessary
  • Submit Good Conduct Certificate and Employee Good Conduct Certificate
