A8: night closures change between Solbiate Arno and Gallarate (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 May 2026

New nighttime restrictions on the A8 motorway between Solbiate Arno and Gallarate from May 6 to 8, 2026. Here's what changes for cross-border workers

Context

In brief

  • New nighttime closures on the A8 between Solbiate Arno and Gallarate from 6 to 8 May 2026
  • Cancellations of planned closures for 4-5 and 7-8 May
  • Recommended alternative routes for travelers heading to Varese and Milan

Key facts

  • What: Update of the schedule for noise barrier reinforcement works
  • When: From 6 to 8 May 2026, from 21:00 to 5:00
  • Where: Section between the D08 junction and Cavaria, towards Varese
  • Who: Autostrade Society
  • Cost: Not specified

Starting from 6 May 2026, commuters traveling on the A8 Milan-Varese highway during nighttime hours will face new closures. Autostrade Society has announced an update to the schedule for the reinforcement and modernization of noise barriers, which includes the cancellation of some previously announced closures and the introduction of new measures. The canceled closures will no longer take place at the beginning and end of the week. Specifically, the closures of the Solbiate Arno Albizzate-D08 junction section towards Milan, scheduled for the nights of 4 and 5 May, have been canceled. The closures of the D08 junction-Cavaria section towards Varese, planned for the nights of 7 and 8 May, have also been canceled.

Operational details

Impact on cross-border workers

The new nighttime closures on the A8 motorway will have a significant impact on cross-border workers who daily cross the border between Italy and the Canton of Ticino. Workers residing in Lombardy and working in Switzerland will need to carefully plan their movements to avoid delays and inconveniences. In particular, those who use the A8 motorway to reach their workplaces in Ticino will need to consider the new restrictions and the recommended alternative routes.

Comparison with the previous situation

Compared to the previous situation, the new nighttime closures represent a significant change. The cancellations of the closures for 4-5 and 7-8 May offer some relief to commuters, but the new restrictions from 6 to 8 May require greater attention. Cross-border workers will need to adapt to the new schedules and alternative routes to avoid delays and inconveniences.

Practical scenarios

For cross-border workers who work in Lugano and reside in the Varese area, the nighttime closures may result in an increase in travel time. In particular, those who use the A8 motorway to reach the Canton of Ticino will need to pay attention to the new closure times and plan their movements accordingly. For those who need to reach the Gallarate-Gattico Branch (A26), the recommended route involves exiting at Solbiate Arno and passing through Jerago and Besnate, where it will be possible to take the D08. This alternative route may result in an increase in travel time, but it is necessary to avoid the nighttime closures.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the recommended alternative routes for those traveling to Varese?
For those heading to Varese, it is recommended to exit at Gallarate, take the SS341 towards Cavaria/Solbiate Arno, and re-enter the A8 at Solbiate Arno Albizzate. Those coming from the D08 can exit at Besnate, follow the SP26 towards Jerago, and take the SS341 until re-entering the highway at Solbiate Arno.
What are the recommended alternative routes for those traveling to Milan?
Motorists heading to Milan will have to exit at Solbiate Arno, follow the SS341 towards Gallarate and the Sempione State Road, and then re-enter the A8 at the Busto Arsizio exit via the SS336 of Malpensa.
Which closures have been canceled?
The closures of the Solbiate Arno Albizzate-D08 connection towards Milan scheduled for the nights of May 4 and 5 have been canceled. The closures of the D08-Cavaria section towards Varese planned for the nights of May 7 and 8 have also been canceled.
