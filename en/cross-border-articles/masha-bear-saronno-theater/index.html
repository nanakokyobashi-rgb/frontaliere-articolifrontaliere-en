Key facts

Event: The new adventures of Masha and Bear Date: Saturday 17 October Place: Teatro Regina Pacis di Saronno Opening hours: 15:00 and 16:30 Company: The Fantastic Puppet World Contact: 392 8744754

Saturday, October 17, the Regina Pacis Theatre in Saronno will host the event entitled "The new adventures of Masha and the Bear". The show is offered by the company Il Fantastico Mondo dei Puppini and is mainly aimed at children, offering them the opportunity to meet live the protagonists of the stories they prefer. The organisation has planned two separate replicas to manage the flow of the public, setting appointments at 3 pm and 4.30 pm. This choice allows you to give different groups of children the opportunity to participate in the event at different times during the Saturday afternoon. The performance is part of a project that aims to combine pure entertainment with the tradition of figure theatre, an art that continues to fascinate new generations through the use of puppets and puppets handled by expert craftsmen of the scene. The event promises to be a moment of aggregation for families in the Saronno area and neighbouring municipalities, bringing the famous characters of the animated series to a physical and tangible dimension on the stage of the local theatre.

Operational details

Interaction and experience for children

The distinctive feature of this staging lies in the interactive nature of the show. Unlike classical theatrical performances where the audience remains passive, here there is the possibility for all the children present to interact verbally with the puppets during the development of the plot. This dynamic transforms the viewer into an active participant, allowing children to influence or comment on the adventures that are staged in real time. This pedagogical and playful approach serves to stimulate children's creativity and ability to express themselves orally, making the experience much more engaging than watching a cartoon on a screen.

In addition to the narrative aspect, the event includes a special moment dedicated to the memory of the experience. In fact, there is a free souvenir photo with Bingo for all young spectators. This detail adds an emotional value to the appointment, allowing the children to bring home a tangible memory of the encounter with the characters. The combination of the tradition of figure theatre and the use of modern and famous characters creates a bridge between different eras of children's entertainment, making the show an accessible and fun cultural product for the whole family, while enhancing the theatrical space as a place of discovery and play for children.
