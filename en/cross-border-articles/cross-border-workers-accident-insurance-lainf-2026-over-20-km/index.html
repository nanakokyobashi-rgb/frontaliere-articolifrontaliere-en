Practical Implications for Frontiersmen

The new Frontier Agreement has important practical implications for frontier workers. LAINF coverage for accidents at work guarantees a daily allowance equal to 80% of gross salary, covering both accidents and occupational diseases. This is particularly relevant for frontier workers living more than 20 km from the workplace, as they face longer travel times and potential additional risks.

Key points

Concrete Actions for Frontiersmen

For frontier workers who live more than 20 km from the workplace, it is essential to follow a step-by-step procedure to ensure the correct management of the accident at work. Here's a handy guide:

Step 1: Notification of Injury

Within 30 days from the date of the event, frontier workers must notify SUVA of the accident. The notification can be submitted online through the SUVA portal or at local offices.

Step 2: Documentation

Keep all documents related to the accident, including medical certificates and police reports. These documents are essential for the complaint procedure and to ensure the delivery of benefits.

Step 3: Report to SUVA

File the complaint with SUVA within 30 days from the date of the event. SUVA verifies the validity of the claim and provides the benefits, which include a daily allowance equal to 80% of the gross salary.

Step 4: Contacts with the Employer

Keep in touch with the employer for updates on the status of the request. The employer can provide assistance and support during the complaint procedure.

Step 5: Legal Assistance

If necessary, seek legal assistance to ensure that rights are respected and benefits are provided. A lawyer specialising in labour law can provide advice and support during the complaint procedure.

What to do in the event of an accident at work?
In the event of an accident at work, you must notify the event within 30 days and file the complaint with SUVA. Keep all documents related to the accident and maintain contact with the employer and SUVA.
What are the differences between an accident at work and an occupational disease?
An accident at work is a sudden and accidental event that occurs during or in connection with working hours. Occupational disease is a pathology caused or aggravated by working conditions. Complaint procedures and benefits differ between the two categories.
