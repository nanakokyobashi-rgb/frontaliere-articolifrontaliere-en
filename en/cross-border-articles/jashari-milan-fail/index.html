Last summer, the Jashari-Milan deal was a sort of telenovela. On one side, there was the player who only wanted the red and blacks, on the other, the Bruges, his former club, which was not willing to give any discount to the Milanese club, forced to wait a long time before being able to embrace the player. In the end, the final handshake arrived at 37 million euros. But is the Swiss national repaying the Devil's trust? 'The Swiss with the 30 did not prove to be up to the level of Milan for this first season in red and black. It is still early for a final judgment, but certainly the debut year is a failure,' writes Corriere dello Sport in an article entirely dedicated to the 23-year-old.

The big chance came last Sunday in the match that Milan lost 2-0 in Reggio Emilia against Sassuolo, also 'thanks' to the absence (until the end of the season) of a certain Luka Modric. 'He fluffed his lines. Ball lost in midfield in the clash with Thorsvedt that opens the field to the green-black advantage: a basic, heavy, decisive mistake. Perhaps also attributable to the Swiss' scarce playing time this season. And, for the moment, it does not validate in any way the 37 million euro purchase made in the summer and the weeks spent chasing him.'
