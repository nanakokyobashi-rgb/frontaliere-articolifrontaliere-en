Withholding tax on employment income represents the main taxation method for certain categories of workers in Switzerland, including those holding a residence permit such as Permit B. Unlike workers residing abroad, Permit B holders reside permanently in Swiss territory and are subject to withholding tax directly by the employer at the source.

The Swiss regulatory framework

The rates and contributions applied to Swiss salaries include various mandatory social security and insurance items established by the competent authorities. These contributions include OASI/IV/EO with an employee-paid percentage of 5.3%, ALV unemployment insurance at 1.1% up to a set maximum income ceiling, accident insurance UVG, and BVG pension contributions for mandatory occupational pension plans depending on the age bracket starting from age 25. The federal and cantonal authorities define these parameters through their administrative bodies, including the Federal Tax Administration FTA/ESTV.

Operational details

The analysis of the tax implications for holders of a B Permit requires careful consideration of the relationship between the Swiss and Italian tax systems, especially in the presence of cross-border income or family ties. The Double Taxation Convention concluded between Italy and Switzerland, originally signed in December 1976, regulates the general principles to prevent the same income from being fully taxed in both States.

Deductions and supplementary pension

An important aspect for those subject to withholding tax concerns the possibility of requesting specific tax deductions, similarly to what is provided for taxpayers taxed under the ordinary procedure following specific income adjustments or requests for reconsideration. Financial and tax planning tools include contributions to the supplementary pension pillar known as Pillar 3a, which allow for a reduction of the overall taxable income within the limits established annually by current federal legislation. Frequently Asked Questions
How is the source tax collected for holders of Permit B?
Tax at source is withheld directly by the employer on the employee's monthly paycheck. Rates are established based on tables approved by federal and cantonal laws and administered at the federal level by the Federal Administration of AFC/ESTV Contributions.
How does the Double Taxation Convention regulate income?
The Convention against Double Taxation between Italy and Switzerland, signed in December 1976, defines the criteria to prevent labour income from being taxed in a duplicative and disproportionate manner, providing for mechanisms such as the tax credit in the EC framework of the Italian tax return.
What social security contributions are withheld from the salary in Switzerland?
Compulsory contributions such as the employee's AVS/AI/IPG at 5.3%, AD/AC unemployment insurance at 1.1% up to the established ceiling, LAINF accident contributions and LPP occupational pension contributions from 25 years of age are retained on the Swiss salary.
