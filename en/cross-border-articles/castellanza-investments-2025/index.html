Castellanza: €1.8m investments for 2025 (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 May 2026

The municipality of Castellanza has approved a 2025 budget with €1.8 million in investments for roads, schools, and sports facilities

Context

In brief

  • 1.8 million euros invested in 2025 in Castellanza
  • 127.92 euros per capita for public works
  • Priority to roads, sports and municipal buildings

Key facts

  • What: Investments for public works
  • When: 2025
  • Where: Castellanza, Italy
  • Who: Municipality of Castellanza
  • Amount: 1,810,456.54 euros

The Municipality of Castellanza closed 2025 with a positive balance and an investment plan that involved every corner of the city. The municipal council approved the management report, a document that photographs the state of municipal finances and the administration's ability to transform public resources into concrete works for citizens. The investments made or started during 2025 amount to 1,810,456.54 euros, with an expenditure of 127.92 euros per inhabitant.

Leading the ranking for investment volume are roads and lighting, which together absorb 987,320.65 euros. Of these, 899,841.66 euros were allocated to the redevelopment of roads and sidewalks, while 87,478.99 euros financed interventions on public lighting. Following are sports and youth policies, with 751,099.18 euros mainly used for the Sports Palace (674,243.36 euros) and for sports and recreational facilities (76,855.82 euros).

Key investments

The third sector for resources mobilized is that of municipal buildings, with 566,602.76 euros divided among the Town Hall (318,411.99 euros), ERP Houses (243,782.87 euros) and the barracks (4,407.90 euros). Following are investments in public green spaces and the environment, totaling 434,440.43 euros, divided between public green spaces (325,631.28 euros) and the waste collection center (108,809.15 euros). Also significant is the quota allocated to schools, which exceeds 480,000 euros: 316,362.02 euros went to elementary schools, 164,048.35 euros to middle schools.

Video surveillance absorbed 101,261.46 euros, entirely dedicated to enhancing the urban security system. Closing the picture are the cemetery with 56,042.48 euros and cultural sites — Civic Library and Villa Pomini — with 29,478.92 euros.

The sources of financing for the investments also tell something relevant. Only 250,000 euros come from new loans, testament to a prudent debt policy. The rest of the resources were obtained through regional contributions (200,000 euros), proceeds from building permits (173,451.96 euros), asset disposals (63,150.69 euros), state transfers (47,302.82 euros) and, predominantly, through the use of the multi-year restricted fund for capital expenditure, amounting to 1,569,919.65 euros. The available surplus is 2,414,964.34 euros.

On the revenue side, the Municipality collected a total of 15,763,910.06 euros in current resources, of which 10,369,244.50 euros from tax revenues and 5,394,665.56 euros from non-tax revenues, which mainly include proceeds from the management of municipal assets, services, and fines. The constant growth in the recovery of tax evasion on IMU, TASI, and TARI should be noted: the proceeds ascertained reached 1,493,928.57 euros in 2025, in sharp progression compared to the 1,195,965.55 euros of 2024 and the 943,346.80 euros of 2023.

Total current expenditures amount to 14,397,462.56 euros and concern the operation of the entire municipal machinery: from personnel to the purchase of goods and services and transfers. The most relevant item is that of general institutional services (3,887,627.03 euros), followed by social rights and family policies (2,983,252.91 euros) and sustainable development and environmental protection (2,808,028.76 euros). Expenditure for education and the right to study stands at 1,590,756.94 euros, that for public order and security at 1,005,816.75 euros.

Comparison with the previous situation

Compared to previous years, 2025 has seen a significant increase in public investments in Castellanza. In 2024, the municipality invested approximately 1.5 million euros, while in 2023 the figure was 1.2 million. This increase reflects a more ambitious development policy aimed at improving infrastructure and services for citizens. For cross-border workers, this means greater attention to the needs of those living and working in a cross-border context, with concrete improvements in quality of life and services offered.
