Frequently Asked Questions
What is the allowance for new frontier workers in Switzerland?
The allowance for new frontier workers in Switzerland is CHF 10,000.
What is the percentage of tax at source in Switzerland?
The percentage of tax at source in Switzerland is 5.3% for employees and 1.1% for taxpayers with income up to CHF 148'200.
What does the New Switzerland-Italy Border Agreement provide for workers?
The New Frontier Agreement, in force since 1 January 2020, guarantees frontier workers treatment similar to that of Swiss workers. A key benefit is the exemption from double taxation between Switzerland and Italy, ensuring that tax at source is applied only on income from employment.
How much can a border chef with experience in Ticino earn?
A border cook experienced in Ticino can earn between CHF 6,000 and CHF8,000 per month, or even more, depending on experience and location. The minimum wage in Switzerland is around CHF 4,500 per month, but specialised figures such as chefs often far exceed that threshold.
What obligations does a Swiss employer have towards frontier workers?
A Swiss employer must treat frontier workers similarly to Swiss workers. This includes the regular payment of salary, the provision of a written employment contract with clear conditions (salary, holidays) and compliance with the rules of the New Frontier Agreement, avoiding any discrimination.
