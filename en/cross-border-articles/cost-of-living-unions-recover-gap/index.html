Carvivita, the unions demand recovery of the gap in the 2027 Quote

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 October 2026

Between December 2020 and September 2026, the cost of living rose by 8.5%, while the salaries of cantonal, parapublic and social-healthcare staff were adjusted by only 3%.

Between December 2020 and September 2026, inflation increased by approximately 8.5%, while the salaries of affected employees were adjusted by only 3%. This is the data on which the three Ticino unions, OCST, VPOD, and SIT, base their petition addressed to the Council of State and the Grand Council.

The request is included in the 2027 Budget: to recognize the cost of living adjustment not granted in recent years and to compensate for the actual inflation of 2027. The initiative arises at a time that the petition describes as difficult for Ticino residents, amidst health insurance, petrol, energy, and rents. To track the impact of these items, you can also consult the cost of living, without attributing data to the tool different from those reported in the source.

Who is included in the claim

The claim directly concerns cantonal personnel. It also includes a large part of the parapublic and social-healthcare sectors, when remuneration is linked to the Canton's salary scale. The text mentions EOC, nursing homes, public SACD, USI and SUPSI, OSI, and numerous Municipalities.

The union document quantifies the effect with a case already indicated in the source: someone earning 5,000 francs monthly can suffer a loss of approximately 3,500 francs per year. The petition adds that the discrepancy is not exhausted by the adjustment of a single year. A lower salary remains so throughout a career, impacts subsequent increases, and reduces pension contributions.

For 2027, the Government recognized only 0.25% for the cost of living. According to the petition, this measure does not recover previous losses and would not even compensate for current inflation. The source does not directly mention cross-border commuters, but the point is relevant for anyone working in Canton Ticino within one of the indicated categories: the verification concerns the link between one's remuneration and the cantonal salary scale.

Operational details

The gap revealed by the payslip

For a cross-border commuter working in one of the listed structures, the practical point is not the worker's origin, but the criterion by which remuneration is set. The source, in fact, links the pay to the Canton's salary scale. This narrows the analysis: the petition cannot be automatically extended to anyone working in Ticino, but neither can it ignore those who fall under the indicated cantonal, semi-public, or social-healthcare personnel.

Key points

From News to Personal Verification

For a cross-border commuter working in Canton Ticino, the most prudent action is to transform the union's request into a circumscribed check, without confusing it with a measure already applied to every salary. The source provides some fixed points and allows one to proceed without adding unindicated amounts or deadlines.

Four Verification Steps

1. Identify the scope. Check if your position falls within the cantonal staff or the cited parapublic and social-healthcare sectors. The decisive criterion indicated by the petition is the link between remuneration and the Canton's salary scale. Named entities include EOC, USI, and SUPSI, in addition to other structures and municipalities mentioned.

2. Set the comparison period. Read the salary situation relative to the interval between December 2020 and September 2026. The petition compares an 8.5% inflation with a 3% adjustment for the affected salaries. The case of 5,000 francs monthly and an annual loss of about 3,500 francs is the numerical reference provided by the source, not a result to be automatically applied to every worker.

3. Separate the recovery from the 2027 measure. The request concerns both the cost of living not granted in recent years and the compensation for actual inflation in 2027. The Government, however, recognized 0.25% for 2027. Keeping these elements distinct avoids interpreting that figure as a full recovery of previous losses.

4. Follow the indicated recipients. The petition is addressed to the Council of State and the Grand Council and links the request to the 2027 Budget. For those working across the border, the aspect to monitor therefore remains the salary and institutional one described by the source, not an automatic modification of an individual's position.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the gap in costs that the unions are asking to recover?
Between December 2020 and September 2026, the increase reached about 8.5%, while the salary adjustments for the personnel concerned were only 3%. The trade unions OCST, VPOD and sit have filed a petition to recover this gap in the 2027 Budget, highlighting an annual loss of around CHF 3,500 for those earning CHF5,000 per month, in addition to the impact on pension contributions.
What categories of workers are affected by the petition?

Among the realities mentioned are the EoC, elderly homes, public SACDs, USI and SUPSI, the OSI and numerous municipalities. What is the position of the Government of Ticino regarding the recovery of the cost of living for 2027?

However, according to the trade union petition, this measure would not be sufficient to recover the losses accumulated in previous years, nor to fully compensate for the actual increase expected in 2027. The unions' request is to recover the non-agreed gap and compensate for the actual increase.
