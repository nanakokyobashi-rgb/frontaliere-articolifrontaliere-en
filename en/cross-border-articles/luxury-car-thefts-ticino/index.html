Four high-end vehicles were stolen over the course of one week in Ticino, with thefts targeting dealers and body shops in Manno, Lamone and Camorino. The investigation launched after the incidents led to the arrest of three young men, while the search continues for a fourth suspect.

For the audience of Frontaliere Ticino, the only verifiable connection is territorial: the facts concern the Canton of Ticino. The source, however, does not report consequences for traffic, border crossings, transport or the activities of cross-border commuters. Nor does it indicate any changes in daily travel. Any mobility data therefore remain separate from the reporting on the investigation and can be consulted in servizio sui tempi di attesa ai valichi.

According to information provided by the Public Prosecutor’s Office and the Cantonal Police, the alleged perpetrators were recruited through social networks. Based on initial reconstructions, they reached the targeted businesses, broke in and took possession of the car keys. They then reportedly fled directly in the stolen vehicles.

The arrests and the vehicle recovered in Italy

The investigation made it possible to locate the stolen vehicles. All were recovered, and one of them was found in Italy. Those arrested were a 19-year-old French national residing in the canton of Zurich, a 21-year-old French national residing in France and a 24-year-old Moroccan national, also residing in France. The source does not assign definitive responsibility to the three, but refers to them as alleged perpetrators.

A fourth suspect is still wanted. The Ticino Cantonal Police, the cantonal police forces of Valais, Vaud, Geneva, Bern and Neuchâtel, the Malcantone Est, Malcantone Ovest, Ascona and City of Locarno municipal police forces, as well as the Federal Office for Customs and Border Security, took part in the operations. The alleged offences include theft, trespassing, damage to property, violation of the Federal Road Traffic Act and violation of the Federal Act on Foreign Nationals and Integration.
