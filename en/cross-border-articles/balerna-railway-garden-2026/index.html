Railway garden in Balerna: a new sustainable green space (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 8 May 2026

The Municipality of Balerna and the SBB inaugurate the Railway Garden, a unique area in Switzerland with urban gardens and community spaces

Context

In brief

  • Railway Garden inaugurated in Balerna
  • Unique of its kind in Switzerland
  • Open to all with gardens for rent

Key facts

  • What: Inauguration of the Railway Garden
  • When: Saturday, May 9, 2026, at 10:00 AM
  • Where: Via Stazione, Balerna
  • Who: Municipality of Balerna and SBB
  • Amount: Area of over 3,000 square meters

The Railway Garden of Balerna is finally a reality. A project that sees the collaboration between the Municipality of Balerna and the Swiss Federal Railways (SBB) has led to the creation of a sustainable area unique throughout Switzerland. The official inauguration will take place on Saturday, May 9, 2026, at 10:00 AM and will be an opportunity to discover and experience this new green space dedicated to the community. The garden, located in Via Stazione, has been described as 'unique of its kind' thanks to the skillful reuse of SBB materials for the furnishings.

The area of over 3,000 square meters includes not only green spaces, but also a communal orchard, relaxation and aggregation areas with tables and benches. The urban gardens, on the other hand, are reserved for those who have taken the space on rent. The inauguration event will see the participation of various authorities, including Adv. Luca Pagani, mayor of Balerna, Roberta Cattaneo, director Region South SBB, Ermes Fiori, SBB project manager, and Enrico Sassi, landscape architect. This will be followed by an aperitif offered.

A sustainable and inclusive project

The Railway Garden represents an example of sustainability and inclusivity. The furnishings, made with recycled materials from SBB, demonstrate how it is possible to create functional and welcoming green spaces without waste. The choice to open the garden to everyone, while the gardens are reserved for those who have taken the space on rent, reflects the intention to create a place of aggregation for the community.

Inauguration program

The inauguration of the Railway Garden will follow a well-structured program. At 10:00 AM there will be the institutional part with the interventions of the authorities. Subsequently, at 11:00 AM, an aperitif will be offered to all participants. This event represents an opportunity for residents and families of Balerna to discover a new green space that promises to become a reference point for the community.

Implications for cross-border workers

For cross-border workers living and working in Ticino, the Railway Garden of Balerna represents an additional opportunity for integration and socialization. The possibility of accessing sustainable and well-kept green spaces is an added value for those living in a cross-border context. Furthermore, the presence of urban gardens for rent could be an interesting opportunity for those who wish to cultivate fresh and quality products, thus contributing to a healthier and more sustainable lifestyle.

Conclusion

The Railway Garden of Balerna is a project that combines sustainability, inclusivity, and innovation. The inauguration on May 9, 2026, will be an opportunity to discover this new green space and actively participate in community life. For cross-border workers, it represents an additional opportunity to integrate and live in a greener and more sustainable environment.
