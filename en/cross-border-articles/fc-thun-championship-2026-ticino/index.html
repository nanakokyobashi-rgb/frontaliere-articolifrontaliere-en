FC Thun, led by Ticinese coach Mauro Lustrinelli, wins the Swiss championship for the first time in its history.

Key facts

  • What: FC Thun wins the Swiss championship
  • When: May 3, 2026
  • Where: Thun, Bernese Oberland
  • Who: FC Thun, coach Mauro Lustrinelli
  • Amount: First victory in club history

FC Thun has written a page in Swiss football history. At 128 years since its foundation, the club from the Bernese Oberland has become Swiss champion for the first time in its history. The team, led by Ticinese coach Mauro Lustrinelli, mathematically secured the title this Sunday at 15:53, after San Gallo, second in the standings, lost 0:3 to Sion.

Several thousand supporters followed the match on the big screen set up at the Stockhorn Arena, the stadium of FC Thun. The football party started with the sun and continued with the rain, but the bad weather did not dampen the enthusiasm. Lustrinelli's team also followed the match within the walls of their home, where at the final whistle of the referee at the Kybunpark in San Gallo, they showed off shirts with the words 'Swiss champions' and displayed a replica of the trophy.

Authorities had previously given the green light for a night of celebration and allowed various public venues to stay open until the early hours. The official celebration of the title will take place on May 15 in Stadthofplatz. For this occasion too, the city has authorized a free night.
