The UBS banking group reduces its workforce to 99,085 employees following the integration of Credit Suisse. Target: 13.5 billion in savings by 2026.

Key facts

  • What: Reduction of UBS bank staff.

  • Who: UBS Group.

  • Target savings: $13.5 billion by the end of 2026.

The UBS banking group has officially announced that it has exceeded its workforce reduction threshold, dropping below 100,000 jobs. According to the quarterly results, at the end of June the bank had 99,085 employees, calculated in full-time equivalents. This figure marks a decrease of about 2,500 units compared to the number of employees recorded at the end of March. The maneuver is part of the broader Credit Suisse integration process, an operation that has profoundly changed the personnel structure of the Swiss banking group.

# Merger Process Analysis

The integration process began following the official announcement of the merger between the two banking giants on 19 March 2023. Prior to that event, the total workforce between UBS and Credit Suisse exceeded 120,000 units.
