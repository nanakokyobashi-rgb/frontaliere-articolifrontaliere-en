Vezia Municipality hosts an event to strengthen dialogue with local businesses

Context

In brief

The municipality of Vezia organized an event to strengthen dialogue with local businesses, aiming to create a constructive exchange and provide useful information to local companies. The meeting, held on Thursday evening, saw the participation of around thirty local businesses, representing various economic sectors.

Key facts

  • What: Meeting between the municipality of Vezia and local businesses
  • When: Thursday evening
  • Where: Vezia
  • Who: The municipality of Vezia and local businesses
  • Amount: Not specified

The executive of Vezia organized an event to strengthen dialogue with local businesses. The meeting, held on Thursday evening, saw the participation of around thirty local businesses, representing various economic sectors. During the evening, current topics related to building procedures, fire safety, and energy strategy were discussed.

Interventions by the head of the Technical Office, Fabio Manetti, and the head of the consulting area of the Ticino Energy Association, Luca Pampuri, provided concrete insights and useful information for the attending businesses. In particular, regulations on building procedures and fire safety were explained, with reference to the Ticino law of December 17, 2008, and the application regulation of May 22, 2012. Furthermore, opportunities offered by new technologies for reducing energy consumption and optimizing costs were presented.

The evening concluded with a convivial aperitif, which offered the opportunity for an informal exchange between businesses and municipal representatives. This event represented an important opportunity for dialogue and collaboration between the municipality and local businesses, with the aim of creating a favorable environment for the economic development of the territory.

Key points

What to do if you are a cross-border worker interested in working in the municipality of Vezia

If you are a cross-border worker interested in working in the municipality of Vezia, you may want to consider contacting local businesses for information on job opportunities. Additionally, you may want to keep an eye on the development of the municipality's construction procedures and energy strategy, so that you can be informed of any potential opportunities that may arise.

The municipality of Vezia, located in the Lugano region, offers several job opportunities for cross-border workers. According to 2021 data, the unemployment rate in the municipality is 3.5%, lower than the Ticino average of 5.2%. Additionally, the municipality has an employment rate of 73.4%, higher than the Ticino average of 68.9%.

Local businesses primarily operate in the industry, commerce, and services sectors. Some of the main companies in the municipality include the hydroelectric power generation company Repower, the telecommunications company Swisscom, and the logistics company DHL.

To be successful as a cross-border worker in the municipality of Vezia, it is important to be flexible and adaptable. You may need to work different hours than you are used to in Italy, or be available to work on weekends. Additionally, it is important to have a good knowledge of French and Italian, as these are the most commonly spoken languages in the municipality.
