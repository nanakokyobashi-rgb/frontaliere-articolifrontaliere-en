Diego kostner leaves ambrì piotta

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 October 2026

The 34-year-old forward from Bressanone leaves Ambrì Piotta and will continue his career with the newly founded Milano Hockey Club.

Context

In brief

  • Diego Kostner leaves Ambrì Piotta with immediate effect
  • The 34-year-old from Bressanone will continue with Milano Hockey Club
  • He was included three times in the first eight games
  • He played 432 National League games with Ambrì Piotta

Key facts

  • Decision → immediate effect
  • New club → Milano Hockey Club
  • Age → 34 years old
  • Origin → Bressanone
  • Lineup inclusions → 3 in the first 8 games
  • Role → thirteenth forward
  • National League appearances → 432
  • Points → 129

The departure

Diego Kostner leaves Ambrì Piotta with immediate effect. The 34-year-old forward from Bressanone will continue his career with the newly founded Milano Hockey Club. These are the two elements of the announcement: the conclusion of his experience in blue and white and the new club indicated for the continuation of his career.

The change comes after the first eight league games. During this phase, Kostner was included in the lineup three times, always as the thirteenth forward. However, he never took to the ice. His presence in the opening games therefore remained on the lineup, without entering the ice.

The club links the decision to its sporting strategy. In the club's explanation, the change is part of the choice to continue giving space and opportunities to young players. The reference to the strategy accompanies the departure without changing the operational fact: Kostner leaves Ambrì and continues his career with Milano Hockey Club.

The announcement thus brings together the change of jersey and the direction indicated for the first team. On the one hand, the player will continue his path with the newly founded club; on the other, Ambrì Piotta links the decision to its desire to offer opportunities to young players. Kostner's position is included in this sporting approach.

For the blue-and-white team, the departure therefore comes after three lineup inclusions in the first eight games and with no minutes played on the ice. For the 34-year-old from Bressanone, what comes next is already defined: his career will continue with Milano Hockey Club.

Operational details

The blue-and-white record

Ten seasons in Leventina

Kostner arrived in Leventina in the summer of 2016. From that moment, he wore the blue-and-white jersey for ten consecutive seasons, a period during which, according to the club, he became an important presence on and off the ice. The club also links his tenure to an important part of Ambrì Piotta's recent history.

The sporting record is reported through the National League figures. Wearing the Ambrì Piotta jersey, the center born in 1992 played a total of 432 games. The tally includes 52 goals and 77 assists, for a total of 129 points. This is the statistical summary of his blue-and-white experience indicated by the club.

Alongside the figures, the club highlights elements related to how Kostner carried out his role. The club cites his experience, his character and the way he interpreted the position. In Ambrì Piotta's assessment, these aspects represented a point of reference for the locker room.

The account of the ten years spent in Leventina therefore unfolds on two levels. The first is that of the games played in the National League, with 432 appearances overall. The second concerns the presence attributed to the player on and off the ice, together with the contribution recognized to the life of the locker room.

The sequence of data makes it possible to distinguish the different entries in the record: 432 games, 52 goals, 77 assists and 129 points. The total number of points is indicated by the club as the overall result of the goals and assists. The journey that began in the summer of 2016 is thus associated both with the continuity of the ten consecutive seasons and with the role played in the club's recent history.

For Ambrì Piotta, Kostner was not merely the center born in 1992 who appeared in the National League statistics. The club describes him as an important figure beyond the ice as well, highlighting his experience, character and interpretation of the role. This is the context with which the club accompanies the player's numerical record.

Key points

The Sporting Decision and What Follows

Space for Young Players

The decision by Ambrì Piotta is framed by the club within a sporting strategy aimed at young players. The stated objective is to continue giving them space and opportunities, fostering their development and gradual integration into the first team. Kostner's departure is therefore linked to a decision about the future composition of the team.

The club also indicates how it intends to manage the position left vacant. Looking ahead, that spot will be occupied by a younger player. The move does not change the immediacy of the departure, but clarifies the sporting direction associated with the decision: the position is brought back into the development path of the younger players.

For Kostner, on the other hand, what comes next is represented by Milano Hockey Club. The 34-year-old from Bressanone will continue his career there wearing the jersey of the newly founded club. The new destination is indicated together with his departure from Ambrì Piotta and constitutes the next chapter in the player's sporting career.

The club's text thus presents two related developments. The first concerns Kostner's transfer, as he leaves Ambrì Piotta with immediate effect. The second concerns the blue-and-white team's project, which intends to continue offering space to young players and fostering their gradual entry into the first team.

The position occupied by the player is the meeting point between the two decisions. On the one hand, a new next step is assigned to Kostner's career; on the other, Ambrì Piotta plans to entrust that position to a younger profile. The strategy indicated by the club thus accompanies the change of jersey without adding any further steps to the announced decision.

The immediate departure from Ambrì and the continuation at Milano Hockey Club close the blue-and-white phase and open the next one. For the Ticino club, the move is part of the continuity of the sporting project dedicated to young players. For Kostner, the news instead indicates the new team with which he will continue his career.
