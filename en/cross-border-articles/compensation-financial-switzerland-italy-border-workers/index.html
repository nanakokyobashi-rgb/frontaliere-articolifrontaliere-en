Refurbishments for border workers: cantons that receive money

Switzerland is one of the leading European countries for cross-border work, with over 410,000 foreign workers active in the Swiss territory. Among these, border workers with a G permit represent a significant share, with 79,812 units registered at the end of September 2025. This trend is in line with the 1.2% increase compared to the same period of the previous year.

The geographical distribution of border workers is interesting: more than half of them (57.6%) are domiciled in France, followed by Italy (22.7%) and Germany (16.5%). This framework is consistent with the strategic position of Switzerland, which is at the centre of Europe and offers easy accessibility to these countries.

But what happens when border workers leave their jobs and return to their countries of origin? Switzerland, like other European countries, has introduced a system of refreshments for border workers who leave work. These refreshments are set at the federal level and vary depending on the border crosser's country of origin.

Ticino is one of the Swiss cantons that receives the most refreshments for border workers from Italy. In 2024, the canton of Ticino received 112 million francs of refreshments, which represents an increase compared to previous years. This increase is due to the growth in the number of border workers working in the canton and the majority of the Democratic People's Party (PPD) and the Swiss Socialist Party (PSS) who decided to increase financial compensation for border workers.

The canton of Geneva, on the other hand, paid 396 million francs to the neighbouring French departments of Haute-Savoie and Ain. This is due to the fact that the canton of Geneva has a greater presence of border workers from France than Ticino. In addition, the canton of Geneva has decided to increase financial compensation for border workers to encourage worker mobility.

Frequently Asked Questions
How many frontier workers are there in Switzerland and where do they mainly come from?
Switzerland has more than 410,000 border crossers, an increase of 1.2% compared to the previous year. Of these, 79,812 hold a G permit at the end of September 2025. The majority comes from France (57.6%), followed by Italy (22.7%) and Germany (16.5%).
What is the amount of the federal allowance for Italian border workers leaving work in Switzerland?
Border workers from Italy leaving work in Switzerland can receive a federal rebate of CHF 2,000.00. This amount is established by the federal law of 15 December 2020 and varies according to the number of months spent in the Swiss country.
How much does an Italian border worker receive as a refreshment if he has worked in the Canton of Ticino?
If an Italian border worker has worked in the Canton of Ticino and leaves his job, he can receive a CHF 1,500.00 rebate. This amount is specific to the measures adopted by the Canton of Ticino for workers who return to their country of origin, as indicated by cantonal regulations.
What is the rate of tax at source withheld by Switzerland on the income of frontier workers?
Switzerland may withhold a tax at source on frontier workers' income. The rate was reduced from 4.5% to 4.2% in 2022, thanks to a reform by the Swiss government. This amount is withdrawn before border crossers receive any refunds.
How much is the total financial compensation for Italian border workers?
The total financial compensation for border workers from Italy amounts to 120 million francs. This amount is subject to change based on economic growth and represents an important aspect of the cross-border agreements between Switzerland and Italy.
