Key facts

  • What: Living in Aymavilles and working in Valais. - When: Currently, with the new cross-border agreement effective from January 1, 2024. - Where: Aymavilles, Italy, and Valais, Switzerland. - Who: Cross-border commuters residing in Italy and working in Switzerland. - Amount: Allowance of €10,000 for new cross-border commuters.

The new cross-border agreement, signed on December 23, 2020, and effective from January 1, 2024, has introduced new rules for cross-border commuters. New cross-border commuters benefit from an allowance of €10,000, while existing cross-border commuters have a transitional regime until 2033 with an exemption of €7,500. This agreement aims to avoid double taxation and simplify taxation for cross-border commuters.

Travel times

Travel times between Aymavilles and Valais vary significantly depending on the chosen mode of transport. By car, the journey can take about 45 minutes, while by train it can range from 1 hour to 1 hour and 30 minutes, depending on intermediate stops. For those who prefer the bus, travel time can range from 1 hour to 1 hour and 40 minutes.

Cost of living

The cost of living in Aymavilles is generally lower compared to Valais. For example, the rent for a two-bedroom apartment can cost around €600 per month in Aymavilles, while the same apartment in Sion, Valais, could cost over €1,000. Energy costs and food prices are also significantly lower in Italy.

What to Consider Before Moving

Before moving to Aymavilles to work in Valais, it is important to consider several factors. First, you need to evaluate travel times and available transportation. For example, the journey from Aymavilles to Sion, one of the main cities in Valais, can take about 1 hour and 30 minutes by car, while the train can take up to 2 hours, depending on connections. Additionally, it is important to make a thorough financial plan to manage the cost of living and any unexpected expenses. The cost of living in Ticino is generally higher than in other Swiss regions, with rents ranging from 1,500 to 2,500 Swiss francs per month for a two-bedroom apartment. Finally, it is advisable to inform yourself about tax regulations and the rights of cross-border commuters to avoid future problems. For example, cross-border commuters residing in Ticino are subject to special taxation, with rates that can reach up to 35% for the highest incomes.

Step-by-Step Procedure

1. Evaluate travel times and available transportation.

  • Car: About 1 hour and 30 minutes from Aymavilles to Sion.
  • Train: Up to 2 hours, depending on connections.
  • Bus: Available, but with variable travel times.

2. Make a thorough financial plan.

  • Cost of living: Rents between 1,500 and 2,500 Swiss francs per month for a two-bedroom apartment.
  • Unexpected expenses: Reserve at least 10% of your monthly income.

3. Inform yourself about tax regulations and the rights of cross-border commuters.

  • Taxation: Rates up to 35% for the highest incomes.
