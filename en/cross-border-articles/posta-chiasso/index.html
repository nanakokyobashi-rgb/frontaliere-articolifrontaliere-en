Frequently Asked Questions
What are the requirements to use the new service?
Border workers will simply have to go to the Chiasso post office and present their documentation.
How can I request information about the service?
Border workers can request information about the service and the requirements necessary to use it by presenting themselves at the Chiasso post office.
In which location is the new border postal service available?
The new service is initially available at Posta di Chiasso. Subsequently, it will be extended to all Swiss Post branches in Ticino, including important centres such as Lugano and Bellinzona. This ensures broad coverage for border crossers wishing to send or receive correspondence between Switzerland and Italy.
What benefits does the new Chiasso Mail service offer border crossers in terms of tax residency and taxes?
The main benefit is the ability to send and receive letters and parcels between Switzerland and Italy without the need for a Swiss tax residence. This eliminates the obligation to pay taxes on shipments to Switzerland, ensuring greater convenience and flexibility than traditional postal services for cross-border workers.
Since when is the new service for border workers operational and how many workers will be able to use it?
The service has been operational since 1 January 2024. It is estimated that around 5,000 border crossers will benefit from this initiative. The management is entrusted to Swiss Post, in agreement with the Municipality of Chiasso, to simplify the exchange of correspondence and parcels between those who work in Switzerland and reside in Italy.
