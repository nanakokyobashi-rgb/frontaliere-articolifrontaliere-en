Amidst the Italian banking risk game, the solidity of accounts and growth become the first line of defense against potential external assaults. This is the message from Bper Banca, through its CEO Gianni Franco Papa, who spoke at the 'Merger & Acquisition Summit 2026' organized by Il Sole 24 Ore. The metaphor of the 'wolves,' used by the main shareholder Unipol, was recalled by Papa to emphasize that when one becomes appealing to competitors, the real protection is not traditional defense but comes from market capitalization. The more a bank performs, the more costly and complex any attempt at a takeover becomes.

Consolidation context

Italy, historically fragmented, is evolving towards a European model based on a few large groups. According to Papa, the goal is clear: the country needs to reach three or four large national players capable of supporting the economy and competing internationally. Currently, there are two in Italy: Intesa Sanpaolo and UniCredit. Over the last 30 years, the number of banks has decreased in many countries, while the average size of institutions has increased and large diversified groups have emerged. In Italy, by the end of 2024, the banking system had 134 intermediaries (53 groups and 81 individual banks), a significant decrease from the 510 in 2014.

Operational details

Practical Analysis: Implications for Cross-Border Workers

The growth and stability of Italian banks can have a direct impact on cross-border workers who work in Ticino and reside in Italy. In particular, bancassurance can offer more diversified and cost-effective insurance products, facilitating the management of financial risks. Moreover, the concentration of the banking sector could lead to a greater offer of cross-border services, facilitating financial transactions between Italy and Switzerland.

Before/After Comparison

Before the concentration of the banking sector, cross-border workers could face difficulties in managing their savings and investments between the two countries. With the increase in the average size of banking institutions, it is likely that the services offered will become more integrated and convenient. For example, the possibility of having a current account in Italy and Switzerland with coordinated services can simplify the management of personal finances.

Concrete Scenarios

A cross-border worker who works in Ticino and resides in Italy could benefit from insurance products offered by bancassurance, such as life insurance or health insurance. These products can be more cost-effective and easier to manage compared to those offered by traditional insurance companies. Furthermore, the concentration of the banking sector could lead to a greater offer of investment services, facilitating long-term financial planning.
