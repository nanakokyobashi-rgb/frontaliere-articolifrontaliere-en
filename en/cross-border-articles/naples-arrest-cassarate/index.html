On October 21, in Cassarate, a 62-year-old Neapolitan man was arrested who, in the documents of the Italian proceedings, is described as essential to the functioning of Pero’s “shadow bank,” dismantled in the Milan area. The reconstruction concerns a system in which, under the direction of ‘ndrangheta and camorra, funds from drug trafficking and tax evasion were allegedly laundered.

The path of the money

The path indicated in the documents is divided into several stages, but three steps are explicitly set out. First came the laundering through the issuing of false invoices. Then the sums were deposited into foreign accounts in Luxembourg. Finally, from those accounts, the money was diverted to digital wallets. The sequence distinguishes the financial starting point, the banking transfer and the digital destination.

According to investigators, the 62-year-old was involved in the operational part of the circuit. He handled the management of financial flows, opened the accounts and subsequently converted the money into cryptocurrencies. During the period from July 2025 to March 2026, approximately 50 million euros allegedly passed through his management. The account also links these movements to companies attributable to him: one based in Lugano, in the Canton of Ticino, and another active north of the Gotthard in the virtual currencies sector.

In the case, Cassarate and Lugano have different but both documented functions: the former is the place of the arrest; the latter is the headquarters of one of the companies mentioned. Luxembourg appears in relation to the foreign accounts from which, according to the reconstruction, the sums were allegedly diverted to digital wallets. The role described for the 62-year-old is limited to managing the flows, opening the accounts and converting the money into cryptocurrencies. The figure of approximately 50 million remains associated with the period from July 2025 to March 2026, again according to investigators.

Recurring elements across investigations

In Lugano, the past of another figure identified as central to the affair also emerges. He is a 51-year-old man of Calabrian origin, one of the three people at the head of the «shadow bank». According to the report, he had already been involved in Operation «Aemilia», carried out more than ten years ago against the ’Ndrangheta in Emilia-Romagna and characterized by significant links to Ticino. Linked to the Crotone-based Arena clan, the man had facilitated the reinvestment of the clan’s illicit proceeds through a network of false invoices.

The Arzo company
