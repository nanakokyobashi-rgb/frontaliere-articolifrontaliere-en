Safety in Bellinzona: motion to improve quality of life (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 8 May 2026

Karim Spinelli (PLR) presents motion to strengthen security and institutional presence in sensitive areas of Bellinzona

Context

In brief

  • Motion to strengthen security in Bellinzona
  • Institutional presence in sensitive areas
  • Pilot projects to improve livability

Key facts

  • What: Motion to improve security and quality of life
  • When: 8 May 2026
  • Where: Bellinzona, Ticino
  • Who: Karim Spinelli (Plr), Municipal Council
  • Amount: Not specified

The motion presented by Karim Spinelli, on behalf of the Plr group in the Municipal Council of Bellinzona, aims to strengthen security and quality of life in the city. The proposal focuses on a balance between institutional presence, care of the territory, and security, benefiting all neighborhoods. In particular, there is a perceived less regular presence of proximity services in various neighborhoods, while in central areas such as Bellinzona Station and viale Stazione, specific situations of discomfort and a decrease in the livability of public spaces are reported.

Proposed measures

The motion invites the executive to evaluate and adopt measures in the most sensitive areas, particularly in the Station area and along viale Stazione. These measures should be aimed at an adequate strengthening of territorial presence and the quality of interventions compared to the current situation. Furthermore, it is proposed to involve social operators or mediators if appropriate. In addition to these measures, the Municipality is invited to evaluate one or more pilot projects in the same areas that integrate in a coordinated manner institutional presence, interventions in public spaces, and social support measures.

Operational details

Implications for cross-border workers

The motion presented by Karim Spinelli could have a significant impact on the quality of life for cross-border workers in Bellinzona. Improving the safety and livability of public spaces can make the city more attractive to transnational workers, who often seek a safe and well-organized environment to live and work in. Furthermore, strengthening the dialogue between municipal authorities and local entities could facilitate the integration of cross-border workers into the community, promoting a sense of belonging and engagement.

Comparison with the previous situation

Before the presentation of this motion, the perception of insecurity in some areas of Bellinzona could have been an obstacle for cross-border workers considering the city as a place of residence. With the adoption of the proposed measures, there could be an improvement in the perception of safety, making Bellinzona a more attractive choice for transnational workers. Additionally, the introduction of pilot projects could offer new opportunities for participation and engagement for citizens, including cross-border workers.

Future scenarios

If the motion is accepted, a series of initiatives aimed at improving the safety and livability of public spaces could be implemented. For example, mediation and social support services could be introduced to address specific situations of discomfort. Furthermore, the involvement of neighborhood commissions could lead to greater transparency and participation in the management of local policies, directly benefiting cross-border workers who reside or work in Bellinzona.

Key points

What to do for cross-border workers

For cross-border workers who live or work in Bellinzona, it's important to stay informed about the initiatives and pilot projects that will be introduced. Participating in periodic consultations and neighborhood commission meetings can offer the opportunity to express their needs and concerns, actively contributing to improving the quality of life in the city. Additionally, cross-border workers can report situations of discomfort or suggest improvements through the communication channels made available by the Municipality.

Step-by-step procedure

1. Stay informed: Follow local news and Municipality announcements to stay updated on current initiatives. 2. Participate: Attend neighborhood commission meetings and periodic consultations. 3. Report: Use the communication channels provided to report situations of discomfort or suggest improvements. 4. Collaborate: Collaborate with social workers and mediators to address specific situations of discomfort.

Useful tools

To delve deeper into the information and calculate the impact of the new measures on their personal situation, cross-border workers can use the salary calculator available on the site. This tool allows them to calculate their net salary and better understand the impact of the new regulations on their economic situation.

Conclusion

The motion presented by Karim Spinelli represents an important opportunity to improve the quality of life in Bellinzona, directly benefiting cross-border workers who work and live in the city. Actively participating in initiatives and consultations can help create a safer and more welcoming environment for everyone.
