Frequently Asked Questions
How is the center of Lavena Ponte Tresa transformed?
The center of Lavena Ponte Tresa is turning into a welcoming and vivable place thanks to the idea of involving shops in the care of public greenery.
What is the expected impact on tourism in Lavena Ponte Tresa following the transformation of the old town?
The transformation should increase the tourist attraction, improving the experience of guests and potentially increasing local visits and purchases.
How can I, as a trader, participate in the maintenance of green in Lavena Ponte Tresa?
Traders can join the “Verde Condiviso” program of the Municipality, dedicating two hours a week to cleaning and irrigation. The mayor provides waterers, gloves and an online calendar. Participants receive a 10% discount on the land employment tax for the following year.
What are the costs and funding provided for the installation of water and flower beds in the centre?
The project provided an investment of 30,000 euros, covered 70% by municipal funds and 30% by cross-border contributions Switzerland-Italy. The money was intended for the purchase of 5 watermelons, 12 flower beds and the compensation of two laying workers, completed in June 2026.
Will the renewed green area affect the availability of parking spaces for frontiers?
The redevelopment has freed five parking spaces to create two new pedestrian areas and three bicycle columns. A temporary parking space of 20 places near Piazza Gramsci has been added to the front doors, available from 1 July 2026.
