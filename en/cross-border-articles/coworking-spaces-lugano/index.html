Tax implications

For cross-border commuters working in Switzerland and residing in Italy, it is important to consider the tax implications. Withholding tax on earned income is only withheld in Switzerland, while Italy avoids double taxation with the tax credit (EC framework of 730).

Double taxation agreement

The Italy-Switzerland double taxation agreement, signed on 9 December 1976, regulates the taxation of income for cross-border commuters. It is important to be aware of this convention in order to avoid double taxation and benefit from tax rebates.

What are the Italian personal income tax rates for border workers?
The Italian IRPEF rates are 23% up to €28'000, 35% between €28'001 and €50,000, and 43% over €50,000.
