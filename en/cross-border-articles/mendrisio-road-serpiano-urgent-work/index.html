Road problems in Ticino: a detailed analysis

The road that connects Mendrisio to Serpiano is one of the most critical stretches of the Ticino road network. This street is frequented by thousands of motorists every day, especially during the summer season, when tourism reaches its peak. Heavy traffic and the transit of heavy timber transport vehicles have further aggravated the situation.

According to official data from the Ticino Department of Transport, more than 1.5 million vehicles travelled this route in 2022, an increase of 20% compared to the previous year. This increase in traffic has led to an increase in stresses on bridges and roadblocks, which were built in the 1960s and 1970s.

Urgent work must be done on the road leading to Serpiano

The Municipality of Mendrisio has submitted a request for credit to finance the safety of the roads and the three bridges along the road that leads to Serpiano. The request was submitted following the analysis of traffic and road safety data, which highlighted the need for urgent interventions to prevent accidents and ensure the safety of citizens.

The road leading to Serpiano is an important artery for the municipalities of Mendrisio, Capolago and Maroggia, and its safety is essential to ensure the mobility of citizens and tourists. According to 2022 data, the road saw a 15% increase in traffic over the previous year, with a peak of 5,000 vehicles per day during peak hours.

The credit request submitted by the Municipality of Mendrisio provides for the allocation of 1.2 million francs to finance the security works. This amount has been calculated taking into account the costs of maintenance and restoration of the roads and the three bridges, as well as the costs of personnel and materials necessary for the execution of the works.

Urgent work on the road leading to Serpiano

The Municipality of Mendrisio has requested a credit of 1.2 million francs for the safety of the roads and the three bridges that connect the town of Serpiano to the rest of the territory. The road was subjected to urgent work in 2023, but normal maintenance is no longer sufficient to guarantee vehicle traffic safety.

The situation has worsened due to the flow of summer traffic generated by tourists and heavy vehicles for the transport of timber. According to data from the Municipality of Mendrisio, in 2023 there were more than 5,000 vehicles a day that traveled the road, an increase of 20% compared to the previous year.

The flow of traffic is particularly intense during the summer season, when the Hotel Serpiano is fully occupied. The Municipality of Mendrisio has decided to request a credit of CHF 1.2 million to ensure the safety of the hotel's residents and guests.

The construction site should be active in conjunction with the closure of the Hotel Serpiano, from November 2026 to March 2027. The work of securing the roads and bridges will be carried out by a specialised company, which will be chosen through a call for tenders.
