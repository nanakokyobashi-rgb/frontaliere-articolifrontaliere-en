Two arrests in one night in Aargau: a Tunisian and a Moroccan (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 May 2026

The Aargau cantonal police arrested two men in two different municipalities in the night between Friday and Saturday.

Context

In brief

  • Two arrests in one night in Aargau
  • A Tunisian and a Moroccan involved
  • Both asylum seekers, one in Switzerland and one in Germany

Key facts

  • What: Two arrests
  • When: Night between Friday and Saturday, May 2, 2026
  • Where: Schinznach Dorf and Döttingen, Aargau
  • Who: Aargau Cantonal Police
  • Amount: Not specified

In the night between Friday and Saturday, the Aargau Cantonal Police made two arrests in two different municipalities. In Schinznach Dorf, a surveillance camera recorded a man entering a private property at 4:00 AM. Residents immediately alerted the police, who found a 38-year-old Tunisian asylum seeker in possession of a moped. The man was arrested on suspicion of attempted theft.

A few hours later, in Döttingen, a resident found a man sleeping in a shed. The police intervened and arrested a 37-year-old Moroccan, registered as an asylum seeker in Germany. The man claimed to have ingested chemical products for pest control and was taken to the hospital for examination. In the end, he was handed over to the prosecution for trespassing.

These arrests raise questions about the living and working conditions of cross-border workers and asylum seekers in the region. With the increasing number of cross-border workers employed in Switzerland and residing in Italy, it is important to understand the practical implications of such events.
