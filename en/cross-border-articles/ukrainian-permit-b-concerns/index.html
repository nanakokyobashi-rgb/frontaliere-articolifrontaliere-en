Ukrainians with B permit, concerns arise (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 27 May 2026

In Lugano, the automatic transition of Ukrainian refugees with S protection status to a B permit in 2027 may raise concerns.

Context

In brief

Operational details

Analysis of the Situation

The shift from an S protection status to a B permit represents a significant change for Ukrainian refugees in Switzerland. This change could have important implications for their integration and for public resources.

Social Impact

The integration of Ukrainian refugees into Swiss society is a complex process that requires adequate resources. The shift to a B permit could influence their ability to access public services and integrate into the labor market.

Economic Concerns

Economic concerns are mainly related to the transfer of social assistance costs. This transfer could represent a significant burden for the Canton and municipalities, which will have to manage these new expenses.

Need for a Social Plan

The preparation of a social plan is essential to address the changes that this shift may bring. The plan should include measures to support the integration of refugees into the labor market and access to public services.

Next Steps

The Municipality of Lugano will have to provide concrete answers to the concerns raised by Wicht. The city will have to work to create a welcoming environment and support the integration of Ukrainian refugees.

Key points

What to do To cope with the transition from Protection Status S to Permit B, Ukrainian refugees and local authorities will have to work together. Here are some steps that might be helpful:

Inquire - Ukrainian refugees should inform themselves about the implications of switching to a B permit. - Local authorities should provide clear and accessible information.

Planning for the Future - Ukrainian refugees should plan their future in Switzerland, considering the new rules. - Local authorities should have social plans in place to support integration.

Useful Tools - Citizens can use tools such as salary calculators or payroll simulators to better understand their status. - How-to guides can help refugees navigate the system.

Useful Contacts - Ukrainian refugees can contact local authorities or support organizations for help. - Official websites can provide up-to-date information and useful resources.
