Frontiers in Ticino: salary requirements and tax rules

💡 Strait requirements: For border workers, the Ticino Region has established some salary requirements. For example, for an annual salary of €50,000, the limit of the monthly net salary requirement is €3,750. If your salary is higher, you must pay International Income Tax (IRI) in your jurisdiction of residence and in your jurisdiction of employment.

📌 Tax Rules: The Italian-Swiss Convention of 1976 avoids double taxation with the use of the tax credit (EC framework of 730). There are several phases of application:

1. Step A: You can use the tax credit for the first time if your salary is less than €15,000 gross per month and you have not yet used any other tax credits.

2. Step B: If your salary exceeds €15,000 gross per month and your total income does not exceed €20,000 gross per month, you can use the tax credit.

Title: "Border Ticino school teacher: requirements, tax credits and useful resources"

Requirements for teaching in Switzerland: The Federal Law on Vocational Training sets out the access requirements for teaching in Switzerland. To teach in Switzerland, the teacher must have a professional qualification recognised in Switzerland or in a third country and appropriate training. Official certification of professional qualification and training can be requested from the State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO).

Tax credit (EC framework of 730): For tuition in Switzerland, the tax credit (EC framework 730) is available. This tax credit provides a 30% refund on expenses incurred for teaching in Switzerland. To benefit from the tax credit, the teacher must submit the application to the Federal Statistical Office (BFS) and submit the required documentation.
