Context

The figure of refunds paid by Ticino to Italy increased to 112 million francs, according to the source. This represents an increase of 4% over the previous year. The refunds were paid to the old border workers, who are taxed at 100% of the taxable amount.

This increase is due to the fact that many border workers have continued to work in the agricultural and hotel sector, despite the economic crisis. In fact, 70% of border workers active in Ticino work in the agricultural sector, while 20% work in the hotel sector. This is also confirmed by the Municipality of Lugano, which noted a 15% increase in border workers registered in the municipal register.

The legislation governing refunds to frontier workers is the federal law of 10 October 1955, which provides for the return of a part of the taxable amount for workers who have carried out cross-border work. Under this law, refunds are calculated on the basis of gross income and are subject to a number of conditions.

For example, if a frontier worker has a gross income of CHF 50,000, the rebate will be CHF 10,000. However, if the frontier worker has a gross income of CHF 100,000, the rebate will be CHF 20,000. This means that border crossers with a higher income will receive a higher rebate.

Operational details

The new tax agreement signed between Italy and Switzerland in 2020 led to an increase in taxes paid in Switzerland. The new frontier workers in Switzerland pay taxes at source to the extent of 80%. This means that for every euro earned in Switzerland, Italian employees are required to pay 0.80 Swiss francs in taxes.

According to estimates by the Revenue Agency, in 2022 Italians working in Switzerland paid over 112 million euros in taxes. This amount represents an increase of 15% over the previous year. Most of these taxes were paid by border workers working in municipalities such as Lugano, Mendrisio and Bellinzona.

The tax agreement between Italy and Switzerland provides that taxes are paid in Switzerland, but that Italy can recover part of these sums through the withholding system. However, in order to recover these sums, Italy must submit a formal request to Switzerland by 31 December of each year.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who are the old frontiersmen?
Old border workers are those who obtained a work permit in Switzerland before 17 July 2023.
What is the difference between new and old frontiersmen?
In Switzerland, new frontier workers pay taxes at source to the extent of 80%, while old frontier workers are taxed at 100% of the taxable amount.
Why were the refunds paid?
Refunds were paid to make up the difference between the taxes paid in Switzerland and those paid in Italy.
