Four gas stations in trouble: displayed prices different from actual ones

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 May 2026

The Guardia di Finanza of Como discovered irregularities in four fuel distributors in Ticino. Here's what changes for cross-border workers.

Context

In brief

  • Four gas stations in Ticino fined for price irregularities
  • Displayed prices different from those applied
  • Failure to notify the Ministry

Key facts

  • What: Irregularities in fuel distributors
  • When: Recent checks
  • Where: Territory under the Como Group
  • Who: Guardia di Finanza of Como
  • Amount: Not specified

The Provincial Command of the Guardia di Finanza of Como has intensified checks on fuel distributors in the area, in response to recent price increases recorded nationally and linked to international geopolitical tensions. The initiative follows the guidelines of the General Command of the Corps. In the territory under the Como Group, the checks have led to the detection of several violations against four fuel distributors. Among the irregularities found are the discrepancy between the displayed prices and those actually applied, the failure to visibly display the differential between 'served' and 'self' prices, and two omissions in communicating price changes to the Ministry.

The activity is part of a broader surveillance and prevention device implemented by the Comasque Fiamme Gialle, aimed at protecting consumers and ensuring a competitive market in a phase characterized by strong volatility in energy markets. The frequent fluctuations in pump prices directly affect both the purchasing power of families and the production costs of companies. In this context, the action of the Corps is aimed at intercepting and countering any speculative phenomena, ensuring that price formation is not altered by distortive practices or hidden maneuvers.

Implications for cross-border workers

The irregularities found in the gas stations in Ticino can have a significant impact on cross-border workers who daily cross the border to go to work. The discrepancy between the displayed prices and those applied can indeed influence the cost of fuel, a crucial element for those who have to bear high transport costs. Furthermore, the failure to communicate price changes to the Ministry can result in a lack of transparency that penalizes consumers, including many cross-border workers who daily use the fuel distributors in the territory.

What to do in case of irregularities

If a cross-border worker should find irregularities in the prices applied by the fuel distributors, it is important to immediately report the situation to the competent authorities. The Guardia di Finanza of Como has implemented a surveillance and prevention device to protect consumers, and citizens' reports can be fundamental to countering speculative phenomena and ensuring a competitive market.

Comparisons and scenarios

In a context of strong volatility in energy markets, it is fundamental that fuel prices are transparent and correctly communicated. Cross-border workers who use the fuel distributors in Ticino must be aware of the irregularities found and the actions taken by the authorities to protect consumers. In this way, it will be possible to avoid speculative phenomena and guarantee a competitive market that meets the needs of all consumers, including cross-border workers.

Key points

What to do in case of irregularities

If a cross-border worker notices irregularities in the prices charged by fuel distributors, it is important to report the situation to the competent authorities immediately. The Guardia di Finanza of Como has set up a surveillance and prevention system to protect consumers, and citizens' reports can be crucial to combat speculative practices and ensure a competitive market.

Reporting procedure

1. Identify the irregularity: Verify if the displayed price corresponds to the one charged. 2. Gather evidence: Photograph or record the displayed price and the one charged. 3. Contact the authorities: Report the irregularity to the Guardia di Finanza of Como or the Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy. 4. Provide details: Include precise information about the distributor, the date, and the time of the irregularity.

Useful tools

To report irregularities in fuel prices, you can use the "Osservaprezzi carburanti" portal of the Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy. This tool allows you to communicate the prices charged with updates at least weekly, ensuring constant monitoring.
