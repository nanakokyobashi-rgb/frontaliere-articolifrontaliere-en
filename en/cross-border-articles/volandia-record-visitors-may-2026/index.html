Volandia: record visitors during May long weekend (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 May 2026

Over 2,200 visitors in three days, 400 helicopter flight baptisms. The flight museum in Somma Lombardo confirms its success

Context

In brief

  • Volandia surpasses 2,200 visitors during the May bank holiday
  • 400 helicopter flight baptisms with Eliossola
  • The museum attracts families and curious young people

Key facts

  • What: Record number of visitors at Volandia
  • When: May bank holiday 2026
  • Where: Somma Lombardo, near Malpensa
  • Who: Volandia, flight museum
  • Amount: Over 2,200 visitors in three days
  • Activity: Helicopter flight baptisms
  • Collaboration: With Eliossola
  • Passengers: 400 people
  • Route: Panoramic view of Malpensa and the Ticino area

The early May bank holiday brought a record influx to Volandia, the Park and Flight Museum located in the historic Caproni workshops near Malpensa Airport. In just three days, over 2,200 visitors passed through the gates of the facility, a number that testifies to the growing appeal of the museum. The success was driven by the initiative of helicopter flight baptisms, organized in collaboration with Eliossola. More than 400 people had the opportunity to experience the thrill of vertical flight, admiring from a privileged perspective the runways of Malpensa and the natural area of Ticino. This type of experience, as emphasized by the museum, is particularly appreciated not only by aviation enthusiasts but also by those who want to overcome their fear of flying.

Operational details

The success of Volandia during the May bank holiday is not an isolated case, but the result of a well-established strategy aimed at offering unique and engaging experiences. The flight museum, located in Somma Lombardo, has managed to attract an increasingly wide audience thanks to the combination of culture and entertainment. The helicopter flight baptisms initiative, in particular, has represented a real springboard for the museum, allowing it to reach even those who, in the past, had never considered the idea of flying.

An unforgettable experience

The helicopter flight baptism is an activity that offers an unforgettable experience. Passengers have the opportunity to admire from a unique perspective the runways of Malpensa, with the giants of the sky in motion, and to fly over the control tower and the naturalistic area of the Ticino. This type of experience not only attracts aviation enthusiasts but also those who wish to overcome their fear of flying. The museum has indeed registered a significant increase in visitors who, thanks to this initiative, have been able to live a unique emotion and overcome their fears.

A cross-sectional audience

The success of Volandia is not limited to aviation enthusiasts. The museum has managed to attract an increasingly wide audience, composed of families with children and curious young people. The formula that combines the conservation of aeronautical culture with experiential entertainment proves to be winning. The museum offers a series of activities and laboratories designed to involve the youngest and make them discover the world of flight in a fun and interactive way.

Key points

For cross-border workers living in Ticino and working in Lombardy, Volandia is an ideal destination for a day of relaxation and fun. The flight museum offers a series of activities and workshops designed to engage children and introduce them to the world of flight in a fun and interactive way. Additionally, the helicopter flight baptism represents a unique experience that allows you to admire the Malpensa runways and the naturalistic area of the Ticino from a privileged perspective.

How to reach Volandia

Volandia is easily reachable for cross-border workers living in Ticino. The museum is located in Somma Lombardo, just a few kilometers from the Italian-Swiss border. To reach the museum, you can use your car or public transportation. By car, you can take the A8 highway towards Malpensa and exit at Somma Lombardo. Alternatively, you can take the train to Somma Lombardo station and then continue on foot or by local bus.

Opening hours and tickets

Volandia is open every day, with hours varying depending on the season. During the May bank holiday, the museum recorded a record attendance, but it is also possible to visit the museum on weekdays. Tickets can be purchased online or directly at the museum's ticket office. For cross-border workers living in Ticino, it is possible to take advantage of discounts and special promotions.
