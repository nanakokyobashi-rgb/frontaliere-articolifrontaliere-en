Kastritis: difficult moment will help us grow, maximum confidence in the group

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 8 October 2026

Varese loses his Champions League debut in Murcia against UCAM Murcia, with the absences of McDowell and Della Valle; Kastritis highlights the defensive intensity and confidence in the group, next game in three days.

The coach's reading

Murcia gave Pallacanestro Varese a first cup match to forget. The red and white team was overwhelmed by UCAM Murcia, while coach Ioannis Kastritis chose to start with compliments to his opponents, recognizing coach Alonso and the Murcia team for a great performance.

However, in the results of the Varese match, Kastritis saved the work done in the defensive half of the pitch. According to the coach, the players gave their all, especially in the defensive phase, with many very aggressive elements ready to fight for every ball. The evaluation concerns the attitude shown during the match, even if it does not erase the debut defeat.

The coach described Varese as a group of players who work hard every day. The team is new and still has to build its mechanisms, growing possession after possession. The construction of the group is therefore indicated as a path that is still open, to be developed through daily work and the experience accumulated in matches.

Kastritis recognized the weight of the absences of McDowell and Della Valle, defined as "a significant factor". At the same time, he reiterated that Varese Basketball does not want and will not make excuses. The coach's position is clear: training difficulties and absences are part of the situation, but must not become a justification for the performance offered.

Even criticism, according to Kastritis, is normal and right at this moment. However, they must have a concrete utility and be productive, so as to push the team out of their comfort zone. The coach accompanied this request with a declaration of full confidence in each of his players, keeping the focus of the discussion on the group and the work to be completed.

Operational details

The reactions after the race

Matteo Librizzi indicated the analysis of errors as the first step after the defeat in Murcia. The Varese basketball point guard explained that the team must fully understand what didn't work, because the approach to the first match of the tournament was not the desired one. His assessment therefore concerns the start of the match and the way in which the group approached their debut in the Champions League.

Librizzi did not hide his self-criticism, but linked the analysis to the possibility of reacting already in the next matches. The team, he said, is "perfectly aware that it can do much better." The goal indicated by the point guard is to work to identify errors and transform that reading into an immediate improvement in subsequent games.

On the UCAM Murcia side, Sito Alonso described a positive evening for the team's first home victory in the Champions League. The coach attributed the result to the intensity with which Murcia approached the match and the desire to limit the main offensive points of reference of the Varese team. The preparation for the match therefore focused on the attacking solutions considered most important for Varese.

Alonso also highlighted the return to the pitch of Kaiser Gates, who was absent due to a very long and serious injury. The coach chose to give him space at the start of the match to allow him to find his rhythm. The response from a physical point of view was considered positive, so much so that Alonso defined recovery as a fundamental element for Murcia.

The UCAM coach recalled that he knew Varese well and that the Italian team lacked high-level players. However, he specified that Murcia also had to deal with the absence of key elements. The reading of the opponents therefore did not stop at the red and white difficulties, but also considered the situation of the Spanish team.

For Alonso, in the Champions League the points difference has a decisive impact on the group. Hence the request to maintain constant defensive aggression for the entire 40 minutes, without drops. His explanation distinguishes two behaviors: when Murcia is not aggressive it becomes vulnerable; when he manages to impose his physicality, he can compete with anyone.

Key points

The work indicated by Kastritis

The message addressed to Varese Basketball already looks to the continuation of the season. Kastritis defined the period the team went through as difficult, but added that in the future it could be considered extremely valuable for the growth of the group. The current difficulty is therefore inserted into a broader path, without separating it from the work necessary to improve.

The condition indicated by the coach is to all remain united and solid. The work must continue every day, with a double objective: improving individual players and helping the team as a whole grow. Kastritis did not separate the two dimensions, because in his message individual and collective development proceed together.

The coach also rejected the idea of ​​immediate perfection. Expecting to already be perfect in October, he explained, would be unrealistic. The defeat doesn't make him happy, but his assessment looks at the ability to face difficulties by remaining united and building the path day after day.

The next match is already indicated shortly: a new match awaits Varese in three days. Kastritis linked this commitment to the need to remain united and continue pushing, without interrupting the work started. The calendar therefore offers the team an immediate opportunity to apply what emerged from the words of the coach and point guard.

A season of ups and downs

Kastritis recalled that the story of a season is made up of high and low moments. The point, in his perspective, is not to ignore the negative phase or declare oneself satisfied after a defeat. It's about facing difficulties with unity, maintaining faith in the players and continuing to build the path.

The follow-up indicated to Pallacanestro Varese therefore remains linked to three recurring words in the coach's statements: unity, work and growth. The match in Murcia opened a difficult moment, but the response required does not involve excuses or promises. It goes from daily work, from the analysis of errors and from the ability to remain solid in view of the next commitment.
