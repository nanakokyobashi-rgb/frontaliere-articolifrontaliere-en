COF Lanzo Hospital has confirmed its support for the Global Hand Hygiene Campaign, which will take place on May 5, 2026. This initiative, promoted by the World Health Organization (WHO) and the Ministry of Health, aims to raise awareness among healthcare workers and citizens about the importance of properly washing and sanitizing hands to prevent the spread of viruses, bacteria, and infections. Washing hands is a simple but fundamental gesture for patient safety and the protection of vulnerable people.

The healthcare director of COF Lanzo Hospital, Gianluca Merlano, commented: “Hand hygiene is a fundamental tool for health protection, both in the hospital and in the daily lives of each of us. Supporting this awareness campaign means reiterating the importance of individual and collective responsibility towards the prevention of the most common hospital and community-transmitted infections, such as influenza and Covid-19.”

Operational details

Practical Analysis: The Importance of Hand Hygiene

Hand hygiene is a fundamental practice for preventing infections, both in healthcare settings and in daily life. The Global Hand Hygiene Campaign, promoted by the WHO and the Ministry of Health, aims to raise awareness among healthcare workers and citizens about the importance of washing and properly disinfecting hands.

Why is it important to wash your hands

Washing your hands is a simple but effective gesture to prevent the spread of viruses, bacteria, and infections. According to experts, hand hygiene is one of the most powerful tools to reduce the risk of infections, both in hospitals and in everyday life. Adopting correct hygiene practices can protect not only your own health but also that of the people around you.

How to wash your hands correctly

To wash your hands correctly, it is important to follow some fundamental steps:

  • Wet your hands with running water
  • Apply soap and rub your hands together for at least 20 seconds
  • Rinse thoroughly with running water
  • Dry your hands with a clean towel
  • Use an alcohol-based hand sanitizer if it is not possible to wash your hands with water and soap

The importance of hand hygiene in the workplace

For cross-border workers who work in Switzerland, hand hygiene is an essential practice to ensure a safe and healthy work environment. Adopting correct hygiene practices can reduce the risk of infections and promote a safer work environment for everyone.

Key points

Concrete actions for cross-border workers

The Global Handwashing Day campaign represents an opportunity for cross-border workers to adopt correct hygiene practices and protect their own health and that of the people around them. Here are some concrete actions that can be taken:

1. Wash your hands regularly

Washing your hands is a simple but effective gesture to prevent the spread of viruses, bacteria, and infections. It is important to wash your hands before and after meals, after using public transport, upon returning home, and in any situation of shared contact.

2. Use hand sanitizers

If it is not possible to wash your hands with soap and water, it is advisable to use alcohol-based hand sanitizers. These products are effective in reducing the risk of infections and can be easily carried with you throughout the day.

3. Promote a culture of hygiene

Participating in the Global Handwashing Day campaign means promoting a culture of hygiene and raising awareness among the people around us about the importance of adopting correct hygiene practices. Sharing information and practical tips can help create a safer and healthier environment for everyone.

4. Participate in local initiatives

Many healthcare facilities and local communities organize initiatives to promote hand hygiene. Participating in these initiatives can be an effective way to learn new hygiene practices and contribute to public health promotion.
