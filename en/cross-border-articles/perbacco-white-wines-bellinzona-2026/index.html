PerBacco! White Wines in Bellinzona: Tastings and Music (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 May 2026

From May 8 to 10, 2026, Bellinzona celebrates Ticino's white wines with tastings, music, and vineyard tours.

Context

In brief

  • Wine tastings and music for Ticino white wines
  • Event from May 8 to 10, 2026 in Bellinzona
  • E-bike tour through the vineyards of Bellinzona
  • Participation: 20 CHF for the official glass, 50 CHF for the e-bike tour

Key facts

  • What: Event dedicated to Ticino white wines
  • When: May 8-10, 2026
  • Where: Historic center of Bellinzona
  • Who: Organized by PerBacco! Bellinzona
  • Cost: Official glass 20 CHF, e-bike tour 50 CHF
  • Regulations: In accordance with the Federal Law on Tobacco and Alcohol Tax (LITA), RS 641.3

Bellinzona is preparing to host “PerBacco! Che Bianchi!”, the event dedicated to newly bottled Ticino white wines, scheduled for May 8 to 10, 2026. For three days, the historic center will come alive with tastings, music, and initiatives designed for enthusiasts and curious visitors, in a convivial atmosphere that combines taste and territory. For over a century, Ticino has been synonymous with Merlot, but in recent years, white wines have gained space and recognition thanks to their elegance, freshness, and character. “PerBacco! Che Bianchi!” was born precisely to tell this evolution and offer the opportunity to discover the new vintages, expressions of a continuously transforming territory.

Operational details

Implications for cross-border workers

The event 'PerBacco! che Bianchi!' represents a unique opportunity for cross-border workers who work in Ticino and reside in Italy. The event, which will take place in Bellinzona from September 1 to 3, 2023, not only offers a moment of leisure and conviviality but also allows you to discover and appreciate Ticino's white wines, a sector that is continuously growing and gaining recognition. According to data from the Federal Customs Administration (FCA), in 2022 over 3.5 million liters of Ticino wine were exported, with a 15% increase compared to the previous year. For cross-border workers who love wine and good food, this event is an opportunity to explore new flavors and get to know the territory where they work better.

How to participate

To participate in the tastings and various activities, cross-border workers can purchase the official glass online or directly on site. The glass, which costs CHF 15, includes tastings at 20 wine and gastronomic stands. It is advisable to book in advance to ensure availability, especially for the e-bike tour which has a limited number of places (maximum 30 participants). Registrations for the tour, which costs CHF 50 and includes a guided visit to the cellars of Gambarogno and a final tasting, are available on the official event website (https://perbaccobellinzona.ch/perbacco-che-bianchi/).

Operational checklist

  • Buy the official glass (CHF 15)
  • Book the e-bike tour (CHF 50, maximum 30 places)
  • Check the tasting times (available on the official website)
  • Plan the trip (consider traffic and border crossing times)

Key points

Procedure to participate

To participate in the "PerBacco! che Bianchi!" event, cross-border workers can follow these steps:

1. Purchase the official glass: The official glass can be purchased online at eventfrog.ch for CHF 20, or directly at the event. The limited-edition glass is an exclusive souvenir that allows you to taste Ticino white wines elegantly and sustainably.

2. Register for the e-bike tour: To join the e-bike tour through the vineyards of Bellinzona, you need to register at PerBacco! Bellinzona. Registrations are limited to 50 participants per day, with a cost of CHF 40 per person. The tour, lasting approximately 3 hours, starts from Bellinzona and passes through the municipalities of Camorino, Isone, and Sant’Antonino, offering breathtaking views and tastings in local vineyards.

3. Plan your visit: Cross-border workers can plan their visit based on the event schedule, which takes place from September 15 to 17, 2023. You can participate for a single day or all three days, depending on your interests and commitments. For example, Friday is dedicated to local white wines, Saturday focuses on guided tastings and meetings with producers, while Sunday is reserved for concerts and family activities.

4. Discover the territory: The event offers the opportunity to discover the Ticino region and its white wines. Cross-border workers can take advantage of this occasion to explore new flavors and get to know the region where they work better. For example, the vineyards of Camorino are famous for Merlot, while Isone is renowned for its white wines such as Chardonnay and Pinot Grigio.
