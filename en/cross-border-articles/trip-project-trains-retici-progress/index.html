TRIP: mobility development between Lombardy and Graubünden (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 24 July 2026

The TRIP project aims to develop mobility between Lombardy and Graubünden.

Context

The Rhaetian Trains in Progress (TRIP) cross-border project aims to develop mobility between Lombardy and Graubünden. If the project for a railway connection between S-chanf in Lower Engadine and Livigno is to be carried out, no direct interventions are currently planned between Valposchiavo and Tirano (IT).

The TRIP is a cooperation project between the municipalities of Bellinzona, Locarno and Lugano, which aims to improve mobility and the connection between Switzerland and Italy. The project is based on the creation of a railway corridor connecting southern Switzerland with northern Italy.

According to plans, the railway connection between S-chanf and Livigno is expected to be completed by 2028. The project involves the construction of a new railway line that allows you to reach Livigno in about 30 minutes from S-chanf. This rail connection should be made with an amount of about CHF 150 million.

The TRIP also envisages the creation of a railway corridor linking southern Switzerland with northern Italy. This corridor should be realized with an amount of about 500 million francs.

Operational details

The TRIP project moves on an eminently strategic track. The objective is to define future scenarios for the development of cross-border mobility, through the analysis of opportunities for strengthening the network and possible new connections. The territory of the Canton of Ticino, located between Lombardy and Graubünden, is an ideal place for this project.

Cross-border mobility is a rapidly evolving sector, which requires careful planning and close collaboration between local authorities, regional authorities and operators in the sector. The TRIP project aims to provide a comprehensive and detailed picture of the opportunities and constraints for the development of cross-border mobility in the territory of the Canton of Ticino.

One of the main objectives of the TRIP project is to identify the areas of greatest impact and development for cross-border mobility. This will make it possible to define investment and implementation strategies to improve the quality of mobility and reduce travel times between the Canton's main urban and commercial centres.

A concrete example of the above is represented by the city of Bellinzona, the capital of the Canton of Ticino and an important commercial and cultural centre. Key points

The recently launched TRIP project involves the Province of Sondrio as leader and the Cantonal Office of Energy and Transport for the Swiss part. The Bernina Region expressed interest in the initiative and stressed the importance of strengthening cross-border dialogue and collaboration. This ambitious project aims to improve mobility and the connection between Lombardy and Graubünden, two regions historically linked by commercial and cultural relations.

The TRIP project focuses on three main areas: the creation of an efficient and sustainable transport network, the development of logistics infrastructure and the promotion of cooperation between the two regions. The Province of Sondrio and the Swiss Cantonal Office of Energy and Transport have already started working together to define the details of the project and establish a concrete action plan.

A concrete example of how the TRIP project can improve mobility between Lombardy and Graubünden is the creation of a new railway line connecting the city of Chiasso, Switzerland, with the city of Como, Italy. This rail connection could reduce travel times by more than an hour and improve the connection between the two cities, favouring the exchange of goods and people.
