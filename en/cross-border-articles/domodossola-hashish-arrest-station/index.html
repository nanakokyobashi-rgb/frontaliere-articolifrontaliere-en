Domodossola: arrest for a kilo of hashish in luggage

A Guardia di finanza check at Domodossola international station led to the arrest of a woman arriving from Milano Centrale. When officers asked her whether she had any goods or currency to declare, the passenger replied, “nothing to declare.”

Her nervousness nevertheless aroused the officers’ suspicions. They therefore decided to inspect the luggage more thoroughly, going beyond the initial answer given during the check.

From luggage to seizure

Inside the paper bag, they found ten blocks: tests identified them as hashish, with a total weight of one kilogram. According to estimates, a potential retail sale could have yielded over 10mila euros.

The operation was carried out by officers from the Domodossola Company of the Guardia di finanza, who were engaged in customs surveillance and economic control of the area at the international station. The activity is aimed in particular at combating illicit trafficking and the smuggling of narcotics.

The officers informed the public prosecutor on duty at the Public Prosecutor’s Office in Verbania of the discovery.

The check forms part of inspections conducted by the Guardia di finanza along the border and at international transit points, aimed at intercepting drug trafficking and other forms of illegality having a greater impact on public safety and public health.

As provided by law, the woman must be considered innocent until any conviction becomes final.

The economic figure should be read within its exact scope. Over 10mila euro is the estimate of what the hashish could have fetched if it had been put on the retail market. The source does not present that sum as money found or as an amount seized. The judicial stage also has a precise meaning. After the discovery, the Public Prosecutor's Office of Verbania was informed through the prosecutor on duty. The woman was arrested and taken to the Vercelli prison, but the text keeps the charge distinct from the final outcome of the proceedings: the presumption of innocence applies until any final, unappealable judgment.
