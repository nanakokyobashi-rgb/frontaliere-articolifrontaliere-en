Tre Valli Varesine: races and road closures

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 28 September 2026

From 3 to 6 October, Varese and the surrounding province host time trial, granfondo and professional races with significant traffic restrictions.

Context

In brief

  • Three days of racing between Saturday, October 3, and Tuesday, October 6
  • Saturday 22.4-kilometer time trial towards Ganna
  • Sunday granfondo and mediofondo in the Upper Varese area
  • Tuesday professional races from Busto Arsizio to Varese

Key facts

  • Dates → Saturday 3, Sunday 4, and Tuesday 6 October
  • Time trial → 22.4 kilometers
  • Granfondo → 126 kilometers
  • Women's Race → 137 kilometers
  • Men's Tre Valli → 200 kilometers

On Saturday, October 3, the great cycling program involving the province kicks off in Varese and runs until Tuesday, October 6. The calendar brings together the weekend of the Gran Fondo Tre Valli Varesine, which this year also serves as the European Granfondo Championship, and the two professional races: the men's Tre Valli Varesine and the Women's Race.

The three days have a different impact on traffic. The weekend mainly affects Valganna, Ceresio, the Luino area, and Valcuvia. On Tuesday, however, the disruptions are concentrated in Varese, along the city circuit of the professional races.

Saturday's program

The first event is the 22.4-kilometer individual time trial, scheduled for the afternoon. The start is set at the Bettole Hippodrome. From there, the route climbs along Viale Valganna and the state road 233, passes through Induno Olona, reaches the Ganna roundabout, and heads back toward Varese.

The roads affected by the route will remain closed from 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. Largo Martiri della Libertà, however, will be closed starting Friday at 1 p.m. These indications concern the first competitive event of the program and mark the start of the traffic changes related to the races.

On Saturday morning, the Tre Valli Gravel Experience is also scheduled. This is a non-competitive gravel ride, with departures between 8:30 a.m. and 9:30 a.m. from the Race Village on Via Sacco. The day's program therefore combines the time trial with an event open to those participating in the ride, keeping the organizational center on Via Sacco as a reference point.

The calendar thus gets into full swing with a route that starts from the Hippodrome area, crosses the Valganna route, and reaches Ganna before returning. During the same day, the closures affect both the time trial route and the area designated for the village and the events connected to the competition.

Operational details

Domenica 4 ottobre is the most extensive day of the cycling program in Alto Varesotto. Between 9 and 9.40, the Tre Valli Varesine granfondo, 126 kilometers long, and the mediofondo, approximately 100 kilometers long, set off from via Sacco.

Granfondo, mediofondo and return to Varese

The riders climb into Valganna and then descend toward Ceresio. The route crosses Valmarchirolo and Valcuvia before returning to Varese along the lake, passing through Gavirate and Calcinate del Pesce. The granfondo also includes an extension into Luinese and Valtravaglia, while the mediofondo follows the shorter route indicated in the program.

Outside Varese, the roads are closed as the riders pass and reopened after the end-of-race vehicle. In the city, closures begin at 5 in the center and along the exit toward viale Valganna. From 11, the roads on the return route are also affected. The restrictions last until 18, and the awards ceremony is scheduled for 13.30.

That same Sunday, Varese also hosts Strawoman. The 5-kilometer race and walk starts at 10.30 from Giardini Estensi, adding a city event to the day's calendar.

The sports program also includes Coppa Bernocchi, which starts and finishes in Legnano. The race, described as the oldest of the Trittico Lombardo races, is 177,8 kilometers long and starts at 12.30.

The heart of Coppa Bernocchi is the climb up Piccolo Stelvio, or Caramamma, between Castiglione Olona and Morazzone. The climb is repeated eight times. The finish is scheduled for the first time on via XX settembre in Legnano, in a window between 16.15 and 16.40.

The Sunday therefore brings together events with different distances and routes that pass through several areas of Varesotto. The granfondo and mediofondo take place between Valganna, Ceresio, Valmarchirolo, Valcuvia, Luinese and Valtravaglia; Coppa Bernocchi, on the other hand, links Legnano to the stretches between Castiglione Olona and Morazzone.

Key points

On Tuesday, October 6, the calendar moves on to the professional races. In the morning, the sixth Tre Valli Varesine Women's Race is scheduled, while in the afternoon, the 105th men's Tre Valli Varesine begins. Both involve the Varese city circuit, with different times and routes.

The women's race and the city circuit

The Women's Race measures 137 kilometers and starts at 9:15 a.m. from the Textile Museum in Busto Arsizio. The athletes enter Varese from the SP1 in Capolago around 10 a.m. From that point, they tackle six laps of the city circuit, including the Montello climb. The finish on Via Sacco is expected between 12 p.m. and 12:30 p.m.

In the afternoon, the men's race kicks off: 200 kilometers, 175 riders, and 25 teams, 17 of which are from the World Tour. The start is scheduled for 1 p.m. from Busto Arsizio. The riders enter Varese shortly after 1:30 p.m. and tackle the laps of the city circuit, including the Ronchi climb.

Following this section are two long laps that head out of the city towards Gavirate and tackle the Cinque Piante climb. The route then continues towards Barasso, Luvinate, and Casciago. The finish on Via Sacco is expected between 5:30 p.m. and 5:40 p.m.

Closures, buses, and schools

On the line section from Busto Arsizio, closures are limited to the passage of the race. In Varese, however, the bans along the circuit take effect 30 minutes before the entry of each race, i.e., around 9:20 a.m. and 1 p.m., and remain in force until the end of the race. Via Sacco will remain closed from 6 a.m. to 8 p.m.

The areas of Capolago, Schiranna, Masnago, Casbeno, Viale Aguggiari, Via Crispi, and the city center are affected. Buses that cross the route will be diverted or suspended from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. For local public transport lines, it is advised to consult the communications from the Local Public Transport Agency of Como, Lecco, and Varese. The local police may order further closures or diversions if necessary.

Schools are also affected by the ordinance signed by the mayor to allow the races to take place and ensure the safety of the route. Service is suspended in institutions located within the route or whose dismissal time coincides with the race. For other facilities, regular operation is expected, although in some cases there may be changes to dismissal times or difficulties in accessing the premises.

On all three days, those who need to travel by car would do well to leave early or avoid the areas along the route, taking into account the different closure times and the passage of the races.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the schedule of cycling competitions in Varese?
The programme runs from Saturday 3 to Tuesday 6 October. Saturday, October 3, there will be a 22.4-kilometer individual time trial starting from the Bettole Racecourse and the Tre Valli Gravel Experience. On Sunday, October 4, the Granfondo (126 km) and the Mediofondo (about 100 km) of the Tre Valli Varesine will take place, as well as the Strawoman (5 km) and the Bernocchi Cup (177.8 km). Tuesday 6 October will see the Women's Race (137 km) and the 105th Tre Valli Varesine men's race (200 km).
What are the main road closures in Varese?
Closures vary per day. Saturday, October 3, the streets of the stopwatch are closed from 11 am to 6 pm, while Largo Martiri della Libertà is closed from Friday to 1 pm. Sunday, October 4, in the city, closures begin at 5 am in the center and towards Viale Valganna, and from 11 am on the return roads, lasting until 6 pm. Tuesday, October 6, the prohibitions along the city circuit start 30 minutes before the start of each race and via Sacco is closed from 6 to 20.
How are public transport and schools affected in Varese?
On Tuesday 6 October, buses crossing the route of the professional race are diverted or suspended from 8 am to 8 pm. For local public transport lines, it is necessary to consult the communications of the local Public Transport Agency of Como, Lecco and Varese. Schools within the track or with exit times coinciding with the races are suspended, while for other facilities they are regularly planned, with possible changes to the exit times or difficulty in accessing them.
