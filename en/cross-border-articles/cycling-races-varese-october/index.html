Tre Valli Varesine: races and road closures (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 28 September 2026

From 3 to 6 October, Varese and the surrounding province host time trial, granfondo and professional races with significant traffic restrictions.

Operational details

Domenica 4 ottobre is the most extensive day of the cycling program in Alto Varesotto. Between 9 and 9.40, the Tre Valli Varesine granfondo, 126 kilometers long, and the mediofondo, approximately 100 kilometers long, set off from via Sacco.

Granfondo, mediofondo and return to Varese

The riders climb into Valganna and then descend toward Ceresio. The route crosses Valmarchirolo and Valcuvia before returning to Varese along the lake, passing through Gavirate and Calcinate del Pesce. The granfondo also includes an extension into Luinese and Valtravaglia, while the mediofondo follows the shorter route indicated in the program.

Outside Varese, the roads are closed as the riders pass and reopened after the end-of-race vehicle. In the city, closures begin at 5 in the center and along the exit toward viale Valganna. From 11, the roads on the return route are also affected. The restrictions last until 18, and the awards ceremony is scheduled for 13.30.

That same Sunday, Varese also hosts Strawoman. The 5-kilometer race and walk starts at 10.30 from Giardini Estensi, adding a city event to the day's calendar.

The sports program also includes Coppa Bernocchi, which starts and finishes in Legnano. The race, described as the oldest of the Trittico Lombardo races, is 177,8 kilometers long and starts at 12.30.

The heart of Coppa Bernocchi is the climb up Piccolo Stelvio, or Caramamma, between Castiglione Olona and Morazzone. The climb is repeated eight times. The finish is scheduled for the first time on via XX settembre in Legnano, in a window between 16.15 and 16.40.

The Sunday therefore brings together events with different distances and routes that pass through several areas of Varesotto. The granfondo and mediofondo take place between Valganna, Ceresio, Valmarchirolo, Valcuvia, Luinese and Valtravaglia; Coppa Bernocchi, on the other hand, links Legnano to the stretches between Castiglione Olona and Morazzone.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the schedule of cycling competitions in Varese?
The programme runs from Saturday 3 to Tuesday 6 October. Saturday, October 3, there will be a 22.4-kilometer individual time trial starting from the Bettole Racecourse and the Tre Valli Gravel Experience. On Sunday, October 4, the Granfondo (126 km) and the Mediofondo (about 100 km) of the Tre Valli Varesine will take place, as well as the Strawoman (5 km) and the Bernocchi Cup (177.8 km). Tuesday 6 October will see the Women's Race (137 km) and the 105th Tre Valli Varesine men's race (200 km).
What are the main road closures in Varese?
Closures vary per day. Saturday, October 3, the streets of the stopwatch are closed from 11 am to 6 pm, while Largo Martiri della Libertà is closed from Friday to 1 pm. Sunday, October 4, in the city, closures begin at 5 am in the center and towards Viale Valganna, and from 11 am on the return roads, lasting until 6 pm. Tuesday, October 6, the prohibitions along the city circuit start 30 minutes before the start of each race and via Sacco is closed from 6 to 20.
How are public transport and schools affected in Varese?
On Tuesday 6 October, buses crossing the route of the professional race are diverted or suspended from 8 am to 8 pm. For local public transport lines, it is necessary to consult the communications of the local Public Transport Agency of Como, Lecco and Varese. Schools within the track or with exit times coinciding with the races are suspended, while for other facilities they are regularly planned, with possible changes to the exit times or difficulty in accessing them.
