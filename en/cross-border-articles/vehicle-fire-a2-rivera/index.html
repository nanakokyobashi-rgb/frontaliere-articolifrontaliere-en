A van caught fire on the A2 motorway in the southbound direction, on the section between the Monte Ceneri tunnel and Rivera. The consequence indicated in the report is disrupted traffic on the route towards Chiasso.

The incident is documented by a video sent to the editorial office. The source describes the vehicle on fire, but does not add details about the causes of the fire, the condition of the vehicle or how the blaze started. The available information therefore remains that of the van fire along the indicated section.

The road coordinates reported are consistent with one another: the motorway is the A2, the direction is southbound, the geographical reference is the area between the Monte Ceneri tunnel and Rivera, and the indicated destination is Chiasso. On this basis, the disruption can be defined without extending it to areas not mentioned.

The traffic restriction

To allow operations to take place, traffic on the right lane was stopped on the affected section. ViaSuisse reported this. The news concerns the direction towards Chiasso and the segment between the Monte Ceneri tunnel and Rivera.

For those travelling on the A2 southbound, the report therefore focuses attention on that motorway segment and on the lane affected by the closure. The source indicates the closure of the right lane and provides no other information about traffic conditions on the section.

Available information

At the time of the report, no information was available regarding any injuries. The source also does not report statements from people involved. The account thus remains linked to two elements: the van that caught fire and the traffic measure adopted to allow the necessary operations to take place.

The direction towards Chiasso makes the news relevant for those using this part of the A2 southbound, including the cross-border commuter reader who has to travel through the indicated section. The certain operational information is the closure of the right lane and disrupted traffic in the area between the tunnel and Rivera.
