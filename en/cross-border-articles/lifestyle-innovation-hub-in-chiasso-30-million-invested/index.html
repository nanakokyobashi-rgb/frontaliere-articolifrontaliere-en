On October 7, 2026, Chiasso inaugurated the Lifestyle Innovation Hub, created through the redevelopment of the former Credit Suisse headquarters in Piazza Indipendenza. The total investment is 30 million francs and transforms the property into a hub dedicated to innovation, technology and artificial intelligence.

The center was co-founded by Dagorà, lastminute.com, A++ Group and Alpha Square Invest. A++ Group, Alpha Square Invest and Dagorà are providing financial support for the redevelopment; lastminute.com, on the other hand, has moved its headquarters to three floors of the building.

A project between business and research

With the patronage of the Canton of Ticino, the project aims to foster contacts among companies, startups, universities, investors and institutions. The focus is on the Travel, Hospitality & Leisure, Design and Media sectors; the program includes initiatives dedicated to artificial intelligence and emerging technologies, applied research, workshops, open innovation activities, startup scouting and events with investors.

For those who live near the border and work in Ticino, the stated territorial connection is Chiasso’s location: the source links it to the border with Italy and to the main Swiss and European economic connections. Stated objectives include bringing companies and talent from both sides of the Alps and promoting collaboration between industry and research. The news does not quantify either the companies involved or the positions available.

At the inauguration, Mayor Bruno Arrigoni emphasized that the project brings one of the city’s most symbolic buildings back to life and aims to bring companies, talent and new ideas to Chiasso.

These are objectives stated by the promoters and the representatives who spoke, not a hiring schedule.

Four verification steps

2. Read the sector. This step serves to understand the project's area, not to infer that a position is available.

3. Distinguish the objective from the procedure. Statements about qualified jobs, investments, skills and territorial impact are objectives indicated by the promoters and speakers. The source does not report forms, contacts, requirements, selection processes or dates for participating: one must not construct a procedure absent from the text.

4. Then look for the individual data. If a concrete opportunity emerges connected to the companies, startups or other entities of the Hub, the data to verify will be the specific announcement, not the inauguration alone. Regarding the economic aspect, the news provides no individual salaries or amounts; the comparison should be kept separate from the project's 30 million investment.

This sequence avoids two opposite errors: ignoring an initiative located on the border and turning a collaboration plan into an employment promise. The cross-border worker can therefore use the Hub as information about the territory of Canton Ticino and check for opportunities only when they are accompanied by concrete details.

Frequently Asked Questions
When was the Lifestyle Innovation Hub in Chiasso inaugurated?
On October 7, 2026, the Hub was inaugurated in the former Credit Suisse headquarters in Piazza Indipendenza, with a total investment of 30 million francs. The building has six floors and over 10'000 square meters, transformed into a hub dedicated to innovation, technology and artificial intelligence.
Which sectors and activities are planned within the Hub?
The Hub focuses on the Travel, Hospitality & Leisure, Design and Media sectors, with a program that includes applied research, workshops, open innovation, startup scouting and events with investors. In addition, it pays particular attention to artificial intelligence and emerging technologies, as stated by the promoters and speakers at the inauguration.
What are the objectives declared by the promoters for the Ticino region?
The promoters cite the creation of skilled jobs, the attraction of investment, and bringing local businesses closer to innovation and artificial intelligence among the expected effects. The aim is to turn the relationships between technology, investment and research into concrete projects, skills and a positive impact on the local area, as emphasized by Mayor Bruno Arrigoni and Stefano Rizzi of the Canton's Economic Division.
