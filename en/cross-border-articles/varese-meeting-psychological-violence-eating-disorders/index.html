Varese: event on psychological abuse and eating disorders (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 4 May 2026

On Friday, May 8 in Varese, an event to reflect on the link between psychological mistreatment and eating difficulties.

Context

In brief

Public meeting on psychological violence and eating disorders in Varese Organized by Anemos Italy ODV and other local associations Featuring talks by experts and sector professionals

Key facts

  • What: Public event "The Invisible Weight"
  • When: Friday, May 8, 2026, at 8:30 PM
  • Where: Sala Montanari, Varese
  • Who: Anemos Italy ODV, Lampi Blu, Ananke Family ETS Villa Miralago, Varese Inter-Agency Anti-Violence Network
  • Entry: Free admission until capacity is reached

On Friday, May 8, 2026, at 8:30 PM, the public event "The Invisible Weight – When Psychological Violence Becomes Eating Disorder" will take place at Sala Montanari in Varese. The event is promoted by Anemos Italy ODV in collaboration with Lampi Blu, Ananke Family ETS Villa Miralago, and the Varese Inter-Agency Anti-Violence Network. The goal is to explore the connection between psychological abuse, emotional fragility, and eating behavior disorders.

During the evening, professionals from various fields will speak, including Giorgia Carabelli, a nutritionist biologist; Nicoletta Manenti, a psychologist and psychotherapist; Maurizio Parisi, a general practitioner; Cinzia Fumagalli, president of Ananke Family APS; and Martina Minari, schools coordinator for Lampi Blu. The session will be moderated by Elena Bertolin, a legal consultant and relational coach.

"Talking about psychological violence means giving voice to wounds that are often unseen but deeply affect mental and physical health," explains Anna Marsella, president of Anemos Italy. "This meeting aims to be a moment of listening, awareness, and prevention."

Operational details

Practical Analysis: Implications for Cross-Border Workers

The event 'The Invisible Weight' offers an opportunity to delve into a highly relevant topic for cross-border workers who commute to Switzerland from Italy. Psychological violence and eating disorders can significantly impact quality of life and work performance. Participating in such events can help recognize signs of distress and seek timely support.

For cross-border workers living in Ticino and working in Lombardy, it is crucial to be aware of the resources available in both Italy and Switzerland. Local associations and support services can provide valuable assistance in dealing with psychological and eating disorders. For example, associations like Anemos Italia ODV and Lampi Blu offer support and counseling services for those in need of assistance.

Comparison with the Previous Situation

In recent years, awareness regarding psychological violence and eating disorders has increased significantly. However, many people still underestimate the impact of these issues on mental and physical health. The event 'The Invisible Weight' represents a step forward in promoting awareness and preventing these conditions.

Concrete Actions for Cross-Border Workers

If you are a cross-border worker living in Italy and working in Switzerland, attending the event "The Invisible Weight" can be an important step toward recognizing signs of psychological and eating disorders. Here are some concrete actions you can take:

  • Attend the event: Join us at Sala Montanari in Varese on Friday, May 8, 2026, at 8:30 PM to listen to experts and ask questions.
  • Recognize the signs of distress: Learn to identify signs of psychological violence and eating disorders, both in yourself and those around you.
  • Seek support: If you feel you need assistance, contact local associations such as Anemos Italia ODV or Lampi Blu for support and counseling.
  • Use available resources: Take advantage of support services offered by local healthcare facilities and volunteer organizations.
  • Talk to a professional: If necessary, consult a doctor, psychologist, or nutritionist for an assessment and personalized treatment plan.
