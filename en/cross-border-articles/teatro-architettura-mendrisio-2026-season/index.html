The Teatro dell'Architettura in Mendrisio is preparing to host a rich and varied exhibition season, divided into three distinct shows. The venue, known for its focus on architecture and design, has announced a calendar that promises to attract enthusiasts and professionals in the field. The third exhibition, meanwhile, will be dedicated to an in-depth look at contemporary design.
