Beatification process for Don Roberto Malgesini begins

Di Laura Bianchi · Published on 2 May 2026

The Diocese of Como has started the process for the beatification of Don Roberto Malgesini, who was killed in 2020.

Context

In brief

  • Diocese of Como starts beatification process for Fr. Roberto Malgesini
  • Edict signed by Bishop Oscar Cantoni on April 30, 2026
  • Community involvement for testimonies and documents

Key facts

  • What: Start of beatification process for Fr. Roberto Malgesini
  • When: April 30, 2026
  • Where: Diocese of Como
  • Who: Diocese of Como, Bishop Oscar Cantoni
  • Amount: Not yet specified

The Diocese of Como has officially started the process for the beatification of Fr. Roberto Malgesini, the priest killed in 2020 and remembered for his commitment to the poor. With an edict signed by Bishop Oscar Cantoni and released on April 30, 2026, the diocesan phase of the process that could lead to his beatification and canonization has been opened. The document, dated April 30, 2026, outlines the steps that led to this decision, which came after the approval of the Dicastery for the Causes of Saints and the favorable opinion of the Lombard Episcopal Conference.

A significant moment for the Church of Como and for those who knew Fr. Malgesini. The edict emphasizes how the decision to start the cause entails 'grave responsibility' and requires direct involvement from the community. For this reason, an explicit invitation is extended to anyone who can provide useful elements. In particular, those aware of facts that could challenge the priest's reputation for holiness are asked to report them to the bishop or the postulator of the cause. Similarly, those in possession of writings, documents, or testimonies related to Fr. Malgesini are invited to submit them to the Curia of Como.

The document will be posted for four weeks in various locations within the diocese: from the Curia of Como to the parishes linked to Fr. Malgesini's ministry, including those in Como, Gravedona, Lipomo, and Cosio Valtellino. Publication in the official Ecclesiastical Bulletin is also planned. Thus, a new phase begins, aimed at collecting testimonies and documents useful for thoroughly reconstructing the life and work of the priest.

A priest close to the last

The text recalls the strong consensus that has grown around the figure of the priest, defined as a 'witness of charity towards the poor.' A recognition that has also been consolidated at a broader level, as recalled by the words of Pope Francis during the general audiences of September and October 2020. The cause is initiated 'super oblatione vitae,' a procedure provided for those who have offered their lives freely and consciously, driven by motives of faith and charity.

Operational details

Implications for the Community

The initiation of the beatification process for Don Roberto Malgesini represents a significant moment not only for the Diocese of Como but also for the local community and for all those who knew and appreciated the priest. The collection of testimonies and documents useful for reconstructing his life and work implies active and participatory involvement from the faithful and citizens.

Procedure and Deadlines

The document will remain posted for four weeks in various locations within the diocese, including the Bishop's Curia of Como and the parishes linked to Don Malgesini's ministry. This period is crucial for gathering all the necessary information to proceed with the beatification process. Publication in the official Ecclesiastical Bulletin represents a further step towards transparency and community participation.

Comparison with the Previous Situation

Before the initiation of this process, Don Roberto Malgesini was already held in high esteem and respect for his social and religious commitment. However, the official start of the beatification process represents a formal and institutional recognition that could lead to a greater dissemination of his message of charity and solidarity.

Future Scenarios

If the beatification process concludes positively, Don Roberto Malgesini could become a reference model for the Christian community and for all those engaged in social work. This recognition could also attract the attention of the faithful and pilgrims from other parts of the world, contributing to further spiritual and cultural development of the Diocese of Como.

Key points

What to do to contribute

If you have testimonies, documents, or information regarding Father Roberto Malgesini, you are invited to hand them over to the Diocesan Curia of Como. This contribution is essential for the accurate reconstruction of the priest's life and work. The community is called to actively participate in this process, providing every useful element for the beatification cause.

Deadlines and required documents

The document will be posted for four weeks in various locations within the diocese. It is important to respect this deadline to ensure that all collected information is considered in the process. There are no costs or complex procedures associated with this document collection, but it is essential to provide accurate and verified information.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who was Don Roberto Malgesini?
Don Roberto Malgesini was a priest of the Diocese of Como, known for his commitment to the poorest. He was killed in 2020 and his figure is remembered for his charity and solidarity.
What does it mean to start the beatification process?
Starting the beatification process means initiating an official procedure to recognize the holiness of a person. This process involves collecting testimonies and documents that attest to the virtuous life and example of faith of the candidate.
How can I contribute to the beatification process?
You can contribute by providing testimonies, documents, or information about Don Roberto Malgesini. These elements must be submitted to the Diocesan Curia of Como by the indicated deadlines.
