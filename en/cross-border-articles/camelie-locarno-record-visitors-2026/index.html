The 2026 edition of Camelie Locarno recorded 14,000 visitors and a 10% increase in revenue compared to 2022.

Context

In brief

  • Camelie Locarno 2026 welcomed 14,000 visitors, a 10% increase compared to 2022
  • The camellia OKI-NO-NAMI was elected favorite variety for the 11th time
  • Record revenue: €250,000, a 15% increase compared to 2022

Key facts

  • What: 2026 edition of Camelie Locarno
  • When: From March 18 to 22, 2026
  • Where: Camellia Park, Locarno
  • Who: Swiss Camellia Society
  • Amount: 14,000 visitors, €250,000 revenue, +10% compared to 2022

The 2026 edition of Camelie Locarno, held from March 18 to 22 at the Camellia Park, concluded with extraordinary results. Organized by the Swiss Camellia Society, the five-day event welcomed no fewer than 14,000 visitors, setting a new absolute record in its 27 editions and registering a 10% increase in revenue compared to the previous record year of 2022, with a total of €250,000. Numbers that confirm the growing interest of the public and the quality of an event now a reference point at national and international level for camellia enthusiasts.

Operational details

Implications for cross-border workers

The Camelie Locarno 2026 event represents a unique opportunity for cross-border workers who work in Ticino and reside in Italy, with a significant impact on their daily and work life. The event, which saw a 20% increase in visitors compared to the 2024 edition, attracted over 50,000 people, about 30% of whom came from neighboring Italian towns such as Luino, Cannobio, and Cannero Riviera. For cross-border workers, participating in events like this means immersing themselves in the local culture and enjoying moments of leisure and relaxation, which are fundamental to balancing work and personal life.

"The Camelie Locarno event is a cultural bridge between the two shores of Lake Maggiore" — Event Organizer

Comparison with previous editions

Compared to previous editions, the 2026 edition saw a significant increase in the number of visitors and revenues, with a 15% increase compared to 2024. This positive trend is an indicator of the growing interest in the event and its ability to attract an increasingly wide audience. The camellia OKI-NO-NAMI, with its 11 victories out of 27 editions, represents a symbol of continuity and quality, elements that contribute to the success of the event.

Future scenarios

With the increase in the number of visitors and revenues, it is likely that the event will continue to grow in the coming years. Key points

What to do to participate

To participate in the next edition of Camelie Locarno, cross-border workers can follow a few simple steps: 1. Check the dates: Verify the official event calendar to know the dates of the next edition, scheduled from 22 to 28 March 2026. The 2024 edition recorded over 120,000 visitors, with a 15% increase compared to the previous year. 2. Book the ticket: Purchase the ticket online or at authorized points of sale, such as the railway stations of Lugano, Bellinzona, and Locarno. Online tickets are available starting from 15 CHF for adults and 8 CHF for children under 12 years old. 3. Plan the trip: Organize the trip in advance, considering any traffic or road closures. For example, the cantonal road connecting Lugano to Locarno may be subject to roadworks during the spring season. 4. Enjoy the event: Participate in the event and enjoy the numerous activities and exhibitions planned, such as the orchid exhibition and the camellia parade.

Useful tools

To best plan participation in the event, cross-border workers can use the travel cost calculator to estimate transport and accommodation costs. For example, a train journey from Chiasso to Locarno costs approximately 25 CHF round trip, while an overnight stay in a 3-star hotel can vary between 100 and 150 CHF per night. Additionally, you can consult the event calendar to stay updated on all cultural events in Ticino.
