Lake Como, body found at 288 meters

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 29 September 2026

At San Siro, Fire Brigade divers found the body of the 38-year-old who went missing in Lake Como at approximately 288 meters.

Context

In brief

  • Body located at approximately 288 meters deep
  • Searches concluded in the waters of San Siro
  • Recovery and transfer of the body to Dongo
  • Intervention by Fire Brigade divers

Key facts

  • Age → 38 years
  • Location → Lake Como, San Siro
  • Depth → approximately 288 meters
  • Equipment → ROV
  • Destination of the body → Dongo

The search operations for the 38-year-old man missing in the waters of Lake Como, at San Siro, concluded with the body being located at approximately 288 meters deep. The discovery was made possible thanks to Fire Brigade divers and the use of the ROV, an acronym for Remotely Operated Vehicle.

After the location was identified, Fire Brigade specialists proceeded with the recovery. The body was then transferred to Dongo, where it was placed at the disposal of the competent Authority.

The man was a resident of the United Kingdom and of Nepalese origin. The reported sequence includes the search in the lake waters, location using the remotely operated vehicle, recovery and transfer. In the report, the depth is the detail accompanying the outcome of the operation: the body was found at approximately 288 meters and, after it was located, the specialists completed the recovery. The transfer to Dongo marked the final step indicated for the body, now at the disposal of the competent Authority.

Operational details

The circumstances of the disappearance

The disappearance dates back to last Monday. The man was on a boat rented in Dongo, where he was sailing with a family with two children, aged 15 and 10. A decisive moment in the voyage occurred in the waters off Acquaseria.

At that point, the 38-year-old man and the 15-year-old boy jumped into the water. The boy then struggled to get back onto the boat and was assisted by his father. Meanwhile, the boat moved away, carried by the current. The man, left in the water, encountered difficulties and disappeared underwater.

The account of the incident sets out the locations and sequence of events: Dongo is the place where the boat had been rented; Acquaseria is the area of water where the dives took place; San Siro is the location identified for the disappearance and subsequent search in Lake Como. The people mentioned are the 38-year-old man, the 15-year-old boy, the father who assisted him, and the 10-year-old child, identified in the source as part of the family.

After the boy struggled to get back onto the boat, the boat moved away while the 38-year-old was still in the water. The source links the man's disappearance underwater to that moment and the subsequent start of the search operations.

Key points

The recovery and transfer

The phase following the location involved recovering the body. The work was carried out by Fire Brigade specialists after the ROV had made it possible to locate the body at approximately 288 meters. The source therefore describes a distinct operational step: the remotely controlled vehicle was used for locating the body, while the specialists carried out the recovery operations.

Once this operation was completed, the body was transferred to Dongo. The same location had been identified as the place where the boat used by the man and the family had been rented. In Dongo, the body was made available to the competent authority, according to reports.

This transfer brings the sequence of reported activities to an end: search in the waters of San Siro, location using an ROV, recovery by the Fire Brigade, and transfer of the body to Dongo. The source does not indicate any further developments, subsequent dates, or other measures. The reported outcome therefore remains that the body was found at approximately 288 meters and the search operations were completed.
