Luino, bottle caps with nails in the parking lot behind Tigros

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 October 2026

Paolo Nicastri reports the presence of caps with nails pointing upwards in the area used by cars, motorcycles and driving schools for driving exams and tests.

Some cork stoppers with nails inserted and the points facing upward were reportedly found in the parking lot behind Tigros in Luino. Paolo Nicastri, a young man from Luino and former candidate for mayor of Luino in the May 2026 municipal elections, made the discovery public through social media.

The site is frequented by cars and motorcyclists and is also used by driving schools for practice sessions and exams for a motorcycle license. The presence of the corks therefore concerns an area where different users move around and where practical training activities also take place.

In the published video, Nicastri shows some corks into which nails appear to have been inserted. The points are oriented upward. The young man from Luino highlights the risk that a motorcyclist might ride over them, puncture a tire and fall. His question in the video draws attention to the possible immediate consequences of those objects being present on the ground.

The focus of the report is the physical danger posed by the nails and the particular use of the parking lot. Nicastri links the discovery to the safety of those who use the area, with specific attention to students taking part in practice sessions and exams for a motorcycle license.

The report published on social media therefore starts from this concrete element: the presence of sharp objects, with the points facing upward, in a Luino parking lot frequented by cars, motorcycles and driving schools.

Operational details

A parking lot also used for motorcycle tests

The role of driving schools is the point Nicastri emphasizes most in the video. He does not merely recall that the parking lot is frequented by motorcyclists: he stresses that practice sessions and motorcycle license tests take place in the same space. The presence of the nails is therefore described in relation to those who might cross the area during a training or examination activity.

It is precisely in this parking lot that driving schools conduct practice sessions and motorcycle license exams

In his reasoning, the risk concerns not only the possible fall of a young person on a motorcycle. Nicastri also raises the possibility that a driving-school vehicle could puncture one or more tires by driving over the nails. In that case, he observes, the danger to people would be accompanied by financial damage. The report is thus linked both to physical safety and to the material consequences for those who use the parking lot.

The video also refers to some complaints that, according to Nicastri, have concerned the area recently. The reference is not turned into an accusation against anyone. Instead, the young man from Luino asks that any disputes over the use of the area be handled without adopting behavior capable of endangering other people.

The message also addresses the issue of motorcycle enthusiasts. Nicastri wonders whether it is possible to identify a dedicated and safe space for them, alongside the discussion of the risks present in a parking lot used for traffic and driving-school activities.

The reaction described by the source therefore remains a position conveyed through social media: safety for those taking the tests, attention to possible tire damage, and a request not to let complaints about the area degenerate into dangerous acts.

Key points

The clarification about who left the caps

Paolo Nicastri accompanies the report with a clear clarification regarding responsibility for the act. He says he does not know who left the objects in the parking lot and makes it clear that he does not want to accuse anyone without concrete evidence. In the video, he states that he has no proof. His report, therefore, does not identify anyone responsible and keeps the discovery of the caps separate from any possible attribution of the act.

This passage also defines the scope of the matter described by the source. The issue reported at present is the presence of cork stoppers with nails pointing upward and the danger they may pose to cars, motorcycles and driving schools. Nicastri does not identify who allegedly left them, focusing his report on the objects found and their position in the parking lot.

The video’s conclusion returns specifically to safety. For the former mayoral candidate, the combination of nails pointing upward and a parking lot frequented by vehicles and motorcyclists constitutes an enormous danger. In his words, the same assessment applies to an area where driving schools take students for practice sessions and motorcycle license exams.

The continuation documented by the source contains neither an indication of who did it nor any further reconstruction of the act. The report therefore remains focused on the alarm raised through social media and on the need, emphasized by Nicastri, not to put the health or lives of those passing through the parking lot at risk.

The position expressed is twofold: on the one hand, it reports the cork stoppers with their points facing upward as a concrete fact; on the other, it avoids turning the report into an accusation without evidence. The central fact remains the one stated in the video: those objects, in that location, can create a very serious risk for anyone using the parking lot.
