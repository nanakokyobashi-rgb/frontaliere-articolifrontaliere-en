Operational details

Italy train strike, inconvenience for TILO 23-24 July

On July 23 and 24, 2026, a national rail transport strike is scheduled in Italy, which could have repercussions on the cross-border connections managed by TILO. Passengers using these services could experience significant inconvenience.

The circulation of TILO trains in Switzerland will continue regularly, but connections with the Lombardy airport may be affected. In particular, the S50 line could be suppressed, forcing passengers to use a substitute bus service between Stabio and Malpensa Airport. This service will be activated without intermediate stops, so passengers must be ready to get on and off the bus in a few minutes.

According to data provided by TILO, around 10,000 passengers use the S50 line daily to reach Malpensa Airport. During the strike, this number could increase by at least 20%, due to the lack of trains. Therefore, it is essential to plan your trip in advance and respect the waiting times.

Replacement bus service

The replacement bus service will be activated between Stabio and Malpensa Airport, without intermediate stops. Passengers must be ready to get on and off the bus in a few minutes. Key points

Italy train strike, inconvenience for TILO 23-24 July

The announced strike of Italian railway workers could cause considerable inconvenience for passengers using the connections between the Canton of Ticino and the Lombardy airport of Malpensa Airport. To ensure the necessary connections, the substitute bus service between Stabio and Malpensa Airport will be activated without intermediate stops. Travelers are encouraged to pay attention to listings and directions at stations and on trains.

What will happen?

If the S50 line is cancelled due to the strike, the substitute bus service will be activated. Passengers wishing to reach Malpensa Airport must take the bus to Stabio and continue without intermediate stops to the airport. It is important to note that the replacement service will not be available for all stops on the S50 line, but only for those that affect the connection with Malpensa Airport.

How to avoid discomfort?

To avoid the inconvenience caused by the strike, passengers can take some preventive measures:

  • Check the official Trenord website and the company's app for the most up-to-date information.
  • Take the bus to Stabio and continue without intermediate stops to Malpensa Airport.
  • Check announcements and directions at stations and on trains to be updated on service changes.
