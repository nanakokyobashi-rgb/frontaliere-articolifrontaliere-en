During Ascension and Pentecost 2026, many Swiss people continue to favor classic holiday destinations such as Spain, Greece, and Portugal. Dertour Suisse's booking data clearly shows that during these holiday periods, preferences focus on a mix of beach vacations, family destinations, and major European cities.

Traditional destinations and new trends

Traditional vacation patterns continue to influence choices for Ascension and Pentecost. Many travelers take advantage of these days for short stays in the Mediterranean basin, particularly in Spain – especially the Canary and Balearic Islands – but also in Greece and Portugal. At the same time, family-oriented offers remain among the most requested: at the top of preferences is Europa-Park, very frequented during the holidays and easily reachable without resorting to the plane. Among the most booked destinations for Ascension are Europa-Park, Italy, Greece, Turkey, and Spain. For Pentecost, on the other hand, Spain, Egypt, Europa-Park, Portugal, and Greece top the rankings.

Operational details

Implications for cross-border workers

For cross-border workers who commute to Switzerland and live in Italy, the Ascension and Pentecost holidays present an opportunity to plan trips that combine relaxation and discovery. The possibility of reaching European destinations by train offers a convenient and sustainable alternative to air travel, reducing travel times and improving comfort. Additionally, new rail connections, such as the one to Sestri Levante, make less-known but equally fascinating destinations more accessible.

Comparison between train and air travel

Train journeys offer numerous advantages over air travel, especially for those who want to avoid airport traffic and enjoy a more relaxed journey. Swiss scenic routes, such as the Glacier Express and the GoldenPass, offer the opportunity to admire breathtaking landscapes without sacrificing comfort. Moreover, European trains are often equipped with on-board services that make the journey even more pleasant, such as restaurants, Wi-Fi, and relaxation areas.

Travel planning

For cross-border workers who intend to take advantage of these holidays for a trip, it is important to plan ahead. Verify the exact dates of the holidays, book train or plane tickets well in advance, and inform yourself about travel conditions. Additionally, it is useful to consider accommodation options and available activities in the chosen destinations to make the most of your time.

Key points

What to do to organize the trip

To best organize your trip during the Ascension and Pentecost holidays, it's important to follow some fundamental steps. First, check the exact dates of the holidays and plan your vacation period. Then, book your train or plane tickets well in advance to secure the best fares and availability. Additionally, inform yourself about travel conditions and the services offered by the chosen means of transport.

Required documents

For cross-border workers traveling between Italy and Switzerland, it's important to have the necessary documents with you, such as the residence permit, a valid passport or identity card for travel abroad, and, if necessary, the work permit. Additionally, it's useful to have copies of your travel tickets and hotel bookings with you.

Useful tips

To make your trip even more enjoyable, it's advisable to inform yourself about the tourist attractions of the chosen destinations and plan your visits based on opening hours and weather conditions. Additionally, it's useful to have a map of the city or area you are visiting and, if necessary, a translator to facilitate communication.

Useful tools

To best plan your trip, you can use tools like the travel calculator to compare transport options and find the best deals. Additionally, it's useful to consult tourist guides and online reviews to get an idea of the attractions and services available at your chosen destinations.

Conclusion

The Ascension and Pentecost holidays in 2026 offer numerous opportunities for cross-border workers to enjoy a relaxing and fun vacation. Planning ahead, choosing destinations that best suit your needs, and using useful tools like the travel calculator can make all the difference for an unforgettable travel experience.
