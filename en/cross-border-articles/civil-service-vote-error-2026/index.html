Civil service: error in voting explanations

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 May 2026

An error in the Federal Council's information brochure for the June 14, 2026 votes concerns applications for admission to civil service.

Context

In brief

  • Error in the printed brochure on civil service questions
  • Correction available only online
  • Provision only applies during recruit school

Key facts

  • What: Error in the explanations for voting on civil service
  • When: June 14, 2026 (voting date)
  • Where: Switzerland
  • Who: Federal Office for Civil Protection
  • Amount: Not specified

The Federal Office for Civil Protection has identified a significant error in the printed information brochure of the Federal Council in view of the votes on June 14, 2026. On page 28, the brochure incorrectly states that "anyone who submits an application for admission to the civil service before or during recruit school will have to carry out the long-term assignment already in the year following admission to the civil service." In reality, according to the legal amendment to be voted on, this provision "only applies to those who submit an application for admission to the civil service during recruit school." Those who apply before the start of recruit school must continue to complete the long-term assignment period by the end of the third calendar year following the year in which admission to the civil service becomes final.

Implications for cross-border workers

The error could affect cross-border workers who are considering civil service as an alternative to military service. It is important that cross-border workers check the updated information online before submitting their application. The Federal Office for Civil Protection has corrected the error in the PDF versions of the voting explanations available on the Government websites, but the already printed voting explanations can no longer be modified.

For cross-border workers residing in Italy and working in Switzerland, it is crucial to be aware of the latest legislative changes to avoid inconveniences. The online correction is available and should be consulted to avoid errors in admission applications.

Procedure for correction

Cross-border workers who have already received the printed brochure must access the updated PDF versions available on the Government websites to obtain the correct information. It is advisable to print the updated voting explanations to have a physical copy of the correct information.

Comparisons and scenarios

Before the correction, cross-border workers who applied before recruit school were subject to a different provision than those who applied during recruit school. Now, with the correction, the provision only applies to those who apply during recruit school, which could influence the planning of civil service for some cross-border workers.

For cross-border workers considering civil service, it is important to consider the new provisions and plan accordingly. The correction could influence the timing and duration of civil service, so it is essential to be well informed.

Immediate actions

Cross-border workers who have already submitted an application or are considering doing so should verify the updated information online. It is advisable to contact the Federal Office for Civil Protection for clarifications on any doubts or specific questions. Additionally, it is important to monitor any future updates to always be informed about the latest legislative changes.

For more information and to access the updated voting explanations, cross-border workers can visit the Swiss Government websites. It is essential to stay updated to avoid errors and ensure a correct submission of the civil service admission application.

This could influence the planning of civil service, especially for those who are considering this option as an alternative to military service.

Now, with the correction, the provision only applies to those who submit an application during the recruit school, which could influence the planning of civil service for some cross-border workers.

Practical Scenarios

In the first case, the cross-border worker must complete the long-term employment period by the end of the third civil year following the one in which admission to civil service becomes final. In the second case, the cross-border worker must carry out the long-term employment already in the year following admission to civil service.
