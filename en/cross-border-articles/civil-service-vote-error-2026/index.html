Civil service: error in voting explanations (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 May 2026

An error in the Federal Council's information brochure for the June 14, 2026 votes concerns applications for admission to civil service.

Context

In brief

  • Error in the printed brochure on civil service questions
  • Correction available only online
  • Provision only applies during recruit school

Key facts

  • What: Error in the explanations for voting on civil service
  • When: June 14, 2026 (voting date)
  • Where: Switzerland
  • Who: Federal Office for Civil Protection
  • Amount: Not specified

The Federal Office for Civil Protection has identified a significant error in the printed information brochure of the Federal Council in view of the votes on June 14, 2026. On page 28, the brochure incorrectly states that "anyone who submits an application for admission to the civil service before or during recruit school will have to carry out the long-term assignment already in the year following admission to the civil service." In reality, according to the legal amendment to be voted on, this provision "only applies to those who submit an application for admission to the civil service during recruit school." Those who apply before the start of recruit school must continue to complete the long-term assignment period by the end of the third calendar year following the year in which admission to the civil service becomes final.

Implications for cross-border workers

The error could affect cross-border workers who are considering civil service as an alternative to military service. It is important that cross-border workers check the updated information online before submitting their application. The Federal Office for Civil Protection has corrected the error in the PDF versions of the voting explanations available on the Government websites, but the already printed voting explanations can no longer be modified.

Operational details

Analysis of Implications

The error in the voting explanations for civil service could have a significant impact on cross-border workers who are considering this option. It is important to understand the practical implications of this correction to avoid inconveniences and adequately plan civil service.

Impact on Civil Service Planning

The correction of the provision means that cross-border workers who submit an application for admission to civil service before the recruit school are not subject to the same timeline as those who submit it during the recruit school. This could influence the planning of civil service, especially for those who are considering this option as an alternative to military service.

Comparisons with the Previous Situation

Before the correction, the provision applicable to all applicants was the same, regardless of the time of submission of the application. Now, with the correction, the provision only applies to those who submit an application during the recruit school, which could influence the planning of civil service for some cross-border workers.

Practical Scenarios

Consider two scenarios: a cross-border worker who submits an application before the recruit school and one who submits it during the recruit school. In the first case, the cross-border worker must complete the long-term employment period by the end of the third civil year following the one in which admission to civil service becomes final. In the second case, the cross-border worker must carry out the long-term employment already in the year following admission to civil service.

Key points

Immediate actions for cross-border workers

For cross-border workers considering civil service, it's important to follow some immediate actions to ensure a correct application submission and avoid inconveniences.

Verify updated information

Cross-border workers who have already received the paper brochure must access the updated PDF versions available on the Government websites to obtain the correct information. It is advisable to print the updated voting explanations to have a physical copy of the correct information.

Contact the Federal Office for Civil Protection

For clarifications on any doubts or specific questions, it is advisable to contact the Federal Office for Civil Protection. This can be done through the official website or through the contact channels provided.

Monitor future updates

It is fundamental for cross-border workers to monitor any future updates to always be informed about the latest legislative changes. This can be done by regularly visiting the Swiss Government websites or contacting the Federal Office for Civil Protection for clarifications on any doubts or specific questions.

Use online tools

For further information and to access the updated voting explanations, cross-border workers can visit the Swiss Government websites. It is fundamental to stay updated to avoid errors and ensure a correct submission of the civil service admission application.

Salary calculator

To better plan civil service and understand the financial implications, cross-border workers can use the salary calculator available on the site. This tool can help estimate income and expenses during the civil service period, ensuring adequate financial planning.
