A 79-year-old man fell from a ladder inside a farm in the area, in Via Madonnetta in Locate Varesino, on the morning of Wednesday, October 7, shortly after 10 am. The causes are still being ascertained.

Rescue operations immediately started in code red, with the arrival on the scene of the medics sent by Areu: a Red Cross ambulance, the self-medicine and the car nurse provided the injured with the first emergency care.

The dynamic referred to by the source stops at the fall from the ladder and the intervention on the farm. The causes have not yet been ascertained. The man's health conditions also remain entrusted to the updates expected in the coming hours, while the intervention of rescuers is still ongoing.

The first point in the report concerns the medical response activated after the fall. The emergency services mobilized under a red code, with a response made up of several units: the Croce Rossa ambulance, the medical car and the nursing car, in addition to the healthcare personnel sent by Areu.
