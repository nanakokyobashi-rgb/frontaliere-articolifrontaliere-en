Salary of commercial employees in Ticino

Trade employees in Ticino can enjoy a competitive salary, which may vary depending on their experience and the industry in which they work. According to 2022 data, the average monthly salary for a commercial employee in Ticino is around €4,200. However, this value can vary greatly depending on your geographical location and business sector.

For example, a trade clerk working in Lugano can earn about €4,800 per month, while one working in Bellinzona can earn about €4,000 per month.

Requirements to work as a trade clerk in Ticino

To work as a trade clerk in Ticino, you need to possess certain qualifications and requirements. Here are some of the main requirements:

  • Be at least 16 years old
  • Have a certificate of qualification as a trade clerk
  • Have a good knowledge of Italian and German (depending on the workplace)
  • Have a good knowledge of the business sector
  • Have a good professional reputation

Key points

To obtain the G permit, you must meet certain requirements. Border workers must have a basic knowledge of the Italian language and be at least 18 years old. In addition, you must have a recognized educational qualification and have a basic knowledge of commerce.

The recognised qualification may be a high school diploma or a university degree. Basic knowledge of trade is essential to be able to carry out trade employment activities. This means that trade clerks must have a good understanding of the basic principles of trade, including accounting, customer relationship management, and knowledge of trade laws.

According to the Ticino law of 1 January 2018, commercial employees must take an examination to obtain the G permit. The exam consists of a written and oral test, which evaluates the knowledge of commerce and the Italian language.

Knowledge of the Italian language is essential for commercial employees in Ticino. According to the Ticino law of 1 January 2018, commercial employees must have a basic knowledge of the Italian language in order to carry out commercial employment activities. This means that sales clerks must be able to communicate effectively with customers and compose business documents in Italian.
