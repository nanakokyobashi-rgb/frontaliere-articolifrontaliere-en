Requirements to work as a cleaner in Ticino

To work as a cleaner in Ticino, the border worker must meet certain requirements. Firstly, you must have an employment contract with an employer registered in the Register of Employers of the Canton of Ticino. In addition, you must have a G permit, which is a residence permit that allows border workers to work in the Canton of Ticino.

Types of G Permits

There are several types of G permits, including:

  • G-A permits: for border workers working as cleaners in a single company.
  • G-B permits: for border workers who work as cleaners in more than one company.
  • G-C permits: for border workers working as cleaners in a school, hospital or shopping mall.

Concrete examples

For example, a border worker working as a cleaner at a school in Bellinzona might have a G-C permit. In this case, the border worker could work up to 20 hours per week and receive a salary of at leastCHF 3,500.00 per month.

# G Permit Requirements

To obtain the G permit, the border crossing must meet the following requirements:

  • Be a national of an EU Member State or a State party to the Schengen Agreement.
  • Have no criminal record for crimes committed abroad.
  • You have not been expelled or expelled from an EU Member State or a State party to the Schengen Agreement.
  • Do not be suspected of having committed a crime abroad.

Relevant qualification

The relevant qualification for border cleaners in Ticino can be obtained by:

  • A high school diploma (maturity) in a related field (e.g., engineering, architecture, etc.).
  • A certificate of professional qualification issued by an accredited institution.
  • Vocational training of at least 6 months in a related field.

Regulations

The legislation that regulates the provision of cleaning services in Ticino by fronters is the Labour Law (L 3 of 24 March 1943) and the Labour Regulation (RS 643.1).
