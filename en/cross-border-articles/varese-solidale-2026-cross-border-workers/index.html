Varese Solidale 2026: two days against healthcare poverty (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 May 2026

From May 15 to 16, 42 associations in Varese mobilize to combat healthcare poverty with conferences, screenings, and a solidarity dinner

Context

In brief

  • 42 associations in Varese united against health poverty
  • Conferences, screenings, and a solidarity dinner on May 15 and 16
  • Focus on the Pharmaceutical Bank and the recovery of medicines

Key facts

  • What: Varese is Solidale against health poverty
  • When: May 15 and 16, 2026
  • Where: Varese, city center
  • Who: 42 associations in Varese, Caritas Zona Pastorale di Varese
  • Amount: Not yet specified

'Varese is Solidale' returns: on May 15 and 16, volunteers mobilize against health poverty. Two days of meetings, testimonies, prevention, solidarity, and fundraising to tell the story of Varese's volunteer network and put one of the most urgent needs of the territory at the center: health poverty. The 2026 edition celebrates its tenth anniversary involving 42 associations from the area united by a common goal: to stimulate attention towards those who have less and to build together concrete responses to the needs that emerge every day. The theme chosen for this edition arises from direct observation of the fragilities that emerge every day: 'More and more people find themselves in the condition of having to choose between eating or taking care of themselves.'

Operational details

Practical Analysis: How to Participate and What to Expect

The Varese è Solidale event offers numerous opportunities for cross-border workers to access healthcare and information services. Here's what you can do to participate and what to expect.

Event Participation

From May 15th to 16th, Varese will be bustling with numerous events dedicated to combating health poverty. Among the main highlights are:

  • Conferences and public meetings on health and social vulnerabilities
  • Free health screenings
  • Informative exhibitions on national and local data regarding health poverty
  • Informative and awareness stands in the health village
  • Solidarity dinner with the participation of local chefs and restaurateurs

Accessing the Services Offered

To access the services offered during the event, you can follow these steps: 1. Get informed about the various events and participating associations through the official website of Varese è Solidale 2. Attend the events of your interest and gather information on the available services 3. Contact local associations to access free or discounted medicines and healthcare products 4. Participate in the solidarity dinner to support the cause and meet other community members

Comparison with Previous Situations

Compared to previous years, the 2026 edition of Varese è Solidale focuses more on health poverty, an increasingly widespread problem. The collaboration among 42 Varese associations represents a significant step forward in the fight against this phenomenon, offering services and support to those in need. The event represents a unique opportunity for cross-border workers to access healthcare and information services that might not be easily available otherwise.

Key points

Concrete actions: how to participate and support the event

Varese è Solidale 2026 offers numerous opportunities for cross-border workers to actively participate and support the fight against health poverty. Here's what you can do:

Participate in events

  • Conferences and meetings: Attend conferences and public meetings to delve into topics related to health poverty and social vulnerabilities. These events offer the opportunity to listen to experts and direct testimonies, as well as ask questions and receive useful information.
  • Health screenings: Take advantage of free health screenings to monitor your health and prevent potential issues. These services are provided by participating associations and represent a valuable opportunity to access medical checks without additional costs.
  • Informative exhibitions: Visit the informative exhibitions to discover national and local data regarding health poverty. The exhibitions offer an excellent opportunity to better understand the phenomenon and the initiatives underway to combat it.
  • Health village: Stop by the health village to discover informative and awareness stands. Here you can find information about health services, local associations, and solidarity initiatives.
  • Solidarity dinner: Participate in the solidarity dinner to support the cause and meet other members of the community. The dinner is an opportunity to enjoy dishes prepared by local chefs and restaurateurs while supporting an important objective.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is Varese è Solidale?
Varese è Solidale is an annual event that involves numerous associations from Varese in the fight against health poverty. During the event, conferences, health screenings, informative exhibitions, and a solidarity dinner are organized to raise awareness and collect funds.
How can I participate in Varese è Solidale?
You can participate in the events organized during the manifestation, such as conferences, health screenings, informative exhibitions, and the solidarity dinner. Additionally, you can support the participating associations through donations, volunteering, and spreading the word.
What health services are offered during Varese è Solidale?
During the event, free health screenings, information on health services, and access to free or discounted medicines and health products are offered. The participating associations provide support and assistance to those in need.
