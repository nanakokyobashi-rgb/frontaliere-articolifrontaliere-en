At the Ciossetto Center in Sementina, the meeting recently organized between the population and the Municipality of Bellinzona brought traffic, parking and pedestrian safety to the foreground. The new elementary school building has been in operation for a year; the nearby kindergarten will be expanded and updated.

The new paths in front of the school

The transfer of elementary school students has changed the home-school journeys for most of them. The increased traffic on foot and on scooters has overlapped with narrow streets which until now were mainly used for local traffic. Among these is via Alla Roggia, without a pavement, with one-way entry from the cantonal road and a speed limit of 30 km/h.

The street is preferred by children arriving from the eastern and southern part of the neighborhood compared to the more distant via Pro Grand. The latter is wider, has a double sidewalk and leads directly to Ciossetto and its car park. In the first months, however, the one-way street of Via Alla Roggia was little respected. The initial provisions, already planned before the opening of the school, were therefore strengthened with horizontal signs on the asphalt and vertical signs. Students have also been sensitized to avoid that path.

The complaints also concerned the Ciossetto car park. You can stay for up to four hours, but the stalls are occupied for a long time by teachers and those who work in the area. This reduces the spaces available for those who participate in activities intended for the elderly and those in the gym. Parking is undersized even during large events, such as gymnastics meets in the double gym. Furthermore, buses have little space to maneuver, with a reported risk to pedestrian safety.

Hence the request to make a nearby meadow, already used occasionally for this purpose, official as an emergency parking space. However, the authorities explained that a change to the master plan would be needed, with a positive result that is not certain.

As for the A2-A13 fast connection, the ongoing consultation has re-proposed an already set aside variant, which provides for the motorway junction at Sementina and a tunnel up to Riazzino on the right side. The hypothesis is supported only by the Federal Environment Office, but it could consume agricultural land near the Boschetti protected area and compromise the tranquility of the residential neighborhood upstream. The Town Hall will express itself in a critical and negative way, preferring the official variant on the left bank, shared at all levels.

The perimeter of the news

This limit avoids attributing effects to the consultation that the text does not announce. No construction sites, closures, diversions, travel times or new rules for those crossing the area are indicated. There is therefore no basis for saying that a cross-border commuter's route has already changed, nor for calculating a delay or indicating an alternative route.

The concrete point for those who have to reach Ciossetto by car is the availability of the stalls. The four-hour limit coexists with a prolonged presence of teachers and workers in the area; during the most popular activities and events, the margin is further reduced. The source does not quantify the number of parking spaces, does not indicate time slots and does not promise that the lawn can become a stable solution. The hypothesis remains linked to a modification of the master plan.

For those who travel on foot or by scooter, the critical issue concerns the relationship between school flows and the local road. The source reports via Alla Roggia as a route used by children and without a pavement, while the measures adopted so far are signage and raising awareness among students. An accident is not reported, so the report cannot be transformed into an accident report.

A2-A13 should also be read as a question still under discussion. The right bank variant is back in the consultation, but the source does not present it as an approved choice. The Municipality anticipates a negative position; the official alternative indicated is the left bank one. To evaluate the impact on a cross-border journey, data would be needed which is not available here. Practical steps

A five-step verification

An operational check must adhere to the situations described in the meeting.

1. If you need to reach Ciossetto, separate the reason for the visit: school, activity for the elderly, gym or big event. The text describes different uses of the same space and each can affect the availability of the parking space.

2. If the route is on foot or by scooter, consider the existing signs. Via Alla Roggia is the road without pavement, one-way entering from the cantonal road and with a speed limit of 30 km/h. The students were made aware of avoiding it; via Pro Grand is described as wider, equipped with a double sidewalk and directed to Ciossetto.

3. If you arrive by car, do not plan to stay for more than four hours and do not consider the nearby meadow already available. Its possible formalization requires a modification of the master plan, with a positive outcome that is not certain.

4. If your presence coincides with a major event or the arrival of coaches, consider the maneuver as a point of attention for pedestrians. The source reports that the buses have little space, but does not indicate timetables, alternative areas or different access methods.

5. For A2-A13, treat the right bank variant as an option within the consultation, not as a definitive work. The Municipality will express itself in a critical and negative way and prefers the official left bank variant; the source does not indicate a decision date.

For the cross-border worker who must organize access to the Canton of Ticino, the mappa dei valichi can be combined with reading the news. It is a separate tool: it does not certify the outcome of the consultation and does not transform the lawn into an official parking space. Before leaving, consult the storico del traffico e delle code ai valichi.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the limits of stay in the Ciossetto car park in Sementina?
