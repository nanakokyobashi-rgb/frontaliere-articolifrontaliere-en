Context

CH: health tax; deduction from allowances contravenes Bern-Rome agreements

The Federal Council has reaffirmed that a partial deduction from allowances of the revenue generated by the health tax would be contrary to the agreements between Bern and Rome on these workers. The so-called health tax is the contribution to the Italian National Health Service set by Law 213/2023 (Art. 1, paragraphs 237-239): from 3% to 6% of the net salary earned in Switzerland, with a minimum of 30 and a maximum of 200 euros for each month worked, set each year by the region of residence.

The situation is particularly complex, as the health tax is a sensitive issue that affects the lives of many Ticinese citizens.
