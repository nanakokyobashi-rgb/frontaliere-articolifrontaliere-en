Operational details

Implications for frontier workers

Border workers wishing to move to Bizzarone, a municipality in the Canton of Ticino, will need to carefully consider the tax and bureaucratic implications associated with this type of transfer. In particular, border workers will have to take into account the Italian and Swiss tax settings, as well as the Italy-Switzerland double taxation convention, which provides for the exemption for border workers.

The Italy-Switzerland double taxation convention was signed on 19 June 1963 and entered into force on 1 January 1964. Under the convention, frontier earners' incomes are exempt from taxation in both countries. However, frontier workers will have to file tax returns in Italy and Switzerland, as required by local regulations.

Procedures

The presentation of the tax return in Italy and Switzerland is mandatory for border workers. Border workers must submit their tax return by 30 April of the year following the year in which they received the income.
