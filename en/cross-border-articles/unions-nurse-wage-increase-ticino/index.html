On October 30, 2026, the OCST and VPOD trade unions announced a new mobilization together with the Staff Committee. The appointment is set for Tuesday, November 10, at 14.30, in Mendrisio, with an agenda focused on salary demands and recovering the cost of living in the cantonal social and healthcare sector.

At the center is the nursing staff of the Cantonal Socio-Psychiatric Organization, the OSC. The organizations are calling for its reclassification and recovery of the cost of living, arguing that the salary adjustment has been stalled for years.

According to OCST and VPOD, the reclassification would entail annual increases ranging from 5'159 to 8'941 francs, depending on the position held. This concerns the economic aspect of the demand, while the classification aspect concerns moving to a different salary grade.

For nurses, the request provides for moving from grade 4 to grade 5. For nurses with specializations, the proposed move is from grade 5 to grade 6. The Joint Commission formally established this reclassification in 2019.

The unions link the recognition to the training, skills and responsibilities required in the cantonal socio-psychiatric context. In their view, the adjustment could also make the profession more attractive.

The political point of the mobilization is that the decision was never implemented. The report therefore concerns a demand that remains open, not an increase that has already been applied: the Government continues to oppose it for financial reasons and, according to the trade union organizations, confirmed this position at the latest meeting devoted to the issue.

For those looking at work in Canton Ticino from across the border, the documented fact is a wage dispute in a cantonal socio-psychiatric facility. However, the source does not indicate how many workers are involved or distinguish between residents and cross-border workers.

The relationship between the 2019 decision and the situation described in 2026 provides the clearest comparison of the matter. Key points

Cost of living and scope of the report

A broader mobilization

The Mendrisio assembly will not be devoted solely to nurses of the Cantonal Socio-Psychiatric Organization. The recovery of the cost of living for public, para-public and social and healthcare staff is also among the topics of the meeting.

The second topic broadens the scope of the mobilization beyond the sole reclassification of the OSC. OCST and VPOD are calling for measures to protect purchasing power, while the claim concerning nurses relates to recognition of duties and responsibilities through the salary grades.

The report also states that, according to the unions, the request remains blocked by the cantonal budget as well. The organizations describe the failure to make the adjustment as a “long-standing freeze” and have decided to relaunch the mobilization following what they regard as a new refusal by the Government.
