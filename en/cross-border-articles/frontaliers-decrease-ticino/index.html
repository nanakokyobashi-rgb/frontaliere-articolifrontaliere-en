What does double taxation mean?

Double taxation is a phenomenon that occurs when an Italian citizen works in Switzerland and is taxed both in Italy and in Switzerland. This can lead to a double tax burden, which can be unfavorable for cross-border workers.

Italian tax + Swiss tax = Double taxation
