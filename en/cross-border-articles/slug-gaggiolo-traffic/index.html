Frequently Asked Questions
Why has traffic increased at the Gaggiolo crossing lately?
Traffic at the Gaggiolo crossing has increased by 15% since the beginning of 2024 compared to the previous year. This increase is mainly attributed to the return of frontier workers to Switzerland due to the economic crisis and the new 2024 tax agreement, which made it more convenient to work across borders by reducing taxes for frontier workers.
How much does it cost to cross the Gaggiolo crossing for border crossers?
Costs for border workers crossing the Gaggiolo crossing have increased by 20% compared to the previous year. For those who work in areas such as Bellinzona, Lugano and Mendrisio, the average daily expenditure is between 50 and 60 Swiss francs. A worker in Bellinzona, for example, can spend about 55 francs a day.
How many vehicles cross the Gaggiolo crossing annually?
In 2022, over 1.2 million vehicles crossed the Gaggiolo crossing, an increase of 10% compared to 2021. Since the beginning of 2024, the number of cars and trucks has further increased by 15% compared to the same period of the previous year, highlighting a steady growth in vehicle flow.
What solutions are planned to reduce traffic at the Gaggiolo crossing?
To address the problem, the Civil Status Office of the Canton of Ticino has decided to increase the checkpoints and improve the infrastructure of the crossing. This upgrading project, with an estimated cost of around 5 million Swiss francs, is expected to be completed by the end of 2024 to smooth transit.
