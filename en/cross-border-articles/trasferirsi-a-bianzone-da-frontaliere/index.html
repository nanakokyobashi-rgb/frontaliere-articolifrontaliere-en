Moving to Bianzone from border crossings: pros and cons

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 11 August 2026

For border crossers wishing to move to Bianzone, it is necessary to consider the pros and cons of this move.

Context

In a nutshell

  • The new Frontier Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020 and is in force from 1 January 2024.
  • Switzerland is not a member of the EU/EEA.
  • The Italy-Switzerland double taxation agreement is dated 9 March 1976.

Key facts

  • What: Move to Bianzone from border crossing
  • When: 1 January 2024
  • Where: Bianzone, Ticino
  • Who: Frontiersmen
  • Amount: Not specified

The border worker who wishes to move to Bianzone must consider the pros and cons of this move. On the one hand, the new Frontier Agreement may offer tax and labour advantages. On the other hand, the border worker must consider having to pay taxes in both countries.

Practical analysis

The border worker who wishes to move to Bianzone must consider the following situation:

  • If the border worker already has a work permit in Switzerland, it may be possible to move to Bianzone without having to pay taxes in both countries.
  • If the border worker does not have a work permit in Switzerland, you may have to pay taxes in both countries.

For example, if a border worker has an income of CHF 60,000 per year and wishes to move to Bianzone, it may be possible to avoid paying taxes in both countries if they already have a work permit in Switzerland. However, if you do not have a work permit in Switzerland, you may need to pay taxes in both countries, with a total amount of CHF 12,000 (20% out of CHF 60,000).
