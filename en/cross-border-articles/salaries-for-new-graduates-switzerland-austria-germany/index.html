Switzerland, Austria, and Germany are the countries that offer the highest salaries to recent graduates. This is what emerges from a recent study that compares the initial remuneration of young graduates in several European countries. Italy, on the other hand, ranks among the last places, closely followed by Poland and Spain.

Salary comparison

The salary comparison among major European countries shows a clear difference between northern and southern countries. Switzerland, with its solid economic system and dynamic labor market, offers the highest remuneration. Austria and Germany follow closely, with salaries that far exceed those offered in Italy.

Italy, despite being one of the largest economies in Europe, struggles to compete with northern countries in terms of remuneration for recent graduates. This salary gap is a factor that pushes many young Italians to seek job opportunities abroad, particularly in Switzerland and Germany.

Implications for cross-border workers

For cross-border workers employed in Switzerland, these data represent an additional incentive to continue seeking job opportunities abroad. Switzerland, in fact, not only offers higher salaries but also a solid pension system and a high quality of life.

Operational details

Practical Analysis

The wage gap between northern and southern European countries is a well-known phenomenon, but recent data confirms that the situation has not improved. Switzerland, Austria, and Germany continue to offer the highest salaries to recent graduates, while Italy struggles to compete.

Comparison with Switzerland

In Switzerland, recent graduates can expect salaries that are almost double those offered in Italy. This gap is even more evident when considering the cost of living, which is higher in Switzerland but offset by higher salaries.

For cross-border workers employed in Switzerland, it's important to also consider withholding taxes and pension contributions. The Swiss tax system can be complex, but with good planning, it's possible to optimize your tax and pension situation.

Comparison with Austria and Germany

Austria and Germany offer salaries that are slightly lower than those in Switzerland but still significantly higher than in Italy. These countries represent an interesting alternative for young Italians seeking job opportunities abroad.

However, in these countries as well, it's important to consider taxes and pension contributions. For example, in Austria, the pension system is similar to Switzerland's, with mandatory contributions to the AHV and the BVG.

Comparison with Poland and Spain

Poland and Spain offer salaries that are closer to those in Italy but still lower than in Switzerland, Austria, and Germany. These countries represent an alternative for young Italians seeking job opportunities abroad, but with lower salaries compared to northern European countries.
