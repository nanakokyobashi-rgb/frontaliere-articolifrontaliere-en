Luino, Artoni promotes meeting on border workers and health

Di Laura Bianchi · Published on 28 September 2026

Saturday 17 ottobre at the Villa Hussy library, Furio Artoni brings together citizens and Futuro Nazionale on cross-border workers, healthcare and businesses.

Context

In brief

  • Meeting on Saturday 17 ottobre at 16.30
  • Meeting at the Villa Hussy library
  • Focus on cross-border workers, healthcare and high fuel prices
  • Free entry and open to all

Key facts

  • Promoter → Furio Artoni
  • Movement → Futuro Nazionale
  • Location → Villa Hussy library
  • Time → Saturday 17 ottobre at 16.30
  • Access → free and open to citizens and the press

Saturday 17 ottobre at 16.30, the Villa Hussy library will host a public meeting promoted by Furio Artoni, a city councillor from Luino. The event, open to citizens and the press, is intended to discuss the problems of the border area and compare local issues with the positions of national politics.

The title chosen for the initiative is “Let's talk about the future. Let's talk about our area”. The meeting will be attended by the local, regional and national leaders of Futuro Nazionale, Roberto Vannacci's movement. The announced topics include high fuel prices, the situation of cross-border workers, healthcare, schools and the prospects for local entrepreneurship.

An agenda that starts from the local area

For those living in the border area and working across the border, the announced discussion directly concerns the situation of cross-border workers. The source indicates that workers' protections, tax rebates and the tax framework governing relations with Switzerland will be discussed. However, the announcement does not specify amounts, regulatory changes or operational measures.

Fuel costs will be addressed from the perspective of their impact on families and businesses. The agenda also includes healthcare and schools, with attention to the future of public services in the area, as well as the development prospects of local businesses.

The meeting will also serve a second purpose: it will be a press conference and an opportunity to clarify the political relationship between Artoni and Futuro Nazionale. In the preceding weeks, the opposition city councillor had stated that he had been a member of the movement even before his candidacy and election to the City Council. The event will therefore serve to address administrative, economic and political issues together.

Entry is free. Participation is open to all citizens, as well as the press.

Operational details

The key point for those who work across the border

For a cross-border worker, the interest of the appointment lies in the topics brought to the table, not in an already approved measure. The source discusses worker protections, tax rebates and the tax framework governing relations with Switzerland, but does not announce changes to permits, withholding tax, double taxation or other elements of personal taxation. Regarding the issue of ristorni Ticino-Italia, the announcement does not specify amounts, recipients or operating procedures. The discussion may therefore help clarify which political positions will be presented, without turning the meeting into an announcement of new rules.

The same caution applies to everyday expenses. High fuel costs are identified as an issue weighing on families and businesses, but the text does not report figures, increases, concessions or public decisions. For those who cross the border to work, the subject is therefore present on the conference agenda, while no amounts or procedures to be applied are described.

Announced topics and undefined topics

The meeting brings together different areas. On the one hand, there are issues concerning cross-border workers and relations with Switzerland; on the other, public services, including healthcare and schools, and the outlook for local entrepreneurship. This combination explains why the initiative is aimed not only at workers, but at the entire population.

The announcement does not go into detail about the G permit, AVS, LPP, LAMal, withholding tax, double taxation or Canton Ticino. These should therefore not be presented as specific subjects of the conference. The only announced tax reference concerns the framework governing relations with Switzerland, without further technical details.

The reference to tax rebates also remains general: the source includes them among the topics for discussion, but does not specify amounts, recipients, deadlines or calculation methods. The same applies to protections for cross-border workers. According to the announcement, it will be the public discussion that connects national positions with the issues affecting the Luinese area and border regions.

For this reason, based on the information available, the practical value of the meeting is above all political and informational: hearing the positions of the leaders of Futuro Nazionale and Artoni’s indications regarding the problems affecting the area.

Key points

How to follow the meeting

The first step for those who want to participate is to mark the appointment: Saturday, October 17 at 4:30 p.m., at the Villa Hussy library. Access is free and the meeting is open to all citizens. The source also indicates that the initiative will be open to the press.

What to expect from the conference

The announced program includes fuel costs, protections for cross-border workers, tax rebates, and the fiscal framework of relations with Switzerland. There will also be discussions on healthcare, schools, and the prospects for local entrepreneurship. Those who follow the topic of cross-border work will therefore be able to focus on the parts dedicated to cross-border workers and relations with Switzerland, distinguishing them from the sections dedicated to public services and businesses.

The minority municipal councilor had already declared that he was a member of the movement before his candidacy and election to the Municipal Council. The conference will allow for hearing the announced discussion with the movement's supporters and representatives directly.
