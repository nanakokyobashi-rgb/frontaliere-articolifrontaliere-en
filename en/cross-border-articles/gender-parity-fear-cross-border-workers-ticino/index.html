In Ticino, gender equality is still a distant goal. According to the data, only 28% of managerial positions are held by women. This figure is in line with the Swiss average, but it represents a challenge for the future. Gender equality is not just a matter of social justice, but also of economic efficiency. Several studies have shown that companies with a greater female presence in managerial roles tend to be more profitable and innovative.

Impact on Work Environment

An inclusive and equal work environment can improve productivity and employee satisfaction. Companies that promote gender equality tend to have a more positive work environment where employees feel valued and respected. This can lead to greater company loyalty and a reduction in turnover.

Reducing Wage Disparities

Wage disparities between men and women are a widespread problem around the world. In Ticino, the situation is no different. However, corporate policies that promote gender equality can help reduce these disparities. For example, the introduction of transparent and objective criteria for determining salaries can help ensure that men and women are paid equally for the same work.

Career Opportunities

Gender equality can also open up new career opportunities for cross-border workers. Companies that promote gender equality tend to have greater diversity in management roles, which can offer more opportunities for career advancement for all employees, regardless of gender.

Key points

Concrete Action

For cross-border workers in Ticino, there are several concrete actions that can be taken to promote gender equality in the workplace. Here are some steps you can follow:

1. Learn About Company Policies

Start by informing yourself about the company policies related to gender equality. Ask your employer if there are specific policies to promote gender equality and how they can be applied in your work context.

2. Participate in Mentoring Programs

If your company offers mentoring programs for women, participate. These programs can provide opportunities for professional development and networking that can help you advance in your career.

3. Promote Salary Transparency

Ask your employer to introduce transparent and objective evaluation criteria for determining salaries. This can help ensure that men and women are paid equally for the same work.

4. Get Involved in Diversity and Inclusion Initiatives

Participate in diversity and inclusion initiatives promoted by your company or your sector. These initiatives can offer learning and networking opportunities that can help you promote gender equality in your work environment.

5. Monitor Progress

Monitor the progress made by your company in promoting gender equality. Regularly ask for updates on ongoing initiatives and results achieved. This can help you stay informed and actively contribute to the process.
