Key facts

On November 25, 2025, in Montegrino Valtravaglia, a man attacked his wife and, according to the accusatory reconstruction, tried to strangle her by tightening an electric wire around her neck. The cable, again according to the prosecution, was about five feet long. For these facts, the judge for the preliminary hearing of the Court of Varese handed down a sentence of five years and two months in prison.

The judgment came at the end of a procedure with an abbreviated procedure. The decision was taken on the basis of the documents collected during the investigations, according to the procedural method referred to in the source. The measure concerns the incident that occurred in the municipality of Montegrino Valtravaglia and identifies the man involved as the recipient of the sentence pronounced by the judge for the preliminary hearing.

The story dates back to a day dedicated internationally to raising awareness against violence against women. At the time of the events, the man was 48 years old. After the attack, the Carabinieri of the Compagnia di Luino had arrested him, initially contesting the attempted murder. The arrest and the subsequent sentence represent the two judicial steps reported in the source: the first after the contested aggression, the second at the end of the abbreviated rite.

The center of the pronunciation remains the fact of November 25: the assault on his wife, the electric wire tightened around his neck and the size of the cable, indicated in about one and a half meters, are the elements of the accusatory reconstruction. The outcome indicated by the Court of Varese is a sentence of five years and two months in prison.

The disputed episode is therefore limited to the date, place and methods described: on 25 November in Montegrino Valtravaglia, with an electric wire about a metre and a half long. Operational details

During the hearing that preceded the sentence, the prosecutor had requested a sentence of four years and eight months. At the same time, he had requested the reclassification of the charge from attempted murder to aggravated injuries. The request therefore included both the duration of the sentence and the transition to a different classification of the contested offence.

The defendant's defence was entrusted to lawyer Elisa Benetazzo and she had requested an acquittal. The wife, assisted by lawyer Ivana Mombelli, had instead requested that the man be held criminally responsible and that the provisional payment be made. The source places these requests at the hearing prior to the judge's ruling.

The procedural framework reported in the news thus presents distinct positions. The prosecutor had indicated a sentence and asked to change the accusation; the defense had asked for acquittal; the wife had supported the criminal responsibility of the defendant and the economic request related to the provisional one. The woman was a civil party, an element that is indicated together with the assistance of lawyer Mombelli.

The decision then arrived with an abbreviated rite, that is, through a procedure in which the judge decides on the basis of the documents collected during the investigations. In this way, the ruling followed the hearing in which the requests of the public prosecutor, the defence and the civil party had been presented.
