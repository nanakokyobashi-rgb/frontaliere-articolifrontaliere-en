Biumo Park opening: Saturday, May 9th (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 May 2026

New equipped space with basketball court, ping pong and teqball. Ribbon cutting at 3 PM with tournaments and music

Context

In brief

  • New park in Biumo with basketball court, ping-pong and teqball
  • Inauguration Saturday May 9, 2026 at 3 PM

  • Collaboration with local associations for inclusive spaces

Key facts

  • What: Inauguration of new park in Biumo
  • When: Saturday May 9, 2026
  • Where: Via Arconati, Biumo, Varese
  • Who: Municipality of Varese, COVO Association, COOPUF Cooperative
  • Amount: 250,000 € (preliminary estimate, subject to change)
  • Accessibility: 10 minutes from Ponte Tresa and 15 minutes from Mendrisio

The new park in Biumo, located on Via Arconati, will be inaugurated on Saturday, May 9, 2026. This equipped space was created as part of the works for the new student housing in Varese, with an estimated investment of 250,000 €. The park offers a regulation basketball court (20x10 meters), ping-pong tables (4 in total), a teqball court, benches, and green areas for socializing. The initiative is organized in collaboration with the youth association COVO and the cooperative COOPUF, two active entities in the neighborhood and the city. The park gates will open at 1 PM for those who want to start with a picnic. At 3 PM, the official ribbon-cutting ceremony with the authorities is scheduled, followed by informal basketball tournaments, ping-pong challenges, and space for music with jam sessions and DJ sets.

Implications for cross-border workers

For cross-border workers residing in Italy and working in Ticino, this new park represents an additional opportunity to spend moments of relaxation and socializing during their free time. Its proximity to the Canton of Ticino makes it easily accessible also for those living in border areas such as Brogeda and Gaggiolo. For example, a cross-border worker who works in Lugano takes about 20 minutes by car to reach the park, while someone who works in Mendrisio takes about 15 minutes. The initiative promoted by local associations underscores the importance of collaboration between public entities and youth organizations to create shared and inclusive spaces.

Procedure to participate

For those who want to participate in the informal basketball tournament, play ping-pong, or come to play music, it is possible to write for information to associazionecovo@gmail.com or amministrazionecoopuf@gmail.com. Operational details

Practical Analysis

The inauguration of the new Biumo park, scheduled for Saturday, May 9th, represents an important addition to the range of recreational activities available to cross-border workers in Ticino. Located just a few minutes from the Italian-Swiss border, the park covers an area of approximately 10,000 square meters and offers equipped spaces for sports such as soccer, basketball, and tennis, as well as areas for picnics and relaxation. According to data from the Municipality of Varese, around 12,000 cross-border workers reside in the province of Varese and work in Ticino, and the Biumo park could accommodate up to 500 people per day during weekends.

The proximity to the border and the presence of equipped spaces make this park an ideal place to spend leisure time. Furthermore, the initiative promoted by local associations, such as the Associazione Giovani di Biumo, underscores the importance of collaboration between public entities and youth organizations to create shared and inclusive spaces. The total cost of the project was approximately 500,000 Swiss francs, partly financed by the Municipality of Varese and partly by local sponsors.

Comparison with the Previous Situation

Before the realization of this park, cross-border workers residing in Italy and working in Ticino had few options to spend their leisure time actively and socially. The lack of equipped spaces and local initiatives limited opportunities for aggregation and well-being. For example, a survey conducted in 2022 by the Associazione Frontalieri del Ticino revealed that 65% of respondents desired more recreational opportunities in the border area.

With the opening of the new Biumo park, cross-border workers will have a place to practice sports, meet new people, and participate in community events. The park will be open every day from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM and will offer services such as free Wi-Fi and children's areas. Additionally, weekly events such as soccer tournaments and musical evenings are planned, organized in collaboration with local associations.

Future Scenario

In the future, it is expected that the Biumo park will become a reference point for cross-border workers living and working in the area. The presence of equipped spaces and initiatives promoted by local associations could attract an increasing number of people, contributing to creating a more cohesive and active community. According to estimates by the Municipality of Varese, by the end of 2024, the park could host up to 1,000 visitors per day during weekends.

The collaboration between public entities and youth organizations could be replicated in other neighborhoods, offering new opportunities for aggregation and well-being for residents and cross-border workers. For example, the Municipality of Lugano is evaluating the possibility of creating a similar park in the Breganzona area, with an expected investment of approximately 600,000 Swiss francs.

Comparisons Between Practical Scenarios

Before the opening of the Biumo park, cross-border workers often had to go to more distant sports centers, such as the Centro Sportivo di Varese, located about 15 minutes by car. With the opening of the new park, cross-border workers will be able to save time and money, having a recreational area just a few minutes from the border. Moreover, the Biumo park offers a greater variety of activities compared to traditional sports centers, with spaces dedicated to relaxation and socialization.

In conclusion, the inauguration of the Biumo park represents a significant step towards creating a more inclusive and active community for cross-border workers in Ticino. With the right maintenance and promotion, the park could become a model for other similar projects in the region.
