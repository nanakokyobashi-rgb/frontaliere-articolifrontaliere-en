The regional councilor of the Pd Samuele Astuti highlighted the paradox of the health tax for frontalieri during a press conference in Luino. This norm, inserted in the budget law without an adequate consultation with trade unions and local entities, generates a 'double binary' discriminatory effect. Astuti criticizes the lack of coordination between Regions, noting that a frontalieri worker residing in Luino would be subject to the tax, while a colleague residing in Verbania would not, working in the same factory.

The health tax

The so-called health tax is the contribution to the Italian National Health Service set by Law 213/2023 (Art. 1, paragraphs 237-239): from 3% to 6% of the net salary earned in Switzerland, with a minimum of 30 and a maximum of 200 euros for each month worked, set each year by the region of residence.
