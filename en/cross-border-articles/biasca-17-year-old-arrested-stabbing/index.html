Biasca: 17-year-old arrested for stabbing near the station

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 October 2026

  • In Biasca, a 17-year-old Syrian resident arrested
  • Wounded in the abdomen, the 21-year-old is out of danger
  • The main charge is attempted murder
  • The assault took place near the station

The incident and the arrest

A 17-year-old Syrian residing in Riviera was arrested for the stabbing that occurred yesterday afternoon in Biasca, on via Bellinzona, near the station. The arrest was announced by the Juvenile Prosecutor’s Office and the Cantonal Police, which identified the young man as being accused of attempted murder. Alternatively, the alleged offenses are grievous bodily harm and simple bodily harm.

The victim is a 21-year-old Italian from Leventina. During a fight, according to the initial reconstruction, he was struck with a bladed weapon and suffered a serious abdominal wound. The wound made it necessary to transport him to hospital, where the young man arrived in serious condition. Doctors later reported that his life was no longer in danger.

The authorities’ statement places the stabbing during the altercation and reports that the fight broke out for reasons that the investigation will have to establish. The initial reconstruction therefore identifies the moment of the attack, while further inquiries will have to clarify how the argument developed.

The investigations and searches carried out after the incident led to the minor’s detention, also with the support of the Transport Police. After questioning, his arrest was ordered. The sequence communicated by the investigators distinguishes the moment of detention from the subsequent measure adopted at the end of the questioning.

The official communication indicates that the investigation remains open to reconstruct the exact dynamics of the events and clarify responsibility. The case is being handled by the Juvenile Prosecutor’s Office and the Cantonal Police, while from a medical standpoint the victim is no longer reported to be in danger of death. The incident thus remains linked to the elements already made public: the fight, the abdominal wound, the transport to hospital and the arrest of the 17-year-old.

Operational details

The information that emerged and the political reaction

Safety at stations

The information gathered in the following hours adds a still partial sequence. According to our information, a woman reportedly saw two young men flee on foot immediately after the assault. This was reported while the circumstances remained subject to investigation, without an official communication clarifying the role of both people seen leaving.

In the evening, Ticinonline reported that the 21-year-old, during the rescue efforts, had reportedly managed to give the names of the assailants. The same information reported that two young men had reportedly been intercepted by the police, detained and questioned. The following morning, investigators confirmed the actual arrest of a 17-year-old.

The incident has meanwhile prompted a political response. Biasca Plr Grand Councillor Omar Terraneo filed an interpellation with the government, pointing out that in recent months there had reportedly been frequent violent incidents, assaults, altercations and situations of marked distress at railway stations and in areas near the main centres.

Terraneo also refers to nuisance behaviour and repeated interventions by law enforcement. In the interpellation, he argues that stations must not gradually turn into lawless zones where citizens feel unsafe or avoid passing through at certain times of day.

«The quality of life in our canton depends both on actual safety and on perceived safety».

Among the questions addressed to the government, the deputy asks whether the reported incidents really indicate a deterioration of the situation or, above all, an increase in the population’s perception of insecurity. He also asks whether the current presence of law enforcement at stations is sufficient, particularly during the evening and night-time hours.

In closing, the interpellation asks whether a cantonal plan specifically aimed at the safety of stations and the main public transport hubs should be drawn up. The political debate thus extends to checks, the presence of law enforcement and the perception of safety in places of transit, in addition to the incident that occurred in Biasca.

Key points

Investigations still ongoing

Lega's questions

The inquiries announced by the juvenile justice authorities and the Cantonal Police are continuing with the aim of reconstructing the exact sequence of events and clarifying responsibility. No confirmation has been given regarding the possible involvement of a second young man: the press release makes no mention of it, and PolTi's communications service, when contacted, is adhering to official communications.

This leaves open one of the elements that emerged in the hours following the assault, when the presence of two young people fleeing was reported and, subsequently, news emerged of two people being intercepted, stopped and questioned. The official confirmation concerns the arrest of the 17-year-old, while the source provides no further information about a possible second person involved.

On behalf of Lega, MP and coordinator Daniele Piccaluga emphasized protecting the population, measures to prevent violence among young people and the effectiveness of checks concerning people authorized to stay in our country. One of his requests also concerns the arrested minor.

Piccaluga is asking for information about the 17-year-old and wants to know whether social services and the authorities were aware of any violent conduct on his part. The question is part of a broader set of questions aimed at clarifying the management of foreign young people within the asylum system and coordination between the authorities.

Among the aspects identified by the MP are also the resources available to the police and juvenile justice. His intervention thus places alongside the inquiries into the stabbing a series of questions about preventing youth violence, checks and the institutional management of foreign young people.

The further course of the matter remains linked to the inquiries already announced by investigators. These investigations will reconstruct the dynamics of the altercation and clarify responsibility, while official communication leaves open the question of the possible presence of a second young man and adds no information beyond the arrest of the 17-year-old.
