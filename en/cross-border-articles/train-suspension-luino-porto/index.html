Train suspensions between Luino and Porto Valtravaglia (cross-border guide)

Di Laura Bianchi · Published on 18 September 2026

RFI infrastructure interventions: stop regional trains between Luino and Porto Valtravaglia from 9.02 to 14.02 on specific days between September and October 2026.

Key Facts

What: Infrastructure upgrade works Manager: Rete Ferroviaria Italiana Sender: FS Group Affected section: Luino and Porto Valtravaglia Time slot: 9.02 AM - 2.02 PM Dates: September 23, 24, 25, 28, 29, 30 and October 1, 2026 Verification channels: Stations, Trenord website and app

Rete Ferroviaria Italiana, a company of the FS Group, has announced the start of major infrastructure upgrade works that will modify regional train traffic on the Pino Tronzano-Luino-Laveno railway line. The changes to the schedule will particularly affect connections in the section between the stations of Luino and Porto Valtravaglia. During the works, rail traffic will be temporarily suspended on specific days and time slots, requiring attention from travelers who regularly use the railway service on this Lombardy route.

Details of the days and times of the rail halt

The works scheduled by Rete Ferroviaria Italiana will require a total stop of regional trains in the time slot from 9.02 AM to 2.02 PM. The traffic restrictions will not be continuous for the entire duration of the works, but concentrated on specific days. More precisely, the suspension of rail traffic between Luino and Porto Valtravaglia will affect September 23, 24, 25, 28, 29 and 30, 2026, as well as October 1, 2026. Outside the indicated time slot, connections will resume regularly according to the ordinary schedule established for the railway line in question.

Operational details

Changes to the rail traffic schedule communicated by Rete Ferroviaria Italiana require careful planning for those traveling along the Pino Tronzano-Luino-Laveno line during the central hours of the morning and early afternoon. With the suspension of regional trains between the Luino and Porto Valtravaglia stations in the time slot between 9:02 AM and 2:02 PM on scheduled days, travelers will necessarily need to check alternative solutions in advance for their cross-border or regional journeys.

Verification methods and official information channels

To avoid inconvenience and stay updated in real time on any changes or additions to the traffic schedule, travelers are invited to carefully check the details directly at the concerned railway stations. As an alternative to the physical channels along the line, it is possible to check the regional train schedule by visiting the official website or using the Trenord mobile application, where all updates regarding rail connections modified due to infrastructure upgrade works are published.

Impact on connections along the route

Modifications and temporary service interruptions will specifically affect connections on the specific route between Luino and Porto Valtravaglia. Regular travelers along this route will need to take into account the five-hour time window, from 9:02 AM to 2:02 PM, during which regional trains will remain halted on September 23, 24, 25, 28, 29, 30 and October 1, 2026, planning their work, personal or study trips in advance to avoid unexpected issues related to the infrastructure upgrade construction sites managed by Rete Ferroviaria Italiana.
