Job and Internship Offers in the Province of Varese - 2 SEPTEMBER 2026 (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 September 2026

Discover the new job and internship opportunities offered by the Employment Centres of the Province of Varese.

Context

In brief

  • Job and internship offers proposed by the Employment Centers of the Province of Varese
  • The offers are dated 2 SEPTEMBER 2026
  • Information on salary transparency

Key facts

  • What: Job and internship offers
  • When: 2 SEPTEMBER 2026
  • Where: Province of Varese
  • Who: Employment Centers of the Province of Varese
  • Amount: Gross hourly wage: euro 5,99

The Employment Center of Busto Arsizio has published new job and internship offers for the province of Varese. The offers are dated 2 SEPTEMBER 2026 and include positions in various sectors. If you are interested in any of the experiences listed, send your CV to the relevant Employment Center as indicated in each announcement.

Information on Salary Transparency

Advertisements lacking information on salary transparency refer to job searches opened before the entry into force of Legislative Decree 96/2026 and/or before the adaptation of the forms to the new regulatory provision. It is also specified that the Employment Sector has complied with the regulation and that all new job advertisements managed by the Preselection Service of the Employment Centers of the Province of Varese already contain the indication on salary transparency.

Operational details

Implications for Cross-Border Workers

The job and internship offers published by the Employment Centers of the Province of Varese represent a significant opportunity for cross-border workers who wish to work in Italy. These positions can offer an interesting alternative for those looking for new work experiences or economic stability.

Application Procedure

To apply for one of the offered positions, it is necessary to send your CV to the relevant Employment Center. It is important to follow the instructions provided in each advertisement to ensure that the application is considered. Salary transparency is a crucial aspect to consider, especially for positions open before the entry into force of D.lgs. 96/2026.

Comparison of Offers

Job and internship offers vary considerably in terms of requirements, hours, and remuneration. For example, the APPRENTICE HAIRDRESSER in Castellanza offers a part-time contract with a gross hourly wage of euro 5.99, while the FOOD PREPARATION ASSISTANT in Busto Arsizio offers a 36-month apprenticeship contract with an annual gross salary (RAL) of 21,500 euros. It is essential to carefully evaluate your skills and preferences before applying.

Possible Scenarios

For a cross-border worker living in Ticino and working in Italy, these offers can represent an opportunity to improve professional skills and increase remuneration. However, it is important to consider the tax and social security aspects, such as withholding tax and AVS/LPP/LAMal contributions, which can affect net income.

Step-by-Step

Deadlines and Procedure
