FIALS: more nighttime security in Varese

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 29 September 2026

The union asks ASST Sette Laghi to review access points and shifts: a single guard in the Emergency Department is not enough.

Context

In brief

  • FIALS Sette Laghi requests more night security
  • A single guard is stationed at the Emergency Room
  • Limited access and internal patrols proposed
  • Intrusions and damaged vending machines reported

FIALS Sette Laghi has asked ASST Sette Laghi to review the organization of security during night hours at the Varese hospitals. The union brings together three requests: stricter regulation of access, enhanced security, and checks within the facilities. The stated reason is the size and complexity of the hospital, considered incompatible with the presence of a single security guard during the night.

The FIALS company secretariat asks to act before assaults or other critical incidents occur. Therefore, prevention in the proposal does not coincide only with the intervention following a problem, but with an organized presence in the hospital areas.

Entries to be reviewed

Among the hypotheses indicated is the night closure of access points deemed non-essential. The union specifically cites the gates on Via Lazio and Viale Borri. The stated objective is to leave open only the necessary entrances and ensure effective verification of the people entering the facilities.

'A hospital cannot become, during night hours, a place where it is possible to enter and move around without effective verification of access,' states FIALS company secretary Francesco Tucci.

More extensive security

FIALS also asks ASST to review the organization of the service and, if necessary, the conditions provided for by the contract. The union considers a single guard concentrated in the Emergency Room area insufficient and proposes increasing the number of staff during the night, complementing entrance control with periodic patrols in the various areas of the hospital.

Attention is also paid to those who may be alone in certain services, such as specific laboratories, and to personnel moving from one area to another to transport tests, biological samples, documents, or other material. According to FIALS, a distributed presence should also take into account these internal movements.

The statement recalls episodes of night intrusion during which vending machines were reportedly damaged and forced open. For the union, the value of the damages does not exhaust the problem: the incidents are a signal that requires more prevention. FIALS concludes by asking for a discussion with the workers' representatives on a comprehensive plan for night security in the ASST Sette Laghi hospitals.

Operational details

The practical issue: coverage and prevention

The FIALS request can be read as a comparison between a concentrated security presence and broader coverage. In the text, the security guard is described as being in the Emergency Department area; the proposal calls for entrance checks and patrols in the various areas in addition to that post. This is not a decision already adopted by ASST: it is the union's position, which calls for a review of the organization and, if necessary, of the contract.

Who remains exposed according to the report

The statement distinguishes, without listing specific departments or shifts, two situations. On the one hand, there are workers who may find themselves alone at night in some services, including certain laboratories. On the other, there is staff who must cross the facility to carry tests, biological samples, documents or other material. The practical issue raised by FIALS is therefore the continuity of protection even outside the Emergency Department area.

In this logic, closing a non-essential entrance and monitoring those that remain open are related measures, not alternatives. The first reduces the number of entrances to guard; the second makes checks on people entering the facilities effective. Patrols then add monitoring of internal areas, including those less frequented. This is the organizational scenario put forward by the request, not the announcement of a new service.

The same applies to intrusions. The union cites the damage to the vending machines not only because of the financial cost, but as a possible sign of an unauthorized presence inside the facility. Tucci's point is what could have happened if a worker had found themselves face-to-face with one of the intruders. FIALS uses this question to call for prevention before an assault or serious incident.

Key points

From the request to the discussion

The source presents a union request, not an operational schedule for employees. The only path explicitly requested is the discussion between ASST Sette Laghi and workers’ representatives. To remain faithful to the statement, the steps can be ordered as follows.

Four steps indicated by FIALS

1. Assess nighttime access points. The request to ASST is to examine which entrances are essential and which could be closed, with reference to the gates on via Lazio and viale Borri. The request does not say that closure has already been ordered: it presents it as a measure to be assessed.

2. Define the monitoring of entrances that remain open. The objective indicated by the union is to effectively verify the people entering the facilities, preventing access and movement without control during the night.

3. Review security and internal coverage. FIALS asks for more staff during nighttime hours, periodic patrols in the various areas, and attention to services where some workers may find themselves alone. This same point also includes assessing the conditions set out in the security contract, if necessary.

4. Open the discussion on the overall plan. The union asks to discuss with workers’ representatives a project combining regulated access, closure of unnecessary entrances, strengthened security, and internal patrols. In FIALS’s position, the safety of nighttime workers should not depend on chance.

This sequence distinguishes a union request from a measure that has actually been approved. The source text does not communicate ASST decisions, implementation timelines, or results already achieved. The process therefore remains the one indicated by FIALS: discussing an overall plan with the company and representatives.

Frequently Asked Questions
Why is FIALS critical of the current night watch?
The union considers that the presence of a single sworn guard concentrated exclusively in the Emergency Room area is insufficient. The size and complexity of the hospital, according to FIALS, require wider coverage to ensure the safety of workers who are alone in some services or who have to move within the structure.
What concrete measures are required for accesses?
FIALS proposes the night closure of non-essential accesses, specifically mentioning the gates of Via Lazio and Viale Borri. The objective is to limit admissions to those necessary and ensure effective verification of people entering the facilities, preventing the hospital from becoming a place of free movement without controls at night.
Have these new measures already been adopted by the ASST?
No, the text only reports the trade union request. FIALS requests a discussion with ASST Sette Laghi and the workers' representatives to define an overall night safety plan. No final decisions of the company, implementation times or changes already approved to the supervisory contract have been communicated.
