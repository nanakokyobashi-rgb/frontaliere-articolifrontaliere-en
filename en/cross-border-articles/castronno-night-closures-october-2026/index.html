Nighttime closures of SS 341 and A8 in Castronno October 2026 (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 26 September 2026

On the nights of 5‑6 and 6‑7 October 2026, from 21:00 to 05:00, SS 341 and the A8 section at Castronno will be closed simultaneously, with a possible delay of a few dozen minutes in the opening of the state road.

Context

In brief

  • In Castronno, SS 341 and a section of the A8 are closing together
  • The closures are scheduled for the nights between 5 and 7 October 2026
  • The affected time window runs from 21:00 to 05:00
  • SS 341 could close a few minutes late

Key facts

  • What: simultaneous closure of SS 341 and the A8
  • Where: Castronno
  • When: nights between Monday 5 and Wednesday 7 October 2026
  • Time: from 21:00 to 05:00
  • Reason: scheduled work on the road network
  • Possible variation: delay in the actual closure of SS 341

Two consecutive nights

In Castronno, State Road 341 and the section of the A8 will be inaccessible to traffic during nighttime hours between Monday 5 and Wednesday 7 October 2026. The measure has been arranged to allow scheduled work on the road network and involves both the state road and the motorway during the same period.

The first interruption will begin on the evening of Monday 5 October, at 21:00, and continue until 05:00 on Tuesday 6. The second is scheduled between Tuesday 6 and Wednesday 7 October, again from 21:00 to 05:00. There will therefore be two consecutive nights with the same restriction.

The simultaneous nature of the closures is what makes local traffic more complex. The source notes that the double interruption leaves few alternatives for traffic and may cause major difficulties for travel in the Castronno area. For those who need to cross the area during the indicated hours, the availability of routes cannot be considered normal: the directions arranged on site must be followed.

Before the actual closure on SS 341, a setup phase is also planned. Staff will have to position signs and vehicles on the motorway section, in order to ensure the safety of personnel and vehicles. For this reason, the restriction on the state road could begin a few tens of minutes later than 21:00. This is an operational delay related to preparing the worksite, not a change to the overall schedule indicated for the two nights.

The mayor of Castronno, Giuseppe Gabri, issued an appeal to residents and motorists:

«Citizens and motorists are therefore urged – the appeal of Giuseppe Gabri, mayor of Castronno – to plan their journeys in advance, pay the utmost attention to the road signs and use alternative routes»

The notice therefore concerns a double nighttime closure, concentrated in the same time window and accompanied by operational instructions for those who will need to travel in the area.

Operational details

Why the overlap complicates the journey

The difficulty for those who need to travel to Castronno during the two nighttime windows arises from the overlap of the restrictions. Only one route is not affected: the source indicates that Strada Statale 341 and the stretch of the A8 will be closed during the same interval. It is precisely this simultaneity that reduces the available alternatives and makes a last-minute decision less effective.

Key points

How to organize the journey

The Municipality's notice makes it possible to devise a simple procedure for anyone who needs to travel in the Castronno area during the two affected evenings. The first step is to check the date, because the restrictions concern two consecutive but distinct nights.

The operational sequence

1. Check whether the journey falls between the evening of Monday 5 and the morning of Tuesday 6 October, or between the evening of Tuesday 6 and the morning of Wednesday 7 October.

2. Consider the time period indicated in the notice: from 21:00 to 05:00 the following morning. During this interval, the SS 341 and the section of the A8 are indicated as inaccessible to traffic.

3. Plan the journey in advance, following the recommendation addressed by the mayor to residents and motorists. The simultaneous closures leave few alternatives for local traffic.

4. Once you arrive in the affected area, pay attention to the road signs. The source asks you to scrupulously follow the directions laid out on site to avoid getting stuck near the closure points.

5. Use the alternative routes suggested by the municipal administration. The notice does not replace the signage present during the roadworks and does not indicate a single route valid for all motorists.

6. Take the preliminary safety phase into account. The blockage on the SS 341 could take effect a few tens of minutes late relative to 21:00, because signs and vehicles must be positioned on the motorway section.

The sequence is also useful for anyone who needs to coordinate multiple journeys on the same night: first identify the date, then check the time period, and finally follow the directions that will be available along the road network. The possible delay in starting the state road should be considered an effect of the setup, not as a general change to the measure.
