Parking and traffic at Circolo Hospital: constructive discussion

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 30 April 2026

Meeting between Asst Sette Laghi, Varese municipality and unions to improve parking and traffic at Circolo Hospital.

The meeting on parking and traffic at Varese's Circolo Hospital, held on April 28, 2026 at Villa Tamagno, saw the participation of Asst Sette Laghi, the Municipality of Varese, represented by Public Works Councilor Andrea Civati, and trade union organizations. The aim was to address the critical issues related to the internal and external parking areas of the hospital, a strategic node for workers and users.

During the meeting, Asst Sette Laghi illustrated the current state of the parking lots and the measures already implemented to increase the availability of spaces. Preliminary project proposals were presented and the need to update the existing management models. Furthermore, a future tender for the management of the parking areas was announced, scheduled by the end of 2026.

Proposals and perspectives

Among the improvement hypotheses that emerged during the discussion, there are interventions on internal traffic, better organization of access flows, and the possibility of developing new parking spaces, even through project collaborations. A point shared by all participants was the framing of the parking issue within a broader vision of sustainable urban mobility.

Operational details

Analysis of the Proposals

The proposals presented during the meeting aim to improve internal traffic and the management of access flows to the Circolo Hospital. Among the proposed solutions, there are interventions on internal traffic, better organization of access flows, and the possibility of developing new parking spaces.

Interventions on Internal Traffic

Interventions on internal traffic are fundamental to improving access to the hospital and reducing waiting times. The proposals include reviewing access routes, optimizing traffic lights, and creating priority lanes for emergency vehicles. These interventions aim to make traffic more fluid and reduce waiting times for patients and visitors.

Organization of Access Flows

Better organization of access flows is essential to ensure easy and safe access to the hospital. The proposals include creating dedicated routes for patients, visitors, and staff, as well as establishing checkpoints to manage traffic more efficiently. These interventions aim to reduce waiting times and improve safety within the hospital.

Development of New Parking Spaces

The development of new parking spaces is one of the most important solutions to address the critical issues related to parking. The proposals include creating new multi-level parking lots, using advanced technologies for parking management, and collaborating with local authorities to identify new parking areas. These interventions aim to increase the availability of parking spaces and reduce the inconveniences related to parking.

Key points

Concrete actions for cross-border workers

For cross-border workers who work or use the services of the Ospedale di Circolo, it is important to be informed about the concrete steps that can be taken to improve the situation of parking and traffic. Here is a step-by-step guide to navigating the next phases of the process.

Monitor developments

The first step is to monitor the developments in the process of improving parking and traffic. The parties have agreed to an update by the end of May to further explore the proposals. It is important to stay informed about developments and actively participate in future consultations.

Participate in consultations

Active participation in future consultations is essential to ensure that the proposed solutions meet the specific needs of cross-border workers. It is important to express your opinions and suggestions during the consultations to help define the concrete actions to be taken.

Use sustainable mobility services

Promoting the use of public transport, safe cycling and pedestrian paths is one of the proposed solutions to improve sustainable urban mobility. Cross-border workers can contribute to this initiative by using the available sustainable mobility services and promoting the use of alternative means of transport.

Collaborate with local authorities

Collaboration with local authorities is essential to identify new parking areas and promote integrated solutions for parking management. Cross-border workers can collaborate with local authorities to help define the concrete actions to be taken to improve the parking and traffic situation.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the main issues related to parking at the Circolo Hospital?
The main issues related to parking at the Circolo Hospital include limited availability of parking spaces, inefficient management of parking areas, and complex internal traffic. These issues cause inconvenience for both employees and hospital users.
What are the proposals to improve the parking situation?
Proposals to improve the parking situation include interventions on internal traffic, better organization of access flows, development of new parking spaces, and updating existing management models. Additionally, a future tender for the management of parking areas is planned.
When is the update on the improvement proposals expected?
The parties have agreed to provide an update by the end of May to further explore the proposals and continue the process of improving accessibility to healthcare services.
