Who is a border customer advisor in Ticino?

A border customer advisor in Ticino is a professional who works for a company or firm based in Switzerland, but who has business relationships with customers or suppliers in Italy or other European countries. This type of consultant often works in municipalities such as Bellinzona, Lugano, Locarno and Mendrisio, where the presence of companies and businesses is higher.

How do you earn a border customer advisor in Ticino?

The profit of a border customer advisor in Ticino depends on various factors, such as his experience, his skills and his position within the company. In general, a border customer advisor in Ticino can earn between CHF 80,000 and CHF 120,000 per year, but there may also be cases where the gain is lower or higher.
