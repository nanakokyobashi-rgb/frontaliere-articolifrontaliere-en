Diesel excise: 232 million from ministries, MEF in the lead (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 8 August 2026

The MEF covers 125 million of the 17 cent cut on diesel (7-24 August). Here's how you split the funds and what changes for border workers.

Context

In brief

  • 17 cents/litre discount on diesel from 7 to 24 August
  • Coverage: 232.3 million from ministry spending cuts
  • The Mef contributes 125 million, more than half of the total
  • For 25 August, coverage with mini extra VAT revenue from July

Key facts

  • What: discount on diesel excise duties
  • When: 7-24 August, then 25 August with another decree
  • Where: Italy, affects those who refuel before the border crossings
  • Who: Council of Ministers on 4 August, decree law in the Official Gazette
  • Amount: 232.3 million from ministries; 125 from the Mef

The Ministry of Economy is the main financier of the 17-cent discount on diesel decided by the Council of Ministers on 4 August. The decree law, published in the Official Gazette, quantifies the coverage obtained through ministry spending cuts at 232.3 million euros, needed to finance the per-litre cut in effect from 7 to 24 August.

The Mef puts 125 million euros on the table, equal to more than half of the entire amount. The other ministries are much further behind: Enterprises and Made in Italy with 14.5 million, Foreign Affairs with 14 million. Also exceeding 11 million are Labour (11.6), Environment and Energy Security (11.3) and Health (11). Completing the picture are Universities and Research (9.8), Interior (8.1), Infrastructure and Transport (7.3), Agriculture (5.5), Tourism (5), Culture (4), Education (3) and Justice (2.15).

Operational details

What really changes for those who fill up

The cut of 17 cents per litre is not uniform: it depends on the distribution network and the period. From 7 to 24 August, those who refuel in Italy benefit from the discount, but only if the final price to the distributor is adjusted. For the border worker who lives in the province of Varese or Como and works in Canton Ticino, the choice of distributor becomes even more strategic.

Before and After: Simulation

Let's take the case of a 50-litre tank. With a discount of 17 cents, the savings are 8.50 euros in full. On a weekly basis (two full), we are talking about 17 euros, or 68 euros per month. Not huge figures, but that added to the Swiss price increases of AVS and LPP can make a difference.

However, it should be considered that the benefit is temporary: it ends on August 24. On August 25, the coverage will arrive with another decree of the MEF, which will use the mini extra income VAT of July. This means that the discount may be extended, but is not guaranteed.

The comparison with Switzerland

In Switzerland, diesel costs on average more than in Italy, even with the discount. Those who cross the border to work are therefore interested in filling up before entering Canton Ticino. But beware: the convenience depends on the CHF/EUR exchange rate. With a strong franc, filling up in Italy remains advantageous even without a discount.

How to make the most of the discount

If you are a frontier worker working in Ticino, here is a practical procedure to maximise the benefit of the cut on excise duties.

Steps to follow

1. Check the dates: the discount is active from 7 to 24 August. Mark the due date on the calendar. 2. Compare prices: Use local apps or sites to find the distributors with the lowest price on your way to Brogeda or Gaggiolo. 3. Fill up before the border: in Italy, with the discount, the cost per litre is lower than in Switzerland. But check the CHF/EUR exchange rate: if the franc is weak, the difference shrinks. 4. Keep an eye on August 25: if the Mef decree extends the discount, you can continue to save. Otherwise, the price will go up again. 5. Frequently Asked Questions
How long does the diesel excise discount last?
The discount of 17 cents per litre is in force from 7 to 24 August. For August 25, the financing will come with a subsequent decree of the Ministry of Economy, which will use the mini extra income from VAT in July. An extension beyond this date is not guaranteed.
Who funds the excise tax cut?
The cut is financed with 232.3 million euros of spending reductions by ministries. The Ministry of Economy contributes 125 million, more than half of the total. This is followed by Companies and Made in Italy (14.5 million), Foreign Affairs (14 million) and other minor departments.
Does the cut also apply to those who refuel in Switzerland?
No, the discount applies to Italian distributors. Those who cross the border to work in Ticino can benefit by refuelling before entering Switzerland, where diesel costs on average more. Convenience also depends on the CHF/EUR exchange rate.
