A2 in Coldrerio, the 73-year-old injured in the accident has died

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 October 2026

The 73-year-old Swiss man residing in the canton of Zurich died in hospital after the accident on the A2 in Coldrerio. He was the passenger in the car driven by a 68-year-old woman.

Context

In brief

  • A 73-year-old man died after an accident in Coldrerio
  • The crash occurred on Friday at 15.30 on the A2
  • The car rear-ended an articulated lorry stopped in the lay-by
  • The 68-year-old female driver sustained minor injuries

Key facts

  • Victim → 73-year-old Swiss citizen residing in the canton of Zurich
  • Location → north-south carriageway of the A2, in Coldrerio
  • Time → 15.30 on Friday
  • Circumstances → car collided with an articulated lorry stopped in the emergency lay-by
  • Rescue → transported to hospital aboard a REGA helicopter

The 73-year-old Swiss citizen residing in the canton of Zurich died in hospital after being seriously injured in the accident that occurred at 15.30 on Friday on the north-south carriageway of the A2, in Coldrerio. The report therefore concerns the outcome of the accident and the evolution of the condition of the front passenger of the vehicle involved.

The man was occupying the front seat of the car. A 68-year-old woman was driving. The car had rear-ended an articulated lorry that was stopped at the side of the carriageway, in the emergency lay-by. The position of the articulated lorry and the rear-end collision are the details indicated in the statement.

After the accident, the 73-year-old was transported to hospital aboard a REGA helicopter. The driver, on the other hand, sustained only minor injuries. The death was reported on Saturday morning by the Ticino cantonal police. The location, time, vehicles and condition of the two occupants make up the picture disclosed by the cantonal authorities.

Operational details

In the report by the Ticino cantonal police, the position of the vehicles is described precisely: the articulated lorry was stationary at the side of the carriageway, in the emergency lay-by, while the car rear-ended it. The incident occurred on the north-south carriageway of the A2, in Coldrerio.

A second element concerns the people on board. The front passenger was the 73-year-old Swiss citizen residing in the canton of Zurich, who later died in hospital. The car was driven by a 68-year-old woman, who sustained only minor injuries. The different severity of the consequences is therefore an integral part of the information released after the accident.

The passenger was transported by a REGA helicopter. Hospitalization and the subsequent death are the two medical stages indicated for the man. For the driver, on the other hand, the statement reports only minor injuries. These are the documented local details regarding the involvement of the two occupants.

Key points

The follow-up to the news is the update issued on Saturday morning by the Ticino cantonal police. The statement announced that the 73-year-old man, who had already been hospitalized after sustaining serious injuries in the accident on the A2, had died.

The timeline indicated is essential: at 3:30 p.m. on Friday, the accident occurred in Coldrerio; the front passenger was taken to hospital aboard a REGA helicopter; then, in the police's subsequent update, his death in hospital was announced. These are the steps reported for the man.

For the driver, the statement indicates minor injuries. The car was driven by the 68-year-old woman and had rear-ended the articulated lorry stopped at the side of the carriageway, in the emergency lay-by. The available report adds no other stages: the documented follow-up ends with the announcement of the death. The police statement is the latest development reported in the news.
