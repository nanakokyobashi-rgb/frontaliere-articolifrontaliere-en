Federal Office of Customs and Border Security cuts 25 management positions

Context

The Federal Office of Customs and Border Security (FOCS) has decided to cut 25 management positions. This decision is part of a broader reorganization that aims to reduce bureaucracy and increase customs efficiency.

According to internal sources, the reduction in managerial positions was necessary to adapt to the new regulations on border security. The Federal Law on Border Security (LSC) of 2019 has in fact introduced new rules for border management and the prevention of the smuggling of irregular migrants.

Ticino customs, in particular, was among the first to implement these new rules. "We were among the first to implement the LSC and create a new model of border management," says a source from the Ticino customs department.

The reorganization of the UDSC also provides for the creation of new operational units that will deal with the prevention of the smuggling of irregular migrants and border management. “It will be possible to contact these new services directly from border stations and borders,” another department official explains.

The reduction in managerial positions was made possible by the Federal Public Employment Reforms (RFPI) Act of 2019. This law introduced new rules for the management of public personnel and allowed administrations to reduce the number of employees.

Key points

This change will be implemented by 31 December 2025, as established by the Federal Council Directive of 15 February 2023, which provides for a 10% reduction in staff by 2026. This decision was taken in order to increase the efficiency and speed of customs operations, while reducing costs to the federal budget.

According to the latest data, in 2022, Lugano customs handled over 250,000 passengers and 150,000 vehicles, with freight traffic exceeding 1,500 containers per day. The reorganization of customs operations aims to reduce waiting times for passengers and vehicle drivers, improving their experience.

Bellinzona Customs, which is located a few kilometers from the border with Italy, is one of the busiest in the canton. In 2022, it handled over 200,000 passengers and 100,000 vehicles, with freight traffic exceeding 1,000 containers per day.

To implement this reorganization, the UDSC has established a set of objectives and performance indicators. These include:

  • Reduction of waiting time for passengers by at least 30%
  • Reduction of waiting time for drivers of vehicles by at least 25%
  • Reduction in the number of complaints for passengers and drivers of vehicles by at least 20%
  • Reduction of freight traffic by at least 10%
