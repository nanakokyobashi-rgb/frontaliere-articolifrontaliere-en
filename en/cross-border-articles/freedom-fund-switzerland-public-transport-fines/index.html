Fines on public transport: the fund that pays for the needy (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 29 July 2026

The association Freiheitsfonds Schweiz supports people in financial difficulties to avoid alternative detention in case of non-payment of fines.

Context

In a nutshell

  • The association pays fines for those who travel without a valid ticket.
  • The main objective is to avoid substitute detention.
  • 228 days in prison with 14,200 francs were avoided.
  • The fund operates through crowdfunding and has raised 92,000 francs.

Key facts

  • What: Payment of fines for the indigent on public transport
  • When: Active since 2025
  • Where: Switzerland
  • Who: Association Freiheitsfonds Schweiz
  • Amount: 14,200 francs spent so far

The association 'Freiheitsfonds Schweiz', officially active since 2025, is responsible for paying the financial penalties of destitute people who, caught travelling on public transport without a valid transport ticket, are unable to pay. The initiative, organized as a grassroots democratic collective, explicitly aims to prevent the extreme consequences related to insolvency, in particular the application of a substitute prison sentence. According to the co-promoter Basil Weingartner, the association's intervention has so far allowed a total of 228 days of detention to be avoided. To achieve this goal, the collective has allocated about 14,200 francs. The mechanism of operation of the fund is based on a crowdfunding fundraising campaign, hosted on the association's website. Operational details

The Swiss penalty system provides that, in the event of non-payment of a fine, the amount due may increase significantly due to recovery procedures. When it is not possible to proceed through forced execution or garnishment of wages, the debt is converted into a substitute prison sentence. This dynamic puts people in a situation of economic precariousness in a condition of extreme vulnerability. The association fits into this context to prevent the lack of immediate liquidity from translating into a deprivation of personal freedom. The issue of alternative detention for fines of up to 5,000 francs is the subject of political debate at different institutional levels. Recently, the National Council rejected a motion calling for alternative measures to imprisonment for this type of debt. However, the comparison remains open at the cantonal level. For example, last May, the Bernese State Council sent the Confederation a cantonal initiative, mandated by the Grand Council, which calls for an adaptation of the federal Constitution. The proposal aims to ensure that, even after the conversion of a fine into a substitute penalty, the possibility of carrying out public utility work as an alternative to detention remains. Key points

For those facing extreme economic difficulties due to public transport sanctions and risking detention, the association serves as a reference point. The procedure to access the support of the 'Freiheitsfonds Schweiz' requires the applicant to meet one of the specified conditions: receiving a summons for detention or already being in prison. The first step is to visit the official association portal. The site, available in German, acts as a central hub for submitting requests. It is crucial for interested parties to carefully review the eligibility criteria, as the fund evaluates each individual situation to ensure that available resources are directed to those who genuinely cannot afford to pay. Once a request is submitted, the collective examines the provided documentation before proceeding with the direct payment of the fine. This process is exclusively aimed at avoiding the imposition of the alternative penalty. Frequently Asked Questions
Who can apply for the fund's help?
The aid is intended for destitute people who have been caught travelling on public transport without a ticket and who, not being able to pay the fine, risk alternative detention. You must have already received a notice to appear for the commencement of detention or be already in prison.
What is the purpose of the association Freiheitsfonds Schweiz?
The aim of the association is to avoid the negative consequences of substitute detention, such as job loss or the inability to take care of one's children. The fund operates as a grassroots democratic collective to pay the fines of those who do not have the means to do so.
How is the association's activity financed?
The association raises the necessary funds through an active crowdfunding campaign on its official website. So far, the fund has raised about CHF 92,000, which is used to pay fines for people who meet the criteria.
