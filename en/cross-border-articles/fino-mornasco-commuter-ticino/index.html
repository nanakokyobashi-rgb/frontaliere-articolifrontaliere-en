Fino Mornasco is located in a strategic position in the southern area of Como, attracting those who wish to work across the border while maintaining lower real estate costs compared to the municipalities immediately close to the border. Those who plan to live in Fino Mornasco and work in Ticino as a cross-border worker primarily evaluate the intermodality of transport and the distance from the main customs points.

Transport logistics and travel times

The presence of the motorway junction on the A9 Lainate-Como-Chiasso motorway allows you to reach the Brogeda motorway crossing in approximately fifteen to twenty minutes in ordinary traffic conditions. At peak times in the morning, however, the queues at the entrance to the commercial and tourist customs can significantly extend the journey times towards Lugano or Mendrisio. The road alternative includes secondary roads towards the minor passes in the Como area, useful on days of intense congestion.

On the public rail transport front, the city railway station guarantees frequent trips to Como San Giovanni and Brianza. From Como it is possible to board the TILO regional trains headed towards Chiasso, Bellinzona and the rest of the Canton of Ticino. For those looking for a comfortable journey without the stress of queues at customs checkpoints, the train option represents a solid and economically sustainable choice. Operational details

From a tax and social security point of view, the worker with G permit must carefully distinguish his position based on the date of hiring. The New Cross-border Workers Agreement, signed on 23 December 2020 and entered into force on 1 January 2024 after Italian ratification with Law 83 of 13 June 2023, introduced differentiated taxation criteria between new and old cross-border workers.

Swiss tax and contribution framework

Workers already active before 17 July 2023 fall under the transitional regime valid until 2033, with an exempt amount set at 7,500 euros. Anyone who started the business subsequently benefits from a tax exemption of 10,000 euros on their total income. Withholding tax on employee income is withheld only in Switzerland; Italy applies IRPEF on foreign income, recognizing a tax credit to avoid double taxation, to be indicated in the EC part of the tax return through the Revenue Agency. The Italian-Swiss Convention on double taxation of 9 March 1976 remains the fundamental pillar of bilateral cooperation, remembering that Switzerland is not a member of the European Union nor of the European Economic Area.

Compulsory social contributions weigh on the Swiss gross salary: old age and survivors' insurance AVS/AI/IPG at 5.3% to be paid by the employee, AD/AC unemployment insurance at 1.1% up to a maximum of 148,200 francs, LAINF non-professional accident insurance (between 0.7% and 1.5%) and the LPP pension fund, with rates varying between 7% and 18% based on age group starting from 25 years. The Italian IRPEF brackets are then applied according to the rates of 23% up to 28,000 euros, 33% between 28,001 and 50,000 euros, and 43% for the excess amount. Key points

Healthcare management requires specific attention immediately after the start of the employment relationship. Cross-border workers have the right to choose between the Italian national health system (with registration for INPS/SSN protection) and the compulsory Swiss insurance KVG. The deductibles for adults in the Swiss system vary from 300 to 2,500 francs per year, an element to be weighed in relation to one's health conditions and family burden. Before formalizing the move to Fino Mornasco, it is also necessary to evaluate the impact of the currency exchange on savings, given that daily living expenses in Italy are paid in euros against a salary received in Swiss francs.

Practical steps for transferring

To organize the transfer efficiently, it is advisable to follow a path structured in sequential steps:

  • Register the new registered residence with the Municipality of Fino Mornasco as soon as the rental or purchase contract for the property is available.
  • Communicate the update of the address to the Swiss employer and to the cantonal migration office responsible for the adaptation of the G permit.
  • Consider opening a multicurrency bank account to optimize conversions and monitor market fluctuations.
  • Formally exercise the right of option relating to health coverage within the deadlines established by cantonal regulations.
  • Prepare the Swiss income documents to fulfill the annual declaration obligations with the Revenue Agency.

Frequently Asked Questions
What tax exemption is due to those who work in Ticino?
For new frontier workers subject to the New Agreement, a deductible of 10,000 euros applies on work income, while for old frontier workers included in the transitional regime 2024-2033, the exempt fee is 7,500 euros.
How is double taxation of income avoided?
Tax at source is only withheld in Switzerland. In Italy, to avoid double taxation on income produced abroad, the taxpayer declares the salary and benefits from the tax credit by filling in the EC framework of the tax model.
What Swiss social contributions are withheld in paychecks?
The gross includes the AVS/AI/IPG at 5.3%, the AD/AC at 1.1% up to 148,200 francs, the LAINF insurance between 0.7% and 1.5% and the LPP occupational pension with rates between 7% and 18% from the age of 25.
How does health insurance work for those residing in Italy?
The holder of permit G has the right of option: he can choose to remain in the Italian health system or join the Swiss compulsory health insurance LAMal, with deductibles for adults between 300 and 2,500francs per year.
