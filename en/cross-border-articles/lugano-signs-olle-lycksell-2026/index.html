Lugano signs Olle Lycksell until 2029 (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 5 May 2026

The Swedish winger joins Ticino with a three-year contract. Here's what changes for the Bianconeri fans

Context

In brief

  • Olle Lycksell signs with HC Lugano
  • Three-year contract until 2029
  • Fifth foreign player signed by the club

Key facts

  • What: Arrival of Olle Lycksell at HC Lugano
  • When: From May 4, 2026
  • Where: Lugano, Canton Ticino
  • Who: HC Lugano, Olle Lycksell
  • Amount: Three-year contract

HC Lugano has officially announced the arrival of Olle Lycksell, a Swedish center-winger born in 1999. The player, hailing from Oskarshamm, will wear the white and black jersey until the end of the 2028/29 season. After Calle Dahlström, Zach Sanford, Rasmus Kupari, and Jere Innala, Lycksell is the fifth foreign player signed by the Ticinese club.

Lycksell, standing at 181 cm and weighing 90 kg, began his career in the youth sector of Linköping, debuting in the top Swedish league at the age of 18. He has played for Färjestad and Växjö Lakers, totaling 231 games and 106 points. Drafted in 2017 in the sixth round by Philadelphia, he has played in the NHL with the Flyers and Senators, and in the AHL with the Phantoms and Senators.

The GM's comment

The General Manager of Lugano, Janick Steinmann, commented: 'Olle is an offensive attacker who is in the prime of his career. Over the past four seasons, he has fought for a spot in the NHL, proving to be a solid player. He works very hard and has a strong competitive spirit. I can't wait to have him on our team where he will increase the quality.'
