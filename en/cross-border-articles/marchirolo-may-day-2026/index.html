Key facts

  • What: Celebration of May Day
  • When: May 1, 2026
  • Where: Piazza Borasio, Marchirolo
  • Who: Municipality of Marchirolo
  • Amount: Not specified

The Municipality of Marchirolo celebrated May Day with a day dedicated to the International Workers' Day, marked by participation and sharing. The program began at 10:15 AM with a gathering in Piazza Borasio, from which a parade departed, reaching the Council Hall of the municipality, accompanied by the municipal band. This was followed by a greeting from Mayor Emanuele Maria Schipani and a theatrical reading titled "The Blacksmith and His Daughter," presented along with "For You and With You" by Francesca Ravioli. During the morning, certificates of merit were also awarded, a moment dedicated to recognizing the commitment and contribution of citizens and local entities.
