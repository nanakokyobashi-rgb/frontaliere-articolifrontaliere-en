Working as a plumber in Ticino: salary, requirements and permit G

Working as a plumber in Ticino can be a rewarding choice for those who wish to practice their profession in a region with a high level of economic development and a high demand for services. However, it is important to know the requirements and regulations needed to work as a plumber in this territory.

Registration and recognition of the qualification

The plumber company must be registered in Switzerland, with the Commercial Register of the Canton of Ticino. It is important to note that the qualification can be recognised at the Federal Department of Economics, Education and Research (SECO) provided that it has been obtained in a country with which Switzerland has a qualification recognition agreement. In this way, foreign plumbers can work as plumbers in Ticino without having to obtain another degree.

G Permit and Work Requirements

To work as a plumber in Ticino, you must obtain the G permit, which allows you to practice your profession in Switzerland. The G permit is issued by the Canton of Ticino and requires the presentation of a series of documents, including the recognised educational qualification and a certificate of good conduct. It is important to note that the G permit is only valid for a certain period of time and must be renewed every two years.
