The plumbing company must be registered in Switzerland, at the Commercial Registry of the Canton of Ticino. It is important to note that the qualification can be recognized by the Federal Department of Economic Affairs, Education and Research (SECO) provided that it was obtained in a country with which Switzerland has an agreement on the recognition of qualifications. In this way, foreign plumbers can work as plumbers in Ticino without having to obtain another qualification.

G Permit and Work Requirements

To work as a plumber in Ticino, you need to obtain the G permit, which allows you to practice your profession in Switzerland. The G permit is issued by the Canton of Ticino and requires the presentation of a series of documents, including the recognized qualification and a certificate of good conduct.
