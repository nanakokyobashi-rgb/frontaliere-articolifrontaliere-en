Kastritis: absences are not excuses, get to work immediately

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 28 September 2026

After the defeat at Masnago against Virtus Bologna, the coach identifies the work and the away trip to Scafati as priorities.

Pallacanestro Varese lost at Masnago to Virtus Bologna. At the end of the home defeat, coach Giannis Kastritis spoke of disappointment at the result, but also of clear-headedness in his analysis. For the Biancorossi coach, the first difference was in the approach to the game and the opponents' physical impact from the opening tip.

Kastritis praised Virtus, who arrived at Masnago with a perfect approach and great solidity from the first minute. Varese knew how dangerous the Bologna team was on the boards and in offensive transition, but in the opening minutes they were slower to react and match the initial intensity. For the coach, this gap affected the course of the game.

The coach did not, however, want to place the blame on his players. Virtus, he explained, is one of the strongest teams in the league, and its performance can serve as an example of how to play at this level.

The reaction in the third quarter

In the third quarter, Pallacanestro Varese made a major effort. The team managed to match Virtus's physical level and do the right things on both ends of the court, almost completely erasing the deficit. The comeback was not completed, however: in the fourth quarter, on two or three key possessions, Varese was unable to stay within reach.

Kastritis recalled that many players have had physical problems in recent weeks and are not yet at their best, despite having worked hard. The coach ruled out the absences of the two sidelined players becoming an excuse. For several players, the game represented their first exposure to the rhythm, speed and physicality of Italian basketball.

The game, in the coach's assessment, therefore serves to understand where the team stands and what it needs in order to grow. The next fixture indicated is the away game in Scafati. As for Della Valle, the situation is being assessed day by day together with the medical staff: the player is eager to return and the group is waiting to have him available again when he is ready.

The crowd also played a role in the evening. The fans filled the arena, creating a fantastic atmosphere, even though Varese was unable to reciprocate with a win.

Operational details

Two readings of the same game

The defeat in Masnago emerges from the assessments of the two coaches, who observe the game from different perspectives. Giannis Kastritis insisted on Virtus's start and on Varese's need to adapt to the opponents' intensity. Mumbru, instead, highlighted his players' response after the return journey from Istanbul and their ability to maintain energy in a game made difficult by the atmosphere in the arena.

The black-and-white coach credited his players with finding the right energy. He also admitted that Varese had important absences, but stressed the concentration maintained for almost all 40 minutes of play. In his account, the game remained open when the home side closed the gap.

Virtus's plan

Mumbru explained that, during the comeback, Virtus temporarily lost aggression in defense and balance in attack. The team nevertheless managed to regain control of the game. The plan prepared in a short time involved limiting Varese's three-point shooting percentages, controlling the rebounds and moving the ball well against the red-and-white team's defensive aggression.

These elements complete Kastritis's reading. The game is not described solely as a contest decided by the final result, but as a confrontation in which Varese had to contend with the physicality, speed and consistency of one of the strongest teams in the league. The response in the third quarter showed the possibility of closing the gap; the fourth-quarter possessions instead marked the limit indicated by the coach.

Mumbru then placed his team at 60% compared with the Super Cup, 10% more than a few days ago. He explained that new players are coming in and that the process is proceeding step by step, because so far a full training session has never been held. The next objective indicated is Tuesday's game in Milano, approached first and foremost with the intention of competing.

Key points

Available practical guidance

After the defeat, Kastritis pointed to a clear direction: Pallacanestro Varese must get to work immediately with the trip to Scafati in mind. This is the only upcoming commitment explicitly mentioned by the coach. The work will have to begin with an analysis of the opening minutes, the physical response required against a team like Virtus, and the management of the key possessions that in the fourth quarter prevented Varese from staying within reach.

To follow the team's progress, the text makes it possible to distinguish three concrete elements:

1. Preparation for Scafati, identified by Kastritis as the next step. 2. The condition of the group, with several players having had physical problems in recent weeks and not yet being at their best. 3. Della Valle's situation, assessed day by day together with the medical staff.

No additional date

For the reader, the possible course of action is therefore to follow the announced work and await updates on the players' availability without anticipating decisions that the coach has left to the medical staff's daily assessment.

The Masnago crowd remains another fixed point of the evening: the fans filled the arena and created a fantastic atmosphere. Kastritis said it was disappointing not to have given them a victory, while the expected response now comes through the indicated away trip and the team's work.
