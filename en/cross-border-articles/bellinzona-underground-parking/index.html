Bellinzona's Parking Crisis: A New Underground Garage Is on the Way

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 17 March 2026

The municipality of Bellinzona has announced the construction of a new underground parking garage with a public park and a new administrative building. The project aims to address the city's parking shortage, which has been affecting residents and visitors alike.

Bellinzona's Parking Crisis

Bellinzona is facing a severe parking shortage, with a deficit of 75 public parking spaces. The city center is particularly affected, with residents and visitors forced to search for parking in increasingly distant areas. This has a negative impact on the local economy and the environment.

A New Underground Garage Is on the Way

The municipality of Bellinzona has announced the construction of a new underground parking garage, which will be built near the Raiffeisen headquarters. The new garage will provide 75 public parking spaces, addressing the city's parking shortage. In addition to the garage, a public park and a new administrative building will also be constructed.

Project Details

The project involves the construction of a three-level underground garage, with a total of 75 parking spaces. The structure will be equipped with advanced security and surveillance systems. The public park will be built on a 1,500 square meter surface and will feature playgrounds for children, pedestrian paths, and green areas.

Financing and Timeline

The construction of the garage is scheduled to begin next year and will be funded by the municipality of Bellinzona and the Canton Ticino, with a total budget of 12 million Swiss francs. The project will be carried out in two phases: the first phase will involve the construction of the garage and the public park, while the second phase will focus on the construction of the new administrative building.

Comparison with Other Ticino Municipalities

Bellinzona's situation is not an isolated case. Other Ticino municipalities, such as Lugano and Locarno, are also facing similar problems. However, Bellinzona's solution appears to be one of the most innovative and comprehensive. For example, Lugano has built an underground garage with 200 parking spaces, but without a public park.

Regulations and References

The construction of the garage and the public park will be carried out in compliance with cantonal and municipal regulations on urban planning and the environment. Specifically, the law on urban planning of 2006 and the federal law on environmental protection of 1986 will be respected.

Conclusion

The construction of the new underground garage and the public park in Bellinzona represents a comprehensive and innovative solution to the city's parking shortage. The project will be carried out in compliance with regulations and will be funded by the municipality and the Canton Ticino. With its completion, Bellinzona will be able to offer a better quality of life to its residents and visitors.
