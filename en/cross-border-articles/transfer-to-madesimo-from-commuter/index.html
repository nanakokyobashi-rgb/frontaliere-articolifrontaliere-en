Moving to Madesimo from a border crossing: pros and cons

If you are a border worker working in Switzerland and wish to move to Madesimo, in the heart of the Alps, you need to consider the pros and cons of this decision. Switzerland is a country with a complex tax system, and border crossers are subject to specific rules.

Tax at source on earned income

Switzerland applies a source tax on labour income, which is calculated on gross salary. However, if you are a frontier worker, the tax is withheld only in Switzerland, while in Italy no tax is withheld.

New Frontier Agreement

On 23 December 2020, the New Frontier Agreement was signed, which comes into force from 1 January 2024. This agreement provides for new rules for frontier workers, including the possibility of withholding tax in Switzerland also for Italian workers.

Frequently Asked Questions
What tax innovations does the New Cross-Border Commuter Agreement between Switzerland and Italy introduce?
The New Cross-Border Labour Agreement, effective from 1 January 2024, amends the tax rules. It provides for the possibility of withholding tax on income from work directly in Switzerland also for Italian workers. Previously, withholding tax was only withheld in Switzerland, with no further withholding tax in Italy.
What are the advantages of choosing Madesimo as a residence for a cross-border commuter working in Switzerland?
Living in Madesimo offers advantages such as easier access to Swiss health and social services and the possibility of higher wages. You can benefit from significantly lower taxation on earned income than in Italy (up to 70% less), more affordable housing costs and the presence of essential services such as school and clinic.
What are the disadvantages or challenges of moving to Madesimo for a cross-border worker?
Cons include the potential subjection to a higher tax in Switzerland and the need to adapt to the local culture and language. Madesimo is an isolated municipality with limited services, which may require travel for more specialized services. In addition, you may encounter residence limits in Switzerland and a complex permit process.
How does Swiss withholding tax affect the net salary of a cross-border commuter?
Swiss withholding tax is calculated on gross wages and, for cross-border commuters, is only withheld in Switzerland, without further tax in Italy. For example, on a salary of CHF 5,000 per month, with a withholding tax of 7.7%, the resulting net salary would be CHF 4,625.
When does the New Cross-Border Agreement between Switzerland and Italy come into effect?
The New Cross-Border Labour Agreement, signed on 23 December 2020, entered into force on 1 January 2024. This agreement introduces new provisions for cross-border workers, in particular as regards the tax regime applicable to their earned income.
