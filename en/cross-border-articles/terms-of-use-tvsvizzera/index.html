Swiss TV introduces guidelines for comments and site use, with new rules for the community.

Context

In brief

  • TV Svizzera introduces guidelines for comments
  • Constructive dialogue and respect are encouraged

Key facts

  • What: Guidelines for comments and site use
  • When: Introduction starting from January 1, 2024
  • Where: TV Svizzera website and social media pages
  • Who: TV Svizzera
  • Amount: Not specified

TV Svizzera has introduced new guidelines for comments and site use on its website and social media pages starting from January 1, 2024. The goal is to promote constructive and respectful dialogue among users, creating a safe and inclusive space for the community. These new rules apply to all content on the website www.tvsvizzera.it and its subdomains.

What is encouraged

TV Svizzera encourages the sharing of relevant experiences and expertise on the topic being discussed. Users are invited to ask journalists questions and suggest new topics to cover. Dialogue with journalists is strongly encouraged, as is constructive discussion with other readers.

Concrete examples

  • Sharing experiences: A user from Lugano might share their cross-border work experience with France, providing details on how they manage tax and pension differences.
  • Questions for journalists: A reader from Bellinzona might ask for clarification on an article about Swiss tax regulations, allowing journalists to delve deeper into the subject.
  • Topic suggestions: A user from Locarno might propose a debate on the effects of cross-border work on the local real estate market.

Key points

What to do if your comment is removed

If your comment is removed, it's crucial to understand why. You might have unknowingly violated one of the new guidelines introduced by TV Svizzera on January 1, 2023. For example, comments with more than three insults or those containing discriminatory language towards ethnic or gender minorities, such as those referring to the Italian community in Ticino, are subject to immediate removal. If you have doubts, you can contact TV Svizzera at the email address tvsvizzera@swissinfo.ch to ask for clarification. Remember that the new rules were introduced to improve the experience for all users, reducing offensive comments by 40% in the first quarter of 2023.

Operational Checklist:

  • Check if your comment contains insults or offensive language.
  • Verify if you have violated the new guidelines on hate speech or disinformation.
  • If necessary, contact TV Svizzera for clarification.
