Public meeting in Via Boldoni between the centre-right parties to listen to citizens' demands in view of the future local elections.

Context

In a nutshell - Center-right gazebo set up in Via Boldoni in Como. - Meeting held between 10.00 and 12.30 to listen to the citizens. - Participation of Fratelli d 'Italia, Lega and Forza Italia. - Focus on local issues and future selection of the mayoral candidate. ## Key facts - What: Information gazebo and listening to citizens. - When: Morning, from 10.00 to 12.30. - Where: Via Boldoni, Como. - Who: Fratelli d 'Italia, Lega, Forza Italia. - Coordinators: Elena Negretti, Stefano Molinari, Alessandro Nardone, Sergio Gaddi, Davide Gervasoni. The morning of political activity in Via Boldoni saw the joint presence of Fratelli d 'Italia, Lega and Forza Italia, with the declared objective of collecting reports and proposals from the residents of Como. The initiative took place in a specific time slot, from 10.00 to 12.30, allowing provincial and citizen coordinators to dialogue directly with the public. Elena Negretti was present for the League, Stefano Molinari and Alessandro Nardone for Fratelli d 'Italia, while Sergio Gaddi and Davide Gervasoni participated for Forza Italia. Frequently Asked Questions
Which parties attended the meeting in Via Boldoni?
The initiative was attended by the political forces of the centre-right: Fratelli d 'Italia, Lega and Forza Italia, represented by their respective provincial and citizen coordinators.
Has the name of the mayoral candidate been announced?
No, the name of the mayoral candidate is not yet specified. The political forces have stated that the choice will be the consequence of teamwork and a common vision in the definition phase.
What issues were reported by citizens during the meeting?
Citizens expressed dissatisfaction with the commercial regulations defined as stringent, the high costs related to the parking of vehicles and the conduct of the current municipal administration.
