The Municipality of Lugano has approved the 2027 Budget and sent the report to the Municipal Council. A deficit of 14,7 million francs is forecast for current operations. The estimate is more favorable than the one previously formulated, but the financial recovery continues.

Comparison with previous estimates

For 2027, the Financial Plan 2026-2033 indicated a deficit of 19,5 million: the current result is more favorable by 4,8 million. A previous multi-year projection indicated a deficit of 30,1 million, against which the gap reaches 15,4 million. The 2026 Budget, on the other hand, had estimated a deficit of 4,2 million.

On the tax front, the Municipality confirms the tax multipliers. For individuals, the multiplier is set at 80%, while for legal entities it stands at 85%. These are the same levels set for 2026, after a three-percentage-point increase for both categories.

Total planned net investments amount to 97 million francs and also include the balance for the purchase of PalaRaiffeisen, whose payment has been postponed. Ordinary expenses are estimated at 510,8 million, while ordinary revenues amount to 224,1 million; the tax-funded requirement is 286,7 million.

The document already incorporates the financial recovery measures adopted by the Municipality and requested by the Municipal Council. Excluded, however, are the measures that, starting in 2028, will have to neutralize the financial repercussions of the popular votes held in 2025.

Financial recovery continues to be a central issue in the political debate. Last June, the Management Commission had expressed divergent positions regarding the Financial Plan and the methods of financial recovery, including those relating to containing current expenditure and resorting to asset revaluations.

It was also announced at the presentation that the current financial year is trending toward closing with a surplus in current operations. The 2025 Final Accounts had closed the books with a surplus of 23,7 million, compared with a forecast deficit of 24 million; the result was largely due to extraordinary factors.
