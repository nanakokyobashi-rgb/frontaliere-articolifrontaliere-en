Clash between two cars on the SP3 between Cantello and Varese: three people involved, two injured in hospital and heavy delays for border workers.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Clash between two cars on the SP3 between Cantello and Varese
  • Rescue call taken around 3.30 pm
  • Three people involved and two injured transported to hospital
  • Slowdowns and one-way alternating on the roadway

Key facts

  • What: Road collision between two cars
  • When: Around 3.30 pm this afternoon
  • Where: On Provincial 3 between Cantello and Varese
  • Who: Three people involved, including a 41-year-old woman and a 47-year-old man
  • Amount: No economic data specified

During the afternoon, a road accident affected the Provincial road 3 between Cantello and Varese, a route also known as the border road that leads to the Gaggiolo customs office. The call for help came around 3.30 pm, when two cars collided on the way due to causes still under reconstruction. The event immediately generated considerable inconvenience to road traffic in the area, creating concern for transit flows. The exact dynamics of the impact between the vehicles remains under scrutiny by the competent authorities involved on the spot to carry out the necessary surveys to clarify the responsibilities. The road affected by the accident plays a crucial role in the daily mobility of those who routinely move towards the border. Many commuters and citizens regularly use this road artery to reach customs areas and work destinations

Operational details

Impact on traffic and traffic management

The immediate consequences of the road clash on Provincial 3 were felt in a marked way on local traffic. Rescue operations and the need to manage damaged vehicles have made the imposition of traffic restrictions unavoidable. To allow the safe intervention of rescue vehicles and law enforcement, it was necessary to narrow one of the roads of the provincial road. This measure involved the temporary establishment of an alternate one-way route along the section affected by the collision, generating understandable queues and slowdowns for vehicles in transit. Many motorists and border workers who routinely travel the road leading to the Gaggiolo customs house have found themselves facing inconveniences in their afternoon journeys. Alternate traffic management continued for the time necessary to complete the surveys and remove the vehicles from the road, gradually restoring normal road traffic. Similar events on this guideline highlight how vulnerable border roads are to unforeseen events, influencing the travel times of those who move for work or personal reasons between Italy and Switzerland.
