The story of the Kurdish family expelled to Turkey has reached a crucial turning point. The State Council of Canton Ticino now has the opportunity to decide whether to give a chance to the two sons who remained in Switzerland. The brothers, Zelal and Yekta, find themselves in a delicate situation after the expulsion of their parents and their autistic brother to Turkey. The State Council's decision could change their fate, allowing them to stay in Canton Ticino with a B permit for integration.

What the B permit for integration means

The B permit for integration is a document that allows cross-border workers to reside and work in Switzerland for an extended period. This permit is different from the G permit, which is temporary and linked to cross-border work. The B permit offers greater stability and access to services such as AHV, BVG, and LAMal, which are fundamental for long-term integration.

The procedure for the recognition of the permits

To obtain the recognition of the B permits, the State Council must give a positive opinion on the application as 'cases of rigor'. This process is complex and requires an accurate evaluation of individual circumstances. The Kurdish brothers must demonstrate their integration into Ticinese society and their ability to adapt to Swiss norms.

What documents are required to apply for a B permit?
To apply for a B permit, you need to gather documents that demonstrate your integration into Ticino society. These documents may include work certificates, school attendance certificates, documents proving your participation in local life, and any other evidence that can support your application.
