  • Amount: Not specified

On the morning of August 13, 2007, it would have been Andrea Sempio, and not Alberto Stasi, who killed Chiara Poggi, as told by the two aggravating factors contested. After more than a year of investigations, consultations, and an expert opinion ordered with a probatory incident, the Prosecutor's Office of Pavia has closed the investigation into the murder of Chiara Poggi, hypothesizing a reconstruction far from that set out in the sentence with which Alberto Stasi, the former boyfriend of the victim, is finishing serving 16 years in prison.

The deputy prosecutor Stefano Civardi, with the public prosecutors Valentina De Stefano and Giuliana Rizza, has notified the conclusion of the investigation notice against Andrea Sempio, the longtime friend of the victim's brother, Marco, who has been defined as 'hostile' and dealing with a 'constant defense of office' of the current clerk in a telecommunications center.

Operational details

  • ### Analysis of New Evidence

The new scientific evidence presented by the Prosecutor's Office of Pavia has led to a completely different reconstruction of the murder of Chiara Poggi. The analysis of blood traces and the testimonies collected have indicated that Andrea Sempio would have acted with hatred and cruelty, entering the villa in Garlasco by taking advantage of the slightly open door and attacking the victim.

Comparison with the Previous Situation

Before this new investigation, the murder of Chiara Poggi had been attributed to Alberto Stasi, the victim's former boyfriend. The new reconstruction, however, suggests that Sempio would have acted with a different motive, related to a sexual rejection. This radically changes the perception of the case and raises new questions about justice and security.
