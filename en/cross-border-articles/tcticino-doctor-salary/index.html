Operational details

How much does a border doctor earn in Ticino?

The New Frontier Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020 and enters into force on 1 January 2024. This agreement provides for an exemption of CHF 10,000 for new frontier workers. Switzerland and Italy signed the Double Taxation Convention on 9 December 1976.

The income of a border doctor in Ticino may vary according to their experience and specialization. However, thanks to the New Frontier Agreement, frontier workers in Ticino can benefit from a higher income than in Italy.

For example, a border doctor in Ticino with 5 years of experience and specializing in cardiology can earn between CHF 150'000 and CHF 250'000 per year. This income may vary depending on the workplace and the number of patients treated.

In comparison, an Italian doctor with the same experience and specialization can earn between CHF 80,000 and CHF150,000 per year. This means that a border doctor in Ticino can earn up to CHF 100,000 more than an Italian doctor.

However, it is important to note that the income of a border doctor in Ticino may vary based on their specialization and experience. For example, a border doctor in Ticino specialising in orthopaedics can earn between CHF 120'000 and CHF 220'000 per year, while a doctor specialising in paediatrics can earn between CHF 100'000 and CHF 180'000 per year.

It is important to note that the Double Taxation Convention between Switzerland and Italy was signed on 9 December 1976.

If you are a frontier worker in Ticino, you can benefit from this agreement and have a higher income than in Italy. It is important to consult an attorney or financial expert for more information.

By comparison, if the border doctor had not benefited from the agreement, his taxable income in Switzerland would have been CHF 150'000, with a tax payment of about CHF 11'700.

Frequently Asked Questions
When does the New Border Agreement between Switzerland and Italy come into force and what benefits does it bring?
The New Frontier Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020 and enters into force on 1 January 2024. This agreement introduces a major tax exemption of CHF 10,000 on taxable income for new frontier workers working in Ticino, improving their tax situation.
How can I claim an exemption of CHF 10,000 for border workers in Ticino?
The exemption of CHF 10,000 can be requested at the tax office of the Canton of Ticino. It is essential to submit the application within the deadline for filing tax returns, accompanying it with documents certifying identity and work as a border worker, and verifying that you have not already applied for it.
What is an example of net income for a border doctor in Ticino with the new exemption?
Imagining a border doctor in Ticino with an annual income of 120'000 Euro (about 129'600 CHF with exchange rate 1:1.08), thanks to the exemption of CHF 10'000 provided for by the New Frontier Agreement, the taxable income on which taxes are calculated would fall to 119'600 CHF per year.
