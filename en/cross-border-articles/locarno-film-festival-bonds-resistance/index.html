Locarno Film Festival: culture as a link (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 5 August 2026

The Locarno Film Festival opens with 'Les Yeux Verts', exploring themes of trauma and resilience through cinema in Piazza Grande.

In brief

Key facts

  • What: Locarno Film Festival opening
  • Where: Piazza Grande, Locarno
  • Theme: Coming-of-age tale, trauma and resilience
  • Event: Screening of 'Les Yeux Verts'

The Locarno Film Festival has officially kicked off its edition with the inaugural screening of 'Les Yeux Verts' in the setting of the renowned Piazza Grande. The festival confirms itself as an essential cultural landmark for the Canton of Ticino, attracting spectators from across the region, including cross-border workers who experience the daily reality of the border area between Italy and Switzerland. The film presented acts as a contemporary coming-of-age tale, capable of weaving deep narratives on trauma and resilience, stimulating a collective reflection that transcends geographical boundaries.

The value of cinema as an aggregator

The strength of this event lies in its ability to transform the square into a place of cultural resistance against resignation. The directors, when asked about the genesis of the work, emphasized how human connection is the central element for overcoming difficulties. For the public that crosses the Brogeda or Gaggiolo border crossings every day to go to work, participating in such moments represents a breath of fresh air, an opportunity to connect with an intellectual dimension that transcends work-related duties linked to the G permit or the management of one's payslip. The festival, through its offerings, not only entertains but also educates, providing analytical insights that enrich the social debate in the Ticino context.

Operational details

The cultural impact of the Locarno Film Festival is also reflected on the quality of life of those who reside in Mendrisiotto or Lugano, areas where the concentration of border workers is particularly high. Participating in a screening in Piazza Grande means immersing yourself in a reality that enhances the regional identity while remaining open to the world. Often, for those who work in Switzerland, free time is limited by the fast pace of commuting, but events of this magnitude allow you to regain ownership of fundamental social spaces. It is in these moments that one perceives the 'resistance of ties' cited by the source, a concept that applies perfectly to the cross-border community.

Time management and cultural opportunities

Many workers, after finishing their shift and passing the queues, often reported on the portali di traffico, choose to dedicate their summer evenings to high-profile events. It is not just a matter of leisure, but of an investment in one's own psychophysical well-being. Resilience, the central theme of the film, is a skill that every border worker exercises daily in managing bureaucratic challenges, such as those related to the new tax regulations or LAMal. The festival therefore becomes the mirror of a community that does not give up, but seeks, through art, to keep human contacts and relationships alive despite distances and work complexities. Key points

For those who wish to participate in the events of the Locarno Film Festival, it is essential to plan your travels carefully, considering the saturation of the car parks and the crowds. Using public transport, such as the train, is often the most effective option for those arriving from Italy, avoiding the problems of road traffic. The festival schedule can be consulted online and allows you to select the screenings according to your availability, making the use of the event compatible with working hours. It is advisable to check in advance the availability of tickets and subscription options for those who intend to attend multiple evenings, thus optimizing the cultural experience.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the main theme of the opera that inaugurates the Locarno Film Festival?
The film 'Les Yeux Verts', which inaugurated the exhibition in Piazza Grande, is described as a fairy tale of contemporary initiation that explores the complex dynamics between trauma and resilience, offering a deep reflection on the strength of human bonds.
How can a border worker integrate participation in the festival with work?
Planning is key. Using public transport to reach Locarno and consulting the official programme allows you to reconcile projections with working hours. Proper budget management, facilitated by the use of the salary calculator, also allows you to plan expenses for cultural events.
Why is the Locarno Film Festival relevant to the cross-border community?
The festival represents a moment of cultural aggregation that crosses borders, offering border workers the opportunity to immerse themselves in a stimulating intellectual context, fundamental for psychophysical well-being and to keep social ties alive on the Ticino territory.
