In recent days, at the Brogeda border crossing, customs officers and financial police officers from Ponte Chiasso intercepted over 240mila euro. The report refers to two separate operations and therefore presents a double currency seizure, not a single episode described as a unified event.

The key point is the combination of location, personnel and amount. Brogeda is the border crossing indicated in the headline and the text; Ponte Chiasso identifies the customs officers and financial police officers involved in the two operations. The figure is expressed as over 240mila euro: this conveys a threshold, not an exact sum to be attributed to the entire episode or divided between the two operations. The headline and the text agree on the operation's dual nature and on the indicated location.

The time reference is deliberately broad: the source speaks of recent days and does not place the two interventions on a calendar date. The phrase separate operations merely clarifies that the cases are separate; it does not make it possible to establish whether they occurred on the same day or at different times.

Five control steps

1. Start with the location. The reference is the Brogeda border crossing. Do not replace it with other border crossings or a different municipality: the source does not extend the case to other locations.

2. Preserve the sequence. The operations are two and distinct. This wording should be maintained when reporting the news, without merging them into a single inspection and without assigning the same amount to each.

3. Respect the figure. The source says over 240mila euro. Writing exactly 240mila or dividing the sum between the two operations would add information that is not present.
