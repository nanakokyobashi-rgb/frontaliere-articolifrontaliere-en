The first assessment

The project launched by the joint Local Police service of Cuveglio and Cuvio to intensify checks has already led to the withdrawal of seven driving licences and the seizure of ten vehicles. The initiative aims to strengthen road safety and crime prevention in the areas concerned.

A significant portion of the operations was devoted to driving under the influence of alcohol. Officers also carried out checks using a breathalyser and, in several cases, detected blood alcohol levels above 1,5 grams per litre.

The operational tally therefore includes the withdrawal of seven driving licences and the seizure of ten vehicles. For four vehicles, the measure was ordered for confiscation. These are the initial figures communicated together with the launch of the project, which combines traffic enforcement with crime prevention in the area.

The Local Police of Cuveglio and Cuvio thus focused part of its activity on one of the behaviours identified in the project, namely driving under the influence of alcohol, supplementing the checks with the tools provided for verifying blood alcohol levels. The result includes measures concerning both licences and vehicles, with a distinction for the four seizures intended for confiscation.

The enhancement therefore does not concern just one type of check. The planning described by the source links road safety with crime prevention, using the initial operations to strengthen the presence of the Local Police in the territories of Cuveglio and Cuvio.

The operational approach thus combines compliance with the rules of the Highway Code with surveillance in residential areas.
