General Regulatory and Tax Aspects

The regulatory framework of reference for frontier workers finds a fundamental pillar in the Convention against Double Taxation between Italy and Switzerland, signed on 9 December 1976. This is accompanied by the New Frontier Agreement, signed on 23 December 2020 and in force from 1 January 2024, ratified in Italy with Law 83 of 13 June 2023. With regard to the taxation of income from work, the tax at source is withheld

Contributions and pension for the border worker

The paycheck of the border worker who resides in Gavirate reflects the complex interaction between the Swiss and Italian social security systems. The competent bodies such as SECO, SEM, INPS and the Revenue Agency manage the different administrative aspects related to the cross-border employment relationship. Tax rates and taxes at source are established by federal or cantonal laws and administered by the Federal Tax Administration at the federal level and by the competent cantonal administrations, while the Federal Statistical Office provides the aggregated reference data without fixing rates.

Frequently Asked Questions
How is the income of the border worker living in Gavirate taxed?
Income from employment in Ticino is subject to withholding tax withheld directly in Switzerland. Italy eliminates double taxation by applying a tax credit through the CE framework of model 730.
What are the rules for old and new frontiersmen?
Old border workers, i.e. those who already worked in Ticino before 17 July 2023, benefit from an exemption of 7,500 euros and a transitional regime from 2024 to 2033. The new frontier workers, on the other hand, have a deductible of 10,000 euros.
How does LAMal health insurance for border workers work?
Border workers with a G permit have the right of option between the Italian health system and the Swiss LAMal. Adult deductibles under LAMal range from CHF 300 to CHF 2,500.
