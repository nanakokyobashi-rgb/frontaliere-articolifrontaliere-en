Cross-border rebates, Pellicini: “Let’s set up a working group right away” (cross-border guide)

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 2 July 2026

The member of parliament and mayor of Luino supports the Ministry of Economy's approach to resolving the impasse over rebates.

Context

In Brief - The Ticino State Council has suspended the payment of a portion of the rebates. - Andrea Pellicini, a member of the Ticino State Council and mayor of Luino, supports Minister Giorgetti’s proposal to convene a working group. - Representatives of border municipalities must be included in the negotiations. ## Key Facts - What: Suspension of the payment of a portion of the rebates. - When: Recent decision by the Ticino State Council. - Where: Ticino, Italy. - Who: Andrea Pellicini, Member of Parliament and Mayor of Luino. - Amount: Not specified. Andrea Pellicini, Member of Parliament and Mayor of Luino, has expressed concern regarding the decision by the Ticino State Council to suspend the payment of a portion of the rebates, despite the Federal Council in Bern’s opposition. This decision has a direct impact on the resources allocated to local governments in the border region. Pellicini supported the mediation process initiated at the government level to break the deadlock. “For this reason, I support Minister Giorgetti’s proposal to immediately bring representatives from Lombardy and Ticino together around a negotiating table so that all outstanding issues regarding the rebates can be addressed directly,” he stated. The mayor’s request focuses on including local administrators in the bilateral negotiations, since municipalities use these funds to provide basic services. “I hope that, alongside the Lombardy Region, representatives of the border municipalities—the recipients of these resources, which are essential for the management of local governments—will be present at the table,” the mayor added. Representatives from the border regions have initiated initial institutional contacts to coordinate the mayors’ position. “In this regard, it is encouraging that Massimo Mastromarino, president of the association of municipalities, has already taken action,” Pellcini concluded.
