On Saturday 26 settembre at 18,30, Mastini Varese face the second matchday of the championship away in Fassa. The yellow-and-black team visit the highly rated Falcons with one clear objective: to sustain a high tempo for longer and try to earn points.

The matchup comes after the debut against the Pirati di Appiano, which ended 2-4. The defeat was not decisive: one of the goals came into an empty net, while analysis of the game left a positive assessment of the way Mastini performed on the ice. Rich's hockey is described as enjoyable, built on the balance between tactical precision and competitive intensity.

The test against Fassa

However, the result against Appiano also highlighted a limitation. The volume of play generated by Mastini uses up a lot of energy; when the collective effort drops, the defensive structure becomes more vulnerable and allows penetrations and shots.

The statistics reinforce this reading. Against the Pirati, the Varese net received more than twice as many shots as the opponents. The defense was asked to do extra work in closing down, especially in the third period, when the fuel seemed to be running low. The trip to Fassa will therefore be a test to understand how long the team will be able to sustain its intensity.

Fassa come from an away win of 3-6 over Pergine, achieved without too many problems. The game promises to be different from the one against Appiano: the Pirati were described as more well-organized and predictable, while the Fassa side offer a less rigid and more varied game.

The composition of the squad also remains an element to watch. The awaited return of Tommaso Terzago, the addition of the long-absent Levi Glasman and the hope of an additional defenseman could provide the fuel needed to make the yellow-and-black team's drive more progressive and consistent. The source specifies, however, that these considerations come after only sixty minutes of play and are therefore partial. After Fassa, the schedule takes Mastini to Caldaro on 1° ottobre, for a tricky away game that on paper is almost impossible.
