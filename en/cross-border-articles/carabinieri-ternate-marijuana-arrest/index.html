On the morning of October 8, in Ternate, the Carabinieri of Ternate Station arrested a 41-year-old Italian man in flagrante delicto. The man is accused of possession and cultivation of narcotic substances. The operation led to the seizure of 37 marijuana plants, a precision seesaw and a few grams of already dried and crushed substance.

The operation was carried out through a house search. The search involved the house and its appurtenances, where the military found the plants and other elements then seized. The news therefore links the dispute to two aspects that emerged in the same operation: the presence of the plants and the discovery of already dried and crushed substance.

The 41-year-old was already known to law enforcement. At the time of the check, he was also subject to the prevention measure of the oral public safety notice. The reference to this measure appears in the reconstruction of the operation together with the arrest in flagrante delicto and the findings recovered during the search.

The operation at Ternate

The central data of the intervention is the seizure of 37 marijuana plants, to which are added a precision seesaw and a few grams of substance stored in a glass jar. The search therefore led to the discovery of plants, a weighing instrument and already dried and crushed substance, all elements indicated in the reconstruction of the Carabinieri.

The complaint includes both the possession and cultivation of narcotic substances. The two conducts are indicated together in the news regarding the arrest of the 41-year-old, carried out in flagrante delicto by the military of Ternate Station. The seizure concerned the plants, the substance and the material found during the home inspection.

Operational details

The home search made it possible to pinpoint exactly where the plants were located. In the storage room belonging to the home, the officers found 36 hanging plants, already in the drying phase. Their heights ranged from 50 centimeters to two meters, an interval that therefore included specimens of different sizes.

A plant separate from those hanging in the storage room was instead located in the courtyard garden. It was planted outdoors and reached about two meters in height. The count reported in the operation consists of the 36 plants in the storage room and this additional plant, for a total of 37 specimens.

Between the storage room, courtyard and bedroom

The inspection also covered the man's bedroom, where the officers found a working precision scale and a glass jar. The container held a few grams of substance that had already been dried and ground, presumably intended for packaging and consumption.

The distribution of the finds adds detail to the seizure's simple numerical tally. On the one hand, there are the plants hanging in the storage room, all in the drying phase; on the other, there is the plant located in the courtyard garden. In the bedroom, meanwhile, the weighing instrument and the substance kept in the jar were found.

The locations identified are therefore three: the storage room belonging to the home, the courtyard garden and the bedroom. Each corresponds to a different type of find: the plants hanging to dry, the plant still in the ground and the scale together with the dried and ground substance. These are the conditions described during the same home search.

Key points

The planned checks

The follow-up to the operation is linked to the seizure and the technical checks announced after the discovery. The plants, the substance and the material found were seized. The measure therefore includes all the items recovered during the home search and indicated in the officers’ account.

Laboratory analyses are planned to determine the active ingredient and assess the quality of the substance.

As regards the personal measure, the 41-year-old man was placed under house arrest. The measure was adopted pending the determinations of the judicial authority. The man's position is thus placed in the phase that follows the arrest and precedes the decisions of the judicial authority.

The sequence indicated for what follows therefore combines the laboratory checks on the active ingredient and the quality of the substance with the judicial authority’s determinations regarding the position of the 41-year-old man. House arrest is the measure indicated while this step remains pending definition.

For now, the follow-up to the intervention comprises the material already seized, the planned technical examinations and the personal measure applied. The 41-year-old man is under house arrest pending the determinations of the judicial authority, as indicated in the account of the operation.
