On October 24, from 9.30, Centro Congressi Varese «Ville Ponti» will host the conference dedicated to cross-border work and its impact on the local area. The event is promoted by Cisl dei Laghi, Cisl Piemonte Orientale, Cisl Sondrio, Ocst and BiblioLavoro.

The research opens the proceedings

At 10, Francesco Girolimetto, director of BiblioLavoro, will present the research «Working across the border: conditions, needs and prospects of cross-border workers». The program places the study at the center of the morning, before the discussion on the local economy and the role of institutions.

The opening remarks will be delivered by Dino Perboni, regional secretary of Cisl Lombardia. After this address, the conference will move on to the first roundtable, dedicated to the impact of cross-border work on the local economy.

The discussion will involve Andrea Puglia, deputy cantonal secretary of Ocst; Michele Rossi of the Chamber of Commerce of the Canton of Ticino; Mauro Vitiello, president of the Chamber of Commerce of Varese; and Elena Ugazio, general secretary of Cisl Piemonte Orientale. Matteo Inzaghi, director of Rete 55, will moderate.

Two roundtables, two topics

At 11, the discussion will focus on the action of institutions in support of cross-border work. Participants will include Marco Contessa, Cisl's national head for cross-border workers; Massimo Sertori, Lombardy regional councillor; Massimo Mastromarino, president of the Association of Border Municipalities; and Luca Della Bitta, mayor of Chiavenna. Journalist Anna Campaniello will moderate.

The closing remarks, scheduled for 12.30, will be delivered by Mattia Pirulli, national secretary of Cisl. The source presents the morning as structured around research dedicated to cross-border workers, a discussion on the local economy and a focus on the institutions involved in cross-border work.
