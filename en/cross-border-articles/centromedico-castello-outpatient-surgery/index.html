Bellinzona is enriched with a new medical facility dedicated to outpatient surgery. Centromedico Castello, part of the Swiss Medical Network group, now offers advanced services for procedures that do not require hospital stays. Among the novelties, a standout is an ophthalmological center managed by Swiss Visio, specializing in eye surgeries.

The facility represents an important opportunity for cross-border workers who work in Ticino and reside in Italy. The geographical proximity and the quality of the services offered can make medical procedures more accessible, which would otherwise require longer trips or extended waiting times.

What Centromedico Castello offers

Centromedico Castello is presented as a reference point for outpatient surgery, offering a complete range of medical services. Among these, ophthalmology, orthopedics, gynecology, and general surgery stand out. The presence of Swiss Visio also guarantees specialized services for eye care, with cutting-edge technologies and qualified personnel.

For cross-border workers, the possibility of accessing these services in a modern and well-equipped facility represents a significant advantage. The proximity to Bellinzona, easily reachable from the main border crossings, makes the facility accessible even for those residing in Italy.

Practical Implications for Cross-Border Workers

The new Centromedico Castello facility offers various opportunities for cross-border workers employed in Ticino. Among these, the possibility of accessing high-quality medical services with reduced waiting times and geographical proximity for those residing in Italy stands out.

Comparison with the Previous Situation

Before the opening of Centromedico Castello, cross-border workers needing outpatient surgical procedures often had to turn to facilities located in other parts of the Canton Ticino or even in Italy. This resulted in longer waiting times and more complex travel arrangements. With the new facility, however, it is possible to access advanced services more quickly and conveniently.

Concrete Scenarios

Consider the case of a cross-border worker needing ophthalmological surgery. Before the opening of Centromedico Castello, they would have had to turn to a specialized center in Italy or wait longer in Switzerland. Now, thanks to the presence of Swiss Visio within the facility, they can access high-quality services more quickly and closer to their workplace.

Another scenario involves a cross-border worker needing orthopedic surgery. In this case as well, the new facility offers the possibility of accessing advanced services with reduced waiting times, ensuring a quick return to daily life.

Comparison with Other Facilities

To better understand the advantages offered by Centromedico Castello, it is useful to compare the facility with other clinics in Ticino. For example, Clinica Luganese Moncucco offers similar services but is located in Lugano, farther from the main border crossings. Centromedico Castello, on the other hand, is situated in Bellinzona, easily reachable from Italy.

Key points

What to do to access the services

To benefit from the services offered by Centromedico Castello, cross-border workers must follow some specific procedures. Here is a step-by-step guide:

1. Booking: The first thing to do is to book an appointment at the facility. This can be done through the Centromedico Castello website or by contacting the center directly. During the booking, it is important to provide all the necessary information, such as personal details and the reason for the visit.

2. Documentation: Before the visit, it is necessary to prepare all the required documentation. This includes the identity card, the G permit, the health insurance card, and any previous medical reports. It is important to verify with the facility which documents are necessary for your specific case.

3. Medical visit: During the visit, the doctor will assess the need for any surgical interventions and provide all the necessary information. It is important to ask questions and clarify any doubts regarding the intervention and post-operative procedures.

4. Costs and insurance coverage: Before proceeding with the intervention, it is important to verify the costs and insurance coverage. Most Swiss health insurances cover outpatient interventions, but it is always advisable to check the details of your policy. In some cases, it may be necessary to advance part of the expenses and request subsequent reimbursement.

5. Intervention and recovery: After the intervention, it is important to follow the doctor's instructions for a quick and safe recovery. This may include taking medication, limiting certain activities, and attending post-operative checks.
