Varese, Marco Manfrinati sentenced to life imprisonment

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 26 September 2026

The Court of Assizes of Varese sentenced Marco Manfrinati to life imprisonment for the murder of Fabio Limido and the attempted murder of Lavinia Limido.

Context

In brief

  • Manfrinati was sentenced to life imprisonment
  • The ruling is a first-instance judgment
  • Fabio Limido was killed in Casbeno
  • Lavinia Limido was seriously injured

  • Court → Varese Assize Court
  • Judgment → life imprisonment and daytime isolation
  • Location → via Ciro Menotti, Casbeno, Varese

The decision

Marco Manfrinati was sentenced to life imprisonment for the murder of Fabio Limido and the attempted murder of his former wife, Lavinia Limido. The ruling came at the end of this morning and was handed down by the Varese Assize Court. For the former lawyer from Busto Arsizio, this is a first-instance judgment, issued in the proceedings concerning the events on via Ciro Menotti, in the Casbeno district.

The legal case concerns the attack of 6 May 2024. On that day, Limido, 71, intervened to defend his daughter, who was attacked by her former husband in the middle of the street and in front of her workplace. Fabio Limido was stabbed to death. Lavinia Limido was struck in the face and neck and was seriously injured; she was rushed to hospital in desperate circumstances.

The Varese judges also ordered daytime isolation for Manfrinati. The defendant, represented by attorney Elio Giannangeli, was not in court today. The report on the judgment brings together the two events for which Manfrinati had been imprisoned: the murder of his former father-in-law, Fabio Limido, and the attempted murder of his former wife, the victim's daughter. The location remains identified as via Ciro Menotti, in the Casbeno district of Varese, where the attack took place and where police and emergency services later intervened.

The report therefore identifies two distinct outcomes of the same attack: the death of Fabio Limido and the serious injuries suffered by Lavinia Limido. These are the events connected to the legal case heard today before the Varese Assize Court.

Operational details

The reconstruction on Via Menotti

The sequence on the street

The focal point of the incident is Via Ciro Menotti, in the Casbeno district of Varese. The scene unfolded in the street, in front of Lavinia Limido’s workplace. According to the reconstruction reported by the source, Manfrinati’s ex-wife had been attacked by her ex-husband. Her father, Fabio Limido, 71, intervened to defend her.

The father’s intervention turned the attack into an incident with two victims, one of them fatal. Manfrinati stabbed his former father-in-law, who died in the street. Lavinia, meanwhile, was wounded in the face and neck and was left seriously injured. She was then rushed desperately to hospital in an attempt to save her.

The police and emergency services arrived on Via Menotti, as also documented by the scene on 6 May 2024. The next stage was Manfrinati’s arrest: the former lawyer from Busto was detained by the police in front of Marta Criscuolo, Fabio Limido’s wife.

The account of the events therefore links the same place to the various moments of the incident: the attack in the street, the intervention of the 71-year-old, the stabbings, the emergency response for Lavinia and the detention of the defendant. The source places all these stages in the Casbeno district and identifies the family relationships indicated in the incident: Manfrinati was Lavinia’s ex-husband, while Fabio Limido was her former father-in-law.

Via Ciro Menotti is therefore the common reference point for the most concrete part of the reconstruction: the attack took place here, the police and emergency services converged here and the police detained Manfrinati here. The source links that place to the relationships between the people involved, without separating the scene from their family relationships.

The procedural framework

The elements of the sentence

The sentence delivered today in Varese is described as a first-instance decision. The Court of Assizes of the provincial capital ruled at the end of the late morning on the proceedings involving the defendant Marco Manfrinati, the former Busto Arsizio lawyer imprisoned for the murder of Fabio Limido and the attempted murder of Lavinia Limido.

For Manfrinati, the reports highlight two elements of the decision: the life sentence and the daytime isolation ordered by the Varese judges. Daytime isolation is indicated as an additional provision relative to the sentence. The ruling therefore concerns the two events for which the former Busto Arsizio lawyer was in prison: the death of his former father-in-law and the assault on his former wife, the victim's daughter.

Manfrinati was not present in the courtroom today. His defense is entrusted to lawyer Elio Giannangeli, mentioned in the reports alongside the news of the sentence. The decision was made public following the events of May 6, 2024, when police arrested Manfrinati in via Ciro Menotti, in Casbeno, while Lavinia Limido was being rescued and Fabio Limido had already been killed.

The day's reports conclude with the ruling of the Varese judges and the classification of the sentence as first-instance. The name of the victim and the name of the injured person remain separate in the formulation of the news: Fabio Limido is linked to the murder, Lavinia Limido to the attempted murder. The source reports the decision through these elements: the level of judgment, the penalty, the daytime isolation, the defense, and the absence of the defendant in court.
