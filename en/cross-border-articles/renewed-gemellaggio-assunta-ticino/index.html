Twinning Sesto Calende - Castelletto Ticino: a tradition unites two communities

The idea of twinning the two parish communities of Sesto Calende and Castelletto Ticino was had 26 years ago by the then provost of Sesto Calende, Don Franco Bonatti, and the provost of Castelletto Ticino, Don Vittorio Moia. The goal was to create a tradition that would unite the two communities, geographically separated by the Ticino River. Today, the twinning of the Assumption is one of the most important traditions in the area.

The twinning was formalized with an agreement between the two communities, which provides for collaboration in various areas, including culture, religion, and economic development. Under the agreement, the two communities commit to sharing resources and expertise to promote the growth and development of the two communities.

One of the main activities of the twinning is the celebration of the Feast of the Assumption, which takes place every year in September. During the party, the two communities organize a series of events, including concerts, theatrical performances, and cultural events. The most important event is the procession of the Assumption, which takes place on 15 August and sees the participation of thousands of faithful from all over the area.

Twinning Sesto Calende - Castelletto Ticino: 26th Edition

On August 15, the 26th edition of the twinning between Sesto Calende and Castelletto Ticino took place, two municipalities in the Canton of Ticino that forged a bond of friendship and collaboration in 1997. The procession, which crossed the two localities, saw the participation of numerous citizens and representatives of the two communities.

The next edition of the twinning will be organized by Don Fabrizio Corno, provost of Castelletto Ticino, who thanked all those present and expressed his gratitude for the help provided by the Alpini and the mayors of Sesto Calende and Castelletto Ticino. "We are grateful for the friendship and collaboration that has bound us for over 25 years," said Fr. Corno. “We hope that this tradition will continue to grow and be a point of reference for our communities.”

The twinning between Sesto Calende and Castelletto Ticino was established in 1997, on the occasion of the 50th anniversary of the unification of Italy. Since then, the two communities have exchanged official visits and participated in numerous cultural and sports initiatives. The procession on 15 August is just one of the many activities that characterise this bond of friendship and collaboration.
