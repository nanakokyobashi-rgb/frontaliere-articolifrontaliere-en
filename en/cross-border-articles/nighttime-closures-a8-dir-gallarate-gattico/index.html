Night closures between A8 and Gallarate-Gattico branch on 4 September (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 1 September 2026

Autostrade per l'Italia announces night closures between the A8 and the Gallarate-Gattico branch for paving work and noise barriers.

Context

In brief

  • Night closures between A8 and Gallarate-Gattico branch on 4 September
  • Planned deviations for paving work and noise barriers
  • Recommended alternative routes

Key Facts

  • What: Night closures for paving work and noise barriers
  • When: From 9:00 pm on Friday, September 4th to 5:00 am on Saturday, September 5th
  • Where: Between Sesto Calende and Besnate on the D08 Gallarate-Gattico branch and on the A8 Milan-Varese motorway
  • Who: Autostrade per l'Italia
  • Amount: Not specified

Autostrade per l'Italia has announced a series of night closures along the motorways in the Varese area to allow paving work and the strengthening of noise barriers. The measures will be active from 21:00 on Friday 4 September until 5:00 on Saturday 5 September. The operations will require temporary changes to the road system and alternative routes on provincial and state roads.

D08 Gallarate-Gattico branch

As for the D08 Gallarate-Gattico branch, traffic will be blocked in the section between Sesto Calende Vergiate and Besnate in the direction of the A8 Milan-Varese. At the same time, the "Verbano ovest" service area will be closed an hour early. The Sesto Calende Vergiate station will not be accessible at the entrance in either direction, either towards the A8 or towards the A26 Genova Voltri-Gravellona Toce. The connection branch that leads from the D08 to the A8 towards Varese has also been interrupted.

Operational details

Impact on cross-border commuters

The night closures between the A8 and the Gallarate-Gattico branch will have a significant impact on cross-border commuters who use these arteries to move between Ticino and Lombardy. The paving works and the strengthening of noise barriers will require temporary changes to the road network, with alternative routes on provincial and state roads.

Recommended alternative routes

For cross-border commuters moving towards Varese, after the mandatory exit at the Sesto Calende Vergiate station, it is advisable to take the ordinary road system: SP17 and SP34 towards Besnate, SP26 towards Jerago con Orago, SS341 towards Albizzate and enter the A8 at the Solbiate Arno Albizzate junction.

For those heading to Milan from the D08, the detour involves the mandatory exit at the Sesto Calende Vergiate station and take the SS33 del Sempione towards Vergiate/Somma Lombardo/Casorate Sempione/Gallarate and enter the A8 in Gallarate.

Those who find the entrance to Sesto Calende Vergiate closed can use the toll booths of Besnate (on the D08 for the A8/Milan), Solbiate Arno Albizzate (on the A8 for the A8/Varese) or Castelletto Ticino (on the D08 for the A26). For the interruption of the D08/A8 branch towards Varese, it is suggested to continue on the A8 towards Milan, exit at Gallarate, take the SS341 towards Cavaria/Solbiate Arno/Albizzate and re-enter the A8 towards Varese at the Solbiate Arno Albizzate junction.
