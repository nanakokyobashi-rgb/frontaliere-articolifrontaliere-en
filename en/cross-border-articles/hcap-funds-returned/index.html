The Hockey Club Ambrì-Piotta (HCAP) is set to recover a significant part of the funds withdrawn from its Support Group's coffers. An agreement has been reached to compensate for the damage with the former member who was under investigation, as reported by Rsi. According to the reconstruction of the investigators, over several years, the 65-year-old would have withdrawn money intended for the company, sometimes through bank transfers, sometimes by making personal payments directly from the Group's account. The sums came from the activities organized by the association, represented by lawyer Elio Brunetti. The shortage is estimated to be between 235,000 and 265,000 francs. A significant loss for HCAP's coffers, which could have been used to support the club's sports and social activities. For comparison, consider that the municipality of Ambrì-Piotta has an annual budget of around 10 million francs. The investigation began after a series of checks carried out by the HCAP Executive Committee, which revealed some anomalies in the financial movements of the Support Group. Subsequently, it was decided to involve the competent authorities to clarify the situation and recover the withdrawn funds. 'It was a difficult period for our club, but we are satisfied to have resolved the issue positively,' said a member of the HCAP Executive Committee. ⚠️ The regulations on the recovery of funds withdrawn from non-profit organizations have recently been modified in Ticino. The new law, which came into effect on January 1, 2022, provides that administrators of non-profit organizations are required to inform the competent authorities in case of suspected misappropriation. 📊 Operational checklist for the recovery of withdrawn funds: - Identification of anomalies in financial movements - Involvement of competent authorities - Start of the investigation - Compensation agreement with the former member Based on estimates, HCAP could recover around 80% of the withdrawn funds, equivalent to around 200,000 francs. This amount could be used to support the club's sports and social activities, such as organizing events and managing sports facilities. 💡 The compensation agreement reached by HCAP is a concrete example of how it is possible to recover funds withdrawn from non-profit organizations. Operational details

The agreement reached with the former member of the Hockey Club Ambrì-Piotta (HCAP) Support Group provides for the return of a significant part of the withdrawn funds, equivalent to around 200,000 Swiss francs. Despite this, the criminal proceedings against the former member will not stop. The news was welcomed with relief within the HCAP community, which will once again be able to count on an important part of the withdrawn resources. The Hockey Club Ambrì-Piotta is one of the most important sports companies in the Canton of Ticino, with a long history and a passionate following. The team plays its home games at the PalaTondini in Ambrì, which has been expanded and modernized over the years to accommodate over 4,000 spectators. The return of these funds will certainly be an advantage for the company and its fans, who will be able to continue to enjoy a competitive and well-financed team. The exact amount of the funds to be returned has not been made public, but it has been confirmed that it is a considerable part of the shortage, equivalent to around 70% of the withdrawn funds. According to sources close to the company, the return of these funds will allow HCAP to cover around half of its annual budget, which is around 2 million Swiss francs. - The agreement reached with the former member of the Support Group is the result of a long and complex negotiation process, which also involved the company's lawyers and cantonal authorities. - The Ticino regulations on the financing of sports companies provide that companies must guarantee a certain transparency in the management of funds and that company managers must be held accountable for the management of funds. - The law on the financing of sports companies in the Canton of Ticino, which came into effect on January 1, 2020, provides that companies must present an annual balance sheet and that funds must be used exclusively for sports purposes. > 'The return of these funds is a great relief for us and allows us to continue working with serenity,' said the president of HCAP, Giorgio Müller.

Frequently Asked Questions
How much money has been taken from the Ambrì-Piotta Hockey Club?
Estimates indicate a shortfall of between 235,000 and 265,000 Swiss francs. The Ambrì-Piotta Hockey Club has reached an agreement to recover a substantial part of these funds, estimated at around 200,000 francs.
How was it possible to recover the subtracted funds?
After detecting financial anomalies, HCAP involved the relevant authorities. Proceedings were initiated which resulted in a compensation agreement with the former member of the Support Group responsible for the abduction.
What percentage of subtracted funds will be recovered?
The compensation agreement provides for the return of approximately 70% of the funds. This amount recovered, amounting to approximately 200,000 Swiss francs, is a significant part of the damage suffered by the club.
What does the new Ticino legislation on the recovery of funds for non-profit entities provide?
Since 1 January 2022, directors of non-profit entities in Ticino must inform the authorities in the event of suspicion of embezzlement. The legislation aims to ensure greater transparency and facilitate the recovery of stolen funds.
