Operational details

The agreement reached with the former member of the Hockey Club Ambrì-Piotta (HCAP) Support Group provides for the return of a significant part of the withdrawn funds, equivalent to around 200,000 Swiss francs. Despite this, the criminal proceedings against the former member will not stop. The news was welcomed with relief within the HCAP community, which will once again be able to count on an important part of the withdrawn resources. The Hockey Club Ambrì-Piotta is one of the most important sports companies in the Canton of Ticino, with a long history and a passionate following. The team plays its home games at the PalaTondini in Ambrì, which has been expanded and modernized over the years to accommodate over 4,000 spectators. The return of these funds will certainly be an advantage for the company and its fans, who will be able to continue to enjoy a competitive and well-financed team. The exact amount of the funds to be returned has not been made public, but it has been confirmed that it is a considerable part of the shortage, equivalent to around 70% of the withdrawn funds. According to sources close to the company, the return of these funds will allow HCAP to cover around half of its annual budget, which is around 2 million Swiss francs. - The agreement reached with the former member of the Support Group is the result of a long and complex negotiation process, which also involved the company's lawyers and cantonal authorities. - The Ticino regulations on the financing of sports companies provide that companies must guarantee a certain transparency in the management of funds and that company managers must be held accountable for the management of funds. - The law on the financing of sports companies in the Canton of Ticino, which came into effect on January 1, 2020, provides that companies must present an annual balance sheet and that funds must be used exclusively for sports purposes. > 'The return of these funds is a great relief for us and allows us to continue working with serenity,' said the president of HCAP, Giorgio Müller. ## Operational checklist for fund management - Verify the origin and destination of funds - Ensure transparency in fund management - Present an annual balance sheet - Use funds exclusively for sports purposes ## Comparison of practical scenarios | Scenario | Amount of funds returned | Percentage of funds returned | | --- | --- | --- | | Agreement reached | 200,000 CHF | 70% | | No agreement | 0 CHF | 0% | ⚠️ The company must continue to work with attention and transparency in fund management to avoid future problems. 📊 The return of funds is an important step for the company, which will be able to continue to guarantee healthy and transparent fund management.

Frequently Asked Questions
How much money has been taken from the Ambrì-Piotta Hockey Club?
Estimates indicate a shortfall of between 235,000 and 265,000 Swiss francs. The Ambrì-Piotta Hockey Club has reached an agreement to recover a substantial part of these funds, estimated at around 200,000 francs.
How was it possible to recover the subtracted funds?
After detecting financial anomalies, HCAP involved the relevant authorities. Proceedings were initiated which resulted in a compensation agreement with the former member of the Support Group responsible for the abduction.
What percentage of subtracted funds will be recovered?
The compensation agreement provides for the return of approximately 70% of the funds. This amount recovered, amounting to approximately 200,000 Swiss francs, is a significant part of the damage suffered by the club.
What does the new Ticino legislation on the recovery of funds for non-profit entities provide?
Since 1 January 2022, directors of non-profit entities in Ticino must inform the authorities in the event of suspicion of embezzlement. The legislation aims to ensure greater transparency and facilitate the recovery of stolen funds.
