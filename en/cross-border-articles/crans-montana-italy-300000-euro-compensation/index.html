Italy seeks at least 300,000 euros in damages for Crans-Montana fire (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 May 2026

The Italian Republic has filed a request to join the criminal proceedings as a civil party, seeking at least 300,000 euros in damages for the New Year's Eve fire in Crans-Montana.

Context

In brief

  • Italy demands 300,000 euros for the Crans-Montana fire
  • 6 Italian citizens killed and 14 seriously injured
  • Expenses incurred for supporting Swiss authorities

Key facts

  • What: Compensation claim by Italy
  • When: Filed on April 29, 2026
  • Where: Crans-Montana, Switzerland
  • Who: Italian Republic
  • Amount: At least 300,000 euros

The Italian Republic has recently filed a request to join the criminal proceedings related to the New Year's tragedy in Crans-Montana as a civil party. The request, submitted through a 7-page letter dated April 29 and addressed to the Valais public prosecutor's office, aims to assert civil claims of at least 300,000 euros for the damage suffered. This sum should be paid by the defendants in case of conviction. However, if the state representatives were to be convicted, the responsibility would fall on the community, i.e., the municipality of Crans-Montana.

Italy's support to Switzerland

In the letter, lawyer Romain Jordan, who represents several families of the fire victims, recalls that 6 Italian citizens died in the blaze and another 14 were seriously injured. In the hours following the tragedy, Italy took numerous measures to support the Swiss authorities. Among these, the dispatch of a civil protection helicopter with medical and technical personnel, the organization of urgent medical transfers of injured Italian citizens, psychological support to families, the sending of assessment teams, coordination and health assistance, support for the identification of victims, and the repatriation by air of the bodies.
