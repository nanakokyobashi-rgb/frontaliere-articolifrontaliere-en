  • What: Wage calculation and verification tools.
  • When: Always accessible online.

  • Service: Analysis of salaries, holidays and allowances.

The Unia union offers workers, including border workers working in the Canton of Ticino, a series of digital tools aimed at pay transparency. The need to monitor your salary is fundamental for anyone working in Switzerland, especially in a labour market characterised by different types of contracts. Through the Unia portal, you can access three different solutions: the CCL control, the USS salary calculator and the main construction-specific tool. Each option responds to different needs, allowing the worker to obtain a clear overview of their duties, whether they are linked to minimum wages or broader contractual conditions.

Operational details

The analysis of the payroll inevitably involves knowledge of the category contracts. If the worker is employed at a company subject to a Collective Labour Agreement (CCL) to which Unia is a contracting party, the dedicated tool provides a detailed summary of the main contents. This does not only concern the minimum wage, but extends to fundamental elements such as holidays, allowances, supplements and paid absences. For a border worker who crosses the crossings such as Brogeda or Gaggiolo on a daily basis, being aware of these rights means being able to dialogue with the employer with greater knowledge of the facts.

Analysis of contractual differences

The distinction between the various calculators is essential to avoid evaluation errors. While the USS calculator is oriented towards the statistical average, also providing the salary band in which the salary should be placed based on qualifications, the main construction tool focuses rigidly on compliance with the minimums established by the National Contract mantle (CNM). Thelatter represents a mandatory point of reference for those working in the construction sector, ensuring that what is paid by the employer complies with current regulations. Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between the USS calculator and the CCL control?
The USS salary calculator is based on national statistical data to indicate how much a person with your qualifications usually earns, regardless of the CCL. The CCL control offered by Unia instead provides specific information on the minimum wages, holidays and allowances guaranteed by your collective labour agreement if Unia is a party to it.
What is the building calculator for?
The construction wage calculator is a specific tool for workers in the main construction sector. It is used to check in a simple and direct way whether the employer is paying the mandatory minimum wage provided for by the National Contract for Main Construction (CNM).
Can I compare my salary with other Swiss cities?
Yes, using the USS wage calculator, which is a statistical tool. It allows you to compare your salary with the usual one in other Swiss cities and to understand in which salary band your salary should be based on your qualifications and professional branch.
