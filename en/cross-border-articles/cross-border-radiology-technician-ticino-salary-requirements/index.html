Requirements to work as a radiology technician in Ticino

To work as a radiology technician in Ticino, you must have a qualification recognised in Switzerland. This means that the bachelor's degree in radiology technician or an equivalent qualification must be recognised by the competent authority of Switzerland. In addition, you must have a G permit, which allows you to work in Switzerland as a border worker.

Permit G: how to get it

The G permit is a document that allows you to work in Switzerland as a border worker. To obtain it, you must submit an application to the competent authority of Switzerland, accompanied by a curriculum vitae and a certificate of professional qualification. In addition, you must prove that you have sufficient income to support yourself financially during your stay in Switzerland.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the requirements to work as a radiology technician in Ticino as a border worker?
A G permit and a recognised qualification are required. The average salary is CHF 80,000 per year.
How do I get a G permit?
By submitting the application to the AFC/ESTV and submitting a qualification issued by an accredited body.
How much does a border radiology technician earn on average in Ticino and what is the guaranteed minimum wage?
The average salary for a border radiology technician in Ticino is CHF 80,000 per year. The Federal Law on Health Professions (LFPs) sets a guaranteed minimum wage (SMG) of CHF 70,000 per year (art. 27 LFPs), which employers must abide by.
How can I check if my diploma as a radiology technician is valid to work in Switzerland?
Your degree is only recognised if it is issued by an institution accredited by the Swiss Academy of Medical Sciences (ASSM), as required by art. 38 of the LFPs. It is essential to consult the ASSM or the resources of the Swiss Confederation to verify the accreditation of your institution before applying.
In addition to salary, what other benefits are included in the work package for a radiology technician in Ticino?
In addition to the average salary of CHF 80,000 per year, border radiology technicians in Ticino can benefit from a package that includes health insurance, pension and a social security system. These elements contribute to an advantageous overall remuneration framework.
