Lugano comes to the confrontation after the success obtained in the debut of the Conference League and starts as an absolute favorite against Pully. For the Tavern the task is the opposite. The home team is second in Group 3 of the First League and will try to surprise the Young Boys, one of the nobles of national football. The goal indicated for the Ticino training is access to the round of 16, but to achieve this, an enterprise will be needed. The comparison then puts in front of a team that starts with a clear advantage in the evaluations and another call to try the surprise.

The schedules separate the two matches, but the meaning for the Ticinese is the same: to continue the path in the Swiss Cup after the first knockout weekend. The double commitment thus assigns the Bianconeri the role of favourites and the Carbon team that of possible surprise.
