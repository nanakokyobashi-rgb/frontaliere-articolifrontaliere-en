Key facts

  • What: Single collection of LAMal health insurance data
  • When: In definition phase
  • Where: Canton of Ticino

  • Amount: Not yet specified

The Canton of Ticino is working on a single collection of data for LAMal, the federal law on health insurance. This novelty, recently announced, aims to simplify procedures for cross-border workers who work in Switzerland and reside in Italy. The new regulation provides for the collection of insurance data in a centralized manner, reducing bureaucracy and improving the efficiency of the system.

What changes for cross-border workers

Currently, cross-border workers must provide their insurance data to multiple entities, which can be complicated and time-consuming. With the single collection, data will be collected once and shared among the various interested parties, such as insurers and cantonal authorities. This will significantly simplify the procedures and reduce the risk of errors or omissions.

Advantages of single collection

The new procedure will offer several advantages to cross-border workers. First, it will reduce the administrative burden, allowing them to focus on work and daily life. Furthermore, the centralization of data will improve the transparency and traceability of information, ensuring greater security and reliability.

Procedures and deadlines

The specific procedures and deadlines for the single collection of data have not yet been defined. However, it is expected that the system will come into force in the coming months. Cross-border workers will be promptly informed about the modalities of adherence and the necessary documents. It is important to stay updated through the official channels of the Canton of Ticino and the insurers.

Comparison with the previous situation

Before the introduction of the single collection, cross-border workers had to provide their insurance data to multiple entities, often with different procedures and different deadlines. This could cause confusion and delays, with consequent penalties or problems with insurers. The new centralized procedure will eliminate these inconveniences, ensuring more efficient and secure management of data.

Practical scenarios

Imagine a cross-border worker who works in Lugano and resides in Varese. Currently, they must provide insurance data to their Swiss insurer and the Italian INPS, in addition to managing any refunds. With the single collection, data will be collected once and automatically shared with the interested parties, significantly simplifying their administrative life.

Conclusions

The single collection of LAMal data represents a significant step forward for cross-border workers in the Canton of Ticino. Thanks to this novelty, administrative procedures will be simpler and more efficient, allowing transborder workers to focus on their work and daily life. It is fundamental to stay updated on the news and follow the indications of the cantonal authorities to best benefit from this new procedure.
