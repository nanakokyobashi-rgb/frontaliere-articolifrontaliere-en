Inflation in Switzerland fell again in July to 0.4%, after hitting 0.5% in June and 0.6% in April and May. This data was published today by the Federal Statistical Office (FSO). The consumer price index stood at 101.1 points, indicating an annual progression in the low end of expectations. Analysts surveyed by the AWP agency bet on values between +0.3% and +0.6%. On a monthly basis, prices contracted by 0.1%, a figure that falls within the low range of estimates, with forecasts ranging from -0.2% to +0.1%. June had marked perfect stability (0.0%).

Causes of the decline

According to UST experts, the decline in the index compared to the previous month is attributable to various factors, including the reduction in the prices of air transport, diesel and gasoline.
