Mesolcina Hosts MisoXperience 2024: Outdoor Sports Festival (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 5 May 2026

From May 9 to 17, the Mesolcina valley will host the MisoXperience Festival featuring kayak, bouldering, and paragliding. Here is the complete program.

Operational details

Practical Analysis: What to Expect from the MisoXperience Festival

The MisoXperience Festival is not just a sports event, but an opportunity for cross-border workers to live a unique experience in a breathtaking natural setting. Here's what to expect and how to prepare.

What to Bring

To participate in the festival, it's recommended to bring:

  • Specific sports equipment for the disciplines of interest (kayak, bouldering, paragliding)
  • Comfortable clothing and gear suitable for outdoor activities
  • Tent, van, or camper for overnight stay
  • Food and drinks for the overnight stay
  • Identification documents and work permits, if required

How to Get There

The Mesolcina Valley is easily accessible by car or public transportation. For cross-border workers who work in Ticino, the festival represents an opportunity to visit a lesser-known part of the Grisons canton, enjoying breathtaking landscapes and sports activities.

Detailed Program

The festival program is varied and offers activities for all tastes. Here are some of the main events:

  • Saturday, May 16th: National final of Boater-X, bouldering competition, and Hike & Fly competition
  • Sunday, May 10th: Family Day with games and activities for the whole family
  • Wednesday, May 13th: Conference by Swiss paraglider pilot Chrigel Maurer

Fiscal Implications

For cross-border workers who work in Ticino, participating in the MisoXperience Festival does not involve any specific fiscal implications. However, it's always a good idea to check for any potential discounts or benefits for Italian residents who work in Switzerland.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the main sports disciplines of the MisoXperience Festival?
The main sports disciplines of the MisoXperience Festival are whitewater kayaking, bouldering, and paragliding. Each discipline offers competitions and activities for all levels of experience, from beginners to ambitious athletes.
Where does the MisoXperience Festival take place?
The festival takes place in the Mesolcina valley, at Stradon 5, Piani di Verdabbio a Cama. The location is surrounded by nature, next to the Moesa river, and offers a picturesque setting for the sports activities.
What are the main days of the festival?
The main days of the festival are Saturday 16 May, with the national final of Boater-X, the bouldering competition, and the Hike & Fly competition, and Sunday 10 May, with the Family Day dedicated to activities for the whole family.
