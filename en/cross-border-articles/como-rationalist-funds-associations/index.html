Como Rationalist: 78mila euro to two associations

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 19 October 2026

Two associations will support Como Rationalist Funding planned from 2026 to 2028 Over 78 thousand euros from the tourist tax Disbursement after the financial reports

Key facts

  • Project → Como Razionalista

For the 2026-2028 three-year period, the Municipality of Como has concluded the public selection for the Como Razionalista project and has allocated funds for initiatives dedicated to the promotion of Larian Rationalism. The decision concerns the enhancement of the architectural and cultural heritage of the Municipality.

Funding coverage is planned for the 2026-2028 three-year period and will be ensured by tourist tax revenues. The total financial commitment indicated for the three-year period exceeds 78,000 euros and involves two associations, selected through a public procedure.

The evaluation commission examined the proposals received and approved the final ranking. The result was built on the quality of the projects and the score assigned to each proposal. In the ranking, MADE IN MAARC ETS obtained 71/100, while WONDERLAKE ETS reached 65/100.

The assignment concerns two distinct initiatives included in the same three-year program. The first is 'L'eredità del moderno', presented by the MADE IN MAARC ETS Association. The second is 'Città Razionalista', proposed by the WONDERLAKE ETS Association.

The Municipality thus links tourist tax revenues to a program dedicated to architectural and cultural heritage. The choice covers the years 2026, 2027 and 2028 and establishes the distribution of contributions according to the provisions of the approved ranking.

Operational details

The ranking

The two allocations

The details of the ranking compare the two funded projects. MADE IN MAARC ETS submitted «The legacy of modernity» and received 71/100. The total contribution awarded to the association is 35.158,50 euro, with a different breakdown across the three years: 5.158,50 euro in 2026, 15.000 euro in 2027 and 15.000 euro in 2028.

WONDERLAKE ETS submitted «Rationalist City» and received 65/100. For this proposal, the total contribution awarded is 43.014,60 euro. The sum is distributed in three equal installments: 14.338,20 euro in 2026, 14.338,20 euro in 2027 and 14.338,20 euro in 2028.

Key points

Disbursement of funds

The submission of reports

The conclusion of the selection process does not coincide with the immediate payment of the entire support awarded. The source specifies that the actual disbursement of the funds will take place gradually, after the associations submit reports on the activities carried out.

The condition indicated by the Municipality concerns both associations and covers the 2026-2028 funding period. For MADE IN MAARC ETS, the financial schedule includes 5.158,50 euro in 2026, 15.000 euro in 2027 and 15.000 euro in 2028. For WONDERLAKE ETS, the expected contribution is divided into three installments of 14.338,20 euro, one for 2026, one for 2027 and one for 2028.

The schedule of installments and the submission of reports are therefore the two elements indicated for tracking the disbursement of the contributions. The associations will have to submit documentation of the activities carried out before the actual disbursement, while the Municipality has already defined the total amounts and their distribution by year.

The support remains linked to the revenue generated by the tourist tax and to the promotion of Lariano Rationalism. The phase described in the communication unfolds over the three years of the project: the funds are allocated in the ranking, assigned to the individual years and will be disbursed gradually following the submission of reports.

With this step, the public selection process is concluded and the method by which the contributions will be disbursed during the 2026-2028 period is defined, according to the approved allocation.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the project funded by the Municipality of Como?
The project is Como Rationalista, a three-year programme covering the period 2026-2028. The public selection was concluded by the Municipality of Como to support initiatives aimed at promoting Lariano Rationalism and enhancing the architectural and cultural heritage. Funding will be drawn directly from the income generated by the tourist tax.
Which associations have been selected?
The final ranking includes the MADE IN MAARC ETS Association, with the project "The legacy of modern", and the WONDERLAKE ETS Association, with "Rationalist City". The first proposal obtained 71/100 and a total contribution of 35,158.50 euros; the second 65/100 and 43,014.60 euros.
How will contributions be disbursed?
The sums will be disbursed gradually following the presentation of the accounts of the activities carried out by the associations. The schedule covers 2026, 2027 and 2028. For MADE IN MAARC ETS there are quotas of 5,158.50, 15,000 and 15,000 euros; for WONDERLAKE ETS there are three annual quotas of 14,338.20 euros.
