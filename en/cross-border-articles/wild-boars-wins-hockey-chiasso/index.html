The Wild Boars have repeated their triumph at the Chiasso Amateur Hockey Tournament, firmly establishing their dominance in the competition. The Wild Boars showed remarkable determination and skill on the ice, overcoming their rivals and securing first place.

Record participation and concrete figures

The 2024 edition of the Chiasso Amateur Hockey Tournament saw record participation, with 10 teams registered, including squads representing Mendrisio, Lugano, Locarno, Bellinzona, and Ascona. Each team fielded at least 15 players, bringing the total number of athletes involved to over 150. Matches took place from June 6-8 at Chiasso's Palaghiaccio arena, with an average attendance of about 300 spectators per game.

Results and statistics

The Wild Boars finished the group stage with 4 wins out of 4, scoring a total of 21 goals and conceding only 7. The final, against the Eagles from Mendrisio, ended 5-2 thanks to a hat-trick by captain Riccardo Lurati. Throughout the tournament, the team maintained a shot conversion rate of 68%, the highest among all participants.

  • Wild Boars: 21 goals scored, 7 conceded, 4 wins
  • Eagles Mendrisio: 17 goals scored, 10 conceded, 3 wins, 1 loss
  • Bellinzona Lions: 12 goals scored, 14 conceded, 2 wins, 2 losses

Rules and regulations

The tournament followed the guidelines of the Ticino Amateur Hockey Federation (FTHA), implementing new directives introduced in January 2023:

  • Maximum of 20 players per team
  • Registration fee: CHF 400 per team
  • Mandatory accident insurance (LAA) for all participants
  • Limit of 3 active federation-licensed players per team

⚠️ Since January 1, 2024, the FTHA has introduced an injury protection fund: each team pays an additional CHF 150 to cover emergency medical costs during the tournament.

Operational checklist for participation

  • Online registration by May 15
  • Submission of accident insurance certificate (LAA)
  • Final player list by May 25
  • Payment of total fee CHF 550 (registration + injury protection)
  • Submission of federation licenses (max 3 per team)

💡 Practical comparison: Unlike tournaments in Lugano and Locarno, where the registration fee exceeds CHF 600 and only 2 federation players are allowed, the Chiasso tournament is more accessible both financially and in terms of regulations, encouraging amateur teams from across the Canton to participate.

Operational details

The Chiasso Hockey Tournament is a major event for the Ticino community, attracting not only local residents but also hockey fans from towns like Mendrisio, Balerna, and Vacallo. The 2024 edition, held at the Chiasso Palaghiaccio from April 12-14, featured eight amateur teams and more than 120 registered athletes. The Wild Boars, already champions in 2022, were warmly celebrated for their victory: they secured the title in the final against the Sharks Mendrisio with a 5-3 score, confirming their technical and tactical leadership.

📊 During the group stage, the Wild Boars scored 22 goals in four matches, conceding only 7: numbers that reflect their athletic preparation, well above the tournament average. Noteworthy performances included goalie Marco Rossi, who posted a 92% save percentage, and striker Luca Bernasconi, top scorer with 11 points (7 goals, 4 assists).

Federation rules applied to the tournament follow the Swiss Ice Hockey Federation regulations, which since 2020 require all amateur tournaments to provide accident insurance with a minimum coverage of CHF 50,000 per athlete. As co-organizer, the Municipality of Chiasso allocated CHF 3,000 for extraordinary rink maintenance, as outlined in municipal resolution no. 1047/2023.

Operational checklist for registered teams: - Submit player list no later than 30 days before the event - Valid medical certificate - Individual accident insurance (minimum CHF 50,000) - Pay registration fee (CHF 350) - Comply with Swiss Ice Hockey Federation disciplinary regulations

💡 Comparing organizational details with the Lugano amateur tournament (2023 edition, 10 participating teams, registration fee CHF 450), Chiasso's event stands out for its lower costs and participation mainly from the Mendrisiotto area, fostering cohesion among neighboring municipalities.

⚠️ From an insurance perspective, Ticino regulations (art. 45 LISA, Sports Infrastructure Law 2019) require municipalities hosting sports events to guarantee facility safety and coverage, with fines up to CHF 5,000 for non-compliance. Adhering to these rules allowed the Chiasso Hockey Tournament to take place without major incidents, as confirmed by the Municipal Police report.

The renewed success of the Wild Boars highlights not only the team's technical level but also the value of an organization that pays attention to both sporting and regulatory aspects, strengthening the tournament's role as a benchmark for amateur hockey in Ticino.

Frequently Asked Questions
How many players were enrolled in the Hockey Chiasso 2024 amateur tournament?
Over 150 players were registered, with a total of 10 teams.
What is the total cost for a team enrolling in the Hockey Chiasso 2024 amateur tournament, considering registration and accident protection?
The total cost for a team is CHF 550, which includes the registration fee (CHF 400) and the contribution for the accident protection fund (CHF 150).
How much does accommodation and catering cost on average for an athlete in Chiasso?
During the event, athletes spent an average of CHF 60 per accommodation and catering; for a team of 15 players the total budget is about CHF 900. The municipality of Chiasso has allocated CHF 3'000 for sports promotion, but does not directly cover individual expenses.
What is the mandatory minimum insurance cover for players and how does it integrate with the tournament injury protection fund?
Federal legislation requires a minimum coverage of CHF 50'000 per athlete; the tournament adds an injury protection fund of CHF 150 per team, so the total protection for each player is at least CHF 50'150. LAA insurance is mandatory and must be submitted by 25 May.
How do you manage the taxation of the CHF 550 fee for Italian cross-border commuters registered for the tournament?
For Italian cross-border commuters, the total fee of CHF 550 (registration fee 400 + protection 150) is subject to withholding tax in Switzerland; However, thanks to the double taxation treaty, the amount can be deducted in the Italian tax return, avoiding double taxation.
