Hotel in Ticino: closes and dismisses 76 employees

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 5 October 2026

The Hotel Villa Principe Leopoldo in Collina d 'Oro invests 12 million francs, closes for months and announces 76 redundancies out of 81 employees.

Hotel Villa Principe Leopoldo in Collina d'Oro, in the Canton of Ticino, is preparing to close its doors for several months. The owner company Dot Life SA has planned a modernization project costing 12 million francs, with the aim of bringing the property up to modern standards. The investment, however, is accompanied by a major downsizing: the announced layoffs concern 76 of the 81 employees currently working there.

The temporary closure had already been foreshadowed by rumors in mid-September. The owner's decisions define both the pause in the work and the management of the staff.

The workforce figure refers to the employees currently working at the property. The closure, on the other hand, is described as temporary and linked to the modernization project: the plan does not announce the end of the hotel, but rather a suspension lasting several months. The outlook indicated by the company remains selective, because it concerns the employees who will be needed when operations resume.

The staff schedule

The most significant impact concerns the cuts. Formal notification of the layoffs is expected between October and November 2026. Only a few department heads will be temporarily spared, while the overwhelming majority of workers will reach their last actual working day on December 30. December 31, on the other hand, will be a paid leave day.

Dot Life's letter also provides for some support measures and a “loyalty bonus” for those who remain present at work until the last day established by the contract. The measure is therefore linked to continued attendance until the contractual expiry date indicated by the company.

The plan after the work

The next phase remains open. Dot Life emphasizes the temporary nature of the measure and expresses its intention to rehire the employees who will be needed when operations resume. Construction is expected to be completed by May 1, but the company has acknowledged that it cannot currently guarantee the rehiring of all current employees. This is the main point of uncertainty for those currently working at the hotel: the investment has a defined horizon, but the return of the staff does not.
