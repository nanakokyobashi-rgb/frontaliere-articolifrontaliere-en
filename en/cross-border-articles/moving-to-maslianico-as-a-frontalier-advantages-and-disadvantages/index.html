Key points

Action

To move to Maslianico as a cross-border worker, it is important to plan well and know the working and residence conditions in Switzerland and Italy. Cross-border workers can request a residence permit in Italy and a work permit in Switzerland. It is also important to know the taxes and permits in Switzerland and Italy.

For example, if a cross-border worker works in Lugano and wants to move to Maslianico, he will have to submit an application for a work permit in Switzerland at the Lugano Section of the Immigration Office of the Canton of Ticino. The cost of this application is CHF 300, as established by the Federal Immigration Law of 16 December 2005 (LFI).

Furthermore, the cross-border worker will also have to submit an application for a residence permit in Italy to the competent authority, which could be the Bellinzona Immigration Office. The cost of this application is €30, as established by Law no. 397/1991 of 29 September 1991.

It is important to note that costs and procedures for work and residence permits may vary depending on the specific case and applicable laws and regulations.
