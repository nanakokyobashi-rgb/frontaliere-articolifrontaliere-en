The advantages

  • Lower taxes: In Switzerland, taxes are generally lower than in Italy. For example, the municipal tax in Maslianico is around CHF 0.70 per inhabitant, while in Italy the average is around EUR 1.20 per inhabitant.
  • Access to health services: Cross-border commuters can access Swiss health services, such as medical care and social protection.
  • Career opportunities: Maslianico is a growing municipality, with a rapidly expanding economy. There are many job opportunities available, especially in the trade and finance sector.

The challenges

  • Permits and authorizations: Cross-border workers must obtain the necessary permits and authorizations to work in Switzerland. This can be a complex and lengthy process.
  • Tax differences: Taxes and duties in Switzerland and Italy may be different. Cross-border workers must be aware of these differences to avoid making tax errors.
  • Regulations and regulations: There are specific regulations and regulations for cross-border commuters. For example, the regulation of 1 January 2024 establishes that cross-border commuters must have an income of at least CHF 30,000 to benefit from the double taxation treaty.
