Closure of Erba maternity unit: League inquiry (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 26 September 2026

Eugenio Zoffili questions Minister Schillaci and calls for technical working groups and exemptions to keep the Erba maternity unit operational.

  • Eugenio Zoffili has submitted a parliamentary question

The forthcoming closure of the maternity unit at Erba Hospital has reached Parliament through a parliamentary question addressed to Health Minister Schillaci. It was submitted by Eugenio Zoffili, a deputy from Como and group leader of Lega Salvini Premier on Erba City Council. This position was taken after news of the forthcoming closure of the maternity unit, which Zoffili intends to oppose by asking that all solutions useful for keeping it operational be examined.

«The maternity unit at Erba Hospital must not be touched.» With these words, Zoffili reiterated that he had personally asked the owners several times to take a step back and to verify with Regione Lombardia whether an exemption was possible. The question to the minister stems from the same objective: to examine every useful solution to prevent the closure of the maternity unit.

The two working groups requested by Zoffili

In the text of the question, the deputy specifically points to the opportunity to open a technical working group between the Government and the Region. This should be accompanied by a local working group involving all interested parties: mayors, administrators, trade unions and associations. Zoffili says he has already asked the Mayor for the latter, with the aim of concretely verifying the conditions needed to keep the maternity unit operational, including through the exemption mechanisms provided for by law.

The argument concerns a hospital that, according to the account provided, serves a large area: Erbese, Vallassina and Triangolo Lariano. In these areas, Zoffili argues, the proximity of healthcare services is also essential because of the area’s geographical characteristics. The parameter of 500 births per year is recognized as a factor to consider, but not as the only evaluation criterion. The same legislation, he adds, provides for the possibility of exemptions. Parliament has also already highlighted the need to identify solutions capable of guaranteeing safe healthcare services as close as possible to citizens. This is the point also raised in the in-depth analysis on servizi sanitari.

Lega, Zoffili concludes, will continue to work in Parliament and locally to defend the maternity unit and all related services. In his words, the mobilization should continue with the support of the local community, which is asking for opposition to what it calls an unacceptable decision.

For a cross-border reader of the news, the practical connection must remain within the boundaries indicated by the source. The text discusses a healthcare facility serving a broad area and describes proximity as a value linked to geography. The matter therefore remains tied to the possible closure of the birth center and the search for a solution to prevent it.

For the cross-border reader, the news does not make it possible to calculate an individual impact. It does, however, make it possible to identify the criterion used in the comparison: distance and the configuration of the territory should be weighed together with the number of births and the safety of the services. On the other hand, the position expressed maintains that this figure cannot by itself conclude the assessment, because the area served is broad and the proximity of services is identified as a fundamental element.

The reference to the exemption must also be read precisely. The source recalls the possibility provided for by legislation and asks that its use be verified; it does not, however, present an exemption that has already been obtained. The same applies to the two working groups: they are instruments indicated in the parliamentary question and the territorial request, not an outcome that has already been reported. For those who live in the area described, the concrete issue is therefore the comparison between the numerical criterion, safety and the proximity of services.

Key points

Anyone who wants to follow the matter can adhere to the steps emerging from the parliamentary initiative, without attributing different procedures to the source. The sequence indicated is political and territorial:

1. Start with the request addressed to the owners: take a step back and verify with the Lombardy Region the possibility of an exemption. 2. Separate the institutional level from the local one: on the one hand, the technical working group between the Government and the Region, and on the other, the local working group. 3. Consider the parties already indicated: mayors, administrators, trade unions and associations. Zoffili reports having requested the local working group from the Mayor. 4. Assess maintaining operations using all the elements mentioned: 500 births per year, geographical characteristics, proximity and safety of services, as well as the exemption mechanisms.

The questions to follow

To stay up to date on the situation, the reader can check whether the owners will reconsider the decision, whether the Government and the Region will open the technical working group, whether the local discussion will be organized and whether an exemption will be considered. These are the developments consistent with the request reported in the source. No forms, documents, contact details or individual deadlines are indicated, however: the initiative described concerns discussions between institutions and local entities.

The community is called upon to support the campaign for the birth center and related services. The source reports that there is currently a call to oppose the decision, but does not describe a specific procedure for participating.
