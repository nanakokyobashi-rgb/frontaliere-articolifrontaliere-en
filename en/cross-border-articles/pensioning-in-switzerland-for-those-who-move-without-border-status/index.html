Retire in Switzerland for those who move non-frontier (cross-border guide)

Di Laura Bianchi · Published on 11 July 2026

The new border agreement of 2024 introduces new rules for border workers wishing to retire in Switzerland.

Context

In short, the updated 2024 border agreement comes into force from 1 January 2024, with significant consequences for border workers who decide to retire in Switzerland. New border crossers who move to Switzerland without being already a border crosser will benefit from a €10,000 allowance, a significant amount that can facilitate the passage between the two countries.

With regard to historical border crossers, the agreement provides for a transitional regime that will end in 2033. In particular, retired frontier workers will be able to continue to benefit from a series of privileges, including the possibility of maintaining their residence on Swiss territory and continuing to benefit from the tax and insurance benefits granted to residents.

Operational details

Retire in Switzerland for non-frontier relocators: driving and benefits

With the 2024 border agreement, people who move from other European countries (ECSC) to Switzerland will be able to benefit from a €10,000 deductible to retire at home. This new opportunity is intended for frontier workers and non-formalized frontier workers, who do not already have a regular residence in Switzerland. With regard to existing frontier workers, the transitional regime will continue until 2033.

What The 2024 border agreement provides for a €10,000 allowance for new border workers, while existing border workers will enjoy a transitional regime until 2033. The deductible covers the first part of the cost of buying a home and other expenses related to its acquisition and its first occupation in Switzerland.

When The agreement is in force from 1 January 2024. The border guards concerned must submit the application for the exemption before 1 May of the same year.

Where The agreement applies to all border workers moving to Switzerland, regardless of their nationality.

Key points

Action

In Ticino, Switzerland has recently greatly improved opportunities for those who want to move and settle more flexibly, particularly for those who have moved from another country without already being a Swiss resident or citizen.

The most interesting measure is the €10,000 deductible, which can be used to cover transfer expenses, such as rent, medical expenses, transfers and documents needed to start life in Switzerland.

To qualify for this allowance, you must apply for a residence permit and move to Switzerland by 31 December 2024.

For more information on the transfer procedure and the application for a residence permit, please consult the AFC/ESTV website.
