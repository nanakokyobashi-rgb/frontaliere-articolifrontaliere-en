Trenord, 3.88% increase on lines with Ticino (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 31 August 2026

From 1 September, tickets and Trenord subscriptions are 3.88% more expensive: an increase ordered by the Lombardy Regional Government, with exceptions for Malpensa Express and Como-Malpensa via Chiasso and Mendrisio.

Context

In brief

  • Trenord: 3.88% price increase from Tuesday, September 1st
  • The increase also affects cross-border lines with Canton Ticino
  • Malpensa Express and Como-Malpensa via Chiasso and Mendrisio: prices unchanged
  • Decision taken by the Lombardy Regional Council
  • What: 3.88% price increase on tickets and season tickets of the Trenord regional rail service
  • When: from Tuesday, September 1st
  • Where: Trenord regional lines, including cross-border lines with Canton Ticino
  • Who: Lombardy Regional Council (decision); Trenord (operator); unions and commuter associations (protesting)
  • Cause 1: adjustment to the average of two Istat indices
  • Cost of living index (April 2025 – April 2026): 2.6%
  • Transport sector index: 3.9%
  • Exceptions: Malpensa Express (tariff frozen until September 1st, 2027); Como-Malpensa connection via Chiasso and Mendrisio (price unchanged)
  • Reactions: unions and commuter associations criticize the price increase, contesting the quality of the service

From Tuesday, September 1st, travelling with Trenord costs more. The decision was taken by the Lombardy Regional Council, which ordered a 3.88% increase applied to tickets and season tickets of the regional rail service. The measure, as reported by the Ticino daily newspaper La Regione, also affects the cross-border lines connecting Lombardy to Canton Ticino, that is, the rail connections used daily by commuters and cross-border workers who return to Italy after work or, conversely, reach Swiss territory.

Operational details

What changes for those travelling between Lombardy and Canton Ticino

The 3.88% increase also explicitly applies to the cross-border lines with Canton Ticino — an important clarification for cross-border workers who return to Italy every day — or several times a week — from stations in Ticino, as well as for Italian commuters who work at companies situated beyond the border. For these people, the ticket or season ticket represents a fixed cost item, on which the new regional fare is now applied.

The fact that the decision was formalised by the Lombard regional government, and not by Trenord on its own, signals that the mechanism is of a political-administrative nature: the increase is tied to national statistical indices (Istat) and to contractual performance targets. In practice, the end user — whether a cross-border worker or a commuter — cannot intervene in determining the fare increase, but can evaluate their own mobility options in light of the exceptions provided.

Who is NOT affected by the increase

There are two cases in which the fare remains unchanged. The first is the Malpensa Express, the service dedicated to Malpensa Airport, whose price is frozen until 1 September 2027. The second is the Como–Malpensa connection via Chiasso and Mendrisio, a route that — crossing the Chiasso border and the Mendrisio hub — links the Lake Como area with the airport hub passing through Canton Ticino. For those who regularly use this route, therefore, the expense item remains unchanged compared with the previous period.

Key points

What to do, concretely

For a cross-border commuter using Trenord lines on the cross-border routes with Canton Ticino, the first step is to check on which routes the 3.88% fare increase actually applies and on which, instead, the exceptions apply. The Malpensa Express and the Como-Malpensa connection via Chiasso and Mendrisio — as reported by La Regione — are not subject to any price changes.

Anyone who holds an annual or monthly Trenord pass can already check on their profile (on the app or on the Trenord website) the renewal date and the updated amount. For single tickets purchased at the ticket machines or at the ticket office, the new price will be automatically applied starting from 1 September. It is advisable to keep the receipts of purchases made close to the date the fare increase comes into force, in case it is necessary to report an abnormal charge.

Reporting service disruptions

Unions and commuter associations have announced their opposition to the fare increase, contesting the quality of the service. Travellers can help document service disruptions by using the official Trenord channels (app, website, contact centre) and reporting delays, cancellations or overcrowding. Trade associations collect the reports to support their claims with the Region and the operator.

Travelling to Malpensa

For those using the Malpensa Express to reach the airport, the fare remains unchanged until 1 September 2027: no price changes are expected on this route over the next year. For the Como-Malpensa connection via Chiasso and Mendrisio, the ticket price remains the same as before, with no freeze deadlines communicated.

Frequently Asked Questions
When does the 3.88% mark-up on Trenord tickets come into effect?
The 3.88% increase in tickets and subscriptions for the Trenord regional rail service comes into force on Tuesday 1 September, as ordered by the Lombardy Regional Council. The measure also affects cross-border lines with the Canton of Ticino.
Which Trenord routes do not see the increase?
The Malpensa Express fare, frozen until 1 September 2027, and the Como-Malpensa connection via Chiasso and Mendrisio, whose ticket price remains unchanged, are excluded from the increase.
How did you arrive at the percentage of 3.88%?
The increase derives from the average of two ISTAT indices: the cost of living index between April 2025 and April 2026, equal to 2.6%, and that of the transport sector, equal to 3.9%, to which is added the evaluation of the performance objectives set in the service contracts.
What are the reactions to being overpriced?
Trade unions and associations of commuters criticize the 3.88% increase, contesting the quality of the regional rail service, in particular on the routes with the highest density of commuter traffic, where delays, cancellations and crowding are concentrated.
What changes for border crossers using Trenord?
Border crossers using the cross-border lines Trenord with the Canton of Ticino will see a 3.88% increase on tickets and season tickets from 1 September, with the exception of the Malpensa Express and Como-Malpensa routes via Chiasso and Mendrisio, whose prices remain unchanged.
