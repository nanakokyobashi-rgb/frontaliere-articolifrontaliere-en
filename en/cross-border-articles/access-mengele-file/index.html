Access to Mengele dossier: what changes for cross-border workers (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 May 2026

Swiss intelligence services now allow access to the Mengele dossier at the Federal Archives, in accordance with the 2001 decision.

Context

In brief

Access to the Mengele dossier now possible at the Swiss Federal Archives The decision stems from a reassessment and a practice from 2001 The dossier was handed over in 2001 to the Swiss Federal Archives (SFA) by the Police Service The request for consultation is subject to evaluations by the Swiss Intelligence Service (SIC) The final decision will be communicated soon

Key facts

  • What: Access to the Mengele dossier at the Swiss Federal Archives
  • When: Announcement on May 4, 2026
  • Where: Switzerland, Federal Archives, Bellinzona
  • Who: Swiss Intelligence Service (SIC), Federal Administrative Court
  • Amount: Not specified

The Swiss Confederation’s Intelligence Service (SIC) announced that, following a reassessment, the dossier on Nazi criminal Josef Mengele will be accessible at the Swiss Federal Archives (SFA). The decision originates from a previous ruling in 2001, which provided for access to archival files evaluated by the Bergier Commission. The dossier, compiled by the police service of the Federal Public Ministry, was handed over to the SFA in the same year. The possibility of consultation, previously rejected, can now be requested after a review involving the Federal Administrative Court (FAC). The final decision will be communicated shortly, with details on procedures and conditions still to be defined.

Practical Actions and Procedures for Cross-Border Workers

For those wishing to consult the Mengele dossier, the first step is to submit a formal request to the SIC, which evaluates each application thoroughly, balancing research interests with the protection of privacy rights. The procedure involves filling out a specific form and attaching any relevant documentation that verifies the purpose of the consultation.

It is advisable to first check the official SIC or Federal Archives website to verify the request procedures, eligibility criteria, and any deadlines. Requests can also be submitted by individuals involved in an ongoing legal case, such as a judicial proceeding initiated in February 2026.

Once the application is submitted, the SIC will carry out the necessary verifications and inform about the outcome. The decision will include any access conditions, such as consultation hours, duration, and required documents. Following these instructions carefully is essential to avoid refusals or delays.

To facilitate the process, it is recommended to use the online tools available on the SIC website, such as the salary calculator to verify potential taxes or reimbursements, and to prepare a list of identification documents and motivations.

Finally, this decision exemplifies how, even in historical and legal contexts, cross-border workers can take concrete actions to deepen their understanding, contributing to collective memory and the search for truth. For further information, you can consult the salary calculator or other operational guides on the dedicated portal.

Frequently Asked Questions
How can I request access to the Mengele dossier?
To request access, you need to submit an application to the Swiss Intelligence Service (SIC), which will evaluate it according to Swiss law. The request requires filling out an official form and attaching any relevant documentation explaining the purpose. The final decision will be communicated through methods and conditions to be defined, and the process can be followed via the official SIC website.
What documents are needed for the application?
It is advisable to prepare an identity document, a detailed motivation, and any relevant documentation attesting to the purpose of the request. The exact procedures are available on the official SIC website, which also provides the online application form.
When will it be possible to consult the dossier?
The final decision will be communicated shortly. The request will be evaluated by the SIC, which may also establish specific access modalities and conditions. Consultation could be possible within the next few months, subject to court approval and the definition of operational procedures.
