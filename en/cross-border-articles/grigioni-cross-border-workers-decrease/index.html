Operational details

The data and statistics manager of the Canton of Graubünden, Luzius Stricher, explained that the reduction is due to several causes, including the reduction of open positions and the digitization of tasks.

According to the latest available data, the number of border crossers arriving in the Canton of Graubünden fell by 12.5% compared to the same period last year. This trend is in line with the general trend of the Swiss cantons bordering Italy, which recorded an overall reduction of 10% of border crossers in 2022.

The reduction in open positions is one of the main causes of this trend. In fact, according to the estimates of the department of economy of the Canton of Graubünden, 30% of border companies reduced their activities in 2022 due to the reduction in job demands. This is especially true for companies involved in the production of consumer goods, which have seen a 20% reduction in job demands.

Another important cause of the reduction of border workers is the digitisation of tasks. According to research conducted by the University of Italian Switzerland, 40% of border companies implemented digital technologies to automate their tasks in 2022. This led to a 15% reduction in cross-border jobs.
