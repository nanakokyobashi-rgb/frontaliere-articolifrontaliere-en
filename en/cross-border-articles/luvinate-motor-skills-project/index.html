The project in Luvinate

In Luvinate, the 26-27 motor activity project of the Kindergarten School began, carried out in collaboration with the Varese Basket School. The initiative is aimed at kindergarten children and renews and strengthens the school's commitment to the healthy growth of its young pupils.

Sport plays a central role in the proposal and contributes to the psychophysical and social growth of children already in the early years. The school thus integrates movement into the educational path, instead of limiting it to a leisure parenthesis.

The project aims to offer children a structured but playful space for movement. The choice is linked to sedentary lifestyle, described in the source as an early educational challenge. The response identified by the Kindergarten of Luvinate is an activity organized through play, but maintaining a size suitable for children.

With the accompaniment of the qualified instructors of the Varese Basket School, children experience the first elements of basketball and test their motor skills. The proposal includes exercises with the ball, paths for coordination and activities dedicated to orientation in space and time.

The start of the project therefore combines collaboration with a sports reality with the daily life of the Nursery School. The expected result of the school is a path in which play and movement accompany the growth of children, with attention to both the physical and social components.

Operational details

The educational value of sport

Body, relationships and autonomy

In the 3-to-6 age group, physical activity is described as a genuine educational tool. The project of the Luvinate Nursery School starts from this approach and combines physical development with a series of objectives related to personal growth and relationships among children.

The program stems from the joint work of the teachers in Luvinate and the professionals from the Varese Basket School and aims to develop body awareness, inclusion and socialization. The proposal also seeks to strengthen self-esteem and autonomy through movement and play experiences in early childhood.

According to the source, the value of the initiative is not limited to learning the first sports techniques. Early movement is identified as a key to preventing postural problems and promoting an active lifestyle, which the children are intended to maintain into adulthood as well.

The project thus brings together the contribution of the teachers and that of the qualified instructors. On the one hand, there is the school, with its educational role; on the other, the Varese Basket School, which brings sports expertise and offers activities related to basketball, the ball, coordination and orientation. The stated aim is to provide 360-degree stimulation.

“We are happy about this collaboration,” says the educational team, which believes that Nursery School should provide 360-degree stimulation. The thanks go to the Varese Basket School and the other sports organizations, which during this year will offer the young pupils opportunities for play and growth.

In the educational team's comment, the collaboration is therefore included in a broader offering of opportunities for play and growth. The Varese Basket School is thanked together with the other sports organizations that, over the course of this year, will help provide the children with additional stimulation.

Key points

From basic techniques to play experiences

Project activities

The program launched in Luvinate takes shape through activities in which basketball represents one of the first sports experiences offered to the children. The qualified instructors from Varese Basket School guide them in learning the basic techniques, keeping the work within a playful setting.

Alongside basketball fundamentals, the program includes exploring motor skills. The ball becomes one of the tools used during the activities, while coordination exercises allow the children to experiment with movement. Spatial-temporal orientation activities are also planned, included in the program together with the other physical activities.

This structure makes it possible to present the project not as a lesson focused on a single discipline, but as a set of physical experiences intended for the children of the Preschool. Basketball remains the focus of the collaboration, while play, the ball, coordination and orientation constitute the means through which the children can explore their abilities.

The continuation indicated by the school also covers the rest of the year. The message from the educational team recalls that, in addition to Varese Basket School, other sports organizations will offer the children opportunities for play and growth. The source does not indicate which organizations these are, but places them within the educational program aimed at the young pupils.

The 26-27 project therefore presents itself as a structured, play-based physical activity, led by qualified instructors and integrated into the life of Luvinate's Preschool. The planned experiences include both an introduction to basketball and work on motor skills through the ball, coordination and spatial-temporal orientation. For the children, the program offers opportunities for play and growth, while for the school it represents the renewal of a commitment to healthy development in early childhood.

Frequently Asked Questions
What does the new motor project in Luvinate consist of?
The 26-27 project, aimed at children in kindergarten, integrates motor activity into everyday school life to counteract early sedentary behaviour. Under the guidance of the instructors of the Varese Basket School, the young students carry out recreational activities that include the use of the ball, coordination paths and spatio-temporal orientation exercises, promoting psychophysical and social development.
What are the pedagogical objectives of the initiative?
The initiative aims to stimulate body awareness, inclusion, socialization, self-esteem and autonomy of children between 3 and 6 years of age. In addition to learning the basics of basketball, the course aims to prevent postural problems and encourage an active lifestyle, offering 360-degree stimuli for the personal growth of young students.
Is the collaboration limited only to Varese Basket School?
No, the project fits into a wider educational offer. The school's educational team pointed out that, along with the Varese Basket School, other sports organizations will also be involved during the year to offer further opportunities for play and growth, guaranteeing a variety of motor stimuli for children.
