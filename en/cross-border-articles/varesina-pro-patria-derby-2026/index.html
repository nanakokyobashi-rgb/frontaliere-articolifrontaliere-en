Varesina-Pro Patria derby: Sunday's clash

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 October 2026

Sunday 4 October at 15 at Elmec Solar Stadium: Pro Patria (12 points) are looking for a reaction, Varesina (4) for a turning point.

Context

In brief

  • Varesina-Pro Patria derby on Sunday 4 October at 15
  • It will be played at the Elmec Solar Stadium in Venegono Superiore
  • Pro Patria have taken 12 points from four wins
  • Varesina have lost their last three matches

Key facts

  • Match: Varesina-Pro Patria
  • Venue: Elmec Solar Stadium, Venegono Superiore
  • Time: Sunday 4 October, 15
  • Varesina's latest result: 4-2 defeat to Casatese Merate
  • Standings: Pro Patria 12 points, Varesina 4

On Sunday 4 October at 15, at the Elmec Solar Stadium in Venegono Superiore, Varesina and Pro Patria will face each other in a derby presented as a match worth more than three points. The blue-and-whites come into it after their first league defeat, while the Phoenixes are looking for a response capable of ending their negative run.

The two sides come into the match with different needs. Pro Patria have collected 12 points from four consecutive wins, before coming to a halt at home against Solbiatese. The setback changed the tone of the build-up, but not the stated objective: the Little Tigers must react immediately to avoid losing ground to the teams at the top. In the standings, Pistoiese are first with 15 points; Pro Sesto and Pro Patria follow on 12.

Pro Patria's build-up

At the pre-match press conference, coach Zironelli asked the team to regain clarity and decisiveness. The coach distinguished technical errors, considered part of football, from tactical ones, which in his view must not be repeated. The defeat against Solbiatese was framed as part of a broader process, with a call to put the setback behind them immediately:

«Now we reset and start again.»

Zironelli also confirmed the decision to play attacking football, with more players involved in ball management and attacking play. He explained that the team does not intend to be influenced by comparisons with their opponents and that, ahead of the derby and another run of closely spaced matches, rotations are possible to involve every member of the squad.

Varesina's comeback bid

Varesina began with a win against Cittadella Modena, but in the following matchdays alternated good performances with difficulties, before suffering three consecutive defeats. The latest, away to Casatese Merate, ended 4-2. The Phoenixes need consistency and confidence, especially since they sit on 4 points in the standings, level with Tropical Coriano. The derby therefore pits a team seeking to defend its momentum against one called upon to turn the quality of its play into points.

Operational details

The most useful statistic for analyzing the derby is not just the gap in the standings. The match pits a Varesina side that has shown an ability to create and score, but has paid for defensive lapses, against a Pro Patria side that wants to reaffirm its attacking identity without repeating the tactical errors committed against Solbiatese. For both, the key is to turn their defining trait into a complete performance.

The two indicators of the matchup

For the Fenici, the 4-2 against Casatese Merate tells the story of a match with contrasting signs. The team came from behind twice, showed quality in its ball circulation, and consistently looked for goal. However, it was unable to maintain control of the match. Lapses on set pieces proved costly: Corno scored twice from corner kicks. The seven goals scored in the first five matchdays confirm the team's ability to make an impact up front, while the derby calls for that attacking impetus to be accompanied by a more attentive defensive phase.

On Pro Patria's side, Zironelli's idea is just as clear: more players involved in building play and attacking, with the conviction that the attacking phase is also the team's main protection. The coach highlighted one precise statistic: the blue-and-whites are the side that concedes the fewest shots on target in the group. After the defeat to Solbiatese, the issue is not abandoning the model, but applying it with clarity and without tactical errors.

A matchup to assess beyond the standings

Zironelli recalled that in a derby, the position in the standings does not decide the outcome by itself. Pro Patria starts with 12 points and Varesina with 4, but the eve of the match points to two different responses: the Tigrotti must regain their top-half momentum, while the Fenici must turn the quality of their play into consistency. For those following the match, there are therefore two things to watch: Varesina's solidity on set pieces and Pro Patria's ability to stay on the front foot without making tactical errors.
