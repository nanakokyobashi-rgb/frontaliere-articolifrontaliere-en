Working as an educator in Ticino: LIS 2026 pay and requirements

This guide brings together documented pay, qualifications and practical steps for people considering early-childhood work in Ticino. The detailed figures below belong to the LIS 2026 competition; for another employer, use that employer’s vacancy and pay scale.

2026 salary: scope and a fair comparison

The verifiable pay figures here come from the 2026 competition of Lugano Istituti Sociali (LIS), an autonomous institution of the City of Lugano. They are not a Ticino-wide average or a federal minimum wage. The LIS notice places early-childhood educators in band XI, grades 12–14, and includes the 13th salary payment. Within each grade, the maximum is about 18% above the minimum. The grade 13 minimum is about 6% above grade 12’s minimum; grade 14 is about 12.6% above it. Grade placement and any credit for prior relevant work depend on the employer’s rules and assessment. This is the LIS 2026 pay scale only, not a rate for every nursery or institution. Operational details

Qualification: SSS or an equivalent title, depending on the post

The LIS 2026 notice accepts a higher professional school diploma (SSS) in early-childhood education or an equivalent qualification. It does not make SSS the sole valid credential for every Ticino employer: check the requirements in each vacancy. A foreign qualification is not automatically equivalent. SERI distinguishes a level attestation, used for non-regulated professions, from recognition of equivalence for regulated professions; the authority and procedure depend on the profession and qualification. Check your case through recognition.swiss and SERI’s official FAQ, and do not start a process that the employer or competent authority has not said is needed.

Key points

Application, work permit and tax status are separate steps

The LIS 2026 competition remains open even when there is no immediate staffing need. The notice sets a deadline of 11:59 pm on Sunday, 13 December 2026, and requires an online application through the LIS portal. Its initial document list includes a cover letter, CV, recent photograph, education and employment certificates, health questionnaire and criminal-record self-declaration; extracts may be requested later. Read the full notice and the LIS opportunities page. An open competition is not a promise of hiring.

For EU/EFTA citizens resident in an EU/EFTA state who work in Switzerland, SEM describes the G permit and a return to the foreign home at least once a week. Check your case with the competent cantonal authority and the official SEM FAQ. A permit concerns immigration; it does not by itself establish tax status. The 2020 Switzerland–Italy tax agreement entered into force on 17 July 2023. Use the Canton Ticino’s official cross-border tax-status simulator and information; do not apply old exemptions automatically. Frequently Asked Questions
Are the LIS 2026 figures the average salary for educators in Ticino?
No. They are the gross annual minimum and maximum for grades 12, 13 and 14 in the LIS 2026 notice, including the 13th salary payment.
Is an SSS diploma always required?
The LIS 2026 notice accepts an SSS diploma in early-childhood education or an equivalent qualification. Each employer can specify its own vacancy requirements.
Are the G permit and tax status the same thing?
No. The permit concerns the right to work and reside; tax status must be checked under the Switzerland–Italy agreement and the Canton Ticino’s official tools.
