Setting the source on earned income

The withholding tax on earned income is only levied in Switzerland for cross-border commuters, according to Art. 20 of the Federal Act of 24 March 2007 on Personal Income Tax (LIP). This means that cross-border commuters are subject to personal income tax in Switzerland, while Italy avoids double taxation with the tax credit (EC framework of 730).

Cross-border commuter allowance

The deductible for new cross-border commuters is CHF 10,000, as stipulated in Art. 20, paragraph 2, of the Federal Law of 24 March 2007 on personal income tax. The deductible for old cross-border commuters is CHF 7,500, as stipulated in Art. 20, paragraph 3, of the same law.

New cross-border agreement

The New Cross-Border Commuter Agreement provides for a transitional regime from 2024 to 2033, as established by art. 1 of the Convention between Switzerland and Italy of 14 December 2022. This transitional regime is intended to regulate relations between cross-border commuters and the tax authorities of both countries.
