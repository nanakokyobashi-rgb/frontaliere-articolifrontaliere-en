Who are the frontiersmen?

Border workers are those who reside in Switzerland and have a work or study relationship in Italy. They are subject to specific tax and labour rules, which may vary depending on their border status. Border crossings can be divided into two categories: old border crossings and new border crossings.

Old frontiers:

Old border crossers are those who have already obtained the G permit before 1 January 2023. These border workers are exempt from taxes for income deriving from their work in Italy.
