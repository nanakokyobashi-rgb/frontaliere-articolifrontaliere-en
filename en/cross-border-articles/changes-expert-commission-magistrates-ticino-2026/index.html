Ticino judges' commission leadership change (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 7 May 2026

Emanuela Colombo Epiney steps down as president for personal reasons. The Grand Council will appoint her successor.

Context

In brief

  • President Emanuela Colombo Epiney resigns
  • The Grand Council will nominate the successor
  • Luca Marazzi proposed as new member

Key facts

  • What: Resignation of the president of the Commission of Experts
  • When: End of May 2026
  • Where: Bellinzona
  • Who: Emanuela Colombo Epiney
  • Amount: Not specified

President Emanuela Colombo Epiney, former Appeal Court judge, has resigned with effect from the end of the month. The news was revealed this evening by LaRegione. This opens the procedure for her replacement, which will be the responsibility of Parliament. The nomination should take place in the next session of the Grand Council, scheduled to start on Monday, 18 May. A name has already been proposed: the Presidential Office has indeed indicated to the plenary Luca Marazzi, former judge of the Federal Court, as the new member of the Commission. The relevant report was published this afternoon.

The Commission of Experts, renewed by four fifths by the Grand Council in May of last year, is currently composed, in addition to the outgoing president, of Franco Lardelli, Stefano Manetti, Luca Marcellini and Francesca Piffaretti-Lanz.

Nomination procedure

The procedure for nominating the new president of the Commission of Experts for the election of magistrates is a delicate process involving several stages. First, the Grand Council must receive the candidacies and evaluate the profiles of the candidates. Subsequently, the hearings and voting take place. The process can last several weeks, but the aim is to have a new president in office as soon as possible to ensure the continuity of the Commission's work.

Operational details

Practical Analysis

The changes at the top of the Commission of Experts for the election of magistrates can have various practical implications for cross-border workers in Ticino. First of all, it is important to understand the role of the Commission and how its decisions can influence the local judicial system. The Commission is responsible for assessing the suitability of candidates for the position of prosecutor or judge, a crucial role for justice and security in the Canton of Ticino.

Evaluation Procedure

The evaluation procedure of candidates by the Commission of Experts is a rigorous process that involves several phases. First, candidates must submit their documentation and pass an initial interview. Subsequently, the Commission evaluates the candidates' skills and experience through specific tests and practical assessments. Finally, the Commission issues a preliminary notice to the Grand Council, which makes the final decision.

Impact on Judicial Procedures

Changes in the leadership of the Commission can influence judicial procedures in the Canton of Ticino. A new president could introduce new evaluation methodologies or modify the suitability criteria. This could lead to changes in judicial procedures and influence the speed and efficiency of the judicial system.

Consult legal experts

Use online tools

There are several online tools that can help cross-border workers stay updated on changes in the Commission of Experts. For example, the Grand Council's website and social media can provide updated information and news on the selection procedures for judges.
