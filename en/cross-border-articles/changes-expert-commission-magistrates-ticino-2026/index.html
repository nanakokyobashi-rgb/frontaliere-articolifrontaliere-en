Ticino judges' commission leadership change

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 May 2026

Emanuela Colombo Epiney steps down as president for personal reasons. The Grand Council will appoint her successor.

Context

In brief

  • President Emanuela Colombo Epiney resigns
  • The Grand Council will nominate the successor
  • Luca Marazzi proposed as new member

Key facts

  • What: Resignation of the president of the Commission of Experts
  • When: End of May 2026
  • Where: Bellinzona
  • Who: Emanuela Colombo Epiney
  • Amount: Not specified

President Emanuela Colombo Epiney, former Appeal Court judge, has resigned with effect from the end of the month. The news was revealed this evening by LaRegione. This opens the procedure for her replacement, which will be the responsibility of Parliament. The nomination should take place in the next session of the Grand Council, scheduled to start on Monday, 18 May. A name has already been proposed: the Presidential Office has indeed indicated to the plenary Luca Marazzi, former judge of the Federal Court, as the new member of the Commission. The relevant report was published this afternoon.

The Commission of Experts, renewed by four fifths by the Grand Council in May of last year, is currently composed, in addition to the outgoing president, of Franco Lardelli, Stefano Manetti, Luca Marcellini and Francesca Piffaretti-Lanz.

Nomination procedure

The procedure for nominating the new president of the Commission of Experts for the election of magistrates is a delicate process involving several stages. First, the Grand Council must receive the candidacies and evaluate the profiles of the candidates. Subsequently, the hearings and voting take place. The process can last several weeks, but the aim is to have a new president in office as soon as possible to ensure the continuity of the Commission's work.

Impact on cross-border workers

Changes at the top of the Commission of Experts for the election of magistrates can have a significant impact on cross-border workers working in Ticino. The Commission is responsible for assessing the suitability of candidates for the position of prosecutor or judge, a crucial role for justice and security in the Canton of Ticino. A change in the leadership of the Commission can influence the direction and priorities of the assessments, with possible repercussions on the local judicial system.

Comparison with the previous situation

Before the resignation of Emanuela Colombo Epiney, the Commission of Experts was composed of members with extensive experience in the judicial sector. The new leadership could bring new perspectives and approaches to the evaluation of candidates. However, it is important that the continuity of the Commission's work is maintained to ensure the stability and efficiency of the Ticino judicial system.
