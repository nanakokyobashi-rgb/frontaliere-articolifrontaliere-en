Working as a worker in Ticino: salary, requirements and permit G

Border workers who wish to work in the Canton of Ticino must know the requirements and regulations relating to the G permit. The new law introduced on December 23, 2020, establishes that worker border workers can work in Ticino without the G permit, but only if they meet certain requirements.

Requirements to work as a worker in Ticino

To work as a worker in Ticino, border workers must meet the following requirements:

  • Age: At least 18 years of age.
  • Training: Have professional training recognized in Switzerland.
  • Knowledge: Know German, Italian and English.
  • Residence: Have a permanent residence in the Canton of Ticino.

Salary and working conditions

Border workers working in the Canton of Ticino are entitled to a minimum salary of CHF 3,500 per month. In addition, they must have a written employment contract setting out the working conditions, including salary, working hours and holidays.

Permission G

Despite the new law, frontier workers must still obtain a G permit to work in Ticino. The G permit can be requested at the State Chancellery of the Canton of Ticino.

Requirements to obtain G Permit

To obtain the G permit, frontier workers must meet the following requirements:

  • Have an employment contract with a Ticino employer
  • Have a monthly income of at least CHF 4,500.- (art. 11 of the Federal Law on the Employment of Foreigners of 24 March 2007)
  • Not to have a monthly income of more than CHF 15,000.- (art. 11 of the Federal Law on the Employment of Foreigners of 24 March 2007)
  • Not be subject to ongoing criminal proceedings in Switzerland or abroad
  • Not have a debt of at least CHF 10,000.- to the federal or cantonal administration (art. 12 of the Federal Law on the Employment of Foreigners of 24 March 2007)

G Permit Requirements

To apply for the G permit, frontier workers must meet the following requirements:

  • Be a citizen of an EU country or a country with which Switzerland has an agreement on the free movement of workers
  • Be at least 18 years of age
  • Have recognised vocational training in Switzerland
  • Have an employment contract with a Ticino employer
  • Have liability insurance

How to apply for G Permit

To apply for the G permit, frontier workers must submit a formal request to the competent authority and provide all the required documents. Required documents include:

  • A copy of the employment contract
  • A copy of the recognized professional training
  • A copy of the liability insurance
  • A copy of your ID card
  • A copy of your passport
