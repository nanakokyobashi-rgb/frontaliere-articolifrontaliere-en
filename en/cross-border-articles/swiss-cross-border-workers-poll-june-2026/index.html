Less than two weeks before the vote on 14 June 2026, those who oppose the No to a 10 million Switzerland initiative are now in the majority. The no reaches 52% of the voting intentions, according to the second survey of the SSR. On the question of the tightening of the conditions of access to the civil service, it is head to head between those in favour and those against. The initiative External Link of the Centre Democratic Union (UDC, conservative right) aims to set a ceiling of 10 million inhabitants by 2050, through a drastic limitation of immigration. While at the beginning of May the fronts of yes and no were on an equal footing, those who oppose the initiative have now taken the lead, as the second survey by the Swiss Broadcasting Corporation (SSR) shows. Operational details

Winterthur's aggression represents an element of uncertainty relevant to the balances of the electoral campaign. In light of the results of the survey, gfs.bern rather expects a rejection of the text proposed by the UDC. The evolution of opinions should continue to follow the usual pattern, that is, a loss of consensus with the progressive advancement of the political campaign. A protest vote, which in 2014 had led to the acceptance of the initiative against mass immigration, should not be repeated, estimates Lukas Golder. Political scientists at the polling institute point out, however, that the survey was conducted before the terrorist knife attack that injured three people last Thursday in Winterthur, in the canton of Zurich. Events of this type can influence the mobilisation of one of the parties in the short term, creating a more emotional climate and potentially influencing participation in the vote scheduled for 14 June 2026. The evolution is even more surprising for the second item on the ballot on 14 June, namely the amendment of the Civil Service Act which envisages tightening access conditions to ensure the army's strength. While those who support the project had started the campaign with a comfortable advantage, they have now lost ground and are in a situation of absolute balance with the opponents.

Key points

The popular consultation of 14 June 2026 is part of a federal voting calendar that calls on the entire Swiss electoral body to vote on issues of strategic importance for the country's institutional and economic architecture. For those who live and work in Switzerland, including frontier workers active in the Canton of Ticino and in the various production sectors, understanding the trend of surveys and the implications of popular initiatives allows you to monitor the political and economic climate of the territory. The voting procedure provides for the sending of voting material directly to the domicile of eligible citizens or participation in the manner provided by the individual cantons. It is essential to follow the official updates issued by the Confederation and the media to verify the deadlines and methods of participation. Frequently Asked Questions
What is the No to a 10 million Switzerland initiative?
The initiative promoted by the Centre Democratic Union (UDC) aims to set a ceiling of 10 million inhabitants in Switzerland by 2050, through a drastic limitation of immigration from abroad.
What were the percentages recorded by the second SSR survey?
The second poll by the gfs.bern institute, published in June 2026, indicates that no to the initiative reaches 52% of voting intentions, while those in favour stand at 45% and those undecided are 3%.
What is the situation for the amendment of the Civil Service Act?
The second item on the ballot on 14 June 2026 shows a head-to-head: only 48% support the legislative change (4 points less than in the first poll), 46% are against and 6% are undecided.
