The Craft House of Dongio, a historic shop located in the Blenio Valley, is seeking support to continue its operations. A fundraising campaign has been launched to secure the future of the shop and preserve the valley's artisanal heritage. The news was reported by laRegione.ch on May 6, 2026.

The Craft House is a landmark for the local community and a symbol of the artisanal heritage of the Blenio Valley. The shop, located in Dongio, is a place where local craftsmen can display and sell their products, helping to keep the region's artisanal traditions alive.

The fundraising campaign has been launched to address the economic difficulties the shop is facing. Without community support, the future of the shop is at risk. The Craft House is an important place not only for local craftsmen but also for tourists visiting the Blenio Valley, attracted by the beauty of the landscape and the richness of the cultural heritage.

The importance of community support

Community support is crucial to ensure the survival of the Craft House. The fundraising campaign aims to raise the necessary funds to keep the shop open and continue promoting local craftsmanship. Without community support, the shop may be forced to close, with negative consequences for local craftsmen and the valley's cultural heritage.

Operational details

The economic challenges faced by the Casa dell'artigianato

The Casa dell'artigianato in Dongio is facing several economic challenges that threaten its survival. These include ever-increasing operating costs, competition from large stores, and a decrease in tourism in the valley. These factors have made it difficult for the shop to maintain its operations without the support of the community.

Impact on the local community

The closure of the Casa dell'artigianato would have a significant impact on the local community. Local artisans would lose an important sales outlet for their products, and the valley would lose a tourist attraction. Furthermore, the closure of the shop could have a ripple effect on the local economy, affecting other shops and businesses in the valley.

Opportunities for support

There are several opportunities to support the Casa dell'artigianato. In addition to direct donations, you can participate in events organized by the shop, purchase local artisan products, or become a supporting member. Every contribution is important and helps to ensure the future of the shop.

The role of cross-border workers in support

Cross-border workers who live and work in the region can play a crucial role in supporting the Casa dell'artigianato. They can contribute to fundraising, participate in events, and purchase local artisan products. Additionally, cross-border workers can spread the word about the fundraising campaign among their friends and family, increasing awareness and support for the cause.
