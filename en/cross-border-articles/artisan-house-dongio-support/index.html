Craft house seeks support to keep going

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 May 2026

Fundraising to secure the future of Dongio's shop and preserve Blenio Valley's artisan heritage

Context

In brief

  • Fundraising to save Dongio's Craft House
  • Preserving the artisanal heritage of Blenio Valley
  • Future of the historic shop at risk

Key facts

  • What: Fundraising for the Craft House
  • When: May 6, 2026
  • Where: Dongio, Blenio Valley, Ticino
  • Who: Local community and craft enthusiasts
  • Amount: Not yet specified

The Craft House of Dongio, a historic shop located in the Blenio Valley, is seeking support to continue its operations. A fundraising campaign has been launched to secure the future of the shop and preserve the valley's artisanal heritage. The news was reported by laRegione.ch on May 6, 2026.

The Craft House is a landmark for the local community and a symbol of the artisanal heritage of the Blenio Valley. The shop, located in Dongio, is a place where local craftsmen can display and sell their products, helping to keep the region's artisanal traditions alive.

The fundraising campaign has been launched to address the economic difficulties the shop is facing. Without community support, the future of the shop is at risk. The Craft House is an important place not only for local craftsmen but also for tourists visiting the Blenio Valley, attracted by the beauty of the landscape and the richness of the cultural heritage.

The importance of community support

Community support is crucial to ensure the survival of the Craft House. The fundraising campaign aims to raise the necessary funds to keep the shop open and continue promoting local craftsmanship. Without community support, the shop may be forced to close, with negative consequences for local craftsmen and the valley's cultural heritage.

The Craft House of Dongio plays a crucial role in promoting local craftsmanship. The shop offers a showcase for craftsmen from the Blenio Valley, allowing them to reach a wider audience and sell their products. Additionally, the shop organizes events and exhibitions to promote craftsmanship and attract tourists to the valley.

Additionally, the shop offers opportunities to purchase high-quality artisanal products that can be difficult to find elsewhere.

Cross-border workers can contribute to the fundraising campaign in various ways. Every contribution, big or small, is important to secure the future of the shop and preserve the artisanal heritage of the Blenio Valley.

Current situation

At the moment, the fundraising campaign is underway, and the local community is invited to participate. The Craft House hopes to raise the necessary funds to continue operating and promoting local craftsmanship. The situation is critical, but with community support, the shop can continue to thrive.

Conclusion

The Craft House of Dongio is a cultural treasure that deserves to be preserved. The fundraising campaign is an opportunity for the local community and cross-border workers to contribute to keeping the artisanal heritage of the Blenio Valley alive. With everyone's support, the shop can continue to be a landmark for local craftsmanship and an attraction for tourists.
