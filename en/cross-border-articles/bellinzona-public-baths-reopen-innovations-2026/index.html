Bellinzona public pools reopen with 2026 updates (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 6 May 2026

From online booking to modernizations, here are the novelties for the 2026 summer season

Context

In brief

  • Bellinzona Public Baths reopen on May 9, 2026
  • Online booking available to reduce queues
  • New 'Cielo Aperto' dining concept

Key facts

  • What: Reopening of Bellinzona Public Baths
  • When: May 9, 2026
  • Where: Bellinzona, Canton Ticino
  • Who: City of Bellinzona
  • Amount: 3.1 million CHF for renovations

The Bellinzona Public Baths will reopen on Saturday, May 9, 2026, with several new features for the summer season. Among the main ones, the possibility to purchase the entrance online to reduce queues at the ticket offices during peak hours. The structure, which has been operating for nearly sixty years, has undergone renovation work, including the renovation of the changing rooms on level +1 with new showers, replacement of the glazing, and refurbishment of lockers and benches. Opening hours will vary during the season: until June 12 from 9 am to 7:30 pm, from June 13 to August 16 from 9 am to 8 pm, and from August 17 to September 13 from 9 am to 7:30 pm.

Dining novelties

The catering service has been completely revised with the new 'Cielo Aperto' concept, which includes the Lounge Terrace with drinks and Thai specialties, a Self-Service Snack Bar with pizzeria, and the Ciccio Burger outlet dedicated to artisan burgers. The aim is to make the facility a reference point for residents and visitors.

Renovations

The renovation work, funded with a credit of 3.1 million CHF, mainly concerned the changing rooms on level +1. The new showers will not be immediately available, but the refurbishment of lockers and benches has already been completed. The glazing has been replaced to improve the safety and aesthetics of the structure.

The innovations introduced at the Public Baths of Bellinzona for the summer season 2026 represent a significant step forward compared to previous years. The possibility of booking entry online is an innovation that will facilitate access to the facility, reducing queues and improving the overall user experience. This is particularly useful for cross-border workers who often have demanding work schedules and little time to spare for recreational activities.

Compared to other similar facilities in Ticino, the Public Baths of Bellinzona stand out for their diversified catering offer and the quality of the renovations. The Lounge Terrace with drinks and Thai specialties, the Self-Service Snack Bar with pizzeria, and the Ciccio Burger sales point offer a wide range of options for all tastes. This makes the facility a reference point not only for residents but also for visitors from Italy.

Key points

What to do to benefit from the new features

To benefit from the new amenities offered by the Public Baths of Bellinzona, cross-border workers can follow these simple steps:

1. Online booking: Access the official website of the Public Baths of Bellinzona and book the entrance online. This will allow you to avoid queues and plan your visit in advance. 2. Check the events calendar: On the official website, you can consult the events calendar and any access restrictions to the pools. 3. Choose your preferred dining option: During the visit, cross-border workers can choose from the various dining options available, such as the Terrazza Lounge, the Self Service Snack Bar, or the Ciccio Burger sales point.

Online booking procedure

1. Access the official website of the Public Baths of Bellinzona. 2. Select the preferred date and time for the visit. 3. Enter personal data and confirm the booking. 4. Receive the booking confirmation via email.

Required documents

To access the Public Baths of Bellinzona, cross-border workers must bring a valid ID and, if necessary, the online booking confirmation. It is advisable to also bring a means of payment for any purchases at the dining facilities.

Deadlines and opening hours

Opening hours will vary during the season: until June 12th from 9 am to 7:30 pm, from June 13th to August 16th from 9 am to 8 pm, and from August 17th to September 13th from 9 am to 7:30 pm. It is important to plan the visit based on these hours to avoid inconveniences.

Useful tools

For further information and to plan the visit, cross-border workers can use the cost calculator available on the Frontaliere Ticino website. This tool allows you to calculate the costs associated with the visit to the Public Baths of Bellinzona and plan the budget effectively.
