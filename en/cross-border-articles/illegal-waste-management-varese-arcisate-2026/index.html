Illegal waste management: 3 reported, seizures

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 28 April 2026

Operation by the Forestry Carabinieri between Varese and Arcisate for illegal waste disposal. Three people reported and seizures of warehouse and vehicles.

Context

In brief

  • Three people reported for illegal waste management between Varese and Arcisate
  • Warehouse, industrial area, and two trucks seized
  • 100 cubic meters of unauthorized waste found

Key facts

  • What: Illegal management of special waste
  • When: April 27, 2026
  • Where: Varese and Arcisate
  • Who: Carabinieri Forestali of Arcisate and Investigative Unit of Varese
  • Amount: 100 cubic meters of waste seized

The military personnel of the Carabinieri Forestale Unit of Arcisate discovered a warehouse and an area of approximately 800 square meters where 100 cubic meters of waste had been disposed of without authorization. During the operation, discarded appliances, mattresses, wood, glass, metal scraps, and car components were found. At the end of the operation, the warehouse, the storage area, and two heavy vehicles still loaded with waste were seized.

Concrete actions for cross-border workers

To avoid incurring penalties and legal consequences, cross-border workers in the Varese and Arcisate area must follow specific procedures for waste disposal.

Waste disposal procedure

1. Identify the type of waste to be disposed of. 2. Check local regulations for disposing of the identified waste type. 3. Use the differentiated waste collection services or authorized collection centers. 4. Keep documentation related to waste disposal for possible inspections.

Using differentiated waste collection services

Cross-border workers can use the differentiated waste collection services offered by their municipalities of residence. It's important to follow the instructions provided by the municipalities and dispose of waste in the designated bins or containers.

Using authorized collection centers

Cross-border workers can also use authorized collection centers to dispose of waste. It's important to check the opening days and hours of the collection centers and bring the necessary documentation for waste disposal.

Collaboration with authorities

Cross-border workers must collaborate with local authorities to ensure a clean and safe environment for everyone. In case of doubts or need for information, it's possible to contact municipal services or competent authorities to receive assistance and support.
