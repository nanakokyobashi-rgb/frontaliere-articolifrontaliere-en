Request for new permit for cross-border commuters

Switzerland, in particular the Canton of Ticino, has introduced various rules to regulate cross-border work. Cross-border workers, i.e. those who work in Switzerland but reside abroad, must be informed of the procedures to follow.

Presence notification

According to Ticino law, cross-border commuters must notify their presence to the municipality where they spend the night in Switzerland. This means that they must go to the local municipality and provide the requested information. For example, if a cross-border worker resides in Lugano and works in Bellinzona, he must report to the Municipality of Bellinzona to notify his presence.

Notification of gainful activity

The start of any new gainful activity and change of workplace must be notified to the Migration Office. This is necessary to verify whether the cross-border worker possesses the necessary requirements to work in Switzerland. For example, if a cross-border worker starts working as a chef in a restaurant in Locarno, he must notify the gainful activity to the Migration Office.

Requirements for requesting permit G

To request the G permit, cross-border commuters must meet the following requirements:

  • Be at least 16 years of age
  • Have an employment contract stipulated with a Swiss employer
  • Have a bank or insurance guarantee for a sum of CHF 10'000.- (art. 12 of the Migration Regulation of 24 March 2007)
  • Have stable accommodation in Ticino
  • Not be subjected to expulsion measures or execution of a sentence
