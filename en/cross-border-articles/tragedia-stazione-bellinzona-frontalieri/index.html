On May 5, 2026, a tragedy occurred at Bellinzona station. The victim, a 33-year-old man with a Swiss passport and residing in Bellinzona, died from electrocution inside the railway station. The death was caused by electrocution inside the station. The formal identification of the victim was communicated in the afternoon by a spokesperson for the Polcantonale. The victim was not an employee or external collaborator of the Ffs or related companies. The rescuers of the 'Croce verde' Bellinzona, who arrived at the scene just before 10:00 PM, could do nothing to save the man's life. The presence of the victim in the area is under investigation, without it being possible to determine the dynamics of the event yet.
