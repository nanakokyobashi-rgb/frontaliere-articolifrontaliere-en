What is the New Frontier Agreement?

The New Frontier Agreement is an international agreement signed between Switzerland and Italy on 23 December 2020. This agreement aims to simplify the procedures for transferring frontier workers between the two countries. The new agreement enters into force on 1 January 2024 and replaces the previous agreement.

Benefits for frontier workers

Border crossers moving to Domaso can enjoy a number of benefits, including:

  • Access to the labour market : frontier workers can access the Swiss labour market without the need for a residence permit.
  • Employment law: frontier workers have the right to work in Switzerland and can be hired by a Swiss employer.
  • Taxes: Border crossers are subject to Swiss tax taxes and can benefit from a reduction in tax rates.
  • Social insurance: frontier workers are entitled to Swiss social benefits, such as medical care and pension.

Exemption of €7,500: how does it work?

To qualify for the €7,500 exemption, certain conditions must be met. Under the New Frontier Agreement, foreigners who move to Switzerland can obtain a removal allowance of €7,500. However, it is important to note that this allowance is subject to certain conditions and requirements.

Exemption requirements

To obtain the exemption of €7,500, you must:

  • be a frontier worker moving to Switzerland;
  • have a monthly income of at least CHF 2,500;
  • have an employment contract of at least 12 months;
  • have a stable accommodation in Switzerland;
  • be able to prove their identity and residence.
