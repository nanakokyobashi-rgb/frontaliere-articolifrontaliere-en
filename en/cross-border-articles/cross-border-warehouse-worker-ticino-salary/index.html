In a nutshell

The salaries of border warehouse workers in Ticino are influenced by Swiss and Italian legislation. The new agreement between Switzerland and Italy provides for an exemption for new frontier workers. The deductible is CHF 10,000.

How the deductible works

The deductible is a maximum amount of CHF 10,000 that new border warehousemen can earn without being subject to Italian taxes. This means that if a border warehouseman in Ticino earns less than CHF 10,000, he will not be obliged to pay Italian taxes.

For example, if a border warehouseman in Lugano earns CHF 8,000 per month, he will not be subject to Italian taxes. However, if you earn CHF 15,000 per month, you will be subject to Italian taxes and will have to pay the differences.

Swiss and Italian legislation

Swiss and Italian legislation regulates the salaries of border warehousemen in Ticino. Switzerland applies the rules of labour law, while Italy applies the rules of tax law.

According to Italian legislation (Presidential Decree no. 600 of 29 September 1973), border warehousemen are considered self-employed and are subject to Italian taxes. However, according to Swiss legislation (Federal Act on International Private Law, 18 December 1987), border warehousemen are considered employees and are subject to the rules of labour law.
