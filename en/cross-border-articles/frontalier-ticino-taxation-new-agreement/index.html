The regulations

The agreement signed on 23 December 2020 provides that cross-border commuter taxes are paid based on the principle of "fiscal residency". This means that workers who reside in Switzerland for more than 183 days per year will be taxed in Switzerland, while those who reside in Italy for less than 183 days per year will be taxed in Italy.
