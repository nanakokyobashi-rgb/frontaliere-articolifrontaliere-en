Incident Details

The accident occurred on the morning of Friday, August 18, in Via Monte Rosa, near Besnate station. The 68-year-old woman had been spotted falling from a railway bridge, probably while she was walking along the road. The helicopter was called quickly and the woman was transported to the Circolo hospital in red code.

Investigations in progress

Polfer in Milan and local police in Besnate are investigating the incident. No official statements have yet been issued about the incident, but it is suspected that the woman may have lost her balance while she was walking along the bridge.

Governing Law

The legislation in force provides that railway bridges must be equipped with safety barriers to prevent such accidents. However, it is unclear whether the bridge in question was equipped with such barriers or not.

The woman, who did not want to be identified, was sitting in first class and was not wearing the mandatory safety goggle for passengers over the age of 65.

Under Italian law, rail transport operators must ensure that passengers over the age of 65 wear the mandatory safety goggle.
