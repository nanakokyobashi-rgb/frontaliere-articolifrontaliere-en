Between the traffic to Milan, the increasingly difficult to find car parks and the daily movements of border workers to Switzerland, choosing a car today means above all finding the right balance between practicality and management costs.

In this context, the SUV continues to be highly appreciated, but it is not always the most suitable solution. For many drivers in the province of Varese, a used compact can offer everything you need, avoiding some often underestimated expenses. Before deciding, it is therefore worth thinking about your driving habits rather than simply following market trends.

Operational details

When the SUV remains the right choice

Those who accompany their children to school every day, often carry sports equipment or go on long trips with the whole family can appreciate the space offered by an SUV. The elevated driving position and the more spacious luggage compartment represent concrete advantages in many situations.

When not

For a commuter who mainly travels the Varese-Milan route or reaches the Canton of Ticino on a daily basis, however, a compact one can be more practical. Smaller dimensions make it easier to manoeuvre in city car parks, while generally smaller tyres and lower fuel consumption can help reduce the overall cost of use over the years.

Before buying it is better to look beyond the price

When evaluating a used car it's easy to focus solely on the sale price. In reality, the initial cost is only part of the expense.

Before signing the contract, it is useful to check:

  • Routine and extraordinary maintenance costs
  • Actual consumption according to your usage profile (city vs motorway)
  • Insurance costs and stamp duty
  • Availability and cost of spare parts
  • General condition of the car (mechanics, bodywork, interior)

For border crossers who cross passes such as Brogeda or Gaggiolo on a daily basis, these factors become even more relevant considering the additional wear and tear due to frequent start-ups and shutdowns in traffic

Key points

What to do before you buy

1. Define your usage profile: weekly kilometres, route type (city, motorway, mixed), number of regular passengers 2. Establish a realistic budget that includes not only the purchase price but also the annual running costs 3. Search for reliable used compact models in the segment (e.g. BMW 1 Series, Volkswagen Golf, Audi A3) 4. Verify the vehicle's history through certified coupons and actual mileage 5. Carry out a test drive that reproduces your conditions of daily use (city traffic, highway, narrow parking lots) 6. Have the vehicle checked by a trusted mechanic before purchase

After purchase

  • Keep a detailed record of consumption and maintenance costs
  • Respect coupon deadlines and revisions to maintain value over time
  • Consider installing an updated navigation system to avoid inefficient routes
  • Use the app to monitor fuel prices along the Varese-Milan-Ticino route
