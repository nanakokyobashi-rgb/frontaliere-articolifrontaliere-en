  • What: Average fuel prices

The Ministry of Business and Made in Italy has released updated data on the trend of fuel prices in Italy. The latest surveys carried out through the Observatory on fuel prices of the Mimit show particularly high rates for motorists who refuel along the country's road arteries. The situation directly affects all those who use the vehicle for daily travel and for journeys related to work activities. The tariff update provides a clear and timely picture of the average expenditure to be incurred for filling up with fuel in self-service mode. The competent authorities constantly monitor the evolution of the price lists to ensure maximum transparency for road users. The analysis of official data allows us to understand the immediate economic impact on family budgets and transport expenses related to mobility

Operational details

The analysis of refueling costs is crucial for managing transportation expenses, especially for those who travel many kilometers daily to get to work. The difference in price between the ordinary road network and the highway network requires a greater attention to the choice of the point where to refuel with gasoline or diesel. For example, stopping at a highway service station results in a higher expense, with self-service gasoline costing 2,069 euros per liter and diesel reaching 2,249 euros per liter. On the other hand, opting for service stations along the national road network allows for a partial reduction in costs, although the general levels remain high at 1,979 euros per liter for gasoline and 2,180 euros per liter for diesel. For those who use a car daily, carefully evaluating where to fill up can generate a significant monthly savings on transportation expenses.

The fluctuations in fuel prices directly affect the costs of daily commuting for those living near the border and facing long road trips. To optimize travel expenses, it is advisable to monitor the tariffs applied by different service stations along the usual routes, using service stations on the ordinary network before entering the highways. Careful management of the budget allocated to fuel requires a meticulous planning of refueling, avoiding as much as possible service stations on the highways with higher tariffs. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the current average prices of gasoline in self-service mode?
The average price of petrol in self-service mode along the national road network is 1,979 euros per litre, while on the motorway network it rises to 2,069 euros per litre, according to data communicated by the Observatory on the prices of the Mimit.
How much does diesel cost on average along the road and motorway network?
The average price of diesel in self-service mode on the national road network stands at 2,180 euros per litre, while along the motorway network it reaches 2,249 euros per litre.
Which institution has reported this fuel price data?
The average data relating to fuel prices were released by the Ministry of Business and Made in Italy, based on the findings made by its Observatory on fuel prices.
