Accident in Camorino, woman injured near the A2

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 October 2026

After losing control on a bend, the car hit a median barrier and overturned. The southbound entrance to the A2 remained closed for more than an hour.

Context

In brief

  • Accident at the Camorino interchange toward the southbound A2
  • Woman injured after crashing into a traffic divider
  • The car overturned and came to rest on its roof
  • Southbound entrance closed for over an hour

Key facts

  • Location: Camorino motorway interchange
  • Direction: A2 southbound
  • Injured woman: transported to the emergency department of Ospedale Civico di Lugano
  • Emergency services: Croce Verde, firefighters and cantonal police
  • Closure: over an hour

How the accident happened

The accident occurred this afternoon in Camorino, at the motorway interchange leading onto the A2 in the southbound direction. The person involved is a woman, who reportedly sustained moderately serious injuries. The initial information gathered at the scene by Rescue Media describes a loss of control while the vehicle was negotiating a bend.

For reasons yet to be established, the vehicle left the road and violently hit a traffic divider. The impact caused the car to overturn, and it came to rest on its roof. The account available in the initial reconstructions stops at these elements: the reason for the loss of control is not indicated.

The southbound motorway entrance was closed by the cantonal police. The measure affected access to the A2 at the point where operations were taking place. The woman was extracted from the vehicle and stabilized before being transferred to the emergency department of Ospedale Civico di Lugano.

The closure was maintained for over an hour to facilitate rescue operations and the recovery of the car. The case therefore combines the reconstruction of the vehicle leaving the road with the activities carried out at the interchange and the subsequent medical care provided to the injured woman.
