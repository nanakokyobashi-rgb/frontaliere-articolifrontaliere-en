Trains stopped between Arbedo-Castione and Biasca: inconvenience (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 31 August 2026

Interruption on Monday around noon on the Arbedo-Castione–Biasca route: SBB report "accident per person", trains cancelled.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Rail traffic blocked between Arbedo-Castione and Biasca
  • Cause: "incident per person" reported by the SBB
  • Interruption reported on Monday around noon
  • Discomfort expected for the next few hours on the line

Key facts

  • What: Interruption of railway connections on the Arbedo-Castione-Biasca section
  • When: Monday, around noon
  • Where: Arbedo-Castione-Biasca railway section (Canton of Ticino)
  • Who: SBB (Swiss Federal Railways), as reported by the online portal
  • Cause: Incident per person, as indicated on the SBB portal
  • Effect: Connections "deleted" and inconveniences expected for the next few hours

Rail traffic on the line connecting Arbedo-Castione to Biasca went haywire in the early afternoon of today, Monday. This was reported by the SBB online portal, which reported the interruption of rail connections on the route. The reason for the blockade is "one accident per person", as stated in the note published by the Swiss Federal Railways.

The incident was recorded in the midday band and resulted in the suppression of trains between the two stations. SBB reported that disruptions to rail traffic are expected to continue in the hours following the report, without at the moment providing a precise forecast on the resumption of regular service. The official note merely indicates the interruption and indicates that delays and cancellations are to be expected

Operational details

Possible impact on commuters

The Arbedo-Castione-Biasca section is traveled daily by commuters who travel between Sopraceneri and Bellinzonese for work or study reasons. The midday service disruption coincided with a time slot where many workers are on lunch breaks or commuting, while others use the train to return to residential areas.

The SBB note does not specify whether substitute bus services have been provided on the route concerned. In the absence of a ferry bus service, those who had to travel between the two locations were faced with two concrete options: wait for the resumption of the train service, communicated as uncertain in the following hours, or fold back on their own vehicle or on alternative routes by road. The Gotthard motorway tunnel and the cantonal road represent the road routes parallel to the railway line, but offer very different timing and frequencies compared to the train.

Key points

What to do if you are involved

Those who were travelling or had to leave between Arbedo-Castione and Biasca at the time of the interruption can follow some practical instructions to manage the discomfort. Before moving to the station, it is advisable to check the status of the connections through the SBB portal, the official app or the information panels in the stations, where the service notices are published in real time.

Secondly, it is worth considering the available transport alternatives: replacement buses that may be activated by SBB, public transport by road (post) that serves the same route, or the use of one's own vehicle, taking into account road travel times and the possible congestion of the motorway section.

For those who use the train regularly for work reasons, it is useful to keep the ticket and the documentation of the interruption: in the event of prolonged delays or cancellations, SBB provides for forms of compensation or reimbursement according to the conditions of transport. Requests can be made through SBB customer service or the contact points at the main stations.

How to track updates

The official channel to follow the evolution of the situation is the SBB online portal, where the news was publicly reported. On the same platform, the estimated recovery times and the possible activation of replacement buses are gradually updated.

What is the cause of the railway interruption between Arbedo-Castione and Biasca?

This led to the cancellation of trains and prolonged inconvenience for several hours on the route, without a precise forecast on the resumption of regular service.
When and where did the train interruption occur in the Canton of Ticino?
The interruption of rail connections took place on Monday, around noon, on the Arbedo-Castione-Biasca section in the Canton of Ticino. This line connects the Bellinzona area to the Leventina, passing through Arbedo-Castione, a major junction, and Biasca, the first major centre after Bellinzona to the north.
What are the transport alternatives for commuters between Arbedo-Castione and Biasca in the event of an outage?

Road alternatives include the Gotthard motorway tunnel and the cantonal road, but offer very different times and frequencies than the train. It is advisable to check SBB updates in real time.
What kind of information does SBB provide in the event of an 'accident per person' and what is missing?
SBB uses the standard formula 'accident per person' to report the event, indicating the cause, the approximate time (around noon), the section involved and the consequences (cancelled connections, disruption). No details are provided about the identity of the person, their condition, the exact location of the accident, nor a precise estimate of the recovery time.
