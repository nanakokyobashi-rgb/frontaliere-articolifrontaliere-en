Requirements to work as a mechanic in Switzerland

According to Swiss federal law, the requirements for working as a mechanic in Switzerland are as follows:

  • Vocational school diploma or equivalent
  • Work experience of at least two years as a mechanic
  • Knowledge of German or French (depending on the region where you work)
  • Valid residence permit for the job

Permit G: the permission to work in Switzerland without paying Italian taxes

The G permit is a type of residence permit that allows foreign workers to work in Switzerland without having to pay Italian taxes. To obtain the G permit, you must meet the following requirements:

  • Be a citizen of an EU country or a country with a free trade agreement with Switzerland
  • Have an employment contract with a Swiss employer
  • Have a monthly income of at least CHF 4,500 (approximately EUR 4,200)
  • Not having a tax income in Italy or in another country
