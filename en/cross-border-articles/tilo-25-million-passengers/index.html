TILO reaches 25 million passengers in 2025

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 5 March 2026

The TILO railway company transported 25 million people in 2025, with a 3.7% increase compared to 2024

Context

TL;DR

  • TILO transported 25 million passengers in 2025, a 3.7% increase from 2024.
  • Passenger numbers have more than doubled since 2016, reaching 25 million in 2025.
  • Lugano, Bellinzona, and Chiasso were the most frequented stations in 2025.
  • TILO plans to reach 30 million passengers by 2030 with infrastructure investments.

Key facts

  • Passengers 2025: 25 million
  • Increase 2024-2025: 3.7%
  • Passengers 2016: 12.3 million
  • Top station 2025: Lugano (4.2 million passengers)
  • New infrastructure: Mendrisio-Varese Railway (2018), Ceneri Base Tunnel (2020)
  • Future goal: 30 million passengers by 2030
  • Investment 2025-2030: 120 million Swiss francs
  • Lugano-Bellinzona investment: 40 million Swiss francs

The TILO railway company has reached a new record in 2025, transporting 25 million passengers. This represents a 3.7% increase compared to the 24.1 million in 2024. The rail traffic in the Ticino region continues to grow, thanks to the expansion of the offer and the introduction of new infrastructure such as the Mendrisio-Varese Railway (FMV) in 2018 and the Ceneri Base Tunnel (CBT) in 2020. The number of passengers has more than doubled compared to the 12.3 million in 2016. The increase in passenger traffic has been constant in recent years, with a 2.5% increase in 2022 and 3.2% in 2023. This positive trend has been favored by the increase in train frequency and the introduction of new routes, such as the Lugano-Mendrisio-Varese line, which recorded a 15% increase in passengers in 2025 compared to the previous year. According to TILO's data, the most frequented stations in 2025 were Lugano (4.2 million passengers), Bellinzona (2.5 million), and Chiasso (2.1 million). The Lugano station recorded a 5% increase in passengers compared to 2024, also thanks to the introduction of new connection services to Milan-Malpensa airport. The expansion of the railway offer has also led to an increase in the use of public transport by commuters. According to a survey conducted in 2023, 75% of commuters who travel to work in Switzerland use the train as their main means of transport. The Chiasso-Zurich line was one of the most frequented, with over 10,000 commuters per day. The CBT, inaugurated in 2020, has significantly contributed to the increase in passenger traffic, reducing travel times between Ticino and the rest of Switzerland. The tunnel has also led to an increase in transport capacity, allowing more trains to run simultaneously. TILO has also invested in the modernization of infrastructure and the digitization of services. In 2022, the company introduced a new electronic ticketing system, which recorded a 20% increase in online ticket sales in the first year of use. For the future, TILO plans to continue expanding its railway offer and improving services for passengers. Investments are planned for the modernization of stations and the introduction of new trains. The company is also working to improve integration with other public transport modes, such as buses and trams.

Operational details

The increase in rail traffic in Ticino has been constant in recent years, confirming the positive trend already observed earlier. In 2019, TILO transported 16.7 million passengers, while in 2025 the number rose to 25 million, representing a 50% increase in six years. The daily average of passengers transported by TILO is around 76,400 people, with peaks recorded during morning and evening rush hours. This increase is also due to the introduction of new stops and the improvement of services. For example, the opening of the new Lugano Paradiso stop in 2022 increased the frequency of trains and reduced travel times between nearby locations. Furthermore, the introduction of the cadenced timetable has made the service more predictable and reliable, encouraging more people to use public transport. The new Lugano Paradiso stop recorded an average of 1,200 passengers per day in the first half of 2023, significantly contributing to the overall increase in passengers. Another factor that contributed to the increase in passengers was the adjustment of tariffs and commercial offers. TILO introduced a new system of monthly and annual subscriptions in 2023, which is more convenient for commuters, with an average saving of 15% compared to previous tariffs. The data show that the municipalities most served by TILO, such as Lugano, Bellinzona, and Chiasso, have recorded the largest increases in the number of passengers. In particular, the Lugano-Bellinzona line saw a 60% increase in the number of daily passengers compared to 2019. To manage the increase in traffic, TILO has implemented a series of operational measures, including the introduction of more frequent security checks and the training of additional personnel for the management of trains and stations. - More frequent security checks - Additional personnel for the management of trains and stations - Improvement of station infrastructure. For the future, TILO plans to continue improving its service offer, with the introduction of new trains and the modernization of existing infrastructure. The goal is to reach 30 million passengers by 2030, maintaining its position as a leader in public transport in Ticino. The Swiss Confederation has allocated 120 million Swiss francs for the improvement of the railway infrastructure in Ticino for the period 2025-2030, of which 40 million are specifically dedicated to the strengthening of the Lugano-Bellinzona railway line. These additional resources will allow TILO to accelerate its development plans and respond effectively to the growing demand for public transport in the region.

Frequently Asked Questions
How many passengers did TILO carry in 2025 and what was the increase compared to 2024?
TILO carried 25 million passengers in 2025, an increase of 3.7% compared to 24.1 million in 2024.
What has been the impact of the Ceneri Base Tunnel (CBT) on TILO's passenger growth?
The CBT, inaugurated in 2020, has contributed significantly to the increase in passengers, reducing travel times and increasing transport capacity, allowing more trains at the same time.
How can I get a discounted TILO travelcard for cross-border commuters between Switzerland and Italy?
TILO offers monthly and annual passes with an average discount of 15% compared to standard commuter fares. To obtain it, it is necessary to present an employment contract or a certificate of residence proving daily transit between Italy and Ticino. Passes can be purchased online or at the ticket offices of the stations.
Which TILO stations are most frequented by cross-border commuters between Italy and Switzerland?
The stations most frequented by cross-border commuters are Chiasso (2.1 million passengers in 2025), Lugano (4.2 million) and Bellinzona (2.5 million). The Chiasso-Zurich route registers over 10,000 commuters a day, making it one of the most used for those working in Switzerland.
What safety measures have TILO introduced to handle the increase in passengers?
TILO implemented more frequent security checks, additional staff for train and station management, and improved station infrastructure. These measures aim to ensure the safety and efficiency of the service, especially during peak hours.
