PizzAutobus in Varese: four autistic people at work

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 October 2026

On 3 ottobre Piazza Monte Grappa in Varese hosts the first outing of the PizzAutobus, the food truck of Abad cooperativa sociale with four autistic people at work, on the occasion of the Granfondo Tre Valli Varesine.

On 3 ottobre Piazza Monte Grappa in Varese will host the first outing of PizzAutobus Varese, the new service launched by Abad cooperativa sociale. The debut is scheduled for the Granfondo Tre Valli Varesine, the first opportunity to see the vehicle and meet the work team.

The project grew out of a collaboration with PizzAut and support from Thea Group, which purchased the vehicle intended for the initiative. The offering is a food truck to be hosted at a company, at a school, or during local initiatives. According to Abad, pizza preparation and service become a concrete opportunity for work and interaction.

A traveling service with an employment purpose

From that date, the PizzAutobus will operate in the province of Varese. Interested companies, schools and local organizations will be able to contact Abad to learn about the proposal and discuss the possibility of hosting it. The cooperative brings to the project more than thirty years of experience in the employment inclusion of people with disabilities and vulnerabilities, described as the foundational reason for its commitment.

The new service expands this experience through mobile catering. The project provides for the employment of four people with autism spectrum disorder, working on the truck under the guidance of a supervisor. The vehicle will therefore be a workplace in which to develop skills, take on responsibilities and interact with the public.

The PizzAutobus's presence in different settings will also help people get to know the project through direct experience of the service. For President Giuseppe Di Carlo, the collaboration with PizzAut stems from a strong affinity of purpose:

«For Abad – says President Giuseppe Di Carlo – the collaboration with PizzAut stems from a strong affinity of purpose: the belief that work is one of the fundamental tools of social integration and personal fulfillment».

Key points

How to go about hosting the truck

For an organization interested in hosting the PizzAutobus, the source outlines an essential operational process: start with the type of venue, ask Abad for information, and agree on the arrangements. Contact with the cooperative is the starting point.

Four steps indicated by the news item

1. Identify the setting. The proposal is aimed at companies, schools and local organizations; the truck can be hosted at a company, at a school or as part of local initiatives.

2. Contact Abad. Requests can be sent to ABAD cooperativa sociale at pizzautobus.varese@abadcoop.it or to 0332 948473.

3. Ask for the proposal. Abad indicates that interested organizations can learn about the service and agree on the possibilities for hosting the vehicle.

4. If it is a school, also ask about the disability-awareness program intended for this audience.

The date to keep in mind is the one announced for the debut: 3 ottobre, at Piazza Monte Grappa in Varese, during the Granfondo Tre Valli Varesine. The event is the first opportunity to learn about PizzAutobus Varese and the working group. After that date, the service will continue in the province of Varese.

For a cross-border worker who wants to involve their company, school or a local organization, the concrete step is not to look for a rule concerning Canton Ticino, but to use the contact details provided and open a discussion about hosting. In fact, the source links the continuity of the service to building collaborations capable of supporting new employment opportunities.

The contact also serves to keep the two aspects separate: on the one hand, a mobile catering and employment-inclusion project; on the other, the personal matters of those who work across the border. For the latter, use calcolatore fiscale.

Frequently Asked Questions
When and where does the PizzAutobus debut?
The debut of the PizzAutobus is scheduled for October 3 in Varese, precisely in Piazza Monte Grappa. The event takes place on the occasion of the Granfondo Tre Valli Varesine, a cycling race that attracts participants and spectators in the province.
Who manages the service and how many people with autism are employed?
The service is managed by the social cooperative Abbot, who has over thirtyyears of experience in the employment of people with disabilities and frailties. Four people with autism spectrum disorder work on the truck, accompanied by a manager, to develop skills and take responsibility.
How can local companies, schools or businesses host the PizzAutobus?
Interested entities must identify the context (company, school or territorial initiative), then contact Abad at pizzautobus.varese@abadcoop.it or on 0332 948473 to request the proposal and agree on the methods of hospitality. Schools may also request information on the disability awareness pathway.
