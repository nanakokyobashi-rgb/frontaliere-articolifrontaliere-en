In 51% of cases, an absence from work related to a mental illness ends with the termination of the contract. And still 51% represents today, in Ticino, the share of beneficiaries of an AI pension for psychiatric reasons, compared to 33% just over twentyyears ago. Two numbers that show how mental discomfort has become one of the central issues in the world of work.

The theme was at the center of a conference organized in Mendrisio da Compasso, where it emerged that mental health is less and less a taboo, but where the early recognition of signs of difficulty remains crucial.

"You lose contacts, you lose the habit of working," Marzio Proietti, vice president of Compasso, explains to the RSI Newspaper. For this reason, he stresses, it is important to encourage a gradual return, without putting pressure on the employee and also providing for trial periods.

Practical analysis: what changes for workers and companies

The two key figures — 51% of contract terminations due to mental health absences and 51% of disability insurance beneficiaries for psychiatric reasons — are no coincidence. They represent a significant increase compared to the 33% recorded twenty years ago, indicating that mental distress is becoming a central issue in the Swiss labor market.

The human and business cost

The cost is not only for those who suffer: we companies risk losing collaborators, experience, and skills. And when an absence is prolonged, the chances of an effective reintegration decrease. This is particularly relevant for cross-border workers, who commute daily between Italy and Switzerland, where every day of absence can compromise the work routine.

For workers with G or B permits, prolonged absenteeism can have direct consequences on access to the LAMal health insurance and disability insurance coverage. Early recognition of signals therefore becomes a systemic issue, not just an individual one.

Risk factors and burnout

The factors that can foster stress conditions up to burnout are varied: lack of recognition and appreciation, a difficult relationship with superiors or colleagues, a non-serene work environment, and the absence of a clear separation between professional and private life. According to Renato Dotti, president of the Forum Gestione Salute in Azienda, leadership is needed that is first and foremost capable of listening to the needs of collaborators.

Frequently Asked Questions
How many absences for mental illness in Ticino end with the end of the contract?
According to the data that emerged from the Compasso conference in Mendrisio, in 51% of cases an absence from work linked to a mental illness ends with the termination of the contract. This data highlights the criticality of managing prolonged absences related to mental health.
How has the share of AI pension beneficiaries for psychiatric reasons changed in Ticino?
Today in Ticino 51% of the beneficiaries of an AI annuity have psychiatric reasons. This percentage is growing strongly compared to the 33% recorded just over twentyyears ago, indicating a significant increase in pathologies related to stress and mental discomfort in the workplace.
What is the priority of disability insurance (AI) in case of absences?
The priority of AI at the Swiss level is professional reintegration, as Monica Maestri Crivelli, head of the Disability Insurance Office, recalls. The objective is to identify at-risk situations early to recover or maintain the person's resources, preventing absenteeism from becoming a structural problem.
