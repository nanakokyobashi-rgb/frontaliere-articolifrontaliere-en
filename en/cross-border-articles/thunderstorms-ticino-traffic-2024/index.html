Severe thunderstorms in Lugano: hail slows traffic (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 7 May 2026

MeteoSwiss has issued a level 3 alert for thunderstorms in Lugano, with a risk of landslides, flooding, and slowed traffic.

Context

In brief

  • Thunderstorm alert level 3 in Lugano area
  • Risk of landslides, flooding, and hail damage
  • Traffic slowed down in Grancia area

Key facts

  • What: Thunderstorm alert level 3
  • When: 18 hours ago
  • Where: Lugano area, Grancia zone
  • Who: MeteoSwiss, ViaSuisse
  • Amount: Expected accumulations between 50 and 80 mm

MeteoSwiss has issued an alert level 3 – significant danger – for thunderstorms in the Lugano area. The main risks include landslides on steep slopes, sudden flash floods in streams, and flooding. Additionally, there is a possibility of hail damage, broken branches, uprooted trees, and lightning strikes. The situation is critical, with specific recommendations for those outdoors.

Risks and recommendations

In the presence of lightning, it is advised to assume a crouched position with feet together, minimizing contact with the ground. Avoid watercourses, exposed locations such as peaks and ridges, trees, poles or towers, as well as open areas like clearings and soccer fields. These precautions are fundamental to reducing the risk of accidents during thunderstorms.

Impact on traffic

According to ViaSuisse, highway traffic in the Grancia area is currently slowed down due to a hailstorm. This slowdown can significantly impact commuters and cross-border workers traveling daily between Italy and Switzerland. It is important to monitor traffic updates and plan for potential delays.

Alert level 2 for rain

In addition to the thunderstorm alert, MeteoSwiss has issued an alert level 2 – moderate danger – for rain. Although the danger is moderate, the rain could cause an increase in the level of streams if the soil is already saturated. The expected accumulations are between 50 and 80 mm, with the snowfall limit at 2,100 meters, lower than previous forecasts.

Practical analysis: implications for cross-border workers

Torrential rains and hailstorms can have a significant impact on the daily lives of cross-border workers in Ticino. Beyond traffic delays, it's important to consider the practical implications of these extreme weather conditions.

Travel planning

For cross-border workers in Ticino, planning travel is crucial. In case of weather alerts, it's advisable to check the forecasts before setting off. Using navigation apps that provide real-time traffic updates can help avoid delays and plan alternative routes.

Emergency preparedness

Being prepared for emergencies is essential. An emergency kit should include water, non-perishable food, a flashlight, a portable phone charger, and a first aid kit. Additionally, it's useful to have up-to-date information on traffic and weather conditions.

Impact on work

Traffic delays can affect punctuality at work. It's important to communicate promptly with the employer in case of delays due to adverse weather conditions. Some companies might offer remote work options in case of weather emergencies, which can be a useful solution for cross-border workers.

Insurance and coverage

Checking your insurance is another important aspect. Auto and health insurance should cover any damages or accidents caused by extreme weather conditions. Additionally, it's useful to know the procedures for claiming reimbursements in case of damage to your car or home.

Conclusion

Extreme weather conditions can have a significant impact on the daily lives of cross-border workers. Planning travel, being prepared for emergencies, communicating with the employer, and checking your insurance are fundamental steps to best cope with these situations.
