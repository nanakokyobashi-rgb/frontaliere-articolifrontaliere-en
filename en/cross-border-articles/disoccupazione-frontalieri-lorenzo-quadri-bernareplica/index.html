National Councillor Lorenzo Quadri expressed his clear opinion on the reform of the law on G permits, which could entail additional costs for Switzerland. Quadri explained that Switzerland may have to increase wage levies on Swiss workers, which could have a significant impact on their ability to support their families.

"It is absurd that tomorrow, in addition to the refreshes, Switzerland was also called upon to finance the unemployment of G-permit holders," Quadri said. "The Canton of Ticino would be forced to strengthen at its own expense the Regional Placement Offices, to cope with the registrations of G permits. This would mean increased costs for the Canton and for Switzerland as a whole. ”

The National Councillor also highlighted that the reform could have a negative impact on the competitiveness of the Ticino economy. "The Canton of Ticino is already one of Switzerland's main trade hubs and the reform could compromise its competitive position," Quadri said.
