Trenord, new increases on tickets for border crossers (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 21 July 2026

Trenord has announced a further increase in rail fares. The new 'Malpensa specific rate' has been postponed to next summer.

Context

In brief

  • Trenord has confirmed a new increase in ticket prices.
  • The 'Malpensa specific tariff' has been postponed to next summer.
  • No immediate changes for standard transfrontier connections.

Key facts

  • What: Increase in Trenord railway ticket fares
  • When: Recent communication with summer postponement for Malpensa
  • Who: Trenord
  • Where: Regional routes and airport connections

Daily commuters who use Trenord's railway services for their daily trips are faced with an additional revision of the cost of travel tickets. The railway company, which manages regional transport in Lombardy, has indeed officially confirmed a new price increase, a dynamic that directly affects the planning of expenses for those who, residing in Italy, cross the border daily to reach their workplace in the Canton of Ticino or use the Lombard lines for intermodal connections.

Managing the Malpensa tariff

A particularly relevant aspect of the communication concerns the so-called 'Malpensa specific tariff'. Initially expected in the past months, the introduction of this new tariff regime dedicated to connections to the airport has been officially postponed to next summer. This delay represents a temporary reprieve for travelers who integrate the railway journey with international flights, but does not exclude the possibility that the revision of regional tariffs remains fully operational. For cross-border workers, who often evaluate the total cost of commuting based on the prices of public integrated transport, the uncertainty related to the timing of these changes requires constant attention to official information channels. The company has not yet provided additional details on the exact structure of the new increases, limiting itself to confirming the intention to proceed with the planned tariff revision.

Operational details

The management of the monthly budget for a frontier worker inevitably passes through the analysis of transport costs, often combined with other cross-border rail routes. Trenord's announcement is part of an operational cost review framework affecting regional public transport. For those who travel regularly between the Lombard provinces and the centres of the Canton of Ticino, the increase in prices requires a revision of the purchasing habits of travel tickets.

Analysis of the impact on commuting

If in the past the cost of rail tickets could be considered a fixed variable, recent market dynamics indicate greater volatility. The average border worker, who often also has to bear car-related costs to reach the interchange stations, finds himself having to calculate precisely the impact of these increases on his net salary. It is essential to monitor not only the cost of the single ticket, but also the convenience of any monthly or quarterly subscriptions that could protect, at least partially, from future sudden increases.

Moreover, the distinction between the standard regional routes and the future 'Malpensa specific tariff' creates two different levels of expenditure. Those who use the train to reach Ticino for professional reasons should check whether the announced changes affect the routes of their relevance or if it is an adjustment focused on connections

Key points

To better deal with tariff increases, a strategy of constant monitoring of Trenord's official communications is necessary. As the technical details of the specific application for each section have not yet been defined, the most correct procedure for the worker is to verify, before each monthly purchase, any change in the rates applied on their reference line.

Verification and savings procedure

The first action to be taken is the systematic control of the official website of the railway manager, where the updated tariff tables are published. Secondly, it is advisable to evaluate the subscription of annual subscriptions or prepaid solutions, which in many cases allow you to lock in the price at the time of purchase, repairing the user from any new increases during the year. For frontier workers, this' cost lock-in 'strategy can be a key element in keeping monthly departures stable.

In addition, the integration between the Lombard and Swiss transport systems should be considered. Often, the use of supra-regional or affiliated subscriptions can mitigate the impact of individual price increases. Although the fare situation is being defined, it is essential not to wait until the last moment to purchase the ticket. Frequently Asked Questions
When will the 'Malpensa specific tariff' come into effect?
The introduction of the 'Malpensa specific tariff' has been postponed to next summer. No more precise dates have been provided at this time, so please check back regularly for official updates from Trenord as we approach the summer season.
Do the increases also concern the rail routes to Ticino?
The source indicates a general increase in ticket prices by Trenord. Although the 'Malpensa specific fare' has been postponed, fare adjustments on regional lines could have a direct impact on commuters using the train to reach the Swiss border.
What can I do to mitigate the impact of these price increases?
The best strategy is to monitor the official channels for price changes and evaluate the purchase of long-term (annual) subscriptions, which usually allow the rate to be blocked at the time of subscription, protecting the worker from any future increases during the year.
