Operational details

Sanctions for work safety in Comasco: 18 companies suspended

The investigations have revealed numerous violations of current legislation on health and safety in the workplace. These include the failure to draw up the Risk Assessment Document, the Operational Safety Plan and the Plan for the Assembly, Use and Disassembly of scaffolding, the irregular application of the Safety and Coordination Plan (PSC), the use of non-conforming scaffolding, ladders and equipment, the failure to prepare provisional works and suitable protections against the risk of falling from a height, as well as further organisational and documentary deficiencies.

According to the findings of the investigations, the 18 suspended companies committed serious and repeated violations. For example, one of the companies, active in the construction of a building in Bellinzona, did not draw up the Risk Assessment Document, as required by current legislation (Law 81/2008). This resulted in a high risk for workers, who were exposed to risks of falling from a height and injury caused by non-compliant equipment.

In particular, the investigations found that the company did not provide adequate protection against the risk of falling from a height, despite the fact that it was a significant work, such as the construction of a balcony on a 5-storey building. Sanctions for work safety in Comasco: 18 companies suspended

The supervision led to the adoption of 18 measures of suspension of business activity, the complaint to the Judicial Authority of 42 people, including business owners, administrators and coordinators of the works, the contestation of criminal fines for 583,108.00 euros and administrative sanctions for 174,105.00 euros, for a total amount of 757,213.00 euros.

According to the information provided by the Supervisory Authority, in 2022 456 companies were verified in Como, with a response rate of 4%. This means that out of 100 verified businesses, 4 have been suspended or reported.

Suspension measures were taken for violations related to occupational safety, such as lack of adequate safety systems, non-compliance with safety regulations and lack of employee training.

Some concrete examples of suspended companies were:

  • Costruzioni Srl of Bellinzona, suspended for 3 months for not having complied with the safety regulations relating to work at height.
  • La Manutenzione SRL of Lugano, reported to the Judicial Authority for failing to provide employees with adequate personal protective equipment.
  • Costruzioni di Locarno SRL, suspended for 2 months for failing to comply with safety regulations relating to work in hazardous environments.

Frequently Asked Questions
What were the results of the recent work safety checks in Comasco?
The Carabinieri of Como carried out 18 checks on 17 companies, verifying 87 workers. Numerous health and safety violations were found, leading to sanctions totalling €757,213.00 and the suspension of 18 companies for serious irregularities.
What types of security breaches were found during inspections in Comasco?
Violations included the omission of essential documents such as the Risk Assessment Document (DVR), the Operational Safety Plan (POS) and the Scaffolding Assembly, Use and Disassembly Plan (PIMUS), the use of non-compliant equipment, and the lack of protections against falls from heights.
Have Swiss companies operating in Comasco been involved in security checks?
Yes, among the suspended companies there are also companies based in Bellinzona, Lugano and Chiasso, active in the construction sector and the production of construction materials. This highlights the attention of the Italian authorities also towards cross-border companies operating in the Italian territory.
What sanctions have been imposed on companies that have violated safety regulations in Comasco?
The 18 suspended companies received significant penalties, including the suspension of activity for 6 months and the payment of a fine of CHF 50,000 each. General penalties for violations can range from 100 to 10,000 euros, with a maximum of 250,000 francs according to Law 81/2008.
What key measures should construction companies take to ensure safety at work?
The Supervisory Committee has drawn up a checklist that includes the verification of a safety and emergency plan, an internal safety organisation, a monitoring system, adequate training and information for workers, and the use of adequate personal protection.
