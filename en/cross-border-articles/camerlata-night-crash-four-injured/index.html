At 1:35 AM, during the night, the alarm was raised in Piazzale Camerlata, Como. The cause of the accident, in which a car violently impacted an obstacle, is still unclear.

There were four young people on board: two 22-year-old women and two men, aged 24 and 25. The text does not provide names or other personal details.

Rescue and transfers

Rescue arrived promptly. A medical car and two ambulances responded to the scene, along with firefighters and police officers, who were present to carry out the necessary assessments. After initial treatment, the four injured were transported with yellow code injuries, defined by the source as medium severity, to the Sant'Anna hospitals in Como and Cantù.

The news reports that none of them are in life-threatening danger. The wording remains conservative, while law enforcement is working to reconstruct the exact circumstances of the incident. The text does not specify what the obstacle was, how the impact occurred, or what preceded the crash.
