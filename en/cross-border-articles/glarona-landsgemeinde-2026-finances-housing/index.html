Glarus Landsgemeinde 2026: tax rate and housing at heart of debates (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 May 2026

The Glarus Landsgemeinde voted on financial and housing issues. Tax rate confirmed at 58%, new deductions for commuters and health insurance subsidies.

Context

In brief

  • 58% tax rate confirmed for 2027
  • Travel deductions up to 6000 CHF for commuters
  • Rejected proposal for moderate-rent housing

Key facts

  • What: Glarus Landsgemeinde vote
  • When: May 3, 2026
  • Where: Canton Glarus, Switzerland
  • Who: Glarus citizens with voting rights
  • Amount: 58% tax rate, 6000 CHF travel deduction

The Landsgemeinde of the canton of Glarus, held as tradition dictates on the first Sunday in May, saw citizens gathered to vote on various proposals regarding finances and housing policy. The event, held on May 3, 2026, lasted four hours and involved all eligible voters, i.e., those who have reached the age of 16.

Among the main topics addressed, the confirmation of the 58% tax rate for the coming year stands out, despite the difficult situation of the cantonal coffers. This decision was made to ensure financial stability in a period of economic uncertainty. In addition, a savings package was approved that includes new deductions for commuters, who will be able to deduct up to 6000 francs for travel expenses, a novelty compared to the previous situation where there was no limit.

Decisions on housing and subsidies

The proposal to eliminate reimbursements for apprentices' trips to school was rejected, as was the initiative of the Liberal Greens aimed at increasing the share of moderate-rent housing. The Liberal Greens' proposal provided for the drafting of a law within four years, but it was defeated with the argument that municipalities already have the necessary tools to assign land to social housing entities.

A project presented by the government and parliament, however, obtained approval: it concerns improvements relating to subsidies for health insurance premiums. In Glarus, no one will have to spend more than 15% of their available income on this item and those entitled to the reduction will receive it automatically, without having to apply.

New measures for farmers and elections

Good news also for farmers who lose animals due to predator attacks. They will receive compensation not only when it is clearly proven that the aggression was caused by a wolf, but also in cases where this is highly probable. At the beginning of the Landsgemeinde, Markus Heer (PS) was elected as Landamman, i.e., the president of the cantonal government, replacing Kaspar Becker (Centre), whose two-year term expired today.

Glarus, together with Appenzell Innerrhoden, is the only canton to maintain the Landsgemeinde, a form of direct democracy that is over 600 years old: it was held for the first time in the distant 1387.

Operational details

Implications for cross-border workers

The decisions made during the Glarus Landsgemeinde have important implications for cross-border workers employed in the canton. The confirmation of the 58% tax rate means that cross-border workers will continue to pay this percentage on their income, with no changes compared to the previous year. This is an important factor to consider in financial planning, especially for those living in Italy and working in Switzerland.

New deductions for commuters

The possibility of deducting up to 6,000 francs for travel expenses represents a significant advantage for commuters. This change allows partially offsetting the costs incurred for daily travel between the place of residence and the workplace. For cross-border workers who regularly cross the Italian-Swiss border, this deduction can make a noticeable difference in the family budget.

Health insurance subsidies

The introduction of automatic subsidies for health insurance premiums is another important novelty. Cross-border workers residing in Italy and working in Switzerland often face high healthcare costs. The decision to limit health insurance premium spending to 15% of available income and to make subsidies automatic is a significant step towards greater equity and accessibility to healthcare services.

Moderate-rent housing

The rejection of the proposal to increase the quota of moderate-rent housing could have a negative impact on cross-border workers seeking affordable housing. However, as emphasized during the vote, municipalities already have the necessary tools to assign land to social housing entities. This means that, despite the rejection of the proposal, there may still be opportunities to purchase or rent housing at moderate rents, especially if municipalities decide to use the tools at their disposal effectively.

Compensation for farmers

The decision to extend compensation to farmers who lose animals due to predator attacks, even in cases where the attack is only highly probable, is a positive signal for the agricultural sector. This could have an indirect impact on cross-border workers employed in agriculture or with activities related to the agricultural sector, offering greater economic security and stability.

Key points

What to do for cross-border workers

For cross-border workers employed in the canton of Glarus, it's crucial to be aware of the new measures introduced by the Landsgemeinde and the implications these have on their financial and work situations. Here are some concrete steps to follow:

1. Check commuter deductions: Contact your employer or the cantonal tax office to get detailed information on how to claim the 6000 francs deduction for travel expenses. Make sure to keep all receipts and documents related to transport costs to present them in case of an audit.

2. Health insurance subsidies: Check if you are entitled to automatic subsidies for health insurance premiums. If in doubt, contact the relevant office to get clarifications and ensure your situation is regular.

3. Search for affordable housing: Although the proposal was rejected, it's possible that municipalities still have opportunities for affordable housing. Contact the municipal offices for information on any social housing programs or initiatives for buying or renting homes at moderate prices.

4. Compensation for farmers: If you are involved in the agricultural sector, inform yourself about the new regulations regarding compensation for predator attacks. Make sure to adequately document any animal losses to claim compensation.

5. Tax planning: With the tax rate confirmed at 58%, it's important to do accurate tax planning. Use tools like the tax calculator to estimate taxes and plan your expenses efficiently.

Conclusion

The decisions made during the Glarus Landsgemeinde have a significant impact on cross-border workers employed in the canton. It's essential to be informed and act promptly to benefit from the new measures and adapt to the changes. Using the available tools and resources, such as the tax calculator, can help manage your financial and work situation effectively.
