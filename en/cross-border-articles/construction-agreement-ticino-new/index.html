Frequently Asked Questions
What are the main developments for site allowances?
The site allowance, currently set at CHF 4 per day, will be gradually increased under the new agreement. Starting from 1 January 2027, the amount will rise to CHF 6.5 per day, and then reach CHF 9 per day in 2028. This adjustment represents a central element of the new contractual structure, aimed at improving the economic conditions of workers active in the field in the Canton of Ticino and throughout Switzerland.
How does the management of travel time to the construction site change?
The contractual amendment establishes that the travel time necessary to reach the construction site must be recorded from the first minute. The exemption of the first 30 minutes previously in force, which weighed on the operation of workers, is therefore eliminated. This provision aims to ensure greater fairness and transparency in the remuneration of time spent commuting to the workplace, obliging companies to count in full.
What is the duration of the new agreement and what does it provide for wages?
The agreement, signed at the beginning of May 2026, has an unusually long duration of six years. As far as wages are concerned, there is an automatic adjustment to the standard of living for minimum wages. In addition, from 2029, a specific mechanism will be activated to guarantee the purchasing power of real wages. The OCST, the protagonist of the negotiations, stressed the importance of constant monitoring to ensure that the collective agreement is fully respected by companies.
