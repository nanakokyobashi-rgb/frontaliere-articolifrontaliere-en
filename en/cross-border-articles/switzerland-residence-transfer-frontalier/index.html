Operational details

The New Frontier Agreement, which entered into force on 1 January 2024, has drastic consequences for border workers resident in Ticino. The new regulatory text provides for the need to fill in a special form, called "Application form for stay for border workers outside their family unit of origin", which must be delivered to the cantonal police within 30 days of the date of arrival in Switzerland.

The completion of the form takes about an hour and involves the identification of the applicant, the recognition of the border worker's title, the declaration that he has not committed crimes and the declaration that he is not in a state of clandestinity. The form must be signed by a resident of Switzerland or by a resident Swiss citizen, who certifies that he/she has consulted the applicant and has found no elements of prejudice.

The fiscal-preventive impact for frontier workers is considerable. The House of Representatives has approved a bill that provides for the imposition of an income tax of about 40% on the income of border workers residing in Switzerland. This tax, which has already led a dozen commuters to give up the border crossing, aims to reduce fiscal instability and prevent capital flight.

Documents to be submitted:

In order to get married in Switzerland, border workers must submit some important documents to the civil registry office, the authority responsible for the preparatory marriage procedure. Here is a list of documents to submit:

  • Original Marriage Certificate (Original Version): This document must be submitted in original to the civil registry office. If the certificate has been translated into German, the civil registration office must certify it in German.
  • Original birth certificate: This document is mandatory for all border crossers who want to get married.
  • Original passport: The original passport must be delivered together with the marriage certificate and birth certificate.
  • Entry and residence permit: If the border worker does not yet have an entry and residence permit, they must present the necessary documents to obtain it.
