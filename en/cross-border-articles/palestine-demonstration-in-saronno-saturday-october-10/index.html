The announced program

On Saturday 10 October Saronno hosts a demonstration for Palestine, as part of the World Day of Mobilization promoted by the Global Alliance for Palestine. The meeting is at 10am in Piazza San Francesco, from where a procession will start through the streets of the city.

The communication therefore delivers a precise scan of the morning: first the meeting in the square, then the city march. The initiative is presented as a local moment of global mobilization, with a meeting point and subsequent participation in the march.

The day, organized by the «Palestina Anima Mundi» Network, focuses on three requests: respect for international law; the adoption of sanctions and an embargo, as well as the abandonment of any complicity with Israel's genocidal and colonialist policies; the possibility to freely express dissent.

The organizers' announcement therefore unites the global framework of the day and the demands expressed by the network. The place chosen for the meeting and departure of the procession is clearly indicated: Piazza San Francesco in Saronno.

Castellanza, Garbagnate Milanese, Paderno Dugnano, Cesate, Limbiate and Uboldo also took part in the event. The list expands the map of membership beyond Saronno, but the source does not attribute separate interventions or an autonomous program to the individual municipalities.

For those who follow local news, the announced picture remains focused on the date of Saturday 10 October, on the 10am time, on the meeting place, on the procession and on the three requests of the «Palestina Anima Mundi» Network. The procession is described as a route through the streets of the city; a more detailed itinerary is not indicated.
