The calendar takes shape

On October 15 and 16, Comune di Varese schedules ADHD Days Varese 2026, two days dedicated to training, institutional dialogue and outreach events. The theme is ADHD, referred to in the source as Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder: an invisible but widespread vulnerability, addressed through an educational alliance.

The project is part of a broader program. Throughout October, Comune di Varese organizes and promotes broad-based educational initiatives and training programs dedicated to invisible vulnerabilities, focusing on the well-being of young people and families.

“In October, we are proposing a calendar of days dedicated to education and broad-based training,” emphasizes Councillor for Educational Services Rossella Dimaggio, “to promote people’s well-being and address vulnerabilities constructively, with a focus on young people, but not exclusively.”

The ADHD Days stem from the partnership of Comune di Varese and the synergy between AIFA Lombardia Associazione Italiana Famiglie ADHD APS and ADHD Life Coach Italia Società Benefit. The Territorial School Office and local economic and social organizations are also participating in the initiative.

The source therefore presents a specific event within a structured calendar. Alongside ADHD, the other events address various aspects of invisible vulnerabilities, with references to young people and adolescents, relational and change processes, the importance of language and health. For the Frontaliere Ticino audience, the verifiable fact remains the local nature of the initiative: Varese brings together a network of associations, schools and institutions active on the issue.

Operational details

A proposal built on multiple levels

A practical reading of the program begins with a distinction: the ADHD Days have a specific focus, while the October calendar broadens its view to invisible vulnerabilities as a whole. This dual approach makes it possible to combine specialized training, widespread education and opportunities for institutional discussion without reducing the initiative to a single topic.

The first level concerns ADHD. The two days are described as a space for training, discussion and public outreach. The second level is the educational one: the source speaks of an alliance capable of bringing together families, specialized training, education, businesses and institutions. The third concerns related topics, including young people and adolescents, relationships, change, language and health.
