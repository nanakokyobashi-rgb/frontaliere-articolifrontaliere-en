Frequently Asked Questions
What is the CFSL Certified Model Solution?
This is an occupational health and safety management system developed by the Coop-SSL Cooperative Society. The certification issued by the Federal Coordinating Commission for Occupational Safety (CFSL) attests that this model complies with Swiss legal requirements. It is designed to support SMEs and public bodies in the Canton of Ticino in fulfilling legal obligations through practical tools and a dedicated software platform for documentation.
What are the advantages for a Ticino company that adopts this model?
Companies that adhere to the Model Solution can adopt an already structured and constantly updated management system. This allows you to promote a culture of concrete prevention, reduce the risk of accidents and ensure legal compliance without having to develop complex internal protocols from scratch. In addition, the software platform greatly simplifies the bureaucratic management and updating of regulatory content.
How does this certification affect border guards' safety?
For frontier workers, the presence of a company-certified Model Solution ensures that the working environment complies with Swiss federal standards. This results in increased protection against accidents at work and professional health management. A safer environment reduces the risk of prolonged absences, protecting the economic stability of the worker and ensuring the regularity of insurance and social security coverage.
