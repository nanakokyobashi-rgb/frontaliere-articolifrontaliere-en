Seasonal work in Ticino is an important source of income for many cross-border workers. During the summer, many companies hire seasonal workers to meet increased demand. However, cross-border workers who work in Ticino must comply with Swiss laws and regulations.

Cross-border workers who work in Ticino during the summer season must obtain a seasonal work permit. This permit is valid for a period of six months and can be renewed once. Cross-border workers who work in Ticino for more than six months must obtain an annual work permit.

Cross-border workers who work in Ticino must pay taxes in Switzerland according to Swiss tax rules. They must also ensure they have valid health insurance in Switzerland.

Cross-border workers who work in Ticino during the summer season must also comply with Swiss labor laws. This includes workplace safety and health regulations.
