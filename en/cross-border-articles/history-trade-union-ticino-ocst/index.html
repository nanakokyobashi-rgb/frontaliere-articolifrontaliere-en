According to what was communicated in January 2021, the history of trade unionism in Canton Ticino has its roots in an economic context initially dominated by agriculture and pastoralism. Towards the middle of the nineteenth century, the first small workers' societies arose, based on principles of solidarity and mutual aid. The economic landscape of the canton began to transform radically with the construction of the Gotthard Railway, completed in 1882. This infrastructural work favoured the decline of the agricultural sector, which nevertheless maintained an important role until the thirties of the twentieth century, and stimulated the birth of the first factories, tourism and other services in urban centres.

Operational details

The year 1919 marked a fundamental stage for Ticino trade unionism. In Bellinzona, a group of Christian-social workers laid the foundations for the creation of a structured organisation, aligning themselves with the birth of the Federation of Christian-Social Trade Unions at the Swiss level. The OCST was officially founded on 18 May 1919, defining its statutes on 16 November of the same year in the Ticino capital. From the first steps, the organization equipped itself with its own information tool: the magazine "Il Lavoro", which began publication in 1920.

Economic Challenges and Spiritual Guidance

The 1920s proved to be particularly complex from an economic point of view, forcing the OCST to maintain a predominantly mutualistic profile to support workers in difficulty. The financial constraints of the time led, in 1927, to the temporary termination of the magazine "Il Lavoro". A turning point for the union was the decision of the Bishop of Lugano, Mgr. Aurelio Bacciarini, to entrust the direction of the organization to Don Luigi Del Pietro. Thelatter assumed the position of secretary on 1 September 1929, giving a new direction to the management of trade union activities. This historical evolution reflects the constant commitment of trade organisations to support workers, a principle that remains current for anyone working in the Ticino labour market today. Frequently Asked Questions
When was the OCST officially founded?
The OCST was officially founded on 18 May 1919, after the formation of a group of Christian-social workers in Bellinzona in 1918.
What role did Don Luigi Del Pietro play?
Don Luigi Del Pietro, appointed by the Bishop of Lugano Msgr. Aurelio Bacciarini, took over the management of the union as secretary on 1 September 1929, playing a fundamental role for the organisation.
What were OCST's main activities in the 1920s?
During the 1920s, due to economic difficulties, the OCST mainly maintained a mutualistic character, offering support to workers in the context of a market in profound transformation.
