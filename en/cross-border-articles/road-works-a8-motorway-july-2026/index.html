A8 Milan-Varese: night closures for work in July (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 July 2026

Construction sites for safety barriers on the A8: closed sections and junctions blocked from 6 to 12 July 2026. Here are the recommended alternative routes.

Context

In short - Closure of the Solbiate Arno-Gallarate-Gattico section in the direction of Milan. - Interventions to upgrade and modernise safety barriers. - Exchanges of Solbiate, Cavaria and Castellanza affected by the blocks. ## Key facts - What: Safety barrier modernisation works - When: From 6 to 12 July 2026 - Where: A8 Milan-Varese, Solbiate Arno-Gallarate section - Who: Autostrade per l 'Italia - Times: 21:00 - 5:00 (6-9 July) and 22:00 - 5:00 (10-12 July) The A8 Milan-Varese motorway will be affected by important works to upgrade the safety barriers, which will involve the closure of the section between Solbiate Arno Albizzate and the connection with the Gallarate-Gattico branch towards Milan. The planning of the construction sites provides for a division into two distinct time phases, with different schedules depending on the days of the week. On the nights of Monday 6, Tuesday 7, Wednesday 8 and Thursday 9 July, closing is scheduled from 21:00 to 5:00 the following morning. Subsequently, on the nights of Friday 10, Saturday 11 and Sunday 12 July, the block time will be postponed to 22:00, keeping the end at 5:00. During these time slots, access to the Solbiate Arno Albizada junction inbound to Milan will also be prohibited, while the Cavaria junction will remain closed inbound in both directions of travel. ### Specific interventions on the Castellanza junction

Operational details

The impact of these construction sites on cross-border commuting is significant, especially for those who regularly travel on the A8 to reach their workplace in the Canton of Ticino or to return to Italy. Mandatory closures require a change in night driving habits. For the stretch towards Milan, after the mandatory exit at the Solbiate Arno Albizzate junction, drivers will have to follow the ordinary road, specifically the SS341 in the direction of Cavaria and Gallarate, and then return to the A8 at the Gallarate junction. This is a deviation that, although well signposted, requires increased attention to temporary signage. For those who are heading towards the Gallarate-Gattico branch (D08) in the direction of the A26 Genoa Voltri-Gravellona Toce, the recommended route includes the exit at Solbiate Arno Albizzate, the transit on the SS341 towards Cavaria and the subsequent route of the SP26 in the direction of Jerago with Orago/Besnate, and then enter the D08 at Besnate station. ### Management of secondary junctions The management of secondary income during construction site nights is equally crucial to avoid traffic jams on local roads. Key points

For those who commute daily between Italy and Switzerland, managing the journey during July nights requires meticulous preparation. If your return or commute coincides with closing hours, the advice is to check the real-time traffic situation before setting off. The correct procedure involves identifying your starting point and selecting the alternative lane indicated by Autostrade before entering the autostrada. Do not ignore the construction signs: deviations on the SS341 and SP26 have been studied to absorb the diverted traffic, but the capacity of these roads is lower than that of the autostrada. There may be localized slowdowns at roundabouts and intersections on the regular road network. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the exact closing times of the Solbiate-Gallarate section?
The section will be closed on nights from 6 to 9 July from 21:00 to 5:00, and on nights from 10 to 12 July from 22:00 to 5:00.
Where can I get on the motorway if the Cavaria junction is closed?
If you are heading towards Varese, you can enter Solbiate Arno Albizzate. If you are heading towards the Gallarate-Gattico Branch, the alternative is the Besnate entrance.
What is the alternative route for those travelling to Milan?
To Milan, after the mandatory exit at Solbiate Arno Albizzate, take the SS341 towards Cavaria/Gallarate and enter the A8 at the Gallarate junction.
