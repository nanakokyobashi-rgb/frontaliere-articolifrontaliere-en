Varese: Torna Fermentino - Giubiano Farmers' Market (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 25 June 2026

On 26 June, the evening farmers' market returns to Giubiano. Local producers and craft beer at a free event.

Context

In short - Return of Fermentino on 26 June to Giubiano - Local agricultural producers and dairies - Free admission from 18:00 to 21:00 - What : Evening farmer's market - When: 26 June from 18:00 to 21:00 - Where: Cooperativa di Giubiano, via Cadore 9, Varese - Who: Local agricultural producers and dairies - Amount: Free admission On 26 June, in Giubiano, the small evening farmer's market Fermentino will return. The event, organized by the Giubiano cooperative, will be held from 6 pm to 9 pm and will be accessible to everyone at no cost. After the success of the first appointment on 22 May, the farmers' market returns for the second evening, offering visitors the opportunity to buy local products directly from the producers. The initiative was born from the experience of the Fermento market, organized in the same spaces in 2025, and the creation of the Cadregas solidarity purchasing group. The goal is to connect the neighborhood with the agricultural and dairy realities of the territory, promoting exchange and community. ### Fermentino and its ingredients Live music and fresh beer will accompany the producers' stalls. The Club will provide space and material to consume an aperitif with the products purchased directly at the stands. In addition, from 17 to 19, Varesenews journalists will also be present to listen to the citizens of Giubiano and Hospitals as part of the 'What will it be' project. Operational details

Practical Implications for Frontalieri in Ticino

For frontalieri living in Italy and working in Ticino, events like Fermentino offer an opportunity to discover local products and support the local economy. The chance to buy high-quality agricultural and dairy products directly from producers can be a significant advantage. Additionally, the presence of live music and artisanal beer makes the event a great occasion for relaxation and socialization.

During the evening, visitors will enjoy a convivial and relaxed atmosphere. The Giubiano cooperative, located at via Cadore 9, will be the event's hub. The products on sale will include a variety of fresh and artisanal foods, ideal for those seeking high-quality ingredients for their meals. Furthermore, the presence of Varesenews journalists will offer the opportunity to discuss local topics and actively participate in the community's life.

How to Reach the Event

For frontalieri working in Ticino, reaching Giubiano is simple. The event will take place in an easily accessible area, and the presence of public transportation and well-connected roads makes the journey convenient and fast. For those who prefer to drive, the availability of nearby parking ensures easy access to the cooperative.

Upcoming Events

The Fermentino calendar includes a third appointment on July 24. This will allow visitors to participate in more evenings, discovering new proposals and products. The participation is free, and the event is open to all, making it an ideal opportunity for families, friends, and communities.
