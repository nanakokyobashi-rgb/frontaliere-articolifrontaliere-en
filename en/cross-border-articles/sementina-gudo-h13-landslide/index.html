The blockade on the cantonal road

In the afternoon, around 4.00 pm, a landslide of a certain size occurred on the cantonal road in Sementina. According to available information, the detachment would have affected the stretch near the football field, towards the south. The movement blocked the roadway and caused heavy traffic disruption. Diversions have also been put in place.

The road data confirmed by the TCS traffic info concerns the H13 road: the connection between Sementina and Gudo is closed in both directions due to landslides. For anyone traveling along this route in the Canton of Ticino, the point to consider is therefore a complete closure of the indicated stretch, not a simple warning of slowdowns.

On-site checks

The cantonal police, contacted by us and also present on site, reported that, fortunately, there were no injuries. The episode only resulted in damage to property, including that caused by a car hit by a rock. The data allows us to distinguish the impact on traffic from the consequences on people: the source reports a damaged vehicle, but no injuries.

Operations are not limited to traffic management. Road workers and geologists are heading to the area to take care of the restoration work and ascertain the origin of the collapse of plants and boulders. Their tasks are therefore two: to fix the situation and to establish the causes of the fall of the materials.

For a cross-border commuter who finds himself passing through Sementina, there are therefore three concrete references: the H13 section between Sementina and Gudo, the closure in both directions and the diversions already activated. These are the elements that define the immediate traffic situation, while the technical teams prepare for the work.

The point of arrival of the information is articulated: the cantonal police describes the consequences on people and vehicles, the TCS confirms the closure of the H13, while road workers and geologists are involved in the arrangement and investigation of the causes.
