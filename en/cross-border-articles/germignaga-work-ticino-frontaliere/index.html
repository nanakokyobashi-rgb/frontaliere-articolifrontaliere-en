How source tax works in Switzerland

Source tax in Switzerland is a tax levy mechanism that is applied to the incomes of frontier workers. Border workers resident in Germignaga, municipality of the Canton of Ticino, who work in Switzerland, will be subject to this levy.

Exemption from double taxation in Italy

Border workers resident in Germignaga are exempt from double taxation in Italy, thanks to the Italian-Swiss Convention against Double Taxation. This means that they will not have to pay the tax in Italy on the same income that has already been taxed in Switzerland.

Concrete example

Suppose a frontier worker residing in Germignaga works in Lugano and earns CHF 60,000 per year. The tax at source in Switzerland will be applied on gross income, for example, at 10%. The tax will be CHF 6,000, which will be withheld directly from the worker's salary.

Being exempt from double taxation in Italy

"Double taxation is a problem for many border workers" (source: UNIPEV, 2020)

Italy has a double taxation agreement with Switzerland, which provides that the same income is not subject to double taxation. This means that if you pay the tax in Switzerland, you do not have to pay the tax in Italy.

Example: If you earn 5,000 CHF per year from work in Ticino and have paid the tax in Switzerland of 1,000 CHF, you do not have to pay the tax in Italy.

Frequently Asked Questions
What tax changes apply to border workers residing in Germignaga who have been working in Switzerland since 2024?
From 1 January 2024, frontier workers residing in Germignaga who work in Switzerland are subject to tax at source in Switzerland. This tax is 20% on earned income and is withheld directly from wages. Thanks to the New Frontier Agreement, they are exempt from double taxation in Italy.
Do border workers from Germignaga who pay taxes in Switzerland also have to pay them in Italy?
No, border workers resident in Germignaga who pay tax at source in Switzerland are exempt from double taxation in Italy. This is guaranteed by the New Frontier Agreement, signed on 23 December 2020 and effective from 1 January 2024, which prevents the taxation of the same income in both countries.
Since when is the new taxation for border workers of Germignaga in force and what agreement is the rule?
The new taxation for border workers resident in Germignaga is in force from 1 January 2024. It is regulated by the New Frontier Agreement, signed on 23 December 2020, which replaces the previous conventions regarding new frontier workers and methods of taxation at source.
How much tax does a border worker from Germignaga with a Swiss income pay at source?
The tax at source in Switzerland for border workers resident in Germignaga is 20% on income from work. For example, on a gross annual income of CHF 60,000, the tax withheld directly from the salary would be CHF 12,000.
What checks should a border worker from Germignaga make for his tax situation?
A border worker resident in Germignaga should verify his/her residence, the gross income received in Switzerland and the start date of the tax at source (1 January 2024). It is essential to ensure that you are subject to tax in Switzerland and that you benefit from the double tax exemption in Italy.
