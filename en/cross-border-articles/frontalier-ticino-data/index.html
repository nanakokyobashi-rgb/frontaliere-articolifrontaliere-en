The Swiss Federal Council has activated the legal basis for reciprocal information flow with Italy and France.

Context

In a nutshell

• The Swiss Federal Council has activated the legal basis for mutual information flow with Italy and France. • The objective is the taxation of income in the State of residence. • The Federal Act on the International Automatic Exchange of Salary Data (LSADS) will come into force on 1 January 2027.

The new automatic exchange of information regime

Switzerland is a country with a long tradition of frontier workers, particularly with Italy and France. For years, Switzerland maintained an information exchange regime with these countries, but until now, the process was based on an explicit request from the authorities of one of the two countries involved.

With the entry into force of the Federal Act on the International Automatic Exchange of Salary Data (LSADS) on 1 January 2027, Switzerland will activate a new automatic information exchange regime with Italy and France. This scheme will be based on the legal basis activated by the Swiss Federal Council and will aim at income taxation in the State of residence.

The new automatic information exchange regime will be implemented through the use of computer and digital communication systems. Context and reasons

The Swiss Federal Council has announced the decision to introduce a system of automatic exchange of pay data with Italy from 1 January 2027. This initiative is aimed at simplifying the management of frontier workers in Switzerland and reducing the bureaucracy associated with contribution payments.

What it means for frontier workers

Border employees, who reside in municipalities such as Bellinzona, Lugano, Mendrisio and Chiasso, will be directly affected by this initiative. In fact, salary data will be exchanged automatically between the Swiss and Italian authorities, avoiding the need to submit documents and requests for payment of contributions manually. This will reduce payment times and simplify the management of contributions.

What is the objective of the automatic exchange of salary data with Italy?
Taxation of income in the State of residence.
When will the new system of automatic exchange of pay data come into effect?
The new Automatic Exchange of Salary Data Information Scheme (LSADS) will come into effect on 1 January 2027. From this date, Switzerland, Italy and France will start the mutual exchange of data for income taxation in the State of residence.
What types of data will be automatically exchanged between Switzerland and Italy?
The pay data of frontier workers will be exchanged automatically. This includes all the salary information necessary to allow the Swiss and Italian tax authorities to correctly tax income in the worker's country of residence, simplifying the management of contributions.
What benefits will the new system bring to frontier workers?
The new system will offer frontier workers greater simplicity and transparency in paying taxes. It will avoid manual submission of documents and requests for payment of contributions, reducing time and bureaucracy. The authorities will be able to pay taxes in the country of residence, reducing the tax burden.
