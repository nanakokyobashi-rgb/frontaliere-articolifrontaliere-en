Tax does not coincide with title

If you are looking for the new 2026 tax agreement, the correct effective date is January 1, 2024, not 2026. The historical reference for double taxation is the Italy-Switzerland Convention signed on March 9, 1976. For the cross-border worker's income from work, the withholding takes place only in Switzerland; Italy avoids double taxation with the tax credit in the EC framework of 730. The same income should therefore not be presented as taxed in both countries.

Tax at source and IRPEF

LAMal and rate administration

LAMal means health insurance, not a "health tax". Border workers with a G permit have the right of option. For adults, the deductibles indicated range from CHF 300 to CHF 2,500. The framework does not report monthly premiums and does not link a deductible to a specific salary as a social health worker.

Tax rates, including source tax and federal or cantonal rates, are set by federal and cantonal laws. At the federal level they are administered by AFC/ESTV and, for the cantonal part, by the cantonal tax administrations. They should not be attributed to UFAS, which concerns the AVS/AI social security, nor to BFS, which collects statistical data and does not set rates.
