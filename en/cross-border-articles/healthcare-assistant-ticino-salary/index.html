Tax does not coincide with title

The historical reference for double taxation is the Italy-Switzerland Convention signed on March 9, 1976. For the cross-border worker's income from work, the withholding takes place in Switzerland; Italy avoids double taxation with the tax credit in the EC framework of 730. The same income should therefore not be presented as taxed in both countries.

Furthermore, Switzerland is not an EU/EEA member. The question on the real salary must therefore be read through the contribution items and the documented tax thresholds, without transforming the G permit or professional title into an unreported salary figure.

LAMal and rate administration

LAMal means health insurance, not a "health tax". Border workers with a G permit have the right of option. For adults, the deductibles indicated range from CHF 300 to CHF 2,500. Article 1(237–238) of Italian Law No. 213/2023 does not apply indiscriminately to all cross-border workers. It covers: (a) Italian residents who work and stay in Switzerland and use the Italian SSN; (b) frontier workers under Article 9(1) of the Italy–Switzerland Agreement whose income is taxable only in Switzerland and who exercised the legal right to choose health insurance; and (c) dependants of those two categories. Paragraph 238 sets a progressive 3–6% share of net Swiss salary, with a €30 minimum and an ordinary €200 maximum for each month worked from 2024. The ordinary regime applies in other cases, with Italian taxation and credit for Swiss tax where applicable.
