The quarter just published by the Federal Statistical Office (BFS) records an increase in border workers in the Canton of Ticino. According to BFS data, total border crossers rose to 79,121 in 2026-Q2.

Key facts

  • Total frontiersmen: 79,121
  • Trend vs previous quarter: growing
  • Same quarter last year: 79,578
  • QoQ change: +505 units, +0.64%
  • YoY variation: -457 units, -0.57%

Practical analysis

The quarterly statistics show an increase in border crossers in the Canton of Ticino. This trend is also confirmed by the comparison with the previous quarter (2026-Q1), which saw an increase of 505 units, 0.64%.

In addition, the comparison with the same quarter of the previous year (2025-Q2) shows a change of -457 units, -0.57%.
