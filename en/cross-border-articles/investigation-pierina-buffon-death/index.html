The investigation between crash and search for evidence

On Friday morning, in Cassano Magnago, the death of Pierina Buffon was initially linked to the suspicion of road homicide. The reconstruction reported by the source, however, describes a change in direction of the investigation: the investigators are looking for elements compatible with the hypothesis of aggravated voluntary homicide and femicide.

The Busto Arsizio Prosecutor's Office requested documents useful for assessing whether Fulvio Ielmini had planned the action in advance. The point to check also concerns the possible control of the ex-partner's movements and habits. A plan is therefore not indicated as a fact that has already been established: the source explains that this is precisely why further evidence is needed.

During the night between Saturday and Sunday, investigators reached Ielmini's house in Gallarate, concentrating their search on papers, notes, documents and above all computer material. In fact, these supports could contain useful elements to understand whether the man could have monitored Pierina Buffon through GPS devices.

The research therefore includes both paper and IT material: on the one hand notes and documents, on the other tools or information potentially linked to the movements. The source does not report what outcome the investigations had. The sequence connects two places and two levels of the investigation: the reconstruction of the crash is concentrated in Cassano Magnago; in Gallarate the search for traces of previous pipelines.

It is a concrete distinction, because the first step concerns the dynamics of the accident, while the second aims to clarify what could have happened before. The source does not present access as the conclusion of the investigation, but as an activity carried out to seek feedback on the hypothesized planning.

The elements preceding the incident

The part of the investigation concerning the possible planning starts from the end of the relationship between Ielmini and Buffon. According to the text, the man reportedly did not accept the conclusion of the relationship. In the following period, the victim's children allegedly found GPS devices in their mother's car. The source indicates the possibility that these instruments allowed him to monitor the movements of his ex-partner, without reporting what concrete use was actually made of them.

The requested documents, according to the Prosecutor's Office, serve for:

«necessary acts to evaluate whether the suspect proactively planned the action, monitoring the victim's movements and habits».

Alongside the IT material, the reconstruction includes the accounts of the neighbors in Cassano Magnago: they report waiting outside the house, small gifts, and surprises. These behaviors are described as insistent; also for this reason, the woman no longer wanted to humor them and had no intention of resuming the relationship.

The picture also includes two formal initiatives attributed to the victim: a formal warning sent to Ielmini through her lawyer and a police report. According to the investigative reconstruction, not even these steps were enough to stop the reported behaviors.

The significance of these elements lies in their different nature. The GPS devices represent a possible technical trace; the neighbors' accounts describe observed behaviors; the formal warning and the complaint show that the woman had asked to stop the situation also through her lawyer. The source places them in the same investigative framework, but does not attribute definitive proof of planning to any single one of these elements: the question remains the one indicated by the Prosecutor's Office, namely to verify whether the control of movements and habits was part of a previously prepared action.

The crash and the location of the suspect

The reconstruction of the collision

According to the first investigations by the local police of Cassano Magnago, Ielmini's Jaguar, a heavy and dated sedan, would have been launched at 140 kilometers per hour against Pierina Buffon's small car. After the lane change, the woman's car would have been hit head-on.

The formulation requires keeping the reported data and its interpretation distinct. The speed, the Jaguar and the change of lane belong to the preliminary findings cited by the source; the hypothesis that the collision was part of an aggravated voluntary action and a femicide is instead the investigative direction that the investigations are trying to verify. The source does not yet present a conclusive decision on this step.

The wait in the hospital

Ielmini is still guarded at the Varese hospital, where he awaits surgery to reduce the fractures in his leg. The lawyer Angelo Luini describes him as being in a state of confusion. The source thus reports the position of the suspect while he remains under hospital surveillance.

According to Luini, a precautionary measure against the suspect should be formalized in the next few hours. This is an expectation, not an indication that the measure has already been formalized. Furthermore, the source does not report the outcome of the intervention and does not report subsequent developments either on the access to Gallarate's house or on the computer material searched by the investigators.
