Brawl in Saronno: two young people injured during the night

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 October 2026

Two men aged 25 and 27 were injured in a brawl in the pedestrian center of Saronno: three ambulances and carabinieri at the scene.

Context

In brief

  • Brawl among young people in Saronno's historic centre
  • Two young men aged 25 and 27 injured
  • Three ambulances in the pedestrian area
  • Investigations by the carabinieri of the Saronno Company

Key facts

  • Date → night between Friday 2 and Saturday 3 October
  • Time → shortly before 0.30
  • Location → between Piazza Libertà and Via Garibaldi
  • Injured → two young men aged 25 and 27
  • Emergency response → three ambulances
  • Outcome → hospital, yellow code and green code
  • Forces involved → carabinieri of the Saronno Company

Shortly before 0.30, on the night between Friday 2 and Saturday 3 October, an argument between some young people turned into a brawl in the historic centre of Saronno. The alarm was raised in the heart of the pedestrian area, between Piazza Libertà and Via Garibaldi, after the group's shouting drew attention.

The confrontation was taking place noisily when the situation quickly worsened. The reconstruction reports that a group of young people, apparently foreigners, were engaged in an argument. When emergency services reached the area, however, the other participants had already left. Two young men, identified as the people in need of assistance, remained on the ground.

They are two young men aged 25 and 27. Both were taken to hospital after the ambulances arrived; they were assigned a yellow code and a green code. Their condition is described as battered, but the two were not in danger. The reported medical picture therefore remains that of injured people taken to hospital, with no indication of danger.

The sequence recounted begins with the shouting and ends, immediately afterwards, with the two young men being taken to hospital. Between these two moments, the argument quickly escalated from a noisy confrontation into a brawl, while the other people involved left the area. It is this sequence of events, which took place in the pedestrian heart of the city, that the reconstruction of what happened focuses on.

Operational details

The emergency response

The emergency response activated after the alarm involved three ambulances. Croce rossa di Saronno, Sos Uboldo and Croce azzurra di Rovellasca arrived at the scene. The source therefore indicates three organizations involved in assisting the two young men, who were then transferred to hospital with yellow and green codes.

The medical outcome reported in the reconstruction concerns two young people, aged 25 and 27, in poor condition but not in danger. Hospitalization is indicated for both, while the codes reported are one yellow and one green. These are the data available on the immediate outcome of the medical intervention.

The intervention by the law enforcement forces accompanied the medical intervention. Several patrols of the carabinieri of the Compagnia di Saronno reached the historic center. The source places the presence of the officers in the same scene in which the screams had signaled the fight and in which, when the emergency responders arrived, the other participants had already left.

The final reference concerns public order during nighttime hours in the city center. The episode in fact draws attention to an issue already mentioned in the description of the city: in the past, the historic center had been the scene of fights and moments of tension. The reference to previous incidents remains part of the local context provided by the source. The outcome of the night is that the two young men were taken to hospital.

Key points

The investigations

The follow-up to the incident is now in the hands of the Carabinieri of the Saronno Company, who are trying to reconstruct precisely what happened. Their activity has three objectives indicated by the source: clarifying the dynamics of the altercation, understanding the reasons that caused it to escalate, and identifying the other people who allegedly took part in it.

The work starts from a situation already defined in its essential features: the shouts of a group in the pedestrian area, the transformation of the argument into a brawl, two young people injured and the other participants already gone by the time emergency services arrived. From here, the reconstruction must focus on the sequence of events described during the night between 2 and 3 October.

The reasons for the altercation are another point to clarify. The available account does not indicate an already established motive and reports that the officers are working precisely to clarify why the discussion escalated. As for the identifications, attention is focused on the other people who allegedly took part in the incident before leaving.
