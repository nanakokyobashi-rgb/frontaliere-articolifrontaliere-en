Night closures A8 and D08: deviations in Varese from 28 September (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 25 September 2026

Three nights of closures between A8 and D08 from 21:00 to 5:00, green maintenance work between Busto Arsizio, Besnate and Cavaria, detours on state, provincial and municipal roads.

Starting lunedì 28 settembre, Autostrade per l’Italia has scheduled a series of overnight closures on the A8 Milano-Varese and the Gallarate-Gattico Branch, identified as D08. The works are concentrated between Busto Arsizio, Besnate and Cavaria and always take place from 21:00 to 5:00 the following morning. The schedule extends through the early hours of giovedì 1 ottobre. For a cross-border commuter, the information to verify is therefore the night on which the route passes through one of the affected sections: the source does not indicate border crossings or routes in Ticino, but describes the road network in the Varesotto area precisely.

The schedule of the three interventions

The first closure is scheduled for the night between lunedì 28 and martedì 29 settembre. On D08, the section between the connection with the A8 and the Besnate exit, in the direction of the A26 Gravellona Toce, is closed. During the same period, the connecting ramp to D08 toward the A26 remains closed to those arriving from Varese on the A8.

Those who need to continue toward the A26 can exit at the Gallarate station on the A8, join the SS33 and continue along via dei Mille, via Bettolino and via Monte San Martino, then take the SP26 and re-enter D08 at the Besnate toll station. Alternatively, they can exit at Solbiate Arno-Albizzate and follow the SP26 and the SP49 to Besnate.

Between martedì 29 and mercoledì 30 settembre, the works move to the opposite carriageway of D08. The section between Besnate and the connection with the A8, in the direction of the Milano-Varese motorway, is closed. The Besnate station remains completely inaccessible for entry in either direction, A8 or A26. Vehicles heading toward the A8, after the mandatory exit at Besnate, must take the SP26, the SS33 del Sempione and the SS341 to the Gallarate toll station. Those who instead need to enter at Besnate toward the A26 can use the Vergiate station.

The final worksite, during the night between mercoledì 30 settembre and giovedì 1 ottobre, affects the A8 between Busto Arsizio and Cavaria in the direction of Varese. For those arriving from the A26 along D08, the connecting ramp to the A8 toward Varese is closed. The company managing the motorway network explained that the measures are needed «to allow tree maintenance work» along the affected sections.

Operational details

To correctly read the notice, the main criterion is to cross-reference the date, time slot and direction of travel. This is not a single repeated block: on the first night the critical point is the connection toward the A26; on the second, the direction toward the A8 and access to the Besnate station; on the third, the direction toward Varese on the A8 and the D08-A8 branch. For those who work in Ticino, the notice becomes relevant only if their route includes one of these sections.

A different decision every night

This breakdown makes it possible to avoid a simple mistake: applying the diversion for the night of the 28th-29th to that of the 29th-30th. The directions also change for those departing from the same area. On the first night, there are two alternatives for the A26, using different exits, Gallarate or Solbiate Arno-Albizzate. On the second, by contrast, access to Besnate is closed in both directions, and the source separates the flow heading directly toward the A8 from that which must enter toward the A26. The reference point indicated for the latter is Vergiate.

The last night also requires a distinction. Those heading toward Varese must exit at Busto Arsizio and use the SS336, SS33 and SS341 before re-entering the A8 at the Cavaria or Solbiate Arno-Albizzate junctions. Those who must continue toward Gravellona from the D08, on the other hand, follow the secondary road network, with SS336, SS33, SP26 and SP49, to re-enter the D08 at Besnate. The two routes start from the same closure, but have different destinations.

The fact that the closures are scheduled between 21:00 and 5:00 limits the analysis to that window. The source does not report daytime closures, so the measure cannot automatically be extended to the entire day. For a commuter, the practical choice depends on the actual time of passage: if it coincides with the indicated time slot, the diversion planned for that night is needed; if it falls outside the time slot, the notice does not describe a closure.

Key points

To turn the notice into a practical decision, a cross-border commuter using one of the indicated sections can follow an essential sequence.

The operating procedure

1. Identify the window. Consider the nights between 28 and 29 September, between 29 and 30 September, and between 30 September and 1 October. The indicated time period is always from 21:00 to 5:00.

2. Determine the direction. Establishing whether the journey is toward the A26, toward the A8 Milano-Varese, or toward Varese makes it possible to match the route to the correct detour.

3. For the night of 28-29, if the destination is the A26, before the closed entrance choose either the Gallarate exit, via SS33, via dei Mille, via Bettolino, via Monte San Martino and SP26 to Besnate, or the Solbiate Arno-Albizzate exit, following SP26 and SP49.

4. For the night of 29-30, anyone heading toward the A8 must take the mandatory exit at Besnate and continue on SP26, SS33 del Sempione and SS341 to Gallarate. Anyone who needs to enter at Besnate toward the A26 can use the Vergiate station.

5. For the night of 30 September-1 October, after exiting at Busto Arsizio, anyone traveling toward Varese follows SS336, SS33 and SS341 to re-enter at Cavaria or Solbiate Arno-Albizzate. Anyone arriving from the D08 toward Gravellona uses SS336, SS33, SP26 and SP49 to re-enter at Besnate.

The method does not require constructing new itineraries: it is based on the exits and roads explicitly indicated in the notice. If one’s personal route does not correspond to these cases, the source does not propose other alternatives, and it is not correct to attribute different detours to it.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the dates and times of the night closures?
Closures are scheduled for three consecutive nights: from the night between Monday 28 and Tuesday 29 September, then that between Tuesday 29 and Wednesday 30 September, and finally that between Wednesday 30 September and Thursday 1 October. In any case, the intervention takes place from 21:00 to 5:00 the next morning, as indicated by Autostrade per l 'Italia to allow the maintenance work of the tree essences.
What deviations are planned for the night between 28 and 29 September towards the A26?
If your route involves continuing towards the A26 Gravellona Toce, you must exit the A8 at Gallarate station, enter the SS33 and continue via dei Mille, via Bettolino, via Monte San Martino, then take the SP26 to Besnate toll booth. Alternatively, you can exit at Solbiate Arno-Albizzate and follow the SP26 and SP49 until you reach Besnate.
How should I behave if I have to enter Besnate towards the A26 on the night between 29 and 30 September?
During the second night of closures, Besnate station is completely inaccessible at the entrance to both the A8 and the A26. For those who must enter Besnate heading towards the A26, the notice indicates to use the Vergiate station as an alternative access point to the motorway network.
