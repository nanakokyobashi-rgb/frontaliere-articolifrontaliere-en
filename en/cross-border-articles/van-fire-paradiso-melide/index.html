MELIDE – Shortly before 17, a company van traveling on the cantonal road on the stretch between Paradiso and Melide was involved in a small fire. The incident occurred in the territory of Melide, on Via Cantonale, during an evening already marked by critical traffic on the canton’s roads.

The sequence of events described

According to information obtained, the driver reportedly immediately pulled over to the side of the road. With cars queued up, he then reportedly sought help from those in the queue to extinguish the flames. The causes of the small fire are not currently known.

No one was injured. The incident is described as another accident of the evening, with further complications for those hoping to avoid the queue on the A2. The reference to the queue on the A2 is part of the critical traffic situation described for the canton.

The news was published on 2 ottobre 2026 at 17:20 and updated at 18:21. In the updated text, the location of the fire, the driver's maneuver, and the absence of injuries remain central.
