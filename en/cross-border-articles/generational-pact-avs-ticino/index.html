A solidarity 13th AVS payment for young people from Ticino

Di Laura Bianchi · Published on 4 October 2026

The Fondo Patto Generazionale invites AVS beneficiaries to donate part or all of their thirteenth payment to support young people from Ticino aged between 0 and 30.

Context

In brief

  • Fondo Patto Generazionale launches an AVS fundraising campaign
  • It is possible to donate part or all of the thirteenth payment
  • The support concerns young people residing in Ticino
  • The projects will be selected by a Committee

Key facts

  • Promoter → Fondo Patto Generazionale
  • Fundraising recipients → AVS beneficiaries
  • Final beneficiaries → people aged 0 to 30 residing in Ticino
  • Structure → Fondazione Mantello Filantropia
  • Selection → Selection Committee

On Wednesday, Fondo Patto Generazionale presented a fundraising campaign aimed at AVS beneficiaries. The initiative, launched by figures from civil society, invites those who receive the thirteenth monthly payment to donate part or all of the amount to help young people in need in Ticino.

The proposal connects the resources available to those receiving the benefit with the needs of the younger generations. Donors are given two choices: allocate part of the monthly payment or donate the entire amount. The text presents both possibilities as forms of participation in the fundraising campaign.

The note states the goal of providing financial support to initiatives that foster the personal and social autonomy of the younger generations and improve their living environment. The support therefore has a precise scope: the population aged between 0 and 30 residing in Canton Ticino.

From fundraising to projects

The Fund operates within Fondazione Mantello Filantropia. According to the description of the initiative, the funds raised could support various organizations. The possibilities indicated include an organization that helps young people enter an apprenticeship or their first job, a cultural project, an association or a sports club.

The choice will not be assigned to a single promoter. A Selection Committee will identify the projects to be supported. This element clarifies the general structure announced: the fundraising campaign is aimed at AVS beneficiaries, while the allocation of resources will take place at a later stage, through the Committee indicated by the Fund.

Operational details

For the cross-border worker, the practical reading begins at a precise boundary. The text discusses AVS and a donation linked to the thirteenth monthly payment, but it does not present the campaign as a change to cross-border work. It does not address the G permit, LPP, LAMal or tax rebates. It does not describe withholding tax, double taxation, INPS, the new 2026 tax agreement or the Brogeda border crossing.

This delimitation avoids attributing effects to the fundraising campaign that the source does not announce. A cross-border worker may recognize the AVS topic, but the text does not establish whether someone who lives outside Canton Ticino and receives a benefit has different conditions: it identifies the beneficiaries of the fundraising campaign without adding further criteria.

Two aspects not to be confused

The first aspect is solidarity: AVS beneficiaries are invited to choose whether to donate part or the entire amount of the thirteenth payment. The second is the destination: the resources should support young people in need between 0 and 30 years old who reside in Canton Ticino. The source does not link this choice to a reduction in withholding tax, special treatment for double taxation or a change to tax rebates.

The variety of possible projects should also be understood in its concrete sense. The assistance is not described as a direct transfer to every young person, but as funding for initiatives: support toward an apprenticeship or first job, culture, association activities or sport. Selection will be entrusted to the Committee designated by the Fund.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who is the Generational Pact Fund campaign aimed at?
The campaign targets the beneficiaries of the AVS. The invitation concerns those who receive the thirteenth monthly payment and includes the possibility of donating part or the entire amount to help young people in need in Ticino. The choice is therefore left to the beneficiary, who can opt for a fee or for the entire sum of the monthly payment.
Which young people can benefit from the support?
The support is aimed at the population between 0 and 30 years of age residing in the Canton of Ticino. The note links the aid to initiatives capable of promoting the personal and social autonomy of the new generations and improving their living environment. A single project already selected is not indicated.
What projects can be funded?
Destinations indicated include an organisation that accompanies young people towards an apprenticeship or first job, a cultural project, an association and a sports club. The Fund operates within the Fondazione Mantello Filantropia and entrusts the selection of projects to a Selection Committee.
