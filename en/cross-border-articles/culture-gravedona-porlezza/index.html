Gravedona ed Uniti, Porlezza: 1,5 million for culture

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 27 September 2026

Lombardy Region allocates over 1,5 million to Gravedona ed Uniti and Porlezza: 502.082 euros to the Chiesa della Madonna della Soledad and 1 million to Villa Marzorati.

Two precise destinations

Gravedona ed Uniti and Porlezza are the two municipalities receiving a cultural investment announced through the ranking just published of the Integrated Culture Projects Call 2026-2029. The initiative is carried out by Lombardy Region in collaboration with Fondazione Cariplo and allocates a total of over €1.5 million to the two territories.

In Gravedona ed Uniti, €502,082 are allocated. The sum will be used for the restoration and enhancement of the Church of Madonna della Soledad, indicated as a historical and cultural asset of particular value. In Porlezza, €1 million is assigned for the recovery of Villa Marzorati.

Commenting on the ranking is Gigliola Spelzini, regional councillor of Alto Lago. In her comment, Spelzini presents the two interventions as an investment in the places of local history and their usability. The allocation of resources therefore follows two precise lines: intervene on the church of Gravedona ed Uniti with restoration and enhancement, and recover the villa of Porlezza.

The funding concerns two different places, but is described as coordinated support for the culture of the two municipalities. For the councillor, the value of the operation is concrete because it allows the recovery of important places of the territory's history and makes them increasingly usable.

Operational details

Benefits for the local area

What it means for communities

Spelzini's statements attribute tangible value to the two projects for local communities. The reason given is the restoration of places important to the area's history, along with the possibility of making them increasingly accessible. The perspective therefore concerns the preservation of heritage assets and their cultural enhancement, according to the terms used in the comment on the ranking.

For Gravedona ed Uniti, the project concerns a historical and cultural asset of particular value. For Porlezza, the restoration of Villa Marzorati is associated with strengthening and enhancing the area's cultural offering. The two projects are thus described with specific objectives, but within the same focus on local heritage.

«Regione Lombardia demonstrates once again its attention to municipalities and the enhancement of local heritage».

Spelzini links this attention to the broader role attributed to investment in culture. Preserving identity is one of the stated aims, together with creating new spaces and opportunities for citizens, associations and visitors. Existing heritage is considered a living resource, capable of generating new opportunities for local communities.

The councillor's reaction therefore highlights the territorial dimension of the operation: the places receiving funding will have to be restored, enhanced and made increasingly accessible, contributing to the cultural offering of the municipalities involved.

Key points

From preservation to opportunities

The expected outcome for the two properties

The stated outlook for the two grants concerns how the restored properties can be made available to the local area. In Gravedona ed Uniti, the restoration and enhancement of the Church of Madonna della Soledad are linked to the recovery of a particularly valuable historical and cultural asset. In Porlezza, the recovery of Villa Marzorati is presented as the intervention that will make it possible to consolidate and enhance the area’s cultural offering.

The aim identified by Spelzini is to make these places increasingly accessible. Culture is described as a tool for preserving identity, but also for creating new spaces and opportunities. The recipients mentioned in the statement are citizens, associations and visitors.

The endpoint announced by the councillor is the transformation of existing heritage into a living resource, capable of generating new opportunities for local communities. This is the follow-up that the source associates with the publication of the ranking: the funds allocated to the two municipalities are linked to the possibility of making important places in local history newly accessible and supporting the cultural offering.

For Gravedona ed Uniti and Porlezza, the investment is therefore presented as an intervention in the heritage and, at the same time, as an opportunity for the communities. The commentary focuses on the cultural and community value of the two projects, carried out as part of the Progetti Integrati della Cultura 2026-2029 call with the collaboration between Regione Lombardia and Fondazione Cariplo.
