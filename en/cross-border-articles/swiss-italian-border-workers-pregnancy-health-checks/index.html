Operational details

Article title: "Frontier pregnancy: LAMal or CMI coverage, hospital choice, practical differences"

Border workers working in Switzerland and residing in Italy face some specific challenges during pregnancy, such as the choice of hospital and health coverage. This depends on the individual needs of the border crossing and the regulations in force.

Health coverage for border workers during pregnancy depends on their type of work permit. For border workers in possession of an unqualified work permit (LAMal), health coverage is guaranteed by the compulsory insurance system (LAMal) and includes medical care and hospital services. For border workers with a residence permit (CMI), health coverage depends on the type of permit and could include an option for compulsory insurance (LAMal) or private insurance.

The choice of hospital depends on the needs of the border worker. If you are expecting a child, the border guard may need to undergo more frequent medical checks at the Ticino Pregnancy and Maternity Centre (CPMT). CPMT offers universal health coverage and guarantees high quality performance. The border guard may choose to be assisted at the public or private hospital. The cost of health coverage at the CPMT depends on the type of permit and can vary from 100 to 200 francs per month.
