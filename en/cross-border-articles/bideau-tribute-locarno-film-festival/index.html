The recent edition of the Locarno Film Festival featured Jean-Luc Bideau, a famous figure in Swiss cinema. On Saturday, the actor was officially awarded for his career. During the event, Bideau had the opportunity to present the film 'Ah que le bonheur est proche' to the public. The event attracted the attention of critics and fans, confirming the festival as a cultural reference point for the entire Canton of Ticino and beyond. The presence of figures of this caliber enriches the cinematographic offer offered every year in the famous Piazza Grande, attracting visitors from all over the region.
