Living in Besnate and working in Ticino: as a border worker (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 2 September 2026

How taxation, rights and practical organisation work for those who live in Besnate and work in Ticino as border workers.

Context

In brief

  • Withholding tax only in Switzerland for cross-border workers
  • New Cross-Border Workers Agreement effective from 1 January 2024
  • EUR 10,000 threshold for new cross-border workers
  • Permit G mandatory for the Ticino-Italy border

Key facts

  • What: Living in Lombardy and working in the Canton of Ticino as a cross-border worker
  • When: Regulation in effect from 1 January 2024
  • Where: Northern Ticino-Italy border (crossings: Brogeda, Chiasso)
  • Who: Workers residing in Italy who work in Switzerland
  • Permit: Cross-border worker permit G (required before employment)
  • Taxation: Withholding tax in Switzerland; Italian tax credit
  • Threshold: EUR 10,000 annually for new cross-border workers (from 17 July 2023)
  • Legal basis: New Agreement signed on 23 December 2020, Italian Law 83/2023

Living in Besnate, province of Varese, and working in the Canton of Ticino is a choice that thousands of cross-border workers make daily. The decision to move across the border involves practical considerations - travel time, cost of living, housing - and tax considerations, with Swiss withholding tax and social security contributions (AVS and LPP). The New Cross-Border Workers Agreement, which came into effect on 1 January 2024, introduced significant changes: new cross-border workers benefit from a EUR 10,000 annual threshold, while those who were already cross-border workers before 17 July 2023 fall under a transitional regime with a EUR 7,500 threshold until 2033. Italy, through the tax credit in the model 730, prevents double taxation.

Operational details

The payslip and contributions

From the Swiss payslip, social security and insurance contributions are automatically deducted. The AVS/AI/IPG rate is 5.3% for the employee, while unemployment insurance (AD/AC) represents 1.1% of the gross salary (capped at CHF 148,200). Accident insurance (LAINF) varies between 0.7% and 1.5%, while the pension fund (LPP) amounts to a percentage ranging from 7% to 18% depending on the worker's age bracket (from 25 years onwards).

A commuter from Besnate working in Ticino is entitled to receive periodic extracts of their contribution position through the SECO system. These payments ensure Swiss pension coverage and protection against workplace accidents, rights that accumulate with the Italian position at INPS (coordinated by the 1976 Convention).

Health insurance (LAMal) and options

Category G commuters have the option to choose Swiss LAMal. The deductibles for adults range from CHF 300 to CHF 2,500 annually, depending on the chosen policy and coverage model. Many commuters choose to keep Italian health insurance (CMI) for continuity of care, a decision that should be carefully evaluated in terms of premiums, coverage, and access to Ticino healthcare facilities. Discover LAMal and CMI options Choosing LAMal allows the commuter to access Swiss healthcare facilities directly (EOC hospitals in Ticino, cantonal polyclinics), while maintaining Italian CMI requires checks for reciprocity.

Key points

Checklist: before moving to Besnate to work in Ticino

Before completing the move, make sure you have all the elements in order:

Essential documentation: G permit for cross-border workers (required before starting employment), employment contract signed by the employer in Ticino, Italian tax code, valid ID card, and a Swiss bank account (optional but recommended for receiving the paycheck).

Declarations and registrations: Notify the Italian Tax Agency of the move and proceed with the change of address at the Italian municipality. Register with INPS as an employee working abroad, providing the data of the employer in Ticino. Inform your Swiss employer of all Italian banking and tax details.

Insurance: Consider switching to Swiss LAMal or keeping Italian CMI. Ensure that accident insurance coverage for the commute to work is active (usually covered by Swiss LAINF if the accident occurs during working hours).

Taxes and refunds: Schedule a consultation with a tax advisor specializing in cross-border workers to open an annual file. Check your eligibility for CVA (Advance Payment Credits) refunds based on your contribution situation.

The first months: what to expect

After the move, your first Swiss paycheck will arrive with the necessary deductions (AVS, LPP, LAINF, and withholding taxes). By June 30 of the following year, you will submit the Italian tax return to the CAF or through a tax advisor, indicating the income earned in Ticino and applying the cross-border exemption. Meanwhile, maintain regular communication with INPS for updates on your foreign contribution status.

Frequently Asked Questions
What permission do I need to work in Ticino from Besnate?
The G (border) Permit is mandatory and must be requested before commencing employment. The procedure is carried out through the SEM (State Secretariat for Migration) upon notification by the Swiss employer. Release usually takes 2-4 weeks. The G permit is renewable every 5 years, as long as the employment relationship continues in Ticino.
How does taxation work after the New Frontier Agreement 2024?
The tax at source is withheld only in Switzerland, based on the Ticino cantonal-communal rate. New frontier workers (registered after 17 July 2023) have a deductible of EUR 10,000 per year, deductible from the Italian tax base. Old frontiersmen enjoy EUR 7,500 until 2033. Italy applies the tax credit via Form 730 to avoid double taxation.
Do I need to register with INPS if I work in Ticino?
Yes, also as a border worker working in Switzerland. Registration takes place with INPS as an employee with foreign income. At the same time, you pay the AVS and LPP contributions from the Swiss paycheck. The two systems are coordinated by the Italy-Switzerland Convention of 9 December 1976, which avoids double payments and guarantees the recognition of contribution periods.
What is LAMal and do I have the right to choose?
LAMal is the mandatory Swiss health insurance. As a border crossing category G, you have the right of option: you can join the Swiss LAMal (CHF 300-2,500 per year exemption for adults) or keep the Italian CMI. The choice must be evaluated with respect to premiums, deductibles, coverage and ease of access to health facilities.
Can I take advantage of CVA (Advance Payment Credits) refunds?
CVA refunds are a possibility for border workers who have paid excess contributions. Applicability depends on your personal tax situation and the number of days worked in Switzerland. Check with a specialist consultant during the annual return (Form 730 or Unico) to see if you fall under the access criteria.
