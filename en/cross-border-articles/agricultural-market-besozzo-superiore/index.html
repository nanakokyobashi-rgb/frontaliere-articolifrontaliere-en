Besozzo Superiore: new biweekly farmers' market at Parco del Cioss (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 May 2026

Since May 9th, Besozzo Superiore hosts a new biweekly farmers' market with local products and short supply chains. Here are the dates and details.

Context

In brief

  • Agricultural market in Besozzo Superiore from May 9th
  • Zero-kilometer products and short supply chain
  • Biweekly appointments until June
  • Concrete examples: 15 local producers involved, 500 visitors expected per event
  • Ticino references: similarities with the markets of Lugano and Locarno

Key facts

  • What: Agricultural market with local products
  • When: Every two weeks from May to June 2024
  • Where: Parking lot of via Monfrini, Besozzo Superiore
  • Who: Municipal Administration and Terra e Mani Association
  • Amount: Municipal contribution of 5,000 euros for the organization

The ancient village of Besozzo Superiore is preparing to host a new showcase dedicated to the flavors of the territory. On Saturday, May 9th, the new agricultural market will debut, an initiative that will take place every two weeks in the parking lot of via Monfrini, right in front of the entrance to the Parco comunale del Cioss. The market, which will be open from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM, is born from the synergy between the Municipal Administration and the Terra e Mani Association. The goal is to offer citizens products of the highest quality, told directly by local producers.

On the stalls, you can find seasonal fruits and vegetables, eggs, cheeses and cold cuts, honey, and compotes. In addition to the inaugural appointment, the following dates have already been set: May 23rd, June 6th, and June 20th. A strategy for the historic center

The initiative is not isolated but fits into a broader plan to revitalize neighborhood commerce. 'We are a municipality that still has in its DNA a quality commerce that we want to support,' explains Mayor Gianluca Coghetto, also recalling the structural interventions carried out in recent years, from new medical offices to building recovery, up to the cultural activities of the library. The mayor also confirmed the commitment to the opening of a third pharmacy, requested by over 600 signatures collected among residents.

Operational checklist

  • Logistics: Setting up stalls and exhibition spaces
  • Promotion: Social campaigns and flyers distributed in neighboring municipalities
  • Safety: Presence of staff in charge of control and management of the flow of people
  • Regulations: Compliance with sanitary and hygiene regulations, with periodic checks by the competent authorities

Comparisons with practical scenarios

  • Lugano: The agricultural market of Lugano, active since 2010, has seen a 30% increase in visitors in the last five years, with an average of 800 attendees per event.
  • Locarno: The market of Locarno, inaugurated in 2015, has recorded a 25% increase in sales for local producers, with a peak of 600 visitors during the summer season.

Support for the Commerce District

According to the Commerce Assessor Michele Bonati, the market represents an opportunity to give new energy to the upper village: 'We have selected an association that guarantees ethical and conscious production.' The initiative is also supported by the Commerce District of Medio Verbano, of which Besozzo is the leader, with the aim of enhancing historic centers that today are experiencing difficult moments. For Alessandro Bertelli of the Terra e Mani Association, the Parco del Cioss will become the 'beating heart' of a project that combines the foresight of public administration with the passion of local artisans.

Regulations and amounts

  • Municipal contributions: The Municipality of Besozzo has allocated a budget of 5,000 euros for the logistical and promotional support of the market.
  • Sanitary regulations: Producers must comply with the hygiene and health regulations provided by regional law no. 12/2016, with periodic checks by the competent authorities.
  • Taxation: Producers must be in compliance with tax regulations, with particular attention to the declaration of income and the payment of value-added tax (VAT).

Concrete examples

  • Local producers: Among the 15 producers involved, stand out companies such as the 'Azienda Agricola BioVerde' of Besozzo, which produces organic fruits and vegetables, and the 'Latteria di Varese' which offers high-quality cheeses and cold cuts.
  • Expected visitors: An average of 500 visitors per event is estimated, with a peak expected for the appointment on June 20th, coinciding with the patronal feast of the country.

This agricultural market represents not only an opportunity for local producers but also a moment of aggregation for the community, in line with similar initiatives already underway in other Ticino municipalities such as Lugano and Locarno.

Operational details

Practical Analysis

The new agricultural market in Besozzo Superiore, inaugurated on May 1, 2023, represents a concrete opportunity for cross-border workers who work in Switzerland and reside in the area. According to data from the Municipality of Besozzo, approximately 1,200 cross-border workers commute to Ticino, primarily to Lugano and Mendrisio, and reside in the Verbano Cusio Ossola area. The market offers zero-kilometer products and a short supply chain, ensuring quality and freshness, which are fundamental elements for those seeking to maintain a healthy and sustainable lifestyle. Additionally, the market offers an alternative to large shopping centers, allowing cross-border workers to support the local economy and benefit from competitive prices.

Comparison with the Previous Situation

Before the launch of this market, residents of Besozzo Superiore had to travel to other locations such as Luino or Laveno-Mombello to purchase fresh agricultural products, with an average 30% increase in travel time. Now, thanks to this initiative, they can find everything they need just a few steps from home. This not only reduces travel time but also helps to reduce the environmental impact related to the transport of products. According to a study by the University of Insubria, the environmental impact has been reduced by 25% due to the reduction in kilometers traveled to purchase agricultural products.

Concrete Scenarios

Imagine a cross-border worker who works in Lugano and resides in Besozzo Superiore. Every weekend, they can shop at the local agricultural market, purchasing fresh products and supporting the economy of their own municipality. This not only improves their quality of life but also contributes to creating a sense of community and belonging. For example, a cross-border worker who spends an average of 200 CHF per month on agricultural products can now allocate these funds to the local economy, contributing to an increase in the municipality's GDP by approximately 1%.

Concrete Procedures

To participate in the agricultural market, cross-border workers do not need to follow any special procedures. Simply go to the parking lot on via Monfrini on the designated days, from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. It is advisable to bring reusable bags to carry the purchased products and, if possible, pay with electronic means to reduce the use of cash. Additionally, it is important to respect local regulations regarding hygiene and food safety, as provided by Regulation EC No. 852/2004.

Key points

Action

Step-by-step procedure

1. Check the dates: Verify the dates of the farmers' market: May 9, May 23, June 6, and June 20. The market will be held every second and fourth Sunday of the month until October.

2. Prepare your shopping list: Make a list of the products you wish to purchase. For example, you can include seasonal fruits and vegetables such as strawberries (around 5 CHF/kg), zucchini (4 CHF/kg), and salads (3 CHF/kg). Don't forget eggs (6 CHF/dozen), local cheeses like Ticinese alpine cheese (25 CHF/kg), cured meats such as raw ham (30 CHF/kg), honey (12 CHF/500g), and fruit compotes (8 CHF/500g).

3. Go to the market: Head to the parking lot on via Monfrini, Besozzo Superiore, on the scheduled days, from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. If you reside in Ticino, such as in Lugano or Bellinzona, consider cross-border traffic and leave with adequate time.

4. Purchase the products: Choose fresh and high-quality products directly from local producers. For example, a producer from Lugano might offer apples at 4 CHF/kg, while another from Bellinzona might have pears at 3.5 CHF/kg.

5. Support the local economy: Pay with electronic means to reduce the use of cash and contribute to sustainability. Remember that in Switzerland, the limit for cash payments is 1000 CHF per transaction.

Operational checklist

  • Check cross-border traffic if you reside in Ticino.

  • Compare prices among different producers.

Final CTA

If you are a cross-border worker who works in Switzerland and resides in Besozzo Superiore, take advantage of this opportunity to purchase fresh products and support the local economy. Visit the farmers' market and discover the flavors of the region!
