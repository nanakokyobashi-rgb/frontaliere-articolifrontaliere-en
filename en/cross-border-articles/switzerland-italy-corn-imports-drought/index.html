Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 August 2026

Extraordinary measure of the DEFR until 31 October 2026 to guarantee fodder to farmers affected by the crisis. Imports of maize up to 60% dry matter duty free.

Context

Drought: duty-free maize imports for Ticino farmers

  • Free maize imports until 31 October 2026
  • DEFR zero duties from 15 September to 31 October 2026
  • UFAG has abolished duties on hay from 1 August 2026
  • Maximum dry matter: 60% for imports

The drought affected hay production in Switzerland, forcing the DEFR to intervene. From 15 September to 31 October 2026 it will be possible to import fresh or silage maize intended for animals with dry matter ≤60% without paying duties. The Federal Office for Agriculture (FOAG) has already eliminated duties on hay and grass silage as of 1 August 2026 under the ordinary legislation. This extraordinary measure aims to ensure the continuity of the Ticino agricultural sector, with particular attention to the refills that have suffered reduced yields due to adverse weather conditions.

The decision comes after a favourable spring followed by a period of drought and high temperatures that have negatively affected fodder production. Recent rainfall, although it has occurred, has not completely recovered the deficiencies. Ticino farmers, already struggling with rising costs, will be able to access this temporary exception to secure quality feed without customs charges. Key points

Drought: duty-free maize imports for Ticino farmers

The nuisance that has hit eastern Switzerland has led to a significant shortage of grain for livestock. The Ticino government has responded with an emergency measure offering a 100% discount on customs duties for importing grain for animals into Ticino. This measure will particularly benefit Ticino farmers, who will be able to import maize without paying any customs costs.

The procedure to take advantage of this opportunity is relatively simple and involves a few key steps.

1. Customs declaration

To benefit from this measure, Ticino breeders must complete form C73, available on the website of the Customs and Statistical Office of the Confederation (www.bazg.admin.ch). This form must be completed before the cargo is delivered to the customs terminal.

2. General permission

Once the C73 form has been completed, breeders must apply for general permission at the organization Schwanengasse 5-7, Bern (www.reservesuisse.ch). This permit will require further documentation and will be issued after a prior check.

3. Documentation

To apply for the general permit, breeders must attach a commercial invoice, a certificate of origin and a dry matter analysis. This information will help ensure that imported wheat is of high quality and compliant with regulations

Frequently Asked Questions
How to declare maize imports?
Fill in the C73 form on www.bazg.admin.ch and submit the documentation to the relevant customs office. Do not forget to apply for general permission to the organization Schwanengasse 5-7, Bern.
Does the measure also apply to Italian fodder?
Yes, as long as it complies with the 60% dry matter limit. Imports must clearly be destined for animal feed, not for industrial processing.
Is there a fee for permission?
General permission is free of charge, but requires the submission of complete documentation. See www.reservesuisse.ch for the detailed form.
