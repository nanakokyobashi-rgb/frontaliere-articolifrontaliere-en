Frontier increase: +0.64% (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 12 August 2026

The Federal Statistical Office (BFS) notes an increase in border crossers Ticino in the 2026-Q2 quarter.

Context

In a nutshell

  • 79,121 border crossers Ticino in the 2026-Q2 quarter.
  • Change compared to the previous quarter (2026-Q1): +505 units, +0.64%.
  • Same quarter of the previous year (2025-Q2): 79,578 border crossers, -457 units, -0.57%.

The 2026-Q2 quarter is characterised by an increase in border crossers from Ticino compared to the previous quarter (2026-Q1). According to the Federal Statistical Office (BFS), in the quarter just ended, the number of border crossers rose to 79,121 units, with a change of +0.64% compared to the previous quarter. This increase represents an increase of 505 units, or 0.64% compared to the number of border workers recorded in the previous quarter.

The quarterly statistics also show a change compared to the same quarter of the previous year (2025-Q2), with the number of border crossers equal to 79,578 units, -457 units, -0.57%. This means that the number of frontier workers decreased by 457 units compared to the same quarter of the previous year, representing a decrease of 0.57%.

However, if we compare the two numbers, we note that the number of border crossers in the 2026-Q2 quarter is only 457 units lower than in the same quarter of the previous year. This suggests that the overall trend is stable and there are no signs of a significant increase in the number of frontier workers.
