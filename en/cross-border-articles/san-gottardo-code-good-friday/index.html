The peak tourist influx towards Ticino and Italy occurred on April 2, 2026, with estimated transit times of around 100 minutes. The first slowdowns were perceived at the Erstfeld-Primocampo interchange. Thursday, April 2, 2026, was marked by intense and congested traffic on the Gotthard Pass, the main communication artery between Switzerland and Italy. The primary cause of these delays was the large number of tourists visiting the Ticino region on this date. According to traffic operators' estimates, the number of vehicles crossing the Gotthard Pass was the highest of the season, with over 20,000 cars and 5,000 trucks using the bridge in a single day. This resulted in a 30% increase in traffic compared to the seasonal average. The first slowdowns were reported from 8:00 AM, as the first tourists began reaching the Gotthard Pass. Transit times increased progressively throughout the day, peaking at 120 minutes around 2:00 PM. Traffic was particularly intense at the Erstfeld-Primocampo interchange, where vehicles had to wait for over 20 minutes before continuing their journey. Other congestion zones were reported at the Gotthard Tunnel and Bodio Station. According to traffic operators' estimates, transit times on the Gotthard Pass were approximately 100 minutes during the day of Thursday, April 2, 2026. This means a journey that would normally take about 30 minutes was extended by over 1.5 hours due to traffic. Traffic management on the Gotthard Pass was entrusted to the US company Vinci, which implemented an intelligent traffic management system. This system uses advanced technologies to monitor traffic in real-time and predict potential congestions. According to Vinci's director, "our intelligent traffic management system reduced transit times by 15% compared to the previous day". However, this was not sufficient to prevent the slowdowns, which were still significant. The Ticino region welcomed tourists with a big smile, but also with some apprehension.
