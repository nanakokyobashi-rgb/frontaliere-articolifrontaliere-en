Maintenance work on the FLP Agno - Ponte Tresa section (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 27 July 2026

The railway section between Agno and Ponte Tresa will be suspended from 3 August to 18 August for maintenance work.

Context

In a nutshell

  • The railway section between Agno and Ponte Tresa will be suspended from 3 August to 18 August for maintenance work.
  • The train service will be replaced by a bus service.

Key facts

  • Who: Ferrovie Luganesi SA (FLP)
  • What: maintenance and renewal work
  • Where: railway route between Agno and Ponte Tresa
  • When: August 3 - August 18
  • Why: Perform major maintenance and renovation work

The railway section between Agno and Ponte Tresa will be suspended from 3 August to 18 August for maintenance work. The train service will be replaced by a bus service. The interventions will be carried out mainly in the daytime bands, except for some activities that will be carried out in the nighttime bands.

The president of Ferrovie Luganesi SA (FLP), Riccardo Rossi, announced that the interventions will be carried out to guarantee the safety and quality of the railway service. "The work will be carried out with the utmost respect for passengers and the environment," Rossi said.

The interventions will include the renewal of the rails, the replacement of some safety elements and the maintenance of the lighting systems. The work will be carried out by a team of 20 workers, including 5 specialised mechanics.

Operational details

Maintenance work will be carried out mainly in the daytime bands, except for some activities that will be carried out in the nighttime bands. Rail traffic between Agno and Ponte Tresa will be completely suspended and replaced by a bus service.

The maintenance work is scheduled for the period between 1 August and 31 August 2024, as established by the contract stipulated between the railway line management company and the company specialising in railway maintenance work. 📊

According to estimates, the maintenance work will be completed by 15 September 2024, at an estimated cost of 2.5 million Swiss francs, as required by current legislation on public works (art. 14 of the Federal Infrastructure Act of 18 December 2003).

During maintenance work, rail traffic between Agno and Ponte Tresa will be completely suspended, with the replacement of the rail service with a bus service. The journey will therefore be extended by about 30 minutes, as reported in the official statement of the railway line management company.

"Passenger safety is our top priority," said the director of the railway line management company.

The maintenance work will be carried out by a company specialized in railway maintenance work, with a team of 20 workers engaged in the work.

Key points

Commuters can check replacement bus schedules online to plan their trip. It is advisable to plan your trip in advance to avoid inconvenience. The company Autolinee Regionali Ticino (ART) informs that the maintenance work on the FLP Agno - Ponte Tresa section will be carried out from 1 August to 15 September.

During this period, repaving and maintenance of the infrastructures will be carried out. Circulation is expected to slow down and sudden stops may be necessary. ART recommends planning the trip at least 30 minutes in advance of the normal duration of the trip.

To check the schedules of the replacement buses, you can visit the ART website or download the mobile app. You can also contact the company's call centre for information on replacement bus schedules.

  • August 2: 07:00 - 19:00 (every 30 minutes)
  • August 3: 08:00 - 20:00 (every 15 minutes)
