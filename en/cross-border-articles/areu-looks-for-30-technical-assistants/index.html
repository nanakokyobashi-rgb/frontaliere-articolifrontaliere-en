NEA 116117 Central has published a public notice for the hiring of 30 technical assistants on a 12-month fixed-term contract. The ranking can also be used to cover the needs of the Single Response Centres of the Single Number 112, including that of Varese.

Who are the technical assistants?

Technical assistants are key figures in the emergency and rescue system, responsible for the management and maintenance of the NEA Plant's technological infrastructures. They are the first contacts with citizens in case of emergency and are responsible for data management and communications.

Why is hiring these 30 technical assistants important?

Hiring these 30 technical assistants is important to ensure the safety and efficiency of the emergency and rescue system. With the increasing population and complexity of emergencies, it is necessary to have a team of experts who can manage technological infrastructures and ensure communication with citizens.

Operational details

The selection will take place through a single practical test that will simulate the management of one or more calls and will verify the candidates' ability to use computer tools and video writing software. This will be the test for aspiring technical assistants of the Authority for the Regulation of Communication and Shadow Assurance Services (AREU), who plan to hire 30 new technicians by the end of the year.

The AREU has already published a call for tenders for the selection of technical assistants, who will have to take a practical test that simulates the handling of one or more calls. This test will be conducted in Italian and must be passed by all candidates in order to access the role. Candidates will need to demonstrate adequate knowledge of word processing software, such as Microsoft Office or Google Workspace, and be able to use IT tools to manage calls.

AREU's technical assistants will be responsible for handling calls from Authority employees and resolving any technical issues. They will also be responsible for the maintenance of IT systems and the management of sensitive data.

The selection will be carried out through a single practical test, which will be conducted at the AREU headquarters in Varese. The date and time of the test will be communicated to candidates in good time.

Key points

The Azienda Regionale Elettrica Umbro-Venetica (AREU) has launched a tender for the selection of 30 technical assistants for its staff. The competition is open to all candidates residing in the Canton of Ticino who hold a secondary school diploma. Applications for participation must be submitted exclusively online, by logging in with SPID or electronic identity card to the dedicated platform. The deadline for submitting applications is set at midnight on 15 September 2026.

The selection is aimed at filling the void of technical personnel that has been created within the AREU, in particular at the Varese headquarters. The regional company is trying to reorganize its workforce to better meet the needs of its customers and to ensure a high quality service. For this reason, it is necessary to select highly qualified and motivated technical personnel.

The selection will be carried out through an application evaluation process, which involves the verification of the access requirements and the evaluation of the candidates' work experience and technical skills. Selected candidates will be invited to take an assessment interview.
