Police, over 4,500 new positions available (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 May 2026

The State Police is looking for 220 commissioners and 4,400 trainee agents. Deadlines and procedures to participate.

Context

In brief

  • Police seek 220 commissioners and 4,400 trainee agents
  • Deadlines: May 20 for commissioners, May 29 for trainee agents
  • Applications online at concorsionline.poliziadistato.it

Key facts

  • What: Two new recruitment tenders
  • When: Published on May 3, 2026
  • Where: Italy
  • Who: State Police
  • Amount: 4,620 total positions

The State Police has published two new recruitment tenders for a total of over 4,500 positions. The first tender is for 220 commissioners, while the second is for 4,400 trainee agents. Applications must be submitted exclusively online through the portal concorsionline.poliziadistato.it. The deadline for submitting applications for commissioners is set for May 20, 2026, while for trainee agents, the deadline is May 29, 2026.

These tenders represent a significant opportunity for anyone interested in pursuing a career in the State Police. Specific requirements and detailed procedures are available on the official State Police website, where you can find all the necessary information to participate in the competitions.

Implications for cross-border workers

For cross-border workers who work in Italy and reside in Switzerland, these tenders could represent an interesting opportunity. However, it is important to consider the tax and pension implications. For example, cross-border workers must take into account withholding tax in Switzerland and AVS and LPP contributions. Additionally, it is crucial to verify whether the new 2026 tax agreement between Italy and Switzerland could affect your tax situation.

Practical Analysis

The new recruitment tenders from the Italian State Police offer a significant opportunity for cross-border workers who wish to work in Italy. However, it is important to consider several practical aspects before submitting an application.

Tax and Pension Implications

For cross-border workers who work in Italy and reside in Switzerland, it is crucial to understand the tax and pension implications. Withholding tax in Switzerland and AVS and LPP contributions are key aspects to consider. Additionally, the new 2026 tax agreement between Italy and Switzerland could influence the tax situation of cross-border workers. It is advisable to consult a tax expert to clarify these aspects.

Comparison with the Previous Situation

Before the announcement of these tenders, job opportunities in the State Police were more limited. Cross-border workers who wanted to work in Italy often had to face more complex procedures and fewer hiring opportunities. With the new tenders, the situation has improved significantly, offering more career and job stability opportunities.

Concrete Scenarios

Suppose a cross-border worker residing in Ticino and working in Lombardy decides to apply for one of the available positions. In this case, they will need to consider several factors, including the distance from the workplace, tax and pension implications, and any benefits provided by the new 2026 tax agreement. It is important to carefully plan every aspect to avoid surprises and ensure a smooth transition.

Key points

Concrete Action

For cross-border workers interested in participating in the new recruitment tenders of the State Police, it is essential to follow a clear and well-defined procedure. Here are the steps to follow:

Step 1: Verify Requirements

Before submitting the application, it is important to verify that you meet all the required criteria. These criteria are detailed on the official State Police website and include, among others, the minimum age, educational qualifications, and any physical requirements.

Step 2: Fill Out the Application

Applications must be completed and submitted exclusively online through the portal concorsionline.poliziadistato.it. It is important to follow the instructions provided carefully and ensure that all required fields are completed correctly.

Step 3: Gather Documents

In addition to the application, it is necessary to attach all the required documents. These documents may include, among others, the curriculum vitae, educational certificates, and any medical certificates. It is important to ensure that you have all the necessary documents before submitting the application.

Step 4: Submit the Application

Once the application is completed and all necessary documents are gathered, you can submit the application online. It is important to respect the indicated deadline and ensure that you receive a confirmation of receipt.

Step 5: Prepare for the Competition

After submitting the application, it is important to prepare adequately for the competition. This may include studying the provided materials, participating in preparation courses, and practicing written and oral tests.
