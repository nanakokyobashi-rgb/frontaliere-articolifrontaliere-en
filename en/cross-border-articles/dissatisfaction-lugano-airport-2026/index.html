Dissatisfaction among Lugano Airport employees (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 10 May 2026

Anonymous letter to Lugano Municipality highlights tensions between staff and director Christian Castelli

Context

In brief

  • Anonymous letter to Lugano Municipality
  • Tensions with director Christian Castelli
  • Labor Inspectorate initiates checks

Key facts

  • What: Anonymous letter from employees
  • When: May 9, 2026
  • Where: Lugano Airport
  • Who: Employees and director Christian Castelli
  • Amount: Not specified

Lugano Airport is at the center of internal tensions. Some employees have sent an anonymous letter to the Municipality of Lugano, expressing dissatisfaction with the management of director Christian Castelli. The news was reported by La Regione and has led the Executive to initiate immediate checks. Filippo Lombardi, head of the Lugano Airport department, confirmed that a similar document had already arrived at the Municipality about a year ago. This time, however, the situation seems more serious, requiring the intervention of the Canton Labor Inspectorate, which will conduct interviews with all collaborators in the coming weeks.

Checks underway

The Canton Labor Inspectorate has initiated a round of interviews with all collaborators of the airport to clarify the situation. This process has been initiated to address the concerns raised in the anonymous letter and to ensure a serene and productive work environment. The checks have been decided to prevent the dissatisfaction from negatively influencing the operations of the airport.

Impact on staff

The dissatisfaction among employees could have significant repercussions on the morale and productivity of the staff. A tense climate can negatively affect the quality of service offered to passengers and the safety of airport operations. It is essential that the Municipality and the airport management promptly address the concerns of the employees to avoid further escalation.

Operational details

Analysis of the causes of discontent

The causes of discontent among the employees of Lugano Airport could be manifold. Among the possible reasons are personnel management, working conditions, and internal communication. It is important that the airport management and the Municipality of Lugano carefully analyze these issues to identify the roots of the problem and find effective solutions.

Personnel management

Ineffective personnel management can lead to tensions and dissatisfaction among employees. It is essential that the airport management adopts personnel management policies that promote a positive and inclusive work environment. This includes continuous training, performance evaluation, and the creation of professional growth opportunities.

Working conditions

Working conditions are another crucial factor that can influence employee morale. It is important that Lugano Airport ensures safe, hygienic, and comfortable working conditions. This includes the provision of adequate equipment, regular maintenance of facilities, and the promotion of a healthy and safe work environment.

Internal communication

Effective internal communication is essential to maintain a positive work environment. The airport management should promote a culture of transparency and dialogue, encouraging employees to express their concerns and suggestions. This can be done through regular meetings, surveys, and anonymous feedback channels.

Concrete actions to address dissatisfaction

To address the dissatisfaction among the employees of Lugano Airport, a structured and collaborative approach is necessary. Here are some concrete actions that can be taken:

1. Initiate an internal investigation

The airport management should initiate an internal investigation to gather detailed information about the employees' concerns. This can be done through individual interviews, anonymous surveys, and group meetings. The investigation should be conducted by an independent team to ensure transparency and objectivity.

2. Create an employee representation committee

The creation of an employee representation committee can provide a direct communication channel between the workers and management. This committee should be composed of representatives elected by the employees and should have the power to express their concerns and suggestions constructively.

3. Implement personnel management policies

The airport management should implement personnel management policies that promote a positive work environment. This includes continuous training, performance evaluations, and the creation of professional growth opportunities. Additionally, measures should be adopted to ensure safe and hygienic working conditions.

4. Promote a culture of transparency and dialogue

A culture of transparency and dialogue is essential to maintain a positive work environment. The airport management should promote a culture of transparency and dialogue, encouraging employees to express their concerns and suggestions. This can be done through regular meetings, surveys, and anonymous feedback channels.
