The report also adds an assessment of security. In this regard, it states that there were no significant interventions. This indication is presented alongside the attendance estimate and the favorable assessment of edition number 70.

The edition's assessment

These elements form the assessment communicated for the Sagra dell'uva in Mendrisio.
