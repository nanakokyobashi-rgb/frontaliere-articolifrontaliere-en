The closure of the Como lakeside has caused enormous traffic disruptions, with endless queues and waits up to 150 minutes. The mayors of the towns between Lariana and Triangolo Lariano have written a harsh letter to the prefect, the chief of police, the president of the Province, and the regional transport assessor, as well as the mayor of Como, Alessandro Rapinese. The mayors are asking for an immediate meeting to discuss and definitively resolve the traffic problem on the Lariana State Road.

Current situation

The work on the Como lakeside began without any official or informal communication from the mayor of Como. This has caused enormous discomfort not only for drivers and commuters but also for ambulances and people who had medical appointments, dialysis patients, and medical emergencies.

The situation has become unsustainable, with traffic even blocked in some sections. The mayors of the affected towns have decided to intervene to request a dialogue with the Municipality of Como and find a definitive solution to the traffic problem.

Operational details

Practical analysis

The closure of the Como lakefront had a significant impact on the daily lives of residents and commuters. Infinite queues and long waits not only cause stress and delays, but also put people's safety at risk, especially in case of medical emergencies.

Impact on commuters

Commuters who use the Lariana State Road to get to work or other destinations have been particularly affected. Prolonged waiting can lead to significant delays, resulting in productivity losses and additional stress.

Disaster for emergency services

Ambulances and emergency services found it extremely difficult to reach patients in a timely manner. This can have serious consequences, especially in medical emergency situations.

Dialogue request

The mayors of the countries concerned requested an immediate meeting with the Municipality of Como to discuss the situation and find a solution. This dialogue is essential to ensure that work on the lakefront is managed in a way that minimises disruption to the population.
