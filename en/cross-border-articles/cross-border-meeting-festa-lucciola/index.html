Frontiersmen and Switzerland: comparison to the Firefly Festival (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 9 August 2026

On Sunday 9 August, trade unions and political representatives will discuss the prospects of cross-border work and the dialogue between Italy and Switzerland.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Public meeting on August 9 at the Firefly Festival
  • Focus on cross-border work and Italy-Switzerland dialogue
  • Italian trade union initials and representatives of Psi Ticino participate
  • What: Border work debate
  • When: Sunday, August 9, 6:00 p.m.
  • Where: Firefly Festival
  • Who: Cgil, Cisl, Uil and Psi Ticino
  • Moderator: Emanuele Vitale, Pd Provincia Vco group leader

The theme of cross-border work returns to the center of the local political debate on the occasion of the Firefly Festival, scheduled for Sunday, August 9. At 6:00 pm, a meeting is scheduled dedicated specifically to the theme 'Frontier workers and Italy-Switzerland dialogue', a matter of primary importance for the territory of Verbano Cusio Ossola, where thousands of workers travel daily to the Canton of Ticino and other Swiss territories. The comparison aims to analyze the current employment prospects and the state of diplomatic and institutional relations between the two countries, often characterized by complex challenges related to the management of commuting flows and current tax regulations.

Participants and round table

The meeting will see the participation of leading representatives of trade unions active in the territory: Gigi Bacchetta for Cgil Novara-Vco, Romina Baccaglio for Cisl Piemonte Orientale and Stefano De Grandis for Uil Novara-Vco. The presence of representatives of Psi Ticino will enrich the discussion with a direct point of view

Operational details

Analyzing cross-border work requires a deep understanding of the dynamics governing employment in Switzerland. For the worker, comparing the Italian and Swiss systems goes beyond mere remuneration, touching upon fundamental aspects such as social security, taxation, and access to services. The discussion scheduled at the Festa della Lucciola follows this path, offering a space for reflection on the daily challenges related to the G permit and the contractual conditions that define the relationship between Swiss companies and employees residing in Italy. It is necessary to consider how the demands put forward by trade unions translate into concrete actions to improve the quality of working life, keeping in mind the specific characteristics of the Canton of Ticino and the regulations governing the daily lives of commuters.

Key points

To best face the challenges of cross-border work, it is essential that every worker has clear tools for managing their career and tax situation. Preparation for discussion meetings, such as the one scheduled for the 'Festa della Lucciola', begins first and foremost with knowing one's contractual conditions and the rights accrued in Switzerland. Workers interested in better understanding their prospects can start by constantly monitoring salary variations and the protections provided by collective labor agreements, which play a decisive role in the Canton of Ticino in defining minimum wages and working conditions.

Procedure for informed management

The first step for those working across the border is correct financial and social security planning. It is advisable to periodically check your payslip to ensure that deductions, such as those for AVS, accident insurance, and professional pension schemes, comply with current regulations. Secondly, it is essential to maintain up-to-date documentation regarding your tax residence, so as to easily access the benefits provided by the agreements between Italy and Switzerland, including the management of the tax credit to avoid double taxation on income. For those wishing to delve into their specific situation, the use of digital tools represents concrete and immediate support.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the objectives of the meeting at the Firefly Festival?
The meeting aims to discuss the prospects of cross-border work and to analyse the state of dialogue between Italy and Switzerland. The debate will see the participation of Italian trade union representatives and the Psi Ticino, with the intention of addressing the issues most felt by workers who cross the border daily to reach their workplace.
Who is taking part in the debate on 9 August?
The debate will be attended by Gigi Bacchetta for Cgil Novara-Vco, Romina Baccaglio for Cisl Piemonte Orientale and Stefano De Grandis for Uil Novara-Vco. Representatives of Psi Ticino are also expected to participate. The event will be moderated by Emanuele Vitale, head of the PD in the Province of Vco.
Why is the issue of frontier work so relevant to the Vco?
The theme is particularly felt in Verbano Cusio Ossola as thousands of workers living in thisarea cross the border daily to serve in Switzerland. The public debate serves to give voice to the instances of this category and to monitor the evolution of the institutional dialogue between the two countries.
