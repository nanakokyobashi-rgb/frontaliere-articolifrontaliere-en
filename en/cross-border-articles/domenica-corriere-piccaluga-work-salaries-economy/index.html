The episode of La domenica del Corriere, airing at 19.00 on Teleticino, will feature in the first part the deputy director of Corriere del Ticino Gianni Righinetti in conversation with the coordinator of the Lega Daniele Piccaluga. It will be an opportunity to assess the state of health of the party and to gauge the pulse of the man, described as having a 'rustic soul', who will have a challenging task ahead: two hot potatoes to handle. Managing relations with the UDC and playing the role of referee in the electoral match that will see Norman Gobbi and Claudio Zali face off.

In the second part, the tone will shift to discuss work, wages, and the economy with a face-off between Cristina Maderni, vice president of the Chamber of Commerce, and Giangiorgio Gargantini, cantonal secretary of UNIA. A debate that promises to be heated and rich in insights into the economic situation of the Canton of Ticino.
