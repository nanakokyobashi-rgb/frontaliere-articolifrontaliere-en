Context

In a nutshell

  • Reorganisation of the Federal Office of Customs

Key facts

  • What: Single customs office
  • Where: Mendrisio and Sopraceneri
  • Body: Federal Office of Customs and Border Security
  • Measure: Reorganisation plan
  • Area: Graubünden merged with the South Region

The measure is part of the reorganization plan of the Federal Office of Customs and Border Security. The project provides for the establishment of a single customs office for Mendrisio and Sopraceneri, included in a broader framework of structural changes that also affect other geographical and administrative areas of the confederation and the cantonal territory. The reorganization directly affects several geographical areas including the Canton of Ticino, specifically Bellinzonese, Locarno, Lugano and Mendrisiotto, in addition to the merger of the Graubünden with the Southern Region. For cross-border workers and those who regularly cross borders, such administrative interventions represent a significant change in the management of local operational structures. The official information published describes an administrative design aimed at optimising territorial flows and skills, modifying the operational map of the historical customs authorities present in the territory. Operational details

By analysing the operational aspects of the new configuration, direct consequences on the personnel and organisational structure of the different departments emerge. Among the decisions envisaged by the plan is the abolition of twenty-five management posts, an incisive measure that reduces the levels of intermediate management within the renovated structure. This cut in managerial staff aims to streamline decision-making procedures, but raises questions about the actual distribution of workload across the remaining operational sites. With regard to the specific situation of the cantonal territory, the source specifies that it is not known whether Ticino will also be affected by this specific suppression of managerial jobs, leaving a phase of uncertainty open for local employees. The repercussions of these decisions are part of the working context of the Canton of Ticino, where the presence of customs offices and specialized personnel constitutes a nodal element for commuting and for the entire cross-border economy linked to trade with Italy.
