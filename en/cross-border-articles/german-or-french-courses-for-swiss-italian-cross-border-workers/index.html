German and French courses for Italian-speaking border workers

Italian-speaking border workers who want to learn German or French in Ticino have several options of schools and online courses available. Some of these schools offer specific funding for Italian-speaking border workers.

According to data from the Statistical Office of the Canton of Ticino (USTI), in 2022, 70% of Italian-speaking border workers residing in Ticino stated that they use German as their working language, while 20% used French.

To learn German or French, Italian-speaking border workers can choose between different schools and online courses. Here are some options available:

  • The Lugano Language School offers German and French courses for Italian-speaking border workers. The courses are structured in modules of 10 hours each and can be followed in person or online.
