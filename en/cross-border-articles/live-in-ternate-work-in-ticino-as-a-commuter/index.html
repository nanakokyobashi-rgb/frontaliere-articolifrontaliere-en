Living in Ternate and working in Ticino as a cross-border commuter is an option that many commuters are considering. With the new cross-border agreement coming into effect on January 1, 2024, many people are evaluating the advantages and disadvantages of this option.

Connections and Travel Times

Travel times vary depending on the border crossing used. For example, the Brogeda border crossing is often used by cross-border commuters living in Ternate. It is important to consider travel times and traffic to plan your daily commute effectively.

Costs and Benefits

The cost of living in Italy is generally lower compared to Ticino. For example, the average cost of an apartment in Ternate is around 800 euros per month, while in Lugano it can exceed 1,500 euros. Even the costs for energy and public services are significantly lower in Italy.

Regulations and Requirements

From January 1, 2024, the new cross-border agreement includes some important changes. Cross-border commuters must have a valid work permit and comply with both Italian and Swiss tax regulations. It is advisable to consult a tax advisor to avoid legal issues.

Operational details

Cost of Living

The cost of living in Italy is generally lower than in Ticino. This can be a significant advantage for frontier workers, who can save on rent, bills and daily expenses. For example, an apartment of 80 square meters in the center of Varese costs an average of 800 euros per month, while in the same district of Lugano the cost rises to 1,500 euros. Electricity and gas bills are also lower in Italy: a family of four spends around 100 euros per month in Italy, compared to 150 euros in Switzerland. However, it is important to also consider the costs of commuting, such as fuel and parking. A commuter who travels 50 kilometers per day can spend around 200 euros per month on fuel, while parking in Ticino can cost between 10 and 30 euros per day.

Recommended Zones

Recommended areas to live in Ternate include those near major border crossings. This reduces travel time and avoids heavy traffic. For example, living in Ternate and working in Mendrisio can reduce travel time to just 20 minutes, compared to the 40 minutes needed to reach Lugano. Other recommended areas include Varese, which offers a good connection to Ticino via the A9 motorway, and Como, which is well connected to Switzerland via the Chiasso border.

Key points

What to Consider Before Moving

Before moving to Ternate, it is important to consider several factors. Among these, travel times, cost of living, and the advantages and disadvantages of commuting. It is also important to evaluate your personal and family needs, such as proximity to schools and services.

Step-by-Step Procedure

1. Evaluate travel times and commuting costs.

  • Ternate is about 20 km from Lugano, which means a travel time of about 30-40 minutes by car, depending on traffic. By train, the journey can take about 45 minutes, with frequent connections.
  • Commuting costs include fuel, tolls, and train tickets. For example, a monthly train pass between Ternate and Lugano costs about 150 CHF.

2. Consider the cost of living in Italy compared to Ticino.

  • The cost of living in Italy is generally lower than in Ticino. For example, renting a two-bedroom apartment in Ternate can cost about 700-900 EUR per month, while the same apartment in Lugano can cost 1,500-2,000 CHF.
  • Food and entertainment costs are also generally lower in Italy.

3. Evaluate the advantages and disadvantages of moving.

  • Advantages: Savings on living costs, more living space, better quality of life in terms of tranquility and safety.
  • Disadvantages: Longer travel times, need to adapt to two tax and regulatory systems, possible language difficulties.

4. Plan the move taking into account your personal and family needs.

  • Evaluate proximity to schools, daycare centers, and healthcare services. Ternate offers elementary and middle schools, but for high schools, it may be necessary to travel.
  • Also consider the availability of recreational and cultural activities for the whole family.
