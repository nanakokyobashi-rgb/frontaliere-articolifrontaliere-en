Key facts

  • What: The salary band of a border ergotherapist in Ticino. - When: From 1 January 2024. - Where: Canton of Ticino. - Who: Frontier ergotherapists. - Amount: About 10% less than in Italy.

The new law on cross-border work, signed on 23 December 2020 and in force from 1 January 2024, provides for a lower wage bracket for cross-border ergotherapists than in Italy. This means that a border ergotherapist in Ticino can earn about 10% less than an Italian ergotherapist with the same qualification and experience.

The pay gap is influenced by various factors, such as seniority, qualification and language. In addition, Italian legislation is not applicable to frontier workers, who are subject to specific legislation.

Concrete examples

For example, a border ergotherapist with 5 years of experience and qualification as an ergotherapist specialising in physiotherapy can earn around CHF 80,000 per year in Ticino, while an Italian ergotherapist with the same qualification and experience can earn around €90,000 per year.
