Lugano bids farewell to Cornaredo Stadium: event on June 4 (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 May 2026

Public celebration for the stadium's 75th anniversary before its replacement with the new AIL Arena in 2026

Context

In brief

  • Farewell party on June 4 for Cornaredo Stadium
  • Picnic, circus, and concert for the entire population
  • New AIL Arena will replace the stadium from 2026-2027

Key facts

  • What: Farewell party for Cornaredo Stadium
  • When: Thursday, June 4, 2024, from 17.00 to 22.00
  • Where: Cornaredo Stadium, Lugano
  • Who: City of Lugano
  • Cost: Free with registration for the picnic

Cornaredo Stadium, a sporting symbol of Lugano, is ready to host its last major event before being replaced by the new AIL Arena. On Thursday, June 4, from 17.00 to 22.00, the city is organizing a party open to everyone to celebrate 75 years of history. The facility, inaugurated on November 25, 1951, has hosted memorable events such as the visit of Pope John Paul II in 1984 and the Cycling World Championships in 1996.

Party program

From 17.00 to 18.00, children aged 5 to 14 will be able to train with the local team players: Mattia Bottani, Mijat Maric, and Jonathan Sabbatini. Subsequently, from 18.30, the field will transform into a large meadow for a picnic with entertainment provided by the City of Lugano. The first 300 registrants via the MyLugano app will receive a free picnic basket. Even without registration, it will be possible to access the stadium for the entire evening, with bar points and a free buffet until it runs out.

The atmosphere will be animated by the artists of Circo Fortuna and, from 20.30, by a concert of the United Soloists Orchestra. In case of bad weather, the event will take place on Sunday, June 7 with the same times.

Operational details

Implications for cross-border workers

The event on June 4th represents a unique opportunity for cross-border workers living and working in Ticino to participate in a historic moment for the city of Lugano. The celebration not only honors the past of the Cornaredo stadium but also looks towards the future with the construction of the new AIL Arena and the sports and events hub.

What to expect

Cross-border workers who decide to participate in the festival can enjoy an afternoon and evening filled with fun and socializing. The picnic on the grass, the circus, and the concert offer a varied program that can interest both families and young people. Additionally, the opportunity to train with local players is a unique experience for children and teenagers.

Comparison with the previous situation

Before the inauguration of the Cornaredo stadium in 1908, a group of young people had created a football field on the Campo Marzio grounds. The construction of the stadium represented a significant step for the city, offering an adequate sports facility for national and international events. The new AIL Arena will continue this tradition, offering modern and functional spaces for sports and cultural events.

Future scenarios

With the construction of the new sports and events hub, Lugano aims to become a reference point for sports and culture not only in Switzerland but also internationally. Cross-border workers will benefit from new entertainment and cultural event opportunities, contributing to improving the quality of life in the region.

Key points

What to do to participate

To participate in the farewell party at Cornaredo Stadium, follow these steps:

1. Sign up for the picnic: Download the MyLugano app and sign up to receive a free picnic basket. The first 300 registrants will be entitled to the basket. 2. Book the soccer training: If you have children between the ages of 5 and 14, email [email protected] to book a spot in the training session with local players. 3. Plan your schedule: Plan your day based on the event program. Don't forget to check the weather forecast and be ready for any date changes.

What to bring

  • For the picnic: If you haven't received a free basket, bring your own food and drinks for the picnic. There will be bar points and a free buffet until it runs out.
  • For the soccer training: Bring sportswear and suitable shoes for the children participating in the training.
  • For the concert: Bring a blanket or a folding chair to sit comfortably during the concert.

Where to go

The event will take place at Cornaredo Stadium, easily reachable by public transport or by car. If you come by car, make sure to park in one of the nearby parking lots to avoid traffic problems.
