On Tuesday, October 13, from 14.30 to 17.30, the Conference Hall of Villa Recalcati, at piazza Libertà 1 in Varese, will host the meeting dedicated to the new developments introduced by Decree 96/2026. The title brings together the day's two main strands: equal pay and pay transparency, with specific attention to the opportunities and challenges for businesses and workers in the province of Varese.

With the transposition of the European directive, the decree marks a new phase: it places greater emphasis on the principle of equal treatment and strengthens the role of transparency in employment relationships. The change involves businesses, workers and social representatives. Beyond the compliance requirements, the legislation offers opportunities to make pay criteria and career paths fairer and easier to understand.

The meeting program

Participant registration begins at 14. Half an hour later, the institutional greetings and the introductory address by Anna Danesi, principal Equality Adviser, open the proceedings. At 15, Lilli Casano, associate professor of labour law at the University of Insubria, presents the regulatory framework following the transposition of the European directive.

A round table follows with representatives of Cgil Varese, Cisl dei Laghi, Uil Lombardia, Confapi Varese, Confindustria Varese and Confcommercio Uniascom of the province of Varese. The discussion brings together trade union and employers' organizations around the practical consequences of the new rules.

The program continues at 16.15 with testimonials from CB Ferrari, Carlsberg Italia and Seconda Strada, which share experiences and reflections on the organization and management of human resources. At 17, time is set aside for questions, followed by closing remarks from Patrizia Pancanti, Deputy Equality Adviser. Moderation is entrusted to Michele Mancino, deputy editor of Varesenews.

The program therefore takes the discussion from the regulatory framework to the positions of the social partners, and finally to three company experiences, maintaining the focus on the province of Varese and on relations between businesses and workers.

Operational details

The meaning for businesses and workers

The new development is presented as a step concerning how employment relationships are interpreted. Decree 96/2026 does not merely refer to equal pay: according to the source, it strengthens the principle of equal treatment and makes pay transparency more central.

For workers, this approach brings the clarity of pay criteria and career paths to the forefront. The issue therefore concerns not only the result shown on the payslip, but also how the criteria accompanying professional growth are described and understood. The source links this objective to the possibility of making employment relationships fairer and more transparent.

The first perspective is regulatory. The second concerns industrial relations: the joint presence of trade unions and employer representatives brings the discussion between different positions to the forefront.
