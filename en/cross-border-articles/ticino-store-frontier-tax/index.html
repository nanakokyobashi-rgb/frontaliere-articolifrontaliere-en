For the border worker who works or intends to work as a store manager in the Canton of Ticino, the first data to be read in the paycheck is taxation: the income tax at source is withheld only in Switzerland.

The Italian ratification took place with Law 83 of 13 June 2023. Italy avoids double taxation through the tax credit indicated in the EC framework of 730. The rule therefore excludes the idea of a deduction on the same income in both countries: the withdrawal at source is Swiss, while the credit operates in the Italian declaration.

The distinction introduced by the tax calendar

The decisive step for a frontier worker is not the name of the commercial role, but the date that determines membership in the indicated regimes. Those who were already frontiersmen before 17 July 2023 are considered old frontiersmen. For them there is an exemption of €7,500 and a transitional regime valid from 2024 to 2033.

For new frontier workers, the excess indicated is €10,000. The two thresholds are not interchangeable: they describe different audiences and must be read together with the date of entry into the regime. The data does not change the job description as a store manager, but changes the tax reference with which the income is interpreted.

Within the framework of border workers, the G. LAMal permit also appears. It concerns health insurance and not income tax; G border workers are granted the right of option. The healthcare choice must therefore be read separately from the tax withholding. To interpret the salary in Ticino, the distinction between source tax, social contributions and health insurance is more useful than an indistinct sum of the items.
