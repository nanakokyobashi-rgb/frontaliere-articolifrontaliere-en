The comeback after the first set

Erreci Busto Arsizio turned the midweek round into another leg of his clean sweep: Marchiaro's team beat Ferraro Smi Roma 3-1, after losing the first set, and remains full-scoring in the women's A2 championship.

For Futura Volley Giovani it is the third league game and the third three-point victory. After Perugia and Marsala, Rome also goes to the carpet: the whites climb to 9 and remain paired at Costa Volpino, waiting for the Altafratte match. The next opponent will be Costa Volpino at the Soevis Arena, on Sunday 18 October.

The Ferraro Smi starts better and wins the first set for 22-25, with Perovic in evidence; Edinara, returned from the injury, still offers a good contribution by scoring 5 points. Busto reacts in the second partial, won 25-22, with the Brazilian protagonist of the attacks from place 2. The finale also features Marconato, who was instrumental in repelling the last attempt at a Roman comeback.

The 25-22 is repeated in the third set. Šiftar grows with the passing of exchanges, while Cosi becomes impassable on the wall. Rome, having failed to return to the game, loses consistency and leaves the Futura free field in the fourth quarter, closed 25-14 with another dominant test of the Slovenian crusher.

Marchiaro explained that the team expected an aggressive Roma, but had started the contracted race, almost as if Busto had to lead the game. The coach then reminded the team of the need to start from scratch each time, without looking at the standings, and to conquer one set at a time.
