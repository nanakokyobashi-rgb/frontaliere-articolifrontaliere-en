Lugano - "No amount has been taken from the damage of the association". After the statement issued by Disti - the association that represents the large Ticino distribution - which announced the removal of President Enzo Lucibello for "improper use of association funds", the reply of the person directly concerned arrives. Lucibello has stated that the allegations are unfounded and that he has never personally enriched or fraudulently used the association's funds.

Regulatory context

The allegation of misuse of funds is in line with Swiss and Ticino regulations governing the administration of associations. According to Article 10 of the Federal Act on the Law of Associations (LDA) 2005, the funds of associations must be used exclusively for statutory purposes. Any misuse may lead to sanctions, including revocation of the legal entity.

Lucibello's reaction

Lucibello, former president of Disti, made it clear that he played his role in the Ticino association with the utmost transparency and professionalism. He stressed that all its operations were carried out in the exclusive interest of the association and its members, strictly adhering to the mandates received and the established internal procedures. He specified that no amount was subtracted from the damage of the association and that every transaction he carried out was traceable, documented and carried out in plain sight.

Concrete actions

Lucibello, president of the Associazione Ticinese per lo Sviluppo Professionale (ATSP), said that he had made available to "the internal control bodies and the competent authorities" every accounting document and element useful to clarify the nature and purpose of the decisions taken during his mandate. "In light of the above, I oppose any reading that implies my personal enrichment or a fraudulent use of the association's resources," he said.

📊 Regulatory and financial context Ticino, like other Italian regions, is subject to regional and national financial regulations, including regional law 28/2000 and national legislative decree 231/2000.
