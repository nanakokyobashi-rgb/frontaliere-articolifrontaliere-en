The Institute of Economic Research (IRE) of the USI has updated the forecasts on unemployment in Ticino, reporting an upward trend for the coming months. According to calculations based on SECO criteria, the cantonal unemployment rate is expected to reach 3.31% in the fourth quarter of 2026. The projection indicates a further deterioration for the beginning of the following year, with an estimate that rises to 3.45% in the first three months of 2027.

Recent data analysis

These estimates, prepared by the Observatory of Economic Dynamics of THE IRE, show a negative trend. In the third quarter of 2026, the unemployment rate in Canton Ticino stood at 2.87%, marking an increase of 0.3 percentage points compared to the same period of the previous year. However, the institute invites you to interpret these numbers with caution because of the margin of uncertainty inherent in forecasting models.

For the period from October to December, the IRE defined a confidence interval ranging from 2.93% to 3.7%. As for the first quarter of 2027, the forecast fork widens, moving between 2.93% and 3.97%. In any case, the model forecasts values higher than those recorded in the previous year, confirming a phase of fragility for the local labour market. This scenario directly impacts those who work in the territory, making it necessary to carefully analyse employment dynamics for those who own a permesso G or reside in the canton.

Operational details

Disparity between categories of residents

The analysis of the data for the third quarter reveals profound divergences between the different categories of residents in Ticino. The unemployment rate among Swiss citizens remained contained, stopping at 2.20%. On the contrary, the situation is decidedly more critical for foreign residents, among whom the rate reached 4.17%. This data shows that foreigners are significantly more exposed to the risk of loss of employment.

On an annual basis, the increase in unemployment was 0.2 percentage points for Swiss citizens and 0.5 percentage points for foreign residents. This disparity suggests a greater vulnerability of non-Swiss workers to the economic fluctuations of the Ticino market. Those working as frontier workers need to monitor these trends, as rising unemployment among foreign residents can indirectly affect the competitiveness and stability of contracts in the private sector.

Gender and age groups

The breakdown by gender and age also shows warning signs. Youth unemployment is growing compared to the previous year, reaching 5.07%. Regarding gender distinction, the rate was found to be 2.77% for women and 2.9% for men. A relevant figure concerns the composition of the unemployed workforce: women represent 44.5% of the total unemployed. This share is up from the previous quarter, although it remains lower than the value recorded a year earlier. Key points

Occupational risk management

Faced with an unemployment rate expected to rise to 3.45% by the beginning of 2027, it is essential that the worker in Ticino adopts prevention strategies. In the event of loss of employment, the unemployment management procedure requires immediate alignment with the competent bodies for the disbursement of benefits. For resident workers or frontier workers, it is essential to verify their contribution position and accrued rights.

A border worker who is in an unstable situation should first check the validity of their work permit and the conditions of the contract. If employment ceases, registration with the employment offices is the first step necessary to access benefits and to start the search for a new job. It is advisable to keep your professional profile updated and constantly monitor the opportunities offered by local companies.
