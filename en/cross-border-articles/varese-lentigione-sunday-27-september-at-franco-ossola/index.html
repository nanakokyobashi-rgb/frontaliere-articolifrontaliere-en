Varese return to Masnago after securing their first league win away at Pontedera. The red-and-whites now seek further confirmation in front of their home crowd, with a fixture scheduled at the Franco Ossola for Sunday 27 September at 15.

Opposing them will be Lentigione, an Emilia-based team that opened the league season with three consecutive wins. The run ended in the home match lost 1-0 against Cittadella Modena. Varese are therefore preparing to host a team coming off a negative result, but which had recorded three wins in its first outings.

A test of maturity at Masnago

For the team led by Andrea Ciceri, the match represents a test of maturity. The coach linked the first win of the season to the need to continue along the same path. In his assessment of the matches played so far, Ciceri distinguished results from performances: the contexts, qualities and strategies of the opponents have changed, while his assessment remains positive of the way Varese have held their own on the pitch.

The home fixture thus takes on a precise meaning in the coach's words. Varese want to turn their home stadium into a constant, playing there more consistently and seeking a flow of play tied to the dimensions of the pitch and the spaces to attack. The coach did warn, however, that the challenge will be different from the previous outings, because Lentigione offer aggression, forward play and attacks into space.

«We are facing a very aggressive team that plays forward and attacks the spaces».

The matchup therefore puts Varese up against an opponent with an attacking identity described directly by Ciceri. The team will have to contend with this approach in a match that comes after the win at Pontedera and gives the red-and-whites the chance to build on the result. The transition from the away trip to the match at the Franco Ossola is the central point ahead of the game: Varese return to their home ground and try to make their performances in front of the Masnago crowd more consistent.

Operational details

Lentigione's profile and Varese's choices

An opponent built for professional football

Ciceri's assessment of Lentigione starts from one precise fact: in the coach's view, it is a top-level team. Over the last two years, he explained, the Emilian side has lost very little. Last week brought the first defeat of the season, after a perfect start.

The coach also recalled that last year Lentigione fought for the title until the final matchdays. The club is described as structured to aim for professional football, with very valuable players chosen very well. Completing the picture is coach Pedrelli, whom Ciceri credits with well-defined playing principles.

According to Ciceri, the greatest dangers do not concern only the quality of the Emilian side. Lentigione comes into the match wanting to bounce back after its latest defeat. To face it, Varese will have to play under pressure, deny depth, compete for second balls and exploit the little space the opponents habitually allow.

Midfield options and the Cogliati situation

The coach also spoke about the arrival of two new midfielders. Their presence, he explained, does not create difficulties; on the contrary, it gives him and the team a greater number of options. Internal competition stems from the way the players train, always at high intensity.

Carbone has been with Varese since the start of preseason. Ciceri has seen him training well and considers the opportunity he is being given deserved. Piantoni, on the other hand, arrived somewhat unexpectedly at the last moment. His addition was described as targeted: besides fitting the team's needs in terms of his characteristics, he is an under-age player and immediately took the field because an element with those qualities was needed.

Cogliati's situation remains to be assessed. The player has a rib problem that bothers him somewhat, but he is trying to recover. The decision on his availability will be made at the very last moment, because he is considered a fundamental player for Varese. The assessment of his physical condition thus adds to the midfield choices and the preparation for a match in which Ciceri is demanding attention to second balls, depth and the available space.
