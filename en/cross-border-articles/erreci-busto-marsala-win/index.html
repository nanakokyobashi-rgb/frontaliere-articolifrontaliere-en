The 3-0 that earns top spot

Futura Volley Giovani won its first away match of the season in Marsala with a 3-0 that awards three points and keeps Erreci at the top of the Serie A2 standings with a perfect record, together with Costa Volpino and Altafratte. The result was clear-cut, but the match was balanced for two sets.

The team coached by Marchiaro had to chase from beyond the halfway point of the first set, at 20-16, and the second, at 21-19. In both cases, Futura found the final burst: 23-25 in the first and 23-25 in the second. In the third set, the red-and-whites closed it out 17-25, confirming their success against a courageous Sigel that was unable to reopen the match.

The standout player was Mija Šiftar, the MVP with 20 points, 53% in attack and 3 blocks. The statistics gave Erreci 44% in attack, while positive reception was 53% and perfect reception 23%. Marsala finished with 54% positive reception, 19% perfect reception and 36% in attack. Both teams recorded 5 blocks. These figures describe a hard-fought contest at the start and a more one-sided third set.

The win also came in a complex lineup situation. Marchiaro preferred not to risk the Brazilian Edinara, who was hit in the eye by a ball in training and was cleared to return to the court only on Friday. Gaia Moroni, meanwhile, is preparing to undergo arthroscopic meniscus surgery and will be out for some time.

The team returned from Sicily overnight and immediately resumed training. The next appointment is at Soevis Arena di Castellanza, on Wednesday, October 14 at 20,30, against Ferraro Smi Roma, who lost at home 1-3 to Costa Volpino. Edinara is expected to return for the match against Roma.
