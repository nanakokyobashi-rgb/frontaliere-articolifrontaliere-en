The regional councillor Romana Dell 'Erba has been appointed secretary of the Special Commission on Health and Safety in the Workplace. The appointment was made official at today's session at Palazzo Pirelli.

The Special Commission on Health and Safety at Work is a body that monitors and improves working conditions in the Swiss cantons. Its mission is to ensure the safety and health of workers in all sectors, from manufacturing to construction, from trade to the tertiary sector.

Regional Councillor Romana Dell 'Erba accepted the appointment with a sense of responsibility, as stated during today's session. His experience and expertise will be crucial for the Special Commission, which will face important challenges such as occupational risk management and accident prevention.
