Icesport Varese: Open Day for over 100 kids on the ice

On Saturday 5 September, Icesport Varese's Open Day attracted over one hundred children and young people onto the ice, with most groups nearing capacity.

On Saturday, September 5, the Open Day promoted by Icesport Varese brought more than one hundred children and young people onto the ice. In the afternoon, participants were able to lace up their skates, take their first steps and get to know the activities of the Varese club up close.

The response had an immediate effect on registrations. Two weeks before the courses began, Icesport is recording a boom in registrations and most groups are nearing capacity. Among those enrolled are former athletes who have chosen to resume their activity and young people taking their first steps in figure skating and synchronized skating.

«We are very satisfied with the response we received from the city of Varese — explain Icesport's management —. It is a great satisfaction to see so many families choose our club again and, at the same time, to see so many children and young people approach skating for the first time.»

A start on two fronts

The Open Day thus marks the start of the new sports season in terms of course participation. The club is experiencing a period of strong interest both from those returning to the ice and those trying it for the first time. The reference remains Icesport Varese and the work carried out over the years, described by its management as the foundation of a path appreciated by the city.

At the same time, the competitive sector is already getting underway. Over the weekend, Bolzano hosts the first event of the Italian Élite category championship, defined as the highest level of national figure skating. For Icesport Varese, in the Junior category, Alessandra Trusiani takes to the ice.

For the Varese athlete, this marks her return to competitive action after the hiatus that began in January, linked to a study stay in Canada. In Bolzano, she will present a renewed short program and a free program refined compared with last season. The news thus brings together two distinct moments: approaching the sport through the courses and resuming high-level competitive activity.
