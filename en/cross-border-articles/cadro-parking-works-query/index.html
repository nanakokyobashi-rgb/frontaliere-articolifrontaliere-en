Chart, question on works at the parking lot in via Scuole (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 9 August 2026

The group Avanti con Ticino&Lavoro questions the Municipality of Lugano about the lack of green islands in the renovation work between Via delle Scuole and Via Lanaggio.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Remaking of car parks between Via delle Scuole and Via Lanaggio in Cadro.
  • New asphalt pavement completed without green islands.
  • Question by Luisella Fumasoli Petrini (Avanti con Ticino&Lavoro).

Key facts

  • What: Question to the Municipality of Lugano.
  • Where: Parking between Via delle Scuole and Via Lanaggio, Cadro.
  • Who: Luisella Fumasoli Petrini (Avanti con Ticino&Lavoro).
  • Subject: Lack of trees and green islands in the renovation.

The construction site located between Via delle Scuole and Via Lanaggio, in the Cadro district, has recently undergone renovation work on the car park. The intervention saw the laying of a new asphalted pavement. However, the project did not provide for the planting of trees or the creation of green islands, elements considered fundamental to mitigate the phenomenon of summer overheating. Luisella Fumasoli Petrini, first signatory for the Avanti con Ticino&Lavoro group, has formalised a request for clarification addressed to the Municipality of Lugano. The act highlights the need to balance the functional aspects of public spaces with environmental and climatic needs. In particular, the councillor emphasises that, in a period characterised by constantly rising summer temperatures, the effects of urban heat islands are now a known and documented fact. Operational details

Analysis of design choices and climate impact

The question raises technical and strategic questions about the management of public spaces in the Municipality of Lugano. The critical move concerns the absence of green solutions in a context where asphalt flooring naturally tends to absorb and retain heat, contributing to the local microclimate. Councilwoman Fumasoli Petrini explicitly asks the Municipality of Lugano to justify the choice not to have included trees within the renovation project. A central point of the act concerns the existence of municipal directives or guidelines that impose, when designing new parking spaces or renovating existing ones, the obligation to include public green elements such as climate mitigation. This is an aspect that closely affects land management and urban planning, with particular attention to the quality of public spaces available to the community.

The document also requires evaluating the possibility of future corrective interventions, aimed at laying trees or adopting alternative solutions aimed at creating shady areas. This scenario opens a reflection on the management of public assets and the effectiveness of investments in infrastructure works. Key points

Inquiry procedure and next steps

The process followed by the Avanti con Ticino&Lavoro group is part of the institutional procedures provided for by the municipal regulations of Lugano for the control of the work of the Municipality. Through this tool, municipal councillors have the right to request official explanations on the administrative and technical choices made by the executive. The question filed by Luisella Fumasoli Petrini obliges the Municipality to provide a written answer, which must clarify the technical and economic reasons that led to the exclusion of green islands from the Cadro project. The institutional response process allows us to clarify the compliance of the project with current regulations on planning and the environment.

No precise timelines are currently specified for the receipt of the response by the Municipality of Lugano, nor are immediate corrective actions indicated on the work already completed. However, the political pressure exerted through the parliamentary act aims to stimulate a broader reflection on the future planning of city parking. For citizens and stakeholders, following the evolution of this practice means understanding if and how the Municipality intends to change its approach to urban sustainability in similar contexts. The matter remains open and awaits a formal response that can define the intentions of the authority

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the subject of the question presented to Cadro?
The question concerns the lack of trees and green islands in the project to renovate the car parks located between Via delle Scuole and Via Lanaggio a Cadro, following the laying of a new asphalt pavement.
Who presented the parliamentary act?
The deed was presented to the Municipality of Lugano by Luisella Fumasoli Petrini, the first signatory for the Avanti con Ticino&Lavoro group.
What are the specific requests made to the City Hall?
The Municipality is asked to explain why no green islands have been provided, if there are directives on the insertion of trees, if corrective interventions are planned to reduce heat and if a permeable flooring would have been a more suitable choice.
