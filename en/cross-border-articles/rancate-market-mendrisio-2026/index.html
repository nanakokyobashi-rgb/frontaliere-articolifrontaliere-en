Rancate: monthly neighborhood market opens (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 5 May 2026

In Rancate, Mendrisio, the neighborhood market has opened, an initiative promoted by the City of Mendrisio and Pro Senectute Ticino and Moesano

Context

In brief

  • New neighborhood market in Rancate, Mendrisio
  • Initiative promoted by the City of Mendrisio and Pro Senectute
  • First event on May 22, 2026

Key facts

  • What: Neighborhood market
  • When: First event on May 22, 2026, from 8:30 AM to 11:30 AM
  • Where: Rancate, Mendrisio
  • Who: City of Mendrisio and Pro Senectute Ticino and Moesano
  • Amount: Not specified

In Rancate, a neighborhood of Mendrisio, the Neighborhood Market has been launched, an initiative that promises to become a regular event for the local community. The market was developed through collaboration between the City of Mendrisio and the Pro Senectute Ticino and Moesano Foundation. This project is part of the activities of "La Dispensa," an initiative launched in January 2025 by the City of Mendrisio and the Rancate District Commission, with methodological support from Pro Senectute Ticino and Moesano.

"La Dispensa," located at the foot of the town hall, has established itself as a socio-aggregative space capable of fostering social cohesion and solidarity among the neighborhood's residents and the entire municipality. Throughout 2025 and the first months of 2026, numerous free activities open to all ages have been organized, including neighborhood dinners and aperitifs, playful moments, and cultural initiatives carried out in collaboration with local associations.

Since May of this year, there has also been a collaboration with the L'Orto di Muzzano association, which every Friday morning offers a stall with their own produce. The initiative has been a great success, becoming a regular appointment for many people in Rancate and surrounding areas, who take advantage not only to shop but also to share convivial moments.

It is on the wave of this enthusiasm that the Neighborhood Market is born, a new monthly event that will see the participation of local associations and foundations, but also of citizens, who will be able to set up their own stall upon registration. The first event is scheduled for Friday, May 22, from 8:30 AM to 11:30 AM. For information and reservations, you can contact the number 058 688 36 28 or write to the address ladispensa@mendrisio.ch.

Implications for the community

The Neighborhood Market represents an important opportunity for the community of Rancate and surrounding areas. Not only does it offer the possibility to purchase local and artisan products, but it also allows for socializing and strengthening ties among residents. This type of initiative is particularly relevant in a context like that of Rancate, where social cohesion is a fundamental value.

For cross-border workers who work in Switzerland and reside in Italy, the Neighborhood Market can be an opportunity to further integrate into the local community, actively participating in neighborhood life and making new friends. Furthermore, purchasing local products can be a way to support the local economy and reduce the environmental impact related to transportation.

How to participate

To participate in the Neighborhood Market as a vendor, you need to register by contacting the number 058 688 36 28 or writing to the address ladispensa@mendrisio.ch. It is important to provide all the necessary information for setting up the stall and to respect the indicated deadlines.

For visitors, on the other hand, no registration is required. Just show up at the market on the day of the event to discover the products and initiatives on offer. It is advisable to arrive early to have the opportunity to purchase the most appealing products and participate in the proposed activities.
