Key facts

Varese achieved their playoff goal with a hard-fought victory against Lavagnese, already relegated to Promozione. The match, ending 3-2, saw Varese fight until the last minute to secure a spot in the playoffs. The decisive goal came from a penalty in stoppage time, scored by Tentoni. Coach Andrea Ciceri's team finished the season in fourth place with 56 points and will face Biellese in the first round of the playoffs. The match was intense, with Varese overcoming several difficulties to prevail over Lavagnese.

The match

The match started with Varese in possession, but it was Lavagnese who took the lead with a goal by Daniello at the 19'. Varese reacted with a goal by Sovogui at the 32', equalizing the score. In the second half, De Ponti put Varese ahead, but a penalty by D'Antoni temporarily turned the result around. Varese's final assault led to the decisive goal by Tentoni, sealing the 3-2 final score.

The playoffs

Varese will face Biellese in the first round of the playoffs. Although the playoffs do not count for promotion, they represent an important goal for the team and the staff. The match against Biellese will be an opportunity to demonstrate Varese's value and continue to fight for sporting glory.
