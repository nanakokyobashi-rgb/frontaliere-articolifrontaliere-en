MILAN - Yann Sommer seems set to experience his final weeks in Appiano Gentile. Inter, considering the age of the Swiss goalkeeper, would not be inclined to confirm him. Despite the league title, the contribution of the former Swiss national team player has been deemed insufficient by former goalkeeper Emiliano Viviano. Appearing live on SportiumFUN, the 40-year-old commented: 'Sommer failed, but behind the board. I have immense respect for him: he was a fantastic goalkeeper, even undervalued. However, this year he gave almost nothing to Inter. I say this with all possible admiration.' Viviano - 251 appearances in Serie A with Bologna, Sampdoria, SPAL, Palermo, and Fiorentina - also expressed doubts about Sommer's backup: 'I have more than one concern about Josep Martinez, not so much from a technical standpoint but for other aspects. To be a goalkeeper with consistency at Inter, you need many qualities, and Sommer had them. Martinez, when he played, I liked, but it's not the same thing. There's a joker on the market, and I don't understand why there's no rush to sign him: it's Alisson. Juventus? Do you really think that, under equal conditions, he would choose that destination? Only a fool would go to Juventus instead of Inter: on one side there's a team that has won three league titles and reached two finals, on the other side they struggle. Akanji, for example, chose Inter and not Milan.'
