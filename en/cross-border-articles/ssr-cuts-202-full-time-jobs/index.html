SSR announced on Thursday the elimination of another 202 full-time jobs. The decision includes 173 cuts in Operations. The other 29 positions concern Human Resources, Finance, Communication & Public Affairs and Staff.

The measure will enable Società svizzera di radiotelevisione to save 41 million francs. The measure is part of recent political decisions, including the reduction of the license fee to 300 francs. As a result of these choices, the company is required to save around 270 million by 2029, of which 80 million already in 2027. The figure of 41 million concerns this measure, while the other amounts indicate overall targets over different time horizons.

The section on 2027

With the measures announced last month for the regional units RSI, RTR, RTS and SRF, SSR says it has fully realized the savings planned for 2027. For RSI, the figure indicated is a reduction of up to 12 positions in 2027. The source does not specify the distribution of the positions among locations, functions or categories of workers.

The categories do not have the same numerical weight: 173 positions are concentrated in Operations, while 29 are distributed among the four areas listed. The text does not provide an internal breakdown of the 29 and does not assign the 202 to individual locations. The overall figure refers to full-time jobs, without a list of people or duties involved.

Personnel management will, where possible, rely on natural turnover and retirements. According to SSR, this approach will absorb around half of the reductions in Operations and support sectors. However, it will not be enough to avoid all the effects: layoffs and early retirements are described as inevitable. The company will apply its social plan.

For those who live near the border and work in Ticino, the verifiable fact is therefore a reorganization that also includes RSI; it is not possible, however, to determine from the source how many cross-border workers are involved or which individual conditions will change.
