Del Ponte Hospital: no block for the third batch (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 13 September 2026

Asst Seven Lakes denies the blockade of the third lot of the Del Ponte Hospital of Varese. Loans of 13.6 million already in cash and work started.

Context

In brief

  • Asst Sette Laghi replies to the open letter from the Municipality
  • The blocking of the third lot is categorically denied
  • Financing of 13.6 million euros already in the cash register
  • Development of the maternal and child hub regularly underway

Key facts

  • What: Institutional reply on the future of the Del Ponte Hospital
  • When: September 2026
  • Where: Varese, Filippo Del Ponte Hospital
  • Who: Asst Sette Laghi and Palazzo Estense
  • Amount: 13.6 million euros

The Varese healthcare company Asst Sette Laghi has issued an official note to directly respond to the open letter delivered to the regional Welfare department by the mayor of Varese and the executive board of Palazzo Estense. At the center of the debate is the future of the Filippo Del Ponte Hospital and, in particular, the construction and completion of the third lot of the Varese healthcare facility. The healthcare company's reply was clear and left no room for interpretation regarding the rumors circulating in recent days.

In the official note released by the Varese health management, it is defined as completely false that a decision has been made not to complete the third lot of the Del Ponte Hospital. On the contrary, the company strongly reiterates that the development path of the maternal-child hub continues without interruption and that a significant part of the intervention already enjoys secure financial coverage. Specifically, the resources amount to 13.6 million euros, are already present in the cash register, and have partly already been used for the first interventions launched in the field.

…

Operational details

Analysis of structural and logistical interventions

The management of the remaining portion of the multi-million funding is enabling the healthcare company to plan and launch the preparatory works essential for the construction of a new building within the hospital complex. In operational detail, the plan involves the creation of a new facility dedicated to staff locker rooms and the company canteen. These are infrastructural works of absolute technical necessity, as they are preparatory to the subsequent phases that will involve the demolition of the Octagon Pavilion and the start of construction of the new building foreseen by the original master plan.

The Regional Welfare Department and Asst Sette Laghi itself unanimously share the extreme strategic importance held by the Del Ponte Hospital in the Lombardy and interregional healthcare panorama. The structure is not simply considered as a local pediatric hub, but as a true highly specialized regional reference center for the protection of women's and children's health. The clinical skills and professionalism present within the hospital represent an absolute excellence, a source of pride for the healthcare company which publicly acknowledges the historical role played over the years by civil society, voluntary associations, and all the subjects who have concretely supported the growth and enhancement of the healthcare offer.

Key points

Schedule of works and future prospects

The structural modernization program of the Del Ponte Hospital is already fully underway and operational on multiple fronts. The new medically assisted reproduction (PMA) facility has been completed, the opening of the new gynecological outpatient clinics is confirmed by the end of 2026, the start of preparatory construction sites for the new building is imminent, and the redevelopment work on the facade of the Michelangelo pavilion will begin within the current year with completion scheduled for the end of 2027. Asst Sette Laghi, benefiting from the constant support of the Lombardy Region, intends to continue building the future of local healthcare step by step, based on the firm conviction that every investment aimed at the health of women, newborns, children, and adolescents represents a fundamental investment for the well-being and development of the entire local community and its catchment areas.

The administrative and managerial action of the healthcare company will therefore continue under the banner of transparency and institutional collaboration with local and regional authorities, maintaining a strong focus on compliance with the announced timelines for each active construction site within the Varese hospital facility. Citizens and local users can thus rely on a detailed schedule backed by funds already approved and available in the company's accounts.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the location of Asst Sette Laghi on the third lot of the Del Ponte Hospital?
Asst Sette Laghi categorically denied the rumours of a blockade or an alleged renunciation of the completion of the third batch of the Filippo Del Ponte Hospital in Varese. The health company reiterated that the development path of the maternal-infant pole continues without interruption and that the intervention enjoys certain financial coverage.
How much funding is available for the health facility?
The economic resources amount to 13.6 million euros. These funds are already in cash and have already been partly used for the first interventions launched in the field, as well as being scheduled to be used in the next two and a half years according to a defined timetable.
What are the main interventions envisaged in the development plan?
Thanks to the available funds, the new headquarters of medically assisted procreation (PMA) and the new gynaecological surgeries, which will be operational by the end of 2026, have been built. It is also planned to install a new Magnetic Resonance in the autumn of 2026, remake the façade of the Michelangelo pavilion between 2026 and 2027, and build a new home for changing rooms and canteens.
