Requirements to work as a gardener in Ticino

To work as a gardener in Ticino, frontier workers must possess the G permit, which is a type of residence permit for frontier workers. The G permit is issued by the Immigration Office of the Canton of Ticino and is valid for one year. Border workers must submit an application for a G permit within 14 days of the date of entry into Switzerland.

Health Insurance (LAMal)

Health insurance (LAMal) is mandatory for all workers in Switzerland, including frontier workers. This means that the employer must insure the worker against medical expenses in the event of illness or accident at work. LAMal also covers the costs of care and rehabilitation.
