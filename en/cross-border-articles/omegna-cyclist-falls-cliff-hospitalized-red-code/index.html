Cyclist falls down cliff near Omegna, rushed to hospital (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 May 2026

A cyclist is in serious condition after falling off a cliff near Omegna. They were rushed to Novara hospital in a code red emergency.

Context

In brief

  • Cyclist plunges into ravine near Omegna
  • Red code admission to Novara
  • Intervention of Firefighters and Alpine Rescue

Key facts

  • What: Cyclist falls into embankment
  • When: Saturday, May 2, 2026
  • Where: Route from Omegna to Nonio, Brolo hamlet
  • Who: Firefighters, Civil Alpine Rescue
  • Amount: Not specified

In the late morning of Saturday, May 2, 2026, a cyclist fell into a ravine alongside the path he was following from Omegna towards the provincial road to Nonio, Brolo hamlet. The cyclist's conditions are particularly serious, requiring transport by helicopter ambulance to the "Maggiore della Carità" hospital in Novara. The intervention was classified as red code, with the collaboration of firefighters from the capital and the Omegna detachment, as well as specialists from the Civil Alpine Rescue.

Rescue operations

Rescue operations were complex and required the intervention of multiple entities. Firefighters and Civil Alpine Rescue worked together to recover the cyclist and transport him to the hospital. The cyclist's conditions were described as critical, necessitating immediate transport by helicopter ambulance to receive necessary care.
