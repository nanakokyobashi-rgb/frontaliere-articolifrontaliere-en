Switzerland beats Czech Republic in penalty shootout, salvaging the weekend (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 May 2026

The Swiss national hockey team wins in a penalty shootout against the Czech Republic, ending the weekend on a positive note after two defeats.

Context

In brief

  • Switzerland beats Czechia on penalties
  • Weekend salvaged after two defeats
  • No goals in regular time

Key facts

  • What: Switzerland's victory on penalties against the Czech Republic
  • When: 3 May 2026
  • Where: Budvar Arena
  • Who: Swiss national ice hockey team
  • Score: 2 out of 5 for Switzerland, 1 out of 5 for the Czech Republic

After the defeats against Sweden and Finland, Jan Cadieux's men find success in their final match of the Fortuna Games. Stemming the defensive bleeding of the previous days (13 goals conceded in two matches), Switzerland smiles again, beating the Czech Republic on penalties, at the end of a match that sees regular time and overtime end without goals. As in the two previous matches, in their third (and final) engagement at the Fortuna Games, Jan Cadieux's men show some flaws in the transition phase. The good news is that, unlike what happened on Thursday against Sweden and then again on Saturday against Finland in the match lost 3-5 by Thürkauf and company, this time the score at the first break is still 0-0. And, indeed, at times it is the Swiss game that is preferred. It is therefore no coincidence that the busiest of the two goalkeepers in the first twenty minutes is Korenar, who is particularly good at 11'57" to thwart a solo shot by Herzog.
