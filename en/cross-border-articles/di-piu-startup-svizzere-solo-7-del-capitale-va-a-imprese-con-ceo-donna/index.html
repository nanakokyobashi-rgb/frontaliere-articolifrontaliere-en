The problem of women's businesses

According to the study conducted by the investor association SECA, companies with female CEOs receive only 7% of the risk capital raised in Switzerland. This means that women represent only a small part of the CEOs of Swiss startups. For example, in Lugano, the economic capital of the Canton of Ticino, only 12% of companies with female CEOs receive capital at risk, compared to 88% of companies with male CEOs. In Bellinzona, on the other hand, the share of companies with female CEOs that receive risk capital is even lower, only 5%.

The causes of the problem

According to experts, the causes of the problem are multiple. First, women are often underrepresented in financial institutions, which are responsible for the distribution of risk capital. In addition, women are often more hesitant to apply for funding for their businesses, due to fear of being rejected or judged unsuitable. How much venture capital do companies with female CEOs receive in Switzerland?
According to a study by the investor association SECA, only 7% of the risk capital raised in Switzerland goes to companies with female CEOs. This percentage is very low and shows significant regional disparities: in Lugano it reaches 12%, while in Bellinzona it falls to 5% of companies with female CEOs who receive such funding.
What are the main strategies to increase funding for women-led startups in Switzerland?
Strategies include increasing female representation in financial institutions and improving communication with female CEOs. Specific funding programmes, training and support for access to risk capital are also proposed. Regulations such as the Federal Law on Women's Businesses (2018) and the Cantonal Law (2020) aim to create support networks and registers for women CEOs.
