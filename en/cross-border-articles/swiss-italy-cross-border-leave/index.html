Details on the new border agreement

On 1 January 2024, the new border agreement will enter into force, which provides for a different duration of leave compared to Italian rules. This agreement was entered into between the Swiss and Italian governments to regulate the working conditions of frontier workers operating in Switzerland. The new agreement provides for a duration of leave of 5 working days for bereavement or illness of a family member, unlike the Italian rules which provide for a duration of 3 working days.

According to the federal decree of 24 April 2019, in Switzerland bereavement or sick leave can vary from 4 to 15 working days, depending on the severity of the situation. In Italy, on the other hand, the leave for mourning or illness is 3 working days. It is important to note that these rules only apply to employees who have an employment contract with a Swiss employer.

A concrete example is the case of an employee in Lugano who loses a family member and has to take a 10-day leave of absence. If the employer does not comply with this rule, the employee may file a complaint with the Ministry of Labour.
