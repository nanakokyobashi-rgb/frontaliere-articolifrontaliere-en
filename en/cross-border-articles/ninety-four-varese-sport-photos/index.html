Varese, 94 photos of cycling and StraWoman

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 5 October 2026

On Varese, approximately 5.000 cyclists from over 40 countries and more than 3.000 women for a Sunday between Gran Fondo Tre Valli Varesine and StraWoman.

Approximately 5.000 cyclists from over 40 countries and more than 3.000 women transformed Sunday morning in the heart of Varese. The Gran Fondo Tre Valli Varesine and StraWoman brought two events from the same sporting day to the city's streets.

Varese city center became a large starting point. The Gran Fondo started on via Sacco, while shortly afterward StraWoman created a festive pink wave along a 5-kilometer route.

The account of the morning thus gives us a city first crossed by bicycles and then by runners. The numbers describe the scale of the event: approximately 5.000 cyclists from over 40 countries and over 3.000 women at the start of the event dedicated to running. The streets in the center made room for sporting participation, with Varese transformed into a starting point for passion.

The picture is that of a Sunday meant to be experienced in the heart of the city. The two events brought people, bicycles and participants along the city streets, before the story moved on to images. The source in fact links the morning's numbers to a gallery of 94 photographs, designed to convey the overall scene of the event.

Operational details

The picture of the cycling start is completed by the names present on Via Sacco. Under the gaze of Binda patron Renzo Oldani, the Gran Fondo featured a parade of champions from the past: Francesco Moser, Giuseppe Saronni and Stefano Garzelli. These are the three names indicated by the source to describe the champions' presence at the event.

Also at the start were Mayor Davide Galimberti, Economy Minister Giancarlo Giorgetti and Sports Councillor Stefano Malerba. The source places them in the same scene as the Gran Fondo, beside the starting point on Via Sacco and the cyclists who arrived from more than 40 countries.

Shortly afterward, StraWoman changed the color of the scene. Varese was crossed by a festive pink wave along the 5-kilometer route, with more than 3.000 women at the start. Among the people mentioned as being present were also Deputy Mayor Ivana Perusin and Budget Councillor Cristina Buzzetti.

The morning is therefore described through two starts and two sporting images: the champions and cyclists of the Gran Fondo, followed by StraWoman's pink race. The common thread is the city center, which the source presents as the space given over to sporting passion. The cycling program, the parade of past champions and the presence of the institutions mentioned in the news coverage came together on Via Sacco. Shortly afterward, the StraWoman route brought its pink wave into the city's streets.

Key points

The continuation of the morning is entrusted to the photo gallery. These are 94 shots by Alessandro Umberto Galbiati, collected to tell what the succession of numbers alone cannot convey: Varese's center transformed into a space dedicated to sport.

The source presents the photographs as a more effective account than words. The images bring together the race, the pedals and the legs of a Sunday experienced in the heart of the city, without separating the Gran Fondo Tre Valli Varesine from StraWoman in the overall picture of the morning.

The gallery thus brings together the two events. On one side is the cycling start on Via Sacco; on the other, shortly afterward, the festive pink wave along the 5-kilometer route. The photo feature preserves the visual impression of a Varese that, according to the source, stopped to make room for sporting passion.

With its 94 photographs, Alessandro Umberto Galbiati's photo feature brings Sunday's story to a close, placing the city and the images at its center. This is the documented continuation: a gallery dedicated to a sporting morning, entrusted to the shots that tell of the presence of the cyclists, the women of StraWoman and the protagonists mentioned at the start.
