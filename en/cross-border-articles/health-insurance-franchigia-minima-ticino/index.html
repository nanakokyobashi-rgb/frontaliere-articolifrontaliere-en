The minimum franchise of the health insurance fund in Switzerland may increase from 300 to 400 francs, following the consultation launched by the Federal Council on the project to modify the Health Insurance Law (LaMal). This measure may have a significant impact on the Ticino population, considering that the canton has one of the most developed economies in Switzerland.
