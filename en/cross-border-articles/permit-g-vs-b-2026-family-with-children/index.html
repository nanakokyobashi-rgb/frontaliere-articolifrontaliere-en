The comparison between the G permit and B permit for cross-border workers in Canton Ticino is a crucial topic for families with children. With the entry into force of the new tax agreement in 2026, many cross-border workers are wondering if it is worth changing their status. The G permit, reserved for cross-border workers, offers significant tax advantages, but the B permit, intended for residents, might be more convenient for families with children.

Advantages of the G permit

The G permit allows beneficiaries to pay income tax at source, which can be more advantageous for cross-border workers. Additionally, the G permit helps avoid double taxation, a non-negligible advantage for those working in Switzerland and residing in Italy. However, the G permit does not grant access to all Swiss social services, such as AHV and BVP, which might be more advantageous with the B permit.

Advantages of the B permit

The B permit, on the other hand, offers full access to Swiss social services, including AHV and BVP, which can be particularly advantageous for families with children. Additionally, the B permit allows beneficiaries to access LAMal, the Swiss health insurance, which can be more convenient compared to Italian insurances. However, the B permit requires paying Swiss taxes, which can be higher compared to the income tax at source.
