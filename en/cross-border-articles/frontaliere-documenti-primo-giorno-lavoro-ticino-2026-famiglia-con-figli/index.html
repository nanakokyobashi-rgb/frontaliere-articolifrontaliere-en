Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 August 2026 · Updated on 27 September 2026

Knowing the necessary documents for the first day of work in Ticino is essential for border workers who work in Switzerland and reside in Italy.

Context

In a nutshell

  • The first day of work in Ticino requires the presence of specific documents for border workers.
  • The family of a frontier worker is entitled to health insurance options (LAMal).
  • Bank details and health insurance are essential for the first day of work.

Checklist for the first day of work in Ticino

Required documents

  • Passport or identity card valid for expatriation
  • Residence permit or residence visa
  • Employment Contract or Letter of Engagement
  • Job Check or Deposit Receipt
  • Bank documentation (bank account, check, etc.)
  • Health insurance valid for Switzerland
  • Medical documentation (for employees with disabilities or handicaps)

Health Insurance Options (LAMal)

  • The family of a border worker is entitled to health insurance options (LAMal) as of January 1, 2023, under the federal law of December 18, 2021 (LAMal 2023).
  • LAMal provides comprehensive health protection, including coverage for medical services, rehabilitation and therapy.
  • The family of a frontier worker can choose from several insurance options, including the LAMal "Low" (CHF 350.00 per annum) and the LAMal "High" (CHF 800.00 per annum).

Key facts

  • The new Frontier Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020 and entered into force on 1 January 2024.
  • Switzerland is not a member of the EU/EEA.
  • The Italian IRPEF is 23% up to €28,000, 33% from €28,001 to €50,000 and 43% over €50,000.
  • AVS/AI/IPG is 5.3% dependent, AD/AC is 1.1% (cap 148,200 CHF), LAINF is 0.7-1.5% and LPP is 7-18% by age group (from 25 years).
  • Border workers are entitled to health insurance options (LAMal) and bank details and health insurance are essential for the first day of work.
