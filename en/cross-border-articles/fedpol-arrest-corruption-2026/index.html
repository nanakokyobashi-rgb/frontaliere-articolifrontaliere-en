Fedpol officer arrested for corruption and breach of official secrecy

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 29 April 2026

Six people, including a Fedpol employee, arrested in an operation against organized crime in Switzerland, France, and Germany.

Context

In brief

  • Fedpol officer arrested for corruption and breach of official secrecy
  • Six people detained in an international operation
  • Operation conducted by MPC, Fedpol, and police forces from Switzerland, France, and Germany

Key facts

  • What: Arrests for passive bribery, breach of official secrecy, and aiding and abetting
  • When: April 28, 2026
  • Where: Cantons of Basel-City, Basel-Country, Aargau, and abroad
  • Who: Swiss Federal Prosecutor's Office (MPC), Fedpol, Federal Criminal Police (PGF)
  • Amount: 12 house searches carried out

A Federal Police (Fedpol) employee was arrested along with five other individuals as part of an operation against organized crime. The officer is accused of passive bribery, breach of official secrecy, and aiding and abetting. According to the Swiss Federal Prosecutor's Office (MPC), the man allegedly transmitted confidential information in exchange for money to at least one other person under investigation. The operation, conducted in collaboration with the police forces of the cantons of Basel-City, Basel-Country, and Aargau, as well as with partner authorities in France and Germany, resulted in 12 house searches.
