The British singer Bonnie Tyler, 74, has been placed in a medically induced coma at a hospital in Faro, southern Portugal, following emergency surgery. This was announced by a spokesperson for the Welsh artist, known for her raspy voice and famous for the 1983 hit 'Total Eclipse of the Heart'. The singer had been operated on earlier in the week due to an intestinal perforation. 'Bonnie has been placed in a medically induced coma by her doctors to aid her recovery,' the spokesperson stated. 'We know you wish her the best and ask for privacy at this difficult time,' they added.

Tyler was preparing to begin a European tour on May 22 in Malta, to celebrate the fiftieth anniversary of the release of 'Lost in France', the 1976 single that marked her international debut. Concerts were also scheduled in Germany, the Czech Republic, and Turkey, with a final show in Cardiff in December, but no official communications have been received from the singer's staff regarding these events.
