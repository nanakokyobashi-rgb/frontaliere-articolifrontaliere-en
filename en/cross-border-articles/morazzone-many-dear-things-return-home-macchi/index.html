Morazzone - "many dear things" return home macchi (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 September 2026

On Saturday 12 and Sunday 13 September, Casa Macchi hosts a new edition of the market dedicated to garbage, antiques and modernity.

Context

Briefly

  • Flea market and antiques market at Casa Macchi
  • Saturday 12 and Sunday 13 September
  • From 10 to 18

Key facts

  • What: Flea market, antiques, and modern items
  • When: Saturday 12 and Sunday 13 September
  • Where: Casa Macchi, Morazzone
  • Who: Promoted by FAI
  • Amount: Not specified

On Saturday 12 and Sunday 13 September, from 10 to 18, Casa Macchi hosts a new edition of the market dedicated to flea markets, antiques, modern items, and the culture of reuse. The initiative, promoted by FAI, brings together a selection of flea market vendors and brocantes from different territories, with unique pieces and objects destined to find a second life.

Among the exhibitors will be realities specializing in vintage, design, modern items, and creative recovery, with proposals ranging from the 19th to the 20th century. There will also be a space dedicated to music with the vinyl records of All Time Tone Records, accompanied by a musical selection played during the market.

An invitation to rediscover the value of everyday objects

"Tante Care Cose" also wants to be an invitation to rediscover the value of everyday objects and the culture of reuse, in harmony with the history of Casa Macchi itself, where furniture, photographs, letters, and household items tell the life of a Lombard bourgeois family between the 19th and 20th centuries.

During the weekend, guided tours of the house will also be available to learn about the environments of the house and the stories of the people who lived there. The event takes place with the patronage of the Municipality of Morazzone. Various entry formulas are planned, from the market visit only to tickets including a guided tour of the house.

Operational details

Implications for frontier workers

The waste and antique market at Casa Macchi represents a unique opportunity for frontier workers to discover unique and valuable objects, while contributing to the culture of reuse. The event, promoted by FAI, offers a selection of pieces ranging from the nineteenth to the twentieth century, allowing visitors to find objects that can enrich their homes or become special gifts.

Guided tours and entry formulas

During the weekend, guided tours of the house will be available, offering border workers the opportunity to learn about the environments of the house and the events of the people who lived there. There are different entry formulas, from a single visit to the market to tickets including a guided tour of the house, allowing visitors to choose the option that best suits their needs.

Key points

What to do to participate

To participate in the junk and antique market at Casa Macchi, border workers can go to the residence located in Piazza Sant'Ambrogio in Morazzone. The event will be held on Saturday 12 and Sunday 13 September from 10 am to 6 pm. There are different entry formulas, from a single visit to the market to tickets including a guided tour of the house.
