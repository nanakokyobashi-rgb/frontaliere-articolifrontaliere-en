The Municipality of Barasso has completed an intervention of urban furniture and green restyling in Piazza San Martino in view of the Feast of the Assumption.

Context

Key facts

  • What: Green Restyling and street furniture
  • When: In view of the Feast of the Assumption and in the coming weeks
  • Where: Piazza San Martino and Piazza San Nicone, Barasso

  • Amount: Not specified

The Municipality promotes a green restyling intervention in the historic centre. Work will continue in the coming weeks in Piazza San Nicone. New planters and botanical essences to redevelop and give colour to the spaces of the historic centre. In view of the celebrations for the Feast of the Assumption, the Municipality of Barasso has started and completed a green-focused urban furniture project in Piazza San Martino. The operation involved the installation of tanks and containers with seedlings of dwarf lagerstroemia, rosemary and oropetal. At the same time, the technicians intervened in the area below the monumental cedar located in the center of the square, where the old plant specimens that are no longer alive were removed and convallaria, a perennial herbaceous plant suitable for developing in the presence of the tree's roots, was planted. Operational details

Details of interventions and plants used

The urban redevelopment project carried out by the municipal administration of Barasso focuses on the enhancement of public heritage and aggregation spaces in the historic centre of the town. The choice of botanical essences meets specific criteria of adaptability and urban decoration, fitting harmoniously into the context of the historic Piazza San Martino. The operation required detailed technical planning, in particular for the management of the area around the monumental cedar, a tree element of great importance for the square that requires special agronomic attention due to its superficial roots. The use of perennials such as convallaria represents a targeted solution to ensure the survival of the greenery even in complex environmental conditions, where other species would not be able to develop properly. The tanks and containers placed in the square contain essences with attention to detail, including dwarf lagerstroemia and rosemary, selected for their resistance and for the chromatic impact that can enrich the visual appearance of the village. Key points

Next steps and maintenance of public greenery

The program for the enhancement of public spaces established by the Municipality of Barasso does not end with the conclusion of the works in Piazza San Martino. The plan of interventions will continue over the next few weeks with the transfer of the operational teams to Piazza San Nicone, where the extraordinary maintenance of the central planter is planned. In addition, the administration's attention will shift to the perimeter area surrounding the monument recently donated to the community by Ruggero Marrani, a work that enriches the cultural and artistic heritage of the country and that needs adequate landscaping care to be better enhanced. The municipal administration hopes for the active involvement and respect of all citizens towards these new elements of urban furniture, which are essential to preserve the decorum of the town centre. Mayor Lorenzo Di Renzo Scolari thanked all the operators and municipal offices involved in the maintenance and care of the public green space.
