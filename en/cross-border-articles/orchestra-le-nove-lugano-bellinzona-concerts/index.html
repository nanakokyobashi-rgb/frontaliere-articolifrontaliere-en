Orchestra Le Nove: May concerts in Lugano and Bellinzona (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 12 May 2026

Orchestra Le Nove performs Tchaikovsky in Lugano and Bellinzona on May 16 and 18, 2026. Here are the details on the programs and concert venues.

Context

In brief

  • Double musical appointment on May 16 and 18, 2026.
  • Featuring Orchestra Le Nove with music by Tchaikovsky.
  • Concerts scheduled in Lugano and Bellinzona.
  • Debut in string orchestra formation.

Key facts

  • What: Orchestra Le Nove concerts
  • When: Saturday, May 16 (11:00 and 20:30) and Monday, May 18 (20:30)
  • Where: Evangelical Church of Lugano and Teatro Sociale in Bellinzona
  • Who: Orchestra Le Nove
  • Program: Serenade for Strings and Souvenir de Florence by Tchaikovsky

Orchestra Le Nove is preparing to return to the Ticinese stage with a program entirely dedicated to the Russian composer Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky. The event represents a significant moment for the regional concert season, offering the public the opportunity to hear two works of great emotional and technical impact: the Serenade for Strings and the famous Souvenir de Florence. The performances will be held on two separate dates and in two different locations in the Canton of Ticino, thus allowing widespread coverage of the territory for classical music enthusiasts.

Operational details

The Orchestra Le Nove, through this debut as a string ensemble, aims to explore new dynamics of artistic expression. The chosen program, which includes the 'Serenade for Strings' and the 'Souvenir de Florence', is not incidental: these are compositions that highlight the expressive richness of chamber music, a genre that often finds fertile ground in Ticino thanks to the presence of active cultural institutions and a historically diverse and engaged audience. For those who live and work between Italy and Switzerland, attending events of this kind also means connecting with the social and cultural fabric of the Canton of Ticino, going beyond the daily work routine.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the dates and venues for the Orchestra Le Nove concerts?
Orchestra Le Nove will perform on Saturday, May 16, 2026, at the Evangelical Church in Lugano, with two shows at 11:00 AM and 8:30 PM. Subsequently, on Monday, May 18, 2026, the orchestra will hold a concert at 8:30 PM at the Teatro Sociale in Bellinzona.
What is the planned musical program?
The concert program is dedicated to the Russian composer Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky. Two main works will be performed: the Serenade for Strings and the Souvenir de Florence, pieces chosen for their expressive intensity and for highlighting chamber music.
What distinguishes this concert series by Orchestra Le Nove?
The uniqueness of these events lies in the debut of Orchestra Le Nove in a specific string orchestra formation. Furthermore, the orchestra has chosen to perform in intimate venues to foster a more direct and close relationship with the audience, a central objective of the new programming.
