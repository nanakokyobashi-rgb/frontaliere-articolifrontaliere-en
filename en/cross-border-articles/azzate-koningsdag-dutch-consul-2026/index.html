Azzate celebrates King's Day with Dutch Consul (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 May 2026

On Sunday, May 3, Azzate celebrated King's Day with markets, music, and the presence of Dutch Consul Mascha Baak.

Context

In brief

  • Azzate celebrates Koningsdag with markets and music
  • Presence of Dutch Consul General Mascha Baak
  • Dutch traditions and typical cuisine

Key facts

  • What: Thirteenth edition of Koningsdag
  • When: Sunday, May 3, 2026
  • Where: Piazza Antonio Ghiringhelli, Azzate
  • Who: Pro Loco Azzate and Dutch Association for Northern Italy
  • Cost: Not yet specified

The Belvedere of Azzate abandoned its usual colors for a day to immerse itself in the vibrant energy of orange. On Sunday, May 3, Piazza Antonio Ghiringhelli hosted the thirteenth edition of Koningsdag, the Dutch national holiday that has transformed the Varesotto village into a lively corner of the Netherlands for years. The day was marked by fixed appointments: the Vrijmarkt, from 10:00 AM, the characteristic flea market managed by very young Italian, Dutch, and Belgian exhibitors, animated the square. Music and entertainment: the atmosphere was warmed up by Dutch minstrels, the notes of the It's all in The Name Rock Coverband, and traditional children's games, concluding with the disco music of OXiiGEN.

The gastronomic offer allowed a real journey through the Netherlands: from meatballs (bitterballen) to fried hot dogs (frikandellen), up to the famous sweets like stroopwafels and poffertjes. The real novelty of this edition was the special Stroopwafel ice cream, a unique flavor in Italy created by the ice cream makers of Azzate specifically for the square.

Operational details

Implications for the local community

The celebration of Koningsdag in Azzate not only enriches the cultural life of the village but also strengthens the bonds between local and international communities. The presence of the Dutch Consul Mascha Baak represents an important recognition for the event and for Azzate's ability to create a bridge between different cultures.

Opportunities for cross-border workers

For cross-border workers living and working in the area, events like Koningsdag offer the opportunity to immerse themselves in a different culture and actively participate in community life. The presence of markets and activities for children makes the event accessible to families and individuals of all ages.

Economic impact

The organization of Koningsdag has a positive impact on the local economy, attracting visitors from across the region and supporting small traders and restaurateurs. The sale of typical Dutch products and the creation of a special Stroopwafel ice cream are examples of how the event can stimulate the local economy and promote creativity.

Future projects

The collaboration between Pro Loco Azzate and the Dutch Association for Northern Italy has led to the realization of concrete projects such as the 'Il Tulipano' fountain and the giant chessboard. These projects not only improve the quality of life in the community but also serve as examples of how international collaborations can bring tangible benefits.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the origin of Koningsdag?
Koningsdag, or King's Day, is the national holiday of the Netherlands that celebrates the king's birthday. Traditionally, it is held on April 27th, but in some locations, like Azzate, it is celebrated on different dates to adapt to the local calendar.
Who organized the event in Azzate?
The event was organized by Pro Loco Azzate in collaboration with the Dutch Association for Northern Italy (De Nederlandse Vereniging voor Noord-Italie).
What are the typical Dutch dishes served during Koningsdag?
During Koningsdag in Azzate, you can taste typical Dutch dishes such as bitterballen (meatballs), frikandellen (fried hot-dogs), stroopwafels (caramel sweets), and poffertjes (small pancakes).
