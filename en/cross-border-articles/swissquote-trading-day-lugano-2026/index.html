The Swissquote Trading Day, now in its fourth edition, will take place on May 13, 2026, at the LAC in Lugano. This free event is confirmed as the leading trading appointment in Switzerland, featuring experts and prominent commentators from the financial landscape. Organized by Swissquote, the event aims to provide concrete tools to interpret a market context increasingly influenced by global political and economic dynamics.

In an ever-evolving landscape, the organizers emphasize that an effective trading strategy cannot overlook the ability to read ongoing changes. The program includes eleven interventions, each dedicated to a different aspect of financial markets, to provide an articulated and up-to-date view of the main trends.

Program and main topics

The Trading Day program includes interventions on various topics, such as the relationship between performance and volatility, financial independence, cryptocurrencies, options strategies, and the use of futures for commodity hedging. Among the speakers are Luca Giusti, Mr.RIP (Giorgio Ugazio), Tiziano Lavizzari, Corrado Cuttica, Vincenzo Zinnà, Arjeta Haskaj, Andrea Vaturi, Angelo Prataviera, Gian Marco Salcioli, and Mariangela Pira.
