Funding Details

Ticino Ticket's financing method was established in 2019 with the Public Transport Fares Act 3 of 2019 (LTTP 3/2019), which came into force on 1 January 2020. According to this law, the cost of Ticino Ticket is shared between the Canton of Ticino and the municipalities concerned. The Canton of Ticino contributes 60% of the cost, while the municipalities contribute 40%.

For example, in 2022 the cost of Ticino Ticket was CHF 8.2 million. The Canton of Ticino contributed CHF 4.9 million, while municipalities contributed CHF 3.3 million

Ticino Ticket funding is divided between the Confederation, the Canton and the Municipalities. The Confederation contributes 30% of the total cost, while the Canton contributes 20%.
