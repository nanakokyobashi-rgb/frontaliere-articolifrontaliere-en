Operational details

The nighttime closures of the highways between A8, Fiera Bypass, and Gallarate-Gattico Branch will have a significant impact on cross-border workers who daily cross the Italian-Swiss border. For workers residing in Ticino and working in Lombardy, these closures represent an additional obstacle in the already complex management of commuting time. The suggested alternatives, such as the exit at Solbiate Arno and the route through SS341, SS33 of the Simplon, and SS336 of Malpensa, could increase travel times by about 30-45 minutes per trip. This means that cross-border workers will need to plan their movements more carefully, especially considering that the closures occur during nighttime hours, when many prefer to travel to avoid daytime traffic.

Impact on Travel Times

For cross-border workers who work in Milan and reside in Ticino, the mandatory exit at Solbiate Arno and the subsequent alternative route through SS341 towards Gallarate, SS33 of the Simplon, and SS336 of Malpensa, could increase travel times by about 30-45 minutes per trip. This means that cross-border workers will need to plan their movements more carefully, especially considering that the closures occur during nighttime hours, when many prefer to travel to avoid daytime traffic.

Impact on Transportation Costs

The nighttime closures could also have an impact on transportation costs. Cross-border workers using public transportation may face additional costs for tickets on alternative buses and trains. Furthermore, those using private vehicles may need to consider an increase in fuel costs due to longer routes and potentially heavier traffic.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the recommended alternative routes for those traveling to Milan?
For those traveling to Milan, the mandatory exit is at the Solbiate Arno interchange. From here, it is advisable to continue on the SS341 towards Gallarate, take the SS33 of the Simplon and the SS336 of Malpensa, and then re-enter the highway at Busto Arsizio.
What are the alternatives for those coming from Varese?
For those coming from Varese, access to the Fiera Milano Ring Road (R37) towards Rho and Monza will be prohibited. The suggested alternative route involves exiting at Cascina Merlata and passing through the ordinary roads towards Pero and the SS33 of the Simplon.
What are the planned closures on the Gallarate-Gattico Branch (D08)?
On the D08, the work will mainly concern the maintenance of tree species, with total closures of the section between Castelletto Ticino and Besnate. On the night between April 28 and 29, the direction towards A8 Milan-Varese will be closed, while on the night between April 29 and 30, the section towards the A26 will be closed.
