Blues to Bop is the main music festival in Ticino dedicated to jazz and blues. In recent years, the event has consolidated a proposal of free concerts in the square, attracting not only residents of the Canton of Ticino, but also border workers who work or live in Switzerland. For those who daily cross the border between Italy and Ticino — from the municipalities of Como, Lecco, Varese to Menaggio — a Blues to Bop evening represents an opportunity for entertainment without access charges.

Free concerts in the square are the main advantage for border families. Unlike many European events, Blues to Bop maintains a completely free open-air model. This aspect is particularly appreciated by those who have to calculate each item of expenditure in evening trips from a work area in Ticino to border Italy, or vice versa.

Operational details

How to Reach Lugano from the Border Region

Organizing a trip to Blues to Bop requires conscious choices regarding transportation, parking, and waiting times.

From Como and Lecco: The Chiasso pass is the most direct route for those coming from the Como province. The state road (then A2 highway) directly accesses Lugano in about 50-60 minutes in the absence of queues. During weekend concerts, the flow of vehicles entering and exiting the Ticino can create delays of 15-30 minutes. Check real-time waiting times before departing.

From Varese and province: The Gaggiolo or Brogeda pass is recommended. Both routes lead to the Lugano axis in 60-90 minutes depending on traffic. Brogeda represents a generally less congested alternative compared to Chiasso during weekends, although it is located slightly further north.

Public Transportation: For those who prefer not to drive, the Italian railway system (Trenitalia) connects Como to Lugano via Chiasso. Regional trains have an hourly frequency and the journey takes about 1 hour. Once in Lugano, the city's bus network reaches the squares where concerts take place. For frequent border crossers accustomed to Swiss public transportation, the Ticinese system is compatible and convenient.

Parking and Waiting Costs

Lugano has paid parking lots in the city center with an average hourly rate of 1.00-1.50 EUR. For extended stays (2-3 hour concerts), the cost amounts to 2.00-4.50 EUR. Parking lots outside the historic center are available at a lower rate; the choice depends on walking availability.

Ensure that your car insurance policy covers circulation in Switzerland (border extension or specific frontier insurance). The insurance certificate must be on board during border crossing.

Key points

Checklist for travel to the festival

Before heading to Blues to Bop, check these essential points.

Documents and car insurance

  • Passport or valid ID (frontier Italians in Switzerland)
  • Valid driver's license
  • Car insurance certificate with border coverage
  • Swiss vignette (approximately 40 CHF per 1 year civil)
  • If using a rented car, rental documentation on board

Transport planning and times

  • Check the waiting times of the chosen border crossing the day before using online platforms
  • Depart 30-45 minutes before the concert start time for a safety margin
  • Book a parking spot in the center if available online for a fixed fee
  • Download an offline map (e.g. Google Maps) in case of poor phone reception

Practical and accessories

  • CHF and EUR coins for automatic parking
  • EUR cash for evening consumption and dinner
  • Jacket or umbrella (outdoor concerts depend on weather conditions)
  • Portable charger for smartphone
  • Vehicle registration document or cloud scan

Return organization

  • Plan the return no later than 23:00-23:30 (traffic flow still manageable)
  • Anticipate check-in documents at the border crossing (approximately 5 minutes in normal conditions)

Frequently Asked Questions
When is Blues to Bop held in Lugano in 2026?
The specific dates for 2026 are not yet communicated. Blues to Bop is an annual event held throughout the year. It is advisable to follow the official Ticino cultural promotion channels for updates. Generally, the event takes place at specific times of the year.
How much does it cost to enter Blues to Bop concerts?
The Blues to Bop square concerts are completely free. There is no access fee. The main costs of the trip for border crossers concern transport (fuel or TPL tickets), parking in Lugano (1-1.50 EUR per hour) and any evening drinks (dinner, coffee). The indicative total for an evening is 20-30 EUR for 2-3 people.
What is the best pass from Como to reach Lugano?
The Chiasso pass is the most direct from Como, offering a journey time of 50-60 minutes in the absence of queues. However, on weekends, Brogeda and Gaggiolo represent less congested alternatives, although they require a slightly longer route (60-90 minutes). Check the waiting times in real time before leaving through the dedicated online platforms.
Do I have to have the Swiss cartoon to reach Lugano as a border worker?
Yes, if you cross the Swiss A2 motorway you must have the valid Swiss vignette affixed to your windscreen. The cost is about CHF 40 for a calendar year. Purchasable at customs service stations, at automated checkouts, or online prior to departure. Driving without a vignette exposes you to a fine.
Can I reach Lugano by public transport from Como?
Yes. The Trenitalia regional train connects Como to Lugano through the Chiasso crossing with an hourly frequency. The journey takes about 1 hour. Once in Lugano, the city buses (TPL) reach all the squares where the concerts take place. It's a convenient option if you prefer not to drive.
