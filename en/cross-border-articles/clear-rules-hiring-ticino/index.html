Recruitment in the public sector in Ticino: clear rules requested (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 21 September 2026

Roncelli and Mirante di Avanti with Ticino & Lavoro ask the government about the Zali case and the 2020 email to the ECO.

Key facts

Clear rules are needed to ensure transparency and impartiality in recruitment. This is the central theme raised in the Canton of Ticino by the political representatives Roncelli and Mirante of the Avanti con Ticino & Lavoro movement. The political leaders decided to submit a formal question to the cantonal government to shed light on specific events that have attracted public and institutional attention.

At the centre of the request is the so-called Zali case and a specific email communication dating back to 2020 addressed to the ECO, the Cantonal Hospital Authority. The question aims to understand in detail how the dynamics took place on thatoccasion and what are the administrative implications related to the selection and appointment procedures within the bodies governed by public law or participated in by the Canton.

The request for institutional clarifications

The initiative of the two members of Avanti con Ticino & Lavoro is not limited to retracing the events of the past, but looks directly at the future of cantonal administrative and health management.

Operational details

The issue raised in the public debate closely touches on the delicate balance between political management and the administration of public entities in Ticino Canton. When discussing healthcare institutions such as the EOC, whose strategic importance for the resident and cross-border population and workforce is central, any doubt about the regularity of recruitment processes risks undermining general trust in institutions. The demand for clear, unambiguous guidelines aims precisely to secure procedures, excluding any margin of discretion not strictly regulated by law.

Within selective procedures, the adoption of rigorous standards ensures candidates are evaluated exclusively on the basis of professional competencies—a fundamental aspect for protecting merit and the quality of services provided. The interrogators stress the need for regulatory intervention or binding directives to act as a shield against any decision-making opacity, offering guarantees to both internal staff and external participants in public competitions.

The impact on the governance of public entities

The response from the Ticino government to the inquiry by Roncelli and Mirante will likely outline the scope of future control actions on competitions and appointments within the Ente Ospedaliero Cantonale (EOC) and other public structures in the territory. For workers and citizens, administrative transparency represents a key indicator of the democratic quality of cantonal management. Political discussion thus shifts toward the need to modernize and make decision-making processes governing access to key roles in Ticino's public administration immune to conflicts of interest.
