Key facts

  • What: CNM wage calculator for construction.
  • When: Reference to the rules of the National Contract 2026.
  • Where: Switzerland, based on company headquarters.
  • Who: Unia.
  • Objective: Control of the hourly or monthly salary due.

The CNM salary calculator is the tool made available to verify whether the salary paid by your employer corresponds to the provisions of the National Contract (CNM) of 2026. The control procedure requires the user to specify whether the objective of the survey concerns the hourly or monthly salary. The system also allows you to enter the salary class, a data that can be found directly on your paycheck. It is necessary to indicate the postal code (NPA) of your company's headquarters, a fundamental element for the correct operation of the computer.

Operational details

The use of digital tools for wage verification represents an important step for transparency in the construction sector. The CNM wage calculator acts as a reference point for workers who wish to ensure that wages are in line with the provisions of 2026. The choice of the correct salary class is also decisive, since each class defines the minimum salary thresholds applicable based on the tasks and experience recognized by the contract.

Frequently Asked Questions
What should I do if the salary indicated by the calculator is higher than the one on the paycheck?
If you find differences between the salary shown on your paycheck and that indicated by the CNM salary calculator, the source suggests that you contact your Unia secretariat immediately for assistance and clarification on your specific situation.
What data do I need to use the salary calculator?
To use the calculator, you need to know your salary class (shown on your paycheck), the postcode (NPA) of your company's headquarters, and decide if you want to know your hourly or monthly salary.
Which contract does the calculator refer to?
The salary calculator allows you to check if the salary complies with the rules of the National Contract (CNM) of 2026.
