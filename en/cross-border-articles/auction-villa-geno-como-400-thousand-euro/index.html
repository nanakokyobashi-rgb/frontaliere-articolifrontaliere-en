Villa Geno in Como up for auction: nearly 400k euros needed (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 4 May 2026

The municipality of Como is auctioning off Villa Geno and the adjacent green area. The concession has an annual rent of nearly 400,000 euros.

Context

In brief

  • Villa Geno in Como up for auction for nearly 400,000 euros
  • 6-year concession for tourist/accommodation activities
  • Participation applications by July 6, 2026

Key facts

  • What: Public auction for the concession of Villa Geno and green area
  • When: Applications by July 6, 2026, opening of bids on July 9, 2026
  • Where: City of Como
  • Who: City of Como
  • Amount: 345,950 euros annually for the villa, 30,118 euros annually for the green area

The City of Como has announced a public auction for the concession of Villa Geno and the adjacent green area. The villa, located between numbers 12 and 13 of the homonymous avenue, was managed until 2024 by the company Sunlake, but the renewal of the concession was blocked. Now, the City is looking for a new manager for tourist/accommodation activities with high-level catering and event organization. The green area, on the other hand, must be maintained as such.

The concession will last 6 years, with an annual fee of 345,950 euros for the villa and 30,118 euros for the green area. The provisional award will go to the competitor who offers the highest annual consideration compared to the auction base. Applications to participate must be received by 12 PM on July 6, 2026 at the City Hall. The opening of the bids is scheduled for July 9.

Implications for cross-border workers

The auction of Villa Geno represents an opportunity for entrepreneurs interested in managing a prestigious tourist facility in the heart of Como. For cross-border workers in the tourism or catering sector, this could be a chance to expand their activities or invest in a high-level project. However, the initial investment required is significant, with an annual fee exceeding 376,000 euros.

Operational details

Analysis of the tourist market in Como

The decision by the Municipality of Como to auction off Villa Geno reflects a strategy aimed at enhancing the city's real estate assets and attracting investments in the tourism sector. Como, thanks to its location on Lake Como and its proximity to Switzerland, is an increasingly appreciated tourist destination, both for Italian and foreign tourists.

Opportunities for cross-border workers

For cross-border workers in the tourism or catering sector, the auction of Villa Geno represents a unique opportunity to expand their activities or invest in a high-level project. The proximity to Switzerland and the presence of a large number of cross-border workers can represent a competitive advantage for those who decide to invest in this structure.

However, it is important to consider that the required investment is significant, and it will be necessary to present a detailed project that respects the allowed functional destinations. Moreover, managing a prestigious tourist structure like Villa Geno requires specific skills and careful cost management.

Comparison with other investment opportunities

Compared to other investment opportunities in the tourism sector, the auction of Villa Geno offers some advantages, such as the privileged location and the possibility of organizing high-level events. Key points

How to Participate in the Auction

To participate in the auction for Villa Geno, it is necessary to follow some fundamental steps. First of all, it is important to consult the tender notice published by the Municipality of Como, which contains all the necessary information to submit the participation application. The notice specifies the participation requirements, the required documents, and the application submission methods.

Step-by-step for Participation

1. Consult the tender notice: The tender notice is available on the website of the Municipality of Como and contains all the necessary information to participate in the auction. It is important to read it carefully to understand the participation requirements and the application submission methods.

2. Prepare the documentation: The participation application must be accompanied by all the documents requested in the tender notice. Among these, it is necessary to include the management project for the villa and the green area, which must respect the allowed functional destinations.

3. Submit the application: The participation application must be sent to the Municipality of Como by 12 PM on July 6, 2026. It is possible to submit the application via registered mail or deliver it in person to the competent office.

4. Wait for the opening of the envelopes: The opening of the envelopes will take place on July 9, 2026. In this phase, it will be possible to know the outcome of the auction and the provisional award.

What to Do in Case of Award

In case of provisional award, the concessionaire will have to sign the concession contract and pay the annual fee provided. Furthermore, it will be necessary to present a detailed plan for the management of the villa and the green area, which must be approved by the Municipality of Como.
