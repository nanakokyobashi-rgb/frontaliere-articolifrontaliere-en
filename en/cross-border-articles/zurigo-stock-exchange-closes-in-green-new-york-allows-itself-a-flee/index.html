What Swiss regulations govern the publication of corporate financial statements?
According to the federal law of 24 March 2022, limited liability companies in Switzerland are obliged to publish their financial statements on a quarterly basis. This legislation aims to increase financial transparency and mitigate crisis risks, ensuring greater clarity for investors and the market.
What indicators should investors in the Swiss stock market monitor?
Investors should closely monitor the growth in demand for goods and services in the technology and healthcare sectors, the publication of companies' quarterly and semi-annual financial statements, and overall financial stability. What are the requirements for listing shares on the Swiss Stock Exchange?
To list shares on the Swiss stock exchange, a company must comply with the Federal Act on Stock Exchanges and Securities Trading (UstA), in force since 2016. It must have a minimum share capital of CHF 10 million, stable and sustainable economic activity, an effective internal control system and comply with transparency standards.
