Approximately 9 thousand square meters added to the 12 thousand already existing, an investment of approximately 15 million euros, and a target set for the next five years: to double the production capacity and revenue of the Pharma division. This is the trajectory presented at the inauguration of the new IWT Pharma building in Casale Litta.

Alessandro Bernardini, the company's managing director, was present at the ribbon-cutting ceremony, together with brothers Pietro and Edoardo, the second generation in the company. All the workers gathered in front of the new building, called to listen to the message chosen for the opening: industrial growth depends on people, their abilities, safety and the quality of the environments in which they work.

«One cannot think of achieving economic results without creating human values».

The reference to Adriano Olivetti, one of the first clients of the Tecniplast parent company, gave the ceremony its interpretive key. The expansion was presented as an industrial step, but also as a choice that places daily work and the spaces in which it is carried out at the center.

A platform for the Pharma division

The new facility brings the industrial hub in Casale Litta from 12 thousand to over 21 thousand square meters. IWT Pharma, part of the Tecniplast Group, designs and manufactures systems and solutions for washing, isolation and decontamination intended for the pharmaceutical, biotech, nutraceutical and advanced therapies sectors.

The program also aims to strengthen its presence in the international Life Sciences market. The expected growth will bring new hires, with a focus on technical, scientific and pharmaceutical professionals and STEM skills, from mechatronics to industrial engineering. The project also includes ergonomics, employee safety and the quality of workplaces: the building was not described solely as an increase in production capacity.

The project also includes photovoltaic panels and electric vehicle charging stations, as well as measures for more efficient use of resources. Also planned are the redevelopment of the streambed that crosses the industrial area and tanks for the collection and management of rainwater. The inaugural speech also acknowledged the clients, the local area and the collaboration with the municipal administration, along with thanks for the design and implementation of the project.

The inaugurated expansion adds approximately 9 thousand square meters to the existing 12 thousand; the doubling of production capacity and turnover is instead a program to be achieved within five years. The time limit concerns the company plan, not an application or a personnel procedure.

3. Verify the professional profile. The source indicates a focus on technical, scientific and pharmaceutical skills and STEM areas, from mechatronics to industrial engineering. Those looking for an opportunity can check the annunci di lavoro and the aziende che assumono, without presenting as available a position that the text does not publish. The project refers to ergonomics, safety and quality of the environments; it includes photovoltaic panels, charging stations for electric vehicles, measures concerning resources, the stream and rainwater. These are stated characteristics of the building, not information about business trips, parking or connections for those arriving from Italy.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the extent of the investment and expansion of the new IWT Pharma building in Casale Litta?
The expansion provides for the addition of about 9,000 square meters to the existing 12,000, bringing the total area beyond 21,000 square meters. The declared investment is about 15 million euros, financed to strengthen the Pharma division of the Tecniplast Group. The project also includes photovoltaic panels, columns for electric charging, interventions on the stream and tanks for the management of rainwater.
What are IWT Pharma's stated growth targets for the next five years?
The company's stated goal is to double both the production capacity and turnover of the Pharma division within the next five years. This target was presented during the inauguration as part of the growth plan linked to the new plant, which aims to take advantage of the added spaces and new technologies introduced.
What skills and professional profiles are indicated for future appointments at IWT Pharma?
Future additions will mainly concern technical, scientific and pharmaceutical profiles, as well as stem skills such as mechatronics and industrial engineering. The source does not specify recruitment numbers, selection dates or contractual conditions, but indicates that research will be focused on these areas to support the expansion of the Pharma division.
