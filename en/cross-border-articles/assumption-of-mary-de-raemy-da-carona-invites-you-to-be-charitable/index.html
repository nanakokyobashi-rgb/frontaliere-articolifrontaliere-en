The current legislation on cross-border work in Ticino is governed by the Convention between Switzerland and the European Union, signed on 21 June 1999. This agreement provides for the free movement of workers and services between Switzerland and the EU, with some exceptions for certain economic activities.

In 2022, Ticino welcomed around 5,000 foreign workers, mainly from the European Union. However, the number of foreign workers can vary greatly depending on economic conditions and labour market needs.
