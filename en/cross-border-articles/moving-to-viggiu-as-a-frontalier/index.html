Moving to Viggiù from border crossings: pros and cons

Why move to Viggiù

Ticino is a strategic geographical area for border crossings, thanks to its position on the borders with Italy and Switzerland. Viggiù is a municipality located in the district of Lugano and offers a high quality of life, with a lower cost of living than other areas of Ticino.

How the exemption for old border workers works

Old frontier workers who move to Viggiù can enjoy an exemption of €7,500. This means that they will not have to pay taxes on income from work for the first 5 years of residence in Viggiù. To benefit from this benefit, border crossers must meet the following requirements:

  • Have worked in the border sector for at least 5 years
  • Have an annual income of at least €30,000
