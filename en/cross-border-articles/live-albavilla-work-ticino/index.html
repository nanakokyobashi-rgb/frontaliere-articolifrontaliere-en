Living in Albavilla and working in Ticino: border guide (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 September 2026

G leave, taxation, AVS, LPP and LAMal for those who reside in Albavilla and work in Ticino. Practical guide to border life in the Como-Switzerland area.

Context

In brief

  • Permit G for cross-border commuters living in Italy and working in Switzerland
  • New Cross-Border Agreement effective from 1 January 2024: refunds up to €7,500 for old cross-border commuters
  • Withholding tax deducted in Switzerland, foreign tax credit in Italy (form CE of the 730)
  • AVS, LPP, and LAMal managed entirely in Switzerland for cross-border commuters

Key facts

  • Who: Cross-border commuters with Permit G living in Albavilla (Como, Italy) and working in Canton Ticino
  • What: Swiss tax and social security regime, with AVS/LPP/LAMal rights
  • When: New Agreement in force from 1 January 2024
  • Where: Albavilla (Como) ↔ Ticino, Brogeda, Chiasso, Gaggiolo border crossings
  • Amount: Exemption €10,000 (new cross-border commuters) or €7,500 (old cross-border commuters, transitional regime 2024–2033)

In Albavilla, a town near Como, hundreds of cross-border commuters have chosen to live in Italy while continuing to work in Ticino. This trend reflects a balanced strategy: living where the cost of living is lower but earning in Swiss francs. To work in Switzerland while residing in Italy, the Permit G (cross-border commuter) is essential, allowing daily entries and exits from the Ticino territory.

Operational details

Daily commuting and distance from border crossings

Albavilla is approximately 30–40 kilometers from the main Ticino border crossings (Brogeda, Chiasso, Gaggiolo). This proximity to the border is one of the factors that attracts cross-border workers to municipalities like Albavilla: by reducing travel times, fuel costs, vehicle maintenance, and overall wear and tear are minimized. A cross-border worker who works in Mendrisio or Chiasso will face significantly shorter commuting times compared to someone living in Como or in the more inland areas of the Como province.

The road connection is ensured by the A2 and A9 highways (in Swiss territory) and the Lombardy links leading to the Brogeda (access to Ticino) and Gaggiolo (via Como) border crossings. During periods of heavy traffic or in case of union strikes in Switzerland, transit times at the border crossings can increase considerably: it is advisable to check road conditions before each crossing and plan adequate time margins on critical days.

Cost of living: Albavilla vs. Canton Ticino

The cost of living in Lombardy, especially in the province of Como, is generally lower than in the Canton Ticino. Rent, food expenses, household utilities, public transportation: each budget item contributes to a significant difference. For a cross-border worker, this dynamic translates into a tangible economic advantage: receiving a salary in Swiss francs (higher than comparable Italian salaries) and paying living and housing expenses in Italian euros generates a substantial net gain. Albavilla, located a short distance from Como and immersed in the quiet residential areas of the Como belt, offers a variety of properties at significantly lower prices compared to the Ticino market.

Key points

Recruitment Procedure and Permit G: the administrative steps

The administrative path to become a Ticino border worker begins with the search for a job offer in Ticino. Once an employer has been found willing to hire you, it is the Ticino company that initiates the application procedure for the G Permit at the Swiss Secretariat of State for Migration (SEM). It is not necessary to submit any preliminary personal application: the employer formalises the application, attaching the employment contract and personal documentation (valid passport, criminal certificate, declaration of residence in Italy). Once the permit has been obtained, it is essential to register with the competent cantonal office in Ticino and communicate the change of situation to the Italian Revenue Agency.

Preliminary checklist for transfer to Albavilla

Before you settle permanently in Albavilla, review these key points:

1. Registered residence: Transfer the residence from the municipality of origin to the municipality of Albavilla at the local registry office. Keep the transfer declaration and the residence certificate; these documents are essential for Italian taxation and for the recognition of border status.

2. Health insurance: Decide between a Swiss LAMal (through the employer or individually) and maintaining Italian coverage. Swiss LAMal is generally more advantageous

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between Leave G and Leave B?
The G Permit is specific to border workers: it authorizes daily entry and exit from Switzerland without a visa and remains bound to the employment contract in Switzerland. Residence Permit B is for those who intend to move permanently to Switzerland and leave their Italian domicile. As a border worker in Albavilla, you need the G Permit to continue working in Ticino while maintaining your residence in Italy.
If I live in Albavilla and work in Ticino, where do I pay for health insurance (LAMal)?
Health insurance (LAMal) is mandatory in Switzerland for those who work there. You can choose a Swiss LAMal or, in specific cases, maintain Italian coverage. The choice of the Swiss LAMal is recommended for border crossers, with deductibles between CHF 300 and CHF 2,500 depending on the plan selected. Who should I contact with questions about refunds and border taxation?
For Swiss issues (AVS, LPP, source tax, Ticino cantonal refunds), please contact the cantonal tax administration (AFC/ESTV). For Italian matters (declaration 730, tax credit), please contact the local Italian Revenue Agency. How much does the transfer of personal data in Albavilla cost?
The master transfer to the municipality of Albavilla requires the presentation of a form, rental agreement or title to the property, valid identity document and declaration of transfer. Administrative expenses vary depending on the municipality (usually between €10 and €50). Please consult directly the registry office of the municipality of Albavilla for details and current costs.
If I change jobs in Ticino, does the G Permit remain valid?
Permit G remains binding on the employer: in the event of a change of company, you must notify the cantonal authority (SEM) of the change and the new employer initiates the procedure for the new permit. In the event of temporary unemployment, the permit remains valid for a limited period (3–6 months) to search for new work; after this period, it must be renewed or it expires.
