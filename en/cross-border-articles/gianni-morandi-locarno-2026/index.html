Key facts

  • Who: Gianni Morandi
  • Tickets: Available for presale on biglietteria.ch

Gianni Morandi brings his summer and autumn tour 'C'era un ragazzo estate 2026' to Switzerland, with a special stop in Locarno. On October 4, 2026, the singer-songwriter will perform at Palexpo FEVI, one of the main sports arenas in the region. This concert will be the occasion to celebrate the 60th anniversary of the song 'C'era un ragazzo che come me amava i Beatles e i Rolling Stones,' written in 1966 by Franco Migliacci and Mauro Lusini.

The tour setlist will also include the new single 'Monghidoro,' written by Lorenzo Jovanotti and released on April 3, 2026, by Epic Records/Sony Music Italy. On stage, Morandi will be accompanied by a band directed by Maestro Luca Colombo, who will contribute to enhancing each performance with energy and musical quality.
