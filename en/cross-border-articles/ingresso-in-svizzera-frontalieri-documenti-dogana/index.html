Required documents

To enter Switzerland, border guards must present the following documents:

  • A valid passport, which does not expire within the next six months.
  • A residence permit, issued by the Federal Tax Administration.
  • The income tax return, if you have an income in Switzerland.

Residence permits

Residence permits are issued by the Federal Tax Administration and are valid for a fixed period of time. Border crossers can request them by submitting the required documentation, such as:

  • A valid passport.
  • A copy of the income tax return.
  • A certificate of residence.

Entry procedure

The procedure for entry into Switzerland for frontier workers is as follows:

1. Report to customs control at the Swiss border 2. Submit the necessary documents 3. Answer the controller's questions 4. Pay any taxes or contributions

Taxes and contributions

Border crossers are subject to tax at source in Switzerland. Tax may vary based on income and nationality. For example, in 2022, the source tax in Switzerland was 20% for incomes up to 7,500 Swiss francs, and 30% for incomes above 7,500 Swiss francs.
