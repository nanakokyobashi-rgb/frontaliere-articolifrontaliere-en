Lombardy: 230.640 euro for bridges and tunnels in the Varese area

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 28 September 2026

Lombardy Region allocates 230,640 euros to the Province of Varese for checks on bridges, viaducts and tunnels, to be completed by 30 November 2028 with final reporting by 15 December 2028.

The Lombardy Region has allocated 3.1 million euros for the three-year period 2026-2028 with the aim of supporting preventive maintenance and safety of the Lombardy road network. The resolution was approved by the regional council on the proposal of Claudia Maria Terzi, councilor for Infrastructure and Public Works, and assigns resources to the Provinces and the Metropolitan City of Milan.

For the territory of the Province of Varese the amount amounts to 230,640 euros. The sums will finance inspections, load tests and structural monitoring, within a regional program dedicated to the verification, control and monitoring of road structures.

The works included

The provision concerns the most delicate points of the local road network. Checks are permitted on bridges and viaducts with a span greater than 6 metres, on tunnels longer than 500 meters and on road itineraries authorized for the transit of exceptional transports.

Operational activities include the installation of continuous monitoring systems, inspection analyzes to identify the causes of degradation, investigations on materials and load tests. The program also includes the control of cracks and structural movements, hydraulic studies, training courses for technical staff and advanced IT services for the census and care of artefacts and flooring.

The Province of Varese and the other interested bodies will have to identify priorities and structures to be subjected to verification based on the level of degradation and the critical issues found in the area. The text does not indicate the individual bridges, the tunnels involved or any construction sites. Therefore the provision defines resources and activities, but does not allow the quota to be associated today with a precise route for those moving towards the border; the map of border crossings remains a separate instrument from the resolution.

All funded activities must be completed by 30 November 2028. The final reporting and reports on interventions must arrive at the regional offices by 15 December of the same year.
