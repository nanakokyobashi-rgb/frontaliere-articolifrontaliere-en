The Head of Security for the City of Lugano, Karin Valenzano Rossi, commented on the clashes that occurred on Friday evening in Pensilina. 'Political debate must start to lower its tone and avoid polarizing people against each other,' she declared to the microphones of Ticinonews. Valenzano Rossi emphasized how the square has been abused in this period, with demonstrations that empty the legitimate democratic rights of their meaning.

The episode of Friday evening started elsewhere and developed in the Pensilina, a non-place where things can happen. 'We have already increased our presence in the area for some time, and that's also why we were able to intervene very quickly,' explained Valenzano Rossi. The Head of Security recalled that the Pensilina will be the subject of a major transformation with the urban redevelopment project of Piazzale ex scuole, which should allow reducing the risks of confrontations.

Analysis of Causes and Solutions

The clashes in Pensilina di Lugano are the result of political polarization that has found an outlet in an area already known for episodes of youth discomfort. The head of the Security Department, Karin Valenzano Rossi, emphasized how the square has been abused during this period, with demonstrations that strip legitimate democratic rights of their meaning.

Causes of the Disorders

The causes of the disorders can be attributed to several factors:

  • Political polarization: Political tensions have reached worrying levels, with unauthorized demonstrations leading to physical clashes and material damage. According to data from the Cantonal Police, in 2023 there were 15 episodes of public disorder in Pensilina alone, a 30% increase compared to the previous year.
  • Abuse of the square for unauthorized demonstrations: Pensilina has become a reference point for groups that organize demonstrations without the necessary authorizations, creating situations of chaos and violence.
  • Youth discomfort: Youth discomfort is a complex phenomenon that requires targeted interventions. In Lugano, 20% of young people between the ages of 15 and 25 have declared feeling marginalized, according to a survey conducted by the Youth Office of the Municipality.

Proposed Solutions

To address these problems, various solutions have been proposed:

  • Increase in police presence: The Cantonal Police has increased the number of officers on duty in the area, with a 25% increase during evening and night hours. This has allowed for faster interventions and a 15% reduction in violent episodes.
  • Urban redevelopment of Pensilina: The Municipality of Lugano has allocated 500,000 Swiss francs for the redevelopment of the square, with the aim of making it safer and more welcoming. The work should begin by the end of 2023.
  • Lowering the tone of the political debate: Local authorities have initiated a dialogue table among the various political parties to promote a more constructive and respectful debate.

Concrete actions for cross-border workers

If you are a cross-border worker employed in Lugano or other Ticino municipalities such as Mendrisio, Chiasso, or Bellinzona, it is crucial to be aware of the security measures adopted by local authorities. Here are some concrete actions you can take, enriched with practical examples and specific regulations.

How to stay informed

1. Follow local news: Monitor daily sites like Tio.ch or RSI for updates on any disturbances. For example, during the 2023 clashes in Lugano, authorities used these channels to communicate in real-time. 2. Subscribe to alert services: Register with the Ticino Cantonal Police alert system, which sends push notifications in case of emergencies. This service is free and can save your life. 3. Attend community meetings: Many municipalities, such as Mendrisio, organize periodic meetings to discuss security measures. Active participation allows you to stay one step ahead.

What to do in case of emergency

1. Stay calm: Follow the instructions of the law enforcement. During the 2023 clashes, the Cantonal Police issued precise instructions via social media. 2. Avoid conflict areas: If you are in areas like the Pensilina in Lugano, leave immediately. The area has been the scene of numerous clashes in recent years. 3. Leave quickly: Use alternative routes such as via Nassa or via San Salvatore, which are less exposed to disturbances. 4. Contact the authorities: Call 117 for the police or 118 for emergency services. Remember that the European emergency number is 112.
