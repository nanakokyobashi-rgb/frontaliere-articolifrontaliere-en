Living in Maslianico and working in Ticino as a border worker: guide 2024 (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 August 2026

Tax at source only in Switzerland, new agreement in force from 1 January 2024, exemption up to 10,000 euros for new border workers

Context

In a nutshell

  • New agreement signed on 23/12/2020, effective from 1 January 2024
  • Tax at source withheld only in Switzerland, Italy avoids double taxation
  • Double taxation agreement signed on 9 December 1976

Key facts

  • What: New Border Agreement between Italy and Switzerland
  • When: Signed on 23 December 2020, effective from 1 January 2024
  • Where: Brogeda pass and other Italian-Swiss border points
  • Who: Frontier workers living in Italy and working in Canton Ticino
  • Amount: Deductible of 10,000 euros for new border workers, 7,500 euros for those already active before 17/7/2023

The new Frontier Agreement, signed on 23 December 2020 and entered into force on 1 January 2024, establishes that income tax at source is withheld exclusively in Switzerland for frontier workers. Italy avoids double taxation through the tax credit to be declared in the EC framework of model 730. This replaces the previous regime and introduces new deductible thresholds depending on the date of commencement of cross-border employment.

According to the Double Taxation Convention between Italy and Switzerland, signed on 9 December 1976 and still in force, Switzerland is not a member of the EU or the EEA, which affects the tax and social security treatment of cross-border workers. The 2020 agreement updates this bilateral framework taking into account the regulatory evolutions of recent years.

Operational details

Practical scenarios for the border crossing from Maslianico

A worker who resides in Maslianico and obtains a job in Ticino after 1 January 2024 benefits from the €10,000 deductible provided for by the new agreement. This means that the first 10,000 euros of employee income are excluded from the Swiss source tax, while the excess part is subject to withholding according to the Ticino municipal and cantonal rates, in addition to social security contributions.

For those who were already border workers before 17 July 2023, the transitional regime valid until 2033 applies, with a lower deductible of 7,500 euros. This group of workers retains certain benefits gained under the old system, although they still have to comply with the new declaratory procedures introduced by the 2020 agreement.

On the Swiss paycheck, in addition to the tax at source, the contributions AVS/AI/IPG (5.3%), AD/AC (1.1% up to the ceiling of CHF 148'200), LAINF (variable between 0.7% and 1.5%) and LPP (between 7% and 18% depending on age) are withheld. These amounts finance the first and second pillars of Swiss social security and are mandatory for all employees, including frontier workers.

Key points

What to do to start working in Ticino from Maslianico

The first step is to obtain the G permit, which is necessary for those residing in Italy and working in Switzerland. The application must be submitted to the competent Ticino cantonal authority, attaching an employment contract, identity document and proof of residence in Italy. Without this permission, it is not possible to start a cross-border business.

Once the G permit has been obtained, the Swiss employer will enroll the worker in the mandatory insurance: AVS/AI/IPG, AD/AC, LAINF and LPP. The corresponding deductions will appear on the monthly paycheck according to the specified rates: 5.3% for AVS/AI/IPG, 1.1% for AD/AC (up to CHF 148'200), LAINF between 0.7% and 1.5%, and LPP between 7% and 18% depending on age.

For health insurance, within three months of starting work, the frontier worker must exercise the right of option: choose whether to keep the Swiss LAMal or join the Italian SSN. This decision is irrevocable for one year and must be communicated to both the Swiss insurer and the competent authority in Italy.

From a tax point of view, although there is no withholding in Italy, it is necessary to declare Swiss income in the Italian model 730, indicating in the EC framework the amount of taxes already paid at source in Switzerland to obtain the tax credit. This step must be carried out annually by the expiry date of the tax return.

Frequently Asked Questions
Is the tax at source withheld in both Switzerland and Italy for border workers?
No, income tax at source is only withheld in Switzerland for frontier workers. Italy avoids double taxation through the tax credit to be declared in the EC framework of model 730, as provided for by the convention between the two countries.
When did the new Border Agreement between Italy and Switzerland come into force?
The new Frontier Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020 and entered into force on 1 January 2024, as determined by the source. The Italian ratification took place with Law 83 of 13 June 2023.
What are the rates of Swiss contributions that are withheld from the border worker's salary?
The Swiss contribution rates are: AVS/AI/IPG equal to 5.3% borne by the employee, AD/AC at 1.1% (with a maximum ceiling of CHF 148'200), LAINF between 0.7% and 1.5% depending on the sector, and LPP between 7% and 18% calculated based on the age of the worker (from 25 years).
