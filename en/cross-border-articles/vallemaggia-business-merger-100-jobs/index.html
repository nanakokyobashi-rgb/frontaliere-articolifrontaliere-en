Vallemaggia, a maxi-construction wedding saves 100 jobs (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 August 2026

Four construction companies are joining forces to form the new VDM Holding Ltd. Operational since 2027, the operation aims to ensure social and employment continuity in the valley.

Context

In brief

  • VDM Holding SA is born, a construction giant in the Maggia Valley.
  • The operation saves about 100 jobs in total.
  • Official operation set for January 1, 2027.
  • Companies will retain original names, traditions, and values.

Key Facts

  • What: Merger of four construction companies into a holding company
  • When: Operational from 1 January 2027
  • Where: Prato Sornico, Vallemaggia
  • Who: Vedova Trasporti SA, Demartini&Moretti SA, Mignami SA, Campana SA
  • Objective: Preservation of about 100 jobs

The news of the union between four historic construction companies in the Maggia Valley was made official last Friday in Prato Sornico, marking a crucial step for the local business landscape. The creation of VDM Holding AG represents a strategic operation aimed at consolidating the regional construction sector, guaranteeing stability to a hundred employees. Gabriele Dazio, mayor of Lavizzara, welcomed the announcement, calling it a strong message for the entire region. The initiative stems from the decision of Vedova Trasporti SA, based in Lavizzara, and Demartini & Moretti SA of Brontallo, to acquire Mignami SA of Cevio and the company Campana SA of Riveo. The latter, active for 90 years in the area, was seriously at risk of closure, bringing with it the real danger of leaving about fifteen families without a source of income. The synergy aims to create a solid entity capable of facing the challenges of the sector, while maintaining a management rooted in the Valmaggese area.

Operational details

The impact of this operation goes beyond the mere economic data, touching the very fiber of the social resilience of peripheral areas. For workers in the sector, this union represents a guarantee of stability, at a time when family businesses risk closure due to lack of succession. The fact that businesses remain run by people from the valley is seen as a positive sign for both current employees and the younger generation. The continuity guaranteed by VDM Holding SA allows it to maintain skills in the area that would otherwise be dispersed. Andrea Sartori, mayor of Maggia, highlighted how this initiative shows that it is possible to live, work and raise one's children in the valley, countering the phenomenon of depopulation.

Key points

The creation of the holding company, although an operation between companies, has direct implications for employees and for anyone looking for a job in the Ticino construction sector. The solidity guaranteed by the new corporate structure, which will become operational in 2027, ensures contractual continuity that represents a cornerstone for employees' family and financial planning. Those who work or intend to work in the construction sector in Ticino must pay attention to the solidity of their employer, especially in a context where company succession is often a critical issue. VDM Holding SA is an example of how the protection of human capital can coincide with the economic interests of the companies themselves, ensuring that families do not have to leave their region of residence to find stable and lasting employment.

Outlook for the construction sector

For the workers, the transition to the new holding company should not result in immediate changes in their daily lives, as the brands and traditions will remain unchanged.
