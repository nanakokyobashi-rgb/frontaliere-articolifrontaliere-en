The race not to be missed

On Sunday, October 11 at 3 pm, Varese returns to "Franco Ossola" to face Oltrepò on the seventh day of Group D of Serie D. The Biancorossi arrive at the event with two consecutive defeats, one in the league and one in the Italian Cup, and with eight goals conceded. The game is indicated as a fundamental junction for the team's path, called to quickly reverse course.

The internal confrontation against Oltrepò is also an opportunity to seek the first seasonal victory in front of your audience. After the two setbacks, Varese are called to react with maximum effort in a race that on the eve presents as a direct clash. The team must therefore find continuity in the game and greater attention during all phases of the game.

Andrea Ciceri presented the challenge on the eve, underlining the weight of the appointment and the need for an immediate response.

"We have to give an important signal: every game counts, but this one even more because it is a direct confrontation."

The red-and-white coach asked his team to continue producing games without letting themselves be conditioned by anxiety. At the same time, he called it imperative to raise the level of attention, because a drop was expensive, especially in Sunday's race. The figure recalled by Ciceri concerns the achievements of the opponents: in recent games they were too many compared to the times when the opponents managed to enter the Varese area.

For the coach, this is a wake-up call. The team must remain focused even when they have the feeling of having the race under control, without considering the apparent tranquility as a danger-free phase. The challenge with the Oltrepò therefore comes after two negative results and with a specific request: to change pace without giving up the game proposal.

Operational details

The work between the pitch and the mind

Varese's preparation for the match against Oltrepò developed on two fronts. In the two or three days following the last matches, work on the pitch was focused exclusively on the next fixture. On the mental side, however, Andrea Ciceri spoke directly with the team to address the lapse in concentration that emerged in recent performances.

The coach's message is above all about managing the different phases of the match. Anyone who wants to play and attack must also accept that in football there is no period of apparent calm during which it is possible to relax without taking risks. Offensive output, therefore, must be accompanied by concentration in every phase of play. This instruction comes after a series of matches in which the number of goals conceded to opponents was considered too high.

The opponent and lineup choices

Ciceri described Oltrepò as a team with a deep squad. For this reason, Varese expects different options or possible surprises and, on the eve, the coach did not yet have a clear idea of the opponent's starting eleven. Oltrepò is considered a hungry opponent eager to earn points.

The awareness required of the red-and-whites is just as clear: Oltrepò will come looking for the three points, but Varese must want the win more than its opponent. The match is therefore being prepared with both an assessment of the visiting squad and the need to heighten the concentration shown on the pitch.

Varese's lineup is also still affected by various factors. The staff is assessing some knocks, with players recovering and others not yet recovered. This is compounded by the energy expended during the week, which featured a closely spaced fixture. Final decisions will depend on the game plan, with no decision yet made about the players set to start.

Ciceri did make clear, however, that the distinction between starters and substitutes coming on must not change the collective objective. All players are training at full intensity to play a leading role and, regardless of who starts the match or comes on later, the requirement is for the entire group to do everything possible to bring home the win. Managing knocks and energy is thus intertwined with the mental response Varese must give after the two consecutive defeats.
