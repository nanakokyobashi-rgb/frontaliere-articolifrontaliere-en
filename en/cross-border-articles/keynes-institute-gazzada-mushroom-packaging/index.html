Frequently Asked Questions
What is the Nycelyum project developed by the students of the Keynes Institute in Gazzada?
Nycelyum is a mini-student company that uses mycelium — the vegetative structure of mushrooms — for the production of organic packaging. The idea is to cultivate mycelium to obtain a material of biological origin to be proposed as an alternative to traditional packaging solutions. The project involved scientific, technical, organisational, economic and communicative aspects.
When and where did the national final take place in which Nycelyum participated?
The national Enterprise in Action final, in which Nycelyum participated, took place in Rome on 4 and 5 June 2026. The mini-business entered the top 60 in Italy after passing the school's internal selection and the Milan regional final at Palazzo Regione Lombardia.
What were the awards received by the Keynes Institute of Gazzada Schianno?
The Keynes Institute has received a double recognition: at the national level, the mini-enterprise Nycelyum arrived among the top 60 in Italy at the national final in Rome; at the European level, the school obtained the 'The Entrepreneurial School' award awarded in Riga during the Gen-E event, recognition linked to the model of collaboration with universities and companies in the territory.
