Giubiasco FFS station: renovation completed

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 October 2026

The Giubiasco FFS station building will reopen on 8 October 2026 after work costing approximately 6,4 million: new services, lift and Minergie standards.

On 8 October 2026, the Giubiasco FFS station building will reopen to the public. The date marks the end of a redevelopment project that lasted several years, with a total investment of approximately 6,4 million francs. FFS has completed the full renovation of the passenger building, one of the most important railway hubs in the Bellinzona area.

A renovation involving structure and energy

The work did not focus on just one element of the building. The project addressed the structural, energy and functional aspects together, with the aim of adapting the building to the current needs of users, commercial businesses and services. For anyone passing through the station, this means finding a building redesigned in its main components, without separating access, technology and the internal offering.

The construction work included renovating the façades and roof, completely renewing the technical and electrical systems, replacing doors and windows and installing a new lift. The latter intervention is linked to improved accessibility, one of the stated elements of the redevelopment. Sustainability was addressed with a heat-pump heating system and by bringing the building into line with Minergie standards.

The reopening also concerns what travellers will find inside the building. Three commercial businesses are planned on the ground floor to meet the everyday needs of travellers and the local population. The source indicates a bakery-pastry shop, a point of sale for quick purchases and a third business that will open soon.

The upper floors, on the other hand, will house various services and professional activities, including services in the fields of health and personal support. For a cross-border commuter who uses the station on their journey, the practical takeaway is therefore a building that brings together mobility, shopping and services. Operational details

A station with multiple functions

For a cross-border commuter who uses the train to reach the Bellinzona area, the tangible effect of the redevelopment is concentrated in the building encountered during the journey. The source does not describe a simple aesthetic intervention: FFS have reorganized a building intended to house retail, services and professional activities. The announced result is a modern, multifunctional station capable of integrating mobility, retail and services into a single point of reference.
