Cotonificio LIUC protagonist of Industrial Quest 2026 (cross-border guide)

On September 12‑13, 2026, Castellanza represented Italy in the first edition of Industrial Quest: 46 sites in 15 countries, 5 teams at Cotonificio Cantoni LIUC, Zamponari 40 points, The foreigners 35.

On 12 and 13 September, LIUC – Università Cattaneo represented Italy in the first edition of Industrial Quest, the initiative promoted by ERIH – European Route of Industrial Heritage. Italian participation focused on Cotonificio Cantoni, with a route dedicated to traces of its history still visible on the university campus in Castellanza.

The initiative concluded after two days designed to bring the public closer to Europe’s industrial heritage through a participatory and itinerant format. At the various venues, museums, historic production sites and places of industrial memory offered activities to be completed with a smartphone: quizzes, observation exercises, estimation questions, searches for details of buildings and photographic challenges.

The first Italian challenge

The stated objective was to transform the visit into an active exploration experience. Participants were called upon to look more closely at spaces, architecture, machinery and evidence of the history of work and industry. Each site created its own quest based on the characteristics of the location, while local rankings made it possible to compare the teams’ results.

The pilot edition involved 46 sites in 15 European countries, from Germany to Finland, from Poland to Portugal and as far as Ukraine. In Italy, the initiative came to LIUC – Università Cattaneo for the first time. Castellanza was the only Italian site present in the first edition, and Cotonificio Cantoni LIUC was among the venues with more substantial participation.

Five teams registered at the Italian site. This places the Castellanza venue in a visible position within the European pilot, as it combines Italy’s presence with the response from the groups that chose the route dedicated to the cotton mill.

The score rankings also produced a positive result. «Zamponari», captained by LIUC lecturer Laura Cattaneo and winner of the local ranking, finished with 40 points, entering the upper part of the European ranking for individual performances. «Gli stranieri», the second LIUC team, obtained 35 points and placed in the same tier.

The outcome of the first edition is therefore twofold: the Cantoni-LIUC site attracted substantial participation in numerical terms, and two teams reached the upper tier of the European comparison. The experience offers an initial indication of interest in formats that combine industrial heritage, exploration of places and direct participation.
