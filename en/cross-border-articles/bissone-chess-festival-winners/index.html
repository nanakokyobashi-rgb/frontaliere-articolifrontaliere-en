The results in Bissone

Sunday, October 11, Bissone hosted the sixth edition of the Chess Festival, a day of chess with young promises and experienced players. The program proposed two distinct moments: a tournament dedicated to the Under-16s and, in the afternoon, a competition also valid for the Ticino Semilampo title.

The first part of the day opened with the Borromini Trophy. The race, reserved for the Under-16s, ended with Christian De Lucia in first place. At the end of the playoff, De Lucia passed Daniele Camponovo, class of 2015. The third step of the podium went to Ulisse Dusci.

Recently, the Panathlon Club Lugano had awarded De Lucia the "Support for young talents" award. The distinction preceded the success obtained in Bissone, decided in the playoff on Camponovo, and accompanied the result of the morning tournament.

In the afternoon, the Hotel Campione Trophy was held, which thisyear also awarded the title of Ticinese Semilampo Champion. It was won by the Lugano-born Vladimir Paleologu, with 6 out of 7 points.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who won the Borromini Under 16 Trophy?
The first place in the Borromini Trophy, reserved for the Under-16s, went to Christian De Lucia. The young chess player won a playoff on Daniele Camponovo, class of 2015. Ulisse Dusci finished in third place. De Lucia had recently been awarded the "Young Talent Support" award by the Panathlon Club Lugano.
Who won the Ticinese Semilampo Championship?
The title of Ticinese Semilampo Champion was won by Vladimir Paleologu, a player from Lugano, with a performance of 6 out of 7 points in the Hotel Campione Trophy. Behind him were Claudio Boschetti, already winner of several Ticino titles, and Giacomo Zecirovic of Club Tre Valli.
What is the next appointment for the Under 16 Grand Prix?
The Under 16 Grand Prix will continue with a new appointment on Sunday 18 October at Osteria Maggio in Viganello. The Grand Prix is supported by the Ticino companies Ennio Ferrari and Chicco d 'Oro. The next appointment, after the day in Bissone, therefore concerns the Under 16 category, that date and the venue already indicated for the Grand Prix.
