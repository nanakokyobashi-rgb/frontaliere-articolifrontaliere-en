With the first of September, Trenord tickets and passes increase (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 1 September 2026

From 1 September 2026, Trenord tickets and passes will undergo significant increases, with impacts on routes such as Gallarate-Milan.

Context

In brief

  • Trenord tickets and passes increase from 1 September 2026
  • The Gallarate-Milan route goes from 5.20 to 5.40 euros
  • Increases also for the Malpensa Express and I travel anywhere in Lombardy
  • What: Increase in Trenord tickets and passes
  • When: From 1 September 2026
  • Where: Lombardy
  • Who: Trenord
  • Amount: Gallarate-Milan ticket from 5.20 to 5.40 euros

From 1 September 2026, travelling on Lombardy's regional trains costs more. The new Trenord fares have come into force, with increases affecting tickets and passes and which are also felt on the routes used by travellers in the province of Varese. The new fares officially came into force today, 1 September 2026: Trenord has published both the new tariff and the previous one, valid until 31 August.

The adjustment concerns the travel tickets of the regional railway service and translates, depending on the kilometre range, into increases of a few cents on the single journey but also into more substantial increases for those who frequently use the train and buy season tickets.

An example particularly close to commuters and travelers in the province of Varese is that of the Gallarate-Milan route, connected directly by Trenord trains with journeys ranging between 35 and 50 minutes. For this relationship, which falls within the 21-50 kilometer kilometer range, the ordinary ticket goes from 5.20 to 5.40 euros.

Twenty cents more for a single trip: a small figure if observed on the single trip, but which takes on a different weight for those who travel the route several times during the month.

Also immediately registering the change is a reader of VareseNews who uses the train occasionally starting from Busto Arsizio. "The Busto Arsizio – Arcisate was 4 euros and now it is 4.10. The Busto – Milan is now 4.60," the reader points out.

Operational details

Implications for cross-border commuters

The increases in Trenord tickets and passes will have a significant impact on cross-border commuters who use trains daily to travel between Switzerland and Lombardy. Commuters using the Gallarate-Milan route, for example, will have to deal with an increase of 20 cents for each journey, which may seem small but which, during the month, can represent a significant additional cost.

Comparison with the previous situation

Before September 1, 2026, the ticket for the Gallarate-Milan route cost €5.20. With the increase, the cost rises to €5.40. This means that a commuter who uses the train five times a week will have to pay €2 more per month, and €24 more per year.

Concrete scenarios

For a cross-border commuter who works in Milan and lives in Gallarate, the ticket increase could represent an additional cost of €24 per year. This could affect spending decisions and travel plans. In addition, increases in weekly, monthly and annual passes could prompt commuters to look for transport alternatives or revise their travel plans.

Increases for the Malpensa Express

The Malpensa Express, Trenord's airport service, is also experiencing increases. The single ride from Milan to Malpensa costs €15, while the return ticket costs €25. These increases could affect travelers who frequently use Malpensa airport for business or leisure purposes.

Increases for I travel anywhere in Lombardy

The fares of I travel everywhere in Lombardy (Ivol) undergo significant increases. The one-day pass now costs 18 euros, while the monthly one rises to 118 euros. These increases could affect commuters who frequently use regional public transport to move around Lombardy.
