The Stabio border crossing represents a fundamental transit point for thousands of daily commuters, including frontier workers. With the entry into force of the New Frontier Agreement on January 1, 2024, the fiscal tax framework for these commuters has undergone significant changes. This agreement, signed on December 23, 2020, and ratified in Italy with Law 83 of June 13, 2023, defines new rules for the taxation of dependent employment income, with a direct impact on paychecks and tax returns in Italy.

The Swiss Withholding Tax Regime

According to the new provisions, the source tax on dependent employment income continues to be withheld in Switzerland. This means that frontier workers are not subject to tax withholdings 'in both countries', but only in the country where they perform their work. Italy, to avoid double taxation, uses the mechanism of tax credit, a principle already consolidated and applied through the EU CE Model 730. This procedure allows frontier workers to recover part of the taxes already paid in Switzerland, ensuring an equitable distribution of the tax burden between the two states.

Operational details

The implications of the New Border Agreement for those crossing the Stabio border are multiple and affect various aspects of personal financial management. Which income does the €10,000 limit apply to?

Where is the labour income tax withheld for frontier workers?
